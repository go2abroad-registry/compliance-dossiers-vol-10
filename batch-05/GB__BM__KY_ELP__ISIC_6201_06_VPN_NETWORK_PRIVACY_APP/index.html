E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN or VAT ID to Exempted Limited Partnerships. On formation, the ELP files with the Registrar of Companies and receives a certificate of limited partnership and company registration number. Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar is fixed at par to the United States dollar. Current-account transactions, including licensing fees, cloud hosting charges and VPN subscription service revenues, are fully convertible through authorised dealers. Capital-account convertibility is conditional for resident entities but generally available to a non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership upon filing the required beneficial ownership, source of funds, and economic substance declarations. Profit remittance and partnership distributions to non-resident limited partners are permissible without Bermuda withholding tax, provided the ELP maintains an exchange-control non-resident designation, files timely economic substance documents where applicable, and demonstrates that the distributed profits were funded by audited retained earnings or verified capital accounts. The primary practical hurdle is bank-level de-risking rather than statutory exchange controls; banks will require the underlying commercial contracts, capital contribution schedules and source-of-wealth evidence before converting large incoming balances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Bermuda's exchange-control practice, export receipts derived from digital services are subject to repatriation through an authorised dealer within 180 days of invoice date unless the ELP has obtained a non-resident designation or a specific exemption from the BMA. The mandatory conversion ratio is effectively 100% of identifiable export proceeds when settled through a Bermuda clearing account, meaning the full amount must be credited to the authorised dealer in BMD or USD and accounted for; no entitlement is granted to retain a percentage offshore. Foreign exchange proceeds may remain in a designated non-Bermuda account only if the underlying service is performed wholly outside Bermuda, the contracting counterparties are non-residents, and written documentary evidence of the exemption is maintained by the ELP. A Bermuda-resident ELP that does not obtain the non-resident exchange-control designation will be required to offer all export receipts to an authorised dealer within the 180-day window.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, and the regulatory framework administered for authorised dealers and deposit-taking institutions under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility is medium-to-high for cross-border operating payments and merchant settlements through regulated UK or European EMIs such as Wise, Currencycloud, Airwallex and embedded treasury providers; however, viability for initial capital escrow is low because the ELP must maintain a Bermuda-based registered address and a bank account capable of receiving partnership capital contributions through the Bermuda clearing system. An EMI may not hold Bermuda-dollar balances or provide a true BMD domestic account unless separately licensed by the BMA as a deposit-taking institution under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. Legal constraints include the requirement that any EMI serving the ELP must be licensed in its home jurisdiction, must maintain segregated client money, and must not be used as a pass-through for third-party settlements in a manner that could be characterised as unlicensed money services business activity in Bermuda. Stripe and Paymentwall are acceptable as payment gateways for VPN subscription receipts, but their settlement must be routed only to the ELP's named account or a licensed EMI operating under an express client-money framework.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Large and immediate capital concentration: more than USD 1 million is received from a limited partner within thirty days of ELP formation, with no operating history or commercial justification.

Root cause: Large and immediate capital concentration: more than USD 1 million is received from a limited partner within thirty days of ELP formation, with no operating history or commercial justification.

Defense evidence: Execute the Limited Partnership Agreement, capital commitment schedules, capital call resolutions, source-of-wealth files for each limited partner including tax returns, bank statements and exit documentation, ELP certificate of good standing from the Bermuda Registry, BMA AML/ATF beneficial ownership register, and a board resolution describing the purpose of the VPN Network Privacy App expansion.

Unusual third-party disbursement pattern: funds are paid to overseas network suppliers, resellers and contractors without simultaneous underlying service agreements or invoices.

Root cause: Unusual third-party disbursement pattern: funds are paid to overseas network suppliers, resellers and contractors without simultaneous underlying service agreements or invoices.

Defense evidence: Maintain a vendor contract file with executed service-level agreements, invoices corresponding exactly to each disbursement, independent verification of supplier business legitimacy, ultimate beneficial ownership of the supplier, and a cash-flow forecast showing the commercial logic of each payment trial.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant routing for a foreign limited partner to fund the ELP is a SWIFT MT103 single-credit instruction or MT104 bulk file sent to the Bermuda accepting bank through its United States dollar correspondent account in New York, with final delivery to the ELP Capital Escrow Account. The Field 70 narrative must clearly state 'PARTNERSHIP CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' and identify the ELP registration number, the Limited Partnership Agreement date and the relevant capital call notice; an ISO 20022 purpose code of CAPT or INV should be included where the routing bank supports the structured purpose code field. Before the first draw, the General Partner must lodge the capital call resolution, executed subscription schedules, source-of-funds declarations for every limited partner, the beneficial ownership register and any exchange-control non-resident designation obtained from the BMA. Dividends, distributions and capital withdrawals of a non-resident ELP may be routed outwards without prior exchange-control approval, but the bank will require the ELP to show a positive capital account, audited financial statements or management accounts, and a signed distribution certificate.Outbound operational and trade settlements should use a tiered clearing structure: SWIFT MT103 for supplier invoices, vendor payments and cloud hosting fees; MT202 COV for correspondent bank liquidity repositioning; and irrevocable documentary commercial letters of credit for high-value hardware or network equipment purchases above USD 100,000, with documents against acceptance or payment at sight in the beneficiary's country. Payments related to the VPN Network Privacy App should carry purpose codes reflecting the true service, using 'SERV' for software subscriptions, licensing and technical support and 'GOOD' for tangible equipment; Payments originating from Stripe or Paymentwall settlement accounts should never be comingled with third-party merchant funds and must be drawn only from the ELP's own merchant settlement balance. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for the Exempted Limited Partnership (Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on ordinary commercial activity) unless the ELP forms part of an in-scope multinational group with consolidated revenue of EUR 750M or more, in which case the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 imposes a 15% income tax on top-up profits under OECD Pillar Two.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda does not levy tax on dividends, distributions, profit shares, or capital gains remitted by an ELP to non-resident partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 60 days of the anniversary of the ELP's registration in Bermuda; the accompanying annual government fee is due by 31 May of each calendar year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before the 15th day of the 9th month after the close of the group's fiscal year; quarterly preliminary top-up tax instalments are due on the 15th day of weeks 1, 4, and 7 of the CITA accounting period where a top-up tax liability is indicated.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No local currency threshold for standard ELPs outside the CIT scope. For CIT-scope multinationals (consolidated revenue at or above EUR 750M, approximately BMD 1,125,000,000 using the Bermuda-USD peg), a Bermuda Local File and a group Master File aligned with OECD Chapter V must be maintained, with the documentation threshold being the EUR 750M group revenue threshold rather than a BMD turnover amount.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) apply to resident entities that carry on a relevant activity. A VPN / digital encryption software business is characterized as a distribution, IP holding, or financing activity if it merely receives royalties or funds intra-group loans; the ELP must demonstrate all core income-generating activities (strategy, risk management, custody of the software IP, and pricing of subscriber licences) are undertaken in Bermuda with adequate employees and expenditure. Annual ESR self-assessment reporting to the Registrar of Companies is mandatory; failure to satisfy the substance test outweighs the 0% headline tax rate and may result in deregistration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent filing (CIT Act 2024, section 73)

Statutory sanction: On conviction: fine up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 years; civil restitution of the full tax understated plus interest at 2% per month, and disqualification from holding a payroll tax licence or CIT registration.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where a partner knowingly participates in the fraud: each general partner is jointly and severally liable for the tax understated, directors of the general partner face travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and the court may order confiscation of personal assets traced to the understated top-up tax.
Shell company and economic substance failure (ESR Act 2018)

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Registrar may impose an administrative penalty of BMD 10,000 per breach, order deregistration of the ELP, strike it from the Exempted Partnerships register, and, for repeat offenders, publish an ESR non-compliance notice.

Director personal liability: At the ESR assessment, the designated general partner (or its directors) must attest the substance; knowingly signing a false ESR declaration attracts personal fines of up to BMD 250,000 and, in severe cases, imprisonment for 5 years; directors may be barred from serving as officers of any Bermuda-registered entity.
Anti-money laundering failure and penalties (Proceeds of Crime Act 1997)

Statutory sanction: A fine of up to BMD 1,000,000 and imprisonment not exceeding 14 years for money laundering; failure to report suspicious activity by the registered agent carries a separate penalty of BMD 250,000; property involved in the offence is subject to civil forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner and the Bermuda registered agent are personally liable for failure to file a suspicious activity report; the Supreme Court of Bermuda can issue a worldwide asset freeze and order the director's personal restitution up to the value of the laundered funds, together with a travel ban and extradition cooperation with the UK's Crown Prosecution Service.
Non-collection of employer payroll tax (Payroll Tax Act 1995)

Statutory sanction: Back-assessment of payroll tax at 16.5% on all Bermuda-sourced remuneration, a late payment surcharge of 10% for each 30-day period, interest of 1.5% per month, and a separate failure-to-register fine of BMD 25,000.

Director personal liability: Officers and the general partner of the ELP are jointly liable for unpaid payroll tax deductions even after liquidation; the Tax Commissioner may apply for an income deduction order and travel restriction against the person controlling the payroll, and criminal proceedings may result in 5 years' imprisonment for furtively diverting employment tax.
