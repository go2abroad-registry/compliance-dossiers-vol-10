Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Government of Bermuda, Office of the Tax Commissioner (portal: portal.taxcommissioner.gov.bm); Registrar of Companies for corporate registration (Portal: gov.bm)

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not operate a general income-tax TIN system. A foreign-owned exempted company obtains its Bermuda Company Number upon incorporation under the Companies Act 1981, filed via a licensed local registered agent. If the company employs staff, a Payroll Tax Account Number is registered with the Tax Commissioner. If the company falls within the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (OECD Pillar Two), a CIT Account Number and compliance registration are obtained. No VAT/GST TIN exists.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda Exempted Company under Companies Act 1981; entity-level tax exemption — no Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT/GST; not a pass-through entity) — Bermuda does not operate a general income-tax TIN system. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda Exempted Company under Companies Act 1981; entity-level tax exemption — no Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT/GST; not a pass-through entity)

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Bermuda domestic law. Exempted companies may elect exchange-control non-resident status, but for tax purposes they benefit from a statutory Tax Exemption Undertaking, which guarantees exemption from all taxes on income, capital gains, and distributions. Bermuda has not adopted domestic hybrid-mismatch elections; the Pillar Two CIT regime applies only to large multinational groups with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Bermuda does not levy VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; customs duties and stamp duties are limited to prescribed imports and local transactions

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST registration regime in Bermuda; importers of dutiable goods must register with Bermuda Customs Declaration Management System (CDMS)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST on domestic or exported supplies. Services supplied by the exempted company to overseas clients, including App Store Optimization and SEO localization services, are outside any Bermuda indirect-tax base; no zero-rating claim, fiscal certificate, or numeric counter-validation is required.

  • Not applicable for services — no Bermuda VAT zero-rating documentation required.
  • In the unlikely event of physical goods movement, Bermuda eCustoms import entry, commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading/air waybill would be required; however, this scope is service-only.

No mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping exists. Bermuda operates an eCustoms import declaration platform; tax authorities do not validate commercial invoices digitally for indirect-tax purposes.

No VAT/GST clawback or zero-rating fraud exposure exists in Bermuda. General compliance penalties apply under the Companies Act 1981 for late annual returns and under the Payroll Tax Act for employer payroll failures. The Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 imposes penalties for late filing, non-payment, or false statements solely for multinational groups within its large-enterprise scope. No export-related foreign-exchange repatriation deadline or associated penalty applies.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD) and enjoys full current and capital account convertibility. No exchange controls or profit remittance restrictions apply to Bermuda Exempted Companies. Dividends and capital repatriation are freely remittable, subject only to AML/KYC reporting thresholds. USD is legal tender accepted widely across all banking channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory rules mandate surrender or conversion of export proceeds. Bermuda Exempted Companies may retain earnings in BMD, USD, or any freely convertible currency, with no mandatory conversion timelines.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility for Wise B2B and Payoneer as non-bank EMIs. Both offer multi-currency accounts, low-cost SWIFT-equivalent transfers, and integrate with Bermuda business needs. Legal constraint: these EMIs do not hold a Bermuda banking license; they operate under UK FCA or US MSB authorization, so funds maintained are e-money not bank deposits. No Bermuda regulatory impediment exists, but the corporate entity must complete FI’s independent KYC and provide a certificate of incumbency and register of directors.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0.0% for standard Bermuda Exempted Companies; Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no dividend or interest withholding tax. A 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 applies exclusively to constituent entities of multinational enterprise groups with consolidated annual group revenue of EUR 750 million or more for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0.0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company to non-resident shareholders; distributions must still satisfy the Companies Act 1981 solvency test, be authorized by the board, and be recorded in board minutes.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 6 months after the end of the financial year; for a 31 December year-end, the return is due by 30 June of the following year. No automatic extensions.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 9 months after the anniversary of incorporation (the annual return date); no filing may be lodged more than one month before the due date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filed within 15 days after the end of each calendar quarter (30 June, 30 September, 31 December, and 31 March) whenever the exempted company employs any individual working in Bermuda.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Local File or Master File obligation applies to a standard exempted company. Mandatory Country-by-Country Reporting under the Bermuda Tax Reporting Regulations arises only for constituent entities of multinational enterprise groups with consolidated annual group revenue of BMD 850,000,000 (approximately EUR 750,000,000); such entities must additionally file the ESR return and the BTRS CbCR notification.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 and the Economic Substance Regulations 2018 require every exempted company carrying on a relevant activity within a financial reporting period to satisfy the income test, expenditure test, and management-and-control test, with all core income-generating activities (CIGA) performed in Bermuda. For this SEO/ASO localization agency, the relevant activity category is typically Distribution and Service Centre when services are billed to related parties, or Intellectual Property Holding Business if the company owns and exploits ASO/SEO algorithms, content libraries, or brand licensing rights. A pure third-party service agency earning revenue only from unrelated clients files a nil ESR and is not required to demonstrate economic substance beyond maintaining a registered office, a licensed resident agent, and statutory records in Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Re-invoicing

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under Bermuda law for fraud and theft with fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 14 years on indictment; HMRC may open a diverted profits tax enquiry at 25% of diverted profits plus interest and penalties up to 100% of the tax; the Bermuda Competent Authority will supply banking and corporate records to HMRC pursuant to the applicable information-exchange agreement.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize fictitious invoices are personally liable for restitution to the defrauded clients and, on conviction, may receive custodial sentences, travel bans, and asset-confiscation orders; the corporate veil is pierced for fraud, making shareholders and directors personally recoverable.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BMD 10,000 per violation plus daily penalties of BMD 1,000 per day of continuing default; escalation to BMD 100,000 for non-compliance with a Competent Authority enforcement notice; the Minister of Finance may apply to the court for a collection order, strike-off, or compulsory winding-up.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false ESR declarations face personal fines and imprisonment up to 5 years, disqualification from acting as directors of any Bermuda company, and civil orders requiring them to reimburse the enforcement costs of the Crown.
Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Criminal money-laundering charges under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 with imprisonment up to 14 years and unlimited fines on indictment; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may impose regulatory sanctions on the licensed corporate services provider and revoke its licence; all tainted funds are subject to civil forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face criminal confiscation orders, travel bans pending trial, freezing of personal bank accounts, and disqualification orders; approving transactions without AML checks is treated as consenting to the offence, exposing the individual to the same penalty as the principal offender.
False Statements in Statutory Filings

Statutory sanction: Fine up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years under the Economic Substance Act and Companies Act 1981; the Registrar may strike the company off the register and annul the certificate of incorporation.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for restitution to third parties who relied on the false registry records; Bermuda courts may make disqualification orders preventing the individual from managing any exempted company for up to 15 years.
Cross-Border Transfer Pricing and UK Diverted Profits Tax

Statutory sanction: HMRC may issue a diverted profits tax notice at 25% of the diverted profit, add penalties of up to 100% of the diverted profits tax for careless or deliberate behavior, and charge interest; no UK-Bermuda DTA exists to compel corresponding relief, leaving the group exposed to double taxation.

Director personal liability: UK senior accounting officers may be fined up to GBP 5,000 for each uncorrected tax-filing failure; directors who deliberately facilitate the avoidance may be prosecuted for the corporate offence of failure to prevent tax evasion under the Criminal Finances Act 2017, incurring personal fines, unlimited penalties, and potential custodial sentences.
