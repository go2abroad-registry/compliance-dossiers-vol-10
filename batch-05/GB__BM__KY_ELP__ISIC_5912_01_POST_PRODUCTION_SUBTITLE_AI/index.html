Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

The Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; entity registration by the Registrar of Companies; trade compliance by the Bermuda Customs Department. Official portal: https://www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

Non-resident entities incorporating an Exempted Limited Partnership in Bermuda are first registered at the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014, obtaining a Registry ID; no income tax or VAT TIN is issued. Once the partnership has Bermuda employees, an employer registration for Bermuda payroll tax and social insurance is made with the Tax Commissioner and the Contributory Pension Fund; if goods are imported, a customs registration/declaration code is obtained from the Bermuda Customs Department. Foreign partners do not receive a local TIN. In-scope multinational groups may additionally be subject to Bermuda Corporate Income Tax registration under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Non-resident entities incorporating an Exempted Limited Partnership in Bermuda are first registered at the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014, obtaining a Registry ID; no income tax or VAT TIN is issued. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No Bermuda check-the-box or hybrid-election regime; the ELP is statutorily transparent for Bermudian tax purposes. For U.S. federal tax classification, a partnership may be elected on Form 8832 or may be deemed a partnership/foreign disregarded entity, and OECD hybrid-mismatch rules may recharacterize payments in cross-border structures; these elections are made outside Bermuda.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda applies import customs duty, stamp duty, and specific consumption taxes, none of which extend to offshore AI services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST; Bermuda does not impose turnover-based indirect-tax registration. Customs registration is triggered at first importation; payroll tax registration is required at first employee payment with no monetary threshold (registered in BMD).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Bermuda does not levy VAT, so there is no export zero-rating or refund scheme. Cross-border AI subtitling and voice-cloning services are outside the Bermuda indirect-tax base and are not subject to customs duty, excise, or stamp duty.

  • No Bermuda VAT zero-rating document is required for service exports.
  • For physical media/goods re-export: Bermuda Customs Export Declaration, air waybill/bill of lading, commercial invoice and packing list.
  • For service transactions: signed contract, statement of work, acceptance certificate, and inbound bank remittance/credit advice evidencing payment.

No VAT e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists. Import/export entries are processed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Department’s declaration platform; exported AI/subtitling services require no digital stamping or API clearance.

There are no VAT zero-rating clawback penalties because no VAT exists; however, fraudulent customs declarations, undervaluation, or false economic-substance filings may attract civil penalties, forfeiture, criminal fines, or imprisonment under Bermuda customs and revenue statutes. Where cross-border tax credits or treaty benefits are claimed in the investor’s home jurisdiction, unsupported repatriation inconsistent with the documented contract may be recharacterized, triggering interest, penalties, and disallowed deductions in the recipient jurisdiction.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital account: fully convertible in both Bermuda and the United Kingdom. The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 and circulates interchangeably with USD; the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 but imposes no prior-approval requirement for fund flows relating to a non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP). The UK abolished exchange controls in 1979; sterling is fully convertible subject only to HM Treasury and Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation screening. Current account: no restrictions on trade-related payments or service fees. Profit remittance: the Bermuda ELP is tax-transparent in Bermuda, and distributions to non-resident limited partners are free of Bermudian withholding tax; in the UK the ELP is treated as a partnership for tax purposes, so remitted subscription income may be taxable to partners depending on their residence, permanent establishment risk, and the UK's non-domicile regime. A UK permanent establishment and UK-source activity assessment is required before repatriating revenue recognised through the UK gateway.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies in either Bermuda or the United Kingdom. Under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972, export receipts may be retained in any currency in a BMA-licensed bank; the ELP is only required to keep an end-of-month record of controlled-value transactions. The UK operates a complete free-market current-account regime, with no obligation to convert export proceeds into sterling and no surrender deadlines. Subscription revenue from automated subtitle transcription and AI voice-cloning services received via Stripe and Wise in USD, EUR, or GBP may be held indefinitely or converted at the ELP's commercial discretion.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (United Kingdom) and Bermuda Monetary Authority (Bermuda, British Overseas Territory)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for Wise and Stripe as payment rails; MEDIUM-HIGH for Airwallex and Currencycloud; LOW for EMIs that categorically exclude British Overseas Territory registered entities. Wise is authorised by the FCA as an Electronic Money Institution under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and accepts Bermuda-registered companies with digital-only KYC (registration certificate, register of partners, beneficial ownership declarations); funds are safeguarded at tier-1 UK banks and are not covered by the UK FSCS or any Bermuda deposit-insurance scheme. Stripe supports Bermuda-registered entities for subscription billing and applies standard beneficial-owner disclosure onboarding. Legal constraints: (a) EMIs are not deposit-taking banks, creating counterparty and safeguarding risk; (b) the ELP must evidence Bermuda economic substance or a valid exemption to satisfy enhanced due diligence; (c) AI voice-cloning and subtitle service descriptions may trigger platform acceptable-use reviews, requiring content-rights, model-provenance, and voice-talent consent documentation.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general corporate income tax (Bermuda imposes no income, capital gains, or VAT/GST tax on an Exempted Limited Partnership). A 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) applies under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 only to Bermuda constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million; a low-revenue, pass-through ELP is outside this charge.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, partnership distributions, profit shares, or capital returns of an Exempted Limited Partnership.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general TP documentation is required for the ELP because it is not subject to Bermuda income tax. Mandatory Local File/Master File obligations arise only if the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approx. BMD 750 million; the Bermuda dollar is pegged 1:1 to USD). In that limited case, documentation follows the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines integrated into Bermuda's CIT/GloBE regime.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Amendment Act 2018 applies to Exempted Partnerships. If the ELP carries on a relevant activity (e.g., service center where services are provided predominantly to related parties, or an IP business), it must demonstrate direction-and-management in Bermuda, physical premises, adequate staff, and local expenditure. A third-party AI subtitle/audio-dubbing studio is ordinarily outside the relevant-activity scope. The CIT Act's Substance-Based Income Exclusion (qualifying payroll and tangible asset based) provides routine-profit relief for in-scope MNE groups.
