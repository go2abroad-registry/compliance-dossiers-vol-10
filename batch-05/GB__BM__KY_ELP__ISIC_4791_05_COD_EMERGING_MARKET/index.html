Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Registrar of Companies (for Exempted Partnerships) and Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax and social insurance); Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection certificates are granted by the Bermuda Minister of Finance. For UK-linked compliance, the competent authority is HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership obtains its tax identity by filing accordingly: (i) an application for a Certificate of Exempted Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 with the Registrar of Companies; and (ii) an application under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (EUTPA) to the Minister of Finance for a certified undertaking that no income, corporation, capital gains, or similar taxes will be implemented until 2035. No separate Bermuda income tax TIN is issued; the EUTPA certificate and registered company number constitute the domestic tax identity. If the ELP is obliged to register for UK VAT or payroll tax, it must obtain a UK Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) and VAT number through HMRC.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership obtains its tax identity by filing accordingly: (i) an application for a Certificate of Exempted Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 with the Registrar of Companies; and (ii) an application under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (EUTPA) to the Minister of Finance for a certified undertaking that no income, corporation, capital gains, or similar taxes will be implemented until 2035. No separate Bermuda income tax TIN is issued; the EUTPA certificate and registered company number constitute the domestic tax identity. If the ELP is obliged to register for UK VAT or payroll tax, it must obtain a UK Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) and VAT number through HMRC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Bermuda. The applicable election is the EUTPA tax guarantee endorsement, which is irrevocable for the specified period and confirms full tax exemption on profits, income, capital gains, and distributions. For hybrid mismatch scrutiny arising from the UK interest on unitary or partnership loss deduction rules, the ELP may rely on the UK's non-resident partnership transparency rules under CTA 2010 Part 6A and hybrid regulations; no separate Bermuda statutory election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: no general VAT or GST; indirect taxation is limited to customs import tariffs, payroll tax, stamp duty, and accommodation taxes. UK gateway: VAT (including use of the Non-Established Taxable Person procedure).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: no VAT registration threshold applies; customs duties apply only to goods physically imported into Bermuda at rates ranging from 0% to 22.25% depending on tariff classification. For UK VAT, the current registration threshold is GBP 90,000 of taxable turnover for UK-established businesses; non-established businesses supplying goods to UK consumers have no threshold and register for VAT (or use an online market place with VAT obligations) when distance-selling conditions are met.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: not applicable because Bermuda does not levy an export VAT; exports of goods or services from Bermuda are entirely outside the scope of indirect taxation. UK: zero-rating may be claimed on qualifying supplies of goods exported to non-UK destinations provided the ELP holds explicit evidence of export (usually within 3 months of supply) and the goods have genuinely left the UK. For offshore services of zero-rated e-commerce, the place of supply rules for digital services must be reviewed per destination country; a Bermuda ELP using the UK as a distribution gateway must satisfy the UK VAT 'direct export' conditions to justify 0%.

  • Bermuda Customs export declaration or electronic entry (if goods physically leave Bermuda)
  • Standard commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or airway waybill (through the UK logistics gateway) evidencing physical export to the final destination market
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or MT103/SWIFT continuity showing collection of cash-on-delivery proceeds repatriated to the ELP's account
  • Destination country customs entry and import VAT receipt where applicable

Bermuda: no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime exists. Bermuda Customs uses the Automated Customs Entry system for declarations. UK: HMRC has mandated Making Tax Digital (MTD) API-based VAT filing for all VAT-registered businesses; e-invoicing and clearance are not mandatory for B2C e-commerce, but electronic supporting documentation must be retained for zero-rating claims.

Bermuda: no export VAT clawback mechanism exists as there is no VAT system. However, false declarations or misleading documentation for customs privileges may result in penalties under the Customs Act 1950, forfeiture of goods, and fines of up to BMD 100,000 plus three times the duty evaded. UK: any incorrect zero-rating claim or failure to produce substantive export evidence within 30 days of HMRC demand will cause VAT to become payable under VATA 1994; HMRC may assess unlimited back-tax, impose 30%-100% penalties under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007, and prosecute for fraudulent evasion where the arrangement involves sham cash-on-delivery documents.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BMD is fully convertible into USD at the fixed currency board peg, giving Bermuda a de facto full convertibility profile on current account and capital account transactions. Bermuda operates exchange control classifications under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and statutorily distinguishes resident from non-resident account holders. A designated non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership is generally granted permission to maintain BMD, USD, and multi-currency accounts without BMA case-by-case approval, provided the initial ECP notification and beneficial ownership forms are filed. Capital account convertibility for inbound contributions is freely permitted for non-resident ELPs, while current account profit distributions, trade settlement payments, intercompany charges, and expense disbursements are fully repatriable once the underlying KYC/AML clearance and exchange control forms are completed. The main hurdles are not convertibility but rather demonstrating economic substance, maintaining accurate partnership registers, and satisfying bank enhanced due diligence for cash-on-delivery cross-border ecommerce models.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda exchange control generally requires a resident exporter to surrender foreign currency export proceeds to an authorised dealer within the regulatory timeline, ordinarily 100% of eligible receipts within a period not exceeding one month, unless BMA-specific Exchange Control Permission grants a foreign currency retention account. For the target non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership, the entity is classified as a non-resident designate under Bermuda law, and accordingly export proceeds are exempt from mandatory surrender or conversion. This permits the ELP to retain USD and emerging-market currencies indefinitely in its operational treasury to fund upstream supplier payments, logistics settlements, and working capital requirements. The surrender obligation would only crystallise if the ELP were later treated as resident for exchange control purposes, which the current offshore structure and ECP designation are designed to prevent.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as the central monetary authority and exchange control administrator for the British Overseas Territory of Bermuda; the Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High as a complementary treasury and payment rail solution, Medium as the sole banking relationship, and Low as the principal BMD deposit holder. Airwallex, and similar licensed offshore digital EMI platforms, can be used effectively for inbound logistics remittance sweeps, multi-currency supplier disbursements, and local payout API settlement in emerging-market currencies. However, these EMIs are not licensed by the Bermuda Monetary Authority as deposit-taking institutions and therefore cannot legally hold BMD deposits, provide Bermuda domestic clearing, or act as an escrow agent with BMA-recognised capital treatment. The legal constraint is that any EMI operating into Bermuda must not conduct regulated deposit-taking activity or hold client funds in Bermuda without a local banking licence or exemption; the prudent treasury architecture therefore uses an EMI solely as a non-Bermuda payment overlay connected to a tier-one BMA-regulated bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions and partner advances must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the partner's bank to the Bermuda bank's corresponding account, with the beneficiary being the ELP's Capital Escrow Account in BMD/USD. Payments must carry a clear purpose code, typically 'C10 — Capital Contribution to Partnership' or the bank's designated investment remittance code, and must reference the partnership's certificate number to allow BMA exchange control matching. The Bermuda bank requires a completed source-of-funds package, including executed subscription notice, bank statement reflecting the originating account, signed LPA, and confirmation that the ELP is designated non-resident for exchange control. Because BMD is pegged at 1:1 to USD, no FX conversion occurs on injection; the USD funds are credited directly in equivalent BMD in the escrow account at cost-neutral conversion. The escrowed capital is released to the operational account only after the bank has received the beneficial ownership declaration and, where the bank's policy so requires, a further sworn source-of-wealth statement.Outbound settlement and trade disbursements flow through three primary rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 company-to-company wires for direct supplier and logistics contract payments; (2) commercial letters of credit issued via SWIFT MT700/MT710 for larger inventory purchases where the supplier requires bank settlement security; and (3) Airwallex or similar EMI APIs for local-currency payouts to last-mile logistics operators and COD remittance agents in emerging markets. For COD ecommerce specifically, the day's cash collections are aggregated by the logistics provider and remitted as a series of MT103 batch payments or via an EMI collection account to the Bermuda operational account; from that account, intercompany disbursements are executed by MT103 against invoices, while local supplier payments are converted from USD to the target emerging-market currency using the bank's sponsored FX rate or a pre-agreed NDF valuation. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% Bermuda corporate income tax on an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP); no capital gains tax, no inheritance tax, no business levy. A 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT Act 2023, effective 1 January 2025) applies only to Bermuda tax-resident constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, and the ELP is treated as a transparent pass-through entity outside that charge unless it is the ultimate parent of such a group.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, partnership distributions, profit shares, or capital gains paid by an Exempted Limited Partnership to its UK parent or any non-resident partner; no domestic WHT machinery exists under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 or the Income Tax Act framework.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Within 6 months after the ELP's accounting reference date (financial year end); the declaration covers the preceding financial year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda transfer-pricing documentation regime applies to an out-of-scope ELP; no Local File or Master File is statutorily required at the Bermuda level for ISIC 4791 retail activity. For in-scope CIT groups the mandatory threshold is consolidated group revenue of EUR 750 million (approximately BD$810 million), at which point OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines are incorporated by reference and the Ultimate Parent Entity must maintain a CbC Report, Master File and Local File for each Bermuda constituent entity engaged in controlled cross-border transactions with related parties.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 (as amended in 2021-2023) applies to relevant entities, including exempted limited partnerships, requiring any such entity carrying on a 'relevant activity' to demonstrate economic substance in Bermuda measured by CIGAs, employees, premises, expenditure, and senior management presence. E-commerce mail-order retail with COD settlement is not a listed relevant activity; therefore the ELP must file an annual ESR declaration, select 'None' as the relevant activity, and complete the applicable non-resident or substance-indicator boxes. Caution: if the ELP performs group-internal warehousing, inventory financing, or logistics services for the UK parent or any related party, the 'Distribution and Service Centre' and 'Financing and Leasing' categories may be triggered, requiring full substance in Bermuda or a transfer of tax residence to the UK, failing which the entity becomes a shell within the meaning of the ESR and HMRC's diverted profits regime.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Materially False Economic Substance Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act 2018 s.18, a materially false declaration is a criminal offence punishable on conviction by a fine up to BD$250,000 and/or up to 5 years' imprisonment; the Registrar may strike the ELP off the Register and the TIEA authorises automatic exchange of the false declaration file with HMRC.

Director personal liability: The general partner (and its directors) are personally and severally liable for the BD$250,000 fine and for restitution of any tax lost; UK HMRC may apply the Criminal Finances Act 2017 corporate offence of failure to prevent the facilitation of UK tax evasion against the corporate general partner, exposing natural-person directors to unlimited fines and disqualification orders; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed under the Proceeds of Crime (Bermuda) 2002.
Shell Company / Artificial Avoidance of UK Corporation Tax

Statutory sanction: HMRC will re-attribute the ELP's trading profits to the UK parent under CTA 2010 Part 9A and Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, charging UK corporation tax at 25%, plus late payment interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007; alternatively the Diverted Profits Tax at 31% may be charged with a notification penalty of up to £1,000 and a potential penalty of 30% of the unpaid DPT.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit the tax avoidance arrangement may be made personally liable to a penalty under Finance Act 2009 Schedule 56 for failure to pay tax; in egregious cases HMRC prosecutes 'cheating the public revenue' at common law, carrying imprisonment up to 7 years, plus confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act and director disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 for up to 15 years.
AML / Cash-on-Delivery Money Laundering Through Remittance Rails

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018 impose civil penalties up to BD$100,000 per breach; the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 makes failure to disclose suspicious activity (s.330) and tipping-off (s.333) offences punishable by up to 2 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines; the medium-risk enhanced tier owed to the ISIC 4791 COD model requires enhanced monitoring, and the FATF mutual evaluation may subject the ELP's UK parent (as a UK FSMA entity using Airwallex) to FCA enforcement.

Director personal liability: Natural-person directors, the general partner, and the UK parent's senior managers are personally liable for money-laundering offences under Bermuda POCA 2002 and UK POCA 2002 ss.327-329, with maximum imprisonment of 14 years for conspiracy to launder; civil recovery and account-freezing orders attach to the personal assets of directors who fail to implement the AML compliance program; UK directors risk FCA prohibition orders and section 56 notifications.
Persistent Non-Filing and Striking-Off

Statutory sanction: The Registrar issues a strike-off notice under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015, after which the ELP is dissolved; the general partner becomes jointly and severally liable for all partnership liabilities incurred after the dissolution date; a struck-off ELP cannot sue debtors, hold title to bank accounts, or enforce the Airwallex agreement; restoration requires a Supreme Court order and unpaid fees plus penalties.

Director personal liability: The general partner's directors are personally liable for the debts of the struck-off partnership where they caused or allowed it to continue trading; they face disqualification from managing any Bermuda entity for up to 5 years and may be named in a Bermuda civil recovery action; HMRC would treat the dissolved ELP as a UK taxpayer under the corporate veil-piercing doctrine of Prest v Petrodel, permitting a charging order over assets held in the ELP's name.
