Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda, via the Bermuda Tax Portal (taxportal.gov.bm); entity registry: Registrar of Companies, Bermuda; customs administration: Bermuda Customs and Revenue Agency (BCRA).

E-government filing portal

1) Appoint a Bermuda-licensed registered agent and file the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) registration under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 (as amended), submitting the partnership agreement, general partner declaration, and Bermuda registered-office address. 2) Obtain the Certificate of Registration and the Bermuda Entity Number, which serves as the primary tax/entity identifier (TIN). 3) If the ELP is part of an in-scope MNE group (consolidated revenue ≥ €750M under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023), register as a taxable entity on the Bermuda Tax Portal and obtain a CIT registration number linked to the Entity Number. 4) If the ELP has Bermuda-resident employees, register separately as an employer for Payroll Tax with the OTC. 5) If the ELP imports goods into Bermuda, register as an importer with BCRA; if it carries on business in Bermuda, obtain a Business Licence under the Business Licensing Act 2002. No VAT/TIN registration is required because Bermuda imposes no VAT or GST.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnerships are tax-transparent: income, deductions, and credits flow through to partners, and Bermuda imposes no income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT on ordinary commercial activity. In-scope MNE groups using an ELP are nonetheless subject, on a group basis, to the 15% domestic top-up tax/QDMTT under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, with profit allocation determined under OECD GloBE rules and the ELP's Bermuda administrative status (annual exempted partnership government fee).

Available election forms

Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, elections include: (i) election to treat a flow-through vehicle (ELP) as tax-transparent or as a taxable entity for CIT purposes where permitted by the GloBE Model Rules; (ii) the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) safe-harbour election; and (iii) elections on computing covered taxes and filing basis. No US-style check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch election exists. The UK VAT and UK Corporation Tax statutes do not extend to Bermuda.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST system in Bermuda. Indirect revenues consist of customs duties (Customs Act 1954 and Customs Tariff Act 2014, as amended), payroll tax, land tax, and annual government/business licence fees. Cross-border dairy commodity trading through an ELP is not subject to Bermuda indirect tax where the goods do not enter Bermuda customs territory.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold. Importers bringing whole or skimmed milk powder into Bermuda must obtain customs clearance from the Bermuda Customs and Revenue Agency (BCRA) and pay applicable duties under the Customs Tariff Act 2014; a Business Licence fee is payable only if the ELP carries on business in or from Bermuda.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Bermuda levies no VAT/GST on exports. Goods exported from Bermuda are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation, and customs duty applies to imports only. If dairy goods are temporarily imported into Bermuda and thereafter exported, a customs duty drawback is available under the Customs Act 1954 upon satisfactory proof of export. For in-scope MNE groups, export gross margin attributable to the ELP must be documented and allocated under transfer-pricing/GloBE rules; no Bermuda VAT refund mechanism exists.

  • Bermuda Customs export declaration or duty-drawback claim form (if goods entered Bermuda customs territory and are being re-exported); statement of non-importation for pure offshore trades
  • Original commercial invoices, purchase/sale contracts, bills of lading/air waybills covering the cross-border movement of whole and skimmed milk powder from origin to destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by a Bermuda-licensed bank under Bermuda Monetary Authority exchange-control procedures, or SWIFT MT103/TT wire proof of export proceeds
  • Contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation and GloBE allocation schedules (where the ELP is part of an in-scope MNE group)

Not applicable. Bermuda does not require real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing APIs, XML/UBL formats, or digital stamping for indirect-tax purposes. Record-keeping follows commercial banking standards (Swift LC, TT Wire) and, for in-scope groups, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 record-keeping and GloBE documentation rules.

Bermuda has no export VAT zero-rating and therefore no VAT clawback mechanism. The analogous exposure arises under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023: misstatement, evasion, or abusive allocation can trigger reassessment to the 15% domestic top-up tax/QDMTT, statutory interest, fixed and daily penalties, additional tax up to 100% of the undercharge, and criminal sanctions. Under the Customs Act 1954, false export declarations or unlawful removal of dutiable goods attract seizure, forfeiture, and fines. The Bermuda Monetary Authority may also impose exchange-control penalties for unapproved or inadequately documented inward remittances.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates no exchange-control restrictions and the Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at par (1:1) to the US dollar (USD), which circulates freely in the domestic payments system. Capital and current account transactions are fully convertible: the Exempted Limited Partnership may receive, hold, convert and remit USD, EUR, GBP or any hard currency without BMA licensing or prior approval, and profit distributions, returns of partner capital and liquidation proceeds may be transferred to non-resident partners without Bermudian withholding tax. Residual remittance friction is bank-level only: beneficial-ownership transparency, economic substance evidence where the partnership carries on a relevant activity in Bermuda, trade documentation substantiating the milk-powder commodity flows, and sanctions/PEP screening of the full correspondent clearing chain.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda has no statutory export-proceeds surrender, conversion or repatriation timeline. Whole and skimmed milk powder sales proceeds denominated in USD, EUR, GBP or emerging-market currencies may be retained indefinitely in Bermudian or foreign bank accounts, applied directly to trade payables, or converted at the bank's prevailing market rate with no mandatory local-currency conversion ratio.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium - suitable as a supplementary treasury rail, not as the primary settlement node. An FCA-authorised UK EMI or EU-licensed e-money institution may lawfully hold segregated client funds for the ELP, supply multi-currency virtual IBANs, and support administrative expense disbursements; however, a foreign EMI is not BMA-licensed to take deposits or carry on money-service business in or from Bermuda, so it cannot represent itself as a Bermudian bank account and must not be used for documentary credits, trade-contract settlement, or collection of third-party trade proceeds. To maintain regulatory cleanliness, EMI balances should be swept daily by SWIFT MT103 into the primary USD operating account at the Bermudian tier-1 bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no dividend/interest/royalty withholding tax, and no remittance tax). From fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, a 15% GloBE top-up tax applies only to in-scope multinational groups with annual consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; the standard Exempted Limited Partnership is outside the scope.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda does not impose any withholding tax, income tax, or capital gains tax on distributions, dividends, profit shares, or capital returns made by the ELP to its partners, whether resident or non-resident.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within six (6) months after the end of the ELP's financial year; e.g., a 31 December year-end return is due by 30 June of the following year. The Registrar issues an annual notification with the exact due date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before sixty (60) days following the anniversary of the ELP's date of registration; the Registrar sends an automated reminder and the online filing window shows the exact due date per entity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold exists in the zero-tax regime. If the ELP or its partners become subject to UK taxation: CbC Report is mandatory for multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750 million or more (approximately BMD 750 million); UK Local File and Master File must be prepared contemporaneously upon HMRC request for any related-party transaction under TIOPA 2010 Part 4, regardless of transaction size, and audit-ready TP documentation is expected for all cross-border dairy commodity trades priced above arm's length benchmarks.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 (as amended) and the Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to exempted partnerships engaged in a relevant activity, including 'Distribution and Service Centre', 'Financing and Leasing', 'Holding Business', 'Headquarters', and 'Shipping'. For ISIC 4633 raw milk powder trading, the relevant activity test is triggered only if the ELP buys goods from, or distributes goods to, group affiliates; pure third-party commodity trading is generally outside the listed relevant activities but must be declared as 'None' and supported by documentary evidence. An ELP conducting a relevant activity must demonstrate: (i) direction and management exercised in Bermuda; (ii) adequate full-time employees, physical premises, and operating expenditure in Bermuda; (iii) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda. For UK tax purposes, the economic substance doctrine under the Ramsay principle and UK CFC rules supplements the Bermuda test: an ELP that is a mere shell with management in the UK will be disregarded or re-characterized by HMRC, and its profits will be taxed at the partner level. ESR declarations are exchanged with HMRC under the UK-Bermuda Tax Information Exchange Agreement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Misleading Economic Substance Declaration / ESR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Automatic late penalty of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 10,000 per month of continued default; false or misleading ESR return attracts a fine of up to BMD 250,000 and/or imprisonment up to 2 years; the Registrar may deregister the ELP and HMRC may receive the file under the UK-Bermuda TIEA and open a UK tax enquiry.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any Bermuda-resident director who consented to or connived in the false declaration is personally liable for the fine and imprisonment; in the UK, the same individuals face penalty up to 100% of the tax understated under TMA 1970 Sch 24, director disqualification orders, and fraud prosecution.
De Facto UK Tax Residence / Tax Evasion Through Shell ELP

Statutory sanction: HMRC assessment of UK corporation tax at 25% on the entire ELP profit if the ELP is treated as UK-resident, or assessment of UK-resident partners on their full profit share; late payment interest; penalties under TMA 1970 Sch 41 (failure to notify, up to 100% of potential lost revenue) and Sch 24 (inaccurate returns, up to 200% for deliberate concealment); potential closure of UK EMI bank accounts and worldwide asset freezes.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners may be jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax debt under UK insolvency principles and CTA 2010 s.767; the Criminal Finances Act 2017 creates a strict liability corporate offence for facilitating tax evasion, with unlimited fines; individuals face imprisonment of up to 7 years for cheating the public revenue and 10 years under the Fraud Act 2006, plus travel bans and restraint orders.
Beneficial Ownership and AML Disclosure Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Beneficial Ownership Act 2017 penalties of BMD 2,000 per offence and BMD 200 per day for continued default; economic substance declaration cross-defaults; UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 gives unlimited fines for money laundering and a maximum of 14 years' imprisonment; the ELP's Bermuda registration may be struck off and the UK banking relationship terminated with a suspicious activity report filed with the FCA/NCA.

Director personal liability: Any designated general partner or compliance officer who makes a false BOR statement is personally liable to imprisonment up to 2 years and an unlimited fine; directors may be disqualified under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986; beneficial owners face personal confiscation orders equal to the value of laundered dairy trade proceeds and UK travel bans.
Trade Sanctions Evasion and Illicit Commodity Financing

Statutory sanction: HM Treasury OFSI civil monetary penalties of up to GBP 20 million or 100% of the value of the trade, whichever is higher; OFAC penalties of up to USD 300,000 per violation (or twice the transaction value); asset freezing and designation of the ELP, its partners, and the underlying shipping/letter-of-credit channels; criminal prosecution under the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 with penalties of up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors who knowingly participate in sanctions evasion face personal criminal liability, UK-wide travel bans, director disqualification, extradition to the US in OFAC cases, and personal civil restitution equal to the value of the trade put through the Bermuda ELP; insurers and banks will subrogate against the individuals under fraud and dishonesty exclusions.
