Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Step 2: File the ELP application (Form ELP1) and certified partnership agreement with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Limited Partnership Act 2015; the Registrar issues a Certificate of Registration (the statutory 'TIN-equivalent' identifier is the Bermuda company/partnership registration number). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. A Bermuda ELP is not a taxable person for Bermuda income-tax purposes (Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax or capital gains tax); the ELP is fiscally transparent and profits flow to the limited/general partners, who are taxed in their respective residence jurisdictions. The ELP is treated as a partnership (default) for US check-the-box purposes and is generally transparent under UK tax law absent a UK permanent establishment or UK central management and control. Out-of-scope for Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (15% Pillar Two/QDMTT) unless it belongs to an MNE Group with consolidated revenue ≥ €750M.

Available election forms

(1) US check-the-box election (Form 8832): the ELP may elect corporate/association classification instead of the default partnership treatment; (2) Bermuda Tax Assurance Certificate election: ministerial exemption from future Bermuda taxes through 31 March 2035; (3) Economic Substance filing elections under the Economic Substance (Bermuda) Act 2018, including the choice of fiscal year end and the classification of the relevant activity as 'distribution and service centre'; (4) CRS/FATCA self-classification election (Financial Institution vs non-financial entity) filed with the Bermuda Tax Authority; (5) UK anti-hybrid planning: the ELP may rely on its transparent classification in the UK only where no mismatched deduction arises under Part 6A TIOPA 2010 and the UK CFC rules are not engaged.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: no VAT/GST or sales/use tax; indirect taxation is limited to Customs Duties (Customs Tariff Act 1992) levied on goods imported into Bermuda for consumption. UK (sovereign gateway): value added tax (VAT) under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 applies to UK-market supplies, with import VAT at the UK border.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover-based registration threshold exists in Bermuda because no VAT/GST is imposed. For UK VAT nexus: (i) a non-UK/EC business that imports matcha into the UK for onward sale must register for UK VAT from the first £1 of import (no threshold); (ii) distance/consignment sales of goods not exceeding £135 per consignment through an online marketplace are deemed supplied by the marketplace, relieving the ELP of UK registration for those transactions; (iii) under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, CIT registration is mandatory only for in-scope MNE Groups with consolidated revenue ≥ €750M (Pillar Two/QDMTT).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Bermuda: Bermuda levies no VAT and no refundable export tax exists (exported goods attract no Bermuda customs duty). Where the ELP sells through the UK gateway, UK zero-rating for direct export of goods outside the UK applies under VATA 1994 s.30 and SI 1995/2518 (0% rate) provided the ELP is VAT-registered in the UK, holds the statutory export evidence, and the goods are physically exported within the statutory time limits; goods moved to EU customers trigger EU place-of-supply/reverse-charge rules rather than zero-rating if supplied to a registered EU B2B customer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK CDS entry or Bermuda Customs export clearance form)
  • Commercial invoice (seller's original) stating matcha product description, organic certification, quantity, and value
  • Packing list
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill/CMR consignment note (road/air to EU)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/telegraphic-transfer credit confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)
  • Organic matcha-specific evidence: EU/UK organic import certificate (TRACES notification) and lot-specific certificate of analysis

No real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance regime exists in Bermuda; Bermuda Customs & Border Protection requires electronic import/export declarations and cargo manifest submissions through its customs clearance system. For the UK gateway: HMRC's Customs Declaration Service (CDS) is mandatory for all UK import/export declarations (CHIEF decommissioned); quarterly VAT returns (and digital record-keeping) are compulsory under Making Tax Digital for all VAT-registered businesses via HMRC-recognised software; HMRC does not currently mandate a national continuous-transmission e-invoicing clearance for B2B invoices, but EU e-invoicing frameworks (e.g., Italian SDI) apply where EU counterparts are invoiced.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged by statute at a fixed 1:1 parity to the United States Dollar (USD), and USD circulates freely as legal tender in Bermuda. The jurisdiction maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility: there are no exchange-control approvals, no surrender requirements, and no restrictions on the repatriation of profits, distributions, capital withdrawals, or loan repayments to non-resident limited partners. An Exempted Limited Partnership may remit trade profits and operating proceeds in the original currency of receipt without BMA prior authorization. The only practical hurdle to profit remittance is the standard AML/CFT screening performed by the paying bank; no substantive FX convertibility barrier exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation for export proceeds derived from the sale of organic matcha powder. The Exchange Control Act 1972 is administered through blanket general permissions issued by the BMA; exporters and trading partnerships may retain receivables in any freely convertible currency, hold BMD or USD balances indefinitely, or convert to other currencies at their discretion. No documentation beyond ordinary trade evidence is required for the retention or deployment of export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the central bank and integrated financial supervisor of Bermuda, a British Overseas Territory under the ultimate sovereignty of the United Kingdom; the Bank of England does not exercise direct regulatory jurisdiction over Bermuda's banking, currency, or foreign-exchange framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium viability as a primary treasury rail. Bermuda does not operate a dedicated fiat electronic money institution licensing framework; firms dealing in digital assets fall under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018, which does not confer fiat settlement-account or e-money issuance powers. BMA-regulated banks treat foreign EMIs as intermediary payment processors rather than clearing counterparties. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Airwallex, Ebury, Corpay) may onboard the Bermuda ELP as a supplementary collection channel for UK/EU e-commerce matcha receipts, but they typically require a demonstrable UK/EU revenue nexus, impose transaction thresholds, cannot execute BMD-denominated clearing, and cannot issue commercial letters of credit. The ELP should therefore adopt a dual-rail strategy: a primary BMD/USD settlement account at a Bermudian commercial bank, with FCA-authorised EMIs layered exclusively for last-mile marketplace collections.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections are executed via SWIFT MT103 / ISO 20022 pacs.008 messages carrying purpose code 'EQUI' for equity contributions to the partnership, or 'GDSV' for working-capital injections tied to the purchase of goods for resale. Each wire references the ELP's Bermuda registration number, the specific capital contribution notice, and a field-70 instruction limited to 'Capital Contribution - Exempted Limited Partnership - Organic Matcha Trade'. No BMA pre-approval is required. Where a limited partner is a Chinese resident entity, the remitting Chinese bank will require proof of SAFE outbound investment registration (ODI) before effecting transfer, and the Bermudian receiving bank customarily requests the SAFE filing evidence as part of enhanced due diligence.Outbound settlements for organic matcha procurement are cleared through: (i) SWIFT MT103 wires with ISO 20022 purpose code 'GDSV' for direct purchase of trade goods from Japanese and Chinese suppliers; (ii) irrevocable commercial letters of credit issued by the Bermuda Tier-1 bank and advised through a Japanese or US confirming bank under UCP600, with documentary compliance against organic certificates, phytosanitary certificates, commercial invoices, and clean bills of lading; and (iii) interbank treasury sweeps from the BMD/USD operational account into multi-currency sub-ledgers supporting GBP, EUR, and JPY disbursements. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% standard Bermuda corporate income tax rate for an Exempted Limited Partnership; 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies only to constituent entities of multinational groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750m in at least one of the preceding four years

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no statutory withholding tax on distributions, dividends, or partnership profit shares paid to non-resident partners

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Bermuda Local File or Master File requirement for a standard exempted limited partnership under ordinary legislation. For an MNE group that becomes subject to the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the relevant threshold is consolidated revenue of BMD 750,000,000 (equivalent to EUR 750m) in at least one of the preceding four years, triggering GloBE aggregate information and transfer pricing documentation aligned with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 requires a Bermuda tax resident entity, including an exempted limited partnership, that carries on a 'relevant activity' to demonstrate sufficient economic substance in Bermuda with regard to its core income-generating activities. For a matcha powder distribution business, the relevant activity category is 'distribution and service centre' if any procurement, logistics, sales contracting, staffing, or management decisions are exercised in Bermuda. An ELP that merely holds foreign distribution contracts and has no Bermuda office, employees, or significant management activity must not claim economic substance in Bermuda and should be prepared to demonstrate that its core income is attributable to foreign operations rather than a Bermuda shell

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Corporate Income Tax Filings

Statutory sanction: Imposition of underpaid tax, interest, and penalties under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; on conviction for tax evasion, fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years, with confiscation orders in egregious cross-border fraud cases

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, or shadow officers who approve false filings can be made personally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; courts may impose travel bans, asset freezing orders, disqualification from acting as a director for up to fifteen years, and custodial sentences in serious cases
Shell Company and Sham Loss Allocation

Statutory sanction: Civil recovery under Bermuda law, penalty arising from false ESR filing or false annual return, potential annulment of the exempted partnership status, and exposure to anti-abuse rules in the partner's jurisdiction; UK HMRC may impose offshore tax penalties of up to 200% of the avoided tax if the shamming is notified under the UK'S enclosure regime

Director personal liability: General partners and directors may face fraudulent trading claims, clawback of distributions, and disqualification; the corporate veil can be pierced where the ELP is used as a shield for personal liabilities, leading to civil restitution, worldwide asset tracing orders, and criminal prosecution under the Proceeds of Crime Act
Anti-Money Laundering and Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties imposed by the Bermuda Financial Intelligence Agency and the Bermuda Monetary Authority, fines on the partnership, and imprisonment of responsible compliance officers for up to five years; repeat or systemic failures can result in revocation of the exempted partnership registration

Director personal liability: Directors or general partners who knowingly permit AML deficiencies are exposed to personal criminal liability, civil fines, travel restrictions, and imprisonment; individuals may also be made subject to director disqualification orders and joint and several liability for compensation orders
Economic Substance Act Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to BMD 250,000, daily penalties for continued non-compliance, suspension or termination of the exempted partnership status, and exchange of information with foreign tax authorities; the partnership may be required to file corrected accounts and undergo a substance review

Director personal liability: General partners and directors who knowingly cause the deficit in economic substance can be held personally liable for fines imposed on the partnership; courts may order restitution to the Crown, impose travel bans, and in egregious cases commit the individual for contempt
Fraudulent Distribution and Dividend Stripping

Statutory sanction: Judicial clawback of distributions, voidable transaction proceedings, civil penalties for wrongful trading, and criminal liability for fraudulent trading under Bermuda's insolvency and partnership legislation

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners can be ordered to repay wrongfully extracted value, jointly and severally with the recipients; the corporate veil is pierced where the distribution is a sham or unlawful, allowing creditors to pursue the personal assets of the directing partner, with potential custodial sentences for fraudulent trading
