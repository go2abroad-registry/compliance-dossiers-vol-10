Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) for UK national taxes, accessed through the HMRC Government Gateway portal; Bermuda local tax authority: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda, accessed through the official Bermuda Government portal at www.gov.bm. Entity registration for the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is administered by the Bermuda Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership does not obtain a Bermuda income tax TIN or VAT ID because Bermuda imposes no income tax and no VAT on the ELP. The ELP is registered with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 and receives a commercial registration number. If the ELP has Bermuda employees, the general partner must register with the Bermuda Tax Commissioner for payroll tax as an employer. If the ELP establishes any UK tax nexus, such as a permanent establishment, UK-resident partners, or UK taxable supplies, it must apply to HMRC through its online services for a Unique Taxpayer Reference, PAYE reference, and VAT registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or local entity classification election because it imposes no income tax. The ELP is not taxed at entity level in Bermuda; each partner is taxed in its own jurisdiction. US partners may make a US federal check-the-box election on IRS Form 8832 to classify the ELP as a partnership or corporation for US federal tax purposes. Under UK tax law, partnership transparency is mandatory and not elective; hybrid mismatch rules under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A may recharacterise tax treatment where another jurisdiction grants entity-level treatment and creates a mismatch.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Bermuda VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax; Bermuda imposes customs duty on imports. UK VAT applies only if the ELP makes taxable supplies through a UK establishment or otherwise acquires a UK VAT nexus.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No Bermuda VAT or GST registration threshold exists because Bermuda does not levy VAT or GST. Commercial importers must be registered with Bermuda Customs regardless of turnover. In the UK, where UK VAT nexus arises, the VAT registration threshold is GBP 85,000 of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so there is no Bermuda zero-rating or VAT refund mechanism. Where UK VAT registration is separately required, exports of goods from the UK to a non-UK customer may be zero-rated under VATA 1994 section 30 if the goods are physically exported within applicable time limits and the ELP retains valid commercial evidence of export. Cross-border business-to-business services supplied to a non-UK customer are generally outside the scope of UK VAT or reverse-charged rather than zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Bermuda has no real-time VAT e-invoicing, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control mandate. Bermuda customs import and export declarations are submitted electronically through the Bermuda Customs clearance system. In the UK, VAT-registered businesses must file returns using HMRC-compatible software under Making Tax Digital, but there is no mandatory UK e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance requirement.

Bermuda customs authorities may forfeit goods, claw back duty relief or drawback, and impose fines and imprisonment for false export declarations or smuggling. For any UK VAT zero-rating claim, HMRC may assess under-declared VAT, charge interest, and impose penalties up to 100% of the potential lost revenue under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24. Fraudulent export arrangements can result in criminal prosecution and unlimited fines or imprisonment. Where UK anti-hybrid or international tax rules apply, failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day benchmark without commercial justification may trigger deemed income, denial of relief, or clawback adjustments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar. Current account payments for trade in goods and services are freely convertible through BMA-authorized dealers. Capital account transactions for a non-resident-designated Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) – including inbound equity contributions, loan injections, distributions, and liquidation surpluses to non-resident partners – are permitted without prior BMA approval provided the partnership holds a non-resident status letter and funds are routed via a Bermuda licensed bank. Profit repatriation is unrestricted for foreign-held ELPs; a standard regulatory filing fee may apply to the bank, not the customer. Hard-currency accounts in USD, EUR, GBP, and JPY are permitted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender, conversion queue, or statutory retention period on export proceeds for an ELP designated as non-resident. Receipts may be held in any freely convertible currency. Bermuda authorized banks file an Exchange Control Return (Form EC2) with the BMA on a quarterly or annual basis for statistical tracking of foreign currency receipts, but no conversion ratio or timing threshold is applicable to such ELPs.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) – designated exchange control authority under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972, operating in coordination with HM Treasury for monetary and financial stability of Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low viability as primary settlement bank, medium as supplementary disbursement rail. Bermuda does not issue standalone EMI licenses; only licensed banks or credit unions may accept deposits or provide settlement services in BMD. UK/EU FCA-regulated digital EMIs (e.g., Payoneer, Wise) are generally not accepted as 'authorized dealers' under BMA exchange control and will trigger enhanced due diligence for any inbound capital. An offshore EMI may be used as an auxiliary outbound disbursement wallet for small vendor payments, but the core liquidity clearing node must be a Bermuda bank with US and UK correspondent networks.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda: nil (no Bermuda corporate income tax, no statutory TP documentation or CbCR threshold). UK perspective: a UK Local File is required for the ELP if its UK PE has related-party revenue above GBP 1 million per annum or intra-group financing above GBP 1 million; a UK Master File is required where the worldwide group has consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million. Transfer pricing adjustments under TIOPA 2010 Part 4 may be raised by HMRC in all cases where connected-party transactions are not at arm's length, regardless of the partnership's offshore form.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda ESR 2018, as amended, applies to resident ELPs (and non-resident ELPs for information purposes) engaged in a relevant activity. The target activity - manufacture of amorphous silicon/CsI DR detectors - is not itself a relevant activity, but distribution and service centre, financing/leasing, headquarters and IP business functions are covered. An ELP exploiting digital X-ray detector IP or centralising procurement/distribution for the UK group must satisfy the full substance test in Bermuda (core income-generating activities, premises, full-time employees, adequate expenditure and senior management in Bermuda); a pass-through treatment does not exempt the ELP from ESR reporting.
