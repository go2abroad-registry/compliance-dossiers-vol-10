Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC) of Bermuda, Ministry of Finance; Registrar of Companies for entity registration; Bermuda Tax Portal (https://www.portal.gov.bm). Administration of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA) is centralised under the OTC.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company (BEC) obtains (1) a statutory Company Registration Number from the Registrar of Companies at incorporation (mandatory, no fee-for-service alternative); (2) a CIT Registration Number from the Office of the Tax Commissioner only if the BEC is part of an in-scope MNE group with annual consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more (CITA effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025); (3) a Payroll Tax Account Number if the BEC becomes an employer of Bermuda-resident individuals. Entities with no CIT nexus, no Bermudian payroll, and no import activity receive no general-purpose TIN; the company registration number serves as the universal identifier.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company (BEC) obtains (1) a statutory Company Registration Number from the Registrar of Companies at incorporation (mandatory, no fee-for-service alternative); (2) a CIT Registration Number from the Office of the Tax Commissioner only if the BEC is part of an in-scope MNE group with annual consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more (CITA effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025); (3) a Payroll Tax Account Number if the BEC becomes an employer of Bermuda-resident individuals. Entities with no CIT nexus, no Bermudian payroll, and no import activity receive no general-purpose TIN; the company registration number serves as the universal identifier.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box analogous domestic classification; BECs are per se income-tax-exempt unless in-scope under CITA. CITA elections available: (a) election to form a Bermuda group tax-filing unit (joint CIT return); (b) election for Economic Transition Adjustment timing; (c) election to apply existing accounting period date; (d) irrevocable election under transitional rules for the determination of qualifying assets/liabilities. Bermuda has no hybrid mismatch legislation given the absence of domestic corporate income tax; off-shore BECs are statutorily excluded from Bermuda income tax under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None. Bermuda operates no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; indirect levies are limited to customs duties on imported physical goods. Registered agent and offshore secretarial services are wholly outside any services indirect tax net.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration regime exists. Importers of goods must obtain customs-import clearance but this does not create recurring indirect tax registration; costs of imported goods are extinguished by customs duty at rates between 0% and 33.25% depending on tariff head.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda has no VAT zero-rating regime; cross-border supply of registered agent or secretarial services is simply outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax. Consequently, no VAT refundable input credit, no zero-rating claim mechanism, and no export of goods (zero-rated) regime arises. CIT, if applicable, is levied on consolidated global profits of in-scope MNE groups, not on export-specific margins.

If physical consumables are shipped, a Bermuda Customs Import Declaration (C-88) applies at import, not export.

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital fiscal-lottery mandate exists. BECs may issue manual or electronic commercial invoices at their discretion without government clearance or cryptographic stamping.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at parity to the US Dollar; both BMD and USD are accepted interchangeably in the domestic financial system. Bermuda operates a fully convertible, no-exchange-control jurisdiction. Current account convertibility is unrestricted for trade in services, management fees, dividends, interest, royalties and distributed profits. Capital account convertibility is equally unrestricted for share capital subscriptions, intercompany loan injections, equity repatriation and loan repayments; no BMA approval or exchange-control license is required for either route. The only genuine profit remittance hurdles are documentary and corporate-form driven rather than FX-related: a Bermuda Exempted Company must maintain a licensed registered agent, be in good standing with the Registrar of Companies, and satisfy any economic substance requirements before the paying bank will release funds. The bank will additionally require a board resolution authorizing the distribution, evidence of solvency, and confirmation that the distribution is not derived from criminal property. Transfer pricing documentation should support intercompany payments to avoid delays in downstream jurisdictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds. There is no regulatory timeline requiring receipt or repatriation of export receipts, and no prescribed mandatory conversion ratio; the applicable conversion ratio is 0%. Exporters and corporate service providers may retain receipts indefinitely in BMD/USD or convert into GBP, EUR or other freely convertible currencies at commercial banking rates. Suspicious or large inbound receipts above USD 15,000 equivalent are subject to BMA-related anti-money-laundering reporting thresholds and standard record-keeping duties under the AML/CFT regime; underlying invoices or contracts must be preserved for at least five years after the transaction. No separate BMA exchange-control declaration is required.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility as a supplementary fee-collection and disbursement channel; low suitability as the sole treasury settlement rail. Offshore digital EMIs authorized in the UK or EEA cannot provide deposit-taking services in Bermuda unless separately licensed or registered by the BMA under Bermuda's Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and the payment-services regulatory framework. An unlicensed EMI may not hold the corporate operating funds of a Bermuda Exempted Company in a layered manner that disguises the ultimate Bermuda bank account. Legal constraints include the prohibition on unauthorized deposit-taking, inability to issue commercial Letters of Credit, lack of access to Bermuda's domestic clearing system, and increased audit risk from commingled EMI wallets. A digital EMI may be viable only for non-commingled client-fee collection where the ultimate payout to the BMA-licensed bank account is made via an identifiable SWIFT wire, with full source-of-funds documentation preserved.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial paid-in capital is typically routed by SWIFT MT103 from a UK clearing bank (HSBC, Barclays, NatWest or Standard Chartered) to the Bermuda Exempted Company's account at HSBC Bank Bermuda, Butterfield or Clarien. The corporate beneficiary line must display the exact legal name, unrestricted account number and BIC; the narrative must state 'Capital Contribution / Share Subscription — BMD/USD'. The receiving bank will require a BMA-compliant purpose code such as foreign-direct-investment capital or direct investment under the SWIFT Category of Purpose; common codes accepted in narrative include 'CAPT', 'K121' or 'FDI' when corroborated by the underlying share subscription agreement. Pre-verification documents include audited parent-company accounts, board resolution approving the capital injection, signed share subscription documents, Memorandum of Association update, certificate of good standing and a source-of-funds declaration. Wires exceeding USD 100,000 merit two-business-day advance notice to the relationship manager and SWIFT gpi tracking.For ongoing operating disbursements — registered-agent fees, AML software subscriptions, legal retainers, accounting fees, UK/EEA supplier invoices and director expenses — the treasury will initiate SWIFT MT103 transfers from the Bermuda operating account to the beneficiary's bank. Bermuda domestic payments between local BMD/USD accounts may use the Bermuda ACH clearing system because the currencies are at parity, reducing transaction cost. For high-value procurement or infrastructure acquisition above USD 250,000, the preferred rail is an irrevocable confirmed commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Bermuda bank and advised through the beneficiary's UK or EEA bank; the LC will be opened in USD to avoid FX markup and must be supported by pro-forma invoice, transport or services-delivery evidence, beneficiary certificate and the underlying procurement contract. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0; no transfer pricing legislation exists in Bermuda. There is no local statutory threshold for preparing a Local File or Master File, and the Bermuda income tax framework does not apply to exempted companies.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda operates an Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) regime under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018. Bermuda Exempted Companies undertaking corporate secretarial or registered agent services may be treated as part of a 'headquarters' or 'service business' if such services seek to support specific activities for group entities; such entities must file an annual Economic Substance Return. An exempted company merely holding the licence and providing local corporate services to clients is not itself a specified activity, but the ESR still requires that a company performing any relevant activity demonstrate physical presence, local employees, and expenditure proportionate to income generated in Bermuda. The licensed corporate service provider must also satisfy the substance requirements under the Corporate Service Provider Business Act 2013, including a local physical office and a qualified compliance principal.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Abuse of Tax Exemption

Statutory sanction: Bermuda has no domestic income tax to evade, but overseas tax evasion represents money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Regulations. The Economic Substance Authority may levy penalties up to BMD 10,000 for a first ESR offence and up to BMD 30,000 for subsequent offences, plus daily default penalties of BMD 500. The Minister of Finance may withdraw the tax protection certificate in cases of deliberate falsehood or fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false declarations of compliance face fines up to BMD 5,000 per offence, disqualification for up to 10 years, and potential personal civil liability to foreign revenue authorities. In severe cases, a Bermuda court may issue a travel ban to prevent a director from leaving the jurisdiction and may impose a custodial sentence of up to 5 years for fraud under the Criminal Code.
Shell Company / Insufficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Failure to satisfy the economic substance test triggers a BMD 10,000 penalty plus BMD 500 per day until compliance. The Registrar of Companies may also refuse to renew the annual company registration; the entity may be struck off and dissolved. The Economic Substance Authority may require a remedial plan, and non-compliance with the plan escalates to court intervention.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for penalties imposed on the company for ESR non-compliance. Courts may order directors to make restitution of profits generated through the shell arrangement. Directors can be prohibited from leaving Bermuda until outstanding penalties are paid, and chronic non-compliance may lead to two-year disqualification and criminal fines under the Economic Substance Regulations.
AML / Corporate Secretarial Misconduct

Statutory sanction: The Corporate Service Provider Business Act 2013 imposes a fine of BMD 100,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years for unlicensed activity. The Bermuda Monetary Authority may revoke the licence, publish enforcement notices, and impose civil penalties for AML breaches. Breaches of the Beneficial Ownership Act 2017 attract fines up to BMD 10,000 plus daily penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners who knowingly breach AML duties face personal fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years. The Supreme Court may grant freezing orders over personal assets, and travel bans may be issued where there is a flight risk. Directors may be made personally liable for restitution of illegal fees derived from secretarial misconduct.
Late Filing and Maintenance of Statutory Records

Statutory sanction: Fines escalate from BMD 250 to BMD 750; the company may be struck off after 90 days and dissolved, losing its license and its ability to enter into contracts. Restoration of a struck-off company costs at least BMD 1,800 plus court fees and requires potential Ministerial consent. Non-filing also triggers automatic reporting to the Registrar of Corporate Service Providers, which may lead to the agent's licence being suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors who allow default are subject to personal fines up to BMD 5,000 and disqualification. Where the company is struck off while liabilities remain, courts may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors personally liable for debts incurred after the date they knew the company was non-compliant. Travel bans may be issued in cash-flow or insolvency investigations.
