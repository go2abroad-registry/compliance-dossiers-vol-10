Default tax classification

Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax in Bermuda; indirect levies comprise customs (import) duties, employer payroll tax, stamp duty, land tax and hotel occupancy tax (Bermuda). Where apparel is sold at a distance to UK end-consumers, UK VAT and the low-value consignment rules apply (VATA 1994).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: not applicable — no consumption tax statute or registration threshold. UK: for a Bermuda seller of imported goods in consignments of intrinsic value ≤ GBP 135, VAT registration is mandatory from the first sale; the GBP 90,000 threshold applies only to UK-established taxable persons.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: not applicable — Bermuda has no export turnover tax and levies no export duty on apparel, so no zero-rating claim is needed. If the ELP holds UK VAT registration, 0% applies under VATA 1994 s.30/HMRC Notice 703 to goods physically exported from the UK within 3 months of supply to a non-UK destination, to direct exports evidenced by shipping documents, and to certain offshore services (Notice 741A).

  • Electronic export declaration (UK CDS/NES exit summary or equivalent) in the ELP's name and EORI
  • Bill of lading, air waybill or postal export receipt showing non-UK consignee and export route
  • Commercial invoice and packing list specifying the apparel, quantity, value and destination
  • Purchase order or sale contract with the overseas customer
  • Proof of payment (SWIFT/remittance advice) or inward remittance certificate where destination-country exchange-control rules require it
  • Bermuda Customs transit/export documents where goods are physically routed through Bermuda

Bermuda: no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API or digital-stamping mandate; import declarations are filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Department's broker platform. If UK VAT-registered, Making Tax Digital requires API-compatible software and quarterly digital VAT returns, but no Chilean-style clearance or digital stamping.

Bermuda: no VAT clawback exists; economic-substance and registration default attracts initial penalties up to BMD 10,000 plus BMD 1,000 per day, and customs penalties per the Customs Act 2010. UK: unsupported zero-rating is clawed back at 20% with default interest (Bank of England base rate + 2.5%) and a penalty up to 100% of VAT laid bare (FA 2007 Sch 24; deliberate concealment), criminal liability under VATA 1994 s.72, 6-year record-keeping, and retrospective assessments up to 4 years (non-deliberate) or 20 years (deliberate); HMRC may require payment and customer-confirmation evidence under Notice 703.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is officially pegged to the US Dollar at 1:1, so current-account transactions such as trade settlement, service fees, and ordinary operating disbursements are freely processed through BMA-authorised dealers. Capital-account movements into and out of an exempted partnership require exchange-control permission where the entity is classified as a Bermuda resident; however, BMA generally grants permission for bona fide offshore e-commerce capital injections, loans, and distributions to non-resident partners provided the partnership is not carrying on business in Bermuda, no Bermuda-source income arises, and economic substance filings are current. Profit remittance to non-resident partners faces no Bermuda withholding tax, dividend tax, or capital gains charge, but the paying bank will require evidence that the distribution satisfies the partnership agreement, source-of-funds rules, and BMA anti-money-laundering (AML) CDD expectations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, export surrender obligations attach principally to goods physically exported from Bermuda. The fast-fashion e-commerce model typically uses UK/EU/Asia drop-shipping and therefore has no Bermuda physical export, so no surrender obligation is triggered. If a Bermuda-resident exporter does export goods, foreign-currency proceeds must normally be repatriated through an authorised dealer and offered for conversion into BMD within one calendar month of receipt; because the BMD/USD peg is fixed at 1:1, the mandatory conversion ratio is effectively 100% at par. BMA may grant general or specific permission to retain USD rather than convert, so the economic effect of surrender is neutral.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) – the central bank, financial services regulator, and Exchange Control Authority acting under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Bermuda does not operate a full UK-style e-money institution (EMI) passport or licence; an offshore EMI cannot lawfully take deposits or hold client funds in Bermuda without a licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act. A UK FCA-authorised EMI can serve the UK parent as a payment gateway, but it is not a viable primary treasury rail for the Bermuda entity. Bermuda's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 covers virtual-asset service providers, not general e-money or payment accounts. Best practice is to maintain the operating treasury at a tier-1 Bermuda bank and use regulated processors such as Klarna, Shopify Payments, and SWIFT for settlement.
