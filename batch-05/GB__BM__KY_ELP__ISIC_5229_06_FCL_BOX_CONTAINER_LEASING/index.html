Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

The Bermuda Ministry of Finance; Registrar of Companies (ELP registration under the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act 2015); Bermuda Customs Department (customs and indirect tax administration). Official portals: www.gov.bm, www.roc.gov.bm, www.customs.gov.bm. HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) does not administer Bermuda taxes.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda imposes no income, corporation, capital gains, or withholding tax; therefore no general TIN/VAT ID is issued. The ELP must (i) file a Declaration of Exempted Partnership with the Registrar of Companies and pay the annual government fee; (ii) if it employs individuals, register for Payroll Tax and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number from the Office of the Tax Commissioner; (iii) if it imports or transships containers into Bermuda, obtain a Customs Importer Account/ID from the Bermuda Customs Department. No statutory tax identification number is required solely for holding or leasing intermodal ISO containers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent; income, gains, and distributions are reportable by partners under their respective residence jurisdictions. Bermuda levies no entity-level corporate tax, no VAT, and no transfer tax on the ELP. The ELP may obtain a tax assurance under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing exemption from future income tax and capital levy until 31 March 2035 (with standard 10-year extensions).

Available election forms

Bermuda has no local check-the-box election. For US federal tax purposes, the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to elect pass-through/disregarded status or association taxable as a corporation. For UK tax purposes, the ELP is treated as transparent and partners are taxed directly on their shares; UK hybrid and other mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) may apply where mismatch outcomes arise from Bermuda transparency, requiring a UK party to self-review and adjust; no Bermuda filing or election exists in respect of hybrid mismatch treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in Bermuda. Indirect taxes comprise Bermuda Customs Import Duty (Customs Tariff Act 1995, administered via ASYCUDA World), Employer Payroll Tax (Payroll Tax Act 1995), and Stamp Duty on limited Bermuda-situs instruments.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. No general indirect-tax registration threshold applies to an ELP in Bermuda. Limited obligations exist: Payroll Tax registration as an employer (no de minimis threshold) and Customs importer registration for any import of container equipment into Bermuda.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda has no VAT system and therefore no 0% export VAT rating or refund mechanism. Shipping containers temporarily imported into Bermuda and subsequently re-exported may be exempt from customs import duty under temporary importation provisions of the Customs Tariff Act, subject to re-export within 12 months; cross-border lease flows for containers are otherwise outside Bermuda customs duty net.

  • Customs Import/Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic entry)
  • Bill of Lading (through or combined transport)
  • Commercial Invoice and Container Equipment Interchange Report (EIR)
  • Inward/Outward Foreign-Exchange Remittance record (for audit trail)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing mandate, digital stamping, or Continuous Transaction Controls exist in Bermuda. Customs import/export declarations are submitted electronically through ASYCUDA World; invoice and equipment interchange records must be retained for at least 7 years.

No VAT clawback exists. Non-compliance with Customs temporary-importation conditions triggers payment of unpaid import duty (up to 25% depending on tariff classification), penalties under the Customs Act 1941 of up to three times the duty, forfeiture of container units, and fines up to BMD 25,000 for false declarations; the Minister of Finance may make a retrospective assessment within 6 years of discovery. Payroll Tax non-compliance attracts a 10% late-filing penalty plus interest at 12% per annum.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US dollar (USD) at 1:1, providing effective full convertibility for current account transactions. Capital account inflows into Bermuda require exchange-control approval if the partnership is resident; however, a non-resident-designated Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) may hold External Accounts in USD and receive partner capital contributions without prior BMA clearance. Profit distributions and dividend remittances to non-resident partners are freely available from external or authorised-dealer accounts, subject only to standard anti-money laundering verification. Practical remittance hurdles are limited to compliance with the Exchange Control Act 1972, maintaining non-resident status, and providing the bank with a distribution resolution and source-of-funds documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, a Bermuda resident must offer any foreign currency received to an authorised dealer within one month of receipt, together with a declaration. The mandatory conversion ratio to BMD is 100% for resident accounts unless a specific exemption or External Account permission is granted. For a non-resident-designated ELP whose container-leasing business is conducted wholly outside Bermuda, export proceeds held in USD External Accounts are not subject to surrender; the effective mandatory conversion ratio is 0%. If the ELP were inadvertently treated as resident for exchange control, the bank would require a BMA permission to retain and deploy USD receipts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Bermuda does not authorise standalone offshore digital EMIs as authorised dealers for exchange control; a non-resident ELP may maintain a UK- or EU-authorised EMI account for collection of small lease payments where the EMI provides GBP/EUR virtual IBANs, but this is not a substitute for a Bermudian clearing account. Legal constraints include the inability of EMIs to perform exchange-control duties, potential restrictions on recurring high-volume business-to-business flows, and the absence of local deposit-taking licences. Such rails may be used for ancillary receivables only, never for capital contributions or trade settlements requiring documentary evidence.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda does not maintain a standalone transfer pricing documentation statute; mandatory OECD TP documentation (Local File and Master File) applies to CIT-in-scope constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated global revenue of at least BMD 750 million (≈ EUR 750 million). Private ELPs below that threshold must nonetheless retain contemporaneous arm's length pricing records for container leasing fees, interest margins, and management charges to satisfy ESR substance reporting and Bermuda's anti-avoidance framework.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Requirements and Compliance) Act 2018 applies to all Bermuda resident exempted partnerships carrying on relevant activities. The intermodal container leasing ELP falls within 'Financing and Leasing' where the activity generates income in Bermuda; the ELP must demonstrate core income-generating activities (container acquisition and funding, lease negotiation, maintenance supervision, risk management, and treasury functions) performed in Bermuda, with adequate physical offices, qualified employees, and board/partner meetings in Bermuda. A passive asset-holding ELP with operations entirely outside Bermuda must accurately declare non-relevant activity status and maintain evidence of offshore management and control to avoid ESR penalties and transfer of information to the UK.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Intentional Misallocation of CIT Profits

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction on indictment attracts a fine of up to BMD 109,290 plus daily penalties of BMD 2,733; civil penalties equal to unpaid CIT plus interest; additional ESR penalty tiers of BMD 5,461, BMD 21,850, and BMD 109,290 apply for false substance declarations, and the Registrar may strike off the ELP and require correction of all filings

Director personal liability: Under Bermuda law and UK HMRC cross-enforcement, directors and general partners who knowingly cause a false filing may be personally liable for civil restitution and payment of the UK corporation tax, interest, and penalties; fraudulent evasion carries imprisonment of up to 14 years; the court may impose travel bans and disqualification orders preventing the individual from leaving Bermuda or acting as a director
Shell Company / Lack of Bermuda Substance

Statutory sanction: Registrar of Companies strike-off and dissolution of the ELP; ESR non-compliance fines of BMD 21,850 (summary) up to BMD 109,290 (indictment) plus daily penalties of BMD 1,093 or BMD 2,733; the Bermuda Tax Administration may issue an information and production order and exchange the file with UK HMRC under the Bermuda-UK Tax Information Exchange Agreement; the UK parent faces CFC attribution assessments on all undistributed profits

Director personal liability: Directors of the UK general partner or the Bermuda corporate services provider who knowingly sign false substance declarations are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid UK corporation tax through veil-piercing; individuals face disqualification as directors for up to 15 years in the UK and corresponding Bermuda prohibition orders; a court may issue a worldwide asset freeze and travel ban pending inquiry
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to BMD 100,000 per breach under the AML/CFT framework; the Beneficial Ownership Registrar may issue enforcement penalties and restrict the ELP's capacity to make distributions; the general partner's licence can be suspended; electronic payment rails may be terminated by correspondent banks; proceeds of the offence are subject to confiscation orders

Director personal liability: AML obligation holders within the ELP (the general partner and its directors) face personal criminal liability: imprisonment of up to 5 years for money laundering assistance and up to 14 years for fraudulent evasion; civil recovery may attach personal pensions and external assets; the court may impose a travel ban and surrender of passports, with the individual barred from managing any Bermuda exempted entity
Late or Non-Filing of Annual Return, ESR, and CIT

Statutory sanction: Automatic late-filing default penalties of BMD 250 to BMD 1,000 per return plus daily surcharges; ESR daily penalties compound at BMD 1,093 per day until compliance; the Registrar may strike off the ELP without further notice; a struck-off ELP's container fleet contracts may become unenforceable and assets vest in the Crown; in the CIT context, failure to file invokes interest and penalties provided in the CIT Act 2023 and possible prepayment orders

Director personal liability: The designated general partner and its directors are personally named in enforcement certificates; authorities may obtain personal judgment against directors for unpaid government fees and penalties; continued default triggers disqualification under the Companies Act 1981 and an order preventing the individual from leaving Bermuda until all outstanding returns and fees are settled
