Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is permissible only for the statutory Bermuda registered-office requirement, which must be the address of the licensed registered agent in Bermuda. For the underlying bulk port stevedoring business, a virtual office is not acceptable: the terminal operator must hold a valid port facility license in the operating jurisdiction and maintain physical on-site infrastructure, equipment, insurance, and a local operational office. Stevedoring (ISIC 5224) is not a 'relevant activity' under Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2018; therefore no Bermuda core-income-generating-activity substance requirement arises, and the ELP may be managed offshore provided its Bermuda registered-agent obligations are continuously satisfied.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; official portal: www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must first obtain a Certificate of Exemption from the Registrar of Companies; this certificate number serves as the prerequisite identification. Where the ELP employs Bermuda-based staff, the general partner must register the partnership with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax purposes and obtain a Payroll Tax Registration Number. If the ELP is a constituent entity of a multinational group with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750m or more, the ELP must additionally register for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and receive a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (BTIN) through the Bermuda Tax Administration portal (via www.gov.bm).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda does not maintain a check-the-box election. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the ELP may make an entity classification election under U.S. Treasury Regulation § 301.7701-3 (Form 8832). Under Bermuda's CIT Act 2023, a partnership that is otherwise within the CIT scope may apply to the Tax Commissioner for treatment as a tax-transparent entity where the partners are subject to an equivalent income tax in another jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (Customs Duties and Excise Taxes only; no VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A for VAT/GST; no such tax exists. For customs duty, any importer (including an ELP) must register with the Bermuda Customs Service; no minimum economic threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, so zero-rating for export is not required. Goods and services exported from Bermuda and all offshore services supplied by a Bermudian entity are outside any indirect tax charge, regardless of destination.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No electronic invoicing, digital stamping, or real-time fiscal clearance regime is in force. Bermuda Customs operates an electronic clearance system (customs declaration submission via email or the island's Single Window portal), but it does not impose API-level fiscal control or digital clearance stamps.

Non-existent VAT prevents export zero-rating abuse; however, misdeclarations in customs entries attract a penalty up to three times the value of the goods or duty evaded, forfeiture, and up to two years' imprisonment under the Customs Act 1949. Exchange Control regulations require repatriation of export proceeds within 180 days; failure attracts penalties imposed by the Bermuda Monetary Authority and may lead to the loss of exchange control privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1.0000 BMD = 1.0000 USD, giving full current-account convertibility for stevedoring fees, port tariffs, and subcontractor settlements. Capital-account transactions, including partner capital contributions, partnership distributions, and intercompany loan repayments, are freely permitted for an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) granted 'non-resident' status by the BMA under the Exchange Control Act 1972; such ELPs may hold external USD/GBP accounts with authorized dealers without conversion obligation. Profit remittance to the UK parent is unhindered: no Bermuda corporate income tax, withholding tax, or remittance levy applies; however, the bank's Exchange Control Officer will require a General Partner distribution resolution, a no-local-creditors certificate, and, for distributions above USD 250,000, a signed remittance certificate confirming no outstanding BMD obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (Bermuda), proceeds for exported services (stevedoring and cargo-handling supplied to non-resident shipping lines) must be repatriated to a Bermuda authorized dealer within 180 days from invoice date. A non-resident designated ELP is normally granted blanket exemption from mandatory surrender; absent designation, 100% of export receipts in foreign currency must be offered for sale to an authorized dealer, and retention of USD requires explicit BMA approval. Because BMD and USD are at par, the surrender rule creates no economic distortion; the statutory compliance timeline is: receipt by D+180, authorized-dealer exchange-control filing at credit, and conversion ratio of 100% for BMD-denominated or 0% for USD held in an approved external account.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (Bermuda) as the central bank and exchange control administrator for Bermuda; Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) and HM Treasury remain relevant as the UK sovereign supervisory and sanctions framework for the UK parent treasury and GBP clearing infrastructure.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for low-value GBP/EUR operational float; Low and generally not permitted for capital escrow or statutory deposit arrangements. UK-domiciled e-money institutions (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) will typically onboard a Bermuda ELP only after enhanced due diligence and may reject Bermuda jurisdictions irrespective of FATF status. Legal constraints: EMIs are not BMA-licensed banks or authorized dealers under the Exchange Control Act 1972, cannot hold BMD-denominated balances, are not subject to Bermuda deposit protection, and cannot issue the bank reference or regulatory confirmation required for the ELP's capital escrow account.
