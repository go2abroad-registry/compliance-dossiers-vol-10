E-government filing portal

Step 1: File a Declaration of Limited Partnership with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2009 (Bermuda) through a licensed Bermuda resident agent; obtain the Certificate of Registration and the unique Bermuda Partnership Registration Number, which serves as the de facto tax identifier (Bermuda issues no TIN or VAT ID). Step 2: Appoint the mandatory Bermuda resident representative and maintain a Bermuda registered office at an approved service provider. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda imposes no corporate or income tax; therefore no domestic check-the-box, entity classification, or hybrid-mismatch elections exist. The partnership agreement is the operative instrument allocating profits, losses, and capital for fiscal-transparency purposes. For US federal tax purposes, the Bermuda ELP may file Form 8832 (check-the-box) to elect association taxable as a corporation; absent such election it defaults to pass-through partnership treatment. For UK purposes, HMRC treats the ELP as fiscally transparent; no election is available, and UK tax residence is a facts-and-circumstances determination based on central management and control.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax exists in Bermuda. Indirect fiscal regime comprises: (i) customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1995; (ii) stamp duties on specified instruments under the Stamp Act 1976; and (iii) annual exempted-entity government fees (fiscal charges, not consumption taxes).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda has no VAT/GST turnover threshold or registration regime. Mandatory registrations: (a) employer payroll tax/social security registration for any Bermuda-based remuneration without a de minimis threshold; (b) Bermuda Customs account for any commercial import/export without a monetary threshold. Separately, UK VAT registration may be required for UK sales by the Bermuda ELP (e.g., supplies of examination gloves into Great Britain), but that arises under UK legislation, not Bermuda law.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so no export zero-rating mechanism applies. Exports of finished nitrile gloves from Bermuda attract no Bermuda customs duty, as duties are chargeable only on importation into Bermuda. Where duty-paid raw materials, components, or packaging are imported into Bermuda and subsequently incorporated into gloves that are exported, duty relief is available by way of customs drawback under the Customs Act 1968 and the Customs Tariff Act 1995, subject to: (i) export within the prescribed statutory period; (ii) production of documentary proof of export; and (iii) filing of the drawback application with Bermuda Customs. Alternatively, qualifying movements may use the Bermuda Freeport (Ireland Island) duty-suspension regime to defer and extinguish import duty on goods subsequently re-exported. Bermuda maintains no exchange-control or statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day parameter in this dossier reflects standard letter-of-credit and telegraphic-transfer settlement practice rather than a Bermuda statutory requirement.

  • Customs export declaration/entry (required where drawback, suspension, or export certification is claimed)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of goods from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for the exported consignment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/TT credit confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds (banking substantiation, not a Bermuda statutory requirement)

Bermuda does not impose any electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based stamping, or digital signature mandate. Invoices and receipts may be issued in paper or electronic form without government approval. Customs import/export declarations for glove shipments are filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs single-window portal, and payroll tax returns are filed online with the Office of the Tax Commissioner; neither process constitutes a fiscal clearance or digital stamping requirement.

Bermuda's customs drawback regime is subject to audit by Bermuda Customs; inaccurate or fraudulent declarations under the Customs Act 1968 expose the ELP and its general partner to: (i) seizure and forfeiture of goods; (ii) monetary penalties up to treble the value of the goods or duty evaded; (iii) criminal prosecution of responsible partners or officers; (iv) revocation or suspension of customs broker licences; and (v) retroactive reassessment of duty with interest. Because no VAT/GST exists in Bermuda, there is no separate export VAT clawback mechanism. In relation to Bermuda payroll tax, under-declaration of staff remuneration may trigger back-assessment, interest, and penalties imposed by the Office of the Tax Commissioner.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermudian dollar (BMD) is legally pegged to the United States dollar at 1:1. Current and capital account operations are fully convertible for non-resident-designated exempted partnerships; there are no advance approvals, profit remittance levies, or capital repatriation restrictions after obtaining exchange-control non-resident status under the Exchange Control Act 1972. Resident/domestic businesses remain subject to exchange control, but the ELP will rely on its non-resident designation to achieve unrestricted multi-currency and USD/BMD clearing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory mandatory conversion ratio applies to an exempted partnership holding non-resident exchange-control status; such an entity may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign-currency accounts without surrender. For any inadvertent resident status, the Exchange Control Regulations impose repatriation of export receipts within 90 days and a requirement to offer foreign-currency surplus to an authorized dealer; the ELP will obtain the non-resident certificate to disapply these obligations.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) – Exchange Control Administration and Integrated Financial Regulator

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; applies only to constituent entities of MNE groups with annual consolidated revenue ≥ €750 million). Domestic-only and below-threshold exempted limited partnerships remain subject to 0% entity-level corporate income tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda does not impose dividend withholding tax, and exempted limited partnership distributions are generally capital in nature. The CIT Act does not create a dividend WHT on in-scope entities.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than 15 months after the end of the accounting period; for the first CIT accounting period, the deadline is extended to 18 months after the effective date or the close of the fiscal year, whichever is later. Quarterly estimated payments are required for in-scope CIT entities with annual CIT liability exceeding BMD 500,000.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 1,000,000 (annual aggregate value of controlled transactions) for mandatory Local File preparation; Master File is required for any in-scope constituent entity of an MNE group with annual consolidated revenue exceeding €750 million, regardless of local threshold. If the Bermuda ELP is not an in-scope CIT entity, no statutory TP file is required, but contemporaneous arm's-length evidence should be kept for any related-party purchases/sales to reduce risk under overseas rules.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Act requires every exempted partnership that carries on a 'relevant activity' (including distribution and service centre businesses, financing, insurance, fund management, shipping, IP holding, and headquarters operations) to maintain substantial presence in Bermuda. Nitrile glove manufacturing/dipping production is not a listed relevant activity unless the ELP holds the glove IP as a pure equity holding or chief administrative office; however, board meetings, decision-taking, and risk management for invoicing and purchase orders must remain in Bermuda if a separate distribution and service centre activity is claimed. The ESR filing is made through the BMA's SEG (Substance Enforcement Group) portal.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: On conviction for tax evasion: fine up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; civil penalty equal to 200% of the tax evaded added to tax; asset freeze orders may be obtained by the Tax Commissioner.

Director personal liability: Directors and managing partners who knowingly cause or participate in evasion are jointly and severally liable for the corporate penalty and unpaid tax; the Bermuda courts may issue travel restrictions under the Revenue Act and a director disqualification order for a period of 10 years.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of the entire SBIE; reassessment at 15% on full Bermuda taxable income; monetary penalty of BMD 75,000 under the Economic Substance Act for submission of misleading substance statements; possible dissolution of the ELP by the Registrar.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any Bermuda-resident director may incur personal civil liability for the economic-substance penalty; travel bans and removal from the Bermuda Company Registry can be ordered where the ELP fails to remedy substance deficiencies.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Imposition of arm's-length adjustment with additional CIT at 15% and a documentation penalty of 10% of the understated tax; late production penalty of BMD 50,000 per month.

Director personal liability: Partners who knowingly approve non-arm's-length pricing may be assessed as 'tax agents' for 10% of the underpaid tax; repeated non-compliance triggers the Anti-Money Laundering Act's fit-and-proper test and personal disqualification.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Up to BMD 1,000,000 fine for contravening the AML/ATF Code; underlying violating transactions subject to asset confiscation; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may issue a public statement and suspend the ELP's banking permission.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners bear personal criminal liability for money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Act if they had knowledge or willful blindness; penalties include up to 14 years' imprisonment and confiscation of personal assets.
