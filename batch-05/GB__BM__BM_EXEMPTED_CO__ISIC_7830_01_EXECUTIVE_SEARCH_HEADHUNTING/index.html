Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Bermuda exempted company; the standard default capitalisation is US$12,000 authorised capital (1,000 ordinary shares at US$0.01 par value per share), with the subscription price (e.g. US$10,000) payable within 30 days of incorporation; no escrow account requirement exists in Bermuda — funds may be held in the corporate bank account as working capital; graduated additional registration fees apply if authorised capital exceeds BMD/USD 12,000.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (primary tax administration for payroll tax and land tax; portal: https://portal.etc.bm); Bermuda Customs and Revenue Department (HM Customs and Revenue, part of the Ministry of Finance; portal: https://www.gov.bm/customs) for indirect/customs duties; Registrar of Companies (Companies and Partnerships, Ministry of Finance; portal: https://www.registrarofcompanies.gov.bm) for entity incorporation and Economic Substance filing.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 and obtains a Certificate of Incorporation; no income tax TIN is issued because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax. If the entity employs Bermuda-resident staff, the employer must register for Payroll Tax with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number / Taxpayer Reference Number. If the entity imports physical goods into Bermuda, it must separately obtain a Customs Account / Commercial User Number from Bermuda Customs and Revenue. For an offshore executive search and headhunting business delivering services exclusively to non-resident clients, no Bermuda tax registration is required absent a Bermuda payroll or local import activity; there is no VAT registration mechanism in Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966: an Exempted Company may apply in writing to the Minister of Finance for a Tax Assurance Certificate, electing to be protected from any future Bermuda income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, and withholding taxes until at least 2035 (extendable by subsequent amendment). Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Amendment Act 2018: each qualifying entity must file an annual economic substance return and elect its characterization as a 'full substance' entity or a 'pure equity holding entity'; this election is administrative, not a tax election. No domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Bermuda law, although the entity may be classified under U.S. Form 8832 check-the-box rules for foreign tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda levies no VAT or GST; the indirect taxation regime comprises Customs Duty on imported physical goods under the Customs Tariff Act 1950, employer Payroll Tax, Land Tax on real property, and Stamp Duty on limited instruments. Executive search and headhunting services supplied by a Bermuda Exempted Company to overseas clients are outside the scope of all Bermuda indirect taxes and attract no charge.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Bermuda. Any person importing goods into Bermuda, without regard to turnover, must be registered with Bermuda Customs and Revenue as a customs account holder and file electronic customs entries; the Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Bermuda: with no VAT/GST in force, there is no export zero-rating mechanism or VAT refund claim. Services rendered from Bermuda, including C-suite executive search retainers and specialist technology headhunting placement fees, fall outside the Bermuda tax base entirely and are exported at a 0% effective charge. For physical goods, customs duty is imposed only at importation; goods subsequently re-exported may qualify for a duty drawback claim under the Customs Tariff Act 1950, provided the goods are exported within 12 months of import and are shown not to have been consumed or used in Bermuda.

  • Bermuda Customs Import Entry (ASYCUDA World declaration) evidencing duty-paid importation
  • Export Declaration / Outward Entry lodged with Bermuda Customs
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Duty drawback application form with proof of original duty payment under the Customs Tariff Act 1950

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists for services. Bermuda Customs uses the ASYCUDA World electronic single-window system for lodgement and clearance of import/export declarations; commercial invoices and supporting shipping documents must be transmitted digitally at entry, but no API-based fiscal stamping or continuous tax-control framework applies to executive-search invoices.

Bermuda has no VAT export-fraud regime. Customs duty drawback claims are audited by Bermuda Customs and Revenue; false statements, forged bills of lading, or drawback claims on goods consumed domestically trigger forfeiture of the goods, mandatory repayment of the drawback with penal interest, civil penalties of up to three times the duty evaded under the Customs Act 1949, and criminal fines or imprisonment for fraudulent conduct. A Bermuda Exempted Company's Tax Assurance Certificate does not shield it from customs or payroll tax penalties; economic substance filings misrepresenting offshore service activity are separately subject to Registrar of Companies sanctions, including strike-off and monetary fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is issued by the BMA, is fully convertible, and is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar, guaranteeing at-par settlement in USD with no exchange margin. A Bermuda Exempted Company wholly owned by non-residents qualifies for BMA 'non-resident designation', which excludes it from exchange-control restrictions on capital transactions. Current-account receipts, including offshore executive-search retainer fees, are freely receivable; capital transactions (share capital injection, intercompany loans, dividends) require only standard bank documentation, not BMA pre-approval, for designated non-resident entities. Profit remittance by dividend to a UK shareholder is unrestricted once audited accounts and the underlying solvency certificate are filed. The only genuine remittance obstacles to this structure are UK taxation considerations (CFC and management-and-control exposure), not Bermudian convertibility rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation exists. Bermuda does not require export or service proceeds to be converted into BMD: a non-resident-designated Exempted Company may retain 100% of retainer receipts in USD, BMD, GBP, or EUR interest-bearing accounts and may deploy them directly to the UK operating entity. Mandatory conversion ratios are nil; the 1:1 USD peg renders any residual BMD balance economically fungible with USD. The sole regulatory touchpoint is that receivables must be accounted for in the annual statutory financial statements and in the BO/Economic Substance declarations filed with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), Hamilton, Bermuda — the currency-issuing authority and Exchange Control administrator under the Exchange Control Act 1972; Bermuda has no separately constituted central bank, and the BMA performs all monetary regulatory functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low for the Bermuda entity itself, Medium via a UK operating arm. Bermuda has no EMT or digital-bank licensing regime, and the leading global EMIs (Wise, Revolut, Mercury, Payoneer, Airwallex) generally refuse onboarding of Bermuda Exempted Companies because their commercial charters restrict eligibility to locally registered businesses. In particular, the designated Wise_B2B rail cannot be obtained directly in the name of the Bermuda treasury entity. The compliant treasury architecture therefore splits functions: the Bermuda Exempted Company holds upstream client fees and group liquidity at a Tier-1 Bermudian bank (Retainer_Wire rail), while the separately incorporated UK operating company maintains a Wise B2B account for low-value vendor disbursements, governed by a documented intercompany services and settlement agreement. This hybrid construct is fully permissible and does not impair the 'Low_Risk_Standard' AML rating of the executive-search business.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103 from the UK director's personal or the UK entity's USD/GBP account to the Bermuda treasury account in USD. The instruction must identify the beneficiary as the Exempted Company using its Bermuda SWIFT BIC plus the domestic account number (no IBAN applies in Bermuda; the BIC is 8 or 11 characters and the account number follows the local 4-4-5 format). The payment narrative must read exactly 'Share Capital Contribution — [Exempted Company name]'. On the UK side, the remitting bank must classify the outward remittance under the BOP direct-investment reporting category 'Direct Investment — Equity to Overseas Affiliate' so that OFAC/FATCA filters pass cleanly; a scanned board resolution authorising the share issue must be delivered to the accepting Bermudian bank prior to credit. Settlement convention is T+1 for cross-border USD and T+0 for BMD/USD internal conversions.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 in USD or GBP from the Bermuda treasury account to the UK operating entity or its Wise B2B account, with the narrative 'Intercompany settlement — executive search retainer fees pursuant to services agreement [reference]'. Because the business is a pure services arrangement, commercial letters of credit are inapplicable and only MT103/MT202 payment-order rails are used. Intra-UK onward disbursements from the operating office execute via CHAPS or FPS after conversion, or via Wise B2B's UK local-currency settlement. The low-risk classification of ISIC 7830 provides a 90% straight-through-processing rate on pre-validated beneficiary details; the Bermuda bank's daily cut-off (typically 14:00 Bermuda time) governs same-day USD clearance, and dual-currency accounts are recommended so that GBP disbursements are converted at the bank's internal interbank rate rather than the retail card rate.Deliverable outright FX forwards (spot-deferred) on GBP/USD executed with HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield's treasury desk; BMD is a USD-pegged currency so no BMD non-deliverable forward market exists or is neededFX swaps and FX rollovers for intercompany liquidity between the Bermuda USD treasury account and the UK operating entity's GBP accountCurrency options and zero-cost collars (risk reversals) on GBP/USD — permissible for genuine hedging of executive-search retainer cash flows, provided each contract is supported by the underlying invoice or cash-flow forecast and booked in the name of the Bermuda Exempted Company

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for the Bermuda entity itself, Medium via a UK operating arm. Bermuda has no EMT or digital-bank licensing regime, and the leading global EMIs (Wise, Revolut, Mercury, Payoneer, Airwallex) generally refuse onboarding of Bermuda Exempted Companies because their commercial charters restrict eligibility to locally registered businesses. In particular, the designated Wise_B2B rail cannot be obtained directly in the name of the Bermuda treasury entity. The compliant treasury architecture therefore splits functions: the Bermuda Exempted Company holds upstream client fees and group liquidity at a Tier-1 Bermudian bank (Retainer_Wire rail), while the separately incorporated UK operating company maintains a Wise B2B account for low-value vendor disbursements, governed by a documented intercompany services and settlement agreement. This hybrid construct is fully permissible and does not impair the 'Low_Risk_Standard' AML rating of the executive-search business.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 10,000,000 - threshold triggering mandatory Local File / Master File for Economic Substance relevant activities in Bermuda; executive search is not a relevant activity, so no Bermuda TP documentation is mandated. However, if the Bermuda entity is a UK-parented group member, UK HMRC TP documentation rules apply where group revenue exceeds GBP 200 million and aggregate cross-border related-party transactions exceed GBP 1 million; maintain an English-language local file and master file at the UK head office.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 applies to entities carrying on enumerated relevant activities (banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centers, and IP businesses). Executive search and headhunting (ISIC 7830) is not enumerated, so no Bermuda CIGA requirement arises; the entity must still file an annual declaration as non-relevant. If the entity in practice conducts headquarters or financing functions for the UK group alongside headhunting, Bermuda substance and CIGA presence would become mandatory.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Diverted Profits Tax

Statutory sanction: HMRC may issue a Diverted Profits Tax (DPT) charge at 25% on deemed diverted profits (Finance Act 2015, ss. 77-114), plus a penalty of up to 100% of the diverted tax and statutory interest; VAT default surcharge of up to 15% and an inaccuracy penalty of up to 100% of unpaid VAT may also apply.

Director personal liability: Officers may be personally assessed under TMA 1970 s. 108 where tax is lost through their neglect or fraud; HMRC may issue a personal liability notice. Directors involved in offshore profit-extraction schemes face disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 and potential criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue, which carries a maximum sentence of life imprisonment in the Crown Court.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act 2018: default penalty of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 10,000 per day for continuing non-filing; a maximum penalty of BMD 250,000 for failing economic substance requirements; knowingly false statements expose the company to fines at the discretion of the Bermuda courts and up to 5 years' imprisonment for responsible individuals.

Director personal liability: Every director who knowingly causes or permits a false declaration is personally liable to the same penalties; the Bermuda court may impose director disqualification, an asset-freezing order, or a travel ban while an investigation is pending.
Money Laundering and AML Breach - Phantom Invoicing

Statutory sanction: UK: money laundering under s. 327 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 is punishable by up to 14 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines; the General Anti-Abuse Rule may add a 60% GAAR penalty on the counteracted tax advantage. Bermuda: the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Act 2018 permits regulatory penalties of up to BMD 500,000 per breach, suspension or revocation of corporate service provider authorization, and bank account freezing under suspicious transaction reporting obligations.

Director personal liability: Directors who facilitate the scheme face personal liability for civil restitution of disguised profits, a court-ordered travel ban under a POCA 2002 restraint order, potential extradition between the UK and Bermuda, and personal criminal fines and imprisonment.
