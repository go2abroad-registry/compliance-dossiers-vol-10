Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Tax Authority (BTA), operating through the Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; official e-filing portal is the Bermuda Tax Authority e-services platform referenced through gov.bm. The Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981 is the corporate registry authority. HM Revenue & Customs is not the Bermudian tax authority and is relevant only if the entity becomes UK tax resident, maintains a UK permanent establishment, or otherwise engages the UK tax system.

E-government filing portal

A foreign founder obtains a Bermuda tax identity primarily through company registration: (1) submit the Bermuda Exempted Company constitution and beneficial-ownership particulars to the Registrar of Companies; (2) pay the statutory registration fee and receive the Certificate of Incorporation with a permanent Bermuda company number; (3) register the entity for annual economic substance declarations with the Bermuda Tax Authority through the BTA portal; (4) if the entity belongs to a multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in either of the preceding two years, complete the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 registration and obtain a Bermuda CIT/TIN reference; (5) register separately with the payroll tax system if Bermuda-resident employees are engaged; and (6) obtain a Bermuda Customs importer/exporter code if physical goods will be imported into or exported from Bermuda. No VAT or GST registration exists because Bermuda has no value-added tax. An Exempted Company is formed for business outside Bermuda and may carry on business in Bermuda only under an express licence, so its tax-registration footprint is ordinarily registry-led and substance-led.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No Bermuda check-the-box election is available. A Bermuda Exempted Company is a non-transparent limited liability corporation and, for U.S. federal tax purposes, is treated as a per se corporation under U.S. Treas. Reg. section 301.7701-2(b)(8); it is therefore not eligible to elect disregarded-entity status. Bermuda law does not provide an entity-level transparency election. Where the entity falls within the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, available statutory elections are limited to the specific CITA elections regarding foreign tax credit pooling and ordering, qualifying loss carry-forward usage, transitional substance-based relief, and OECD Pillar Two top-up tax designations. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are not cured by a Bermuda election; UK and other anti-hybrid rules may recharacterize payments involving instruments or branches of the Exempted Company.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax is imposed in Bermuda. The Bermudian indirect nexus is instead composed of customs import duties, employer-paid payroll tax, land tax, stamp duty and tourism-related levies. A Bermuda Exempted Company providing offshore TikTok MCN and creator-agency services from outside Bermuda has no Bermudian VAT/GST registration, invoicing or filing obligation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Bermudian dollars (BMD). Bermuda has no VAT/GST economic-turnover registration threshold. Payroll tax registration is required from the first dollar of Bermuda employment income; customs registration is required whenever goods are imported or exported; economic substance registration and CIT registration are obligation-based rather than turnover-based. CIT registration applies to any Bermuda constituent entity of a multinational enterprise group with global revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least one of the prior two years.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST, so there is no export zero-rating rate, no VAT refund mechanism and no input VAT recovery. For physical goods, exports from Bermuda are outside the customs duty base; temporary importation and re-export relief or drawback arises only where duty-paid goods are lawfully exported within the prescribed statutory period. Offshore services supplied by the Exempted Company, including TikTok creator incubation, talent representation, content monetization analytics and payout management, are outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxes. If the entity is within CIT scope, such foreign-source service revenue is allocated under the CITA formula and may be taxable in Bermuda unless attributable to a foreign permanent establishment or otherwise excluded under the statutory allocation rules.

No electronic-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital-stamping, API clearance or continuous transaction control mandate exists in Bermuda. Customs import and export declarations are processed electronically through Bermuda Customs Service systems. Corporate income tax returns, payroll tax filings, economic substance declarations and beneficial-ownership filings are submitted through the Bermuda Tax Authority e-portal and the corporate registry. Voluntary e-invoices may be used commercially but have no tax clearance or fiscal mark requirement under Bermudian law.

Bermuda has no VAT export-refund clawback because no VAT is collected. For customs duty purposes, false or fictitious export and re-export claims are audited by the Bermuda Customs Service; the duties are clawed back with interest and penalty duty of up to three times the evaded duty, together with possible forfeiture of goods, proceeds and criminal prosecution. For economic substance and corporate income tax purposes, the Tax Commissioner has broad audit, information-gathering and reassessment powers; knowingly false declarations designed to allocate profit outside Bermuda or to understate the effective tax rate attract civil penalties, interest, publication, loss of exempted status and cancellation of the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection assurance. In-scope multinational groups remain subject to OECD Pillar Two administration and may suffer disallowance of substance-based income exclusions or foreign tax credits if the transfer pricing, payroll, physical presence and strategic management evidence is not contemporaneous and sufficient.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account convertibility is available for a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as 'non-resident' for exchange control purposes. The company may apply for non-resident status under Section 4 of the Exchange Control Act 1972, which removes BMA approval requirements for capital-account transactions, foreign-currency retention, and outward remittances. Profit remittance to UK shareholders is unrestricted, and Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or management fees; no capital-gains or income tax applies to a tax-exempted company by virtue of the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (guarantee valid until at least 2035). The only practical hurdle is bank-level governance: evidence of source of funds, beneficial-ownership disclosure, and Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 reporting compliance must be demonstrated before any dividend or capital repatriation is cleared.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies to a non-resident-designated Exempted Company. Bermuda's Exchange Control Act 1972 imposes surrender obligations only on 'resident' entities and individuals, requiring them to offer foreign currency to an authorized dealer within a prescribed period; a Bermuda exempted company holding non-resident status is wholly outside that regime and may retain TikTok MCN agency commission receipts in USD, BMD, GBP, EUR, or any freely convertible currency with no BMA filing or conversion mandate. If the company were ever treated as resident, the proscribed timeline under the Regulations would require surrender 'forthwith' to an authorized bank, underlining the operational necessity of maintaining the non-resident certificate.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; acts as the central bank and sole currency issuer of the Bermudian Dollar (BMD), pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) under the Currency Act 1972. BMA administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, supervises banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and operates the domestic wholesale payment/clearing settlement system in conjunction with Bermuda Automated Clearing House (BACH). The UK sovereign retains ultimate constitutional oversight via the Governor and the Foreign and Commonwealth Office, but BMA is the operative regulatory authority for FX control, licensing, and prudential supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Moderate-to-High viability as a disintermediated payout rail, but LOW viability as the primary treasury or escrow bank. Wise B2B is not licensed or deposit-taking regulated in Bermuda and cannot hold BMD balances as a full-risk bank book; Wise serves as an electronic money institution (EMI) under FCA authorization in the UK, and Bermuda does not operate an EMI passporting regime with the EEA post-Brexit. Therefore, the compliant architecture is: (i) a Tier-1 Bermudian bank (HSBC or Butterfield) for capital injection, commission collection, and FX hedging collateral; (ii) Wise B2B API (or an equivalent multi-currency wallet such as Airwallex) exclusively as a low-value batch disbursement rail for creator payouts in GBP, EUR, and USD where the beneficiary does not require a SWIFT credit; (iii) all Wise balances swept daily back to the Bermuda bank to avoid unregulated float. This satisfies BMA guidance on the use of unregulated third-party payment processors by requiring the ultimate settlement account to remain with a licensed deposit-taker.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injection from the UK parent is executed via SWIFT MT103 (for amounts below USD 5 million equivalent) or MT202/MT205 (for high-value interbank transfers in excess of USD 5 million) to the Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda account, with the purpose narrative 'Equity subscription - Bermuda Exempted Company [name], ISIC 7310', and corresponding ISO 20022 purpose code 'COMC' (payment of a commission/fee) or 'SERV' (service) depending on whether the remittance is a capital contribution or pre-revenue operating advance; capital contributions must be recorded on the statutory share register and matched to the corporate resolution approving the issue of shares, with the bank's Capital Verification pack (source-of-funds declaration, board minutes, certificate of incorporation, and certificate of non-resident status). Ltd. is received as USD SWIFT MT103 via CHIPS/Fedwire through the Bermudian bank's New York correspondent; the narrative must reference the agency agreement and the exact invoice number, and the beneficiary account must be a non-resident-designated BMD/USD operational account to avoid any exchange-control reference to BMA.Creator and talent settlements are executed on three clearing rails in strict order of priority: (1) SWIFT MT103 for creator banks in jurisdictions where EMI coverage is absent (e.g., Brazil, Thailand, Nigeria) using purpose code 'COMM' (payment of commission) with the creator's full contract reference and TikTok Creator Marketplace payout report attached as cover; (2) Wise B2B batch API for GBP/EUR/USD payouts to creator IBANs, wallet accounts, and local clearing systems (SEPA, Faster Payments, ACH), with each recipient pre-verified and sanctioned-screened before inclusion in the batch file; (3) Commercial letters of credit are not applicable to MCN service flows and are expressly disfavored; instead, documentary collection is replaced by monthly service attestation reports signed by the MCN and the creators. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Moderate-to-High viability as a disintermediated payout rail, but LOW viability as the primary treasury or escrow bank. Wise B2B is not licensed or deposit-taking regulated in Bermuda and cannot hold BMD balances as a full-risk bank book; Wise serves as an electronic money institution (EMI) under FCA authorization in the UK, and Bermuda does not operate an EMI passporting regime with the EEA post-Brexit. This satisfies BMA guidance on the use of unregulated third-party payment processors by requiring the ultimate settlement account to remain with a licensed deposit-taker.
