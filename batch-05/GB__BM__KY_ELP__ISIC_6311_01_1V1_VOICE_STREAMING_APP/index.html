Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD), with USD legal tender status in Bermuda. Bermuda maintains full capital and current account convertibility; there are no exchange control restrictions or licensing requirements for cross-border fund movements under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (as amended). Profit remittance, dividend distributions, and partnership capital withdrawals by non-resident partners or shareholders are freely permitted without prior BMA approval or withholding, provided the entity is an exempted undertaking. The UK parent jurisdiction applies a fully liberalized capital account regime with no residual exchange controls; no convertibility hurdles exist for either GBP, USD, or BMD flows. Settlement currency risk is minimal given the BMD/USD peg, but GBP depreciation risk to treasury is a genuine economic exposure for UK-based operating expenditures.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds, including digital service revenues derived from voice streaming and app store disbursements. Receipts from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing may be held indefinitely in USD, BMD, GBP, or any freely convertible currency of the ELP's choosing without forced conversion or repatriation timeline. UK regulations similarly impose no surrender obligation for export earnings. The only applicable obligations are anti-money laundering recordkeeping and economic substance reporting, which require that the ELP maintain adequate physical presence, income, and expenditure within Bermuda relative to its core income-generating activities.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as primary licensing and supervisory authority for banking, with the Bank of England and HM Treasury retaining ultimate sovereign oversight over Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory; BMA operates under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 for deposit-taking institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for Bermuda-licensed fintech/EMI solutions due to the absence of a standalone EMI licensing regime in Bermuda; the BMA does not license electronic money institutions as a distinct category, and unlicensed digital wallet providers are not permitted to hold deposits or issue e-money in Bermuda. UK-licensed EMIs regulated by the FCA under the Payment Services Regulations 2017 (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business) may serve as secondary settlement rails for GBP operating expenses, but are considered non-bank high-risk counterparties by Bermudian banks and cannot replace a licensed Bermuda or UK bank for primary escrow and treasury functions. Optimal structure: maintain a primary BMA-licensed bank in Bermuda for capital and dividend flows, supplemented by one UK EMI for fine-grained disbursements, subject to full funds flow transparency to the primary bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial capital injection from UK parent to Bermuda ELP must route via SWIFT MT103 with a compliant purpose code: use the OECD Enhanced 4/5th character purpose type 'Equity injection to non-resident enterprise' or BOP code 'Direct investment equity capital'; include 'Bermuda Exempted Partnership — Capital Contribution under Clause [X] of LPA' in the remittance narrative. UK issuing bank expects a certified copy of the ELP's Certificate of Exempted Partnership, the LPA capital schedule, and a board resolution of the UK general partner authorising the transfer. The receiving Bermudian bank will request proof that the remitter (UK parent) holds the ELP interest; the funds must land in the dedicated Capital Escrow Account first, be held for a minimum of two to three business days for source-of-funds verification, then be swept to the Operational Account. Apportion any in-kind contributions (IP licences) to a separate non-cash side-letter to avoid triggering a foreign direct investment notification requirement under the UK's National Security and Investment Act 2021. Verify the purpose code is not erroneously classified as 'royalty' as that may attract Bermuda withholding tax obligations on copyright royalty payments to non-residents.Primary outbound clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for all vendor/trade payments above GBP 5,000, executed via the BMA-licensed bank's treasury portal; routing through CHIPS (USD) or CHAPS (GBP) with the Bermudian bank as correspondent. For platform fee settlements to Apple and Google, maintain dedicated settlement accounts nominated in USD; Apple IAP payouts arrive via ACH in the US or via UK bank transfer in GBP, requiring reconciliation to the ELP's revenue ledger under each platform's developer agreement. Commercial Letters of Credit (documentary credits) are permissible for physical infrastructure procurement (e.g., server equipment, on-premise transcoding racks) should the business acquire assets; use UCP 600-compliant drafts with the Bermudian bank as issuing bank and any UK or US bank as advising bank. For sub-5,000 GBP operating disbursements to UK suppliers, CHAPS (same-day) or FPS (Faster Payments Service) via a UK-domiciled reference account is operationally efficient; the GBP reference account must be disclosed to the Bermudian compliance officer. All trade disbursements over USD 50,000 require submission of the underlying invoice and the ELP's board-approved expense authorisation matrix to the bank's transaction surveillance unit. Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% base rate — Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or dividend taxation on Exempted Limited Partnerships. A 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT / QDMTT top-up tax) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 applies exclusively to Bermuda constituent entities of multinational enterprise (MNE) groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years, with effect for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no dividend withholding tax, and because the ELP is fiscally transparent, partnership distributions to non-resident limited partners are remitted gross without deduction or notification to the Bermuda tax authorities.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 15 months after the ELP's date of registration, and thereafter annually by the same rolling anniversary; the Registrar's practice permits a volume lodgement schedule for service provider portfolios, but overdue filings attract progression to compulsory dissolution.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Form A registration is due by 31 March 2025 or within 3 months after the entity becomes a constituent entity. Form B is due within 15 months after the end of the first fiscal year of qualifying status, and within 12 months after the end of each subsequent fiscal year; the self-assessed top-up tax is payable on the same deadline with interest for late payment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general threshold below BMD/EUR 750 million; Bermuda imposes no income tax on ordinary ELPs, so no Local File or Master File is required below the GloBE line. At the threshold (MNE group consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, approximately BMD 750 million, in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years), each Bermuda constituent entity must prepare OECD-aligned Local File and Master File, country-by-country data, and contemporaneous intercompany pricing evidence in BMD under the CIT Act 2024 and CIT-FIRS.
