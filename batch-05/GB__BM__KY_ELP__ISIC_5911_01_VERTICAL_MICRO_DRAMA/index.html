Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Bermuda ELP is mandatorily tax-transparent; profits flow through to partners who are taxed in their respective jurisdictions of residence. Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no withholding tax on distributions or royalties; it is also outside the UK VAT territory, so no UK VAT applies solely by reason of the ELP's Bermuda formation.

Available election forms

Bermuda: there is no US-style check-the-box election; the ELP cannot elect entity-level taxation. The partnership may elect to obtain a Tax Assurance Certificate under section 11(5) of the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 and may elect its Economic Substance reporting category (pure equity holding, intellectual property, or distribution and service centre). UK: the partnership may elect UK tax transparency under the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, Part 9; hybrid mismatch elections under CTA 2009 Part 6A and SI 2020/1156 may be filed with HMRC to align UK treatment with the Bermuda transparent classification and to neutralize any deductible-payment mismatches.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: No VAT, GST, or retail sales tax; indirect taxation is limited to Bermuda Customs Duty levied on imported goods under the Customs Tariff Act 1970. UK: Value Added Tax (VAT) under VATA 1994 applies to supplies of the micro-drama streaming app to UK consumers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: BMD 0 - not applicable; there is no VAT/GST regime and no indirect tax registration obligation for the ELP; customs duty applies only at the point of physical importation with no turnover threshold. UK: GBP 90,000 general taxable turnover threshold for UK-established businesses, but a de facto nil threshold applies to non-established suppliers of digital services (micro-drama subscriptions) to UK consumers, requiring UK VAT registration from the first pound of UK B2C digital revenue; B2B supplies of streaming rights to UK recipients are generally subject to the reverse charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: not applicable - there is no VAT/GST against which zero-rating can be claimed; exports of digital micro-drama streaming rights and global distribution services are simply outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation. UK: supplies of digital micro-drama streaming rights to non-UK-resident customers are outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rules in VATA 1994, section 7A and Schedule 4A; B2B supplies to UK VAT-registered customers are zero-rated or reverse-charged; B2C exports to non-UK consumers are outside UK VAT. The ELP cannot claim refunds of UK input VAT if it makes no UK taxable supplies; any UK VAT recovery must be linked to UK taxable activities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fully liberalised exchange-control regime. The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is legal tender at a fixed 1:1 par with the U.S. Dollar, and USD circulates freely as an accepted parallel settlement currency. The BMA does not impose prior approval gates, licensing requirements, or quota restrictions on current-account or capital-account transactions of an Exempted Limited Partnership, including capital contributions, intra-group funding, streaming-platform royalty receipts, production disbursements, and profit distributions. Bermuda levies no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on non-resident partners, and the remittance of distributable profits is unrestricted; the only procedural conditions are the GP's distribution resolution, a solvency statement where required by the Exempted Partnerships Act 2006, and the filing of the annual economic substance declaration with the Registrar of Companies. In practice, the sole friction points are banking-source-of-funds due diligence and BMA-required suspicious-transaction reporting, neither of which restricts convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The BMA imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no minimum in-Bermuda retention period, and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts derived from Apple, Google, and Stripe settlement inflows related to the global distribution of vertical micro-dramas. A Bermuda ELP may lawfully hold such proceeds in USD, BMD, GBP, or EUR in any licensed financial institution, remote EMI ledger, or correspondent account without conversion or surrender to the BMA. The former Exchange Control Act framework has been liberalised and there remains no obligation to repatriate receivables into Bermuda or to effect any foreign-exchange conversion; the only relevant obligations are standard AML/ATF record-keeping under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations 2008, FATCA/CRS account reporting, and the economic substance notification, none of which alters the free disposition of export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as an ancillary disbursement rail; Low as the principal treasury spine. Legal constraints: Bermuda reserves deposit-taking business for BMA-licensed banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and foreign fintech EMIs cannot obtain a Bermudian banking licence or hold themselves out as deposit-taking institutions in or from Bermuda. An FCA-authorised UK EMI (e.g., Wise Platform, Airwallex, Currencycloud) may open a virtual multi-currency ledger for the Bermuda ELP as a non-resident corporate customer, but the EMI's settlement ultimately requires a BMA-licensed correspondent relationship, and Bermudian tier-1 banks frequently decline correspondent clearance from EMIs for exempted-partnership structures with non-resident GPs and distributed UBOs. The practical architecture is therefore a hybrid: low-value production payouts and staff reimbursements below USD 50,000 per instruction may run through a UK EMI ledger with monthly reconciliation; all capital formation, profit distribution, and core treasury concentration flows must remain on the Butterfield domestic rail to preserve BMA-regulated clearing and audit integrity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Routing: SWIFT MT103 is sent from the UK parent's FCA-authorised bank to the ELP's Butterfield USD account, with the ISO 20022 remittance classification 'C-capital transfer' and field-70 reference 'ELP CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / CAPITAL CALL EXHIBIT A'. The UK remitter records the payment as direct investment under the international transaction reporting code; no HMRC or Bank of England pre-clearance is required. The Bermuda receiving bank records the credit to the ELP's capital account and applies standard enhanced due diligence. Verification bundle pre-submitted to the receiving bank: executed capital commitment agreement; GP capital-call resolution; updated register of partners and UBO declaration; source-of-funds statement supported by the UK parent's audited accounts and bank reference; certificate of good standing from the Bermuda Registrar; and the economic substance acknowledgement. No BMA exchange-control permission is required, and funds become immediately available for operational disbursement after sanctions screening.Two clearing constructs apply. (1) SWIFT MT103 bank transfers for routine trade settlements: production-studio outsourcing fees, talent and agent payments, post-production services, platform commissions, and licensing royalties; each transfer attaches the underlying invoice reference, the ISIC 5911 cost code, and an automated sanctions-screening certificate. (2) Commercial Letter of Credit (UCP 600, irrevocable and confirmed) for high-value or milestone-based production contracts exceeding USD 250,000 per season, issued by Butterfield in favour of the producing studio and payable against production milestone certificates, acceptance documents, and IP-delivery manifests. Collections from Apple and Google arrive via their settlement banks, either directly or through Stripe, with reference 'APPLE_IAP / GOOGLE_PLAY VERTICAL DRAMA NET REVENUE'; daily sweeps concentrate funds into the Butterfield USD account under a BMA-compliant notional cash-pooling mandate executed by the GP. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium as an ancillary disbursement rail; Low as the principal treasury spine. Legal constraints: Bermuda reserves deposit-taking business for BMA-licensed banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and foreign fintech EMIs cannot obtain a Bermudian banking licence or hold themselves out as deposit-taking institutions in or from Bermuda. An FCA-authorised UK EMI (e.g., Wise Platform, Airwallex, Currencycloud) may open a virtual multi-currency ledger for the Bermuda ELP as a non-resident corporate customer, but the EMI's settlement ultimately requires a BMA-licensed correspondent relationship, and Bermudian tier-1 banks frequently decline correspondent clearance from EMIs for exempted-partnership structures with non-resident GPs and distributed UBOs. The practical architecture is therefore a hybrid: low-value production payouts and staff reimbursements below USD 50,000 per instruction may run through a UK EMI ledger with monthly reconciliation; all capital formation, profit distribution, and core treasury concentration flows must remain on the Butterfield domestic rail to preserve BMA-regulated clearing and audit integrity.
