Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda (jurisdiction of establishment): Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; operational tax administration via the Bermuda Government e-Tax/Business portal at www.gov.bm; Registrar of Companies (Registry General) for ELP incorporation and Economic Substance filings. United Kingdom (sovereign territory extension): HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), 100 Parliament Street, London, UK; UK VAT registration, Making Tax Digital (MTD) service, and Customs Declaration Service (CDS) accessible via GOV.UK portal.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Entity formation: file the Exempted Limited Partnership registration with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992; obtain the Certificate of Registration and the Register of Partners; no minimum capital requirement applies and 100% foreign (non-resident) ownership is permitted. Step 2 - Statutory tax identity: Bermuda does not issue a universal taxpayer identification number; the ELP obtains line-specific tax accounts from the Office of the Tax Commissioner only as triggers arise: (a) employer payroll tax registration within 14 days of first Bermudian payroll, producing an employer payroll tax account number (Payroll Tax Act 1995, rates from 0% to 10.25%); (b) Corporate Income Tax registration within the relevant registration window under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 if the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million (15% CIT, phased rates from 9% in fiscal 2025, 12% in 2026, 14% in 2027, 15% from fiscal 2028); small and medium ELPs are outside CIT scope; (c) Business Licence number under the Business Licensing Act 2014 for any trade or business carried on in Bermuda. Step 3 - UK VAT identity (territorial UK supplies): apply to HMRC via Form VAT1 (online VAT registration) for a GB VAT registration number when the ELP has a UK establishment (branch, depot, employees, or vehicles) or when UK taxable turnover exceeds GBP 90,000 in a rolling 12-month period (VATA 1994, Schedule 1); for B2B cross-border courier services to UK customers the reverse charge may apply, making UK VAT registration unnecessary for inbound supplies unless the ELP makes UK-domestic taxable supplies. Step 4 - Pass-through documentation: the ELP should obtain a Certificate of Tax Status/Residence from the Bermuda Ministry of Finance (where available) and an annual Economic Substance Declaration filing confirmation, as these instruments substantiate the pass-through classification and Ber-muda tax residence to foreign tax authorities and banks.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is tax transparent by default: the ELP itself is not a person for Bermuda income tax (Bermuda levies no income tax, capital gains tax or withholding tax) and for UK tax purposes the profits are attributed to, and taxed in the hands of, the limited and general partners according to their distributive shares (UK CTA 2009, s.59 for UK-resident partners). Territorial scope: the ELP is subject to Bermuda payroll tax (if Bermudian employees), Bermuda business licensing fees, and the Bermuda Economic Substance regime; if the ELP is managed and controlled from the UK, it additionally risks UK tax residence and a UK permanent establishment for corporation tax (19%-25% rates depending on profit level, CTA 2010). No tax-exempt zone status applies because the entire Bermuda system is essentially tax-neutral for the offshore ELP; however, the ELP may rely on the 'pure equity holding' economic substance exemption only if its activities are limited to holding equity participations and receiving dividends/capital gains - active cross-border express courier operations do not qualify for that exemption and will require satisfaction of Bermuda economic substance tests to the extent the activity is carried on in or from Bermuda.

Available election forms

Available statutory elections: (1) Bermuda CIT Act 2023 - 'look-through entity' election: an ELP that would otherwise be a look-through entity may irrevocably elect to be treated as a taxable unit for Bermuda CIT purposes (election is filed with the Tax Commissioner; a taxable-unit election is generally inadvisable for an offshore pass-through vehicle unless required for OECD Pillar Two group alignment). (2) UK hybrid mismatch elections under TIOPA 2010, Part 6A: the ELP or its UK partners may elect to align the treatment of the ELP for UK tax purposes with its Bermuda look-through classification to prevent double counting, double deduction, or deduction-without-inclusion outcomes; UK anti-hybrid rules may recharacterise payments involving hybrid entities and impose adjustments on UK partners. (3) UK VAT group election (VATA 1994, Schedule 1A): if the ELP becomes a member of a UK VAT group with a UK-established affiliate, supplies between group members are disregarded for VAT. (4) UK option to tax (election under VATA 1994, Schedule 10) - not applicable to courier/transport services, which are standard-rated or zero-rated, not exempt. (5) No US 'check-the-box' (Form 8832) election is applicable to a Bermuda ELP as such; however, default US classification of a foreign eligible entity with two or more members is partnership (pass-through), and a single-member ELP (if owned entirely by one US person) would be a disregarded entity unless a Form 8832 election to be taxed as a corporation is made. This dossier assumes election-neutral pass-through treatment in Bermuda, the UK, and the US.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK VAT: GBP 90,000 taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period (VATA 1994, Schedule 1, from 1 April 2024); additionally, mandatory registration applies where the ELP has reasonable grounds to believe its UK taxable supplies will exceed GBP 90,000 in the following 30-day period, or where it has a UK establishment (fixed establishment) from which taxable supplies are made. For UK B2B customers receiving courier services, the reverse charge (s.8 VATA 1994, SI 1992/3121) shifts accounting to the customer, so no UK VAT registration is required for outbound-only B2B supplies. Bermuda: no VAT registration threshold exists; however, any person carrying on a trade or business in Bermuda must obtain a business licence (flat/annual fee schedule under the Business Licensing Act 2014) and employers must register for payroll tax at a Nil rate band up to BMD 10,000 per employee (rates thereafter up to 10.25%).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK zero-rating for international courier transport: Under VATA 1994, Schedule 8, Group 8 (and HMRC VAT Notice 703), the supply of a transport of goods from a place in the UK to a place outside the UK is zero-rated. The ELP may zero-rate the international leg of an express courier movement where: (i) the transport begins in the UK and ends outside the UK or in a non-UK destination (including Bermuda, Crown Dependencies and EU/global destinations); (ii) ancillary services directly in connection with that international transport (e.g., collection, consolidation, handling, customs broking) supplied to the transport provider or consignor may also be zero-rated on the same evidence basis; (iii) the ELP obtains, within 3 months of the time of supply (HMRC may allow up to 6 months with prior approval), valid evidence of export including the UK customs export declaration/Goods Departed message (MRN), the parcel-level consignment record, and proof of physical departure; (iv) the domestic-only segment of a shipment (UK origin to UK destination) is standard-rated unless it is part of an international transit movement. Where the ELP acts as a 'groupage' consolidator, a single consolidated export declaration may be acceptable for customs, but the parcel-level waybills must identify each consignment and ultimate foreign destination. Bermuda levies no VAT, so no export zero-rating claim exists locally; however, customs duties paid on imported goods that are subsequently re-exported may be reclaimed via the customs duty drawback and temporary importation mechanisms under the Bermuda Customs Tariff Act 2018, provided the goods are re-exported in the same or manufactured state within the statutory period and documentary proof of export is retained.

UK: No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government pre-clearance of e-invoices is mandated. HMRC requires all VAT-registered businesses to maintain digital records and file VAT returns through the Making Tax Digital (MTD) service using HMRC-recognised software connected via HMRC's APIs; the VAT return must be submitted quarterly with a 5-year record retention obligation. E-invoicing is voluntary (UK supports PEPPOL/EN 16931 for B2B exchange) and there is no mandatory government certification of e-invoices. For exports, customs declarations must be lodged electronically via the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS), which generates a Movement Reference Number (MRN); pre-lodgement and goods-departed messages are transmitted via API (CTS/Inventory linking); for road freight the Goods Vehicle Movement Service (GVMS) generates a Goods Movement Reference (GMR). Express courier operators must also submit electronic Air Waybill data (IATA e-AWB) and EU Import Control System 2 (ICS2) entry summary declarations for consignments in transit through the EU. Bermuda: no e-invoicing mandate; Bermuda Customs operates an electronic single-window declaration system for import/export entries, and duty drawback claims require electronic record linkage with the Customs Tariff Act 2018.

UK VAT: HMRC may issue an assessment for undeclared output VAT within the statutory discovery window - 4 years for ordinary under-declarations, 6 years for careless inaccuracies, and 20 years for deliberate concealment (VATA 1994 s.77 and FA 2008 s.118). Incorrectly claimed zero-rating is clawed back in full and recharacterised as standard-rated output tax, with default interest (s.101 FA 2009) running from the original due date. Penalties for inaccuracies are imposed under FA 2007 Schedule 24 at up to 100% of the potential lost revenue (mitigated for disclosure); a deliberately dishonest zero-rating claim also triggers the civil evasion penalty under FA 2003 (maximum 100% of the VAT evaded plus interest) and may constitute the criminal offence of fraudulent evasion of VAT under VATA 1994 s.72 and CEMA 1979 - carrying penalties of up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines. Where export evidence is not obtained within the 3/6-month window, HMRC may deny zero-rating on a retrospective basis and assess all previously zero-rated consignments. Bermuda: no VAT exists and therefore no VAT clawback applies; however, false or inflated duty drawback/exemption declarations are prosecuted under the Bermuda Customs Tariff Act 2018 and Customs regulations (forfeiture of goods plus fines of up to three times the duty evaded). Additional Bermuda compliance risk: failure to file an Economic Substance Declaration, or filing false substance information, attracts civil penalties under the Economic Substance Act 2018 and potential strike-off by the Registrar of Companies; non-compliance with the UK Tax (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010 hybrid mismatch rules or DPT (FA 2015) can additionally result in 25% diverted profits tax and uncapped penalties where arrangements lack economic substance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility is achievable. The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to the USD, and USD circulates as legal tender in Bermuda. The Exchange Control Act 1972 distinguishes 'resident' and 'non-resident' persons; a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) owned wholly by non-resident partners can obtain 'non-resident designated' status, eliminating all BMA consent gates for both current-account and capital-account transactions. Profit remittances, capital repatriation, inter-company loans, and asset-disposal proceeds are freely transferable in any currency once this designation is applied. A resident-designated ELP would face BMA approval for capital transfers, which is unnecessary for the KY_ELP structure. On the UK side, there are no convertibility hurdles: the UK permits unrestricted capital movements, a UK branch of a transparent ELP is not subject to withholding tax on profit allocations, and only UK-source trade profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment are taxable for non-resident partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda currently imposes no fixed statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. The residual obligation to offer foreign-currency export receipts to an authorised dealer applies only to exchange-control 'residents' and is disapplied for a non-resident designated ELP; in practice the BMA permits full retention of USD, EUR, and GBP in multicurrency accounts held with authorised dealers. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; surrender window: not applicable (no 30/60/90-day rule). The UK likewise imposes no export-proceeds surrender rules; exporters may retain 100% of export receipts in any currency with no repatriation or conversion duty.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, operating as the exchange-control and financial-services regulator under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (Bermuda) and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973. For sterling clearing in the parent sovereign (GB), the Bank of England operates as the ultimate settlement bank, but the UK abolished exchange controls in 1979 and has no operative foreign-exchange administration; HM Treasury's residual powers under the repealed Exchange Control Act 1947 no longer apply.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Pure digital e-money institutions (Revolut Business, Wise Business, Tide, Zopa) generally refuse Bermuda-incorporated offshore ELPs because their terms require UK/EU incorporation and their risk frameworks flag 'offshore partnerships' as enhanced jurisdiction. UK FCA-authorised B2B payment fintechs (Airwallex, Ebury, Currencycloud/Visa, WorldFirst, IFX) will only consider onboarding if the ELP establishes a UK-registered branch or UK corporate vehicle with genuine courier-operating substance, a UK-resident MLRO and signatory, and verifiable trade flows evidenced by freight and last-mile invoices. Legal constraint: under the Payment Services Regulations 2017 and FCA e-money safeguarding rules, the EMI must hold client money in UK banks; cross-border services to a third-country (Bermuda) entity are permissible only through compliant reverse-solicitation and may not be used as the primary treasury account. Recommendation: use an EMI solely as a settlement accessory or merchant-acquiring concentrator only after the local Bermudian tier-1 account is live.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Each LP capital injection is routed by SWIFT MT103 from the LP's own bank account directly to the ELP's escrow/operational account at a Bermudian authorised dealer. Field 70 (Remittance Information) must carry the capital-call reference, and the payment must bear the receiving bank's mapped balance-of-payments purpose code for direct-investment equity capital (IMF BPM6 cross-border classification) as supplied by the remitting bank. Pre-validation requirements include a signed capital-call notice, the LP's executed commitment schedule, a source-of-funds statement, and a bank statement of the remitting LP matching the remitter account name (third-party payments are rejected). For remittances above USD 1,000,000, an enhanced due-diligence form and wealth report are mandatory. No BMA approval is required for a non-resident designated ELP; the authorised dealer will file the transaction for BMA balance-of-payments statistics. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Bermuda exempted undertakings (no Bermuda corporate income tax on overseas-source income); 15% for a constituent entity of a multinational group with consolidated annual revenue ≥ EUR 750 million under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, for fiscal years beginning on/after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% in Bermuda. The ELP may make distributions to partners without any Bermuda withholding tax. No withholding tax on partnership profits, dividends, or capital returns is levied under Bermuda law.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No standalone Local File or Master File requirement is imposed for Bermuda exempted partnerships outside the CITA scope. Once the ELP becomes a CITA constituent entity, contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is required when annual controlled transactions exceed BMD 10,000,000 (approx. USD 10 million). For MNE groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750,000,000, a Bermuda GloBE Information Return and CbC report notification are required; the Minister may lower the BMD threshold by statutory instrument.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance (Entities) Regulations, as amended by the Companies and Partnerships (Economic Substance) Acts 2018-2019, apply. Cross-border express courier logistics is a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity when the ELP sells goods or services to connected parties and performs the core income-generating activities in Bermuda. The ELP must demonstrate: (i) direction and management physically in Bermuda; (ii) adequate premises and full-time staff; (iii) operating expenditure proportionate to the income; and (iv) the core income-generating functions, chiefly route planning, contract negotiation, inventory management and invoice billing, are actually performed in Bermuda. A failure to meet this test may result in a 'returns-based economic substance adjustment' and the imposition of the UK's diverted profits tax or HMRC counteraction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / CITA Registration Omission

Statutory sanction: Bermuda CIT Act 2023 penalty provisions: fine of BMD 250,000; daily continuation penalty of BMD 10,000 per day; civil penalty equal to the CIT evaded plus 50%; re-assessment up to six back years; publication on the Bermuda Registry's public sanctions list.

Director personal liability: The managing partner and directors are jointly and severally liable for fraudulent evasion; on conviction, imprisonment for up to 5 years; the Tax Commissioner may issue a travel interdiction order preventing the official from leaving Bermuda while the investigation is pending.
Shell Company / Insufficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and dissolution of the ELP; immediate revocation of the exempted undertaking certificate; penalty of BMD 25,000 per month until substance is restored; directors may be prohibited from holding any Bermuda corporate office for 10 years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: the General Partner is jointly liable for all unpaid Bermuda and UK taxes; HMRC can obtain a court order appointing a receiver over the partner's British assets; a senior officer may receive a 10-year director disqualification order and a business prohibition order.
AML / Proceeds-of-Crime Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2013: fine up to BMD 2 million and imprisonment up to 15 years on conviction; civil forfeiture of the entire ELP account balance; HM Treasury sanctions may freeze all payments routed through UK correspondent banks.

Director personal liability: Every general partner and director is criminally liable if the money laundering was carried out with their consent or knowledge; the High Court may order disgorgement of all management fees received; on conviction, mandatory cancellation of professional licences and inclusion on the UK sanctions list.
Unlicensed Courier / Regulatory Licensing Breach

Statutory sanction: Communications (Regulation) Act: cease-and-desist order; daily fine of BMD 5,000 until the service stops; confiscation of courier vans and e-commerce sorting equipment; repeated breaches lead to revocation of all import and customs licences and material disqualification from holding a Bermuda postal licence for 15 years.

Director personal liability: The General Partner is personally liable for the daily fines under the principle of voluntary participation; conviction carries 3 years' imprisonment; the Bermuda Customs Service may impound consignments and recover unpaid duties directly from the partner's personal assets.
