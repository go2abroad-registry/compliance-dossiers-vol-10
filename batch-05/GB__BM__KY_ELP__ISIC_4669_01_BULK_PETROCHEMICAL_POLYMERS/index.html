E-government filing portal

Bermuda has no income-tax TIN, VAT ID, or general taxpayer identification number; the ELP registration number issued by the Registrar of Companies is the de facto identifier for government filings. Standard procedure: (1) Register the Exempted Limited Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 and obtain the Certificate of Exemption and partnership registration number. (2) Open a Bermuda bank account; the ELP may maintain BMD or foreign-currency accounts under Bermuda's exchange-control general permissions, and the bank will perform AML/KYC due diligence. (3) If the partnership carries on business within Bermuda, apply for a Trade Licence with the Ministry of Finance and obtain the licence number; no licence is required for purely offshore trading. (4) If the ELP employs individuals based in Bermuda, register for Payroll Tax with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and file payroll-tax returns and payments. (5) If goods are imported into Bermuda for local consumption, register a customs clearing account with Bermuda Customs, appoint a licensed customs broker, and file electronic entries through ASYCUDA World. (6) File the annual Economic Substance Declaration and pay the ELP's annual government fee; if no relevant activity is conducted, file as Non-Relevant Activity. (7) If the ELP is a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group (consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million), register and file returns under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, which imposes a 15% CIT/QDMTT for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Available election forms

Bermuda does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election because it imposes no general corporate income tax. The ELP is fiscally transparent by default; partners are taxed in their respective residence jurisdictions, and Bermuda tax outcomes are unaffected by entity classification. The only statutory elections and designations relevant at the Bermuda level are (a) the ESA reporting category designated in the annual Economic Substance Declaration (e.g., Non-Relevant Activity, Distribution and Service Center, or Headquarters); and (b) for in-scope MNE groups, the GloBE/QDMTT compliance elections under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023. Partners may separately make entity-classification elections in their home jurisdictions (e.g., US Form 8832 for a default-transparent partnership); Bermuda imposes no conflicting classification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom applies full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. Bermuda, as a UK Overseas Territory, does not maintain independent exchange controls; the Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to the US dollar (USD). Profit remittances from Bermuda to the UK or other jurisdictions are unrestricted, subject only to standard anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CTF) due diligence. The UK's retained EU-derived capital account liberalisation and OECD capital flow liberalisation obligations apply; inward direct investment and repatriation of sales proceeds from the wholesale petrochemical polymer distribution activity are freely permitted. No statutory profit remittance prohibition, currency rationing, or prior approval requirement exists for the described pass-through ELP structure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement applies. The UK does not mandate conversion of foreign currency receipts; a UK-resident entity may maintain foreign currency accounts domestically or with banks in Bermuda and the UK without any prescribed conversion ratio or forced surrender timeline. Bermuda likewise imposes no obligation to convert USD or other export receipts into BMD. The entity may retain and disburse gross settlement inflows in USD, EUR, or GBP as contractually required.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England and Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium feasibility for the bulk petrochemical polymer wholesale model, conditioned on enhanced KYC, contract corroboration, and service-level restrictions. Permissible UK-regulated EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Business, ClearBank via banking-as-a-service) and Bermuda-licensed digital asset/EMI-adjacent institutions may provide multi-currency operational accounts, SWIFT/SEPA payment initiation, and treasury visibility. However, the mandatory target payment rails for this ISIC 4669 business (SWIFT MT103 and irrevocable letters of credit) exceed the settlement capabilities of most EMIs, which generally do not issue or confirm documentary credits. EMIs can be used for ancillary disbursements and collections under GBP 50,000 per transaction, but the core commodity settlement ledger and LC issuance must run through tier-1 commercial banks. Regulatory constraint: EMIs operating in Bermuda require BMA licensing under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act or a restricted fintech sandbox licence; foreign EMIs with no physical Bermudian presence cannot offer deposit-taking services to a Bermuda ELP without BMA recognition.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injection is to be structured as a bank-to-bank wire from the ultimate beneficial owner's home jurisdiction to the Bermuda ELP's USD or GBP operational account, using SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'G304 - Equity capital' (or UK-Westminster corresponding code) and the ELP's mandatory payment reference (task_id / entity identifier). The capital contribution should be documented via a capital contribution certificate and partnership ledger entry. For UK onward booking, the ELP may receive loans as trade finance advances with purpose code 'G302 - Intercompany loan' or use a standby letter of credit from the parent's bank to evidence the availability of funds. All incoming wires from higher-risk jurisdictions must be pre-screened against FATF and EU/OFAC sanctions lists; a source-of-wealth statement for funds above GBP 75,000 per single remittance is recommended. No exchanger control permission is required in the UK or Bermuda.Outbound trade settlements for bulk polyethylene (PE) and polypropylene (PP) granules must use commercial clearing rails that mirror the documentary sales contract. Primary rail: irrevocable confirmed letter of credit (L/C) issued by the buyer's bank, with the ELP as beneficiary, via SWIFT MT700; the nominated seller's bank in London (HSBC, Barclays, or Lloyds) acts as advising/paying bank. Disbursement to the upstream refiner/producer follows a back-to-back L/C or a SWIFT MT103 after presentation of shipping and quality certificates under the master supply contract. Settlement currency may be USD, EUR, or GBP; USD clearing occurs via the Clearing House Interbank Payments System (CHIPS) or Fedwire with correspondent bank routing. For non-LC open-account top-ups, the ELP must send MT103 with purpose codes 'S103 - Merchanting trade', 'T101 - Payments for imported goods' or 'T102 - Payments for exported goods' as applicable. CAD and D/P documentary collections (SWIFT MT400 series) are permissible for small parcel cargo.
