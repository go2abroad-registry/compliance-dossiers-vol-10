In standard exempted-ELP practice this office is the premises of a BMA-licensed corporate services provider, law firm, or trust company that holds a physical lease, employs responsible officers, and maintains the statutory registers; this arrangement is accepted for a non-resident, low-risk hard-enamel-pin merchandising partnership, but the registered agent must itself demonstrate real physical substance to the BMA.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner of Bermuda (OTC), Ministry of Finance, 43 Church Street, Hamilton HM CX, Bermuda — official portal: taxcommissioner.gov.bm. Entity formation and registration are administered by the Registrar of Companies, Registry General, Bermuda, accessible through the GovBermuda e-registration portal (gov.bm). Customs compliance is administered by the Bermuda Customs Service through the Bermuda Customs Automated System (CAS).

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not levy corporate income tax on exempted partnerships and therefore does not issue an income-tax TIN at formation. The exempted limited partnership (ELP) must: (1) submit the application for exemption under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014 (Form EPA-1) to the Registrar of Companies, Bermuda, together with the declaration of the general partner; (2) obtain name approval from the Registry General; (3) pay the prescribed registration and annual fees; and (4) receive the Certificate of Exempted Partnership Registration, which bears the Bermuda registration number. That registration number is the sole national identifier of the ELP for non-tax purposes. If the ELP is part of a multinational enterprise group with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750 million or more, it must additionally register for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 on the OTC's CIT registration portal and will then obtain a Bermuda tax identification number. If the ELP directly employs staff in Bermuda, the general partner must register as an employer for Bermuda payroll tax with the OTC; a non-resident ELP with no Bermuda employees has no such registration obligation.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda exempted limited partnership is treated as tax-transparent in Bermuda (no Bermuda income tax at entity level) and, for U.S. federal tax purposes, defaults to foreign partnership classification (pass-through) where there are two or more members, or is disregarded as an entity where there is a single owner, unless an affirmative election is made to treat it as an association taxable as a corporation. — Bermuda does not levy corporate income tax on exempted partnerships and therefore does not issue an income-tax TIN at formation. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda exempted limited partnership is treated as tax-transparent in Bermuda (no Bermuda income tax at entity level) and, for U.S. federal tax purposes, defaults to foreign partnership classification (pass-through) where there are two or more members, or is disregarded as an entity where there is a single owner, unless an affirmative election is made to treat it as an association taxable as a corporation.

Available election forms

(i) U.S. Form 8832 check-the-box election: with no election filed, the ELP is a foreign pass-through entity; the ELP may elect to be taxed as a corporation in the United States where commercially required. (ii) Non-resident designation election under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014: an ELP whose central management and control is exercised wholly outside Bermuda may apply to be designated as non-resident, confirming that it is not carrying on business in Bermuda and preserving its exemption from future Bermuda direct taxes. (iii) Tax-assurance certificate: the Minister of Finance may issue an assurance certificate insulating the ELP from any future Bermuda direct tax legislation enacted within the certificate's protected period. (iv) Under the CIT Act 2023, in-scope groups may elect between the transitional tax credit regime and other CIT transitional provisions; no hybrid-mismatch election exists under Bermuda domestic law because the ELP is fiscally transparent in Bermuda.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None in Bermuda — Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax. The only indirect levies relevant to the ELP are non-recoverable Bermuda customs duties on imports and stamp duty on specified instruments; UK VAT may arise separately on distance sales into the United Kingdom, but such VAT is outside the Bermuda fiscal system.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT/GST and therefore no VAT registration threshold. For customs purposes, every commercial importer and exporter must be registered with the Bermuda Customs Service irrespective of turnover, and customs duty is payable from the first importation into Bermuda. Separately, if the ELP sells enamel pins into the United Kingdom, it may become liable to register for UK VAT once UK taxable distance sales exceed the GBP 90,000 UK VAT registration threshold, or earlier where Etsy is treated as the seller and UK import VAT applies under marketplace rules; that obligation is governed by UK law, not Bermuda law.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating regime, no input VAT refund mechanism, and no VAT-export substantiation requirement. Exports of enamel pins from Bermuda are simply not subject to Bermuda customs duty, provided a customs outwards declaration is filed. The substantive substantiation obligation is to demonstrate that the ELP is non-resident, has no Bermuda employees, and does not carry on business in Bermuda, so that no Bermuda payroll or future direct-tax exposure arises; the ELP must also document that the economic substance relevant activities in Bermuda do not apply to ISIC 3240 manufacturing/merchandising operations.

  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List stating Bermuda exporter, overseas buyer, Incoterms, and ISIC 3240 merchandise description

  • Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe settlement reports evidencing cross-border sales proceeds and the identity of the foreign purchasers

None — Bermuda imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no statutory e-invoicing API, and no digital tax stamping regime. Record-keeping obligations require the general partner to maintain accounting records adequate to explain the ELP's transactions and financial position, as required under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014. Customs declarations for physical exports and imports are lodged electronically through the Bermuda Customs Automated System (CAS) using customs broker or registered trader credentials, but CAS is a customs clearance platform rather than a tax clearance or fiscal stamping system.

There is no export-VAT fraud or zero-rating clawback regime in Bermuda because no VAT is imposed. Enforcement exposures are nonetheless real: (i) goods declared for export but diverted into the Bermudian domestic market are subject to forfeiture and penalties under the Bermuda Customs Act, including fines of up to BDA 5,000 per offence, an amount equal to double the duty evaded, and imprisonment for up to two years; (ii) unpaid customs duty attracts interest at 10% per month in addition to the duty itself; (iii) misrepresentation of residency or exemption status in filings with the Registrar of Companies can result in revocation of the non-resident designation or tax-assurance certificate, with retrospective direct-tax liability; (iv) the Office of the Tax Commissioner may impose default penalties of BDA 250 per month, plus interest on arrears, under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 if the ELP is later determined to be part of an in-scope multinational group; and (v) materially false statements made in exemption filings may attract fines of up to BDA 100,000 and disqualification of the general partner or administrator.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the USD; USD is legal tender and universally accepted. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, Bermuda distinguishes Resident from Non-Resident (External) persons. An Exempted Limited Partnership designated 'Non-Resident' by the BMA may hold external accounts at Authorised Dealers and enjoys full capital- and current-account convertibility without BMA approval. Capital account: unrestricted conversion and cross-border transfer of funds for partner contributions. Current account: no licence required for trade settlements, platform disbursements (Etsy Direct Pay, Stripe) or profit remittances to non-resident partners. Profit remittance hurdles: nil at Bermuda level (zero-tax jurisdiction, no withholding), but the practical hurdle is satisfying the bank's source-of-funds due diligence before the first outward payment. Failure to obtain Non-Resident designation subjects the ELP to monthly foreign-currency surrender obligations and per-payment consent, so designation should precede banking activation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Authorised-dealer licence conditions under the Exchange Control Act 1972 require any Resident account in Bermuda to offer all foreign-currency export receipts (including goods produced or sold by the partnership) to the authorised dealer within 30 days of receipt and to surrender such proceeds into BMD within 90 days, unless the BMA grants a longer period. A BMA-designated Non-Resident ELP is exempt from these surrender rules: the partnership may retain USD/EUR/GBP balances indefinitely in an external account and may apply them to supplier payments or partner distributions without conversion. Since Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe settle in USD (with automatic FX into buyer currencies already performed by the PSP), no mandatory conversion will apply to e-commerce proceeds once the external-account designation is endorsed by the appointed bank. The non-resident designation certificate must be presented at account opening and re-validated on any material change in partner composition.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - Foreign Exchange Control Department; established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; exchange control administered under the Exchange Control Act 1972 through Authorised Dealers (licensed banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for UK/EU-regulated EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer) used as auxiliary collection/settlement rails; LOW for a Bermuda-licensed EMI because Bermuda does not operate a standalone e-money or payment-service licensing regime. Legal constraints: (1) A Bermuda-incorporated ELP is not prohibited from opening an account with an overseas EMI, but the EMI applies its own home-state KYC rules; (2) issuing e-money or accepting deposits in Bermuda would trigger the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 requiring a full banking licence, so EMIs must be used purely for inbound collection and onward settlement to the Bermuda external account; (3) the BMA regulates digital asset businesses under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018, but fiat payment services are reserved for licensed banks, meaning EMIs cannot onboard Bermuda-resident customers. Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe UK operate under UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and act as the natural front-end rails; settlement flows downstream to the Butterfield or HSBC external account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Protocol for initial capitalisation from UK partners to the Bermuda ELP external account: (1) The general partner issues a capital call notice referencing the ELP and the designated BMD/USD external account at Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda. (2) The partner initiates a SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'EQUI' and remittance field 'KY_ELP Capital Injection - [Partner Name] - Capital Call Notice #[number]'; the UK remitting bank relies on the underlying partnership agreement to evidence commercial purpose. (3) The receiving Bermuda bank, acting as BMA Authorised Dealer, credits the external account without separate BMA consent because the Non-Resident designation pre-authorises the inflow. (4) The verification pack delivered to the bank operations desk comprises: certified copy of the ELP agreement capital provisions, capital call notice, six months of partner bank statements as source-of-wealth evidence, and the BMA Non-Resident designation certificate. (5) When capital derives from already-collected e-commerce float, no wire is necessary: the Stripe/Etsy settlement payout is directed to the external account and the corresponding payout batch report serves as the purpose documentation.Supplier and trade settlement rails: (1) Standard supplier wires to the contract manufacturer (typically China or Hong Kong) via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'TRA' and reference 'Goods purchase - ISIC 3240 hard enamel lapel pins'; attach the proforma invoice, packing list and bill of lading where sea freight applies; amounts exceeding USD 10,000 trigger OFAC and UK sanctions screening at both correspondent banks. (2) For first-time purchase orders of USD 50,000 or more, use an irrevocable, confirmed, at-sight letter of credit issued under UCP 600 and ISBP 745 through the Butterfield or HSBC trade desk, backed by 100% cash cover or a 50% secured credit line, mitigating supplier counterparty risk and strengthening trade-based AML evidence. (3) Platform disbursements: Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe support scheduled SWIFT payouts in USD to the Bermuda external account; for euro-denominated buyer flows, Stripe UAB converts at mid-market plus 0.5-1.0%, an acceptable frictional cost at current volume, and no additional forward overlay is required below USD 25,000 monthly settlement. (4) Every outbound disbursement must carry the beneficiary's VAT or company registration number to enable reconciliation against the invoice ledger and to support the bank's transaction monitoring.Outright deliverable forwards on USD/BMD (available via Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda treasury; the 1:1 hard peg means no forward discount or basis risk, but forwards are used only to lock GBP/USD and EUR/USD receipt conversions).Cross-currency swaps (USD/GBP or USD/EUR) for notional principals of at least USD 1,000,000, executed under an ISDA 2002 master agreement with the relationship bank, suitable for term partner loans or supplier financing.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not applicable for BMD because the full BMD/USD convertibility and the absence of an offshore NDF market make them redundant; for Chinese supplier settlement in CNY, an offshore deliverable CNY forward via the supplier's bank is permissible subject to the supplier's SAFE approval.At-the-money option collars and range forwards on GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD receipt currencies, recommended when monthly Etsy or Stripe settlement exceeds USD 25,000, executed through the bank's FX desk.Platform-native FX conversion embedded in Stripe and Etsy Direct Pay, contractually permissible for micro-flows and not subject to BMA licensing; the PSP acts as the hedging counterparty for each transaction and daily sweeps of residual balances mitigate large conversion exposure.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: HIGH for UK/EU-regulated EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer) used as auxiliary collection/settlement rails; LOW for a Bermuda-licensed EMI because Bermuda does not operate a standalone e-money or payment-service licensing regime. Legal constraints: (1) A Bermuda-incorporated ELP is not prohibited from opening an account with an overseas EMI, but the EMI applies its own home-state KYC rules; (2) issuing e-money or accepting deposits in Bermuda would trigger the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 requiring a full banking licence, so EMIs must be used purely for inbound collection and onward settlement to the Bermuda external account; (3) the BMA regulates digital asset businesses under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018, but fiat payment services are reserved for licensed banks, meaning EMIs cannot onboard Bermuda-resident customers. Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe UK operate under UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and act as the natural front-end rails; settlement flows downstream to the Butterfield or HSBC external account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Bermuda transfer pricing documentation threshold. Local File/Master File obligations are not triggered under Bermuda domestic law; however, if the ELP is part of a UK-headquartered group meeting HMRC's thresholds, UK documentation rules may apply for UK tax purposes.

Economic substance scope: Applicable under the Economic Substance (Bermuda) Act 2018 and associated Regulations. The retail e-commerce distribution of enamel lapel pins is not a 'relevant activity' (banking, insurance, fund management, financing, headquarters, IP, shipping, distribution and service centers, or holding entity). The ELP must file an annual Economic Substance Declaration only if a relevant activity is performed. For the described enamel pin business, no returnable economic substance test is triggered, but sufficient physical presence, local management, and operational substance should be maintained to avoid shell company characterization.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Non-Filing of Payroll Tax

Statutory sanction: Imposition of assessments for unpaid tax, penalties up to 100% of tax evaded, daily penalties of up to BD$500 for default, and potential criminal prosecution with fines up to BD$20,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 2 years

Director personal liability: General partners and controlling officers are jointly and severally liable for unpaid payroll taxes and penalties. Civil restitution may be ordered. Serious evasion can lead to travel bans, asset freezing orders, and disqualification from acting as a partner or director.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Inversion

Statutory sanction: Economic substance penalty of BD$250,000 for a core relevant activity; failure to provide information carries further penalties. For non-relevant activity shell structures, enforcement may arise through anti-avoidance rules in the UK or EU, resulting in disallowance of claims, interest, and penalties up to 200% of understated tax

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners can be personally liable for penalties if they consented to or connived in the shell arrangement. UK HMRC may apply the CFC rules or 'offshore receipts' legislation causing the profits to be attributed to UK shareholders. Personal liability for UK tax, National Insurance, and penalties is possible.
AML / Money Laundering Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Anti-Money Laundering fines up to BD$1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008; licence revocation or cancellation of Etsy/Stripe merchant accounts

Director personal liability: Partners and officers who fail to prevent money laundering are criminally liable if the offence was committed with their consent. Civil recovery of all funds or property laundered may be pursued. Travel bans and asset restraint orders are available as protective measures.
Cross-Border Withholding Tax Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: HMRC may raise determinations for the full amount of UK withholding tax not deducted, plus interest, and penalties under the Taxes Management Act 1970 (up to 100% of the tax unpaid).

Director personal liability: If the UK paying entity is a UK company, its directors can be personally liable for a formal determination if they were knowingly involved in the failure to deduct/account for withholding tax. Recovery and garnishment of overseas assets may follow.
Unregistered Foreign Entity Activity

Statutory sanction: HMRC penalties for failure to register for UK corporation tax or VAT starting at £100, plus daily penalties up to £10 per day, and assessments for any UK tax due on profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment

Director personal liability: Where a permanent establishment is deemed to exist, the ELP's UK representative (e.g., general partner) can be personally liable for the UK tax liabilities of the overseas partnership if they fail to comply with registration and filing duties.
