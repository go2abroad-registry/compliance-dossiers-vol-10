Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a bifurcated exchange-control regime. Current-account transactions (air-cargo brokerage commissions, freight forwarding fees, charter hire, landing and handling charges) are freely convertible and remittable without prior approval. Capital-account items (subscription for partnership interests, capital contributions, distributions to foreign limited partners and liquidation proceeds) require BMA permission unless the ELP obtains formal non-resident designation, which is the recommended route; once designated non-resident, the ELP enjoys unrestricted capital convertibility, zero remittance friction and no requirement to route settlements through a Bermudian resident account. No Bermuda withholding tax, capital gains tax, income tax or remittance tax is imposed, so profit repatriation is a pure compliance formality rather than a tax event; the practical hurdle is the bank's internal FX transfer surveillance, not any statutory conversion gate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio for export or service receipts. A non-resident designated ELP may retain 100% of USD, EUR, GBP and other foreign-currency proceeds in the currency originally received, and may freely convert to BMD at the pegged rate only when local operating accounts require Bermudian Dollars. Statutory obligations are limited to the filing of exchange-control declarations (Form EC1/EC2) and an annual exchange-control return to the BMA where the ELP is not yet designated non-resident; designated non-resident ELPs operate under a general permission and file solely on any material change in capital structure or partnership membership.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting as the central bank and exchange-control administrator in Bermuda (jurisdiction BM), exercising statutory powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973. The Bank of England remains the sovereign monetary authority of the parent state (United Kingdom) but does not regulate Bermudian banking, currency or capital flows because Bermuda is a self-governing Overseas Territory with fiscal and monetary autonomy. The official currency is the Bermudian Dollar (BMD), pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar (USD); USD is freely circulating legal tender and is interchangeable with BMD for all domestic and cross-border settlements.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for a Bermuda-domiciled ELP. The BMA does not issue European-style Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licences; any deposit-taking activity must be licensed under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. UK-regulated digital EMIs and challenger banks generally decline Bermudian partnerships because the entity is not UK/EEA incorporated and the air-cargo brokerage industry triggers enhanced AML screening. A viable alternative is a BMA-regulated Money Service Business (licensable under the Money Service Businesses Act 2018) used as a payment-facilitation layer alongside a tier-1 Bermudian bank account; the MSB may route USD and GBP settlements through its correspondent network, but all treasury balances must ultimately be held in a bank licensed by the BMA to preserve audit integrity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections must arrive by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the contributing limited partner's bank to the BMA-licensed Bermudian bank's correspondent account; the remittance must use purpose code 'Capital Contribution to Exempted Limited Partnership' and reference the ELP registration number in SWIFT field 70 (Remittance Information). For a non-resident designated ELP, prior BMA approval is not required, but the bank will demand the designation letter and the executed subscription document before crediting the account. Funds should first be placed in a dedicated Capital Escrow Account and released only after the compliance unit confirms source of wealth and the partnership has issued its capital-contribution receipt; any deferred-contribution schedule must be documented in the partnership agreement to avoid structuring alerts.Trade settlements flow via SWIFT MT103/MT202 for charter hire, fuel uplift, landing and handling fees, and brokerage commission payables; Fedwire is used where the beneficiary maintains a US correspondent account. Commercial letters of credit (documentary and standby) are advised or confirmed through Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda under UCP 600, with an MT700 issuer; a standby letter of credit is the preferred clearing instrument for charter performance bonds and deposit guarantees.
