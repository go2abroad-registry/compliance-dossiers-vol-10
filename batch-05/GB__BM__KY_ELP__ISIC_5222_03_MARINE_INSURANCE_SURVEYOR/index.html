Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda, accessible via the Government of Bermuda e-Tax portal and the official tax information portal at gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

The foreign investor forms a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership through a licensed registered agent filing an application with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992. The Registrar issues the partnership registration number, which acts as the legal entity identifier. For tax registration: if the ELP has Bermuda employees or otherwise carries on payroll-taxable activities in Bermuda, it must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner through the Government e-Tax portal; there is no separate invoice or VAT registration because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST. If the ELP becomes a constituent entity of a multinational group in scope of the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the ELP must also obtain a Bermuda Tax Reference Number through the CIT registration module and file the prescribed registration within the statutory deadline.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent by default; no general check-the-box election is available. For Bermuda Corporate Income Tax / OECD GloBE purposes, an eligible ELP may elect to be treated as a tax transparent entity for Pillar Two calculations, may elect the transitional substance-based income inclusion safe harbour, and must record any hybrid mismatch or transparent-entity treatment on the annual CIT information return. These elections are made by written notice to the Office of the Tax Commissioner and require supporting partnership agreement and constituent ownership documentation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST (Bermuda has no general consumption tax; the applicable indirect levies are customs duty, stamp duty, and employer payroll tax).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST. Payroll tax registration is mandatory once the ELP has any Bermuda salary-paying employees without a monetary de minimis; customs registration as an importer or customs broker is required for importation of goods, also without a general revenue threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST. Marine claims inspection and survey services supplied by a Bermuda ELP to non-resident underwriters are sourced outside Bermuda for payroll tax and CIT purposes unless a Bermuda permanent establishment or substance nexus exists; services supplied outside Bermuda are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect tax and do not require zero-rating, credit, or refund mechanics. Goods physically exported from Bermuda are generally exempt from customs duty if properly declared and documented, but this is an export duty exemption rather than a VAT zero-rating regime.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed through the Bermuda Customs Declaration System
  • Original or electronic Bill of Lading / Sea Waybill (or Air Waybill for airfreight) proving export from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Underwriter's claim assignment or survey service contract evidencing offshore service supply
  • Bank SWIFT advice or Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing collection of foreign-currency fees

Bermuda does not mandate real-time e-invoicing, continuous transaction control (CTC) clearance, or digital tax stamping. Customs declarations are processed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Declaration System; payroll tax and CIT returns are filed through the Government of Bermuda e-Tax service. There is no API-based fiscal invoice clearance regime applicable to marine survey invoices.

Bermuda Customs may audit export declarations and may impose forfeiture, monetary penalties up to three times the value of the goods or duty evaded, and criminal sanctions for false declarations under the Customs Tariff Act 1995. For CIT, an ELP that mischaracterises offshore income, improperly applies a tax transparency or hybrid election, or fails to comply with substance requirements is subject to reassessment, additional tax, interest, and penalties under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, together with OECD BEPS anti-abuse rules that can disallow the intended tax treatment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. Current-account payments and receipts by an exempted limited partnership (ELP) designated non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972 are fully convertible and may be settled in USD through external accounts. Capital-account convertibility is equally free for non-resident-owned ELPs; no specific BMA deal-by-deal approval is required once the ELP has obtained 'non-resident' status from the BMA Exchange Control Department. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal: Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no withholding tax, and no distributable-profit consent; a non-resident ELP may remit profits to partners offshore through an authorised dealer. The only practical hurdle is preserving the non-resident designation; should the ELP transact with Bermuda resident counterparties beyond the permitted scope, BMA exchange-control permission and a resident-resident conversion exposure may arise.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Bermuda's Exchange Control Act 1972, a Bermuda-resident person must collect export proceeds in a permitted currency and offer external currency to an authorised dealer within the applicable statutory period, generally 180 days, subject to extension by BMA. Bermuda-resident exporters are normally required to convert the percentage stipulated by exchange-control authorisation, often 100% for USD receipts. For this ELP, the non-resident designation disapplies the mandatory surrender and conversion rules for marine survey fee receipts from foreign underwriters and carriers; foreign-currency earnings may be retained in external designated accounts and used for working capital, survey costs, and partner distributions without BMA conversion consent.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - the central monetary authority, currency-issue authority, and integrated prudential/AML regulator of Bermuda under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for direct offshore EMI account in the ELP name because Bermuda is not a common supported jurisdiction for UK FCA EMIs and Bermuda law does not recognise an EMI licence; Medium for using a regulated UK EMI, including Wise, as a collection/disbursement rail only when the Bermuda-licensed bank executes MT103 instructions to the EMI's settlement account. Legal constraints: any entity holding customer deposits or issuing e-money in Bermuda requires a deposit-taking/electronic-money concession under the Banks and Trust Companies Act 1991 or BMA exemption. Wise may be used as a named payment rail in treasury operations, but not as the ELP's primary account-holder. Onboarding defence: provide underlying bank confirmation, professional indemnity insurance, and a lawful basis for using Wise cross-border.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Bermuda exempted limited partnerships; a 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) applies only to multinational enterprise groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda does not impose any withholding tax on distributions made by an Exempted Limited Partnership, including profit distributions, deemed dividends, liquidating distributions, or returns of capital, to resident or non-resident partners.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory threshold for Local File or Master File because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax. For in-scope multinational groups under the QDMTT, documentation and the GloBE Information Return requirement are triggered when consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750 million (approximately US$810 million). A sensible threshold for maintaining a defense file is any intercompany transaction exceeding US$1 million per annum, aligned with OECD transfer pricing guidelines for the parent jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations apply to entities carrying on relevant activities, including insurance, banking, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centers, intellectual property holding, and pure holding. A marine insurance surveyor conducting vessel hull and cargo damage claims inspection will not ordinarily fall within the 'insurance' relevant activity unless it is a licensed insurer or reinsurer or performs core insurance risk management and claims settlement functions on behalf of an insurer. Nevertheless, the ELP should file a nil ESR and maintain documentary evidence of substance if it receives underwriting fees, claims adjustment commissions, or reinsurance-linked income from related parties, because foreign revenue authorities may recharacterize such fees as insurance or risk-bearing income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Offshore Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Flow-Through Reporting

Statutory sanction: Bermuda imposes no penalty due to the absence of corporate income tax, but foreign jurisdictions impose severe sanctions: civil fraud penalties up to 75% of the understated tax (US), UK HMRC penalties up to 200% of the tax under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007, criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to five years, and automatic exchange of information under Bermuda's tax information exchange agreement network.

Director personal liability: Courts will pierce the ELP's limited liability veil if the entity is a sham or mere conduit. General partners and any controlling director who participated in the concealment are jointly and severally liable for the underpaid tax, interest, and penalties; worldwide freezing orders, travel bans, and asset confiscation orders may be issued.
Economic Substance and Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2018 and ESR regulations, the sanctions include automatic late filing fines of BD$3,000 plus BD$100 per day, escalating administrative penalties up to BD$250,000, Registrar strike-off, and compulsory reporting to the tax authorities of the ultimate parent jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any shadow director who signs false substance declarations face criminal prosecution, disqualification from acting as an officer in Bermuda, personal liability for unpaid top-up tax if the ELP is a constituent entity of a large multinational group, and restitution to affected insurers or policyholders.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2020 and applicable sanctions legislation, the offense is punishable by imprisonment of up to five years and an unlimited fine; the Financial Intelligence Agency may also impose civil penalties for deficient AML systems, seize proceeds, and revoke any required professional license.

Director personal liability: General partners, senior managers, and compliance officers who knowingly or recklessly process illicit funds are personally liable for money laundering offenses, may be named in financial sanctions listings, face asset freezing orders and travel bans, and are subject to extradition to the United States or United Kingdom for prosecution.
Unlicensed Insurance and Insurance Intermediary Activity

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed insurance activity renders affected contracts voidable, subjects the ELP to an Enforcement Order, monetary penalties of up to BD$50,000, restitution to the affected insured, and prohibition from continuing business in Bermuda; the Supreme Court may wind up the partnership and appoint a liquidator.

Director personal liability: The general partners and any officers exercising control are jointly and severally liable for the liabilities of the unauthorized insurance business; directors and general partners may be personally fined, disqualified, and ordered to compensate claimants where the partnership lacks assets to satisfy judgments.
