Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (portal: www.gov.bm), together with the Registrar of Companies within the Companies and Corporate Service Providers division for entity registration and the Bermuda Customs Department for import/export duty administration. UK, where cross-border VAT and customs obligations are triggered: HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC), portal www.gov.uk, for UK VAT registration, Customs Declaration Service (CDS) and Making Tax Digital filing.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda levies no income tax and issues no general TIN or VAT ID; a foreign entity obtains its Bermuda Business Registration Number (BRN/company number) automatically upon registration of the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Registrar of Companies, together with a Certificate of Exemption; the BRN serves as the Bermuda TIN for CRS/FATCA and for the annual economic substance declaration. If the ELP makes taxable supplies into Great Britain, it must separately register for UK VAT by submitting Form VAT1 (or applying online through Government Gateway) and obtain a 9-digit UK VAT number after HMRC verification; HMRC may require a UK tax representative under VATA 1994 s.57, and a GB EORI number must be obtained for CDS export/import declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda: the ELP is fiscally transparent by default and is not subject to Bermuda income tax; the partnership agreement may elect general partner indemnity and unlimited liability, and may submit the annual economic substance declaration. US inbound investors may file IRS Form 8832 (Check-the-Box) because a Bermuda limited partnership is a non-per-se eligible entity, electing corporation, partnership, or disregarded entity status. UK inbound investors cannot check the box; the mixed membership partnership election (ITA 2007 s.850C, inserted by FA 2014) permits reallocation of partnership profits among corporate and non-corporate partners, and hybrid mismatch defensive notices may be filed under TIOPA 2010 s.259BD and CTA 2010 Part 17 (as amended by FA 2020) to neutralise differential UK/Bermuda treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; import and excise-style customs duties are the only indirect taxes. UK: VAT under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (standard rate 20%) applies only where the Bermuda ELP imports goods into or makes taxable supplies within Great Britain.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: not applicable (no VAT); a Business Licence under the Business (Business Licence) Act 2014 is required from commencement of trading, with annual fees graduated by gross receipts. UK: the registration threshold is GBP 90,000 of UK taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period (VATA 1994 s.25 and Sch 3A); there is no threshold where an overseas business imports goods into Great Britain and supplies them onwards, including e-commerce consignments not exceeding GBP 135 which are placed within the deemed supplier/online marketplace rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: a UK VAT-registered supplier may zero-rate electric cargo bikes under VATA 1994 s.30(6) and the VAT (Zero-Rating) Order 1989 art. 37 only if the goods are physically exported from the UK to a third-country destination within statutory time limits and the supplier holds the full evidence set specified in HMRC VAT Notice 703, including proof of removal and receipt by the overseas customer. A non-UK-registered Bermuda ELP cannot claim zero-rating; supplies to UK customers are UK supplies subject to 20% VAT, with import VAT recoverable only if the ELP is UK-registered and the goods are imported for onward taxable supply. Services: B2B offshore services supplied to a non-UK recipient are outside the scope of UK VAT (place of supply at customer location; VATA 1994 s.7A), provided the supplier retains evidence of the customer's non-UK business status (certificate of taxable status or foreign VAT/GST registration number).

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK Customs Declaration Service CDS SAD or full export declaration, including the exit summary declaration and the EORI number of the exporter)
  • Bill of Lading (original signed on-board negotiable ocean bill or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the country of departure to the third-country destination)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank-issued foreign exchange repatriation certificate, or SWIFT MT103 field-70 payment instruction, evidencing inward remittance of the full export invoice value)
  • Commercial export invoice (incoterms, HS 2022 tariff codes, ECCN/dual-use classification, quantity, unit price and currency)
  • Packing list (colli count, gross and net weights, and IMDG Class 9 UN3480/UN3481 declaration where lithium-ion batteries are packed with or within the cargo e-bikes)
  • Export Cargo Shipping Instructions (ECSI) filed with the ocean or air carrier
  • Proof of export report from the UK CDS (Goods Departed status) where the goods transit through the UK supply chain

Bermuda: no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API clearance mandate; commercial invoices and EDI suffice, and customs declarations are filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Department portal. UK: Making Tax Digital for VAT requires VAT-registered businesses to maintain digital records and submit VAT returns via HMRC-recognised functional compatible software using API endpoints; no invoice-by-invoice clearance applies today, but HM Treasury/HMRC have published an e-invoicing roadmap proposing mandatory B2B e-invoicing (PEPPOL-based) on a phased basis from 2028-2030 after the Digitalising and Modernising VAT consultation, with PEPPOL already mandatory for UK public procurement invoices.

UK: HMRC may assess recovered VAT and claw back zero-rating relief within 4 years of the end of the prescribed accounting period, extended to 20 years for deliberate non-disclosure (VATA 1994 ss.73-77); civil penalties for inaccuracy or concealment reach 100% of potential lost revenue (Finance Act 2007 Sch 24); dishonest evasion is a criminal offence with an unlimited fine and up to 7 years imprisonment (VATA 1994 s.72); statutory default interest runs from the date the return was due; fraudulent export claims also trigger denial of input tax credit and recovery of any VAT repayment. Bermuda: false export declarations and forged documents attract seizure and forfeiture of goods and fines of up to BMD 10,000 under the Customs Act 1948, and the Tax Commissioner may claw back business licence or customs duty relief obtained by misrepresentation for up to 6 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fully convertible currency regime tied to the US dollar at a fixed 1:1 peg via the Bermuda Monetary Authority's currency board arrangements. There are no restrictions on current account transactions or capital account movements for non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnerships. Profit remittances, dividend distributions, and capital repatriation may be made freely in US dollars or any other major currency. There is no exchange control approval requirement for bona fide trade settlements. However, banking relationships require compliance with the BMA's AML/ATF regime, the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008, and the Economic Substance Act 2018 (for relevant activities; the manufacture of electric cargo bikes is not a relevant activity). The practical hurdle is correspondent banking due diligence, not statutory FX controls. Inbound capital from the UK parent is unrestricted, and no hard currency surrender or conversion obligation applies. All exports may be invoiced in US dollars, GBP, or Euros at the entity's commercial discretion, with no requirement to convert export proceeds into BMD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender requirement for export receipts. An Exempted Limited Partnership engaged in the manufacture and export of electric cargo bikes may retain 100% of export proceeds in any currency. There is no BMA directive requiring repatriation within a specified period. For operational treasury efficiency, the entity should maintain USD receivables in a US-dollar-denominated operational account in Bermuda or a UK fintech EMI, and convert to GBP only for UK tax payments or partner distributions. The absence of surrender rules should be documented in internal treasury policies for bank onboarding purposes. Documentary evidence of export proceeds (e.g., bills of lading, customs declarations, SWIFT MT103 messages) should be retained to evidence commercial substance and satisfy AML/CFT source-of-funds verification by the Bermuda bank or UK clearing bank.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: High for UK-authorised fintech EMIs and e-money institutions authorised by the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 (e.g., Wise Platform, Revolut Business, or Airwallex). Legal constraints: A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership may open an FCA-regulated EMI account remotely, provided the entity can evidence beneficial ownership, a valid Bermuda Business Permission, and registered office confirmation. The EMI must perform customer due diligence equivalent to UK AML standards, which is generally acceptable. However, a pure Bermudian on-island bank account should still be opened as a primary treasury conduit because certain US correspondent banks and trade-finance LC-issuing banks may require an account relationship with a physical licensed bank, not an opaque pass-through EMI. EMIs may not offer letter-of-credit issuing capacity or trade finance facilities; their permitted use is limited to high-volume, low-value operational payments, supplier disbursements under USD 50,000, and rapid conversion to GBP/EUR. Avoid reliance on a single EMI for large export settlement receipts. Compliance defense: retain board resolutions, the partnership agreement, and proof of underlying trade invoices to counter any bank de-risking trigger.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The primary inbound route is SWIFT MT103 from the UK parent company's HSBC, Barclays, or Lloyds corporate account to the Bermuda ELP's USD account at HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield. The wire must clearly reference: (1) invoice or credit note number; (2) purpose code 'EQUI' (equity contribution) or 'LOAN' (general partner loan); (3) BBK/IBAN for the beneficiary; (4) the unique entity identifier KY_ELP. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 850,000,000 consolidated global revenue threshold for Bermuda CbCR notification and CIT registration. There is no separate lower BMD threshold for Local File documentation outside CIT scope. An ELP inside Bermuda CIT scope must maintain contemporaneous Local File and Master File whenever cross-border related-party transactions exceed BMD 850,000,000 group revenue and include Bermuda-attributable profit.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 and associated regulations apply to relevant entities carrying on relevant activities. Manufacturing of electric cargo bicycles and scooters is not categorically a listed relevant activity if the underlying production has genuine physical substance, but an ELP that is a pure equity holding entity of a manufacturing group must meet reduced substance requirements. An ELP holding IP, financing intra-group operations, or operating as a distribution/HQ service centre will be treated as a relevant entity and must demonstrate direction and management in Bermuda, proportionate employees, physical assets, and core income-generating activities in Bermuda. Tax-transparent status does not exempt the ELP from economic substance filing. Non-compliance can result in penalties, mandatory strike-off, and cancellation of any tax protection certificate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the unpaid CIT plus statutory interest; civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated; criminal prosecution on indictment carrying a fine up to BMD 100,000 and/or imprisonment for up to five years.

Director personal liability: The general partner of the ELP is jointly and severally liable for partnership tax obligations if the partnership is insolvent. UK HMRC may pursue the controlling UK partner under transfer-of-assets and CFC rules; travel bans and asset freezing orders may be obtained while criminal proceedings are active; imprisonment is available on conviction.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 up to BMD 25,000 per offence plus continuing daily default fines; the Registrar may strike off the ELP and revoke its exemption certificate; a UK court may recharacterise the ELP as UK tax resident or a sham partnership.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any shadow director may be disqualified, ordered to make personal restitution to creditors, and held liable for unpaid taxes and fees. A court can impose a travel ban during investigation and issue a warrant for arrest if the partner fails to comply with disclosure obligations.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: Proceedings under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and AML Regulations 2008: money laundering on conviction is punishable by a substantial fine and/or imprisonment for up to 14 years; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may impose financial penalties up to BMD 500,000; forfeiture and confiscation orders are available.

Director personal liability: The beneficial owner acting as de facto director is personally criminally liable. Courts may issue bench warrants, travel bans, and property freezing orders against individuals in Bermuda and the United Kingdom. Civil liability for creditor losses may also be enforced against the partnership's general partner.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Repatriation Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Tax authority adjustment to the arm's length result, denial of deductions, imposition of interest and penalties up to 100% of the understated profit, and CbCR non-compliance penalties of BMD 10,000 per month. If false evidence is submitted, criminal sanctions apply.

Director personal liability: Controlling partners must certify the TP documentation; knowing submission of false documentation gives rise to perjury and fraud liability, disqualification, and imprisonment. UK HMRC may pierce through the ELP as a sham and hold the UK parent jointly liable for diverted profits tax and penalties.
