Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Bermuda (Government of Bermuda central portal: www.gov.bm; Tax Registration e-services under the Government of Bermuda online services); Registrar of Companies, Bermuda (www.roc.gov.bm) for statutory ELP registration; Bermuda Customs Department for import/export declarations and duty administration

E-government filing portal

Bermuda levies no general income or corporation tax, so a foreign-controlled ELP is not automatically registered with the tax authority. A Bermuda Tax Identification Number (TIN) is procured only upon creation of a Bermuda economic nexus: (i) employer registration under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 via Form PE-1 filed with the Office of the Tax Commissioner, which issues a TIN to the employing ELP; (ii) customs registration as importer/exporter at the Bermuda Customs Department, which issues a Customs Code for electronic declarations and duty accounting; (iii) CRS/FATCA financial institution registration with the Bermuda Competent Authority, which issues a reporting TIN where the ELP conducts relevant financial business. A passive non-resident ELP that has no Bermuda employees, no Bermuda-source activity, and no physical importation into Bermuda may lawfully operate without obtaining any Bermuda TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: the Exempted Limited Partnership is tax-transparent for Bermuda purposes, and no Bermuda entity-level tax is imposed on its profits. Bermuda's direct tax is strictly territorial and limited to employer payroll tax on Bermuda-resident employees, land tax on Bermuda-situs real estate, and stamp duty on Bermuda-situs instruments. Non-resident partners' trading profit from the offshore export and distribution of goods is outside Bermuda's tax perimeter.

Available election forms

An ELP may apply to the Bermuda Minister of Finance for a Tax Assurance Certificate under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2001, which guarantees exemption from future Bermuda direct taxes, typically for a period of up to 50 years. Bermuda law itself permits no other direct-tax classification election because no income tax is in force. For foreign tax planning, the ELP may file a US Form 8832 check-the-box election to alter its US federal entity classification, and the UK hybrid mismatch rules under TIOPA 2010 / Finance Act 2016 will characterise the ELP's transparency status accordingly.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar and is fully and freely convertible across both current and capital accounts. Bermuda operates a liberalised exchange-control regime under the Exchange Control Act 1972, administered by the BMA, which grants generalised exchange-control permission to exempted undertakings such as the KY_ELP Exempted Limited Partnership. There is no profit-remittance restriction, withholding tax, or distribution-blocking mechanism applicable to non-resident partners, provided the ELP maintains real economic substance under the Bermuda Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) Act 2021 for its wholesale distribution activity. CNY-sourced baijiu export proceeds may be freely liquidated into USD, GBP, or EUR, and the venture may simultaneously hold multi-currency balances (USD, BMD, GBP, EUR, CNH) across Bermudian and UK-licensed institutions without BMA conversion approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, no repatriation quota, and no centralised collection account for trade receipts. The ELP may lawfully retain 100% of baijiu export proceeds in original currency denominations within its Bermuda operational accounts; only the standard BMA anti-money-laundering large-transaction reporting thresholds and OFAC/HMT/UN sanctions-screening obligations apply.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — primary currency and exchange-control regulator for Bermuda; supervised at the sovereign level by HM Treasury and coordinated with the Bank of England for GBP/CHAPS clearing through the UK digital-trade gateway, together with the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) for UK sanctions alignment.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH as a complementary GBP/EUR disbursement gateway, but LOW as a primary treasury host. A Bermuda ELP may open transactional e-money and payment-float accounts with UK FCA-regulated EMI/Authorised Payment Institutions to gain inexpensive pan-European clearing and mid-market FX; however, the Bermuda banking regulator and correspondent banks will materially de-risk any structure where the majority of cross-border liquidity arcs through an unlicensed non-bank. Legal constraints: (i) an EMI is not a bank under Bermuda's Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and cannot issue letters of credit, provide trade loans, or accept deposits; (ii) the EMI must independently complete full KYC over the ELP's UBOs under UK money-laundering regulations; (iii) assets held at an EMI are not protected by deposit-guarantee schemes. Recommended deployment: the EMI float is restricted to European buyer receipts and is automatically swept end-of-day (zero-balance) to the Butterfield operational account to preserve bank-led clearing control.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All direct inbound buyer remittances are cleared via SWIFT MT103 wires into the ELP's Butterfield operational account. USD-denominated receipts clear over CHIPS/Fedwire through the bank's New York respondent; GBP receipts clear via CHAPS through the UK gateway; EUR receipts clear over TARGET2 through a UK/EU correspondent. On the PRC side, funds are remitted under SAFE-recognised purpose codes 121010 (export trade in goods) and 121020 (trade-related collections); the MT103 must carry full ISO 20022 CBPR+ remittance data and a clear field-70 descriptor: 'payments for exported distilled sorghum spirits (baijiu)'. Verification protocol requires SWIFT gpi end-to-end tracking, OFAC/HMT/UN sanctions pre-screening of both the remitting and receiving banks, single-wire-value reconciliation against the commercial invoice and house bill of lading, purchase-order matching in the treasury ledger, and BMA large-value transaction notification where applicable. All UK-gateway GBP receipts passing through the FCA EMI are automatically swept daily to the Bermuda operational account to maintain a unified settlement position.Settlement to PRC baijiu suppliers uses two principal clearing rails: (i) direct wire — SWIFT MT103 or MT202 COV from the Bermudian clearing bank to the supplier's CNY/CNH account, routed through CIPS/CNAPS where a PRC correspondent exists or through offshore CNH settlement books in Hong Kong; and (ii) documentary — irrevocable commercial letters of credit (MT700) issued through the Butterfield/HSBC trade desks and confirmed by banks in Hong Kong or Shanghai, covering the supplier's export drafts under UCP600 with required documents comprising a commercial invoice, full-set clean on-board bill of lading, packing list, alcoholic-beverage compliance certificates, and certificate of origin. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 810,000,000 (approximately €750,000,000 group consolidated revenue threshold under OECD Pillar Two) for mandatory Bermuda and GloBE documentation; no separate domestic transfer-pricing documentation threshold exists for wholly local ELPs below that figure.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as revised) apply where the ELP carries on a relevant activity. Global export distribution of Chinese Baijiu qualifies as a ‘distribution and service centre’. The ELP must demonstrate: (1) the activity is directed and managed in Bermuda; (2) core income-generating activities are performed physically in Bermuda; (3) adequate premises, employees and annual operating expenditure are maintained in Bermuda relative to the profitability of the distribution business; and (4) an annual economic substance declaration is filed with the Registrar. A pure invoicing hub or shell arrangement will fail the substance test and trigger penalties and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and general revenue law: penalty up to 100% of the tax evaded; maximum financial penalty BMD 100,000; criminal prosecution for tax evasion; and recapture of any foreign tax benefits obtained through false certificates.

Director personal liability: The general partner and directors of a corporate general partner risk personal liability, civil restitution, travel bans, and imprisonment for up to 5 years. The corporate veil is pierced where the ELP is a mere instrumentality of tax evasion.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Economic substance penalty BMD 10,000 for the first non-compliance; BMD 250,000 for persistent failure; administrative strike-off of the ELP; foreign tax authorities may recharacterise the entity as a conduit or sham under OECD action plans.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors may be personally liable for unpaid penalties and foreign taxes; director disqualification for up to 10 years; restriction or revocation of Bermuda banking and EMI accounts; personal asset freezing orders.
Anti-Money Laundering Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to BMD 100,000 per contravention; criminal liability for money laundering with penalties up to 14 years’ imprisonment and unlimited fines; revocation of the ELP’s exempted status.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner are personally prosecutable where they knowingly, or with reckless disregard, permit the ELP to process illicit funds. Sanctions include forfeiture of proceeds, travel bans, and extradition for tax or money laundering offences.
Cross-Border Permanent Establishment Exposure

Statutory sanction: Foreign tax authority assessment of corporate income tax, interest, and surcharges on profits attributable to the permanent establishment; penalties for late or non-disclosure under the relevant exchange of information framework; additional Bermuda penalties for inconsistent declarations.

Director personal liability: The general partner is liable for foreign taxes and penalties; directors of a corporate general partner may be subject to UK/EU director liability orders, travel bans, and disqualification for repeated non-compliance. Civil restitution and overseas asset enforcement will follow a determination of deliberate PE concealment.
