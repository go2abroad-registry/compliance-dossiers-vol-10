Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda (British Overseas Territory outside the UK VAT area): (1) Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance - entity registration, partnership certificates; (2) Office of the Tax Commissioner - sole competent tax authority for the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 / Tax Administration Act 2024 (CIT, Pillar Two/GLoBE), filing via the Government of Bermuda online tax portal; (3) Bermuda Customs Department - customs duty, consignee codes, Asycuda World electronic declarations; (4) Payroll Tax Office - employer registration for Bermuda-based staff. UK HMRC is not the competent tax authority for the Bermuda ELP; UK VAT/HMRC rules apply only to UK customer-facing supplies.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: appoint a Bermuda-registered agent and file with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2017 to register the ELP; obtain the Certificate of Registration and unique partnership registration number, which serves as the de facto tax reference. Step 2: if the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of EUR 750m or more in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years, register each Bermuda constituent entity with the Tax Commissioner for CIT within the statutory registration window and obtain a CIT profile/taxpayer number through the online tax portal. Step 3: if the ELP employs individuals working in Bermuda, register as an employer with the Payroll Tax Office (no registration needed for remote or non-Bermudian staff). Step 4: no VAT ID exists or is required (Bermuda has no VAT/GST); the same registration number supports any Bermuda Customs consignee code application if physical imports occur.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is fiscally transparent under Bermuda law; Bermuda imposes no general corporate income tax, and under the CIT Act 2023 the ELP is a flow-through entity for GLoBE/Pillar Two purposes (CIT attributed to partners, subject to Bermuda permanent-establishment rules). The ELP is not a Tax_Exempt_Zone entity; ordinary territorial scope applies for CIT only if the parent MNE group exceeds the EUR 750m threshold. The Economic Substance Act 2018 relevant-activity filings are generally not triggered by web-scraping/API data services unless the activity is re-characterized as IP or financing.

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic 'check-the-box' election - partnership transparency is mandatory. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the ELP may file Form 8832 to elect entity classification (default: partnership). Under the CIT Act 2023, in-scope groups may elect GLoBE transitional safe harbours, the substance-based income exclusion (SBIE), the Qualified Refundable Tax Credit (QRTC) regime, and any enacted transitional CIT deferral options for the 2025-2026 transition years. No hybrid-mismatch election exists under Bermuda domestic law; hybrid arrangements are tested under investor-jurisdiction rules (e.g., UK/US anti-hybrid legislation).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Bermuda imposes no VAT, no GST and no sales/use tax; the only indirect taxes are import customs duties (Customs Tariff Act 1966, as amended), stamp duty and land tax, none of which attach to exported digital services. UK VAT (headline 20%) does not extend to Bermuda and is relevant only to UK customer-facing supplies made by the ELP (B2B reverse charge for UK VAT-registered customers; mandatory UK VAT registration for B2C digital supplies to UK consumers from the first supply, as the GBP 85,000 threshold does not apply to non-UK established suppliers).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda has no VAT/GST registration threshold. For tangible imports, a business must obtain a Bermuda Customs consignee code; duty is assessed per tariff line on the c.i.f. value (rates generally 0% to 25%+ by classification). Digital services, API subscriptions and data-pipeline exports are outside the customs duty net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - there is no VAT/GST in Bermuda, so no zero-rating claim exists. Export of web-scraping/API gateway services from the Bermuda ELP is outside the Bermuda tax net and requires no export licence or fiscal approval. UK customer interface: (i) B2B supplies to UK VAT-registered persons are outside UK VAT scope (reverse charge on the UK customer); (ii) B2C supplies of digital services to UK consumers trigger mandatory UK VAT registration by the ELP (no threshold applies to non-UK suppliers) with UK VAT at 20% unless the service is outside scope. Bermuda imposes no foreign-exchange repatriation deadline (exchange controls abolished effective 2022/2023); the 180-day parameter retained in this dossier is a pipeline harmonization convention, not a Bermuda statutory requirement.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a liberalised exchange-control regime. The Bermuda dollar is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar. For an Exempted Limited Partnership designated as 'non-resident' for exchange-control purposes, capital and current account convertibility is effectively full: capital contributions, operational receivables, and investment returns may be denominated and settled in USD/GBP/EUR without BMA licensing. Profit remittances and partnership distributions enjoy unrestricted outward transfer once the partnership maintains separate non-resident foreign-currency accounts and complies with the BMA's periodic reporting and anti-money-laundering record-keeping obligations. Residual hurdles are confined to Bermuda-dollar domestic accounts used by local resident entities, where approval may be required for payments to non-residents or conversions exceeding prescribed thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There exists no mandatory export-proceeds surrender rule in Bermuda. Export receipts invoiced in foreign currency may be retained in approved foreign-currency accounts without conversion to Bermuda dollars. The BMA does not prescribe a statutory timeline, conversion ratio, or surrender quota for non-resident exempted partnerships. Where the partnership transacts in Bermuda dollars with local residents, the BMA may require documentation and consent, but no forced conversion or surrender is applicable. Exempted partnerships should nonetheless preserve invoices, payment confirmations, and route ledgers to evidence the non-resident character of the underlying transactions.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) is the sole public authority entrusted with currency management, foreign exchange control, and banking supervision under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969. The Bank of England does not exercise a supervisory or exchange-control mandate over Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI viability is rated High for operational treasury and transactional settlement, but Low for escrowed capital holding in a Bermuda regulated context. Because Bermuda does not issue e-money licences under domestic law, UK FCA-authorised EMIs and EU PSD2-authorised EMIs may provide cross-border multi-currency IBAN services to the exempted partnership under the Business Conduct (Banks and Deposit Companies) Exemption Order or the business activity/solicitation carve-outs. Such EMIs must remain outside the scope of 'deposit-taking' and cannot hold Bermuda-dollar balances for Bermuda residents. The BMA requires that no unlicensed provider holds funds on behalf of a Bermuda-regulated entity; accordingly, the ELP should utilise EMI only as a float/fee collector with onward sweep to a tier-1 bank. If USDC payment rails are used, the EMI/VASP must be either Bermuda licensed under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 or a recognised/registered crypto service provider in an equivalent FATF jurisdiction, with segregated stablecoin reserves and independent audit attestation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions should be transmitted via SWIFT MT103 using purpose code A001 (Direct Investment) or G20 (Corporate Investment), quoting the ELP reference ELF_CUSTOM_KY_ELP. The payment should be sent from the ultimate parent's UK corporate bank account to the Bermuda bank account, with the remittance instruction referencing the executed Limited Partnership Agreement, the capital account ledger entry, and contribution schedule.
