Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance of Bermuda — Registrar of Companies (Corporate Registry, www.bermudacompanies.gov.bm); Revenue Division (payroll tax, customs, and tax assurance, www.gov.bm/revenue)

E-government filing portal

A foreign sponsor forms a Bermuda Exempted Company through a licensed local registered agent: file incorporation documents (memorandum and articles, director consents, registered office consent) under the Companies Act 1981; obtain a Company Registration Number. Bermuda does not issue a TIN or VAT ID because there is no income tax and no VAT/GST. If the SPV employs staff, a Payroll Tax account must be opened with the Revenue Division. An optional statutory tax protection certificate (Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966) may be obtained from the Minister of Finance. The SPV must file an annual Economic Substance Declaration (ESR) and a Beneficial Ownership return (Beneficial Ownership Act 2015).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Bermuda Exempted Companies enjoy a territorial tax-exempt regime: no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, no dividend remittance tax, and no estate/inheritance tax. A private equity buyout SPV is treated as an exempted resident company for Bermuda legal purposes but outside the economic substance taxation net unless engaged in a 'relevant activity' (holding entity status applies, requiring reduced substance compliance).

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or domestic hybrid mismatch election. Available elections: (i) application for a temporary tax assurance certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (ii) designation as a non-resident company for exchange-control purposes. US owners may file Form 8832 under IRC § 301.7701-3 to classify the Exempted Co as a corporation, partnership, or disregarded entity; UK and EU investors must evaluate hybrid mismatch rules (UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) on cross-border payments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Bermuda does not levy VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; customs duties are levied on certain imported goods only

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST registration regime exists; importers must clear goods through Bermuda Customs by filing a Single Administrative Document, with duty rates per the Customs Tariff Act 1968; no economic turnover threshold for indirect tax registration is applicable

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

N/A — Bermuda has no VAT/GST, therefore no zero-rating or export refund regime exists. Goods exported from Bermuda are not subject to export duty; services supplied offshore to non-residents fall outside Bermuda's tax net; a PE buyout SPV is not expected to export goods or charge VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD Form C50, where any goods movement occurs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 payment evidence
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (if required by the destination jurisdiction)

No electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs uses the Single Administrative Document (SAD) for import/export declarations and an electronic processing portal for customs brokers; real-time fiscal API clearance is not applicable.

No VAT zero-rating or clawback regime exists in Bermuda; retrospective VAT recovery is legally impossible. Customs fraud and misdeclaration attract penalties under the Customs Tariff Act, including seizure and forfeiture of goods, fines, and imprisonment. False statements in tax assurance applications or Economic Substance filings expose the SPV and its officers to civil penalties, potential criminal liability, revocation of the exemption certificate, and mandatory exchange of information with the UK and EU. If the SPV is managed or controlled from the UK, HM Revenue & Customs may assert a UK permanent establishment under CTA 2010 s. 18A, exposing profits to UK corporation tax and diverted profits tax.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 and is fully convertible for international transactions. The Bermuda Exchange Control regime distinguishes between resident and non-resident status. A Bermuda Exempted Company used as a private equity buyout SPV may, by application and BMA approval, be classified as non-resident for exchange control purposes, enabling it to maintain offshore foreign-currency accounts, receive subscriptions in USD from overseas investors, and remit dividends, interest, and redemption proceeds without Bermuda central-bank conversion requirements. Capital account convertibility is effectively unrestricted for non-resident entities so long as transactions are in foreign currency and do not involve Bermudian-dollar domestic settlements. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal; no withholding tax or exchange-control levy is imposed on distributions by a Bermuda Exempted Company. Practically, the main compliance gate is the BMA-approved banking relationship and the SPV's demonstrated exemption from Bermuda economic substance requirements where the relevant PE activities are managed and controlled outside Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory surrender timelines or mandatory conversion ratios for export receipts in Bermuda for a non-resident classified exempted company. The BMA does not require an exempted private equity SPV to sell export or investment proceeds to an authorized dealer. Where an entity is treated as resident for exchange control, the BMA may require foreign-currency receipts to be either retained in an authorized currency account or converted at commercial rates, but this does not apply to the non-resident SPV structure. The account documentation should state non-resident exchange-control status to avoid any auto-surrender expectation. No UK HMRC export proceeds repo obligation applies because the entity is a Bermuda domestic company operating outside UK exchange-control rules.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the exchange control authority for Bermuda under the Foreign Exchange Control Act 1972. Bermuda is a UK Overseas Territory; however, the BMA exercises independent statutory regulation over banking, money transmission, and exchange control, and the Bank of England does not directly supervise Bermuda banking or FX operations except through residual Crown dependencies oversight frameworks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury rail for the Bermuda Exempted Company; medium only as a supplementary collection or disbursement mechanism through a UK FCA-authorized Electronic Money Institution (EMI) or an EU-authorised EMI passporting into the UK. Bermuda does not issue EMI licenses, so a Bermudian SPV cannot use a purely Bermuda-incorporated fintech payment firm unless that firm is a licensed bank. Many FCA-regulated EMIs are cautious about booking an offshore exempted company with no substantive UK branch and may decline the relationship unless the SPV has a clear British economic nexus, UK director, or UK domiciled fund administrator. Legal constraints include UK AML/KYC expectations, travel rule requirements, and the EMI's need to perform enhanced due diligence on the Bermudian ownership chain and the ultimate private equity principals. The recommended solution is to open the primary operating account at a tier-1 Bermuda commercial bank and use an FCA-authorised EMI only for small disbursements and corporate card payments to UK/EU service providers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from the UK parent fund or the PE syndicate should be wire-transferred in US dollars using SWIFT MT103 to the Bermuda SPV's operational USD account. The wire instruction should include the SPV name, account number, the fund deal identifier, and an investment purpose code such as 'direct investment' rather than a generic payment. The receiving Bermuda bank will execute standard BMA reporting as applicable to a non-resident exempted company. If the subscription is in EUR or GBP, the remitter should consider converting to USD before transmission to avoid Bermuda-dollar settlement in the domestic system. Correspondent routing is commonly through HSBC London or Bank of New York Mellon for Butterfield; for HSBC Bank Bermuda, the New York or London arm can be used. The bank needs the executed escrow agreement and commitment letter to distinguish equity injection from loan proceeds; debt funding should be accompanied by the facility agreement and, if relevant, a cross-border interest withholding analysis under the US/UK treaty jurisprudence. For syndicated loan wires, use the credit facility agent's payment instructions and reference the loan facility number, so the bank does not classify the funds as a distribution or third-party deposit. In all cases, depositors must complete a funds-origin questionnaire and the SPV must maintain a transaction file with the supporting SPA, capital call notice, and ultimate beneficial ownership details.Outbound acquisition proceeds should be cleared by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the Bermuda bank to the seller's or target company's bank in the United Kingdom, United States, or the relevant closing jurisdiction. Because the Bermuda SPV is a buyout vehicle rather than an operating exporter, commercial letters of credit are not standard for equity or debt acquisition settlements; however, a standby letter of credit may be used to secure a deferred purchase price obligation or an indemnity escrow. For operating subsidiary trade payments, the SPV or its operating group can use SWIFT MT103 payments through the same Bermuda treasury account, with separate purpose codes for goods, services, royalties, or intercompany loans. United States dollar clearing should be done via the correspondent's CHIPS or Fedwire network; euro clearing via TARGET2 and GBP via CHAPS or Faster Payments through the relevant London correspondent. All outgoing transactions require matching against the underlying transaction file, a sanctions screening confirmation, and a compliance record of the final recipient. The Bermuda bank will accept MT103 field 70 references identifying the acquisition SPA, escrow release order, or loan drawdown certificate.
