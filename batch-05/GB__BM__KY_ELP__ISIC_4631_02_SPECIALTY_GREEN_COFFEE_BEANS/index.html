Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Bermuda exempted limited partnership; capital commitments and contribution schedules are determined solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement. The initial capital need not be deposited at registration and no escrow or trust account is mandated by Bermuda law; for banking convenience the initial Limited Partner may wire funds to the partnership account via SWIFT upon the first capital call.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the Bermuda Fire and Rescue Service covering the container handling warehouse, including accessible hydrants, extinguisher placement, and staff evacuation plan; structural load inspection and approval from the Department of Planning for racking, mezzanine floors, and stacking heights; workplace clearance under the Occupational Safety and Health regime covering forklift certification, worker signage, dust control for coffee chaff, reefer-container confined space entry protocols, and first aid readiness. For bonded warehouse operation, Bermuda Customs approval of the customs bonded facility is also required.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Bermuda exempted limited partnership; capital commitments and contribution schedules are determined solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement. The initial capital need not be deposited at registration and no escrow or trust account is mandated by Bermuda law; for banking convenience the initial Limited Partner may wire funds to the partnership account via SWIFT upon the first capital call.

Signed by the General Partner and consented to by the initial Limited Partner.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial manufacturing permit is required for this activity under Bermuda law; the competent administrative authorities for the import and container distribution of unroasted green coffee beans are Bermuda Customs Department (Ministry of Finance) for import entry, and the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) for plant health and environmental matters. If warehousing facilities are later established, the Department of Planning and the Department of Health may require site plan and food storage clearance.

Category: Wholesale Import and Distribution Registration Exemption Notice — a Bermuda Trade and Business Licence is not required for the wholesale distribution of unprocessed green coffee beans conducted from a bonded or ordinary warehouse; an import declaration, phytosanitary certificate and customs clearance are required per shipment. Where a physical warehouse is operated, a Certificate of Occupancy and a fire safety certificate are required from the relevant municipal and government authorities.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance (Government of Bermuda); official portal: https://www.gov.bm; electronic customs filing via Bermuda Customs Department on UNCTAD ASYCUDA World; entity registration via the Bermuda Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

Engage a Bermuda licensed corporate services provider (ABRA member) to form the Exempted Limited Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014; file the partnership agreement and register the general partner with the Registrar of Companies; no corporate income tax TIN is issued because Bermuda imposes no CIT; obtain an ASYCUDA World customs registration code for import/export declarations; register as an employer for Bermuda payroll tax if hiring Bermuda-resident staff; where required, obtain a Bermuda Business Development Agency reference for 'Blocked/Permitted' activity clearance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Tax_Exempt_Zone — the ELP is fiscally transparent and exempt from Bermuda income, corporate, capital gains, and withholding taxes; territorial scope covers only Bermuda payroll tax on locally employed staff and customs duty on imported goods; partners are taxed solely in their respective residence jurisdictions.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists; the ELP may elect in its partnership agreement a centralized or decentralized management structure; no Bermuda hybrid mismatch election is available, leaving the partners' home-country check-the-box rules and OECD BEPS Action 2 provisions to apply; a legacy Assured Tax Exemption applies only to specific licensed entities such as insurers, not to an ordinary ELP; Bermuda's Economic Substance regime applies to any relevant activity and requires annual substance filings.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax in Bermuda; indirect taxation is limited to Bermuda customs import duties under the Customs Act 1998 and Customs Tariff Act; Bermuda is outside the UK VAT territory (VATA 1994, s. 96), so no UK VAT arises on Bermuda transactions.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST system exists, hence no economic turnover threshold triggers registration; customs importer registration through ASYCUDA World is required for any regular importing activity without a minimum value; Bermuda payroll tax employer registration applies only when Bermuda-resident employees are engaged.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable at Bermuda level — with no VAT/GST, exports by the ELP are inherently tax-free and no zero-rating claim is required; the ELP is not UK-established, so UK VAT zero-rating under VATA 1994 s. 30 does not attach; goods exported from Bermuda qualify for customs duty relief under the transit/re-export provisions of the Customs Act 1998, subject to the export entry and movement evidence.

  • Bermuda Customs e-Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World e-SAD / C17)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (HS 0901.11 green coffee)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Swift MT103 bank confirmation evidencing foreign currency receipt

All Bermuda import and export declarations must be filed electronically through UNCTAD ASYCUDA World with automated risk assessment and electronic duty payment; no statutory e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance/API mandate applies to commercial invoices in Bermuda; no digital fiscal stamping is required; UK Making Tax Digital obligations do not extend to Bermuda-resident entities.

Fraudulent export declarations attract penalties under the Bermuda Customs Act 1998 including fines up to BD$100,000, forfeiture of goods, and recovery of double the duty evaded; absent a VAT regime there is no VAT clawback; false economic substance filings expose the ELP to fines of US$10,000–US$100,000 and potential striking-off; AML/CFT breaches under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1998 and the AML/ATF Regulated Business Act carry enforcement action and disqualification of the general partner.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. Bermuda repealed its exchange-control legislation in 1973; no statutory exchange-control licensing, capital-account pre-approval, or BMA permit is required for current-account trade flows, intra-group capital contributions, or profit/distribution remittances. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible in BMD, USD, GBP, and EUR. The USD peg eliminates settlement FX risk for USD-invoiced green coffee container sales. Profit remittance from the Bermuda ELP to its UK corporate general partner is unhindered by Bermuda withholding tax, given the absence of Bermuda income tax and the ELP's pass-through classification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender period, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline for export receipts. Re-sale proceeds from specialty green coffee may be retained in USD or any freely convertible currency, held onshore or offshore, and reapplied to working capital without BMA reporting obligations.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium for the Bermuda ELP as direct account holder. The BMA does not issue E-Money Institution (EMI) licences, and domiciled fintech treasury providers capable of onboarding an offshore restricted partnership are negligible. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (e.g., digital-first banking platforms) generally decline to open accounts for Bermuda pass-through vehicles without a UK establishment. Feasibility improves when the union is inverted: the UK corporate general partner holds a high-velocity EMI GBP/USD disbursement account, while the Bermuda ELP's collection, LC, and settlement rails remain with Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda. Any EMI usage must not displace BMA-licensed custody for documentary credits or BMD-denominated settlements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No threshold. Bermuda has no domestic transfer pricing legislation and does not require creation or filing of Local File, Master File, or Country-by-Country Report at the Registrar. The ELP may, however, be required to provide transactional documents to a foreign tax authority under a tax information exchange agreement if the foreign authority is examining the pricing of coffee import or distribution transactions.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018, as amended, apply to relevant entities carrying on a relevant activity. A specialty green coffee bean container distribution business is treated as a distribution and service centre relevant activity when the ELP imports, exports, distributes, warehouses, or provides ancillary logistics services for physical goods. The ELP must demonstrate adequate physical presence, employees, business expenditure, and core income-generating activity in Bermuda. A pure equity holding regime does not apply; an annual ESR declaration is mandatory. Failure to meet economic substance may result in financial penalties and exchange of information to treaty partners.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Foreign Tax Advantage

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under Bermuda criminal law and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2007. Conviction may result in imprisonment for up to 10 years, confiscation of the proceeds of the coffee distribution venture, and significant financial penalties. False declarations to the Registrar also attract perjury-related sanctions.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner who knowingly participate in tax evasion are subject to civil restitution, personal fines, travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and imprisonment. The corporate veil may be pierced where the ELP is used as a mere façade for fraud.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations, the Registrar may impose an initial penalty of up to BMD 250,000, additional daily penalties for continued default, a penalty of up to BMD 500,000 for a second or continued violation, and may order the provision of information. Persistent non-compliance exposes the ELP to strike-off and reputational damage in tax information exchange reports.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who allow the ELP to operate as a shell without substance can be personally fined, disqualified from managing Bermuda entities, and made liable for penalties where they authorised or acquiesced in the false economic substance declaration.
Anti-Money Laundering and Suspicious Activity Reporting Failure

Statutory sanction: The Proceeds of Crime Act 2007 and the AML/ATF Code impose criminal penalties including imprisonment for up to 20 years for money laundering, confiscation of assets, and fines. The Bermuda Monetary Authority may also impose regulatory sanctions, public censure, and restriction on the use of banking and payment rails.

Director personal liability: Criminal liability may attach personally to directors, nominated officers, and partners who authorised or failed to rectify the AML breach. Travel bans, asset freezes, and disqualification orders are available to the court.
Beneficial Ownership Reporting Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies and Partnerships (Beneficial Ownership) Regulations 2017, default attracts a fine of up to BMD 50,000 and imprisonment for up to 2 years. Continued non-compliance may trigger daily default fines and loss of good standing that disables SWIFT wire instructions and letter-of-credit approvals.

Director personal liability: Each partner, director, and registered nominee is separately liable for the offence. Courts may impose civil restitution, travel bans, and personal criminal sanctions where the beneficial ownership information was deliberately falsified.
