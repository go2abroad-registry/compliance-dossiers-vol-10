Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance – Office of the Tax Commissioner (Payroll Tax Act 1995) and Corporate Income Tax Compliance Office (Corporate Income Tax Act 2023); Bermuda Customs Department (Customs Tariff Act 1966 and Customs Act 1948) as the indirect duties and border authority; official portals: www.gov.bm and the Bermuda Customs electronic trade portal

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is first registered with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2009. It must then obtain a Payroll Tax registration (and payer reference) from the Office of the Tax Commissioner for employer withholding reporting, regardless of whether it has employees in Bermuda. If the ELP is a constituent entity of a multinational group with annual revenues of EUR 750 million or more, it must additionally obtain a Corporate Income Taxpayer Identification Number (CTIN) through the CIT registration portal under the Corporate Income Tax Registration Act 2023. For import transactions, the ELP must appoint a licensed Bermuda customs broker and file an Importer Registration with the Bermuda Customs Department; no general national income-tax TIN is issued because Bermuda does not impose a general income tax on ordinary commercial partnerships.

It must then obtain a Payroll Tax registration (and payer reference) from the Office of the Tax Commissioner for employer withholding reporting, regardless of whether it has employees in Bermuda. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda's CIT Act 2023 does not adopt the US 'check-the-box' nomenclature, but the CIT Rules contain elective mechanisms for grouping of constituent entities, for tax-transparent treatment of partnerships, and for tracking of deferred taxes consistent with the OECD Pillar Two GloBE template. A Bermudian ELP may elect transparency so that partners bear taxation in their own jurisdictions or, where in scope of the 15% domestic CIT, may elect inclusion in a designated filing group. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are adjusted under OECD BEPS Action 2 principles as reflected in the CIT legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax. Indirect taxation consists exclusively of customs and excise duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1966 on imported goods; Bermuda is outside the UK VAT territory and the EU customs union, notwithstanding its status as a UK Overseas Territory.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – no VAT registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST is levied. Every person or entity importing goods into Bermuda must be registered with the Bermuda Customs Department irrespective of transaction value. Cold-chain meat and seafood importation additionally requires a perishable-food import licence from the Bermuda Department of Health, sector-specific import controls, and compliance with food safety inspection at the point of entry.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating as a VAT concept is not applicable in Bermuda because no VAT is levied. Exports of frozen beef carcasses and pelagic fish from Bermuda, as well as transit or transshipment movements, attract no Bermuda indirect tax and no export customs duty. To preserve the non-liability position and to claim relief or refund of any duty previously paid on imported goods that are subsequently re-exported, the exporter must demonstrate that the goods physically departed the customs territory and are supported by transport, commercial, and customs documentation.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration / Re-Export Entry (Form C-EXP) lodged via licensed customs broker
  • Customs pro-forma commercial invoice with HS Chapter 02 or Chapter 03 classification, weight, and cold-chain packing terms
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Master Air Waybill evidencing departure from a Bermuda port of loading
  • Bermuda Customs outward manifest and port clearance certificate
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank payment evidence showing full settlement of the trade receivable
  • Department of Health sanitary/export health certificate for meat and fishery products
  • Certificate of origin or certificate of free sale where re-export duty relief is claimed

Bermuda does not operate a real-time e-invoicing mandate, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime. Import and export declarations are lodged via licensed customs brokers through the Bermuda Customs electronic data interchange; physical inspection, port clearance, and documentary verification remain mandatory. There is no API-connected tax authority clearance step for invoice data, but customs entries require electronic pre-lodgement and broker digital signature.

The Bermuda Customs Act 1948 and Customs Tariff Act 1966 impose strict-liability penalties for misdeclared or fictitious exports: forfeiture of goods, fines of up to three times the value of the goods, and imprisonment for fraudulent documentation. False declarations made to obtain duty relief or export exemptions trigger retrospective clawback of all relieved duty plus statutory interest, suspension or cancellation of the customs broker licence, and loss of the importer bond. Under the CIT Act 2023, a constituent ELP that fails to register for a CTIN, files late, or under-reports GloBE income is subject to daily late-filing penalties and collection of the 15% domestic top-up tax, plus interest. Bermuda has no exchange-control restrictions; the 180-day FX repatriation deadline is a contractual, banking, and AML/CTF monitoring standard applied by Bermuda-licensed banks in substantiating inward remittances.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar. For an Exempted Limited Partnership designated as non-resident, both capital account and current account transactions are fully convertible without prior BMA approval. The ELP may hold USD, BMD, GBP and EUR accounts freely; profit distributions to non-resident partners are not subject to withholding and are not blocked by exchange control. The principal remittance hurdle is not regulatory convertibility but bank-level source-of-funds and beneficial-ownership due diligence. The ELP must maintain its non-resident designation in writing and ensure all partner capital injections are traceable to registered partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion ratio applies to a non-resident designated ELP. Resident exporters would normally be subject to a statutory obligation to offer foreign-currency export proceeds to an authorized bank within 30 days of receipt if demanded by the BMA, but this obligation is disapplied for non-resident exempted partnerships and for external accounts held in USD/BMD. Export or re-export proceeds incidental to the cold-chain import business may therefore be retained indefinitely in foreign currency and reused for trade settlements without BMA approval.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and exercising exchange-control functions delegated under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low / conditional. Bermuda does not operate a standalone electronic money institution licensing class; deposit-taking and payment services fall under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and require a BMA banking licence. Offshore digital EMIs are not viable as primary treasury rails because they cannot issue documentary LCs, cannot hold a Bermudian clearing account in the capacity of an authorized bank, and are not supervised by the BMA for exchange-control purposes. A regulated UK or EEA EMI may be used only for ancillary pre-funded expense payments, never for capital escrow, LC issuance, or settlement of commercial import documents.

Third-party gifts, loans, or payments from unrelated creditors must be supported by a written loan or gift agreement before credit is accepted. 0% - Bermuda imposes no domestic withholding tax on dividend distributions or partnership profit distributions. An ELP distributing frozen-meat trading profits to any non-resident limited partner does so free of Bermudian withholding tax. For in-scope CITA entities, tax is collected at entity level on the taxable profit; distribution to partners is not re-taxed at source.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: In-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750 million are subject to the CITA transfer pricing code and OECD TP Guidelines; mandatory Local File and Master File documentation is expected where intercompany transactions with related parties exceed BMD 1 million per transaction category in the fiscal year. Out-of-scope ELPs have no statutory TP documentation requirement, but ESR compliance still necessitates functional analysis and cost records to evidence Bermudian substance for its distribution and service centre activity.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply because wholesale importation and distribution of frozen beef and seafood constitutes a 'Distribution and Service Centre' relevant activity. The ELP must demonstrate, in Bermuda: (i) management and control exercised by the general partner or qualified directors; (ii) an adequate physical office and cold-chain-related administrative presence; (iii) a minimum complement of full-time employees; (iv) material operating expenditure; and (v) CIGA such as logistics coordination, price negotiation, inventory risk assumption and supplier/customer contract execution actually performed in Bermuda. Outsourcing CIGA to the parent's Hong Kong or mainland Chinese trading office does not discharge the obligation and triggers a compliant-and-rebuttable statement. The Registrar will issue a default assessment and exchange the file with foreign tax authorities on failure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent CIT Filing

Statutory sanction: Summary conviction: fine up to BMD 50,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; conviction on indictment: fine up to BMD 200,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; civil restitution equal to the tax evaded plus interest at the central bank rate plus 2%; punitive assessment of up to 100% of the under-declared tax.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any Bermuda-resident tax filing agent who knowingly authorised the false return are jointly and severally liable for the restored tax, penalties and costs. Courts in Bermuda and the Crown dependencies may impose travel bans, asset freezing orders, prohibition from acting as a trustee or manager, and, for serious cases, imprisonment of the natural-person general partner or compliance officer.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR initial penalty of BMD 5,000 and additional penalty of BMD 10,000; daily default fines up to BMD 100 per day; the Registrar may order deregistration and striking-off the ELP from the register; cross-border information exchange to UK HMRC, US IRS, China SAT and Hong Kong IRD, exposing the group's underlying CbCR position to coordinated audit.

Director personal liability: The general partner of the ELP faces disqualification as a controller and a separate fine up to BMD 25,000 for continuing default; where the ELP is used to misrepresent substance for banking and customs licences, the general partner assumes personal civil liability to creditors, licence revocation notices are issued to the individual, and travel restrictions may be imposed during regulatory investigations.
Anti-Money Laundering (Proceeds of Crime) Offence

Statutory sanction: Money laundering conviction: imprisonment up to 20 years and a fine at the discretion of the court; civil forfeiture of the cargo, vessels, bank balances and the partnership's trading assets; AML Act 2018 penalties include corporate fines up to BMD 500,000 per breach and licence suspension for the ELP's payment-service and banking counterparties.

Director personal liability: Natural-person general partners and compliance officers are criminally liable where they knew or suspected the criminal origin of meat-trading proceeds. The court may impose travel bans, confiscation orders against personal assets, termination of the individual's director/manager status, and imprisonment; the corporate veil is pierced to hold the controlling partner personally liable for the full pecuniary penalty.
Beneficial Ownership and Nominee Concealment

Statutory sanction: Fine up to BMD 10,000 per offence and imprisonment up to 5 years upon conviction; the BOSSs registry may impose daily penalty notices and suspend the ELP's active status; adverse international sanctions reporting to the British Overseas Territories Beneficial Ownership Network.

Director personal liability: The general partner is deemed to be the reporting person; personal penalty orders and custodial sentences are available for false declarations. In cross-border enforcement, the UK Privy Council-level civil orders may freeze the personal bank accounts of the nominee general partner and impose a travel ban pending full disclosure.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Primary adjustment re-determining the ELP's taxable profit at arm's length; secondary adjustment of up to 2% of the mispriced transaction value in penalties; interest accrues from the original due date; under CITA the tax authority may impose a penalty equal to 50% of the additional tax attributable to deliberate mispricing, capped at BMD 200,000 for non-criminal cases.

Director personal liability: Where the general partner signed or directed deliberately mispriced invoices, that partner becomes personally liable for the adjusted tax and penalty to the extent the ELP assets are insufficient. The Bermuda court may issue a personal restitution order, a board management prohibition, and an overseas travel ban pending certification that the revised transfer pricing documentation has been filed.
