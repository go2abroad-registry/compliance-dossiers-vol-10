Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital is prescribed for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital commitments are fixed contractually in the Partnership Agreement and no escrow or deposit account is mandated. Limited partner liability is capped at unpaid capital contributions; all contributions must be settled through AML-compliant banking channels.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner of Bermuda (OTC), Hamilton, Bermuda - tax registration portal accessible via https://www.gov.bm; partnership registration: Registrar of Companies, Bermuda (registry.gov.bm); customs: Bermuda Customs Department (ASYCUDA World portal).

E-government filing portal

Foreign organizers of a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) file an Application for Registration with the Registrar of Companies (with memorandum, partnership agreement, registered office address, and declaration that all partners are non-resident for Bermuda tax purposes). The Registrar issues the Certificate of Registration and a statutory Register Number, which is the ELP's primary corporate identifier and its TIN for CRS/FATCA, economic substance, and customs filings. Because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, no VAT ID is issued and no separate TIN is required absent payroll tax liability. A Payroll Tax Account Number from the Office of the Tax Commissioner is triggered only if the ELP employs Bermuda-resident staff or incurs Bermuda-source wage expenditure. For customs, the ELP, acting through a licensed Bermuda customs broker, opens an Importer/Exporter Account with the Bermuda Customs Department. No exchange-control or inward-investor government approval is required for non-resident partners of an exempted partnership.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election: ELPs are mandatorily tax-transparent and never entity-level taxable, as Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax. The partnership agreement governs allocation of profits, losses, and distributions. For US federal tax purposes only, the ELP (if eligible) may make an entity-classification election under US Form 8832, without altering its Bermuda law transparency. Under the Economic Substance (Bermuda) Act 2018, the ELP should file a nil substance return or an election to be treated as carrying on only 'non-relevant activity' (e.g., passive wholesale distribution with no Bermuda intellectual property or core income-generating activity).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda has no VAT/GST. The operative indirect tax is the Bermuda customs-duty / excise tariff regime under the Customs Tariff Act 1975; internal payroll tax, stamp duty, and land tax apply only to domestic Bermuda activities and have no nexus to the beer export business. For separately established UK group entities, the UK VAT Act 1994 at the 20% standard rate applies.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold applies in Bermuda because no VAT/GST exists. A Bermuda Customs Importer/Exporter Code is mandatory for every commercial shipment, regardless of turnover. If a distinct UK establishment of the same group makes UK taxable supplies, the UK VAT threshold is GBP 85,000 (rolling 12-month turnover), but that threshold is irrelevant to the Bermuda ELP itself.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT zero-rating certificate can be issued in Bermuda because Bermuda has no VAT. Instead, the ELP may claim a customs-duty drawback or export exemption under the Customs Tariff Act 1975 for imported kegs, raw beer, and packaging that are re-exported in an unaltered or substantially transformed state, provided the goods physically leave Bermuda within the customs-prescribed period and are not consumed locally. If the UK parent makes a separate supply of goods from the UK to the Bermuda ELP, that UK supply may be zero-rated under VATA 1994 s.30(6) only when HMRC-prescribed export evidence (Notice 703; electronic or C85 export declarations) is obtained and retained; the Bermuda ELP cannot claim UK VAT refunds because it is not established in the UK VAT territory. The 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation benchmark in distribution contracts is a trade-finance lender covenant, not a Bermudian statutory rule.

  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill (original negotiable or equivalent electronic data)

Bermuda does not impose any electronic-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate for indirect tax purposes. Export and import declarations are lodged electronically with the Bermuda Customs Department through the ASYCUDA World system, referencing the Importer/Exporter Code and supporting commercial documents. UK Making Tax Digital obligations apply only to any separately UK VAT-registered group entity, not to the Bermuda ELP; any contractual B2B e-invoicing with EU distributors remains private rather than mandated by the State.

Bermuda Customs may retroactively claw back duty drawbacks and impose forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties up to treble the duty evaded, interest, and criminal imprisonment for fraudulent re-export declarations; the ELP and its general partner are jointly and severally liable for the clawback. For any UK-linked VAT zero-rating, HMRC may assess penalties up to 100% of the VAT plus interest where proof of export is missing, false, or late, and may apply joint liability to the UK group company. Failure to satisfy the 180-day FX repatriation covenant, where agreed with trade financiers, triggers contractual default and acceleration; Bermuda statutory law itself prescribes no FX repatriation deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is fixed at a 1:1 parity with the US Dollar (USD), with both currencies circulating as legal tender in Bermuda. Bermuda's Exchange Control Regulations are disapplied for 'exempted' vehicles, conferring upon the Exempted Limited Partnership unrestricted capital and current account convertibility; profit remittances, partnership distributions and capital redemptions to non-resident partners require no BMA approval and attract zero Bermuda withholding tax. The material frictions are confined to the UK-side: controlled-foreign-company attribution and transfer-pricing scrutiny where ultimate partners are UK-resident, plus CRS / FATCA information reporting on the financial accounts of the ELP and its partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender rule and no mandatory conversion ratio; the ELP may retain export receivables indefinitely in USD, BMD, GBP or EUR. Compliance duties are limited to BMA AML/ATF record-keeping for underlying trade documents, the Economic Substance Act 2018 filing applicable to the general partner, and UK HMRC customs / intrastat declarations for cold-chain keg movements crossing the United Kingdom.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as Bermuda's central monetary authority, currency issuer and banking supervisor under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Banking and Trust Companies Act 1991; the Bank of England and HM Treasury retain residual sovereign oversight of the United Kingdom, including the OFSI financial sanctions regime and the CHAPS / Faster Payments clearing systems that anchor the UK treasury leg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Bermuda does not license standalone electronic money institutions; digital payment platforms must hold either a deposit-taking licence under the Banking and Trust Companies Act 1991 or an MSB registration under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008. UK-authorised EMIs (Wise Platform, Revolut Business, Modulr-type BaaS providers) are viable only as supplementary GBP/EUR settlement wallets on the UK distribution leg, not as the primary treasury bank for the Bermuda ELP, because they lack BMA supervisory recognition and cannot support BMD/USD operational accounts, SWIFT MT103 trade disbursements or commercial-letter-of-credit structures.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Each UK partner wires capital as a SWIFT MT103 from its UK clearing bank to the ELP's HSBC Bank Bermuda Limited capital escrow account, with the Bermuda leg settled in USD or BMD at the 1:1 fixed rate; CHAPS provides the same-day high-value departure leg in the UK. The UK remitting bank records a direct-investment balance-of-payments purpose code (equity injection into an overseas partnership), executes OFSI/OFAC sanctions screening, and releases value upon matching the beneficiary name to the partnership's KYC file. The Bermuda receiving bank verifies the executed capital contribution agreement, books the proceeds as committed partnership capital, and issues a credit confirmation for the annual exempted-partnership filing; no BMA exchange-control authorisation is required. Turnover: No statutory audit is imposed on Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnerships under the ELP Act 2015. An independent audit becomes compulsory only if (i) the partnership agreement expressly requires an audited annual account, (ii) the ELP is licensed under regulated-sector legislation such as insurance, banking, or fund management, or (iii) a corporate general partner is itself a Bermuda exempted company requiring audit. A craft-beer distribution ELP is therefore outside the compulsory audit net unless the LPA mandates otherwise.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% for in-scope constituent entities of multinational enterprise groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million under Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax Act 2024; 0% for all other Bermuda entities, including the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), because Bermuda does not impose a general corporate income tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no dividend or partnership distribution withholding tax on payments by an Exempted Limited Partnership to non-resident partners.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda Local File or Master File obligation applies to an out-of-scope SME ELP; mandatory transfer pricing documentation arises only when the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, approximately BMD 800 million at prevailing exchange rates. Even below this threshold, the Bermuda tax authorities may require contemporaneous evidence of arm's-length pricing for any controlled transactions.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 and applicable Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to the ELP as an exempted entity engaged in a relevant activity. Wholesale keg and hazy IPA distribution constitutes a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity, requiring the ELP to demonstrate substantive management, employee presence, premises, expenditure, and core income-generating activities within Bermuda proportional to its distribution revenues. Annual Form ESR-1 filing is mandatory. A failure to satisfy economic substance, particularly a structure operated solely from the UK, will expose the ELP to penalties, deregistration, and, for in-scope MNE groups, mandatory taxation under Bermuda's 15% corporate income tax.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent CIT Reporting

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded, statutory interest, confiscation of the profit arising from the permanent establishment, and criminal fines of up to BMD 500,000. Imprisonment for up to 10 years may be imposed on conviction for fraudulent evasion of the Corporate Income Tax Act.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced where the ELP's general partner directed or knowingly participated in the evasion; the general partner becomes jointly and severally liable for restitution, faces a travel ban pending deregistration, and is liable to imprisonment on individual conviction.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act 2018, the initial penalty is BMD 50,000; a repeat failure attracts BMD 100,000 and the Registrar of Companies may strike the ELP off the register, nullify the exempted status, and revoke tax certificates.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors of the corporate general partner will be personally liable for the ELP's debts if it continues trading after deregistration; they may be disqualified from acting as Bermuda directors for five years and face enforcement of personal restitution orders through the Supreme Court of Bermuda.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Bermuda's AML/CFT regulatory regime imposes fines of up to BMD 1 million per breach, suspension or revocation of the ELP's money service business or trade compliance approval, and asset freezes where the transactions are connected to designated sanctioned persons or jurisdictions.

Director personal liability: Relevant officers, general partners, and compliance officers face personal criminal liability including imprisonment and confiscation orders, travel bans, and disqualification from directing any Bermuda-registered entity, particularly where suspicious activity reports were knowingly suppressed.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Commissioner may adjust the ELP's income to the arm's-length price under the transfer pricing rules embedded in the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, impose an understatement penalty of 50% to 100% of the additional CIT, and charge accrued interest; UK HMRC may simultaneously adjust the UK supplier's pricing and apply transfer pricing penalties in the UK.

Director personal liability: Where the pricing structure is found to be artificial and the general partner personally orchestrated the margin shift, the general partner and directors of the corporate general partner may be held liable for the adjusted tax amount, subjected to civil restitution proceedings, and prohibited from leaving Bermuda until the assessment is settled.
