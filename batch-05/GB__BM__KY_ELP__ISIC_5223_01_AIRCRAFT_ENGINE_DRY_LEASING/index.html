Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (service portal: www.gov.bm online services; corporate registry administered by the Registrar of Companies on the ProCrowd platform). Bermuda does not issue a separate TIN or VAT ID; the statutory tax identifier for an Exempted Limited Partnership is the Tax Certificate of Compliance (TCC) number.

E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. The Bermuda ELP is fiscally transparent; Bermuda levies no corporation tax, income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. Aircraft engine dry-leasing rentals derived by non-resident partners from engines operated wholly outside Bermuda are outside the Bermuda tax net. The result is tax neutrality rather than a designated 'tax-exempt zone' concession.

Available election forms

Under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015, the ELP may elect to be a separate legal person distinct from its partners; the election does not alter fiscal transparency. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 (check-the-box) to elect transparent partnership status or association taxable as a corporation. For U.K. purposes, no check-the-box mechanism exists; the partnership is statutorily transparent, but the U.K. hybrid and other mismatches rules (CTA 2010 Part 6A) require an offsetting adjustment if the ELP is a reverse hybrid with a U.K. partner. Bermuda itself offers no hybrid-mismatch election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None. Bermuda has no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. Import customs duties apply only to goods physically entering Bermuda; an engine dry-leased and operated outside Bermuda is outside the customs duty net. U.K. output VAT may arise only where the engine is located in, or supplied to a recipient in, the U.K.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda imposes no VAT or GST registration threshold. Payroll tax registration is triggered only upon first Bermuda-resident hire, with banded levies on Bermuda-source remuneration; customs registration applies only upon physical importation of dutiable goods into Bermuda. Neither trigger is present in a pure offshore dry-leasing structure.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Strict VAT zero-rating is unavailable because Bermuda has no VAT system; the functional equivalent is automatic territorial exclusion. Lease rentals are entirely outside Bermuda taxation where the engine is registered, located, and operated outside Bermuda and the lessee is non-resident. If U.K. VAT exposure is separately assessed, zero-rating may apply to the supply of a means of transport used for international commercial aviation; the lessor should retain a declaration from the lessee covering non-U.K. use and international operation.

Not applicable - Bermuda operates no e-invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance, API transmission, or digital stamping system. Commercial invoices and standard accounting records satisfy the Tax Commissioner's documentation requirements; the economic substance return is the principal compliance filing.

Bermuda's Registrar of Companies, Office of the Tax Commissioner, and Bermuda Monetary Authority may audit economic substance filings, beneficial ownership records, and payroll records. False declarations attract penalties up to BMD 250,000 and/or imprisonment for responsible officers, plus daily default penalties under the Economic Substance (Requirements and Compliance) Act 2018 and the Revenue Administration Act 1999. No VAT clawback exists in Bermuda because no VAT is levied. The principal restitutionary risk is HMRC recharacterization: if central management and control is found in the U.K., net lease income becomes subject to U.K. corporation tax (up to 25%) with possible diverted profits tax, retrospective assessment, and interest for up to 6 years. Exchange of information under the Bermuda-UK TIEA and CRS facilitates discovery. The 180-day rental repatriation benchmark is a contractual and lender-driven covenant; failure to repatriate within that window weakens the non-Bermuda source position in any audit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 with the United States dollar (USD) and is freely usable alongside USD in Bermuda. For a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) that is designated non-resident, capital account transactions (initial contributions, capital redemptions, return of partnership capital) and current account transactions (lease rental income, interest, fees, profit distributions) are freely convertible and remittable in USD or BMD without a separate BMA approval. The principal practical hurdles are not convertibility controls but: (i) demonstrating non-resident status to the bank and BMA through constitutional documents and UBO declarations; (ii) satisfying enhanced KYC/AML source-of-funds checks; and (iii) ensuring no Bermuda-source trading/profit nexus (lease payments under a dry engine lease to a non-resident lessee are generally sourced outside Bermuda). Profit remittance by an ELP to non-resident limited partners is not subject to Bermuda withholding tax and can be paid from a non-resident designated account without exchange control formality.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio for goods or services export receipts. Aircraft engine lease rentals received in USD may be retained in USD or converted to BMD at a licensed bank's commercial rate; there is no fixed timeline to convert or repatriate. The BMA's oversight focuses on AML/ATF record-keeping under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018, not on foreign exchange bundling or surrender.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the de facto central bank and foreign exchange control authority for Bermuda, administering the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, and supervising Bermuda-licensed banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Bermuda does not currently provide an EU/UK-style electronic money institution (EMI) passporting licence, and deposit-taking activity by non-bank entities is prohibited under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. An offshore digital EMI can be used only as an ancillary channel for payments/currency conversion; it cannot lawfully hold the ELP's operating treasury or act as the primary clearing bank. A BMA-licensed commercial bank is therefore mandatory for capital escrow and lease cash management.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions must be wired by SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's USD-denominated non-resident designated account at a BMA-licensed bank. The beneficiary account name should be the exact full legal name of the ELP; the originator must include its full name and account number. The payment narrative/reference in MT103 field 70/72 should state 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / [ELP NAME] / CAPITAL ACCOUNT'. Where the receiving bank requires a purpose code, the code for capital/equity contribution under that bank's SWIFT service must be used; for banks not requiring a code, 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' is sufficient. Because the BMD is pegged at par, USD is credited directly and no conversion or surrender is required. For contributions above the bank's reporting threshold, the bank should be pre-notified and supplied with the source-of-funds pack; all SWIFT messages should comply with FATF Recommendation 16 enhanced data requirements (full originator and beneficiary information). Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an Exempted Limited Partnership that is not a member of an in-scope multinational group; 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax on Bermuda-source taxable profits of multinational groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 where consolidated group revenue exceeds EUR 750 million.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda law imposes no withholding tax on distributions, dividends, profit participations or capital remittances paid by an Exempted Limited Partnership to non-resident partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

CIT Return due on the last day of the 15th month following fiscal year-end (e.g., 31 December 2025 year-end files no later than 31 March 2027); quarterly instalment payments of estimated tax fall due during the fiscal year; Economic Substance annual return due 30 June following the calendar year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual government fee and registry return due before 31 January following the ELP's fee anniversary; Economic Substance annual return for the preceding calendar year due 30 June.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For in-scope MNE groups under the CIT Act 2024, transfer pricing documentation follows OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines: a Local File is required where aggregate controlled transactions exceed approximately BMD 1,000,000; a Master File is required for all in-scope groups above the EUR 750 million consolidated revenue threshold; Country-by-Country reporting follows the same threshold. Out-of-scope ELPs face no statutory Bermuda TP documentation threshold, but must nonetheless retain evidence of arm's-length pricing for cross-border engine leasing transactions to support partner-jurisdiction filings and the economic substance review.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 applies because finance and leasing is a listed 'relevant activity'. An aircraft engine dry-leasing ELP must demonstrate, at group level, the presence of: (i) partner-management meetings with substantive minutes in Bermuda; (ii) qualified employees physically located in Bermuda; (iii) adequate operating expenditure on premises and staff; and (iv) core income-generating activities - contract negotiation, lease pricing, engine asset management, risk assumption and hedging - performed in Bermuda or outsourced to a genuinely active and monitored Bermuda service provider. A paper-only ELP with foreign management fails the substance test and will be subject to sanctions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Material Misstatement under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to BMD 250,000 plus the 10% late-payment penalty on any underpaid CIT and interest at the prescribed rate; on conviction, unlimited fines recoverable by the Tax Commissioner within a 6-year limitation period.

Director personal liability: General partner directors and partner-managers face personal prosecution, restitution orders for the tax evaded, director disqualification, and travel bans pending resolution; on indictment, imprisonment of up to 2 years.
Economic Substance Failure (Shell Company Arrangement)

Statutory sanction: Administrative financial penalties of BMD 20,000 escalating on a sliding scale to BMD 250,000 for repeated or deliberate non-compliance; registry strike-off of the ELP and forfeiture of exemption benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners are jointly and severally liable for the value of tax benefits denied to the wider group; individual false declarations attract fines of up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment of up to 6 months on summary conviction, or 2 years on indictment; courts may issue security-for-cost orders and pierce the partnership veil to recover penalties from personal assets.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Regulatory fines up to BMD 1,000,000 per offence under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008; financial intelligence agency censure; asset freezes and transaction revocations; the ELP's bank or EMI account is closed and reported to the Bermuda Monetary Authority.

Director personal liability: General partners, compliance officers and directors are personally liable to fines of up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering offences under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997; these criminal personal penalties operate as a de facto piercing of the partnership veil and override any limitation of liability in the partnership agreement.
Transfer Pricing Avoidance and Artificial Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation of the arrangements under the CIT Act 2024 and, for UK-connected chains, the UK transfer pricing and diverted profits tax legislation; secondary adjustments and penalty surcharges of up to 10% of the adjusted profit; denial of the Bermuda Tax Residence Certificate and cancellation of treaty or TIEA relief in the partner jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors of the group's chain are jointly and severally liable to reimburse diverted corporation tax and interest; the UK HMRC and the Bermudian Tax Commissioner may exchange information under the Bermuda-UK Tax Information Exchange Agreement; director-level financial penalties plus personal restitution orders are enforceable across the UK common-law jurisdiction, including travel restrictions and, for aggravated cases, custodial sentences.
