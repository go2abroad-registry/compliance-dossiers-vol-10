Competent tax authority

The Office of the Tax Commissioner, Government of Bermuda (2nd Floor, Government Administration Building, 26 Church Street, Hamilton; official e-filing portal: Bermuda e-Tax system at https://etax.taxation.gov.bm). Supporting authorities: Registrar of Companies / Corporation & Land Registry (entity incorporation) and the Customs Department, Ministry of Finance (import duties). E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the ELP under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 via the Registrar of Companies and obtain (a) the partnership registration number and (b) a Certificate of Compliance; concurrently file the application for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 certificate. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent: profits and losses flow to partners under the Partnership Deed. Bermuda currently imposes no entity-level income, capital gains, corporate, or withholding tax on such transparent vehicles. Non-resident partners have no Bermuda tax exposure. Exception: in-scope MNE groups (consolidated revenue over €750 million) become subject to 15% CIT/QDMTT at the constituent-partner level from 1 January 2025, and IP-related relevant activity triggers economic substance obligations under the Economic Substance Act 2018.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no US-style check-the-box regime; transparency is the statutory default for an ELP. Available elections: (i) the 1966 Tax Protection Certificate election (guarantees exemption from new Bermudian taxes on income, profits, capital gains, and distributions until 28 March 2035, subject to the CIT carve-out); (ii) partnership-deed profit-sharing and capital-allocation elections; (iii) for US federal tax, the ELP may file Form 8832 to affirm partnership classification (default when two or more members) or elect corporate classification; (iv) under CITA 2023, an in-scope group may elect QDMTT rules with the alternative loss carry-forward computation for the 2025 transition year; (v) the Economic Substance Act 2018 allows a core-income-generating-activities-outside-Bermuda treatment upon filing a declaration and an annual economic substance return with the Registrar.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Duties regime (no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax is levied in Bermuda; UK VAT does not extend to Bermuda)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — there is no VAT/GST registration threshold in Bermuda. Importers of physical goods must clear customs duty (rates generally 0% to 22% ad valorem, with additional specific excise on alcohol and tobacco). Digital EdTech subscription exports routed through Apple IAP or Google Play Billing are outside the scope of Bermudian customs duty and require no indirect tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Conceptually not applicable: because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, exported EdTech subscription services are outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxation and no zero-rating claim is required. Where subscription revenue is collected via Apple App Store or Google Play, the platform is the deemed merchant for VAT in the end-customer market. For physical goods exported from Bermuda, customs duty is relieved upon proof of export (export declaration plus carrier documentation); digital IP licenses and offshore software services carry no Bermudian indirect tax.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (electronic single-window submission, required only if physical goods move across the border)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export (where goods are shipped)
  • Commercial invoice and subscription revenue ledger (for EdTech SaaS/IAP revenue traceability)
  • Inward remittance or EMI settlement statement evidencing receipt of foreign consideration (prudent audit evidence; not a statutory requirement in Bermuda)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate applies to digital services or subscription sales in Bermuda. Physical imports must be declared electronically via the Bermuda Customs Department single-window declaration system. Apple/Google, acting as merchant of record in the consumer's jurisdiction, are responsible for foreign VAT/digital-services-tax compliance; Bermuda imposes no equivalent levy on those flows.

There is no VAT refund or export-VAT clawback regime to contravene in Bermuda because no VAT is imposed. Enforcement risk concentrates on: (i) customs duty evasion — forfeiture of goods plus penalties up to treble the duty and potential prosecution; (ii) payroll tax misdeclaration — 10% monthly penalty up to a 50% maximum plus statutory interest; (iii) CITA 2025 under-declaration of IP/subscription income by an in-scope MNE — transfer-pricing adjustments, statutory late-payment interest, and fines up to BMD 250,000 with senior-officer liability; (iv) Economic Substance Act 2018 non-compliance — fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment of officers for false declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar. Both current-account and capital-account payments are convertible, but residents must route transactions through BMA-licensed authorized dealers. Non-resident owned Exempted Limited Partnerships generally enjoy high capital-account freedom, provided exchange control permission is obtained for capital inflows and subsequent profit remittances. Principal remittance hurdle: documentary proof of non-resident beneficial ownership, underlying commercial substance, and BMA Form-based approval where the partnership account holds BMD balances. Profit distributions from an exempted partnership are not subject to Bermudian withholding tax, but the paying bank will require an exchange control declaration and standard AML/KYC evidence before releasing funds to non-resident partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, a Bermuda-resident entity must offer foreign currency export proceeds to an authorized dealer within one calendar month of receipt unless the BMA has granted specific or general approval to retain those proceeds in a foreign currency account. For a non-resident owned ELP, the BMA generally grants approval to maintain USD and other foreign currency accounts without mandatory conversion into BMD, provided the entity files a valid certificate of non-residence and updates the authorized dealer on any change in beneficial ownership. If the ELP is treated as resident for exchange control purposes, 100% conversion of export service proceeds into BMD would be required, but commercial DSPs routinely seek and obtain 'retention in foreign currency' authorization.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the central bank and the designated Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for direct settlement through an offshore digital EMI within Bermuda. Bermuda has no standalone electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime; deposit-taking and payment-account services are reserved for banks and deposit companies regulated by the BMA. An overseas EMI cannot independently hold customer funds or provide a Bermudian account routing number unless it establishes a BMA-licensed subsidiary or enters into a sponsored-bank arrangement with a licensed Bermuda bank. Practical alternative: use an EU/UK EEA EMI as the low-cost treasury front-end, backed by a BMA-licensed clearing bank for cross-border USD/BMD settlement. Legal constraint: introduction of unregulated third-party funds into a Bermuda bank account may trigger enhanced due diligence and refusal unless the EMI operates under a recognized equivalent regulatory regime (e.g., FCA, BaFin, CBI).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing requires a SWIFT MT103 from the contributing partner's ancestral bank to the ELP's BMA-licensed bank account. Before remittance, the ELP must file the applicable BMA exchange control application (Form EA-1 or successor digital notification) identifying the capital contribution as 'Capital Contribution to Non-Resident Exempted Limited Partnership'. The wire reference must include purpose code 'CAP / EQ / 1000' or, if required by the correspondent bank, a comparable ISO purpose code supporting 'Capital account transfer', accompanied by the partnership agreement, capital call letter, and beneficial ownership declaration. USD wires may be received directly into an approved USD account; conversion to BMD occurs at the pegged rate of 1:1. The receiving bank will normalise the credit under a 'capital escrow' or 'pending investment' memo until exchange control documentation is approved, after which funds are released to the operational account.Outbound operational disbursements (licensing fees, cloud infrastructure, content production, marketing) should be executed via SWIFT MT103 from the ELP's Bermuda operational account to the supplier's bank. For large contracts exceeding USD 250,000 or when suppliers demand documentary assurance, a commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700 series) or standby LC may be used, but it is not customary for digital EdTech service purchases. All MT103 messages must carry the underlying invoice number, a purpose code describing the service and clear instruction 'NOT FOR USE OF THIRD PARTIES'. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for entities outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; 15% for Constituent Entities of MNE Groups with consolidated annual revenue of €750m or more (fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividend distributions, profit share, or other partnership distributions to non-resident partners).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No transfer pricing documentation threshold for out-of-scope entities under Bermuda's general tax code. For in-scope MNE Groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the Master File and Local File are mandatory when consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds BMD 1.5 billion (approximately €750 million) and the Bermuda ELP engages in controlled transactions involving intellectual property, intra-group financing, or subscriptions. Bermuda's CIT administrative rules adopt the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and governing principle that intercompany prices must reflect arm's length conduct.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2018, as amended, apply to all Bermuda-resident ELPs carrying on 'relevant activities'. The EdTech language-learning business falls within 'Intellectual Property Business' if the ELP holds, licenses, or exploits software or copyrighted teaching content. The ELP must annually file an ESR return, and for high-risk IP businesses a rebuttable presumption of non-compliance arises if core income-generating activities are not performed in Bermuda with adequate employees and premises. Non-resident ELPs without Bermuda substance are exposed to penalty, strike-off, and disclosure of the ultimate beneficial owners.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate income tax evasion / misrepresentation under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and Tax Administration Act 1995

Statutory sanction: On summary conviction, a fine not exceeding BMD 100,000; on indictment, a fine not exceeding BMD 500,000 and/or imprisonment not exceeding 10 years. The court may order restitution of evaded tax plus interest and daily default fines of BMD 1,000 for continuing non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors and corporate general partners who knowingly participate in the evasion may be personally assessed for evaded tax, fall under civil restitution orders, be subject to travel bans and asset freezes, and face up to 10 years' imprisonment; the corporate veil will be pierced where the entity is used as a facade.
Shell company / economic substance non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the ESR 2018, penalties escalate: BMD 10,000 for non-filing; BMD 1,000 per day for continued default; up to BMD 250,000 for false or misleading information; and up to BMD 400,000 for failure to satisfy the economic substance test for a high-risk IP business. Recidivist breaches may lead to a court order striking the ELP off the register and directing liquidation.

Director personal liability: The general partner's directors may be held personally liable for the penalties where they directed or approved the sham substance arrangements; they can be barred from serving as directors of any Bermuda entity, subjected to injunctions, and ordered to personally compensate the Treasury for lost revenues.
Anti-money laundering / countering the financing of terrorism (AML/CFT) failures

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Monetary Authority can impose civil fines up to BMD 250,000 per contravention, issue public censure, and require the appointment of a compliance consultant. Criminal prosecution under the Proceeds of Crime (AML/CFT) Regulations 2008 carries unlimited fines and imprisonment not exceeding 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, and compliance officers are personally liable for AML failures; the BMA may issue prohibition orders banning them from holding positions in regulated businesses, and the court may impose travel bans and seizure of passports while the investigation is ongoing.
