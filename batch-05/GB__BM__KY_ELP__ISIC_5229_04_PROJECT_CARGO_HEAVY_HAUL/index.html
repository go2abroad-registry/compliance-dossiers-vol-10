Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK: Zero-rating applies to the export of goods outside the UK provided the seller retains official proof of export within the prescribed time limits (HMRC Notice 720). Services for offshore project cargo logistics may qualify as exports of services if the recipient belongs outside the UK; if related to land or goods located outside the UK, place of supply rules determine zero- or outside-of-scope treatment. Bermuda: no VAT exists, so zero-rating is not applicable; customs export documentation is required for removal from iInsular customs territory

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / CDS export declaration) validated by UK or Bermuda customs authorities
  • Commercial Bill of Lading / Airway Bill evidencing goods shipped to destination outside the relevant customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence showing settlement of the export consideration within the contractual payment term

UK does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping; Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires VAT-registered businesses to keep digital records and submit VAT returns via HMRC-approved software APIs. Bermuda follows no real-time clearance; e-invoicing is voluntary. Customs declarations for goods are filed electronically through the UK Single Trade Window / CDS (Customs Declaration Service) with API integration

Under UK VAT law, fraudulent zero-rating claims or missing evidence of export attract denial of the zero rate, VAT assessment, and penalties under Finance Act 2003 s.60 for dishonest evasion; penalties range up to 100% of the unpaid VAT, and officers may obtain retrospective clawback via assessment within 4 years (or 20 years in cases of deliberate concealment). In Bermuda, misdeclaration in customs duty can lead to seizure, fines under the Customs Act, and forfeiture of goods; false documentation may be criminal under the Theft Act or Customs provisions

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BMD is pegged at a fixed 1:1 rate to the USD and is fully backed by US dollar reserves; current-account transactions (project cargo service fees, freight disbursements, operating expenses) are freely convertible and remittable without BMA approval. Capital-account transactions, defined as any issue or transfer of partnership interests, capital contributions, liquidation distributions, or repatriation of contributed funds, require BMA approval unless the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) has been formally designated 'non-resident' for exchange-control purposes. A non-resident-designated ELP, where all general and limited partners are non-resident, may maintain external accounts in USD, GBP, EUR, or any freely convertible currency and may remit profits and capital abroad without per-transaction approval. Practical hurdles: the non-resident designation must be applied for at formation and any change in partner residency must be notified to the BMA within 14 days; resident-designated ELPs face case-by-case prior approval for distributions, with review times of 5-10 business days. Profit remittance is otherwise unimpeded for non-resident ELPs, subject only to AML/KYC verification by the account-holding bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, or repatriation timeline on export proceeds, freight-forwarding commissions, heavy-haul logistics fees, or any invisible earnings received in foreign currency. The ELP may retain all receipts in the currency of collection (typically USD for project cargo contracting, GBP or EUR for European legs). The United Kingdom similarly applies no proceeds-surrender regime under the now-dormant Exchange Control Act 1947, so consolidation of receipts at a UK clearing bank is equally unrestricted.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) is the integrated regulator acting as Bermuda's central-bank equivalent, with exclusive statutory responsibility for issuing the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) and administering exchange control under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973. As Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory, the Bank of England retains nominal apex monetary oversight for the United Kingdom, but all day-to-day currency issuance, reserve management, licensing of deposit-taking institutions, and FX authorisations for Bermuda are delegated wholly to the BMA. For cross-border flows touching the UK, Her Majesty's Treasury and the Bank of England exert no direct exchange-control authority over Bermuda-designated non-resident entities.

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium for operational receivables and payables in GBP/EUR/USD via UK-regulated electronic money institutions (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) where the ELP maintains a UK clearing address or a UK-resident general partner; Low for trade settlement because EMIs do not issue documentary letters of credit (MT700), standby LCs (MT760), or provide the correspondent-banking depth required for project-cargo milestone escrow and heavy-haul disbursements. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident limited partner contributions should be routed via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer from the originating UK, European, or US bank to the ELP's Bermuda external account in BMD or USD. Remittance instructions must include: (a) in Field 70, the purpose code 'CAPT' (Capital Contribution to Partnership) or the free-text descriptor 'Capital contribution to Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership GB__BM__KY_ELP - non-resident'; (b) in Field 72, the ELP's Bermuda company registration number and the BMA non-resident designation reference; and (c) the ultimate remitter's full name and account number to allow source-of-funds matching. Pre-funding verification requires an executed capital-commitment schedule signed by the general partner, a certificate of incumbency for each contributing LP, FATCA/CRS self-certification (Form W-8BEN-E or equivalent) with the partnership's GIIN issued via the BMA IGA portal, and source-of-wealth evidence for any single tranche equal to or exceeding USD 50,000. Funds are typically credited same-day for USD wires via the New York clearing leg (CHIPS/Fedwire) and settled in BMD at par.Approved clearing rails for project-cargo trade settlements: (a) SWIFT MT103 for milestone-based customer credit transfers, subcontractor payments, and customs-duty disbursements in USD, GBP, or EUR; (b) Documentary Commercial Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700 conforming to UCP600 for heavy-haul equipment purchases and ocean-freight settlement, with presentation through the ELP's bank and a confirming bank where required; (c) Standby Letter of Credit via SWIFT MT760 for bid bonds, performance bonds, and advance-payment guarantees common in project-cargo tenders; and (d) MT202/MT202 COV cover payments for interbank reimbursement of a foreign advising or confirming bank. All outbound remittances from a non-resident-designated ELP external account are free of exchange-control approval; for any residual resident-designated balances, BMA Form EC-4 prior approval is required per remittance, adding 5-10 business days of lead time.FX Forward (Outright) - available from BMA-licensed banks (HSBC Bank Bermuda, Butterfield) for EUR/GBP, GBP/USD, and EUR/USD tenors of 1 to 12 months; unnecessary for BMD/USD given the fixed peg, but recommended to lock milestone-receipt conversion costs.Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) - permitted for restricted currencies on project-cargo routes (CNY, INR, BRL) executed through the London NDF market via a UK clearing bank, with cash settlement in USD; no BMA objection for non-resident ELPs.Cross-Currency Swap (CCX Y / XCCY) - permitted for EUR/GBP versus USD alignment of large OOG heavy-haul capex and long-duration project disbursements, subject to the bank's internal derivative-product approval and board-authorized ISDA/CSA execution.FX Vanilla Option and Zero-Cost Collar - permissible for single-tender or single-project protection on large bid and milestone exposures, available from the Bermudian bank's treasury desk with BMA-licensed counterparty status and standard master-agreement documentation.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general Bermuda income tax; 15% Bermuda CIT Act 2023 rate for constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated annual revenue ≥ EUR 750 million

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Registrar validation: annually, generally within 30 days after the partnership's anniversary or 31 May for calendar-year entities. CIT Return: within 15 months after the fiscal year-end of the in-scope MNE group (e.g., calendar-year FY 2025 is due by 31 March 2027).

Deadline: · Frequency:

ELP validation window as above; for CIT-in-scope entities the return is due within 15 months after the end of each fiscal year.

Annual Validation / Strike-Off Prevention Block Status of registered office; confirmation of general partner; payment of annual government fee. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold applies to the ELP in the general tax-exempt regime. For CIT-in-scope MNE groups, local file, master file and benchmarking must be prepared for all controlled transactions with no minimum exemption threshold in Bermuda Dollars (BMD); materiality follows OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and the CIT Act 2023.

Economic substance scope: Under the Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) Act 2018, a Bermuda ELP carrying on a relevant activity — including distribution and service business relevant to ISIC 5229 project cargo logistics — must maintain (a) management and control exercised in Bermuda, (b) adequate employees, premises and physical assets in Bermuda, and (c) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda. Annual ESR declarations must be filed; the ELP cannot operate as a pure shell even if fully tax-exempt. Pure equity-holding entities have reduced substance requirements, but a heavy-haul logistics vehicle is not a pure equity-holding entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False CIT Filings

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of 10% of understated tax; daily default penalties; criminal fine up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years; publication of non-compliant entity status.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any controlling partner who knowingly participated in evasion are jointly and severally liable for tax and penalties; the court may order civil restitution, freezing orders and travel debarment; repeat offences attract disqualification as directors or partners of Bermuda entities.
Economic Substance (Shell Company) Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR review by the Competent Authority; penalty up to BMD 250,000; automatic deregistration of the ELP; Notice of Non-Compliance sent to the Registrar; the ELP may be struck off and lose its legal existence.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners face civil recovery for distributions made while insolvent, restoration claims on strike-off, and potential personal liability for debts if the ELP transacted without substance; the Registrar may issue a travel ban against the general partner for failure to wind up.
AML / Tax-Transparency Red Flag

Statutory sanction: Fines and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime (AML/CFT) Regulations 2018; regulatory penalties up to BMD 1,000,000 and closure of bank account facilities.

Director personal liability: The general partner and compliance officers are personally liable for AML breaches; statutory liability extends to senior management; travel restrictions and disqualification from acting as regulated-institution officers may be imposed.
Improper Distribution and Partner Capital Withdrawal

Statutory sanction: Invalidation of the distribution and restoration of assets to the ELP; civil penalties equal to the amount withdrawn; criminal liability for fraudulent conveyance.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any partner who consented to the improper distribution are jointly and severally liable to restore the amount; directors may face disqualification orders and travel bans where enforcement orders remain outstanding.
