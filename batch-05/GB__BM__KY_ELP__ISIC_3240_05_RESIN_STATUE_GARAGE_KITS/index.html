Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Bermuda Ministry of Finance – official government tax portal; HM Customs Bermuda for indirect tax/customs matters; Registrar of Companies, Bermuda for entity registration; UK HMRC as sovereign-contact tax administration for UK VAT and cross-border trade.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must file the relevant registration forms with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnership Act 1883, resulting in an Exempted Partnership Certificate. No Bermuda TIN is issued automatically; a Payroll Tax Account must be obtained from the Office of the Tax Commissioner only if the ELP has Bermuda-resident employees or Bermuda-source remuneration. The ELP should apply to HM Customs Bermuda for an Importer/Exporter Account for customs duty and export clearance. Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST, therefore no Bermuda VAT registration is required. If the ELP voluntarily registers for UK VAT to manage UK or EU parcel deliveries, it applies through HMRC online services and receives a UK VAT registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda law treats the ELP as fiscally transparent; income and gains generally flow through to partners and are not taxed at the partnership level. There is no check-the-box election in Bermuda. Where a corporate partner exists, tax is applied according to that partner's residence. The Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, applying only to multinational enterprise groups with consolidated revenue above €750 million, permits a single filing entity election; a small resin statue garage-kit enterprise is not in scope. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available because Bermuda does not operate a general income tax for this entity type.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda Customs Duty (No VAT/GST in Bermuda; UK HMRC VAT applies at destination for UK consumer supplies)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Bermuda because no VAT/GST exists. A commercial business importing or exporting goods must register with HM Customs Bermuda regardless of turnover. For UK VAT purposes, an offshore seller is not automatically subject to the UK £85,000 taxable turnover threshold; import VAT and parcel consignment rules apply, including the £135 low-value consignment threshold, and a UK VAT registration may become necessary if the ELP makes relevant taxable supplies through a UK establishment or appoints a fiscal representative.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda imposes no VAT, so no export zero-rating is required in Bermuda; exports are outside Bermuda's indirect tax net. For UK VAT purposes, a registered UK VAT trader may zero-rate exports of goods from the UK to a non-UK destination provided the goods are exported within statutory time limits, the ELP retains contemporaneous evidence of physical export, and the supply is recorded as zero-rated in the UK VAT return. Offshore services such as statue mould design and licensing are zero-rated only if the recipient is a non-UK business and the services are used outside the UK.

Bermuda has no real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate for domestic sales. HM Customs Bermuda requires electronic customs declarations for import/export control. For UK VAT, Making Tax Digital mandates HMRC-compatible software and digital VAT records; e-invoicing is not compulsory, but where used, the standard is UK adopted PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0.

Bermuda enforces customs penalties for false export declarations, including fines up to triple the value of the merchandise and imprisonment. In the UK, HMRC may impose a penalty of up to 100% of the VAT understated or overclaimed, plus interest, and may retrospectively claw back zero-rating relief where export substantiation is inadequate. The assessment window can extend to 20 years for fraud. A false export claim also exposes the ELP to civil fraud investigation and, in egregious cases, criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar. Bermuda maintains a liberalized exchange-control regime: current-account transactions, capital movements, and profit repatriation for non-resident entities such as an Exempted Limited Partnership are fully convertible and not subject to material approval hurdles. Non-resident ELPs are able to hold foreign-currency accounts freely, and remittance of distributable profits to partners in the United Kingdom, the European Union, or other jurisdictions is permitted without exchange-control license requirements, subject only to standard banking due-diligence and AML/CFT screening through the BMA-supervised banking sector.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists for an ELP to repatriate, surrender, or convert export proceeds into BMD. Bermuda does not impose mandatory surrender timelines or conversion ratios for export receipts. Export sales denominated in USD, GBP, or EUR may be retained in the respective currency and used directly for working capital, supplier settlements, or partner distributions.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility: High for supplementary payment rails, but not as a substitute for a BMA-licensed bank account. Reputable offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business, or PayPal Business) may be opened remotely for an ELP and are effective for receivables collection from retail customers in the UK/US/EU and for low-value supplier disbursements. Legal constraints: these EMIs are not regulated by the BMA; they are subject to their home-state licensing and may restrict the volume of transactions flowing from a Bermuda-domiciled entity. The ELP should maintain a genuine BMA-supervised primary account for fiduciary integrity, working capital concentration, and correspondent clearing for high-value trade payments.
