Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

A foreign-sponsored Exempted Limited Partnership is constituted by a licensed Bermuda registered agent filing an Exemption Certificate application under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 with the Registrar of Companies. Bermuda issues a Certificate of Exemption rather than a TIN because domestic law levies no income, corporate, capital gains or distribution tax. Where the ELP employs staff, a payroll tax account is opened with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and a Bermuda Employer Tax ID is issued; import and export operations require registration with the Bermuda Customs Service and, for consignees, an importer code. No minimum capital and no local ownership requirement apply; the ELP must file an annual economic substance declaration if it carries on relevant activity in Bermuda.

Bermuda's ELP is a transparent limited partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 and may obtain a statutory tax exemption certificate guaranteeing no Bermudian tax on profits, income, capital gains or distributions until 2035. For US federal tax purposes, a Bermuda ELP is not a per se corporation and may file IRS Form 8832 (check-the-box) to elect partnership, disregarded entity, or association taxable as corporation status; a single non-US member may instead default to disregarded status without filing. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No export zero-rating regime exists because Bermuda does not impose VAT or GST. Goods exported from Bermuda attract no customs duty, duty being exigible only upon importation; services supplied offshore by an exempted partnership are outside any Bermudian consumption tax. Consequently, an ELP manufacturing cosplay prop apparel and shipping to UK, EU and US customers holds no domestic indirect-tax refund entitlement and requires no zero-rating substantiation in Bermuda.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a 1:1 fixed rate to the United States Dollar and is fully convertible for eligible non-resident entities such as an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP). Current account transactions, including trade in goods and services and profit remittances, are unrestricted for non-resident designated entities. Capital account movements also face no exchange control approval once a valid 'non-resident' designation is obtained from the BMA. Dividend or income distributions by an ELP to non-resident partners are generally settled freely in BMD or foreign currency via an authorized dealer. No capital controls, stamp duties, or remittance taxes are imposed on outbound distributions, though statutory filing and annual government fees must be current.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For non-resident exempted partnerships and their non-resident partners, Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds. Such entities may retain foreign currency receipts in offshore, local, or external accounts without conversion obligations. For resident entities and individuals, the Exchange Control Act and Bermuda Monetary Authority Regulations require, unless expressly exempted, that any foreign currency received within a prescribed period (typically one month) be offered to an authorized dealer in exchange for BMD at the official pegged rate. Since the ELP is treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes, no statutory conversion ratio or timeline applies to its trade receivables from the manufacture and export of cosplay prop apparel.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions from UK or EU partners should be executed via SWIFT MT103 (or SEPA CT for EUR-denominated injections) directly to the Bermuda ELP's operational account at a tier-1 Bermuda bank. The payment purpose code should clearly state 'Contribution of Capital to Partnership' with supporting legal documentation referencing the LPA and capital call notice. For amounts exceeding USD 100,000, the originating bank normally requires an underlying source-of-funds statement and a certified copy of the partnership agreement. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File or Master File obligation for standard out-of-scope ELPs. Under the CIT Act 2023, an in-scope MNE group (consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750M, approximately BMD 1.1B) must maintain OECD-aligned contemporaneous documentation (Local File for Bermuda transactions, Master File, and Country-by-Country Report) and deliver it within 30 days of a Bermuda Revenue Authority notice.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply only to 'relevant activities'. Manufacturing and retail of cosplay props, theatrical costumes, and synthetic wigs (ISIC 3240 / NAICS 315280) is not a relevant activity; the ELP must nevertheless file an annual ESD declaring 'no relevant activity'. If the ELP also performs distribution, financing, or headquarters functions from Bermuda, it must satisfy full substance requirements: directed and managed in Bermuda, adequate physical office, qualified employees, and proportionate operating expenditure. A pure equity-holding ELP is subject only to reduced substance requirements (registered office and statutory records in Bermuda).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the CIT Act 2023: summary conviction fine up to BMD 250,000 and/or imprisonment up to 2 years; under the Economic Substance Regulations: fine of BMD 10,000 (first offence) / BMD 20,000 (subsequent); full restitution of underpaid CIT plus interest at 3% above the Bermuda Monetary Authority bank rate.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors of the corporate general partner are jointly and severally liable for restitution if they knew or ought to have known of the falsity; the Supreme Court may issue travel bans; conviction on indictment carries imprisonment up to 5 years and disqualification from acting as a Bermuda officer.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and deregistration by the Bermuda Registrar; loss of the Tax Exemption Certificate with retroactive effect; penalty of BMD 10,000 to BMD 20,000 per offence; the UK HMRC may treat the ELP as UK-resident, assessing corporation tax at 19%/25% on worldwide profits plus penalties up to 100% of the tax and interest; the IRS may apply CFC rules.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: general partners are personally liable for the ELP's debts if it traded while insolvent or while struck off; UK directors face personal liability under UK CTA 2010 s.1144 for tax unpaid due to dishonest conduct; the Bermuda court may order restitution from personal assets and restrict travel to Bermuda.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breaches

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018: criminal fine up to BMD 500,000 per contravention; licence revocation; forfeiture of laundered funds; underlying money laundering offences under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 carry imprisonment up to 14 years; merchant accounts may be permanently terminated by Stripe or Shopify.

Director personal liability: Directors, the Money Laundering Reporting Officer, and general partners may be prosecuted personally; sanctions include director disqualification for up to 10 years, personal fines, seizure of passports, and travel bans pending trial; registered agents and banks are contractually obliged to sever relationships immediately upon any adverse AML finding.
