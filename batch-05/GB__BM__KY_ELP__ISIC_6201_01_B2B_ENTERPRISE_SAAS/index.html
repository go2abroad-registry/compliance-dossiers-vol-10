Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance – Office of the Tax Commissioner; corporate registry portal: Bermuda Registrar of Companies (Department of Corporate Services)

E-government filing portal

Foreign exempted limited partnerships must register with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2018. Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, so no income tax registration is required. If the ELP has Bermuda employees, it must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Bermuda employment/payroll tax account. For FATCA/CRS purposes, a Bermuda financial institution (including an ELP that is a FFI) must obtain a GIIN and register with the Bermuda Tax Information Authority. No general VAT/TIN certificate exists.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Bermuda has no entity-level income tax and the ELP is fiscally transparent, with partners taxed in their respective residence jurisdictions.

Available election forms

Bermuda does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. An ELP may elect to be an exempted partnership under Bermuda law, and for foreign tax purposes, the partnership may elect classification in the partner's jurisdiction (e.g., U.S. IRS Form 8832). Such an election does not alter Bermuda domestic tax treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT/GST/sales tax; Bermuda imposes customs duties and payroll tax only

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no turnover-based VAT/GST registration regime exists. Importers must register with Bermuda Customs for duty assessment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT zero-rating regime exists in Bermuda. Exports of goods are outside the scope of Bermuda customs duty, and services supplied to non-residents are outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax; accordingly, no VAT zero-rating claim or refund mechanism applies.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (electronic declaration under BCAS)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice (customs-format)
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (where applicable)
  • Ship's Manifest / Cargo Movement Reference (for sea freight)

No VAT e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate. Import/export declarations are filed through the Bermuda Customs Automated System (BCAS); no digital stamping or API clearance is required.

Bermuda Customs may audit export declarations; false declarations or unlawful removal of goods attract penalties under the Customs Act 1941, including forfeiture, fines up to BD$5,000, and imprisonment. As no VAT export credit is claimed, no VAT clawback penalty applies. The 180-day FX repatriation deadline is not a Bermuda statutory requirement and is noted for group treasury policy only; non-compliance may trigger partner-level adjustments under foreign tax laws.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is freely convertible and pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar. Bermuda and the United Kingdom both maintain unrestricted current-account and capital-account convertibility; there is no exchange-control approval for foreign-currency deposits, capital contributions, or reinvestment. Profit remittance hurdles are limited to bank-level source-of-funds due diligence: distributions to foreign limited partners attract zero Bermudian withholding tax, zero UK withholding on partnership distributions, and no minimum retention period, provided the ELP is in good standing under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 and has satisfied the Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2018 obligations. Because the ELP is pass-through taxable and not a Bermuda taxpayer, no dividend-blocking, thin-capitalisation, or transfer-pricing clearance is engaged on repatriation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Neither Bermuda nor the United Kingdom imposes export-proceeds surrender. Bermuda: there is no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio; SaaS subscription and enterprise licence receipts denominated in USD, EUR, or GBP may be retained indefinitely in the original invoice currency and freely applied to operating expenses, FX hedging, or partner distributions. United Kingdom: all surrender obligations have been abolished since the Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed in 1979; no HM Treasury reporting is triggered by the ELP unless it becomes UK tax-resident through central management and control, in which case standard corporate reporting rules apply. The ELP must nevertheless maintain export contracts, invoices, and bank settlement records for AML/CDD purposes and to evidence compliance with Bermuda's economic substance regime.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) is the exchange-control and banking regulator for the Bermuda-domiciled ELP; UK-located treasury accounts and clearing rails are supervised by the Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) and the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). HM Treasury retains ultimate exchange-control policy competence for the United Kingdom. Neither authority requires prior licensing, approval, or reporting for the ELP's routine capital flows, FX conversions, or profit distributions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High operational viability for non-escrowed, day-to-day treasury flows; low viability as a sole or capital-escrow banking solution. FCA-authorised electronic money institutions (Wise B2B Pay UK Ltd, Airwallex (UK) Ltd, Currencycloud Ltd, and Revolut Business) onboard the ELP within 1-3 business days using the Bermuda certificate of exemption, the general partner resolution, and video KYC, and provide multi-currency virtual accounts (GBP, EUR, USD) with SEPA, Faster Payments, and SWIFT-out access. Legal constraint under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011: EMI balances are safeguarded client funds, not bank deposits, and carry no Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) protection; EMIs cannot issue Commercial Letters of Credit or supply independent escrow-certification for capital release conditions. The compliant topology is therefore: HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield holds capital escrow and reserved liquidity; one UK EMI is used for Stripe Billing payout sweeps and Wise B2B vendor disbursements, with nightly zero-balancing sweeps of the EMI float back to the Tier-1 Bermuda account.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Opaque offshore ownership chain: a foreign corporate general partner or a discretionary trust in the ownership waterfall triggers automatic higher-risk scoring.

Root cause: Opaque offshore ownership chain: a foreign corporate general partner or a discretionary trust in the ownership waterfall triggers automatic higher-risk scoring.

Defense evidence: (1) Current organisational chart from the ELP to each ultimate beneficial owner, signed by the general partner; (2) Bermuda certificate of exemption and notice of registration; (3) corporate registry extract of the general partner dated within 3 months; (4) trust deed excerpt and trustee resolution demonstrating the legal owner's authority; (5) audited or management accounts for the last 2 years evidencing genuine B2B SaaS operations; (6) statutory declaration by the general partner confirming investor identities and that no bearer shares or unrecorded transfers exist; (7) tax counsel letter confirming the ELP's pass-through status and non-tax-resident classification in Bermuda.
Zero-tax offshore regime tag: the Bermuda ELP exempt status triggers negative news screening and enhanced due diligence for tax-evasion risk.

Root cause: Zero-tax offshore regime tag: the Bermuda ELP exempt status triggers negative news screening and enhanced due diligence for tax-evasion risk.

Defense evidence: (1) Bermuda's membership of the OECD Inclusive Framework on BEPS and its largely compliant OECD Global Forum peer-review rating; (2) Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2018 declaration and compliance certificate; (3) licensed tax counsel opinion that the ELP is tax-transparent and all underlying partners are taxed on the income in their residence jurisdictions; (4) signed enterprise master subscription agreements and commercial invoices demonstrating arm's-length SaaS activity; (5) regulatory statement that ISIC 6201 software development requires no statutory licence; (6) bank screening snapshots showing no EU, UK, OFAC, or UN sanctions and no adverse media matches.
Source-of-funds provenance: international equity injections from multiple investor jurisdictions with no pre-existing bank settlement history are flagged for unexplained wealth.

Root cause: Source-of-funds provenance: international equity injections from multiple investor jurisdictions with no pre-existing bank settlement history are flagged for unexplained wealth.

Defense evidence: (1) Executed capital-contribution agreements or subscription side letters for each investor; (2) three to six months of bank statements from the remitting investor accounts; (3) SWIFT MT103 or Fedwire settlement records with the payment narrative labelled 'equity contribution'; (4) passport and residency evidence for every contributor; (5) independent accountant's source-of-wealth certificate where aggregate funding exceeds the bank's enhanced due diligence threshold; (6) the ELP capital-account ledger reconciling each contribution to the corresponding bank credit; (7) written confirmation that the funds are not loan proceeds.
