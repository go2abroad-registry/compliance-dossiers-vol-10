Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance, comprising the Registrar of Companies (entity registration under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014), the Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax, land tax, stamp duties), the Collector of Customs / Bermuda Customs Service (customs duties), and the Corporate Income Tax Agency (CITA) administering the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for the OECD Pillar Two regime. Filing and registration portal: Bermuda OneGov portal (portal.gov.bm) plus the CITA e-filing portal for CIT/GloBE filings.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with the Registrar of Companies and obtain the Certificate of Registration, the company register number, and the exempted undertaking certificate under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014; the register number serves as the Bermuda entity identifier / TIN-equivalent. Step 2: If the ELP employs Bermuda-resident staff or occupies Bermuda premises, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number; otherwise no payroll tax registration is required. Step 3: If the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million, apply for CIT registration through CITA and obtain a CIT reference number for the GloBE / Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax regime. Step 4: No VAT/GST or general income-tax TIN is issued because Bermuda has no VAT, GST, income tax, or capital gains tax for exempted undertakings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

(i) US check-the-box: as a foreign eligible entity, the Bermuda ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to affirm partnership/transparent status or elect corporate/opaque status for US federal tax purposes. (ii) Bermuda CIT/GloBE: elections under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 include the Substance-Based Income Exclusion, the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax safe harbour, and the transitional Country-by-Country Reporting safe-harbour election. (iii) UK hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010, Part 6A): UK-resident partners must neutralize any deduction/non-inclusion mismatch arising from Bermuda/UK classification differences; mismatch-reduction and structured-arrangement exclusion elections are available in narrow circumstances. (iv) Bermuda exempted-undertaking status exempting the ELP from local income tax and providing an exemption from specified local stamp duties and exchange-control restrictions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax in Bermuda; indirect taxes consist solely of Bermuda Customs Duties on imported goods (Bermuda Tariff Schedule) and stamp duties on specified transactions/documents

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Import customs duty is assessed on each consignment at clearance and is not subject to an economic turnover registration threshold. For the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the registration threshold is MNE group consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million, Bermuda dollar at parity with USD).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: Bermuda levies no VAT/GST, so no export zero-rating mechanism and no input-VAT refund mechanism exists. Goods exported from Bermuda are outside the scope of the Customs Tariff and attract no export duty. For CIT purposes, export revenue from manufacturing or trading may be subject to the 15% Bermuda CIT only if the ELP is a constituent entity of an in-scope MNE group; the Substance-Based Income Exclusion applies to routine returns, and no indirect-tax refund is available.

No electronic-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate applies to indirect tax. Import declarations and duty payments are filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs automated clearance system (eClearance). The CIT regime requires digital registration, top-up tax returns, and notifications through the CITA e-portal; no digital stamping or e-invoice clearance applies to commercial sales.

Bermuda has no VAT export-fraud, zero-rating clawback, or export-subsidy regime, and no statutory FX-repatriation deadline. False or misleading export declarations under the Customs Act 1948 (as amended) attract fines, forfeiture of goods, and possible imprisonment at the discretion of the courts. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and CITA regulations, late CIT registration or filing triggers administrative penalties plus statutory interest on unpaid top-up tax, and CITA may reassess top-up tax attributable to the ELP's UK or foreign partners under GloBE rules. HMRC may separately apply hybrid-mismatch counteractions against UK-resident partners under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A, with assessments and penalties under UK tax legislation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD) under a currency-board arrangement, and USD is legal tender. Current-account transactions are fully convertible with no approvals. Capital-account transactions are likewise free for any entity designated non-resident, which is the mandatory and automatic status for an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) holding foreign business. Profit remittance hurdles are nil: Bermuda imposes no withholding taxes on dividends, interest, management fees or partnership distributions, and funds may be repatriated to LPs or onward-lent to the UK operating subsidiary without BMA pre-authorisation. The only formalities are the bank's quarterly exchange-control return and the ELP's annual declaration to the Registrar of Companies. Practical caution: if the ELP's central management and control were to sit in the UK, UK corporation tax and FCA payment oversight would apply, but no convertibility restriction would arise.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda legislation imposes no mandatory surrender, no minimum conversion ratio and no statutory time limit for holding export receipts. A non-resident ELP may retain 100% of proceeds from the sale of laparoscopy towers and components, in BMD, USD, GBP or EUR, and may defer conversion indefinitely for working-capital needs. Record-keeping obligations apply: export invoices, bills of lading and shipping documents must be preserved for six years for the BMA annual return and the Economic Substance filing. The UK manufacturing subsidiary is likewise free of surrender requirements, as the United Kingdom abolished exchange controls in 1979.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the sole central bank, currency-board operator and exchange-control administrator under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972 as extended to Bermuda. Ultimate sovereign monetary oversight rests with the Bank of England, Bermuda being a British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM. Bermuda has no standalone electronic-money licence under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018; an e-money issuance business must obtain a deposit-taking endorsement or be licensed as a money-service business by the BMA, which entails the same prudential capital and AML/CFT burden as a bank. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Platform, Airwallex, Revolut Business) are legally usable by the UK subsidiary for high-velocity GBP/EUR operational payables, but they cannot serve as the primary treasury account of the Bermuda ELP because the BMA exchange-control regime requires the partnership's capital and specified assets to be held with a licensed Bermudian financial institution. Optimal structure: maintain the Bermuda ELP's capital and treasury accounts with HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield; open an FCA-regulated EMI account in the UK for daily trade payables; and restrict the EMI to flows mirror-funded from the Bermuda treasury account to avoid commingling.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital calls must be issued by the General Partner at least 10 business days before the value date; each LP remits by SWIFT MT103 from its own account in its own name, with no third-party funding. The remittance narrative must carry the ELP registration number (LP2025-XXXX), the capital-call serial number (CC-001) and the BMA non-resident designation reference; the Bermudian receiving bank matches the amount and LP identity against the call notice, and any deviation triggers enhanced source-of-funds enquiries. ISO 20022 purpose code CNCB (contribution to a non-financial entity) is preferred. For GBP-denominated contributions from UK-based LPs, the UK remitting bank applies statistical purpose code 4600 (overseas direct investment) or M1 (investment loans) only for BoP statistics, requiring no Bank of England approval; funds are credited to the Bermuda capital escrow account in BMD at the currency-board rate (1:1 USD), converted from GBP at the bank's prevailing commercial mid-rate documented by a dated FX dealing ticket.Two-tier clearing design. Tier 1: SWIFT MT103 / TT Wire for trade payables below GBP 200,000, covering component semiconductors, optical lenses, high-grade stainless steel, cleanroom consumables, freight and CE-mark certification; settlement rails are CHAPS for GBP, Fedwire/CHIPS for USD and SEPA Instant for EUR, with instructions submitted before 13:00 Hamilton time for same-day value. Tier 2: irrevocable documentary Letters of Credit (UCP600, SWIFT MT700/MT707) for capital expenditures equal to or above GBP 200,000, such as Japanese optical grinding machines and German laser welding cells; the beneficiary must present a commercial invoice, full-set clean on-board bills of lading, packing list, SGS inspection certificate, certificate of origin and the UK MHRA/export-control licence reference. Charges are shared (SWIFT Field 71F set to SHA). Every instruction must concatenate the project key ISIC_3250_06_LPTWR and the purchase-order number in SWIFT Field 70 to enable line-item reconciliation in the ERP.Deliverable vanilla FX forwards (USD/GBP, USD/EUR) with tenors of 1 to 24 months, documented under an ISDA Master Agreement and Credit Support Annex with the Bermudian bank, used to lock gross margin on UK manufacturing costs and EU distributor receipts.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for restricted-currency trade payables (CNH, INR, IDR) arising from Asian component procurement; permissible because the BMA imposes no prohibition on currency derivatives and the bank operates an NDF desk out of Hong Kong, with settlement in USD/BMD.Cross-currency swaps (USD-to-GBP and USD-to-EUR, fixed-to-floating) used to convert USD-denominated LP capital into long-term sterling funding for the UK operating subsidiary without spot conversion risk; execution requires the bank's treasury to hold the relevant permitted derivative activities under its BMA banking licence and the ELP to be assessed as a professional counterparty under the ISDA 2002 archetype.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: MEDIUM. Bermuda has no standalone electronic-money licence under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018; an e-money issuance business must obtain a deposit-taking endorsement or be licensed as a money-service business by the BMA, which entails the same prudential capital and AML/CFT burden as a bank. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Platform, Airwallex, Revolut Business) are legally usable by the UK subsidiary for high-velocity GBP/EUR operational payables, but they cannot serve as the primary treasury account of the Bermuda ELP because the BMA exchange-control regime requires the partnership's capital and specified assets to be held with a licensed Bermudian financial institution. Optimal structure: maintain the Bermuda ELP's capital and treasury accounts with HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield; open an FCA-regulated EMI account in the UK for daily trade payables; and restrict the EMI to flows mirror-funded from the Bermuda treasury account to avoid commingling.
