Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda: Ministry of Finance, Office of the Tax Commissioner (www.gov.bm); UK: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) for VAT and indirect tax purposes.

E-government filing portal

The exempted limited partnership (ELP) is registered with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; no Bermuda income tax or VAT registration number is required because Bermuda does not impose income tax or VAT. If the ELP makes taxable supplies in the UK (or has a UK fixed establishment), it must register for UK VAT using the non-established taxable person procedure via HMRC (Form VAT1 or online Government Gateway), obtaining a UK VAT registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible – partnership is fiscally transparent in Bermuda and, generally, for UK tax purposes; Bermuda imposes no entity-level income tax; territorial scope applies so only UK-connected or Bermudian-source income is potentially taxable, and non-resident partners are not subject to Bermudian income tax.

Available election forms

No UK check-the-box election exists; transparency is determined by foreign law and UK case law. Bermuda does not levy entity-level income tax on partnerships. For UK anti-hybrid and CFC purposes, the ELP may be treated as transparent, and any relevant election under UK CTA 2010 / TIOPA 2010 (e.g., hybrid mismatch elections) can be made by the partnership or partners, where applicable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Non-UK established person: £0 (mandatory registration from first taxable UK supply); UK-established person: £85,000 annual taxable turnover threshold. Bermuda: N/A (no VAT registration regime).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to direct exports of goods from the UK to a destination outside the UK and to certain services supplied to overseas customers where the place of supply is outside the UK. The ELP must obtain and preserve valid evidence of export (usually within three months of the time of supply) and submit that evidence with its VAT return to substantiate the 0% rate.

  • UK Customs Export Declaration (SAD/C21 or electronic exit summary declaration)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / consignment note
  • Commercial invoice and purchase order
  • Proof of payment / bank transfer, and inward remittance certificate where relevant
  • Packing list and freight forwarder's certificate

UK VAT: Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires digital records and API-based submission of VAT returns; no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate. Bermuda: electronic customs declaration via the Bermuda Customs Service EDI system may be required for import/export documentation, but no VAT clearance is applicable.

No statutory UK FX repatriation deadline; 180 days is the customary validation benchmark for export-evidence documentation. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims may trigger HMRC assessments, penalties up to 100% of the VAT evaded, interest, criminal prosecution, and possible seizure of goods. In Bermuda, false customs declarations expose the ELP to civil penalties, forfeiture, and other sanctions under the Customs Act.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is freely convertible at a fixed 1:1 parity with USD and both currencies circulate interchangeably. For a Bermuda exempted limited partnership designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972, current-account trade payments and capital-account transactions (equity injections, partner loans, distributions and profit remittances) are freely permitted without BMA consent. No Bermudian income tax, withholding tax or remittance tax applies to an ELP, so profit repatriation encounters no fiscal hurdle; the practical preconditions are solely bank-level AML/ATF/KYC verification, economic substance documentation and sanctions screening clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rule, mandatory conversion timeline or conversion ratio applies to a non-resident Bermuda exempted partnership. Export receipts in USD, GBP or EUR may be retained in external bank accounts without surrender to the BMA or any authorised dealer. Where the ELP had not previously secured formal non-resident exchange-control status, the onboarding package must include a BMA non-resident status letter to prevent the application of the resident exchange-control regime.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) in its central bank and monetary authority role, with the Bank of England participating only for GBP wholesale clearing and settlement via CHAPS; the BMA administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 and issues the Bermuda dollar (BMD), which is pegged at par to the United States dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplementary collection/payment layer but Low as the sole treasury rail. UK-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Business, Payoneer, Airwallex and Revolut Business can open multi-currency virtual accounts for a Bermuda ELP and execute low-value supplier disbursements and customer receipts. Legal constraints are material: they are not Bermuda-licensed banks under the Banking Act 1998, cannot provide commercial credit, trade finance or letters of credit, hold client funds under safeguarding rather than deposit-protection arrangements, apply enhanced EDD to non-resident partnerships due to no physical premises, and may reject payments involving industrial hardware unless the commercial substance file is complete. The Stage 4 treasury operating model must therefore keep the primary clearing account at a Bermuda licensed bank and use an EMI only for separately funded, ancillary FX optimisation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from the UK or group treasury must be executed by SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer from the sender's UK bank to the Bermuda beneficiary bank using the beneficiary BIC and account number (Bermuda has no IBAN). Use SWIFT payment purpose code 'CAPC' or the bank-mandated capital-contribution code and include the narrative 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - KY_ELP / GB__BM__KY_ELP__ISIC_4791_23_INDUSTRIAL_MRO_SUPPLIES'. USD inbound should be routed through a correspondent in New York (CHIPS/Fedwire); GBP inbound should use CHAPS to the Bermuda bank's London correspondent; EUR inbound should use TARGET2 via the Bermuda bank's euro correspondent. The bank requires the underlying capital introduction form, source-of-funds evidence and tax substance statement to credit the capital escrow account rather than the operational current account.Supplier settlements for industrial MRO inventory should use an MT103 SWIFT wire for ordinary disbursements and a commercial letter of credit issued by the Bermuda bank by SWIFT MT700 for larger or credit-backed purchases, governed by UCP 600 and ISBP 745. USD clearing runs through CHIPS/Fedwire, GBP through CHAPS, and EUR through TARGET2; for MRO customer card receipts, the merchant acquirer settles net card proceeds by MT103 directly into the Bermuda operational account. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Bermuda exempted partnership; 15% CIT for in-scope large multinational enterprise groups under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (global group revenue EUR 750m or more).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda does not impose withholding tax on dividends or partnership distributions from an exempted partnership.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The CIT return must be filed within 12 months after the end of the reporting period. For a fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025, the filing deadline is 31 December 2026 unless extended by the Tax Commissioner.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda has not enacted stand-alone domestic transfer pricing thresholds for exempted partnerships; in-scope CIT groups must maintain OECD-compliant Master File and Local File documentation where related-party transaction turnover exceeds BMD 10,000,000 in a reporting period, and must make documentation available within 45 days upon written request from the Tax Commissioner.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to exempted partnerships carrying on a relevant activity. Electronic catalogue retail of industrial MRO supplies may constitute a 'distribution and service centre' activity if the partnership physically distributes goods or provides services to third parties in Bermuda. The partnership must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Bermuda, including local management of distribution contracts, appropriate qualified employees, physical premises, and annual economic substance reporting. A pure equity holding classification is not available where active catalogue sales and logistics are undertaken; passive holding status only applies to entities that solely hold equity participations and earn only dividends or capital gains.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent CIT Reporting

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the tax understated, plus statutory interest; criminal tax fraud carries a fine of up to BMD 10,000,000 and imprisonment for up to 8 years under Bermuda tax administration and criminal law.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, and designated managers who knowingly participate in fraudulent filing are jointly and severally liable for the entity's unpaid tax; travel bans and passport surrender may be imposed during investigation; conviction may lead to custodial sentences and disqualification from managing Bermuda entities.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Economic Substance Authority may impose a first penalty of BMD 25,000; continued non-compliance may attract a second penalty up to BMD 100,000 and the Registrar may strike the partnership off the register. Findings of non-compliance are shared with HMRC and foreign tax authorities, exposing the group to taxation and penalties in the UK and other jurisdictions.

Director personal liability: General partners of the ELP and directors of a corporate general partner face personal liability for unpaid penalties; courts may issue disqualification orders of up to 5 years, asset seizure, and travel bans for failure to comply with economic substance investigations.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: Under the Beneficial Ownership Act 2017 and Bermuda AML/ATF code, penalties reach BMD 100,000 plus BMD 5,000 per day for continuing default; money laundering offences under the Criminal Proceeds Recovery Act may trigger confiscation orders covering the full value of assets laundered.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, beneficial owners, and general partners face imprisonment of up to 5 years for knowingly failing to comply with beneficial ownership obligations; personal financial restitution orders, asset forfeiture, and travel restrictions may be imposed pending trial and post-conviction.
