E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), being non-UK-established, obtains a UK VAT registration number by submitting form VAT 1 through the online non-established person's registration route via the Government Gateway, supported by the Bermuda Certificate of Exempted Partnership, partnership deed, general partner identity documentation, financial forecasts, and digital store contracts with Valve/PlayStation. UK VAT registration is mandatory without turnover threshold for non-UK-established persons whenever taxable supplies are made to UK consumers; if all UK B2C retail sales are routed through Steam or PlayStation Store, the marketplace operator is the deemed supplier and the ELP need only retain the marketplace settlement agreements and service contracts. A UK Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) must be obtained only if the ELP creates a UK permanent establishment (employees, office, or UK-based agency negotiation activity) by filing form CT1 for Corporation Tax; alternatively, Form 64-8 authorises a UK tax agent to act for the non-resident ELP under HMRC's non-resident business procedures.

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No UK equivalent of the US check-the-box election exists. The Bermuda ELP is automatically transparent for UK direct tax under partnership treatment (ITTOIA 2005 and FTA 2008) unless the anti-hybrid rules at TIOPA 2010 Part 6A recharacterise the entity in the presence of a hybrid mismatch yielding double deduction or deduction without inclusion. Available elections include: (i) voluntary UK VAT registration for non-established persons even below the £90,000 threshold where recovery of UK input VAT on marketing and operating costs is sought (VATA 1994 Sch 1 para 10); (ii) annual accounting and flat-rate schemes for VAT (only once UK-established); and (iii) the EU non-Union One Stop Shop (OSS) route in a selected EU Member State for B2C electronic supplies to EU consumers after Brexit.

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 annual taxable turnover for UK-established businesses; £0 (no threshold) for non-UK-established persons such as the Bermuda ELP, with a transaction-channel caveat: marketplace deemed-supplier rules (VATA 1994 Sch 9A) shift UK VAT registration and compliance obligations to Steam/PlayStation Store for UK retail digital sales.

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping are mandated in the UK. HMRC enforces Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT: registered businesses must maintain digital records and file VAT returns through MTD-compatible software using HMRC Application Programming Interfaces (APIs). E-invoicing remains voluntary, although HMRC post-2024 consultation signals a future Peppol-based B2B e-invoicing mandate; until enacted, only substantive VAT invoice content, tax point timing, and six-year record retention rules under the Value Added Tax Regulations 1995 apply, with platform transactions evidenced by marketplace settlement statements rather than individual invoices.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD) and is fully convertible. For a Bermuda exempted limited partnership (ELP) designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act, both current-account transactions (Steam and PlayStation Store royalty receipts, platform fees, intercompany royalties) and capital-account transactions (partner capital subscriptions, shareholder loans, profit distributions) are unrestricted and may be denominated in USD, GBP, EUR, or any foreign currency without prior BMA approval. The ELP must obtain a formal non-resident designation through its licensed Bermuda corporate services provider; this designation removes all exchange-control restrictions applicable to resident entities. Profit remittance to non-resident partners is effected by a general partner distribution resolution with no ministerial consent, no withholding tax, and no requirement to convert proceeds into BMD. A caveat applies to the Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018: an ELP engaged in digital game publishing may be classified as an intellectual property business or pure equity holding entity for substance purposes, and the annual substance filing must evidence core income-generating activities (strategic publishing decisions, contract negotiation, platform management) directed and managed in Bermuda, or the bank will consider the account compliance profile unsatisfactory.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rules apply to a non-resident designated ELP. Steam/Valve and PlayStation Store remittances may be retained in full in USD or any foreign currency with no required conversion ratio and no statutory timeline. Bermuda's Exchange Control Regulations contain no surrender obligation for exports of services or digital goods; resident-designated entities only owe a reporting duty to the BMA via Form FCR-1, and non-resident designated entities owe no reporting duty beyond standard transaction monitoring under the AML/ATF regime.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, and the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 1998, as amended.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All capital injections must be routed via SWIFT MT103 (never MT202 cover) from each partner's own bank to the ELP's USD escrow account at Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda. The MT103 field 70 remittance information must state: Capital contribution to Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership, non-resident designation reference [BMA ref], and the specific capital call clause of the limited partnership agreement. For GBP-origin capital from the UK, the receiving Bermuda bank converts at the BMD/USD parity with no UK exchange control approval required; for EUR-origin capital, conversion is executed at the bank's treasury desk. For PRC-origin capital, the remitting Chinese bank must hold a SAFE ODI registration and the MT103 must include outward direct investment purpose code 621011; the beneficiary bank will verify that the remitter name in the MT103 matches the registered partner schedule. Recommended structure: maintain the primary settlement account at Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda and use a UK FCA-regulated EMI solely for low-value GBP developer payments after the Bermuda bank has approved the counterparty list.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 – all intercompany transactions of a Bermuda ELP engaged in intellectual property or digital publishing activities must be supported by contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation to satisfy the Economic Substance Regulations and U.K. HMRC's global alignment requirements.

Economic substance scope: Under the International Tax Cooperation (Economic Substance) Act 2020, a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership conducting a relevant activity, including intellectual property business, must demonstrate adequate physical presence, qualified full-time employees, and direction and management in Bermuda. The ELP is required to file an Economic Substance Return annually and may be subject to a rebuttable presumption of being a pure holding entity if it derives income solely from Steam or PlayStation store of IP-related games. Failure to satisfy the economic substance test exposes the ELP to corrective actions, financial penalties, and mandatory strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Filing Default

Statutory sanction: Daily penalty of BMD 50 per day up to BMD 1,000 for the annual declaration; for the ESR, fines up to BMD 250,000 and potential deregistration with restoration fees of BMD 500.

Director personal liability: The general partner is automatically liable for all penalties; limited partners who knowingly participate in the omission lose limited liability and may be jointly liable up to BMD 1,000,000.
Shell Company / Inadequate Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Corrective directions from the Tax Administration; financial penalty of BMD 250,000; for intentional evasion, a penalty up to BMD 750,000 and imprisonment for 2 years.

Director personal liability: General partners may be disqualified as partnership managers; severe cases trigger travel bans, asset freezes, and personal civil restitution for taxes evaded in the United Kingdom or other fiscal domains.
AML / KYC Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's AML/ATF Act 2018, penalties of up to BMD 1,000,000 per violation; revocation of the partnership's exempted status; custodial sentence up to 5 years for concealment of criminal property.

Director personal liability: All general partners and compliance officers are personally liable; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed pending investigation; for regulated payment flows, the resident agent may also be sanctioned.
Fraud and False Representation

Statutory sanction: Prosecution in the Supreme Court of Bermuda on indictment; unlimited financial penalties; restitution orders for underpaid taxes; imprisonment for up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil is automatic: general partners are personally liable for full restitution of evaded taxes; court-mandated travel bans and passport seizures may be applied; limited partners who knowingly participate are treated as general partners for liability purposes.
