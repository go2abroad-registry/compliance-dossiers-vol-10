Tax ID Application Infrastructure

E-government filing portal

Engage a Bermuda-licensed agent and resident registered office; file with the Registrar of Companies an application under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 to obtain the EP registration number (the Bermuda identifier; no separate income-tax TIN is issued). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. The Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent under Bermuda law and is not itself chargeable to Bermuda income tax; partners are taxed only on Bermuda-source trading income, which is absent in a pure digital export model. The ELP enjoys statutory protection from future income and capital taxes until 31 March 2035 under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. For UK tax purposes the ELP is transparent under general entity-classification principles, so UK-resident partners are assessed on their profit shares; Bermuda does not impose worldwide income tax and the digital publishing activity is treated as performed and sourced outside Bermuda.

Available election forms

US check-the-box: the ELP, being an eligible entity, may file Form 8832 to elect partnership (default) or corporate status for U.S. federal tax. UK: no transparency election is available; HMRC classifies the entity factually, and any jurisdiction mismatch (e.g., US corporate election versus UK transparency) is policed by the UK hybrid and other mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A), which do not permit elective hybrid treatment. Under CITA 2023, in-scope MNE groups may elect a consolidated calculation basis and make transitional attribute elections in the first CIT filing period, but these are irrelevant for a micro digital media ELP below the EUR 750 million threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; indirect levies are Customs Duties on physical imports and employer-side Payroll Tax on Bermuda-sourced remuneration. UK: value added tax (VAT) is the operative indirect tax on digital magazine portal sales to UK consumers, with the place of supply fixed at the customer's usual country of residence.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST is imposed; Payroll Tax registration is triggered by the first payment of Bermuda-sourced remuneration (no de minimis exemption since 2011). UK: non-established businesses supplying digital services to UK consumers have a zero statutory VAT threshold and must register from the first UK supply; the domestic GBP 90,000 turnover registration threshold applies only to UK-established businesses. VOSS filings are made quarterly.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: export earnings from digital magazine subscriptions and sponsored media placements are outside any Bermuda indirect tax, so no zero-rating claim is required. UK: where the ELP is UK VAT-registered, zero-rating (or outside-the-scope treatment) applies to B2B and B2C supplies of digital content made to customers outside the UK, provided the supplier holds and relies on two non-conflicting pieces of commercial evidence of customer location (e.g., billing address, IP address, card BIN country, bank account country, telephone country code). Supplies to UK consumers remain standard-rated at 20% and are reported through VOSS; absence of sufficient evidence converts the supply into a UK taxable supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration: HMRC export declaration (entry) for physical goods; for digital services the equivalent is the merchant log of download/sale transactions plus the VOSS return position.
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill: applicable only to physical distribution; for a digital magazine portal, substitute the B2B service contract, delivery receipts and digital usage logs.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate: bank or EMI payment settlement proof (Stripe/AdSense payout statements, Swift or Nostro credit advices) evidencing receipt of export proceeds into the ELP's Bermuda or UK account.

Bermuda imposes no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping requirement. The UK does not mandate real-time clearance or e-invoicing; however, all UK VAT-registered businesses above the GBP 90,000 threshold, including non-established filers, must keep digital records and submit returns through MTD-compatible software via HMRC APIs, or alternatively account through VOSS. Stripe and AdSense payout flows are not subject to any fiscal clearance; ordinary commercial records and payment statements suffice.

Bermuda: the Tax Commissioner may revoke or modify the tax protection certificate on material misrepresentation or breach of the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, retroactively exposing the ELP to Bermuda income and capital taxes; non-compliance with the Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2018 attracts administrative fines, daily default penalties and potential strike-off. UK: HMRC may raise protective VAT assessments for up to 4 years (20 years for deliberate concealment), charge interest, and impose penalties under Schedule 24 FA 2007, ranging from 30% for careless errors to 70% for deliberate and 100% for deliberate and concealed errors; clawback of zero-rated treatment and VOSS refunds occurs where location evidence is fabricated, missing, or unreliable, and intentional abuse may lead to criminal VAT sanctions and removal from VOSS.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP, USD, BMD and KYD are freely convertible. The UK, Bermuda and Cayman maintain no current-account or capital-account exchange controls and observe IMF Article VIII. BMD is hard-pegged to USD at 1:1; KYD is pegged at 1 KYD = USD 1.20. Digital-export receipts (AdSense/Stripe) may be credited in USD or GBP to ELP accounts and converted without regulatory approval. Profit remittance: distributions from the Cayman ELP to the UK LP are unrestricted and free of UK withholding because the ELP is a transparent partnership for UK tax purposes; the only friction is bank-level source-of-funds and sanctions screening plus FATCA/CRS filing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender, conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline applies to export proceeds in the United Kingdom, Bermuda, or the Cayman Islands. AdSense and Stripe USD/GBP receipts may be held offshore indefinitely in the ELP's accounts. Banks apply only monitoring declarations under the Proceeds of Crime Act and AML supervisory requirements; BMA and CIMA impose no statutory conversion of digital-service export earnings.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (United Kingdom monetary authority); Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) (Bermuda jurisdiction regulator); Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) (Cayman Islands regulator under the Monetary Authority Act (2020 Revision) and Currency Law (2021 Revision)); HM Treasury (UK financial sanctions administration). No exchange-control licence is required for non-resident business in any of the three territories.

USD regional payments may use Fedwire/ACH via the Butterfield ABA; GBP route via HSBC Bermuda's UK clearing (CHAPS/Faster Payments) with settlement at Bank of England; EUR via TARGET2/SEPA through a euro correspondent. Commercial documentary credits (L/C) are not appropriate for ISIC 5813 digital services and are rejected; contracts are settled open-account against invoices. Royalty payments use purpose code 'ROYL' to reduce bank friction; net sweeps to the UK collection vehicle occur periodically post-VAT/OSS return.FX Forward (deliverable) — USD/GBP and USD/EUR tenors up to 24 months with Butterfield Cayman or HSBC Bermuda under the bank's ISDA 2002 Master Agreement; cash-collateral CSA required for the ELP.Cross-Currency Swap — GBP/USD fixed-for-floating under ISDA 2002 with CSA; permitted for hedging USD ad-receipts against GBP partnership distributions.FX Swap (simultaneous spot and forward) — 1–6 month tenors for working-capital conversion; no premium.Vanilla European FX Option (long-only) — premium-paid zero-cost collar; permitted with BMA/CIMA-licensed counterparties; speculative short options are prohibited by the ELP's treasury policy.Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) — NOT permissible/available for USD, GBP, EUR, BMD or KYD because all are fully convertible and deliverable; NDFs would apply only to restricted currencies (CNY/INR/KRW) outside the business scope.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Rating: LOW (2/10) for the Cayman ELP as a direct onboarding client. UK EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Tide, Mettle, Airwallex, Currenxie) and Bermudian EMI-type licensees require locally incorporated counterparties; none will underwrite a non-resident exempted limited partnership. Legal constraints: FCA EMR 2011 safeguarding and client-money rules, EMI settlement-bank (ClearBank/Modulr) de-risking of offshore passthrough entities, and CIMA/BMA licensing which does not recognise foreign ELPs as EMI customers. Stripe and Google AdSense likewise do not support Cayman-resident payees; a UK-resident collection company (wholly-owned UK Ltd) is mandatory to contract Stripe and AdSense, and it remits net receipts to the ELP by MT103. Fallback treasury solution: Butterfield Cayman USD account with Fedwire/ABA clearing, plus HSBC Bermuda GBP account for UK clearing.
