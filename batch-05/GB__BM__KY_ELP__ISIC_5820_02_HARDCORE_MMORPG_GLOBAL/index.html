Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner — Bermuda Tax Administration (BermudaTA), official portal: taxcommissioner.gov.bm; Bermuda Customs Department (ASYCUDA World) for customs duties; Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance, for Exempted Limited Partnership registration and annual declarations.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda maintains no traditional TIN registry because it is a zero-tax, no-VAT jurisdiction for offshore structures. A foreign-owned ELP obtains its statutory identity by: (1) filing an application under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 (EPA 1992) with the Registrar of Companies and paying the annual government fee, obtaining a Certificate of Exempted Partnership (this certificate number functions as the corporate identifier; no VAT ID exists); (2) maintaining a registered office in Bermuda and a Bermuda-resident person authorized to accept service of process; (3) filing the annual EPA declaration with the Registrar; (4) registering separately with BermudaTA only for Payroll Tax (if staff are Bermuda-resident) and acquiring a customs importer code only if physical goods are imported; and (5) obtaining a Bermuda Tax Reference Number for CIT registration solely if the wider MNE group is in scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2025 (consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in either of the two preceding fiscal years). Platform payments from Apple App Store and Google Play can be collected in Bermuda bank accounts without any VAT or withholding tax registration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, sales tax, or digital services tax; the only indirect-type taxes are customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1974 (standard rate approximately 22.5% on most physical merchandise) and a hotel-occupancy Tourist Tax. UK VAT (20%) does not extend to the British Overseas Territory of Bermuda and creates no registration nexus for this ELP absent independent UK taxable supplies.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No indirect-tax turnover threshold exists. Customs registration is required only for persons physically importing goods into Bermuda via the ASYCUDA World importer code. For CIT only: in-scope multinational groups with consolidated global revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approx. USD 800 million) in either of the two preceding fiscal years must register with BermudaTA within the Corporate Income Tax Act 2025 deadlines.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT concept: with no VAT, there is no 0% export rate, no input-VAT refund claim, and no offshore-services zero-rating procedure. Structural substitute treatment for the global hardcore MMORPG model: (i) digital content delivered through app stores is outside all Bermudian consumption tax and requires no export declaration; (ii) any physical goods (e.g., limited-edition merchandise) exported from Bermuda attract no export duty and are evidenced only for the destination jurisdiction's customs; (iii) an ELP with a real Bermuda office can claim customs duty concessions on imported development hardware/software used exclusively for the qualifying international business.

  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing export movement
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bermuda Chamber of Commerce Certificate of Origin where required by the destination customs authority
  • Inward remittance or platform payout evidence (Apple App Store/Google Play settlement statements, SWIFT MT103) as sub-statutory proof of consideration for economic substance or group audit purposes

No continuous-transaction-control e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory tax-API regime exists in Bermuda. Import/export declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World (UN/EDIFACT CUSP messages). MMORPG in-app purchase receipts from Apple App Store and Google Play require no local e-invoice, fiscal stamp, or clearance because the digital supply has no Bermudian consumption-tax nexus.

No VAT clawback is possible because no VAT is charged. Customs duty evasion under the Customs Tariff Act 1974 is punishable by seizure and forfeiture of goods, fines of up to three times the duty evaded, and imprisonment; customs duty concessions are retrospectively clawed back with interest if the goods are diverted or the qualifying status ceases. For CIT: late or inaccurate BermudaTA filings attract interest and penalties under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2025, and an in-scope ELP failing to pay the local QDMTT exposes the wider group to top-up tax abroad under the GloBE IIR/UTPR rules. Continuing non-compliance can result in the Registrar of Companies striking the ELP off the exempted register or revoking its exempted status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 and both currencies circulate interchangeably in the domestic economy. Bermuda maintains exchange control under the Exchange Control Act 1972, but the regime is administered with a liberal posture: payments for current account transactions, including dividends, profit remittances, and trade-related disbursements, receive routine approval where the underlying transaction is evidenced and compliant with BMA reporting thresholds. The convertibility risk for an Exempted Limited Partnership is not currency inconvertibility but regulatory timing: certain transactions require prior notification or post-event reporting, and funds routed via the United Kingdom may be subject to the UK's retained sanctions and Counter-Terrorism sanctions screening. Capital account transactions, especially capital injections above the BMA or UK reporting thresholds, may require a Regulated Activity or Financial Sanctions assessment but are otherwise freely convertible. Repatriation of profits by a Bermuda ELP with non-Bermuda partners is permissible provided the partnership is not resident for Bermuda tax purposes on the relevant basis, or if it is tax-resident in another jurisdiction with acceptable treaty or economic substance posture, and the distribution is supported by audited or accountants' certificates evidencing available profits. Practical hurdle: US-dollar finality and UK correspondent clearing require sound counterparty due diligence; no material convertibility restriction is expected for a hardcore MMORPG in-app-purchase business, which is a digital services revenue stream with no physical Bermudian market nexus.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement in Bermuda for exporters to surrender foreign currency receipts to the central bank or any monetary authority; Bermuda is not an EEA member state and does not operate a non-resident convertibility or surrender regime akin to PRC or other Asian exchange-control frameworks. Bermuda does not maintain reserve requirements obliging the conversion of export earnings. The relevant obligations fall under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the BMA's Exchange Control Notices: Bermudian-dollar accounts and foreign-currency accounts may be freely held by an Exempted Limited Partnership as a designated 'external account' holder, and receipts in USD, GBP, EUR, or other currencies can be retained without mandatory conversion. Reporting obligations arise only where the transaction value engages the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) threshold for record-keeping (Bermuda AML/ATF Regulations 2018) or where the payment involves a sanctioned jurisdiction or person. If a Bermuda-domiciled ELP is treated as a tax resident of the United Kingdom under the UK's partnership transparency rules or the corporate residency tests, UK exchange-control rules do not require surrender either; the UK has no general foreign-currency surrender rule since the end of the 1970s. The practical compliance duty consists of maintaining accurate records of the receipt of Apple and Google payout settlements, and ensuring that any intra-group or partner capital distributions are coded correctly for the underlying platform's payout reporting, which is primarily a US withholding and VAT/GST reporting concern, not an FX surrender concern.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as the integrated regulator for banking, insurance, and AML/ATF in Bermuda; the Bermuda Ministry of Finance remains responsible for exchange control policy under the Exchange Control Act 1972, with the BMA acting as the delegated agent for exchange-control approvals.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility with legal constraints. Bermuda is a regulated 'fintech-friendly' jurisdiction under the Digital Assets Business Act 2018 (DABA) and the Banking, Trust Companies, and Investment Companies Act 1987, but the BMA does not issue European-style Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licenses. A UK-issued EMI (e-money institution) authorized by the Financial Conduct Authority under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011, or an EU/EEA EMI passporting into the UK, can be operationally used by the Bermuda ELP to bank in GBP and EUR, but it will not provide a Bermuda banking relationship or a US-dollar clearable Bermudian account. EMI viability is Medium because the entity is a digital gaming publisher receiving payouts from Apple and Google, which route via commercial bank accounts in the US and Hong Kong, and the EMI can support multi-currency account abstraction, but the ELP still needs a principal bank with US clearing ability to receive USD settlement from the app stores. Legal constraints: EMIs cannot lend, cannot take deposits from the public under a banking license framework, and may impose sub-account restrictions; additionally, Bermuda's Exchange Control Act requires local banking activity to be licensed by the BMA. A UK EMI is not licensed in Bermuda and therefore must not hold out to be a depositary or engage in Bermuda-regulated banking business; its role must be limited to electronic money distribution and payment facilitation. The name and address of the Bermuda ELP must align across the EMI, the principal bank, and the app store payout tax documents to avoid an AML/ATF mismatch.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for capital injecting from UK/EU/US investors into the Bermuda ELP should be denominated in USD, GBP, or EUR and transmitted by bank wire (SWIFT MT103) or, for GBP domestic UK funding, Faster Payments for sub-£1 million with subsequent SWIFT onward transfer. For Bermuda bank accounts, the recommended protocol is: (i) the investor instructs its UK or offshore bank to effect an international SWIFT payment to the ELP's Bermuda bank account using the beneficiary's full registered ELP name, the ABA/IBAN equivalent, and the bank's SWIFT code; (ii) the payment purpose/message field must include a clear reference such as 'Capital Contribution to Bermuda ELP [certificate no.] pursuant to LP Agreement' and should use the appropriate cross-border payment purpose code. The UK's BOP/IDR and the EU/EEA transactions are regulated for sanctions purposes, so the payment must screen against OFAC, HMT, EU, and UN sanctions lists. If the injection is paid to a UK-based EMI account (e.g., a UK EMI EUR or GBP account), the ELP can accept the funds as electronic money, then onward-transfer in stages to the Bermuda bank; however, careful attention must be paid to the AML character of the second leg because a UK EMI may treat the ELP as a pass-through, requiring a purpose code and a source-of-funds declaration. For FATCA/CRS, the inbound capital injection should be documented under the partnership capital accounts and the contributing partner must provide a self-certification of its tax residence. Since Bermuda is not in the EU's blacklist and has an AEOI arrangement with the UK, the receiving bank will report CRS details of the partner who receives an allocation of capital as an equity interest (the ELP is treated as a non-reporting NFE for CRS purposes if it is not a financial institution). The capital must be used for the stated MMORPG publishing activity; if the contribution exceeds the bank's internal threshold (typically US$250,000 or US$500,000), the bank will require a source-of-funds questionnaire and possibly a site visit or video call. A prudent approach is to make the injection from the partner's own account, not an intermediary or pooled E-wallet, and to avoid structuring or 'smurfing' through multiple small transfers, which would violate Bermuda's AML/ATF suspicious-transaction reporting rules. All inbound wires should include a purpose code of 'Capital subscription to a Bermuda exempted limited partnership' and must be recorded with the bank's FX conversion desk if the currency differs from the account base currency.Outbound disbursements for the MMORPG publishing business consist pre-paid expense for (a) the UK/Czech development studio, (b) the United States-based app-store platform taxes or the Bermuda ELP's remunerations, (c) Apple and Google payout settlement proceeds to the partners, and (d) vendor payments for server hosting and intellectual-property licensing. The clearing rails: for trades denominated in USD, the standard SWIFT MT103 payment order is suitable for correspondent-bank-cleared wires, with the credit in the beneficiary bank's account via the US Clearing House Interbank Payments System (CHIPS) or Fedwire; the MT103 must include the beneficiary bank's ABA and the ELP's full name and CC code, and the 'Details of Charges' field should be set to 'BEN', 'SHA', or 'OUR' depending on the vendor contract. Payments greater than a normal threshold to a China-based server vendor would require the reverse correspondent chain or direct renminbi settlement through an intermediary bank, but given that the MMORPG is global, the primary vendor currencies are USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD. For Trade Settlement through a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC), which is not needed for digital game publishing but may be used for licensing of physical merchandise or peripheral items, the ELP can use an irrevocable, confirmed at-sight LC payable via SWIFT MT700/MT710 and MT752 series through HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield; for digital-only revenue, a commercial LC is irrelevant. For disbursements to partners' annual profit distributions, the payout route is SWIFT MT103 or, for smaller distribution amounts, electronic funds transfer through the app-store payout account (but app-store payouts cannot be split directly to partners). Any outbound disbursement must be reconciled to the underlying Apple and Google payout schedules, ensuring that the VAT/withholding tax / GST obligations in the country of the payment receiver are the responsibility of the recipient or are handled by the ELP under the applicable platform-level withholding. The ELP's bank account in Bermuda should be the single 'gateway' for outbound liquidity, with a sub-ledger in the treasury ERP for each payment rail and a strict policy that no outbound payment is made to an unverified third party without correspondence to an executed contract and KYC evidence.Bermuda Dollar (BMD) / United States Dollar (USD) is a pegged currency at 1:1, so no material FX hedging is required between BMD and USD accounts; the ELP may nonetheless execute spot conversions when depositing in BMD or use offshore USD clearances.For USD-to-GBP exposure arising from UK development costs and Apple or Google GBP trade account receipts, the ELP may use deliverable forward FX contracts (plain-vanilla USD/GBP and EUR/USD forwards) with a licensed Bermuda bank or UK-listed bank trading desk, provided the bank is a BMA-licensed institution or the counterparty is an appropriately capitalised professional market counterparty; Bermuda's Exchange Control regime does not restrict forward hedging for non-financial end-user entities, but the transaction must be recorded with the bank and may be treated as a 'derivatives' transaction requiring a board/partner resolution.For exposures to EUR/GBP and USD/EUR volatility in app-store settlements, the ELP may use no-deliverable forwards (NDFs) only where the underlying currency is a non-deliverable currency (e.g., emerging-market positions) or in circumstances where settlement is constrained; because USD, GBP, EUR, and CAD are deliverable, NDFs are not a natural instrument for the core MMORPG revenue, but if the ELP elects to hedge a portfolio of app-store distributions from jurisdictions with non-convertible or lightly traded currencies (e.g., RUB if Russian app-store payments are permitted through certain payout corridors), a non-deliverable forward referencing the RUB/USD rate with a London or New York prime broker may be permissible under UK EMIR hedging/end-user clearing exemption and Bermuda's DABA-related derivatives licensing exemption.Currency swaps (cross-currency swaps) are permissible in principle for funding a USD-denominated profit distribution to a UK partner when the ELP holds a surplus in EUR: the ELP may enter a cross-currency interest rate swap (USD/EUR or USD/GBP) with an investment-grade bank under an ISDA Master Agreement, subject to the ELP's ability to post collateral and to a partnership authorisation under the Partnership Act 1902; because the ELP is a pass-through entity, any hedging gain or loss at the partnership level is allocated to partners for tax purposes under their respective tax regimes, and the bank will require a signed ISDA and a Credit Support Annex (CSA) if netting is needed.In addition, the ELP may hold a multi-currency concentration account with the principal bank and execute internal 'natural offsets' without entering financial derivatives, e.g., by sweeping user-purchase GBP into a GBP operating account and using that account directly for UK vendors, and by sweeping USD payout funds into the USD account for Apple and Google settlement, thereby reducing external FX counterparty risk.The ELP should establish an internal treasury policy limiting any leveraged or speculative FX positions, requiring board-level pre-approval for derivative notionals greater than the annual app-store payout volume, and excluding Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoin swaps, and other crypto assets from the permitted hedging set, given that the KY_ELP structure is a low-risk standard treasury and not a digital-asset business.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium feasibility with legal constraints. Bermuda is a regulated 'fintech-friendly' jurisdiction under the Digital Assets Business Act 2018 (DABA) and the Banking, Trust Companies, and Investment Companies Act 1987, but the BMA does not issue European-style Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licenses. A UK-issued EMI (e-money institution) authorized by the Financial Conduct Authority under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011, or an EU/EEA EMI passporting into the UK, can be operationally used by the Bermuda ELP to bank in GBP and EUR, but it will not provide a Bermuda banking relationship or a US-dollar clearable Bermudian account. EMI viability is Medium because the entity is a digital gaming publisher receiving payouts from Apple and Google, which route via commercial bank accounts in the US and Hong Kong, and the EMI can support multi-currency account abstraction, but the ELP still needs a principal bank with US clearing ability to receive USD settlement from the app stores. Legal constraints: EMIs cannot lend, cannot take deposits from the public under a banking license framework, and may impose sub-account restrictions; additionally, Bermuda's Exchange Control Act requires local banking activity to be licensed by the BMA. A UK EMI is not licensed in Bermuda and therefore must not hold out to be a depositary or engage in Bermuda-regulated banking business; its role must be limited to electronic money distribution and payment facilitation. The name and address of the Bermuda ELP must align across the EMI, the principal bank, and the app store payout tax documents to avoid an AML/ATF mismatch.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% at the Bermuda exempted limited partnership (ELP) level; Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax and no VAT/sales tax, and the ELP is tax-transparent (pass-through) with profits allocated to partners.
