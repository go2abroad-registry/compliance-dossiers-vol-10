Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance (portal: taxcommissioner.gov.bm) for payroll tax and the new Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT); Customs Department (portal: customs.gov.bm) for import duties; Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance (Bermuda corporate registry portal) for Exempted Limited Partnership registration and economic substance filings. United Kingdom (sovereign context): HM Revenue & Customs (portal: gov.uk/hmrc) for UK VAT and UK direct taxes where a UK nexus arises.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014 via the Registrar of Companies; obtain the ELP registration number and Certificate of Exempted Partnership. Step 2: No income tax TIN is issued because Bermuda imposes no tax on profits/incomes of exempted partnerships; obtain a payroll tax account only if employing Bermuda-resident staff. Step 3: If the ELP belongs to a multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, register under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025) with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and file the requisite CIT returns, including any qualified domestic minimum top-up tax election. Step 4: For UK VAT, register with HMRC online (VAT Form 1) if the ELP supplies taxable services in the UK or is a non-established taxable person making any UK taxable supply; the ELP receives a UK VAT registration number and Making Tax Digital (MTD) credentials. Step 5: File annual economic substance declarations with the Registrar of Companies under the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2018.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda: no check-the-box system; the ELP is transparent under Bermuda law and may designate exempted status at formation; where the ELP falls within Bermuda CIT scope, the group may elect to apply the qualified domestic minimum top-up tax (DMTT) or rely on the income inclusion rule/undertaxed profits rule architecture to the extent permitted by the CIT Act 2023. UK: no formal check-the-box; UK tax transparency of a Bermuda partnership is determined under UK partnership tax rules (ITTOIA 2005 / CTA 2009) and the UK hybrid and other mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A), with notifications required for structured mismatch arrangements; US partners may file Form 8832 to elect entity classification for US federal tax purposes only.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: no VAT or GST; the only indirect-type levies are customs duties (Customs Tariff Act 1973), stamp duties, and payroll tax (on remuneration rather than sales). UK VAT (Value Added Tax Act 1994) is the relevant transactional consumption tax only to the extent the ELP makes taxable supplies within the UK territorial scope.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: Not applicable (no VAT/GST registration threshold; customs account registration applies for importers). UK VAT: GBP 90,000 annual taxable turnover threshold for UK-established businesses; non-established (Bermuda-domiciled) entities have no threshold and must register where they make any taxable supply in the UK.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: no VAT is imposed, so zero-rating and export refund mechanisms do not apply; international Ro-Ro transport income is simply outside any Bermuda indirect tax base. UK VAT: zero-rating under VATA 1994 Schedule 8, Group 15 applies to the supply of transport of goods (including deep-sea roll-on/roll-off carriage of vehicles) to or from a place outside the UK and to directly related services, subject to the supplier retaining prescribed evidence that the goods have been exported; ancillary port and stevedoring services qualify when linked to an export or import voyage.

  • Customs export declaration (UK CDS/SAD entry or equivalent third-country export declaration)
  • Bill of lading or sea waybill evidencing roll-on/roll-off vehicle loading and foreign destination
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank SWIFT/wire transfer confirmation evidencing receipt from the foreign charterer or cargo owner
  • Commercial invoice and port manifest matching the shipped vehicles

Bermuda: no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists. UK: Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT requires VAT-registered businesses to use functional-compatible software, maintain digital records, and submit VAT returns via HMRC's MTD application programming interfaces; there is no government pre-clearance digital invoice stamping regime.

UK: HMRC may claw back zero-rated VAT with interest where export evidence is not obtained or retained within the required periods; civil penalties up to 100% of the VAT lost may apply for careless or deliberate inaccuracies (FA 2007 Schedule 24), together with late-payment penalties; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion is available under VATA 1994 s.72 and CEMA 1979. Bermuda: no VAT clawback exists; customs duty evasion triggers penalties, interest, and forfeiture under the Customs Act 1941, and payroll tax non-compliance penalties apply under the Payroll Tax Act 1995.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged to the U.S. Dollar at 1:1 and both currencies circulate interchangeably. For an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) designated as non-resident, current-account transactions and profit draws are fully convertible without BMA approval, provided the partnership does not transact with resident counterparties or hold local-currency assets. Capital-account contributions, including partner capital injections, are also unrestricted when the ELP maintains its BMA non-resident designation and conducts its treasury through external bank accounts. The practical profit-remittance hurdle is not exchange control but the obligation to prove the ELP has not become resident through day-to-day management in Bermuda, to keep economic substance outside Bermuda, and to document that the RoRo shipping income arises from ocean transportation rather than domestic Bermudian business.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda does not impose mandatory conversion of export receipts for a non-resident exempted partnership. RoRo vessel transport revenues, including freight, deadfreight, demurrage, and detention, may be retained in the original invoice currency in external bank accounts without surrender. If the ELP were inadvertently treated as resident under Exchange Control legislation, all export proceeds would need to be collected within six months of shipment and offered for conversion into BMD through an authorised dealer, unless BMA granted a general or specific exemption. The ELP should therefore maintain clear evidence that all vessel operations, cargo contracts, and management decisions occur outside Bermuda, and file a negative confirmation of resident trading activity where required.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), exercising exclusive monetary and exchange control authority in Bermuda pursuant to the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972. The Bank of England has no regulatory jurisdiction over Bermuda currency controls; the Bermuda Ministry of Finance retains residual policy direction and may issue or approve exchange control notices.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility only as a secondary payment conduit; Low as a primary treasury bank. Bermuda has no electronic-money-institution (EMI) licence, and UK-FCA-authorised EMIs are not deemed licensed banks by the BMA. A UK EMI can accept remittances on behalf of the ELP but cannot provide the BMA-regulated banking layer needed for capital escrow, commercial letters of credit, or clearance of Bermudian regulatory filings. Legal constraints include the absence of BMA passporting for UK EMIs, the requirement that any EMI maintain its own full correspondent banking chain into USD and GBP, and the risk that funds held with an unregulated EMI will trigger enhanced due diligence at the Bermuda receiving bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound partner capital must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the partner's own corporate account directly to the ELP's designated capital escrow or operational account at a licensed Bermuda bank. The payment narrative must reference 'Capital Contribution – [ELP Name and Registration Number]' and include an ISO 20022 remittance purpose code equivalent to 'Equity injection' or 'Intercompany funding'. The first contribution must be accompanied in advance by a certified copy of the partnership agreement, the ELP certificate of registration, a completed BMA non-resident declaration, and a positive sanctions screening of all source-party bank names. For funds from the UK or Cayman, no Bermuda-specific tax clearance is required; however, the originator's bank may require proof that the contributor is an authorised signatory and beneficial owner to satisfy correspondent-bank KYC.Outbound RoRo disbursements should be executed primarily via SWIFT MT103 payment orders for port dues, stevedoring, crewing agencies, bunker suppliers, sub-charter hire, and maintenance. USD clearing occurs through correspondent bank relationships in New York via CHIPS or Fedwire, while GBP disbursements clear via a London CHAPS correspondent if the creditor is a UK party. Larger or high-value trade obligations, including vessel acquisition deposits and terminal construction or major repair financing, should be structured as commercial documentary letters of credit issued by the Bermuda bank under ICC UCP600, with presentation of commercial invoice, bill of lading, certificate of marine insurance, and origin certificate. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: No statutory audit is mandatorily imposed on a Bermuda ELP under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992. An annual independent audit becomes compulsory only where: (i) the ELP holds a BMA licence for a regulated activity (e.g., banking, insurance, fund or investment business); (ii) the Economic Substance Tax Commissioner requires audited financial statements with the ES Return for a shipping relevant activity; or (iii) the ELP is consolidated into a UK/EU audited group or investor loan agreements covenant an audit. A Ro-Ro vessel-owning ELP with no licensed activity and no audit covenant may lawfully remain unaudited.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% standard Bermuda corporate income tax; 15% for in-scope multinational enterprise groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Bermuda does not impose dividend withholding tax)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 (no general Bermuda transfer pricing documentation regime); for in-scope CIT MNE groups, mandatory OECD-aligned Local File and Master File documentation applies when consolidated group revenue exceeds BMD 750 million (EUR 750 million equivalent), per Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and OECD Pillar Two rules.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR 2019) apply. 'Deep-sea Ro-Ro shipping' is a relevant activity requiring the ELP to conduct core income-generating activities in Bermuda, maintain a physical office, have a minimum number of full-time employees, and exercise direction and management locally. Qualified tax-exempt entities can rely on the pure equity holding exemption only if they hold no shipping operations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Aggressive Avoidance / False CIT Declaration

Statutory sanction: Re-assessment of undeclared income at 15% CIT plus interest; monetary penalties of up to BMD 250,000; criminal prosecution for false declaration; disqualification of the ELP from receiving tax clearance.

Director personal liability: General partners and controlling directors are jointly and severally liable when they knowingly cause the ELP to make false CIT declarations. Liabilities include civil restitution, travel bans, disqualification orders, and imprisonment up to 7 years per fraud-related offence.
Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: ESR determination of non-compliance: penalty of BMD 50,000 for first offence, BMD 100,000 for subsequent offences; mandatory striking-off of the ELP from the Register; for repeated non-compliance, the court may order dissolution and freezing of Bermuda assets.

Director personal liability: General partners are personally liable for unpaid ESR penalties where they were the controlling mind of the substance failure. Directors may face enforcement notices restricting travel to and from Bermuda and seizure of personal assets in the territory.
Shell Company / Paper Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Substance denial under ESR; reclassification as a taxable entity; penalties under Bermuda CIT and source-country anti-avoidance rules (e.g., UK HMRC DOTAS and GAAR) may apply, including accelerated payment notices and fines of up to 100% of the tax advantage.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners who orchestrate paper-only structures can be personally assessed for facilitation of tax evasion. Civil restitution orders and imprisonment up to 10 years may be imposed under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 where relevant.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Concealment / Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and AML regulations: fines up to BMD 1,000,000 per breach; criminal forfeiture of vessel or charter proceeds; suspension of the ELP's statutory shipping licence; mandatory corporate dissolution.

Director personal liability: General partners, directors, and compliance officers face personal criminal liability for money laundering and sanctions violations, including imprisonment up to 15 years, travel bans, and seizure of personal assets. The corporate veil is pierced where the partnership is used as a sham vehicle for unlawful conduct.
