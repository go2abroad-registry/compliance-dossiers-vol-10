Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda (Ministry of Finance); government portal: https://www.gov.bm/ (and Bermuda Registrar of Companies for entity incorporation).

E-government filing portal

Bermuda exempted companies are incorporated via a registered corporate service provider with the Registrar of Companies; there is no income tax TIN or VAT ID issued because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, no withholding tax, and no VAT/GST. If the exempted company has employees, the employer must obtain a Payroll Tax number from the Office of the Tax Commissioner; foreign entities with no Bermuda payroll do not require any domestic tax ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Company is resident-exempt, subject to annual government fees; profits, dividends, and capital gains are tax-exempt; no corporate income tax or payroll tax absent Bermuda employees.

Available election forms

No local check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist. A Bermuda exempted company is treated as non-resident for UK/foreign tax if controlled and managed abroad; where UK tax residence applies, UK hybrid mismatch rules under TIOPA 2010 may integrate Bermuda's tax-exempt status. No voluntary tax classification election is available under Bermuda law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Tax Regime; Bermuda Customs Import Duty only

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no economic-turnover VAT/GST registration threshold; importers of dutiable goods must register with Bermuda Customs under the Customs Act 1965.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT/GST zero-rating exists. Duty relief may be claimed under the Customs Tariff 1965 only for physical goods that are subsequently re-exported (temporary importation or duty drawback); digital webtoon syndication and related licensing payments are intangible and outside the customs duty base.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CusDoc/EDX)
  • Bill of Lading or House Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from an approved Bermuda bank (when export proceeds are repatriated)

No e-invoicing/clearance or fiscal-stamping regime. Bermuda Customs uses the Electronic Data Interchange (EDX) portal for import/export declarations; no API or state-level pre-clearance for digital services is required.

Bermuda Customs may impose penalties under the Customs Act 1965 for fraudulent drawback/re-export claims, including forfeiture of goods, a civil penalty equal to up to three times the duty evaded, and monetary fines; deliberate false statements can be prosecuted criminally. Because no VAT exists, retrospective clawback of export VAT refunds is not applicable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar and is freely convertible for non-resident exempted companies. Current account remittances, profit distributions, and intercompany management fees required for webtoon syndication may be remitted without BMA exchange-control approval. Capital account transactions, including issuance and redemption of exempted company shares and inbound shareholder loans, are also unrestricted for a non-resident entity. The United Kingdom has no exchange controls limiting the convertibility of GBP for current account or capital account transfers, subject to sanctions screening and FATF-compliant AML checks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Bermuda or the United Kingdom. A Bermuda exempted company may retain 100% of its Apple IAP and Google Play royalty proceeds in USD or BMD, with no statutory timeline for repatriation or conversion into GBP.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as the central bank and foreign exchange regulator of Bermuda; the Bank of England and Her Majesty's Treasury retain residual monetary, sanctions, and anti-money laundering oversight for the UK-GB sovereign parent.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplementary payment collection/settlement rail, but Low as a primary treasury account. A UK FCA-authorized electronic money institution may provide a virtual IBAN for outbound creator payments and app-store custody clearing, but it cannot accept deposits in Bermuda or issue Bermuda local account numbers without a BMA banking license under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. A non-Bermuda EMI may process cross-border transactions; however, all net treasury cash balances must be swept to a licensed Bermudian bank account to avoid unlawful deposit-taking and to ensure regulatory protection.

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax, income tax, or capital gains tax on dividends paid by an exempted company to shareholders, whether resident or non-resident.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda levies no income tax and has no general Local File or Master File documentation threshold. Mandatory transfer-pricing documentation and Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR) obligations apply only to constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million (BMD-equivalent) under Bermuda's CbCR regulations and, where the ultimate parent is UK-resident, under the UK CbCR rules; no Bermuda Local File requirement exists below the large-MNE threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply fully. Digital webtoon publishing and IP monetisation is classified as a relevant activity (Intellectual Property Business). The exempted company must demonstrate: (i) a physical office in Bermuda, (ii) full-time employees with suitable qualifications, (iii) minimum annual operating expenditure in Bermuda, and (iv) core income-generating activities (development, enhancement, maintenance, protection, and exploitation of the webtoon IP) performed in Bermuda. Outsourced CIGAs must be supervised and directed by Bermuda-resident directors. A binding annual Economic Substance Declaration is mandatory; the pure-equity-holding exemption is not available for an operating IP business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: HMRC may treat the Bermuda company as UK-resident (worldwide taxation) or attribute its profits to a UK permanent establishment under CTA 2009, applying corporation tax at up to 25%, plus interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax under FA 2007 Schedule 24; the Criminal Finances Act 2017 imposes corporate criminal liability for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in the sham face personal civil restitution for the tax loss, negligence-based penalties, travel bans, disqualification, and imprisonment for dishonest conduct under TMA 1970 and the Fraud Act 2006.
Shell Company / ESR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Economic Substance Act 2018 of up to BD$10,000 per default plus daily continuing fines; the Registrar may strike off the company and forfeit its assets or obtain a winding-up order; information may be exchanged automatically with HMRC and IRS under the TIEA, exposing the group to coordinated audits.

Director personal liability: Officers and directors are personally liable for fines and up to 2 years' imprisonment for knowingly making false declarations; the court may issue travel bans, asset freezes, and director disqualification orders.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Register

Statutory sanction: Fines and daily default penalties under the Companies and Limited Liability Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Amendment Act 2018; the Beneficial Ownership Secure Search System (BOSS) grants law-enforcement access; continued default leads to strike-off and prohibition from carrying on business in Bermuda.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines, imprisonment of up to 2 years, and travel bans for breach of beneficial ownership filing duties; non-compliant directors are also ineligible to act as directors of other Bermuda entities.
IP Migration / Transfer Pricing

Statutory sanction: HMRC may recharacterise the IP transfer under the transfer-pricing rules in CTA 2010 Part 4, substitute an arm's-length consideration, impose corporation tax on the uplifted profit, and assess interest and penalties of up to 100% of the underpaid tax; secondary adjustments may create deemed distributions and shareholder loan accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve an undervalued IP migration breach fiduciary duties to shareholders and creditors and may be sued for civil restitution and equitable compensation; intentional undervaluation can support criminal fraud charges and personal penalty assessments under FA 2007 Schedule 24.
