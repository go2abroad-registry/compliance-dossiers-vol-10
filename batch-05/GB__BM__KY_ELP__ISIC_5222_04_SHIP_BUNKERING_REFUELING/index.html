Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda — filing portal 'Bermuda Tax Online' at https://www.gov.bm/corporate-income-tax; Registrar of Companies (Bermuda Registry) at https://www.bermudaregistry.bm for entity incorporation under the Exempted Limited Partnership Act 2015; Bermuda Customs and Revenue Agency (BCRA) for customs duty registration and the Bermuda Customs Electronic Single-Window declaration platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: File the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) registration application with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Limited Partnership Act 2015; upon approval, the Bermuda Registry issues a Certificate of Registration and an entity registration number (the statutory corporate identifier). Step 2: If the ELP forms part of an in-scope Multinational Enterprise (MNE) group with consolidated global revenue of at least EUR 750 million, or if it expects to derive Bermuda-attributable profits within the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, register for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT) with the OTC through Bermuda Tax Online and obtain a CIT Tax Identification Number (TIN). Step 3: If the ELP employs Bermuda-resident staff, register as an employer under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number via Bermuda Tax Online. Step 4: For import/export of bunker fuel and lubricants, procure an Importer/Exporter Customs Code from the BCRA and establish a bonded warehouse or storage licence application file where duty-suspension is sought. Step 5: Obtain the Bermuda Economic Substance filing reference if the ELP conducts relevant insurance, finance, or shipping activities in Bermuda. The same ELP may lawfully hold separate identifiers (CIT TIN, Payroll Account, Customs Code, Substance Reference) for distinct statutory purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda historically levies no corporate income, capital gains, or withholding taxes on foreign-source income; the ELP is presumptively transparent for tax purposes. Effective 1 January 2025, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 imposes a 15% CIT on profits attributable to Bermuda for in-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, subject to transitional relief, economic substance adjustments, and QDMTT mechanics; ordinary foreign-source bunkering income remains outside the Bermuda tax net for non-in-scope groups).

Available election forms

Partnership Classification Election: Under draft OTC guidance implementing the CIT Act 2023, an ELP may elect transparent (default, CIT at partner level) or non-transparent (entity-level CIT) treatment; transparency is mandatory unless an election is approved to prevent mismatch. Hybrid Mismatch Alignment: An ELP may file an election with the OTC to conform Bermuda classification to the U.S. check-the-box or the UK/Cayman classification of the partnership so as to neutralise BEPS Action 2 hybrid mismatch outcomes. QDMTT Safe Harbour Election: In-scope MNE groups may elect the Bermuda qualified domestic minimum top-up tax regime as the jurisdictional top-up mechanism under Pillar Two GloBE rules. Transitional Substance Election: An ELP may elect to apply the substance-based carve-out and economic substance rules for CIT attribution. No general check-the-box regime analogous to the U.S. exists; elections are made in writing to the OTC within the first CIT filing period, with binding effect for 5 fiscal years.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or Sales and Use Tax in Bermuda (UK VAT at 20% does not apply; Bermuda is a separate customs and tax territory outside the UK VAT area). Bermuda relies on employer-level Payroll Tax, Stamp Duty, and Customs Duty (importation tariff): tariffs on imported fuels and goods range from 0% to 33.25% ad valorem depending on the Customs Tariff classification; bunker fuel entered into the customs area for domestic use is dutiable, whereas duty-suspension and foreign-going vessel relief apply to international bunker supply.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A for VAT/GST (no such tax exists). For CIT registration: in-scope MNE group global consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million), with CIT applying to Bermuda-attributable profits. For Payroll Tax registration: any employer (including an ELP) with Bermuda-resident employees, subject to the annual exempt allowance per employee under the Payroll Tax Act 1995. For Customs Duty: no turnover threshold; registration requires an Importer/Exporter Customs Code and, for duty-suspended bunkering, a Bonded Warehouse or Freeport licence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating is inapplicable because Bermuda has no VAT. Customs duty relief applies to goods entered for export or re-export under section 21 of the Customs Act 1963, provided customs control is maintained and export occurs within the statutory period (generally 12 months; 30 days for transshipment). Bunker fuel and lubricants delivered to ships engaged in foreign-going international trade are eligible for duty-free supply when withdrawn from a bonded warehouse, from a duty-suspended storage facility, or through the Bermuda Freeport Zone, provided the product is not consumed within the customs area, the vessel qualifies as foreign-going, and a valid Bunker Delivery Note and export/re-export declaration evidence the international voyage. No VAT refund mechanism exists because no indirect tax was charged; the substantive benefit is duty relief at import/supply, which requires complete documentary substantiation.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration / Re-Export Entry (electronic declaration filed via the Bermuda Customs single-window system)
  • Bill of Lading (through-negotiable ocean bill evidencing carriage from a Bermuda port or bonded storage to a foreign port)
  • Bunker Delivery Note (BDN) issued in accordance with MARPOL Annex VI regulation 18, stating the vessel, IMO number, port, product grade, quantity, and delivery date
  • Inward/Outward Foreign Currency Remittance Certificate issued by a Bermuda-licensed bank or the ELP's external account bank, evidencing repatriation of export/bunker sale proceeds into the ELP's account
  • Charter Party or International Sale and Purchase Contract (including the bunker supply agreement) demonstrating that the supply serves a foreign-going vessel
  • Certificate of Foreign-Going Status / Vessel Clearance Certificate issued by the Bermuda Maritime Administration or port authority confirming departure to a foreign destination

No real-time electronic fiscal clearance, Continuous Transaction Control (CTC), or digital stamping regime applies in Bermuda for commercial invoices or Bunker Delivery Notes. Customs declarations must be lodged electronically through the Bermuda Customs single-window / electronic customs declaration system, supporting automated risk profiling and digital release of excisable and bonded goods. CIT returns and payroll tax filings are made electronically through Bermuda Tax Online, and supporting transfer pricing documents must be retained in electronic or physical form for at least 7 years. Bunker Delivery Note requirements under MARPOL Annex VI (including digital or paper copies retained for 3 years) are observed as a maritime compliance matter, not a tax clearance mandate.

Bermuda Customs may re-assess duty on exported or duty-free bunker fuel if the documentation bundle is incomplete, if the vessel is not foreign-going, if the fuel is diverted into the domestic market, or if export does not occur within the statutory period; penalties include recovery of the full unpaid duty plus a penalty of up to 50% of the duty (strict liability) and, in cases of fraud or misdeclaration, forfeiture of goods, a penalty of up to three times the duty value, and fines up to BMD 5,000 per offence. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, fraudulent failure to register, failure to file, concealment, or false CIT declarations attract a penalty of 20% of under-assessed CIT plus interest at the statutory rate (Treasury bill yield plus 5%), and criminal prosecution on indictment. The OTC may apply the General Anti-Abuse Rule to disregard artificial arrangements producing a tax benefit, extend the assessment period to 6 years (unlimited in the case of fraud), and retrospectively claw back CIT, QDMTT, or duty relief with interest compounded from the original due date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. Current-account transactions are fully convertible for both residents and non-residents, and a non-resident-designated Bermuda Exempted Partnership may hold, credit, and disburse USD freely without BMA application. Capital-account flows (capital contributions, distributions, and liquidation proceeds) are liberalized for non-resident-controlled ELPs, subject only to source-of-funds documentation; there is no materiality threshold or BMA prior-approval requirement. Profit remittance to the UK parent is unrestricted once audited financial statements and a distribution resolution are in place.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and BMA Exchange Control Notices, resident persons must offer foreign-currency proceeds to an authorized dealer within 30 days of receipt, or obtain an open-currency-account exemption. A Bermuda-domiciled ELP that is formally designated 'non-resident' (with a non-resident BMD account in the ELP's name through its resident representative) is exempt from statutory surrender; bunkering revenue received in USD may therefore be retained offshore indefinitely. The ELP should maintain a current non-resident designation letter from the BMA or its resident agent to evidence this exemption.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972; the BMA acts as exchange-control administrator and prudential supervisor of all Bermuda-licensed banks and authorized dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW-MEDIUM (supplementary rail only). UK FCA-regulated e-money institutions cannot operate local Bermuda accounts or clear BMD, and the BMA's Financial Institutions Act 1998 reserves deposit-taking and payment services to licensed persons; an overseas EMI may therefore process GBP/EUR supplier payments on an outsourced basis but can never serve as the ELP's primary treasury anchor, capital escrow location, or AML/KYC hub. Any EMI usage must be ring-fenced below 10% of monthly settlement volume and supported by a supplemental due-diligence file on the EMI's UK license status.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions from the UK parent or partners are effected by SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's USD account at HSBC or Butterfield, quoting the ELP's registration number, the payment purpose 'Investment - Capital Contribution to Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership', and the UK sender's purpose code 'Investment in overseas associated undertaking'. A signed capital-call notice, a constituent-partner resolution, a source-of-wealth form, and a copy of the ELP's non-resident designation must accompany the transfer to prevent return or rejection. For funds above USD 1 million, pre-notification to the receiving compliance team through a local relationship manager is advisable. No BMA exchange-control approval applies because the ELP is non-resident; any USD/BMD conversion is executed at the fixed 1:1 peg with no FX cost.Core rails for bunkering settlements: (1) SWIFT MT103 for prompt payment of stems, terminal fees, and port dues to verified supplier accounts; (2) SWIFT MT700 documentary Letter of Credit for bulk VLSFO and HSFO purchases from refiners, with presentation documents comprising commercial invoice, ISO 8217 quality certificate, bill of lading, delivery receipt, and weighing or handling certificates; (3) SWIFT MT760 Standby LC issued by a Bermuda bank in favor of credit suppliers to unlock open-account terms; and (4) USD, GBP, and EUR treasury sweep sub-accounts that net intercompany flows between the ELP and the UK parent. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 (equivalent to EUR 750,000,000) consolidated group revenue triggers optional or mandatory transfer pricing documentation only for CIT Act in-scope multinational groups. Exempted partnerships outside CIT scope have no statutory Local File or Master File requirement under Bermuda income tax law.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance (Partnerships) Act 2018 applies to exempted partnerships conducting relevant activities, which may include shipping, support services, or distribution and service centres. A ship bunkering and refueling ELP must demonstrate compliance if it derives income from activities connected to Bermuda or claims tax residence outside Bermuda; core income-generating activities must be performed with adequate physical presence, full-time employees, and management in Bermuda, unless the partnership is tax resident in a qualifying jurisdiction with no Bermuda substance obligation. Annual ESR filing is administered by the Bermuda Registrar of Companies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to 200% of the understated tax, statutory interest at 10% per annum, daily default penalties, and criminal fines.

Director personal liability: Directors or general partners who knowingly cause false returns face personal civil restitution, disqualification from acting as a director in Bermuda, travel bans, and imprisonment for up to 7 years.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Compulsory winding up or strike-off of the exempted partnership; civil penalty of BMD 100,000; revocation of exempted status; denial of tax residence certificates.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid penalties, banned from obtaining new Bermuda licenses or registrations for up to 5 years, and subject to director disqualification orders.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and Sanctions Act may reach BMD 1,000,000 per breach, asset forfeiture, suspension of the ELP registration, and revocation of any required fuel supply license.

Director personal liability: Officers knowingly engaged in laundering or sanctions breaches face imprisonment up to 14 years, seizure of personal assets, travel bans, and civil claims by creditors under piercing-of-the-veil doctrines.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment to the 15% CIT base, underpayment interest at 10% per annum, and penalty of 50% of the resulting tax shortfall if intent to avoid tax is shown.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve non-arm's length pricing documentation are personally liable for the penalty portion if they knew of the deviation; they may be disqualified and, in egregious cases, criminally prosecuted for false accounting.
False Declaration and Fraudulent Enforcement

Statutory sanction: Immediate suspension of the declaration filing; administrative penalty of BMD 50,000 for each false statement; cancellation of exempted status; order for restoration of all unpaid taxes and penalties.

Director personal liability: Individual signatories face perjury charges, imprisonment up to 5 years, civil liability to the Registrar and tax authority for losses caused, and a permanent bar on representing any Bermuda entity.
