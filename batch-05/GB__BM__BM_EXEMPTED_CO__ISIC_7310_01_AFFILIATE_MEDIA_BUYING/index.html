Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Bermuda Exempted Company is outside the net of Bermuda income tax; profits, capital gains, dividends, and outbound service fees suffer no Bermuda tax and no withholding tax. The entity is non-UK-resident provided central management and control is exercised from Bermuda or through properly delegated authority. Caution: the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (15% top-up tax model) applies only to in-scope multinational groups with global revenue of at least EUR 750 million; a standalone affiliate-media-buying entity below that threshold remains fully tax-exempt.

Available election forms

Bermuda levies no income tax, so no check-the-box, s.1154, or hybrid-mismatch election exists at the domestic level. Available statutory elections: (i) election under Section 132A of the Companies Act 1981 to register as a non-resident; (ii) election of the annual fee band applicable to the exempted company's share structure; (iii) election for classification as a non-relevant-activity entity or pure equity holding entity for Economic Substance Act 2018 filing purposes. For UK/EU parents, the UK CTA 2010 Part 6A and the EU Anti-Tax-Avoidance Directive hybrid-mismatch rules apply to payments flowing through the Bermuda entity; a qualifying 'hybrid mismatch election' under the relevant foreign statute may be available to align deduction and inclusion timing on intra-group advertising charges.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (exempt indirect-tax territory). Bermuda does not operate a VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. The only indirect levy is customs duty on imports of physical goods, typically at rates from 0% to 22.25% ad valorem; exports of goods and all services, including digital advertising and affiliate media buying, fall outside the Bermuda customs duty perimeter. Bermuda is outside the UK VAT territory for the purposes of the Value Added Tax Act 1994.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A. Bermuda has no statutory indirect-tax registration threshold because no Bermuda VAT/GST regime exists. An importer of goods must register for a customs entry/importer code with the Bermuda Customs Department irrespective of shipment value. Employer payroll-tax registration under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 is triggered on hiring the first employee (payroll tax rates range from 0% to approximately 6.5% of total emoluments). No UK VAT registration arises unless the entity makes taxable supplies within the UK or voluntarily registers as a non-established taxable person; the UK registration threshold of GBP 90,000 applies only to UK-established businesses.

Invoices issued by the Bermuda entity for cross-border advertising services follow ordinary commercial practice and require no Bermuda fiscal validation. The customer's jurisdiction may govern invoice content: a UK business customer must self-account for reverse-charge VAT and retain a reverse-charge invoice under the VAT Regulations 1995 reg. 16A, while EU customers face analogous obligations under Article 226 of the VAT Directive.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged to the United States dollar at 1:1 under the Exchange Control Act. Bermuda maintains formal exchange control, but an exempted company that is designated as non-resident by the BMA enjoys effective on-shore banking freedom for foreign currency transactions. Current account flows (sales proceeds, dividends, interest, fees) are freely convertible and remittable in USD without prior BMA approval; capital account movements are unrestricted for non-resident designated companies, although a non-resident entity with Bermudian-resident shareholders still requires BMA consent for issue or transfer of shares and for certain domestic borrowings. Profit remittance is not subject to withholding tax, exchange control, or mandatory conversion in Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda has no export proceeds surrender requirement and imposes no mandatory conversion ratio. Digital advertising and media-buying export receipts may be credited freely to the exempted company's USD-denominated or other foreign currency account; no obligation exists to convert into BMD or to remit within any statutory period.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the integrated central bank and financial services regulator for Bermuda under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Moderate-High as a supplementary treasury rail, not as the sole or primary banking relationship. Bermuda exempted companies are not generally within the supported jurisdiction matrix of US digital banks such as Mercury; UK-regulated e-money institutions (e.g., Wise Business) may onboard the company via the registered agent and principal KYC, but no FCA e-money passport extends into Bermuda. Any deposit-taking in Bermuda by an unlicensed EMI would breach the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; therefore the EMI must be used for outbound payment facilitation and receivable concentration in the UK/EU rather than as a Bermuda-resident bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial seed capital and intra-group funding should be wired by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's UK or global bank to the Bermuda exempted company's HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield USD account. Remittance instructions must identify the exempted company by full legal name and registered number, with beneficiary reference 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN / PAID-IN CAPITAL - ISIC 7310 MEDIA BUYING'. The bank requires a board resolution, signed intercompany funding agreement, source-of-funds evidence, and completed corporate financial transaction form; if the injection is represented as debt, a simple interest-free loan term is acceptable for BMA purposes, but transfer pricing documentation must be prepared for UK corporate tax purposes. The inbound movement is not reported to the BMA for exchange control when the company is non-resident and the account is in foreign currency; banks nevertheless will file the transaction under their AML/CTF reporting obligations.Outbound treasury processing for digital media procurement should be tri-modal: (1) large strategic publisher settlements via SWIFT MT103 from the Bermuda operating account; (2) mass affiliate and performance-network payouts via USD ACH or Fedwire through a US payment facilitator such as Mercury or via Wise batch payout API, with each payment carrying purchase-order or invoice reference; (3) EU and UK publisher payouts via SEPA CT or Faster Payments for amounts below GBP 1 million, with the risk of frozen funds addressed by maintaining a segregated commercial settlement account. Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: A Bermuda exempted company is statutorily required to appoint an independent auditor if it is listed or publicly traded; is licensed or regulated under insurance, banking, investment business, fund administration, trust company, or similar legislation; is a 'permit company' under the Companies Act 1981; or if one or more shareholders holding at least 10% of issued voting shares demand an audit. Private exempted companies carrying on non-regulated activities may lawfully dispense with an audit by unanimous shareholder resolution and an election recorded in the minute book.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda domestic Local File or Master File obligation exists: the zero corporate income tax regime creates no transfer-pricing computation and no statutory arm's-length adjustment. Separate obligations arise at group level: as an OECD Inclusive Framework jurisdiction, Bermuda requires Country-by-Country Reporting where the consolidated multinational group revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 812 million); the CbC report is filed via BOSS within 12 months of the group accounting year end. For sub-threshold groups, the UK parent must nonetheless ensure the Bermuda affiliate's margin satisfies OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines where the affiliate is shown in the UK master file as an interposed subcontractor; the practical UK benchmark for an affiliate media-buying entity is a net operating margin of 3-8% against a TNMM tested party, although no Bermuda statute enforces that ratio.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018, Economic Substance Regulations 2018/2019 and the OECD Harmful Tax Practices Framework require a Bermuda exempted company conducting a Relevant Activity to satisfy enhanced substance: (i) physical office premises in Bermuda, (ii) full-time employees in Bermuda or proportionate outsourced personnel managed locally, (iii) operating expenditure in Bermuda proportionate to the income generated, (iv) board meetings convened in Bermuda with real quorum, minutes and independent deliberation, and (v) core income-generating activity conducted from Bermuda. Affiliate media buying that executes campaign traffic, negotiates publisher rates and manages bid strategies from the UK is not economically managed in Bermuda; the Registrar will classify the company as failing substance and HMRC will treat the UK directors as the effective management. A company earning only dividends and capital gains from passive equity holdings may elect pure-equity-holding status; the receipt of media-buying commission fees disqualifies that exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Artificial UK Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: HMRC assessment of the full UK tax liability on the Bermuda entity's profit; FA 2016 penalty of 100% (up to 200% for deliberate, concealed and offshore matters); UK GAAR counteraction plus 60% penalty on the tax advantage; Senior Accounting Officer penalties of GBP 3,000 per failure.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly certify false ESR statements face Bermuda imprisonment up to 1 year and disqualification up to 5 years; UK directors face personal liability under FA 2022 corporate criminal offence of failure to prevent tax evasion, civil restitution, worldwide asset-freezing orders and travel bans where the UK tax debt exceeds GBP 10,000.
Shell Company / Sham Incorporation

Statutory sanction: ROC strike-off and dissolution under the Companies Act 2006 s. 113; forfeiture of assets to the Crown; loss of the right to sue in Bermuda courts; UK GAAR counteraction; HMRC may recharacterize the whole arrangement as a sham trust or agency, retroactively assessing 9 years of profits.

Director personal liability: The Supreme Court of Bermuda pierces the corporate veil where incorporation is a mere facade concealing the true trading entity; directors are personally liable for the unpaid UK corporation tax and interest, face disqualification for 2-10 years, and may be subject to a UK-issued travel restriction order preventing departure pending settlement.
AML / Sanctions Violations on Payment Rails (Mercury ACH, Wire, Wise)

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Bermuda) and AML/ATF Code of Practice 2021 enforced by the Bermuda Monetary Authority: civil penalties up to BMD 250,000 per contravention; correspondent bank account termination; BMA formal warning, enhanced monitoring or prohibition notice; UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 provides for confiscation orders of the entire benefit.

Director personal liability: Directors as senior managers are personally liable for money laundering, failure to disclose, and tipping off under s. 46, s. 52 and s. 53 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015: imprisonment up to 7 years; BMA prohibition orders may include a travel ban barring departure from Bermuda and personal pecuniary penalties without cap.
Misuse of Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection / False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Default surcharge of 15% of the government fee plus BMD 1,000 late filing fee and BMD 500 daily penalty; ROC strike-off and loss of the 2035 tax-exemption guarantee; civil proceedings by the Registrar for recovery of unpaid fees; ESR dual-filing failure exposes separate fines of BMD 14,000 to BMD 42,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false statutory declarations under the Oaths Act face imprisonment up to 2 years and fines; directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid government fees and penalties; the Bermuda Supreme Court may make a contempt order, asset-freezing order and personal costs order against the director responsible.
