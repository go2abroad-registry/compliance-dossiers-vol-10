Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None applicable in the traditional sense as Bermuda levies no corporate income tax and has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. The only relevant elections are: (i) designation as an 'exempted undertaking' under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which statutorily guarantees freedom from all Bermuda taxes imposed after 31 March 2035 until 31 March 2035, extendable at the Minister's discretion; and (ii) voluntary registration as an employer for Payroll Tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 if Bermuda-resident payroll is incurred.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST – Bermuda imposes no general consumption or value-added tax; the only material indirect levies are Bermuda customs import duties (on physical goods) and the employer/employee Payroll Tax (employment-based, not a transaction tax).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. For physical goods imports, an importer must register with Bermuda Customs (Bermuda Customs Tariff applies per heading); there is no economic turnover threshold for services, as cross-border services are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, there is no 'zero-rating' mechanism to elect. AI data-labelling, RLHF curation, and BPO services supplied to overseas clients are inherently outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax and enjoy an effective 0% tax burden by statutory default, without need for export invoice endorsement or refund claims.

  • If physical media is shipped: Bermuda Customs export declaration/manifest plus bill of lading or airway bill evidencing departure from Bermuda

Not applicable – Bermuda has no e-invoicing clearance regime, no real-time fiscal data API, and no digital stamping mandate. Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fully convertible Bermudian dollar (BMD) pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar. Both capital and current account transactions are freely convertible without exchange control approval. There are no restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or repatriation of share capital to foreign shareholders. No withholding tax is levied on interest, dividends, or capital gains for exempted companies. The BMA exercises prudential and AML/ATF oversight only for licensed financial institutions and does not impose transaction-level approval on non-regulated corporate treasury flows. A Bermuda Exempted Company may hold multi-currency accounts in USD, EUR, GBP, or other freely convertible currencies without prior BMA authorization.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no stipulated timeline for repatriation of export receipts. Export revenues from AI data-labeling and BPO services may be retained in any currency in the corporate operational account or cleared onward to contractor payout rails at the company's discretion. The BMA does not require export documentation to be filed for exchange control purposes; the only record-keeping obligation arises from the Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2008 requiring full audit trail retention for five years.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business and PayPal Business are rated Medium-High viability as operational disbursement rails for low-risk AI data-labeling BPO payouts. They are not licensed deposit-taking institutions under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and therefore cannot hold client escrow funds, issue Bermudian corporate banking references, or serve as the primary treasury account for capital injection verification. Their legally permissible use is limited to the payout layer: receiving SWIFT-cleared corporate funds from the Bermuda operational account and distributing mass micro-payments to annotation contractors, subject to individual EMI platform KYC of the beneficial owner and counterparty sanctions screening. A Bermuda Exempted Company may lawfully engage such EMIs as payment processors, but the BMA-regulated relationship bank must remain the anchor account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injection and subordinated loans enter through SWIFT MT103 or MT202 wire transfers to the Bermuda operational account with stated purpose codes of 'Direct Investment' or 'Share Capital Subscription'. The receiving bank applies standard AML screening and may request a copy of the executed share certificate, register of members update, and the source-of-funds bank statement. Funds cleared in USD are immediately fungible in the BMD 1:1 pegged account; EUR or GBP balances may be converted via the same bank's spot desk. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — no general corporate income tax levied on Bermuda exempted companies. A 15% Domestic Top-Up Tax implementing OECD Pillar Two (targeting in-scope multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more) is expected for fiscal years commencing on or after 31 December 2024, but the implementing legislation has not been substantively enacted at the current date.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company; distributions to non-resident shareholders are made free of Bermuda tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Return is due within 30 days after the company's Annual Return Date (the anniversary of incorporation). The Economic Substance Declaration is due within 60 days after the fiscal year-end.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and full payment due on or before the last day of the month following the end of each calendar quarter.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Bermuda has no statutory Local File or Master File documentation threshold for transfer pricing. The Bermuda Tax Authority follows OECD-aligned arm's-length principles in the context of audit and international tax compliance, but no local form or contemporaneous documentation obligation currently applies.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) and the Tax Compliance (Economic Substance) Act require exempted companies carrying on a 'relevant activity' to maintain substantial economic presence in Bermuda and to file an annual Economic Substance Declaration. A data labeling and AI training BPO performing routine operational services for third-party principals is generally not a prescribed relevant activity; however, if the entity derives intellectual property income or positions itself as a distribution or service hub, the regime may apply.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to BMD 1,000,000; imposition of rectification orders; in aggravated cases, striking off by the Registrar or court-ordered restriction on the company carrying on business.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers, including non-resident appointees, may be held personally liable for unpaid penalties and costs; the court may issue director disqualification orders and travel restrictions.
Shell Company Misuse

Statutory sanction: Loss of the statutory tax protection guarantee; lifting of the corporate veil; strike-off by the Registrar; adverse audit determination by the Bermuda Tax Authority; compulsory information exchange to the relevant foreign revenue authority under a Tax Information Exchange Agreement.

Director personal liability: Directors may face civil restitution for distributions made while the company was insolvent, personal penalties for misdeclarations, court-imposed travel bans, and asset freezing orders on grounds of fraud.
Anti-Money Laundering / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Proceeds of Crime (AML/ATF) Regulations 2018 up to BMD 250,000; confiscation and international asset freezing; failure to maintain the Beneficial Ownership Register attracts a fine of up to BMD 75,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 2 years.

Director personal liability: Any director who knowingly permits an AML breach is personally liable to fines and imprisonment of up to 2 years; travel bans and asset restriction orders may be imposed during a financial crime investigation.
Tax Evasion / False Declaration to the Bermuda Tax Authority

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the underpaid payroll tax plus interest; criminal fine of BMD 25,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years for wilful false statements.

Director personal liability: Directors and executive officers are jointly and severally liable to collect, account for, and remit payroll tax; personal liability may extend to travel bans, liens on personal assets, and imprisonment.
