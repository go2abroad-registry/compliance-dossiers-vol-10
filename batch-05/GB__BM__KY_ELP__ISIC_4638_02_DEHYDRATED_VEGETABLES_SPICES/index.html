Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (payroll tax, social insurance, customs and stamp duty administration) and the Registrar of Companies, Department of Corporate Services (company/ELP registration). Official portals: https://www.gov.bm and https://www.roc.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is not granted an income-tax TIN. Registration is effected through a licensed Bermuda resident agent with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2009; the ELP receives a Certificate of Exemption and an ELP/companies register number. If the ELP hires Bermuda-resident employees, an employer payroll-tax and social-insurance registration must be filed with the Office of the Tax Commissioner. There is no VAT ID as Bermuda has no VAT/GST. No minimum capital is required; non-resident ownership is unrestricted. Online filing is available through Bermuda Business Express / the Registry’s portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; territorial scope: Bermuda does not impose corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. An ELP is tax-transparent and only Bermuda-sourced payroll/social insurance liabilities (and customs duties on importation) arise. The ELP is non-resident for exchange-control purposes and eligible for exemption from local taxation.

Available election forms

None under Bermuda law: there is no check-the-box election, no hybrid-mismatch election, and an ELP cannot elect corporation status in Bermuda; it is treated as a transparent partnership for all Bermuda fiscal purposes. Foreign owners may nonetheless apply their own domestic transparent/opaque classification rules, and must complete FATCA/CRS partnership self-certification forms where required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax in Bermuda; indirect taxes consist of customs import duties, payroll tax, and stamp duty. Export sales of dehydrated vegetables/spices are not within the Bermuda VAT net.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST (no such tax exists). For Bermuda customs purposes, a commercial importer must obtain a customs importer registration/registry bond irrespective of turnover; the effective registration threshold is zero for any trader importing dutiable goods into Bermuda. Exportation from Bermuda does not itself require turnover-based registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating claim or VAT refund mechanism. Bermuda customs duty is not levied on exports. Compliance therefore centres on truthfully declaring the consignment as an export to Bermuda Customs, retaining shipping and payment records, and satisfying any phytosanitary/agricultural requirements under the Bermuda Department of Environment and Natural Resources (for dehydrated vegetables/spices).

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration/Entry (through Bermuda Customs or appointed agent) where the consignment is subject to out-of-territory declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list for the dehydrated vegetables/spices
  • Certificate of Origin (issued to satisfy the importing jurisdiction if required)
  • Phytosanitary certificate / customs food-safety documentation where required by the importing country
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds

No statutory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime applies in Bermuda. Importers/exporters must file customs declarations through the Bermuda Customs electronic systems (or approved broker software) and retain source documents; the Office of the Tax Commissioner requires periodic payroll returns where employers are registered.

No VAT clawback regime exists in Bermuda because no VAT is chargeable. False customs export declarations, under-declaration, smuggling or duty evasion are investigated by Bermuda Customs and the Department of Public Prosecutions: goods may be forfeited; penalties may reach treble the duty/value involved; custodial sentences may apply under the Customs Management Act. Because the commercial buyer is likely to claim VAT relief in its destination jurisdiction, the exporting owner must hold the full documentary bundle for at least 180 days from export; failure in the destination audit can lead to foreign VAT clawback from the customer, joint-and-several liability notices (e.g. HMRC in the UK), civil penalties, and loss of zero-rating on future overseas sales.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to USD. The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), once designated 'non-resident' for exchange-control purposes (standard for Bermuda ELPs), enjoys full convertibility on both current and capital account transactions. Profit remittances to UK and foreign partners attract zero Bermuda withholding tax and require no BMA exchange-control approval. Practical friction is limited to bank-level screening: wires above USD 500,000 may be deferred 1–2 business days for source-of-funds verification. Capital repatriation following a dissolution is similarly unrestricted for non-resident designated ELPs.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or remittance timeline on export proceeds for non-resident designated ELPs. The Exchange Control Act 1972 requires only resident entities to submit quarterly statistical returns (Form E/1) to the BMA; the ELP is exempt by virtue of its non-resident designation, permitting indefinite retention of export receipts in USD, EUR, or GBP as primary operating currency.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — central bank and financial services regulator, administering the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, and the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH in principle, MEDIUM in operational practice. Legal constraints: (1) BMA exchange control does not license non-Bermudian EMIs, so a Bermuda ELP cannot treat an EMI as its sole settlement bank; (2) UK-regulated EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Tide) may onboard the ELP remotely under UK MLR 2017, but all BMD-denominated and Bermuda vendor payments must clear through a Bermudian authorized dealer; (3) the BMA's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 does not extend passports to foreign EMIs. Recommended structure: UK EMI for GBP receivable acceleration and EUR payables combined with a Butterfield USD sweep account for Bermudian trade settlement.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - no Bermuda corporate income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or capital duty is imposed on an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP); tax neutrality is an inherent statutory consequence of the exempted status.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no statutory withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or profit transfers paid by an Exempted Limited Partnership to its partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before 31 May following the end of the preceding calendar year, unless extended by the Registrar of Companies; the annual government fee must accompany the return.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing deadline is generally within six months after the ELP's financial year end; the Tax Authority may require notification of the financial year end upon registration and issue a specific filing deadline.

Deadline: · Frequency:

No Bermuda statutory deadline; the applicable deadline derives from the foreign tax law of the partner/parent jurisdiction, e.g., UK Corporation Tax self-assessment returns generally due 12 months after the end of the period of account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A - Bermuda has no local currency transfer pricing documentation threshold and no statutory requirement to prepare Local File or Master File for income tax purposes. A prudential threshold of USD 1,000,000 in connected-party cross-border transactions is recommended because UK CFC/Pillar Two and OECD BEPS review may impose documentation obligations on the UK parent or non-resident partners.

Economic substance scope: Under Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations 2018, any ELP that is tax resident in Bermuda and carries on a relevant activity must maintain adequate substance. A bulk dehydrated vegetables and spices trading vehicle engaged in purchasing from connected suppliers and selling to connected distributors falls squarely within 'distribution and service centre business', requiring physical office presence, full-time staff in Bermuda, local board/general partner management, sufficient operating expenditure, and performance of core income-generating activities in Bermuda. If the ELP is not tax resident in Bermuda and instead files as non-resident, it must notify the Registrar and prove non-residence with all relevant income generated outside Bermuda; failure to satisfy the conduct test results in a pure-shift of profits back to the foreign parent under OECD rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Sham Arbitrage / UK Criminal Finances Act

Statutory sanction: HMRC can re-open prior assessments, impose penalties up to 100% of the diverted tax, charge interest, and prosecute for fraudulent evasion of income tax / cheating the public revenue. Under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017, a corporate entity that fails to prevent the facilitation of tax evasion by the ELP's fronting person is liable to an unlimited fine.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners are personally liable for conspiracy to defraud HMRC, subject to disqualification, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 7 years. The corporate veil may be pierced where the ELP is found to be a mere façade, causing the general partner's personal assets to be attached for unpaid tax.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement under OECD BEPS and Bermuda ESR

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Registrar may impose a penalty up to USD 250,000 plus daily default fines, revoke the ELP's registration, and refer the matter to the Financial Investigative Agency. At the OECD level, an artificial arrangement attracts a substance over form denial, 70% OECD/G20 PLUS denied treaty benefits, and potential mandatory disclosure penalties under BEPS Action 12.

Director personal liability: General partners who consciously maintain a shell ELP may face equitable compensation, freezing orders, and disqualification orders. Where the general partner is a Bermudian corporate director company, its directors can be criminally liable for false ESR declarations and be banned from serving as corporate directors in Bermuda.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Register Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations 2008, an offending ELP is subject to unlimited fines and its officers are liable to imprisonment up to 14 years. The Bermuda Monetary Authority can impose administrative penalties, suspend or revoke the corporate service provider licence of the entity's resident agent.

Director personal liability: Limited partners lose the benefit of limited liability if they engage in management or fail to supervise AML obligations; general partners are jointly and severally liable for all fines, and directors of the corporate general partner are subject to individual prosecution, travel restrictions, and seizure of offshore assets.
Transfer Pricing and Economic Substance Underallocation

Statutory sanction: Although Bermuda does not impose TP penalties, the UK parent will suffer transfer pricing adjustment under CTA 2010/ICTESA in the form of an upward profit adjustment, interest, and penalties up to 200% of the underpaid tax; where the adjustment creates an additional tax charge in the UK, Bermuda's income tax holiday does not mitigate the foreign penalty.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign inaccurate UK group TP documentation are personally exposed to HMRC 'senior accounting officer' penalties, up to GBP 5,000 per material inaccuracy, plus personal assessment for deliberate misstatements; general partners may be joined in offshore enforcement proceedings if funds are traced into their personal bank accounts.
Foreign Tax Evasion Facilitation and DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: UK and OECD treaty abuse rules deny the DTA benefit, trigger the UK General Anti-Abuse Rule (GAAR) with 60% DPT rate, or a 100% tax-geared penalty for deliberate unpaid tax; the transaction itself may be recharacterized with retroactive effect, causing imputed interest and dividend withholding tax to arise.

Director personal liability: The General Partner of the ELP is personally liable for the value of the abusive payments received, the company veil is pierced as a transaction-sham remedy, and the directors are barred from obtaining new Bermuda exemptions; the foreign court may issue a worldwide freezing order preventing disposal of assets.
