E-government filing portal

No general statutory VAT or income-tax TIN registration applies to a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership. A foreign-owned ELP with no Bermuda employees has no registration obligations. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists because Bermuda does not impose entity-level income tax. Tax transparency follows the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 and the general partnership law of Bermuda. For foreign tax purposes, U.S. check-the-box regulations and UK hybrid rules (CTA 2010 Part 6AA) may independently recharacterise the ELP; those elections are made outside Bermuda and are not registrable with the Tax Commissioner.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax in Bermuda; the relevant indirect tax system is customs duties on imports, together with duties and fees administered by Bermuda Customs under the Customs Act 1948 and Customs Tariff Act 1972.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. Bermuda has no VAT-style economic-turnover registration threshold and no indirect tax registration requirement for the ELP's worldwide sales. Customs duty is triggered by the first importation, and payroll tax registration is triggered by the first Bermudian-resident employee, regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in VAT terms because Bermuda has no value-added tax and therefore no VAT zero-rating claim. Exported physical designer toys are outside the Bermuda customs duty base, which applies to imports; however, exports must be correctly declared at the point of export. Purely offshore toy design, licensing, and merchandising services supplied to non-Bermudian customers do not attract any Bermuda indirect tax.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the designer blind-box toys and delivery terms
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing the goods' departure from Bermuda
  • Proof of payment or inward remittance certificate where a financial institution requires evidence of the foreign sale proceeds

Not applicable. There is no real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate for Bermudian B2B or B2C invoices. All import and export declarations must be filed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs management system; the exporter/importer records in that system serve as the digital clearance record.

False declarations on export, under-declaration of value, or misdescription of vinyl art collectibles can result in seizure and forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties of up to three times the applicable duty, cancellation of any duty-relief approval, and prosecution of directors or partners. Because there is no VAT, there is no export VAT clawback; however, if goods were imported with duty relief or temporary admission, Bermuda Customs may require re-export proof within a set period and recover relieved duty upon non-compliance. Export proceedings also face AML/CFT scrutiny for invoice mismatches, over/under-invoicing, and unusual routing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom maintains full current and capital account convertibility under IMF Article VIII: no exchange controls, no prior approval for inbound or outbound transfers, and no restriction on profit remittance, dividend or partnership distributions, interest, management fees, or liquidation proceeds. The Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) may invoice in Sterling (GBP), US dollars (USD), or other major currencies as the commercial model dictates; funds entering UK-owned bank accounts may be converted spot at the clearing bank without HM Treasury consent. The only residual hurdle is documentary: payments to or from jurisdictions on the UK sanctions and AML lists will be blocked by UK bank sanctions-screening engines.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable to the United Kingdom. The UK abolished its exchange-control surrender regime in 1979; there is no statutory timeline to convert export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio. USD export and marketplace proceeds may be retained indefinitely in USD Nostro balances at the UK clearing bank and converted at the treasury's discretion on a monthly hedging schedule. The ELP's repatriation of net trading profits to Bermuda limited partners is equally unrestricted, subject only to bank-level source-of-funds verification and FATCA/CRS reporting.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (prudential regulator and FX liberalisation oversight); the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) remains the supervisory authority for Bermuda-licensed banks, although the Bermuda ELP vehicle itself requires no BMA authorisation for this UK-centric treasury strategy.

Feasibility rating: Medium-High with selection discipline. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no transfer pricing rules and therefore no documentation threshold. Under the Economic Substance Regulations, however, a relevant entity conducting a relevant activity must satisfy a core-income test and proportionate local expenditure test; there is no de minimis revenue threshold. Pure toy manufacturing does not trigger ESR substance filing.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations apply only to entities conducting 'relevant activities' in Bermuda, including banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centre, and intellectual property business. Manufacturing and airbrush finishing of vinyl art collectibles (ISIC 3240) is not a relevant activity. Therefore the ELP is outside the substantive footprint requirement unless it separately engages in IP holding or distribution from Bermuda; any related-party arrangement must still document ordinary commercial substance and be prepared to rebut anti-abuse inferences.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Payroll Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Civil reassessment of unpaid payroll taxes plus penalties up to 100% of the underpaid amount; criminal fines up to BD$100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years on indictment for evasion under the Payroll Tax Act.

Director personal liability: General partners may be personally liable for remuneration illegally characterised as distributions; a court may pierce the partnership to award restitution, impose a travel ban pending settlement, and convert any evasion into custodial liability for the senior managing partner.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Failure of economic substance for a relevant activity: penalty under the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2020 up to BD$250,000 per instance, plus a recovering daily penalty of BD$250; the Registrar may apply to strike off the partnership, and offshore banking relationships may be terminated.

Director personal liability: Bermuda courts will look beyond the ELP structure where the general partner had responsibility for the substance failure; personal liability extends to civil restitution of fees received, travel restrictions until ESR compliance is certified, and imprisonment up to 2 years for deliberate false declarations.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) / Counter-Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties for missed CDD, enhanced due diligence, or failure to file a suspicious activity report: up to BD$250,000 per breach; criminal prosecution under the Proceeds of Crime Act may lead to fines and imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering.

Director personal liability: The general partner is a 'senior managing official'; if the offence is committed with consent or connivance, that individual is personally liable to confiscation orders and civil recovery, and a travel ban will be imposed pending completion of the AML investigation.
False Statements and Fraudulent Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Summary conviction: fine up to BD$50,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years. Indictment: fine up to BD$150,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years for fraudulent misrepresentation; the Registrar may also deregister the ELP and declare its limited liability void.

Director personal liability: The individual signing the return is personally liable for the false statement; the corporate/partnership veil is pierced when fraud is proven, permitting creditors and tax authorities to attach the partner's personal assets, mandate civil restitution of all improperly remitted profits, and enforce an international travel ban.
