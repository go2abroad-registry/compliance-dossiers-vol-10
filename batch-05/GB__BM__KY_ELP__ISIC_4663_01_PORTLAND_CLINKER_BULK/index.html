Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance; online portal: www.tax.gov.bm (Bermuda Tax and Social Insurance system)

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must first be registered with the Registrar of Companies to obtain a Bermuda Entity Number (BEN). The foreign partner must then apply for a Bermuda Tax Account Number (BTAN) through the Tax Commissioner using the entity's incorporation deed, registered office address, and designated agent details. No minimum capital or local ownership is required; issuance typically takes 5 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must first be registered with the Registrar of Companies to obtain a Bermuda Entity Number (BEN). The foreign partner must then apply for a Bermuda Tax Account Number (BTAN) through the Tax Commissioner using the entity's incorporation deed, registered office address, and designated agent details. No minimum capital or local ownership is required; issuance typically takes 5 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

The Bermuda ELP may elect for pass-through treatment under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT) Act 2023, ensuring that only the partners are taxable. Non-resident partners without a Bermuda permanent establishment may elect to be treated as outside the scope of Bermuda CIT. For U.S. partners, an IRS Form 8832 check-the-box election may be filed to classify the ELP as a partnership. The entity is not subject to UK hybrid mismatch elections in the absence of UK tax residence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda Customs Duty (no general VAT or GST)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no economic turnover threshold for VAT/GST. Customs registration and a valid customs broker mandate are required for any import or export consignment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda does not levy VAT; therefore, no VAT zero-rating mechanism exists. For customs duty, goods exported from Bermuda are exempted from export duty under the Customs Tariff Act 1985 where proof of export is provided. A bulk Portland clinker wholesaler shipping via ocean freight to a non-Bermuda destination without berthing in Bermuda is outside the Bermudian customs territory, and no indirect tax applies.

  • Bermuda Export Declaration (BSAD lodgement)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading
  • Commercial Invoice (full description and vessel details)
  • Certificate of Origin (if required by destination country)
  • Transhipment or transit entry (if goods touch Bermuda waters)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence

Bermuda uses the Bermuda Customs Integrated Management System (CIMS) and requires submission of the Bermuda Single Administrative Document (BSAD) electronically for all ship and cargo clearances. There is no real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital invoice mandate for wholesale; commercial invoices are submitted as PDF/electronic attachments through the customs broker.

Under Bermuda's Customs Revenue Regulations, any fraudulent or careless export claim triggers retrospective reassessment within 5 years, a penalty of up to 300% of the duty evaded, a monetary fine not exceeding BSD 100,000 (approx. USD 100,000), and imprisonment for up to 5 years. The Tax Commissioner may also suspend or revoke customs broker licenses of implicated parties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 parity. Current-account transactions — trade receivables/payables, management fees, insurance, and operating disbursements — are freely convertible through BMA-licensed authorised dealers for non-resident-designated accounts. Capital-account convertibility is also broad for an Exempted Limited Partnership: equity and loan injections, partnership capital withdrawals, and profit remittances to non-resident partners do not require individual Exchange Control Act licences, provided the ELP is designated non-resident and does not carry on business in Bermuda. The only material hurdles arise for land/real-estate ownership, resident-designated securities, lending against Bermudian collateral, or any Bermuda-source investment income, each of which may require BMA or other regulatory approvals. Profit distribution to the UK parent is structurally unimpeded because Bermuda imposes no withholding tax and the ELP is treated as a pass-through entity for the partners' own tax filings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio. Export receipts may be retained in USD or any freely usable currency in a non-resident designated account for an indefinite period; there is no statutory six-month conversion timeline and no obligation to sell foreign currency to the BMA. If the ELP is classified as a resident-designated account (which is not expected here), the BMA's Exchange Control Regulations would require dealings only through authorised dealers and could impose reporting, but still do not compel conversion of USD due to the 1:1 BMD/USD peg.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — Bermuda's central bank and exchange-control administrator, operating within the United Kingdom's Overseas Territories framework; UK HM Treasury retains final monetary sovereignty, with the FCA/PRA supervising the UK parent bank side.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High with strict conditions. UK FCA-authorised electronic money institutions and specialist cross-border payment platforms are viable secondary rails for GBP/EUR receipts, supplier payments, and treasury pooling; they are not a substitute for a Bermuda bank account. Legal constraints include: (i) no EMIs are licensed as banks in Bermuda, so any offshore EMI must be FCA or equivalent-authority supervised; (ii) the EMI must segregate client funds in safeguarding accounts with a UK PRA-authorised bank; (iii) the Bermuda ELP's lack of public beneficial-ownership register and bulk cement trade generates heightened enhanced due-diligence, and many digital-only EMIs will reject letter-of-credit, charterparty, or freight settlement flows without an underlying bank guarantee; (iv) the EMI application must not have been previously denied by a bank without disclosure to the chosen EMI or Bermuda bank; and (v) BMA exchange-control reporting obligations remain with the ELP even if payments are executed through a non-Bermuda EMI.

Rapid movement of funds through the partnership, including fast distribution on to partners or third-party factoring/forfaiting counterparties.

Root cause: Rapid movement of funds through the partnership, including fast distribution on to partners or third-party factoring/forfaiting counterparties.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections and initial subscribed funds should be routed by MT103 from the UK parent's corporate bank to the Bermuda ELP's account at an approved Bermuda bank, using a named partner as remitter and the exact registered legal name of the ELP as beneficiary. The SWIFT field 50K must show the UK parent, not an unknown third party; field 59K must match the certificate of limited partnership. The remitting bank should apply ISO 20022 purpose code 'INTC' (intra-company payment) or a capital-transfer/parent funding code required by UK BOP reporting, and the description line must state: 'Capital contribution to Bermuda ELP for portland clinker wholesale trade.' For large balances, an MT202 COV cover message may be used in conjunction with the MT103. The beneficiary bank will verify: director/partner resolution approving the contribution; certified copy of the amended partnership schedule; source-of-funds bank statements for the preceding six months; CRS/FATCA self-certifications; and BMA-supervised authorisation where the account is denominated in BMD. No crypto bridge, cash custody, or customer aggregation account is permitted.For freight, charter hire, port expenses, warehousing, and supplier payments to the mill or loading terminal, the principal clearing rail is an outbound SWIFT MT103 direct wire using the recipient bank's SWIFT BIC and IBAN/account number, each backed by a matching commercial invoice, charterparty contract, loadport/dischargeport cost statement, or proforma supplier invoice. For major clinker/cement purchase contracts exceeding the bank's comfort level, use a UCP600-compliant commercial letter of credit: issuing bank sends MT700/MT701, applicant is the Bermuda ELP, beneficiary is the overseas cement/clinker seller; MT707 covers amendments; presentation documents typically include signed commercial invoice, full set of clean on-board bills of lading made out to the order of the issuing bank, insurance certificate, weight certificate, and independent surveyor report. The bank will additionally require the ELP to maintain a funds-based or guarantee-backed limit, invoices not double-counted, and sanctions-compliant vessel name/IMO details. All payments must preserve the purpose code 'TRAD' (trade) or appropriate freight/hire code so that the UK/Bermuda balance-of-payments record reconciles.Deliverable USD/BMD FX forwards at 1:1 parity, transacted only with a Bermuda authorised dealer or UK PRA-authorised counterparty bank, for hedging dollarised capital account balances.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in emerging-market or non-convertible currencies (e.g., CNY, IDR, VND, PYG, EGP) where the cement/clinker supplier's domestic currency is not fully deliverable and a notional settlement is required in USD, provided the underlying trade exposure is documented with invoices and signed purchase orders.Cross-currency swaps between USD, GBP, EUR, and BMD to match the ELP's treasury assets against trade liabilities, including structured swaps where the BMD leg is pegged 1:1 to USD and the swap is governed by ISDA 2002 and subject to BMA exchange-control general permission.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold exists. The ELP must nevertheless retain contemporaneous records demonstrating Bermuda substance, headcount, expenditure, management meetings, and the arm’s length character of related-party cement purchase and freight charter contracts for HMRC or other foreign tax authority review.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations apply. The ELP’s wholesale distribution of hydraulic cement and coordinated ocean freight requires the ELP to carry on core income-generating activities in Bermuda through adequate employees, premises, and expenditure. If the ELP also functions as a shipping business, separate shipping CIGA requirements apply. Pure outsourcing or holding-company labelling will fail the economic substance test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false economic substance declarations

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines, daily default penalties, deregistration, compulsory information exchange with HMRC or other competent authorities, and forfeiture of any benefit obtained through the false declaration.

Director personal liability: The general partner or designated manager who signs a false declaration is personally liable to fines and imprisonment; limited partners who participate in management lose the statutory limited-liability shield.
Shell company or inadequate economic substance

Statutory sanction: Failure of the economic substance test; financial penalties, daily continuation penalties, and disclosure of the ELP’s information to foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: The corporate general partner’s directors and officers may be disqualified, made subject to compliance orders, and held personally liable for unpaid penalties or government fees if the partnership is treated as a sham.
AML and beneficial ownership non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties under Bermuda’s Beneficial Ownership Transparency Act and the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations, including fines, record-keeping penalties, and suspicious activity report failures.

Director personal liability: General partners, compliance officers, and directors of a corporate general partner may be personally fined, imprisoned, or subjected to asset freezing orders and travel restrictions while investigations are pending.
Cross-border related-party structuring and UK anti-avoidance

Statutory sanction: HMRC may recharacterise transactions, impose transfer-pricing adjustments, apply the UK CFC or diverted profits tax regime, and assess interest and penalties for deliberate non-disclosure.

Director personal liability: UK-resident partners may be personally assessed; directors of a UK corporate partner who approve or conceal non-compliant structures may face penalties, disqualification, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion in serious cases.
