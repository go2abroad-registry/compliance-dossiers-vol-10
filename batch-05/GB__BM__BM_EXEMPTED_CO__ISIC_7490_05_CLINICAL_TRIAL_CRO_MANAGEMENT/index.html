Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow account requirement under Bermuda law. The only capital condition is the authorized share capital stated in the Memorandum of Association; standard exempted company practice is USD 12,000 authorized capital, with the minimum government incorporation and first-year annual fee of approximately USD 1,725 paid to the Registrar.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Government of Bermuda (payroll tax, customs duty administration, and 15% Corporate Income Tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope multinational groups); Registrar of Companies (RoC), Ministry of Finance (exempted company registration, Permissions to Carry on Business, and Economic Substance Returns). Filing portals: https://www.otc.bm (CIT/GMT), https://www.esr.ebermuda.com (ESR), and https://www.roc.gov.bm (company registry).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated by filing a Memorandum of Association with the RoC through a licensed Bermuda registered agent; the RoC issues a Company Registration Number (CRN), which serves as the statutory taxpayer identifier. No VAT/GST identifier exists because Bermuda imposes no general consumption tax. If the company employs Bermuda-resident workers, it must register with the OTC for employer payroll tax and obtain a payroll tax account number. A company conducting business in Bermuda (including maintaining a local office or staff) must additionally hold a Permission to Carry on Business issued by the RoC for an initial period of up to 5 years with renewal obligations. If the company belongs to a multinational group with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more, the constituent entities must register with the OTC under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 via the CIT e-portal and obtain a CIT reference number; all exempted companies, regardless of scope, must file an annual Economic Substance Return through the ESR portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda domestic law provides no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. An exempted company enjoys a statutory certificate of exemption from Bermudian income, capital gains, withholding, and estate taxes for a period currently extending to 31 March 2035, notwithstanding the application of the 15% Corporate Income Tax to in-scope multinational groups. For US federal tax purposes, an owner may file IRS Form 8832 to classify the Exempted Company as a disregarded entity or as an association taxable as a corporation. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, an in-scope group may elect to treat the Bermudian domestic minimum top-up tax as the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) safe harbour to relieve foreign top-up taxes, and may elect the OECD Pillar Two substance-based income exclusion computations applicable to payroll and tangible assets.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales and use tax - Bermuda levies no general indirect tax on supplies of services; customs duty applies only to physical importation of tangible goods

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - there is no indirect tax registration threshold in Bermuda. Customs duty tariffs (0% to 33% by harmonised tariff classification) are border taxes on imported goods and are not triggered by turnover. Clinical trial CRO management services exported by the Bermuda Exempted Company to foreign sponsors are entirely outside any Bermuda indirect tax base.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, no export zero-rating mechanism exists to claim. CRO and clinical trial management services rendered by the Bermuda Exempted Company to foreign biopharmaceutical sponsors (US/EU/APAC clients) are outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax and carry no tax charge. If the company imports physical clinical trial supplies into Bermuda, customs duty is payable unless a specific statutory exemption, temporary importation relief, or duty drawback applies.

None. Bermuda maintains no mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API connectivity, or digital stamping regime for commercial invoices. The only recurring statutory digital filings for a Bermuda Exempted Company are: (i) the annual Economic Substance Return filed through the ESR e-portal (https://esr.ebermuda.com); (ii) the annual company registration renewal and government fee computation; and (iii) for in-scope multinational groups, quarterly CIT instalments and annual CIT returns filed through the OTC CIT portal.

Bermuda imposes no exchange-control regime and no statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day convention is adopted as prudent evidence of arm's-length commercial conduct and to satisfy bank enhanced due diligence and economic substance expectations. No VAT export-fraud clawback regime exists because no VAT is in force. However, false statements or omissions in Economic Substance Returns attract civil penalties of up to BMD 250,000, continuing daily default fines for persistent non-compliance, and possible strike-off of the exempted company. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, failure to register, file, or pay the 15% domestic minimum top-up tax incurs statutory interest, late-payment penalties at prescribed percentages of unpaid tax, and penalties of up to BMD 1,000,000 for deliberate or fraudulent misrepresentation; erroneously issued CIT refunds are recoverable with interest and, where fraud or gross negligence is established, an additional penalty of up to 100% of the refund amount.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) and USD is accepted as legal tender in Bermuda. Current-account transactions for CRO services are freely convertible and remittable without prior BMA approval when routed through an authorized dealer. Capital-account transactions, including inward equity injections from the UK parent, dividend declarations, and liquidation proceeds, remain subject to exchange control consent; a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as an 'external (non-resident) company' may obtain blanket permissions for such flows. Profit remittance is not taxed locally, provided the exemption certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 is current and the entity maintains its non-resident exchange control status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Bermuda Exchange Control Regulations, a resident-designated entity must offer foreign currency receipts to an authorized dealer within the BMA-prescribed window (generally 30 days of credit), unless granted a currency retention facility. A Bermuda Exempted Company that secures non-resident (external) exchange control status is exempt from mandatory surrender; the CRO may therefore retain 100% of USD/BMD clinical trial milestone receipts in external multi-currency accounts, subject to annual attestation of continued non-residency and submission of a Form of Declaration to the BMA if requested.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), administering the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, and the Currency Act 1972; the BMA delegates routine approvals to authorized dealers (commercial banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium viability. UK/EU-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) do not possess a Bermuda deposit-taking license and may not maintain BMD-denominated balances for Bermuda-resident enterprises without breaching the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. The CRO may engage an overseas EMI as an ancillary payment rail for USD disbursements to non-Bermuda clinical sites (investigator grants, lab fees, sub-CRO settlements), provided the EMI does not solicit Bermuda clients, does not process BMD, and all settlement arrives in Bermuda only via the primary SWIFT correspondent. EMIs cannot replace the BMA-licensed bank relationship for exchange control reporting, capital escrow purposes, or tax certificate maintenance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the UK parent's equity injection into the Bermuda Exempted CRO: route the wire via HSBC UK or Barclays UK as the instructing bank, using SWIFT MT103 type code 'CAPT' (capital contribution) or, if the instruction system restricts type codes, field 72 narrative '/PURP/CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION UNDER COMPANIES ACT 1981'. Deliverables must be pre-notified to the receiving Bermuda bank via a compliance email containing the board resolution approving the issuance of shares, the BMA exchange control consent letter for non-resident capital payments, and the bank's KYC form for inward remittances. In practice, USD must clear through Fedwire/CHIPS via the Bermuda bank's US correspondent, e.g., Butterfield's USD clearing facility at Citibank New York (ABA 021000089) in favor of the BMD/USD external account. BMD conversion is automatic at the 1:1 peg; no separate FX ticket is required. Any inbound wire exceeding USD 1,000,000 may trigger a BMA balance-of-payments statistical return, which the bank files; the CRO must respond to the bank's Form D request within three business days. All funds should be received within a 5-business-day escrow window before being released to the operational account, supported by a capital escrow agreement signed by the parent, the Bermuda entity, and the bank.Outbound CRO payments, including investigator site grants, central laboratory settlements, sub-CRO fees, and patient indemnification, must be executed via SWIFT MT103 messages using the beneficiary bank's SWIFT BIC and intermediary bank routing through New York CHIPS/Fedwire for USD settlement; SWIFT GPI must be enabled for end-to-end tracking. Standard trade settlement for large procurement of clinical trial supplies (e.g., comparator drugs, lab kits, temperature-controlled logistics) of USD 500,000 or more should use an irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued by Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda pursuant to ICC UCP 600, with the UK sponsor's bank as confirming bank, to mitigate counterparty risk. For high-volume low-value disbursements to investigators in the EU/APAC, batch MT103s with separate beneficiary identifiers are required; ACH is not permissible for international settlement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (no Bermuda corporate income tax for exempted companies; a 15% CIT may apply only to very large multinational groups with consolidated revenue of at least €750M, effective from fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company to any shareholder, resident or non-resident)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within six months after the end of the financial year. For a 31 December year-end, the due date is 30 June; for a 30 June year-end, the due date is 31 December.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 60 days after the anniversary of the date of incorporation, or within 60 days after the annual general meeting if the company has adopted that alternative cycle.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Not applicable for this exempted company unless it is part of a large multinational group with consolidated revenue of €750M or more. For in-scope groups, the return is due 15 months after the fiscal year-end (or such extended period as the Bermuda Tax Administration provides).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to ordinary Bermuda exempted companies. For in-scope MNE groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, a Master File, Local File and Country-by-Country Report will be required where the consolidated group revenue is at least €750M (approximately BMD 750M). This CRO entity, absent such group size, is below any mandatory documentation trigger.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (ESR) apply to every exempted company that conducts a 'relevant activity' during a financial period. Contract research and management services supplied to related parties, including clinical trial protocol management, patient recruitment coordination and central data management, fall within the 'service center' category if the activity constitutes substantive support to other group entities. If the CRO serves exclusively independent unaffiliated clients, then no relevant activity exists and the entity must still confirm its position in the ESD. For in-scope activities, the company must maintain a physical office in Bermuda, employ an adequate number of qualified full-time employees in Bermuda, incur an appropriate amount of operating expenditure, and ensure all core income-generating activities are directed and managed in Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False ESD Declaration

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BMD 25,000 for the first offence, with a further BMD 10,000 per day for continuing non-compliance. On conviction for fraud, a fine of up to BMD 75,000 and/or imprisonment up to 2 years may be imposed. The Registrar may also strike the company off the register and forfeit its property.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign or approve a false declaration are personally liable. Courts may pierce the corporate veil to order civil restitution, impose travel bans, disqualify directors from acting in any Bermuda company, and impose custodial sentences of up to 2 years. Fines are enforceable against personal assets.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Registrar will assess the entity as non-compliant under the Economic Substance Regulations and may impose a penalty of BMD 250,000 per financial period, publish an adverse compliance notice, and share full information with the UK tax authorities and any foreign competent authority under exchange of information agreements. A final sanction is compulsory strike-off and seizure of assets.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable for substance failures. A court may issue travel bans and freezing orders, require restitution of profits, and disqualify directors for up to 10 years. If the shell arrangement is used as part of a tax evasion or money laundering scheme, the director may face imprisonment of up to 5 years.
AML Non-Compliance by the CRO

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to BMD 1,000,000 per violation, revocation of the company's clinical trial operating licence, and cancellation of the exempted company's registration. Criminal liability may include imprisonment of directors or senior officers for up to 5 years and confiscation orders under anti-money laundering legislation.

Director personal liability: Senior management and compliance officers are personally liable where they tolerated AML deficiencies. Courts will pierce the corporate veil to enforce monetary restitution, bar the individuals from any regulated activity in Bermuda, and may impose extradition-compatible criminal sanctions in cooperation with the UK National Crime Agency.
False Annual Return / Defective Filing

Statutory sanction: Daily penalty of BMD 500 per day of default; the Registrar may strike the company off the register, causing the company's assets to vest in the Crown. The company may also be subject to a financial penalty of up to BMD 50,000 for materially false filings under the Companies Act 1981.

Director personal liability: Every director is jointly and severally liable for the daily penalties. If false filings are made intentionally, directors may face criminal sanctions including imprisonment up to 2 years, personal restitution orders, and disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act. Failure to disclose the ultimate beneficial owner can expose directors to a separate penalty of BMD 30,000 and a further BMD 1,000 per day.
