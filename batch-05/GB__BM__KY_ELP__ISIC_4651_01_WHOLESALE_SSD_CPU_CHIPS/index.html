Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital and no escrow account requirement apply to a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contribution levels are fixed solely in the Limited Partnership Agreement, and the General Partner's statutory declaration confirms that at least one General Partner and one Limited Partner have subscribed to the partnership.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (national tax authority administering payroll tax and the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023); Registrar of Companies, Bermuda Registry, Ministry of Finance (entity registration and statutory partnership identifier); Bermuda Customs Service, Ministry of Finance (customs duty and transit/re-export clearance); official e-services portal: www.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Register the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992; the issued Certificate of Registration and registration number serve as the ELP's primary statutory and tax reference because Bermuda does not issue a separate TIN for exempted partnerships. Step 2 - Register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax only if the ELP employs individuals working in Bermuda. Step 3 - Register for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) with the Office of the Tax Commissioner only if the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more; otherwise no CIT registration is required. Step 4 - Obtain a customs declarant code from the Bermuda Customs Service if the ELP imports, exports, or transships goods through Bermuda. A foreign entity with no Bermuda employees, no Bermuda premises, and no MNE-group CIT exposure has no mandatory Bermuda tax registration and may evidence its status by its Certificate of Registration and an optional tax assurance letter from the Ministry of Finance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. An ELP is fiscally transparent and tax-neutral in Bermuda (general income tax rate of 0%), so classification elections are only relevant under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope MNE groups. Available elections include: (i) single-filing election whereby the Bermuda ultimate parent entity submits a consolidated CIT return for Bermuda constituent entities; (ii) irrevocable election to apply the Qualifying Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT) before foreign parent jurisdictions; (iii) exclusion elections for governmental, non-profit, pension, and international shipping entities; and (iv) OECD Pillar Two administrative elections for investment entities, offshore/onshore pooling, and safe harbour relief consistent with the OECD Agreed Administrative Guidance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales and use tax exists in Bermuda. As a British Overseas Territory, Bermuda sits outside the UK VAT area, so a Bermudian ELP has no UK VAT registration obligation absent a UK-established presence or a UK-nexus taxable supply. Bermuda's only indirect tax is customs import duty imposed by the Customs Tariff Act 1965, chargeable exclusively on goods entering Bermuda for local consumption; exports, transit, and re-exports are not subject to duty.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A in BMD - Bermuda levies no VAT/GST, so no local-currency turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration. A customs declarant code is required per importer of record regardless of annual import value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so there is no export VAT zero-rating claim, no refund mechanism, and no statutory export-rate certificate to obtain. SSDs and server CPUs exported from or transshipped through Bermuda attract no Bermudian duty; traders must simply declare the goods as exports or goods in transit. For completeness, if the ELP were ever part of an in-scope MNE group (EUR 750M+ consolidated revenue), the 15% Bermuda CIT could reach offshore wholesale profit under OECD GloBE rules, but that is an income tax matter and carries no zero-rating or refund feature.

  • Bermuda Customs electronic export entry or transit/transshipment declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical movement of SSD and CPU chips
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Certificate of Origin (when preferential or statistical treatment is claimed)
  • SWIFT MT103 / inward remittance evidence of export proceeds settlement, retained for the 180-day FX repatriation file
  • ELP Certificate of Registration and underlying trade documentation

Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal e-invoicing, continuous transaction controls (CTC), API-based clearance, or digital stamping for B2B wholesale or brokerage transactions. Import and export/transit declarations must be filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs single-window e-Declaration system; clearance occurs upon accepted declaration, duty payment or security, and any risk-flagged physical inspection. No electronic invoice clearance is required for spot semiconductor trades.

No export VAT clawback exists because no VAT is chargeable. Customs misdeclarations, including false transit or re-export declarations, are penalized under the Customs Act 1965 and the Customs Tariff Act 1965 by fines, forfeiture of goods, and potential imprisonment on conviction. If the ELP becomes subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the Tax Commissioner may invoke general anti-avoidance rules, recharacterize or disallow artificial export/transit structures, assess understated GloBE income, and impose penalties of up to 100% of the tax shortfall plus interest; knowingly false documents in a tax or customs return constitute criminal fraud offences.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general corporate income tax; a 15% corporate income tax applies solely to constituent entities of multinational enterprise (MNE) groups with consolidated annual revenue ≥ EUR 750 million under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory withholding tax on dividends and partnership distributions. Bermuda does not impose corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or distribution withholding tax on profits repatriated to foreign partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due by the last day of the fifteenth month following the end of the fiscal year (e.g., for a 31 December year-end, filing is due 31 March of the second subsequent year); quarterly estimated payments are required based on provisional CIT liabilities.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due not later than 6 months after the end of the ELP’s fiscal year (e.g., for a 31 December year-end, filing is due 30 June of the following year).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For Bermuda CIT purposes, a local file and master file are required where the combined group revenue equals or exceeds BMD 750 million (≈ EUR 750 million) and either: (i) related-party transactions in a fiscal year exceed BMD 1.5 million each, or (ii) aggregate related-party transactions exceed BMD 7.5 million. These thresholds mirror the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and are the first applicable Bermuda TP documentation regime; a normal off-shore ELP wholesale operation will not meet these thresholds.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations apply to legal entities registered in Bermuda (including ELPs) that undertake any of the listed 'relevant activities'. Wholesale and retail distribution of goods from third parties is a relevant activity. However, a tax-transparent ELP that is not tax-resident in Bermuda and does not carry on the relevant activity from a fixed place in Bermuda may fall outside the substance requirements; it must still file an annual ESR declaration. Where the ELP has Bermuda management and is a 'registered entity', it must demonstrate economic substance by showing: (i) local generation and management of strategic decision-making, (ii) sufficient full-time employees, (iii) physical business premises, and (iv) annual operating expenditure proportionate to the value of the activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Tax Administration Act 2014, conviction for tax evasion carries a fine not exceeding BMD 100,000 and up to 5 years imprisonment; the partnership may be struck off and its assets forfeited.

Director personal liability: General partners of an ELP may be personally liable for restitution of evaded tax plus penalties; a travel ban and export order may be issued under the Proceeds of Crime Act; imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering offences.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may impose an economic substance fine of BMD 100,000 for the first offence and BMD 250,000 for subsequent offences; in egregious cases, the ELP is struck off and a declaration of non-compliance is published.

Director personal liability: Each general partner is jointly and severally liable for the penalties; the court may order disqualification from acting as a partner or director for up to 5 years; personal breach of ESR duty may attract a separate fine and up to 1 year imprisonment.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations 2008, the Financial Intelligence Agency can impose a civil penalty up to BMD 250,000 per violation; criminal conviction carries a fine and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner’s compliance officer assume direct personal liability; failure to file a Suspicious Activity Report can lead to custodial sentences of up to 2 years and a permanent ban from holding a licence under the Trust or Company Service Providers Act.
Misapplication of Treaty Relief / False Compliance Statement

Statutory sanction: A false statement made to a foreign tax authority constitutes fraud at common law and a breach of the Bermuda Criminal Code; penalties include an unlimited fine, seizure of assets, and extradition agreements enabling prosecution in the country of the treaty abuse.

Director personal liability: Any signatory partner of the ELP who knowingly makes a false declaration is criminally liable; civil remedies include recovery of all unpaid withholding taxes, interest at 2% monthly, and a court order freezing personal bank accounts; imprisonment of up to 7 years may also be imposed.
