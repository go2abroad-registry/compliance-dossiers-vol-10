Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), United Kingdom; online service portals: GOV.UK Personal Tax Account, HMRC VAT online services, HM Revenue & Customs Making Tax Digital for VAT service (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc), and the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) at https://www.gov.uk/customs-declaration-service.

E-government filing portal

A non-UK established exempted limited partnership (ELP) whose partners are non-resident and which carries on a UK taxable economic activity (including online beauty retail through a UK gateway) must first appoint either a UK tax representative or, if eligible, apply directly under HMRC's non-resident registration procedures. The partnership must obtain a UK Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) by submitting Form SA401 (partnership registration for non-resident partners) or through the HMRC Online Partnership Registration Service; a separate UTR and 10-digit reference is issued to the partnership, while each non-resident partner is advised to secure an individual National Insurance number or UTR for self-assessment. Where UK VAT registration is required (including under the non-UK established VAT scheme or the One Stop Shop 'OSS' for distance sales to consumers), the partnership or its tax representative must file Form VAT1A through the HMRC VAT Registration Service, supplying the partnership agreement, evidence of identity of each general partner, proof of business address, and projected UK taxable turnover. Upon approval, HMRC issues a GB + 9-digit VAT registration number. The Bermuda tax authority (Bermuda Registrar of Companies and the Ministry of Finance) imposes no income tax or VAT registration obligation on the exempted partnership itself; the ELP is required to file an annual Economic Substance Declaration (ESD) with the Bermuda Registrar where it carries on a relevant activity, and to maintain a registered office and local principal representative.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — The Bermuda exempted limited partnership is fiscally transparent under Bermuda law: Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT, and the ELP is not itself a taxable person; each limited partner is internationally tax-transparent unless locally resident. For UK tax purposes, HMRC disregards the ELP as a separate taxable entity and assesses each partner personally (or, if a corporate partner, under corporation tax) on their share of UK-source trading profits arising through the underlying operations. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — The Bermuda exempted limited partnership is fiscally transparent under Bermuda law: Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT, and the ELP is not itself a taxable person; each limited partner is internationally tax-transparent unless locally resident. For UK tax purposes, HMRC disregards the ELP as a separate taxable entity and assesses each partner personally (or, if a corporate partner, under corporation tax) on their share of UK-source trading profits arising through the underlying operations. A UK permanent establishment may arise for foreign partners where the beauty retail activity is carried on through a fixed place of business or dependent agent in the UK, creating a UK filing obligation. Bermuda Economic Substance Rules classify the ELP as a non-resident entity with an obligation to demonstrate economic substance only where it conducts a relevant activity; passive holding of investments and pure online retail intermediation without a Bermuda physical presence will generally be outside the scope of Bermuda economic substance requirements.

Available election forms

United Kingdom: HMRC does not operate a 'check-the-box' election regime; partnership transparency is statutory under the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005 and the Partnership (Tax) Regulations 1995. A partnership may elect, under certain conditions in the Taxation of Transparent Entities Regulations 2021, to be treated as opaque for certain double-taxation-treaty claims, and corporate partners may elect to have their partnership income taxed under the corporation tax regime as opposed to income tax. Hybrid mismatch rules under the Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010 Part 6TA disallow deductions and impose counteracting adjustments where structured arrangements exploit dual classification or no-tax outcomes. Bermuda: The ELP may elect within its partnership agreement the application of Bermuda's Exempted Partnerships Act 1992, including election for limited partner liability and internal governance; no Bermuda direct tax election exists because Bermuda has nil corporate taxation. Where the ELP is managed and controlled from the UK, HMRC could apply central management and control tests to treat it as UK resident to the extent that the majority of partner decision-making occurs in the UK.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 annual UK taxable turnover threshold (2024/25 fiscal year) for UK-established businesses; non-UK established businesses (including the Bermuda ELP) have no threshold and must register from their first UK taxable supply unless they use the UK OSS non-union scheme for cross-border B2C distance sales of goods; low-value consignment relief was abolished on 1 January 2021, so online marketplaces and 'deemed suppliers' must collect UK VAT on imported goods consignments not exceeding GBP 135 where the sale is facilitated through an electronic marketplace; beauty and cosmetic products imported for onward retail sale are subject to import VAT and customs duty at the border, with deferral available under the Postponed VAT Accounting (PV A) scheme for VAT-registered businesses.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A UK VAT-registered supplier (including the Bermuda ELP operating through a UK establishment) may zero-rate the supply of cosmetics and beauty products exported to a destination outside the UK where all of the following conditions are met: (i) the goods are physically exported from Great Britain or Northern Ireland to an overseas destination within the statutory time frame; (ii) the supplier holds valid commercial evidence of export — including the customs export declaration, bill of lading or airway bill, and payment/remittance records showing the consideration was received from the overseas buyer; (iii) the export is made by the supplier, the consignee's carrier, or an authorized agent, and the goods are not subsequently redirected back into the UK market; (iv) in the case of distance sales to EU consumers supplied via a UK online marketplace, zero-rating applies only where the supply has not already been routed through the OSS, and the business retains the full digital trail of orders, dispatch dates, and courier tracking. Offshore services — including digital marketing and back-office services provided by the ELP to its overseas beauty retail customers — are generally outside the scope of UK VAT or eligible for zero-rating where the recipient belongs in a non-UK jurisdiction and the supply falls within Article 59 VAT (Place of Supply of Services) rules; the supplier must retain a written 'belongs outside the UK' certificate or business evidence. The exporter must secure proof of export before the VAT return is submitted or, at latest, within the later of 3 months from the time of supply (freight export) or 6 months for goods exported by passengers in their luggage.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CDS SAD / electronic export declaration with MR number) referencing the Exporter's Registered Number (ERN) of the UK establishment and confirming the goods are exported outside the UK
  • Bill of Lading (sea freight) or Air Waybill (air freight) signed by the carrier, showing the exporting party, consignee outside the UK, quantity, and description of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the bank or a verified payment gateway remittance report (e.g., PayPal, Shopify Payments) evidencing receipt of foreign currency or GBP proceeds from the overseas buyer within the FX repatriation deadline
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Proof of order and dispatch trail, including purchase order, courier tracking status, and delivery confirmation signed by the overseas consignee

United Kingdom has no mandatory continuous transaction control (CTC) / real-time fiscal clearance model for e-invoicing as of the 2024/25 tax year; no digital stamping or government clearance of invoices is required. HMRC mandates Making Tax Digital for VAT (MTD-f), effective for VAT-registered entities, requiring that VAT accounting records be kept in digital form and that VAT Returns be submitted via HMRC's national digital platform using MTD-compatible API software; all returns must be transmitted functionally through an API bridge or a recognized third-party software provider. B2B and B2C e-invoicing is optional and may be issued as PDF, EDI, or in a structured electronic format; however, a valid VAT invoice is required to claim input VAT recovery, and the e-commerce payment gateway must preserve an audit trail of each transaction in the digital finance system. For goods exported out of the UK, customs declarations must be submitted electronically through the CDS (Customs Declaration Service), which is the only legal filing channel, replacing the old CHIEF system for new declarations.

HMRC treats export zero-rating as high-risk for fraud: where the supplier fails to demonstrate a genuine physical export, the zero-rating is denied and standard-rated VAT becomes due from the time of supply; penalties are assessed under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24 (accuracy-related penalty up to 100% of the VAT lost), Finance Act 2008 Schedule 41 (failure to notify, up to 100%) and FA 2008 Schedule 40 (VAT evasion, up to 100%). Deliberate and concealed withholding of VAT information attracts penalties of 100–200% of the VAT involved. Where the payment proof is missing or the FX receipts do not arrive within the 180-day deadline, HMRC may retrospectively claw back input VAT recovered and issue a VAT assessment for up to 4 years (extended to 20 years for off-shore structures and deliberate non-disclosure), together with interest at the statutory rate of base rate plus 2.5%. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims constitute an offence under VATA 1994 s.72 (evasion of VAT), carrying on conviction, a fine of up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited criminal compensation; civil 'falsity' penalties apply under FA 2007 for officers of corporate partners making false statements. The Bermuda ELP's offshore status will not shield partners from UK liability because HMRC will look through the partnership and may also serve joint and several liability notices on general partners and on the UK tax representative.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD). Current-account transactions are freely convertible through authorised dealers, with no BMA approval required for bona fide trade settlements, service payments, or distributor profit remittances. Capital-account transactions generally require Exchange Control permission unless a general permission applies. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) that is non-resident for Exchange Control purposes obtains a general permission to maintain accounts in foreign currency, transact with non-residents, and remit distributable profits to non-resident partners without individual exchange-control applications. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal because Bermuda does not impose withholding tax on partnership distributions, although the bank will require a board/GP resolution and a distribution certificate to evidence that the remittance represents realised, allocable profits rather than a capital repatriation requiring separate approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory statutory surrender of export proceeds for non-resident ELPs. The Exchange Control Regulations require resident persons, in limited circumstances, to offer surplus foreign currency to an authorised dealer, but non-resident exempted partnerships and their foreign-currency accounts are excluded from such surrender obligations. Therefore, no conversion ratio or statutory timeline applies to the ELP's USD sales proceeds from online cosmetics retail.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972. The UK's Exchange Control Act 1947 is not in force in Bermuda, and HM Treasury exercises no direct foreign-exchange or capital-control jurisdiction over Bermuda.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections from the United Kingdom or offshore investors to the Bermuda ELP must be routed via SWIFT MT103 using the ELP's BMD or USD account at an authorised Bermuda dealer. The bank instruction should clearly identify the beneficiary as the ELP and include field 70 (Reference for Beneficiary) as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – [Partner Name] – LPA [Date]'. The remitter should use the bank's BOP/purpose code for direct investment equity, typically 'DIA' or the bank-specific code for 'Other Direct Investment Capital', and must include the ELP's registered agent reference to facilitate BMA exchange-control monitoring. For any single capital payment exceeding BMD/USD 100,000, the receiving bank may require a Form A (Exchange Control declaration) or an equivalent BMA e-consent confirmation, and the partnership must be able to produce a subsequent certificate of capital contribution for auditors and future distribution controls.Trade settlements for cosmetics inventory and beauty products should use SWIFT MT103 for standard supplier payments with explicit invoice references and Incoterms. For high-value bulk purchase orders from non-affiliated overseas manufacturers, an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (UCP600) is the preferred clearing rail, confirmed by a UK or Bermudian bank, with the ELP as applicant and the supplier as beneficiary. Final Shopify/PayPal sales payouts should be settled directly into the Bermuda operational account in USD or BMD; where a UK domestic digit-currency or GBP account is necessary, the ELP may use a segregated UK GBP account operated by an FCA-authorised payment institution solely for payout conversion, after which funds are swept daily to the primary Bermuda bank via cross-currency SWIFT MT103. All outbound disbursements must be accompanied by underlying commercial documentation to preserve clean audit trail and avoid FX-control lookbacks.BMD/USD deliverable FX forwards with an authorised dealer in Bermuda (common because of the fixed peg, allowing zero-basis hedging).Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for exotic cross-currency conversion into GBP, EUR, or CNY where settlement in the non-convertible currency is not possible or where the ELP's bank does not maintain a correspondent line.Cross-currency swaps (BMD/USD against GBP/EUR) for medium-term cash-flow matching on large supplier inventory purchases and multi-currency revenue streams.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. UK-regulated Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, or Airwallex can be considered only if the Bermuda ELP is able to evidence a genuine UK nexus (e.g., a UK agent, UK-resident general partner, or a UK operating subsidiary). Most FCA-regulated EMIs do not onboard offshore Bermuda partnerships due to their restrictive jurisdiction lists under UK money-laundering regulations and e-money safeguarding rules. If an EMI account is opened, funds are safeguarded as e-money, not bank deposits, so no Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) protection applies. For the designated payment rails (Shopify Payments and PayPal), the ELP should use a licensed Bermuda bank as the core settlement account and treat EMI accounts, if used, only as secondary disbursement wallets for UK/European supplier payments.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda levies no general corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no corporation tax. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is tax-transparent; income is assessable in the hands of partners according to their respective residence jurisdictions.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, partnership distributions, capital gains, or liquidation proceeds paid by an Exempted Limited Partnership to its partners, whether resident or non-resident.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 (not applicable) — Bermuda maintains no statutory transfer pricing documentation regime and no Local File/Master File filing threshold. Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation should nonetheless be prepared if required by the parent entity's or partners' tax jurisdiction, and retained to evidence arm's-length pricing and economic substance. Currency: Bermudian Dollar (BMD), pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply to ELPs carrying on relevant activities, which include 'Distribution and Service Centre', 'Headquarters', 'Finance and Leasing', and 'Intellectual Property'. An ELP engaged in cross-border online retail of cosmetics may fall within 'Distribution and Service Centre' if it performs distribution or procurement functions for affiliated persons. In-scope entities must file the annual ESD and satisfy the core income-generating activities test in Bermuda, which requires adequate premises, employees, expenditure, and local management. A non-resident ELP that is tax resident in a jurisdiction with a corporate tax rate of 15% or higher may claim exemption by filing the relevant declaration and producing the foreign tax residence certificate. Failure to satisfy economic substance: penalty up to BD$250,000; knowingly false declaration: penalty up to BD$1,000,000; officers, directors, and general partners who cause the default may be personally liable.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Cross-Border Tax Evasion / UK Permanent Establishment

Statutory sanction: Under UK CTA 2010 and TMA 1970, the Revenue may assess understated tax, issue penalties of up to 100% of the tax liability, plus interest; failure to notify chargeability to UK tax: up to 100% penalty; VAT registration failure: penalty equal to 100% of the VAT evaded. Bermuda itself imposes no correlating tax, so the full force of the penalty regime applies in the UK.

Director personal liability: Directors and the corporate general partner's officers may be criminally liable for fraudulent tax evasion (UK FA 2007 s. 106/107: imprisonment up to 7 years); disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act; civil restitution orders; and UK HMRC can issue travel bans/persecution of assets under a WFO or worldwide freezing order. Under Bermuda law, directors knowingly party to carrying on business to defraud creditors face imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to BD$10,000.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations: late filing penalty BD$500 plus BD$100/day (max BD$10,000); substantive non-compliance notice leads to penalty up to BD$250,000; material false information in the ESD: up to BD$1,000,000. The Registrar of Companies may strike the ELP off the register and forfeit the registration deposit.

Director personal liability: Every director, officer, or general partner who knowingly causes or permits non-compliance is personally liable to the same penalties; the court may impose disqualification orders, travel restrictions, and imprisonment up to 18 months for wilful non-compliance or false declaration.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008: fines up to BD$1,000,000 per contravention; money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997: imprisonment up to 14 years and/or an unlimited fine. Beneficial Ownership Act excursions: criminal fines up to BD$110,250 for natural persons and BD$250,000 for bodies corporate.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, compliance officers, and MLROs may be prosecuted personally: confiscation orders up to the value of laundered funds, travel bans pending surrender of passports, up to 14 years' imprisonment, and regulatory debarment from holding a licensed CSP or financial services position.
Fraudulent Trading and Misrepresentation to the Registrar

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Companies Act 1981 (applied by analogy to partnerships) and Fraudulent Trade provisions: criminal conviction carries a fine not exceeding BD$10,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; civil liability for the full amount of the debts incurred with fraudulent intent. The court may order the winding-up of the ELP and public disqualification of the partners.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil of the general partner is pierced where the limited partnership is used as a mere facade: directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution to creditors, subject to travel bans, asset seizures, and imprisonment up to 5 years; disqualification from acting as a director in Bermuda or the UK typically follows.
