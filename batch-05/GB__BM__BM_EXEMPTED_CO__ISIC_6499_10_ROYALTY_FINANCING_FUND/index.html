Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance, Bermuda; corporate registry: Registrar of Companies; customs authority: HM Customs (Bermuda Customs & Police Services). The principal official tax portal is the Government of Bermuda Tax Portal (tax.public.gov.bm).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Bermuda Exempted Company must file an Application for Exempted Company Incorporation with the Registrar of Companies together with a Certificate of Compliance issued by the Bermuda Monetary Authority and a registered office/secretary nomination. Send the incorporation fee; the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a company registration number. Because Bermuda does not impose income tax, no tax ID is issued for corporate profits. To engage in taxable administrative activities, the entity must register as an employer with the OTC if it hires Bermuda workers, obtaining a Payroll Tax Account Number; if engaged in import/export, it must register with HM Customs and obtain a customs trading/clearing code; it must also register for Economic Substance (ES) filing under the Economic Substance (Regulation and Enforcement) Act 2020, receiving an ES filing reference. For foreign ownership, no specific TIN is required for the Exempted Company itself, but beneficial ownership details must be filed with the Registrar under the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Act.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone – a Bermuda Exempted Company is not subject to Bermudian income, corporation, capital gains, or withholding tax. It may apply for a tax exemption undertaking under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing no future income tax until 2035 (extendable to 2050). The entity remains subject to non-income taxes such as payroll tax (if employees), customs duties, stamp duty, and land tax. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – a Bermuda Exempted Company is not subject to Bermudian income, corporation, capital gains, or withholding tax. It may apply for a tax exemption undertaking under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing no future income tax until 2035 (extendable to 2050). The entity remains subject to non-income taxes such as payroll tax (if employees), customs duties, stamp duty, and land tax. It is not a pass-through entity for Bermuda income tax purposes, as no income tax exists; for foreign tax classification it is typically treated as a corporation in its home jurisdiction unless check-the-box elections apply elsewhere.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections available in Bermuda because there is no local income tax. Elective mechanisms include: (1) applying for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act undertaking; (2) selecting the financial reporting period and filing route under the Economic Substance Act; (3) electing whether to obtain a currency account with a Bermudian bank; (4) for foreign owners, an election to hold real estate under a specific Bermuda land holding license may be required. There are no hybrid mismatch or transparency elections because the jurisdiction does not impose corporate income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST – Bermuda does not impose value-added tax or general sales tax. Indirect revenue is derived from customs import duties (Customs Tariff Act 2012), stamp duties on instruments, and land tax. For this tax identity dossier, the applicable indirect regime is classified as 'Customs Duty & Stamp Duty Regime'.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – there is no VAT/GST and therefore no indirect tax registration threshold. Employers must register for payroll tax regardless of salary amount (except minimal thresholds for part-time domestic workers under the Employment Act). Importers and exporters must be registered with HM Customs for the issuance of import/export codes and licences; this registration applies to all traders regardless of annual turnover. The local currency is Bermudian Dollar (BMD), pegged 1:1 to USD.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

As Bermuda levies no VAT or GST, there is no export VAT zero-rating scheme. In the customs context, goods exported from Bermuda are exempt from export customs duties; goods imported for processing and subsequent re-export may benefit from temporary importation or inward-processing relief provided they leave Bermuda within the customs-approved period. For services (including offshore royalty financing), services supplied to non-residents from Bermuda are outside the scope of any Bermudian indirect tax and cannot be zero-rated because no tax is levied. The term 'zero-rating eligibility' is therefore interpreted as the criteria for customs duty exemption on exports and the absence of VAT on offshore services.

  • Customs Export Declaration as filed through the ASYCUDA World portal (including unique consignment reference)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of goods from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and inward remittance certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds (if payment is remitted to a Bermuda bank; no legal FX repatriation requirement applies)

No real-time electronic invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate exists. HM Customs operates the ASYCUDA World electronic single window for the lodgement of customs declarations, but there is no digital stamping, no e-invoicing clearance obligation, and no requirement to report invoice data to a tax authority for VAT/GST purposes. Commercial invoices may be transmitted electronically through ASYCUDA for import/export clearance but no central continuous transaction control is imposed.

There is no VAT zero-rating clawback mechanism because no VAT exists. For customs duty relief, misdeclaration or fraudulent export claims are subject to proceedings under the Customs Management Act 2010 and the Customs Tariff Act 2012. Penalties include forfeiture of goods, fines up to BD$10,000 per offence, recovery of unpaid duties, and imprisonment up to 2 years. If an exporter wrongfully diverts export-destined goods into domestic consumption, customs duties become due immediately with surcharges and penalties. For payroll tax or economic substance filings, false statements may trigger OTC penalties and cross-border exchange of information. The Economic Substance (Regulation and Enforcement) Act 2020 provides for financial penalties and potential criminal liability for non-compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 and is freely convertible within the Island's exchange control regime. For a non-resident Bermuda exempted company, current-account items (royalty receipts, financing fees) and capital-account items (equity injections, loan redemptions) are remittable without exchange control approval. Profit remittances to shareholders are subject to no Bermudian withholding tax and require no foreign-exchange permit. Restrictions apply only to Bermudian resident persons or transactions involving local currency; these do not affect a properly designated non-resident exempted company.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender or conversion ratio for export receipts. Export proceeds and trade proceeds may be freely retained in convertible foreign currency or cleared through Bermudian banks without conversion. A non-resident exempted company holding foreign-currency accounts is outside any exchange control surrender or repatriation timeline.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the exchange control authority for Bermuda under the Exchange Control Act 1972

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium for primary treasury use; viable only as a supplementary collection gateway. Legal constraints: offshore digital EMIs (EU/UK-licensed) cannot passport into Bermuda because Bermuda is outside the EEA and the UK's FCA regime does not extend to Bermuda; they are not licensed by the BMA and cannot deliver Bermudian bank/BIC/IBAN clearing. Using an EMI for core escrow or operational accounts is not recommended from a compliance and clearing-rails perspective; a licensed Bermuda bank account is mandatory for regulated payments.
