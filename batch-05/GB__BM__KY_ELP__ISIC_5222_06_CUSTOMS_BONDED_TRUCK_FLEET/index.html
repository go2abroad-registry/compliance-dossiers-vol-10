Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC) and Bermuda Registrar of Companies; official portal: https://www.gov.bm/taxation and https://www.bermudabusinessregistry.com. HM Revenue & Customs (UK) is not the primary tax authority for a Bermuda exempted limited partnership unless the ELP creates a UK permanent establishment or separately registers for UK VAT.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a separate national TIN; the Company Registry Number issued by the Registrar of Companies serves as the statutory taxpayer identifier for Customs clearance, economic substance filing, payroll tax and FATCA/CRS reporting. A foreign entity establishes the Exempted Limited Partnership by filing an EF2 formation application with the Registrar of Companies, obtaining Minister of Finance approval, and receiving a Certificate of Limited Partnership. If the ELP elects to be treated as a U.S. partnership or disregarded entity, a U.S. EIN is obtained via IRS Form SS-4; if a UK permanent establishment is later created, HMRC will issue a UK UTR or VAT number, but that is ancillary rather than part of the Bermuda registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: The Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is legally fiscally transparent under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2011, is not subject to Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax, and is eligible for pass-through treatment. Because Bermuda levies no income tax, the ELP is generally not taxed at entity level; partners are taxed only in their respective residence jurisdictions and the ELP may elect disregarded-entity or partnership classification for U.S. federal tax purposes.

Available election forms

There is no Bermuda income tax against which a hybrid-mismatch or entity-level election could operate. The principal available election is the U.S. check-the-box election: the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to elect corporate classification or remain a foreign pass-through eligible entity. Bermuda economic substance law requires the ELP to file annual economic substance returns and, if carrying on a relevant activity, to maintain and report adequate substance in Bermuda; this is a compliance obligation, not an income tax election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales tax; Bermuda customs duty regime (Customs Tariff Act 1990) and Bermuda payroll tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold because Bermuda does not levy value-added tax. The effective customs threshold for a bonded fleet operator is BMD 0: the operator must be registered as a bonded carrier or importer of record with Bermuda Customs, hold a valid customs bond and bonded operator licence, and file electronic declarations for all cargo movements. Payroll tax registration is likewise required from the first Bermudian dollar of Bermuda-source payroll.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The ELP's bonded road feeder services qualify for non-taxation rather than zero-rating because Bermuda has no VAT/GST. For customs duty purposes, eligible exports consist of goods delivered into the bonded truck fleet at an international airport or port and moved under continuous customs supervision to an approved customs control area for direct departure from Bermuda. The goods must not enter free circulation in Bermuda, must remain under bond, and must be documented as export, transit, or re-export cargo by the licensed bonded operator.

  • Bermuda Customs export declaration (ASYCUWorld electronic declaration) with unique movement reference
  • Master or house Bill of Lading / Air Waybill naming a final foreign destination
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank credit evidence confirming receipt of transport proceeds
  • Customs-approved bonded removal manifest and vehicle seal record
  • Commercial invoice and packing list showing HS codes, weight, and declared value
  • Proof of physical departure, including carrier cargo manifest, security log, and destination delivery receipt

No mandatory real-time fiscal e-invoicing, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control regime applies in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs uses the ASYCUWorld electronic clearance platform for customs declarations; bonded road feeder operators must file digital export/entry declarations, maintain a bonded inventory account, transmit commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, and vehicle manifests, and comply with customs seal and movement-control requirements. If a UK establishment is separately created, HMRC Making Tax Digital for VAT would apply, but that is not part of the Bermuda ELP default profile.

Bermuda Customs may reject export documentation, claw back duty exemptions, forfeit goods, call in the operator's bond, and revoke the bonded fleet licence where goods are not physically exported, are diverted into domestic free circulation, or the export declaration is fraudulent. Penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 1990 and Customs Management Act include monetary penalties of up to three times the duty or value of the goods, forfeiture of conveyances, imprisonment terms, and permanent disqualification of the bonded operator. If the ELP separately registers for UK VAT through a UK establishment, HMRC may also assess VAT, impose penalties up to 100% of understated tax, and extend the assessment period to 20 years in cases of deliberate or concealed tax fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to the US Dollar and both currencies circulate interchangeably within Bermuda; current-account convertibility is unrestricted for trade settlements and ordinary business remittances. On the capital account, the ELP as a non-resident-owned exempted partnership may obtain 'designated' exchange-control status and a tax-exemption certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which removes the remaining capital-account permission requirements. Inward capital injection and outward profit distribution may be executed without BMA pre-approval through an authorised dealer. Bermuda levies no income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, or VAT on the ELP; the sole profit remittance hurdles are documentary rather than fiscal, comprising the capital call resolution, partner register, source-of-wealth statement, and CRS/TIEA transparency filing to UK HMRC where UK-resident partners exist. The BMA requires the appointed bank to include cross-border transactions in statistical returns, but a designated entity faces no convertibility quota, no prior-approval gate, and no allocation restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Bermuda. Receipts from the customs-bonded truck-fleet road-feeder services invoiced in GBP, EUR, or USD may be retained indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts at BMA-licensed banks under the Exchange Control Act 1972 standing General Permission. There is no prescribed statutory timeline for conversion and no minimum percentage of export receipts that must be exchanged into BMD; any conversion into BMD/USD is discretionary at market or par rates and is driven solely by treasury liquidity forecasting rather than regulation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - the central bank and foreign-exchange administration of Bermuda, established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the delegate for exchange control administration under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and its standing General Permissions. All treasury flows of the ELP must clear through a BMA-licensed authorised dealer carrying a deposit-taking banking license under Bermuda's Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for Bermuda domestic clearing; MEDIUM for ancillary GBP/EUR receivables collection. Bermuda has no e-money institution licensing regime equivalent to the UK FCA PSD2 framework. A lawfully authorised offshore EMI (for example a UK FCA or EU-authorised EMI) may be engaged on a limited basis to collect GBP/EUR air-cargo receivables in the UK road-feeder operating channel and to initiate payments to petty suppliers, provided the EMI does not issue BMD, does not offer Bermudian clearing, and does not serve as the ELP's exchange-control treasury core. The statutory treasury core, FX execution, counterparty credit, and final liquidity must remain at a BMA-licensed bank; using an unlicensed EMI for Bermudian-currency settlement would breach the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the deposit-taking prohibition of the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The UK parent (GB) injects capital by SWIFT MT103 from a UK FCA-regulated bank to the ELP's Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda escrow/operating account in BMD, with the message field 70 carrying the reference 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / ELP PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT CLAUSE [X]' and the remitting bank's outward-direct-investment purpose classification. Because BMD trades at par to USD, a GBP-sourced contribution is converted to USD/BMD once in the London inter-bank market and then wired, avoiding dual-conversion cost. The beneficiary bank holds the credit in a segregated escrow sub-account until it receives the capital call resolution, the signed partnership agreement extract, and source-of-wealth forms; no BMA exchange-control approval is required for a designated ELP, but the bank will record the credit in its BMA statistical return and apply CRS/TIEA disclosure to UK-resident partners. Where the injection is structured as a partner loan rather than equity, a separate funding agreement must be executed and, if secured over UK land or the UK fleet assets, the charge documentation must accommodate registration on the UK Register of Overseas Entities at Companies House where applicable; the inbound wire must be free of any sanctions nexus and traceable to the contributing partner's own account.Payments cascade from the Bermuda treasury along four cleared rails: (i) high-value supplier wires for fuel, tolls, insurance, and customs-bond guarantees via SWIFT MT103 with global-payments-innovation tracking from Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda to UK/EU counterparty banks; Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda entity-level: no corporation tax, income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on ELP profits). UK-resident partners and the UK operating company remain chargeable at the 25% UK Corporation Tax main rate on their allocable share of trading profits attributable to the UK; the 19% small profits rate applies where UK profits do not exceed GBP 50,000.
