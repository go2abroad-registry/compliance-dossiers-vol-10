Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office acceptance is permitted only when the virtual office provider is a licensed Bermuda corporate service provider (CSP) with a physical address in Bermuda, and the CSP acts as the registered office. The provider must accept service of legal process and maintain physical presence. For a bakery franchise central kitchen operation, a virtual office is acceptable for the ELP's registered office; however, the commercial substance requirements under the Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) Act 2018 will require that core income-generating activities (IP ownership, logistics, royalty management) occur in Bermuda or through a resident manager. If the ELP is tax resident in a foreign jurisdiction, economic substance requirements may be relaxed, but the ELP must maintain adequate physical presence in Bermuda if it claims tax residence in Bermuda. For master franchise royalty income, the ELP must demonstrate a substance ratio appropriate to its activity level; the Bermuda Tax Authority will apply a proportionate and risk-based analysis.

No minimum paid-up capital is required for an Exempted Limited Partnership. At least one general partner and one limited partner must exist; capital contributions are contractually fixed in the partnership agreement. No statutory escrow account is required.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to USD. The currency account is fully convertible for non-resident designated entities. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) may apply for 'non-resident' designation, which exempts it from exchange control restrictions on current and capital account transactions, in particular capital contributions, loan repayments, and profit remittances. Current account convertibility is open; capital account remittances from non-resident accounts are unrestricted. Profits may be freely remitted abroad without BMA approval once the ELP holds a certificate of non-residence, though evidence of paid-up capital and beneficial ownership is required by the established bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda's Exchange Control Regulations require a resident, within three months of becoming entitled to sell foreign currency, to offer it for sale to an authorised dealer. However, a non-resident designated ELP is exempted from the mandatory surrender obligation. No conversion ratio is imposed; receipts may be held in USD/foreign currency accounts and disbursed without compulsory conversion to BMD.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - under the Exchange Control Act 1972, acting as Exchange Control Officer for Her Majesty's Treasury in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Bermuda does not operate a dedicated fiat EMI licensing framework; the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 covers digital asset custodians/exchanges, not fiat wallet EMIs. Offshore EMIs from UK or Europe cannot passport into Bermuda, and a Bermuda-based EMI would require a full 'Subsidiary banking licence' or 'Restricted banking licence' under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, which is time- and capital-intensive. For the bakery chain's operating flows, a Bermuda domestic commercial bank or a UK fintech with a Bermuda correspondant is preferable, but the fintech rail is not viable as a primary settlement route.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general statutory headline rate for Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnerships; 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies exclusively to in-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenues of at least €750M (BMD 750M) in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no dividend withholding tax and no deemed distribution tax on an Exempted Limited Partnership; partner distributions of profits are not subject to Bermuda income tax or withholding tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 15 months after the end of the ELP's fiscal year, aligned with the OECD GloBE Information Return deadline; for a 31 December year-end the return is due by 31 March of the second following year. Quarterly estimated CIT instalments are due in four equal payments based on the preceding year's computed liability.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the 15th day of the month following the payroll period; an annual reconciliation statement is due by 31 March following the close of the payroll tax year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 in group consolidated revenue, pegged to USD, being the CIT Act 2023 / GloBE scope threshold. At or above this threshold the ELP must prepare and maintain OECD-aligned Local File and Master File documentation supporting partner allocations, covered taxes, and the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax computation. Below the threshold, no standalone Bermuda Local File or Master File obligation arises, but the UK and Chinese operating entities remain subject to their respective statutory transfer pricing documentation rules.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 applies to relevant activities, namely pure holding business, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centre, shipping, and intellectual property business. A bakery central kitchen and retail franchise operation is not itself a relevant activity; however, where the ELP receives brand royalty income or provides intra-group financing to UK or China franchise entities, the IP holding or financing activity becomes relevant and requires directed and managed senior management in Bermuda, adequate physical premises, full-time local employees, and proportionate local operating expenditure. An annual Economic Substance Declaration must be filed with the International Tax Authority (ITA), and failure attracts compliance reviews, fines up to BMD 250,000, daily default penalties, and strike-off risk.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate CIT Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: BITA assessment of the full tax avoided plus statutory interest at 10% per annum; fixed penalty up to BMD 250,000 and daily default penalties up to BMD 100,000; criminal prosecution for false declarations under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and the Tax Collection Acts.

Director personal liability: The general partner and directing officers face civil restitution, travel bans, and asset freezes; imprisonment up to 5 years for fraudulent evasion; the corporate veil is pierced because partners are jointly and severally liable for taxes arising from their allocable shares.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Economic Substance Act 2018 of up to BMD 250,000, daily default fines, mandatory corrective directions from the International Tax Authority, loss of exempted status, and strike-off from the Bermuda Register; denial of any Certificate of Tax Residence for foreign relief purposes.

Director personal liability: General partners and UK or China directors who caused the substance failure are personally liable for fines; disqualification orders prohibit service as a director for up to 10 years; false ESR declarations are punishable by imprisonment up to 4 years.
