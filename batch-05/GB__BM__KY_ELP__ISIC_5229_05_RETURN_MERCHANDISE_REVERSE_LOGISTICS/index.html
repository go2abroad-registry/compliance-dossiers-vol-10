Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (established under the Tax Commissioner Act 2022, Ministry of Finance; official portal: taxcommissioner.gov.bm); Bermuda Registrar of Companies / Department for Corporate Services (Ministry of Finance; entity registration for Exempted Limited Partnerships; main government portal: gov.bm); His Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC, UK; portal: hmrc.gov.uk) is the relevant authority only where a separate UK customs, VAT, or PAYE nexus is established by the ELP in the United Kingdom.

E-government filing portal

Procedure for a foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership: (i) register the ELP with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992, obtaining a unique Bermuda Registration Number (BRN), which serves as the statutory identifier for customs declarations, banking, payroll tax filings, and the corporate registry; (ii) because Bermuda imposes no income tax and no VAT, no TIN or VAT ID is issued to the ELP for Bermudian purposes, and the BRN is the sole Bermudian tax-relevant identifier; (iii) if the ELP is a constituent entity of a multinational group included in an Adjusted Annual Report with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million, the group must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (headline rate 15%, effective for fiscal years commencing on or after 1 January 2025) and must comply with GloBE, QDMTT, and Economic Substance (ESRA 2018) filing obligations, including designation of a local representative or tax filing agent; (iv) if goods are declared to UK customs in the ELP's own name, the ELP must obtain a UK EORI number from HMRC under the customs provisions of the Finance Act 2019 before making export or import declarations in the United Kingdom; (v) for ISIC 5229 reverse-logistics activity, no separate statutory tax licence is required and the target payment rails (Local ACH, Airwallex) do not attract Bermudian withholding taxes, as Bermuda levies no dividend, interest, or royalty withholding tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

(a) US check-the-box: the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to elect its US federal classification (default pass-through partnership, or alternatively association taxable as a corporation), with the election to be consistent across all intended US partners; (b) Bermuda CIT/GloBE: in-scope groups may make the elections and notifications available under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, including the computation of the Substance-Based Income Exclusion and the application of the transitional Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT) approach; (c) UK look-through: no election is required for UK income tax purposes because a foreign-law limited partnership is treated as statutorily transparent by operation of ITTOIA 2005 s852 and TCGA 1992 s59, with UK-resident partners taxed on their respective shares and partnership returns filed by a nominated UK representative partner; (d) no Bermuda-specific entity-classification election exists, because Bermuda does not levy income tax on the ELP itself.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax applies in Bermuda; the only Bermudian indirect import levy is customs import duty imposed under the Customs Act 1945 and the Customs Tariff Act 1992 on goods brought into Bermuda. UK VAT under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 would be relevant only to taxable supplies made through a UK establishment or where the ELP otherwise meets UK VAT registration criteria.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Bermuda: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no such tax is levied; customs duty liability arises per import declaration irrespective of turnover, and regular commercial importers act through a licensed customs broker. For the United Kingdom, the statutory GBP 90,000 taxable-turnover threshold for mandatory VAT registration applies only if the ELP acquires a UK business establishment or makes taxable supplies in the UK through an unincorporated permanent presence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating is not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT, and therefore no export zero-rating or VAT refund machinery exists in the jurisdiction. Instead, the applicable relief lies in Bermudian customs duty: under the Customs Act 1945, goods in transit, goods entered for temporary admission, and returned or rejected goods subsequently re-exported unused may qualify for drawback or remission of import duty at the discretion of the Collector of Customs, provided the goods are exported within the period and upon the conditions stated in the customs permit, are not consumed or disposed of in Bermuda, and are supported by the original import entry. The ELP should maintain a complete return-consignment file per shipment, substantiate no-consideration dispositions with the underlying contractual return policy, and apply the 180-day FX repatriation benchmark as the internal substantiation window for any commercial consideration (Bermuda itself imposes no statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline on non-resident ELP account holders).

  • Customs Export Declaration filed through the Bermuda Customs single-window cargo system
  • Commercial Invoice evidencing the return-sale or no-consideration disposition
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing the physical export movement from Bermuda
  • Original Import Entry and duty payment receipt (supporting duty drawback or remission)
  • Proof-of-delivery or freight records for the returned goods (supporting the due-diligence and grading file)

None: Bermuda does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance mandate, a mandatory e-invoicing regime, or a digital stamping requirement, and commercial invoices are not certified or cleared by the tax authority. The Bermuda Customs Department accepts electronic cargo declarations and electronic duty payment through its online single-window cargo system, but that digital border filing does not constitute an invoice clearance or fiscal stamping requirement. The UK's Making Tax Digital VAT reporting framework would apply only if a UK VAT registration obligation is separately triggered.

Under the Customs Act 1945 (Bermuda), knowingly false export, transit, temporary-admission, or drawback declarations render the goods liable to seizure and forfeiture and the offender liable to statutory monetary penalties, commonly assessed by reference to the value of the goods, and, on conviction on indictment, to a term of imprisonment as prescribed by the Act; the Collector may also retrospectively revoke any duty relief or drawback previously granted and demand repayment of the duty with interest. No VAT clawback applies in Bermuda because no VAT is levied; however, an in-scope multinational group remains exposed to penalties under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for incorrect CIT/GloBE filings, and a UK-resident partner that misstates ELP profits is exposed to HMRC discovery assessments, penalties of up to 100% of the tax under FA 2007 Schedule 24, and statutory interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD); there is no BMD float and the banking system clears predominantly in USD. Current-account payments for goods and services are unrestricted. Capital-account transactions, including subscription of partnership interests and capital injections by non-resident partners, require exchange-control permission unless the exempted limited partnership is designated non-resident and operates external accounts. Ordinary Bermuda resident entities face authorised-dealer hurdles on profit remittance, but this ELP, when validly designated non-resident through its licensed corporate service provider, obtains external-account status that removes material profit-remittance and capital-movement hurdles and permits free distribution to UK/overseas partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, a Bermuda resident exporter must surrender foreign-currency export proceeds to an authorised dealer, generally within six months of the export date, unless the BMA or the authorised dealer grants external-account treatment. The target ELP, once designated non-resident for exchange-control purposes, is not a resident person; proceeds derived from cross-border reverse-logistics trade may be credited directly to its external USD account without mandatory conversion into BMD. The business model is import/returns-driven, so no Bermudian export-proceeds surrender trigger arises in the ordinary course.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), Hamilton, Bermuda — exercising central banking, bank supervision and exchange-control functions under the BMA Act 1969, the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1991, and the Exchange Control Act 1972 as the designated monetary authority for Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium. Bermuda has no domestic EMI licence category and does not passport UK/EEA EMI authorisations; the BMA regulatory sandbox under the Business Development Act 2021 does not authorise unlicensed EMI deposit-taking in Bermuda. Airwallex and similar cross-border EMIs are viable ancillary rails for low-value returns refunds, foreign-currency payables and small-vendor disbursements, but the ELP must maintain its primary treasury account with a BMA-licensed authorised-dealer bank to ensure compliant USD clearing, FX hedging and exchange-control record-keeping. Legal constraint: the ELP must not create the appearance of deposit-taking with an unlicensed EMI and must not hold partner capital contributions in an EMI during the escrow phase.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 (no statutory transfer pricing rules; Economic Substance Regulations require documented core income-generating activities and adequate substance for relevant activities)

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to ELPs engaged in relevant activities. Reverse logistics may constitute a 'distribution and service center' where the ELP earns income from the purchase, import, distribution, or service provision of goods. Entities must maintain physical presence, full-time employees, and direction and management in Bermuda, and produce financial records demonstrating local expenditure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Fine up to BMD 50,000 and, for serious offences, imprisonment up to 5 years under the Revenue and Financial Services Acts.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners may be personally liable for restitution of amounts avoided, debarment from acting as director or partner, and travel bans if fraud conducted with their knowledge.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BMD 10,000 plus mandatory re-domiciliation or strike-off. If deemed to be in the scope of EU/OECD anti-tax avoidance measures, the entity may lose tax residency benefits.

Director personal liability: General partners may be required to indemnify the partnership and third parties for losses caused by misrepresentation; in egregious cases, the corporate veil is pierced and personal assets seized.
AML / Counter-Terrorism Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and Sanctions Act: fines up to BMD 1,000,000 for the entity and up to 15 years imprisonment for responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners face criminal prosecution, civil confiscation orders, and travel bans. Professional disqualification is automatic upon conviction.
Failure to File Annual Returns

Statutory sanction: Automatic strike-off from the Bermuda Register, accompanied by forfeiture of all assets held in Bermuda to the Crown.

Director personal liability: Struck-off entities render partners personally liable for debts incurred during the period of non-compliance; directors may be fined up to BMD 10,000 and banned from managing other Bermuda entities.
