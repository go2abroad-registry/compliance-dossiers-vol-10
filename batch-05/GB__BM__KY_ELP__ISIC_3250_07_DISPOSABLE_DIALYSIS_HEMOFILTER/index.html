Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (tax administration portal: gov.bm; customs portal: customs.gov.bm); Bermuda Customs Department (import/export duty); Registrar of Companies (ELP registration). For UK-linked obligations, HMRC (gov.uk/hmrc) administers UK VAT, customs, and any UK CIT/Pillar Two matters. Bermuda has no income tax authority, no income tax filing, and no income TIN; the Office of the Tax Commissioner administers Payroll Tax, Social Insurance, and the new Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CIT Act) for in-scope multinational groups.

E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone. Bermuda exempted limited partnerships are transparent for Bermuda tax and foreign tax classification purposes, and are not subject to Bermuda income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. Territorial scope is limited to Payroll Tax and Social Insurance on Bermuda-sourced employment remuneration and Customs Import Duty on goods entering Bermuda. An ELP that is a constituent entity of a large multinational group (consolidated revenue ≥ €750 million) may additionally be subject to entity-level taxation under the Bermuda CIT Act 2023 at 15% on qualifying accounting profits where economic substance is insufficient.

Available election forms

US check-the-box election: The ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to be classified as a partnership (default with two or more members), a disregarded entity (single eligible member), or an association taxable as a corporation. UK hybrid mismatch rules: An election may be filed under Part 6A of the UK Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010 to align the ELP's transparency status between Bermuda, the UK, and the US to neutralize hybrid financial instrument or hybrid entity deductions. Bermuda CIT Act 2023: No substantive entity-classification election is permitted; the Tax Commissioner determines scope on a consolidated-group basis. No other Bermuda statutory election or de minimis exemption is available with respect to Payroll Tax or Customs Duty.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Bermuda. Indirect taxes comprise: (i) Bermuda Customs Import Duty on the CIF value of goods entering Bermuda; (ii) Payroll Tax imposed on the employer at statutory rates of 0% to 10.25% on Bermuda remuneration, with the first BMD 1,000 monthly remuneration exempt; (iii) Social Insurance contributions at approximately 5.25% employer and 4.75% employee; (iv) hotel occupancy tax and betting tax where applicable. Exports are duty-free and outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST and therefore has no VAT registration threshold. Payroll Tax: every employer, without exception, must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner upon first hiring Bermuda-resident staff. Customs: every commercial importer/exporter must register with the Bermuda Customs Department irrespective of value or volume; there is no de minimis threshold for commercial shipments. For UK VAT purposes (if sales occur in the UK), registration is required once UK taxable turnover exceeds GBP 90,000 in any 12-month rolling period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT/GST base, so no domestic zero-rating mechanism exists. Exports of disposable dialysis hemofilters from Bermuda are duty-free and subject to no Bermuda export tax. For sales routed through the UK, zero-rating applies under UK VAT law only when the ELP (through a UK-established branch or agent) makes a qualifying direct export supply to a destination outside the UK within the statutory time limits, or when the place of supply is outside the UK. A supply of goods located in Bermuda at the time of sale with delivery to a UK customer is outside the scope of UK VAT and instead triggers UK import VAT on entry; input VAT credits are unavailable in Bermuda (no VAT system). No VAT refund mechanism exists in Bermuda, and any UK input VAT recovery requires a UK VAT registration in the name of the UK taxable entity.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs SAD/ASYCUDA World electronic declaration)
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill (airfreight) evidencing export from Bermuda
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/FX confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (UK/Bermuda preferential origin where applicable)
  • Medical device conformity documentation (UKCA marking evidence) as required by UK customs for dialysis hemofilter imports

Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping. Import and export declarations must be filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Department's ASYCUDA World-based single administrative document (SAD) platform; all commercial consignments require pre-arrival electronic lodgement. For goods entering the UK, the declarant must submit electronic import declarations to the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS); no Bermuda digital stamp is required for UK customs clearance. Medical device shipments (disposable dialysis hemofilters) require harmonized system classification and, where applicable, UKCA marking evidence for UK customs risk assessment.

Bermuda imposes no VAT or export-tax clawback penalties because no VAT regime exists. Penalties may apply to inaccuracies in customs declarations: a false declaration or attempted duty evasion can result in a penalty of up to three times the customs duty evaded, forfeiture of goods, and/or imprisonment up to three years (Bermuda Customs Tariff Act). For UK VAT, HMRC can assess tax for up to 4 years for non-deliberate errors and up to 20 years for deliberate concealment; maximum penalties reach 100% of the tax under-declared, with mitigation per Schedule 24 of the UK Finance Act 2007. Wrongly claimed export zero-rating is retrospectively clawed back at the UK standard VAT rate plus interest. Under the Bermuda CIT Act 2023, late filing of top-up tax returns attracts a penalty of 10% of the top-up tax due, plus interest at the prescribed rate.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged to the U.S. dollar (USD) at 1:1 and is freely convertible for current account transactions, including trade settlement and service fees. Capital account convertibility is unrestricted for non-resident exempted limited partnerships (ELPs) that hold 'non-resident' status under the Exchange Control Act. Remittance of profits, capital, and liquidation proceeds outside Bermuda generally proceeds without prior BMA approval, provided no resident interest is involved. Hurdles may arise only where local land, Bermudian resident trusts, or certain designated securities are transacted, requiring specific exemptions or BMA consent.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or minimum conversion ratio applies to export proceeds of a non-resident ELP. Receivables invoiced in USD, EUR, GBP, or other designated foreign currencies may be held indefinitely in foreign currency-denominated operational accounts. The BMA, however, requires statistical balance-of-payments reporting for exports above the BMD 250,000 equivalent per annum, filed via the appointed licensed bank as a one-line transaction code (e.g., 'Exports of PES hollow-fiber dialyzers'). There is no obligation to convert into BMD or to liquidate on the interbank market.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the central bank and integrated financial services regulator under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, with additional powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low-Medium. Bermuda does not recognise the UK Financial Conduct Authority EMI passport or the EU PSD2 e-money licence; therefore, an offshore digital EMI cannot directly obtain a Bermudian bank sort code or become a member of the Bermuda Automated Clearing House. Digital EMI services may be used as a compliant pre-funding conduit if the EMI itself is regulated (e.g., UK FCA or Liechtenstein FINMA) and holds funds in segregated accounts at a BMA-licensed bank, but the primary treasury architecture must rely on traditional BMA-licensed banking rails. A Bermuda-incorporated EMI would require a full 'bank or deposit-taking' licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act and is not commercially viable for this Stage-4 operating vehicle.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 (EUR 750 million) consolidated annual group revenue in at least one of the four preceding fiscal years triggers Bermuda CbC Reporting, Master File/Local File readiness and CIT Act transfer-pricing documentation. Below this threshold, no Bermuda TP documentation is mandated.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations apply to banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, holding, IP, distribution and service center activities. Manufacturing of disposable dialysis hemofilters (ISIC 3250) is not a relevant activity; a pure equity holding ELP is exempt from substance tests but must still file the ESG01 declaration. If the ELP also licenses IP or distributes product, it must maintain real presence, employees, premises and core income-generating activities in Bermuda proportionate to income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / CIT Act Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the CIT Act 2023: civil penalty up to BMD 250,000 per contravention, daily continuing penalties, interest at 8% per annum on unpaid tax, and compelled production orders; on criminal conviction, fines at the discretion of the court and imprisonment up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: A director of the corporate general partner who knowingly signs false returns or directs evasion may be criminally liable; courts may disqualify the director for up to 15 years, impose personal restitution orders, and issue travel bans pending determination of tax debts.
Money Laundering / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2008: on conviction on indictment, imprisonment up to 14 years and/or an unlimited fine; regulatory fines up to BMD 250,000; freezing and forfeiture of bank accounts and assets.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors acting with knowledge or intent face personal criminal liability; civil asset-recovery suits, director disqualification, and deprivation of travel documents are common ancillary orders.
Economic Substance Non-Compliance / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BMD 25,000 plus continuing penalties of BMD 10,000 per month for failure to satisfy the substance test; after three consecutive years of failure, the Registrar reports the ELP to the Minister of Finance, leading to compulsory strike-off and dissolution; automatic exchange of information with HMRC under the Bermuda-UK TIEA and DTA.

Director personal liability: The corporate general partner is exposed to civil liability for unpaid penalties; individual directors who made false ESG01 declarations may be personally fined and, in egregious cases, banned from acting as directors in Bermuda and denied re-entry on criminal charges.
Transfer Pricing / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Under the CIT Act 2023 and GloBE Pillar Two rules: re-computation of Bermuda taxable income, imposition of the 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax on shifted profit, penalties up to BMD 250,000 for documentation failure, and CbC penalties of BMD 10,000 per month for non-filing.

Director personal liability: Directors of the ELP's general partner are liable for ensuring adequate TP documentation and tax governance; personal culpability arises for knowingly executing artificial transactions, with restitution of the tax shortfall and travel bans for failure to pay.
Late or Non-Filing of Payroll and Annual Returns

Statutory sanction: Late filing penalty of 10% of tax due (minimum BMD 100), interest at 8% per annum, and a fine of BMD 250 per month for late annual declarations; persistent defaults lead to automatic strike-off of the ELP.

Director personal liability: The general partner is personally liable for payroll tax debts of the ELP if collected but not remitted; directors may face summary conviction fines and disqualification orders for non-remittance.
