Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner within the Bermuda Ministry of Finance, administering payroll tax, customs duty, land tax and the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; company incorporation and the Companies Register are administered by the Registrar of Companies in the Economic Development Department. Official information portal: www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN or VAT ID. The registration sequence for a foreign-owned exempted company is: (1) submit incorporation documents to the Registrar of Companies, including the exempted-company licence application and declaration of business activity, to obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and the unique Companies Register number (standard turnaround 1-2 business days); (2) if the entity employs staff, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain the payroll-tax employer reference before first remuneration payment; (3) if the multinational group has global revenue of at least EUR 750 million, register for Bermuda corporate income tax and receive a CIT TIN, with a designation notice naming the responsible member and the ultimate parent identifier; (4) apply separately for non-resident exchange-control designation under the Exchange Control Act 1972 where unrestricted fund conversion is required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN or VAT ID. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or transparent-entity election in its domestic law. Available elections are: (i) designation of the exempted company as non-resident for exchange-control purposes under the Exchange Control Act 1972; (ii) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope groups, elections over fiscal-year alignment, loss-utilisation mechanics, responsible-member designation, and the substance-based income exclusion; (iii) an election to apply the domestic minimum top-up tax so that Bermuda imposes the 15% effective minimum rate locally and preserves the group's control of the top-up allocation; (iv) elective treatment of distributions from segregated accounts or regulated investment structures where permitted by the Registrar.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Bermuda has no VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax; the only indirect-style taxes are customs duties on imported tangible goods and the employer/self-employed payroll tax, neither of which applies to the cross-border payment-processing service turnover

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST as no such regime exists. Payroll tax registration is mandatory for every Bermuda employer from the first dollar of Bermuda payroll, with a statutory annual payroll allowance for smaller employers; customs duty registration applies only to physical importers of goods. Cross-border service receipts by a Bermuda exempted company do not trigger any indirect-tax registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda's indirect tax system contains no VAT, so no export zero-rating claim or refund mechanism exists for the cross-border payment gateway services. The gateway's interchange, processing and settlement fees are intangible services outside customs duty. If the group is in-scope for the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the 15% Bermuda corporate income tax applies to Bermuda-sourced income and to income attributable to a Bermuda permanent establishment, mitigated by the substance-based income exclusion; that is an income-tax charge, not an indirect export tax.

  • Not applicable - cross-border digital payment services require no Customs Export Declaration or bill of lading
  • For any ancillary tangible exports (e.g., POS hardware): commercial invoice, customs export declaration, and bill of lading or airway bill
  • Inward remittance certificates and bank settlement statements retained as transfer-pricing and economic-substance evidence for CIT audit purposes

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or API-based fiscal reporting mandate applies in Bermuda. Service invoices for payment gateway fees are issued under the Companies Act 1981 and general contract law; customs duties on tangible imports are declared using the Bermuda Customs Department import procedures. No government e-invoice clearance platform exists for services.

No indirect-tax clawback exists because no VAT is chargeable. The substantive clawback risk arises under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023: inflated substance-based income exclusion claims, artificial transfer-pricing allocations, or false economic-substance declarations trigger penalties of up to BMD 250,000 per offence, reassessment of underpaid 15% corporate income tax with statutory interest, and possible revocation of the exempted-company licence or registration. Payroll tax arrears attract up to 30% penalties plus interest; unlicensed payment-activity in Bermuda additionally exposes the entity to Bermuda Monetary Authority enforcement measures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar, and the BMA operates a fully convertible current and capital account framework. There are no general exchange controls restricting the conversion of BMD into USD or any other major currency. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and capital repatriation are freely permitted for non-resident-owned exempted companies, subject only to compliance with the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing regime administered by the BMA. Practical hurdles are limited to banking documentation and source-of-funds verification, not exchange control licensing. The payment gateway scope (merchant acquiring and clearing) attracts enhanced scrutiny under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations, but no separate FX conversion permit is required for BMD/USD transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for the surrender of export proceeds. There is no equivalent to the territorial mandatory repatriation regimes found in certain emerging markets. Export receipts from a cross-border payment gateway may be held in any freely convertible currency, including USD, GBP, or EUR, in both Bermuda-licensed banks and approved offshore clearing accounts. The only relevant obligations arise from the BMA record-keeping and transaction monitoring requirements under the Anti-Money Laundering regime, requiring that all export or merchant settlement receipts be traceable, documented, and retained in accordance with the Proceeds of Crime Regulations.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for locally licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs), Medium for internationally recognized offshore EMI services. Bermuda has not established a dedicated non-bank EMI licensing framework comparable to the UK FCA regime. Digital asset payment firms require a BMA Digital Asset Business license, and fiat-money EMIs may be treated as unlicensed financial service providers unless they register under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act or obtain a Money Service Business (MSB) license. Offshore EMIs such as Airwallex or a UK-licensed EMI can be used as ancillary transactional conduits, but the BMA-regulated banks remain the substantive high-risk PSP account solution. The legal constraint is that any EMI maintaining a domicile or place of business in Bermuda and operating payment services must obtain BMA authorization; simply operating through a foreign EMI without key management in Bermuda is viable but requires clear contractual separation and enhanced AML/KYC for the underlying merchant flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for inbound capital uses SWIFT MT103 wire transfers in USD, GBP, EUR, or BMD-equivalent USD, transmitted through a U.S. correspondent clearing bank (e.g., JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citibank) into the Bermuda bank’s nostro account. The wire must state a clear purpose code such as 'CFD – Financial Derivatives', 'LCS – Loans or Credits', 'SHS – Purchase of Shares', or 'ICJ – Inter-company Financing'. Beneficiary account name must match the exact registry name of the Bermuda Exempted Company, and the reference must include the board resolution approving the capital injection. The bank will require an instruction letter executed by two authorised signatories, a certified copy of the board resolution, and supporting legal documentation of the corporate capacity. For transactions exceeding a bank-defined threshold, usually USD 250,000, pre-matching of source-of-funds evidence and a beneficial ownership confirmation are mandatory before crediting the account. The funds remain subject to a 3–5 business day holding period pending BMA-required post-validation.Outbound merchant settlements and trade disbursements are cleared through SWIFT MT103 priority payments, via the Bermudian bank's correspondent network, using Fedwire or CHIPS for USD, and CHAPS or Faster Payments for GBP. For commercial trade settlements involving goods or services, a documentary Commercial Letter of Credit (L/C) may be issued under UCP 600 for high-value disbursements above USD 100,000; the rail requires presentation of transport and insurance documents, commercial invoice, and compliant packaging lists. Bulk lower-value merchant payouts should be batched using ISO 20022 XML (pacs.008) messages to global acquiring banks, with unique transaction references and hashed merchant IDs. Every outbound disbursement is subject to real-time OFAC/HM Treasury sanctions screening and transaction monitoring; non-standard counterparties will trigger additional AML review for up to 48 hours. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax on a tax-exempt Exempted Company. Payroll Tax and Social Insurance apply only to locally engaged employees. A 15% minimum tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 applies solely to constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750M or more.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — no dividend withholding tax, no remittance tax, and no capital gains tax on Exempted Company distributions.

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Bermuda has no statutory transfer-pricing legislation or Local File/Master File threshold. ESR requires contemporaneous substance documentation for relevant activities; MNE groups within the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 must file OECD GloBE information returns where group revenue is EUR 750M or more.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations 2018: relevant activities (banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, distribution/service centre, IP) must demonstrate CIGA in Bermuda. A pure merchant-acquiring PSP serving unrelated merchants is generally outside the list; intra-group treasury, settlement, or service-centre activity is in scope. Non-compliance: BDA $250,000 fine, automatic TIEA exchange, and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unauthorised Payment Services (PSP Licence Infraction)

Statutory sanction: Criminal offence: summary conviction fine up to BDA $100,000 or imprisonment up to 2 years, or both; on indictment, fine up to BDA $250,000 or imprisonment up to 10 years, or both; the BMA may additionally levy administrative financial penalties and revoke the licence.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may be prosecuted as participants; the corporate veil is pierced where the company is a mere façade; the BMA may issue prohibition directions and the Supreme Court may impose travel bans and disqualification orders.
Tax Evasion / Abuse of EUTP Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: The Tax Exemption Certificate is void if local business is conducted; the company may be struck off. Foreign tax authorities apply GAAR and CFC rules: relevant profits revert to the U.K. at the 25% corporation tax rate with penalties and interest; TIEA exchanges are triggered by ESR substance failures.

Director personal liability: Directors of a sham arrangement face civil restitution, disqualification under Companies Act 1981, and fraudulent-trading liability; HMRC may lift the veil against a controlling-mind director for the underlying U.K. tax.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance (Substance Requirements) Act 2018: non-compliance penalty up to BDA $10,000 plus BDA $10,000 per day; failed substance test: fine up to BDA $250,000 with automatic exchange of information with U.K. and TIEA partners; persistent default leads to winding-up.

Director personal liability: Directors sign the substance declaration and are personally liable for knowingly false certification; the Supreme Court may order personal payment of penalties, impose travel bans, and disqualify directors; corporate indemnity is void for fraud.
AML / Sanctions / Proceeds of Crime

Statutory sanction: BMA financial penalty up to BDA $1,000,000 per contravention; money-laundering conviction: imprisonment up to 14 years; sanctions breach: fine and imprisonment up to 7 years; PSP licence revoked with immediate effect.

Director personal liability: Senior management is strictly accountable to the BMA: individual fines, prohibition orders, public censure; directors may be charged for consenting or conniving in laundering; travel bans and asset sequestration are available; veil-piercing applies where the PSP is used as a fraud conduit.
