Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 and is freely convertible. Because all partners are non-Bermudian, the Exempted Limited Partnership is designated 'non-resident' for exchange control purposes under the Exchange Control Regulations 1973 and may hold multicurrency foreign-currency operational accounts (USD, EUR, GBP) and remit partner drawings/profits without BMA consent. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax or profit-remittance levy on distributions to non-resident partners; the only ongoing screens are the Economic Substance Declaration under the Economic Substance Act 2018, annual CRS/FATCA reporting of partners, and Beneficial Ownership filing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda maintains no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline; non-resident exempted entities may retain 100% of export receipts in the currency of invoice indefinitely, subject only to bank record-keeping and AML source-of-funds verification.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat rate imposed by Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA 2023), effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025; no progressive bands, surtax, or branch tax apply.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, partnership distributions, or capital gains realised by non-resident partners; CITA 2023 contains no dividend WHT provisions.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing due date is 15 months after the end of the fiscal year (e.g., a 31 December 2025 year-end is due by 31 March 2027); the self-assessed CIT shown on the return is payable on the same due date. Registration as an in-scope entity must occur within 30 days after the entity becomes in scope.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The GIR is due within 15 months after the end of the reporting fiscal year, or 18 months for the first qualifying year; the QDMTT top-up schedule (Form CIT-1a) is due with the ordinary CIT-1 filing deadline.

Module 2 - QDMTT Computation Bermuda jurisdictional effective tax rate: total adjusted covered taxes divided by GloBE income of the in-scope entities in Bermuda. For the transparent ELP, the QDMTT is zero at entity level; include only in-scope partners' shares in the Bermuda jurisdictional pool. Failure to file the GIR or CbCR: BMD 10,000 fixed penalty plus BMD 100 per day of continued default; deliberate non-filing can attract a civil penalty of the greater of BMD 50,000 or 10% of the group's Bermudian revenue, with the general partner personally liable for the default.
Module 3 - Substance-Based Income Exclusion Eligible payroll and tangible fixed assets of the die-cast manufacturing operations and the applicable carve-out multiplier. Attach a schedule of the ELP's Bermudian employees and plant assets; foreign-owned assets used outside Bermuda are excluded from the carve-out. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory OECD Master File, Local File, and CbCR/GIR arise when the MNE group has consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million) in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years. For a trading ELP below that threshold, contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation is not statutorily mandatory, but CITA and TAA TP Guidelines require that controlled transactions - zinc alloy purchases, tampo-printing machinery, brand royalties, and management fees - satisfy the arm's-length standard; bank AML/KYC reviews and PayPal or SWIFT onboarding will also demand intercompany pricing evidence.
