E-government filing portal

File the exempted partnership registration application with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2016. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda law provides no check-the-box election; an Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent for Bermudian income tax purposes. For non-Bermuda tax purposes, the partnership may elect its classification under applicable foreign tax laws (e.g., U.S. Form 8832) and should assess hybrid mismatch outcomes under UK/EU anti-hybrid rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST; Bermuda imposes customs duties on imported goods and payroll tax on Bermuda employers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Customs duty registration applies to importers of goods, and payroll tax registration applies once the employer has a Bermuda payroll.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable; Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST on exports of goods or services, so no export zero-rating or refund claim exists. Goods exported are generally outside the scope of customs duties upon proof of export.

  • Customs export declaration submitted to Bermuda Customs
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Proof of removal from Bermuda (e.g., carrier receipt or Customs endorsed exit certificate)

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime in Bermuda. Import and export declarations are submitted electronically through the Bermuda Customs automated system, with paper or electronic commercial documents retained for the statutory limitation period.

No VAT clawback can arise because no VAT is levied. However, misdeclaration or fraudulent documentation to obtain duty relief on exports may trigger penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 2015 and the Customs Management Act 2015, including fines, forfeiture, and criminal liability. The Bermuda Tax Commissioner may also conduct payroll tax audits and impose interest and penalties for underreporting.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. Bermuda imposes no exchange control restrictions, no prior BMA approval for cross-border payments, and no withholding tax on distributions of an Exempted Limited Partnership to non-resident partners. US Dollars circulate freely as de facto legal tender alongside BMD. The esports platform's service fees, sponsor receipts, and tournament entry fees (current account items) and capital injections/distributions (capital account items) are all freely remittable. The only practical hurdle is the bank's internal compliance review of the economic substance of the counterparty transaction, not any statutory convertibility limit. BMD pegged to USD eliminates BMD/USD conversion risk.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender or mandatory conversion regime exists in Bermuda. Export receipts and any foreign currency proceeds (USD, EUR, GBP) may be retained indefinitely in multi-currency accounts without conversion. The ELP is required only to comply with the Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2008 (AML/CFT) record-keeping standards, including a five-year audit trail evidencing the source of all receipts and the business rationale of each settlement. No percentage-based conversion ratio applies.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; prudential regulator of banks and deposit companies under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; sole issuer of the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) and administrator of the BMD/USD currency peg at 1:1; independent of the Bank of England, though Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Moderate for use as a non-Bermuda settlement conduit; Low as a primary treasury account. Bermuda does not license local Electronic Money Institutions; therefore any foreign EMI (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie, or Meru, FCA-licensed under the UK EMRs 2011 / EMD2) may lawfully service the ELP only as a payment facilitator abroad and must not hold itself out as a Bermuda bank. The EMI cannot provide a Bermuda domestic clearing number and its platform may decline the ELP for KYC reasons if the ELP's underlying beneficial owners are undisclosed. Feasibility is acceptable only if the EMI's sanctions screening aligns with the platform's geoblocking and the ELP maintains a licensed Bermuda deposit-taking bank as the master account holder. For tax and substance protection, the BMA-licensed bank account remains the mandatory primary relationship.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Misclassification of esports tournament entry fees as online gambling or sports betting proceeds.

Root cause: Misclassification of esports tournament entry fees as online gambling or sports betting proceeds.

Defense evidence: (i) Platform tournament rulebook proving skill-based competition; (ii) legal opinion under the U.K. Gambling Act 2005 confirming a 'game of skill' exemption; (iii) Bermuda legal opinion confirming the activity falls outside the Betting Act 1968 (Bermuda); (iv) historical settlement reports from Stripe/PayPal with MCC codes evidencing non-gambling merchant classification; (v) undertaking not to onboard real-money wagering verticals without prior bank consent.
Cross-border prize payouts to sanctioned countries (OFSI/OFAC exposure).

Root cause: Cross-border prize payouts to sanctioned countries (OFSI/OFAC exposure).

Defense evidence: (i) GeoIP geoblocking configuration logs (MaxMind/Cloudflare) demonstrating blocked access from sanctioned territories; (ii) automated OFSI/OFAC sanctions screening output for all prize payouts; (iii) prize-payout refusal policy and player terms of service incorporating embargoed-country exclusions; (iv) quarterly sanctions-compliance attestation to the bank signed by the General Partner.
Custodial player balances interpreted as unlicensed e-money or deposit-taking.

Root cause: Custodial player balances interpreted as unlicensed e-money or deposit-taking.

Defense evidence: (i) Player terms confirming in-platform balances are receivables owed by the operator, not e-money; (ii) audit trail showing no interest accrual on player balances; (iii) client-money consent letter and safeguarding policy under the U.K. Payment Services Regulations approach; (iv) legal opinion confirming the esports community platform falls outside the Deposit-Taking Business Licensing regime in Bermuda.
Beneficial ownership opacity inherent to the Exempted Limited Partnership structure.

Root cause: Beneficial ownership opacity inherent to the Exempted Limited Partnership structure.

Defense evidence: (i) Bermuda Certificate of Limited Partnership and (if obtained) Section 11 Certificate of Exemption; (ii) registered partnership register and limited partner register; (iii) chain-of-title legal opinion on each limited partner from the formation agent; (iv) source-of-wealth documentation for capital contributions; (v) full ultimate beneficial owner disclosure forms (organizational charts) provided to the bank's compliance team.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

From the U.K. parent: SWIFT MT103 with U.K. purpose code 'D100' (direct investment abroad — equity/partnership capital), describing the wire in field 70 as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — EXEMPTED LIMITED PARTNERSHIP'; the Bermuda receiving bank records the purpose code 'CAPT' (capital transfer) under local reporting practice. Verification bundle on the receiving side: certified ELP certificate, capital contribution schedule, ultimate beneficial owner declaration, and the notarized partnership agreement. For recurring operational inflows, platform revenues are first settled by Stripe/PayPal into a U.K. Settlement Account (GBP/EUR), then swept by weekly SWIFT MT103 or via bank API batch to the Bermuda master account in USD; each sweep references the Stripe/PayPal payout reconciliation report to evidence underlying consumer-tournament revenues rather than deposits.Tournament prizes and sponsor payouts above USD 15,000 are routed via SWIFT MT103 individually with purpose code 'S202' (royalties or licence fees) or 'S301' (other business income) as applicable, supported by payout manifests. Sub-USD 15,000 mass payouts to players are batched via the banking API or through commercial payout rails (Stripe Connect, PayPal MassPay, Tipalti) with the Bermuda master account retaining a reserve for refunds and fraud. Procurement of European hardware/venue services is settled via Commercial Letters of Credit governed by UCP 600 where the contract value exceeds USD 100,000, or via MT103 supplier payments for smaller contracts. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda general transfer-pricing documentation threshold for non-CITA ELPs; under CITA 2023 the mandatory group threshold is EUR 750 million consolidated revenue. For ESR purposes, contemporaneous Local File and Master File records are recommended whenever connected-party annual gross revenue exceeds BMD 500,000.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 requires an ELP carrying on a relevant activity to maintain real economic presence in Bermuda. An esports community platform is generally classified as 'service business' if it earns fee income from connected parties or from non-residents, or 'IP business' if its principal income is royalty paid for software or brand IP. The ELP must maintain physical premises, full-time employees, local payroll, board meetings in Bermuda, and perform core income-generating activities there; otherwise it is treated as a shell and is exposed to ESR penalties, strike-off, and UK controlled-foreign-company attribution.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Penalties of up to BMD 200,000 and/or imprisonment; HMRC penalties of up to 100% of the UK tax lost where the misstatement causes a UK tax advantage; late payment interest and criminal conviction for fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner and any person knowingly participating in the false filing are jointly and severally liable for restitution. HMRC may seek travel bans, director disqualification, and custodial sentences of up to 5 years on conviction.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR breach penalties up to BMD 100,000 plus continuing penalties up to BMD 20,000 per month; strike-off by the Registrar; loss of limited liability protections; UK HMRC may challenge the alleged Bermuda residence and tax the entity as UK resident at 25% corporation tax.

Director personal liability: The general partner's directors may be disqualified and personally assessed if they allowed the partnership to transact while insolvent or without valid registration. Courts may pierce the corporate veil and treat the ELP as a sham, exposing partners to direct UK taxation.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Bermuda CITA transfer pricing adjustments for in-scope groups; UK transfer pricing and CFC adjustments; penalties up to 100% of the additional tax; interest accrual from the date the tax should have been payable.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve non-arm's-length pricing without documentation may be liable for breach of duty; in egregious cases, UK civil fraud proceedings and disqualification orders apply to directors of the UK trading company and the corporate general partner.
UK Controlled Foreign Company and Residence Attraction

Statutory sanction: UK CFC apportionment charge on the UK corporate owner; UK Income Tax transfer of assets abroad charge under ITA 2007 Part XIII; interest and penalties for deliberate non-disclosure.

Director personal liability: UK individual partners or shareholders may be personally assessed for the income of the Bermuda ELP; directors of the UK parent may face disqualification and personal liability if they caused the ELP to accumulate profits without legitimate economic purpose.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Failures

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018 of up to BMD 100,000 per contravention; freezing orders; money laundering criminal liability where funds originate from fraud or unlawful gambling.

Director personal liability: Directors, the general partner, and persons with significant control face personal prosecution, fines, and imprisonment for money laundering in serious cases; travel restrictions and asset forfeiture may be imposed.
