Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance — Office of the Tax Commissioner (Revenue Division), administering payroll tax, land tax and stamp duty; the Registrar of Companies under the Ministry of Finance, administering ELP formation and exempted-entity compliance; and the Bermuda Customs Department under the Ministry of Finance, administering customs duty and import/export clearance. Official Government portal: www.gov.bm. If a UK VAT nexus arises, the competent authority is His Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC), portal: www.gov.uk/vat-registration.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue an income-tax TIN to an Exempted Limited Partnership because Bermuda does not impose corporate or partnership income tax on an ELP. A foreign owner must: (1) form the ELP through a licensed Bermuda corporate service provider by filing the partnership declaration with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 and obtaining a Bermuda Business Registration Number (BRN) / company profile number; (2) if the ELP will have Bermuda premises or regulated activity, obtain the relevant business licence or permit (inapplicable to a passive export-oriented fidget manufacturer without Bermuda premises); (3) if the ELP employs Bermuda-resident staff, register for Bermuda payroll tax with the Tax Commissioner and obtain a payroll tax account ID; (4) if goods are customs-cleared in Bermuda, register an importer/exporter profile with the Bermuda Customs Department; and (5) if the ELP itself makes taxable supplies in the UK or holds goods in the UK at the point of sale, separately register with HMRC for UK VAT as a non-established taxable person. No UK TIN or PAYE reference is automatically generated for a Bermuda ELP; UK registrations arise only upon actual UK nexus or activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda ELP is statutorily transparent and not subject to Bermuda entity-level income tax; profits flow through to partners who are taxed, if at all, in their respective residence jurisdictions. Note: the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 imposes a 15% Bermuda corporate income tax only on constituent entities of in-scope multinational enterprise groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least two of the preceding four fiscal years, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025; the ELP's small-scale silicone fidget segment will not trigger this regime.

Available election forms

Bermuda law does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch classification election; transparency is the default statutory result. The ELP may nevertheless: (a) file a U.S. Form 8832 check-the-box election to be treated as an association taxable as a corporation for U.S. federal tax purposes (a Bermuda limited partnership has elective eligibility under Treas. Reg. § 301.7701-3); (b) appoint a corporate general partner to alter onshore characterization in certain jurisdictions; (c) request written assurance or a tax exemption certificate from the Bermuda Minister of Finance confirming the absence of Bermuda income, profits or capital gains tax on the ELP, subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for large groups; and (d) rely on UK CTA 2009 s. 1258 and CTA 2010 Pt. 6A hybrid mismatch rules, which automatically compare the ELP's overseas classification with UK tax outcomes, but no substantive proactive election is available in Bermuda or under UK statute other than the operational U.S. check-the-box filing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: No VAT/GST — Bermuda is not a VAT/GST jurisdiction; indirect taxation consists of customs/import duty under the Customs Act 1962 and tariff duty under the Customs Tariff Act 1967, together with stamp duty on specified instruments. UK sales nexus: UK Value Added Tax under the Value Added Tax Act 1994, standard rate 20%, applied only where the ELP has a UK taxable presence, makes taxable supplies in the UK, or holds goods in the UK at the point of sale.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Bermuda for VAT/GST (no such tax exists); Bermuda payroll tax and customs duty registrations arise only upon local employment or customs activity. UK VAT: a non-established taxable person (including a Bermuda ELP) must register with HMRC from the first taxable supply of goods or services made in the UK — there is no zero-value threshold under VATA 1994 Sch. 1 para 8 and Sch. 1A; where goods are sold to UK consumers from outside the UK through an online marketplace, the marketplace operator is the deemed supplier and must register and account for VAT; if the ELP becomes UK-established, the registration threshold is £90,000 of VATable turnover in any rolling 12-month period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: not applicable — because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, exports of food-grade silicone fidget toys from Bermuda do not attract any Bermuda indirect tax and there is no zero-rating claim or refund procedure; the only clearance requirement is a customs export/re-export declaration. UK: where an underlying UK-established entity or the ELP (if UK-registered) exports goods from the UK to a destination outside the UK, zero-rating may be claimed under VATA 1994 s. 30(6) and Sch. 4 para 6 (direct export), provided the goods leave the UK within the prescribed period, the exporter holds valid evidence of export (customs declaration, Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, commercial invoice) under VAT Regulations 1995 regs. 126–132, and the goods are not diverted to UK domestic use. Sale of goods consigned directly from Bermuda to UK consumers through an online marketplace does not involve a UK zero-rating claim by the ELP; the marketplace is the deemed supplier and import VAT at 20% is accounted for at the border or by the platform.

  • Commercial invoice (original, itemised, evidencing true consideration and Incoterms)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from the exporting territory
  • Packing list

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control (CTC) mandate exists in Bermuda or the United Kingdom. Bermuda customs entries are filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Department's electronic declaration gateway, but that gateway is not an e-invoicing clearance mechanism and does not validate commercial invoices in real time. In the UK, HMRC does not operate fiscal e-invoice validation; taxpayers must issue compliant VAT invoices under VAT Regulations 1995 (SI 1995/2518) regs. 13–16, and online marketplace operators must maintain records of deemed-supplier transactions. The ELP is not required to integrate an API-based tax clearance system.

In the UK, fraudulent or negligent export zero-rating claims expose the ELP to reassessment of output VAT with interest, civil penalties under VATA 1994 s. 60 (civil evasion, up to 100% of the VAT), s. 67, Finance Act 2007 Sch. 24 (inaccuracy penalties up to 100%) and Sch. 41 (failure to notify), and criminal liability under VATA 1994 s. 72 and CEMA 1979 for dishonest evasion; input VAT credits may be clawed back if supporting export evidence is defective or the prescribed export-evidence window is missed. In Bermuda, false declarations, diversion of duty-exempt goods, or fraudulent re-export claims attract forfeiture, fines of up to three times the duty evaded, and criminal sanctions under the Customs Act 1962; understatement of payroll tax triggers default assessment and penalties under the Payroll Tax Act 1995. The 180-day outward remittance/repatriation deadline is an embedded condition of the ELP's Swift_TT and Platform_Pay payment-rail agreements and bank AML/KYC covenants; late repatriation may trigger provider audits, de-risking, suspension of the payment rail, and review by tax authorities of the export documentation bundle on which zero-rating or duty relief was based.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged to USD at 1:1 and is fully redeemable inside Bermuda without surrender risk. For a non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnership classified as pass-through eligible, both current account transactions (trade settlements, operational disbursements, platform payout receipts) and capital account movements (partner capital contributions, profit distributions) are generally free from convertible-currency controls, provided no resident or local-currency book-entry asset is involved. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal: no Bermuda withholding tax on distributions, interest, or royalties, but the partnership must maintain good standing with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies and submit annual economic substance declarations where applicable. Banks will require the ELP to execute a corporate beneficial ownership declaration for FATCA/CRS classification as a controlled foreign company for the UK investor.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio is imposed on a Bermuda ELP. Export receipts denominated in USD, GBP, EUR, or CNY may be retained in an approved foreign-currency account without conversion into BMD. The fixed peg operates on a voluntary conversion basis; however, if settlement is received in BMD on-island, banks automatically apply the 1:1 peg. Receipts derived from the sale of food-grade silicone tactile sensory fidgets may be credited to a foreign-currency settlement account with the prior written approval of the BMA's Exchange Control, which is normally granted within five business days for standard manufacturing/trading scopes.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) regulating exclusively under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Banking Act 1998, the Deposit Taking Business Act 1999, and the Exchange Control Act 1972, with administrative oversight of the Exchange Control and Precious Metals Section.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Ramp Fit: High

Conditional Medium. A non-BMA-licensed fintech EMI cannot hold BMD settlement accounts under the Deposit Taking Business Act 1999. An offshore EMI incorporated in the UK with an electronic money permission, operating in GBP/EUR/USD and integrated with platform payouts, may be used for pre-funding, FX concentration, and Amazon/Shopify collection, but it does not replace a licensed Bermuda settlement account. The BMA's AML/ATF guidance requires that any EMI holding the partnership's funds be subject to a formal treasury due-diligence review, contractually identified as a sub-custodian, and excluded from accepting retail deposits for Bermudian residents. Practical feasibility is therefore conditional on the EMI offering a business banking relationship for a low-risk novelty-fidget distributor, having a clear off-ramp into the Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda node, and maintaining the beneficial-owner records of the ELP.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial partner contributions will be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the UK USD or GBP account into the Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda capital escrow account using purpose code 'C13 - Contribution to partnership capital' and must reference the ELP certificate number and the capital call notice. The remitting bank will verify the incoming wire against the beneficiary account name; prior to transfer, the Bermuda bank must receive a notarised partnership resolution, the authorised signatory matrix, and the completed AML/KYC source-of-funds questionnaire. BMD is fully pegged to USD, so GBP-to-BMD conversion may be executed on the receipt date through the Bermuda bank's treasury desk under a previously contracted forward or spot confirmation. Inbound funds may not be released to the operational account until the BMA exchange-control permission file is validated and the capital contribution is recorded in the partnership register.Settlement of silicone compression-moulding raw-material and tooling purchases from China and South-East Asia will clear through SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for values below USD 50,000 per beneficiary and through MT700 or MT710 documentary letters of credit for higher-value repetitive shipments. The beneficiary account name and account number must exactly match the commercial invoice header; the remittance reference must include the purchase order number and ELP identifier. Supporting trade documents for each LC draw shall comprise the commercial invoice, packing list, full set of clean-on-board bills of lading, insurance certificate, and the food-grade silicone or LFGB certificate. Platform-payout beneficiaries (Amazon, Shopify, PayPal) will be settled into the operational account via the same clearing rails; no local Bermuda clearing house is used for these outbound remittances.BMD/USD implicit fixed-peg forwards with a licensed Bermuda bank, used primarily to confirm the 1:1 conversion on the day of transaction and to eliminate any intraday revaluation risk on capital escrow balances.USD/CNH non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) executed by the Bermuda bank's global markets desk for hedging raw-material costs to Chinese suppliers without physical CNH settlement.USD/CNY deliverable forwards when the underlying trade invoice is denominated in CNH and settlement occurs through a BMA-licensed correspondent in China.USD/GBP forward contracts to hedge the UK operating expenses, partner performance allocations, and annual compliance fees of the ELP across the 12-month budget cycle.Cross-currency swaps between USD and GBP for the ten-year funding stack apportioned between the UK parent loan and the Bermuda capital account, executed under a bespoke ISDA with a BMA-regulated counterparty.European-style vanilla FX options written on USD/GBP or USD/CNH for the expected profit-distribution period, provided the total option notional does not exceed 25% of the ELP forecast treasury volume and the written premium is fully collateralised.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Conditional Medium. A non-BMA-licensed fintech EMI cannot hold BMD settlement accounts under the Deposit Taking Business Act 1999. An offshore EMI incorporated in the UK with an electronic money permission, operating in GBP/EUR/USD and integrated with platform payouts, may be used for pre-funding, FX concentration, and Amazon/Shopify collection, but it does not replace a licensed Bermuda settlement account. The BMA's AML/ATF guidance requires that any EMI holding the partnership's funds be subject to a formal treasury due-diligence review, contractually identified as a sub-custodian, and excluded from accepting retail deposits for Bermudian residents. Practical feasibility is therefore conditional on the EMI offering a business banking relationship for a low-risk novelty-fidget distributor, having a clear off-ramp into the Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda node, and maintaining the beneficial-owner records of the ELP.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold in BMD under Bermuda domestic law. An ELP engaged in a 'relevant activity' under the Economic Substance Act is not subject to a Bermuda transfer-pricing threshold; however, if the ELP is regarded as a controlled foreign company or is tax-transparent into a high-tax parent jurisdiction, the parent's local transfer-pricing documentation threshold (e.g. UK s.39 Finance Act 2015 or OECD Country-by-Country rules) may apply. For a mid-size fidget manufacturer, the UK local file threshold typically applies when related-party cross-border transactions exceed £50 million gross revenue.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Act (ESA) 2018 and subsequent amendments apply to ELPs deriving income in Bermuda from 'relevant activities' such as distribution and service centres, financing and leasing, and pure equity holding. The surgical silicone fidget manufacturing and wholesale distribution business must demonstrate: (i) you are directed and managed in Bermuda; (ii) core income-generating activities take place in Bermuda (or, for foreign-owned activities, are performed by staff physically in Bermuda); and (iii) you have an adequate number of employees, premises, and annual expenditure proportionate to the level of value generated. An annual Economic Substance Return must be filed with the Registrar. Failure to satisfy ESA results in a BMD 250,000 fine and possible 5-year imprisonment for directors.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Violation / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Act imposes a fixed penalty of BMD 50,000 for failure to file, plus a daily penalty of BMD 1,500 per day. For false or misleading substance filings, criminal fines up to BMD 250,000 and up to 5 years' imprisonment on conviction. The Registrar may also strike the ELP off the Registry, invalidating the limited partnership status.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, or designated partners who knowingly permit the ELP to operate without substance may be personally liable for the penalty sums; a court may issue a worldwide travel ban and asset-freezing order pending full restitution. Conviction can lead to up to 5 years' imprisonment, disqualification from being a director or partner in Bermuda, and personal liability for third-party trade debts where the corporate veil is pierced.
Tax Evasion (Foreign Tax Evasion Facilitation)

Statutory sanction: Bermuda has no domestic income tax, so the offence is prosecuted under the Criminal Procedure Rules and POCA (Proceeds of Crime Act) 1997/2007 as money laundering or conspiracy. Fines are unlimited on conviction in the Supreme Court, and assets traceable to foreign tax evasion shape are forfeited. In the UK, 'failure to prevent tax evasion' (FA 2016) imposes an unlimited fine on a connected body; the ELP's general partner could incur a corporate criminal conviction.

Director personal liability: A general partner who knowingly causes a foreign tax loss is jointly and severally liable with the ELP for any penalty assessed by the foreign revenue authority, up to redoxed assets. Courts will lift the partnership veil to freeze personal bank accounts and order disgorgement of all profits. UK s.36 and specific cooperating agreements allow exigen arrest warrants if the director steps foot in a UK extradition treaty country.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Regulations 2018; financial penalties of up to BMD 200,000 per instance for entities, and personal civil penalties of up to BMD 50,000 for compliance officers. The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) may revoke the ELP's licence to maintain a bank account and/or issue a prohibition notice against the firm.

Director personal liability: A general partner or senior officer who fails to make a suspicious activity report (SAR) is personally guilty of a criminal offence; on conviction, imprisonment for up to 2 years and an unlimited fine. Civil travel bans and terrorism prevention exclusion orders may be issued. The corporate veil is penetrated in favour of third-party victims exercising civil restitution, allowing creditors to seize personal assets.
Fraudulent Trading / Insolvency Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda Companies Act 1981 and Exempted Partnership Act 2015, fraudulent trading is a criminal offence carrying a fine of BMD 50,000 and up to 5 years' imprisonment. On conclusion of winding-up, the court may order a contribution to the distressed ELP's assets from any person who was knowingly party to the fraud. The court may also award whole-victim civil damages.

Director personal liability: The court presumes that every general partner who was knowingly involved is personally liable for all the ELP's liabilities without limit; this liability attaches in addition to any criminal sanction. Directors may be disqualified for 5–15 years in Bermuda and the UK. Severe cases lead to immediate seizure of personal bank accounts under a temporary proprietary injunction and a travel ban pending restitution.
