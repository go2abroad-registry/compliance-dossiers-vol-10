Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the Bermuda Exempted Company via the Registrar of Companies to obtain a Certificate of Incorporation and the unique company registration number, which functions as the de facto tax identifier. 2) File Form 1A (Application for Tax Exemption Certificate) under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (EUTPA) through the Registrar of Companies to secure the statutory guarantee of no future Bermudian income, capital, or withholding taxes. 3) If hiring Bermuda-resident staff, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax within 15 days of the first remuneration payment. 4) File the annual Economic Substance Act 2018 declaration; if a relevant activity is carried on, submit the economic substance return with the Registrar. 5) No VAT/TIN registration obligation exists because Bermuda levies no VAT, sales tax, or corporate income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — The Bermuda Exempted Company is treated as non-resident for exchange control and tax purposes; it is exempt from all Bermudian taxes imposed after incorporation, with protection running until 2035 or up to 50 years from incorporation date under EUTPA 1966; no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax applies; Payroll Tax is borne only where Bermudian payroll exists; Economic Substance Act 2018 obligations may apply to core income-generating activities.

Available election forms

i) EUTPA 1966 Tax Exemption Certificate application (mandatory to activate class-A tax protection). ii) Economic Substance Act 2018 relevant-activity declaration and filing election. iii) Bermuda Monetary Authority exchange control designation as a non-resident entity. iv) U.S. federal 'check-the-box' election on IRS Form 8832 is available to the entity for U.S. tax classification purposes if U.S. owners or U.S.-source digital ticketing activities arise.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_General_Indirect_Tax_System — Bermuda levies no VAT or GST; indirect charges are limited to customs import duties, cargo processing fees, and specific landing or passenger taxes.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not Applicable — no VAT registration threshold exists; importers must register for a customs broker/declarant account with the Bermuda Customs Department at nil value threshold, and employers register for Payroll Tax at a BMD 0 payroll threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not Applicable — absent a VAT regime, the 0% export VAT concept does not exist. Virtual concert metaverse services supplied by a Bermuda Exempted Company to overseas audiences are outside any Bermudian indirect tax charge; no zero-rating election, refund procedure, or export VAT validation is required. U.K./EU place-of-supply or reverse-charge rules apply to the purchaser, and the Bermuda entity should not collect U.K. VAT absent a U.K. establishment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BMD is fully convertible into USD at parity through BMA-licensed authorized dealers; current-account transactions (virtual ticketing revenues, digital licensing, artist royalties) and capital-account movements (share subscriptions, shareholder loans, intercompany repatriations) are freely remitted for a Bermuda Exempted Company that has obtained BMA non-resident exchange-control status. Profit remittance to the UK parent in GBP/USD carries no Bermuda withholding tax and requires no BMA prior approval, subject only to the solvency certificate mandated by the Companies Act 1981 and, from 1 January 2025, the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (15% for multinational groups with ≥EUR 750M consolidated revenue). Residual friction: a company that remains resident for exchange-control purposes must obtain BMA specific approval on each cross-border transaction; therefore the non-resident declaration must be filed at incorporation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender applies to an Exchange-Control Non-Resident Exempted Company. By default, a Bermuda-incorporated exempted company is resident for exchange control and, absent exemption, would be required to offer all eligible foreign-currency export proceeds to an authorized dealer within a statutory surrender window (practically one month). Once the BMA grants non-resident status (Form BMA 101 / general permission for non-resident exempted undertakings), the surrender duty is disapplied in its entirety: the entity may retain USD, GBP, EUR, and USDC revenues in offshore accounts indefinitely and may convert into BMD only at its own election. Maintenance requirement: file the annual BMA exchange-control return and notify any allotment or ownership change within 21 days; lapse of status would retroactively trigger surrender duties and BMA enforcement.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA); sole central bank, monetary authority and exchange-control administrator under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Companies Act 1981, the Beneficial Ownership Act 2017, and the Digital Asset Business Act 2018. The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is issued at a statutory 1:1 peg to the U.S. Dollar (USD), with USD legal tender accepted interchangeably.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW-MEDIUM for direct onboarding of the Bermuda entity. EEA/UK EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business) require a principal establishment in their licensing jurisdiction; Bermuda is outside the PSD2/Electronic Money Regulations 2011 perimeter, and correspondent de-risking of offshore structures further depresses approval rates. The compliant workaround is the 'UK parent-sweep' architecture—the UK parent holds an Airwallex/Wise Business UK account and settles intercompany to the Bermuda treasury via SWIFT MT103. For the USDC leg, a Bermuda DABA-licensed custodian (e.g., Copper, BitGo) or a UK FCA-registered cryptoasset firm provides a regulated stablecoin treasury wallet, effectively substituting for an EMI without regulatory violation. A Bermuda exempted company should not open an EMI under a false UK address; that constitutes a cross-border passporting breach.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a qualifying Bermuda Exempted Company that is not part of a large multinational group; 15% for a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (Globe Minimum Top-up Tax).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Bermuda does not impose any withholding tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to its shareholders, whether resident or non-resident).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 6 months after the financial year-end; annual government fee generally due on or before the anniversary of incorporation or 30 June for calendar-year filers.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Initial return due no later than 15 months after the end of the first in-scope fiscal year; subsequent returns due within 12 months after the fiscal year-end. Payment of any top-up tax is due on or before the statutory return deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general Bermuda transfer-pricing documentation threshold applies to a domestic exempted company. However, when the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue exceeds EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million), the Bermuda CIT Act and OECD Global Anti-Base Erosion Rules require preparation of a GloBE information return, local file/master file equivalent, and supporting tax computations. The Bermuda Tax Authority may also request contemporaneous pricing documentation in any top-up tax audit or economic substance review.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act and associated regulations require any registered entity carrying on a 'relevant activity' to demonstrate adequate substance in Bermuda. Relevant activities include banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centres, and intellectual-property-holding businesses. Virtual performance hosting and digital ticket tokenization will be captured only if the business is re-characterized as an IP-holding activity or a distribution/service-centre activity. If the entity merely processes ticket sales through a Stripe or USDC payment rail without local management, it should still file an annual ESR declaration confirming no relevant activity; inadequate substance for a relevant activity can result in significant financial penalties and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration under CIT Act 2023

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to BMD 100,000 plus daily default surcharges, reassessment of top-up tax with interest, forfeiture of exempted status, and criminal fines for knowingly submitting false documents.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false CIT return or knowingly procure a false declaration may be personally assessed for restitution, prohibited from acting as directors in Bermuda, subjected to travel restrictions, and imprisoned under Bermuda's criminal tax provisions.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR penalties up to BMD 250,000, additional daily penalties, court-ordered winding up, and automatic strike-off of the company from the Bermudian registry; the entity may lose its tax exemption and be deemed taxable at 15%.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly allow the entity to operate without substance are jointly and severally liable for unpaid penalties; travel bans can be imposed pending payment, and disqualification from holding any corporate office in Bermuda may apply.
AML / Financial-Crime Breach

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism) Regulations 2018, suspension or revocation of any licence or registration, forfeiture of criminal assets, and imprisonment for serious economic crime.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Bermuda compliance officer may face criminal prosecution, disqualification orders, and personal fines if they consent to or negligently permit AML failures; a travel ban may be imposed where there is a risk of dissipation of assets.
Improper Profit Repatriation and UK Source-Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: UK HMRC may apply transfer-pricing adjustments, assess diverted profits tax, impose penalties for offshore structures, and charge interest; Bermuda may separately impose false-return penalties if the related documents filed in Bermuda are inconsistent.

Director personal liability: UK and Bermuda directors can be pursued jointly for unpaid diverted profits tax and penalties where the structure was devised principally to avoid UK tax; civil recovery orders can reach personal assets, and criminal liability may arise for cheating the public revenue.
