Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda ELP is tax-transparent under Bermuda law and is not an income-tax subject; Bermuda currently imposes no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or dividend/interest/royalty withholding tax, and the ELP's non-Bermuda-source profits are outside Bermuda's territorial scope. The annual cost is limited to a modest government fee scaled to partnership capital bands. From FY2025, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (15% on Bermuda business of large MNE groups) may apply, but the default ELP form remains transparent, with profits flowing to partners unless group attribution rules recharacterise the activity. No Bermuda VAT/GST or sales-and-use tax exists, so the ELP is not a Bermudian indirect-tax registrant.

Available election forms

Bermuda offers no check-the-box income-tax election because no general Bermuda income tax exists. For U.S. partners, the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 under Treasury Regulation section 301.7701-3 to elect corporate (opaque) treatment for U.S. federal tax purposes; the default is transparent partnership status. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, in-scope groups may make Minister-prescribed elections addressing partnership treatment, group registration, domestic minimum top-up tax, and substance-based transition relief. For UK-exposed structures, the ELP is a non-UK overseas partnership under CTA 2010 sections 1149-1161, and UK members are taxed on allocated profits; elections under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A may be deployed to neutralise hybrid mismatches. The ELP deed may also include tax classification elections for third jurisdictions, but no separate Bermuda statutory tax classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda has no VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax; the only indirect tax is import customs duty (tariff rates generally 0-25%). UK VAT under VATA 1994 at the standard rate of 20% applies only if the ELP makes taxable supplies in the United Kingdom through a UK establishment; offshore bulk sale, brokerage, chartering, and Letter of Credit settlement activities performed wholly outside the UK are outside the scope of UK VAT, and any UK export supply of goods would be zero-rated.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: N/A — no indirect tax registration threshold exists for the ELP. United Kingdom: mandatory VAT registration arises when UK taxable turnover exceeds GBP 90,000 (approximately BMD 90,000 given the Bermudian Dollar peg at 1:1 USD) in any rolling 12-month period; a non-established taxable person must register regardless of threshold if it makes UK taxable supplies, unless the supplies are relevant supplies not exceeding GBP 8,000 per 12 months.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda does not tax exports: no zero-rating claim or refund procedure is necessary for granular elemental sulfur shipped from or transshipped through Bermuda. For UK export flows, goods physically removed from the UK to a destination outside the UK are zero-rated under VATA 1994 section 30 and Schedule 8 Group 14 provided the ELP holds valid commercial and customs transport evidence and the goods leave the UK within 3 months of the time of supply; offshore brokerage, chartering, demurrage, and ancillary services rendered to non-UK customers are outside the scope of UK VAT under the B2B place-of-supply rule in section 7A VATA 1994.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 USD peg under a currency-board style mechanism. Bermuda maintains a liberalized exchange-control regime: bonafide current-account payments, trade settlements, and profit remittances can be made freely through BMA-licensed authorized dealers, subject to documentary anti-money-laundering and balance-of-payments reporting. As an exempted limited partnership (ELP) treated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes, KY_ELP is not ordinarily required to convert trade receipts or capital contributions into BMD; USD-denominated operating accounts are accepted. The practical remittance hurdles are documentary: the bank must hold supporting agreements, invoices, shipping documents, partner contribution schedules, and beneficial-ownership declarations. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax, capital gains tax, or profit-repatriation restriction on an exempted partnership, so convertibility does not create a material tax or treasury friction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations, export proceeds from bulk sulfur/cargo shipments must be routed through a BMA-authorized dealer in Bermuda and must be received within six months from the date of export. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts held by a non-resident exempted partnership; in practice, the BMA permits USD trade proceeds to be retained in the partnership's foreign-currency working-account, provided each receipt is matched to a commercial contract, invoice, bill of lading, and export/customs declaration. For resident entities or where BMA direction applies, the authorized dealer may request that foreign-currency proceeds be offered for disposal or conversion into BMD, but for KY_ELP the default acceptable structure is USD retention in Bermuda licensed bank accounts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), empowered under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972 as the currency-issuing authority and exchange-control administrator for the British Overseas Territory of Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium as an auxiliary settlement rail, but not as a primary statutory banking channel. FCA-authorized UK EMIs, EU EMIs, and OECD-licensed digital banking platforms can support multi-currency pay-outs to overseas suppliers and provide convenient SWIFT-style rails; however, they are not authorized dealers under Bermuda exchange control, cannot replace a BMA-licensed bank for export-proceeds receipt, cannot hold the partnership's capital escrow in Bermuda, and must not be used to obscure the partnership's true banking relationship. Legal constraints include: (i) an offshore EMI is not supervised by the BMA and cannot provide a Bermudian account number; (ii) payments routed via an EMI should be clearly transparent to the underlying Bermuda authorized-dealer relationship; (iii) the EMI must itself be licensed in its home jurisdiction and capable of demonstrating OFAC/EU/UK sanctions screening; and (iv) the general partner should obtain a written legal/AML opinion before relying on any EMI as the principal collection vehicle.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

USD capital contributions should be wired by SWIFT MT103 from the contributing partner's corporate or fully identified personal account to KY_ELP's Bermuda account maintained at a BMA-licensed authorized dealer. The remittance instruction must identify the exact legal name of the exempted limited partnership, the beneficiary account number, the Bermuda bank's BIC/SWIFT code, and must clearly state the purpose as 'Capital contribution to exempted limited partnership for wholesale granular sulfur mineral ore trade.' Where the USD leg is cleared through the United States, the remitter bank should use Fedwire/CHIPS with an MT103 SWIFT conversion and include the originator's LEI or full corporate identity. The receiving Bermuda bank will map the purpose to a direct-investment/partner-capital balance-of-payments classification and will perform source-of-funds verification before allowing commingling with trade cash flows. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium as an auxiliary settlement rail, but not as a primary statutory banking channel. FCA-authorized UK EMIs, EU EMIs, and OECD-licensed digital banking platforms can support multi-currency pay-outs to overseas suppliers and provide convenient SWIFT-style rails; however, they are not authorized dealers under Bermuda exchange control, cannot replace a BMA-licensed bank for export-proceeds receipt, cannot hold the partnership's capital escrow in Bermuda, and must not be used to obscure the partnership's true banking relationship. Legal constraints include: (i) an offshore EMI is not supervised by the BMA and cannot provide a Bermudian account number; (ii) payments routed via an EMI should be clearly transparent to the underlying Bermuda authorized-dealer relationship; (iii) the EMI must itself be licensed in its home jurisdiction and capable of demonstrating OFAC/EU/UK sanctions screening; and (iv) the general partner should obtain a written legal/AML opinion before relying on any EMI as the principal collection vehicle.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or distribution tax on an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP). In-scope multinational groups (consolidated revenue >= EUR 750m in at least two of the four preceding years) are subject to a 15% Corporate Income Tax under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025; the subject ELP is treated as out of scope.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda levies no withholding tax on dividends, distributions, capital gains, or profit remittances of an ELP; distributions to the UK parent or UK limited partners are made gross and free of Bermudian tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File/Master File threshold exists for out-of-scope ELPs under Bermuda law. Prudent compliance: maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation for all cross-border related-party transactions in the sulfur chain (procurement, freight, brokerage) once aggregate connected-party turnover reaches approximately BMD 1,000,000 (or EUR/USD equivalent). For CITA in-scope groups (EUR 750m+ revenue), the OECD three-tier approach applies: Master File for the group, Local File for Bermudian constituent entities, and Country-by-Country reporting where turnover exceeds EUR 850m; the UK parent must also satisfy HMRC's CbC notification duties.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) 2018 apply to any Bermuda ELP carrying on a relevant activity and tax resident in Bermuda. Dry bulk marine transport and brokerage of granular elemental sulfur will be classified as 'Shipping' or 'Distribution and Service Centre'. A resident ELP must: (i) be managed and directed in Bermuda (general partner meetings, board minutes, strategic decisions in Bermuda); (ii) maintain an operating office and local records; (iii) engage adequate full-time employees (typically two or more for a distribution and freight operation); (iv) incur expenditure proportionate to the core income-generating activity; and (v) file the annual ESR return. If the ELP is managed and controlled in the UK, it is not Bermudian tax resident and may be excluded from Bermuda ESR, provided the Registrar accepts the foreign residence declaration and the ELP demonstrates UK substance; UK HMRC may then treat the ELP as UK resident and tax its worldwide income at up to 25% corporation tax.
