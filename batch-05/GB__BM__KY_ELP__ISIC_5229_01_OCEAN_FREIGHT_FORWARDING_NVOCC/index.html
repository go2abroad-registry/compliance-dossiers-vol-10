Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent in Bermuda (no entity-level income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax; profits and losses pass through to partners). For UK tax purposes, classification follows UK domestic look-through principles: an ELP without separate legal personality is treated as transparent; an ELP electing separate legal personality under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2019 may be treated as opaque (company-like), creating UK Permanent Establishment and Corporation Tax exposure under CTA 2010. Available election forms

Bermuda: (i) separate-legal-personality election under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2019 s.4; (ii) ministerial tax-exemption undertaking / tax-assurance election under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 affording a protected period free of future Bermuda taxes. United Kingdom: no check-the-box regime; entity classification is determined by statute and case law. UK anti-hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) may recharacterise payments or disallow deductions where the ELP is treated inconsistently in another jurisdiction. US investors may file IRS Form 8832 electing partnership (default) or association (corporation) classification for US federal tax purposes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

United Kingdom: 0% VAT zero-rating is claimable where the NVOCC supplies (i) transport of goods from the UK to a destination outside the UK or into the UK from a place outside the UK, (ii) arranging, broking, or forwarding services directly connected with such international transport of goods (VATA 1994 Sch. 8 Group 8), or (iii) services supplied to a non-UK customer under the B2B place-of-supply customer-location rule. The ELP must be the UK-established or deemed-established supplier, must obtain valid export/removal evidence within 3 months of the time of supply, and must retain the evidence pack for six years. Bermuda: no export tax or VAT exists, therefore no zero-rating claim is required; export receipts are tax-neutral in Bermuda.

United Kingdom: no real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping requirement. Making Tax Digital for VAT mandates digital record-keeping and API-based filing through MTD-compatible software to HMRC; export declarations must be lodged via the Customs Declaration Service (CDS), which has replaced CHIEF, with Safety and Security declarations filed through the UK Single Trade Window API. Bermuda: no e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime.

HMRC scrutinises NVOCC zero-rating claims by cross-checking the CDS export declaration, bill-of-lading trail, and payment remittance. Missing, false, or untimely export evidence triggers a VAT assessment plus interest (VATA 1994 s.73), retrospective clawbacks for up to 4 years (extended to 20 years for deliberate non-disclosure or fraud), penalties up to 100% of the VAT in issue under FA 2007 Sch. 24, and criminal liability, including imprisonment up to 7 years, for fraudulent evasion under VATA 1994 s.72. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar, and USD circulates freely alongside BMD. Bermuda operates an externalised exchange-control regime: payments to and from the BMA External Area (comprising the United Kingdom, the United States, Ireland, and other scheduled sterling-area territories) are fully convertible on current account without prior BMA consent. Capital-account transactions, including partnership capital subscriptions, redemption of limited-partner interests, capital-return distributions, and cross-border loans from non-External Area lenders, require BMA consent or a bank-attested notice filing. Profit remittances to non-resident limited partners face no Bermuda withholding tax, income tax, or statutory currency charge because the ELP is tax-transparent and offshore income is exempt; the material hurdle is banking-level form submission, external-area account classification, and source-of-funds re-verification rather than any monetary authority restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation timeline for export or freight receivables. USD collections of the NVOCC may be retained indefinitely in USD-denominated operating accounts without conversion to BMD. The only legal duties are: (i) retention of underlying transport documents (house and master bills of lading, booking notes, carrier invoices, and payment instructions) for at least five years under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008; and (ii) transaction reporting by the authorised bank where aggregated credits exceed the internal reporting threshold, in practice USD 10,000 equivalent. No BMA declaration is required for holding or reinvesting USD export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the integrated financial regulator of Bermuda, exercising delegated statutory powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (Bermuda). The Bank of England and HM Treasury retain sovereign connection through the UK Crown relationship but exercise no direct regulatory or exchange-control authority over Bermuda banking; the BMA is the sole local foreign-exchange and prudential regulator.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM as a supplementary receiving rail, LOW as a primary treasury bank. Bermuda is not within the EEA Electronic Money Directive passport, and an FCA-authorised EMI cannot itself maintain a Bermuda-licensed banking relationship without a sponsor-bank or segregated client-money arrangement with a licensed Bermuda bank. Leading UK EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Modulr) generally decline Bermuda-incorporated exempted partnerships because the jurisdiction is classified as a non-EEA offshore centre and the NVOCC scope carries an enhanced AML profile. The viable structure is to open a UK GBP sweep account in the name of a UK operating affiliate, if one exists, and auto-sweep balances via SWIFT MT103 to the Bermuda USD operational account; the ELP itself should not rely on an EMI for ocean-freight settlement or capital custody.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for the initial USD capital injection: (i) the remitting UK or US limited partner issues a SWIFT MT103 with field 50K naming the remitter, field 59 naming the 'KY_ELP' account holder with the Bermuda bank account number and the receiving bank's SWIFT BIC as per the account-opening schedule, and field 70 narrative 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - EXEMPTED LIMITED PARTNERSHIP SUBSCRIPTION - NO GOODS EXPORTED'; (ii) the USD leg settles via CHAPS or Fedwire to the Bermuda bank's New York correspondent before SWIFT posting to the Hamilton branch; (iii) the receiving authorised dealer matches the credit to the capital-escrow sub-account against the executed subscription agreement; (iv) no BMA prior consent is required where the originator is within the External Area, but the bank must complete source-of-funds verification and file a suspicious-activity report where aggregate credits exceed USD 10,000 without a documentary trail; (v) posting to the partner capital ledger occurs no later than T+1, with a capital-call notice and contribution receipt retained in the audit file. Where the remitting bank supports ISO 20022, purpose code 'CAPC' (Capital Payment) is used in place of a free-format narrative.The primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer) for carrier freight, terminal handling charges, customs duties, and haulage disbursements from the Bermuda operational account. MT202 COV is appended as interbank cover where the beneficiary bank requires a separate cover instruction. For high-value ocean-freight contracts (charter-party hires or milestone settlements of USD 250,000 and above), an irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued by SWIFT MT700 from Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda is the recommended secondary rail, with the house bill of lading and carrier master bill of lading as the required transport documents. The 'ACH Direct' rail referenced in the task payload is mapped to USD ACH entries routed via the Bermuda bank's US correspondent; this is permissible only for low-value domestic US payables (e.g., US port and customs fees under USD 10,000) with a 3-5 business day clearing horizon. All outbound payments to non-External Area beneficiaries require the authorised bank's exchange-control release and, where capital amounts are involved, a BMA notice-to-transfer confirmation.Foreign-exchange forward contracts (FX Forwards) in USD/BMD and GBP/USD with tenors up to 12 months, executed through Butterfield, HSBC Bermuda, or their liquidity providers, covering NVOCC freight receivables and operating-cost exposures.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in USD/EUR and USD/CNH, used where the ELP invoices European or Asian shippers; permitted only through the tier-1 Bermuda bank's offshore treasury desk and documented under a signed ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or a bank-form FX Master Agreement.Cross-currency swaps (GBP/USD and USD/EUR) for converting multi-currency receivables into USD operating liquidity; subject to BMA exchange-control notice, the bank's derivatives-eligibility approval, and hedge-accounting documentation where the swaps are designated as cash-flow hedges of trade receivables.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM as a supplementary receiving rail, LOW as a primary treasury bank. Bermuda is not within the EEA Electronic Money Directive passport, and an FCA-authorised EMI cannot itself maintain a Bermuda-licensed banking relationship without a sponsor-bank or segregated client-money arrangement with a licensed Bermuda bank. Leading UK EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Modulr) generally decline Bermuda-incorporated exempted partnerships because the jurisdiction is classified as a non-EEA offshore centre and the NVOCC scope carries an enhanced AML profile. The viable structure is to open a UK GBP sweep account in the name of a UK operating affiliate, if one exists, and auto-sweep balances via SWIFT MT103 to the Bermuda USD operational account; the ELP itself should not rely on an EMI for ocean-freight settlement or capital custody.
