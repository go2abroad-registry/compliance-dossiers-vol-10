Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda, Hamilton (administering payroll tax and customs duties; no profits or income tax regime). Portal: https://www.gov.bm/. UK VAT: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), authority under the Value Added Tax Act 1994; portal: https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda has no statutory income-tax TIN. A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must: (1) incorporate under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 and obtain a Business Registration Number (BRN) from the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; (2) register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Bermuda payroll tax on Bermudian employees; (3) apply for a Certificate of Exemption granting statutory tax-exempt status until 2035; and (4) if the ELP intends to make taxable supplies in the UK, register for UK VAT via HMRC form VAT1 (non-established entity uses form VAT1GB and must appoint a tax representative or agent), obtaining a UK VAT registration number with a 'GB' or non-established prefix. The ELP may alternatively register for UK VAT voluntarily even if below the threshold, subject to HMRC approval and fiscal safeguards.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; the ELP is fiscally transparent and not subject to entity-level taxation on profits, capital gains, income, distributions, or interest in Bermuda. Bermuda levies only employer payroll tax (a territorial charge on Bermudian payroll) and customs duties on imports. The ELP's exemption certificate confers Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment through 31 March 2035, with no minimum capital requirement; full non-resident ownership and management from overseas are permitted.

Available election forms

Bermuda law provides no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election because the jurisdiction does not tax profits. For United States owners, the ELP may file a U.S. Form 8832 check-the-box election; in the absence of an election, a multi-member ELP is treated as a partnership by default. Under UK taxation, the ELP is within the UK hybrid mismatch rules of Part 6A, Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010; the partners may need to elect to ensure transparency or counteraction where a deduction/non-inclusion mismatch arises, and the UK CFC rules may apply to UK-resident partners. No separate Bermudian election is required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None in Bermuda; the territory applies import customs duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1975, but has no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. UK VAT at 20% standard rate applies when the ELP makes taxable supplies of goods in the UK, including importation of electro-medical equipment from Bermuda into Great Britain or Northern Ireland.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Bermuda (no VAT/GST regime; customs duty is transaction-based, not turnover-based). In the UK, compulsory VAT registration arises when taxable turnover exceeds GBP 90,000 in a rolling 12-month period, or when it becomes apparent that turnover will exceed that threshold within 30 days; a non-established ELP supplying goods in the UK must register if it makes any taxable supply regardless of threshold unless zero-rated, with a voluntary registration available to recover input VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda imposes no export VAT, export duty, or customs exit charge; goods exported from Bermuda are outside the scope of local taxation. To claim UK 0% export zero-rating, the ELP must export goods to a destination outside the UK and retain conclusive evidence of export within 3 months of the supply; the supply must qualify as an export under VATA 1994 s.30, Schedule 8, Group 15, Item 4. For ISIC 3250 patient monitoring hardware, the goods must also comply with UKCA or CE marking requirements and, where relevant, MHRA registration for medical devices; neither the electronic nature nor installation or calibration services alter the strict documentary evidence burden.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK CDS/CHIEF SAD C88) with Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing departure from the UK or Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice describing the multi-parameter bedside telemetry monitors and ISIC 3250 classification
  • Packing list and shipping instructions
  • Port or airport export evidence (stamped/electronic departure confirmation)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice proving settlement by the overseas customer
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Insurance certificate covering the consignment
  • UKCA/CE certificates and MHRA or equivalent regulatory records for the medical device

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory B2B electronic-invoicing clearance regime exists in Bermuda. For UK VAT, Making Tax Digital for VAT requires the ELP to maintain digital records and submit VAT returns to HMRC via API-enabled functional compatible software; this is a post-return digital reporting mandate, not a pre-clearance approval or digital stamping requirement. HMRC may require electronic filing of customs declarations through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) for exports.

Bermuda imposes no clawback or penalty in respect of export zero-rating because exports are untaxed. For UK VAT claims, HMRC may retrospectively assess VAT under VATA 1994 s.73 with interest where zero-rating evidence is missing, incorrect, or fraudulent. Deliberate inaccuracies attract penalties up to 100% of the VAT (Finance Act 2007, Schedule 24); fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence punishable by up to 7 years' imprisonment, an unlimited fine, and seizure of goods (VATA 1994 s.72); customs civil penalties apply under the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979. HMRC may claw back wrongfully zero-rated VAT for a standard period of 4 years, extended to 20 years where fraud, neglect, or deliberate misconduct is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar (USD) and is fully convertible. The Exchange Control Act is administered with a permissive licensing framework; current account transactions, including profit remittances, royalties, and management fees, generally require no prior approval. Capital account transactions, including partnership capital injections, distributions, and dissolution payments, are permitted without quota or surrender obligations, subject to regulatory reporting and AML/CFT screening. No material hurdles are anticipated for a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership distributing profits to non-resident partners, provided the partnership maintains proper FATCA/CRS filings and economic substance or exemption filings, where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts in Bermuda. Traders may retain export proceeds in BMD, USD, or any foreign currency. Banking records must support the underlying commercial transaction, and the BMA may inquire into unusual flows under the Proceeds of Crime (AML/CFT) Regulations 2018. The ELP should maintain export invoices, bills of lading, and proof of medical-device calibration certification for audit trails.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — integrated financial services regulator and de facto monetary authority; exchange control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for full banking reliance. Bermuda does not passport European Economic Area (EEA) Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licences into the jurisdiction. Offshore digital banks and EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Airwallex, Wise Business) may support ancillary FX conversion and payments but are not substitutes for a BMA-licensed deposit-taking institution. The ELP must maintain a primary operating account with a Bermuda-licensed bank or a qualifying Class A/B bank for statutory solvency, capital-call custody, and trade settlement. Foreign EMIs may be used only as low-value disbursement rails if the domestic bank permits payouts to third-party settlement accounts, and this is discouraged by Bermuda's AML/CFT expected standards.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% ordinary Bermuda domestic income tax rate; 15% headline rate under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for constituent entities of multinational enterprise groups with consolidated group revenue >= EUR 750 million

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on distributions declared by an exempted limited partnership, including dividend-equivalent pass-through allocations to limited partners

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general transfer pricing documentation threshold under Bermuda domestic law for a standard ELP. For in-scope MNE groups, the threshold is consolidated group revenue of EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 805 million at current rates); constituent entities recording any related-party transaction with a Bermuda resident or non-resident connected person must maintain a local file and master file consistent with OECD TP Guidelines, irrespective of the transaction amount.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply to the ELP if it conducts a relevant activity. Assembly and calibration of monitoring devices may be regarded as a distribution and service centre activity where the ELP provides purchasing, warehousing, calibration, or technical services to group affiliates. The ELP must then demonstrate adequate physical premises, qualified employees, active management and oversight in Bermuda and incur 'core income generating activity' in Bermuda. Annual filing is mandatory, and a shell ELP with consolidated revenue in excess of the threshold but devoid of substance is treated as a high-risk entity for automatic exchange of information and UK public country-by-country reporting.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / filing false economic substance returns

Statutory sanction: On conviction on indictment, a fine of BMD 250,000 plus daily default fines of BMD 5,000; in addition, the Court may order restitution of all taxes evaded plus interest. For CIT matters, a penalty equal to 20% of the gross tax adjustment applies; deliberate concealment attracts a 100% penalty.

Director personal liability: The general partner and its directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution where the ELP is found to be a vehicle for tax abuse. The Bermuda Court of Appeal has upheld worldwide freezing orders and travel bans on non-resident general partner directors; up to 14 years' imprisonment may be imposed for fraudulent evasion of tax under the Revenue Fraud Act 1997 (as incorporated by the Criminal Code).
Shell company / economic substance failure

Statutory sanction: Annual penalty up to BMD 500,000 for each relevant activity failure; cancellation of the exemption from international tax; the Registrar may strike the ELP off the register. Additionally, the financial regulator may issue a direction to cease activity and impose a BMD 25,000 per month penalty.

Director personal liability: The court may 'look through' the ELP and make a contribution order against the general partner for the full amount of unpaid taxes and penalties. Directors who cannot demonstrate decision-making in Bermuda are subject to disqualification orders of up to 10 years and a personal fine of BMD 100,000.
Anti-Money Laundering / beneficial ownership return violations

Statutory sanction: BMD 50,000 fine payable by the partnership and an additional BMD 10,000 for each individual contravention; the Registrar may issue an information notice and freeze transfers. Under the Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2020, knowingly providing false information is an arrestable offence carrying up to five years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: The general partner directors and any person exercising 'senior managing official' responsibility are personally liable for the fines if it is proved that they consented or connived in the concealment. An international restriction order can be placed on non-resident individuals, barring them from leaving Bermuda until the AML inquiry is completed.
Transfer pricing misreporting and CIT base erosion

Statutory sanction: Where the Bermuda CIT applies, the tax authority may recharacterise the margin to an arm-length return, impose an additional tax of 20% of the undercharging adjustment, and charge late payment interest of 3% above the central bank discount rate. The same adjustment is exchangeable with HM Revenue & Customs under the UK-Bermuda mutual assistance procedure.

Director personal liability: Senior accounting officers of the general partner must sign a certificate of tax accuracy; if the certificate is false, they are liable for a BMD 50,000 civil penalty and a prohibition on holding directorships in Bermuda for seven years. Criminal prosecution can follow if the misreporting was part of a fraudulent arrangement, exposing the directors to imprisonment of up to seven years.
Illicit profit repatriation through dividend/fee-stripping structures

Statutory sanction: Bermuda imposes no WHT, but under the UK anti-hybrid rules and the UK's 'Offshore Receipts in respect of Intangible Property' (ORIP) regime, the UK parent may be taxed on deemed income; the ELP can be subject to a UK information notice with a penalty of GBP 3,000 plus GBP 600 per day. If the arrangement involves arrangements to defeat the UK tax, the UK tribunal may issue a disclosure order against Bermuda-incorporated entities.

Director personal liability: Directors of the UK corporate general partner face joint and several liability for the UK tax under the transactions in securities provisions. In severe cases, a freezing order may be granted against the Bermuda ELP's bank accounts and the director's personal assets; imprisonment of up to 12 months may be imposed for non-compliance with a court order to repatriate funds.
