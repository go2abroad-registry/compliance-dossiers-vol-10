Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; exempted undertaking under Bermuda's Exempted Partnerships Act 2015; ELP is not resident or ordinarily resident for Bermuda tax, is statutorily exempt from Bermuda income, corporate, capital gains, dividend, estate, and withholding taxes; no VAT/GST is levied; the partnership is treated as fiscally transparent, with partners taxed in their own jurisdictions; the ELP becomes subject to the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 only if it is within an MNE group exceeding the EUR 750 million revenue threshold.

Available election forms

Bermuda law does not use a check-the-box election; an ELP is tax transparent unless foreign tax law requires otherwise. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the partnership may make an entity-classification election (Form 8832) to be treated as an association taxable as a corporation. Under the Bermuda CIT Act 2023, limited elections exist for Bermuda taxable entities in MNE groups, including the election to calculate taxable income on prudential regulatory/certified basis or transitional notional deductions. There is no general hybrid mismatch election in Bermuda domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Bermuda does not impose VAT, GST, or retail sales tax; the relevant indirect levies are customs and excise duties under the Bermuda Customs Tariff Act 1996, stamp duty, and import duty.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no such tax is imposed. For customs duty, all commercial imports into Bermuda require registration with Bermuda Customs via ASYCUDA World and clearance by a licensed customs broker; there is no general monetary exemption threshold for commercial importers, but certain de minimis personal exemptions apply. An Annual Customs Declaration/import license is required for continuing import activity.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT/GST; exports of goods and offshore services are not subject to Bermuda consumption tax, so there is no 0% VAT rate to claim and no VAT refund mechanism. Exporters must nevertheless comply with Bermuda Customs export declaration rules, applicable economic substance requirements, and foreign currency repatriation expectations in their underlying partner jurisdictions.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or bank receipt evidencing export proceeds received in a Bermuda licensed bank where applicable)
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Importer/exporter code certificate issued by Bermuda Customs

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing clearing mandate, or digital stamping requirement exists in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs operates the UN/CEFACT-compliant ASYCUDA World electronic single-window system; imports and exports must be declared electronically, and customs documentary packages are processed through pre-arrival electronic submission. There is no API-based continuous transaction control or government-mandated e-invoice standard.

Fraudulent export misdeclarations are punishable under the Customs Act 1967, with fines of up to BMD 10,000 for individuals and BMD 50,000 for bodies corporate, forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment of responsible officers for up to two years on conviction. False or misleading statements made to the Office of the Tax Commissioner under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 attract penalties of up to BMD 25,000 plus interest at up to 15% per annum, and may lead to retroactive revocation of exempted undertaking status. Foreign tax authorities may also re-characterize the ELP as a controlled foreign corporation if the export flow lacks substance, clawing back profits at the partner level.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at par (1:1) to the US Dollar (USD) and is fully convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions. Bermuda maintains no exchange-control restrictions applicable to Exempted Limited Partnerships: dividends, partnership distributions, redemption proceeds, interest, and liquidation surpluses may be remitted freely to non-resident partners without BMA pre-approval or conversion obligation. The only practical constraints are documentary — evidence of economic substance, accurate FATCA/CRS classification, and AML/ATF compliance with the ELP's Bermuda-regulated bank. Profit remittance enjoys a zero-rate Bermuda tax environment; there is no withholding tax or capital gains levy on outbound distributions. Cross-currency friction is minimal because USD-denominated treasury positions eliminate BMD/USD conversion risk, while GBP and EUR flows are freely convertible through the commercial banking system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Bermuda. The BMA neither requires repatriation of export receipts within any fixed period nor mandates conversion of foreign-currency income into BMD. For the AI pet robot trade model (ISIC 3240 hardware assembly), export receipts in GBP/USD generated via the UK Stripe and card-payment rails may be retained in multi-currency accounts indefinitely; conversion into USD/BMD is a discretionary treasury function. UK-connected flows are also free of surrender rules under the UK's post-1979 liberalised exchange-control regime; however, HMRC record-keeping obligations, the UK Transfer Pricing rules, and CRS/FATCA reporting duties apply to the economic activities of the General Partner and any UK-arranged distribution activities.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting as the integrated financial services regulator, central monetary authority, currency issuer of the Bermudian Dollar, and supervisor under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, the Digital Asset Business Act 2018, and the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008; ultimate sovereign oversight resides with HM Treasury and the UK Privy Council for the Bermuda Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium feasibility subject to legal constraints. Bermuda does not maintain a dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing category; payment-service providers must hold either a restricted banking licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, a money-service-business licence under the Moneylenders Act, or a relevant digital-asset business authorisation. Offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex, Currencycloud, and Wise Business may be onboarded as supplemental multi-currency rails for collecting UK/EU e-commerce payouts from Stripe and card acquirers, but they cannot lawfully serve as the primary clearing institution because BMA-regulated banks require a qualifying Bermuda banking relationship for substance, FATCA, and regulatory transparency. The recommended operating model is a hybrid: a Bermuda tier-1 bank as the treasury anchor holding the capital escrow and settlement accounts, with a UK-authorised EMI (FCA-regulated) as a short-cycle collection conduit that batched-sweeps GBP receipts to the Bermuda operational account at least weekly. Structured correctly, this arrangement reduces cross-border clearing fees and speeds reconciliation while remaining squarely within BMA guidance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions are routed via SWIFT MT103 or UK CHAPS from partner accounts to the Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda escrow account in USD/GBP. For sub-£1M contributions, UK Faster Payments may be used but SWIFT is preferred for an unambiguous audit trail and to align with BMA inspection expectations. The remittance purpose code on the wire should indicate 'Partner Capital Contribution / Equity Injection' and the beneficiary reference must quote the ELP registration number and the capital-call resolution number. On first funding, the bank requires (i) a signed General Partner capital-call resolution, (ii) the LPA contribution schedule, and (iii) an executed subscription/acceptance form from each limited partner; for wires exceeding USD 50,000, current FATCA/CRS self-certifications (Form W-8BEN-E for non-US partners or W-9 for US persons) and proof of the underlying commercial relationship must be pre-filed. The escrow account is contractually restricted so that funds cannot be released for operating expenditure until the capital-call condition is confirmed by the General Partner, protecting both investor and bank expectations.Trade settlements for component purchases (servo motors, speech-synthesis modules, polymer enclosures, battery systems) and logistics use SWIFT MT103 corporate credit transfers — preferably with SWIFT GPI/UETR — from the Bermuda USD operational account to supplier accounts in China, the UK, and the EU. For bulk component orders exceeding USD 100,000, a Commercial Letter of Credit issued by Butterfield/HSBC Bermuda and advised via HSBC UK or a Chinese confirming bank provides added counterparty protection; usance LC terms of 30–60 days are standard. Stripe and credit-card settlements arrive in GBP at the UK EMI or Butterfield GBP account and are mechanically batched-swept to the Bermuda treasury for conversion into USD on a predetermined weekly cycle, ensuring trade-payable liquidity is synchronised with receivables. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium feasibility subject to legal constraints. Bermuda does not maintain a dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing category; payment-service providers must hold either a restricted banking licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, a money-service-business licence under the Moneylenders Act, or a relevant digital-asset business authorisation. Offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex, Currencycloud, and Wise Business may be onboarded as supplemental multi-currency rails for collecting UK/EU e-commerce payouts from Stripe and card acquirers, but they cannot lawfully serve as the primary clearing institution because BMA-regulated banks require a qualifying Bermuda banking relationship for substance, FATCA, and regulatory transparency. The recommended operating model is a hybrid: a Bermuda tier-1 bank as the treasury anchor holding the capital escrow and settlement accounts, with a UK-authorised EMI (FCA-regulated) as a short-cycle collection conduit that batched-sweeps GBP receipts to the Bermuda operational account at least weekly. Structured correctly, this arrangement reduces cross-border clearing fees and speeds reconciliation while remaining squarely within BMA guidance.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable in Bermuda: Bermuda has no transfer-pricing documentation regime, no local-file threshold, no master-file threshold, and no arm's-length adjustment for income tax. If the ELP belongs to a UK multinational group, the UK parent may be required to prepare OECD-compliant Master File and Local File under UK CTPA where UK-group turnover exceeds £200 million and related-party cross-border transactions exceed stated thresholds. Bermuda's country-by-country reporting notification applies only to ultimate parents of MNE groups with consolidated revenue above USD 850 million; the ELP should maintain contemporaneous evidence of the commercial rationale and arm's-length nature of any intercompany pricing.

Economic substance scope: The International Tax (Economic Substance) (Bermuda) Act 2018 and the Economic Substance Regulations apply. Manufacturing, assembly, retail, and online sale of bionic companion robotic animals with speech synthesizers are not inherently a listed relevant activity unless structured as a distribution and service centre, headquarters, financing, or intellectual-property holding activity. A pure passive holding ELP must still file the ESR, maintain a registered office, file annual returns, and keep proper books. If a relevant activity is present, the ELP must be directed and managed in Bermuda, have adequate employees and premises in Bermuda, incur material local expenditure, and perform its core income-generating activities in Bermuda proportionate to the income derived.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misdeclaration (Bermuda-UK cross-border)

Statutory sanction: Bermuda: false ESR statements punishable by fines up to BD$100,000, strike-off, and criminal prosecution. UK: cheating the public revenue, tax evasion under TMA 1970 and the Fraud Act 2006, penalties up to 100% of the tax, interest, and criminal fines.

Director personal liability: The general partner and its directors may be subject to personal civil restitution for false filings and regulatory fines. Criminal convictions can result in imprisonment, confiscation orders, disqualification as directors, and court-ordered travel bans while HMRC or criminal asset-recovery proceedings are pending.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Act penalties of BD$10,000 to BD$100,000 for non-compliance and continued default; the Registrar may strike off the ELP, vest its assets in the Crown, and transmit adverse information to the UK tax authority.

Director personal liability: In an ELP, the general partner has unlimited liability. Where the general partner is a corporate entity, its directors may be liable for wrongful or fraudulent trading, failure to maintain substance, and the partnership's regulatory penalties. Courts may pierce the corporate veil and impose travel restrictions where individuals are linked to a sham offshore structure.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Monetary Authority and the Financial Sanctions regime impose substantial civil monetary penalties, regulatory fines, and criminal prosecution for money laundering. Beneficial ownership failures incur separate statutory penalties and potential custodial sentences under Bermuda's criminal code.

Director personal liability: General partners, money-laundering reporting officers, and directors who consent or connive in AML breaches are personally indictable. They may be disqualified, subjected to confiscation orders, restricted from international travel by court order, and imprisoned in serious cases.
Cross-Border Profit Repatriation / CFC Abuse

Statutory sanction: The UK CFC charge applies to the attributable profits of a UK shareholder at the UK corporate tax rate, and HMRC may apply Diverted Profits Tax at 25%. Penalties for failure to notify and inaccurate returns are imposed in addition to tax and interest. Bermuda may also impose ESR penalties for inconsistent substance filings.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who design or approve the abusive structure face personal liability for penalties, tax-geared fines, and disgorgement of fees. HMRC can obtain freezing orders over personal assets, and courts may issue travel bans and custodial sentences where the conduct is found to be fraudulent evasion.
