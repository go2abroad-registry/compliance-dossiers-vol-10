Governing statute

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; the capital contribution is fixed by the Limited Partnership Agreement and no escrow deposit is legally required for a SaaS fleet-dispatch entity. As a bankability condition, an initial capital receipt of at least USD 1 should be evidenced by the general partner's capital receipt certificate prior to formation.

Category: Not required - the Certificate of Registration is the sole operating instrument; no sectoral permit applies to pure B2B fleet-optimisation telematics SaaS, and the ELP benefits from the Business Licences Act 1976 exemption for partnerships not carrying on business in Bermuda.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner / Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Agency, Ministry of Finance (CIT electronic filing portal); Bermuda Customs Department for customs duty; Registrar of Companies, Registry General for exempted limited partnership registration.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a VAT/GST TIN. For a non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnership, the statutory identifier is the partnership registration number issued by the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992. A tax ID is not routinely procured unless the partnership becomes a constituent entity of a multinational group within the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; such an entity must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner within 30 days of entering scope and will be assigned a Bermuda Tax Identification Number. No other general business tax registration is imposed on offshore SaaS/telematics revenue.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda does not operate a check-the-box or fiscal-transparency election regime. The ELP is transparent by default under Bermudian law; classification follows the governing partnership statute and the residence/character of each partner. For foreign partners, underlying US/UK classifications may be elected at the partner level (e.g., US Form 8832 check-the-box for a corporate partner), but no Bermuda domestic entity-election exists. The Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 provides limited administrative elections (fiscal year alignment and group relief), not hybrid-mismatch elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Bermuda has no VAT, GST, or general sales/use tax; indirect levies are limited to customs duties, payroll tax, land tax and stamp duties.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no broad-based consumption tax is imposed. Bermuda Customs importer registration is required only for persons importing physical goods; SaaS and fleet-dispatch services are outside any indirect tax registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Bermuda does not impose VAT, so there is no export zero-rating or export refund claim regime. SaaS exports and telematics-platform services supplied to non-residents are outside Bermuda's indirect tax net. Customs duty is an import-side levy; duty drawback may theoretically apply to imported goods later re-exported under the Customs Act 1964, but such relief is not a VAT-style zero-rating mechanism.

  • Not applicable — no statutory customs documentation bundle is prescribed for VAT zero-rating or refund in Bermuda because no VAT exists.

None — Bermuda does not require electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or an API-based VAT clearance mechanism. Import/export declarations are transmitted electronically via the Bermuda Customs Automated System (BCAS). In-scope CIT filers must file through the Bermuda CIT electronic portal when corporate income tax registration applies.

No VAT export clawback exists in Bermuda. However, false or fraudulent declarations made to Bermuda Customs (including drawback claims) are subject to penalties, forfeiture and prosecution under the Customs Act 1964; in the CIT context, underreporting, late registration or inaccurate filings may attract statutory late-filing penalties, administrative penalties and interest under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and its regulations. Any retrospective assessment follows the normal Bermudian tax-return amendment rules.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is statutorily pegged at par to the US dollar, and both currencies circulate interchangeably in the local economy. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted for non-resident exempted limited partnerships funding operational software costs, licensing fees, and trade-related services. Capital-account transactions, including partner capital injections, LP contributions, and profit distributions, are freely convertible for non-resident investors without prior BMA exchange-control approval, provided settlement occurs through a licensed Bermudian bank and satisfies standard AML/reference checks. Profit remittance is subject only to governance formalities: an authorised general-partner resolution, a solvency or capital-account statement, and beneficial-ownership verification; no Bermuda withholding tax or remittance tax applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to channel foreign currency receipts through a centralised official market. Receipts for logistics-software subscriptions or fleet-telematics services may be retained in USD or any freely usable currency in a licensed Bermudian bank account; conversion into BMD, if needed, is executed at the fixed official parity of 1 BMD = 1 USD.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, is the central supervisory authority for banking, currency issuance, exchange-control administration, AML/ATF compliance, and the licensing of deposit-taking institutions in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium, with the EMI functioning only as an auxiliary collection or disbursement rail, never as the primary regulated treasury bank. A UK FCA-authorised electronic money institution (EMI) such as Wise Business, ClearBank, or Transpay can receive Stripe Billing payouts and originate GBP/USD transfers for small operational floats. However, under the Bermuda Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, the taking of deposits from the Bermudian ELP remains a licensable banking activity; an EMI cannot lawfully hold the capital escrow balance or act as the main depositary. The legal arrangement must therefore be a pre-funded EMI operating account whose balance is swept daily to a licensed Bermudian bank, and all investor subscriptions must first be received at the licensed bank before any onward EMI funding.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Provide BMA-compliant beneficial-ownership schedule showing each natural-person owner above each corporate layer; certified copy of the certificate of limited partnership; executed limited partnership agreement; current register of limited partners; full organisational chart from the ELP to all individual UBOs; passport and proof of address of each UBO; and a legal opinion from a Bermuda-qualified counsel confirming the exemption from public BO filing, if applicable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All investor capital must be wired from a properly KYC'd UK or international bank directly to the Bermudian licensed account using SWIFT MT103. The remittance instruction must name the beneficiary exactly as the Bermuda exempted limited partnership, quote the Bermuda ELP registration number, and include a reference such as 'INV/2025/001; CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION; REGISTERED PARTNERSHIP NO. [ELP-NO]'. For UK GBP outbound wires, the remitting bank should be instructed to complete the purpose code field as 'Direct investment — equity capital' consistent with IMF BPM6 category DI.2; never use a generic 'general goods' code. The receiving Bermudian bank will perform sanctions screening, source-of-funds verification, and a UBO match before crediting the account; no separate BMA foreign-exchange approval is required, but the bank may request a capital-account instruction from the general partner if the ELP has made an election to maintain capital in BMD.Operating disbursements for logistics-fleet-optimisation software and contractor invoices should be executed by SWIFT MT103 for USD/GBP payments. If the Bermudian account is a BMD account, conversion at 1:1 into USD occurs and the USD payment is cleared through the US correspondent banking system, either Fedwire or CHIPS, with an MT202 cover payment where required. For large hardware procurement, fleets, or telematics equipment purchases, the trade disbursement rail is a commercial irrevocable letter of credit issued via SWIFT MT700, available by negotiation against compliant documents (commercial invoice, transport documents, insurance certificate, and packing list). Any London or EU disbursements in GBP may be routed through the UK clearing system via CHAPS or Faster Payments once the Bermudian bank engages a UK clearing correspondent; the bank must ensure the purpose code states 'payment for information technology services' and includes the underlying invoice number. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general rate for Bermuda-incorporated ELPs outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024; 15% for in-scope multinational enterprise groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0.0% — Bermuda does not levy withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or partnership profit shares. An ELP may distribute cash or in-kind proceeds to partners without any Bermuda source deduction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 10,000,000 (Bermudian dollars, pegged 1:1 with USD) aggregate annual value of controlled transactions triggers the mandatory Local File and Master File requirement for in-scope constituent entities subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024; all in-scope MNE groups must further maintain TP documentation on an OECD-aligned basis regardless of amount, and the UK parent must consider UK CFC attribution rules.

Economic substance scope: The International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act 2018 applies to every registered ELP. A logistics-tech SaaS business performing fleet route optimisation and telematics is treated as a 'Relevant Activity' if it carries on a service business or headquarters activity. The ELP must demonstrate that its core income-generating activities (strategic decision-making, employee management, and risk assumption) are performed in Bermuda, with adequately qualified staff, physical premises, and a genuine Bermuda bank account. If economic substance is absent, the penalty regime and automatic information exchange with HMRC under the UK–Bermuda TIEA apply.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax evasion / false CIT-1 declaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged; criminal liability founded on the False Statements provisions: a fine not exceeding BMD 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment not exceeding 7 years; in-scope liability also carries daily surcharges accruing until the return is corrected; HMRC can use information obtained under the Bermuda TIEA to bring parallel UK proceedings.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any UK-resident director of a corporate general partner are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution, costs, and penalties where they knowingly authorised the filing; a serious fraud conviction allows the Bermuda court to impose a travel ban (restriction on exiting Bermuda); disqualification of the partner from acting in Bermuda and potential extradition or mutual legal assistance in UK criminal tax investigations.
Shell entity / Economic Substance non-compliance (ESR)

Statutory sanction: Under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act 2018 s.32, fines of up to BMD 250,000 and a further BMD 10,000 for each day of continuing failure; the Registrar may cancel the exemption certificate, strike the ELP off the register, and pass the full economic substance assessment and taxpayer details to HMRC under the UK–Bermuda automatic exchange; the ELP is disqualified from claiming the 0% regime for the relevant accounting period.

Director personal liability: General partners are personally liable for the ESR penalties on a strict-liability basis; directors who knowingly caused the deficiency face civil asset-restraint orders, travel restrictions, and imprisonment of up to 5 years in persistent cases; a UK court may treat the ELP as a 'relevant transferee' and invalidate pre-insolvency distributions.
AML / Beneficial Ownership disclosure failure (Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2008)

Statutory sanction: On conviction on information or indictment: fines up to BMD 1,000,000; custodial sentence up to 10 years; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may impose administrative fines and revoke the ELP's banking facilities; the defaulting ELP is exposed to civil forfeiture of assets connected to money laundering and is referred to HMRC for offences under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (UK).

Director personal liability: AML obligations attach to the general partner as a 'relevant person'; the general partner faces personal prosecution and imprisonment; any director abetting the offence commits a secondary liability offence; non-resident directors may be extradited to Bermuda; a UK court may issue a proprietary freezing order against the individual's UK assets.
UK CFC (Controlled Foreign Company) exposure and UK Residence risk

Statutory sanction: UK CFC charge assesses the apportioned profits of the ELP at the UK corporate tax rate (25% with small profits rate of 19% and marginal relief for FY2024); HMRC adds late-payment interest and penalties of up to 100% of the CFC tax under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 for deliberate errors; if residence shifts to the UK, the full worldwide income of the ELP is chargeable to corporation tax at up to 25%; additionally, the UK corporate partner is liable to the 'offshore receipts in respect of intangible property' regime if the structure exploits UK-linked IP.

Director personal liability: Directors of the UK parent exposed personally to penalties for non-payment where the company becomes insolvent under CDDA 1986; travel bans are applied by UK courts against directors who leave jurisdiction to evade CFC payments; there is also joint liability with the ELP for the diverted profits tax (DPT) if HMRC determines a UK-PE avoidance scheme.
