Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; electronic portal: GovConnect Bermuda (www.gov.bm) and Bermuda Customs Department Single Window. Bermuda is a distinct tax jurisdiction from the United Kingdom; UK taxes do not apply in Bermuda, but HMRC (Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs) becomes the competent authority for any UK VAT registration arising from distance sales into the United Kingdom.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Engage a Bermuda resident corporate service provider and register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2009 with the Registrar of Companies, filing the Certificate of Limited Partnership and the partnership agreement. Step 2: Obtain the Ministerial Certificate of Exemption confirming the ELP is exempt from Bermuda income, profits, capital-gains and withholding taxation; Bermuda issues no income-tax TIN because no income tax is imposed. Step 3: Register with the OTC via GovConnect for a Payroll Tax Account Number and Social Insurance number only if the ELP employs Bermuda-resident individuals. Step 4: Register as an importer with Bermuda Customs for an importer/customs account where goods are physically imported into Bermuda. Step 5: Where the ELP sells pet supplies to UK consumers, apply for compulsory UK VAT registration with HMRC (non-established taxable person rules; no turnover threshold for imported goods in consignments not exceeding GBP 135) and obtain an HMRC VAT number plus EORI-GB number for any UK customs declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Bermuda ELP is fiscally transparent and outside any Bermuda income tax; liability is limited under the partnership agreement; no Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax or VAT applies.

Available election forms

Bermuda domestic law imposes no income tax, so no domestic fiscal election exists; the partnership agreement governs transparent allocation of profits to non-resident partners with zero Bermuda tax. Foreign elections available: (i) US check-the-box — the ELP defaults to partnership (disregarded/transparent) classification under Treasury Regs, with Form 8832/8858 available; (ii) UK hybrid mismatch rules (CTA 2010 Part 6A) require analysis where the ELP is treated as opaque in another jurisdiction; (iii) Bermuda Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2018 — e-commerce retail of pet supplies is not a relevant activity, so no economic substance filing is required; (iv) the Ministerial Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2009 statutorily confirms no Bermuda tax liability.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No domestic VAT, GST or sales tax in Bermuda; Bermuda levies customs import duty only upon physical importation. Extraterritorial UK VAT at the standard rate applies to online B2C distance sales of pet supplies into the United Kingdom.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda (BMD, pegged 1:1 to USD): no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no such tax is legislated; any importer of goods into Bermuda requires a Bermuda Customs registration, irrespective of turnover. UK nexus: non-UK-established online sellers supplying goods in consignments valued at or below GBP 135 directly to UK consumers must register for UK VAT from the first sale (no de minimis threshold); the general UK GBP 90,000 VAT registration threshold applies only to UK-established businesses and is not available to this Bermuda ELP. Where consignments exceed GBP 135, UK VAT is collected at importation by the carrier, not by the seller.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: not applicable — no VAT is levied on Bermuda exports, and services/goods exported from Bermuda are outside the domestic tax net; imported goods subsequently re-exported may qualify for customs duty drawback or temporary admission relief under the Customs Tariff Act. UK VAT (where the ELP is registered): export zero-rating at 0% is available for goods physically exported from the UK to a non-UK customer where title transfers to the overseas buyer, the goods leave the UK within three months of supply, and the ELP retains prescribed export evidence; supplies of digital/e-commerce services not performed in the UK are outside the scope of UK VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK CDS C21 entry or Bermuda re-export/transshipment entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (or logistics carrier's proof of export)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate and payment gateway settlement statements from Stripe and PayPal evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list identifying ISIC 4791 pet supplies and devices
  • Delivery confirmation or export certificate from the freight forwarder or postal carrier
  • EORI-GB number and customs agent authority where UK customs clearance is used

Bermuda has no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, digital stamping or continuous transaction control mandate; customs declarations are submitted electronically through Bermuda Customs' single-window portal. For UK VAT purposes, HMRC mandates Making Tax Digital (MTD) compliant software with quarterly digital VAT returns, but no real-time e-invoicing clearance applies; UK customs declarations are processed through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) using an EORI-GB number.

Bermuda: false re-export declarations or duty-drawback claims attract forfeiture of goods, fines of up to three times the duty evaded, and imprisonment of up to five years under the Customs Management Act 1955. UK: invalid export zero-rating is clawed back in full with statutory interest; HMRC may assess penalties up to 100% of the VAT for deliberate inaccuracies (Finance Act 2007, Schedule 24) and civil evasion penalties (VATA 1994, s. 60), and fraudulent export claims expose the ELP's general partners to criminal prosecution of up to seven years' imprisonment under CEMA 1979. Evidence of export must be obtained and retained within the three-month export period and for the standard six-year HMRC record-keeping period.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. The BMD is pegged to the US Dollar at 1:1 under a currency-board-style arrangement, and USD banknotes circulate freely as legal tender in Bermuda. An Exempted Limited Partnership designated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes is exempt from the Exchange Control Act 1972 approvals: partners may inject capital, the ELP may remit profit shares, repay partner loans, and distribute liquidation proceeds in any currency without BMA consent, Bermuda withholding tax, or capital gains tax (Bermuda levies none of these). The only practical frictions are the FATCA/CRS reporting duties of the custodial bank and the AML/ATF record-keeping obligations under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None exist. Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender, conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline on export proceeds. An ELP receiving e-commerce settlement from Stripe and PayPal may retain 100% of export receipts in any currency (typically USD) for an indefinite period, may hold multi-currency balances at a BMA-licensed bank, and is subject to no central-bank reporting on the amount or timing of such receipts beyond the ordinary suspicious-transaction reporting threshold of USD 10,000 under the BMA AML/ATF Code of Conduct.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, acting as central monetary authority, currency board, issuer of the Bermudian Dollar (BMD), and integrated prudential and conduct regulator for banks, trust companies, investment businesses, and digital asset businesses in Bermuda. The Bank of England and the UK Prudential Regulation Authority have no direct supervisory jurisdiction over the Bermudian ELP; their remit is limited to the UK-resident corporate General Partner and any UK-authorised EMI concentration accounts used by the group.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

MEDIUM — viable as a receivables concentration and disbursement layer, but not as a substitute for a BMA-licensed settlement account. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud) operate under UK/EEA electronic-money licences (FCA under PSD2; Luxembourg CSSF for PayPal) and can issue GBP/EUR virtual IBANs to aggregate Stripe/PayPal settlements and pay EU suppliers more cheaply. Legal constraints: (i) the ELP's statutory capital, profit distributions, and economic-substance file must reference a BMA-licensed bank, so a Bermuda bank account at Butterfield or HSBC is mandatory; (ii) the BMA AML/ATF Code of Conduct requires Bermuda banks to perform enhanced due diligence on any non-BMA EMI used by a client, so the ELP must disclose the EMI structure, provide the EMI's licence evidence, and explain each sweep; (iii) funds held at an unlicensed EMI are not covered by the Bermuda Deposit Insurance framework and are exposed to provider solvency risk; (iv) Stripe and PayPal act as acquiring payment service providers under their own licences and should not be treated as banks of record. Recommended structure: UK FCA-authorised EMI concentration account (Wise or Revolut) for low-value payouts, with weekly SWIFT MT103 sweeps to the Bermuda Settlement Account; all partner capital and all distributions to remain only on BMA-licensed books.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All capital contributions must be routed by tested SWIFT MT103 (serial) or MT202 COVER from the contributing limited partner's bank directly into the ELP's USD capital account at Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda. Bermuda grants non-resident ELPs automatic exchange-control approval, so no BMA remittance permit is needed; however, the receiving bank must hold a pre-funding compliance file comprising the capital call notice issued by the UK General Partner, the executed subscription agreement, the signed partnership agreement, the BOSS UBO register extract, and the contributor's CDD pack. Field 70 remittance must use the syntax 'KY_ELP CAP CONTRIBUTION / CC-2024-001 / NON-RESIDENT PARTNERSHIP'. For UK partners remitting in GBP, the contributor converts to USD at its UK bank and transmits via CHAPS to the bank's London correspondent, then through Fedwire or CHIPS to the Bermuda USD account, at which point the credit is booked at 1:1 BMD. Intrabank transfers clear T+0; correspondent-routed wires settle T+1. All wires of USD 10,000 or more trigger automated AML screening and, where warranted, a BMA suspicious-transaction report.The ELP will use four clearing rails. (1) SWIFT MT103 supplier wires: direct USD payments to overseas manufacturers from the Bermuda operational account, cleared via the bank's New York correspondent (JPMorgan or HSBC) for USD, London correspondent for GBP, and Paris correspondent for EUR; each payment references the supplier purchase-order number to prevent compliance holds. (2) Commercial Letters of Credit: for bulk inventory purchases above USD 100,000 per shipment, the Butterfield or HSBC trade desk issues irrevocable documentary credits under UCP 600 (SWIFT MT700 series), with the supplier negotiating against bills of lading, commercial invoices, and packing lists. (3) Stripe and PayPal payout rebalancing: settlement funds are received T+2 into the Bermuda USD account, and the treasury function uses same-day sweeps to move excess liquidity into a money-market or fixed-deposit sweep managed by the bank. (4) UK EMI concentration: for small recurring GBP supplier payments and refunds, the UK FCA-authorised EMI account uses Faster Payments (FPS) and SEPA Instant rails, with the Bermuda account replenishing the EMI via a weekly MT103 for the net aggregate. No BMD conversion cost applies to USD flows because of the 1:1 peg; GBP disbursements are dealt through the corporate dealing desk at mid-market plus a maximum 0.2% spread.Deliverable outright USD/GBP FX forward contracts (1-month to 12-month tenors) executed under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or a bilateral Master FX Agreement with Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda; available for hedging the ELP's GBP operating disbursements and the UK affiliate's management fees against USD e-commerce receipts.FX swaps (spot sale and forward repurchase of USD against GBP) used to rebalance short-term liquidity between the USD settlement account and the GBP EMI concentration account.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for restricted supplier currencies such as CNY where onshore deliverability is unavailable; permitted strictly as transaction hedges against firm supplier purchase orders and documented under a master NDF confirmation with two-way margining.Vanilla currency options (USD call / GBP put and USD put / GBP call) to hedge contingent cash flows of the UK General Partner's distributable profit share, subject to the bank's credit approval and option-valuation collateral.Cross-currency swaps (USD floating versus GBP fixed) permissible where the ELP is later converted into a group financing vehicle, but not recommended for the current pure e-commerce pass-through structure.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: MEDIUM — viable as a receivables concentration and disbursement layer, but not as a substitute for a BMA-licensed settlement account. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud) operate under UK/EEA electronic-money licences (FCA under PSD2; Luxembourg CSSF for PayPal) and can issue GBP/EUR virtual IBANs to aggregate Stripe/PayPal settlements and pay EU suppliers more cheaply. Legal constraints: (i) the ELP's statutory capital, profit distributions, and economic-substance file must reference a BMA-licensed bank, so a Bermuda bank account at Butterfield or HSBC is mandatory; (ii) the BMA AML/ATF Code of Conduct requires Bermuda banks to perform enhanced due diligence on any non-BMA EMI used by a client, so the ELP must disclose the EMI structure, provide the EMI's licence evidence, and explain each sweep; (iii) funds held at an unlicensed EMI are not covered by the Bermuda Deposit Insurance framework and are exposed to provider solvency risk; (iv) Stripe and PayPal act as acquiring payment service providers under their own licences and should not be treated as banks of record. Recommended structure: UK FCA-authorised EMI concentration account (Wise or Revolut) for low-value payouts, with weekly SWIFT MT103 sweeps to the Bermuda Settlement Account; all partner capital and all distributions to remain only on BMA-licensed books.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to a standard Bermuda exempted partnership outside the CIT net. For an in-scope CIT group with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million, a Local File and Master File are required when the aggregate value of Bermuda constituent entity controlled transactions exceeds BMD 1,000,000 per fiscal year or BMD 250,000 for any single related-party transaction.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations apply to 'relevant activities' like banking, insurance, shipping, IP holding, financing/leasing, distribution, and service centre operations. Pure retail e-commerce of pet supplies is not a relevant activity; however, if the ELP undertakes substantial procurement and distribution functions from Bermuda, it may fall within the 'distribution and service centre' category and trigger an ESR-2 return. The partnership must always maintain a registered office and a general partner or agent with a local physical presence. UK law will also apply the permanent establishment and economic employer concepts to prevent contrived offshore structures.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK Tax Evasion via Concealed Permanent Establishment

Statutory sanction: HMRC will treat the partnership as carrying on a trade in the UK through a permanent establishment and assess income tax or corporation tax on the UK partner(s). Penalties under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 can reach 100% of the tax if the behaviour is deliberate and concealed, plus interest; criminal prosecution can be brought under Finance Act 2000 s.144 and the Criminal Finances Act 2017 for tax evasion, with imprisonment of up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The UK general partner is jointly and severally liable for all tax assessed; HMRC may seek freezing orders over personal bank accounts, impose travel bans, obtain director disqualification orders, and instigate bankruptcy proceedings against the individual partner.
Shell Company / Abusive Offshore Structure

Statutory sanction: HMRC can apply the Diverted Profits Tax at 25% on profits routed through the offshore entity, recharacterise the invoices under the UK GAAR, and impose civil penalties of 60% of the diverted tax. Bermuda Registrar may strike the partnership off for having no real activity and for filing a false annual return; the false declaration offence carries a fine of BMD 10,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years.

Director personal liability: UK courts may pierce the veil because the partnership is a mere facade; the general partner will be personally liable for the entire trading income, and the UK owner may be deemed a shadow director under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, resulting in disqualification, confiscation orders, and potential extradition proceedings.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Shifting with Related Suppliers

Statutory sanction: HMRC may make transfer pricing adjustments under CTA 2010 Part 4, increasing UK taxable profits, adding interest, and imposing penalties up to 100% of the avoided tax; the Diverted Profits Tax may also apply to the UK service affiliate. If Bermuda CIT applies, the BTA will require TP documentation and may impose a local top-up tax adjustment.

Director personal liability: The general partner remains personally liable for the partnership's tax liabilities through the transparent pass-through; an individual or corporate partner that controls pricing may be made liable for tax-geared penalties; a UK shadow director can be disqualified for up to 15 years and be ordered to contribute to assets for wrongful trading.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda's Beneficial Ownership Amendment Act and General Partnership Act require beneficial ownership records; failure to comply with a Registrar notice is indictable, with penalties up to BMD 75,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years. If funds represent UK evaded tax, money laundering offences under UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 may be prosecuted, leading to confiscation of assets and imprisonment for up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any compliance officer are personally liable; UK courts can issue restraint orders and travel bans; the individual will be disqualified as a director if convicted of an offence connected to the management of the entity, and will be required to repay the confiscated amount.
