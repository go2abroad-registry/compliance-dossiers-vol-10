Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

United Kingdom: Her Majesty's Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — GOV.UK VAT registration portal and the Customs Declaration Service (CDS). Bermuda: the Office of the Tax Commissioner (Ministry of Finance, gov.bm) and the Registrar of Companies administer Bermuda statutory filings; Bermuda levies no VAT/GST and issues no VAT TIN.

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership files its Declaration of Status, obtains the partnership registration certificate, and registers with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and social security. For UK VAT, the non-established entity applies via HMRC's online 'Register for VAT' service (forms VAT1/VAT1C) before making the first taxable supply in the UK; no turnover or distance-selling threshold applies to non-UK sellers of goods as of 1 July 2021. HMRC issues the 9-digit GB VAT registration number within 30 working days; the ELP must also register with CDS as an exporter and appoint a UK fiscal/customs agent if it has no UK establishment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda ELP is fiscally transparent and, as a non-resident partnership with no Bermuda income/capital gains tax and no UK central management and control or permanent establishment, has no entity-level corporation tax exposure; for UK indirect-tax purposes it is nevertheless a separate 'taxable person' and must charge 20% UK VAT on supplies of goods located in the UK at the point of sale.

Available election forms

Bermuda transparency is automatic under the Exempted Limited Partnership Act 2015; no 'check-the-box' election exists in Bermuda or the UK. Available elections: (i) UK VAT group registration under VATA 1994 s.43A once a UK branch/PE exists; (ii) Bermuda Economic Substance (Exempted Partnership) status declarations; (iii) IRS Form 8832 'check-the-box' for any U.S. federal classification of the ELP; and (iv) UK anti-hybrid mismatch rules (CTA 2010 Pt 6A) apply compulsorily and cannot be waived by election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK: no threshold for non-established sellers — registration is mandatory from the first GBP 1 of taxable supplies where goods are located in the UK at sale; for goods dispatched from abroad to UK consumers in consignments up to GBP 135 the seller/online marketplace must also register and charge VAT; consignments above GBP 135 attract import VAT at the border. Domestic UK-established sellers use the GBP 90,000 annual threshold. Bermuda: no VAT registration threshold exists; customs duty registration applies on importation from the first shipment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies where goods are exported from Great Britain to a destination outside the UK, provided the ELP removes or ships them within 3 months of supply (6 months for goods exported from a warehouse under the 'special removal' rule) and holds comprehensive evidence of export: exporter identity, consignee identity, overseas destination, carrier route, goods description and commercial terms. Substantiation must be 'reliable, coherent and verifiable'. Offshore e-services (e.g., digital subscriptions) supplied to overseas business recipients fall outside the UK place-of-supply rules and are outside/zero-rated. Bermuda has no VAT, so no separate zero-rating application is possible locally.

  • Customs Export Declaration (C88/SAD dataset lodged on the UK Customs Declaration Service — CDS) naming the ELP as exporter and the foreign destination
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, CMR consignment note, or Post Office Certificate of Posting evidencing physical removal from the UK
  • Commercial invoice and Shopify order-level file (HS codes, unit values, consignee) cross-referenced to the export declaration
  • Inward remittance certificate / foreign payment instruction from the settlement bank showing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency or GBP
  • Shipment tracking events and signed delivery confirmation from the 3PL/courier (Shopify fulfilment) demonstrating arrival in the destination country

UK: no real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or mandatory B2B e-invoicing is in force at the 2025 stage; HMRC is consulting on an e-invoicing framework. Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires VAT-registered businesses to keep digital records and submit VAT Returns via HMRC-recognised API-compatible software; the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) requires all export entries to be filed electronically. Bermuda: no e-invoicing mandate; customs declarations are lodged electronically on ASYCUDA World.

HMRC may audit export evidence under CEMA 1979 s.161A and post-clearance review; missing, late or false zero-rating proof triggers retrospective clawback of output VAT at 20%, denial of input VAT, a civil penalty of up to 100% of the VAT evaded (VATA 1994 s.60), default surcharge up to 15%, late-payment interest (Bank of England base rate + 2.5%), and criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72 (unlimited fine and up to 7 years' imprisonment) for fraudulent evasion. Bermuda Customs may separately impose penalties of up to 200% of evaded duty and forfeiture of goods for customs fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US Dollar (USD) at a fixed 1:1 parity under a currency board-style arrangement with the BMA. For a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972, both current account transactions (trade in goods, services, royalties, commissions) and capital account transactions (equity contributions, loan injections, profit distributions, redemption of partnership interests) are freely convertible without the need for individual exchange control approvals. Profit remittance to international partners is unrestricted and is subject to zero Bermuda withholding tax. However, all cross-border flows must be routed through a BMA-licensed authorised bank, supported by proper purpose codes and underlying documentation sufficient to satisfy AML/CTF, FATCA/CRS, and economic substance reporting requirements. No currency restriction or convertibility hurdle exists for the DTC brand payment flows from global processors.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for the surrender of export proceeds applicable to a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership that has been granted non-resident designation under the Exchange Control Act 1972. Export receipts, including Shopify, PayPal, and Stripe settlements, may be retained in the original currency (USD, GBP, EUR) or converted to BMD/USD at the 1:1 peg at the account holder's discretion. For direct resident entities in Bermuda, the historical Exchange Control Regulations had required conversion within 30 days, but this regime is inapplicable to the non-resident ELP structure, which is the operative entity for this DTC brand.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) – Foreign Exchange Control Department (the central bank and financial services regulator for Bermuda). The Bank of England acts only as the sovereign central bank for sterling clearing in the UK market and has no direct supervisory authority over Bermuda resident or designated non-resident entities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Medium feasibility for offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business, Currencycloud) in an ancillary capacity. The Bermuda ELP may open an EMI account in the UK or EEA, but only after the EMI completes enhanced due diligence on the offshore partnership structure. Legal constraints include: (1) EMIs often reject or conditionally accept entities domiciled in non-EEA offshore jurisdictions, requiring additional evidence of substance and the general partner's identity; (2) the EMI cannot hold the statutory capital escrow or substitute for a BMA-licensed bank where creditor protections under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014 are required; (3) PayPal and Shopify Payments may require a primary settlement account at a recognised bank in the country of incorporation, forcing the ELP to maintain its operational account at a Bermudian bank; and (4) the BMA maintains anti-money-laundering expectations that all core treasury transactions be conducted through regulated financial institutions with physical presence or demonstrable correspondent oversight. Therefore, EMIs are viable as a secondary FX and low-value disbursement rail, not as the primary clearing platform.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections should be executed via SWIFT MT103 wires from each limited partner's personal or corporate bank account to the ELP's capital escrow account at a BMA-licensed bank. The SWIFT purpose code for transfers originating from a UK bank is G300 ('Investment in Securities / Assets') or alternatively 'Capital Contribution to Exempted Partnership' when using a Bermudian bank. Supporting documentation must include the executed partnership agreement, a capital call notice signed by the general partner, the escrow instruction letter, board resolutions, and source-of-funds declarations. All wires above USD 100,000 require a prior AML clearance from the receiving bank's compliance department. No BMA exchange control approval is required for a non-resident designated ELP, but the bank must complete its standard counterparty verification before the funds are credited.Outbound trade and operating disbursements (inventory purchase, freight, digital marketing, logistics) are cleared from the operational account using SWIFT MT103 with the relevant purpose code (e.g., 'Payment for Goods' or 'Commercial Services'). For high-value import shipments, a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) may be established through the Bermuda bank's correspondent network, with payment against presentation of shipping documents. Daily treasury sweeps should be implemented to move settlement balances from Shopify Payments, PayPal, and Stripe into the operational account, then onward to suppliers. Multi-currency supplier invoices are settled either by spot conversion at the BMD/USD peg or through dedicated foreign currency clearing accounts maintained in GBP/EUR with the same Bermudian bank.Plain-vanilla FX forwards (up to 12 months) for USD/GBP and USD/EUR inventory and supplier commitmentsNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for emerging market currencies (CNY, INR, BRL) where physical delivery is restricted or unavailableCross-currency swaps (USD/GBP, USD/EUR) for matching medium-term working capital facilities and capital escrow rollovers

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% – Bermuda does not impose corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP). The Minister of Finance may issue a statutory tax exemption certificate under s.10 Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 guaranteeing no income, corporate, or withholding taxes until 31 March 2035. Exception: the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 imposes a 15% CIT on Bermuda constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750m in either of the two preceding fiscal years, effective for fiscal periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025; a typical SME Shopify DTC brand is outside this scope.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Bermuda does not impose any withholding tax on dividends, partnership distributions, or capital returns paid by an ELP to its Cayman Islands partner. The distribution is also exempt from Bermuda stamp duty and exchange control under the 2035 exemption certificate.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda TP legislation under the CIT Act 2023 applies only to Bermuda constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750m (approx. BMD 1.02 billion) in the preceding fiscal year; in-scope entities must prepare OECD-aligned Local File and Master File for all material cross-border controlled transactions, regardless of transaction size in Bermuda. For sub-threshold ELPs there is no Bermuda TP documentation requirement, but the UK CFC and UK Diverted Profits Tax regimes can impose de facto documentation obligations if a UK-resident UBO or UK permanent establishment is alleged; HMRC expects arm's length pricing for e-commerce supply chains and can apply a 25% tax adjustment plus penalties.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to all registered ELPs carrying on a relevant activity: distribution and service centre, intellectual property business, pure equity holding entity, headquarters, insurance, banking, fund management, financing and leasing, and shipping. A Shopify DTC brand ELP typically falls into one of three categories: (i) 'distribution and service centre' if it directs the purchase, storage, transport, and order management of goods from Bermuda; (ii) 'intellectual property business' if it holds brand IP and earns income from exploitation; or (iii) 'pure equity holding entity' if it only holds the shares of operating entities. For category (i) and (ii), the ELP must demonstrate CIGA in Bermuda: an adequate number of full-time employees in Bermuda, adequate physical premises, and adequate operating expenditure proportionate to the business. Category (iii) has reduced substance requirements but still requires statutory compliance. The OECD Pillar Two and UK DPT 'insufficient economic substance' tests will be assessed in parallel where the UK is the market.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK VAT Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil misdeclaration penalty up to 100% of the VAT evaded under VATA 1994 s.60 and FA 2009 Schedule 24; interest on unpaid VAT; criminal prosecution on indictment for dishonest evasion under s.72 VATA 1994 with unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 2 years; confiscation proceedings under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 can recover the entire hidden profit stream.

Director personal liability: The general partner and its directors are jointly and severally liable for the ELP's UK VAT debts; HMRC may issue personal liability notices, freeze bank accounts via Summary Judgment worldwide freezing orders, impose UK travel bans for suspected fraudsters, and request mutual legal assistance from Bermuda under the TIEA to seize GP assets.
UK CFC and Management & Control Risk

Statutory sanction: UK corporation tax at 25% on the ELP's worldwide profits if UK resident (De Beers test); CFC charge under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A applying 25% corporation tax to apportioned offshore profits; assessments may be raised up to 10 years for deliberate non-disclosure; penalties up to 100% of the understated tax for deliberate and concealed arrangements.

Director personal liability: Officers of the general partner knowingly concerned in the evasion are personally liable under s.72 VATA 1994 and analogous corporation tax offences; UK director disqualification orders, personal liability orders against shadow directors, and HMRC 'naming and shaming' of offshore arrangements are available; Bermuda courts will enforce HMRC civil judgments against GP assets.
UK Diverted Profits Tax

Statutory sanction: DPT at 25% of the diverted profits plus interest; DPT is a designated tax under FA 2015 Part 3, with a one-year deadline for HMRC determinations and no appeal only on the merits; penalties for failure to notify DPT of up to 100% of the tax; the UK entity and Bermuda entity are jointly and severally liable for the diverted profit amount.

Director personal liability: DPT itself is entity-level, but general partner directors who sign false DPT returns or participate in the contrived arrangement face additional criminal charges for cheating the public revenue, personal liability notices for the recovered tax, and public exposure in HMRC's DPT determinations announcements.
Bermuda ESR and Shell Company Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations penalties: BD$25,000 for failure to file an ESR Return; BD$100,000 for knowingly filing a false or misleading return; for high-risk IP entities, BD$250,000 fixed penalty plus BD$50,000 per day of continued non-compliance up to 30 days; the Registrar may petition the court for dissolution or striking-off; each registered entity also loses its tax exemption certificate protection.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner and the general partner itself are jointly and severally liable for the statutory penalties; the Registrar can disqualify directors of defaulting entities; a struck-off ELP causes the Cayman partner to inherit the tax liabilities, triggering UK CFC charges and possible personal bankruptcy proceedings for the responsible UK-resident officers.
UK Offshore Receipts in respect of Intangible Property (ORIP)

Statutory sanction: ORIP income tax under ITTOIA 2005 Part 13A at 20% on the net UK-source receipts deemed attributable to the offshore intangibles; HMRC assessment without a cap on the number of years; a 100% penalty for deliberate non-disclosure, plus interest; the charge operates even if the ELP has no UK permanent establishment.

Director personal liability: HMRC can impute knowledge to the general partner's directors; directors who signed inaccurate arrangements are exposed to joint and several personal liability notices, confiscation of proceeds, and disqualification; professional advisers who enabled the avoidance can be sanctioned under the UK Promoters of Tax Avoidance Schemes rules.
AML and Banking Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Merchant account termination and 120-day reserve freezing; under the Bermuda AML Act 2020 and the UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, civil recovery of assets derived from unlawful conduct, penalties up to twice the laundered value, and criminal conviction for money laundering punishable by imprisonment up to 14 years; the acquirers are jointly required to terminate and report suspicious activity to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: The general partner and its directors can be prosecuted in Bermuda and the UK for money laundering offences; UK courts can issue interim receivership orders over the ELP's assets, travel bans, and passport surrender; the Cayman partner's ultimate beneficial owner may face worldwide asset freezing and extradition risk where fraud is shown.
