Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports are not subject to VAT/GST in Bermuda because no such tax exists; therefore no export zero-rating claim may be made. For customs duty purposes, goods leaving Bermuda are not dutiable unless re-entered. Offshore services provided by the ELP to non-Bermuda recipients do not trigger any Bermuda consumption tax.

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital clearance mandate applies to domestic sales. Import declarations are filed through the Bermuda Customs Cargo System (BCS)/EDI channels, but standard commercial invoices, packing lists, and shipping documents are accepted without digital stamping.

No VAT refund or zero-rating clawback exists given the absence of VAT. However, Bermuda Customs may audit export valuations and misdeclarations under the Customs Tariff Act; penalties include forfeiture of goods, fines up to BMD 5,000 per offence, and retrospective collection of duties plus interest where undervaluation or incorrect tariff classification is discovered. Fraudulent documentation may also trigger the Criminal Code offences for forgery and fraudulent trading.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to the US Dollar (USD). Bermuda maintains unrestricted current-account convertibility and, for entities designated as non-resident under the BMA's Exchange Control framework, unrestricted capital-account convertibility. A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) may apply to the BMA for non-resident designation, which permits all banking operations to be conducted in foreign currency (USD, GBP, EUR) and removes any exchange-control approval requirement for capital subscriptions, profit distributions, capital redemptions, or liquidation remittances to UK partners. Profit remittance from the Bermuda ELP is subject to zero Bermuda withholding and zero Bermuda income tax; the only practical hurdle is documentary, namely evidencing the partner distribution resolution, the non-resident designation certificate, and the underlying source of funds for the banking relationship.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. The ELP may retain 100% of IoT hardware export proceeds in USD or any permitted foreign currency without conversion into BMD. The BMA requires only statistical reporting by the licensed bank (quarterly balance-of-payments returns); no exporter declaration is required for non-resident designated accounts. The UK parent likewise faces no surrender obligation, although the Bank of England may collect cross-border flow statistics from UK reporting institutions under its monetary statistics framework.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Financial Institutions Act 2012; the Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) and His Majesty's Treasury retain supervisory and statistical oversight over the UK parent banking corridor.

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM. UK-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Ebury, Currencycloud) may open corporate multi-currency accounts for a Bermuda ELP for GBP, EUR, and USD settlement, but they cannot accept Bermudian Dollar deposits or provide domestic Bermuda clearing because deposit-taking and money services business in Bermuda are reserved to BMA licensees under the Financial Institutions Act 2012. UK EMI authorisation does not passport to Bermuda. The legal constraint is that a non-licensed EMI must not hold funds by way of deposit in Bermuda or market local payment services to Bermuda-resident persons; the ELP should therefore use EMI rails strictly for outbound supplier payouts and marketplace collection disbursement (Stripe, Shopify Payments) while maintaining the primary corporate account and all capital flows with a BMA-licensed bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda has no CIT and therefore no statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold. The operative threshold is the UK TIOPA 2010 Part 4 requirement: a UK-resident partner that is not an SME must prepare contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation where cross-border controlled transactions with the ELP exceed GBP 1,000,000 per counterparty per accounting period; for SMEs, a de minimis of GBP 1,000,000 applies only to transactions that are not 'potentially advantaged'. A UK master file and local file are aligned with OECD standards where UK group turnover exceeds GBP 200 million and cross-border related-party transactions exceed GBP 1 million.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2019, as amended, applies to all Bermuda ELPs. A retailing ELP that buys smart-home IoT hardware from a UK affiliate and sells to independent end customers as principal is normally not engaged in a relevant activity and must declare no relevant activity in the annual ESD. However, if the ELP performs distribution, service, logistics, or procurement functions for a foreign group entity, it must classify as a distribution and service centre and prove that it is managed and directed in Bermuda, maintains adequate full-time employees, premises, and expenditure, and performs core income-generating activities in Bermuda. Failure to satisfy the substance test triggers information notices, financial penalties, and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Breach / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Act: information-gathering notices; default penalty of BMD 10,000 plus daily penalty up to BMD 100,000; criminal fines and imprisonment up to 2 years for false ESD; administrative strike-off and revocation of ELP registration.

Director personal liability: The general partner and its directors are jointly and severally liable for penalties and restitution; UK parent directors face HMRC CFC reassessments, interest, and penalties; travel restrictions may be imposed on the general partner.
Tax Evasion / UK CFC

Statutory sanction: HMRC penalties up to 200% of the tax for deliberate and concealed offshore non-disclosure, daily penalties, accelerated payment notices; criminal prosecution under the UK Fraud Act 2006 and Criminal Finances Act 2017 for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion, with unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Senior accounting officers, directors, and the general partner face personal penalty assessments; director disqualification up to 15 years; travel bans; imprisonment up to 7 years; court-ordered restitution of evaded taxes.
Anti-Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years on conviction; forfeiture and seizure of assets; termination of bank and payment processor relationships; regulatory censure.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partner may be prosecuted personally; substantial civil restitution, confiscation orders, travel bans, and imprisonment in cases of deliberate or reckless conduct.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement / Offshore Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: UK: HMRC adjusts profits under TIOPA 2010; corporation tax, interest, and penalties up to 100% of understated tax; GAAR counteraction with reduction up to 60%; DOTAS disclosure penalties of GBP 600 plus GBP 300 per day.

Director personal liability: Directors and the designated responsible partner may be personally liable for incorrect return penalties; UK director disqualification and financial sanctions apply where the inaccuracy is attributable to the individual.
Statutory Filing Default / Registrar Strike-off

Statutory sanction: Registrar monthly late fees, administrative strike-off, and deregistration; the ELP loses authorization to maintain bank accounts and payment processor accounts; continued trading after strike-off is unlawful; restoration fees apply.

Director personal liability: Upon strike-off, limited partners lose the limitation shield for debts incurred after dissolution; the general partner remains jointly and severally liable for all partnership obligations; UK directors face disqualification proceedings in the UK.
