Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Revenue Authority (BRA) - official portal www.bra.bm with e-services via www.gov.bm; corporate and partnership registry administered by the Registrar of Companies, Registry General, Hamilton, Bermuda. UK HMRC exercises no direct fiscal authority in Bermuda because Bermuda is a self-governing British Overseas Territory; the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT) and Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) filings are submitted through the BRA e-Tax platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Register the Exempted Limited Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 with the Registrar of Companies (filing of partnership statement or deed, declaration of general partner, registered office in Bermuda, resident general partner or manager where applicable, and registration fee). Step 2 - Obtain a Bermuda Tax Account Number from the Bermuda Revenue Authority if the ELP has Bermuda employees or lodges customs declarations; no general-purpose corporate TIN exists because Bermuda historically levies no income tax. Non-resident partners are not required to obtain any Bermuda tax identifier merely by virtue of holding interests in the ELP.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - An Exempted Limited Partnership is statutorily tax-transparent for Bermudian tax purposes: income, gains, deductions, and credits flow through to partners, and no Bermuda entity-level income tax applies to a mere international wholesale distribution operation. Bermuda does not levy corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on the ELP, except the 15% Corporate Income Tax applicable solely to large multinational groups under the CIT Act 2023. A purely offshore distribution business involving acetate eyeglass frames has no Bermuda taxable nexus unless it maintains Bermuda payroll, imports goods into Bermuda customs territory, or carries on local operations.

Available election forms

Bermuda law does not provide a domestic 'check-the-box' election; partnership transparency is mandatory and statutory. Under the CIT Act 2023 and accompanying regulations, qualifying MNE groups may elect: (i) to apply the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax in lieu of the Income Inclusion Rule or Undertaxed Profits Rule in respect of Bermuda constituent entities; (ii) to apply OECD-sanctioned simplified safe harbour calculations for top-up tax purposes; and (iii) to use the group relief, loss consolidation, or proportional consolidation mechanisms available to flow-through entities. A US partner may file an IRS Form 8832 check-the-box election solely to determine US federal classification, with no effect under Bermuda law. Bermuda also adopts OECD hybrid mismatch rules (BEPS Action 2) to neutralise deductions arising from hybrid or reverse-hybrid arrangements involving Bermuda transparent vehicles.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax whatsoever. The only indirect fiscal charges are customs import duties levied under the Customs Act 1948 and the Customs Tariff, plus employer payroll tax on Bermuda-sourced remuneration; exports and offshore wholesale distribution of ophthalmic eyewear frames are therefore subject to a 0% effective indirect tax rate with no VAT registration, collection, or refund machinery.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold can arise in Bermuda because no VAT/GST exists. Customs duty is payable on the CIF value of every commercial import entry (no de minimis turnover threshold for B2B consignments), while payroll tax requires employer registration with the BRA from the first Bermudian employee remuneration payment; all figures are denominated in Bermuda dollars (BMD), which is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in classical VAT terms because Bermuda has no VAT to zero-rate and no export VAT refund scheme. Bermuda does not tax export turnover, offshore services, or non-Bermudian-source income; acetate eyeglass frames distributed internationally from Bermuda, or sold offshore without entering Bermuda customs territory, are entirely outside Bermuda's indirect tax base. Where goods are imported into Bermuda and subsequently re-exported, customs duty relief or draw-back is available under the Customs Tariff on production of proof of export within the prescribed time limits; no VAT refund claim exists because no VAT is ever charged or collected.

Import and export declarations are lodged electronically through the Bermuda Customs Management System, a single-window automated cargo entry platform; underlying commercial invoices and shipping records must be retained for seven years and produced on demand of the Bermuda Revenue Authority or Customs. No third-party fiscal stamp or pre-clearance of invoices is required for commercial transactions.

No VAT export refund claw-back can arise in Bermuda because no VAT refund mechanism exists. Sanctions attach instead to customs and CIT non-compliance: (i) false export declarations, customs duty evasion, and diversion of goods to local consumption attract forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties of up to three times the value involved, and imprisonment of up to five years under the Customs Act 1948; (ii) failure by a large MNE group to file Bermuda CIT/QDMTT returns may trigger fixed monetary penalties in the tens of thousands of Bermuda dollars, daily continuing penalties, interest, and BRA-enforced collection measures under the CIT Act 2023; (iii) material misstatements in BRA e-tax filings expose the ELP and its general partner to reassessment, audit adjustments within the statutory limitation window, and automatic exchange of information with tax authorities of partner jurisdictions under Bermuda's extensive treaty and information-sharing network with the UK, US, and OECD members; (iv) deliberate structuring designed to exploit hybrid or tax-transparent mismatches is challengeable under Bermuda's hybrid mismatch rules and the general anti-avoidance principles in the CIT Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda’s currency, the Bermudian dollar (BMD), is pegged to the United States dollar at a fixed 1:1 parity, and both currencies circulate as legal tender for most wholesale purposes. For a non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership such as KY_ELP, capital and current account convertibility is effectively unrestricted: partner capital injections, supplier payments, export proceeds and profit remittances may be settled in USD/BMD without BMA pre-approval. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal as Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on distributions to non-resident partners; however, the bank will require a valid partner distribution resolution, evidence that the partnership remains solvent, and standard AML source-of-funds documentation. Capital-account transfers require a capital call notice and partner register confirmation; current-account transfers require underlying trade invoices and shipping documents. No material currency restriction applies to an ELP that is non-resident for exchange-control purposes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Bermuda’s Exchange Control Act 1972, resident exporters may be required to offer foreign currency received from exports to an authorised dealer within the administrative window, typically 30 days of receipt, and to convert 100% of the gross proceeds into BMD unless an exemption or retention allowance is granted. A non-resident exempted partnership whose partners are non-resident and whose trade is conducted offshore is not subject to the resident surrender obligation; the ELP may therefore retain export proceeds in USD/BMD without any mandatory conversion ratio. To preserve this position, the partnership must file and maintain its non-resident exchange-control status with the BMA and ensure that no Bermuda-resident person exercises control over the underlying export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the integrated financial services regulator, central bank and exchange control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972, responsible for currency issuance and financial sector supervision in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Low as a sole primary banking relationship and Medium as an auxiliary payout and currency-conversion layer. Bermuda does not maintain an e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime and does not recognise UK or EEA EMI passporting; an offshore digital EMI cannot lawfully hold the ELP’s primary Bermudian deposit or provide domestic account services without a BMA banking licence. However, an FCA-authorised EMI or similar regulated payments institution can be used as a supplementary rail for collecting marketplace receivables, converting small balances and issuing payments, provided the ultimate settlement account is maintained in the ELP’s name at a BMA-licensed bank. The EMI must operate under applicable foreign payments authorisations, and its funds must be routed through a licensed correspondent to avoid unlicensed deposit-taking in Bermuda.

Compliant inbound wires must originate from the contributing partner’s own bank account and flow by SWIFT MT103/MT202 to the ELP’s BMA-licensed bank in Bermuda, using the partner bank’s UETR as the end-to-end reconciliation key. The remittance instruction must include the field 'PARTNERSHIP CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – KY_ELP – NON-RESIDENT EXEMPTED ELP'; Bermuda does not impose a statutory purpose code for non-resident capital injections. The receiving bank will hold funds pending execution of the capital call notice, the source-of-funds statement, the partner register extract and, if the contribution exceeds the bank’s threshold, the contributing partner’s bank reference. Wire routing through third-party or platform-owned accounts is acceptable only where expressly authorised in the partnership agreement and supported by a legal cover note to the bank.Outbound settlements to eyewear-frame manufacturers may be made through (i) SWIFT MT103 USD/BMD credits to supplier accounts, (ii) confirmed irrevocable documentary commercial letters of credit issued by Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda and advised through a confirming bank in the supplier’s jurisdiction, or (iii) platform-payout arrangements routed via local acquirers to the ELP’s operational account. LC disbursements require a clean on-board ocean bill of lading or air waybill, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and third-party inspection certificate, payable at sight in USD or BMD. A dedicated USD/BMD liquidity pool with same-day internal sweeps is recommended to minimise daylight overdraft exposure, and all settlement messages should carry the UETR and ISIC 4646 purchase-order reference for audit trail consistency.Outright forward FX contracts in USD/BMD, USD/GBP, USD/EUR and USD/CNH with maturities up to 12 months, executed under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a BMA-licensed authorised dealer.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for Asian currencies with deliverability restrictions, such as INR, IDR and VND, used solely to hedge committed trade payables to component suppliers.Cross-currency basis swaps and rolling FX swaps to manage the currency mismatch between platform-payout receipts in GBP or EUR and supplier settlements in USD.Zero-cost collars and case-specific FX options, permissible where the authorised dealer confirms an underlying commercial exposure and the hedge is recorded in the ELP’s treasury policy and partner-approved risk memorandum.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility is Low as a sole primary banking relationship and Medium as an auxiliary payout and currency-conversion layer. Bermuda does not maintain an e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime and does not recognise UK or EEA EMI passporting; an offshore digital EMI cannot lawfully hold the ELP’s primary Bermudian deposit or provide domestic account services without a BMA banking licence. However, an FCA-authorised EMI or similar regulated payments institution can be used as a supplementary rail for collecting marketplace receivables, converting small balances and issuing payments, provided the ultimate settlement account is maintained in the ELP’s name at a BMA-licensed bank. The EMI must operate under applicable foreign payments authorisations, and its funds must be routed through a licensed correspondent to avoid unlicensed deposit-taking in Bermuda.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% Bermuda income tax for the Exempted Limited Partnership in ordinary course; 15% under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 only where the ELP is a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group with consolidated global revenue of at least EUR 750 million in two of the four preceding accounting periods.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on distributions, whether characterised as dividend, profit share, partnership distribution, or return of capital. The ELP may repatriate profits to UK or non-resident partners without Bermuda source taxation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda-specific Local File or Master File monetary threshold has been separately codified; mandatory Country-by-Country Report filing is triggered where the group's consolidated revenue reaches at least BMD 750,000,000 or equivalent (EUR 750,000,000). For all related-party transactions, including wholesale distribution of ophthalmic frames, contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation should be maintained as a practical requirement because tax authorities may disregard arrangements lacking commercial and economic substance.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance (Amendment) Acts 2018-2023 apply to the ELP. Wholesale distribution of optical frames is a relevant 'distribution and service centre' activity, requiring the ELP to perform core income-generating activities in Bermuda: procurement contracting, inventory management, pricing decisions, distribution risk management, and supplier/customer negotiations. The ELP must maintain adequate full-time employees, adequate annual operating expenditure, a physical office in Bermuda, and documented board or general-partner meetings held in Bermuda. A pure invoicing or purchase-and-resale arrangement without local decision-making substance is a shell-company red flag and may cause HMRC to treat the ELP as UK tax resident.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent CIT understatement

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the tax understated; late-filing surcharge of 10% plus daily statutory interest; criminal prosecution may lead to a maximum fine of BMD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years under the Tax Administration Framework Act 2021.

Director personal liability: The court may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors and the general partner jointly liable for unpaid CIT and penalties. Enforcement measures include asset freeze orders, travel bans, striking-off of the ELP, and director disqualification; personal restitution is ordered where funds were extracted in lieu of tax.
Shell company and sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Act 2018: initial penalty of BMD 20,000; continuing default penalty up to BMD 5,000 per day; serious non-compliance attracts fines up to BMD 100,000, suspension or deregistration, and revocation of tax protection.

Director personal liability: Officers and controlling partners are personally liable for ESR penalties where they knowingly make false declarations. Civil recovery follows deliberate asset depletion; HMRC can additionally assess the ELP as UK-resident and liable to UK corporation tax at 25% on worldwide profits, with personal liability for failure to account.
Anti-money laundering and beneficial ownership failures

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations and the Beneficial Ownership Act 2021, penalties may reach BMD 250,000 per offence; confiscation orders may cover the full value of laundered property; directors can be disqualified for up to 15 years.

Director personal liability: Individuals who direct concealment or knowingly file false beneficial ownership information are personally liable to criminal prosecution. Custodial sentences up to 14 years may be imposed for money laundering; travel bans and restraint orders are routinely granted; the corporate veil is pierced in favour of creditors and the state.
Transfer pricing manipulation and artificial profit shifting

Statutory sanction: The CIT Act 2024 applies the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, authorising the Tax Commissioner to adjust the ELP's taxable income to the arm's length result. Additional CIT is assessed at 15% plus interest and penalties; HMRC CFC or transfer pricing adjustments may create UK corporation tax liability of up to 25% of the shifted profit.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign tax returns knowing transfer pricing documentation is false are personally liable for penalties. Where the general partner strips assets from the ELP to defeat tax judgments, the court may order restitution against the general partner and its directors, with imprisonment available for contempt or fraudulent trading.
