Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (administers Payroll Tax and the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023); Bermuda Registrar of Companies (statutory entity registration); official portals: https://www.gov.bm/taxes and https://www.roc.gov.bm; HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) is the relevant authority for any UK-resident partners or UK group entities.

E-government filing portal

Neither Bermuda nor the UK issues a VAT registration number for a Bermuda ELP because the partnership is outside UK VAT and Bermuda has no VAT. Procurement steps: (1) register the ELP with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014 and obtain the partnership registration number; (2) if the ELP employs Bermudian staff, register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax and obtain a Payroll Tax/TRN account number; (3) if the ELP is a constituent entity of an in-scope MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least €750 million, register under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 through the Bermuda Tax Administration and obtain a CIT identification number; (4) UK-resident partners must quote their UK Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) in HMRC filings because the ELP is fiscally transparent under UK partnership tax rules.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda: the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (as amended) treats qualifying partnerships as fiscally transparent, allowing exclusion of partnership-level CIT where partners are taxed in their residence jurisdictions; no check-the-box election exists in Bermuda. UK: the ELP is treated as transparent under the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, Part 9, with each partner taxed on its distributive share; UK CTA 2010 Part 6A hybrid and other mismatch rules require consistent treatment to prevent double non-taxation; limited elections exist for intra-group CIT loss surrender or consolidated filing under Bermuda's CIT regime as amended by the Corporate Income Tax Amendment Act 2024.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST or sales-and-use tax in Bermuda; indirect taxation comprises customs import duties, Payroll Tax, and stamp duty; UK VAT (VATA 1994) applies only to UK-established group entities, not to the Bermuda ELP.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST; Payroll Tax registration is mandatory from the first Bermuda dollar of employer payroll with no exemption threshold; UK VAT registration threshold is £90,000 from 1 April 2024 but applies only to UK taxable persons, not to the Bermuda ELP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: there is no VAT/GST, so the ELP's offshore engineering and maintenance services exported to non-Bermudian clients are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect tax and cannot be zero-rated or refunded. UK: B2B supplies of consultancy or software services by a UK group company to a Bermuda customer are outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rule because the customer belongs in Bermuda, with input VAT recovery preserved; physical exports of goods from the UK to Bermuda qualify for 0% VAT under VATA 1994 Sch. 8 Group 1 provided the goods are exported within three months and conclusive evidence of export is retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK CDS export entry or Bermuda customs entry where goods transit or are re-exported)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 evidencing foreign-currency repatriation within 180 days
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Digital service contract, statement of work, and time-sheet evidence supporting offshore development services
  • Bermuda customs entry (TP or commercial form) and any applicable duty exemption certificate

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda; import declarations are processed electronically through the Bermuda Department of Customs' automated customs system; the UK parent, if VAT-registered, must comply with HM Revenue & Customs Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT using API-conformant software for UK supplies, but no government stamping or real-time clearance applies to Bermuda transactions.

UK: incorrect or fraudulent export zero-rating claims expose the claimant to HMRC assessments (up to 4 years, or 20 years for deliberate conduct), late-payment interest under VATA 1994 s.35, penalties of up to 100% of the VAT evaded under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24, and criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72. Bermuda: no VAT clawback exists, but misdeclarations under the Customs Act 1946 attract penalties up to treble the value of the goods and risk of forfeiture; late or inaccurate economic substance filings trigger fines up to BMD 250,000 and potential designation as a defaulting entity.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) under BMA foreign exchange policy. The Bermuda-domiciled Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) qualifies as a non-resident entity for exchange control purposes where no general or limited partner is a Bermudian resident. Consequently, capital account convertibility and current account convertibility are unrestricted: inward capital injections, operational payments, software service fees, and profit remittances to the UK parent require no prior exchange control authorization and are free from Bermudian withholding tax. Practical remittance hurdles are limited to standard source-of-funds documentation, economic substance evidence, and correct SWIFT purpose coding on the UK remitting side; there is no central bank approval gate or conversion certificate required for non-resident ELP accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation applies to a non-resident Bermuda ELP. There is no mandated repatriation timeline and the mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts is 0%; the ELP may lawfully hold, invoice, and settle in USD, BMD, GBP, or any freely convertible currency without converting export proceeds into BMD. BMA AML/ATF record-keeping obligations require retention of underlying export transaction records and commercial documentation for a minimum of 5 years from the date of the transaction, but impose no repatriation, conversion, or surrender condition.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, and the AML/ATF regime applicable to Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM for the Bermuda ELP as the primary treasury node. Wise (UK/FSU-regulated EMI) and Payoneer do not hold Bermuda banking or EMI licences and cannot maintain BMD-denominated settlement accounts for a Bermuda-domiciled ELP; their practical utility is confined to the UK parent's GBP contractor payout node. Legal constraint: under the BMA AML/ATF Guidance Notes, inbound credits to Bermuda bank accounts from foreign EMIs must carry fully populated originator and beneficiary metadata, and Bermuda-licensed banks generally reject transfers from EMIs lacking a direct correspondent relationship with a BMA-supervised institution. Recommended architecture: retain Wise B2B and Payoneer exclusively as disbursement rails for the UK parent, while all ELP-level treasury receipts and payments flow through a Bermuda-licensed commercial bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or dividend tax; under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA), a 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax applies only to 'Bermuda businesses' that are members of multinational enterprise groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on distributions, dividends, or partnership profit allocations to the UK parent.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda has no domestic transfer-pricing documentation threshold (BMD 0). The 15% CITA applies OECD TP to 'MNE groups' with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approx. BMD 810 million given the 1:1 BMD/USD peg); ESR separately requires contemporaneous records proving economic substance for all ELPs, irrespective of revenue. UK-parent obligations to maintain HMRC Master File (group revenue over EUR 750 million) and Local File (UK-controlled transactions over GBP 1 million) apply in the UK.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to all exempted entities, including ELPs, carrying on Relevant Activities. An ODC delivering software services to UK/group clients is a 'Distribution and Service Centre': it must be managed and directed in Bermuda (quorum of partner/board meetings in Bermuda), perform CIGA in Bermuda (contract negotiation, client management, risk acceptance, service delivery), and maintain adequate employees, premises, and expenditure proportionate to the activity. Pure IP-holding arrangements fall under 'Intellectual Property Business' and face a rebuttable presumption of insufficient substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: ESR penalties: BMD 10,000 default fine, BMD 500 per day (capped BMD 250,000 p.a.); mandatory re-filing and correction; failure to comply: strike-off of the ELP and cancellation of the tax-exemption certificate; false declaration: BMD 250,000 fine and/or imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: General Partner and its directors/officers are personally liable to the Registrar; the court may impose disqualification orders, travel bans, and orders for restitution of group benefits; the corporate veil is pierced where the GP is a shell and the UK parent exercises de facto management (Bermuda Exempted Partnerships Act 1992, s. 41 on GP liability).
Tax Evasion / Fraudulent CIT Declaration (CITA 2023)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to BMD 250,000 plus 200% (trebling) of the tax evaded, daily interest, and criminal fines; officers convicted of fraudulent evasion face imprisonment of up to 5 years and unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner are jointly and severally liable for the ELP's unpaid CIT and penalties where they signed false declarations or directed the evasion; personal asset freezing, international travel bans, extradition exposure, and imprisonment up to 5 years.
UK Parent - Failure to Prevent Tax Evasion (Criminal Finances Act 2017) and CFC Rules

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine on the UK corporate parent for failure to prevent the facilitation of UK or foreign tax evasion (Corporate Criminal Offence); CFC charge under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A on apportioned profits of the ELP attributable to the UK; interest and penalties up to 100% of the CFC tax.

Director personal liability: Individuals (UK directors and the ELP GP directors) who intentionally facilitate evasion are criminally liable with imprisonment up to 14 years (Criminal Finances Act 2017); HMRC can impose a travel ban (notice under FA 2015) and seek personal civil restitution and director disqualification of up to 15 years.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BMD 25,000 per offense and BMD 1,000 per day for continued default; for regulated AML breaches under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 (if the ELP engages in regulated activity): BMD 100,000 fine and/or imprisonment up to 10 years for officers; the ELP may be refused a tax-residence certificate and struck off.

Director personal liability: General partner and directors personally liable for fines; imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering or tipping-off; disgorgement of profits and UK Serious Fraud Office extradition routes to Bermuda.
