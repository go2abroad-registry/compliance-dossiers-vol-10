Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance; Registrar of Companies (for entity registration); Bermuda Customs Department (for import/export controls); correspondence and e-portal: https://www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

Bermuda imposes no income tax and issues no general VAT or corporate TIN. A foreign entity organising as an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must file a declaration with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 and receives a Bermuda Registry Number. If the ELP becomes an employer subject to Bermuda payroll tax, it must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number. For customs transactions, the ELP or its customs agent must register for a Customs Electronic Account through the Bermuda Customs Department.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (Territorial/Exempt scope for non-Bermudian partners; no Bermuda income tax on non-residents; ELP may nevertheless hold Bermuda payroll-tax obligations only if local employees are engaged)

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box entity classification regime and no separate corporation income tax election. The ELP's governing partnership agreement determines the internal allocation of profits and losses and confirms pass-through status for Bermuda law. Where the ELP is part of a multinational group that includes UK or other foreign entities, UK/global hybrid-mismatch rules may independently recharacterise the ELP for treaty or controlled-corporation purposes; Bermuda domestic law does not offer a statutory mechanism to elect out of such foreign recharacterisation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No General VAT/GST/Sales Tax; Destination-Based Bermuda Customs Duties on Importation (Customs Tariff Act 1990)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not Applicable — Bermuda does not operate a VAT/GST regime and therefore sets no registration threshold. Any person or entity importing dutiable goods into Bermuda must be registered with Bermuda Customs to obtain a Customs Bonded/Electronic Entry clearance regardless of annual turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT/GST exists in Bermuda, therefore there is no export VAT zero-rating mechanism. Goods exported from Bermuda are simply outside the scope of Bermuda customs duties (provided they were not previously imported for domestic consumption). Offshore services rendered by a Bermuda ELP are not subject to Bermuda indirect tax and do not qualify for any duty drawback or zero-rating refund. Where raw materials/consigned equipment are imported into Bermuda solely for re-export (for example, microfluidic cartridge components), a bonded or temporary importation regime may apply and duty is suspended on proof of eventual export.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration / Entry (WebHS filing)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export from Bermuda
  • Inward Remittance / Foreign Exchange Certificate or bank SWIFT evidence confirming export proceeds repatriation

Bermuda has no real-time electronic VAT clearance, API-based fiscal stamping, or central e-invoicing mandate. Furnishing of import declarations is performed through the Bermuda Customs electronic single-window (WebHS); exporters and their brokers file standard export entries and shipping documentation digitally. For valuation and duty liability, customs invoices and supporting shipping documents must be retained in accordance with Bermuda Customs requirements.

Because no VAT zero-rating or duty drawback is available, the principal anti-abuse risk concerns fraudulent customs duty suspension or bonded-export declarations. Bermuda Customs may conduct post-entry audit reviews and require corroborating proof of physical export, commercial invoices, shipping contracts, and payment receipts. False declarations are prosecuted under the Customs Act 1941 and Customs Tariff Act 1990, carrying fines (including penalties up to 100% of the duty at risk), forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment for responsible officers; unpaid customs duties are clawed back with interest. Misuse of any duty-suspended regime triggers immediate demand for duties, additional penalties, and cancellation of the bonded approval.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar under the Currency Act 1972. The BMA administers exchange control under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and associated regulations. Current-account payments are generally liberalised when routed through an authorised dealer, and trade-related import/export settlements do not require prior BMA approval. Capital-account treatment is bifurcated: a resident entity requires authorised-dealer or BMA permission for many outward capital movements, while an exempted limited partnership designated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes may receive capital from non-resident partners, maintain foreign-currency and US-dollar accounts, and remit profits offshore without BMA conversion or surrender approval. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no withholding tax; the practical gate is the bank's enhanced due diligence evidence that the ultimate beneficial owners are non-residents and that incoming funds have no Bermuda-resident source.

Export proceeds surrender rules

A Bermuda-resident exporter must repatriate foreign-currency export proceeds through an authorised dealer and offer the receipted foreign currency for sale to that dealer within 30 days of receipt; the mandatory conversion ratio is 100% where the proceeds are required for Bermuda-dollar settlement, unless a general permission, special permission, or non-resident account designation permits retention. A designated non-resident ELP is exempt from this surrender rule: export proceeds from offshore IVD manufacturing and distribution sales may be retained in a non-resident foreign-currency account, converted only as commercial need arises, and used immediately for trade and operational disbursements. The bank will still require a trade-file evidence trail—shipping documents, customs declarations, invoices, and underlying sales contracts—to substantiate that the proceeds are non-resident sourced and to prevent any BMA exchange-control challenge.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - Exchange Control Division

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Bermuda does not maintain a distinct e-money institution licence class, and offshore digital EMIs—Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, and similar—are not authorised to take deposits or settle payments for a Bermuda ELP as a primary treasury rail. A foreign EMI may serve only as a collection front-end if the final settlement account remains at a BMA-licensed authorised dealer and the EMI has no unlicensed Bermuda nexus; any Bermuda-nexus money transmission would require registration as a money services business under Bermuda law. Practical viability is low for primary clearing because USD correspondent and commercial LC flows require a tier-1 Bermuda banking relationship. The blueprint therefore uses a licensed Bermuda bank as the single liquidity base and restricts EMIs to non-core receivable collection channels.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound partner capital must be injected by SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident partner's bank to the ELP's BMA-licensed authorised dealer in Bermuda. The remitting bank must show the partner's full legal name in field 50K; the beneficiary field 59 must show the exact registered legal name of the ELP; the remittance information field 70 must state 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' together with the specific capital-call reference. The receiving bank will code the purpose as an inward direct investment or equity-capital contribution and may require the BMA non-resident designation certificate, the partnership agreement, a capital-call notice, and a completed source-of-funds questionnaire. Funds must first land in a capital escrow account and remain there until the bank completes exchange-control and enhanced due diligence clearance; for a medium-risk-enhanced IVD manufacturing ELP, typical clearance is one to three business days, extended to approximately ten business days in cases of complex ownership or high-risk source jurisdiction.The primary settlement rails are: (i) SWIFT MT103 for supplier prepayments, reagent procurement, research and development fees, and fast-turnaround purchase of microfluidic polymer materials; (ii) commercial documentary letters of credit for high-value analyser component shipments, with the Bermuda bank acting as issuing or confirming bank; and (iii) SWIFT MT202 for bank-to-bank correspondent funding between the Bermuda bank and the domestic clearing bank of the counterparty. Every disbursement must be supported by the standing trade-flow file: purchase order, commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, customs tariff code, and applicable IVD export-control reference. Because the BMD is pegged to USD and most trade is priced in USD, CHIPS or Fedwire settlement through the bank's US correspondent account will be used for USD disbursements without any dual-currency conversion friction.Outright deliverable forward foreign-exchange contracts in USD, EUR, GBP, or JPY against BMD for underlying trade exposures, executed only with a BMA-licensed authorised dealer.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referenced to emerging-market or restricted currencies, used solely to hedge IVD component purchases from jurisdictions with non-convertible currencies, provided the underlying trade obligation is documented and settlement is separately effected through deliverable rails.Cross-currency swaps between USD and EUR or USD and GBP to match manufacturing costs with customer-denominated revenue streams, with maturity capped at the duration of the underlying commercial receivable or purchase cycle.Vanilla purchased FX options, including zero-premium collar structures, where the position is demonstrably a genuine hedge of forecast IVD product sales or procurement; speculative naked option writing is not permissible.

0% – Bermuda does not impose any withholding tax on distributions by an exempted limited partnership, dividends of underlying companies, or profit shares remitted to non-resident partners.

Filing due no later than 15 months after the end of the fiscal year; advance quarterly estimated tax payments are required if the ELP is within CITA scope; a nil-return is mandatory for a Bermuda constituent entity even if no taxable income arises.

Filing due within 60 days of the anniversary of the date of formation of the exempted limited partnership; the filing period cannot be extended by merchant banking or Liquidator provisions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BD$1,000,000 (BMD) per intercompany category; under Bermuda's CITA transitional guidance, a Local File and Master File must be prepared when the MNE group exceeds EUR 750 million global revenue and Bermuda constituent entities have intercompany transactions exceeding BD$1,000,000 in a fiscal year; lower thresholds apply for insurance, financing, and IP-related transactions; the BMD is pegged 1:1 to the USD.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to every ELP that is tax resident in Bermuda and carries on a relevant activity. For a company manufacturing microfluidic cartridges and immunoassay analyzers, the relevant activity will ordinarily be 'distribution and service centre' if the ELP distributes finished goods or 'intellectual property business' if the ELP holds patents or trade secrets and earns royalties. The ESR require that the ELP (i) be managed and controlled in Bermuda, (ii) maintain an adequate physical presence (premises, employees, and equipment appropriate to the activity), (iii) incur a level of operating expenditure proportionate to income, and (iv) perform core income-generating activities in Bermuda. Pure equity-holding entities benefit from a reduced substance test, but only if they hold only equity participations and earn only dividends and capital gains; a mixed commercial function voids the reduced test and triggers full ESR compliance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and the Penal Code: a fine of up to BD$1,000,000, or twice the amount of tax evaded if greater, plus confiscation of the proceeds of evaded CIT; the BTA may impose a civil penalty of BD$250,000 and interest at 2.5% above reference rate; the Registrar may strike off the ELP and identify it as a non-resident shell.

Director personal liability: General partners and designated members who sign or approve a false return incur joint and several civil liability for the evaded tax; on conviction they may face imprisonment of up to seven years, personal travel bans, and disqualification from acting as a director or manager of any Bermuda entity. The court may order civil recovery of the value of proceeds concealed.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BD$10,000 to BD$25,000 for the first non-compliance; daily penalty of BD$500 after 30 days; increased assessment of BD$250,000 for a deliberate material misstatement; the Registrar may publish the ELP as a 'Non-Operating Entity', strike it off the Bermuda Register, and notify the UK tax authorities under the exchange-of-information arrangement.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner can be personally liable for the statutory penalty and for restoration costs; the BTA may apply to court for an order requiring the subsidiary's UK parent to disclose all group records; repeated breaches carry a one-year travel ban imposed by the Supreme Court of Bermuda and a Cessation Order prohibiting the ELP from carrying on any business in Bermuda.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Under CITA transfer pricing modalities and the Bermuda Tax Administration's advance pricing arrangement framework, the BTA may adjust taxable losses/profits to an arm's length standard, assess additional CIT at 15%, add interest accruing from the original tax due date, and impose a conduct-related penalty of up to 100% of the deficit for deliberate mispricing; disregard of sham transactions is also available.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve TP policies without contemporaneous benchmarking can be liable to the ELP for any restitution paid to the BTA; in egregious cases, the Bermuda court may extend a garnishee order to the directors' personal assets; professional advisers may be named jointly and several if they induced the mispricing; a director may be banned from serving as a manager of any exempted entity for up to three years.
Money Laundering / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to BD$1.5 million for the ELP for failure to maintain AML systems; senior officers liable to fines up to BD$1 million and imprisonment up to twenty years; proceeds of the IVD sale may be forfeited; the ELP may be deregistered as a prohibited entity.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner face personal criminal prosecution for conspiracy to launder; travel bans and extradition may be pursued through the UK-Bermuda Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty; the Supreme Court may issue a Mareva injunction freezing personal bank accounts and ordering the transfer of property to a receiver where proceeds were illicit.
