Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The ELP Act imposes no capital floor; the initial contribution is fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement (typically USD 1,000). No escrow or deposit account is mandated; capital may be contributed in cash or in kind.

Funding & unlock path: If a studio or render-farm facility is established in Bermuda, obtain: (1) a fire safety certificate from the Bermuda Fire and Rescue Service; (2) a building occupancy and structural load permit under the Building Act 2004 from the Building Authority (Department of Planning); and (3) workplace health and safety registration under the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1982 administered by the Department of Workforce Development. For a purely administrative offshore ELP with no physical Bermudian premises, these clearances are not applicable.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The ELP Act imposes no capital floor; the initial contribution is fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement (typically USD 1,000). No escrow or deposit account is mandated; capital may be contributed in cash or in kind.

The General Partner is authorized to execute all statutory forms, pay Registry fees, and take all steps necessary to register the Partnership under the Exempted Limited Partnership Act 1883.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable - a statutory industrial license is not required for commercial CGI visual effects and 3D character animation (ISIC 5911) in Bermuda. The Economic Development Department (Ministry of Economy and Labour) may be engaged voluntarily for sector incentives, and the Bermuda Department of Tourism (Film and Content Unit) can issue production-support letters for overseas clients.

Category: No operating or manufacturing permit is required; the Certificate of Registration of the Exempted Limited Partnership is the sole formation authorization. If a physical office or studio is later established in Bermuda, a Business License under the Business Licensing Act 2014 would be required from the Department of Licensing, Ministry of Finance.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance, Hamilton, Bermuda; Economic Substance Registry administered by the OTC via the Economic Substance Online Portal; Bermuda Customs Department (ASYCUDA World); Registrar of Companies (corporate registry portal: www.gov.bm / www.otc.bm)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) registers with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Limited Partnership Act 2015 and obtains a Certificate of Registration; no income-tax TIN is issued because Bermuda levies no corporate or personal income tax. The ELP must maintain a registered office and a resident representative. If it employs Bermuda-based staff, immediate employer registration with the OTC for Payroll Tax is required (employer account number). An economic substance entity reference is required only if the ELP performs a 'relevant activity' under the Economic Substance Act 2018; commercial 3D animation/VFX production (ISIC 5911) is not a relevant activity, so no ESR filing is typically required. For physical imports/exports, a Customs Tariff Number is obtained through ASYCUDA World.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box regime; the ELP is not taxed at entity level and is transparent by default, with profits flowing through to non-resident partners free of Bermuda income tax. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the ELP may file Form 8832 to confirm partnership classification (default) or elect corporate classification. No Bermuda hybrid mismatch elections are available because Bermuda has no income tax act; the ELP should maintain a partnership agreement with explicit profit-allocation and capital-account provisions to support pass-through treatment in investor jurisdictions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda imposes customs import duties, stamp duties on instruments and land transactions, and Payroll Tax on employers. No sales and use tax regime exists.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - No VAT/GST registration threshold applies. Payroll Tax employer registration is mandatory upon hiring any Bermuda-resident employee (no turnover threshold). Customs registration via ASYCUDA World is required only for physical goods movements; export of 3D animation services triggers no registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda has no VAT or GST and therefore no zero-rating mechanism. Export earnings of a Bermuda ELP from offshore commercial 3D animation and VFX services (ISIC 5911 / NAICS 512110) are entirely outside the scope of Bermuda taxation. No input VAT refund mechanism exists and no zero-rating certificate is required.

None. Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, digital stamping, or tax-authority invoice clearance. Commercial invoices for physical goods must comply with Bermuda Customs Department declaration rules in ASYCUDA World, but services and digital deliverables are not subject to any clearance obligation. Payment rails (Wise B2B, SWIFT wire) require no tax authority approval.

No VAT or export zero-rating clawback risk exists. Audit scrutiny centers on: (i) Economic Substance Act 2018 - if the ELP inadvertently conducts a relevant activity (e.g., IP business or financing), late filing by 31 July incurs penalties of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 100 per day, escalating to enhanced fines up to BMD 250,000 and information exchange; (ii) Payroll Tax - late filing penalties plus 10% per annum interest where Bermuda-resident staff are engaged; (iii) Customs Act 1957 - false export declarations attract penalties up to three times the duty evaded and forfeiture of goods; (iv) AML/beneficial ownership failures under the Proceeds of Crime Regulations - fines against the ELP and its resident representative. The 180-day FX repatriation deadline is a template convention, not a Bermuda statutory requirement; Bermuda has no exchange controls, but the ELP should document invoice-to-settlement within 180 days to preserve pass-through integrity and commercial substance evidence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) operates under a currency-board arrangement pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. There are no exchange controls, no quantitative limits on current or capital account transactions, and no licensing requirement for ordinary cross-border fund transfers. Profit remittances by a non-resident exempted limited partnership are freely permitted, subject only to standard AML/ATF screening, sanctions checks, and CRS/FATCA reporting where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export receipts. Exporters and service providers may retain all foreign currency proceeds in offshore or domestic accounts, and may convert such proceeds at their discretion through BMA-licensed banks or broker dealers.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — integrated financial regulator, central bank and currency board authority; ultimate sovereignty rests with the UK Crown through the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, but the Bank of England does not operate as the domestic central bank for Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium as an auxiliary payment rail; Low as a primary treasury or capital-escalation rail. BMA-licensed banks remain mandatory for the core custody of capital and operating liquidity. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Wise and Revolut Business can be used for small-value operational disbursements and collections in GBP/EUR/USD, but they are not licensed by the BMA and may not lawfully hold client escrow funds in Bermuda. Their acceptance of a Bermudian ELP depends on each EMI’s own jurisdiction and sanctions policy; a UK-FCA-authorised EMI is the strongest counterparty, but the EMI must not be used to circumvent Bermudian banking regulation or AML/CTF obligations.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 2,000,000 (US$ 2,000,000) aggregate controlled transactions in the fiscal year trigger a Local File obligation; a Master File is required where the MNE group has consolidated revenue of at least BMD 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's International Business (Economic Substance) Act applies to 'relevant activities'. A commercial 3D animation/VFX business may fall within 'distribution and service centre' or, if it owns or exploits intangibles, the 'intellectual property' category. The ELP must demonstrate Core Income Generating Activities are performed and directed in Bermuda, with adequate premises, employees, and expenditures, and must file annual economic substance declarations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to 200% of the tax understated and a fixed fine of BD$200,000; criminal imprisonment for up to 7 years on indictment.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner are personally liable for civil restitution, tax penalties and investigation costs; travel bans and disqualification from acting in Bermuda may be imposed.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act, the ELP faces deregistration and a penalty of up to BMD 250,000; the Registrar may also issue a default assessment of CIT at 15% on gross intangible income and deny a Tax Residence Certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for the ELP's debts on a piercing-of-the-veil analysis if the partnership was a facade from inception; personal assets may be subject to civil seizure.
AML/CFT Compliance Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BD$250,000 per instance, freezing of the ELP's bank and EMI accounts, and forfeiture of assets under the Proceeds of Crime Regulations.

Director personal liability: The designated compliance officer and general partner can be criminally prosecuted with imprisonment up to 5 years; directors face personal civil liability and a ban on serving in the financial sector in Bermuda.
