Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Tax Administration (Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance); portal: https://www.gov.bm/tax-information (electronic filing via Government of Bermuda e-Tax system)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities, including Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnerships, are not subject to a general income tax registration regime. Any entity with Bermuda-sourced payroll or customs activity must register under the Payroll Tax Act and Customs Act via the e-Tax portal, obtaining a Payroll Tax Account Number and a Customs Broker/Importer Code. ELPs with no Bermuda payroll or imports enjoy no registration duty. For indirect tax purposes, apply to the Tax Commissioner for a Bermuda Tax Reference Number (BTRN) if required to account for customs duties or payroll tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Bermuda law; partnership classification follows the Exempted Partnerships Act and Income Tax (Bermuda) exemption orders. Hybrid mismatch elections are not applicable as there is no corporate income tax; however, an ELP may apply for a specific exemption certificate under the Exempted Partnerships Act and may elect foreign-currency functional status for reporting to the Bermuda Monetary Authority.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Duties (Import Tariff); no VAT/GST on sales of services. Bermuda has no general sales tax, VAT, or gross receipts tax on services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None for VAT-equivalent; Customs import registration is required for any entity importing goods regardless of value. Payroll tax registration is required only when Bermuda-sourced payroll exceeds BMD 0 (first BMD 500,000 p.a. exempt for qualifying entities).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST on services; cross-border SaaS and AI video generation services are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect tax. Export goods are not subject to Bermuda export duty, and services supplied to overseas customers carry no Bermuda tax to zero-rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (if goods exported from Bermuda)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or banking evidence of foreign currency receipt

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate for digital services; e-Tax and Bermuda Customs Automated Commercial Environment (CACE) support electronic declarations. No API-based VAT clearance or digital stamping regime applies to AIGC SaaS services.

No VAT zero-rating refund or clawback regime exists in Bermuda. General anti-avoidance provisions under the Tax Commissioner Act permit penalties up to 100% of underpaid tax plus interest for misdeclaration in customs or payroll filings; fraudulent customs declarations attract fines up to BMD 10,000 and/or imprisonment. Export-related documents must be retained 6 years for Customs audit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1 BMD : 1 USD, and US Dollars circulate as parallel legal tender, eliminating currency mismatch risk for USD-denominated AI SaaS revenues. Full current and capital account convertibility applies to a properly designated non-resident exempted limited partnership: the BMA grants a non-resident designation at formation through the registered agent, which carries general exchange control permission for unlimited foreign currency bank accounts, unrestricted inward/outward capital and profit flows, and free conversion of BMD into any external currency without prior BMA approval. Profit remittance hurdles are nil at Bermuda level: Bermuda levies no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax on dividends/interest/royalties, or distribution restrictions; the ELP is fiscally transparent and profits flow to partners directly. The only practical friction is documentary—each distribution above USD 100,000 requires a GP distribution resolution, an updated allocation/register schedule, and a source-of-funds trail acceptable to the paying bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory baseline: the Exchange Control Act 1972 requires Bermuda-resident exporters to receive export proceeds through an authorised bank within 6 months of the date of export; however, a non-resident designated exempted partnership is carved out of the resident surrender regime. Mandatory conversion ratio for the ELP: 0% — proceeds of AI software and video-generation services collected via Stripe/Paddle as Merchant of Record may be retained 100% in USD, BMD, GBP or EUR in any jurisdiction, with no obligation to convert to BMD or to repatriate to Bermuda. Maintenance condition: the BMA non-resident designation must be kept current through the Bermuda registered agent, and the bank's standing instructions must show the designation certificate; if the ELP were re-characterised as Bermuda-resident, the 6-month realisation and conversion rules could apply.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, is the central bank, currency issuing authority, exchange control administrator and bank supervisor for Bermuda, an internally self-governing British Overseas Territory with the United Kingdom (GB) as the sovereign parent state. The BMA administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 and licenses deposit-taking institutions under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; it also operates the virtual currency/Digital Asset Business licensing framework where applicable, although the AI avatar SaaS model does not trigger that regime.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: MEDIUM. Legal feasibility is affirmative: (a) the non-resident exchange-control designation removes any BMA consent requirement for the ELP to hold foreign-denominated accounts at an overseas Electronic Money Institution; (b) no Bermuda banking licence is triggered where the EMI itself is licensed in its home state (e.g., FCA e-money licence for Wise or Revolut Business, or equivalent EU/APAC licences); (c) the ELP may lawfully receive SaaS payouts through an EMI. Practical constraints: EMIs consistently risk-tier British Overseas Territory entities and may decline under internal offshore-shell policies; where accepted, onboarding requires the Certificate of Exempted Partnership, registered partnership agreement, GP register of directors, source of capital evidence and proof of genuine SaaS activity; e-money balances are safeguarded but uninsured (no FSCS or Bermuda deposit insurance), so EMIs should hold only low-float operating balances. Recommended deployment: Wise Business multi-currency accounts for GBP/EUR vendor payments and staff reimbursements under USD 25,000 per month; Airwallex or Nium only as backup receiving rails; the Capital Escrow Account and strategic operating reserve must remain at a Bermuda Tier-1 licensed bank.
