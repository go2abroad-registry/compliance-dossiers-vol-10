Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Registrar of Companies and Bermuda Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda. Primary portals: https://www.gov.bm and https://www.bermudabusinessregistry.com

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is registered with the Registrar of Companies and receives a Bermuda Entity Number. There is no income tax TIN or VAT ID issued by Bermuda because the territory does not impose income tax or VAT. Should the ELP employ staff, it must separately register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax and Social Insurance contributions. For non-Bermuda tax jurisdictions, the ELP or its general partner may need to obtain a foreign tax identification number only when such jurisdiction requires entity-level reporting.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda has no domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election because no direct tax is levied. For foreign tax purposes, a Bermuda ELP may be classified as transparent or opaque according to the laws of the foreign jurisdiction. For US federal tax purposes, the ELP can file Form 8832 to affirm partnership classification; for UK tax purposes, the ELP is regarded as a non-UK resident partnership and generally treated as transparent unless it carries on a UK trade through a permanent establishment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (Bermuda has no general VAT or GST; customs duty applies only to imported goods)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Bermuda. An employer may become liable to register for payroll tax as an employer tax, but that is not an indirect tax threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda has no VAT/GST regime, therefore there is no statutory zero-rating mechanism for exports of cloud software or offshore services. Bermuda customs duty applies only to imports and does not apply to exports. Export revenues derived by a Bermuda ELP are outside the scope of Bermuda direct and indirect taxation.

  • Not applicable under Bermuda tax law: no VAT/GST export refund or zero-rating substantiation bundle exists in Bermuda.
  • If tangible goods are exported from Bermuda, standard shipping records include a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, and any customs authorization required under the Bermuda Customs regime.
  • Bermuda does not require an inward remittance certificate or FX repatriation certificate to substantiate a tax benefit; such documentation may be requested only for banking or AML compliance.

Bermuda does not mandate electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API reporting, or digital stamping. E-invoices are acceptable as primary business records provided they satisfy Bermuda records-retention and AML record-keeping requirements, which generally require retention for at least five years.

Bermuda has no VAT/export zero-rating clawback or retrospective VAT recovery adjustment because no VAT/GST exists. However, customs misdeclarations can result in fines, forfeiture, and penalties under Bermuda customs legislation; false statements to the Registrar of Companies may attract civil penalties; and fraudulent record-keeping or money-laundering offences are subject to criminal prosecution and substantial monetary penalties under Bermuda's Anti-Money Laundering and Economic Crime laws.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the U.S. dollar and fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Bermuda imposes no exchange control restrictions on inward or outward remittances. Profit repatriation is unrestricted for non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnerships; no prior approval is required, but remitting banks may require evidence of Economic Substance Declaration compliance and current know-your-client (KYC) data. Capital account convertibility is effectively free, although transactions exceeding certain AML thresholds trigger automated BMA reporting by financial institutions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda does not impose statutory export proceeds surrender requirements or mandatory conversion ratios for export receipts. Businesses may hold foreign currency accounts in any arbitrary denomination, and all incoming export proceeds may be retained, converted, or remitted at the discretion of the account holder without BMA approval.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Bermuda does not maintain a domestic electronic money institution (EMI) licensing framework; the BMA's Digital Asset Business Act governs digital asset businesses but does not provide for EMI passporting for offshore entities. UK or EU based EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise) may offer multi-currency IBANs to Bermuda ELPs on a discretionary basis, but such providers are not subject to local BMA supervision and may reject Bermuda-licensed or registered entities unless a sufficient commercial rationale and AML compliance standard is demonstrated. Traditional Bermudian banks remain the most reliable clearing and settlement rails, particularly for SWIFT-linked transactions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For capital injection from a UK parent or non-resident investor, the compliant routing is a SWIFT MT103 payment to the ELP's Bermudian account using purpose code 'GIFT' (General Investment Fund Transfer) or 'TFA' (Transfer of Funds from Own Account). The bank will require a board resolution authorising the issuance/contribution of ELP capital, a signed subscription agreement, and an anti-money laundering questionnaire, but no exchange control approval is required. For amounts above USD 100,000, the paying bank must provide a source-of-funds statement; incoming transfers to a Bermudian bank may be subject to enhanced scrutiny if the paying bank is in a non-cooperative jurisdiction. Remittances to be used for active SaaS/CRM operations should be labelled and recorded as capital contributions, not intra-company loans, to preserve the pass-through tax character of the ELP.Clearing rails for trade settlements include SWIFT MT103 for high-value vendor payments and invoice settlements, SWIFT MT202 for interbank liquidity transfers, and commercial letters of credit (documentary credits) for physical hardware or procurement related to the vertical cloud infrastructure. For routine SaaS subscription payments, BACS/ACH direct debit rails (UK and EU) may be used if the ELP holds a UK settlement account; otherwise, Stripe Billing is the preferred merchant-of-record rail for recurring customer collections. Each disbursement must be supported by a valid invoice, service contract, or tax advice memorandum, and retained under the BMA's required document retention rules (minimum five years from the transaction date). Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on corporate distributions, partnership profit allocations, or deemed payments to non-resident partners.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 (approx. €750M) consolidated group revenue under the CIT Act 2023. Below this threshold there is no statutory transfer-pricing documentation requirement in Bermuda; nonetheless, for any related-party software royalty, management fee, or partner loan interest, the ELP should maintain CUP or TNMM pricing models.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 and ESR Regulations apply. An ELP engaged in IP-business or holding activities must be managed and directed in Bermuda, maintain full-time employees and premises in Bermuda, and perform core income-generating activities there. A services-only cloud business is generally outside the defined relevant activities unless the catch-all provision for in-scope MNE constituent entities applies; deliberate shell structuring triggers CIT top-up, ESR penalties, and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misleading Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 250% of the understated top-up tax plus BMD 500 per day; criminal conviction punishable by fine up to BMD 500,000 and/or imprisonment up to five years; disgorgement of tax benefits and interest.

Director personal liability: The court can pierce the ELP veil and impose joint and several personal liability on the directors of the corporate general partner; civil restitution orders, asset freezing, travel bans, and director disqualification for up to 15 years are available.
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: ESR warning followed by a financial penalty of up to BMD 250,000 or 100% of the relevant income (IP business); strike-off from the Bermuda Register; the entity loses capacity to enforce contracts and may have its tax residence re-attributed to the UK for CIT purposes.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner become personally liable for the penalty if the partnership fails to pay; personal restitution orders and travel restrictions may be imposed; repeated default attracts criminal liability and imprisonment up to three years.
Fraudulent Trading / Misuse of Partnership Capital

Statutory sanction: On winding up, the court may declare the general partner and any knowing participant personally liable to contribute to the partnership assets; criminal fraudulent trading is punishable by a fine and/or imprisonment up to seven years.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner face unlimited personal liability for partnership debts; civil recovery, director disqualification, worldwide asset freezing orders, extradition, and travel bans are available.
AML / Money Laundering and CDD Breach

Statutory sanction: On conviction: fine up to BMD 100,000 per offence and/or imprisonment up to five years; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may impose administrative penalties, suspend the ELP's banking relationships, or require repatriation and disgorgement of funds.

Director personal liability: Directors, the corporate general partner, and the MLRO are personally liable for fines and imprisonment; they may also be barred from regulated functions, subjected to travel bans, and made subject to proceeds-of-crime confiscation orders.
