Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — Government of the United Kingdom; online portal: GOV.UK / HMRC Online Services

E-government filing portal

Bermuda is an overseas territory of the United Kingdom with fiscal autonomy and no general corporate or income tax. A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is not automatically registered for UK VAT or UK corporation tax merely by virtue of being constituted in Bermuda. If the ELP has a UK establishment (e.g., a fixed place of business through which business is carried on in the UK) or makes taxable supplies in the UK, it must register for UK VAT with HMRC and obtain a UK VAT registration number (VAT Reg No.). For UK direct tax purposes, a non-UK resident partnership is generally treated as transparent unless it has a UK permanent establishment; the ELP should file Form SA401/SA400 as appropriate and apply for a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) if a UK tax return obligation arises. Where the ELP is not UK tax resident and has no UK taxable activity, no UK TIN/VAT ID is required; the Bermuda Registrar of Companies and the Bermuda Tax Commissioner (where applicable under the payroll tax or land tax regimes) are the relevant authorities.

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No UK check-the-box election exists for a Bermuda ELP; the UK treats partnerships as fiscally transparent under UK income tax and corporation tax rules unless the partnership carries on a UK trade through a UK permanent establishment. Bermuda has no income tax election regime. The UK's hybrid mismatch rules (Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, Parts 6A and 6B) may apply to deny deductions or require counteracting adjustments if the ELP is treated as opaque in Bermuda or a third jurisdiction and transparent in the UK, or vice versa. The ELP may elect to be treated as a non-resident partner partnership for UK self-assessment purposes, but no entity-level classification election equivalent to the US check-the-box is available.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK: GBP 90,000 (annual taxable turnover of UK taxable supplies within any rolling 12-month period). Bermuda: no VAT registration threshold applicable; customs duties apply on imports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK: Zero-rating applies to the export of goods from the UK to a destination outside the UK, provided the exporter retains evidence of export within the time limits (generally 3 months from the time of supply, extended to 6 months in certain circumstances) and complies with HMRC VAT Notice 703. Supplies of services to a person who belongs in a country outside the UK (including the EU) may be zero-rated if the services fall within the place-of-supply rules (generally B2B services supplied to a non-UK recipient are treated as supplied where the recipient belongs, so they are outside the scope of UK VAT unless specified exceptions apply). Bermuda: No export VAT or zero-rating regime exists; exports are generally not subject to customs duty, but the ELP must substantiate the export for customs and regulatory purposes.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK: electronic export declaration via CDS; Bermuda: ASYCUDA World export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Proof of the non-UK destination and consignee evidence
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer/FX confirmation evidencing payment from the overseas buyer

UK: No mandatory real-time clearance or digital stamping for B2B VAT invoices. VAT-registered businesses must issue VAT invoices containing prescribed particulars and submit Making Tax Digital (MTD) VAT returns using HMRC-recognised software via the MTD API. E-invoicing is not mandated; PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0 is accepted for public-sector e-invoicing. Bermuda: no electronic fiscal clearance regime; import declarations are filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs' ASYCUDA World system.

UK: HMRC may assess VAT underdeclared plus default interest and penalties under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 (accuracy penalties) up to 100% of the tax understated for deliberate errors. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims may additionally incur civil penalties under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 and, for dishonest evasion, criminal prosecution under the Fraud Act 2006 or VATA 1994 s.72. Retrospective clawback may extend to a 4-year assessment period (extended to 6 years for careless errors and up to 20 years for deliberate/concealed errors). Bermuda: No VAT clawback regime since no VAT exists; false customs declarations attract penalties under the Bermuda Customs Department regulations, including forfeiture and fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. The Exchange Control Act 1972 applies formally, but exempted partnerships designated as non-resident are granted deemed general permission for all foreign currency transactions; no prior BMA approval is required for capital inflows, profit remittance, distributions, or liquidation proceeds. The BMD is pegged at parity to the USD with no band and no mandatory conversion spread. USD is legally accepted in practice as a parallel treasury currency. Remittance of profits and capital faces zero exchange-control friction and no Bermudian withholding tax. The only residual compliance is bank-level source-of-funds verification and CRS/FATCA reporting. GBP, EUR, and CNY exposures are freely convertible through BMA-licensed banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligations exist. Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline for repatriation of export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio into BMD, and no requirement to deposit export earnings with the BMA. Export receivables may be retained in USD, GBP, EUR, or CNY in trade-currency sub-accounts held at Bermudian banks. For this medical-device export entity, USD receipts are operationally fungible with BMD at parity, eliminating conversion friction. BMA statistical trade-data collection is aggregated and does not require per-transaction filing or an exchange-control certificate for exports of ISIC 3250 diagnostic ultrasound consoles.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; the BMA administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 and licenses all deposit-taking institutions in Bermuda. Banking conduct is supervised under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and AML/CFT obligations are enforced under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008. The BMA acts as the central bank and foreign exchange authority, with the Bermudian dollar (BMD) issued at a statutory parity of 1 BMD = 1 USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low feasibility. Bermuda is outside the UK FCA and EEA EMI passporting zone; UK-licensed EMIs such as Wise and Revolut Business are not authorized to onboard Bermuda-domiciled exempted limited partnerships. US digital banks (Mercury, Brex) and EU EMIs uniformly exclude Bermuda from serviceable jurisdictions. Bermuda's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 licenses fintech and digital-asset firms but none currently provide multi-currency EMI clearing comparable to tier-1 bank accounts. A UK EMI account may serve as a supplementary non-core collection rail only if the EMI accepts an offshore partnership, which most decline; principal liquidity and trade settlement must be anchored at a Bermuda tier-1 bank to avoid clearing failure.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT is levied in Bermuda or the Cayman Islands. A 15% Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies exclusively to constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda and the Cayman Islands impose no withholding tax on dividends or on partnership profit distributions. Distributions to limited partners are paid gross, subject only to the partnership agreement and to a distribution profile consistent with the submitted Economic Substance Return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda and the Cayman Islands prescribe no statutory Local File or Master File thresholds. As prudent best practice aligned with UK HMRC expectations and OECD Pillar Two documentation standards, the ELP maintains a full transfer pricing file where annual cross-border related-party revenue exceeds GBP 10,000,000 (approximately BMD/USD 12,000,000), or where intercompany royalties from the Doppler ultrasound software exceed GBP 1,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Fully applicable. Under the Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2020 and the Cayman Islands International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act (2021 Revision), an ELP performing 'distribution and service centre' activity (selling the Color Doppler Ultrasound Console to EU and Hong Kong distributors) must demonstrate: (i) direction and management exercised in the jurisdiction; (ii) local employees, operating expenditure, and physical assets proportionate to the activity; and (iii) CIGA performed locally by senior officers. The 'pure equity holding entity' exemption is unavailable because the ELP conducts active trading. Any licensing of the console software IP to related distributors is a high-risk IP relevant activity and requires the enhanced substance file; use of an IP shell company is prohibited.

Statutory sanction: Upward transfer pricing adjustment and recharacterization by UK HMRC under TIOPA 2010 Part 4; UK Diverted Profits Tax of 25% on the routed profit; Bermuda ESR review reclassifying the arrangement as IP-holding with a demand for commensurate substance; penalty interest on all adjustments.
