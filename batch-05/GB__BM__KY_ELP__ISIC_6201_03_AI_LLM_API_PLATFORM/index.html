Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Bermuda Ministry of Finance; online portal https://www.gov.bm/tax-and-duties; Bermuda Customs Department for trade and duty matters at https://www.gov.bm/customs

E-government filing portal

Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, corporate income tax, or withholding tax; therefore no TIN/VAT ID is issued for general corporate activities. A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership must obtain a Certificate of Exempted Partnership from the Registrar of Companies. If the ELP has employees in Bermuda, it must register for Payroll Tax with the OTC using Form OTC/PT-1 and obtain an employer registration number. No VAT number can be obtained because no VAT regime exists.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is tax-transparent under Bermuda law and, for U.S. federal tax purposes, is classified as a partnership by default under the U.S. check-the-box rules provided it has at least two members and does not affirmatively elect corporate status.

Available election forms

For U.S. purposes, an ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to elect or confirm classification (partnership or association taxable as a corporation). Bermuda itself has no domestic income tax or entity classification election. In cross-border arrangements, OECD BEPS Action 2 hybrid mismatch rules may require defensive or adjusting elections in a host jurisdiction; such elections are governed by foreign tax law, not Bermuda law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_VAT_GST; Bermuda levies customs duties, stamp duty, payroll tax, and foreign currency purchase tax; no value-added tax or goods and services tax applies

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not_Applicable — no VAT registration threshold exists. An importer or exporter of goods must register with the Bermuda Customs Department for a Customs Business Registration/Import-Export License, irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT or GST, so no export zero-rating mechanism or VAT refund regime exists. Exports of goods are not subject to any sales tax, VAT, or GST. Goods imported then re-exported may qualify for customs duty drawback under the Customs Tariff Act 1971, subject to compliance with Bermuda Customs procedures.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (Form CE-1)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice or Packing List
  • Export Permit (where the goods are regulated or subject to export controls)

No VAT e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists in Bermuda. Customs clearance is performed through the Bermuda Single Administrative Document, accompanied by commercial invoices and supporting transport documents; no digital fiscal stamping or API-based tax reporting is required.

No VAT clawback applies because no export VAT refund exists. Customs fraud or a false export declaration may result in penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 1971, including seizure of goods, monetary penalties up to three times the value of the goods, and imprisonment up to five years. Any duty drawback improperly claimed is subject to demand for repayment plus interest and penalties. Bermuda Customs, the Financial Intelligence Agency, and the Bermuda Monetary Authority may audit cross-border transactions to detect trade-based money laundering or false invoicing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD). For an Exempted Limited Partnership designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972, both current account and capital account transactions are freely permitted. Profits and capital can be remitted to partners without prior BMA approval, provided the ELP maintains its non-resident status and files accurate annual declarations. The only practical hurdles are compliance with beneficial ownership disclosure to the licensed bank, the ELP's own registered office and legal representation requirements, and the absence of any capital gains or withholding taxes in Bermuda. Full convertibility is available for USD/BMD; other currency conversions occur through the USD market.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export proceeds. Bermuda's exchange control framework permits a non-resident ELP to retain all export receipts in the original currency (including USD, EUR, or GBP) indefinitely. There is no prescribed timeline for surrender or conversion; the statutory default is immediate availability of proceeds in the receiving account. The BMA may require a declaration in the annual financial return identifying the currency composition of foreign assets, but this is not a conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low to Medium. Bermuda does not recognise a separate EMI licence; oversea fintech payment companies (e.g., Wise, Revolut) are not authorised by the BMA to hold BMD or provide payment services from Bermuda. Such EMIs can only provide ancillary settlement in foreign currencies (USD/GBP/EUR) via partner banks, but not as a substitute for a Bermuda bank account. Using a UK or EU EMI for cross-border receivables is legally viable because the ELP's substantive treasury operations remain in Bermuda, yet the BMA's anti-money-laundering framework will require disclosure of all passporting payment rails. Crypto-native EMIs or digital asset custodians offering USDC settlement must be assessed against the Digital Asset Business Act 2018; the ELP does not need a licence for its own treasury usage, but the intermediary must hold a DABA approval if it is a Bermuda resident company.

Capital contributions are routed via SWIFT MT103 from the foreign investor's bank to the ELP's Bermuda operational or escrow account. Bermuda's external payments statistical code for partnership capital injection is 'Direct Investment — Equity Capital'; the purpose code used on the wire should be 'Capital Transfer to Non-resident Partnership' or the equivalent BOP code recognized by the receiving bank. For an ELP designated as non-resident, no BMA prior approval is required before receipt. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For CIT-in-scope ELPs: Master File required where the multinational group has consolidated revenue ≥ €750M (approx. BMD 750M); Local File required where Bermuda ELP annual turnover exceeds BMD 25M or where related-party transactions with UK or other affiliates exceed BMD 25M per annum. Zero-CIT ELPs are not subject to Bermuda CIT transfer-pricing documentation, but contemporaneous pricing evidence (including OpenAI-API benchmarking) should be maintained for UK CFC and OECD substance defense

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 applies to all resident ELPs. An AI LLM API platform engaged in licensing, leasing, or otherwise exploiting IP (model weights, source code, SDKs, API algorithms) is almost certainly classified as an 'Intellectual Property Business' and must demonstrate: (i) core income-generating activities performed by qualified full-time employees physically in Bermuda; (ii) adequate physical premises and operating expenditure proportionate to UK-conducted R&D; (iii) active board/partner management in Bermuda. A passive ELP that only collects API subscription revenue without Bermuda substance is a shell entity and faces ESR penalties and possible strike-off

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 10,000 per month under ESR Act 2018; simultaneous CIT assessment if the ELP is part of a €750M+ group; Registrar-initiated strike-off and dissolution; HMRC may recharacterize the ELP as UK-resident under the UK deemed-domicile and CFC 'gateway' rules

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner are personally liable for knowingly submitting false ESR declarations; civil restitution of tax savings; 7-year UK tax-geared penalties; travel bans against senior officers under HMRC worldwide disclosure procedures
Tax Evasion / UK CFC Misuse

Statutory sanction: HMRC assessments extending back up to 20 years for deliberate concealment; penalties up to 200% of the tax evaded under FA 2007 Schedule 24; daily penalties for failure to provide CFC information; charging orders and freezing orders on UK real estate and bank accounts; criminal prosecution under FA 2000 s.144 with imprisonment up to 7 years

Director personal liability: Joint and several partner liability for the ELP's undisclosed profits; general partner personal liability for taxes and interest; directors can receive personal penalty determinations up to £100,000; HMRC 'naming and shaming' publication and UK immigration exit bans
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) fines of up to BMD 250,000 per contravention; Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 confiscation orders of the full value of laundered proceeds; ELP struck off and the corporate general partner disqualified; criminal AML penalties of up to 15 years' imprisonment

Director personal liability: Beneficial owner and senior managing officer must be registered with BMA and face personal confiscation; directors who signed false beneficial ownership returns are subject to civil restitution, travel bans, and immediate arrest upon re-entry to Bermuda
Late / Fraudulent Filing of Tax Returns

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Registrar fines BMD 10,000 plus BMD 100–500 daily penalty per return; loss of tax-residence certificate; dissolution of the ELP; immediate denial of the ELP's ability to onboard with Stripe or UK alternative banking rails; UK Companies Act director disqualification for cross-border filing fraud

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for all unpaid Bermudian payroll tax and surcharges; fraudulent trading claims by liquidators with joint and several principal liability; summary criminal conviction with imprisonment up to 6 months; permanent UK director disqualification orders
