Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance; official portal: https://www.gov.bm. Entity registration is administered by the Registrar of Companies (Government of Bermuda) under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015.

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Bermudian exempted limited partnerships (ELPs) are not required to obtain a domestic income tax TIN or VAT identification because Bermuda does not impose income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT. The statutory commercial identity is the Certificate of Exemption issued by the Registrar of Companies after filing the ELP’s declaration of non-resident status and non-carrying-on-business-in-Bermuda confirmation. If the ELP later employs persons in Bermuda, it must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for employer-related payroll tax purposes and obtain a payroll tax account number. If the ELP imports physical media or equipment, it must register with the Bermuda Customs Tariff System and obtain a customs importer record. Registration is completed electronically through the Bermuda Government Portal (portal.gov.bm).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda does not operate a US-style check-the-box election or a formal hybrid mismatch election. An ELP is automatically fiscally transparent for domestic income tax purposes. The partnership agreement may elect to allocate items in accordance with customary partnership rules, and partners may obtain a Certificate of Exemption confirming no local tax liability. Under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, an ELP that is part of a large multinational group (global revenue exceeding EUR 750 million) may be treated within the scope of Bermuda’s 15% top-up tax, subject to statutory filing and payment obligations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda imposes customs duty, excise duty, and stamp duty as the principal indirect levies.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no such tax is imposed. For customs purposes, every importer of commercial goods must register with the Bermuda Customs Tariff System from the first import; there is no de minimis monetary threshold for business registration, only an exemption for goods covered by approved duty-free categories.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda does not impose VAT or GST, so there is no export zero-rating mechanism and no input VAT refund regime. Film licensing royalties earned by a non-resident ELP from foreign sources are outside Bermuda’s income tax net. The ELP’s status as an exempted partnership, evidenced by a valid Certificate of Exemption, constitutes the statutory basis for asserting no Bermuda tax liability on offshore copyright income. If the ELP exports physical goods (e.g., film masters or hard drives), customs duty may be zero-rated or relieved under the Customs Tariff Act, but this is a customs measure, not a VAT zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (applicable only to physical goods; not required for pure licensing transactions)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for physical shipment of film masters or media)
  • Original or certified copy of the film licensing agreement and royalty invoice
  • Certificate of Exemption issued by the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015

Bermuda has no real-time electronic invoice clearance, API-based fiscal data transmission, or digital stamping mandate. Customs declarations for imports or exports are processed through the Bermuda Customs single electronic declaration portal. Tax filings, where applicable, are submitted via the Government of Bermuda online portal. No continuous transaction-control e-invoicing regime applies in this jurisdiction.

No VAT zero-rating fraud can arise absent a VAT system. However, criminal penalties under Bermuda’s Customs Act and general criminal law apply to fraudulent customs declarations, forged invoices, or false documentation submitted to the Bermuda Revenue or Customs authorities. If an ELP loses its exempted status by carrying on business in Bermuda without authorization, all income and assets may become subject to Bermuda taxation, exposing the partnership to reassessment, interest, and penalties up to 100% of the tax due. Willful tax evasion or deliberate concealment may result in imprisonment and monetary fines. The Bermuda Tax Commissioner may also disallow claimed offshore sourcing status and clawback any exemption benefit if the documentary evidence fails to show substance, commercial purpose, or arm’s-length pricing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains full capital- and current-account convertibility for non-resident entities, with no legal distinction that impairs profit or capital repatriation. The BMD is fully backed by US-dollar assets, so funds held by a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership can be freely converted and remitted abroad without BMA permission, withholding, or investment restrictions. ISIC 5911 film-licensing royalties, minimum guarantees, and distribution receipts entering or leaving the ELP are treated as ordinary current transactions and face zero convertibility friction; CRS/FATCA reporting obligations apply, but no exchange-control hurdles exist.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation, repatriation timeline, or mandatory conversion ratio. A qualifying ELP may retain license fee receipts in BMD, USD, GBP, EUR, or any other freely traded currency on or off the island, subject only to statistical balance-of-payments reporting by the licensed bank to the BMA. The ELP is thus unrestricted in treasury management of foreign film-licensing revenues.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, acting as the central bank and sole foreign-exchange administrative authority. Exchange control powers derive from the Exchange Control Act 1972, but in practice the BMA delegates routine approvals to licensed banks and applies no ex ante restrictions on BMD/USD dealings; the Bermuda Dollar is legally pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Conditionally Low. Bermuda is not an EEA state, so UK FCA-authorized E-Money Institutions and digital payment firms cannot passport into Bermuda; use of an offshore EMI (e.g., Wise Platform, Currencycloud, or MultiPass) is therefore restricted to incidental ancillary collection/payment rails for small-balance European receipts, never as the principal treasury account. Any unlicensed deposit-taking in Bermuda would violate the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and the ELP's economic-substance file should evidence primary settlement through a BMA-licensed domestic bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections from the contributing UK or Cayman partner must be sent by SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's designated account at Butterfield Bank or HSBC Bank Bermuda, using purpose code EN900 / CAPL (capital import) with the narratage 'Partnership capital contribution — Bermuda ELP Agreement s.7'. Wires may settle in USD or BMD at the statutory 1:1 peg; no BMA prior approval is required.
