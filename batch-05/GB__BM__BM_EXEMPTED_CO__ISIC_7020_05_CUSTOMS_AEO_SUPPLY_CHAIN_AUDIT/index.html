Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; administrative portal: https://www.gov.bm/tax-administration; customs duties administered separately by the Bermuda Customs Department via ASYCUDA World.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is first incorporated with the Registrar of Companies. It must then register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (TIN). Because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, no VAT registration process exists; for customs purposes, the entity must register with the Bermuda Customs Department as an importer/exporter to obtain a Customs Importer Code and an ASYCUDA World user account. Payroll tax, social insurance, and corporate income tax (CIT) registration are triggered separately where applicable.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is first incorporated with the Registrar of Companies. It must then register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (TIN). Because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, no VAT registration process exists; for customs purposes, the entity must register with the Bermuda Customs Department as an importer/exporter to obtain a Customs Importer Code and an ASYCUDA World user account. Payroll tax, social insurance, and corporate income tax (CIT) registration are triggered separately where applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic pass-through or check-the-box election is available; the Exempted Company is an entity-level taxpayer. For U.S. federal income tax purposes, the entity (as a foreign eligible entity) may file Form 8832 to elect between disregarded entity, partnership, or corporation treatment. Under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, in-scope constituent entities of multinational groups may make specific elections regarding loss usage, tax credits, and the application of the qualified domestic minimum top-up tax. Certain Exempted Companies may also elect resident status for exchange control purposes under the Exchange Control Act 1972.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST or general sales tax; Bermuda applies customs duties, stamp duty, and other selective consumption duties on imported goods and certain local transactions.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Any importer must obtain a Bermuda Customs Department importer code (no monetary threshold). Specific licenses/permits may apply to controlled goods such as arms, chemicals, or agricultural products; the supply chain audit scope at ISIC 7020 does not require a separate customs license.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT therefore no 0% export VAT mechanism applies. Exported goods are generally free of customs duty once bona fide exportation is proven. A Bermuda Exempted Company providing authorized economic operator (AEO) and supply chain security audits must demonstrate that any goods physically exported from Bermuda have left the customs territory and that no local consumption occurred. Offshore services supplied to non-residents are outside the Bermuda indirect tax net and do not attract duty; no refund mechanism exists for non-existent VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CEx1 lodged via ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or House/Master Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Shipment or carrier-issued proof of export
  • Bermuda Exchange Control documentation or Inward Remittance Certificate where foreign exchange proceeds are repatriated

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate is in force. Customs declarations are submitted electronically through ASYCUDA World (Bermuda's UNCTAD-based customs management system) with mandatory lodging of export/import declarations, risk assessment, and customs release via the system. Exempted companies performing AEO-style supply chain audits must ensure client customs records are ASYCUDA-compatible where export proofs are required.

False export declarations, duty drawback abuse, or fraudulent zero-rating claims attract penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 2005 and the Exchange Control Act 1972, including monetary penalties up to the value of the goods, forfeiture of goods and conveyances, customs audit reassessments, and imprisonment for culpable officers. The Bermuda Tax Administration may retrospectively claw back customs duty relief, drawback, or exemptions within statutory audit periods and impose interest on unrepatriated export proceeds, with additional AML/CTF scrutiny where AEO supply-chain audit credentials are misused.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 rate to the US Dollar (USD); USD is legal tender throughout Bermuda alongside BMD, producing de facto full convertibility at par with zero spread for USD/BMD. Exempted companies enjoy unrestricted current-account convertibility for trade in services (including cross-border AEO supply-chain audit consulting fees) and unrestricted capital-account convertibility for inbound equity injections, intercompany loans, dividend distributions, and capital repatriation. No prior exchange-control approval is required from the BMA for non-resident-owned exempted companies, and there are no restrictions, quotas, or licensing requirements on the holding of foreign currency (GBP, EUR) in resident or offshore accounts. The BMA in practice retains powers only to monitor unusual movements for AML/CTF purposes; profit remittance hurdles are nil, and any delay arises solely from the commercial bank's sanctions screening rather than statutory FX controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds or services receipts. A Bermuda Exempted Company providing AEO certification and supply-chain security audit services may retain 100% of its foreign-currency service revenue in any freely convertible currency, may maintain multi-currency accounts at Bermuda's licensed banks, and may deploy such proceeds directly for operating disbursements without any obligation to surrender, repatriate, or convert currency to the BMA or an authorized dealer. This full-surrender exemption is memorialized in the standard exchange-control permission granted to every exempted company at incorporation and remains subject only to the bank's internal transaction monitoring for suspicious activity under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and exercising residual foreign-exchange administration powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (as applied to exempted companies via the Exchange Control (Exempted Companies) (Bermuda) Regulations). The BMA also issues banking licenses, regulates the Bermuda Stock Exchange, and collects balance-of-payments statistical data from authorized dealers; however, no separate foreign-investment approval agency exists for an exempted company.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility as an ancillary clearing rail but Low-to-Medium viability as a primary banking relationship. Wise Business (FCA-authorised electronic money institution, UK, with EU and US licenses) is the designated supplementary rail, mapping directly to the target payment rails Wire_Transfer and Wise; it enables low-cost multi-currency receipts from UK, EU, and US clients with local account details (sort code, IBAN, ACH/routing) and sub-threshold disbursements to subcontractors. Legal constraint: a UK EMI is not a BMA-licensed bank and cannot hold BMD, is ineligible to issue commercial letters of credit, and cannot provide overdraft or credit facilities. Client funds are safeguarded under FCA e-money regulations rather than held as bank deposits, so the entire treasury structure must retain a BMA-licensed commercial bank as the primary custody and settlement layer. The 1:1 BMD/USD peg neutralizes conversion cost on core operations, but interbank FX spread for GBP/EUR conversions on Wise remains a budget line and must be quarantined below the bank's own reference rate to justify duel-rail usage.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: the shareholder (or member) remits through a regulated bank using SWIFT MT103; the beneficiary account is the HSBC Bank Bermuda 'Capital Escrow Account'. The originator must populate SWIFT field 70 with an unambiguous payment reference, e.g., 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION / SHARE PREMIUM' plus the company registration number; for inbound remittances from the UK, the originator should cite the BoE/ONS purpose code 'G301 — Investment in equity capital' (or 'G303' for intercompany financing); for EUR-area originators, the ECB Balance of Payments purpose code 'S302 — Direct investment, equity capital' must be stated; US remitters use the Fedwire tag 'A70' (equity capital) or 'B22' (intercompany loan). The Bermuda receiving bank requires, before or at the time of credit, a board resolution authorizing the capital increase, a share allotment certificate, and the corporate secretary's compliance certificate. No BMA approval or exchange-control license is required; however, the bank's back office may submit the transaction as part of its mandatory balance-of-payments statistical return to the BMA. If the injection creates a premium over par, no stamp duty arises in Bermuda, and the entry will simply augment share premium in the statutory financial statements; for AML-comfort, the remitter's bank statement must match the remitting account name exactly to avoid a source-of-funds query.Primary rail for operational disbursements (audit subcontractor fees, software licensing, customs-bond premiums, third-party security testing) is bank-originated SWIFT MT103 from the Butterfield multi-currency account; USD payments clear through the correspondent network (typically JPMorgan or Citibank in New York) and GBP/EUR payments clear via CHAPS/TARGET2 through the bank's UK/EU correspondents. For high-value procurement of AEO security equipment (X-ray screening, C-TPAT-aligned infrastructure) or client project disbursements, a Commercial Letter of Credit (UCP 600) may be issued by HSBC Bermuda in favor of the supplier, with a standard tenor of 30-90 days after acceptance of shipping documents; the exempted company must maintain a collateralized deposit or an approved line to support LC issuance. Wise Business is the designated secondary rail for sub-threshold disbursements (less than GBP 50,000 per transaction) to UK/EU subcontractors, using Faster Payments and SEPA Instant settlement; Wise cannot issue LCs or take documentary credit risk. All outbound USD wires to high-risk jurisdictions are subject to enhanced screening; the treasury protocol requires the audit engagement contract, AEO certification scope, and end-beneficiary declaration to be attached to every MT103 instruction. Corporate cards (HSBC/Butterfield) provide a tertiary retail rail for low-value travel and supplier payments, settled daily from the operational account.Plain-vanilla deliverable FX forwards (GBP/USD and EUR/USD outright forwards, tenors up to 12 months, with rollover feature) available from HSBC Bank Bermuda and Butterfield under ISDA/CSA documentation; BMD/USD forwards are unnecessary and not quoted given the fixed 1:1 peg.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) permitted only for exotic/emerging-market currencies in the client's supply-chain counterparty portfolio (e.g., INR, BRL, TRY), executed through the Bermudian bank's offshore dealing desk with an authorized NDF dealer; NDF use is restricted to hedging forecast net audit-fee exposures and requires board-approved treasury policy; NDFs on BMD are unavailable and never appropriate due to the peg.Cross-currency swaps (USD/GBP and USD/EUR) for matched asset-liability management of multi-year AEO retainer contracts and intercompany loan servicing; executed under ISDA with Butterfield or HSBC, with a minimum notional of USD 2,000,000 equivalent; maturities aligned to the average client contract duration.European-style FX options (USD/GBP, USD/EUR, and USD/CHF) for contingent deal-flow hedging in cross-border M&A-related supply-chain audits; premiums paid in USD; available from HSBC Global Markets desk with 3-6 month tenors, capped by a board-approved option-policy limit of 20% of forecast annual USD exposure.Cross-currency basis swaps involving BMD are not needed and are not offered because the peg renders the BMD/USD basis at zero; treasury therefore consolidates all foreign-currency risk into the USD/GBP and USD/EUR axes via the instruments above.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda-specific transfer pricing documentation threshold exists in local currency (Bermudian Dollar, BDG). Bermuda does not have a transfer pricing code. Multinational groups must nevertheless comply with OECD transfer pricing documentation standards if the ultimate parent entity files a Country-by-Country Report in another jurisdiction, and Bermuda may exchange information under the multilateral competent authority agreement.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda adopted the Economic Substance Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations 2018. All Bermuda Exempted Companies engaged in relevant activities must satisfy economic substance tests unless they are tax resident in a foreign jurisdiction. Relevant activities include intellectual property business, distribution and service business, financing business, headquarters business, and shipping business. A company conducting one of these activities must be directed and managed in Bermuda, have adequate employees, premises, and equipment, and derive core income-generating activities in Bermuda. A supply chain audit company engaged in management consultancy under ISIC 7020 is typically classified as a 'holding business' or 'headquarters business' only if it earns income from providing services to affiliates; otherwise it may be considered a non-relevant activity subject only to standard annual return requirements.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BDG 10,000 per contravention and an additional BDG 10,000 for each month of continuing default. The Registrar may also apply to the court to strike the company off the register and deprive it of the ability to enforce contracts or claim legal standing.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may be personally liable if they knowingly permit continued non-compliance. The court may order disqualification of directors for up to 5 years, impose fines, and require restitution of any financial benefit obtained through non-compliance.
Misrepresentation of Tax Exemption

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties for false statements under the Companies Act 1981, including fines up to BDG 100,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years. The company may be subject to revocation of its exempted status.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or who knowingly participate in misrepresentation face personal criminal liability, civil fines, and disqualification orders. Travel bans may be imposed pending court proceedings.
Anti-Money Laundering Failures

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Anti-Money Laundering Regulations 2018, breaches attract fines up to BDG 500,000 and imprisonment of up to 10 years for the company and responsible individuals. The Bermuda Monetary Authority may also impose enforcement action against licensed corporate service providers.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and money laundering reporting officers face personal criminal liability, including custodial sentences, financial penalties, confiscation orders, and restrictions on international travel during investigation.
Shell Company Abuse / Tax Evasion Assistance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda courts may order the winding-up of the company, impose civil penalties under the Tax Information Exchange Act, and the company may be penalized under the Economic Substance Act. Additionally, foreign tax authorities may recover taxes directly through mutual legal assistance or tax information exchange agreements.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in facilitating tax evasion may be personally liable for the foreign tax debt, subject to confiscation orders, travel bans, and extradition proceedings. Criminal fines and imprisonment may also be imposed under Bermuda's general criminal law.
