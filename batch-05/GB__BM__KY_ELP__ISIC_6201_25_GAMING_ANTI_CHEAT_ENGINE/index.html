Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Government of Bermuda, Ministry of Finance, Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax) and the Registrar of Companies (exempted partnership registration/tax exemption assurance). The official self-service portal is gov.bm; no dedicated electronic VAT/TIN portal in Bermuda.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) does not obtai an income tax TIN. If it has individual employees physically working in Bermuda, it must register with the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and obtain a Payroll Tax Number. For general tax existence, the ELP must file for an 'Assurance of Tax Exemption' and a Certificate of Exempted Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015, lodged with the Registrar of Companies. For UK/Cayman purposes, the ELP may also be privately advised to obtain local tax identification numbers in the jurisdiction of effective management or immediate parent entity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda does not have a check-the-box or US-style entity classification regime. The ELP is, by default, tax transparent. For certain cross-border holdings, an entity may elect to be treated as a corporation in the United Kingdom under the UK's 'SAPE' rules if centrally managed and controlled there, or may file Form 8832 in the US to elect classification solely for US federal tax purposes. No hybrid mismatch election is available in Bermuda because no corporate income tax is imposed.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST; Bermuda levies customs/import duties only.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold. An importer is required to register with Bermuda Customs and acquire an import/export licence exclusively for goods physically crossing Bermuda's border. For pure digital services or software middleware, no indirect tax registration threshold exists.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST on any supplies, including exports of gaming anti-cheat middleware, offshore services, or digital goods. Therefore there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim and no refund entitlement to substantiate.

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoice, or digital stamping regime applies. Bermuda Customs uses the Berwick/ASYCUDA platform for import declarations, but that is an import control system and not a VAT clearance mechanism.

There are no VAT or export zero-rating clawback penalties because no consumption tax exists. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar. Current account transactions for non-resident controlled exempted partnerships are generally freely convertible without prior BMA approval, provided payments are routed through an authorized dealer. Capital account transactions, including inward capital contributions above statutory thresholds and repatriation of contributed capital, require prior exchange control permission or notification via prescribed forms. Profit and dividend remittances to non-resident partners are permitted after submission of audited financial statements, a certificate of compliance, and confirmation that no local tax liabilities are outstanding; Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on such distributions, but the BMA may require evidence of underlying trade or service transactions to prevent disguised capital flight. The exempted partnership should secure formal 'non-resident status' designation to eliminate mandatory conversion of foreign currency receipts and to obtain unrestricted foreign currency accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a resident enterprise, all export proceeds from sales of goods or services must be repatriated to Bermuda within six months from the date of export and surrendered to an authorized dealer for conversion into BMD, subject to any specific BMA dispensation. However, an exempted partnership granted non-resident status under the Exchange Control Act is not subject to mandatory surrender and may retain and disburse foreign currency receipts in their original currency. For BMD-denominated receipts, the proceeds are already local currency and no surrender event arises. The ELP treasury function must maintain a retained export proceeds ledger and, if a temporary resident authorization is ever triggered, comply with the 100% surrender obligation to the BMA's authorized dealer network.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), Exchange Control Department, acting under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low to Medium. Global fintech EMI platforms such as Wise, Airwallex, and Payoneer are not licensed by the BMA as deposit-taking institutions or money service businesses capable of holding BMD; they can support USD or EUR accounts, but full integration with Bermuda's clearing system and exchange control compliance is absent. Legal constraints include the absence of EEA or UK passporting rights into Bermuda, BMA restrictions on unauthorized money transmission, and the requirement that all resident or entity-level BMD accounts be held at an authorized dealer. Fintech EMIs may be used only for ancillary collections in foreign currency, never as the primary treasury rail, and must not receive BMD deposits. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inward capital from UK or overseas investors must be wired via SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's BMD or USD clearing account at a Bermuda authorized dealer. The wire instruction must reference the partnership name, the ELP's registration number, and the purpose code 'GPCI' (General Payment for Capital Investment) or equivalent ISO 20022 code as specified by the receiving bank. A board-approved capital call notice and a subscriber certificate must accompany the transfer. For contributions exceeding the BMA's notification threshold (currently USD 10,000 or equivalent), an exchange control application (BMA Form EC-1) must be filed through the receiving authorized dealer before or within seven days of receipt. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, applicable from fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025 to in-scope MNE groups (consolidated global revenue ≥ EUR 750M in at least two of the preceding four years); the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is tax-transparent for Bermuda CIT, with tax charged on eligible partners, unless an election or status makes the ELP itself a taxable entity.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no dividend or distribution withholding tax on ELP profit allocations, and no tax is levied at the partnership level to the extent the ELP is tax-transparent; partner allocations are paid gross with no Bermuda source withholding.

CIT return and final payment due no later than 15 months after the fiscal year-end; transitional filing deadline for the first in-scope year (FYE 31 December 2025) is 31 March 2027; estimated tax payments are payable in quarterly installments as directed by the OTC for in-scope groups.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 10,000,000 (Bermudian dollar, pegged 1:1 to USD) aggregate related-party revenue or expense in a fiscal year triggers mandatory preparation of a Transfer Pricing Local File; a Master File is required where the in-scope MNE group has consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more, per the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 and the Transfer Pricing Regulations 2025 adopting the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Amendment Act 2019 applies to Bermudian entities carrying on relevant activities; a gaming anti-cheat and integrity middleware business involving IP development, licensing, and distribution/service centers is a relevant activity. The ELP must demonstrate: (i) direction and management in Bermuda; (ii) Core Income Generating Activities (CIGAs) performed in Bermuda; and (iii) adequate physical premises, employees, and operating expenditure proportionate to the activity. A pure equity-holding exemption is not available for active IP licensing; additionally, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 contains a substance-based carve-out that must align with real economic presence, and the UK parent must not carry on central management and control of the ELP in the UK or the ELP may become UK tax resident and subject to UK corporation tax.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Filing

Statutory sanction: On summary conviction, a fine up to BMD 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years; on indictment, a fine up to BMD 500,000 and/or imprisonment up to 10 years; the OTC may also impose tax-geared penalties of up to 100% of the underpaid CIT plus irrebuttable statutory interest, and may disallow the partnership's loss carryforwards entirely.

Director personal liability: The General Partner and its directors are personally liable where they knowingly authorised the false return; the corporate veil of the ELP is pierced for fraudulent conduct, the OTC may make joint and several assessments against partners for unpaid CIT, and the court may impose director disqualification orders, travel bans, and civil restitution of the evaded tax.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Amendment Act 2019, failure to satisfy the economic substance test attracts an initial registrar penalty of BMD 10,000, escalating fines up to BMD 250,000 for continued non-compliance, and ultimately deregistration/strike-off of the ELP; under the CIT Act 2024 anti-avoidance rules, the OTC can recharacterise or reattribute the ELP's profits to the UK parent or the partners.

Director personal liability: Directors and the General Partner may be personally liable for penalties where the shell is used to mislead the Registrar or the OTC; HMRC can apply the UK CFC rules to the UK parent and charge UK corporation tax on the diverted anti-cheat IP profits; directors may face disqualification in Bermuda and the UK, travel restrictions, and personal civil liability for the resulting tax shortfall.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Beneficial ownership non-compliance carries fines up to BMD 25,000 for the ELP plus BMD 5,000 per continuing day of default; AML regulation breaches attract fines up to BMD 100,000 per contravention and criminal penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years on summary conviction; the Registrar may file a court application to strike off the ELP and freeze its Bermuda bank accounts.

Director personal liability: The General Partner, designated directors, and any beneficial owner who knowingly facilitates breaches are subject to personal fines, imprisonment, and civil damages; courts may pierce the partnership veil to impose personal liability for unpaid penalties, issue travel bans, and order restitution of funds laundered through the ELP's payment rails.
