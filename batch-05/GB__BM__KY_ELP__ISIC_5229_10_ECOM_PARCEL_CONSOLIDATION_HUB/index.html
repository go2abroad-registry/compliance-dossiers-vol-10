Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (portal: https://www.gov.bm/); for UK tax nexus where applicable, HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) via https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs

E-government filing portal

Bermuda has no general income tax, VAT, or TIN/VAT ID regime for a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership. A foreign entity obtains a statutory identity number by filing the requisite incorporation/registration documents with the Registrar of Companies through the Bermuda Corporate and Business Registry, which issues a Company/Partnership Registration Number. If the ELP later becomes an employer, it must separately register for a Bermuda Payroll Tax account with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a payroll tax reference number. No UK TIN/VAT ID is required solely as a result of Bermuda registration unless the partnership creates a UK VAT or corporation tax nexus.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda law does not impose entity-level income tax, so no domestic tax election is required. For US federal tax purposes, the Bermuda ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to affirm pass-through partnership treatment or elect corporate classification under check-the-box rules. For UK tax purposes, the ELP is generally treated as tax-transparent to the extent that no UK permanent establishment exists; UK hybrid mismatch rules may disallow certain deductions if a hybrid instrument or disregarded entity is used.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in Bermuda; Bermuda applies import customs duties and excise duties on specified goods under the Customs Tariff Act 2014

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists in Bermuda; commercial importers and customs brokers must register with Bermuda Customs for import-duty purposes regardless of turnover

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable to Bermuda because Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST and therefore has no VAT zero-rating or input VAT refund mechanism. If the Bermuda ELP separately makes UK VAT-able supplies through a UK establishment, exports of goods from the UK to third countries may be zero-rated under UK VAT law, provided the entity holds and preserves valid evidence of export within the prescribed time limits.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Proof of Export / Carrier Confirmation

No mandatory e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or real-time API reporting regime is operated in Bermuda. Commercial invoices and packing lists must be submitted to Bermuda Customs for import/export clearance using the customs declaration process; electronic filing options may be accepted but no continuous fiscal-clearance mandate exists.

Bermuda Customs may issue penalties, forfeiture orders, and seizure where export declarations are false or goods are misdescribed; officers may also demand duty and impose fines. For UK VAT purposes, HMRC may charge interest and assess the VAT clawed back as output tax, with civil penalties of up to 100% of the VAT for deliberate inaccuracy or fraud; supporting export evidence must be retained ordinarily for at least six years and produced on request during an HMRC audit.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD) under the Currency Act 1972. Bermuda maintains full convertibility on both the current and capital accounts: no exchange controls exist, no restrictions apply to the purchase of foreign currency, no profit remittance approval is required, and no withholding or deferral constraints attach to distributions by an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) to non-resident partners. The UK/EU e-commerce operations will predominantly settle in GBP and EUR, both freely convertible; the ELP may hold multi-currency accounts and repatriate capital and profits without BMA authorisation, subject only to standard AML verification, economic substance filing obligations, and residual UK tax compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender regime exists in Bermuda. There is no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Proceeds from cross-border parcel consolidation, sorting, and direct-injection linehaul services may be retained in the invoicing currency (GBP, USD, EUR, or any freely convertible currency) or converted at the commercial rate of a licensed bank. UK group entities remain subject to HMRC transfer-pricing attribution rules, but no forced sale or mandatory repatriation of foreign currency applies.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA); for UK-linked operations the Bank of England's Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) provide the relevant regulatory overlay

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for treasury optimisation, FX conversion, and cross-currency disbursement; MEDIUM as a standalone primary bank account. Airwallex, together with Wise Business and Revolut Business, offers pan-European sort codes, multi-currency wallets (GBP, USD, EUR), SWIFT/SEPA/Faster Payments access, and local clearing rails consistent with the project's target payment rails. Legal constraints: these platforms are licensed as Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) under the UK FCA or EU e-money directives, not as Bermuda deposit-taking institutions. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions and intercompany loans from the UK parent to the Bermuda ELP must be routed via licensed correspondent banks using SWIFT MT103 or MT202 serial payments with purpose code 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' (UN/EDIFACT 060) or 'INTER-COMPANY LOAN' (UN/EDIFACT 465). The instruction must reference the executed capital contribution schedule or loan note, board resolutions of both entities, and tax classification forms (US W-8 or UK SIC proof where requested). The receiving Bermudian bank validates CAB/FRB filters, OFAC sanctions screening, and enhanced due diligence checks. For funding above £100,000 equivalent, transfer-pricing documentation must accompany the remittance. Funds are typically credited in same-day or next-day value and become unrestricted for trade settlement.Trade settlements to freight forwarders, linehaul carriers, sorting facility operators, and customs brokers will primarily execute via SWIFT MT103 with complete structured remittance information, using a commercial letter of credit (LC) only for high-value, duty-unbonded consignments above USD 250,000. For UK and EEA counterparties, direct local clearing is preferred on a cost basis: UK Faster Payments or CHAPS for GBP, SEPA Credit Transfer Instant for EUR, and US ACH rails for USD via the sponsored EMI or partner bank.
