Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Partial. A registered office in Bermuda is strictly mandatory for an Exempted Limited Partnership, and a licensed registered agent must maintain the physical registered office at a real, street-level address in Bermuda. A pure virtual office may be accepted for ancillary correspondence and administrative purposes only during the formation stage, but it is not permissible as the sole registered office, and it is not acceptable for substantive commercial substance claims. If no physical presence in Bermuda is established, the entity risks being treated as 'tax resident' elsewhere, and may fail Bermuda economic substance comfort for pass-through classification.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

E-government filing portal

Bermuda has no income tax, VAT, or general corporate TIN; the ELP's statutory identity is its Certificate of Compliance issued by the Registrar of Companies. Step 1: Register the ELP under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 and obtain the Certificate of Compliance number (de facto entity identifier). Step 2: If the ELP employs staff, register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number before the first payment of remuneration. Step 3: As importer of IVD reagents, file a Customs Registration Application with the Bermuda Customs Department to obtain an Importer-of-Record code, and appoint a licensed customs broker. Step 4: If the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more, register under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and obtain a Corporate Income Tax reference; otherwise no income-tax registration is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone. A Bermuda ELP is fiscally transparent by default; wholesale trading profit from IVD reagents is allocated to partners and is not taxed at the entity level. Offshore-source trading income is outside Bermuda's territorial tax base, no withholding tax applies on distributions, and the ELP benefits from the statutory no-income-tax assurance regime subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 in-scope MNE rules.

Available election forms

U.S. check-the-box: the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to elect corporate classification (partnership is the default). Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023: a fiscally transparent constituent entity that is a hybrid may elect to be treated as a separate taxable entity for QDMTT and IIR computations. The ELP may also file an elective transparency notice with the Tax Commissioner to mitigate OECD hybrid mismatch outcomes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

22.25%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. All importers are required to obtain an Importer-of-Record code without minimum turnover. The residual general customs tariff rate in Bermuda is 22.25% ad valorem; clinical diagnostic ELISA reagents under HS 3822 are assessed at the residual rate unless a Bermuda Health Council import permit or other exemption applies to reduce or eliminate duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in VAT form because Bermuda has no VAT. Equivalent reliefs: (i) full customs duty drawback under the Customs Act 1949 where goods imported into Bermuda are re-exported within six months of import; and (ii) upstream UK VAT zero-rating where the ELP, as a non-UK customer, acquires goods that are exported directly from the UK to a destination outside the UK (VATA 1994 s.30(6)), subject to retention of export evidence. No Bermuda exchange-control statute restricts FX repatriation; the 180-day term is a group treasury standard.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic entry lodged via ASYCUDA World)
  • Original Bermuda C75 Import Entry with duty payment receipt (for drawback matching)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Bermuda or the UK
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank FX credit advice evidencing settlement from the foreign buyer

No mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime exists in Bermuda. Customs declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World (single-window EDIFACT/API), and commercial invoices must accompany entries but carry no state-issued fiscal stamp.

Under the Bermuda Customs Act 1949 ss.49-55, drawback is clawed back if goods are not exported within the statutory six-month period unless the Comptroller grants an extension; fraudulent drawback claims may result in forfeiture of goods and penalties of up to three times the duty or the value of the goods. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, inaccurate export-related records trigger a 20% understatement penalty plus late-filing penalties. In the GB group context, HMRC may audit the UK zero-rating evidence pack; failure to substantiate export entitles HMRC to reverse the 0% rate and assess VAT at 20%, with consequential input VAT clawbacks and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar. Current-account remittances (trade payments, dividends, interest, royalties) are freely permitted for entities holding a resident exchange-control licence. Capital-account transfers, including partner capital injections and non-resident profit distributions, are generally permitted where the ELP qualifies as non-resident, but formal BMA exchange-control approval or a letter of no objection may be required. For the ELP configured as a non-resident pass-through entity, USD-denominated IVD distribution income can be remitted to non-resident partners with limited practical friction; the BMA maintains a pragmatic, US-dollar-aligned convertibility regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda does not operate a statutory export-proceeds surrender regime with fixed conversion ratios or deadlines comparable to emerging-market FX controls. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the BMA may, by notice or licence condition, require a resident exporter to offer foreign currency proceeds to an authorized dealer within a prescribed period. In current BMA policy, no blanket percentage surrender ratio is imposed. Exporters and non-resident ELPs are expected to receive proceeds through a licensed Bermuda bank, preserve underlying trade documentation (invoices, bills of lading, airway bills, certificates of analysis), and settle in normal banking terms. A non-resident ELP may hold export proceeds offshore without mandatory conversion, subject to its exchange-control permission.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Exchange Control Regulations 1957 (as amended), and the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI (electronic money institution) accounts have low-to-medium viability as a primary treasury rail for a Bermuda ELP. A UK FCA-authorized EMI can provide auxiliary MT103-equivalent payment processing and foreign-exchange execution for inbound/outbound trade flows, but it cannot replace a Bermuda licensed bank for exchange-control approval, capital-escrow segregation, official account statements, or AML KYC attestation accepted by the Registrar of Companies and counterparties. Legal constraints: holding trade proceeds in a non-Bermuda EMI account may require exchange-control permission for a resident ELP; a non-resident ELP may use offshore EMIs but must ensure that the ultimate operational and compliance nexus remains demonstrable through the Bermuda licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions should be routed as SWIFT MT103 wires in USD or BMD to the ELP's primary account at a Bermuda licensed bank. The payment must be tagged in field 70 with a clear narrative such as 'Capital Contribution – ELP Partner Injection' or 'INVEST – EQUITY' and must not be labelled as trade settlement. A purpose code consistent with OECD/BPM6 guidance (e.g. 'Capital transfers – equity' or 'Direct investment') must be used. The receiving bank will verify the source of funds through the onboarding KYC file; the ELP should maintain a capital introduction register, signed partner resolutions, and the underlying exchange-control approval to avoid quarantine or adverse reporting.Outbound IVD reagent trade settlements should be executed over SWIFT MT103 for open-account supplier payments, and through a commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700/MT707) for higher-risk clinical or government tendered procurements. USD and BMD are treated as freely interchangeable in the jurisdiction. Trade disbursements should be supported by invoices, packing lists, and transport documents; Bermuda exchange-control permissions, if imposed, must be referenced in payment narratives. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general Bermuda income tax; 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (QDMTT) for constituent entities of in-scope multinational enterprise groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750m under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is generally tax-transparent.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Limited Partnership or any resident company.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda-specific transfer pricing documentation threshold exists in local currency. However, UK-parented multinational groups with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750m must maintain OECD-aligned Local File, Master File, and UK CbCR notification, with the UK threshold also expressed in GBP. Bermuda entities within such groups should retain the same documentation to support economic substance and QDMTT computations.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Requirements apply under the Economic Substance Act 2018 to relevant entities, including exempted partnerships, carrying on a relevant activity such as distribution and service centre activity. The ELP distributing diagnostic reagents must demonstrate in Bermuda: adequate physical presence, full-time employees, operational expenditure, local management and control, and the exercise of core income-generating activities. Failure to satisfy the substance test exposes the entity to financial penalties and information exchange to HMRC.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Abusive Avoidance

Statutory sanction: HMRC assessment of unpaid UK tax, interest, and penalties up to 100% of tax; potential criminal prosecution under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion; unlimited fines and forfeiture orders under Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (UK).

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner of the ELP may be personally liable if knowingly concerned in fraudulent tax evasion; they face civil restitution, confiscation orders, travel bans, and imprisonment up to seven years.
Shell Company Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Act penalties: up to BD$250,000 for serious non-compliance; compulsory registration strike-off; exchange of information to HMRC resulting in a UK CFC adjustment, diverted profits tax at 20–30%, and denial of treaty benefits.

Director personal liability: The general partner's directors and managers are exposed to personal disqualification, fines, and may be made subject to restriction orders preventing operation of any company in Bermuda or the United Kingdom.
AML and Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and AML/ATF Code: civil fines up to BD$250,000 per breach; imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; additional OFAC/UK OFSI fines and exclusion from Swift payments if sanctioned.

Director personal liability: Senior management, compliance officers, and the general partner's principals face personal criminal liability for conspiracy to launder, plus personal financial penalties, and may be prohibited from financial services participation in the UK and Bermuda.
Transfer Pricing and CbCR Misstatement

Statutory sanction: HMRC transfer pricing adjustment under TIOPA 2010 Part 4, diverted profits tax of 25%, late payment interest, and penalties for incorrect CbCR information up to GBP 5,000 per return plus daily penalties.

Director personal liability: The UK group's Senior Accounting Officer may be personally fined up to GBP 5,000 for each tax accounting failure; directors of the Bermuda ELP may be held jointly liable for fraudulent undervaluation or mispricing orchestrated through the partnership.
False Declarations and Fraudulent Economic Substance Returns

Statutory sanction: Bermuda fines up to BD$50,000 and imprisonment up to five years for making false or misleading statements; in a tax context, penalties up to 100% of the tax avoided plus rescission of exempted status.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced under Bermuda's Economic Substance Act and Tax Administration Act; directors, the general partner, and the individual culpable employees are jointly and severally liable for restitution, fines, travel restrictions, and imprisonment.
