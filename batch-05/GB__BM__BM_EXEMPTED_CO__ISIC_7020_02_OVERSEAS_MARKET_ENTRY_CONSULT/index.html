Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax and has no central income-tax authority. Competent authorities: (i) Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax, stamp duty) with online portal at www.taxcommissioner.gov.bm; (ii) Registrar of Companies (incorporation registry, Economic Substance Act filings, CRS/FATCA reporting) with portal at www.roc.gov.bm; (iii) Bermuda Customs Department (Ministry of Finance) for customs duty on the ASYCUDA World clearance platform. The UK HMRC exercises no direct tax jurisdiction over Bermuda as a separate fiscal territory.

E-government filing portal

No corporate TIN is issued because no income-tax regime exists. Standard procedure: (1) incorporate the Exempted Company with the Registrar of Companies to obtain a Company Number, which serves as the universal identifier for Economic Substance Declarations, CRS/FATCA registrations and all official filings; (2) if the company makes any remuneration payments (including to a director-employee), register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain an Employer Payroll Tax Account Number; (3) apply for a Tax Protection Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 to freeze any future direct-taxation exposure until 31 March 2035; (4) obtain a customs broker/declarant registration if importing physical goods into Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The Bermuda Exempted Company may irrevocably elect Tax Protection under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing that future legislation taxing profits, income, dividends or capital gains will not apply. Under the Economic Substance (Requirements) Act 2018, the company must file an annual Economic Substance Declaration and may elect its status as a non-relevant entity, a relevant entity conducting a relevant activity (pure equity holding company reduced return), or a dormant entity; no check-the-box election exists under Bermuda law, though a US parent may file federal Form 8832 to classify the entity as disregarded or partnership for US federal tax purposes, and UK managers must preserve offshore management-and-control to avoid UK residency.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax is levied in Bermuda. The only quasi-indirect charges are Customs Duty on imported tangible goods, employer-paid Payroll Tax on Bermuda-source remuneration, and Stamp Duty on limited listed instruments.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Payroll tax registration is mandatory for any employer whose annual remuneration subject to tax exceeds BMD 500,000 (banded employer rates approximately 4.75% to 23%); customs duty applies to all imports regardless of value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - zero-rating is a VAT mechanism that does not exist in Bermuda. Cross-border market-entry consultancy services are inherently outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax; no zero-rating claim, VAT refund application or export margin scheme filing is required.

  • N/A - no VAT export declaration; standard substantiation records: signed client engagement agreement and statement of work; commercial invoice issued and settled via Wise B2B or bank wire; bank statements evidencing inward remittance of fees; board minutes and management accounts evidencing offshore management-and-control; Economic Substance Declaration records retained for six years.

N/A - Bermuda has no real-time invoicing, fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing or digital stamping mandate. Imported goods are cleared through the Bermuda Customs Clearance System on ASYCUDA World; business records, invoices, contracts and bank statements for consultancy services must be retained in Bermuda (or with the resident agent) for at least six years to support economic substance and EUTPA compliance.

No VAT clawback is applicable. Penalty exposure arises on adjacent obligations: failure to file an Economic Substance Declaration attracts a fine of up to BMD 100,000 plus BMD 10,000 per day of continued default and criminal sanction for knowingly false declarations; payroll tax late payment attracts a 10% per month penalty plus interest; customs duty evasion attracts a civil penalty of up to treble the duty and forfeiture of goods. Bermuda maintains no exchange controls, and the Bermudian dollar is pegged at par to the USD, so no FX repatriation period or clawback is imposed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BMD is pegged to the USD at a fixed 1:1 ratio under a quasi-currency-board arrangement backed by BMA reserve assets. For a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as 'non-resident' (a 'permitted' entity under the Exchange Control Act 1972), current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible: the entity may freely hold and operate foreign-currency accounts (USD, GBP, EUR), receive equity or subordinated-debt injections, and remit dividends, loan repayments, capital, and consulting fees offshore without BMA prior approval. Profits remittance is unencumbered by withholding or remittance taxes because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no withholding tax. The only ongoing hurdles are procedural: (i) maintaining the non-resident designation through the annual BMA exchange-control declaration; (ii) routing all BMD-denominated settlements through an authorized dealer; and (iii) preserving a clean economic-substance position by ensuring no 'relevant activity' is conducted inside Bermuda, which is satisfied because foreign market-entry consultancy is not a Bermuda relevant activity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and subsidiary Exchange Control Regulations, resident-designated entities must surrender foreign-currency export or service-fee proceeds to an authorized dealer within 30 days of receipt and are subject to prescribed conversion ratios (generally 100% conversion into BMD unless specific permission is granted). A Bermuda Exempted Company holding non-resident designation is exempt from mandatory surrender and from conversion-ratio obligations; consulting fee receivables and other export proceeds may be retained in any freely convertible currency. This exemption must be evidenced by the BMA non-resident designation letter filed with the corporate bank account. Caution: if the company's central management and control are exercised from Bermuda, reclassification as a resident entity would re-impose the 30-day surrender obligation and trigger an otherwise avoidable exchange-control approval burden.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the central bank and foreign-exchange administrator under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972; the BMA supervises authorized dealers (licensed Bermuda banks), administers exchange control for the Bermudian Dollar (BMD), and oversees correspondent banking and cross-border clearing compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High operational viability as an ancillary disbursement rail: Wise B2B, the designated target rail, and comparable UK/EEA EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Currencycloud) can efficiently handle low-value multi-currency supplier payments, mid-market FX conversion, and concentration-bank relief for a Low_Risk_Standard, unlicensed market-entry consultancy. Legal constraints: (i) EMIs are not licensed deposit takers in Bermuda and cannot open BMD-denominated accounts or serve as the primary capital-escrow holder; (ii) incoming capital injections must never be routed initially through an EMI because bank-wire source-of-funds traceability would be diluted — EMIs may only be used downstream for operational float; (iii) all EMI onboarding remains subject to the EMI's own UK/EU KYC, sanctions screening, and UBO verification; and (iv) EMIs are unsuitable for client-money segregation or any regulated payment-service activity beyond the consultancy's own treasury flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing uses a SWIFT MT103 from the UK parent bank to the Bermuda operating or escrow account in USD or GBP, with 'No Charge to Beneficiary' instructions so the credited amount matches the registered share consideration. Protocol controls: (i) the beneficiary account name must exactly match the certificate of incorporation and the bank's KYC records; (ii) the wire narrative must state 'Bermuda Exempted Company - subscription for ordinary shares' or 'Shareholder loan - ISIC 7020 overseas market-entry consultancy' and reference the supporting board resolution; (iii) OFAC, HMT, and BMA sanctions screening is performed by the receiving authorized dealer; (iv) no BMA pre-approval is required for non-resident-designated inflows, but the bank may apply a 24-48 hour post-receipt verification hold until provenance documents are lodged; (v) for amounts above USD 25,000 equivalent, the source-of-funds letter, board resolution, and updated share register must be delivered within five business days; and (vi) originator cover payments (MT202) that obscure the remitter must be avoided because Bermuda banks will reject them under correspondent-banking best practice.Primary clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 corporate-to-corporate payments for subcontractor, licensing, and advisory fees, cleared via USD (CHIPS/Fedwire), GBP (CHAPS), and EUR (TARGET2) using ISO 20022 purpose codes — COMM for consulting fees, INTC for intercompany reimbursements, and ADVA for agency advances; (2) Wise B2B as the designated low-value disbursement rail for supplier payments under USD 10,000 per leg, providing mid-market FX conversion and avoiding correspondent-chain fees, subject to maintenance of a beneficiary KYC record for each payee; and (3) commercial letters of credit (SWIFT MT700 series) permissible only where the consultancy contractually acts as a buying agent and the underlying purchase order is attached, with issuance through Butterfield or HSBC requiring the signed trade contract and the client's credit line authorization. Execution controls: same-day Atlantic-time clearing requires instruction before 14:00; BMD-denominated disbursements convert at the authorized-dealer rate anchored to the 1:1 USD peg; the bank retains a copy of each underlying invoice or service agreement to evidence business purpose; and all outbound remittances remain subject to a five-business-day post-transaction monitoring window.Deliverable FX forwards: BMD/USD (1:1 peg-protective cover), GBP/USD, and EUR/USD forwards transacted under ISDA 2002 or bank master FX terms with HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield; no BMA authorization is required for a non-resident-designated exempted company.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs): permitted for restricted or illiquid-currency fee receivables (e.g., offshore CNY, INR, BRL) to hedge anticipated consulting fee income without requiring BMD/USD settlement; counterparty credit limits apply under the bank's derivatives policy.Cross-currency swaps: GBP/USD and EUR/USD principal-and-interest swaps matching the UK parent's intercompany debt with the Bermuda entity's expected revenue currency; the swap is documented as a treasury instrument ancillary to the shareholder loan and must not create a speculative open position.FX swaps and vanilla FX options: permissible as short-term liquidity instruments (spot-forward combinations) and protective puts or calls with BMA-authorized counterparties, subject to the entity's non-resident designation and a board-approved treasury policy limiting net open speculative exposure to zero.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Bermuda has no corporate income tax and no statutory transfer pricing documentation rules. For UK-controlled entities, the parent may need to satisfy UK transfer pricing and CFC legislation; OECD TP principles should be maintained in parallel where intra-group transactions exist.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Amendment Regulations 2018 require tax-exempted companies carrying on 'relevant activities' to maintain genuine economic substance in Bermuda proportionate to their activities. Since the subject company's primary business (ISIC 7020 foreign market entry consultancy) is not a relevant activity, ESR does not automatically apply. However, if the entity evolves into headquarters management, IP exploitation, or distribution, full ESR compliance becomes mandatory, including local management, employees, and expenditure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Foreign-Country Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: UK Corporation Tax at 25% of worldwide profits (19% for small profits rates), plus penalties of up to 100% of the tax underpaid under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007, daily penalties of £100 for failure to file, and criminal prosecution for fraud or tax evasion under the UK Finance Act 2020.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal liability where they knowingly facilitate tax evasion; HMRC may impose civil restitution, obtain a travel ban, freeze personal assets, and apply for imprisonment of up to seven years under the Criminal Finances Act 2017.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Economic Substance Amendment Regulations 2018, failure to satisfy substance requirements for relevant activities attracts penalties of BDA $100,000 per year, BDA $500 per day of non-compliance, automatic exchange of information with HMRC under the TIEA, and potential strike-off from the Bermuda Companies Registry.

Director personal liability: Directors may be fined up to BDA $10,000 each and imprisoned for up to six months for causing or permitting the company to make false substance declarations; asset forfeiture and travel restrictions may be imposed pending civil proceedings.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Offences attract a maximum fine of BDA $250,000 on the company; daily default fines of BDA $1,000 apply. The company may be stopped from filing, struck off, and its assets frozen by court order.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers face civil restitution, disqualification as directors, travel bans, and imprisonment for up to two years for wilful default under the Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Act 1997.
Non-Filing of Annual Return / Striking-Off

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike the company off the register after two consecutive years of default; all assets owned by the company vest in the Crown (Bermuda Government) upon dissolution, and the company loses its legal capacity to continue business.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's debts incurred after the dissolution date, and may be subject to civil proceedings for restitution and a ban from acting as a director of any Bermuda company for up to five years.
