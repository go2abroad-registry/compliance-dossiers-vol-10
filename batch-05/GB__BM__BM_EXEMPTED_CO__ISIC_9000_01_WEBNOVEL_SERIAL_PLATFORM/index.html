Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC) under the Ministry of Finance, operating via the Bermuda e-Tax portal (etax.gov.bm); entity incorporation and tax-exempt certificate issued through the Bermuda Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

Foreign owners incorporate a Bermuda Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981 through the Registrar of Companies, obtaining a unique Bermuda Company Registration Number (BCRN). The company then files for a Certificate of Exemption under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 with the Minister of Finance, which guarantees no income tax, capital gains tax, dividend withholding tax, or wealth tax until at least 2035 (extendable to 2050 under certain conditions). No separate VAT/GST registration exists because Bermuda has no broad-based indirect tax; a payroll tax registration may be required if the entity has Bermuda-based employees, but the online webnovel platform with no physical Bermuda payroll can remain administratively passive.

Available election forms

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Bermuda. The jurisdiction levies no VAT/GST on digital services, and therefore no export zero-rating mechanism exists. The webnovel platform's global subscription revenue is outside the scope of any Bermudian indirect tax; consequently, no refund claim mechanism is available or necessary for input VAT.

  • No statutory requirement for VAT export documentation; if physical exports were ever introduced in Bermuda, the standard bundle would include a Bermudian Customs Export Declaration, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading/Airway Bill, and proof of foreign exchange remittance.
  • For the current digital service line, the banker's remittance record for Apple IAP/Google Play Bathing settlements and the platform subscription contracts constitute the primary export substantiation for home-jurisdiction withholding tax credit purposes.
  • A functional economic substance report covering IP ownership, development, and licensing arrangements should be maintained to support the real economic character of Bermuda income in any foreign CFC challenge.

No real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate applies in Bermuda for business-to-consumer digital services. Invoicing from the Bermuda entity to global end-users is governed by contractual and accounting norms; no Bermudian platform clearance or state tax invoice validation is required.

There is no Bermudian VAT zero-rating clawback because no VAT system exists. General anti-avoidance concerns are limited to false customs declarations under the Customs Act 1962 and false representations to the Registrar of Companies, which may attract fines and/or imprisonment. Additionally, if the Bermuda exempted company is taken to be a UK‑resident company by Her Majesty's Revenue & Customs (HMRC) due to central management and control being exercised in the UK, the UK corporate tax regime (25% headline rate effective April 2024) could retrospectively apply with full penalties and interest; anti-hybrid rules may also deny UK deductions for royalty payments if the Bermudian payee is treated as tax-transparent or non-taxable in Bermuda. Proper board minutes, separate UK/Bermuda substance, and transfer pricing documentation therefore remain critical.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar is convertible at the fixed official rate of 1 BMD = 1 USD. For exchange control purposes, a Bermuda Exempted Company is normally designated as non-resident, enabling it to retain, borrow, and remit foreign currency (primarily USD) without exchange control approval. Current account remittances, including webnovel subscription revenue, digital advertising receipts, and royalty/licensing payments, are treated as freely transmissible. Capital account transactions, such as injected share capital, shareholder loans, and liquidation distributions, are permitted for non-resident designated Exempted Companies; where resident designation applies, specific BMA consent may be required. Profit remittance to foreign shareholders is practically unrestricted, and no Bermuda withholding tax applies to dividends, royalties, or interest paid by an Exempted Company holding a valid Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion obligation on a non-resident designated Bermudian Exempted Company. Digital content export proceeds may be collected in USD and held in foreign-currency accounts or offshore. For any entity treated as exchange-control resident, export receipts must be collected within 180 days of the underlying export and, absent a BMA retention-currency approval, converted into Bermuda dollars through an authorized dealer. Applicable mandatory conversion ratio: 0% for non-resident designated Exempted Companies; up to 100% for resident exchange-control entities without retention approval.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, and as the issuer and supervisor of Bermuda's financial market infrastructure.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Conditionally feasible at a B+ rating for BMA-regulated fintech and EMI-style payment service providers. An offshore digital EMI without a Bermuda licence cannot directly hold settlement accounts at BMA-authorised banks. Bermuda legal constraints require a licensed local money service business or payment service provider sponsor, full AML/CFT onboarding under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018, and demonstrable safeguarding of client funds. Cryptocurrency or stablecoin-based EMIs face heightened compliance because digital tokens are not legal tender in Bermuda and are treated as non-fiat assets requiring additional source-of-funds and travel-rule controls.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Local File or Master File obligation exists under Bermuda general law. For in-scope MNE groups, Country-by-Country reporting obligations arise under the CIT Act 2023 when group consolidated revenue reaches or exceeds €750m (equivalent BMD 750m, pegged 1:1 to USD); contemporaneous TP documentation is otherwise mandated by the Economic Substance regime for IP holding and distribution/support-service entities.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019 applies to exempted companies carrying on 'relevant activities'. A web-novel serial licensing/IP holding vehicle is a high-sensitivity relevant activity requiring substantial local business presence, proportionate staffing and expenditure, and senior management located in Bermuda. The ESR return is assessed by the Registrar and a failure to satisfy economic substance results in financial penalties, corrective directions, and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / CIT Act Fraud

Statutory sanction: For in-scope entities: penalty up to BMD 250,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 10 years; underpaid tax is enforceable with interest at 3% per annum, and the corporate veil may be lifted by the Supreme Court of Bermuda.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who signed the false return are personally liable to civil restitution for the shortfall; courts may impose a travel ban, 5-year directorship disqualification, and up to 10 years' imprisonment for fraudulent evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Defect

Statutory sanction: First ESR default: BMD 10,000; repeated default: BMD 100,000; continued failure: BMD 250,000 and mandatory corrective orders; the Registrar may revoke the exempted status and strike the company off without a court process.

Director personal liability: Directors owe a statutory duty of compliance; knowingly allowing the shell character of the entity exposes the director to joint and several liability for penalties and a 5-year ban on participating in the management of a Bermuda company.
AML / Money Laundering & Counter-Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2008 and the AML/ATF Regime: on conviction on indictment, imprisonment for up to 14 years, an unlimited fine, confiscation of the proceeds, and a travel prohibition order against the beneficial owner.

Director personal liability: Directors, the resident representative, and the compliance officer can be criminally charged in their personal capacity; the court may waive corporate limited liability and order personal asset confiscation or wrap-up costs of the platform.
Non-Filing & Recurrent Registrar Default

Statutory sanction: The Registrar strikes the company off the register; the company's assets escheat to the Crown in right of Bermuda; the entity loses the right to enforce contracts, with no continuing capacity to hold the web-novel IP or payment merchant accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's debts incurred after the striking-off; individual fine exposure is up to BMD 10,000 per director, with a further 3-year disqualification from holding a management position in any Bermuda company.
