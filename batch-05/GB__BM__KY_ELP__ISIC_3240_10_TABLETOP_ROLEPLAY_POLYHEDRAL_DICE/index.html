VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda Customs Duty on Imports (there is no VAT, GST, or sales tax in Bermuda); Foreign Currency Purchase Tax (FCPT) at the standard rate of 4% applies to the purchase of foreign currency for settling import payables unless an exemption is granted; payroll tax applies only to Bermuda-resident employee compensation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Customs registration (ASYCUDA World importer code) is mandatory for every import without regard to turnover. Payroll-tax registration applies upon engagement of the first Bermuda-resident employee. FCPT is administered at the bank/authorized-dealer level rather than through a statutory registration of the ELP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as an input-credit zero-rating mechanism because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST; exports from Bermuda are outside the scope of Bermuda customs duty and generate no Bermudian tax liability or refund claim. To secure this treatment for cast resin dice, the ELP must (i) physically export through the ASYCUDA World export-entry process, (ii) retain shipping and delivery evidence proving arrival in the destination economy, and (iii) route all sales proceeds into the ELP's designated foreign-currency account. If selling into the UK, UK import VAT and UK VAT registration become mandatory once distance sales of consignments not exceeding GBP 135 exceed GBP 10,000 per year, or if the ELP holds inventory in the UK.

Bermuda Customs operates the ASYCUDA World electronic single window, requiring real-time electronic cargo declarations, duty computation, risk-based selectivity, and clearance through integrated broker-led filing. There is no statutory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate for commercial sales invoices because no VAT/GST administration exists; export entries are filed electronically and exit dutiable scope without levy.

Bermuda Customs may conduct post-clearance audits within a three-year review window; false declarations or misdescription of exports attract forfeiture of goods, fines of up to BMD 25,000, and evasion penalties of up to treble the duty payable under the Customs Act 1971. Because the ELP pays no Bermuda profit tax, there is no fiscal clawback of export tax benefits. If the ELP instead claims UK VAT zero-rating as a deemed exporter, insufficient evidence can trigger HMRC assessments, penalties up to 100% of the VAT under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007, interest, and criminal prosecution under section 72 VATA 1994 for fraudulent claims; records must be retained for at least six years. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 and is fully convertible for both current account and capital account transactions. There is no statutory exchange control regime, no central bank approval for profit remittance, and no required local reinvestment. Dividends, partnership distributions and capital repatriations from an Exempted Limited Partnership may be remitted freely to any non-resident partner, subject only to standard AML/KYC verification and compliance with Bermuda's economic substance filing expectations. The absence of capital controls makes Bermuda an efficient jurisdiction for cross-border treasury operations of a low-risk tabletop gaming accessories business.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no surrender obligations, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no statutory timeline for export proceeds. All export receivables, including payments received through crowdfunding platforms, payment gateways and commercial trade settlements, may be retained in any convertible currency without requiring repatriation or central bank approval. The only applicable discipline is corporate record-keeping and accurate financial reporting consistent with the partnership agreement and Bermuda tax monitoring requirements.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — issuing authority for the Bermuda dollar and principal financial regulator under the Currency Act 1972 and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections should be routed via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for correspondent tiering) to the ELP's Bermuda bank account. The payment narrative must state 'Capital Contribution to Exempted Limited Partnership' and reference the registered partnership agreement and relevant capital schedule. The receiving bank will require the ELP certificate, identity of all partners, and a source-of-funds questionnaire. BMD/USD accounts use a US correspondent bank; no IBAN is required. For crowdfunding proceeds, Stripe and PayPal are permitted as upstream aggregators, followed by periodic settlement sweeps to the Bermuda treasury account using standard payout rails with automatic reconciliation.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Moderately viable. Foreign unlicensed digital EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex or Revolut may be used as technical payment initiators and settlement processors for non-deposit-taking treasury flows, but they are not permitted to operate as deposit-taking institutions in Bermuda without a BMA licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. For the target business, using a licensed Bermuda commercial bank as the primary treasury anchor while layering a foreign EMI for low-value Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal settlement proceeds is defensible, provided the EMI holds a valid license in its home jurisdiction, maintains segregated client accounts, and does not market deposit facilities in Bermuda.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no VAT and no withholding tax. The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is a transparent pass-through entity; profits are taxable only in the hands of the partners. A UK-resident corporate partner is subject to UK corporation tax at up to 25% on its distributive share of the ELP's trading or property income.
