Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Hamilton. Online portal: www.gov.bm (e-Tax services via MyBermuda).

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not operate a general income tax or VAT registration system. A Bermuda Tax Reference (BTR) number is issued only when an entity registers as an employer for payroll tax (within 14 days of first local hire). To import/export goods, the entity must register with the Bermuda Customs Department for a Customs Business Number (CBN). Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, a foreign-owned multinational enterprise group with EUR 750M+ annual revenue must register for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT) and obtain a CIT Reference Number; otherwise, no tax ID is required for the ELP itself.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; territorial scope (Bermuda does not tax income derived outside Bermuda); no entity-level income tax for Ordinary or Exempted Limited Partnerships

Available election forms

1) Under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, a partnership may elect to be treated as a CIT taxable entity instead of look-through. 2) For foreign tax purposes, an ELP may make a US check-the-box election on Form 8832 to be taxed as a corporation or partnership. 3) Bermuda permits election for separate legal personality (subject to partnership agreement).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST or sales/use tax. Bermuda imposes customs import duties and limited service taxes (e.g., 10% hotel occupancy tax). Exports are not subject to any Bermuda indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – no VAT/GST registration threshold. Any person engaging in import/export must obtain a Customs Business Number (CBN) regardless of turnover; customs duty applies to imported goods.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda has no VAT/GST and does not impose export taxes. Goods and services destined for foreign markets are outside the scope of Bermuda's indirect taxation; therefore, no zero-rating claim or refund mechanism exists.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs Form C60 for controlled exports)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (only where invoicing is routed through Bermuda financial institutions; not for Bermuda tax purposes)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. Bermuda Customs uses the ACROSS single-entry system; commercial invoices, packing lists, and bills of lading must be submitted electronically for import/export declarations.

As there is no VAT, there are no zero-rating clawback penalties. However, false export declarations or customs documentation violations are penalized under the Customs Act 1965 (up to the value of goods, forfeiture, or imprisonment). The CIT Act 2023 imposes penalties on in-scope MNEs for tax avoidance transactions (up to 100% of underpaid CIT), but no indirect-tax export fraud regime exists.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD) and is fully convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions. Bermuda operates a liberalised exchange-control regime under the Exchange Control Act 1972, administered by the BMA, with standing general permissions allowing unrestricted inward foreign direct investment, profit remittance, dividend distributions and capital repatriation by non-resident Exempted Limited Partnerships (ELPs). The ELP may invoice, settle and retain proceeds in USD, EUR or GBP without BMA approval. Profit remittance is subject only to standard audited-account substantiation and the protections of the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; no economic-substance or balance-of-payments justification can be invoked to defer or block dividend distributions to the UK/European limited partners. The practical constraint is not convertibility but bank-side documentary proof of source of funds and beneficial ownership of the ventilator-manufacturing ELP.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation is imposed on Bermuda ELPs. There is no BMA mandate to convert export receipts into BMD, no fixed repatriation timeline, no minimum conversion ratio and no requirement to hold export sale proceeds onshore. The ELP may freely hold export collections in USD, EUR or GBP either in Bermuda or at offshore correspondent accounts. Authorised dealers (Bermuda licensed banks) retain monitoring duties, but the BMA does not impose invoice-level surrender or reporting timelines on non-resident ELPs engaged in ISIC 3250 exports.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for a Bermuda-domiciled fintech/EMI as the primary settlement bank; Medium-to-High for an FCA-authorised UK Electronic Money Institution (EMI) as a collection-only rail for EU/UK government tender proceeds. Bermuda's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 creates a digital-asset business licence, not an e-money/clearing institution licence, so a Bermuda-licensed fintech cannot legally execute SWIFT MT103 or act as a reimbursing bank for documentary letters of credit. A UK FCA EMI (regulated under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011) may be used for GBP/EUR receivables collection with strict safeguarding rules, but it must not be used for the final leg of LC discounting or for holding escrowed equity capital, which requires a full commercial-bank counterparty in Bermuda.
