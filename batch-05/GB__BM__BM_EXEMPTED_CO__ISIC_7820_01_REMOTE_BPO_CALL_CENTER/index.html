Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (payroll tax, land and stamp duties, and corporate income tax/DMTT administration) together with the Bermuda Customs and Border Control Service for customs duty; the Registrar of Companies, Companies and Insolvency Division, for incorporation, annual returns and Economic Substance Requirement declarations. Principal electronic portals: Bermuda Trade Facilitation Portal (customs single window) and Registry e-filing via gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda issues no entity-level income-tax TIN for exempted companies. Procedure: (1) incorporate through a resident agent and obtain the Company Registration Number; (2) apply for a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (exemption from income, corporate, capital-gains and withholding taxes until 31 March 2035); (3) if employing Bermuda-resident staff, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner within 14 days of first remuneration payment; (4) if clearing commercial imports, obtain a Customs Importer Code and duty bond via the Trade Facilitation Portal. MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more must additionally register for Bermuda's 15% Corporate Income Tax (domestic minimum top-up tax) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Bermuda Exempted Company is a limited-liability offshore vehicle holding a statutory tax exemption certificate; it is outside Bermuda income, corporation, capital-gains, dividend and withholding taxes. Payroll tax and customs duty apply only to in-scope local employment and imported goods; the OECD Pillar Two domestic minimum top-up tax may apply to constituent entities of large MNE groups notwithstanding the exemption.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist in Bermuda. The operative election is the Tax Exemption Certificate application under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, valid until 31 March 2035. An exempted company may also self-assess its activity as 'non-relevant' for Economic Substance Requirement purposes (a third-party BPO call centre is generally non-relevant unless it services foreign related parties), and may elect payroll-tax filing frequency subject to Tax Commissioner approval.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax. Bermuda's indirect taxation comprises customs duties under the Customs Tariff Act 2021 on imported goods (general ad valorem rate approximately 22.25%, with category-specific rates up to 75% on certain vehicles) and limited stamp duties on instruments. Cross-border exported services, including remote BPO call-centre services, are entirely outside the indirect tax base.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT registration threshold exists because no VAT is imposed on services. Every employer with any Bermuda remuneration liability must register for payroll tax without a de minimis threshold, and every commercial importer must file customs entries on all commercial imports with no numeric duty registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Automatic de facto zero-rating: because Bermuda does not levy VAT/GST, services supplied by a Bermuda exempted company to non-resident clients are automatically outside the indirect tax net; no application, prior ruling, or zero-rating claim is required. The company should retain evidence of client non-residence and offshore service delivery; for physical goods, duty is vacated on proof of actual export rather than via a VAT zero-rating mechanism.

  • Customs e-Export Entry transmitted through the Bermuda Trade Facilitation Portal (physical goods only)
  • Commercial Invoice / signed BPO services supply contract (service exports)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (physical goods only)
  • Packing List (physical goods only)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 / bank credit advice evidencing offshore settlement

No mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping applies to BPO service invoices. Import and export declarations are cleared electronically through the Bermuda Trade Facilitation Portal (BCAPS single window) with risk-based inspection; there is no government API-based invoice clearance or fiscal document authentication regime for services.

No VAT exists, so no input-tax credit clawback mechanism applies. False or fraudulent customs declarations attract forfeiture of goods, financial penalties of up to treble the duty evaded, and criminal sanctions for wilful evasion. Economic Substance Requirement violations carry administrative fines of up to BMD 250,000 per offence with possible strike-off; payroll-tax underreporting attracts interest at the prescribed rate and penalty surcharges, plus criminal liability for fraud. No statutory FX repatriation deadline exists in Bermuda; the 180-day benchmark reflects standard export treasury policy.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar under the BMA currency board mechanism, providing full and free convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. A Bermuda Exempted Company is treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes and may therefore maintain BMD, USD, GBP, and EUR accounts freely, convert funds at the pegged rate without central bank approval, and remit dividends, fees, and loan repayments to UK shareholders without Bermuda withholding tax. Current-account convertibility for BPO services export is unrestricted; capital-account items such as share allotments, intercompany shareholder loans, capital reductions, and liquidation distributions require no prior BMA consent or external-debt registration. The material frictions are documentary: the authorised dealer requires source-of-funds evidence, the BMA applies FATCA/CRS information exchange, and the entity must file an annual economic substance declaration under the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2020 to confirm that any relevant activity is not a passive or diversionary structure. For a typical remote BPO call-center vehicle, no material convertibility hurdle remains.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists in Bermuda. There is no mandatory conversion timeline, no minimum ratio of foreign currency to convert into BMD, and no obligation to repatriate proceeds to any designated escrow. A Bermuda Exempted Company may lawfully retain USD, GBP, EUR, and other foreign-currency receivables in multi-currency accounts, arrange conversions at the authorised dealer's 1:1 BMD/USD peg, and remit payments offshore without surrender. The only regulatory overlay is the authorised dealer's duty to report balance-of-payments and inward foreign-currency transaction data to the BMA for macro-statistical purposes, each payment requiring an underlying invoice or service contract in the compliance file. By contrast, the beneficiary-country surrender rules in the Philippines, India, or Bangladesh bind the subcontracted call-center exporter receiving the outbound disbursement; the Bermuda principal is not a mandated surrendering party.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and exercising exchange control functions delegated by the Minister of Finance under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973; day-to-day regulatory supervision is effected through licensed authorised dealers and banking institutions under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium under strict conditionality. Bermuda does not issue an EU-style electronic money institution (EMI) licence; a non-Bermudian fintech such as Wise, Airwallex, or Currencycloud can serve a Bermuda Exempted Company only through its foreign regulated entity (for example, a UK FCA-authorised EMI or a US MSB) and only if that fintech's country-acceptance policy admits Bermuda-incorporated offshore companies. For the target low-risk BPO receivables and payables profile, Wise B2B and wire-transfer rails are viable for outbound vendor disbursements when the Wise account is fed by daily sweeps from the primary BMA-supervised Bermuda bank account. The practical and regulatory constraint is that the fintech may not constitute the first or sole treasury anchor: the BMA's consolidated expectations, read together with the anti-shell-bank provisions of the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, require a real onshore Bermuda authorised dealer as the systemic liquidity spine. No separate Bermuda licensing is required for the foreign EMI because it is not deemed to be carrying on deposit-taking business in Bermuda in respect of the exempted company's cross-border payment flow, and the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 applicability is excluded absent digital-asset activity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for initial capital injection from the UK parent or third-party sponsor uses SWIFT MT103 or MT202 (where a correspondent intermediate sits) from the funding entity's UK bank to the Bermuda authorised dealer's USD or GBP account, with beneficiary equal to the Bermuda Exempted Company and purpose code classified as equity investment and investment fund shares under the IMF Balance of Payments (code C4) or, for shareholder loans, as intercompany direct investment lending (code F1). The SWIFT field 70 reference must cite the board resolution number and share allotment serial number. The receiving Bermuda bank will credit the account only on satisfaction of the onboarded KYC and source-of-funds checks; the funding entity's bank must complete its own sanctions and anti-money-laundering verification. There is no prior BMA foreign-investment approval or exchange-control licence for non-resident owned exempted companies. Verification package comprises certified board minutes authorising the allotment, an updated share register, shareholder resolution, registered agent Certificate of Incumbency, legal opinion confirming lawful issuance under the Companies Act 1981, and the registered agent's timely filing of the share allotment return with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies. Any requested BMD conversion occurs automatically at the 1:1 peg through the authorised dealer without surrender obligations.Outbound settlements to subcontracted call centers in the Philippines, India, or Bangladesh are executed through RMA-verified SWIFT MT103 messages using outgoing correspondent banking relationships at HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield; field 70 must reference the invoice number and contract identifier, and charges typically follow 'OUR' or 'SHA' to preserve settlement predictability. For high-frequency payroll-like disbursements, Wise B2B payouts are a permissible auxiliary rail, with the authorised dealer account sweeping funds into the Wise account daily to secure local-currency settlement in PHP, BDT, or INR while the legal payable obligation remains denominated in USD or GBP. Commercial letters of credit (MT700) are legally permissible but structurally unsuitable for services; they should be reserved for cases where the sub-supplier requires bank-undertaken payment security. All disbursed amounts are screened against BMA, OFAC, EU, and UN sanctions lists at initiation and on settlement, and records are retained under the BMA five-year AML/ATF record-keeping rule. No Bermuda withholding tax applies to service payments to non-residents, so the outbound rail does not attract any statutory tax friction beyond ordinary correspondent-bank fee and verification steps.Deliverable forward contracts on GBP/USD executed with a Bermuda authorised dealer under an ISDA or FX master agreement, used to hedge GBP-denominated revenue against USD-linked operational costs; BMD/USD hedging is unnecessary given the statutory 1:1 peg.Non-deliverable forward contracts (NDFs) on GBP/BMD where physical BMD delivery is administratively impractical, permitting hedge settlement in USD against the BMD peg.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) converting GBP-denominated intercompany shareholder loans into USD floating-rate cash flows referencing SOFR or SONIA to align interest exposure with USD BPO receivables.FX swaps combining spot and forward legs to smooth treasury liquidity for dividend and loan-repayment schedules without speculative position-taking.Plain vanilla FX options, specifically GBP/USD put options, acquired for dividend-repatriation protection; proprietary speculative trading is prohibited under the entity's treasury policy and BMA fit-and-proper expectations.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium under strict conditionality. Bermuda does not issue an EU-style electronic money institution (EMI) licence; a non-Bermudian fintech such as Wise, Airwallex, or Currencycloud can serve a Bermuda Exempted Company only through its foreign regulated entity (for example, a UK FCA-authorised EMI or a US MSB) and only if that fintech's country-acceptance policy admits Bermuda-incorporated offshore companies. For the target low-risk BPO receivables and payables profile, Wise B2B and wire-transfer rails are viable for outbound vendor disbursements when the Wise account is fed by daily sweeps from the primary BMA-supervised Bermuda bank account. The practical and regulatory constraint is that the fintech may not constitute the first or sole treasury anchor: the BMA's consolidated expectations, read together with the anti-shell-bank provisions of the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, require a real onshore Bermuda authorised dealer as the systemic liquidity spine. No separate Bermuda licensing is required for the foreign EMI because it is not deemed to be carrying on deposit-taking business in Bermuda in respect of the exempted company's cross-border payment flow, and the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 applicability is excluded absent digital-asset activity.
