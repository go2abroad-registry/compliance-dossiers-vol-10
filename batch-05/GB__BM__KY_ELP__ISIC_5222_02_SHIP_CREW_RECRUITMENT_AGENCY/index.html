Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance (Payroll Tax and Corporate Income Tax units); Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance (incorporation and Bermuda Business Registration Number); CIT e-Services portal hosted by the Bermuda Government; UK nexus (if the ELP creates a UK fixed establishment): HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) via gov.uk online services.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: File the Certificate of Limited Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 through the Bermuda Companies e-Registry to obtain the partnership registration code. Step 2: Obtain the Bermuda Business Registration Number (BRN) from the Registrar of Companies for banking, payroll and Customs purposes. Step 3: Register for Bermuda Payroll Tax with the Office of the Tax Commissioner if the ELP has Bermuda-resident employees or a Bermuda employment nexus; a payroll tax account number is issued. Step 4: If the ELP belongs to a large multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million and derives Bermuda-sourced profits, register for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT Act 2023) and obtain a Bermuda Income Tax Identification Number (BITIN) via the CIT e-portal within 30 days of becoming in-scope. Step 5: Non-resident partners require no Bermuda TIN for transparent income, but their residence-country TINs must be disclosed in the annual partnership information return. Step 6: If a UK fixed establishment is created, apply to HMRC for a UK VAT number when taxable turnover through that establishment reaches GBP 90,000; voluntary registration is available for input VAT recovery.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Bermuda exempted limited partnerships are fiscally transparent — no Bermuda entity-level income tax on exempt profits, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax on distributions. Bermuda applies a territorial payroll tax and, from 1 January 2025, a 15% Corporate Income Tax under CIT Act 2023 (QDMTT, IIR, UTPR) only to in-scope multinational groups; a non-resident-owned ELP without a Bermuda fixed establishment is outside the CIT scope and is effectively tax-exempt in Bermuda.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box regime. Available elections: (i) under CIT Act 2023, an in-scope ELP may elect safe-harbour and transitional de-minimis reliefs, file consolidated GloBE notifications, and elect to apply the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT); (ii) partnership transparency is the default and irrevocable, but a partner may elect branch/PE treatment in its home-jurisdiction return; (iii) for UK VAT, an overseas entity may elect voluntary registration effective from its first taxable supply in the UK (VATA 1994 Schedule 1, paragraph 2). No specific hybrid-mismatch election exists in Bermuda; UK partner-level hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) apply to UK-resident partners.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None in Bermuda (no VAT, GST, or sales/use tax on goods or services; Bermuda Customs applies import duties on physical goods only). UK VAT (Value Added Tax Act 1994) may apply only where the ELP has a UK fixed establishment and makes taxable supplies in the UK.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: N/A (no VAT registration regime). UK: GBP 90,000 taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period through a UK fixed establishment (HMRC threshold from 1 April 2024); voluntary registration is permitted below this threshold for input recovery.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: crew recruitment and vessel-crew management services are offshore services, and since Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, no zero-rating claim is required; revenue from non-Bermudian shipowners is outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation. UK (if the ELP has a UK fixed establishment): the supply of the services of a master or crew of a qualifying ship used for international commercial navigation is zero-rated under VATA 1994 Schedule 8 Group 8 Item 4(b); the agency must hold a written crewing contract with the shipowner or operations manager and evidence that the vessel qualifies (gross tonnage over 15 tonnes and used for commercial transport or business on the high seas). Input VAT attributable to zero-rated export supplies and crew-welfare costs may be recovered in the normal VAT return; a direct refund scheme is not required.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs Declaration or UK C88/SAD if any physical spares or crew equipment are moved through a UK export touchpoint)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for any physical crew relocation cargo or vessel supply components)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (SWIFT MT103/MT950 statement evidencing FX repatriation to the Bermuda ELP's multi-currency payroll account)
  • Signed Crew & Manning Contract between the ELP and the shipowner or manager specifying the insured vessel, trade route and crew list
  • Flag-state or P&I Club endorsement confirming qualifying-ship status for UK zero-rating claims

Bermuda: none. UK: no real-time fiscal clearance, central clearance API, or digital stamping; HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires all VAT-registered businesses to maintain digital records and submit VAT returns via HMRC-recognised API software; e-invoicing is commercially accepted but not government-cleared.

Bermuda: the Customs Act 1963 applies only to physical imports (forfeiture of goods plus penalties); payroll tax non-filing attracts interest of 8% per annum and fines up to USD 2,000 per offence; there is no export VAT clawback mechanism. UK: HMRC may deny zero-rating and issue retrospective assessments for up to 4 years (20 years for deliberate non-disclosure), with penalties under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24 up to 100% of the VAT lost, plus daily penalties and interest; knowingly making a false zero-rating claim is a criminal offence under VATA 1994 Section 72, punishable by imprisonment. Bermuda CIT (GloBE) non-compliance for in-scope groups attracts administrative penalties under the CIT Act 2023, including default fines up to USD 50,000 and tax-geared penalties of 10% of the understated tax.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 parity with the US dollar. Current account transactions, including trade payments for ISIC 5222 crew management services, are freely convertible for an Exempted Limited Partnership designated as non-resident. Capital account transactions are also permitted for non-resident ELPs, subject to BMA review via standard exchange control declarations. Profit and dividend remittance to UK/EU partners faces no statutory approval hurdle, but practical delays may arise if the BMA's annual return, business licence fee, or economic substance declaration is outstanding; documentary proof of invoicing and crew payroll disbursement must be retained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender or fixed conversion ratio for export proceeds. Receipts from crew recruitment and crew management fees may be retained in the original foreign currency in BMA-authorised foreign currency accounts. The Exchange Control Act 1972 requires only resident entities to file schedule-based declarations; a non-resident ELP is exempt from statutory surrender timelines, though all inward foreign currency receipts must be routed through an authorised bank and identifiable by purpose code.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the jurisdiction's central bank, monetary regulator, and foreign exchange control administrator

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Wise or Revolut are not licensed to take deposits or open Bermuda-dollar accounts within the meaning of the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. A fintech provider may engage only as a licensed Money Service Business under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018, and cannot replace a local BMA-authorised bank for capital escrow or crew payroll clearing. Any EMI wishing to operate in Bermuda must establish a physical licensed subsidiary and satisfy full prudential supervision by the BMA.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda does not prescribe a statutory transfer pricing threshold because no corporate income tax is levied. For economic substance purposes, any ELP engaged in a relevant activity (including crew management for international shipping) must maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation covering intercompany transactions, even when there is no tax lien. The documentation threshold is therefore BDA 0, meaning the obligation arises immediately upon entering the relevant activity; the local file should detail fees charged to foreign shipping principals and operating expenditures allocated to Bermuda.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Regulations apply to all ELPs carrying on 'relevant activities'. A ship crew recruitment agency will typically fall within the scope of 'Shipping' if it operates or manages ships for international traffic, otherwise it may be classified as a 'Service' or 'Headquarters' activity. The ELP must demonstrate in Bermuda: a physical office, board meeting functions, qualified full-time employees, and core income-generating activity physically undertaken in Bermuda. Outsourcing those functions to a non-resident affiliate does not satisfy the test and will trigger automatic exchange of information with the United Kingdom and other EU member states.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Payroll Tax)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 200% of the evaded tax, interest accrual at 0.5% per month, daily surcharge of BDA 500 after 30 days, and criminal liability with fines up to BDA 100,000.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: the general partner and any Bermuda-resident director are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the evaded payroll tax plus penalties. The Bermuda court may issue travel bans preventing the individuals from leaving the jurisdiction and may impose imprisonment up to 5 years upon conviction.
Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BDA 250,000 or 1% of the gross income derived from the relevant activity (whichever is greater); possible strike-off of the partnership after 30 days' notice; mandatory exchange of information with treasury authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who permit a sham substance arrangement face personal penalty orders of up to BDA 250,000 and disqualification as partnership administrators for 5 years. Travel restrictions may be imposed pending full payment of all fines and administrative costs.
AML / Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Civil financial penalties imposed by the Bermuda Monetary Authority up to BDA 1 million per contravention; suspension or cancellation of the ELP's AML compliance registration; referral to the financial intelligence unit for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners operating in Bermuda face personal fines, confiscation of proceeds, and imprisonment up to 14 years under the Proceeds of Crime Act and the Anti-Money Laundering and Financial Conduct Act. The courts allow separate travel bans to guarantee attendance at criminal hearings.
Shell Company / Sham Transaction

Statutory sanction: Strike-off of the ELP from the Bermuda Register, immediate cancellation of all permits, and publication of the strike-off notice in the Official Gazette; the partnership may not carry on any further business after such cancellation.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced on grounds of fraud: the general partner and any shadow directors are liable for all third-party claims emanating from the sham structure. Personal assets may be frozen, civil restitution ordered, and travel bans issued pending liquidation of the partnership's obligations.
