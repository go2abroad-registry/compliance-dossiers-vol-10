Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — For Bermuda tax purposes the ELP is a transparent pass-through vehicle: Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, income tax, or VAT on the partnership; the ELP is not a taxable entity and profits accrue directly to partners, who are taxed only in their respective tax-residence jurisdictions. Bermuda charges only an annual government fee, payroll tax and social insurance on Bermuda-resident employees, and economic substance reporting; the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax of 15% (Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025) applies solely to Bermuda constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups with consolidated global revenue of at least EUR 750 million — a standalone toy-manufacturing and e-commerce ELP is outside scope. For UK purposes the ELP is likewise transparent and has no UK tax presence unless it carries on a trade in the UK through a permanent establishment; the ELP is outside the UK VAT area except for specifically identified UK taxable supplies.

Available election forms

Bermuda: there is no US-style check-the-box election; ELPs are statutorily transparent and cannot elect to be taxed as corporations for Bermuda income tax purposes. Under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, in-scope groups may exercise elections provided under the Act and its technical guidance, including elections relating to transparent entities and group relief or loss-transfer; the ELP may also make optional elections in its partnership agreement to govern profit allocation, but these carry no Bermuda tax classification effect. United Kingdom: under CTA 2009 s. 59, a Bermuda ELP carrying on a trade in the UK through a permanent establishment may elect to be treated as a body corporate for corporation tax purposes; for UK VAT, a non-established person has no classification election but may apply for voluntary registration before the mandatory threshold, cannot use the Flat Rate Scheme until UK-established, and may not use the Cash Accounting or Annual Accounting schemes unless UK-established; the Tour Operators Margin Scheme is irrelevant to zero-rated goods sales.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK VAT: £85,000 (GBP) taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period for UK-established persons; for non-established (overseas) sellers the same £85,000 threshold applies to ordinary distance sales, but no seller threshold applies to goods consigned at the point of sale to UK consumers with a consignment value not exceeding £135, where the online marketplace is the deemed supplier from the first sale and must register and account for UK VAT. Bermuda: no VAT registration threshold exists because Bermuda has no VAT/GST; a Bermuda customs importer registry number is required for imports of goods into Bermuda subject to customs duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

United Kingdom: exports of goods from the UK to a place outside the UK (including Bermuda and the EU) are zero-rated under VATA 1994 s. 30 and VAT Notice 703 where the supply is made in the course of business to a customer outside the UK, the goods physically leave the UK, and the exporter holds valid commercial evidence of export within the statutory time limits (generally one month from the time of supply for direct exports and three months for exports effected by the purchaser or a third-party carrier). Services of an offshore-services character are governed by the place-of-supply rules: B2B services supplied to a business customer outside the UK are outside the scope of UK VAT (customer-location rule); B2C specified electronically supplied services to non-UK consumers are outside the scope of UK VAT or subject to destination-country VAT via the One Stop Shop / Import One Stop Shop. Bermuda: because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, there is no Bermuda export zero-rating or refund mechanism to claim; export sales from Bermuda to overseas markets attract no Bermuda indirect tax. A Bermuda ELP can only zero-rate UK export sales if it is UK VAT-registered; for marketplace-facilitated sales the marketplace determines and accounts for the correct VAT treatment.

  • Customs Export Declaration — UK CDS export entry (or SAD/EX1 where applicable), electronically submitted by the exporter or agent prior to export
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note evidencing physical departure of the goods from the UK, naming the exporter and consignee
  • Commercial Invoice and packing list stating the full description, 8-digit commodity code, quantity, value, destination and declared 0% VAT status
  • Purchase order / contract of sale evidencing the commercial transaction and overseas customer
  • Proof of payment (SWIFT credit advice, card settlement statement, or payment-rail evidence from Amazon Pay or Stripe) evidencing that export consideration has been or will be received
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank statement evidencing inward remittance of export proceeds where an FX-repatriation assertion is made — required only where the exporter operates under an exchange-control export-incentive regime; the Bermuda ELP is not subject to Bermuda exchange controls but the certificate is retained as good evidence practice

United Kingdom: no statutory mandate for real-time fiscal invoice clearance, government pre-clearance, or digital stamping (the UK does not operate an Italy/Spain-style e-invoicing clearance regime). VAT-registered businesses must comply with Making Tax Digital for VAT: all VAT records must be kept digitally and VAT returns (form VAT 100) must be submitted through MTD-compatible software using HMRC application programming interfaces; B2B Peppol e-invoicing is permitted on a voluntary basis. UK customs exports are declared electronically through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS). Bermuda: no e-invoicing, clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists; the Office of the Tax Commissioner does not operate any real-time transactional clearance system, and paper or standard PDF invoicing is unrestricted.

United Kingdom: export zero-rating is denied and the VAT is clawed back if the exporter cannot produce the full basket of export evidence within the HMRC time limits, or the goods do not in fact leave the UK; HMRC may assess the VAT in full (VATA 1994 Sch 11) plus interest. Deliberate or careless inaccuracies attract penalties under FA 2007 Sch 24 up to 100% (deliberate with concealment: 100%; deliberate without concealment: 70%; careless: 30%); failure to comply with Making Tax Digital record-keeping attracts penalties of up to £800 per return plus daily penalties; fraudulent evasion of VAT by false export declarations is a criminal offence under CEMA 1979, VATA 1994 s. 72 and the Fraud Act 2006, punishable by imprisonment up to 7 years, unlimited fines, and director disqualification. HMRC may also issue post-clearance demand notes for customs duty if commodity codes are misdeclared. Bermuda: no VAT zero-rating clawback regime exists as Bermuda levies no VAT; however, false customs declarations, undervaluation or misclassification of exported goods attract penalties, forfeiture and prosecution under the Customs Tariff Act 1995, and failure to comply with economic substance requirements attracts penalties up to US$100,000 per offence under the Economic Substance Act 2018, with daily default penalties and potential strike-off. All UK export evidence must be retained by the ELP for not less than 6 years from the date of supply.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) with full convertibility on both current and capital account for entities designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972. The target Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) qualifies for non-resident designation, enabling unrestricted holding of foreign-currency accounts, free inbound and outbound capital transfers, and unencumbered profit remittance without BMA prior approval, withholding, or stabilisation charges. Current-account transactions (trade settlements, service fees, and commissions) are freely convertible, while resident-designated entities require BMA permission for selected capital-account movements. Residual hurdles: the general partner must file the non-resident designation particulars, maintain a Bermuda registered office, and refrain from carrying on Bermuda-source trading activities; non-compliance revokes convertibility privileges and subjects distributions to Exchange Control scrutiny.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion or surrender ratio for export receipts. A non-resident designated ELP may retain 100% of export proceeds indefinitely in the original invoice currency (USD, GBP, EUR) and may deploy them directly toward raw-material procurement (beechwood, non-toxic finishes) and supplier settlements. Reporting obligations arise only for Bermuda-resident individuals or non-designated entities transacting in specified foreign currencies; these are administrative notifications to the BMA and are not applicable to the target ELP structure.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the de facto central bank and exchange-control authority under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Exchange Control Act 1972, and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. The Bank of England and HM Treasury retain sovereign oversight over Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory, but the BMA exercises delegated autonomy in currency issuance, monetary administration, and foreign-exchange approvals.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general corporate income tax; 15% Corporate Income Tax under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for Constituent Entities of Multinational Enterprise Groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more in at least two of the last four fiscal years.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory dividend withholding tax. Bermuda does not impose withholding tax on distributions, dividends, interest, royalties, or capital gains of an exempted partnership.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File / Master File is required for ELPs outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023. For in-scope MNE Groups, a Local File must be filed where the total value of controlled transactions with all associated enterprises exceeds BMD 250,000 in a fiscal year; a Master File is required where group revenue exceeds the EUR 750M threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 and associated Income Tax Guidance Notes require any Bermuda entity conducting a 'relevant activity' to demonstrate economic substance commensurate with the income generated. Relevant activities include banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, intellectual property holding, and distribution and service centres. Manufacturing of Montessori wooden blocks is not a prescribed relevant activity; however, if the ELP holds the brand IP and licenses intercompany production, it may be classified as an IP company and must demonstrably control the IP exploitation activities in Bermuda with adequate local staff, assets, and expenditure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Regime (ESR) Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of BMD 250,000 for first default; BMD 500,000 for second default; potential compulsory strike-off and forfeiture of the ELP's assets to the Crown. Third default results in mandatory winding up.

Director personal liability: Every general partner and any person acting as a director or manager of the ELP is jointly and severally liable for the penalty if they knowingly determine or permit a default. Persistent default triggers personal liability for tax and penalties, a travel ban issued by the Supreme Court, and up to 12 years' imprisonment for fraud-related offences.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML/ATF) Breach

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to BMD 1,000,000, confiscation of the ELP's entire property derived from the breach, and civil penalties of BMD 50,000 per un-filed beneficial ownership information. The entity may be prohibited from holding a corporate card or operating a bank account in Bermuda.

Director personal liability: General partners, directors, and shadow managers may be held personally liable for knowingly, recklessly or wilfully failing to prevent money laundering. Consequences include civil restitution orders, personal fines up to BMD 250,000, a travel ban, and imprisonment for up to 14 years. Corporate veil-piercing is automatic in cases of intentional concealment of beneficial ownership.
False Statements to the Tax Authority

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty equal to 200% of the tax understated, plus a fixed penalty of BMD 25,000. A criminal prosecution may result in a fine of BMD 100,000 and up to five years' imprisonment. Any unpaid CIT becomes a statutory first charge over the ELP's assets.

Director personal liability: Directors or general partners who sign or approve a false tax return are personally liable for the full underclaimed tax, penalties, and interest. The Bermuda Tax Administration may impose a debt recovery order against their personal assets, freeze their bank accounts, and preclude them from leaving Bermuda if there is evidence of intention to transfer assets abroad.
Shell Company Abuse and Criminal Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Revenue Authority may reclassify the ELP as a non-tax-transparent entity and impose 15% CIT on deemed Bermuda-source income; there is no statute of limitations for fraudulent evasion. Concurrently, the Registrar may strike off the ELP, and any distribution to a non-resident partner under a taxable transaction becomes voidable.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the ELP veil where the general partner uses the partnership as an alter ego and engages in a sham: orders include civil restitution of all diverted funds, personal criminal fines up to BMD 500,000, a travel ban, and imprisonment of up to 14 years. International assistance under the TIEA may trigger UK or US charges where the ultimate beneficial owner is resident there.
Transfer Pricing and Thin Capitalisation Misstatement

Statutory sanction: A transfer pricing adjustment assesses the difference at 15% CIT with interest at 8% per annum. Penalties for negligent or deliberate errors range from 20% to 200% of the tax adjustment. The ELP's acquisition of a beneficial concession or licence may be revoked.

Director personal liability: A general partner who knowingly participates in a transfer pricing manipulation becomes jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax, interest, and penalties. The Tax Authority may issue a charging order over the partner's personal property, impose a direction restricting their absences from Bermuda, and recommend criminal indictment for cheating the public revenue, carrying up to seven years' imprisonment.
