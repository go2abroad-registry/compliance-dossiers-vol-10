Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (portal: www.gov.bm; electronic filing through Bermuda Government tax and customs digital services). UK exposure, if any: HM Revenue & Customs (portal: www.gov.uk), noting that Bermuda is outside the UK VAT territory.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda imposes no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or VAT; therefore no general income-tax TIN or VAT ID is issued. A foreign owner forms a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 through a licensed Bermuda registered agent. The ELP obtains a Registrar of Companies entity number. If the ELP employs Bermuda-resident individuals, it must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for a Payroll Tax Account Number. If it imports or exports goods, it must register as importer/exporter with Bermuda Customs Department. A Bermudian VAT ID is not available. UK tax identifiers arise only where the ELP separately creates UK taxable nexus, in which case HMRC registration for VAT, PAYE, or UK corporation tax may be required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. Under Bermuda law, the ELP is outside Bermuda income-tax scope and is treated as transparent for any limited Bermuda fiscal measures such as payroll tax and customs duty. For U.S. federal tax purposes, a foreign eligible ELP with two or more partners defaults to partnership pass-through treatment unless an IRC Form 8832 check-the-box election to be taxed as an association is filed.

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic check-the-box election exists. A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership may apply for exempted undertaking tax protection under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, providing statutory assurance against future Bermuda taxes on income, profits, capital gains, and dividends until 31 March 2035. For foreign tax classification, the entity may make an IRC Form 8832 check-the-box election (partnership vs corporation) under U.S. law, and appropriate UK/OECD hybrid mismatch arrangement notifications or elections must be considered where payments flow between transparent and opaque classifications.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales tax in Bermuda. Bermuda operates mainly on customs duties, payroll tax, and other selective levies; imports are subject to Bermuda customs duty, but domestic sales and exports are not subject to VAT or GST.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold in Bermuda. Registration is event-based: an importer of record must be registered with Bermuda Customs Department before dutiable imports, and an employer, including a foreign-owned ELP with Bermuda-resident employees, must register for Bermuda payroll tax from the first employment date without any turnover exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so export zero-rating is not applicable: exported goods and offshore services sit outside the Bermuda indirect tax base. Where customs duty has been paid on goods imported into Bermuda and the goods are subsequently exported in the same condition or used in the manufacture of exported titanium orthopedic implants, a customs duty drawback or refund may be claimed under Bermuda Customs law upon satisfactory proof of export, compliance with prescribed conditions, and timely submission.

  • Customs outward declaration or export manifest filed with Bermuda Customs Department where export processing is required
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice describing the porous titanium orthopedic implant product, ISIC/NAICS scope, purchaser, and sale proceeds
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/letter-of-credit payment confirmation evidencing receipt of export sales consideration
  • Bermuda Customs duty drawback claim form, where applicable, referencing the original import entries and duty-paid status

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous controls, digital stamping, or government API e-invoicing mandate applies in Bermuda. Commercial invoices and customs declarations are submitted electronically through Bermuda Customs Department manifest and clearance procedures. No dedicated VAT-fiscal e-invoice clearance is required; standard recordkeeping under Bermuda customs, tax, AML, and economic substance rules remains compulsory.

Bermuda does not operate VAT export zero-rating, so VAT clawback risk is absent domestically. Fraudulent export declarations or false duty-drawback claims are subject to Bermuda customs and criminal enforcement: goods may be forfeited, duty relief or drawback may be recovered with interest, monetary fines may be imposed, and responsible partners or officers may face imprisonment. If the ELP establishes a separate UK VAT presence and claims UK export zero-rating, HMRC may assess unpaid VAT, impose penalties of up to 100% of the VAT evaded, and pursue criminal prosecution in cases of fraudulent conduct.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains a liberal convertibility regime for non-resident designated entities. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) whose general partner and all limited partners are non-resident can apply to the BMA for 'non-resident' status; once granted, the ELP is permitted to maintain accounts in any foreign currency (predominantly USD), execute free transfers to and from any jurisdiction, and repatriate capital, dividends, and profits without exchange control permission. Current-account transactions are fully convertible for all entities; capital-account operations (inward equity injections, intra-group loans, liquidation distributions) are unrestricted for non-resident ELPs but require Form EC/2 notifications or prior BMA consent for resident entities. Profit remittance hurdles are effectively nil for non-resident ELPs; no withholding tax is imposed on interest, royalties, or partnership distributions, and no capital gains tax applies. The only material compliance duty is filing an annual economic substance declaration under the Economic Substance Act 2018, although an ELP with a UK-tax-resident ultimate parent may need to evidence that its core income-generating activities occur in Bermuda or rely on the 'pure equity holding entity' exemption.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion requirement for export proceeds. A non-resident ELP may retain 100% of export receivables in USD-denominated offshore accounts without conversion to BMD. For a resident-designated entity, the BMA expects export proceeds to be credited within a reasonable period (customarily 180 days) to a Bermuda licensed bank account and may require conversion of BMD surpluses only to settle Bermudian tax or statutory liabilities, none of which apply in material form to an exempted partnership. Trade invoices for titanium orthopedic implants must be denominated in USD or GBP and supported by standard shipping documents (Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, commercial invoice, packing list) to evidence the export transaction for BMA audit purposes, but no conversion certificate or central bank approval is needed for non-resident entities.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, acting as the island's central bank and sole financial services regulator; exchange control administration is delegated to the BMA under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973. The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) and US coinage and notes circulate as legal tender alongside BMD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW-MEDIUM as sole treasury rail; HIGH as supplementary payment channel. Bermuda does not license electronic money institutions under a dedicated regime; the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 covers virtual-asset wallet and custodial services but does not authorize deposit-taking or e-money issuance. UK-authorized EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Business, Clear Junction) may lawfully provide GBP and EUR collection accounts, multi-currency IBANs, and SWIFT correspondent access for the UK parent entity, but they cannot provide Bermuda-regulated current-account banking for the ELP. To achieve an optimal treasury structure, use Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda as the primary USD settlement bank for the ELP, and integrate a UK EMI solely for SaaS receivables, trade commissions, and disbursement acceleration in GBP/EUR; a Bridge-to-Bank wiring protocol (EMI-to-bank SWIFT transfer) must be documented to satisfy anti-money-laundering inter-institution verification. Legal constraint: Section 5 of the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 prohibits unlicensed entities from taking deposits; EMIs cannot offer Bermuda-domiciled account numbers or hold Bermudian client funds in local jurisdiction.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: LOW-MEDIUM as sole treasury rail; HIGH as supplementary payment channel. Bermuda does not license electronic money institutions under a dedicated regime; the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 covers virtual-asset wallet and custodial services but does not authorize deposit-taking or e-money issuance.
