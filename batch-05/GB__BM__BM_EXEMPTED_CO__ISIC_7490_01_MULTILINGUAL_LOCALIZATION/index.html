Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner of Bermuda (Ministry of Finance), online portal: https://www.taxcomm.bm and https://www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda exempted company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 through the Registrar of Companies via the OneGov portal, obtaining a 'Certificate of Exemption'. No general income-tax TIN or VAT ID is issued because Bermuda imposes no income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT. If the company has employees, it must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax purposes. If the company belongs to a multinational enterprise group in scope of the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, it must register as a constituent entity for Bermuda CIT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or US-style entity classification election is available in Bermuda. No hybrid mismatch election exists. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, constituent entities of a large multinational group may elect to apply transitional relief and submit 'qualified refundable tax credit' elections, but this does not affect the ordinary exempted-company tax-exempt status for entities outside the Pillar Two scope.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT; no GST; no sales and use tax. Bermuda operates only import customs duties.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no VAT or GST registration regime exists in Bermuda.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Because Bermuda has no VAT/GST or sales tax, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim. Goods exported from Bermuda and offshore services supplied from Bermuda are outside any Bermuda consumption-tax charge.

  • No VAT zero-rating documentation is required
  • For customs and bank remittance purposes, the customary bundle includes: commercial invoice, Bermuda Customs declaration/entry, bill of lading or airway bill, packing list, and inward remittance certificate for export proceeds

Not applicable; Bermuda does not mandate electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, real-time API reporting, or digital stamping for indirect tax purposes.

No export zero-rating mechanism exists, so no VAT clawback liability can arise. Import-related customs duty evasion is prosecutable under the Bermuda Customs Act 1941, with penalties, forfeiture of goods, and criminal sanctions. No retrospective VAT adjustments apply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fixed exchange rate regime with the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. The BMA administers the Exchange Control Act 1972, but the territory maintains a highly liberal system: current and capital account transactions are freely convertible, and no prior BMA approval is required for inward remittances, dividend distributions, or the repatriation of share capital for a Bermuda Exempted Company. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal; the relevant compliance obligations are limited to maintaining proper corporate books, filing economic substance returns where applicable, and, for share transfers to non-resident persons, satisfying any residual BMA reporting condition under the blanket permissions granted to exempted companies. No currency restrictions apply to the collection of translation, dubbing, or localization fees from clients in any convertible currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio or timeline for export receipts. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, exporters enjoy a general permission to retain foreign currency earnings in any authorized bank without surrendering to the BMA. The Bermuda Exempted Company may therefore hold invoiced revenue in USD, EUR, GBP, or other convertible currencies and settle trade payables directly from those balances; the 1:1 BMD/USD peg ensures that USD deposits are treated as local currency equivalents for operational purposes.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility is rated High for using supervised offshore electronic money institutions (e.g., Wise B2B, Payoneer) as an operational payment rail for a Bermuda Exempted Company, provided the EMI holds an e-money licence in the UK or an EU member state and does not maintain a physical branch or solicit deposits within Bermuda. The BMA does not operate a standalone EMI licensing regime; payment services from or into Bermuda are restricted to licensed banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, or to entities registered under the Digital Assets Business Act 2018 for digital payment activities. Offshore EMIs are legally viable for third-party merchant collections and low-value disbursements where settlement occurs through correspondent banks, but they are not authorized to serve as statutory deposit accounts, hold client capital, or provide BMA-recognized treasury services. Enhanced due diligence is triggered when EMI concentration accounts are domiciled in higher-risk jurisdictions, though the target agency's Low_Risk_Standard classification materially reduces that friction.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the shareholder's or parent's bank to the Bermuda Exempted Company's USD or BMD account at a licensed Bermudian bank. Given the 1:1 BMD/USD peg, the beneficiary account is credited at par. The remittance narrative must clearly state the purpose, typically 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – [COMPANY NAME]', and must reference a bank-specific capital purpose code (e.g., 'CAP' or 'EQUI') where required by the receiving institution. If shares are issued against the injection, the bank will retain a copy of the BMA-verified capital certificate or a statutory declaration by a director confirming receipt of subscription monies; no separate exchange control approval is required for exempted companies under the blanket permission regime. For capital escrow structures, the funds may be held on a segregated interest-bearing account pending issuance of shares or drawdown of shareholder loans, with the bank's account mandate requiring dual director authorization for any release.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File arise only when the exempted company is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BD$810 million at the BD$1:US$1 peg), per the CIT Act 2023. Sub-threshold translation/localization companies have no statutory TP documentation requirement, but intercompany pricing for IP royalties, management fees, and localization throughput payments must be contemporaneously substantiated at arm's length.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 (as amended) requires every relevant entity carrying on a relevant activity to demonstrate economic substance in Bermuda. Translation, dubbing, and multilingual localization (ISIC 7490 / NAICS 541930) is not a listed relevant activity, so the entity files a nil ES Return unless it also performs headquarters, financing, leasing, or pure equity holding functions. In-scope CIT groups must additionally show real physical presence, qualified full-time employees, and core income-generating activity in Bermuda to support GloBE profit allocation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Reassessment with statutory interest; penalty of up to BD$250,000 for wilful false statements; criminal conviction and forfeiture of the 0% status; automatic inclusion of the entity in the MNE group's consolidated CIT return.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for fraudulent or negligent misstatements; courts may impose restitution orders, asset freezing, travel bans pending payment, and imprisonment of up to 3 years.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: ES Act 2018: fine up to BD$250,000 plus a daily default fine of BD$5,000; compulsion orders; strike-off and dissolution; revocation of the exempted company certificate.

Director personal liability: Officers face daily default penalties assessed personally; director disqualification for up to 5 years; civil restitution of fees and profits; travel bans while contempt is uncured.
AML and Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: Beneficial Ownership legislation: fine up to BD$25,000 plus a continuing daily fine of BD$1,000; Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 money-laundering offences: fines up to BD$1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 14 years; confiscation of proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly facilitate layering or sanctions evasion are subject to personal confiscation orders, disqualification orders, and custodial terms; mutual legal assistance may trigger extradition.
Corporate Restriction

Statutory sanction: Companies Act 1981: local contracts are void and unenforceable; penalty up to BD$5,000 plus a daily default fine of BD$2,000; mandatory disclosure to the Registrar; liability to compensate the local counterparty.

Director personal liability: Personal liability for counterparty losses; court-imposed restitution; prohibition from holding officer positions in any Bermuda company for a fixed term.
Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: General anti-abuse and misrepresentation rules permit the tax authority to recharacterise the arrangement with an increased tax liability and a penalty of up to 100% of the understated top-up tax; investor and consumer fraud statutes also apply.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines, disqualification under the Companies Act 1981, civil restitution to aggrieved parties, and extradition exposure under mutual legal assistance frameworks where cross-border fraud is proven.
