Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Government of Bermuda, Ministry of Finance — Office of the Tax Commissioner (administers Payroll Tax and the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023) and Bermuda Customs Department (administers customs duties). Corporate registration: Registrar of Companies, Department of Corporate Services, Hamilton. Official portal: www.gov.bm; customs declarations filed through the Bermuda Customs single window (SINGL/ASYCUDA World); payroll tax and CIT returns e-filed through the Tax Commissioner's portals. The UK is the sovereign power, but Bermuda is a fiscally autonomous, separate customs and VAT territory; HMRC exercises no direct tax administration over Bermuda.

E-government filing portal

(1) Register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 with the Registrar of Companies to obtain a registration number. (2) Apply to the Bermuda Monetary Authority for a Certificate of Compliance, confirming the ELP operates outside Bermuda and is exempt from Bermuda income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax and stamp duty. (3) If employing staff, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax and obtain the employer's tax account number; also register for Bermuda National Pension Scheme and health insurance obligations as applicable. (4) If handling dutiable third-party goods for the kitting/fulfilment activity, register an importer/exporter code with Bermuda Customs Department and obtain any licensed customs bonded warehouse approval needed for storage of unliquidated goods. (5) If the ELP is part of a multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, register for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax and obtain the CIT reference number via the Tax Commissioner's e-portal. (6) File the annual Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) declaration with the Registrar of Companies even where the ELP has minimal substance in Bermuda. No UK HMRC tax registration arises from a Bermuda-only presence, because Bermuda is outside the UK VAT area and the UK's direct tax net.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. A Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent by default; it bears no Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on distributions. Profits flow through to partners and are taxable only in their respective residence/exposure jurisdictions. The Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 can, however, deem certain in-scope multinational partnerships to be within the 15% Bermuda CIT net.

Available election forms

Bermuda law has no equivalent of the US check-the-box election; partnership pass-through treatment is the default statutory classification. US partners may nevertheless file IRS Form 8832 to elect the US federal classification of the ELP (default: partnership). UK-resident partners are subject to the UK hybrid and other mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) to prevent deduction/non-inclusion mismatches arising from a tax-transparent Bermuda ELP. Under the CIT Act 2023, in-scope groups may make joint designations/elections to treat the ELP as the CIT-paying entity or to designate a constituent entity as the responsible CIT payer, and transparent-entity treatment may be preserved through qualifying elections. The ELP may also elect to obtain a Certificate of Compliance from the Bermuda Monetary Authority, which crystallises its exempted status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / sales and use tax. Bermuda's indirect tax regime consists principally of customs import duties levied under Bermuda customs tariff legislation (ad valorem duties, specific duties on selected goods, fuel tax and certain tourism taxes), plus employer-paid payroll tax which is generally treated as a direct labour tax. Exports and re-exports are not subject to Bermuda indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Nil for VAT/GST because Bermuda imposes no such taxes. Customs registration applies to all persons importing or exporting dutiable goods regardless of transaction value; no de minimis threshold for trader registration exists. Payroll Tax registration is obligatory from the first BMD payroll payment made by a Bermuda employer. Under the CIT Act 2023, the relevant threshold for CIT in-scope status is membership of a multinational group with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 820 million) in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export 'zero-rating' in the VAT sense is not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT; goods physically located in Bermuda are outside the scope of UK VAT, and the customer accounts for import VAT in the destination EU/UK territory. The functional equivalent in Bermuda is customs duty relief on export/re-export: goods imported into a licensed customs bonded warehouse or inward-processing arrangement and physically exported within the statutory period (customarily 12 months) are relieved from import duty, as are transshipment cargo, ship and aircraft stores, and diplomatic goods. Kitting, labelling and other light assembly performed in bond do not invalidate relief so long as the goods are not released into domestic free circulation and the outward movement is customs-supervised.

  • Customs export/re-export declaration filed through the Bermuda Customs SINGL/ASYCUDA World single window
  • Original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (including house waybills) evidencing physical departure from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and packing list reconciled to the export declaration
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank settlement evidence where export consideration is payable
  • Bonded warehouse removal/transfer warrant where goods exit a licensed customs facility

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, API stamping or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda. Import and export declarations are submitted electronically through the Bermuda Customs single window (SINGL/ASYCUDA World); payroll tax and CIT returns are filed through the Office of the Tax Commissioner's portals. Standard commercial invoices are retained under general record-keeping rules for at least six years to support tax and customs audits.

Bermuda Customs and the Tax Commissioner may conduct retrospective audits of export relief claims. Diversion of bonded or export-destined goods into domestic consumption triggers immediate duty clawback, forfeiture of goods, and monetary penalties of up to BMD 25,000 per offence; fraudulent evasion carries criminal liability, with fines on conviction of up to three times the duty evaded and possible imprisonment. Missed statutory export deadlines cause automatic withdrawal of relief and assessment of duty previously deferred. Payroll Tax and CIT misstatements attract late-filing and late-payment interest and penalties up to 10% of the tax unpaid, with escalating recurring penalties for continued default; false economic substance filings may additionally lead to monetary penalties and strike-off of the ELP.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) and is fully convertible; no exchange control approval, quota, or registration is required for current-account payments. Capital-account inflows are freely permitted for OECD-resident contributors under the Exchange Control Act 1972 General Permission No. 1 (Capital Transactions by Exempted Partnerships), subject only to a post-transaction e-Declaration filed with the BMA for monetary statistics. Profit remittance is unrestricted: the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) may pay distributions and allocations to any non-resident limited partner free of all Bermuda and UK withholding taxes, provided the ELP maintains its active economic substance status and does not shift its central management and control (and thus UK tax residence) to the United Kingdom. The GBP leg (UK operational account) is fully convertible with zero capital controls; HMRC does not restrict cross-border capital movements. The only friction point is UK statistical reporting: cross-border payments exceeding GBP 10,000 must be reported on the Bank of England ITRS C1 form within five business days after month-end, which is a statistical, not a permission-based, requirement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Bermuda maintains no export-proceeds surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio under the Exchange Control Act 1972; all export receipts from fulfilment, kitting, and assembly services (ISIC 5210 / NAICS 493110) may be retained freely in BMD, USD, GBP, or any convertible currency at the ELP's discretion. The United Kingdom likewise imposes no surrender or conversion obligation on sterling receipts. The only compliance duty is statistical: export-related cross-border receipts must be reported on the Bank of England ITRS C1/C2 forms where the gross cumulative monthly value exceeds GBP 10,000; no repatriation or conversion is mandated.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - the de facto central monetary authority and foreign exchange control regulator of Bermuda, established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, administering the Exchange Control Act 1972 and supervising all Bermuda-licensed commercial banks; and the Bank of England (BoE) - the central bank of the United Kingdom, responsible for GBP monetary stability and UK clearing system oversight (CHAPS, Faster Payments Service, CREST), with the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) regulating electronic money institutions such as Airwallex under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High feasibility. Airwallex (UK) Limited is authorised and regulated by the FCA as an Electronic Money Institution (FRN 900461) and provides the Bermuda ELP with a segregated multi-currency virtual account (GBP/USD/EUR), enabling SWIFT MT103, SEPA Instant, UK Faster Payments, and US ACH Direct (NACHA CCD/CTX) rails in a single onboarding cycle. Legal constraints: (i) funds are held in a safeguarding account with an eligible UK bank (e.g., Barclays, HSBC UK, Lloyds) per EMR 2011 Regulation 20; (ii) the EMI relationship does not of itself create a UK permanent establishment or a Bermuda exchange-control licence obligation; (iii) the ELP must submit its Bermuda certificate of limited partnership, UBO declarations, and a certified economic substance filing to pass the EMI's enhanced due diligence. The applicable AML risk tier is Low_Risk_Standard, consistent with the statutory profile of ISIC 5210 (warehousing and storage).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda has no standalone transfer pricing documentation requirement for groups below the OECD threshold. Mandatory GloBE Information Return / Country-by-Country Report threshold is EUR 750 million (approximately BD$750 million) in annual consolidated group revenue. A local file and master file are not required for Bermuda resident entities, but contemporaneous evidence must be retained to substantiate economic substance and GloBE effective tax rate computations.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2018 and regulations apply to exempted partnerships registered in Bermuda that are resident in Bermuda (i.e., not tax resident in another jurisdiction) and carry on a 'relevant activity'. Relevant activities include distribution and service centres, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, IP, fund management, banking, and insurance. Kitting/packaging for third-party customers (3PL) is generally not a relevant activity; however, if the ELP provides purchasing or distribution services to group affiliates, it may be classified as a distribution and service centre. Such entities must be managed and directed in Bermuda, perform core income-generating activities in Bermuda, have adequate physical premises, employees, and expenditure, and file an annual Economic Substance Declaration. Failure to comply can lead to fines and the entity's tax residence status being disregarded.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unrecognised UK Permanent Establishment (UK PE)

Statutory sanction: UK CTA 2010 and TMA 1970: failure to notify registration - up to £13,125; failure to file CT600 - £750 escalating to £10,000 plus daily penalties of £10 per day; deliberate inaccuracies - penalty of 70% of the understated tax plus interest and possible criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: HMRC may issue personal penalty notices of up to £50,000 to directors who cause serious non-compliance; under FA 2014, joint and several liability notices can be issued for unpaid tax where offshore arrangements are used; directors can be disqualified for up to 15 years and travel bans imposed.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure (Bermuda ESR)

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018: initial penalty BD$50,000 plus a daily default penalty of BD$5,000; on continued default the Registrar may strike off the ELP; all partners are jointly and severally liable for penalties; the ELP's transactions may be recharacterised for UK or international tax purposes.

Director personal liability: The general partner and directors of a corporate general partner are personally liable for the BD$50,000 fine; in cases of deliberate non-compliance, imprisonment of up to 5 years may be imposed, along with asset freezing and a travel ban pending payment of penalties.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Under UK TIOPA 2010, HMRC can adjust profits to the arm's length standard and impose penalties up to 100% of understated tax; under Bermuda's CIT Act 2023, in-scope MNE groups that misprice cross-border services face top-up tax assessments equal to 15% of total net income, with default interest and penalties of up to BD$250,000 for incorrect returns.

Director personal liability: Directors can be liable for penalties of up to £5,000 for failing to retain adequate transfer pricing records; where the ELP cannot pay due to profit stripping, HMRC may issue a joint and several liability notice to directors under FA 2014, and the UK's corporate criminal offence of failing to prevent facilitation of tax evasion can lead to prosecution of the board.
Anti-Money Laundering / Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008: on indictment, fines up to BD$1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 15 years for individuals; civil penalties up to BD$25,000 per contravention; UK sanctions regulations may freeze assets and terminate payment rail access.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who consent to or connive in an AML offence are personally criminally liable, with penalties of up to 10 years' imprisonment; a travel ban and asset-freezing order can be issued pending investigation; under the UK Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022, unexplained wealth orders may be used to recover illicit gains.
