Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

The Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; taxpayer portal: Government of Bermuda Tax Portal (gov.bm)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership registers with the Office of the Tax Commissioner by submitting the ELP registration certificate, registered office consent, particulars of general and limited partners, and contact details. Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN; the entity receives a Payroll Tax Account Number for employer obligations and a Customs Importer Code for import transactions. Registration is completed through the Bermuda Tax Portal and the relevant Customs application forms.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership registers with the Office of the Tax Commissioner by submitting the ELP registration certificate, registered office consent, particulars of general and limited partners, and contact details. Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN; the entity receives a Payroll Tax Account Number for employer obligations and a Customs Importer Code for import transactions. Registration is completed through the Bermuda Tax Portal and the relevant Customs application forms.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda has no domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime because it imposes no income tax. A Bermuda ELP is default fiscally transparent under Bermuda law. For foreign tax purposes, the ELP is treated as an eligible entity and may be classified as a partnership or disregarded entity under check-the-box rules where applicable (e.g., U.S. Form 8832); UK hybrid-mismatch analysis applies where the ELP is fiscally transparent in Bermuda but treated differently in the UK or another investor jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in Bermuda; indirect taxation consists primarily of customs duties, stamp duties, and payroll-related taxes

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Bermuda. A Customs Importer Code is required for all importers, and payroll tax registration is triggered once the ELP has employees in Bermuda.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so there is no export zero-rating regime and no refund mechanism for output VAT. Exports from Bermuda are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation; customs duties apply only to imports into Bermuda. Documentary evidence is nonetheless prepared to satisfy the importing jurisdiction's VAT zero-rating and customs requirements.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs, where required)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence

No real-time VAT clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs uses ASYCUDA World for import/export declarations; commercial invoices must be retained for customs valuation and audit purposes.

Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST export fraud penalties because no such tax exists. However, fraudulent customs declarations, misdeclaration of goods, or false export documentation may trigger penalties under the Bermuda Customs Act, including forfeiture of goods, fines up to BMD 25,000, imprisonment, and retrospective duty recovery. The ELP may also face regulatory sanction under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime and anti-money-laundering framework if false documents are used to support foreign VAT claims.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 rate to the U.S. Dollar. Both the current and capital accounts are fully convertible without exchange control restrictions for exempted undertakings. Profit remittances, capital distributions, redemption proceeds, and liquidation payouts may be effected freely. The ELP, as a non-resident exempted partnership, may maintain accounts in USD, EUR, GBP, or any other currency and transact without mandatory conversion or BMA approval. The only practical limitation is the on-island use of BMD banknotes and coinage; all commercial electronic settlements are cleared in USD via major international correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion obligation applies to export proceeds under Bermuda law. While the Exchange Control Act retains a residual reserve power to require residents to offer foreign currency to an authorized dealer, this is not exercised for exempted partnerships designated as non-resident businesses. Export receipts from FPV drone sales may be held, allocated, or remitted without any time limit or ratio requirement.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the integrated financial regulator and central bank of Bermuda, established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and administered through the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium–Low. PayPal and Stripe accept the Bermudian ELP as a merchant and will settle U.S. dollar proceeds to a beneficiary bank account in Bermuda, typically through SWIFT or an ACH-to-correspondent arrangement. Pure offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) generally require an EEA or UK-incorporated entity with local operational presence; Bermuda, as a British Overseas Territory, is not an EEA state and therefore fails standard EMI onboarding rules. A few EMIs licensed under the BMA Money Service Business regime can issue virtual IBANs, but these are not banks and require a sponsor bank relationship. The recommended approach is a concentrated USD operational account at Butterfield or HSBC, with PayPal/Stripe payouts routed to that account and supplier payments executed via SWIFT.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% for in-scope MNE groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; nil rate for out-of-scope exempted limited partnerships.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on distributions made by an exempted limited partnership.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, in-scope MNE groups must file and pay within 15 months of the fiscal year-end. The subject ELP, being below the EUR 750 million group revenue threshold, has no CIT filing obligation.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing window runs from 1 January to 31 January each calendar year, irrespective of the ELP's accounting year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No local transfer pricing documentation threshold for out-of-scope ELPs. Mandatory Master File and Local File obligations arise only for constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated annual revenue exceeding EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 817 million).

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 applies to entities carrying on relevant activities such as banking, insurance, headquarters, shipping, financing, leasing, fund management, distribution and service centers, or pure holding. Manufacturing and retail sale of FPV drones is not a relevant activity; however, if the ELP claims head office functions or distribution activity in Bermuda, it must demonstrate adequate physical presence, employees, and management locally.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Bermuda Criminal Code and Tax Administration provisions; fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years on conviction, with asset forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner may be personally liable for reckless false declarations; civil restitution, confiscation orders, and prohibition from serving as directors in Bermuda or the UK.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off by the Registrar; loss of Bermuda tax-neutral status; HMRC may recharacterize the arrangement under UK GAAR, impositing UK corporation tax, interest, and penalties up to 100% of avoided tax.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the ELP veil; general partners and controlling directors become jointly and severally liable for unpaid UK tax and penalties; travel bans and worldwide freezing orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act.
Anti-Money Laundering / Sanctions

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations 2009 and the Sanctions Act, fines up to BMD 1,000,000, licence suspension, and custodial penalties up to 14 years for serious violations.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors can be criminally prosecuted for failing to prevent money laundering; personal asset seizure and travel restrictions apply.
Cross-Border Profit Repatriation Abuse

Statutory sanction: Bermuda applies no economic adjustment for out-of-scope ELPs, but HMRC may impose transfer pricing adjustments on UK connected companies, deny deductions, and apply Diversified Profits Tax at 25% plus interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or knowingly benefit from artificial repatriation may incur facilitation penalties equal to 100% of the tax underpaid and be disqualified under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986.
