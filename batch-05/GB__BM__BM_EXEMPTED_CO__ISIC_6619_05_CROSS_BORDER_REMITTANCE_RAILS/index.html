Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Bermuda Exempted Company is non-resident and taxed at 0% on income, gains, and dividends; subject only to payroll tax, the Exempted Company annual fee (in lieu of corporate tax), and economic substance reporting; no capital gains, withholding, or remittance taxes.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Bermuda law. Available mechanisms: (1) EUTPA 1966 Certificate of Exemption election (future-tax protection until 2035); (2) Payment Services Act 2019 licensing election for remittance activity; (3) economic substance reporting-period election; (4) UK parent-level CFC (TIOPA 2010 Part 9A) and hybrid mismatch rules (Part 6A) may apply, but no election is filed in Bermuda.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

None — Bermuda has no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; UK VAT (20%) does not extend to this Overseas Territory. Bermuda levies only customs duties on physical imports (Customs Tariff Act 1970) and payroll/land/hotel taxes, none of which apply to cross-border remittance fee income.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists; no Bermudian indirect tax is triggered by remittance-service turnover. Customs importer registration is required only for physical goods imported into Bermuda.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD), and USD circulates freely in Bermuda as legal tender. Current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible for non-resident Bermuda Exempted Companies; there is no statutory exchange-control restriction on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or repatriation of liquidation proceeds. The BMA administers the Exchange Control Act 1972, under which an exempted company designated as 'non-resident' automatically receives exchange-control permission, eliminating separate approval requirements. The operative frictions are purely bank-level: AML/CFT source-of-funds verification, FATF-corridor risk classification, and correspondent-bank de-risking appetite, none of which constitutes a convertibility or capital-control barrier.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory export-proceeds surrender, no prescribed conversion timeline, and no minimum conversion ratio for export or cross-border service receipts. A Bermuda Exempted Company may lawfully retain 100% of export proceeds and cross-border remittance fees in any freely convertible currency, including USD, GBP, EUR, or corridor settlement currencies. For the remittance-agency model, export of services is treated as ordinary trading income for economic-substance and tax purposes; the only filings are BMA statistical reporting, economic substance declarations, and standard corporate annual returns, none of which requires conversion of foreign currency earnings.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM, and only under a segregated correspondent architecture. A UK FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institution (EMI) may be used as a collection and settlement springboard for GBP/EUR-denominated beneficiary flows, but it cannot serve as the sole or primary banking rail for a Bermuda-licensed payment service provider because PSD2 passporting rights do not extend to Bermuda, and BMA-regulated banks will not accept an unauthorised EMI as a final settlement counterparty. The compliant structure is: UK/EU EMI accounts for euro or sterling collections, safeguarded client funds, swept daily via SWIFT MT103 to the Bermuda treasury settlement account, and then onward routed through licensed domestic correspondent banks. Offshore EMIs issuing debit instruments directly into local cash-pickup networks are constrained by destination-country money-transmission licensing requirements and by UK FCA safeguarding rules that restrict downstream agent disbursements; any such activity requires the Bermuda entity to hold a BMA Payment Services licence and, where US corridors are served, state-level money transmitter licences.

The receiving bank requires the BMA exemption certificate, legal-entity identifier, corporate authorisation resolution, and source-of-funds declaration. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a standard Bermuda Exempted Company outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA); 15% applies only to a Bermuda constituent entity of an MNE group with annual consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda does not impose dividend withholding tax on payments by an Exempted Company, whether to residents or non-residents, and regardless of the payment rail used.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

15 months after the end of the fiscal year for in-scope entities; registered payers must file a nil return if no CITA event occurs. Out-of-scope Exempted Companies are not required to file CIT-1.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the last day of the month following the quarter end (30 April, 31 July, 31 October, 31 January).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 consolidated group revenue (equivalent to EUR 750,000,000) triggers mandatory CITA transfer pricing documentation: both the Local File and Master File must be prepared for the Bermuda taxpayer entity; below this threshold an Exempted Company is not statutorily required to prepare transfer pricing documentation, but contemporaneous documentation is recommended for all cross-border related-party transactions involving remittance fees, SWIFT costs, and cash-pickup clearing charges.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) apply to 'relevant activities' such as banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centres, shipping, intellectual property holding, and pure equity holding. Consumer money transmission and cross-border remittance processing is not a designated relevant activity, but the Exempted Company must demonstrate adequate physical presence, local management, and substantive AML/regulatory operations to satisfy the Bermuda Monetary Authority and to resist sham or shell characterization. A licensed money transmitter must maintain a registered office in Bermuda, a resident compliance officer, and at least one physical place of business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Tax Administration Act 2024 and Corporate Income Tax Act 2023: fixed penalty up to BMD 50,000, understated tax surcharge of up to 100%, recovery of evaded tax with interest, and on conviction a discretionary court fine plus imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorize the false filing are jointly assessed for the unpaid tax plus penalties; courts may issue travel bans, asset freezes, restitution orders, and disqualification from serving as directors of a Bermuda company.
Unlicensed Money Transmission

Statutory sanction: Cease-and-desist orders, revocation of the exempted company certificate, a fine of up to BMD 100,000 per transaction or activity, and imprisonment of responsible officers for up to 5 years; illicit transaction proceeds are subject to confiscation under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997.

Director personal liability: Directors and senior officers face civil enforcement by the BMA, personal fines, travel bans imposed under regulatory supervision orders, and disqualification orders; the corporate veil will be pierced where the company is found to be a mere facade for unlicensed transmission.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: CITA general anti-abuse provisions may recharacterize imputed income and impose 15% tax with penalties; the Registrar may strike the company off the register; UK tax authorities may issue protective assessments and exchange information under the Bermuda-UK TIEA.

Director personal liability: Operation of a sham company constitutes fraudulent trading under the Companies Act 1981, rendering directors personally liable for corporate debts; courts can impose restraint orders, travel bans, and restitution obligations on individuals who signed false substance declarations.
AML/ATF Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Regulations 2008: fines up to BMD 2,000,000 on conviction, imprisonment up to 5 years, confiscation of assets, and criminal liability for the regulated entity's licence.

Director personal liability: Directors, the compliance officer, and the Money Laundering Reporting Officer face individual criminal prosecution, personal monetary penalties, disqualification, travel bans, and civil restitution; regulators may hold directors personally liable for systemic compliance failures.
