Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Bermuda Government; entity registration via the Registrar of Companies, portal: https://www.gov.bm/ (onegov.bm e-Registration system).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership must file the Exempted Partnership Declaration and Statement of Appointment through the Registrar of Companies. The Registrar issues a BRN (Bermuda Reference Number) upon registration. For payroll obligations, the partnership must obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number from the Tax Commissioner; for imports or customs-related activities, it must obtain a Customs Tariff ID from the Bermuda Customs Service. No VAT TIN or general income tax registration is applicable because Bermuda does not levy VAT or income tax.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible / Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity establishing a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership must file the Exempted Partnership Declaration and Statement of Appointment through the Registrar of Companies. The Registrar issues a BRN (Bermuda Reference Number) upon registration. For payroll obligations, the partnership must obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number from the Tax Commissioner; for imports or customs-related activities, it must obtain a Customs Tariff ID from the Bermuda Customs Service. No VAT TIN or general income tax registration is applicable because Bermuda does not levy VAT or income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda law provides no domestic check-the-box election. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to elect classification as a partnership or corporation under the check-the-box regime. Hybrid mismatch and BEPS-aligned outcomes are addressed under Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2018 and the U.S./UK treaty framework, but no local hybrid mismatch election mechanism applies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda operates a customs duty and stamp duty tariff system

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold is applicable. However, any person or entity importing goods into Bermuda must obtain a Customs Tariff Registration. There is no monetary threshold; the obligation arises at the first importation event.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in a VAT sense. Bermuda has no VAT, so export zero-rating does not arise. Exports of tangible goods from Bermuda are outside the customs duty net and are duty-exempt. Marine towage and salvage services performed wholly outside the territorial sea of Bermuda are not subject to Bermuda taxation or customs duties.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (Form C-EXP)
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill evidencing export destination
  • Towage/Salvage Contract or Charter Party Agreement
  • Commercial Invoice and freight manifest
  • Cargo declaration for controlled goods (export permit where applicable)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists for VAT/GST. Customs declarations are processed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Automated Processing System (CAPS) and must be filed by, or through, a licensed customs broker for all inward and outward cargo movements.

Under the Bermuda Customs Act 2010 and Exchange Control Regulations, misdeclaration or false documentation in support of duty-exempt exports may trigger retrospective duty assessment, penalties up to BD$25,000 per consignment, forfeiture of goods, criminal prosecution for evading duties, and revocation or suspension of the Exempted Limited Partnership's exempted status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda's official currency is the Bermudian dollar, pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar and freely convertible into USD for all commercial purposes. The capital account is open for non-resident designated entities: an exempted limited partnership that obtains BMA non-resident designation may hold, retain, and remit foreign currency without exchange-control permission. The current account receives unrestricted convertibility for towage and salvage invoices rendered outside Bermuda, and there are no withholding taxes or profit remittance licensing requirements in Bermuda. Profit remittance from Bermuda operating activities to foreign partners is not blocked, provided the ELP's recorded operations are conducted offshore and the non-resident designation remains in force.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For Bermuda-resident companies, foreign-currency export and service receipts must be surrendered to an authorized bank within the statutory period prescribed under Bermuda's Exchange Control Regulations, generally not later than three months from receipt, with a mandatory conversion ratio of 100% to BMD except for a BMA-approved working balance. The maritime salvage and towage ELP should rely on BMA non-resident designation, which exempts it from the surrender obligation; all USD and BMD receipts may then be retained in designated foreign-currency accounts and used for operational disbursements without conversion.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority, acting as Bermuda's central monetary authority and Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972; the Bank of England does not exercise direct FX regulation over Bermuda as a self-governing British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as principal treasury provider; Medium only as an ancillary payment channel for crew disbursements and small operational expenses. The Bermuda Monetary Authority does not issue a standalone electronic money institution licence, and a UK or EU FCA-regulated EMI cannot directly access Bermuda's domestic clearing or hold a BMD/USD escrow account on behalf of the ELP. An offshore digital EMI may be used only when safeguarding funds are held at a BMA-licensed bank and no feature of the EMI arrangement creates unauthorized deposit-taking, foreign-exchange dealing, or money-service activity regulated under Bermuda law.

Root cause: Use of digital assets, virtual-asset payment processors, or unregulated cross-border EMIs anywhere in the funding or settlement chain.

Defense evidence: Negative declaration if no virtual assets were used; if virtual assets were used, liquidation path via licensed exchanges with complete source-of-funds reports, on-chain transaction analysis, and enhanced due diligence documentation acceptable to the Bermuda bank's compliance department.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections must originate from bank accounts held in the legal name of the contributing limited partner or beneficial owner. The compliant route is a SWIFT MT103 wire from a top-tier UK, EU, or US bank to the ELP's Bermuda capital escrow account, with field 70 narrative reading 'capital contribution to ELP' followed by the partner reference; inbound USD is settled at par with BMD through the bank's US correspondent account. No purpose code is required by Bermuda, but if the remitting jurisdiction requires one, the sender must use the code for capital contribution to an overseas partnership rather than generic consultancy or miscellaneous transfer. The bank requires a pre-advice package containing the contribution schedule, the remitter's bank statement, the certified contribution notice, and the ELP partnership agreement before funds are credited.Operational disbursements for fuel, port dues, crew wages, surveyors, and emergency salvage repairs are cleared by SWIFT MT103 in USD or GBP. Major dry-docking, tug construction, engine overhaul, and shipyard payments above fifty thousand dollars are routed by commercial letter of credit under UCP600 using SWIFT MT700, with presentation documents including commercial invoice, bill of lading, repair certificate, and surveyor's release report. Lloyd's Standard Form salvage awards may be paid from the escrow account by MT103 immediately upon issuance of the salvage award or arbitration settlement, referencing the Lloyd's Standard Form number in the wire narrative and attaching the final settlement certificate to the bank's compliance file.Deliverable FX forwards between USD and GBP, EUR, and JPY for matched operating expenses, cargo-disbursement liabilities, and tug-purchase commitments with tenors up to 24 months.Cross-currency swaps between USD and GBP or EUR to manage long-term debt, finance-lease, and fuel-procurement currency mismatches under an ISDA Master Agreement with a BMA-licensed bank.Vanilla FX call options, put options, and zero-cost collars on USD/GBP and USD/EUR; leveraged, barrier, or exotic options are not permitted.Non-deliverable forwards strictly limited to hedging actual emerging-market currency exposures such as port disbursements and crew payments in restricted currencies; speculative NDFs are not permitted because BMD/USD is fixed and deliverable at parity.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for stand-alone Bermuda exempted limited partnerships and non-MNE groups; 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 applies to constituent entities of multinational enterprise (MNE) groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750 million or more, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025 (Pillar Two QDMTT/IIR framework)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, profits, capital distributions or partnership remittances paid by an ELP to non-resident limited partners

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750 million (EUR 750 million) consolidated MNE group revenue — Bermuda has not enacted classic Local File/Master File transfer-pricing documentation rules; the mandatory triggering threshold applies for GloBE Information Return and QDMTT filing under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, and the ELP must prepare GloBE-calibrated transfer-pricing records for any intercompany towage, salvage and management fees

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) fully apply. Towage and salvage operations carried on by a Bermuda ELP fall within the 'Shipping Business' relevant activity and require: management and direction in Bermuda; sufficient employees, premises and expenditure proportionate to the activity; and core income-generating activities (contract negotiation, crew management, invoicing, risk and emergency response control) performed in Bermuda. An ELP deriving only passive holding income files a nil ESR declaration, but any towage/salvage revenue removes eligibility for the nil classification. Failure to meet the substance test triggers penalties, dissolution risk and automatic exchange of information with EU member states

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / CIT Fraud

Statutory sanction: Corporate Income Tax Act 2024: fixed penalty of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 100 per day for late or non-filing; interest on all unpaid CIT at the statutory rate; false declarations punishable by a fine up to BMD 15,000 and/or imprisonment up to 6 months on summary conviction; the Minister may publicly list non-compliant entities and revoke exempted status

Director personal liability: The general partner of the ELP is jointly and severally liable for CIT debts; directors and officers of a corporate general partner may be prosecuted as 'management' for wilful false filings; the Supreme Court of Bermuda can pierce the veil where the ELP is a mere sham or façade, enabling confiscation orders against personal assets; travel restrictions and disqualification orders may follow a serious CIT fraud conviction
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations 2018, regulation 15: BMD 10,000 penalty for the first assessment year and BMD 100,000 for each continued year of non-compliance; referral to the Tax Administrator for exchange of information with EU/OECD jurisdictions; automatic revocation of the exempted partnership certificate and court-ordered dissolution of the ELP

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner may be joined as respondents to the Registrar's compliance order; unpaid penalties are recoverable personally where the ELP's assets are insufficient; repeated breaches result in appearance before the Supreme Court, disqualification as a director/manager, personal fines and, in aggregation cases, travel bans pending final determination
AML / Beneficial Ownership / Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Beneficial Ownership Act 2017: fine of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 1,000 daily default penalty; Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018: corporate fine up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years per offence; Financial Sanctions Act 2013: asset freezing, civil monetary penalties up to BMD 1,000,000 and forfeiture of tugs and towage proceeds as proceeds of crime

Director personal liability: The general partner, directors and compliance officers incur automatic criminal liability for assisting money laundering through the ELP's payment rails; the Governor may impose travel bans and asset-freezing orders on individuals; the corporate veil is pierced where the ELP is used as a conduit for sanctions evasion or terrorist financing, leaving owners personally liable for civil restitution
Unlicensed Marine Towage/Salvage Operations

Statutory sanction: Shipping Act 2002 and Marine Board Regulations: fines per unlicensed voyage, immediate detention of the vessel and suspension of the Bermudian flag; civil restitution to vessel owners for wrongful salvage; administrative revocation of the towage/salvage operating licence; port closure orders in Bermuda harbours

Director personal liability: The general partner of the ELP is personally liable for maritime torts including collision damage and wrongful salvage; flag-state authorities may blacklist the master and the managing director; criminal fines attach to officers for false safety and crewing declarations; the ELP's limited partners can lose their liability shield if they participate in the unlicensed operations. A 'no cure, no pay' salvage claim without proper contract documentation exposes the ELP and its GP to unlimited civil liability in Admiralty courts
