Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Bermuda (portal: https://www.gov.bm/tax-information)

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not maintain a general income-tax TIN. A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) registers with the Registrar of Companies and may obtain a Bermuda Business Number (BBN) via the Corporate Registrar portal for administrative and trade-license purposes. Payroll tax registration with the Office of the Tax Commissioner is required only if the ELP employs Bermuda-based staff. Non-resident partners without Bermuda-source payroll derive no statutorily required TIN; a voluntary BBN is available for tax-treaty and banking compliance.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - Bermuda imposes no value-added tax (VAT), general sales tax (GST), or turnover-based sales tax. Customs duty is levied on imports only, at rates varying by tariff heading; no registration threshold applies for a tax on goods entering the island.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

N/A - As Bermuda does not levy VAT/GST on goods or services, there is no zero-rating concept. Exports from Bermuda are outside the scope of indirect taxation. A Tax Assurance Certificate on the ELP confirms nil direct taxation but does not substantiate VAT refunds because no VAT system operates.

  • Not Applicable - Bermuda export declaration from the Customs Department, if required, must be filed for statistical purposes only; no VAT zero-rating claim is available

No national e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists. Commercial invoices for royalty payments and cross-border services are governed by common-law record-keeping standards; digital stamping is not required. Customs declarations are filed electronically through the Customs Tariff and Trade System, but this is not a VAT clearance mechanism.

As there is no VAT/GST zero-rating regime, no export zero-rating clawback or fraud penalty is applicable. The relevant penalties for misdeclarations to the Bermuda Customs Department are governed by the Customs Tariff Act and the Taxes Management Act 1976, with fines up to USD 10,000 and forfeiture of goods; false statements in a Tax Assurance Certificate application may result in revocation of tax-exempt status and penalties under the Ministry of Finance rules.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar and enjoys unrestricted convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. No exchange control regime operates in Bermuda; no prior central bank approval is required for profit remittance, dividend distributions, capital repatriation, or current account payments. The BMA maintains oversight for prudential and AML/CFT purposes only, not for FX rationing or convertibility restrictions. For the ELP pass-through entity, profit distributions to non-resident partners can be remitted freely in USD through the partnership bank account, subject solely to the partnership agreement, tax compliance certificates, and standard banking due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender requirements in Bermuda. No mandatory conversion ratios apply to export receipts, royalty payments, or service revenues. All export and trade receipts may be retained in USD or any freely convertible currency. Bermuda's absence of surrender rules extends to the ELP's franchise royalty and licensing receipts from Chinese and international master licensees; such proceeds are fully free of any forced sale or conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), exercising central bank and financial regulatory functions for the Bermuda dollar (BMD) under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. Bermuda is not a member of the UK/EU e-money institution (EMI) regulatory passporting framework. Digital asset business licenses and banking-like authorizations exist under the Bermuda Digital Asset Business Act 2018 and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, but a pure offshore EMI (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business, or a UK FCA-licensed EMI) is not a substitute for a Bermudian bank account. Offshore EMIs that are not licensed or recognized by the BMA cannot provide sovereign-grade custody of ELP capital or maintain clear segregation for escrow. Their use should be limited to ancillary operational settlement and never for statutory capital escrow, which must be held with a regulated Bermudian bank or, if so structured, with an eligible UK bank in the parent chain.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound funds from Chinese master franchisees, P.R.C. parent entities, or UK group treasury to the Bermuda ELP shall be routed via compliant SWIFT telegraphic transfer in USD or BMD (settled as USD) to a designated Bermudian commercial bank account. The remitter must include a full legal entity name, bank account number, SWIFT/BIC, and an unambiguous payment purpose code. Common purpose codes: 1. SERVICES (for royalty and licensing fees under BPM6 current account), 2. DIRECT INVESTMENT / CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION (for ELP subscription capital), or 3. TRADE CREDIT ADVANCES (for intercompany funding). The ELP must maintain a matching capital-call notice, partnership capital account ledger, and a copy of the underlying master license agreement to demonstrate that each inbound wire aligns with the declared purpose. No BMA exchange control approval is required, but banks will verify the source of funds and may request a letter of indemnity for cross-border wires from P.R.C. banks subject to PRC outbound remittance quotas or SAFE registration if the payer is a P.R.C. entity. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% standard Bermuda rate; no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or branch profits tax. Constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups (consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750m) become subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2025 flat 15% rate (GloBE Pillar Two) for fiscal years commencing on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda levies no withholding tax on dividends, partnership distributions, interest, royalties, or capital gains paid to any non-resident partner or shareholder.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before 31 January following the end of the calendar year; the return covers the preceding calendar year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within six months after the end of the ELP's accounting period (for example, 30 June for a 31 December year-end).

Deadline: · Frequency:

CIT-1 is due within 15 months after the close of the reporting fiscal year; quarterly estimated payments are due 15 days after each quarter-end, with the Q4 balancing payment due at the annual deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda domestic TP documentation threshold applies to a standalone ELP; however, once consolidated group revenue reaches EUR 750m under GloBE scope, CITA requires OECD-compliant Master File and Local File documentation. Best practice: maintain a Local File for the Bermuda ELP where annual intra-group royalty or franchise income exceeds BMD 500,000, because HMRC or TIA may request comparability evidence during substance and CIT audits.

Economic substance scope: ESR under the Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) Act 2018 fully applies. Providing intangible property to connected persons and holding-entity activity are relevant activities. The ELP must satisfy: (i) board and management meetings held in Bermuda with quorum; (ii) qualified full-time employees in Bermuda; (iii) a physical office; (iv) adequate operating expenditure proportionate to royalty income; and (v) CIGA decisions such as IP protection, franchise quality control, and pricing directed from Bermuda. Outsourcing to the UK parent is limited and must be locally supervised. Non-compliance triggers the penalty ladder described in the ESR filing section, and the ELP may be struck off as a shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / UK Facilitated Tax Evasion (UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 ss. 45-46)

Statutory sanction: UK: unlimited fine on the corporate franchise group and up to 7 years' imprisonment for the natural-person offender. Bermuda: prosecution for false declarations to the TIA under the Tax Information Exchange Act 1986 and the Criminal Code.

Director personal liability: UK directors face personal prosecution for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion; HMRC may obtain civil restitution equal to evaded amounts plus penalties up to 200%; asset transfers to the ELP may be reversed under the UK Insolvency Act 1986 s. 423.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Bermuda: progressive civil penalties of BMD 10,000, then BMD 25,000, and up to BMD 250,000 plus a daily default penalty of BMD 2,000; the ROC may strike off the ELP, voiding the limited-liability shield; knowingly false filings are criminally punishable by up to 5 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Officers of the general partner may be personally liable for unpaid penalties and, after strike-off, for ELP debts incurred post-dissolution; directors may face disqualification orders under Bermuda company law and HMRC may pursue shadow-director liability.
AML / Financial Crime Breach

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2008 (as amended): on conviction, a fine of up to BMD 100,000 and/or up to 5 years' imprisonment per offence; licence revocation for the master franchisor; suspension of the ELP registration.

Director personal liability: AML compliance officers and directors may be prosecuted personally; UK senior managers may also be charged under the UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017, facing unlimited fines and up to 2 years' imprisonment.
Fraudulent Misrepresentation / Piercing the Corporate Veil

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Criminal Code fraud provisions: imprisonment of up to 10 years; civil liability for restitution of the full diverted amount; the court may lift the limited-liability veil and hold the GP personally liable for partnership obligations.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners are jointly and severally liable; courts may issue asset-freezing orders and travel bans against principals where dissipation risk exists; UK disqualification orders of up to 15 years may be imposed under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 for cross-border mismanagement.
