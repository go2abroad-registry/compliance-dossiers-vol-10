Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner of the Government of Bermuda, official portal at taxcommissioner.gov.bm; corporate registration is administered by the Registrar of Companies, Government of Bermuda

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities incorporating as Bermuda Exempted Companies file incorporation documents with the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981 and the Exempted Companies Act 1981; upon incorporation the entity receives a Registration Number and is liable to the annual company fee. No separate income-tax TIN is issued because Bermuda levies no income or corporation tax. A payroll tax account number must be obtained from the Office of the Tax Commissioner only if the entity incurs Bermuda-source employment payroll; a passive REIT holding offshore real estate assets generally requires no tax ID registration beyond corporate registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

An exempted company may apply for a Tax Exemption Certificate under Section 6 of the Exempted Companies Act 1981, obtaining a statutory assurance that, until at least 2035, the company and its shareholders will be exempt from Bermuda income, capital gains, and distribution taxes. No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist under Bermuda domestic law because there is no income-tax base to elect against.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; indirect levies comprise customs duties, payroll tax, and land tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists; customs duty registration arises on first import, and payroll tax registration is triggered when Bermuda-sourced remuneration is paid to employees

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Bermuda has no VAT/GST framework, so no supply can be zero-rated for indirect tax purposes. A Bermuda Exempted Company is not subject to Bermuda income tax on foreign-source receipts, and offshore services or treasury activity conducted from Bermuda remain outside the Bermuda indirect tax net. For physical goods, temporary import and re-export may qualify for a duty drawback, but no VAT refund mechanism exists.

N/A – no VAT e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists; import declarations are processed through Bermuda Customs Department using the ASYCUDA World electronic declaration system, and duty may be paid or bonded upon cargo clearance.

Penalties under the Bermuda Revenue (Tax Administration) Act 2004 and the Customs Act for non-compliance range from BMD 2,000 to BMD 25,000 plus interest, and fraudulent customs declarations attract penalties up to three times the duty evaded and possible imprisonment. Because Bermuda does not impose VAT, there are no VAT zero-rating clawback penalties. However, a Bermudian REIT with UK-resident investors or assets may remain subject to UK anti-avoidance, CFC, and withholding tax regimes, and failure to repatriate earnings within the intended 180-day window could trigger UK recharacterisation or controlled-foreign-company income inclusions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD), eliminating currency conversion risk for USD-denominated treasury operations. For Bermuda Exempted Companies treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes, the current and capital accounts are fully convertible; there are no exchange controls on inward capital injections, interest, dividends, or profit repatriation. The BMA reserve powers apply to resident Bermuda persons and BMD-denominated accounts, but a properly structured exempted REIT undertaking offshore real-estate investment retains unrestricted multi-currency account capability. Profit remittance to the UK parent requires no BMA consent, provided the company maintains an authorised foreign currency account and files the requisite exchange control declarations (Forms EC) through an authorised bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline for export proceeds surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to repatriate foreign currency receipts. Export or offshore service proceeds may be retained in the currency received or held in USD/BMD indefinitely. For a Bermuda exempted REIT generating rental and property disposal income, the effective surrender ratio is 0% and the free retention ratio is 100%. The only compliance obligation is BMA exchange-control reporting through the authorised bank when any transaction exceeds threshold limits, as part of periodic EC declarations.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the central bank and exchange control authority under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972, with residual UK Treasury oversight for designated territories.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Offshore digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) licensed in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area (such as Revolut Business, Wise Platform, or Modular Bank-backed EMIs) exhibit Medium feasibility as supplementary collection and disbursement rails for GBP/EUR operational cash flows. They cannot, however, serve as the primary banking relationship because no EMI in Bermuda holds a deposit-taking license under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; BMA regulation and the Bermuda exchange-control framework require a licensed authorised bank as the principal settlement point. Legal constraints further restrict EMIs from acting as safekeeping agents for REIT capital or holding shareholder funds, and using an EMI as a cyclical pass-through may raise substance concerns under the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 if the company claims non-resident status.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no corporate payroll tax on profits or distributions. Exempted companies are outside the scope of any future Bermudian income tax when covered by an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act guarantee, typically effective through 2056.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% domestic withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda exempted company to any non-resident shareholder; no Bermuda source taxation applies to dividend distributions or any other profit repatriation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File or Master File threshold exists in Bermudian domestic law. Multinational groups with consolidated group revenue above USD 850 million (or BMD 850 million) must file a Country-by-Country Report with the BTA no later than 12 months after the group reporting period. Local File is only required on specific request by the BTA or a treaty/TIEA partner during an audit; no advance pricing agreement programme exists in Bermuda.

Economic substance scope: The International Tax Cooperation (Economic Substance) Act 2018 and associated Regulations apply to every 'relevant entity' carrying on a 'relevant activity'. A real estate investment trust may fall under 'fund management' if it manages collective investment vehicles, 'holding company' if it merely holds real estate participations, or 'distribution and service' if active property brokerage/management is performed. A pure equity holding company is subject to a reduced economic substance test. All entities must demonstrate sufficient physical premises and employees in Bermuda, board meetings of adequate quorum in Bermuda, and core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda. ESR failures are reportable to the UK tax authorities under the automatic exchange of information framework because the UK is the parent jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / artificial avoidance of UK tax on property income

Statutory sanction: HMRC assessments over 12 years, penalties up to 100% of the avoided tax, interest on late tax, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion of income tax under the Taxes Management Act 1970. UK CFC or anti-deferral rules may attribute Bermuda profits back to UK controllers.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil and impose joint liability on directors. A director complicit in the scheme faces civil restitution, UK freezing orders, travel bans, and imprisonment for up to 7 years under the Fraud Act 2006 or the Criminal Finances Act 2017.
Shell company / economic substance failure

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda ESR, penalties for non-compliance include BMD 10,000 initial fines and BMD 10,000 per day for continued non-compliance; the BTA may notify the UK tax authority and the entity may be struck off the register. The list of non-compliant entities is published.

Director personal liability: Directors can be disqualified under the Companies Act for up to five years and personally fined up to BMD 50,000. They may also be held in contempt for failing to produce records; if the company is used for fraud, personal liability in civil proceedings arises.
AML / KYC and beneficial ownership register failure

Statutory sanction: Bermudian Proceeds of Crime Act enforcement: fines up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment for up to 10 years for money-laundering offences. The Registrar may freeze the entity's bank accounts, and the Financial Intelligence Agency may temporarily suspend wire transfers.

Director personal liability: Any director who knowingly permits faking UBO reporting commits a criminal offence. The court may impose custodial sentences of up to 5 years and disbarment from serving as a director of any Bermuda company or any UK-regulated company.
Sanctions and exchange of information breach

Statutory sanction: The International Tax Cooperation Act gives BTA power to demand records; non-compliance leads to BMD 50,000 fines plus daily penalties. Breach of UK sanctions is a criminal offence carrying up to 10 years imprisonment and unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors authorising non-disclosure become personally liable for fines and may face extradition requests between the UK and Bermuda; a director may be detained pending trial and subject to forfeiture orders from the Crown Court.
