Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

United Kingdom: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), www.gov.uk/hmrc. Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner, www.taxcommissioner.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

For the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), no Bermuda income-tax TIN or VAT ID is issued because Bermuda has no income tax, no VAT/GST, and no sales tax. The ELP must first obtain a Bermuda entity/registration number from the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act 2015. If the ELP becomes an employer, it must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for a Payroll Tax Account Number and Social Insurance registration. Only if the ELP has UK taxable supplies must it apply to HMRC for a UK VAT registration number and, if importing/exporting goods, a GB EORI number; a non-established taxable person may register using the HMRC VAT registration service.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Tax_Exempt_Zone for Bermuda direct tax purposes (no Bermuda income tax on the ELP itself, except possible Pillar Two top-up tax for qualifying large MNE groups); Territorial_Scope for UK direct tax purposes (profits taxable in the UK only if the ELP carries on a trade in the UK through a permanent establishment).

Available election forms

Bermuda does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election; the ELP is treated as a partnership under Bermuda law. For US federal tax classification, if relevant, the ELP may make a check-the-box election under Form 8832 to be taxed as an association/corporation or remain a pass-through entity. The UK and Bermuda have no reciprocal hybrid-mismatch election; OECD BEPS hybrid mismatch rules may apply to deny inappropriate deduction outcomes in cross-border arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; indirect taxes are customs duty and payroll tax. UK: VAT applies only to taxable supplies with a UK nexus; B2B cross-border platform services may be outside the scope of UK VAT where the recipient belongs outside the UK.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: no VAT/GST registration threshold and no value-added tax. UK: mandatory VAT registration is required once UK taxable turnover exceeds £90,000 in a rolling 12-month period; voluntary registration is available for some persons, and a non-established taxable person may become required to register immediately in prescribed UK VAT circumstances.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda does not impose VAT, so no Bermuda export zero-rating claim, VAT refund mechanism, or export VAT certificate exists. Under UK VAT, if the ELP is VAT-registered, exports of goods from the UK are zero-rated provided the goods are exported to a destination outside the UK within the statutory period (normally three months) and evidence of export is retained; qualifying exported services supplied to a non-UK business customer are generally outside the scope of UK VAT under the business-to-business place-of-supply rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration or relevant UK export system record (e.g. EX or C88 equivalent)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill clearly evidencing goods leaving the UK customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank payment evidence confirming payment from the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and executed sale or platform service contract
  • Proof of export such as port/airport clearance records, export inventory verification, or logistics tracking report

Bermuda has no real-time fiscal clearance, no e-invoicing mandate, and no digital stamping system. If UK VAT is applicable, HMRC requires digital VAT record keeping and filing through Making Tax Digital (MTD)-compatible software and ordinary commercial invoicing; there is no real-time clearance, API stamping, or national e-invoicing authorization requirement for standard UK VAT invoices.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating is subject to HMRC assessment for the full VAT not accounted for as a retrospective clawback, a penalty of up to 30% for careless inaccuracies, 70-100% for deliberate errors, and criminal sanctions under the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 and the Value Added Tax Act 1994. A UK VAT-registered ELP that cannot produce retained export evidence within the prescribed period must correct its VAT account and repay any input VAT or zero-rating benefit. In Bermuda, false trade declarations and customs offences attract statutory penalties and prosecution under the Customs Act; retrospective adjustments may be made for up to four years, extended to up to 20 years where dishonesty or fraud is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged to the U.S. dollar at a fixed 1:1 rate, with USD accepted interchangeably as legal tender. Bermuda maintains a liberal exchange control regime. An Exempted Limited Partnership granted 'non-resident' status under the Exchange Control Act enjoys full convertibility on both current and capital account transactions. Current account operations – platform trade settlement, supplier payments, and service fees – flow freely without BMA prior approval. Capital account transactions, including partner capital injections and profit distributions, are unrestricted for non-resident designated ELPs; only routine post-transaction statistical reporting via BMA returns is required. Profit remittance to UK/EU/Asia partners faces zero FX conversion friction on USD-denominated flows and no dividend withholding tax, though the ELP must file Economic Substance annual declarations to maintain its tax-transparent, non-resident status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion obligation applies to a non-resident designated ELP. Export proceeds and platform revenue received in foreign currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CNY) may be retained in their original currency in external accounts without any duty to offer them to an authorized dealer. For ELPs that fail to obtain non-resident status, the Exchange Control Regulations 1973 impose a duty to offer foreign currency to an authorized dealer within one month of receipt; however, standard Bermuda practice is for exempted partnerships used in cross-border structures to secure non-resident designation, thereby eliminating this obligation. The BMA also permits pre-approval of specific foreign currency accounts for operational flexibility.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, with the Minister of Finance retaining ultimate policy oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium (ancillary) / Low (primary). Bermuda has no direct Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime analogous to the UK PSD2 framework. The Digital Asset Business Act 2018 regulates digital asset exchanges, custodians, and issuing platforms, but does not create a generalized EMI passport. A UK-authorised EMI or EU-licensed EMI can be engaged as an auxiliary multi-currency collection rail to originate cross-border deposits from B2B marketplace counterparties, but the ELP must maintain its primary settlement and escrow accounts with a BMA-licensed bank to satisfy substance, economic-activity, and banking oversight expectations. Restriction: the BMA requires that all 'core treasury functions' be performed in/through a licensed entity, and the UK FCA or EBA passport does not extend enforcement supervision to Bermuda. The viable archetype is a layered structure: Bermuda bank (first-layer settlement) + UK EMI (second-layer collections via virtual IBANs) with contractual isolation of client funds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) Partner capital contributions are wired via SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's Bermuda settlement account with reference 'Capital Contribution – [Partner Name] – [LP Agreement Section]'. (2) Remittance purpose code: for USD/BMD transfers, the code 'EQUI' (equity injection) or 'CAP' is acceptable; for GBP transfers from the UK, use HMRC/BOP purpose code 'DX' (direct investment in overseas entities) or 'C1' (capital investment) as appropriate. (3) Verification: incoming funds must reconcile exactly with the executed capital commitment schedule; the ELP's auditor issues a capital contribution certificate within 10 business days; any variance or source of funds mismatch is escalated to the bank's compliance team. (4) Statutory reporting: BMA notification on Form EC1 for any single transaction exceeding USD 100,000, and annual declaration of non-resident status on the exchange control returns. (3) For repetitive high-volume Alibaba Pay settlement cycles, the rail is automated: the platform collects buyer payments, and the ELP's treasury executes a pooled disbursement file via SWIFT FileAct to suppliers' bank accounts using beneficiary pre-validation (Name Check / account screening). (4) FX legs for non-USD disbursements are executed through the Bermuda bank's FX desk at midpoint pricing with a stated spread cap; settlement instructions are sent via SWIFT MT210 (advice of credit) followed by MT103.
