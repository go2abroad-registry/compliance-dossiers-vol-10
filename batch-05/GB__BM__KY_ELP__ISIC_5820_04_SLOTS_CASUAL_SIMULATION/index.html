Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; the ELP is fiscally transparent, with partners taxed only on Bermuda-source payroll and land holdings; no entity-level income tax applies.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no domestic check-the-box or fiscal transparency election. Partners may elect separate legal personality under Section 12 of the Exempted Partnerships Act; foreign hybrid treatment (e.g., US check-the-box) is available solely under foreign tax law and has no Bermuda domestic tax effect.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Duties and Stamp Duties (no VAT/GST system)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Bermuda has no VAT/GST registration threshold. Importers of dutiable goods must obtain a customs broker accreditation and pay applicable customs duties at time of clearance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable; Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST on goods or services. Goods exported from Bermuda are outside the scope of customs duties, which apply only to imports; offshore services are not subject to Bermuda indirect tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration via ASYCUDA World
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill

No electronic invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate for VAT. Bermuda Customs Service uses ASYCUDA World for electronic submission of import/export declarations; no real-time fiscal API or digital stamping required.

Penalties for false export declarations arise under the Customs Tariff Act 1980, including fines up to $10,000 and forfeiture of goods. Because no VAT exists, there is no VAT clawback mechanism; however, payroll tax non-compliance (e.g., failure to remit employee deductions) attracts separate penalties, interest, and recovery action by the Tax Commissioner.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 parity to the US dollar (USD) by statute. Bermuda maintains no exchange-control legislation; both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible without official approval. Profit remittance, partner distributions, liquidation proceeds, and intra-group disbursements from an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) are unrestricted in any freely usable currency. The United Kingdom likewise operates a fully liberalised, exchange-control-free capital regime following the repeal of the Exchange Control Act 1979, creating a fully fungible GBP/USD corridor for the UK parent and Bermuda ELP. The only practical hurdles are AML/ATF documentation, sanctions screening, and the BMA's economic substance and beneficial ownership reporting obligations, none of which restricts convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rules exist in Bermuda or the United Kingdom. The ELP is not required to repatriate, convert, or surrender any percentage of export receipts, including Apple App Store and Google Play settlement inflows. Settlement proceeds may be retained indefinitely in USD, converted to GBP at commercial rate, or held in multi-currency accounts at Bermuda-licensed banks at the sole discretion of the General Partner. No central-bank conversion ratio, mandated timeline, or balance-sheet ceiling applies.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, acting as the statutory financial services regulator, central monetary institution, and supervisory authority for foreign exchange and clearing conduct in Bermuda. The Bank of England (BoE) exercises sovereign monetary oversight over the United Kingdom only and holds no exchange-control authority over Bermuda, a British Overseas Territory with fiscal autonomy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-Low feasibility for cross-border use of FinTech Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs). UK-licensed EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, and Modulr permit remote multi-currency account opening, but their automated underwriting engines frequently classify ISIC 5820 software publishers with virtual slots/social casino mechanics as gambling-adjacent high risk, resulting in upward of 30-60% policy-decline probability at application stage. EMIs that do approve typically impose monthly volume ceilings, transaction velocity caps, and recurring Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) reviews. Legal viability is nevertheless defensible because a simulated social casino product with no real-money wagering, no cash-in/cash-out, and no prize redemption does not constitute licensable gambling services under the UK Gambling Act 2005; however, EMIs can lawfully decline based on independent internal business-risk appetite. Bermuda itself has no standalone EMI licence category, so the ELP must rely on full Bermuda-licensed banks as primary treasury counterparties and may use UK EMIs only as secondary settlement rails after obtaining written compliance pre-approval.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections from the UK parent or Limited Partners must be executed by SWIFT MT103 credit transfer in GBP or USD to the ELP's designated account at HSBC Bank Bermuda Limited or Butterfield Bank. Remittance instruction Field 70 must state 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION / ELP PARTNERSHIP CONTRIBUTION' together with the BMA-registered ELP number; Field 72T is used to append the beneficiary identifier and reference to the Capital Contribution Schedule. USD transfers route via Fedwire/CHIPS through Butterfield's New York correspondent or HSBC's New York clearing branch; GBP transfers route via CHAPS to the bank's London correspondent. Amounts exceeding USD 50,000 per instruction are supported by a General Partner resolution and an updated capital schedule, and the bank's FX desk confirms no mandatory conversion into BMD given the fixed 1:1 peg; funds are maintained in USD ledger currency to match Apple/Google settlement receipts. For FATCA/CRS purposes, the remittance narrative must include the UK parent's UTR and the ELP's BMA reference to avoid disclosure mismatches.Outbound disbursements are cleared through three complementary rails: (i) operational supplier, royalty, and licence payments via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers priced in GBP through CHAPS or Faster Payments, with supporting invoices attached for all individual disbursements above USD 15,000; (ii) large-scale development or content-licensing agreements via commercial documentary Letters of Credit issued under SWIFT MT700 and governed by UCP600, with confirmation by a top-25 UK clearing bank where counterparty credit risk justifies confirmation; and (iii) treasury concentration via HSBC Bermudas dual-currency notional pooling engine, enabling USD and GBP end-of-day sweeps into a single interest-bearing ledger. Apple Payments and Google Payments USD settlements are received directly into the ELP's USD account through the app-store merchant settlement network; any forced currency conversion is limited to the pool bank's posted commercial rate. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% under Bermuda's ordinary tax system for an exempted limited partnership; 15% under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA) only if the ELP is an in-scope Bermuda Business Entity that is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no dividend withholding tax and no withholding tax on partnership distributions to non-resident partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

CIT-1 is due within 12 months after the end of the ELP's fiscal year; the exempted partnership annual declaration is due on or before the anniversary date prescribed by the Registrar of Companies.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annually before the ELP's registration anniversary; an extension is generally available for not more than 60 calendar days where a valid reason is certified by the registered agent.
