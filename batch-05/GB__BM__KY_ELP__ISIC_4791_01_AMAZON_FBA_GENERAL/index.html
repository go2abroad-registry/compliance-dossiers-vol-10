Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — UK national tax authority (Gov.UK, www.gov.uk/hmrc) for VAT and direct tax supervision; Bermuda Registrar of Companies and Office of the Tax Commissioner (www.gov.bm) for Bermuda entity registration, Bermuda Business Number (BBN), payroll tax and customs duties.

E-government filing portal

Register the Bermuda ELP under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies through a licensed agent; obtain the Certificate of Registration; then apply for a Bermuda Business Number (BBN) via the Bermuda Business Portal. If employees are engaged, register for Bermuda payroll tax and social insurance with the Tax Commissioner. For UK VAT, file a non-established taxable person (NETP) registration with HMRC via the Government Gateway, providing the ELP certificate, BBN, partnership agreement, UK establishment or agent details, and evidence of UK taxable supplies; HMRC will issue a UK VAT registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is transparent for Bermuda and UK tax purposes; no Bermuda entity-level income tax, and partners are separately taxed on their shares. The ELP may hold non-resident owner status and does not incur Bermuda capital or withholding taxes on distributions.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. For US federal tax, the ELP may use Form 8832 to affirm partnership classification under Treas. Reg. §301.7701-3. In the UK, the ELP is treated as a partnership without a corporate election, but the UK hybrid mismatch rules under CTA 2010 Part 6TA may require adjustments where a deduction is duplicated across Bermuda, UK, or third jurisdictions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 (GBP) taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period for UK-established businesses; a non-established Bermuda ELP has no registration threshold and must register with HMRC as soon as it makes any taxable supplies in the UK or holds FBA stock in the UK for onward supply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from the UK to a non-UK destination within 3 months of the time of supply qualify for zero-rating under VATA 1994 s.30 and VAT Notice 703 if the seller obtains and retains suitable export evidence. Amazon FBA stock in the UK sold to UK consumers is standard-rated; FBA stock dispatched from the UK to non-UK consumers may be zero-rated on proof of export. B2B services supplied to non-UK customers are outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rules. For input VAT recovery on export-related costs, the ELP must retain the same export documents supporting the zero-rated supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic acceptance through the Customs Declaration Service or C88/SAD legacy printout)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or carrier-issued consignment note showing the goods left the UK
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and purchase order identifying the Bermuda ELP, the non-UK buyer, and the goods
  • Inward remittance certificate or equivalent bank payment evidence (SWIFT confirmation, card settlement record) evidencing export proceeds received by the ELP

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls apply. Since the full rollout of Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT, all VAT-registered businesses must maintain digital VAT records and submit VAT returns using MTD-compatible software through HMRC's MTD API. A VAT invoice must be issued within 14 days of the supply; e-invoicing is voluntary under UK law.

HMRC can disallow zero-rating, assess unpaid VAT plus interest, and apply penalties of up to 100% of the tax for deliberate inaccuracies under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24. The ELP must preserve all export evidence for at least 6 years; failure invalidates the zero-rating and exposes the ELP to repayment of input VAT and clawback of refunds. Fraudulent claims are subject to criminal sanctions under VATA 1994 s.72, with retrospective assessments up to 20 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US dollar (USD) at a fixed 1:1 rate, with USD circulating freely as de facto legal tender. Bermuda maintains no meaningful current-account or capital-account convertibility restriction for exempted partnerships. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (as extended to Bermuda) and the BMA's delegated administration, capital movements by an Exempted Limited Partnership are effected under general permission, and profit remittances to the UK parent are not subject to prior approval, exchange allocation, or Bermudian withholding tax. The only practical hurdles are bank-level documentary requirements: (i) valid registration under the Limited Partnerships Act 2017; (ii) compliance with the Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2018 if a relevant activity is in scope; and (iii) a beneficial-ownership declaration satisfying the BMA AML/ATF framework and the Bermuda beneficial-ownership register.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export-receipt currencies. Amazon FBA settlement proceeds received in USD may be retained in full in a Bermudian account without any BMA conversion obligation. The 1:1 BMD/USD peg eliminates exchange-rate attribution risk, and the ELP may elect to maintain USD-denominated operational and escrow accounts with no obligation to convert into BMD.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: (1) the UK parent executes a capital contribution resolution referencing the Partnership Agreement and evidencing value transfer to the ELP's capital account; (2) remittance is effected by SWIFT MT103 from a UK bank using ISO 20022 purpose code 'CAPI' (capital injection) with a narration containing the ELP legal name, contribution reference, and Partnership Agreement clause; (3) the Bermuda receiving bank applies the BMA general-permission exchange-control treatment and posts the credit as contributed capital; (4) the UK remitter must retain the MT103, SWIFT UETR, sanctions-screening evidence, and FATCA/CRS documentation. Where an FCA-regulated EMI is used as a conduit, the EMI sweep must terminate at the BMA-licensed Bermuda master account, and the EMI must not open any US bridge account without prior Bermuda bank consent. The receiving bank independently verifies the sender, purpose code, and UBO list before releasing funds to operational accounts.Primary clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 direct transfers for supplier payments covering inventory, freight forwarders, and warehousing, with purpose narration 'goods trade settlement'; (ii) commercial documentary letters of credit (sight or usance) issued by Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda for large inventory batches, complying with UCP 600 and Incoterms 2020; (iii) Amazon disbursements settled via ACH, SEPA, and Payoneer or Airwallex virtual IBANs into the ELP's Bermudian operational account, subsequently streamed to suppliers via MT103; (iv) operating disbursements below USD 5,000 may be executed through the EMI's local rails (SEPA Instant, UK Faster Payments) strictly as pass-through subsistence costs, provided each disbursement carries the ELP's unique narration and the residual balance is swept to the Bermuda master account within 48 hours.Vanilla deliverable FX forwards (USD/GBP and USD/EUR) executed on the ELP's own account with a BMA-licensed bank or FCA-licensed counterparty, documented as commercial hedging of Amazon settlement exposure and not as speculative dealing.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for third-country emerging-market currencies (e.g., CNY, INR) arising from supplier payments or marketplace receipts where a deliverable BMD/USD/GBP market fixing is unavailable in the Bermudian interbank market.Cross-currency swaps (USD/GBP and USD/EUR) employed to structurally match the UK parent's GBP liability base with the USD-denominated Amazon FBA inventory procurement cycle.Zero-premium FX collar options (put/call combinations) with a BMA-licensed or FCA-licensed counterparty, used solely to cap the conversion cost on repatriated profits to the UK parent.
