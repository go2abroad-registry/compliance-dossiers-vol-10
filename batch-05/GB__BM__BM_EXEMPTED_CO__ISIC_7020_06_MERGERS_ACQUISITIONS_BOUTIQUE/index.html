E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Bermuda Exempted Company must: (1) incorporate under the Companies Act 1981 through a licensed registered agent; (2) receive a Certificate of Incorporation and a unique Company Registration Number from the Registrar of Companies; (3) obtain a Business Permit from the Ministry of Finance if conducting business in Bermuda; (4) if employing individuals, register under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 and obtain a Payroll Tax Registration Number; (5) file an annual company declaration and an Economic Substance Declaration. No corporate TIN is issued because exempted companies are taxed at 0% on income; the Company Registration Number serves as the primary identifier for tax-related filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box entity classification election or hybrid mismatch election comparable to the United States or OECD jurisdictions. The sole formal election is the voluntary designation as an 'Exempted Company' under the Companies Act 1981, conferring tax-exempt status subject to annual fees and economic substance compliance. Pass-through treatment is not available. No hybrid mismatch elections under local law apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Bermuda levies no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax. The only indirect taxes are customs duties on imported goods and stamp duties on certain instruments and transfers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – there is no VAT/GST registration threshold. For customs purposes, any person or entity importing goods into Bermuda must register with Bermuda Customs & Revenue as the importer of record and obtain an importer code; no economic turnover threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, and therefore no export zero-rating mechanism exists. Exports of physical goods from Bermuda are outside the scope of any Bermudian indirect tax. Offshore services, including cross-border M&A advisory services, are not subject to Bermuda indirect taxation. No tax refund is claimable because no output tax is chargeable.

  • Not applicable for VAT purposes. For physical exports, Bermuda Customs requires a Customs Export Declaration (BERCUS electronic declaration), commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, and any applicable export permit for restricted goods.

No mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime applies in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs operates the Bermuda Customs Automated System (BERCUS) for electronic submission of import/export declarations. Commercial invoices are required only as supporting documentation for customs valuation and clearance; there is no API-based fiscal invoice clearing obligation.

Because there is no VAT/GST zero-rating regime, no export zero-rating fraud penalties or VAT clawbacks exist. However, misdeclaration of customs value or false export documentation attracts penalties under the Bermuda Customs Tariff Act 1995, including fines up to twice the duty evaded and potential imprisonment. M&A advisory export income is not taxable in Bermuda; no clawback applies. Non-compliance with economic substance filing obligations under the Economic Substance Act 2018 may result in financial penalties up to BMD 250,000 plus possible criminal sanctions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the USD and circulates interchangeably with USD across the economy. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, current-account transactions (advisory fees, operating expenses, professional services) are freely convertible for Exempted Companies holding Designated Status, which is the standard general permission granted to BM_EXEMPTED_CO. Capital-account transactions comprising share capital injections, shareholder loans, dividend remittances, and liquidation distributions are freely permitted for Designated Status entities without prior BMA approval, subject to post-transaction reporting. Non-designated entities require individual BMA consent via Exchange Control Forms EC1/EC2 for transfers involving non-scheduled territories. Profit remittance to the UK parent is tax-free in Bermuda: no income tax, no withholding tax on dividends, and no capital gains tax apply under the exempted company regime, leaving statutory remittance hurdles at nil for a compliant Designated Status company.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda has no mandatory percentage-based surrender or conversion ratio for export proceeds applicable to advisory services. A Designated Status entity retains all foreign-currency receipts (USD, GBP, EUR success fees) in multi-currency accounts with no obligation to convert to BMD. For a non-designated entity, Section 16 of the Exchange Control Act 1972 requires that foreign currency received from non-scheduled territories be offered for sale to an authorised dealer within a reasonable administrative period (BMA practice: 30 days from receipt), unless BMA retention permission is granted. The M&A advisory boutique will obtain Designated Status at incorporation, eliminating any conversion or surrender obligation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), exercising statutory powers under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972; the BMA serves as the exchange control authority for Bermuda and supervises all licensed banks in relation to Exempted Companies and cross-border capital flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium viability for pure offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business). Bermuda has no standalone EMI licensing regime, and the BMA restricts deposit-taking to licensed banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. An offshore EMI cannot serve as the primary treasury rail for the Bermuda entity because it bypasses BMA exchange-control reporting, fails the AMLA customer-due-diligence standard for a licensed bank, and typically cannot service success-fee flows above GBP 500,000 for a Bermuda-domiciled Medium-Risk Enhanced client. Feasibility rating: Low as a substitute for a Tier-1 bank; Medium as a supplementary collection rail solely for the UK parent side, provided the EMI maintains a BMA-recognised correspondent and the Bermuda operating account remains at a licensed domestic bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies; Bermuda has no corporate income tax or direct TP filing requirement. An exempted company in an international M&A advisory group may nevertheless face group-level transfer pricing documentation obligations in a parent jurisdiction if the group exceeds relevant revenue thresholds, but no Local File or Master File arises within Bermuda.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to an exempted M&A advisory boutique classified as a distribution and service business. The entity must demonstrate adequate physical premises in Bermuda, qualified full-time employees, sufficient operating expenditure, and board meetings held at adequate intervals in Bermuda. Core income-generating activities, including deal sourcing, valuation, structuring, negotiation, and client advisory sign-off, must be performed or directed from Bermuda. The pure equity holding entity exemption does not apply to a fee-earning service business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Facilitation of Foreign Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017, a corporate offence may arise for failure to prevent the facilitation of tax evasion by an associated person; penalties include unlimited fines, confiscation orders, and public naming. In Bermuda, knowing presentation of false information in a statutory declaration or ESR filing can result in monetary penalties up to BMD 50,000 and criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in evasion are subject to civil liability for dishonest assistance, disqualification, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to 7 years where UK criminal evasion offences are proven; the corporate veil may be pierced where the company is a facade for the director's personal tax avoidance.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda ESR 2018, the Registrar may impose BMD 10,000 for the first offence, BMD 25,000 for the second, and BMD 50,000 for subsequent or false declarations; the entity may be struck off, and the Exemption Certificate may be revoked, exposing future profits to any newly enacted tax.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified for up to 5 years, made personally liable for unpaid penalties, subjected to travel restrictions, and imprisoned up to 5 years for knowingly filing a false substance declaration; restitution can be ordered where shell profits have been extracted as dividends.
AML and Investment Business Licensing Breach

Statutory sanction: Acting without the required investment business licence is punishable by fines up to BMD 250,000 and the revocation of the exempted company's registration; AML breaches can result in penalties up to BMD 1,000,000, forfeiture, and imprisonment up to 5 years. The Bermuda Monetary Authority may suspend or remove directors and impose compliance costs on the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors carry a statutory personal duty to implement CDD, screening, record-keeping, and SAR filing. Breach creates personal criminal liability, civil restitution to victims or the Crown, director disqualification, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 5 years; the corporate veil will be pierced for fraudulent misuse of the exempted structure.
