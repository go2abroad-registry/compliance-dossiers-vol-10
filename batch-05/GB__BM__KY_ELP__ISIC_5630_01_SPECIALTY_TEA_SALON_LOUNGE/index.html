Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda (British Overseas Territory): Ministry of Finance; Office of the Tax Commissioner (Payroll Tax Act 1995, Land Tax); Bermuda Customs Department (Customs Tariff Act 1968); Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Economy and Labour (Exempted Partnerships Act 2015). Official government portal: https://www.gov.bm. HM Revenue & Customs remains the relevant authority only at the UK/sovereign level and has no direct role in Bermuda domestic registration.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Form the ELP with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015 (no minimum capital requirement; non-resident ownership permitted); the Registrar issues a Bermuda partnership registration number. Step 2 - No income-tax TIN or VAT ID is issued because Bermuda has no income tax and no VAT/GST. Step 3 - If the ELP employs staff in Bermuda, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax (Form P1 registration; annual P2/P3 returns). Step 4 - If importing tea or ceramics, engage a licensed Bermuda customs broker and obtain a customs import account for BerTrade declarations. Step 5 - For FATCA/CRS self-certification, cite the Registrar of Companies number as the functional identifier; a general-purpose Bermuda TIN does not exist.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible | Tax_Exempt_Zone | Territorial_Scope. The ELP is flow-through with no entity-level income tax; Bermuda does not tax capital gains, dividends, interest, or distributions. Liability exposure is limited to Bermuda payroll tax (employment nexus) and customs import duty (consumption nexus). Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 (15% OECD Pillar Two domestic top-up, effective fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025) applies only to constituent entities of MNE groups with at least EUR 750 million global revenue; a standalone specialty tea salon ELP structure is outside that scope.

Available election forms

Bermuda law provides no domestic check-the-box election because no income tax exists. US partners may file Form 8832 under Treas. Reg. § 301.7701-3 to elect classification; the default is partnership because the ELP has at least one general partner with unlimited liability. For UK tax purposes, the ELP is transparent by default and no election is required. Hybrid mismatch rules (OECD BEPS Action 2 as implemented in the UK through TIOPA 2010 Part 6A, ITA 2007 Part 13, and CTA 2010 Part 6) may deny deductions or require income inclusion if the ELP is treated as opaque in a third jurisdiction while treated as transparent in Bermuda/UK.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general sales tax exists in Bermuda. Indirect-type levies are limited to: (i) customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1968; and (ii) employer-paid payroll tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995, which taxes the payroll base rather than consumer transactions.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None for VAT/GST because no such system exists. Payroll tax registration: any employer with Bermuda payroll must register; 2024-2025 rates are 5.5% on the first BMD 487,500 of annual remuneration per employee, with excess taxed at 13% (2024) and at 10% from 1 January 2025. Customs: an import account is required for importers of goods; no turnover threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable domestically: Bermuda levies no VAT/GST, so there is no export zero-rating claim, no output tax to relieve, and no input-VAT credit or refund regime. Exports of physical goods from Bermuda are simply outside the customs duty base and are generally unrestricted. UK-group note: if the UK parent supplies tea or ceramics to the Bermuda ELP, the UK export is zero-rated for UK VAT subject to HMRC commercial evidence requirements (invoice, export declaration, transport proof). Bermuda applies no exchange controls and the Bermuda dollar is convertible at par with USD, so no statutory FX repatriation period exists.

  • Bermuda Customs export declaration filed electronically via the BerTrade National Single Window
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Bill of lading, air waybill, or other transport document evidencing physical export from Bermuda
  • Where applicable, Bermuda sanitary, phytosanitary, or restricted-goods export permit (tea and ceramicware are generally exempt from licensing)

No e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs operates the BerTrade National Single Window for electronic import/export declarations via licensed brokers, but this is a border-control filing mechanism, not an invoice-clearance regime. No digital stamping, continuous transaction controls, or government e-invoice clearance API applies to tea salon retail receipts.

Bermuda: no VAT fraud or clawback regime exists because no VAT is imposed; customs offences under the Customs Tariff Act 1968 may trigger forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties, and imprisonment for smuggling or false declarations. UK-group exposure: fraudulent export zero-rating or VAT-refund claims by a UK member are criminal offences under VATA 1994 s. 72; HMRC may make retrospective VAT assessments (normally 4 years, up to 20 years where dishonesty is proven), impose civil evasion penalties up to 100% of the VAT, and pursue criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda's currency, the Bermudian dollar (BMD), is pegged to the US dollar at 1:1 and is freely convertible on both current and capital accounts. The BMA administers a liberal exchange control regime: no prior approval is required for current account payments (trade settlement, fees, distributions) or capital account movements (capital contributions, loan repayments, partner withdrawals). An Exempted Limited Partnership may hold multi-currency account balances without restriction. Profit remittance to non-resident limited partners faces no withholding tax or repatriation hurdle, provided the ELP maintains its exempted status, files the annual BMA declaration, pays the annual government fee, and keeps the register of limited partners current under Bermuda's exempted-partnerships and limited-partnership legislation (as amended). In the United Kingdom, exchange control was abolished; no UK authority restricts the convertibility of sterling or foreign-currency receipts. Net assessment: full convertibility in both jurisdictions, with zero remittance or approval frictions for a non-resident-owned ELP operating a UK specialty tea salon.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Neither Bermuda nor the United Kingdom imposes statutory surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion ratios on export proceeds. The specialty tea salon is predominantly an importer of loose-leaf tea and cultural ceramic ware; to the extent any wholesale tea or ceramic ware is exported, the ELP may retain proceeds in any invoicing currency, hold them in offshore accounts, and deploy them for disbursements without a prescribed timeline or conversion obligation. Bermuda's exchange control regime requires only that Bermuda-resident payments be routed through a BMA-licensed bank; no quota, surrender window, or conversion rate applies.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - the sole financial services regulator and exchange control authority for Bermuda; for sterling-denominated clearing and payment services in the United Kingdom, the Bank of England and HM Treasury exercise concurrent regulatory oversight.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Contributing limited partners instruct their own banks to remit via SWIFT MT103/MT202 to the ELP's Butterfield Capital Escrow Account or HSBC Bermuda Operational Account. UK-origin contributions are sent by CHAPS or Faster Payment for amounts below GBP 1 million, with the payment reference 'Capital contribution to Bermuda ELP - [Partner Name]'; US-origin contributions use Fedwire with the beneficiary BIC, account number, and the same reference. The receiving Bermuda bank requires a funds-receipt instruction identifying the contributor, the ELP, and the intended partnership capital account credit. No BMA pre-approval or exchange-control declaration is required for inbound capital; the ELP must update its register of limited partners and include the capital movement in its annual BMA return. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general rate in Bermuda for entities outside the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024; 15% for constituent entities of MNE Groups with annual consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more; a UK-resident corporate partner may be subject to UK corporation tax at 25% on its attributable share of the transparent ELP income if the ELP is not excluded from a permanent establishment in the UK.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% under Bermuda domestic law. Distributions made by a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership are not subject to Bermuda withholding tax. UK-resident corporate partners remain subject to UK corporation tax on their allocated profit shares unless an applicable UK exemption applies; this is a partner-level tax, not a Bermuda WHT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general Bermuda transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to non-CIT entities; in practical filing terms, the requirement is nil and indicated as BMD 0. Under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, mandatory transfer pricing documentation and Local File/Master File alignment arise only where the MNE Group has annual consolidated revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more, approximately BMD 810,000,000 at the pegged exchange rate. UK-resident corporate partners may come under UK transfer pricing rules where the relevant value of related-party transactions exceeds HMRC designatory limits.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations apply to entities carrying on relevant activities. A specialty tea salon and cultural lounge is not automatically a relevant activity. However, if the ELP books distribution revenue, maintains intellectual property, manages Bermuda-based financing, or performs headquarters functions, it must demonstrate substance in Bermuda through locally resident directors or general partners, employees, business premises, and expenditure proportionate to the activity. The ELP must also maintain a registered office and registered agent in Bermuda. A declaration that no relevant activity is carried on will be challenged where the actual revenue, contracts, and management conduct contradict the filing.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Economic Substance Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018, any person who provides false or misleading information is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding BD$250,000 and imprisonment of up to seven years. UK HMRC may also apply offshore anti-avoidance counteractions, accelerated payment notices, and penalties of up to 200% of the tax at risk where the arrangement lacks commercial substance.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner may be pursued personally, the court may pierce the partnership limitation where the structure is a façade, and civil restitution may attach to personal assets. Bermuda courts may impose travel bans during enforcement and disqualify directors for up to 15 years.
Shell Company and Artificial Loss-Absorbing Structure

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Registrar may strike off the ELP, revoke its exempted status, and impose daily penalties. A finding of an offshore tax evasion arrangement can trigger UK penalties under Finance Act 2015 and user-generated penalties equal to 200% of the tax. The ELP may also lose its pass-through benefits if the general partner is found to be a nominee controlled by the UK beneficial owner.

Director personal liability: The individuals directing the arrangement can be treated as shadow directors under both Bermuda and UK law. Personal liability includes disgorgement of profits, contribution orders in an insolvency, disqualification, travel restrictions, and imprisonment of up to seven years for fraudulent trading or deliberate non-compliance.
