Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

United Kingdom: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), HMRC online services at GOV.UK (www.gov.uk/hmrc). Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Government portal (www.gov.bm), and the Bermuda Registrar of Companies for entity registration.

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership must first be registered with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies, obtaining a Bermuda Registrar Number and partnership certificate. Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax or VAT identification number unless the ELP is a constituent entity of a multinational group subject to the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (group revenue at least EUR 750 million in two of the prior four years), in which case it must enrol with the Bermuda Tax Commissioner and obtain a Bermuda tax reference. For UK VAT, a non-UK-established entity making any taxable supply in the United Kingdom must register with HMRC using the non-established taxable person procedure, submitting form VAT1 or the partnership-specific equivalent online through the Government Gateway; HMRC issues a UK VAT registration number. There is no UK VAT registration threshold for non-UK-established sellers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

The ELP may file US Form 8832 check-the-box election to be treated as a pass-through partnership or disregarded entity, or alternatively as an association taxable as a corporation. The UK has no unilateral company classification election; an ELP is generally transparent under UK partnership tax principles unless central management and control is exercised in the UK. Hybrid mismatch rules under Part 6A TIOPA 2010 and Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax/GloBE framework require coordinated treatment and, where permitted, elections to prevent deductions in one jurisdiction without a corresponding taxable inclusion in another.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 0 for non-UK-established sellers making any taxable supply in the United Kingdom; GBP 90,000 for UK-established businesses. Bermuda has no VAT/GST registration threshold; importers must comply with Bermuda Customs declaration requirements and pay applicable import duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

POD custom apparel exported from the United Kingdom to an overseas destination qualifies for 0% VAT if the goods are physically exported within the statutory timeframe, the sale is made to an overseas customer, and HMRC-approved commercial and customs evidence is retained. Goods shipped from Bermuda are outside UK VAT scope and cannot create UK export zero-rating. POD design and related services supplied to overseas business customers generally fall outside UK VAT under place-of-supply rules or may be zero-rated where the recipient belongs outside the UK. A non-UK-established taxable person may reclaim UK input VAT, such as UK fulfillment and logistics costs, using HMRC's VAT refund procedure and must retain original invoices, evidence of business use, and supporting documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Declaration Service entry summary)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / foreign-currency or international payment receipt
  • Commercial invoice, POD order file, and packing list
  • Delivery confirmation and tracking data evidencing export and final overseas delivery

UK VAT does not impose mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. Making Tax Digital requires digital record-keeping and submission of VAT returns through HMRC-approved API software. Goods exported from Great Britain must be declared electronically through the Customs Declaration Service, with safety and security declarations where relevant. Bermuda operates an electronic customs clearance system for imports and exports, but does not impose continuous transactional clearance.

HMRC may disallow zero-rating and assess VAT at 20% if documentation is incomplete or inconsistent. Careless or deliberate inaccuracies attract penalties up to 30% to 100% of the potential lost revenue under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007, plus statutory interest; fraudulent export claims may result in criminal prosecution under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 and the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979. Retrospective clawbacks typically cover four years of under-declared VAT, extended to 20 years for fraud. Bermuda customs misdeclarations may lead to seizure of goods, monetary penalties, and suspension of customs privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) and is freely convertible into USD through authorised banks. Current-account transactions (trade settlements, service fees, royalties, platform fees) are unrestricted and require no BMA consent. Capital-account transactions, including partner capital contributions and distributions, are subject to exchange control but benefit from BMA General Permission when the entity holds 'non-resident' designation. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) that is wholly owned by non-residents, has no resident partner, and conducts all business outside Bermuda may obtain non-resident exchange-control status, which confers unrestricted foreign-currency account operation and free remittance of profits and distributions without advance BMA approval. Profit remittance hurdles are de minimis: Bermuda levies no withholding tax on ELP distributions, and partner payouts are executed by normal bank transfer with BMA eForm statistical reporting only — no remittance licence is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Act 1972 imposes a statutory surrender obligation on persons 'resident' in Bermuda who become entitled to foreign currency: the foreign currency must be offered for sale to an authorised dealer within one month of entitlement, representing a default surrender ratio of 100% of qualifying foreign-currency export receipts. For a Bermuda ELP designated as 'non-resident' by the BMA, this obligation is disapplied: the mandatory conversion ratio is 0%, and export proceeds may be retained, held and disbursed in USD, GBP or EUR operational accounts without forced conversion. If non-resident designation is not obtained or is revoked, the resident regime applies strictly, and the BMA may require surrender of export proceeds to the authorised dealer within the statutory one-month timeline unless a specific exemption or special permission has been granted.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the statutory monetary, regulatory and exchange-control authority acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972 (as amended). The BMA issues exchange-control permissions, administers the eForm statistical reporting regime, and supervises all licensed banks in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM as a supplementary clearing rail; LOW as a replacement for a Tier-1 Bermuda licensed bank. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) will generally onboard a Bermuda-domiciled ELP only with transparent beneficial ownership, a physical director, and a meaningful operating history; many decline offshore partnerships with corporate General Partners. Legal constraints: (i) provision of money transmission, currency exchange or payment services to persons in Bermuda requires a licence under the Bermuda Money Service Business Act 2016; (ii) the BMA does not recognise EEA or UK EMI passporting into Bermuda, so a UK FCA-authorised EMI cannot operate in Bermuda without a local licence; (iii) USD settlement into most EMIs is routed through US partner banks that frequently refuse Bermuda counterparties on correspondent-risk grounds; (iv) funds held with an unlicensed EMI are not protected deposits and cannot serve as statutory escrow or capital reserves. Recommended usage: outbound GBP/EUR supplier payouts through the UK digital-trade hub, with records retained for five years under BMA general permission.

Legal constraints: (i) provision of money transmission, currency exchange or payment services to persons in Bermuda requires a licence under the Bermuda Money Service Business Act 2016; (ii) the BMA does not recognise EEA or UK EMI passporting into Bermuda, so a UK FCA-authorised EMI cannot operate in Bermuda without a local licence; (iii) USD settlement into most EMIs is routed through US partner banks that frequently refuse Bermuda counterparties on correspondent-risk grounds; (iv) funds held with an unlicensed EMI are not protected deposits and cannot serve as statutory escrow or capital reserves. Recommended usage: outbound GBP/EUR supplier payouts through the UK digital-trade hub, with records retained for five years under BMA general permission.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general TP documentation is required in Bermuda. The OECD Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR) filing threshold in Bermuda is BMD 750,000,000 (or USD equivalent) consolidated group revenue for MNE groups; no Local File or Master File is statutorily mandated.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 require entities carrying on 'relevant activity' (banking, insurance, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, shipping, IP holding, holding entity) to satisfy economic substance in Bermuda. E-commerce retail (POD custom apparel) is not a relevant activity unless it is conducted as a 'pure equity holding' or holds IP separately. Because the ELP is tax-transparent, substance is assessed at each partner level; non-resident partners without a permanent establishment in Bermuda have no local substance requirement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Registration Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: On conviction: fine up to BD$10,000 for the partnership and up to BD$20,000 and/or 5 years' imprisonment for officers and general partners (Bermuda Criminal Code, Theft Act 2019); in addition, the Registrar may strike off the ELP and the UK HMRC may apply to wind it up as a 'mushroom' company.

Director personal liability: Directors/general partners are personally liable for restitution of unpaid UK taxes if the ELP is treated as a sham; UK HMRC can pierce the partnership veil where it is a facade; travel bans and director disqualification orders up to 15 years are available under UK CDDA 1986 only for UK-connected activities.
Shell Company / ESR Breach

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Tax Authority may levy a penalty of BD$10,000 per annum for failure to satisfy the economic substance test; subsequent years: BD$100,000; persistent failure may lead to enforcement and exchange of information with UK HMRC under the Bermuda-UK Tax Information Exchange Agreement (TIEA).

Director personal liability: General partners of an ELP, as controlling officers, may be personally fined and disqualified; a direction may require the ELP to be struck off; for UK-connected partners, HMRC may apply targeted anti-avoidance rules to attribute income personally.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Beneficial Ownership (Bermuda) Amendment Act 2018: fines up to BD$5,000 per day per offence; under the Proceeds of Crime (Amendment) Act 2007, fines and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; AML supervision by the Bermuda Monetary Authority for FSPs only, but e-commerce retail is normally exempt as low-risk.

Director personal liability: General partners and beneficial owners face personal fines and imprisonment; the court may issue a confiscation order against personal assets; the ELP account may be frozen and Stripe/PayPal merchant account terminated.
