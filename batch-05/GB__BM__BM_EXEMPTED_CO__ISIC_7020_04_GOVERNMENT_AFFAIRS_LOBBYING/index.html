Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Government of Bermuda (Ministry of Finance); Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance; Bermuda Customs Department. Government portal: www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 by filing the Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association with the Registrar of Companies; the Certificate of Incorporation is issued after fee payment. No income-tax TIN exists because Bermuda levies no income tax. Upon hiring the first employee, the company must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain a Payroll Tax account number and file monthly returns. If goods are imported, the company must register with the Bermuda Customs Department for import declarations. An exempted company with premises or business activity in Bermuda must obtain a Trade Business License under the Trade Business Licensing Act 2013; non-resident companies without local premises are typically exempt from licensing but must file annual government fees with the Registrar of Companies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - The Bermuda Exempted Company is not liable to Bermuda income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, or VAT; it is subject only to payroll tax, social insurance contributions, customs and stamp duties, and annual government fees payable to the Registrar of Companies and Corporate Service Provider.

Available election forms

Bermuda law provides no check-the-box or hybrid election because Bermuda has no income tax. For US federal income tax purposes, a Bermuda company incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 is a per se corporation under Treas. Reg. §301.7701-2(b)(8)(i), ineligible for pass-through or disregarded-entity status via IRS Form 8832. For UK purposes, the entity is treated as a non-UK resident body corporate, taxable only on a UK permanent establishment; UK hybrid mismatch rules (Part 6A TIOPA 2010) and CFC rules (Part 9A CTA 2010) may apply, but no statutory election can make the Bermuda company fiscally transparent. Any hybrid arrangement is analysed under OECD/G20 BEPS Action 2.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Tax in Bermuda; indirect taxation comprises Customs Duties (Customs Tariff Act 2012), Payroll Tax (Payroll Tax Act 1995), Social Insurance Contributions, Stamp Duties, and Hotel Occupancy/Tourism Taxes.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST and therefore no economic-turnover threshold exists for indirect-tax registration. Employer registration for Payroll Tax is mandatory immediately upon the first hire; customs registration applies only at the first import transaction. A Trade Business License is required for physical business presence, with annual fees based on class and sector; professional services may qualify for remission where no local trade occurs.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda levies no VAT, GST, sales tax, or export tax, so there is no 0% export rate to claim and no refund mechanism for input tax. The lobbying advisory services supplied to foreign principals are outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxation. The company may obtain a written ruling from the Office of the Tax Commissioner confirming no payroll tax exposure on non-resident contractors and may seek customs duty relief from the Ministry of Finance for temporary importation of professional equipment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar and benefits from full and unrestricted convertibility on both current and capital account transactions. Profit remittance and capital repatriation by a Bermuda Exempted Company require no exchange-control approval. The BMA imposes no quantitative limits on inward or outward transfers; nevertheless, banks apply standard AML/CTF screening and documented source-of-funds assessments to every cross-border transfer, which are the only practical hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender requirements exist in Bermuda. Statutory provisions mandate neither compulsory repatriation timelines nor obligatory conversion ratios for foreign-currency export receipts. Bermuda's legal framework treats all export proceeds as freely disposable, subject solely to banking record-keeping and sanctions compliance.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low-Medium. Offshore digital EMI providers (e.g., electronic-money institutions licensed in the UK or EU) are not authorised under Bermuda's Banking Act 1969 to take deposits or hold client funds in Bermuda. They cannot act as a settlement hub for the exempted company's primary treasury operations. Their permissible use is limited to ancillary payment routing and card issuance, subject to the EMI's own risk appetite; however, the enhanced risk profile of a government-affairs lobbying firm causes most EMIs to decline onboarding. Legal constraints include the absence of any passporting recognition for EMIs in Bermuda and the need for BMA consent if any e-money issuance or payments-account activity were deemed to occur within Bermuda.

Compliant inward remittances utilise the SWIFT MT103 or MT202 network through BMD/USD correspondent banking lines. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope multinational groups; otherwise 0% CIT for Bermuda Exempted Companies outside the Pillar Two scope.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda Exempted Company.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

15th day of the 15th month after fiscal year-end, with an extended 18-month filing window for the first in-scope reporting period. Out-of-scope Bermuda Exempted Companies are not required to file Form CIT-1 unless a CIT registration election is made.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1a – Gross Revenue Total gross revenue from all sources, excluding dividends or gains that are constitutionally excluded under the Bermuda CIT Act. Enter consolidated global revenue if the entity is the ultimate parent of an in-scope group; for a non-resident owned exempted company, report only Bermuda-sourced revenue and attach a schedule of exclusions. Late filing attracts an initial fixed penalty of BMD 1,000 plus a daily surcharge of BMD 100 up to a maximum of BMD 25,000. Inaccurate returns may incur a penalty of up to 50% of the additional tax assessed; deliberate evasion attracts criminal fines up to BMD 250,000 and potential imprisonment.
Box 2b – Substance-Based Income Exclusion Calculated carve-out for qualifying payroll costs and tangible assets located in Bermuda, used to reduce GloBE excess profit. Non-resident owned entities must substantiate all payroll and asset figures with Bermuda payroll records and fixed asset register schedules. Late filing attracts an initial fixed penalty of BMD 1,000 plus a daily surcharge of BMD 100 up to a maximum of BMD 25,000. Inaccurate returns may incur a penalty of up to 50% of the additional tax assessed; deliberate evasion attracts criminal fines up to BMD 250,000 and potential imprisonment.
Box 3c – Qualified Refundable Tax Credits Credits treated as income under GloBE rules, including any refundable investment tax credits granted by the Bermuda Government. Non-resident investors must attach the official credit award notice and reconcile the credit against the CIT return and financial statements. Late filing attracts an initial fixed penalty of BMD 1,000 plus a daily surcharge of BMD 100 up to a maximum of BMD 25,000. Inaccurate returns may incur a penalty of up to 50% of the additional tax assessed; deliberate evasion attracts criminal fines up to BMD 250,000 and potential imprisonment.
Box 4d – Total CIT Payable Net corporate income tax due after applying QBAC, QBAI, and offsets; amount must reconcile to the Bermuda CIT Act computation schedule. Non-resident filers must settle payment in BMD via approved electronic payment rails; do not net dividends or management fees against this box. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 consolidated group revenue as the trigger for in-scope CIT filing and Country-by-Country reporting. Below this threshold, Bermuda law does not mandate a Local File or Master File, but transfer pricing evidence must still be retained to support ESR compliance and any UK tax audits.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (ESR) apply to Bermuda Exempted Companies engaged in any ‘relevant activity’, including service and headquarters business. A government affairs advisory/lobbying entity must demonstrate substantial activity through Bermuda-based employees, premises, expenditure, and board management, and must file an annual ESR return with the Registrar of Companies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated; fine up to BMD 250,000; confiscation of profits; and in serious cases criminal prosecution under the Bermuda Criminal Code and the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who approve or participate in the evasion are jointly and severally liable for the tax, subject to civil restitution orders, disqualification from acting as directors, travel bans during investigation, and imprisonment of up to five years.
Shell Company / ESR Evasion

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of BMD 10,000 for the first compliance failure, escalating to BMD 100,000 for subsequent failures; mandatory deregistration and striking-off of the company; annulment of the tax-exempt status and retroactive application of the 15% QDMTT.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined up to BMD 50,000, prohibited from managing a Bermuda company for up to five years, named in public enforcement notices, and denied leave to remain in Bermuda following enforcement proceedings.
Anti-Money Laundering Failure (Enhanced Risk Tier)

Statutory sanction: Regulatory fines up to BMD 1,000,000 per contravention under the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Rules; forfeiture of assets subject to confiscation proceedings; suspension or revocation of the financial services license; referral for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face imprisonment up to ten years for money laundering, unlimited personal civil liability for losses, confiscation of personal assets, and a regulatory prohibition order barring them from exercising corporate management functions in Bermuda.
Unlicensed Lobbying / Foreign-Principal Interference

Statutory sanction: Fine up to BMD 500,000; revocation of the statutory license; freezing of retainer accounts maintained at Bermuda banks; disqualification from public contracts; and public censure by the regulatory authority.

Director personal liability: Directors acting with consent or neglect are personally liable for unlawful lobbying, subject to fines up to BMD 100,000, imprisonment up to five years, and personal travel restrictions while the investigation is pending. Corporate veil piercing applies where the company is a façade for foreign political influence operations.
