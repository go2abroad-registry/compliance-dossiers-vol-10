Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. The ELP is a statutorily transparent partnership in the Bermuda/Cayman tradition; it is not taxed at the entity level in Bermuda, the UK, or the Cayman Islands, and is not UK-resident unless central management and control is exercised in the UK (territorial scope caveat). Bermuda levies only payroll tax, social insurance, and customs duties; app-store licence income is outside Bermuda's territorial tax net.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election; tax transparency is automatic. In the UK, the ELP is treated as a transparent partnership under CTA 2010 s.59; without a UK permanent establishment, no UK corporation tax return is required, and a TIOPA 2010 Part 6A hybrid mismatch election is unnecessary because the ELP is fully transparent and no hybrid branch exists. For US partners, the ELP is a per-se partnership under US Treas. Reg. 301.7701-3; no IRS Form 8832 check-the-box election is available or required.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK: GBP 90,000 annual taxable turnover threshold for UK-established suppliers; however, non-established suppliers of digital e-services to UK consumers face a zero threshold — registration is mandatory from the first sale unless the marketplace (Apple/Google) accounts for VAT as the deemed supplier under VATA 1994 s.49B-49C. Bermuda: no VAT registration or turnover threshold exists (no VAT); payroll tax registration is required only if the ELP engages Bermuda-resident employees.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For Bermuda: zero-rating is inapplicable because Bermuda has no VAT; customs duty relief may be claimed only for temporary imports or qualifying re-exports of demonstration hardware. For UK VAT zero-rating: physical goods exported from the UK to Bermuda or other third countries qualify at 0% under VATA 1994 s.30 and Schedule 2 if exported within three months of supply, with the exporter retaining direct evidence (customs declaration, bill of lading/air waybill) and indirect evidence (commercial contract, payment proof) per HMRC Notice 703. Digital app sales via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing are outside the scope of UK VAT for non-UK consumers; B2B digital services to Bermuda-based businesses are outside UK VAT (reverse charge applies in the customer's territory), so the ELP treats those supplies as outside scope rather than zero-rated in the UK, supported by platform geolocation and billing-address evidence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fixed exchange rate regime with the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD). Current account transactions are generally unrestricted and freely convertible, including service receipts, royalties, and app store remittances. Capital account transactions are subject to notification or approval under the Exchange Control Act, particularly foreign-currency borrowing, issuance of shares or partnership interests to non-residents, and repatriation of capital. Profit remittance by way of dividends or partnership distributions is freely permitted once audited financial statements and a solvency/declaration are filed with the tax administration; no withholding tax is imposed on distributions to non-residents. Practical hurdles are limited to completing the BMA's 'Notification of External Account' and demonstrating the underlying economic substance in accordance with the Economic Substance Act 2018, which for a pure mobile utility app business is typically satisfied by core income-generating activities (decision-making, server hosting, and development oversight) conducted in Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no general statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds to the BMA or to convert foreign currency earnings into BMD within a fixed timeline. The Exchange Control Act permits a resident person to hold foreign currency in an External Account, provided the account is designated with an authorised bank and the BMA is notified. Receipts from App Store / Google Play sales, which are typically settled in USD, may be retained in USD or converted to BMD at the holder's discretion. The only soft requirement is that local banks may request documentary evidence (e.g., app store statements, invoices) to satisfy anti-money laundering monitoring; however, no mandatory conversion ratio or surrender deadline exists.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), also carrying out exchange control functions under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with significant legal constraints. Bermudian law requires any person carrying on a 'banking business' in or from within Bermuda to hold a banking license under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. Offshore digital e-money institutions (EMIs) without a Bermudian licence cannot provide deposit-taking or payment-account services to a Bermuda-domiciled entity. However, the ELP may lawfully open a settlement account with a licenced EMI incorporated in the UK or Gibraltar (e.g., a UK-authorised e-money institution) to receive Apple/Google payout proceeds, provided such EMI is used solely as a pass-through payment aggregator and the underlying app revenue is ultimately swept to the Bermuda operating account. The BMA does not object to foreign EMIs as receiving agents, but the ELP must maintain its principal operational bank account in a BMA-supervised bank to satisfy economic substance record-keeping. Legal constraints include: (i) EMIs cannot extend credit; (ii) EMI account opening for a Bermudian partnership requires the same shareholder/partner UBO disclosures as for a bank; (iii) the EMI must not be used as a gateway for prohibited 'virtual asset' conversions unless the EMI holds a Class F licence under the Digital Assets Business Act 2018.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions from non-resident partners are routed via SWIFT MT103 with a purpose code of 'CF' (capital funds) or 'GDEQ' (equity). The following protocols apply: (i) the remitter must designate the beneficiary as 'Exempted Limited Partnership – [Name]' and include the ELP's BMA regulatory reference and bank account number; (ii) the partner must include a supporting letter stating the contribution is a genuine capital investment under the Limited Partnership Act 1883; (iii) each contribution must be recorded in the partnership capital account and reported to the BMA via the annual exchange control return if the contribution exceeds BMD 50,000; (iv) the incoming wire must be free of any financing-related purpose code (e.g., 'LOAN') unless the partnership registers the debt with the BMA. Verification is completed via bank confirmation and a legal opinion from Bermudian counsel confirming the contribution does not constitute a regulated deposit.Outbound treasury payments, including trade settlement for the partnership's operating expenses (e.g., payment to app developers, cloud hosting providers, marketing agencies), are cleared using: (i) SWIFT MT103 for direct correspondent-bank wire transfers from the Bermuda operating account, typically via the bank's USD correspondent account in New York (CHIPS/Fedwire); (ii) Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) only when a historical relationship exists with a supplier requiring documentary collections, although standard recurring SaaS payments do not warrant LC issuance; (iii) EFT (Automated Clearing House / CHAPS) for intra-Bermuda or UK-denominated payments to related service providers. For recurring app-store payout remittances, the Apple/Google treasury teams settle to a designated USD account in the US or Ireland; those funds must be swept daily/weekly to the Bermuda account via MT103 with purpose code 'S001' (service revenue) to maintain a single source of treasury liquidity.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) on USD/BMD – permitted but rarely economically necessary due to the 1:1 peg and the BMA's commitment to maintaining the fixed exchange rateFX swap (USD/BMD) – available through Bermudian licensed banks for temporary liquidity mismatches, requiring the ELP to have a signed ISDA Master Agreement or the bank's standard FX swap term sheetForward outright contract on USD/GBP or USD/EUR – permissible for hedging conversion of app-store settlements that are denominated in GBP or EUR before conversion into USD/BMD, only for hedging of estimated future cash flows (no speculative positions)Currency options (European-style) on USD/GBP – permissible for small notional amounts (up to BMD 2 million per contract) where the partnership has an underlying commercial exposure in GBP or EUR, subject to the bank's annual credit approvalCross-currency swap on USD/BMD – not generally needed; if used, it must be fully collateralised and documented under a master netting agreement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% standard Bermuda corporate income tax; 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CITA 2023) applies only to a 'Constituent Entity' of an in-scope multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least USD 750 million in two of the preceding four fiscal years. The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) conducting mobile-utility-app development through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing is outside CITA 2023 scope and therefore bears 0% Bermuda corporate tax.
