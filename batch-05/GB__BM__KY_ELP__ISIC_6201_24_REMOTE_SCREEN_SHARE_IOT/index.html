Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance (Bermuda) and the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; filing and registration services are accessed via the Bermuda e-Tax portal and the Registrar of Companies portal respectively.

E-government filing portal

An Exempted Limited Partnership is not automatically given a tax identification number upon formation. The ELP is first registered with the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2014 to obtain a partnership registration number. Where the ELP is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated annual revenue exceeding EUR 750,000,000, the ELP must separately register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to receive a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (BTIN). Non-resident partners generally do not receive a BTIN unless they become directly liable for Bermudian payroll tax or corporate income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda does not operate a check-the-box election regime. The ELP is treated as a flow-through entity under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and for Bermudian payroll tax purposes unless it falls within the definition of a taxable Bermuda business entity. In-scope MNE groups may elect to apply a qualified domestic minimum top-up tax. For US federal tax purposes, the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to choose its classification, and UK anti-hybrid rules may require a concurrent notice to HMRC where income or deductions are inconsistent with the UK result.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST. Bermuda levies customs duties on imports, payroll tax on local remuneration, and stamp duty on selected instruments. Remote desktop access, screen-share, and unattended telemetry software services supplied from Bermuda are not subject to any Bermudian indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold applies in Bermuda. A supplier of remote software services has no supply-side indirect tax registration requirement unless it imports physical hardware (customs account) or employs local staff (payroll tax registration). For corporate income tax purposes, registration is required only where the Bermudian entity is part of a multinational group with consolidated annual turnover of at least EUR 750,000,000 in one of the preceding two fiscal years.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT or GST. The supply of remote screen-share, IoT telemetry, and unattended machine access software from Bermuda is outside the scope of Bermudian indirect tax, so there is no zero-rating rate, no input VAT on supplies, and no VAT refund entitlement. If the partnership separately registers for UK VAT as a third-country supplier of digital services to UK consumers, supplies to non-UK customers are outside UK VAT and supplies to UK consumers are subject to 20% UK VAT unless another exemption applies; zero-rating is not relevant to this service type.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for any physical hardware or software medium shipped from Bermuda)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for physical exports)
  • Commercial invoice identifying the software licence, delivery method, and customer jurisdiction
  • SWIFT / MT103 or inward remittance certificate evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Executed SaaS or remote support agreement substantiating place of supply and beneficial entitlement

Bermuda has no real-time fiscal clearance, no mandatory e-invoicing, and no digital stamping regime. Commercial electronic invoices are acceptable for accounting and tax record-keeping. Physical imports clear through Bermuda Customs using electronic manifests and customs entries, but this regime is unrelated to the invoicing of remote software services.

There is no export zero-rating regime in Bermuda and therefore no VAT clawback or export fraud penalty. Fiscal risk is concentrated in corporate income tax: an in-scope ELP that fails to register, files late, or misstates its flow-through character can be assessed penalties up to 10% of the unpaid CIT plus statutory interest. Mischaracterising a hybrid partnership, absent transfer-pricing documentation, can also lead to adverse adjustments in the UK, US, or other partner jurisdictions, but those clawbacks arise under direct tax or anti-hybrid legislation rather than export zero-rating rules.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar, and USD is legal tender and circulates freely in Bermuda. Bermuda operates a liberalised exchange-control regime: a Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership designated as a non-resident person for exchange-control purposes may maintain External (non-resident) Accounts, which can be freely credited and debited for current-account payments without individual BMA licence. Capital-account transactions, including capital contributions and certain inter-partner transfers, require the transaction to be effected through an Authorised Dealer pursuant to delegated general permission, with the bank filing Exchange Control Notifications where prescribed. Profit allocations, priority returns, and capital distributions to non-resident limited partners are freely remittable in BMD or USD with no Bermuda withholding tax; the BMA imposes no lock-up, no prior-approval threshold for genuine commercial distributions, and no convertibility queue. In practice the regime is fully convertible for a software-IP holding and licensing ELP, subject only to standard AML/CFT reporting and the Authorised Dealer's delegated record-keeping obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export receipts. A Bermuda ELP earning subscription, telemetry, or remote-software licensing revenue may retain invoiced proceeds in BMD, USD, GBP, or EUR in External Accounts without obligation to convert or remit. The only administrative duty is dealer-level advance reporting to the BMA for large receipts under the annual exchange-control reporting regime, which is handled automatically by the receiving bank; no entity-level filing is required for export-derived software licensing revenue.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Exchange Control Act 1971 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1950 as the central bank and integrated financial regulator of Bermuda, with delegated exchange-control administration performed by Authorised Dealers (licensed Bermuda commercial banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for collections and operating disbursements; Low as a primary treasury or escrow backbone. A Bermuda ELP is outside the UK/EEA authorisation perimeter, so UK FCA-regulated EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business) and EU payment institutions can onboard the ELP only on a cross-border acceptance basis for GBP/EUR operational balances and do not provide Bermudian settlement finality, BMD/USD parity concentration, or documentary LC issuance. The BMA expects the core cash-management and capital-receipt architecture to sit at a licensed Bermuda bank. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections are remitted in USD, GBP, or EUR via SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's segregated Capital Escrow Account at the Bermuda Authorised Dealer. The instructing remitter must cite the capital-call reference and the narrative 'Capital contribution — [ELP legal name], capital call dated [date], LP percentage [X%]'. The receiving bank holds a pre-verified contributor list matching the Register of Limited Partners; for each inbound wire it requires a certified copy of the capital call notice, the executed exempted limited partnership agreement, and confirmation that the contributor is an admitted limited partner. Wires settle same-day in USD via Fedwire/CHIPS and in GBP via CHAPS; the BMD/USD 1:1 parity eliminates conversion on the USD leg. For receipts exceeding USD 1,000,000 or the bank's internal threshold, the Authorised Dealer files the prescribed Exchange Control Notification with the BMA, and the ELP must retain the source-of-funds dossier for five years.Operational disbursements (IaaS hosting, subcontracted telemetry engineering, legal and CSP fees) are executed by SWIFT MT103 through the Bermuda bank's USD correspondent in New York or GBP CHAPS via London, with same-day value and a two-signatory payment-release matrix. For large one-off technology procurement, an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700) may be issued on the escrow balance with a 30–90 day tenor and documents against payment terms. Recurring distributions to limited partners are swept from the operational account and routed through a fixed Schedule C partner-payment instruction; each outward payment must match an original supplier invoice or partner drawdown resolution retained in the treasury file. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda levies no corporate income tax and no capital gains tax on an exempted limited partnership registered under the Exempted Partnerships Act.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no dividend or distribution withholding tax on an exempted limited partnership.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 — Bermuda has no transfer-pricing return, master file or local file threshold because it imposes no direct income tax. UK/OECD thresholds apply only where an ultimate UK parent has consolidated group revenue above GBP 200 million, triggering UK Country-by-Country Reporting; a defensive OECD-compliant local file should still be prepared for the ELP's connected-party SaaS arrangements.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda ESR applies to a relevant ELP carrying on a relevant activity under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018. Remote desktop access, screen sharing and IoT telemetry software supplied to unrelated end customers is not ordinarily an ESR relevant activity, but if the ELP provides relevant services to an overseas affiliate, the service business category applies. Even outside ESR, the ELP must maintain adequate physical presence, employees and expenditure in Bermuda commensurate with its claimed profile.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK central management and control tax evasion

Statutory sanction: HMRC assesses the ELP as UK resident and charges UK corporation tax at 19%–25% on worldwide profits, plus interest; penalties for careless or deliberate error can reach 100% of the understated tax. The UK Diverted Profits Tax at 25% may also apply to arrangements lacking economic substance.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors of a corporate general partner face joint and several liability for penalties and restitution if they knowingly engineered the sham; dishonest conduct can lead to prosecution for cheating the public revenue, imprisonment up to 7 years, HMRC imposed travel restrictions and director disqualification.
Transfer-pricing manipulation of SaaS software and telemetry data revenue

Statutory sanction: HMRC adjusts profits under TIOPA 2010, assesses corporation tax and interest, and applies penalties up to 100% of the understated tax. The UK GAAR can impose a 60% penalty, and Diverted Profits Tax of 25% can apply to contrived arrangements involving the Bermuda ELP.

Director personal liability: Senior accounting officers of a UK corporate general partner are liable to fines of GBP 5,000 for each failure in transfer-pricing certification; directors can be personally liable in civil restitution and may be disqualified where the ELP is used as a vehicle to strip UK profits.
Economic-substance failure and false regulatory declaration

Statutory sanction: Bermuda ESR penalties start at BD$50,000 for failure to satisfy the economic substance test and can rise to BD$250,000 for failure to comply with a direction, plus daily surcharges. The Registrar may strike the ELP off, cancel the exemption and report the principals to foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner who sign a false declaration commit an offence and are personally liable for fines and restitution; in egregious cases the Bermuda courts may impose travel bans and the corporate veil is pierced to hold principals liable.
AML money-laundering and failure to prevent tax evasion

Statutory sanction: The Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Act 2018 in Bermuda impose imprisonment up to 14 years, confiscation orders and fines. The UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 separately criminalises the failure to prevent tax evasion facilitated by associated persons.

Director personal liability: The ELP's general partner and MLRO are personally exposed to imprisonment, civil restitution, and confiscation of proceeds; directors can be disqualified and subjected to travel bans in both Bermuda and the UK where the pass-through structure is used to defeat tax and AML controls.
