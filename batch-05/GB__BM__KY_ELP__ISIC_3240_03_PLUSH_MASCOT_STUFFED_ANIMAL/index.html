Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Domestically, no check-the-box or hybrid/entity classification election is available; Bermuda law makes an exempted limited partnership tax-transparent and no statutory election alters this status. For foreign tax compliance, the ELP may make a protective entity classification election under the relevant foreign jurisdiction rules (e.g., U.S. Treasury Reg. § 301.7701-3) to be treated as a deemed partnership or disregarded entity, including submissions for hybrid mismatch planning.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; no sales and use tax; Bermuda levies only customs import duties and stamp duty on certain transactions

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – there is no VAT/GST or sales tax registration threshold in Bermuda; import duty liability is determined transactionally at customs clearance, not through a turnover-based registration system.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – since Bermuda has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating mechanism and no corresponding refund claim system. Exports are outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax; the only indirect taxes are levied on imports into Bermuda.

  • Customs Export Declaration (as required by the Bermuda Customs Department for goods leaving Bermuda, where applicable)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Bermuda
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds in a Bermuda-licensed financial institution, though no statutory deadline applies in the absence of VAT rules

No domestic e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate is in force for Bermuda. Un audited standard invoicing is allowed. Import clearance uses electronic single-window filings via the Customs Department’s ASYCUDA World system, which requires API or web-based submission of customs declarations, invoice data, and tariff codes, but not a digital stamping mechanism.

No VAT clawback or export zero-rating penalty regime exists in Bermuda. However, fraudulent misrepresentation of export documentation to avoid customs import duties or to obtain foreign tax benefits may attract penalties under Bermuda’s Customs (Control) Act and common law offenses, including fines and imprisonment; retrospective reassessment of any customs exemption and clawback of duty shortfalls may be imposed for false declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BMD is fully convertible to USD at par within Bermuda through licensed authorised dealers. For an Exempted Limited Partnership that has obtained the standard non-resident designation under the Exchange Control Act, both capital-account flows (partner capital contributions, distributions, capital repatriation) and current-account flows (trade settlement, operating expenses, profit remittance) operate under general permission without case-by-case BMA approval. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal: Bermuda imposes no withholding tax, no dividend controls, and no FX-restriction on outbound distributions for a non-resident-designated ELP; the only conditions are maintenance of the non-resident designation, filing of an up-to-date Notice of Particulars with the Registrar of Companies, and presentation of a solvency statement to the bank under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2009. If the ELP were inadvertently treated as resident for exchange-control purposes, prior BMA approval would be required for foreign-currency account holding and for any outward remittance, and the partnership would be subject to monthly foreign-currency surrender obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion obligation applies to an ELP holding a valid non-resident exchange-control designation; export receipts and operational revenue may be retained indefinitely in USD, GBP, EUR, or other clearing currencies in accounts held with an authorised bank. By contrast, a resident-designated entity must offer all foreign-currency export proceeds to an authorised dealer within one month and may not retain such proceeds without specific BMA permission. As the plush-toy manufacturing and physical export flow is executed outside Bermuda (typically via OEM factories in Asia), no Bermudian export declaration, customs form, or surrender certificate is triggered for this structure; the Bermuda ELP functions purely as the contracting, treasury, and settlement vehicle.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the central monetary authority and administrator of the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973; the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 parity to the United States Dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-to-High on specialist cross-border platforms, Low on mainstream UK high-street EMIs. UK-regulated e-money institutions such as Revolut Business, Tide, and Wise generally restrict onboarding to UK/EEA-incorporated entities and will reject a Bermuda ELP without a UK-registered branch or substantial UK nexus. However, a Bermuda ELP with a UK corporate-services provider acting as general partner, or with a UK trading branch, can obtain an account from Airwallex or Currencycloud/Visa under their offshore-SPV acceptance policies. Legal constraints: UK EMIs operate under the FCA Money Laundering Regulations 2017 and will classify the ELP as an enhanced-risk offshore structure, imposing lower transaction thresholds, documentary trade checks, and quarterly reviews. The ELP must not rely on an EMI as its only treasury and clearing bank; the Bermuda bank account must remain the primary settlement node, with the EMI used as a supplementary FX and platform-payout channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partners remit capital contributions via SWIFT MT103 to the Butterfield or HSBC Bermudian USD/EUR sub-account. The payment narrative field must state exactly: 'Capital contribution to [ELP name] per LP Agreement Clause X and Contribution Schedule dated YYYY-MM-DD'. For intra-group remittances from a UK affiliate, the remitting bank applies ISO 20022 purpose code 'INTC' (intra-company) or the bank's standard corporate-purpose narrative; for third-party investors, the purpose code 'CORT' (trade settlement) or descriptive free-format is accepted. No BMA pre-approval is required for a non-resident-designated ELP; however, the receiving bank must hold on file, before first credit, the BMA non-resident designation certificate, the executed Contribution Schedule, and a source-of-funds declaration for any partner contributing more than USD 50,000. Verification of each credit is completed against the bank account mandate, the CRS/economic-substance questionnaire, and the underlying LP Agreement signature page.The primary clearing rail for supplier settlement is SWIFT MT103 direct payment to the Chinese factory's bank (typically HSBC China, Bank of China, or Standard Chartered Shanghai) with the narrative referencing 'P/O [number] / OEM Plush Doll Contract [number] / HS 9503.00'. For pre-shipment or contract-size funding, a Commercial Letter of Credit is issued by Butterfield (issuing bank) under UCP600 and advised or confirmed by Bank of China or Standard Chartered Shanghai; the required negotiation documents are commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading, certificate of origin, and pre-shipment inspection certificate. Simultaneously, the UK EMI route (Faster Payments in GBP and SEPA in EUR) is used for platform payouts and periodic sweeping of balances into the Bermuda USD account via MT103 to maintain margin cover for open LCs and to concentrate liquidity. Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda imposes no general corporate income tax, capital gains tax, value-added tax, or goods-and-services tax; the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 superimposes a 15% CIT exclusively on constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups with consolidated revenue equal to or above EUR 750 million / BMD 750 million for fiscal years commencing on or after 1 January 2025; a standalone plush-toy OEM exempted limited partnership is outside the scope of that CIT)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda levies no withholding tax on profit distributions, dividends, or liquidation proceeds paid by an exempted limited partnership to non-resident partners, irrespective of the partner's country of residence

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 - mandatory Local File and Master File obligations arise only where the ELP is a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; below this threshold no statutory Bermuda transfer pricing documentation is required. For the standalone OEM ELP, contemporaneous benchmark documentation remains strongly recommended to defend a normal profit margin on intercompany plush-toy manufacturing and to rebut UK HMRC diverted-profits enquiries

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations apply to every registered entity, including exempted limited partnerships; an annual ESR Return must be filed within 6 months of the financial year end. Plush-toy OEM manufacturing is not a designated relevant activity, so only a nil declaration is required. However, if the ELP expands into captive distribution of finished plush dolls to a UK affiliate or licenses the mascot/pattern intellectual property, it becomes subject to the substantial-presence test for those relevant activities, requiring local management, full-time employees, physical premises, and core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - False ESR Declaration and UK CFC / Diverted Profits Recharacterisation

Statutory sanction: Bermuda: penalty up to BMD 10,000 plus a daily default penalty of up to BMD 1,000 for ESR non-compliance or false declaration, and potential deregistration. United Kingdom: corporation tax at 19-25%, penalty up to 100% of the understated tax, and diverted profits tax at 25% of diverted profits; HMRC may also issue a follower notice and charge 60% of the counteracted advantage under the General Anti-Abuse Rule

Director personal liability: The general partner and UK directors who orchestrate the booking arrangement may be pursued jointly: HMRC can impose personal liability notices for unpaid corporate taxes and make a CFC charge against UK shareholders; civil restitution orders, freezing injunctions, travel bans under departure prohibition orders, and prosecution for cheating the public revenue with imprisonment of up to 7 years are available in egregious cases
Shell Company / Sham Arrangement Lacking Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda: Registrar strike-off, revocation of exempted status, ESR penalties, and forced restoration costs. United Kingdom: GAAR counteraction with a 60% penalty on the counteracted tax advantage; HMRC may recharacterise the offshore entity as a sham and assess the UK parent on the full plush-toy OEM margin

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner are jointly and severally liable for ELP obligations contracted while the partnership is struck off; UK disqualification orders and personal assessments under the controlled foreign company and transfer pricing provisions may follow; the resident agent's resignation typically triggers involuntary liquidation
Anti-Money Laundering - Proceeds of Crime Act and AML/ATF Regime Offences

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the Proceeds of Crime Amendment Act 2007 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Regulations 2008: imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering, unlimited fines, civil forfeiture of the plush-toy sales proceeds, and regulatory penalties up to BMD 1,000,000 for systemic AML failures in the regulated financial services environment

Director personal liability: The general partner and any UK director who 'turns a blind eye' to third-party funds face personal prosecution for money laundering and tipping-off; courts may impose worldwide asset freezing orders, travel bans, and the ultimate beneficial owner may be criminally charged with laundering the OEM trading income
Registrar Non-Compliance - Partner Information Return and Annual Government Fee Default

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the exempted partnerships register, default penalties, and restoration fees; after strike-off the ELP cannot enforce contracts, sue, or defend legal claims, and the Swift_TT and Platform_Pay settlement accounts are typically frozen by overseas banks

Director personal liability: The general partner becomes personally liable for all partnership debts incurred after the default; limited partners who participate in management forfeit their statutory limited-liability shield; court summons and travel restrictions may be imposed during the Registrar's investigation, and the resident agent is compelled to produce the full partner file
