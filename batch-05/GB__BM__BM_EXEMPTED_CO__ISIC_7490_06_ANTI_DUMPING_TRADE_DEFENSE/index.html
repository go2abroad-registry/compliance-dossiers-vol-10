Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital or escrow account is imposed on a Bermuda exempted company. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance – Office of the Tax Commissioner; online filing portal: Bermuda Tax e-Services at www.gov.bm/taxation (Companies Registry for incorporation: www.bermudacorporaterecord.com)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned exempted company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 through a resident Bermudian registered agent. Incorporation generates a unique Company Number but no TIN/VAT ID because Bermuda does not levy corporate income tax or VAT. A payroll-tax employer code may be obtained from the Tax Commissioner only if the company hires local employees. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, an exempted company may apply to the Minister of Finance for an undertaking that no taxes on profits or income shall be imposed for a specified period (typically through 2050). Bermuda has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. UK hybrid-mismatch rules may apply to UK-resident persons but do not alter the Bermuda entity's tax classification absent UK anti-avoidance re-characterization.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No value added tax or goods and services tax; Bermuda imposes customs duties and stamp duties.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Importers and manufacturers may require customs or excise licences under the Customs Tariff Act 1989, but such licences are not indirect-tax registrations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT/GST. Anti-dumping defense and legal-accounting services supplied by a Bermuda exempted company fall outside any local indirect tax. If the same services were supplied by a UK business to a non-UK client, UK VAT would treat them as outside the scope of UK VAT (subject to place-of-supply rules) rather than zero-rated; only export of goods attracts a UK 0% rate.

  • No customs or export declaration is required for professional offshore services.
  • For physical exports, if any: Bermuda Customs Export Declaration, commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading or airway bill.
  • For evidencing cross-border service fees: executed legal-services agreement, time-and-billing ledger, SWIFT credit advice, and inward remittance certificate as requested by the bank or auditor.

Bermuda imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no electronic invoicing mandate, and no digital stamping or API clearance. The Bermuda Customs Automated Commercial Environment (ACE) is used solely for customs declarations. Professional-service invoices may be issued in plain paper or PDF format.

Bermuda has no VAT zero-rating mechanism, so no VAT clawback applies. Fraudulent export statements intended to obtain customs advantages are punishable under the Customs Tariff Act 1989 and the Criminal Code Act 1907 with fines up to BMD 25,000 and/or imprisonment. If UK VAT rules were artificially engaged, penalties for dishonest conduct could reach 100% of the tax due. The Bermuda exempted company must also satisfy the Economic Substance for Geographically Mobile Activities regime; failure to meet substance reporting may invalidate exempt-status protections and trigger remedial enforcement.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BMD is pegged at 1:1 to the USD, and USD legal tender circulates freely throughout the jurisdiction. Bermuda maintains full current-account convertibility for exempted companies and full capital-account convertibility for non-resident shareholders: share capital, dividends, management fees, interest, and liquidation proceeds are freely remittable without BMA approval, exchange-control allocation, or withholding tax. The entity holds a Tax Exemption Undertaking, so no Bermuda income, dividend, interest, or capital gains tax arises on cross-border treasury transactions. The only practical remittance hurdles are internal corporate formalities - a board-resolved solvency certificate for distributions and maintenance of the statutory beneficial-ownership register - rather than any monetary-authority restriction. No parallel-market, convertibility-quota, or prior-approval mechanisms exist for USD, EUR, GBP, or other major currencies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - Bermuda's integrated central bank, currency board, and financial services regulator. The BMA issues the Bermudian dollar (BMD) on a 100% USD-backed currency-board basis; administers the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2001, the Investment Business Act 2003, and the Digital Asset Business Act 2018; and licenses all deposit-taking institutions, money transmitters, and foreign-exchange dealers operating in Bermuda.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File documentation is required for in-scope CIT entities when Bermuda related-party transactions equal or exceed BMD 10,000,000 annually. A Master File is required when the MNE group has consolidated revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750,000,000 (approximately BMD 750,000,000). Exempted Companies outside the CIT Act scope are not subject to mandatory TP documentation, but must still avoid sham pricing that could trigger anti-avoidance, economic substance, or money-laundering scrutiny.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply. A trade-remedy defense consulting company providing services to foreign clients will generally be treated as carrying on an active service business; it must demonstrate physical presence, qualified employees, and core income-generating activities in Bermuda. A letterbox company managed from the UK, with no Bermudian board meetings, staff, or expenditure, will fail the economic substance doctrine and may be penalised or struck off. Pure equity holding entities benefit from reduced substance requirements but still must file an annual declaration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Corporate Income Tax Filing

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to BMD 50,000 and a default penalty of up to 100% of the understated tax; interest accrues monthly. Criminal conviction can result in fines and imprisonment for up to five years.

Director personal liability: A director who signs a false return or directs the concealment may be jointly and severally liable for civil restitution, disqualified from directorship, subjected to travel bans, and imprisoned following extradition proceedings in cross-border cases.
Shell Company Structure and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may impose a fixed penalty of BMD 5,000, daily penalties of BMD 200, strike off the company, or issue a formal direction to correct the substance failure. The company may also be excluded from UK and EU public procurement and banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors face disqualification orders, freezing injunctions, and personal liability for expenses of the insolvent struck-off company. In serious cases, the directors may be criminally prosecuted for fraudulent trading.
Money Laundering and AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and Bermuda AML/CFT Regulations, penalties include fines up to BMD 1,000,000, forfeiture of assets, and imprisonment up to fourteen years for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable and criminally prosecuted if they knowingly facilitate or turn a blind eye to money laundering. Bank accounts can be frozen, travel bans imposed, and asset-restraining orders issued.
Transfer Pricing and Sham Intercompany Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The Tax Commissioner may make a primary transfer-pricing adjustment, disallow deductions, impose an underpayment penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax, and charge interest. No double-tax relief is available due to the absence of a DTA.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve sham transfer-pricing documentation can be personally liable for civil penalties; professional advisers who knowingly facilitate the sham face disciplinary sanctions and potential criminal liability for dishonest assistance.
Fraudulent Trade Defense Documentation and Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: The company may be debarred from trade defense proceedings, excluded from EU/UK legal representation, fined under foreign customs and anti-fraud legislation, and sued for damages. US False Claims Act liability may include treble damages.

Director personal liability: Directors may face criminal sanctions for fraud, extradition requests, travel bans, and imprisonment in the jurisdiction where the misleading trade defense filing was made; individual signatories of false submissions may be held personally liable for restitution.
