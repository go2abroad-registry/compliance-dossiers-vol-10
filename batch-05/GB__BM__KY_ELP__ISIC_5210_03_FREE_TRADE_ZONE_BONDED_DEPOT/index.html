Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (under the Ministry of Finance); portal operated via Bermuda Government's online tax and customs filing portal. Customs administration: Bermuda Customs Department (under the Bermuda Revenue Authority).

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN. Foreign entities establishing an Exempted Limited Partnership first obtain a Bermuda Registrar of Companies (ROC) number. For customs-bonded warehouse operations, the operator must obtain a Customs Business Number / Revenue Registration Account from the Bermuda Customs Department. If the entity employs staff, it must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax purposes. If the entity becomes subject to Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, an application must be made to the Bermuda Tax Commissioner for a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (BTIN) for CIT, using the entity's registered particulars.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope; additionally qualifies as a Tax_Exempt_Zone operator only to the extent the CIT Act's specific exemption for licensed free-trade-zone and bonded-depot businesses is independently established. The Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent under Bermuda domestic law and is not itself the default CIT taxpayer unless a member-based CIT look-through is overridden by statute.

Available election forms

No Bermuda 'check-the-box' election exists. Exempted Limited Partnerships are automatically pass-through. For cross-border UK/anti-hybrid purposes, an ELP may be subject to the UK's hybrid mismatch rules if it is treated as a separate taxable person in a non-UK jurisdiction. The partnership may file a US Form 8832 check-the-box election with the US IRS where relevant, and may elect to apply the limited scope of the Bermuda CIT Act's transitional rules for continuation of exempted partnerships; no other domestic elections are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not Applicable (no VAT/GST/sales and use tax). Bermuda administers customs duties and excise taxes under the Customs Tariff Act; bonded warehouse operations are subject to customs control rather than general indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold. For customs duty, every importer/exporter maintaining a bonded warehouse or free-zone depot must be registered with Bermuda Customs regardless of value. An Economic Operator / Registered Exporter status is mandatory for goods moving under customs seal.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT. Export transactions are not zero-rated for VAT. For customs duty purposes, goods held in a bonded warehouse and subsequently re-exported from Bermuda may be relieved from import duty, provided they remain under customs control, are entered in the bonded inventory, and are physically shipped out within the statutory bonded storage period. Services exported by a Bermuda ELP are not subject to any Bermuda indirect tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Single Goods Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Bonded Warehouse Movement Record / Warehouse Removal Warrant
  • Re-Export Clearance Certificate from Bermuda Customs
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List

Bermuda has no real-time fiscal e-invoicing or digital stamping regime. Customs clearance is filed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Automated Declarations system; the portal requires an approved customs broker or licensed warehouse operator to submit the Single Goods Declaration, supporting manifest, and bond schedule. Bonded depots operate through licensed inventory control and are subject to periodic digital reconciliation by Bermuda Customs.

No VAT clawback exists. Bermuda Customs may audit bonded warehouse records and demand import duty on goods not properly exported, plus a fine of up to 200% of the duty payable. Misdeclaration or diversion of bonded goods into domestic consumption without payment attracts seizure and forfeiture, monetary penalties of up to BMD 10,000 per offence, and in serious fraud cases criminal prosecution under the Customs Act 1965. If the ELP is subject to Bermuda CIT, false customs documentation will not support a CIT offset claim, and the usual CIT penalties for under-reported liability apply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is fully convertible and legally pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar (USD). Current account settlements for cross-border trade in bonded goods, warehousing services, and logistics are free of material exchange-control friction. Capital account movements into or out of an Exempted Limited Partnership are subject to exchange-control authorisation in certain cases, but the non-resident ownership structure of the ELP generally permits profit and capital remittance without additional taxes, provided all remittances are executed through an authorised BMA-licensed dealer and supported by audited financial statements or board-approved distribution resolutions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender of export proceeds to the BMA and prescribes no mandatory percentage conversion ratio. Export receipts from the bonded depot may be retained in USD or BMD at the election of the depositor, subject to Exchange Control Act 1972 notification requirements and standard authorised-dealer record keeping. The operator must route all inward export proceeds through a licensed Bermuda bank and maintain supporting customs warehouse documents, invoices, and bills of lading for periodic review.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Currency Act 1972 and the Companies Act 1981 as the integrated financial services regulator and de facto central monetary authority for Bermuda; the Bank of England does not exercise direct banking supervision over Bermuda, a British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low to Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not permitted to act as the primary deposit-taking institution for a Bermuda-regulated exempted partnership engaged in a licensed bonded-depot operation, because the Exchange Control Act 1972 requires the entity to maintain its principal operating and settlement accounts with an authorised Bermuda dealer. Such EMIs may be used only as supplementary payment instruments where the underlying funds pass through a licensed Bermuda bank; any EMI holding customer funds in a manner that circumvents authorised-dealer supervision is likely to be treated as an unlicensed banking or money-services activity in Bermuda.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda transfer pricing statute imposes a Local File or Master File threshold; the practical monetary trigger is BMD 0 where group cross-border transactions are undertaken. Bermuda economic substance review evaluates arm's-length pricing and local substance through the ESR process rather than through transfer pricing documentation.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations 2018 as amended apply to entities carrying on relevant activities. A bonded warehouse depot may constitute a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity when purchasing, storing and distributing goods, requiring physical presence, full-time staff, local management, and substantial activity in Bermuda. A pure holding entity exception does not apply to operational bonded-warehouse income. ESR returns must evidence arm's-length substance for outsourced third-party logistics and foreign-parent administration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019, a non-compliant entity faces an initial financial penalty of BMD 10,000, daily penalties, and potential additional penalties up to BMD 250,000; continued failure can result in striking-off, revocation of exempt status, and mandatory dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, and local representatives may be personally liable for knowingly making false ESR declarations. Civil penalties, disgorgement, director disqualification, and imprisonment for up to five years apply in cases of fraudulent misrepresentation or evasion.
Shell Company and Concealed UK Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Registrar may strike the entity off for failing to demonstrate real commercial activity. HMRC may apply UK CFC, GAAR, and anti-hybrid rules to attributed profits, impose penalties up to 200% of the tax understated, and open criminal investigation where deliberate concealment exists.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow controllers may be assessed personally under UK Proceeds of Crime Act civil recovery; travel bans, asset freezing orders, and sequestration are available where the partnership is used as a vehicle to hide taxable profits.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda AML/ATF Regulations, the Proceeds of Crime (Amendment) Act 2020, and the Beneficial Ownership Act 2017, fines up to BMD 250,000 may be imposed on regulated entities, and senior officers may be imprisoned for up to ten years. Licences for financial activity can be revoked and registrations suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners risk joint and several liability for inadequate AML systems, disgorgement of illicit gains, asset forfeiture, civil penalties, travel restrictions, and disqualification from acting as company officers.
Annual Registry Filing Default

Statutory sanction: The Registrar of Companies will impose monetary late fees, may refuse to certify the partnership's good standing, and can strike the ELP from the register. Restoration requires settlement of all outstanding penalties and costs, including Court costs.

Director personal liability: General partners are jointly and severally liable for obligations arising before dissolution; where striking-off occurs without settling registry penalties, agents and partners may be personally sued for the official fees and default penalties.
