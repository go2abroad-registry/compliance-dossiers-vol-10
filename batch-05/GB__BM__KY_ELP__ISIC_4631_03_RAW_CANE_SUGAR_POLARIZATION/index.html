Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Bermuda Ministry of Finance; E-Tax portal: https://etax.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned exempted limited partnerships must (a) obtain a Certificate of Limited Partnership from the Bermuda Registrar of Companies, (b) file an annual Economic Substance Declaration, and (c) register with the OTC for a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (BIN). If the ELP has no Bermuda employees or payroll, no payroll tax account is required. For in-scope multinational groups, separate CIT registration is mandated under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, including the appointment of a resident representative.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda does not provide a check-the-box regime. An ELP is statutorily flow-through; however, under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, an ELP may elect to be treated as a taxable person for group filing purposes, or may be disregarded as a separate entity where all underlying members are in the same CIT group. Hybrid mismatch elections are governed by the parent jurisdiction's rules, not Bermuda domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST — Bermuda levies customs import duties only

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists. Employer payroll tax registration arises from the first employee; no economic-turnover threshold applies to the ELP for indirect tax purposes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda does not levy VAT/GST, so 'zero-rating' is replaced by a statutory customs duty exemption on exports. Goods physically shipped from Bermuda qualify for duty-free export if an export entry is filed with Bermuda Customs and the goods leave the island. For vessel-based sugar transactions that never call at a Bermuda port, the supply is outside Bermuda's customs charge and requires no Bermuda export filing.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs export entry)
  • Bill of Lading (through-carrier or original mate's receipt)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT confirmation of payment
  • Certificate of Origin (if requested by buyer or under trade agreement)
  • Commodity documentation: polarization certificate, moisture/ash analysis, and weight certificate

No e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-mandated invoice clearance exists in Bermuda. Customs entries are processed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Automated System (BCAS). B2B invoices do not require government pre-approval.

Misdeclaration of exports to obtain duty relief is sanctioned under the Customs Tariff Act 1964 (penalties up to three times the duty avoided, seizure/forfeiture of goods, and administrative prosecution). A false export declaration supporting a CIT refund may trigger retrospective clawback, interest at the statutory rate, and a penalty of 25% of the underpaid tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BMD is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 peg to USD. The Exchange Control Act 1972 treats non-resident designated Exempted Limited Partnerships as outside the core capital-account restrictions; current-account payments for trade, freight, insurance and inspection fees are unrestricted. Capital-account inflows (partnership subscriptions) and outbound distributions to non-resident partners require BMA specific or general permission, routinely granted. Profit remittance is wire-out free of any Bermudian income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax or remittance tax, subject only to the bank's Source-of-Wealth documentation and BMA annual reporting on non-resident transfers. Practical convertibility hurdle: BMD/USD parity removes currency conversion risk, but any GBP/EUR treasury leg from the UK parent attracts normal foreign-exchange spreads at the Bermudian clearing bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory surrender ratio or statutory conversion timeline for export proceeds in Bermuda. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, a resident exporter is generally required to offer export proceeds to an authorised dealer (licensed Bermudian bank) within 6 months of receipt; the non-resident-designated KY_ELP is exempt from this offer requirement and may retain VHP raw cane sugar sale proceeds indefinitely in approved USD/BMD external accounts. Obligations are limited to disclosing inbound/outbound transfers above BMA notification thresholds (typically USD 100,000) and filing the annual exchange-control confirmation; no commodity-specific or polarisation-linked surrender rules apply to ISIC 4631 sugar wholesale.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), constituted under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and exercising delegated currency and exchange-control powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972; ultimate Crown sovereignty rests with HM Treasury and the Bank of England, but BMA is the operative central bank and Exchange Control Authority for Bermuda (BMD issued at BMD 1 : USD 1).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low for primary treasury operation. Bermuda does not issue standalone e-money institution (EMI) licences; offshore digital EMIs (UK FCA-authorised e-money firms or EU EMIs) cannot hold BMD balances, do not participate in Bermuda dollar local clearing, and are not accepted as settlement banks by BMA-regulated counterparties. A UK EMI may be layered in only for ancillary GBP/EUR operating expenses via the UK parent, but any attempt to route VHP sugar trade settlement or LC collateral through an EMI will trigger pass-through AML rejection by Bermudian and correspondent banks. The viable architecture is a BMA-licensed clearing bank as the sole treasury spine, with any UK EMI restricted to petty operating cash flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All LP subscriptions route over SWIFT MT103 (or Fedwire for USD) into the dedicated 'KY_ELP Capital Escrow/Subscription Account' at the Bermudian clearing bank in USD (BMD 1:1). The remitting partner must be the sole account holder of the originating account; SWIFT field 70 must state 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – KY_ELP / ISIC 4631 RAW CANE SUGAR' plus the BMA file number and LP reference; for amounts > USD 1M the BMA general/specific permission number is quoted in field 72 to enable straight-through processing. The bank matches each receipt to the subscription agreement and runs a polarisation/quality contract review before releasing funds to the operational trading account. Tranche-splitting to avoid screening is prohibited; each contribution must be consolidated and pre-notified to the bank via a signed capital call.Suppliers are paid by two rails. (1) SWIFT MT103 GPI wires for ancillary costs and small trade payments (freight, inspection, demurrage) below USD 250,000; references include invoice and shipment numbers plus BMA exchange-control notification. (2) Irrevocable documentary letters of credit issued via SWIFT MT700 from the Bermudian issuing bank to the sugar exporter's bank, confirmed when required by a money-center bank; MT707 for amendments, MT754 for acceptance advice, and reimbursement via MT202/MT910. VHP raw cane sugar CRF-stowed LC document sets include: full set of clean on-board marine B/L endorsed to the order of the issuing bank, certificate of origin, SGS/Intertek quality and weight certificates, polarisation certificate (96.0°Z minimum), phytosanitary certificate, and commercial invoice. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 5,000,000 (Bermudian Dollar, pegged 1:1 to USD) of covered Bermuda CIT related-party transactions triggers a Local File requirement; Master File and Country-by-Country reporting apply where the ultimate MNE group has consolidated revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Liability Companies) Requirement Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations apply to relevant activities, including 'distribution and service centre' activity. A wholesale raw cane sugar trading ELP carrying on that relevant activity must demonstrate physical presence in Bermuda, locally employed or engaged staff, local management, adequate operating expenditure, and core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda. Compliance is evidenced through an annual ESR return filed with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Knowingly False Filing

Statutory sanction: Civil default assessment, interest, penalties up to 100% of the tax shortfall, and criminal liability; on conviction, fines and imprisonment may be imposed under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and Tax Administration Act.

Director personal liability: Directors of a corporate general partner and any controlling officer who knowingly participate in the false filing may be personally liable, jointly and severally, may face confiscation orders, and may be barred from leaving Bermuda pending proceedings.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Rejection of the ESR return, financial penalties, loss of exempted status, strike-off or restoration on adverse terms, and disqualification of the entity from holding a certificate of exemption.

Director personal liability: The general partner and controlling directors may be ordered to restore lost annual fees, pay administrative penalties, and be prohibited from acting as a partner or director in Bermuda for up to five years.
Money Laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997

Statutory sanction: Criminal money laundering offences carry an unlimited fine, imprisonment up to 14 years, confiscation of assets, account freezes, and compulsory forfeiture of the cargo or sale proceeds.

Director personal liability: A general partner or director with knowledge or connivance is personally criminally liable; travel bans, search orders, and worldwide asset freezing orders may be imposed.
AML/ATF Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines, regulatory censure, revocation of the entity's access to banking infrastructure, and court-ordered restitution for losses caused to the financial system.

Director personal liability: Responsible officers and the corporate general partner may be personally fined up to BMD 250,000, imprisoned up to five years, and disqualified from managing financial relationships.
Sanctions Violation and Embargoed Commodity Trading

Statutory sanction: Civil monetary penalties under Bermuda sanctions regulations, criminal liability, forfeiture of cargo and vessels, denial of banking services, and referral to overseas regulators and financial sanctions enforcement bodies.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who knowingly process prohibited transactions face extradition risk, travel restrictions, director disqualification, and personal criminal liability.
