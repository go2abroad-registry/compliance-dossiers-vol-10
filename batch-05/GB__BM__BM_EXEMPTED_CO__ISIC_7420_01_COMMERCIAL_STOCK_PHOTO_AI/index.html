Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Bermuda Government; official portal: https://www.gov.bm/taxation. The Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Agency (BermudaCITA) administers the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope multinational groups.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda exempted company is incorporated with the Registrar of Companies and obtains a company registration number. There is no general TIN/VAT ID: the company is tax-exempt and ordinarily has no registration obligation. If the company will have Bermuda employees, it must register for payroll tax and social insurance under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a payroll tax account. If the exempted company is a Bermuda constituent entity of a multinational group with consolidated revenue at or above the CIT threshold, it must register under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 through BermudaCITA and obtain a CIT identification number. VAT ID is not available or required because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda exempted company is not subject to Bermuda income, capital gains, corporation or withholding tax on its offshore business; it enjoys statutory protection from future Bermuda taxes until at least 2035 under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope multinational groups and to payroll tax if Bermuda employees are engaged.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or tax classification election exists because Bermuda has no general corporate income tax. An exempted company may apply for an undertaking/certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 confirming exemption from future Bermuda profits, income, capital gains and distribution taxes. For in-scope CIT groups, the CIT Act 2023 provides for registration and filing but does not create hybrid-mismatch elections; the OECD Pillar Two administrative mechanics, including potentially qualified domestic minimum top-up tax treatment for Bermuda profits, are administered by BermudaCITA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; the only material indirect tax is Bermuda customs duty on imported goods levied under the Customs Act and Customs Tariff Act, together with minor stamp duty in limited transactional contexts. No sales or use tax is imposed.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A in BMD: no indirect-tax registration threshold exists. Any importer of dutiable goods must file a customs entry and pay applicable customs duty regardless of turnover. The Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 threshold is separate: registration applies to Bermuda constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated annual revenue of Euro 750 million or more (BMD equivalent), subject to exclusion criteria.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT concept: Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so no 0% export VAT can be claimed and no output tax arises on offshore stock-photo licensing or digital media sales. Digital exports and offshore services supplied by a Bermuda exempted company remain outside the Bermuda indirect tax net. Only the physical importation of goods into Bermuda attracts customs duty; no export customs duty is levied.

  • Bermuda Customs export declaration or customs entry (required only if physical goods/media are shipped out of Bermuda)
  • Commercial invoice or license/royalty statement identifying the export transaction, licensor, licensee and consideration
  • Bill of lading, airway bill or electronic delivery log evidencing shipment or digital transfer of the licensed assets
  • Bank wire remittance advice or payment processor settlement report (e.g. Stripe/PayPal) evidencing receipt of export consideration

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping regime is mandated for Bermuda exempted companies. Bermuda Customs accepts electronic declarations for import/export formalities, but there is no statutory requirement to clear invoices through a government fiscal platform. Tax and AML record-keeping obligations require retention of underlying invoices and payment records.

There is no export zero-rating or input-credit clawback regime because no VAT/GST is chargeable in Bermuda. Penalties arise for circumventing the protections of Bermuda's tax-exempt status through false or fraudulent customs declarations (customs fines and forfeiture), for late or incorrect CIT filings by in-scope MNE groups (15% corporate income tax, interest and penalties), for non-compliance with the Economic Substance Act 2018 (substantial financial penalties and potential strike-off), and for AML/record-keeping failures under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and anti-money laundering regulations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar (USD) under the currency board principle administered by the BMA. Bermuda maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account transactions. A Bermuda Exempted Company faces no exchange control restrictions, no prior licensing approvals, and no limitation on profit remittance, dividend distributions, or capital repatriation. The UK parent-subsidiary relationship does not re-impose the UK Exchange Control Act on the Bermudian entity; the territory's foreign exchange control legislation expressly exempts non-resident-owned exempted companies. Residual hurdles are exclusively bank-level know-your-customer and source-of-funds verification requirements, not regulatory authorisation constraints.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation exists in Bermuda. There is no mandated timeline for repatriation, no forced conversion ratio, and no requirement to deposit export proceeds with a designated bank. For a stock-photography licensing entity, all foreign-currency receipts derived from platform sales (USD, EUR, GBP) may be retained offshore, held in multi-currency operational accounts, and applied directly to outbound disbursements without any mandatory conversion into BMD. The BMD/USD peg eliminates conversion risk for USD-denominated treasury flows.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for BMA-licensed domestic payment providers and UK-authorised EMI accounts used purely as payment collection gateways; Low-Medium for offshore digital EMIs seeking to hold funds or operate virtual accounts in Bermuda. Bermuda is not an EEA member, so FCA e-money passports do not extend into the territory. Under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, an unauthorised EMI cannot lawfully accept deposits or perform payment services in Bermuda. The commercially sound architecture is a UK or US EMI (Stripe, PayPal, or FCA-authorised digital bank) collecting platform receipts, followed by daily or weekly MT103 sweeping into a BMA-supervised Bermudian USD account; the exempted company must not maintain a Bermuda-situs ledger balance with an unlicensed EMI if that balance represents regulated deposit-taking activity. Domestic banks accept incoming sweeps from recognised payment institutions when supported by platform settlement statements and a clear treasury policy.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocol: route all inbound equity or intercompany loans via SWIFT MT103 with unambiguous purpose coding. For equity subscription, instruct the remitting bank to complete tag 70 with 'CAPITAL INJECTION - SHARE SUBSCRIPTION' and, where requested, reference BPM6/IMF code EQR for equity securities. For shareholder loans, use 'INTERCOMPANY LOAN - SHAREHOLDER FINANCING' plus the executed loan agreement. The remitting UK entity should attach board resolutions approving the transfer and a UK bank reference confirming the source of funds, satisfying BMA-level verification without exchange-control approval. Deposits should be made in USD into a BMD/USD pegged operation account to obtain 1:1 parity and avoid conversion friction. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax on exempted companies (no income tax, capital gains tax, branch profits tax, or accumulated earnings tax). Effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CIT Act 2023) applies a 15% domestic top-up tax (QDMTT and IIR) only to constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated global revenue of at least €750 million; ordinary SME stock-photo platforms are outside this scope.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company to non-resident shareholders, whether ordinary, preference, or redeemable shares.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A — Bermuda domestic law has no transfer pricing, Master File, Local File, or Country-by-Country Reporting requirement for ordinary exempted companies. For in-scope CIT groups (consolidated revenue ≥ €750 million), OECD Pillar Two GloBE Information Return and CbCR thresholds apply; contemporaneous TP documentation must still be prepared to support IP profit allocation against foreign CFC audits. For ESR purposes, every relevant entity must maintain contemporaneous substance records regardless of revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Companies) Act 2018, as amended, applies. A Bermuda exempted company earning income from 'intellectual property business' (including stock-photo/AI visual asset licensing) is a relevant entity carrying on a relevant activity and must satisfy the economic substance test annually: (1) managed and controlled in Bermuda (board meetings, strategic decisions); (2) adequate physical premises in Bermuda; (3) adequate full-time employees in Bermuda; (4) adequate operating expenditure in Bermuda proportionate to income; and (5) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda. For IP businesses, mere invoicing or contract-holding in Bermuda will be deemed a failure. The 'pure equity holding entity' exemption is not available to an active trading platform. Mitigation: appoint a Bermuda-resident executive director for AI content operations, hold substantive board meetings in Bermuda, and document IP development decisions in Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent False Filing

Statutory sanction: Fine up to BDA $250,000; imprisonment up to 5 years; confiscation of the tax benefit; penalty of 10% of unpaid tax plus 1% monthly interest; enforcement charges against Bermuda assets.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly participate face personal fines, imprisonment, disqualification under the Companies Act 1981, civil restitution, and travel bans; no indemnity insurance applies to willful wrongdoing.
Economic Substance Non-Compliance (High-Risk IP Business)

Statutory sanction: BDA $10,000 filing penalty; BDA $10,000 per 30-day period of default; failure of substance test for IP business: BDA $250,000 plus daily penalties; court-ordered winding-up; strike-off from the register.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable where the failure arises from their act or omission; personal compliance orders, travel bans, restitution orders, and imprisonment up to 5 years for false declarations.
AML/CFT Violation

Statutory sanction: Civil fines up to BDA $250,000 per contravention by the Bermuda Monetary Authority or Financial Sanctions Implementation Unit; asset confiscation; criminal money-laundering prosecution up to 14 years imprisonment; revocation of the exempted company's operating status.

Director personal liability: Directors qualify as 'relevant employees' under the Proceeds of Crime Regulations; failure to file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) carries up to 5 years imprisonment; senior officers face personal fines, disqualification, seizure of personal assets, and termination of Stripe/PayPal merchant accounts.
Offshore Shell / CFC Attack and Foreign Tax Repatriation Risk

Statutory sanction: HMRC assessment on the full Bermuda profit at up to 25% UK corporation tax, plus interest and penalties up to 100% of tax; retrospective reassessment up to 6 years (12 years for deliberate conduct); possible re-characterization of the company as UK tax resident based on place of effective management.

Director personal liability: UK-resident directors exerting de facto control become UK tax resident and liable on worldwide income; personal PAYE/NIC assessments; penalty up to 100% of tax under the TAAR; travel bans; disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act; the Bermudian corporate veil will be pierced where the entity is a mere façade.
