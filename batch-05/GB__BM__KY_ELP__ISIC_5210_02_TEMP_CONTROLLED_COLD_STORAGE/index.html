Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (https://tax.gov.bm); Registrar of Companies for entity registration; Bermuda Customs Department (https://www.customs.gov.bm) for import/export controls.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership obtains its Bermuda legal-entity number at formation from the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015. Because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax or VAT, no separate tax TIN/VAT ID is required for the ELP. If the ELP becomes an employer in Bermuda, it must apply to the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll-tax registration and obtain an employer tax reference number for filing and remittance. Non-resident partners claiming passthrough treatment should rely on the partnership's formation documents plus a Bermuda residency/non-residency certificate issued by the Registrar to evidence absence of Bermuda tax status.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership obtains its Bermuda legal-entity number at formation from the Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2015. Because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax or VAT, no separate tax TIN/VAT ID is required for the ELP. If the ELP becomes an employer in Bermuda, it must apply to the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll-tax registration and obtain an employer tax reference number for filing and remittance. Non-resident partners claiming passthrough treatment should rely on the partnership's formation documents plus a Bermuda residency/non-residency certificate issued by the Registrar to evidence absence of Bermuda tax status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Bermuda has no statutory check-the-box election. The ELP is tax-transparent under Bermuda law: the partnership itself is not subject to Bermuda income tax; partners remain liable in their own residence jurisdictions. For US federal tax purposes, a foreign eligible entity with at least two members may file Form 8832 to elect classification as a corporation or a partnership, with default passthrough treatment; for UK tax purposes the ELP is generally transparent. Bermuda does not adopt hybrid-mismatch elective regimes as a domestic tax matter, but foreign-parented groups must apply OECD hybrid-mismatch rules in the relevant residence state.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not applicable - Bermuda imposes customs and stamp duties rather than VAT, GST, or retail sales tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda does not impose VAT, GST, or retail sales tax; therefore no turnover threshold for indirect-tax registration exists. Customs registration applies to importers and exporters, with no general exemption threshold for cargo entry.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda has no VAT, so there is no 'export zero-rating' claim or VAT refund mechanism. Cold-chain warehousing services and physical goods exported from Bermuda are outside any local consumption-tax charge. Where a foreign partner's residence jurisdiction taxes a cross-border supply, export substantiation should follow that jurisdiction's zero-rating rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (or out-of-bond / re-export entry if goods were imported temporarily)
  • Transport document: Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing departure from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer evidence for the consideration
  • Beneficial-owner and partner certifications for the ELP if requested by anti-avoidance authorities

None. Bermuda has no real-time fiscal e-invoicing clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping requirement. Import declarations are filed electronically through Bermuda Customs' declaration and manifest systems, but there is no live API-driven fiscal stamp or pre-clearance of commercial invoices.

No VAT zero-rating refund exists in Bermuda, so no VAT-clawback applies. Nevertheless, false export or customs declarations attract penalties under the Customs Act 1941 and related regulations: forfeiture of goods, fines of up to three times the value of the goods, and in aggravated cases imprisonment. Foreign tax authorities may separately recapture input VAT credits or impose penalties if export substantiation fails in the partner's residence jurisdiction.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is freely convertible, pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Exempted Limited Partnership is classified as an external (non-resident) entity, making both current account and capital account transactions fully convertible. Profit remittances, capital distributions, and liquidation proceeds are unrestricted, and Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest, or partnership distributions. The only practical hurdles are providing the receiving Bermuda bank with an external-status confirmation and a current economic substance filing evidencing core income-generating activity in Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Bermuda. The ELP is free to retain USD, GBP, or EUR balances in offshore and onshore clearing accounts and to settle supplier obligations directly without BMA approval.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium viability for primary treasury. The Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 confines deposit-taking to licensed Bermuda banks; overseas digital EMI accounts are not recognized as Bermuda deposit-taking licenses and are unlikely to be accepted as the entity's principal clearing host. Such EMIs serve only as supplementary low-value disbursement rails after BMA-oriented AML/ATF due diligence. HSBC Bermuda and Butterfield remain mandatory for escrow and core working capital.
