Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged to the United States dollar at a fixed 1:1 rate under a currency-board-style reserve framework, guaranteeing full and immediate convertibility of domestic notes and bank reserves into USD. Bermuda operates an Exchange Control Act 1972 regime that applies exchange-control restrictions to 'resident' persons, while exempted limited partnerships designated as 'non-resident' are largely outside these restrictions. A Bermuda ELP engaged in bonded-warehouse distribution of wines and spirits may therefore maintain unlimited multi-currency accounts and conduct capital-account transfers, trade-related current-account remittances, and profit distributions without prior BMA approval. Profit remittance to foreign partners is unrestricted and is not subject to any Bermudian withholding tax or corporate income tax; there is no tax-clearance certificate requirement for repatriation because the ELP is treated as tax-transparent and non-resident. For any incidental conversion into Bermudian dollars, the authorised commercial banks effect exchange at spot rates determined in line with the BMA peg, with no surrender tax or exchange-control commission. Overall assessability: capital and current account are operationally fully convertible for a non-resident ELP.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds received by a non-resident exempted limited partnership. There is no requirement under the Exchange Control Act 1972 or BMA directives requiring sales proceeds from bonded-trade deliveries of aged Scotch or Bordeaux wines to be surrendered, converted, or repatriated into Bermudian dollars. The ELP may lawfully retain the full gross export receipts in the original transaction currency, whether USD, GBP, EUR, or another freely convertible currency, and may hold such proceeds indefinitely in offshore accounts at licensed Bermudian banks or regulated foreign institutions. If the partnership were ever to become resident, the BMA-delegated authorised banks would apply the standard resident framework, but the commercial structure and constitutional documents will maintain non-resident designation to preserve the nil-surrender regime. Consequently, export proceeds are fully fungible for working capital, supplier settlements, inter-company financing, and distribution to partners, subject only to standard AML/CFT reporting thresholds and FATCA/CRS recordkeeping.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the integrated financial services regulator, central bank, and exchange-control authority for Bermuda; the Bank of England has no direct exchange-control jurisdiction over Bermuda but acts as the final settlement bank for Sterling-denominated clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH for collection-side and disbursement-side payment aggregation through FCA-regulated UK EMIs and EU payment institutions, but with legally binding constraints. A non-resident Bermuda ELP is generally not categorised as a classic high-risk offshore structure when registered in an FATF-compliant and OECD-equivalent jurisdiction, so EMIs such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer, and similar virtual account providers may accept the partnership subject to enhanced due diligence. However, the specific business scope of spirits and wine bonded trade triggers sector-specific AML concerns, including excise-duty evasion, counterfeit goods, and diversion into unlicensed premises. The EMI must be used strictly as a payment collection and conversion rail, not as a treasury storage platform; deposits at EMIs must be maintained below the e-money safeguarding threshold in the EMI's institution account and swept at least weekly to the primary Bermudian bank account. Contractual terms must prohibit cash withdrawal, zero-knowledge ownership structures, and the use of personal cards for trade settlement. The corporate banking blueprint therefore designates EMIs as auxiliary current-account rails for marketplace collections and small-ticket disbursements, while all bonded-inventory title payments exceeding GBP/EUR 50,000 must settle through tier-1 bank correspondent clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All initial and supplemental capital contributions shall be executed exclusively through regulated banking channels using SWIFT MT103 for direct shareholder-to-ELP transfers, or SWIFT MT202 for upgraded bank-to-bank underlying transfers, to the ELP's segregated capital escrow account at Bank of N.T. Butterfield or HSBC Bank Bermuda. The contributor must initiate the wire from a bank account of the same legal name; third-party funding is strictly prohibited unless prior written approval is obtained from the bank's compliance department. The SWIFT message shall include the cable information field 70 structured as 'CAPC / INTC / BMELP' and an explicit reference to the dated capital contribution clause of the limited partnership agreement; the ISO purpose code should be set to CAPC (capital account contribution) or the receiving bank's equivalent category for equity injection into a non-resident investment vehicle, and never the generic personal remittance code. Before activation, the treasury team must provide the bank with: (i) a certificate of non-resident status issued by the Bermuda Tax Authority or the Ministry of Finance; (ii) the applicable CRS and FATCA registration confirmation; (iii) a funding breakdown per limited partner, including passport copies, proof of address, and source-of-wealth statements; and (iv) a signed bank-side anti-money-laundering declaration from the general partner. The bank shall verify the initiating account with a call-back confirmation to the partner's registered mobile number and will not credit the operational ledger until the capital escrow ledger has passed postal-mail dual confirmation. This rail is available in USD, GBP, EUR, and any BMA-delegated freely convertible currency without a surrender requirement.Outbound bonded-trade and operating disbursements shall use a two-lane clearing architecture: (i) SWIFT MT103 direct remittance for standard supplier invoices, excise duty settlements, logistics fees, and warehouse charges; and (ii) irrevocable commercial letters of credit, standby LCs, and documentary collections for large-ticket inventory purchases of aged Scotch and Bordeaux wines, typically exceeding USD 250,000. Commercial LC issuance will be distributed through HSBC Bermuda's trade desk with advising through HSBC London or HSBC France to the bonded-warehouse seller. Presentation packets must include a commercial invoice, the warehouse duty-suspension movement record, certificate of insurance, weight/volume certificate, and any appellation or authentication certificate required by the LC terms. For duty-bonded transfers between EU and UK storage facilities, the payment is instructed by MT103 with field 70 saying 'BONDED TRANSFER / DUTY SUSPENSION' and the EMCS administrative reference code; no funds are released prior to the warehouse transfer confirmation. All outbound wires must be denominated in the underlying commercial currency of the invoice; spot conversion is permitted at the primary bank's dealing desk; and same-day settlement is possible for USD/GBP/EUR via CHAPS, Fedwire, and TARGET2, respectively. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax on Exempted Limited Partnerships). From fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 imposes a 15% CIT on constituent entities of in-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750M; the ELP's tax-transparent status generally passes the incidence to its UK partners in the ordinary case.

Part 3 – General Partner Name, address and jurisdiction of the General Partner and its governing law. A non-resident GP must still appoint a Bermuda-resident authorized representative to accept service of process. Late filing: default fee of BD$250 per month or part month of delay; continued default triggers a Registrar's notice of intention to strike off, dissolution of the ELP, and loss of limited-liability protection for partners from the strike-off date.
