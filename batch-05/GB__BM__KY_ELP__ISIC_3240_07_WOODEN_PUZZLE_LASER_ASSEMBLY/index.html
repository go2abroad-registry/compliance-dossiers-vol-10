Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (Government of Bermuda; online services via www.gov.bm); Bermuda Customs Department for import/export duty administration

E-government filing portal

Foreign sponsors register the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Limited Partnership Act 2015, obtaining a Business Registry (BR) number. No corporate income tax TIN or VAT ID is issued because Bermuda levies no income tax. Upon hiring Bermuda-based employees, the ELP must register as an employer with the Tax Commissioner under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 to receive a Payroll Tax Account Number. For cross-border goods movements, registration with Bermuda Customs for an importer/exporter code is required, normally arranged through a licensed customs broker.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Bermuda Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent and bears no Bermuda entity-level income, corporation, capital gains, or withholding taxes. Each partner recognises its allocable share of profits in its own tax residence jurisdiction. The ELP has no Bermuda assessable income profile and falls outside the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 unless it is a constituent entity of a large multinational group with global revenue of €750 million or more.

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic election permits entity-level taxation; the ELP cannot elect to be opaque in Bermuda. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are evaluated under OECD and UK rules rather than by Bermuda election. For U.S. federal purposes, the ELP may lodge a Form 8832 check-the-box election to be classified as a partnership or disregarded entity; such election is independent of Bermuda treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST; indirect levies comprise Bermuda Customs Duties under the Customs Tariff Act and Customs Act, plus employer-paid Payroll Tax and excise-type charges on specified goods such as fuel, alcoholic beverages, and tobacco products

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT registration threshold exists because no VAT is imposed. Customs duty is assessed on commercial imports from the first Bermudian dollar of value; personal traveller allowances are separate. Payroll Tax registration is triggered only when the ELP employs Bermuda-resident staff, with no turnover-based indirect-tax registration requirement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Bermuda neither levies VAT on domestic supply nor taxes exports, so export zero-rating and input VAT refund mechanics have no statutory foundation. Physical exports leave Bermuda free of customs duty upon filing a truthful export declaration; no refund application is possible or necessary. For exports routed to the UK or EU, the ELP must comply with destination-country import VAT rules, and Bermuda issues no certificate supporting VAT zero-rating claims.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (electronic goods declaration)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Certificate of Origin (as required by the destination customs authority)

No electronic invoicing, continuous transaction control, or real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists. Bermuda Customs requires an electronic goods declaration for shipment clearance, filed in advance through a licensed broker or the customs portal, but there is no government API clearance layer, digital stamping, or e-invoice validation obligation.

Not applicable to VAT refunds because no VAT exists. Bermuda Customs may audit export/import entries and, in cases of false or negligent declarations, impose forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties, and retrospective duty assessments equal to duty evaded plus interest. Given the absence of Bermuda exchange controls, the 180-day repatriation figure is a contractual and documentary benchmark tied to UK inbound remittance evidentiary practice, not a Bermuda statutory deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a 1:1 fixed exchange rate to the United States Dollar (USD). Bermuda maintains an open, fully convertible current and capital account regime with no exchange controls. Non-resident entities, including Exempted Limited Partnerships, may freely receive, hold, and remit funds in any internationally accepted currency. Profit remittances and dividend or distribution repatriation are unrestricted, subject only to standard anti-money laundering compliance, beneficial ownership disclosure, and relevant Economic Substance reporting obligations. There are no material hurdles to currency conversion or capital repatriation for a low-risk commercial manufacturer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio is imposed by Bermuda. Export receipts may be retained in any convertible currency without forced exchange into BMD, and no timeframe exists for repatriation or conversion of trade proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with restrictions. Bermuda does not have a full licensing framework for offshore digital EMI-only operations; cross-border EMI providers (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business) can offer multi-currency collection and payout services to Bermuda entities, but they are not regulated as Bermuda deposit-taking institutions. Legal constraints include termination risk for non-bank counterparties, lack of protected client money segregation in the same manner as BMA-licensed banks, and stronger know-your-customer (KYC) standards when routing via UK or EU-licensed EMIs. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 wire transfers from a partner's or parent company's bank account to the Bermuda ELP's operational account, using the following compliant protocols: (1) reference the purpose of payment under the BOP category 'T1011 – Equity Investment' or 'Direct Investment – Share Capital'; (2) include the ELP's unique tax/company reference and bank account number in the beneficiary field; (3) use a UK or US correspondent bank for fastest clearing due to the USD/BMD peg; (4) provide a board or partnership resolution approving the capital contribution; (5) ensure all beneficial owners' identities have been pre-cleared by the receiving bank; and (6) obtain a credit advice or SWIFT IRT confirmation for audit trail.Trade disbursements for production supplies, logistics, and supplier payments are cleared through SWIFT MT103 standard wire transfers for low-value repetitive transactions, and through irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit (LCs) at sight for high-value or first-time supplier relationships. For immediate settlement, CHAPS (UK), FEDWIRE (USD), and TARGET2 (EUR) rails can be used via correspondent banking. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold is prescribed in BMD because there is no income tax. For a Bermuda ELP that is a UK-affiliated multinational entity, UK CTA 2010 Part 4 transfer-pricing rules apply when UK filings are relevant, and UK Country-by-Country Reporting is mandatory where the ultimate parent's consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds GBP 750 million. Under Bermuda ESR, all entities carrying on relevant activities must retain contemporaneous documentation evidencing substance, staff costs, and CIGA, regardless of revenue level.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to any Bermuda-registered entity carrying on a 'relevant activity'. Precision CO2 laser cutting and wooden-puzzle assembly is not itself listed as a relevant activity, but if the ELP combines it with worldwide distribution and service provision, an Amazon-FBA logistics hub or procurement centre will be classified as a Distribution and Service Centre; holding or licensing IP can be classified as an Intellectual Property Business. The ELP must be directed and managed in Bermuda, have adequate full-time employees and qualified personnel in Bermuda, incur adequate operating expenditure in Bermuda, maintain physical premises in Bermuda, and perform core income-generating activities in Bermuda. A 'pure equity holding entity' has a reduced substance requirement but must still hold shares or equity interests and comply with filing duties. An ELP that proves tax residence in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction, such as the United Kingdom, and has economic substance there may be excluded from the Bermuda ESR for the relevant activity but must file an annual declaration and supply the foreign tax residence certificate.
