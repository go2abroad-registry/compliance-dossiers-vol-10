Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Bermuda law imposes no minimum paid-up capital for an exempted limited partnership. The partnership agreement governs the amount, timing and form of capital contributions, and there is no statutory escrow account requirement. Where the partnership accepts customer settlement funds before delivery, such funds must be held in a designated client-money account with a licensed bank or authorised payment institution; escrow terms imposed by payment processors are contractual rather than statutory.

Funding & unlock path: No fire-safety certificate is required for a registered office without public access. For a leased fulfilment space, a Bermuda Fire and Rescue Service clearance and a structural load inspection under the Bermuda Building Code 2014 are required before occupancy. If five or more employees work physically in Bermuda, the Department of Labour must issue an Occupational Safety and Health clearance before operations commence.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda: Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC) — official portal https://taxcommissioner.gov.bm; Registrar of Companies — https://www.roc.gov.bm. United Kingdom: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration for UK VAT registration of the overseas ELP.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda levies no income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT, so no general TIN/VAT ID is issued. The ELP obtains its statutory identity by: (1) registering under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992 with the Registrar of Companies, obtaining a Registered Number and Certificate of Formation; (2) filing annually for a 'Certificate of Compliance' within 30 days of each anniversary as proof of good standing; (3) registering with the OTC for Payroll Tax and Social Insurance (obtaining a Payroll Tax Registration Number) only if it has Bermuda-resident employees; (4) obtaining automatic Exchange Control 'Standard Consent' from the Bermuda Monetary Authority as a foreign-owned non-resident entity, permitting USD/BMD foreign-currency bank accounts; (5) for UK supply-side activities, registering online for UK VAT via HMRC's 'Register for VAT' service using the Bermuda Registered Number as the corporate identifier, with HMRC assigning a GB VAT number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Bermuda ELP is fiscally transparent; each partner is taxed on its share of profits by residence (a UK corporate partner is charged under UK CTA 2009 Part 5). Bermuda imposes no tax on worldwide or Bermuda-source income, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, and no indirect tax. The ELP benefits from the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing immunity from any future Bermuda income or capital tax.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box regime. Available elections: (1) under the Exempted Partnerships Act 1992, the Partnership Agreement may elect that the ELP have separate legal personality or remain a pure contractual partnership; (2) under the Economic Substance Act 2018, an ELP carrying on a 'relevant activity' may elect to be treated as tax resident in another jurisdiction if centrally managed and controlled there, but online retail distribution of jewelry and timepieces is not a relevant activity, so no economic substance filing is required; (3) under UK Part 6A TIOPA 2010 hybrid-mismatch rules, the ELP is treated as transparent, and a UK parent must ensure deductible payments (e.g., management fees or financing) made to the ELP are matched by corresponding UK inclusions to prevent deduction denial, as Bermuda imposes no tax on the ELP's receipt.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: No VAT, GST, or Sales and Use Tax; import customs duties and stamp duty apply at the border. United Kingdom (supply-side nexus): UK VAT at 20% standard rate applies to the ELP's online sales of jewelry and timepieces to UK consumers; the ELP is an 'overseas seller' and is the deemed supplier for low-value consignments not exceeding GBP 135, and the importer of record for higher-value goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: BMD 0 — no indirect tax registration threshold. UK: GBP 0 mandatory registration for non-established sellers of imported goods in consignments not exceeding GBP 135 to UK consumers (no turnover threshold); GBP 90,000 threshold for other taxable supplies made by an overseas person, effective from 1 April 2024.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: N/A — no VAT is chargeable on exports from Bermuda, so zero-rating is inapplicable. UK: If the ELP registers for UK VAT and sells goods to customers outside the UK, zero-rating applies where the goods are exported from the UK to a third-country destination (including Bermuda) within 3 months of the sale, and the ELP holds prescribed export evidence; zero-rating also applies to certain services related to goods supplied to overseas recipients who belong outside the UK. For internet sales imported directly into the UK from Bermuda, zero-rating is not available; the point-of-sale import VAT rules instead apply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK CDS export entry with MRN reference evidencing departure from GB)
  • Bill of Lading (sea freight) or Air Waybill (air freight) proving physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank/FX receipt evidencing payment from the overseas buyer into the ELP's Bermuda or UK account)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (itemized jewelry/timepiece descriptions, values, and HS codes)
  • Proof of Export (P2P) documentation where simplified declarations are used

Bermuda: No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping regime. UK: Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT is mandatory — the ELP must maintain digital records and file VAT returns via MTD-compatible software through HMRC APIs; no real-time HMRC clearance or e-invoicing stamping requirement exists. Commercial e-invoicing (e.g., PEPPOL) is contractual, not statutory, for this B2C jewelry/fashion online trade.

HMRC may issue VAT assessments for 4 years (extended to 20 years for deliberate or concealed conduct), plus inaccuracy penalties of 30% to 100% of the VAT loss under Schedule 24 FA 2007. Unsupported zero-rating claims trigger retrospective clawback of output VAT at 20% plus interest under section 73 VATA 1994. Fraudulent export claims may lead to criminal prosecution under section 72 VATA 1994 and civil penalties of up to 100% of the evaded VAT. Bermuda: no indirect-tax clawback exists; however, the OTC may audit payroll tax and impose penalties of up to 25% of the underpaid amount plus interest for non-compliant employers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar, and US dollars circulate freely as legal tender. Capital-account and current-account transactions are freely convertible for an Exempted Limited Partnership that has been granted non-resident exchange-control status by the BMA, a status automatically obtainable for entities registered under the Exempted Partnerships Act 2009 and used exclusively for business outside Bermuda. Profit remittance in the form of distributions and capital redemptions to non-resident partners faces no BMA approval hurdle provided the ELP's partner contribution registers are correctly endorsed; if the partnership were instead classified as resident for exchange-control purposes, prior BMA consent would be required for each remittance, which is unlikely given the intended e-commerce business scope.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For an ELP designated as non-resident, export proceeds arising from online jewelry and watch sales are not subject to mandatory surrender to an authorised dealer, and may be retained in foreign-currency or BMD/USD accounts. Any resident-registered entity would be required under the Exchange Control Regulations to surrender export receipts within one month unless granted a specific non-resident designation or exemption. As the target ELP operates wholly outside Bermuda and intends to obtain non-resident exchange-control status, the surrender time-limit is not applicable; however, the general partner must maintain a current certificate of non-resident status from the BMA to avoid any future requirement.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972, the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and the Bermuda Companies Act; the BMA is the central bank and exchange control administrator for the Bermudian dollar (BMD).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Rating: Medium for cross-border payment-service integration; Low for core banking. Bermuda does not licence domestic electronic money institutions, and only entities licensed under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 may take deposits. A Bermuda ELP may open settlement accounts with UK or EU-licensed EMIs (for example, Wave, Revolut Business, or Wise) to collect customer payments from Stripe/PayPal and to execute EUR/GBP supplier payments, but such EMIs cannot offer the same counterparty reliability, US clearing access, or capital-es-crow features as Tier-1 Bermudian banks. Legal constraints include the inability of a foreign EMI to conduct regulated banking business in Bermuda without a domestic license, and certain Bermudian banks will not accept EMI-originated Apple-pay/digital-purse funds as 'bank-funded' clearing without enhanced source-of-funds evidence.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant injection of partner capital should be executed via SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's designated BMD or USD account at a Bermudian Tier-1 bank. The remittance information field must explicitly state 'Contribution to capital of an Exempted Limited Partnership' and reference the relevant date of the partners' capital commitment. For non-resident exchange-control status, the inbound wire may carry purpose code 'GIFO' (General investment-fund / capital-transfer) or, if the originating bank requires, 'B100' for capital contributions; the receiving Bermudian bank will verify the remittance against the certified register of partners. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general corporate income tax on Bermuda-source and worldwide income for an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP); 15% Corporate Income Tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA) applicable only to Constituent Entities of MNE Groups with consolidated revenue of at least USD 750 million, operative for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda does not impose withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or profit allocations made by an Exempted Limited Partnership to its UK parent or any non-resident partner.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda CITA 2023 applies only to Constituent Entities of MNE Groups with consolidated revenue of at least BMD 750 million (USD 750 million). Below this threshold, no Bermuda Local File or Master File is statutorily required. For in-scope groups, the BRA may require contemporaneous documentation supporting the arm's-length basis of intercompany transactions, and the GloBE Information Return must be prepared; a UK parent should nevertheless prepare OECD-compliant Master File and Local File because HMRC will assert transfer-pricing documentation obligations.

Economic substance scope: The Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2018, as amended, and the Economic Substance Regulations 2019 require every relevant entity carrying on a relevant activity to maintain adequate economic substance in Bermuda. Online merchandising of jewelry, watches, and fashion goods constitutes a Distribution and Service Centre Business. The ELP must demonstrate (i) management and direction in Bermuda, (ii) a physical office, (iii) suitably qualified full-time employees proportionate to activity, and (iv) minimum operating expenditure in Bermuda. The annual substance declaration is mandatory irrespective of CIT scope; a purely passive or letterbox structure fails the test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Understatement

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the evaded tax, statutory interest, and daily default penalties; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion carries fines up to BMD 250,000. In the UK, the same profits may attract penalties up to 100% of the tax and the 25% Diverted Profits Tax on artificially diverted UK profits.

Director personal liability: The general partner and UK directors may be pursued for fraudulent trading, with civil restitution orders, statutory disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion of tax.
Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Initial ESR penalty of BMD 10,000 and daily penalties of BMD 1,000; fines up to BMD 250,000 for false declarations; strike-off of the ELP registration; information-sharing notices issued to HMRC under the exchange of information provisions.

Director personal liability: Court orders compelling the general partner to restore the ELP to good standing; revocation of the general partner's authorisation; UK parent directors face liability under the UK CFC and DPT rules, including personal liability for penalties where they knowingly approved or tolerated the sham structure.
Transfer Pricing / Diverted Profits

Statutory sanction: HMRC transfer-pricing adjustments under TIOPA 2010, application of the 25% Diverted Profits Tax, interest, and penalties up to 100% of the tax charge; for in-scope groups, the BRA may impose QDMTT-based top-up adjustments and the UK parent becomes liable for the GloBE Income Inclusion Rule top-up.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign incorrect returns or cause the creation of artificial transactions can be personally assessed for penalties under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007; directors of the ELP general partner can be made personally liable for the diverted profits tax where the corporate veil is pierced for sham pricing arrangements.
AML / Sanctions / High-Value Goods Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under the UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002: unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; under Bermuda's AML/ATF regime: fines up to BMD 1 million per breach, suspension of the ELP's registration, and asset-freeze orders; Stripe/PayPal may terminate payment processing and report to the financial intelligence unit.

Director personal liability: The Money Laundering Reporting Officer, general partner, and UK directors face personal criminal liability, seizure of personal assets, travel bans, extradition under the UK–Bermuda Mutual Legal Assistance framework, and disqualification from acting as company directors.
Non-Filing / Compliance Obstruction

Statutory sanction: Daily default penalties for non-filing, judicial restoration procedures, and strike-off of the ELP; HMRC failure-to-notify penalties up to 100% of the tax due, information notices enforceable by the First-tier Tribunal, and search and seizure under the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984.

Director personal liability: Officers can be made personally liable for penalties where non-compliance is attributable to their consent or neglect; the general partner may be sued by limited partners for unlawful distributions, and civil recovery proceedings may compel directors to restore misapplied funds, subject to travel restrictions pending final judgment.
