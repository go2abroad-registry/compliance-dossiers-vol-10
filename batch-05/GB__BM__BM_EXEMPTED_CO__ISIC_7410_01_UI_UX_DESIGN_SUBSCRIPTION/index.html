Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance, Office of the Tax Commissioner (Hamilton, Bermuda); corporate registration authorized via the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981. Official government portal: https://www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated by a locally licensed advocate or corporate service provider. The Registrar of Companies issues a Bermuda Company Registration Number (BCRN) and a Certificate of Incorporation. Bermuda does not currently impose direct income tax, so no standard corporate TIN is issued for income tax purposes. If the exempted company has employees, it must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 and obtain a payroll tax account number. The company must also file annual economic substance declarations with the Economic Substance Authority through the Registrar's portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist in Bermuda because there is no corporate income tax. A Bermuda Exempted Company may apply to the Minister of Finance for a Certificate of Exemption under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which contractually guarantees exemption from future Bermuda taxes (excluding payroll tax, land tax, stamp duty on real property transfers, and taxes expressly covered) generally until 31 March 2035 or, for certain entities, up to 2056. Entities undertaking relevant activities must elect to comply with the Economic Substance Act 2018 and file an economic substance report.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (Bermuda does not impose VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; limited customs duties under the Customs Act 1990 and stamp duty under the Stamp Act 1976 apply to specified transactions and goods)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists in Bermuda. Customs registration is required for any importer or exporter of dutiable goods with no minimum financial threshold; payroll tax registration is required for any employer, with the standard employer contribution becoming payable above the BMD 10,000 weekly wage exemption band.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT, GST, or sales tax regime, and therefore no export zero-rating mechanism. Digital design subscription services provided by a Bermuda Exempted Company to non-resident clients are entirely outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation. No zero-rated invoice or refund claim arises under Bermuda law.

  • Customs export declaration required only for physical export of goods under the Bermuda Customs Act 1990
  • Commercial invoice and bill of lading/airway bill retained for shipping records
  • Inward remittance records maintained for compliance with the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing regulations

None — Bermuda does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping system for indirect taxes. Payroll tax and other tax filings, where applicable, are submitted electronically through the Bermuda PAYE and Payroll Tax portal; electronic customs declarations are handled through the BES (Bermuda e-Customs) system, but without automatic tax clearance requirements.

Not applicable to VAT zero-rating because no indirect tax refund system exists. However, fraudulent customs declarations are prosecuted under the Bermuda Customs Act 1990 and attract civil penalties of up to BMD 25,000 plus forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment up to three years on conviction. An exempted company that misdeclares its economic substance or misrepresents its Bermuda residency for foreign tax purposes may face financial penalties issued by the Economic Substance Authority (up to BMD 100,000 per initial failure and BMD 10,000 per day of continuing default) and restitution orders. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility in both current and capital accounts. The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged to the United States Dollar (USD) at a fixed rate of 1.00:1.00, and the BMA maintains full external backing of the currency issue under the Currency Act 1972. USD is accepted as legal tender and circulates freely within the banking system, granting a Bermuda Exempted Company de facto parity access to USD banking infrastructure. The BMA does not operate exchange controls over any transaction, and no approval, allocation, or quota applies to profit remittance, dividend declarations, or liquidation proceeds. The only residual friction lies at the bank level: compliance verification of economic substance, beneficial ownership, and source of funds under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda removed its exchange control regime through the Exchange Control (Removal) Order 1993, and the BMA no longer administers exchange control consent. Export proceeds generated by the UI/UX design subscription business may be held in any currency, converted freely, swept into Bermuda operational accounts at any time, or retained abroad without a statutory surrender timeline. No mandatory conversion ratio applies to any foreign-currency revenue, including USD-denominated Stripe Billing settlements and EUR or GBP subscription receipts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% standard Bermuda exempted company income tax rate; 15% Bermuda corporate income tax (CIT) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies only to constituent entities of multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue ≥ EUR 750 million in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends distributed by an exempted company to resident or non-resident shareholders

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For in-scope CIT entities, the return is due no later than seven months after the fiscal year end; annual ROC declaration due within 6 months after the fiscal year end or as otherwise determined by the Companies Act 1981 regulations

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 5,000,000 (approximately USD 5,000,000) in aggregate related-party transactions for in-scope CIT entities; for out-of-scope exempted companies no transfer pricing documentation is statutorily required, but ordinary business records must evidence arm's length pricing because the CIT Act incorporates the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines for qualifying multinational groups

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) impose a substance test on relevant activities: banking, insurance, fund management, financing, headquarters, shipping, intellectual property holding, and holding business. A UI/UX design subscription agency engaged in active digital-delivery services is not a prescribed relevant activity, provided it does not earn income from the exploitation of intangible property; if the company merely holds or licenses design-related IP as a separate profit stream, it would need to satisfy geographic-specific direction and management, adequate premises, full-time employees, and operating expenditure in Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Misrepresentation of CIT Status

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded or a fixed fine of BMD 100,000 for an indictable offence; on summary conviction a fine of up to BMD 50,000; court may order revised assessments with interest; the company may be liable for restoration orders and withdrawal of exemptions if perpetrated through the Registrar

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly assist in filing false returns or who sign misleading declarations are personally liable for fines up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years on conviction; civil recovery proceedings may pierce the corporate veil to recover tax plus interest
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BMD 10,000 for each failure, BMD 25,000 for continued non-compliance, and possible striking off; if subject to ESR relevant activity, the Registrar may impose BMD 250,000 for knowingly providing false information; the company's tax exemption may be revoked with retrospective effect in egregious cases

Director personal liability: Directors risk being disqualified from acting as a director for up to 5 years, travel restrictions, and personal liability for company debts if they permitted trading while the company lacked substance and was subsequently struck off; insolvency law may unwind transactions
Anti-Money Laundering / Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018, penalties include a fine of up to BMD 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering; failure to register as a Trust Service Provider or impose customer due diligence leads to regulatory penalties and suspension of the company's bank accounts

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers can be criminally prosecuted for knowingly facilitating money laundering; personal fines up to BMD 500,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years are available; civil asset forfeiture may attach to the personal estates of directors if they knowingly benefited from the scheme
Failure to File Annual Returns and Receive Tax Clearance

Statutory sanction: Default fines under the Companies Act 1981 of BMD 100 per day for non-compliance with filing; striking off by the Registrar and dissolution of the company; the company's assets may escheat to the Crown; failure to file CIT return attracts BMD 500 per month up to BMD 5,000 and possible assessment by the Tax Commissioner

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for any penalties unpaid by the company where statutory notices were ignored; upon striking off, personal liability to creditors may arise, and directors may be temporarily barred from managing any Bermuda company and face a personal fine of BMD 10,000
