Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce de Paris, operating through the Guichet unique des formalités des entreprises (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) and online RCS repositories.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce) Articles L.210-1 to L.210-11, L.224-1 to L.224-3, and L.227-1 to L.227-20; Décret n°84-406 of 30 April 1984 on the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés; Décret n°2023-215 on the Guichet unique des formalités des entreprises.

Formation / constitutional filings

Déclaration de constitution de société (legacy CERFA form M0) filed electronically via the Guichet unique, together with the signed statuts and ancillary registration certificates.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 28 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No legal minimum capital for a SAS; the amount is freely fixed in the statutes. All cash contributions must be fully paid up and deposited with a licensed French bank or a notaire in a blocked account until the RCS registration delivers the Kbis extract.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification for the registered office if open to the public (ERP type 5) issued after a visit by the commission de sécurité; structural load inspection confirming floor capacity for office occupancy; electrical installation conformity certificate (NF C 15-100 or Consuel); workplace clearance preventing occupational hazards and access for emergency services, together with the declaration to the occupational health service and the longitudinal health monitoring programme.

Paid-in capital rule: No legal minimum capital for a SAS; the amount is freely fixed in the statutes. All cash contributions must be fully paid up and deposited with a licensed French bank or a notaire in a blocked account until the RCS registration delivers the Kbis extract.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

DREAL Île-de-France (environmental) and Chambre de Commerce et d'Industrie de Paris - Île-de-France (business licensing interface); no sectoral industrial board applies to an international tax advisory consultancy.

Category: Extrait Kbis issued by the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce as the official right-to-operate certificate for the SAS; no additional classified industrial operating permit is required for a pure consultancy under the ICPE regime.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) via the official portal at impots.gouv.fr

E-government filing portal

French entities including SAS must register with INSEE to obtain a SIREN number, followed by mandatory registration with the tax authorities to secure a VAT identification number (FR followed by SIREN). Foreign-owned entities adhere to identical procedures upon incorporation or acquisition in France.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — French entities including SAS must register with INSEE to obtain a SIREN number, followed by mandatory registration with the tax authorities to secure a VAT identification number (FR followed by SIREN). Foreign-owned entities adhere to identical procedures upon incorporation or acquisition in France.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

20%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to exports of goods and offshore services upon proof of actual export or non-domestic service delivery, substantiated by customs documentation and evidence of foreign performance.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains a regime of free convertibility for both current and capital accounts pursuant to EU single market rules and OECD commitments. Profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are permitted without prior approval or surrender, subject only to standard tax filing obligations and ongoing AML/CFT verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds are not subject to mandatory surrender or conversion requirements. Exporters may retain foreign currency receipts in dedicated accounts. All export transactions remain subject to mandatory customs declaration formalities and statistical reporting to INSEE for aggregate data purposes.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasible for regulated electronic money institutions licensed by the ACPR under MiCA and PSD2 frameworks; offshore EMIs must maintain full French AML/CFT compliance and may face operational restrictions on certain cross-border payment corridors.

25% (standard rate; reduced 15% on first €42,500 of taxable profit for SMEs)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

30% (12.8% income tax + 17.2% social contributions) for non-residents, unless reduced by tax treaty

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Full documentation (Master File and Local File) required if group revenue > €400 million or balance sheet > €400 million; simplified documentation for smaller entities

Economic substance scope: France applies anti-abuse rules (e.g., substance-over-form, GAAR) but no specific ESR; however, for tax structuring, substance must be demonstrated to avoid sham transaction allegations

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to €500,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; civil penalty of 80% of evaded tax

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for unpaid taxes if they participated in fraudulent acts; travel bans and asset freezes possible
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of transactions, denial of treaty benefits, penalty of 40% of tax understated

Director personal liability: Directors may face civil restitution and disqualification from managing companies; criminal charges for abuse of law
AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine up to €5 million or 5% of turnover; criminal penalties for money laundering up to 5 years imprisonment

Director personal liability: Directors can be held criminally liable for AML breaches; personal fines and imprisonment; travel bans may be imposed
