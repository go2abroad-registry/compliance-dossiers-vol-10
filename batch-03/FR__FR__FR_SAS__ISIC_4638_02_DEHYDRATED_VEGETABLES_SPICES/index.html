Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A French SAS must have a registered office in France under Code de commerce Articles L.123-11 et seq. and L.227-1 et seq. A domiciliation company or serviced-office address may be used if the provider is authorized under Code de commerce Articles L.123-11-2 et seq., a written domiciliation contract is maintained, and the address permits receipt and retention of official correspondence. A simple mail-forwarding address, undisclosed nominee address, or address incapable of supporting customer due diligence is unsuitable. For bulk dehydrated vegetables and spice trading, a virtual office may be acceptable for a holding, sales and contracting function, but it does not replace any required warehouse, food-business, import, customs, traceability, labeling, storage, or product-safety arrangements. The company should maintain real management records, accounting records, banking controls, supplier files and transaction evidence in France or another demonstrably controlled location.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The competent commercial registry is the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés maintained by the competent Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce. Filing is submitted electronically through the national Guichet unique des formalités des entreprises operated by the Institut National de la Propriété Industrielle.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code, particularly Articles L.227-1 to L.227-20 and R.227-1 to R.227-2 governing the Société par Actions Simplifiée, together with Articles L.123-1 et seq. and R.123-1 et seq. governing the Trade and Companies Register and corporate formalities.

Formation / constitutional filings

Electronic declaration of company creation through the Guichet unique, accompanied by the signed SAS articles of association, legal-publication certificate, capital-deposit certificate, beneficial-owner declaration, and supporting evidence for the registered office and officers.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum share capital applies to an SAS; capital may be EUR 1 or any higher amount stated in the articles. At least 50% of cash contributions must be paid on incorporation, with the balance payable within five years of registration. Cash capital is deposited with a French bank, notary, or other legally authorized depositary, which issues the certificate of deposit. The deposited funds are released after registration upon presentation of the Kbis extract. Contributions in kind generally require an auditor's valuation unless the statutory exemption conditions are satisfied.

Funding & unlock path: Before opening premises, obtain or maintain fire-safety compliance appropriate to the building classification, including emergency exits, extinguishers, alarms, evacuation signage, and fire-prevention controls. Verify structural floor loading and racking safety for bulk goods. Complete the required occupational-risk assessment, electrical and equipment inspections, workplace hygiene controls, dust-exposure assessment, and employee training. If the site is an établissement recevant du public or otherwise subject to public-access rules, obtain the applicable accessibility and safety approval from the competent municipal or departmental authority.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum share capital applies to an SAS; capital may be EUR 1 or any higher amount stated in the articles. At least 50% of cash contributions must be paid on incorporation, with the balance payable within five years of registration. Cash capital is deposited with a French bank, notary, or other legally authorized depositary, which issues the certificate of deposit. The deposited funds are released after registration upon presentation of the Kbis extract. Contributions in kind generally require an auditor's valuation unless the statutory exemption conditions are satisfied.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No general manufacturing licence is required for ordinary wholesale trading and export of dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper. The relevant local authority for food-business registration, inspections, and sanitary controls is the Direction Départementale de la Protection des Populations. Where premises fall within an environmentally classified activity, the competent authority is the Préfecture, generally supported by the Direction Régionale de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement.

Category: No general operating or manufacturing permit for ordinary dehydrated-spice trading. The operator must comply with food-business registration and notification requirements applicable to its activities, premises, storage, importation, and handling operations.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains a fully convertible euro currency regime aligned with EU and IMF standards. Current account transactions, including trade settlements and profit remittances, face no prior authorization requirements for resident SAS entities. Capital account transactions are generally permitted without restriction for standard corporate operations, subject only to post-transaction reporting to tax authorities. Profit distributions to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted once tax compliance and beneficial ownership verification are satisfied.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or forced conversion of export proceeds exists under French law. Exporters may retain foreign currency in offshore or multi-currency accounts. All export transactions must nevertheless be supported by commercial invoices, packing lists, and shipping documents, and declared to Douane (customs) for statistical and fiscal reporting purposes. Repatriation remains voluntary but is strongly encouraged to mitigate FX and AML risks.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility for licensed French fintech EMI providers or international platforms such as Wise Business, subject to AMF authorization, full KYC/AML compliance, and adherence to French data localization rules. Offshore digital EMIs remain constrained by banking confidentiality and potential licensing requirements.
