Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce (Registrar of the Commercial Court) of the registered office's jurisdiction, operating through the Guichet Unique des Formalités des Entreprises (single business formalities window) of the INPI (Institut National de la Propriété Industrielle) at formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce): SAS regime, Articles L.227-1 to L.227-20; general incorporation and registration, Articles L.210-1 to L.210-9, L.224-1 to L.224-8, R.224-3 et seq.; RCS (Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés), Articles L.123-1, R.123-30.

Formation / constitutional filings

Electronic dossier de création d'entreprise submitted via the Guichet Unique; registration produces the RCS certificate 'Extrait K-Bis' and the SIREN/SIRET numbers; the statutory Articles of Association (statuts) are annexed as the constitutive instrument.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 7 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital for an SAS; the Articles may validly fix capital at €1. Cash subscriptions must be paid up to at least 50% of nominal value upon subscription; the balance must be fully paid within five years of registration (Art. L.227-2, Code de commerce). All cash contributions must be escrowed in a blocked account (compte bloqué) at a French bank, the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, or a notary; funds are released to the President only upon presentation of the Extrait K-Bis. In-kind contributions are fully liberated upon subscription.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

DGFiP - Direction Generale des Finances Publiques (French General Directorate of Public Finances), Ministry of Economy, Finance and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty; taxpayer portal: impots.gouv.fr; business-formation portal (Guichet Unique): formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr; customs and export clearance: DGDDI - Direction Generale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (douane.gouv.fr); VAT identification is assigned by the local Service des Impots des Entreprises (SIE).

E-government filing portal

Standard path for a French SAS (including 100% foreign-owned): (1) incorporate via Guichet Unique (single electronic window) submitting the articles of association, shareholder/UBO detail and the appointed president; (2) the file is transmitted to the Registre du Commerce et des Societes (RCS) through the commercial court registry; the Kbis extract is issued containing the 9-digit SIREN and SIRET, which constitute the statutory French tax identification number; (3) for a taxable activity the SIE automatically issues the intra-Community VAT number (format FR + 2-digit key + 9-digit SIREN, 13 characters in total); if not auto-issued, the company files VAT-registration form 11407 with the competent SIE; (4) a foreign company with no French registered office but with a French fixed establishment files the same Guichet Unique foreign-entity dossier and obtains a French FR VAT ID; (5) a non-EU company with no French permanent establishment selling DTC goods into France may either register directly for French VAT under art. 286 ter CGI or use the IOSS for imported consignments up to EUR 150; direct non-EU registration requires a DGFiP-accredited fiscal representative under art. 289 A CGI unless an exemption applies; (6) standard processing time is 5 to 10 working days for SIREN/SIRET, with VAT ID reference back-datable to the first taxable supply.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope (territoriality principle). An SAS is mandatorily subject to corporate income tax (impot sur les societes) under arts. 206-209 CGI with no default pass-through status. Under art. 209-I CGI, only profits from activities carried on in France are taxable in France; profits of foreign permanent establishments are exempt in France unless a fiscal-integration election or treaty provision brings them into the French net. The DTC Shopify webstore is a fully taxable French commercial activity; no Tax_Exempt_Zone or Pass_Through_Eligible classification is available for this scope.

Available election forms

France has no US-style check-the-box classification; the SAS is irrebuttably taxed at entity level. Available elections: (i) fiscal integration (integration fiscale, art. 223 A CGI) - a parent holding at least 95% of the SAS may consolidate French taxable results under a firm 5-year engagement; (ii) optional pass-through for qualifying newly created SMEs under art. 239 bis AA CGI (taxation of shareholders under personal income tax during the first fiscal years, subject to strict statutory conditions and a timely filing with the SIE); (iii) VAT elections: option for the real normal regime when the franchise en base exemption applies; election of the Union One-Stop-Shop (OSS) or Import One-Stop-Shop (IOSS) to centralize EU distance-sales VAT reporting; option for the simplified real regime (regime reel simplifie) below the EUR 840,000 goods-turnover ceiling; (iv) hybrid-mismatch outcomes under art. 212 bis CGI (ATAD) are mandatory and non-elective, but the taxpayer may align financial statements and tax consolidations to invoke the tie-breaker rules avoiding double non-taxation.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France provides full current-account and capital-account convertibility for the euro pursuant to Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. There are no exchange-control authorizations, no prior permits, and no administrative limits on inbound equity injection, profit repatriation, dividend distribution, royalty payments, or intercompany loan servicing for an SAS. The practical frictions are indirect: (i) a French 25% withholding tax on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders unless reduced under an applicable double-tax treaty (e.g., 15% under the France-Singapore treaty, 10% under the France-Hong Kong treaty with conditions, 0-15% under various EU directives/treaties), and (ii) the requirement to document economic substance and arm's-length transfer pricing where a DTC brand invoices French retail clients while sourcing goods from affiliated non-French suppliers. All cross-border flows are automatically captured by the receiving bank through the SITCO tele-transmission system; no prior regulatory validation is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. French law and EU law impose no statutory surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline for export receivables. A French SAS may retain Shopify Payments, PayPal, Stripe, and direct trade-settlement proceeds indefinitely in EUR or in any foreign currency, at any bank inside or outside the EEA. The sole compliance duty is the annual French foreign-account declaration (déclaration d'un compte détenu à l'étranger) that must be filed with the corporate income-tax return if the company maintains an account with a financial institution outside France.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), operating within the Eurosystem and the European System of Central Banks; monetary policy authority for the euro is the European Central Bank, while the Banque de France administers balance-of-payments statistical reporting (SITCO), the direct-investment survey regime, and the prudential infrastructure alongside the ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution) for banking and payment-institution licensing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High feasibility as an auxiliary treasury rail for remote FX and SEPA-instant payments; low-to-medium as the sole operational bank for a France-based SAS. EEA-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Qonto, Manager.One, Payhawk, Airwallex EU entity, or Mangopay) can be onboarded fully virtually, offer French IBANs, and are authorized under PSD2 to hold e-commerce settlement funds. The controlling legal constraints are: (i) client monies held by an EMI are safeguarded under segregation rules but are not covered by the French deposit-guarantee scheme (FGDR); (ii) the EMI must be passported into France and appear in the ACPR register of payment institutions; (iii) documentary trade instruments (Commercial LCs, large SWIFT USD/CNY settlements, import guarantees) require the full balance-sheet credit capacity of a licensed bank and are generally outside EMI product capabilities; and (iv) the treasury must maintain at least one relationship with a tier-1 credit institution for statutory capital, sponsor lines, and regulatory credibility.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident shareholder's bank to the SAS's EUR operational account at BNP Paribas Paris or Société Générale Paris, with the remittance information field (Field 70) reading 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION - SHARE PREMIUM - INWARD DIRECT INVESTMENT' and the SAS's full legal name and registration number. The receiving bank will map the transaction into the Banque de France SITCO balance-of-payments classification as a direct investment; the company must file the annual declaration of financial relations with non-residents where the cross-border investment relationship reaches the EUR 1,000,000 statutory reporting threshold, and the bank will notify the company if a specific survey or états déclaratifs are triggered. No exchange-control clearance or commercial invoice is required. The evidentiary pack consists of the notarized or electronic shareholders' resolution approving the capital increase, the updated articles of association following the share subscription, the share-register entry, and the bank's own account-opening file confirming the corporate UBO. The funds must remain at the disposal of the company; if placed on a 'compte bloqué' for capital formation, the release instruction must match the registration certificate at the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce.For suppliers within the SEPA zone, SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) and SCT Inst provide the efficient low-value clearing rail for recurring dropshipping, logistics, and marketing payouts. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: High feasibility as an auxiliary treasury rail for remote FX and SEPA-instant payments; low-to-medium as the sole operational bank for a France-based SAS. EEA-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Qonto, Manager.One, Payhawk, Airwallex EU entity, or Mangopay) can be onboarded fully virtually, offer French IBANs, and are authorized under PSD2 to hold e-commerce settlement funds. The controlling legal constraints are: (i) client monies held by an EMI are safeguarded under segregation rules but are not covered by the French deposit-guarantee scheme (FGDR); (ii) the EMI must be passported into France and appear in the ACPR register of payment institutions; (iii) documentary trade instruments (Commercial LCs, large SWIFT USD/CNY settlements, import guarantees) require the full balance-sheet credit capacity of a licensed bank and are generally outside EMI product capabilities; and (iv) the treasury must maintain at least one relationship with a tier-1 credit institution for statutory capital, sponsor lines, and regulatory credibility.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% for financial years ending on or after 31 December 2022; previously phased from 33.33% to 28% and then to 25%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

25% statutory withholding tax on gross French-source dividends paid to non-resident corporate shareholders under Article 119bis(2) of the CGI. Non-resident individuals may be subject to the 30% flat tax, comprising 12.8% income tax and 17.2% social levies, subject to treaty. Treaty relief may reduce the rate to 15%, 5%, or 0% depending on the convention, and the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive may eliminate withholding where the EU parent holds at least 10% for at least 24 months.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 400 million consolidated group turnover or total balance-sheet assets triggers the mandatory Local File and Master File requirement under Article L13 AA of the LPF. The Country-by-Country Report threshold is EUR 750 million consolidated group turnover. A standalone Shopify DTC brand below these thresholds is not administratively obligated to prepare full TP documentation, but contemporaneous documentation should be available if intercompany transactions exist.

Economic substance scope: France does not apply domestic Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) modeled on the offshore regimes, but the anti-abuse doctrine and OECD/BEPS minimum substance standards apply. A French SAS must demonstrate real business purpose, local decision-making, physical premises, personnel, and risk control; an artificial mailbox entity without substance may have its corporate tax residence, permanent establishment, and treaty benefits challenged.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Filing

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of the full tax deficiency plus late-payment interest; 40% penalty for bad faith, 80% penalty for fraud or abuse of law under Article 1729 CGI; criminal prosecution under Article 1741 CGI carries a fine of up to EUR 500,000 and five years imprisonment, increased to EUR 3,000,000 and seven years where committed by an organized group.

Director personal liability: Directors who participate in or direct fraudulent schemes are personally liable for criminal fines and imprisonment; tax authorities may pierce the corporate veil and demand civil recovery against directors for concealed assets; travel bans, freezing orders, and disqualification from management may be imposed.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of intercompany deductions and treaty benefits; adjustment of taxable profit to the arm’s length amount; 80% penalty for abuse of law under Article 1729 CGI; possible application of the General Anti-Abuse Rule under ATAD; the company may also be reported to the OECD as a no-substance entity.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held civilly liable for the full tax deficit and penalties where the shell arrangement is personal or fictitious; in cases of misuse of corporate assets, directors face criminal liability for abus de biens sociaux; travel restrictions may accompany investigation and prosecution.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to the arm’s length outcome under Article 57 CGI and the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines; disallowance of excessive interest under thin-capitalization rules; late-payment interest and penalties, including 80% in cases of fraud; cross-border information exchange and mutual agreement procedure risk.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false transfer pricing documentation or knowingly use sham contracts risk criminal sanctions for forged documents and tax fraud; the tax administration may assert joint and several liability for penalties, and foreign directors may face entry bans or extradition proceedings in aggravated cross-border cases.
AML / Foreign Financial Settlement Irregularities

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties from the ACPR or TRACFIN for breaches of anti-money laundering obligations; criminal money-laundering sanctions under Articles 324-1 et seq. of the French Criminal Code carry imprisonment of up to five years and fines up to EUR 375,000; enhanced penalties for organized laundering and recidivism; Forfeiture of assets obtained from the proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors may be prosecuted as principals or accomplices in money laundering, exposing them to imprisonment, personal fines, asset confiscation, and professional disqualification; civil liability for damages caused to third parties and the State is enforceable against personal estates where corporate veil abuse is proven.
