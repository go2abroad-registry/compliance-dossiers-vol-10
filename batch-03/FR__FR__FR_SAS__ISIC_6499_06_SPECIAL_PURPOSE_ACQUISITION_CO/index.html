Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), maintained by the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce; filings are made electronically through the INPI Guichet Unique (formalites.inpi.fr).

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce), Book II, Title II – Articles L.227-1 to L.227-20 on the Société par Actions Simplifiée (SAS); common commercial company rules Articles L.210-1 to L.210-10, L.211-1 to L.211-12, L.224-1 et seq.; implementing rules R.227-1-1 et seq.

Formation / constitutional filings

Déclaration de constitution de société (M0 – Cerfa no. 14448*01) and the signed Articles of Association (Statuts), transmitted to the RCS through the Guichet Unique.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

By-law determines the capital (no statutory minimum under L.227-1). Cash contributions are subscribed in full; at constitution, the shareholders must release the amount required by the by-laws (commonly 50% of the nominal value) into a blocked deposit account at a French bank or notary. For SPAC operations, the subscription trust/escrow account must remain segregated under AMF guidelines; the bank release is instructed only upon delivery of the RCS Kbis.

Funding & unlock path: For the registered office: fire safety certification and inspection for ERP class 5, maintenance of fire extinguishers, and occupational risk assessment (DUERP) under Articles L.4121-2 et seq. of the Labour Code. No industrial civil-defense clearance is required unless the SPAC later acquires physical facilities subject to capacity or environmental permits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT rate applies to exports of goods outside the European Union provided the supplier furnishes proof of export and the goods are not re-imported into France or the EU. For offshore services, 0% VAT applies if the service is provided to a non-EU customer and the conditions of use are met outside France. SPAC listing vehicles must substantiate any export transactions with documentary evidence meeting French customs and VAT export rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration en Douane d'Exportation - DAU)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document (CMR)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or proof of payment to non-EU recipient

Mandatory real-time e-invoicing regime (e-facturation) for all B2B transactions requiring transmission via the PEPPOL network with digital stamping, API integration for fiscal clearance, and automated reporting to the French tax authority; non-compliance triggers administrative penalties and potential blocking of payment accounts.

French tax authorities conduct targeted post-export audits on zero-rating claims. Fraudulent or unsupported claims result in full retrospective clawback of zero-rated VAT, penalties up to 80% of the evaded amount, interest charges, and in cases of willful fraud, potential criminal prosecution under VAT fraud provisions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible across both current-account and capital-account transactions. France maintains a liberalised regime grounded in Articles 63-66 TFEU (free movement of capital) and the FRF 'décret 2019-153' foreign-investment screening framework, which applies only to strategic sectors (defence, dual-use, critical infrastructure). For a non-resident-owned SPAC, equity contributions, loan capital, dividend distributions, and redemption payments to public shareholders carry no convertibility restriction or prior exchange-control approval. Profit remittance and distribution of SPAC liquidation/redemption proceeds to non-resident investors are permitted, subject to standard beneficial-ownership (issued-capital, voting-rights, and 24-month holding) conditions of Directive 2011/96/EU for parent-subsidiary dividend withholding exemption. Practical hurdles include: (i) mandatory Banque de France statistical declaration for each cross-border funds transfer exceeding EUR 15,000 (or monthly net transfers) under the balance-of-payments reporting system (DÉBAR/COVADON); (ii) the bank's source-of-funds documentary verification for inbound capital from offshore investors; (iii) no segregated trust arrangement may repatriate funds to a foreign target account before AMF approval of the business-combination circular and release conditions of the escrow deed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France imposes no mandatory surrender, no compulsory conversion ratio, and no statutory time limit for realising export proceeds; exporting entities are free to retain, convert, or redeploy foreign-currency receipts. Obligations are purely statistical and transactional: resident legal entities must report to Banque de France any cross-border payment or receipt exceeding EUR 15,000 per transaction or any balanced netting position, including SPAC trust disbursement and placement inflows, under the DEBAR balance-of-payments regime. A SPAC engaged in no underlying trade exports is unaffected by export-proceeds rules; however, sponsor capital, underwriting proceeds, and acquisition continuation funds routed from non-resident investors are reportable credit-side entries, and the ordering bank performs the corrective/observational reporting on the entity's behalf without conversion requirement.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France) as the central monetary authority; foreign-exchange control and balance-of-payments statistical oversight is exercised through the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury) within the Ministry of the Economy and Finance, with prudential licensing and AML/CFT conduct supervision by the ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution). Securities-market and SPAC-governance oversight is vested in the AMF (Autorité des Marchés Financiers). EUR clearing and settlement infrastructure operates under TARGET2 ownership administered by the Banque de France.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low for SPAC escrow and trust functions; Medium for ancillary operational liquidity up to EUR 25,000. Digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Qonto, Shine, Revolut Business, and Wise Business may lawfully hold an operating account for a French SAS under a PSD2 (Directive (EU) 2015/2366) e-money licence registered with ACPR; however, French law (Articles L522-3 and L522-11-1 Code monétaire et financier) restricts the holding of third-party funds and escrow/segregated 'séquestre' services to licensed credit institutions. A SPAC's IPO proceeds, underwriting allocations, and trust-release mechanics therefore cannot be placed with an EMI; only the non-escrowed operational float may sit as e-money. Legal constraint: an EU-passported EMI (e.g., Irish or Luxembourgish license) may operate in France via freedom of services but must be ACPR-registered for local distribution; no passport allows an EMI to perform bank-guarantee or escrow functions. In practice the underwriting syndicate and AMF-required custodian will reject settlement via EMI rails.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Non-resident sponsor opacity / failure to evidence ultimate beneficial ownership: French AML law (Article L561-5 CMF) compels the bank to identify the beneficiaire effectif; a blank-check vehicle with Cayman/Delaware/Luxembourg shareholders or a discretionary trust sponsor raises an automatic high-risk flag and potential refusal under Article L561-8-1.

Defense evidence: Defense bundle: (1) certified corporate statutory documents (statuts) with full share register and all amendments; (2) Registre des bénéficiaires effectifs (RBE) filing certificate from the relevant Tribunal de Commerce; (3) individual KYC passports of each beneficial owner whose stake exceeds 25%, plus their professional track-record dossier; (4) 24-month sponsor-source-of-wealth statements validated by an external licensed auditor; (5) structured ownership chart with LEIs and jurisdictional tax-residence certificates; (6) executed underwriting and placement agency agreements; (7) copy of the AMF prospectus visa evidencing regulatory transparency.
SPAC pre-deal / 'blind pool' structure without identified target: French internal banking policies treat undisclosed-target blank-check vehicles as a money-laundering red flag ('coquille vide' risk), potentially triggering SAR under Article L561-15 and suspension of the account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital for the SPAC must route as direct equity placement proceeds or sponsor margin loans through regulated bank rails only: (1) EUR-denominated wires via SWIFT MT103 (or SEPA CT where sender and receiver are within SEPA) using the purpose code 'Capital subscription / prise de participation' and the AMF prospectus visa reference in field 72; (2) placement proceeds from the underwriting syndicate are credited to the escrow trust account (compte séquestre) at the French custodian bank, never to the sponsor's private account; (3) for USD-denominated sponsor capital, conversion into EUR at the receiving correspondent bank is executed as a spot transaction, with the Banque de France DÉBAR declaration threshold (EUR 15,000) triggering automatic reporting by the operator; (4) a 'transparency letter' from the lead underwriter or placement agent is lodged at the bank, identifying the ultimate subscription amounts and investor categories; (5) the receiving bank verifies the LEI of the SPAC and the legal capacity of the signatory before posting; (6) any equity injection exceeding EUR 1,000,000 is flagged for enhanced due-diligence review and must be accompanied by the board resolution authorising issuance of additional shares in the SAS.Outbound clearing infrastructure is layered as follows: (a) primary acquisition consideration on closing: SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer from the escrow release account to the target shareholders' accounts at their correspondent banks, executed only after (i) AMF visa/approval of the business-combination prospectus, (ii) shareholder/target approval resolutions, and (iii) trustee release certificate; (b) EU target consideration: SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) or SEPA CT batch, with 'REMIT' or 'PURP' structured remittance code (e.g., ISAE1) where required; (c) deferred or earn-out consideration: transferable stand-by letters of credit (ISP98) or commercial letters of credit (UCP600) issued by the French bank in favour of target shareholders, payable against invoice-deed certificates; (d) public shareholder redemptions: SWIFT MT103 per redemption payment alongside formal capital-reduction notarisation, or through the Euroclear/CACEIS network for dematerialised share redemption settlement; (e) all outbound transactions are subject to Bank of France balance-of-payments declaration by the ordering institution when the individual remittance exceeds EUR 15,000 or when cumulative weekly transfers to a third-country jurisdiction exceed that amount;
