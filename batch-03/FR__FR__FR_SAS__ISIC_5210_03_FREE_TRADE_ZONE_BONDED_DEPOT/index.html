Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

SAS has no legal minimum capital; capital must be fixed in the Statuts. Cash contributions must be fully subscribed at incorporation and at least 50% paid up upon signature, with the balance paid within 5 years. The paid-in amount must be deposited into a blocked bank account (compte bloqué) with a French bank or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations until the registration certificate (extrait Kbis) is issued.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification (avis de la commission de sécurité) verifying compliance with French fire and rescue standards for logistics facilities; structural load inspection certificate issued by a qualified controller; occupational workplace clearance under the Labor Code (Code du travail) covering access, ergonomic, and emergency exit; compliance with the French insurance requirements for high-value bonded goods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France applies the EU framework for free movement of capital and payments. The euro is freely convertible, and there is no general exchange-control approval requirement for a French SAS to receive foreign investment, pay overseas suppliers, or remit dividends, interest, or service fees. Remittances remain subject to corporate-law procedures, distributable-profit and solvency rules, withholding-tax requirements where applicable, treaty and EU-law conditions, transfer-pricing rules, bank AML checks, and sanctions screening. Cross-border payments may be subject to statistical reporting through financial institutions; French residents holding reportable accounts outside France may have separate tax-return disclosure obligations. Banks may request supporting documents and may delay or reject transactions that cannot be substantiated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France has no general mandatory export-proceeds surrender, conversion ratio, or statutory conversion deadline. Export receipts may be retained in euros or foreign currency, subject to ordinary accounting, tax, sanctions, AML, and applicable statistical-reporting requirements. Customs export evidence and commercial records should be retained to support VAT and trade-finance treatment.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France is France's national central bank and part of the Eurosystem. The European Central Bank sets euro-area monetary policy. The French Treasury and the Directorate-General of Customs and Indirect Taxes (Direction générale des douanes et droits indirects, DGDDI) administer relevant financial and customs rules.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for ancillary payment services and foreign-currency collections; low as the sole treasury bank for a bonded-depot operator. Use only an EU-authorised credit institution or payment/e-money institution operating lawfully in France under its passport or local authorisation. Verify the provider and permissions in the applicable ACPR and EU registers. An EMI account is not a bank deposit and may not support capital deposits, customs guarantees, credit facilities, or commercial letters of credit. It does not remove French AML, sanctions, accounting, tax, or customs-documentation obligations.

Sanctions exposure, politically exposed persons, high-risk jurisdictions, or adverse media concerning owners, customers, suppliers, or goods.

Defense evidence: Risk-based screening records; ownership and control checks; enhanced due-diligence files; documented assessment and approval; licences or competent-authority authorisations where legally required; evidence that prohibited dealings are blocked.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Receive shareholder funding into the SAS's French account from an account in the investor's own name or a clearly documented group account, using SEPA Credit Transfer for euro payments or SWIFT for other currencies. State the payment's true purpose, such as share capital subscription, share premium, shareholder loan, or intercompany financing; France does not prescribe a universal foreign-exchange purpose-code system. Provide the bank with subscription or loan documents, corporate approvals, beneficial-owner information, and source-of-funds evidence. For formation capital, follow the depositary bank's capital-deposit and release process. Record the transaction correctly in the company's accounts and complete applicable corporate, tax, statistical, and beneficial-owner formalities.Use SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer where supported for eligible euro-area payments; use SWIFT MT103 or the bank's equivalent cross-border payment instruction for other currencies and destinations. Match each payment to an approved invoice, purchase order, contract, customs declaration, transport document, and warehouse or delivery evidence as applicable. For larger or higher-risk trades, use a documentary commercial letter of credit subject to agreed UCP 600 terms, documentary collection, or bank-guaranteed payment where commercially appropriate. Confirm beneficiary details independently, screen counterparties and banks, and reconcile fees, FX rates, and payment references. Customs duties, VAT, excise, and warehouse guarantees must be handled through the applicable customs and tax procedures, not treated as ordinary supplier payments.Deliverable foreign-exchange forwards with an authorised bank or appropriately authorised investment firm, supported by documented underlying exposure and applicable MiFID, EMIR, and reporting controls.Non-deliverable forwards where commercially available and legally appropriate; confirm counterparty permissions, product classification, collateral terms, and reporting obligations.Currency swaps and FX swaps with eligible counterparties, subject to documentation, valuation, collateral, EMIR clearing or reporting requirements where applicable, and internal counterparty limits.
