Applicable AML statute: Articles L.561-2 to L.561-2-2 and R.561-1 et seq. of the French Monetary and Financial Code / Code monétaire et financier; Decree No. 2015-1750 of 24 December 2015 on UBO registers; Ordinance No. 2020-115 of 12 February 2020 transposing AMLD5; TRACFIN regulations.
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office acceptance is permitted subject to strict legal conditions. An SAS may register its registered office (siège social) at a licensed domiciliation company (société de domiciliation) located in France, provided a written domiciliation contract is executed and the domiciliation address is capable of receiving official correspondence. The Commercial Code (Article L.123-11-2; Decree 84-406) requires that the domiciliation provider be an approved agent. For the hyper-casual mobile game publishing industry, there is no industry-specific license, and a virtual office is acceptable for incorporation. However, the SAS must maintain real economic management and control in France to preserve French corporate tax residency and substance; absence of any physical presence and actual staff increases the risk of administrative challenges under Article 209 B and the anti-abuse doctrine.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

E-government filing portal
French entities obtain SIREN from INSEE and VAT ID (FR + SIREN) upon registration with DGFiP; foreign entities must appoint a fiscal representative and follow procedures for obtaining a French tax identification number under the Code Général des Impôts

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion
        
Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties up to 5 years imprisonment and fines up to €80 million or 80% of turnover

        
Director personal liability: Personal criminal liability, fines, and disqualification; veil piercing in fraud cases

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Director personal liability: Personal liability including fines and possible imprisonment for fraudulent acts
