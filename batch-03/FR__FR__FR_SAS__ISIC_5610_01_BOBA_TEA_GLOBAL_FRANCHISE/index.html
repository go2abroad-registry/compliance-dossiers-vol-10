Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The competent local Commercial and Companies Registry (Registre du commerce et des sociétés, RCS), maintained by the relevant commercial court registry (greffe du tribunal de commerce); applications are submitted through the national Guichet unique managed by INPI.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce), including Articles L. 210-1 to L. 210-9 (commercial companies), L. 227-1 to L. 227-20 (SAS), and R. 123-1 et seq. (company registration formalities). The SAS articles of association determine its governance, subject to mandatory statutory rules.

Formation / constitutional filings

Electronic company-formation declaration (formality of creation) filed through the Guichet unique, accompanied by the signed articles of association (statuts) and supporting documents; registration results in an RCS registration and a SIREN number.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum share capital applies; the articles must state the share capital, which may be €1. At least 50% of cash contributions must be paid at incorporation, with the balance payable within five years of registration. Cash contributions are deposited with an authorized bank, notary, or Caisse des dépôts et consignations; the deposit certificate is included in the filing, and funds are released after registration. In-kind contributions are generally fully contributed at formation and may require a contribution auditor, subject to statutory exemptions.

Funding & unlock path: The premises will generally be an établissement recevant du public (ERP). Before opening, comply with applicable fire-safety and accessibility rules, obtain any required mairie authorization for construction or material alterations, and complete required inspections or commission procedures where applicable. Maintain fire-safety equipment, evacuation arrangements, and ERP records. Comply with occupational health and safety obligations, including workplace risk assessment and a documented DUERP; no universal structural-load clearance is required absent relevant works, equipment, or building-specific requirements.

Cash contributions are deposited with an authorized bank, notary, or Caisse des dépôts et consignations; the deposit certificate is included in the filing, and funds are released after registration. No general industrial licensing board or manufacturing permit applies to an ordinary bubble-tea franchise outlet. The competent municipal authority (mairie) handles applicable public-access building and works formalities; the departmental Directorate for the Protection of Populations (DDPP) oversees relevant food-safety requirements.

Category: No general operating license is required for an ordinary non-alcoholic bubble-tea outlet. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France uses the euro and has no general exchange controls. Current-account payments, including imports, franchise royalties, service fees, dividends, interest, and ordinary commercial remittances, are generally freely transferable, subject to sanctions screening, anti-money-laundering controls, tax withholding and transfer-pricing requirements. Capital-account transactions are also generally liberalized under EU free-movement principles, but foreign direct investment in protected strategic sectors may require prior authorization or notification. A French SAS may hold foreign-currency accounts and may retain foreign-currency receipts. Profit distributions require distributable profits, approved accounts and shareholder approval; dividends may be subject to French withholding tax, treaty relief or EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive conditions. Interest, royalties and management fees require arm's-length support, beneficial-owner verification and applicable withholding-tax analysis. Banks may delay or refuse transfers lacking a clear economic purpose, supporting contracts, tax evidence, source-of-funds evidence or sanctions clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France has no general statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds to a French bank, convert export receipts into euros or convert a prescribed percentage of foreign-currency receipts. No general statutory repatriation deadline applies solely because proceeds arise from exports or international franchise activity. Export proceeds may be received into euro or foreign-currency accounts, subject to contractual settlement terms, accounting recognition, VAT and customs documentation, bank transaction-monitoring requirements and applicable statistical reporting. Cash movements of EUR 10,000 or more into or out of the European Union require customs declaration by the traveler or carrier. Banks and designated reporting entities may request transaction information for Banque de France balance-of-payments and statistical reporting. Sector-specific sanctions, export-control, customs and tax rules may impose additional restrictions.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France, operating within the Eurosystem under the European Central Bank; foreign-exchange policy is governed principally by European Union law and administered domestically, where relevant, by the French Treasury Directorate General (Direction générale du Trésor) of the Ministry for the Economy, Finance and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for a supplementary operational account and Low to Medium as the sole treasury bank. An EU-authorized electronic money institution or payment institution may provide EUR payment accounts, SEPA transfers, cards and certain foreign-exchange services under passporting or applicable local authorization. It does not replace a French bank for every capital-deposit, escrow, lending, cash-management, cheque, guarantee or documentary-trade requirement. The institution must identify the SAS, directors, ultimate beneficial owners, controllers, source of funds, expected payment corridors, franchise counterparties and high-risk geographies. Client-money safeguarding is not equivalent to French deposit-guarantee protection. Offshore digital EMIs without applicable EU authorization, passporting or a regulated EU partner should not be used for core receipts or capital funding.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Unclear or layered ownership involving offshore companies, nominee shareholders, trusts or an inability to identify the natural-person ultimate beneficial owner.

Root cause: Unclear or layered ownership involving offshore companies, nominee shareholders, trusts or an inability to identify the natural-person ultimate beneficial owner.

Defense evidence: Current French corporate extract or registration evidence; articles of association; complete ownership chart to each natural-person ultimate beneficial owner; shareholder registers; certificates of incorporation and good standing for foreign entities; passports and proof of address; director and authorized-signatory resolutions; beneficial-owner declaration; explanation of control rights and a signed ownership certification.
Capital funding or operating funds received from a third party, newly formed holding company or jurisdiction viewed by the bank as elevated risk.

Root cause: Capital funding or operating funds received from a third party, newly formed holding company or jurisdiction viewed by the bank as elevated risk.

Defense evidence: Executed subscription or shareholder-loan agreement; board and shareholder approvals; bank statements showing source accumulation; audited financial statements or tax returns of the funding party; sale, dividend or financing evidence where relevant; proof of beneficial ownership of the funding party; payment instruction identifying the exact economic purpose; sanctions and adverse-media screening record.
Large or unexplained inbound franchise royalties, syrup purchases, intercompany charges or payments inconsistent with the stated bubble-tea franchise business.

Root cause: Large or unexplained inbound franchise royalties, syrup purchases, intercompany charges or payments inconsistent with the stated bubble-tea franchise business.

Defense evidence: Franchise, supply, trademark and distribution agreements; invoices; purchase orders; delivery and customs records; pricing policy; transfer-pricing support; expected-volume schedule; counterparties' registration and ownership data; explanation of payment corridors; VAT treatment; reconciliation between contracts, invoices, customs records and bank statements.
Payments involving sanctioned persons, sanctioned territories, restricted goods, dual-use products or a counterparty with adverse sanctions or corruption indicators.

Root cause: Payments involving sanctioned persons, sanctioned territories, restricted goods, dual-use products or a counterparty with adverse sanctions or corruption indicators.

Defense evidence: Counterparty due-diligence file; sanctions-screening results; end-use and end-user statement; product classification; customs and export-control analysis; beneficial-owner confirmation; contractual sanctions clause; shipping documents; documented escalation and legal sign-off where a potential match is identified. Do not process a transaction while a genuine sanctions concern remains unresolved.
Non-resident directors or shareholders unable to provide acceptable proof of address, tax residence or identity documents.

Root cause: Non-resident directors or shareholders unable to provide acceptable proof of address, tax residence or identity documents.

Defense evidence: Recent utility bill, bank statement or government-issued residence document; certified translation where required; valid passport or national identity document; tax-residence certificate; notarization, apostille or legalization when requested; video-identification record; local attorney or accountant confirmation; documented explanation of residential and tax status.
Cash-intensive point-of-sale activity, unexplained cash deposits or weak controls over franchisee collections.

Root cause: Cash-intensive point-of-sale activity, unexplained cash deposits or weak controls over franchisee collections.

Defense evidence: POS-provider agreements; cash-handling policy; daily till reconciliation; store-level sales reports; deposit records; segregation-of-duties matrix; franchisee settlement schedule; refund and chargeback controls; internal audit reports; suspicious-activity escalation procedure; evidence that cash deposits correspond to recorded sales.
Tax or regulatory inconsistency, including missing beneficial-owner filing, overdue accounts, unexplained tax arrears or unsupported royalty deductions.

Root cause: Tax or regulatory inconsistency, including missing beneficial-owner filing, overdue accounts, unexplained tax arrears or unsupported royalty deductions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route paid-in share capital to the SAS capital-deposit account opened in the company's name or through the formation professional handling the deposit. Use EUR SEPA Credit Transfer where available, otherwise SWIFT cross-border transfer with complete ordering-customer, beneficiary, ultimate-beneficiary and purpose information. The payment reference should state the subscriber, SAS legal name, capital subscription or shareholder-loan purpose, and relevant agreement or resolution. The bank will normally require the draft or executed articles, subscription form, shareholder or board resolution, ownership chart, source-of-funds evidence and identification of the remitter. The depositary issues a certificate of deposit after cleared funds are received; funds are released to the operational account after registration evidence is provided. A shareholder loan must be routed and documented as debt rather than capital, with a written agreement, arm's-length terms, accounting treatment and any applicable French tax, interest-withholding and transfer-pricing analysis. Do not route capital through unrelated personal accounts or unidentified third parties. For non-EU investors or protected sectors, conduct French foreign-investment authorization and reporting analysis before funding.Use SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant where supported for EUR suppliers within the SEPA area. Match each payment to a purchase order, invoice, contract, customs entry, transport document and goods or services description. For syrup and equipment imports, retain customs classification, import declarations, origin documents, transport records and VAT evidence. For franchise royalties, retain the franchise agreement, royalty calculation, invoice, withholding-tax review, transfer-pricing support and evidence of beneficial ownership.
