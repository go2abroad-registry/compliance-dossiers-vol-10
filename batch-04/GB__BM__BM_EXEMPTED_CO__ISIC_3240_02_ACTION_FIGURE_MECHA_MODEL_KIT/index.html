Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Company is non-resident for exchange-control purposes and statutorily exempt from all Bermuda income, capital gains, dividend, and distribution taxes; no UK corporation tax, VAT, or CFC attribution applies to Bermuda-resident exempted companies. Business profits from global sales of action-figure model kits accrue free of Bermuda tax.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or US-style entity classification election exists; Bermuda law applies a per-se opaque corporate classification for all exempted companies. Available elections include: (i) voluntary tax-resident election under the Economic Substance (Bermuda) Act 2018 where core income-generating activities are performed/controlled from Bermuda; (ii) election under Section 195 of the Companies Act 1981 to hold land or use a foreign-language company name; and (iii) Section 133B election for share capital denomination in a foreign currency. Hybrid-mismatch outcomes are neutralized by UK CTA 2009/2010 and Bermuda's equivalent filing framework, but no explicit hybrid election is granted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax: None. Bermuda levies no VAT, GST, or retail sales tax; the only indirect tax is customs import duty under the Customs Tariff Act 1968, assessed on goods physically entering Bermuda and not on sales, exports, or services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST registration regime or economic turnover threshold exists. Importers must hold a Customs Broker license and register in the ASYCUDA World electronic system, but this is a customs procedural registration, not an indirect-tax threshold. Exports of snap-fit plastic mecha model kits are outside the scope of Bermuda customs duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT mechanism – Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so no 0% export rating or input VAT refund exists. Exports of high-precision steel injection-molded action-figure and mecha model kits are a priori exempt from all Bermuda taxation under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; the UK's zero-rated export VAT regime is expressly inapplicable to Bermuda because Bermuda is outside the UK VAT perimeter and the EU VAT area.

  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or courier receipt evidencing physical departure from Bermuda and destination country entry

Not applicable for sales invoicing – Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, QR/digital stamping, or SAF-T reporting. Not applicable to VAT clawbacks (no VAT regime exists). Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD); USD circulates as equivalent legal tender in Bermuda, which eliminates convertibility risk for USD-denominated export, procurement, and capital flows. The Bermuda Exempted Company (BM_EXEMPTED_CO) is classified as a 'non-resident' person for exchange-control purposes when its business is conducted wholly outside Bermuda or upon BMA designation; non-resident persons operate 'external accounts' and enjoy unfettered current-account convertibility for trade settlement, intercompany charges, and service payments, and unfettered capital-account convertibility for equity subscription, loan drawdowns, and intercompany debt repayment, subject only to the authorised depository bank's internal source-of-funds verification and, where a transaction exceeds bank-delegated thresholds, prior notification to the BMA. Profit remittance is structurally frictionless: Bermuda levies no corporation tax, no withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or management fees, and no capital gains tax; a dividend resolved by the Board may be remitted immediately from the operational account in USD without BMA permits or statutory waiting periods. Practical convertibility hurdles are limited to (i) proving economic substance under the Economic Substance Act 2018 (Bermuda) if treasury or distribution activities are classified as 'relevant activities', (ii) maintaining a Bermuda registered office and resident agent, and (iii) satisfying bank documentary requirements for the underlying trade invoice and end-user declaration.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion obligation applies: a Bermuda Exempted Company classified as non-resident is not required to convert export proceeds into BMD, may retain export receipts indefinitely in USD, GBP, EUR, CNH, or any permissible currency in its external accounts, and has no mandatory conversion ratio or repatriation timeline. Resident persons (including ordinary Bermuda-resident companies) are subject to BMA Exchange Control Notices requiring surplus foreign currency received on current account to be offered to an authorised dealer within a prescribed period; the Exempted Company structure avoids this regime. To reduce AML friction at the reviewing bank, treasury policy should nevertheless consolidate export proceeds into a USD concentration account within 90 days of receipt and evidence a clear cash-conversion cycle from customer payment to supplier settlement.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the integrated financial services regulator and de facto central bank of Bermuda, operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and administering the Exchange Control Act 1972 as the designated exchange-control authority of the British Overseas Territory of Bermuda; HM Treasury of the United Kingdom retains constitutional oversight over Bermuda's external financial relations, currency peg, and sanctions implementation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Medium feasibility as an ancillary settlement and payment-collection rail; Low feasibility as a primary treasury depository. The Exempted Company may use UK-authorised FCA e-money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie, or Revolut Business to obtain multi-currency virtual IBANs for low-value (below USD 10,000) supplier payments and e-commerce marketplace settlements, aligned with the PayPal/SWIFT target payment rails; however, under Bermuda law, unlicensed deposit-taking is prohibited, so an EMI that is not a BMA-licensed bank cannot lawfully hold the company's funds on an enduring deposit basis, and EMI balances are not protected by any deposit insurance scheme. The compliant architecture is two-tier: statutory capital and operating surplus reside with Butterfield or HSBC Bank Bermuda, while the EMI is used strictly as a pass-through collection/settlement account with a same-day sweep instruction back to the Bermuda master account. Compliant routing is a direct SWIFT MT103 wire from the investor's own bank account (no cash deposits, no third-party interposition, no crypto exchange) denominated in USD because of the BMD 1:1 peg. Field 70 (Remittance Information) must state 'EQUI / SHARE CAPITAL / BM_EXEMPTED_CO ISIC 3240'; field 72 (Bank-to-Bank Information) should reference the BMA General Permission applicable to non-resident external accounts and the authorised depository bank's client reference. The receiving Bermuda bank credits the Capital Escrow Account after completing KYC verification against the board resolution authorising the share issue and the registered-agent appointment. For injections above USD 250,000, the bank requires 48-hour prior wire notification, a completed source-of-funds questionnaire, and a share-allotment return filed with the Registrar of Companies within 30 days. Any over-credit above the subscribed amount is held in suspense pending an updated source-of-funds declaration. Secondary rail: Irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700, or MT710 for transferable credits, for large mould-tooling purchases above USD 250,000, payable at sight or deferred up to 90 days, conditioned on presentation of transport documents and an inspection certificate (not merely clean-on-board) to retain title control. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium feasibility as an ancillary settlement and payment-collection rail; Low feasibility as a primary treasury depository. The Exempted Company may use UK-authorised FCA e-money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie, or Revolut Business to obtain multi-currency virtual IBANs for low-value (below USD 10,000) supplier payments and e-commerce marketplace settlements, aligned with the PayPal/SWIFT target payment rails; however, under Bermuda law, unlicensed deposit-taking is prohibited, so an EMI that is not a BMA-licensed bank cannot lawfully hold the company's funds on an enduring deposit basis, and EMI balances are not protected by any deposit insurance scheme. The compliant architecture is two-tier: statutory capital and operating surplus reside with Butterfield or HSBC Bank Bermuda, while the EMI is used strictly as a pass-through collection/settlement account with a same-day sweep instruction back to the Bermuda master account. Legal constraints include the BMA AML/CFT expectation that the ultimate beneficiary of any payment routed through an EMI remains fully traceable on the underlying SWIFT messages; pooled-purpose EMI structures that obscure export invoices will be rejected by correspondent banks and may trigger a freezing order under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 (Bermuda).

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Bermuda Exempted Company that is not part of an in-scope multinational enterprise group; a 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) applies only to Bermuda constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda does not impose corporate income tax, withholding tax, or dividend distribution tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to its non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable for ordinary Exempted Companies outside the CIT net because Bermuda has no general transfer pricing statute. For entities within the CIT regime, Bermuda applies the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and requires contemporaneous documentation, with an indicative related-party transaction threshold of BMD 1,000,000 for preparing a local file.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply only to Exempted Companies that carry on a relevant activity in Bermuda, such as intellectual property holding, distribution and service centre, financing, headquarters, insurance, fund management, banking, shipping, or investment holding as defined. Pure contract manufacturing of plastic model kits is not a listed relevant activity; nonetheless, an Exempted Company that merely holds foreign subsidiaries or licenses IP to its own foreign operating companies may be treated as a holding or IP entity and must demonstrate administration, employees, and adequate premises in Bermuda. A shell company with no substance cannot rely on the tax exemption certificate and risks being dissolved.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Misuse of Exemption Certificate

Statutory sanction: Registration cancellation, restitution of avoided CIT, penalties up to 100% of tax understated, and criminal fines; the Tax Administration Act permits public disclosure of serious tax offenders.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly cause or permit the misrepresentation face personal assessment, fines, disqualification as directors in Bermuda, travel restrictions, and imprisonment for up to 5 years in cases of fraudulent evasion.
Empty Shell Company Without Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations impose fines up to BMD 100,000 for the initial failure and continuing fines up to BMD 10,000 per day; deliberate non-compliance may lead to strike-off, and the Registrar may require a statutory declaration of compliance before reinstatement.

Director personal liability: Directors may be fined personally for ESR non-compliance; continuation of trading by a shell after direction can result in personal liability for company debts, restoration orders, and a prohibition from acting as a director in any Bermuda entity.
Beneficial Ownership and AML Breaches

Statutory sanction: Non-filing fines up to BMD 20,000 plus daily penalties; AML offences carry custodial penalties and confiscation under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997.

Director personal liability: Directors are treated as relevant persons and can be convicted for knowingly permitting money laundering or tipping off; civil freezing orders, asset tracing, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 14 years may apply.
Fraudulent Trading and False Statements to Registrar

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies Act 1981, persons knowingly parties to fraudulent trading are liable to contribute to company assets; criminal conviction can result in imprisonment and fines.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil can be pierced: directors are jointly and severally liable for debts incurred during fraudulent trading, disgorgement of profits, global asset seizure, travel bans, and on conviction imprisonment up to 12 years.
