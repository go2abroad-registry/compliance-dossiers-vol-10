E-government filing portal

Bermuda has no direct corporate income tax registration for exempted companies. The foreign entity must (1) incorporate through a licensed Bermuda service provider and register with the Registrar of Companies; (2) apply for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act (EUTPA) certificate of tax exemption, if desired, through the Ministry of Finance; (3) obtain a Bermuda Payroll Tax identification number only if appointing local employees; (4) obtain a Customs Brokers/Import Declaration number only if importing physical goods. No VAT/TIN registration is applicable.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box elections exist. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST regime is in force in Bermuda; payroll tax is the only material indirect employment levy (employer/employee rates up to 5.25% depending on allocable remuneration bands), and customs import duty applies to tangible goods only.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — with no VAT/GST, no '0% export VAT rate' may be claimed. For customs purposes, exports of goods from Bermuda are outside the scope of import duty and thus inherently non-taxable. Digital health app services (ISIC 6201) are treated as offshore services and are entirely outside Bermuda's indirect tax net.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs Department – exports wholly excluded from duty scope)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for any tangible movement)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (only relevant for foreign tax credit or bank reporting; not for Bermuda tax)
  • Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Certificate (for treaty/assurance evidence)
  • Audited financial statements or corporate records for tax-exemption maintenance

No Hammer / real-time fiscal clearance regime exists. Bermuda does not mandate electronic invoicing nor require fiscal stamping for services or digital goods. Traditional paper and standard e-invoice formats are accepted for recordkeeping.

Bermuda imposes no direct penalties for export zero-rating fraud because there is no VAT or GST mechanism. However, misrepresentation to the Registrar or the Tax Commissioner for customs or payroll purposes triggers fines under the Companies Act 1981 (up to BMD 20,000 plus daily default penalties), the Tax Administration Act 2002, and the Customs Act 1944 (up to triple the duty evaded), with potential imprisonment up to 12 months for fraudulent declarations. Tax exemption certificates may be revoked retrospectively upon material misstatement, and any future taxes lawfully imposed would apply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar. The BMA administers the Exchange Control Regulations 1973. A Bermuda Exempted Company may be designated as a non-resident for exchange control purposes, in which case the entity is outside the Bermuda exchange control net; it may maintain foreign currency accounts, borrow offshore freely, and remit dividends, management fees, and liquidation proceeds to shareholders without BMA approval. Capital and current account transactions are accordingly fully convertible for non-resident designated entities, and no profit remittance restrictions or administrative approvals apply. This non-resident designation is standard for exempted companies and must be recorded in the company's exchange control licence or consent.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion requirement applies to export proceeds received by a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as non-resident. The company may retain receipts in BMD, USD, or any freely convertible currency. For entities without non-resident designation, the Exchange Control Regulations contemplate that residents must offer foreign currency receipts to an authorised dealer within one month; however, exempted companies overwhelmingly obtain non-resident status to avoid this obligation, and in practice the BMA will not require conversion where proceeds are retained abroad or in USD-denominated accounts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility is rated Low. Bermuda does not maintain a domestic electronic money institution licensing regime; entities providing payment or e-money services in or from Bermuda must be licensed as banks or deposit-taking institutions. Offshore EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Airwallex) generally do not onboard Bermuda-incorporated companies because of jurisdictional policy restrictions and the absence of a Bermuda EMI passporting arrangement. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital injections from shareholders in the United Kingdom or elsewhere should be settled by SWIFT MT103 wire transfers to the Bermuda bank's correspondent account in New York (for USD/BMD) or London (for GBP). The payment field 70 must include the Exempted Company's full name, the bank reference, and the purpose code 'EQUITY CAPITAL INJECTION - SHARE SUBSCRIPTION'. For UK remitters, the remitting bank's statutory purpose code for outward direct investment should be cited in the International Transactions Reporting System (ITRS) return. Supporting documentation for the receiving bank includes a board resolution of the Bermuda company approving the allotment and share issue and an updated register of members; if the capital contribution exceeds Bermuda's anti-money laundering thresholds, a source-of-funds declaration and the executed share subscription agreement must accompany the wire reference.Settlement of operational expenses, software development fees, distributor commissions, and any trade payables will be executed primarily through SWIFT MT103 messages across the USD/GBP/EUR clearing corridors. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File or Master File arises under Bermuda domestic legislation. Bermuda’s Economic Substance Regulations require multinational groups with consolidated revenue of BMD 850,000,000 or more to file a Country-by-Country Report with the Bermuda Tax Commissioner. All cross-border transactions between the Bermuda entity and the UK parent or app-store distributors must be priced at arm’s length under OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines because of the UK CFC regime, UK transfer pricing rules, and Bermuda economic substance assessment.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply. A Bermuda Exempted Company deriving income from the fitness app software, algorithms, or intellectual property licences is likely conducting an IP business or, alternatively, a distribution and service centre or headquarters relevant activity. Such a company must maintain substantial physical presence, qualified full-time employees, routine operating expenditure, and actual control of core income-generating activities within Bermuda. A passive equity-holding company is outside the elevated CIGA test but must still file an annual economic substance declaration. UK CFC rules act as a parallel economic substance gate: profits booked in Bermuda without commensurate people, premises, and functions in Bermuda will be reattributed to UK shareholders and taxed at the UK corporation tax rate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations impose a financial penalty of up to BMD 250,000 for non-compliance and up to BMD 500,000 for deliberate evasion. The UK can reattribute the Bermuda entity’s profits to the UK parent under CFC rules and may apply Diverted Profits Tax at 25% on artificially diverted profits.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false economic substance declarations risk personal fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years. In the UK, HMRC can seek civil restitution of unpaid tax and piercing the corporate veil where the Bermuda entity is a facade; travel bans may be imposed in serious fraud investigations.
Shell company / minimum substance evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations, the Tax Commissioner must levy a financial penalty of at least BMD 50,000 and up to BMD 250,000 where the entity fails the economic substance test; persistent failure can lead to compulsory strike-off and loss of exempted status.

Director personal liability: Directors as responsible officers may be prosecuted for intentionally making a false declaration; sanctions include imprisonment for up to 10 years, unlimited fines, confiscation orders, and travel restrictions pending enforcement.
AML / beneficial ownership transparency

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Beneficial Ownership Transparency Act and AML/ATF Regulations, failure to file beneficial ownership particulars attracts a financial penalty of up to BMD 25,000 per failure, continuing daily penalties, and on indictment a fine of up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are reporting persons and may be personally fined and imprisoned for knowingly making false reports or obstructing the Registrar; civil confiscation and travel bans may accompany AML enforcement actions.
Breach of exempted company conditions / unlicensed Bermuda operation

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Registrar may impose a penalty for unlawful local business and strike the company off the register. In the UK, operating a trade through the Bermuda entity without filing a UK corporation tax return can lead to tax-geared penalties, daily interest, and a separate penalty equal to the unpaid tax plus surcharges.
