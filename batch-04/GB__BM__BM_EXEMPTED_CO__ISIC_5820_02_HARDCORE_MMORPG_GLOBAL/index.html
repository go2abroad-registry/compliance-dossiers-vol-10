Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Bermuda imposes no general income tax or VAT and issues no general-purpose TIN. The statutory identity of a Bermuda Exempted Company is the Company Registration Number (CRN) issued by the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981. Procedure: (1) obtain CRN and tax exemption assurance (exempted undertaking) at incorporation; (2) appoint a Bermuda registered office and resident director; (3) rely on the automatic Exchange Control permission from the Bermuda Monetary Authority for non-resident shareholding; (4) register as an employer with the OTC for payroll tax only if Bermuda employees are engaged; (5) if the group is a CITA 2023 in-scope MNE (consolidated revenue >= EUR 750 million), register with the OTC for Corporate Income Tax and obtain a CIT registration number; (6) file annual economic substance declaration with the Registrar of Companies. Foreign entities do not obtain a VAT ID in Bermuda because no VAT exists.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Bermuda domestic law. A Bermuda Exempted Company limited by shares is a per-se corporation for US federal tax purposes and cannot be treated as a disregarded entity via Form 8832. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA), in-scope MNE groups may make OTC-prescribed elections, including the loss carry-forward transitional election, the substance-based income exclusion safe-harbour election, and the deferred re-determination election; under the Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2020, entities must elect and substantiate a 'relevant activity' status. Hybrid mismatch adjustments, if any, are driven by the ultimate parent country's rules (e.g., UK CTA 2009 Part 6A), not by Bermuda law.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains a currency board system with the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) pegged at parity to the U.S. Dollar (USD 1.00 = BMD 1.00). There are no significant restrictions on current account transactions or capital account transfers for exempted companies, including profit remittances, dividend distributions, and repatriation of capital. However, all foreign exchange transactions require compliance with BMA reporting procedures and may require specific consents in exceptional circumstances. For a Bermuda Exempted Company with no local trading operations, the BMA grants a general permission for foreign currency accounts and unrestricted conversion of BMD to USD and other major currencies. Practical hurdles are minimal, but banks may impose their own documentary due diligence before allowing outward remittances to related parties or high-risk jurisdictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory obligation for Bermuda entities to surrender export proceeds or to convert foreign currency receipts into BMD. The Exchange Control Act 1972, as amended and supplemented by BMA Notices, provides that exempted companies are deemed to have gold and foreign currency status, permitting them to retain, use, and disburse export receipts in any currency without forced conversion. In practice, exporters are required to ensure that all transactions are accurately recorded and reported to the BMA through the authorized bank if the transactions involve BMD or the local capital account. No minimum holding period, mandatory conversion ratio, or central bank surrender timeline applies to MMORPG licensing revenue, in-app purchase fees, or App Store/Google Play payouts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Exchange Control Act 1972, with delegated administration from the UK Treasury for exchange control purposes.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Bermuda does not maintain a domestic electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime analogous to the UK FCA or EU EMI passports. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Platform, Airwallex) can occasionally open USD or GBP accounts for Bermuda Exempted Companies, but such accounts are typically governed by foreign regulatory frameworks and may not support BMD, local clearing, or direct BMA reporting. Legal constraints include the inability to obtain a Bermudian banking license on a remote basis, restrictions on providing payment services to Bermuda residents without registration under the E-Commerce Act 2021, and enhanced scrutiny under BMA's Anti-Money Laundering (AML) rules. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial capitalization and any subsequent shareholder loans must be routed via the Bermudian banking system using the standard SWIFT MT103/SCT process with the beneficiary being the Bermuda Exempted Company's USD or BMD account at the chosen Tier-1 bank. The wiring instruction must state the purpose code 'NEWLTR' (new loan to estate) or, if equity, historical purpose code 'EQUIT' (equity injection). The remitter's bank in the UK or US should include the exact corporate legal name and the EIN/Bermuda registration number (ROC No.) in the beneficiary reference. The company's treasury must procure a Board Resolution authorizing the capital raise and a copy of the shareholders' register updated with the issuance of shares to the non-resident owners. For amounts exceeding USD 250,000, BMA Exemption from Exchange Control Consent must be obtained or confirmed to be automatic under the General Authority for Exempted Companies. The account receiving the funds should be clearly labeled as a 'Capital Escrow / Shareholder Contribution' ledger in the internal Treasury Management System, with all verification documents digitally archived for future audits.For outbound trade settlements related to the MMORPG operations (e.g., AWS hosting, CDN charges, licensing fees to game engine vendors, third-party development studios, and regional distribution partners), the primary clearing rail is the SWIFT MT103 via the Bermudian bank's automated correspondent network. The treasury must include the required BOP/USD purpose code (e.g., 'BOP 6214' for royalties and license fees) and the reciprocal beneficiary bank's SWIFT/BIC code. For larger infrastructure contracts exceeding USD 100,000, a commercial documentary Letter of Credit (LC) might be used, but the standard practice for digital service agreements is a plain SWIFT MT103 with attached pro-forma invoice and executed SOW. Bermudian banks do not impose exchange control license requirements on the settlement of genuine trade invoices, but the company must maintain a Trade Invoice Register linked to each SWIFT MT103 acknowledgment, with a six-year retention period to satisfy BMA record-keeping requirements. For payouts received from Apple and Google in USD, the treasury can either hold the USD directly or convert to BMD at the bank's published mid-market rate; since BMD is pegged 1:1, conversion fees should be negligible and the treasury should negotiate a zero-margin conversion schedule with the bank.USD/BMD are not available as a market instrument due to the 1:1 currency board fix, eliminating the need for any hedge between the operating currency and the Bermudian Dollar; the treasury may hold all operating and reserve funds in USD at zero conversion cost.For non-USD revenue streams, such as EUR, GBP, or JPY generated by App Store, Google Play, or Steam wallet payouts in regional currencies, permissible FX hedging instruments include outright forward foreign exchange contracts (vanilla forwards) up to 24 months tenor, offered by the Bermudian commercial bank under ISDA or a bank-specific FX master agreement.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permitted only for exotic currencies not subject to active deliverability onshore (e.g., CNY, KRW, INR) to bridge conversion restrictions; the Bermuda Exempted Company must evidence a genuine commercial exposure and maintain a BMA-approved FX hedge policy.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) and basis swaps are permissible for aligning the liability structure of the operating entity with multi-currency intercompany loans, but require pre-authorization from the BMA if the notional exceeds USD 5 million; the treasury should mark-to-market these swaps quarterly and reflect them in the statutory financial statements.Plain vanilla currency options (puts/calls) are permissible for protecting the treasury on a non-recurring high-value acquisition or intellectual property licensing payment; the bank will require a margin agreement with a minimum credit facility of USD 250,000.

Bermuda does not maintain a domestic electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime analogous to the UK FCA or EU EMI passports. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Platform, Airwallex) can occasionally open USD or GBP accounts for Bermuda Exempted Companies, but such accounts are typically governed by foreign regulatory frameworks and may not support BMD, local clearing, or direct BMA reporting. Legal constraints include the inability to obtain a Bermudian banking license on a remote basis, restrictions on providing payment services to Bermuda residents without registration under the E-Commerce Act 2021, and enhanced scrutiny under BMA's Anti-Money Laundering (AML) rules. The primary viability path is to use a fully licensed Bermuda bank as the treasury anchor while using an offshore EMI solely for niche payout collection from App Store and Google Play, subject to the EMI's own cross-border onboarding policies.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — no Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax applies to an Exempted Company. A 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies only where the Bermuda entity is part of a large multinational group meeting the OECD Pillar Two €750M consolidated revenue threshold.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory Local File / Master File threshold exists for out-of-scope Exempted Companies. The ESR 'intellectual property business' substance test applies without any de minimis monetary threshold. In-scope CIT groups must retain OECD GloBE documentation, country-by-country data, and transfer pricing documentation sufficient to substantiate internal app store payout allocations.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) are fully applicable. An MMORPG publisher holding game IP in a Bermuda Exempted Company must demonstrate substantial activity in Bermuda: full-time employees, physical premises, annual expenditure, local strategic management, and core income-generating activities performed locally. An IP business facing concrete indicators of overseas decision-making is rebuttably presumed non-compliant; the exemption for pure equity holding entities is unavailable where income arises from active licensing and micro-transaction exploitation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Economic Substance Declaration

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BMD 25,000 under the ESR regime and the Tax Administration Act 2018, daily default penalties, automatic exchange of information with HMRC under the Bermuda–UK Tax Information Exchange Agreement, revocation of the tax exemption certificate, and reassessment by foreign tax authorities applying CFC or GILTI rules with interest and penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute or authorize false ESR statements are jointly and severally liable to restore diverted profits, face personal asset restraint orders, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to 2 years on conviction for knowing falsification of statutory declarations.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR determination of non-compliance, dissemination of the entity's non-compliant status to foreign tax authorities, strike-off under the Companies Act 1981, cancellation of the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection certificate, and possible inclusion on OECD/EU listing regimes triggering punitive countermeasures in the United Kingdom.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Bermuda resident representative are personally liable for statutory penalty amounts and unpaid annual fees; overseas directors may be served civilly through the resident representative, declared ineligible for future Bermuda directorships, and held liable for the company's obligations if it traded while insolvent.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Penalties up to BMD 10,000 per non-compliance and imprisonment up to 2 years on conviction; prolonged default authorizes the Registrar to strike off the company and freezing orders may be placed on App Store income held in Bermuda bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Each director and the resident representative is individually exposed to prosecution; courts may impose disqualification orders for up to 5 years, personal fines, and asset forfeiture where the underlying gaming revenue is connected with proceeds of crime.
Annual Fee Default and Automatic Strike-off

Statutory sanction: Immediate 10% late penalty, automatic dissolution and strike-off after 90 days of continued default, vesting of the company's assets in the Crown, and loss of legal capacity to continue collecting App Store or Google Play revenue.

Director personal liability: Directors' authority terminates automatically on dissolution; directors remain personally liable for debts incurred after insolvency, for Apple and Google contractual defaults, and for restitution of any company assets distributed during the unlawful trading window.
