Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance - Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax and Corporate Income Tax administration) and Bermuda Customs Department (import/export duty and clearance); corporate registry: Registrar of Companies, Bermuda Ministry of Finance. Official portals: www.gov.bm (Government of Bermuda tax and customs pages) and the Bermuda Online Services government portal; no separate VAT authority exists because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Bermuda Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981 through a licensed corporate service provider; obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and the company registration number, which serves as the statutory corporate identifier for all tax and customs filings. Step 2: Apply to the Registrar of Companies for a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, securing the statutory zero-rate regime (protection against any future Bermuda tax on profit, income, gains or distributions, other than land tax, payroll tax, employer contributions, customs duties and taxes on persons ordinarily resident in Bermuda; protection generally runs to 31 March 2035, with extended sunset dates for qualifying newly incorporated exempted undertakings under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 amendments - verify the certificate-specific expiry date). Step 3: Register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner as an employer (Payroll Tax Account Number) when any Bermuda-resident or Bermuda-based remuneration is paid; exempted companies with no employees may still be liable to the statutory minimum payroll tax. Step 4: Register with the Bermuda Customs Department as an importer/exporter or appoint a licensed customs broker to obtain the customs account reference and clearance codes for commercial shipments. Step 5: If the Bermuda entity is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in two of the four preceding fiscal years, register for the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 (15% rate effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025). No VAT registration number exists because Bermuda has no VAT regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Bermuda Exempted Company is a limited liability corporation subject to entity-level taxation at a statutory 0% rate pursuant to the Tax Exemption Certificate; it is not a pass-through entity. The only entity-level charge for in-scope entities is the 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax for MNE groups meeting the Pillar Two revenue threshold, plus immaterial annual company fees and government fees.

Available election forms

Bermuda domestic law provides no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election: the Exempted Company is automatically a tax-exempt non-resident corporation for Bermuda purposes. For U.S. federal tax purposes, a Bermuda entity is not a per se corporation and the foreign owner may file Form 8832 under Treasury Regulation Section 301.7701-3 to elect disregarded-entity or partnership classification; absent an election, default U.S. classification is a corporation for a single owner and a partnership for multiple owners. UK hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) may recharacterise structures utilising Bermuda entities, and no Bermuda statutory counter-election exists. For CIT-scope MNE groups, elections are available under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 (including QDMTT safe harbour, consolidated-group basis, and credit-mixing elections) and must be filed with the Tax Commissioner within the prescribed return deadlines.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Bermuda has no VAT, GST or sales and use tax. The only indirect taxation relevant to this 3PL fulfilment, kitting and assembly operation is import customs duty imposed under the Customs Act 2005 and the Bermuda Customs Tariff; goods departing Bermuda are not subject to any Bermuda indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists because no VAT/GST regime is in force. Customs duty liability arises on the first commercial import (no de minimis exemption for commercial consignments); payroll tax registration triggers upon the first Bermuda-resident payroll; Corporate Income Tax registration triggers only when the Bermuda exempted company is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least two of the four prior fiscal years. The local currency is the Bermudian dollar (BMD), pegged 1:1 to the U.S. dollar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because Bermuda imposes no VAT or GST, there is no export zero-rating claim, no export VAT refund mechanism and no offshore-services zero-rating to substantiate before the Bermuda authorities. Outward dispatches from Bermuda incur no export duty. Import duty is assessed at landing; goods entered for transshipment, temporary storage or re-export may qualify for duty exemption or suspension where the Bermuda Customs conditions (identification of goods, statutory time limits and broker-lodged declarations) are satisfied. Any VAT benefit claimed in the destination jurisdiction (for example UK or EU zero-rating of exports) must be substantiated by the foreign counterparty using shipping evidence that demonstrates physical departure of the goods from the point of origin.

  • Bermuda Customs transshipment/export entry declaration for goods departing Bermuda (where applicable)
  • Signed commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of the goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer confirmation evidencing export proceeds (documentary benchmark only; Bermuda abolished exchange controls in 1989 and imposes no statutory FX repatriation requirement)
  • Certificate of Incorporation and customs account reference of the Bermuda exempted company for broker filing

No mandate - Bermuda does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance regime, government e-invoicing platform, API-based tax authentication, digital stamping or continuous transaction control system for commercial invoices. Customs clearances and export/transit entries are lodged through the Bermuda Customs electronic declaration system (typically via a licensed customs broker); commercial invoices, packing lists and transport documents are maintained in paper or PDF form for statutory record-keeping. No third-party e-invoicing network (PEPPOL, CFDI, SII or equivalent) is required by Bermuda law.

No Bermuda VAT zero-rating exists, so no Bermuda clawback can arise on that specific mechanism. Customs offences under the Customs Act 2005 - including false declarations, misdescription of goods, evasion of duty and unauthorized transshipment - attract seizures, forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties, and imprisonment for knowing falsehood. A false or unsupported export declaration in Bermuda can also undermine foreign VAT zero-rating claims made by customers, exposing the Bermuda entity and its counterparties to retrospective VAT assessments, interest and penalties in the destination jurisdiction (commonly 30% to 100% of the VAT under UK and EU rules). Bermuda entities conducting any relevant activity must additionally maintain economic substance under the Economic Substance (Requirements) Act 2018; failure to satisfy the economic substance test or information notices can result in substantial statutory fines and striking-off action. MNE groups within the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 must maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation and country-by-country reporting to avoid CIT adjustments, penalties and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the United States dollar, with unrestricted capital and current account convertibility for non-resident controlled exempted companies; profit remittance, dividend distribution, and capital repatriation are freely permitted through authorised banks, subject to standard anti-money laundering and sanctions screening. No material hurdles exist for kitting/fulfilment operations invoiced in USD, EUR, or GBP.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no general surrender or mandatory conversion ratio on export receipts for exempted companies; receipts may be retained in any designated foreign currency account or converted to BMD at the purchaser's discretion; statutory filing of Exchange Control forms (e.g., E-forms) is limited to certain issuances/dealings requiring BMA consent, not routine trade receivables.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Exchange Control Act 1972, administered for the Bermuda Government

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Wise, Revolut Business) are viable as supplemental payment channels for low-risk kitting and assembly receivables/payables, but they are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in Bermuda and cannot serve as the primary settlement bank for statutory capital or BMA oversight; their use is acceptable for operational flows if funds are ultimately swept to a licensed Bermuda or UK bank account to maintain audit trail and regulatory comfort.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general corporate income tax on Bermuda-source profits for an Exempted Company outside Pillar Two scope; 15% under the Corporate Income Tax (CIT) Act 2023 solely for constituent entities of multinational groups with consolidated global revenue of EUR 750 million or more, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or service fees, and no exchange-control or capital-repatriation restrictions apply; an Exempted Company may remit dividends, loan repayments, and liquidation proceeds freely to UK or EU shareholders via ACH/direct payment rails, subject only to the company's solvency test under the Companies Act 1981.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None (BMD 0) for ordinary Bermuda Exempted Companies; mandatory OECD-aligned Local File and Master File obligations and transfer-pricing adjustments arise only for constituent entities of multinational groups with consolidated global revenue above EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 858 million) under the CIT Act 2023. Independently of that threshold, the Economic Substance Act 2018 requires that profits attributable to a distribution and service center be commensurate with the functions performed, assets used, and risks assumed in Bermuda, supported by contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation where related-party services are supplied.

Economic substance scope: Applicable - the Economic Substance Act 2018 (as amended) and Economic Substance Regulations 2018 impose a full economic-substance test on entities carrying on relevant activities. A 3PL fulfilment/kitting/assembly provider performing services for related parties qualifies as a 'distribution and service center' and must, in each accounting period: (i) be managed and directed in Bermuda (board quorum and minutes held in Bermuda), (ii) maintain adequate full-time employees, physical premises, and local operating expenditure proportionate to the services, and (iii) perform its core income-generating activities (contract negotiation, packaging-material procurement, kitting-process management, client billing, and collection) in Bermuda. An annual ESR return must be filed within 6 months of the financial year-end. A company serving only unrelated third parties (pure 3PL warehousing) generally falls outside the relevant-activity list but must still file a nil ESR declaration and must not engage in related-party distribution without triggering the regime.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Abuse of Offshore Status (UK CFC/GAAR intersection)

Statutory sanction: Bermuda ESR penalties of BMD 10,000 to BMD 250,000 per year plus daily default penalties; UK CFC charge and Diverted Profits Tax at 25% on the apportioned profits; UK GAAR counteraction of up to 60% of the avoided tax; Bermuda payroll-tax evasion on summary conviction is punishable by fines up to BMD 50,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years on indictment.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's payroll-tax defaults; a director who knowingly signs a false ESR declaration or participates in the offshore profit-shifting arrangement is personally prosecutable (fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years); HMRC may pursue directors of the UK parent for uncovered CFC tax, seek worldwide freezing orders, and impose travel restrictions in serious cases.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: Annual ESR failure escalates from a BMD 10,000 fixed penalty to daily penalties of up to BMD 10,000 and aggregate fines up to BMD 250,000 (non-IP activities); a second consecutive year of non-compliance permits the Registrar to strike the company off; upon dissolution, all assets vest in the Crown and the company loses the right to enforce contracts in Bermuda courts.

Director personal liability: Directors of a struck-off company face disqualification from acting as directors in Bermuda for up to 5 years, personal fines under the Companies Act 1981, and personal civil-liability claims by liquidators for trading while in default; the corporate veil is pierced where the entity is found to be a sham, exposing directors to creditors' claims for the contracted 3PL obligations.
