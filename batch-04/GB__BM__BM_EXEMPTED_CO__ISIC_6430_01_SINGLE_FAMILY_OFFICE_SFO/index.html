Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Bermuda; indirect taxation consists solely of customs import duty on physical goods, supplemented by employer payroll tax and land-transfer stamp duty; family office services are entirely outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST is imposed; customs duty applies to all dutiable physical imports into Bermuda regardless of turnover; employer payroll-tax registration is mandatory from the first day of hiring with no de minimis exemption for family offices.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no export VAT zero-rating regime because no VAT/GST is chargeable on sales of goods or services; customs duty attaches only at the point of importation into Bermuda, so goods lawfully exported are automatically outside the charge and no refund claim mechanism is necessary; family office services (portfolio management, custody, treasury, reporting) supplied to non-residents are not within Bermuda's taxing jurisdiction and cannot attract indirect tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs declaration for exported or transshipped goods)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (documentary proof of physical export)
  • Packing List
  • Bermuda Export Permit (required only for controlled goods under the Customs Act 2008)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank-level documentary evidence of foreign-currency repatriation where relevant to underlying financial flows)

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control mandate exists in Bermuda; Bermuda Customs operates the Bermuda Customs Automated System (BCAS) for electronic import/export declarations, but services and offshore activities are not subject to invoice clearance or fiscal stamping obligations.

No VAT clawback exists or is permissible in Bermuda as no zero-rating refund is available; false or erroneous export/customs declarations are prosecuted under the Customs Act 2008 with forfeiture of goods and fines of up to BMD 5,000 or three times the value of the goods on summary conviction, plus potential imprisonment; economic-substance non-compliance under the Economic Substance Act 2018 attracts penalties of BMD 50,000 if a failure is not remedied within six months, an additional BMD 250,000 if the failure continues after twelve months, and an additional BMD 500,000 if it persists beyond twenty-four months, together with potential striking-off and director disqualification; false statements to the Bermuda Monetary Authority exchange-control regime can result in revocation of the family office's permission to maintain custody and payment-rail licenses.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is fully convertible at a statutory one-to-one peg with the United States dollar. Current-account payments, capital subscriptions, dividend declarations, redemption proceeds, and liquidation distributions can be converted and remitted freely by a Bermuda Exempted Company that has been designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972. Profit-repatriation hurdles arise only if the entity is treated as resident for exchange-control purposes, in which case prior BMA consent, audited financial statements, and a board solvency resolution may be required before outbound remittance. Bermuda imposes no income tax, capital gains tax, or dividend withholding tax, so no domestic tax-clearance certificate is required for cross-border fund movements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda has no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no prescribed conversion timeline, and no mandatory conversion ratio for foreign currency receipts. Non-resident designated Exempted Companies may retain export or investment proceeds in USD, BMD, or any approved foreign currency in a BMA-licensed bank account. Reporting obligations remain limited to BMA/Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) threshold reporting, sanctions screening, and the bank's internal transaction-monitoring rules; there is no exchange-control restriction on deploying retained proceeds for offshore disbursements.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting as central monetary authority, currency board administrator, and integrated financial services regulator under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium as a standalone treasury rail. Offshore digital electronic-money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed deposit-takers under Bermuda law and cannot hold BMD-denominated deposits, clear through the domestic banking system, or provide a statutory escrow relationship. Such EMIs may be used as supplementary friction-reducing wallets for low-value operating expenses or currency conversions, but they are legally unsuitable for capital escrow, securities settlement, or corporate treasury clearing. Any use of an offshore EMI must be ring-fenced and supported by at least one BMA-licensed bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital must be routed by SWIFT MT103 through a BMA-licensed commercial bank using the Exempted Company's verified Capital Escrow Account. The remitter should use the beneficiary's full legal name, the entity registration number, and the reference 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION / PAID-UP SHARE CAPITAL'. The bank's required payment purpose code, if applicable, should be selected to indicate direct investment equity contribution; if a formal purpose-code directory is not used, the SWIFT field 70 Remittance Information must state clearly 'EQUITY CONTRIBUTION / SHARE SUBSCRIPTION'. Supporting verification includes an updated share register, board resolution approving the capital call, a funds-receipt declaration from the corporate services provider, and evidence of the remitter's identity and source of funds. All USD wires should be settled on Fedwire/CHIPS via the bank's correspondent network; BMD credits remain at 1:1 with USD.Outbound disbursements for trade settlement and family-office investments should be executed over SWIFT MT103 for direct beneficiary payments and MT202 COV for interbank or custody settlement. For documentary trade transactions, a Commercial Letter of Credit confirmed by a BMA-licensed bank is permissible where the underlying transaction involves genuine trade finance. Before any outbound wire, the bank requires a board resolution, underlying agreement or invoice, proof of source of funds, and a purpose code consistent with FDI, private portfolio investment, or family-office operating expenditure. Treasury instructions should pre-agree FX conversion rates with the bank to reduce failed payments and to ensure STP (straight-through processing) through USD clearing and CHIPS.Foreign-exchange forward contracts denominated in BMD/USD and major trade currencies with a BMA-licensed bank, limited to forecast cash flows and committed investment disbursements.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in non-convertible or thinly traded currencies, settled in USD, used strictly for hedging net foreign investment exposure.Cross-currency swaps between BMD, USD, EUR, and GBP for maturity-matching of foreign-currency assets and long-term family-office liabilities, prohibited for speculative trading or leveraged repurchase activities.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda does not impose a general corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on Bermuda Exempted Companies. The Economic Substance (Corporate Income Tax) Act 2024 introduces a 15% corporate income tax effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, but it applies only to in-scope multinational enterprise groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years. A stand-alone single-family office holding private investment assets does not satisfy that group-level threshold and remains subject to the headline zero rate)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda Exempted Company to UK resident individuals, a UK corporate parent, or non-resident family trusts. Dividends distributed from the SFO's investment profits leave Bermuda with no company-level tax and no source withholding.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Information Update must be filed within 60 days after the anniversary of the company's incorporation date under Bermuda's staggered rolling-cycle filing system, which replaced the former fixed 31 January deadline. Where the SFO qualifies as a pure equity holding relevant entity, the annual Economic Substance Declaration must be filed with the Bermuda Tax Administration within six months after the end of the financial year (for a 31 December year-end, on or before 30 June of the following year).

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual CIT return must be filed within 15 months after the end of the MNE group's reporting fiscal year; any top-up tax payable must be paid by the 15th day of the 15th month after the fiscal year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 for a standalone SFO under ordinary Bermudian law: Bermuda has not enacted mandatory transfer pricing documentation rules for private companies below the CIT threshold. A mandatory Local File and Master File obligation arises only when the SFO is a constituent entity of an in-scope MNE group with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million) in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years, in which case OECD-compliant transfer pricing documentation aligned with Pillar Two filing is required. For a single-family office below the threshold, prudent best practice is to maintain contemporaneous arm's length records for any loans, management fees, guarantee fees, or transfers of family investment assets, given HMRC and Bermuda Tax Administration scrutiny of owner-managed structures.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply to relevant entities carrying on relevant activities, and the pure equity holding business is expressly a relevant activity. A single-family office qualifies as a pure equity holding entity and must satisfy a reduced economic substance test: adequate number of employees or other persons engaged in Bermuda, adequate physical assets or premises in Bermuda, and adequate annual expenditure in Bermuda. The SFO must file an annual economic substance declaration and maintain records demonstrating that the strategic stewardship of its participations, including the exercise of shareholder voting rights, approval of investment mandates, and review of custodian arrangements, is genuinely performed in Bermuda rather than by UK family members or advisers.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and sham arrangement with UK cross-border exposure

Statutory sanction: Civil: HMRC may re-attribute the SFO's profits to the UK, treat the company as UK resident from inception, and assess UK corporation tax at rates up to 25% together with interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 for deliberate and concealed behavior. In Bermuda, false declarations are offenses under the Companies Act 1981 and the Tax Administration Act 2014, carrying criminal fines, forfeiture of benefits derived from the deception, and potential strike-off of the exempted status.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute a sham structure may be jointly and severally liable for the SFO's UK tax liabilities under the facultative veil-piercing doctrine; they further face HMRC civil penalties for deliberate inaccuracies, travel restrictions and foreign travel bans where UK tax debt thresholds are reached, and in aggravated fraud cases imprisonment of up to seven years for cheating the public revenue. Bermuda courts may additionally disqualify directors for up to five years and order restitution to the Crown or company creditors.
Economic substance deficiency and false substance declaration

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 and subsequent amendments empower the Tax Administration to impose civil penalties of up to BMD 250,000 for a first failure and up to BMD 500,000 for a second failure within 24 months, plus daily default penalties of BMD 10,000 per day for continuing non-compliance. The Tax Administration may also request the Registrar to strike off the exempted company or terminate its long-term tax exemption, converting the zero-tax structure into a taxable local entity and exposing all historical profits to assessment.

Director personal liability: Directors and the principal representative are personally exposed to civil penalties for knowingly causing the company to submit false declarations; the Bermuda Supreme Court may order disqualification of directors for a term of up to five years on application by the Tax Administration, and deliberate misrepresentation in a declaration constitutes an offense punishable by fines and imprisonment where fraud or falsehood is established.
AML / anti-money-laundering and beneficial ownership transparency violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 (Bermuda) and the Financial Sanctions Act 2021, the SFO faces confiscation orders covering the full value of tainted assets, civil fines for beneficial ownership breaches of up to BMD 100,000 per violation, and forfeiture of bank accounts. Engaging in regulated financial business without a license exposes the company to substantial regulatory penalties and a direction to cease operations, while the Registrar may strike off a non-compliant company.

Director personal liability: Criminal liability attaches personally to directors and officers who knew or suspected money laundering: imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering and up to 20 years for terrorist financing; the corporate veil is disregarded so that the personal assets of the principals become available for confiscation; the Attorney General may seek asset-freezing injunctions and travel bans pending investigation, and non-compliant officers may be disqualified from serving as directors of any Bermuda company.
