Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (payroll tax and miscellaneous taxes); Bermuda Customs Department (customs duties); Registrar of Companies (entity registration and Economic Substance Act filings). Official government portal: https://www.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company does not obtain a TIN or VAT ID because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax and no VAT. Procedure: (1) incorporate through a licensed registered agent with the Registrar of Companies and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and Companies Register (CRO) number; (2) if employing staff, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner (Payroll Tax) before payroll commences; (3) if importing goods, register as an importer/declarant with the Bermuda Customs Department; (4) file the annual Economic Substance Declaration and annual return with the Registrar of Companies. The CRO number and employer payroll tax number serve as the entity's Bermuda tax identity references.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company does not obtain a TIN or VAT ID because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax and no VAT. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists. Under the Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2018, an exempted company may self-certify the jurisdiction in which it is tax-resident; a claim of tax residence in a qualifying onshore jurisdiction can discharge Bermuda economic substance requirements. Because Bermuda imposes no income tax, hybrid mismatch outcomes are not recognised domestically.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; Bermuda levies customs duties on imported goods and a payroll tax on employers

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration regime exists. Any entity importing goods into Bermuda must hold a customs declarant account with the Bermuda Customs Department; there is no monetary threshold for that account and no threshold-based indirect tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, so there is no export zero-rating scheme and no input-credit refund mechanism. Offshore software development and maintenance services (ISIC 6202) supplied by a Bermuda exempted company are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation; exports of physical goods leave the customs territory without duty and without any VAT zero-rating certificate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (required only for tangible goods leaving Bermudian customs control)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (pipeline standard substantiation, not a Bermuda statutory form)

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists. Bermuda Customs processes import entries electronically through its automated declaration system, but no government API tax clearance or fiscal stamp is required; invoices for exported services require no electronic clearance.

No VAT-driven clawback exists because no VAT is chargeable. Penalties arise primarily under the Customs Act (fines, forfeiture, and prosecution for false declarations or duty evasion) and the Economic Substance Act (financial penalties and possible strike-off for failure to file or for false substance declarations). The 180-day repatriation figure is the compliance pipeline's default substantiation deadline; Bermuda law imposes no statutory FX repatriation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to the US Dollar. For a Bermuda Exempted Company operating as an Offshore Development Center, current account convertibility for software service fees and capital account convertibility for equity injections, dividends, and profit remittances are fully liberalized. There are no ordinary exchange control approvals required for non-resident-owned exempted companies transacting in foreign currency, provided the BMA's reporting, retention, and recording obligations are satisfied. Profit remittance is unrestricted through an authorized financial institution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda does not impose a statutory surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. A Bermuda Exempted Company may retain 100% of export receipts in the original currency (USD, EUR, GBP, or other foreign currency). Only statistical or transaction-level reporting to the BMA through the relevant authorized bank is required; no compulsory conversion to BMD applies.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: High for treasury execution, multi-currency virtual account management, and global disbursement. Legal constraints: offshore digital EMIs such as Wise and Payoneer can provide B2B settlement and FX conversion for a Bermuda Exempted Company, but they are not licensed as Bermuda deposit-taking institutions under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. They cannot provide domestic Bermuda clearing or BMA-regulated fiduciary services. A Bermuda-licensed commercial bank remains mandatory for statutory capital custody, public-facing invoicing, and high-value trade settlement. Remote EMI onboarding is possible, but strict corporate KYC and source-of-funds evidence are required.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for ordinary Exempted Companies; a 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT Act 2023), aligned with OECD Pillar Two, applies only to constituent entities of in-scope MNE groups whose consolidated annual revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750 million.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends and no tax on distributions of a Bermuda Exempted Company; dividends paid to the UK parent are fully exempt from Bermuda income tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

CIT return due within 15 months after the end of the relevant accounting period; quarters 1-3 instalments are due within 3 months of each quarter-end and the final instalment with the return.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing is due within 6 months after the end of the company's relevant fiscal year (e.g., a 31 December year-end requires filing by 30 June).

Deadline: · Frequency:

Monthly payroll tax return and payment due by the 15th day of the following month; quarterly filers follow the prescribed quarter-end statutory cycle.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 - Entity and Group Identification Constituent entity legal name, Bermuda company number, ultimate parent entity jurisdiction, GloBE status, and accounting period. UK-parented Bermuda ODC must disclose GB as the ultimate parent jurisdiction, the group consolidation structure, and the OECD GloBE notification reference. Late filing of a CIT return attracts daily default surcharges up to BMD 10,000 per day, interest at statutory rates on unpaid CIT, and penalties of up to 5% of the assessed top-up tax for negligent understatement; directors are separately examinable.
Box 2 - GloBE Income/Loss Reconciliation Accounting profit reconciled to GloBE income or loss, adjusted for fair-value changes and intra-group transactions. Include Bermuda software engineering service revenue and exclude profits not economically attributable to Bermuda under the arm's-length standard. Late filing of a CIT return attracts daily default surcharges up to BMD 10,000 per day, interest at statutory rates on unpaid CIT, and penalties of up to 5% of the assessed top-up tax for negligent understatement; directors are separately examinable.
Box 3 - Adjusted Covered Taxes Covered taxes, equivalent taxes, and deferred tax adjustments used in the effective tax rate calculation. Ordinary Bermuda profits carry no covered taxes; payroll tax is treated as a non-covered business expense. Foreign withholding taxes on payments from the Bermuda entity must be separately reported. Late filing of a CIT return attracts daily default surcharges up to BMD 10,000 per day, interest at statutory rates on unpaid CIT, and penalties of up to 5% of the assessed top-up tax for negligent understatement; directors are separately examinable.
Box 4 - Top-Up Tax and Safe Harbour Jurisdictional effective tax rate, top-up tax computation, substance-based income exclusion, and safe harbour election. For a Bermuda ODC earning 0% taxed profit, the jurisdictional top-up tax defaults to 15% of excess profit unless a qualified GloBE safe harbour applies; document any CIGA-based exclusion scrupulously. Late filing of a CIT return attracts daily default surcharges up to BMD 10,000 per day, interest at statutory rates on unpaid CIT, and penalties of up to 5% of the assessed top-up tax for negligent understatement; directors are separately examinable.
Box A - Entity and Activity Classification Legal entity details, tax residence status, class of relevant activity, and whether a pure equity-holding exemption is claimed. The offshore software development centre must classify income under the service-centre or distribution-and-service-centre relevant activity if services are supplied to UK or third-country group companies; the pure equity-holding exemption is unavailable once operating income exists. First ESR offence: BMD 50,000 penalty; subsequent offences: BMD 100,000 per offence plus daily default penalty of BMD 10,000-50,000; officers knowingly in default are liable to imprisonment up to 5 years, and the Registrar may strike off the company.
Box B - Income and Expenditure Revenue, expenses, and net income attributable to the relevant activity in Bermuda. Declare all fee income from UK affiliates and group companies, supported by executed contracts and intercompany invoices; no deduction is permitted for the mere passage of funds. First ESR offence: BMD 50,000 penalty; subsequent offences: BMD 100,000 per offence plus daily default penalty of BMD 10,000-50,000; officers knowingly in default are liable to imprisonment up to 5 years, and the Registrar may strike off the company.
Box C - Substance Indicators Number of full-time employees, physical premises, annual expenditure, and location of core income-generating activities (CIGA) in Bermuda. Demonstrate a physical Bermuda office, locally resident engineers, local board and management meetings, and actual software engineering decision-taking in Bermuda; remote-only UK control is a fatal failure. First ESR offence: BMD 50,000 penalty; subsequent offences: BMD 100,000 per offence plus daily default penalty of BMD 10,000-50,000; officers knowingly in default are liable to imprisonment up to 5 years, and the Registrar may strike off the company.
Box D - Outsourcing and CIGA Declaration Declaration of any outsourcing arrangements and the place where CIGA are conducted. Outsourcing software development to UK personnel does not satisfy ESR substance; the entity must supervise and perform CIGA itself in Bermuda and keep contemporaneous board minutes. First ESR offence: BMD 50,000 penalty; subsequent offences: BMD 100,000 per offence plus daily default penalty of BMD 10,000-50,000; officers knowingly in default are liable to imprisonment up to 5 years, and the Registrar may strike off the company.
Payroll Tax Schedule Gross remuneration by employee, statutory exemption thresholds, employer and employee payroll tax computed at graduated rates. Report remuneration paid to Bermuda-resident software engineers and any expatriate staff working in Bermuda; salaries paid to personnel outside Bermuda to offshore contractors are generally outside the Bermuda payroll tax base. Payroll tax arrears attract monthly interest on unpaid amounts, penalty assessments for failure to file, and criminal prosecution of directors for fraudulent non-payment under the Payroll Tax Act 1995.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750 million (group-wide consolidated annual revenue exceeding EUR 750 million triggers the Bermuda CIT Act 2023 transfer-pricing framework and OECD Pillar Two documentation duties; Exempted Companies of groups below this threshold maintain no statutory Bermuda Local File or Master File, although UK transfer-pricing documentation obligations under CTA 2010 Part 4 may still apply to the UK parent).

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) require every relevant entity to demonstrate adequate substance in Bermuda proportionate to its relevant activity. Software engineering and related services rendered to group affiliates are treated as a distribution-and-service-centre relevant activity when the entity earns income from such services. The entity must have a physical office in Bermuda, qualified full-time employees, local expenditure, and board meetings at which strategic decisions are made in Bermuda; CIGA must be performed by the entity itself, not outsourced abroad. A pure equity-holding entity is exempt from full substance requirements only if its income consists solely of dividends and capital gains from passive holdings, it conducts registered agent and administrative functions domestically, and it files a reduced ESR declaration. Non-compliance attracts penalties up to BMD 100,000 per offence, daily default penalties, corporate strike-off, and officer imprisonment of up to 5 years. The UK CFC regime simultaneously prevents the attribution-avoidance of UK profits unless the Bermuda ODC carries on genuine economic activity in Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / UK CFC, Diverted Profits and Transfer-Pricing Avoidance

Statutory sanction: HMRC may assess diverted profits tax at 20%-25% of the diverted profits, issue a CFC charge under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A, make transfer-pricing adjustments under CTA 2010 Part 4, and levy interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax understated; the Bermudan CIT Act 2023 additionally imposes the 15% GloBE top-up tax on in-scope groups.

Director personal liability: Directors of the UK parent who sign incorrect CFC or transfer-pricing returns commit the UK offence of cheating the public revenue, carrying imprisonment up to 7 years; senior accounting officer penalties of GBP 5,000 per tax period apply for certification failures; Bermudan directors face ESR disqualification proceedings and court-imposed travel restrictions while HMRC or the Minister investigates the underlying sham.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Economic Substance Regulations 2018 triggers BMD 50,000 penalty for the first year of failure and BMD 100,000 for each subsequent year, cumulative daily default penalties of up to BMD 50,000, and the Registrar's discretionary strike-off of the company; false ESR declarations are also prosecuted as an offence.

Director personal liability: Each officer knowingly concerned in the non-compliant entity is personally liable to imprisonment for up to 5 years and to separate daily fines; courts may pierce the corporate veil to order civil restitution of profits earned from the shell, issue travel bans pending criminal trial, and disqualify directors from holding any Bermuda registered entity office.
AML / Proceeds of Crime and Fraudulent Conduit

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Monetary Authority may impose civil penalties up to BMD 250,000 and revoke the entity's permitting status; conviction for money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 carries imprisonment up to 14 years, confiscation orders over criminal benefit, and freezing of corporate bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors qualify as relevant persons and are individually liable for AML failures; the Supreme Court of Bermuda may freeze their personal assets, impose travel restrictions and passport surrender, order civil restitution of laundered sums, and sentence knowingly implicated directors to imprisonment, with extradition available to the UK under the Bermuda-UK extradition arrangements.
Late Filing, Annual Return and CIT Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Companies Act 1981 late filing penalties accrue monthly (BMD 1,000 per month) with automatic strike-off after unpaid fees exceed 6 months; the CIT Act 2023 imposes daily default surcharges up to BMD 10,000 per day plus 5% interest on the unpaid top-up tax; the ESR regimes add first-offence penalties of BMD 50,000 and higher repeated-year fines.

Director personal liability: Directors may be joined as personally liable respondents in strike-off and penalty proceedings; the court can order each director to compensate creditors for trading losses sustained during non-compliance, impose disqualification orders, apply travel restrictions preventing departure from Bermuda, and upon fraud or bankruptcy circumstances expose directors to imprisonment for up to 5 years and personal civil restitution of diverted assets.
