Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, for payroll tax and tax certificate matters; Bermuda Registrar of Companies, Companies Registry, for incorporation and statutory filings. Primary portal: Bermuda Companies Registry eServices and Bermuda OneGov.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda exempted company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 by filing a Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association with the Registrar of Companies, designating a resident registered office and principal. No corporate income tax TIN is issued because Bermuda imposes no income tax on companies. The entity receives a Bermuda company registration number at incorporation. If it will employ persons in Bermuda, it must separately enrol with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for employer payroll tax and obtain a payroll tax file number. If the company will import goods, it must also register a customs declaration profile with Bermuda Customs for ASYCUDA World.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under Bermuda law. The material statutory election is an application for a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which guarantees that the exempted company will remain exempt from any future Bermudian direct taxes until at least March 2035. Additional optional designations include regulatory statuses such as a non-resident insurer or special purpose vehicle under sector-specific legislation, but none alters the default tax-exempt classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST or sales and use tax in Bermuda. The indirect tax analogues are customs duties on imported goods and payroll tax on Bermudian employers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no such tax exists. Employers have payroll tax registration obligations once they have any remuneration paid to a Bermudian employee, and importers must declare goods through Bermuda Customs regardless of turnover. There is no economic threshold for indirect tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda does not levy VAT or GST and has no export zero-rating procedure. Services supplied by a Bermuda exempted company, including remote desktop support, screen sharing, and IoT telemetry software, are outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxation. The relevant export-related tax relief is the direct tax exemption conferred by exempted company status and the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 certificate.

  • Customs Export Declaration via ASYCUDA World electronic Bill of Entry
  • Commercial Invoice or Export Invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing transportation
  • Original or certified certificate of origin where required by the consignee jurisdiction
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence where export proceeds are repatriated through a Bermuda bank

No national VAT e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists. Import and export entries are filed electronically through Bermuda Customs using ASYCUDA World. Businesses must retain underlying invoices and shipping documents for audit inspection.

There is no VAT export fraud or clawback regime because Bermuda has no VAT. Audit scrutiny instead focuses on the Economic Substance Act 2018, payroll tax compliance, customs declaration accuracy, and continued eligibility for exempted company status and the 2035 tax protection certificate. Submitting false information in support of exempted status can result in loss of the tax exemption certificate, monetary penalties, and regulatory sanctions. Under the Tax Administration Act 2014, inaccurate returns and records attract civil penalties, and customs offences may lead to forfeiture and additional fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda's financial system operates under the Bermuda Monetary Authority as the de facto central bank and exchange control administrator. The Bermudian dollar is pegged 1:1 to the United States dollar; however, US dollars are legal tender in normal commerce. Both capital and current account transactions are fully convertible, with no foreign exchange controls, restrictions on dividend distribution or bottlenecks to profit remittance. For a Bermuda Exempted Company engaged in remote screen-share/IoT software, repatriation of profits to shareholders is unhindered, provided the company maintains the required economic substance in Bermuda and observes anti-money laundering reporting under the Proceeds of Crime Regulations 2008 (as amended). Banks apply standard enhanced due diligence for non-resident owned exempted companies, and the absence of an income tax treaty network means that remittance documentation must support the underlying commercial substance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no mandatory export proceeds surrender timeline or conversion ratio. Export receipts from software licences, IoT telemetry subscriptions, and remote support fees may be retained in Bermudian dollars, US dollars, or any freely convertible foreign currency at the discretion of the entity. The BMA maintains exchange control forms for statistical and anti-money-laundering purposes, but no surrender or conversion mandates apply.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low to Medium. Bermuda is not a principal European or Commonwealth EMI (electronic money institution) licensing centre; the BMA's Digital Asset Business Act covers digital asset activity but not eu-licensed EMI status. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise or Revolut Business can be integrated as ancillary payment rails for collecting small-volume SaaS fees, but they are not regulated by the BMA and often restrict onboarding of Bermuda-registered corporate entities lacking local operational substance. Consequently, the primary treasury account should be held with a licensed Bermuda commercial bank, with EMIs used only for multi-currency collection and zero/low-balance clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections are effected via SWIFT MT103 with a purpose description indicating equity subscription or capital contribution. The receiving Bermuda bank will require the share allotment certificate and an authorising board resolution; transactions exceeding the BMA's exchange control reporting threshold must be declared on the applicable Bermuda Exchange Control form. The bank will also perform sanctions screening and verify the beneficial owner register under Bermuda's AML legislation. No prior BMA approval is needed for an exempted company's capital call, but documentary retention for five years is required.Outbound settlements for remote screen share and IoT licence fees are cleared primarily by SWIFT MT103 messages using standard correspondent banking routes. Payment facilitators (Stripe, Paddle) deposit aggregated net revenues into the Bermuda bank account via ACH/SEPA equivalent rails after deducting processing fees. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Bermuda Exempted Companies outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CIT Act); 15% for Constituent Entities of in-scope MNE Groups whose consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750 million in at least one of the two preceding fiscal years. The CIT Act takes effect for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda Exempted Company to shareholders of any residence, and no withholding tax on distributions in liquidation or on deemed distributions. The UK does not levy withholding tax on dividend payments from a UK payer to a Bermuda recipient under the UK dividend exemption, but the UK anti-abuse diverted profits tax and CFC rules must be considered where a Bermuda entity is interposed in a digital subscription chain.

Within six (6) months after the end of the financial year; a Bermudian entity with a 31 December year-end must file by 30 June of the following year (Economic Substance (Submission of Declaration) Rules 2018).

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 60 days of each anniversary of the date of incorporation. An audit is not mandatory for most exempted companies unless shareholder resolutions or the CIT Act in-scope status require one.

Deadline: · Frequency:

15 months after the end of the fiscal year (18 months for the first GloBE transitional filing period).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory local-file or master-file documentation obligation for out-of-scope Bermuda Exempted Companies because no corporate income tax applies to them. For in-scope MNE groups under the CIT Act 2023, arm's length transfer pricing is mandatory and aligned with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, with the compliance trigger being group consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million, at parity) in a preceding fiscal year rather than a transaction-level threshold. A prudent risk-management documentation threshold for intercompany service fees in the remote screen-share and IoT telemetry business is BMD 1,000,000 of relevant turnover; below this amount quarterly transfer pricing memos are recommended, and above this amount a full OECD-compliant benchmarking study should be maintained.
