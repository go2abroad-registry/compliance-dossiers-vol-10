Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Registrar of Companies within the Department of Registry (portal: https://www.bermudabusinessregistry.com/) and the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax matters (portal: https://www.gov.bm/taxation).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Bermuda Exempted Company must file a Memorandum of Association and Declaration as to the Beneficial Owner with the Registrar of Companies. Upon incorporation, the company is issued a company registration number. No VAT or income tax TIN is issued because Bermuda has no such taxes. If the company has Bermuda-resident employees, an employer payroll tax account must be opened with the Office of the Tax Commissioner. Additionally, an application for a tax exemption certificate must be submitted at incorporation to invoke the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, thereby securing an enforceable undertaking that no taxes on income, profits, capital gains, or distributions will be imposed until 31 March 2035.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged to the United States dollar at a fixed 1:1 rate. Bermuda maintains an exchange-control regime, but an exempted company with non-resident exchange-control status is treated as outside the Bermuda exchange-control area. Current-account transactions relating to the SaaS subscription export business, including EMR licence fees, maintenance fees, and cloud service charges, are freely convertible and remittable in USD, GBP, or EUR. Capital-account transactions, such as inward share capital injections, shareholder loans, and profit distributions, may require specific BMA permission only if the company holds resident status or deals with resident Bermuda counterparties; in the standard exempted-company structure with non-resident designation, profit remittance and capital account conversion procedures are frictionless.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda's Exchange Control Act 1972 and associated regulations require resident persons to offer foreign currency proceeds to an authorized dealer immediately upon receipt or within the applicable statutory period. For the Bermuda Exempted Company with non-resident exchange-control status, no statutory surrender timeline applies and the mandatory conversion ratio is zero. Export receipts from medical-clinic EMR SaaS subscriptions may be retained in USD or any approved foreign currency without conversion to Bermudian dollars.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the central monetary authority, currency issuer, financial services regulator, and foreign exchange control administrator under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Medium-High as a collection rail; Low as the primary capital escrow or treasury settlement rail. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed deposit-taking institutions under Bermuda's Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and cannot alone satisfy BMA-supervised treasury requirements. A UK FCA-authorised EMI may be used to collect medical-clinic EMR SaaS subscription payments in GBP or EUR, provided that all settlement balances are swept daily into a BMA-licensed Bermudian bank account. Unlicensed offshore EMI accounts must not hold long-term working capital or shareholder capital in excess of transactional clearing balances.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A — Bermuda has no statutory transfer-pricing documentation regime or Local File/Master File thresholds applicable to entities outside the CIT/GloBE scope. For a company earning revenue from related UK/EU entities, the UK entity's own transfer-pricing documentation (UK CTA 2010 Part 4) applies and requires arm's length pricing for cross-border controlled transactions. If the group ever exceeds €750M consolidated revenue, Bermuda's GloBE rules impose OECD-aligned documentation and country-by-country reporting obligations.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2018, as amended 2020 and 2024, apply mandatorily to exempted companies carrying on a 'relevant activity'. For this EMR SaaS entity, the relevant activity is either an IP business (licensing cloud software to medical clinics) or a distribution and service centre (rendering services to group entities). The company must demonstrate: (i) board management and control exercised in Bermuda; (ii) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda; (iii) adequate employees, physical premises, and annual expenditure in Bermuda; and (iv) compliance with the filing requirements of the Companies Act 1981. Outsourcing of CIGAs is permitted only to a Bermuda service provider. A 'pure equity holding entity' classification is available only where the company does not trade and earns solely dividends, equity gains, and incidental interest; that classification should not be used for an active SaaS billing entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / UK Taxation of Management and Control

Statutory sanction: UK corporation tax at 25% on unreported profits; diverted profits tax at 25% on profits artificially diverted from the UK; penalties up to 100% of tax due; interest at HMRC rates; potential partner/joint liability under UK Finance Act 2016 for facilitating tax evasion.

Director personal liability: UK Finance Act 2016 and the Criminal Finances Act 2017 impose criminal and civil liability on directors/officers who facilitate tax evasion; HMRC can issue personal liability notices for penalties, freezing orders over UK assets, travel bans pending investigation, and imprisonment up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion of tax.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Ministerial penalty up to BMD 250,000 (BMD 500,000 for IP businesses) plus daily default fines; automatic strike-off and dissolution of the company by the Registrar; on strike-off, all assets of the company escheat to the Crown and directors lose the protection of limited liability.

Director personal liability: Every director, manager, or officer with knowledge of the substance failure is personally liable for the penalties unless a 'reasonable excuse' is established; directors of a struck-off company are prohibited from acting as directors or managing companies in Bermuda for up to 6 years and face fines of BMD 1,000 per day of continued default.
Anti-Money Laundering / Proceeds of Crime

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 15 years for money laundering offences under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997; the Bermuda Monetary Authority can impose regulatory penalties, suspend the company's ability to maintain banking relationships, and order the forfeiture of proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly or negligently permit the company to be used for money laundering face criminal prosecution as principal offenders; civil restitution orders can be made requiring recovery of tainted funds; travel bans can be issued by the Bermuda court pending investigation, and imprisonment for up to 15 years applies on conviction.
False Declarations / Fraudulent Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Fine up to BMD 10,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years for each false declaration under the ESR legislation; civil penalties equal to the amount of tax avoided in foreign jurisdictions; disqualification of the company's right to hold an Exemption Certificate.

Director personal liability: Bermuda law pierces the corporate veil for fraudulent acts: directors are personally liable for fines, penalties, and restitution; the courts may impose travel bans, freeze personal bank accounts, and sentence directors to imprisonment; foreign jurisdictions (including the UK) may separately prosecute for perjury or false accounting with extradition treaties available.
