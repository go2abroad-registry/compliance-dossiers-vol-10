Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (Bermuda, Ministry of Finance) for payroll tax and Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 administration, with the Corporate Income Tax Agency operating the tax portal at taxportal.gov.bm; Registrar of Companies (roc.gov.bm) for exempted company incorporation and annual Economic Substance filings.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity forms a Bermuda Exempted Company through a licensed local registered agent by filing the Memorandum of Association and Form 1 with the Registrar of Companies; upon issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation, the company obtains its statutory company number which serves as the sole tax identifier (no VAT ID exists). Non-resident owners may hold 100% equity without local directors; the company must file an annual economic substance return and a company return. If the entity is a constituent entity of a multinational group with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million, it must separately register for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax under the CIT Act 2023 and file CIT returns; otherwise no income tax registration is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box election; an Exempted Company is a per se corporation for U.S. federal tax classification and is not eligible for disregarded-entity treatment. Statutory elections available include: (i) elections under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for filing basis and group treatment, transitional election for the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax charge, and permanent establishment elections for Pillar Two purposes; (ii) election to be treated as a dormant company for annual return filing; (iii) election to pay the annual exempted company government fee on a standard or reduced basis under the Companies Act 1981.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general consumption tax in Bermuda; the only indirect levy is Bermuda Customs Duty imposed on imported goods under the Customs Act 1969. There is no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. A Bermuda-incorporated seller of custom mechanical keycaps making online supplies to United Kingdom consumers is separately subject to United Kingdom VAT as a non-established taxable person once the UK distance-selling turnover threshold is exceeded.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT or GST registration threshold exists within Bermuda. For separate United Kingdom VAT exposure, the overseas vendor registration threshold is GBP 90,000 on a 12-month rolling basis, measured on UK B2C remote sales.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda levies no VAT, and therefore there is no export zero-rating or refund mechanism. Bermuda customs duty is levied only at importation; goods physically exported from Bermuda, including goods in transit and re-exported items, remain outside the scope of Bermuda customs duty and no VAT refund can attach. Exporters of keyboard keycaps manufactured outside Bermuda (e.g., double-shot PBT caps produced in PRC or Taiwan) should ensure the goods do not enter Bermuda's customs territory at any point; where goods transit Bermuda, the transit/transshipment regime under the Customs Act 1969 should be used. No statutory FX repatriation deadline applies in Bermuda; the 180-day parameter in this dossier mirrors the conventional export proceeds realisation covenant used by banks, EMI providers and trade finance insurers and is not a Bermuda legal requirement.

  • Commercial Invoice (seller, buyer, Incoterms, goods description, unit values) and itemised packing list
  • Bill of Lading, House Airwaybill, or courier shipment manifest evidencing physical export movement
  • ASYCUDA export declaration and Customs transit entry where goods pass through Bermuda under transshipment or re-export treatment
  • Certificate of Origin (only where preferential tariff treatment is claimed in the destination market)
  • Supporting bank/EMI payment records for FX repatriation tracking under commercial covenants

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandated e-invoicing, API clearance, or digital stamping regime applies in Bermuda. The Customs Department operates ASYCUDA World for electronic processing of import declarations and duty assessment. Under the CIT Act 2023, in-scope taxpayers must file and pay electronically through the Bermudian tax authority's approved portal.

Because no VAT zero-rating regime exists, no VAT clawback is possible. False or fraudulent customs declarations, including misdescription of goods or undervaluation, attract penalties under the Customs Act 1969 of up to three times the duty evaded or BMD 2,500 per offence, together with forfeiture of goods and potential imprisonment for up to two years. If the entity becomes subject to Bermuda CIT as part of a large multinational group, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 imposes late-filing interest at the statutory rate, penalties up to 25% of the tax understated, and criminal sanctions for wilful evasion; the Tax Commissioner may re-open assessments within six years of the filing date, and clawback of incorrectly claimed exemptions or treaty benefits is prospective plus interest.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar. A Bermuda Exempted Company designated as 'non-resident' for exchange-control purposes enjoys full current-account convertibility and unrestricted capital-account repatriation of dividends, capital, interest, and trade proceeds, subject only to standard declarations filed through an Authorized Dealer. No withholding taxes apply on dividends, interest, or royalties to the UK parent, and no capital-gains tax exists in Bermuda. Profit-remittance hurdles are predominantly documentary: the bank requires a current Certificate of Compliance, a filed beneficial-ownership return, a board-approved dividend or loan-repayment resolution, and, where the company is within scope of the Economic Substance Act 2018, evidence of economic-substance compliance or confirmation of tax residence outside Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender ratio and no statutory conversion timeline for export or trade proceeds received by a non-resident exempted company. The BMA retains discretionary powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972, but in practice foreign-currency receipts (USD, GBP, EUR) may be retained, swept, converted, or disbursed freely via an Authorized Dealer. The account must be flagged 'External Account' in the bank's exchange-control records, and each credit above USD 50,000 must carry the underlying commercial invoice reference for the bank's periodic BMA reporting.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Exchange Control Act 1972, and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, supported by Bermuda commercial banks licensed as Authorized Dealers under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-Medium. Bermuda has no standalone e-money institution licensing regime equivalent to the UK FCA or EU EMI framework; the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 covers digital-asset firms but not classical EMI treasury accounts. Leading offshore EMIs (Wise Platform, Airwallex, Currencycloud) generally restrict onboarding to UK/EU-domiciled entities, so a full-service EMI account for a Bermuda Exempted Company is rarely obtainable. The recommended architecture is hybrid: retain the Bermuda Exempted Company as the principal treasury and settlement entity with a licensed Bermuda bank, and use a UK-incorporated group payouts company to hold the Shopify Payments and Stripe merchant accounts (Stripe does not currently list Bermuda as a supported merchant country), clearing downstream via MT103 sweeps to the Bermudian non-resident account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda domestic transfer pricing documentation threshold. OECD Pillar Two compliance (GloBE Information Return and Bermuda QDMTT) applies only when the group's consolidated revenue reaches EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million, given the 1:1 BMD/USD peg) in at least two of the four preceding financial years. A small keycap manufacturer is below this threshold and has no mandatory Local File or Master File obligation.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance (Obligations of Exempted Entities) Regulations 2018 apply to exempted entities carrying on a 'relevant activity': headquarters, distribution and service centre, financing and leasing, insurance, banking, fund management, shipping, and intellectual-property business, among others. Pure plastic injection manufacturing of keyboard keycaps is not itself a relevant activity. However, the doctrine applies if the company (i) derives IP-based royalties from double-shot PBT designs or brand trademarks, (ii) operates a Bermuda distribution and service centre, or (iii) functions as a mere holding entity. IP business carries the highest substance expectation: core income-generating activities and locally conducted board management must be demonstrable, and outsourcing rules disregard third-party functions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Artificial Profit Stripping in Source States

Statutory sanction: HMRC may apply transfer-pricing adjustments under TIOPA 2010 and charge diverted profits tax at 25% on overseas entities, together with interest and penalties up to 100% of the tax. In Bermuda, the BMA may recharacterise the entity as a shell, levy penalties up to BMD 10,000, publicise the default, and exchange information with HMRC under the UK-Bermuda TIEA.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false accounts or knowingly directed artificial profit stripping may be sued for fraudulent trading, disqualified for up to five years, and subjected to asset-freezing and travel bans. In the UK, senior accounting officers face personal penalties of GBP 5,000 for tax-governance failures.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Monetary Authority may impose fines up to BMD 10,000 per violation, continuing daily penalties, a formal compliance order, and referral to the Registrar for strike-off. Tax-exemption status may be revoked, exposing the company to future Bermudian taxes and forced liquidation costs.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for unpaid ESR fines and government fees after strike-off. Travel restrictions may be imposed to prevent departure from Bermuda until penalties are settled, and the Minister of Finance may bring civil proceedings for restitution of enforcement costs.
AML/CTF Breach and Unauthorised Payment Aggregation

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the AML/ATF Codes, the company faces criminal fines, forfeiture of tainted proceeds, and cancellation of the merchant registration. Unlicensed payment activity may draw separate sanctions under Bermuda's National Payment (Rights and Accounts) Act 2017 or Digital Assets Business Act 2018.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners who consented to or connived in money laundering bear personal criminal liability, with imprisonment up to 14 years, international travel bans, extradition cooperation with the UK, and civil asset recovery by Bermuda's Asset Recovery Unit.
Beneficial Ownership and Annual Return Filing Failures

Statutory sanction: Under the Beneficial Ownership Act 2016 and Companies Act 1981, each omission is a separate offence carrying fines and, on conviction on indictment, imprisonment up to one year. The Registrar may strike off the company without prior notice, after which the company loses capacity to sue on its payment accounts and its assets risk vesting in the Crown.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders are jointly and severally liable for unpaid annual government fees and restoration expenses. A director supplying false beneficial-ownership particulars is personally liable for damages to the company and creditors and may be restricted from leaving Bermuda pending resolution.
