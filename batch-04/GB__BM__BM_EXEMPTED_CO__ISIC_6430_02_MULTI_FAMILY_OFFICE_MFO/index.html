Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Government Ministry of Finance – Office of the Tax Commissioner (administering corporate income tax, payroll tax, and stamp duty) and the Registrar of Companies (for corporate registry matters); primary portal: www.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company obtains its statutory identity through incorporation with the Registrar of Companies, receiving a Certificate of Incorporation and a unique company registration number. There is no general income-tax TIN for ordinary exempted companies. For in-scope entities under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (MNE groups with annual revenue exceeding EUR 750 million), a specific CIT registration must be completed with the Tax Commissioner via the Bermudian tax portal; foreign-ownership documentation and a registered office address in Bermuda are prerequisites.

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Duty regime (no VAT/GST or sales tax)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BMD 0.00 – no VAT/GST registration threshold; importers of goods into Bermuda must register with the Bermuda Customs and Border Protection for customs duty purposes and obtain an import licence where applicable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Bermuda has no VAT/GST system, so there is no statutory zero-rating mechanism for exports. Exported goods and offshore services fall outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxation; a Certificate of Export may be obtained to evidence that goods have left Bermuda for customs-duty purposes and to support foreign-claim substantiation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs Form C5)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Port departure certificate or shipping line receipt
  • Proof of consideration and foreign currency conversion (where applicable)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists. Customs declarations are processed through the Bermuda Customs and Border Protection electronic single-window (Customs Import/Export Systems) but no API-based invoice clearance or tax-stamping obligation applies.

No VAT zero-rating or input-tax refund regime exists, so no VAT clawback penalty applies. Customs misdeclaration and duty evasion are penalised under the Customs Tariff Act 1950 and the Bermuda Customs Act 1950 (fines up to three times the duty evaded, forfeiture of goods, and criminal liability for fraudulent documentation). For in-scope CIT entities, late registration or filing under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 attracts administrative penalties and enforcement by the Tax Commissioner.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains full current and capital account convertibility. There are no exchange control regulations, no restrictions on the conversion of BMD to USD or any other currency, and no approval requirements for inward or outward remittances. Profits and dividends of exempted companies may be freely repatriated to shareholders, subject only to standard Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Financing of Terrorism (CFT) obligations imposed on financial institutions. Transfer pricing and economic substance rules do not impede capital mobility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no statutory timelines or mandatory conversion ratios for export proceeds. A Bermuda exempted company may retain export receipts in any currency, in Bermuda or offshore, without surrendering foreign currency to the BMA or any local bank. The only applicable requirement is the filing of periodic financial returns and adherence to record-keeping obligations under the Companies Act 1981.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, functioning as Bermuda's central financial services regulator and monetary authority. It oversees currency issuance (Bermudian dollar, BMD, pegged 1:1 to USD), banking, trust, insurance, investment, and digital asset businesses. The BMA operates without restrictive exchange control powers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Offshore digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) licensed in the UK or EEA can provide payment accounts and SWIFT rails to Bermuda entities, but they generally cannot offer custody, escrow, or investment-grade treasury services required for a Multi-Family Office (MFO). Legal constraints: (i) FCA-authorised EMIs are restricted to e-money payment services under the E-Money Regulations 2011; they cannot hold client securities or provide discretionary portfolio management. (ii) Bermuda's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 applies to digital asset-related custody and payments; an EMI operating into Bermuda without a licence may trigger registration requirements. (iii) Private international banking often requires a regulated bank physically present in Bermuda to meet local substance rules. Thus, EMIs are viable only for auxiliary travel or operational expense flows, not for core treasury liquidity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injection from United Kingdom shareholders or ultimate principals to the Bermuda exempted company must be routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 for interbank transfers. The wire must specify the sender's/recipient's legal name and account number, the ISO currency code (USD often used alongside BMD), and a clear purpose code consistent with the BPM6, such as 'Direct Investment' (code 3A) or 'Equity Capital Contribution' if the entity is funded as a holding vehicle. To satisfy AML/KYC and Bermuda Economic Substance regulations, the transfer must be accompanied by the board resolution approving the injection, a copy of the share subscription agreement, UBO declarations, and a funds-source confirmation letter. All transfers passing through a Bermudian bank must be reported to the BMA if they exceed prescribed thresholds under the Transaction Reporting Regulations. No exchange control approval is needed.Outbound disbursements for asset acquisitions, investment settlements, service fees, and family distributions are executed via SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer) or MT202 (bank-to-bank cover) for fiat payments. For securities trades, use Delivery-versus-Payment (DVP) through CREST (UK), Euroclear, or Clearstream; physical trade settlements for private assets use MT103 with a copy of the sale agreement and beneficiary bank confirmation. For trade finance where a commercial transaction requires documentary credit, a Letter of Credit (LC) can be issued by a Bermuda bank through MT700, although MFOs typically do not use LC rails. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For in-scope MNE groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million, approximately BMD 810 million, every Bermuda constituent entity must maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation, including a Master File and Local File, in accordance with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines. There is no general de minimis threshold in Bermuda; all cross-border related-party transactions of in-scope groups are subject to documentation requirements.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Requirements) Act 2018 applies to Bermuda exempted companies carrying on a relevant activity, including fund management, financing or leasing, holding entity, and distribution and service centre activities. A multi-family office performing discretionary portfolio advisory or investment management must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Bermuda, adequate full-time employees, physical premises, operating expenditure, and board management and control in Bermuda. A pure equity holding entity is subject to a reduced substance test but must satisfy the filing and reporting obligations; mischaracterizing active fund management as a mere holding activity is an immediate substance violation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction under Bermuda law carries fines up to BMD 250,000, imprisonment up to 5 years, full repayment of evaded amounts with interest, and publication of the offender’s identity; civil penalties may be imposed cumulatively by the BTA.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise false filings are personally liable for restitution, may be disqualified for up to 10 years, subjected to travel bans and asset freezes, and sentenced to imprisonment where fraud is proven.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: BTA penalty up to BMD 362,100 for failure to meet the economic substance test, additional daily penalties, strike-off or compulsory winding-up, revocation of tax residence certificate, and disclosure to foreign competent authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or acquiesce in sham substance arrangements are jointly and severally liable for resulting penalties and restitution; courts may pierce the corporate veil, impose travel restrictions, and order disqualification or custodial sentences.
AML /CFT Breach in High-Risk Multi-Family Office Operations

Statutory sanction: Conviction under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Regulations may result in fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may revoke the regulated licence, impose monetary administrative penalties, and debar the corporate entity from financial services.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers and directors face personal fines, repaying losses to clients, AML supervision orders, disqualification by the BMA, and travel bans pending investigation; criminal liability attaches to wilful blindness to money laundering.
Unlicensed Investment Business Under the Investment Business Act 2003

Statutory sanction: Summary conviction may result in a fine up to BMD 12,400 and/or imprisonment up to 12 months; conviction on indictment carries a fine up to BMD 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years. The court may also declare contracts void and order full restitution to clients.

Director personal liability: Directors of an unlicensed entity are personally liable for client losses where the corporate form is used as a facade; personal asset orders, travel bans, disqualification, and imprisonment may be imposed.
