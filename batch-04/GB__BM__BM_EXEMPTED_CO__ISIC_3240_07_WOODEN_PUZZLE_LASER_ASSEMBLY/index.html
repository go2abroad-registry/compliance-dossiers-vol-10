Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance of Bermuda, acting through the Registrar of Companies (Companies and Insolvency Division) for entity registration, the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and any corporate income tax administration, and the Bermuda Revenue Authority for customs duties and other indirect-type levies. Official portal: www.gov.bm; Companies Registry e-filing is accessed through the Registrar of Companies portal. Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC) of the United Kingdom is NOT the competent authority for a Bermuda-incorporated Exempted Company unless a UK permanent establishment or UK tax-residence issue separately arises.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a general-purpose TIN or VAT ID. For a foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company the procedure is: (1) appoint a Bermuda resident representative / licensed corporate service provider; (2) incorporate under the Companies Act 1981 and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and Bermuda company file number; (3) apply to the Minister of Finance for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, granting statutory assurance that no Bermuda tax will be imposed on income, dividends, interest, royalties, capital gains or distributions until at least 31 March 2035; (4) if the worldwide group has consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, assess whether the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (Pillar Two / GloBE-inspired regime) applies and, if in scope, register with the Tax Commissioner; (5) register for Bermuda payroll tax only if the company has Bermuda-linked employees; (6) file annual Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) returns with the Registrar of Companies. No Bermuda VAT registration is possible because Bermuda has no VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No domestic check-the-box election exists in Bermuda. The principal statutory election is the application to the Minister of Finance for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection certificate (effective through at least 31 March 2035), which shields the Exempted Company from Bermuda income and capital taxes. For United States federal tax purposes, the beneficial owner may make a check-the-box election under U.S. Treasury Regulations (IRS Form 8832) to classify the Bermuda entity as a corporation, partnership, or disregarded entity, subject to CFC, hybrid entity, and anti-hybrid rules. Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, where applicable to in-scope multinational groups, operates consistently with the OECD GloBE model and denies hybrid mismatch outcomes that would frustrate minimum-tax collections; smaller Exempted Companies remain covered by the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection certificate and are not subject to Bermuda CIT.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax is levied in Bermuda. Bermuda levies customs import duties (on goods imported into Bermuda) and payroll tax (on Bermuda-based employment), but neither applies to an offshore Exempted Company that manufactures and sells wooden puzzles entirely outside Bermuda.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BMD 0 — not applicable. Bermuda imposes no indirect-tax turnover threshold and no VAT/GST registration. Customs duty registration is relevant only for an importer of record bringing goods physically into Bermuda; an Exempted Company with no Bermuda import/export flow has no Bermuda indirect-tax nexus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT/GST, so no Bermuda export zero-rating claim is available or required. For goods physically exported from another jurisdiction on behalf of the Bermuda Exempted Company, zero-rating eligibility is governed by that exporting jurisdiction (for example, UK 0% VAT on exported goods where the goods are removed from the UK within statutory limits, or the ship-to customer is outside the UK; offshore services zero-rating where the recipient is outside the UK). The Exempted Company must maintain sufficient rebuttal evidence — commercial documentation, transport documents, bank settlement records, and Amazon Pay / Stripe reporting — to support any zero-rating claim made by the exporting entity in the same supply chain.

  • Customs Export Declaration from the jurisdiction of physical departure (not a Bermuda filing requirement)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing movement of the goods across a border
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/FX settlement statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds (recommended for substance; no statutory Bermuda requirement)
  • Commercial Invoice and detailed Packing List covering ISIC 3240 / NAICS 339930 precision CO2 laser-cut 3D wooden puzzle sets
  • Certificate of Origin where a tariff preference claim is made
  • Amazon Pay / Stripe settlement reports and marketplace VAT invoices for remote-sales substantiation

Bermuda has no real-time fiscal clearance, continuous-transaction-control, government API stamping, or Peppol-based e-invoicing mandate. The Exempted Company may issue standard electronic invoices in USD or BMD. Where the wooden puzzles are exported from a VAT-imposing jurisdiction or sold via Amazon Pay / Stripe marketplaces, the exporting jurisdiction's e-invoicing and VAT digital-reporting rules must be observed, and the marketplace settlement and VAT documents retained as substantiation.

Bermuda does not operate a VAT clawback or export zero-rating penalty regime. However, false statements made in any application for tax protection, economic substance returns, or filings with the Registrar of Companies are offences under the Companies Act 1981 and the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, punishable by fines and imprisonment, and may lead to revocation or denial of the tax exemption certificate. For in-scope multinational groups, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 provides for interest, penalties, and retrospective adjustments for incomplete, false, or unsubstantiated GloBE / domestic minimum top-up filings. Separately, any foreign exporting jurisdiction may recover underpaid VAT through retrospective assessments and misdeclaration penalties (for example, in the UK, penalties up to 100% of a deliberate error), and those obligations are enforceable against the exporting entity and, where applicable, the Bermuda group member as a joint economic group.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is fixed at parity to the US dollar (USD) at 1:1 under a 100% foreign-reserve-backed currency board arrangement. For a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as non-resident, both current-account convertibility (trade payments for wooden-puzzle components, CO2 laser machinery, merchant settlement receipts) and capital-account convertibility (equity injections, intercompany loans, dividend distributions) are entirely unrestricted and require no BMA prior approval. Profit repatriation to the United Kingdom parent is free of exchange control barriers, withholding taxes, or mandatory conversion; dividends can be paid in USD, BMD, GBP, or EUR at the board's election. The only practical hurdle is ensuring that the bank's in-house compliance officer ratifies the source-of-funds narrative for each material distribution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion timeline applies to a non-resident-designated Exempted Company under the Exchange Control Act 1972. The entity may retain 100% of export proceeds in USD or any freely convertible currency without any mandatory conversion ratio. Statutory retention of underlying trade documentation (invoice, airway bill, customs export declaration, and proof of receipt) is required for a minimum of five years for AML/CFT audit purposes, and the BMA requires an annual economic substance declaration showcasing real commercial activity in or from Bermuda, though no foreign-exchange reporting on export values is triggered at the corporate treasury level.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA); banking supervisor and exchange control administrator under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

UK FCA-regulated electronic money institutions (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business, Currencycloud) are rated High feasibility as auxiliary clearing channels for marketplace settlements (Amazon Pay and Stripe) and low-value supplier disbursements, leveraging the UK digital trade gateway status of the sovereign parent. However, Bermuda does not issue EMI licences to offshore digital firms under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 regime, so an offshore EMI cannot serve as the primary statutory treasury rail or replace a BMA-licensed tier-1 commercial bank account. The recommended architecture is a dual-rail structure: a BMA-licensed Bermuda bank account for capital escrow, substance verification, and intercompany funding; and a UK FCA-regulated EMI account for high-frequency, low-value e-commerce payouts and supplier payments, with daily or weekly sweeps back to the Bermuda treasury account to preserve audit clarity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing is through the SWIFT MT103 channel from the UK parent's FCA-regulated UK bank to the Bermuda tier-1 USD account at Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda. The remittance must reference SWIFT purpose code GIF (General Purpose / Capital Contribution) or, where intercompany financing is used, code 601 / 69C for cross-border intercompany loans. The verification pack must accompany the first wire: board resolution approving the share subscription or loan drawdown; updated share register; source-of-funds statement evidencing six months of legitimate accumulated profits; and an AML sworn statement executed by the UK parent director. No BMA exchange-control approval or filing fee applies to the injection, and the funds may be settled directly in USD without conversion, preserving the BMD 1:1 peg.Primary clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier payments in USD/EUR/GBP through the CHIPS and Fedwire correspondent networks; SWIFT MT700 documentary letters of credit for high-value machinery imports exceeding GBP 50,000 from EU and East-Asian vendors, particularly CO2 laser sources and optical cutting heads; and marketplace batch settlements via Amazon Pay and Stripe routed through the UK FCA-regulated EMI, which sweeps daily into the Bermuda USD treasury account. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 equivalent of consolidated group revenue (per the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 global minimum top-up tax threshold); for an in-scope Bermuda entity, a Local File is required when aggregate related-party transactions exceed BMD 15,000,000 in the fiscal year. Below this level, the entity is not required to prepare a separate Bermuda Local File but should retain functional and risk documentation for ESR compliance.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2018 (as amended) require any exempted company carrying on a relevant activity — including pure equity holding, financing, distribution and service centres, and intellectual property holding — to maintain substantial economic presence in Bermuda. A pure equity holding company must comply by ensuring all corporate governance, decision-making, and employee functions occur in Bermuda, and must file an annual Economic Substance Declaration with the Registrar. A manufacturing entity (ISIC 3240) physically operating outside Bermuda, such as a CO2 laser assembly plant in the UK, is not subject to ESR for that overseas trade provided the Bermuda entity is not merely leasing labour, equipment, or IP to itself; careful substance planning is required to avoid an HMRC challenge that the Bermuda company is a UK taxable permanent establishment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of BMD 100,000 for the first non-compliance and BMD 250,000 for continued default; the Registrar may also suspend the exemption undertaking and exchange the company's details with HMRC under the Bermuda-UK Tax Information Exchange Agreement, exposing the profit shift to UK corporate tax assessments and DPT.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable to a penalty of up to BMD 10,000 each for knowingly signing a false ESR declaration; civil restitution from the company's beneficial owners and disqualification orders banning directors for up to five years may also be imposed.
Beneficial Ownership / AML Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BMD 15,000 plus BMD 500 per day for each day of default; under the Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (AML/ATF) regulations, the company may be fined up to BMD 50,000 and the compliance officer imprisoned for three years for failing to conduct customer due diligence.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to undergo a 5-year travel ban, seizure of Bermudian property, civil restitution for any funds laundered through the company, and imprisonment for a maximum of five years if fraud or money laundering is proven.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration / Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: If the group is in scope of the Bermuda CIT regime, the Bermuda tax authority may adjust the taxable profit and apply a 35% penalty on the top-up tax understatement; simultaneously, HMRC can assess a UK corporation tax adjustment plus interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax due for deliberate behaviour.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or sign inaccurate transfer pricing documentation may become subject to civil liability for the whole group's tax underpayment, a denial of cross-border relief under the UK's diverted profits tax, and a disqualification order of up to 15 years in the UK.
Failure to File / Fraudulent Evasion of Required Returns

Statutory sanction: The Registrar will strike the company off the register after 90 days of continued default, causing all assets and contractual rights to vest in the Crown; monetary penalties reach BMD 2,000 for each false statement plus daily interest of BMD 50 on unpaid penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors remain personally liable for the company's unpaid Bermudian payroll tax and are barred from acting as directors of any Bermudian company for 10 years; criminal proceedings allow fines of up to BMD 50,000 and custodial sentences of up to seven years for fraudulent evasion.
