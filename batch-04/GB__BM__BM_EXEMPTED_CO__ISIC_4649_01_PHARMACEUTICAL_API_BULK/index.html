Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance; Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC) for payroll tax and Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT); Registrar of Companies for entity incorporation and annual compliance; Bermuda Customs Service (Bermuda Customs and Revenue) for customs duties; portals: Registrar of Companies e-Registry and the OTC electronic filing portal.

E-government filing portal

Foreign shareholders engage a licensed Bermuda resident registered agent to incorporate an Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981. The Registrar of Companies issues a Bermuda Company Registration Number (BCRN). No VAT ID exists because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST. Employers must register for payroll tax with the OTC and obtain an employer reference number. A multinational group within the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million) must additionally register for CIT with the OTC to obtain a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (TIN). For the standard Exempted Company outside CIT scope, the BCRN plus annual company fee filings serve as the primary tax identity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties for exempted companies. Annual government fees apply. The Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 introduces a 15% corporate income tax effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, but only for entities that are members of multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist in Bermuda. Available elections and mechanisms: (i) application to the Minister of Finance for a tax assurance certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 guaranteeing protection from future income and capital gains taxes; (ii) election for the Economic Transition Levy exemption; (iii) group elections under Bermuda's CIT rules including designation of the ultimate parent entity or filing entity and election to apply domestic top-up tax in lieu of foreign top-up tax; (iv) constitutional or structural choices impacting tax residence include place of central management and control analysis.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales tax. Bermuda levies customs import duties under the Customs Act 1966 and Tariff Act 1969, stamp duty on instruments and land transactions, and employer payroll tax; none of these apply as a general indirect tax on supplies of goods or services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT/GST registration threshold. Importers and customs brokers must be registered with Bermuda Customs to clear goods; customs duty applies on the c.i.f. import value. Pharmaceutical API and pharmaceutical-related goods may benefit from preferential or zero-rated duty classifications under the Bermuda Tariff, subject to classification and documentary proof.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No export VAT zero-rating regime exists because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST. Goods exported from Bermuda are outside the Bermuda indirect tax base, and customs duty applies only to imports. A Bermuda Exempted Company distributing pharmaceutical APIs via principal-to-principal contracts should rely on the absence of Bermuda indirect tax plus the applicable destination-jurisdiction rules (for example, UK place of supply and export exemption for goods removed from the UK, or non-EU/non-UK VAT treatment in the buyer's jurisdiction). No Bermuda export VAT refund is claimable or required.

  • Customs Export Declaration via ASYCUDA World where goods physically transit through Bermuda
  • Commercial Invoice describing the pharmaceutical API including INN, strength, batch number, quantity, and price
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing the consignee and the destination outside Bermuda
  • Packing List showing harmonized tariff classification (HS 2934, 2939, 3002 or relevant API heading)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank wire confirmation evidencing settlement through SWIFT LC or Bank Wire
  • Certificate of Origin where required by the destination customs authority
  • Pharmaceutical wholesale/distribution license and any export-related health certificates (e.g., MHRA or equivalent) where goods physically originate from the UK

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists. Bermuda Customs operates the ASYCUDA World electronic customs management system for submission of declarations, risk assessment, and clearance. Standard commercial invoices accompany customs entries; no government-approved e-invoice clearance is required for B2B trading transactions of a Bermuda Exempted Company.

No VAT clawback applies in Bermuda. Scrutiny arises under two regimes: (i) Bermuda customs — false declarations, undervaluation, or misdescription attract penalties under the Customs Act 1966 including forfeiture of goods, fines of up to three times the duty evaded, and criminal sanctions for smuggling; (ii) economic substance and CIT review — an Exempted Company engaged in pharmaceutical API wholesale must maintain adequate economic substance or demonstrate tax residence elsewhere to avoid reclassification under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; penalties for CIT non-compliance include interest, late-filing fines, and assessment-based penalties of up to 100% of the tax shortfall, with potential retrospective recharacterization where substance arrangements are deemed artificial.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts. The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to USD and circulates interchangeably with USD as legal tender. No exchange control approval is required for inbound or outbound capital movements, dividend repatriation, profit remittance, or liquidation distributions for non-resident owned exempted companies, provided that AML/ATF obligations, beneficial ownership filings, and economic substance requirements are current.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or holding period on export receipts. Exporters of pharmaceutical APIs may freely retain BMD, USD, or any foreign currency balances with licensed clearing banks.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the central bank and integrated financial services regulator; administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 but exercises no material convertibility restrictions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderate-to-Low. Bermuda has no standalone Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licence class; digital payment firms are captured under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 or the Banking Act 1969, neither of which supports documentary credit operations required for bulk API trade. Offshore digital EMIs (UK/EU) are generally declined for pharma API wholesale due to sector risk, sanctions geography, and absence of LC capabilities. Use of an EMI is restricted to a pre-funded disbursement wallet, never as the primary inbound capital or settlement rail, and only after approval by the principal Bermuda bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital or shareholder loan from the UK parent must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 referencing payment purpose code 'Equity injection to Bermuda Exempted Company'. The receiving Bermuda bank requires the board resolution authorising issuance of shares, an updated issued-share register, and a funds-origin trail. Because BMD is pegged at par with USD, USD remittances suffer no FX conversion; GBP remittances are converted at the bank's prevailing spot. There are no capital import limits or exchange control permits required. USD settlement via the bank's nostro network is the standard rail to avoid HK or EUR conversion frictions. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold (BMD 0). Bermuda does not maintain a Local File or Master File regime. Where a UK parent or UK affiliate is the counterparty, UK transfer pricing rules apply at the UK participant level; UK HMRC may require contemporaneous evidence for cross-border API procurement and resale margins. Practical compliance threshold used by HMRC: documentation should be prepared where intercompany goods transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 or connected-party services exceed GBP 250,000 per accounting period.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply to all Bermuda exempted companies engaged in relevant activities. 'Distribution and Service Centre' is expressly listed as a relevant activity, and wholesale trading of pharmaceutical APIs on own account falls under it. A qualifying entity must exercise directed and managed activity in Bermuda, carry out its core income-generating activities through adequate physical presence, employees, and local expenditure, and maintain contemporaneous evidence. Failure to satisfy ESR results in penalties up to BMD 250,000, mandatory exchange of information with the UK, and possible strike-off. The entity cannot rely on the pure holding company reduced substance test if it actively procures and resells inventory.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Artificial UK Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: HMRC may make UK corporation tax assessments, impose penalties up to 100% of the tax understated, apply the UK CFC charge under Part 9A CTA 2010, and invoke the Criminal Finances Act 2017 for failure to prevent tax evasion. Bermuda may also strike the company off and refer the case to the Tax Information Authority for exchange of information.

Director personal liability: UK resident directors who knowingly create a sham arrangement may be prosecuted for evasion, disqualified under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, and made personally liable for UK tax where corporate assets are transferred without commercial substance. Bermuda directors who certify false declarations face personal fines, imprisonment, and travel restrictions pending criminal enquiries.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance (Penalty) Regulations 2018: financial penalty of up to BMD 250,000 for non-compliance, daily fines of BMD 500 for continued breach, electronic exchange of information with UK HMRC, and mandatory deregistration or strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for fines where they deliberately cause the company to submit false substance declarations. A Bermuda court may impose imprisonment for false statements, and HMRC may issue director penalty notices against UK resident directors for failing to prevent the creation of an offshore shell.
Money Laundering and AML/CTF Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 (Bermuda) and the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Code, corporate penalties may reach BMD 5,000,000 plus up to 20 years' imprisonment for convictions. Failure to file beneficial ownership declarations carries separate civil penalties and freeze of bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners who knowingly launder funds face asset confiscation, travel bans, extradition, and imprisonment. UK prosecute of directors can be pursued despite the Bermuda corporate form because the mental and physical elements of the offence are committed in the UK.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and CFC Attribution

Statutory sanction: HMRC may make a transfer pricing adjustment under UK TP rules, recalculate taxable profits, and charge corporation tax plus late payment interest. The UK CFC charge may attribute the Bermuda entity's excess profits to the UK parent. Penalties for careless or deliberate inaccurate returns may reach 70% or 100% of the additional tax.

Director personal liability: UK parent company directors may be personally penalised for failure to notify HMRC under FA 2009 penalties. If the mispricing is orchestrated to evade tax, HMRC can pursue dishonest tax evasion facilitation and the Bermuda directors may also be disqualified for knowingly endorsing transfer pricing records that have neither documentary support nor economic reality.
