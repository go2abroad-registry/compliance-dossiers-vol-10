Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (portal: www.gov.bm/department/tax-commissioner); Bermuda Customs and Excise Department (portal: www.gov.bm/department/customs-excise); Registrar of Companies, Registry General (portal: www.gov.bm/department/registry-general).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda entity is incorporated as an exempted company under the Bermuda Companies Act 1981 through the Registrar of Companies, obtaining a Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles, and a unique company registration number. Bermuda levies no corporate income or capital gains tax, so no income-tax TIN is issued. The company must apply to the Office of the Tax Commissioner for a payroll-tax employer registration number once it has Bermuda-sourced employees or remuneration, and it must obtain a Customs declarant account with the Bermuda Customs and Excise Department for import/export operations. Exempted companies may apply for a certificate of protection under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 confirming statutory exemption from future Bermudian taxes. Registration is effected online via the Registrar of Companies' e-services and the Government of Bermuda portal; no minimum capital requirement applies and 100% non-resident ownership is permitted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: A Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-resident in Bermuda but is exempt from corporate income tax, capital gains tax, dividend withholding tax, interest withholding tax and profits tax until at least 2035 under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. The entity remains liable only for Bermuda stamp duty on relevant instruments, land-related taxes, and payroll tax in respect of employees working in or deriving remuneration from Bermuda. No territorial sourcing of active business income, no controlled-foreign-corporation attribution, and no domestic income-tax regime applies to exempted companies.

Available election forms

Bermuda domestic law provides no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election mechanism because no income-tax regime exists. Available statutory designations and filings include: (i) application for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 certificate confirming tax-exemption and protected status; (ii) payroll-tax registration and quarterly filing obligations, including the statutory election for the 'commuted payroll option' where applicable; (iii) designation of 'non-local' status affecting landholding and licensing permissions under the Companies Act 1981; (iv) annual economic substance declaration and filing under the Companies (Economic Substance) Rules, including elections as to relevant activity classification; and (v) on the foreign side, the entity may be classified for US federal income tax purposes by an IRS Form 8832 check-the-box election and may be treated inconsistently by other parent jurisdictions for hybrid-mismatch purposes, subject only to Bermuda's substance requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Bermuda. Indirect taxation consists of (a) customs duties on imported goods under the Customs Tariff Act 1967, (b) stamp duties on instruments, land transfers and certain transactions, and (c) payroll tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995, which operates as a payroll levy rather than a consumption tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT or GST is levied in Bermuda. Customs duty applies from the first dollar on all imported goods unless a statutory exemption applies, and all importers/exporters must hold a Customs declarant account or bond with the Bermuda Customs and Excise Department. Payroll tax registration is mandatory once the exempted company pays Bermuda-sourced remuneration, with small-employer exemptions and annual payroll thresholds available under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 as published by the Office of the Tax Commissioner.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: because no VAT exists, exports of goods or services from Bermuda are outside the scope of any Bermuda consumption tax and are not subject to zero-rating or refund procedures; exported goods are also excluded from Bermuda customs duty where they leave the customs territory under the Customs (Export) provisions. UK/GB leg: where the Bermudian exempted company or a UK-established connected entity supplies goods for export from the UK to a destination outside the UK (including Bermuda), the supply is zero-rated for UK VAT under VATA 1994 s.30 and the Zero-Rating (Exports) Order, provided that (i) the goods are exported within 3 months of the supply or an extended approved period, (ii) the supplier retains valid proof of export, and (iii) the supplier exports the goods in the course of its business pursuant to the customer's order; ancillary services such as return-merchandise inspection, grading, repackaging and freight may be zero-rated where they are directly connected to and supplied for the purposes of the exported goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (e.g., UK CDS export declaration or EIDR bulk entry on the UK Customs Declarations Service)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure of the goods
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, bank SWIFT or other proof of payment for overseas goods flows
  • Bermuda Import Declaration (Bill of Entry) and duty receipt where the returned merchandise enters Bermuda
  • Certificate of Origin or import/export permit where required under UK or Bermuda trade documentation practice

Bermuda does not operate a real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping or government-clearance mandate, as there is no VAT system. Import and export declarations must be submitted electronically to the Bermuda Customs and Excise Department through its online customs clearance platform with supporting documents retained; no government-issued e-invoice stamp or API clearance is required. For the UK-connected leg, any UK VAT-registered group entity must comply with Making Tax Digital (MTD) rules, submitting VAT returns through MTD-compatible software, and must retain digital records and relevant export evidence where UK VAT zero-rating is claimed.

UK: fraudulent or incorrect export zero-rating claims attract penalties under FA 2007 Schedule 24 up to 100% of potential lost revenue, evasion penalties under VATA 1994 ss.60 and 72, default surcharge, and criminal prosecution; HMRC may assess and claw back wrongly zero-rated outputs with retrospective effect for up to 4 years (6 years for deliberate non-disclosure), plus statutory interest. Bermuda: no VAT clawback regime exists, but false customs declarations, under-declared export values, or smuggling attract forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties, and suspension or revocation of the declarant's Customs account under the Customs Tariff Act 1967 and the Bermuda Customs Act; an exempted company that fails economic substance obligations or misuses its tax-protection certificate may face statutory fines, loss of compliance standing, and strike-off by the Registrar of Companies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US Dollar at 1:1, with full current-account convertibility and a permissive capital-account regime. A Bermuda Exempted Company is classified as non-resident for exchange-control purposes, enabling free retention, transfer, and settlement of USD, GBP, and EUR balances without case-by-case central bank approval for ordinary trade receipts and payments. Profit remittance is structurally unimpeded: Bermuda levies no corporate income tax and no withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or management fees, so the only material hurdle is the bank's source-of-wealth and sanctions due diligence during high-value outbound wires and dividend distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Bermuda. Reverse-logistics receivables (quality-grading fees, return freight, restocking surcharges, and disposition proceeds) may be retained in any freely convertible foreign currency, with no designated repatriation timeline. The only administrative requirement is a Section 3 Exchange Control declaration submitted through the receiving licensed bank upon account opening and for each significant credit; foreign currency balances may be held in offshore accounts with deemed consent where the receiving financial institution is licensed by the BMA.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, with administrative currency oversight for the British Overseas Territory of Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for FCA-regulated UK and EEA EMIs (Airwallex, Currencycloud, Wise Business, Revolut Business) as the primary settlement backbone, leveraging the UK strategic role for rapid pan-European onboarding in 3 to 10 business days. Legal constraints: (i) the BMA requires exchange-control deemed consent for the Exempted Company to hold offshore accounts, obtainable through the EMI's account-application documentation without a separate Bermuda license; (ii) UK EMIs are not licensed banks in Bermuda and cannot provide BMD clearing, local Bermudian IBANs, or UK FSCS deposit protection, so balances above the FSCS threshold must be swept regularly to a Tier-1 Bermudian bank; (iii) high-volume refund and returns flows may trigger EMI risk-engine review, requiring a documented compliance narrative and transaction-matching evidence; (iv) all wires must identify the final beneficiary to avoid pass-through account treatment under correspondent banking rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For equity injection from a UK parent or UK-resident shareholder: route SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV where correspondent cover requires a second leg) from the UK sponsor bank to the Bermudian bank's London or New York USD correspondent, or use direct GBP CHAPS to the Bermudian bank's GBP nostro. The wire narrative must state: 'Purpose: capital subscription under Exchange Control Act 1972 Section 3(1) — Exempted Company non-resident', with the ISO 20022 purpose code 'COLT' or 'GFCO'. The receiving licensed bank applies deemed exchange-control permission and records the inward Foreign Direct Investment declaration. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 / Not applicable. Bermuda has no corporate income tax and therefore no statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold. The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations require documentation of substance for relevant activities but do not impose local file or master file requirements based on transaction amount. If the Bermuda exempted company is connected to a UK group or is itself UK tax resident, UK transfer-pricing documentation obligations under the UK Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010 may apply when the UK filing thresholds are exceeded.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) require every exempted company carrying on a relevant activity to demonstrate adequate economic substance in Bermuda. Relevant activities include banking, insurance, fund management, financing, headquarters, shipping, intellectual property, holding, and distribution and service centres. A reverse-logistics company engaged only in returned-goods grading and cross-border coordination outside Bermuda may fall outside the listed relevant activities, but if it performs distribution, procurement, or service-centre functions in Bermuda, it must satisfy the substance test by showing management and direction in Bermuda, adequate employees, physical premises, and core income-generating activities carried on in Bermuda. The entity must also file the annual ESR Return and retain contemporaneous records sufficient to prove substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / UK tax residence abuse

Statutory sanction: HMRC may assess the company as UK tax resident under UK case law and corporate tax rules, subjecting its worldwide profits to UK corporation tax at 25% (from April 2023) plus interest and penalties. Fraudulent evasion of tax may lead to criminal prosecution, fines, and confiscation orders.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly cause the company to understate UK tax liabilities may face personal liability under UK tax penalty rules, disqualification as directors, travel bans while under criminal or civil investigation, and imprisonment for up to 7 years for cheating the public revenue or fraudulent tax evasion. Civil recovery proceedings may also require directors to make personal restitution.
Bermuda Economic Substance non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda law imposes penalties of BMD 10,000 for failure to file the ESR Return, BMD 50,000 for failure to comply with a regulatory notice, and BMD 25,000 for knowingly false or misleading information. The Registrar may strike off the company, and the Bermuda authorities may exchange information with UK and EU tax authorities, leading to downstream tax assessments abroad.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers can be made personally liable for unpaid ESR penalties where the failure is attributable to their intentional or negligent conduct. Bermuda courts may issue disqualification orders, asset freezes, and travel restrictions pending investigation, and directors may be required to restore company assets or pay civil restitution.
Shell company / sham lack of substance

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Registrar may strike off the company for non-compliance or abandonment. In the UK, HMRC and the courts may disregard the Bermuda entity as a sham, allocate all profits to the actual operating entity, and impose UK corporation tax, penalties, and interest. The company may also be placed on public registers of non-compliant overseas entities.

Director personal liability: Directors of a sham company may be personally liable for the company's debts if the corporate veil is pierced, particularly where the company traded while insolvent or after strike-off. Additional personal consequences may include civil restitution orders, director disqualification, travel bans, and criminal liability for preparing false documents.
Anti-Money Laundering / proceeds of crime

Statutory sanction: Violations of Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and AML/ATF regulations can result in unlimited fines, forfeiture of assets, and imprisonment up to 14 years. If the conduct is connected to the UK, the UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 and Money Laundering Regulations 2017 carry separate penalties including imprisonment, unlimited fines, and criminal asset recovery.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be prosecuted as principal offenders if they consented to or negligently failed to prevent money laundering. Courts may impose travel bans, passport surrender, freezing orders, and imprisonment. Personal liability for civil compensation to victims is also possible.
Unlicensed local business and violation of exempted-company conditions

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Business Licensing regime and Companies Act 1981 impose fines, revocation of the exempted status, unenforceability of contracts, and possible Court-ordered winding-up. The company may also be required to pay all business taxes, payroll taxes, and fees from which it was not in fact exempt because it was operating locally.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorize unlicensed Bermuda operations may be personally fined, disqualified, and held liable for restitution to the company or third parties. In serious cases, Bermuda courts may impose travel bans and, where the conduct involves fraud, imprisonment.
