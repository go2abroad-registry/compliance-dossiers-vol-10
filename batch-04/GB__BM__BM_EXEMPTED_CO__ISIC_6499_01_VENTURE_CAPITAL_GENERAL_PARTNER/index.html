Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance; tax administration is divided between the Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax, land tax, stamp duty collection) and the Corporate Income Tax Agency (CITA) for the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024. The Registrar of Companies within the Ministry of Finance handles corporate registration. Official government portal: https://www.gov.bm (tax section: https://www.gov.bm/taxation).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity is first incorporated as an exempted company under the Companies Act 1981, receiving a Certificate of Incorporation and a Company Registration Number (CRN). The exempted company may file an application for a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which shelters the entity from future Bermuda taxes on income, dividends, capital gains, and appreciation until at least 31 March 2035. For employment taxes, the exempted company must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Payroll Tax Registration Number. For corporate income tax purposes, if the entity is an in-scope constituent entity of a multinational group under the CIT Act 2024, the designated Bermuda entity must register with CITA and obtain a Bermuda Tax Identification Number (BTIN). Bermuda does not levy VAT/GST, so there is no VAT registration or VAT ID.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity is first incorporated as an exempted company under the Companies Act 1981, receiving a Certificate of Incorporation and a Company Registration Number (CRN). The exempted company may file an application for a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which shelters the entity from future Bermuda taxes on income, dividends, capital gains, and appreciation until at least 31 March 2035. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda does not employ a US-style check-the-box election. Available elections include: (i) application for a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, extending protection from future taxation until 31 March 2035; (ii) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, the group may appoint a specific Bermuda entity as the designated filer, and an election can be made for the simplified qualifying loss regime; (iii) hybrid-mismatch-aligned elections are limited to those consistent with the OECD Pillar Two approach, with no unilateral hybrid entity check-the-box. Any election must be documented and approved by the Minister of Finance or CITA in accordance with the applicable Bermuda law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Bermuda does not impose VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax. The only import-side indirect tax is customs duty levied under the Customs Act 2014; a payroll tax also exists but is a direct levy on emoluments, not an indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – there is no VAT/GST registration threshold in Bermuda. For customs duty purposes, any person, including a foreign entity, that imports commercial goods into Bermuda must register with the Bermuda Customs Service or appoint a licensed customs broker; no de minimis monetary threshold applies where commercial imports occur.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Bermuda has no VAT or GST, so exports cannot be 'zero-rated' in the VAT sense. Exports of goods from Bermuda are outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxation and require no zero-rating certificate. Where an imported good is subsequently exported without being consumed in Bermuda, a customs duty drawback may be claimed under the Customs Act 2014, provided the exporter files proof of export within the statutory period (generally 12 months) and can substantiate that the goods have left Bermuda.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs Service)
  • Bill of Lading or Sea/Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice showing export sale price and INCOTERM
  • Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank Credit Advice (where export proceeds are repatriated to a Bermuda account within the prescribed period)

Bermuda does not mandate real-time continuous-transaction control e-invoicing or fiscal digital stamping. Customs declarations must, however, be lodged electronically through the Bermuda Customs Service portal (via gov.bm), with pre-arrival manifest data required for sea and air cargo. Import/export entries must include tariff classification, valuation, and shipment details; no third-party clearance API is presently mandated by law.

Fraudulent attempts to claim export exemptions, duty drawbacks, or customs reliefs under the Customs Act 2014 may result in forfeiture of goods, retrospective clawback of all duty benefits with interest, monetary penalties of up to 200% of the value of the goods or five times the duty payable (whichever is greater), and criminal liability for directors/officers with imprisonment of up to 2 years. The Tax Commissioner and the Bermuda Customs Enforcement Unit can conduct enhanced forensic audits; findings of fraud are also reportable under Bermuda's Anti-Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime legislation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is legally pegged at USD 1.00 = BMD 1.00 and is fully convertible. An exempted company owned and controlled by non-Bermudian persons may be designated as a non-resident entity for exchange-control purposes, which grants full current-account and capital-account convertibility. Such designation permits unrestricted inward subscription capital, unrestricted payment of carried interest, and unrestricted outward remittance of dividends, profits, and liquidation proceeds. Resident-designated entities remain subject to limited BMA exchange-control approval for certain payments to non-residents, but a non-resident exempted company holding capital-call funds from foreign LPs is outside that restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no export-proceeds surrender ratio and no statutory mandatory conversion timeline for export receipts. Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, a resident entity must offer foreign-currency receipts to an authorised dealer within one month unless the BMA grants a specific exemption. A non-resident-designated exempted company may retain USD/BMD export proceeds, service fees, carried interest, dividends, and capital-call collections indefinitely in a BMA-licensed non-resident multicurrency account without mandatory conversion into BMD and without BMA surrender approval.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the integrated central bank and Exchange Control Authority under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, Exchange Control Act 1972, Exchange Control Regulations 1973, and AML/ATF (Financial Institutions) Regulations 2017.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low for primary treasury settlement; Medium only as an ancillary payment-rail controller. Bermuda does not operate an electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing regime equivalent to the UK FCA E-Money Regulations 2011. A UK-authorised EMI such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex may process GBP/EUR outbound payment instructions but cannot hold BMA-protected client money as a bank account, cannot issue a Bermuda bank sort code, and is not an authorised depositary under the Bermuda Banking Act 1969. For a Bermuda-exempted VC general partner, LPA fiduciary obligations and BMA AML expectations require the main capital-call and distribution ledger to sit with a BMA-licensed bank; an EMI may be used only as a supplementary FX execution or low-value euro/GBP payment channel after settlement has occurred at a regulated bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

LP capital calls must be settled by direct SWIFT MT103 from the remitting LP bank to the GP's BMA-licensed bank account, never via a third-party unregulated payment aggregator. The standard routing is: remitter LP bank -> SWIFT MT103 to Butterfield/HSBC Bermuda USD correspondent account; field 50K must show the LP legal name; field 59 must show the GP exempted company and the unique capital-call ledger reference; field 70 must include 'CAPITAL CALL, FUND I, LP REF, LPA CLAUSE' and, where required by the remitting bank, the ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' or 'INVE' for direct investment or equity capital contribution; field 71A must be 'SHA' to ensure fees are shared. The inbound amount must reconcile exactly to the signed capital-call notice; amounts above USD 100,000 trigger BMA reporting and require a pre-advice email plus capital-call notice delivered to the relationship manager before value date. For U.S.-domiciled LP remittances in USD, the Bermuda bank may use its U.S. correspondent at a Fedwire participant; for UK/EU LP remittances, GBP uses CHAPS/Faster Payments through a UK correspondent and EUR uses SWIFT via SEPA with the same MT103 reference block.Outbound deployment from the GP treasury account to early-stage portfolio companies should use SWIFT MT103 with full beneficiary name and bank details, payment reference 'INVESTMENT / CONVERTIBLE NOTE / SAFE; DISBURSEMENT MEMO'; for large U.S.-dollar disbursements above USD 1 million, the Bermuda bank will instruct its U.S. correspondent over Fedwire or via SWIFT MT202COV. Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) are permissible and facilitated through a UK or international confirming bank for trade-backed capital transactions, but the standard VC GP disbursement rail is MT103. For trade settlements involving imports or hardware supply agreements, an irrevocable LC at sight or usance may be issued by a UK bank and advised through the target's bank; settlement then follows LC presentation rather than raw wire. All outbound payments must be supported by signed investment-committee minutes, the executed term sheet, a deal-disbursement memo, and invoice or share-purchase documentation to satisfy the Bermuda wire-transfer record-keeping rule.Plain-vanilla deliverable foreign exchange forwards (FXA) with a BMA-licensed bank or an OECD-authorised offshore dealer, maturity up to 12 months, used only to hedge committed capital calls and distribution mismatches.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) entered into with a regulated UK, U.S., or EU bank where the underlying currency has no onshore deliverable market, documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a BMA-licensed Bermuda bank as an authorised dealer.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) matching GBP/EUR/CHF capital-call inflows against USD distribution requirements for the underlying portfolio fund, with economic substance evidenced by capital call schedules and LP notices.FX swaps (deliverable vs. deliverable) for temporary treasury liquidity management between BMD, USD, EUR, and GBP, provided the exposure arises from actual capital-call and portfolio-disbursement obligations, not proprietary speculation.ISDA-documented FX options may be used only on a zero-cost collar or protective basis where the underlying exposure is a committed portfolio investment; naked or speculative FX options are not permissible at GP level for an exempted-company VC fund manager.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (zero) standard corporate income tax for a Bermuda Exempted Company outside OECD Pillar Two scope. A 15% corporate income tax applies under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (effective for accounting periods commencing on or after 1 January 2025) only to Bermuda constituent entities of large multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more (BMD 750 million at the 1:1 Bermuda dollar peg) in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years. A venture capital GP fund vehicle will generally remain at 0% unless it belongs to such an in-scope MNE group.

Non-resident owners and directors should ensure the name matches the certificate of incorporation exactly; any authorised change of name must be filed first. Section A – Registered details Bermuda company number / certificate of incorporation reference, date of incorporation, registered office, financial year-end, and standard industrial classification code (ISIC). Use the same identification as the Companies Act register; a non-resident exempted company remains obliged to file the ESR return even if it has no Bermuda income. Late ESR filing attracts an escalating administrative penalty; failure to meet the substance test can yield penalties of up to BMD 250,000, daily default fines for continued non-compliance, and eventual strike-off recommendation to the Registrar of Companies. The Bermuda Tax Authority may also assess penalties against directors for false declarations.
Section B – Relevant activity classification Select the relevant activity: banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centres, IP holding, pure equity holding, or not relevant. A VC GP that performs discretionary portfolio management for the fund must select 'fund management'; choosing 'pure equity holding' to hide fund management is a fatal misdeclaration. Late ESR filing attracts an escalating administrative penalty; failure to meet the substance test can yield penalties of up to BMD 250,000, daily default fines for continued non-compliance, and eventual strike-off recommendation to the Registrar of Companies. The Bermuda Tax Authority may also assess penalties against directors for false declarations.
Section C – Gross income and assets Report gross income derived from the relevant activity and, for pure equity holding or IP holding entities, the value of assets held at year-end. Report in the currency of the financial statements; all income received from non-Bermuda portfolio companies must be included. Late ESR filing attracts an escalating administrative penalty; failure to meet the substance test can yield penalties of up to BMD 250,000, daily default fines for continued non-compliance, and eventual strike-off recommendation to the Registrar of Companies. The Bermuda Tax Authority may also assess penalties against directors for false declarations.
Section D – Management and control in Bermuda Provide the number of board meetings held in Bermuda, the location where strategy is set, and where statutory decisions are made. If GP directors meet in London, New York, or Hong Kong, the return must state this accurately; the result will be failure of the directed-and-managed test. Late ESR filing attracts an escalating administrative penalty; failure to meet the substance test can yield penalties of up to BMD 250,000, daily default fines for continued non-compliance, and eventual strike-off recommendation to the Registrar of Companies. The Bermuda Tax Authority may also assess penalties against directors for false declarations.
Section E – Employees, premises, and expenditure Average number of full-time employees in Bermuda, floor area of Bermuda premises, and operating expenditure incurred in Bermuda. A shell GP with no employees or premises fails the economic substance test unless it is a pure equity holding entity, which has reduced requirements; that classification is not available where fund management activity is actually exercised. Late ESR filing attracts an escalating administrative penalty; failure to meet the substance test can yield penalties of up to BMD 250,000, daily default fines for continued non-compliance, and eventual strike-off recommendation to the Registrar of Companies. The Bermuda Tax Authority may also assess penalties against directors for false declarations.
Section F – Core income-generating activities (CIGA) State the locations where the core functions (e.g., investment origination, due diligence, portfolio monitoring, exit decisions) are performed. A GP whose CIGA takes place outside Bermuda cannot demonstrate substance in Bermuda and must disclose the factual location. Late ESR filing attracts an escalating administrative penalty; failure to meet the substance test can yield penalties of up to BMD 250,000, daily default fines for continued non-compliance, and eventual strike-off recommendation to the Registrar of Companies. The Bermuda Tax Authority may also assess penalties against directors for false declarations.
