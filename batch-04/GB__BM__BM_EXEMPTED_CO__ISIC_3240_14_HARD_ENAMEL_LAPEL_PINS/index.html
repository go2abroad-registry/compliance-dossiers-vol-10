Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a Bermuda exempted company. Subscribers may incorporate with a nominal authorised capital (typically USD 12,000) without any paid-up or escrow deposit; at least one issued share is recommended to perfect membership, and the company must appoint a Bermuda-resident representative and maintain a registered office in Bermuda.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Bermuda Fire and Rescue Service for the workshop and any storage areas; structural floor-load inspection of the leased premises under the Bermuda Building Code (brass stock and kiln loads); and registration of the workspace with the Department of Workforce Development under the Bermuda factories and occupational safety regulations, together with a workplace emergency response plan covering fire, burns, and chemical exposure.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (gov.bm portal) — statutory administration of Bermuda fiscal levies; Registrar of Companies, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (roc.gov.bm) — corporate registry and exempted-company annual fees; Bermuda Customs Department — customs tariff and import/export declaration administration via the ASYCUDAWorld single window.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda issues no VAT/GST tax identification number because no turnover tax exists. Step 1: Incorporate under the Companies Act 1981 as an Exempted Company; the Registrar of Companies issues a Certificate of Incorporation bearing a Company Number, which serves as the entity's de facto TIN for all fiscal, economic-substance, CRS and FATCA filings. Step 2: Register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Bermuda Payroll Tax only if Bermuda-resident staff are employed. Step 3: Open a Customs Debit Account with the Bermuda Customs Department to import raw materials (brass stock, enamel paints, plating chemicals) and file CUSDEC declarations for each shipment. Step 4: File the annual Exempted Company Fee Return with the Registrar of Companies, and complete the non-resident declaration / economic substance confirmation via the gov.bm portal; manufacturing of hard enamel lapel pins (ISIC 3240) is not a relevant activity and imposes no substance-filing burden beyond that declaration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda domestic law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. Available elections and designations: (1) election at incorporation for Exempted versus Local company status under the Companies Act 1981, conferring 100% non-Bermudian ownership, exemption from the 60% Bermudian equity rule and tax-exempt status; (2) designation as a non-resident company for Exchange Control Act purposes, permitting unrestricted holdings of and dealings in US dollars and other foreign currencies; (3) for US federal income tax purposes only, a Form 8832 check-the-box election may classify the Bermuda Exempted Company as an association taxable as a corporation, a partnership, or a disregarded entity (Bermuda is not on the US per-se corporation list) without changing its Bermuda fiscal status; (4) no UK hybrid-mismatch election is applicable because the entity is non-UK resident and Bermuda imposes no income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Bermuda VAT, GST or sales and use tax; indirect taxation is confined to Bermuda Customs Duties (import tariff) under the Customs Tariff Act 1975. Export sales of hard enamel lapel pins manufactured in and exported from Bermuda are outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST and therefore no turnover-based registration threshold. Registration obligations are nil-threshold: (a) any importer of goods into Bermuda must hold a Customs Debit Account and file CUSDEC declarations; (b) any employer of Bermuda-resident staff must register for Payroll Tax from the first employee; (c) the annual Exempted Company Fee is payable regardless of turnover, profitability, or exports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the VAT sense — Bermuda levies no VAT/GST, so no export zero-rating claim, VAT credit, or refund mechanism exists. Exports of die-struck cloisonné lapel pins manufactured in and exported from Bermuda are inherently outside the scope of Bermuda indirect tax and attract zero Bermuda export duty. The Bermuda entity should nevertheless document that the physical goods departed Bermuda and that export proceeds were received into its corporate Etsy Direct Pay or Stripe accounts, in order to support any foreign buyer's importer-of-record records, any destination-country duty/VAT deferral, and the entity's own non-dutiable export status. Any UK/EU/US indirect tax obligations would arise only under the destination jurisdiction's rules and are not part of Bermuda's tax net.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CUSDEC lodged via ASYCUDAWorld, Bermuda Customs Department)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Bermuda
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (HS classification for enamel/novelty badges, e.g., HS 7326, HS 7117, or HS 8306)
  • Bermuda Certificate of Origin (required where buyers or preferential trade arrangements claim Bermudian/UK origin)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing export-proceeds repatriation into the Bermuda entity's account

No statutory e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate operates in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs requires electronic customs declarations through the ASYCUDAWorld single window (CUSDEC and NEXDOC formats) for import and export clearances. There is no digital stamping, no continuous tax-authority transaction reporting, and no API-linked fiscal signature requirement for B2B or B2C invoices; ordinary commercial invoices and Etsy/Stripe payment records suffice.

No VAT zero-rating clawback exists in Bermuda because no VAT is imposed; however, falsified export documentation or duty evasion attracts severe penalties under the Customs Act 1968 and the Customs Tariff Act 1975, including confiscation and forfeiture of goods and conveyances, monetary fines of up to treble the duty evaded, and criminal prosecution by the Collector of Customs. False Certificate of Origin declarations can result in revocation of preferential origin treatment and penal sanctions. If the Exempted Company holds any incidental intellectual property (e.g., trademarks) that gives rise to 'relevant activity' status under the Economic Substance (Requirements) Act 2018, failure to file or satisfy economic substance requirements triggers an initial penalty of BD $250,000 and a continuing default penalty of up to BD $500,000, plus potential strike-off of the company. False CRS/FATCA self-certifications are separately penalised under Bermuda's International Tax Compliance Regulations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to USD. Capital and current account items are fully convertible; profit remittance and dividend distribution to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted, subjected only to routine AML/KYC verification by the licensed financial institution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Bermuda. Exporters may freely retain, convert, or remit foreign currency receipts without Central Bank reporting.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium as an auxiliary payment channel for merchant settlement, but legally bounded: unlicensed non-resident EMIs may only process merchant settlement (Stripe, Etsy Direct Pay) and cannot serve as the primary deposit-taking clearing rail. A locally licensed EMI is required to hold BMD/USD corporate balances.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 1,000,000 annual related-party transaction value for an in-scope CIT MNE group; no mandatory local file for ordinary exempted companies outside the Pillar Two threshold.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2018 and Regulations 2018 apply. A relevant activity (banking, insurance, headquarters, distribution & service centre, shipping, IP holding, financing, leasing) must be directed and managed in Bermuda with adequate premises, full-time employees and expenditure. An in-scope CIT entity additionally requires substance aligned with OECD GloBE and CBCR rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent CIT Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment of up to 7 years upon indictment; civil penalty up to 100% of the deficit; order for restitution of refund or underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid CIT if they caused or allowed the evasion; courts may issue travel bans, freezing orders, and disqualification from managing an exempted company for 2 to 10 years.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: BMA and Registrar penalties: BMD 10,000 - BMD 250,000 depending on severity; administrative compulsory winding-up and strike-off from the Register; detention of trade in imported goods by customs authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors risk disqualification and personal liability for the expenses of formal investigation; false ESR declaration carries up to 2 years' imprisonment and a BMD 50,000 fine.
AML/CTF and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: BMA penalties up to BMD 100,000 per contravention, among them the benchmark strip-down of regulated financial intermediary licenses; an aggravating breach enables civil forfeiture of defrauded funds.

Director personal liability: The relevant officer can be punished with 2 years' imprisonment and unlimited fine; a serious crime prevention order and travel ban may be issued under the Proceeds of Crime Act.
Wrongful Trading and Insolvent Distribution

Statutory sanction: A court may order restitution of the distributed sum, plus interest; fraudulent trading exposes the company to a fine up to BMD 50,000 and a compulsory winding-up order.

Director personal liability: Directors are personal guarantors of debts incurred under wrongful trading, can be disqualified for 15 years, and may face criminal imprisonment for fraudulent preference of one creditor over others.
