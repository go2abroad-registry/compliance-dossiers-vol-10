Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (Hamilton, Bermuda); registration is effected wholly through the Registry's statutory incorporation procedure, in practice conducted by a licensed corporate services provider.

Governing statute

Companies Act 1981 of Bermuda, as amended: Part II on incorporation, memorandum of association and bye-laws (including the statutory definitional provisions for 'exempted company'), together with the duties of the Registrar of Companies under that Act; Exchange Control Act 1972 and subsidiary regulations (non-resident designation through the Bermuda Monetary Authority); Business Licensing Act 2000 (Business Licence only when carrying on business in Bermuda); Economic Substance Act 2018 (periodic substance reporting for relevant activities); Personal Information Protection Act 2016 (privacy registration if processing the data of Bermuda residents).

Formation / constitutional filings

Form FE (Application for Registration of an Exempted Company) filed together with the executed Memorandum of Association and Bye-Laws; the Registrar issues the Certificate of Incorporation upon approval.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed by Bermuda law. An exempted company must have at least one issued share; the Memorandum must state an authorized share capital (commonly USD 12,000 or the equivalent for fee-banding), and no escrow, deposit, or capitalization requirement applies. The Bermuda Monetary Authority requires the company to be designated 'non-resident' where all shareholders are non-residents.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Office of the Tax Commissioner, Government of Bermuda — official tax administration portal: www.gov.bm; corporate registry and company/TIN records: Registrar of Companies, www.roc.gov.bm; Pillar Two/CIT administration under the Tax Administration Act 2024 is also run by the Ministry of Finance (Tax Commissioner). Bermuda is a self-governing British Overseas Territory: Her Majesty's Revenue & Customs (HMRC) has no administrative authority over Bermuda tax affairs.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981; the Registrar of Companies automatically issues a company registration number which functions as the statutory TIN. No VAT ID exists because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, and no VAT registration can be made domestically. For payroll tax, the company must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner via www.gov.bm as soon as it compensates Bermuda-based employees (no minimum payroll threshold). For an in-scope MNE group (consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750 million), a CIT TIN and e-filing account are obtained from the Ministry of Finance under the Tax Administration Act 2024 (for FY commencing on or after 1 January 2025). Non-financial entities such as a no-code website builder require no FATCA/CRS registration and no GIIN.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — exempted company status confers automatic statutory exemption from income tax, capital gains tax, corporation tax, and withholding/distribution taxes until at least 31 March 2035, pursuant to the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. Territorial scope: only Bermuda-source payroll tax and customs duty on physical imports can apply; digital export revenue is tax-neutral. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — exempted company status confers automatic statutory exemption from income tax, capital gains tax, corporation tax, and withholding/distribution taxes until at least 31 March 2035, pursuant to the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. Territorial scope: only Bermuda-source payroll tax and customs duty on physical imports can apply; digital export revenue is tax-neutral. Entity-level taxation is absent for SMBs; the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (15% rate) applies exclusively to constituent entities of large MNE groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, which is out of scope for an ISIC 6201 low-code builder operating below that threshold.

Available election forms

No election is required for the 1966 Tax Protection guarantee — it applies automatically to all exempted companies. Bermuda has no US-style check-the-box regime and no pass-through classification election under local law. For CIT Act 2023 in-scope groups only: election to apply transitional country-by-country reporting safe harbour, election for a local constituent entity to file on behalf of the group, and the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT) mechanism are available. No hybrid mismatch elections are relevant because there is no material income tax base for SMB exempted companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Bermuda levies no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. The only indirect-type duties are customs/import duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1971 (on physical goods entering Bermuda), plus the employer-side Payroll Tax Act 1995 which is a direct payroll levy, not an indirect tax. Cross-border digital services supplied by a Bermuda exempted company (no-code website builder) are outside all Bermuda indirect taxes. Separately, UK VAT may arise for B2C digital services supplied to UK consumers under UK law (non-established supplier threshold GBP 8,818 per annum) — this is a UK tax obligation, not a Bermuda one.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT turnover threshold and no indirect-tax registration mechanism in BMD. Customs duty is transactional per import entry; no general importer registration threshold applies to a pure digital service exporter. The employer payroll tax registration is triggered simply by engaging any Bermuda-based employee, regardless of the total remuneration level. All export proceeds from visual web template and low-code SaaS subscriptions are entirely outside the Bermuda customs and payroll base.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — with no VAT/GST in place, there is no 0% export rating to claim, no output VAT to discharge, and no input VAT pool to refund. Exports of no-code website builder services under ISIC 6201/NAICS 511210 are automatically outside Bermuda's taxation and are never subject to Bermuda duties. If the entity also exports physical hardware or media, the goods leave Bermuda without export duty; only an accurate customs export entry is needed. Consequently, the entire export revenue base is tax-neutral at the Bermuda level.

  • Pure digital services (no-code website builder): no mandatory documentation — no customs export declaration, no bill of lading, and no inward remittance certificate is required by Bermuda law
  • Physical goods exported from Bermuda (e.g., servers, pre-loaded media): Bermuda Customs Export Entry filed via the Bermuda Customs Management System, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Bill of Lading

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing mandate, digital stamping, or B2B transaction e-reporting exists in Bermuda. Physical imports/exports are cleared electronically through the Bermuda Customs Management System only for goods movements. Digital service invoices issued via Stripe Billing or Paddle require no government clearance, no API integration, and no sequence-number validation with the tax authority.

No VAT export zero-rating fraud or clawback can arise because no VAT system exists. The residual compliance risks are: (i) Customs Tariff Act 1971 penalties, including significant statutory fines and forfeiture, for misdeclared physical exports or imports; (ii) Payroll Tax Act 1995 and Tax Administration Act 2024 interest and late-filing penalties if Bermuda employees are engaged without registration; (iii) for groups above the EUR 750 million threshold, Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 penalties on understated top-up tax and late filing, applicable only from tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2025. The Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 prevents retrospective imposition of income/capital taxes on SMB exempted companies, and Bermuda has no exchange-control legislation, so there is no statutory FX repatriation deadline and no remittance-based clawback whatsoever.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and free convertibility on both current and capital accounts. Bermuda abolished exchange controls via the Exchange Control (Repeal) Act 2005; the Bermudian dollar (BMD) operates under a currency-board-style arrangement at a fixed 1:1 parity with the US dollar, backed by USD reserves. No BMA approval is required for inbound/outbound investment, dividend declarations, profit remittance, intercompany loans, or liquidation distributions. Residual practical hurdles are limited to bank-level internal enhanced due diligence on high-risk counterparty jurisdictions, CRS/FATCA transparency classification, and documentary evidence of economic substance where profit is repatriated from a tax-exempt entity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender, conversion, or retention restriction on export proceeds or any foreign-currency receipts. Receipts from payment service providers (Stripe Billing, Paddle) may be held indefinitely in USD, BMD, GBP, or EUR in any BMA-licensed account; no mandatory conversion into BMD exists.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the de facto central bank and integrated financial services regulator for Bermuda, a British Overseas Territory under HM Treasury oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

No exchange-control authorization is required. Inbound equity injections should be routed via SWIFT MT103 from the UK/global remitter using OECD BPM6 purpose code '611 – Direct Investment: Equity Capital'; the UK remitter's bank may additionally map this to a domestic outward-remittance purpose category such as 'Direct Investment' or 'Capital Contribution'. The Bermuda receiving bank (Butterfield/HSBC) will credit the USD/BMD capital account upon receipt of a board resolution, a share allotment form filed under the Companies Act 1981, and a source-of-funds statement from the remitting bank. The transfer should reference the entity's full legal name and account number; a scanned copy of the bank confirmation is sufficient for the BMA's ex-post supervisory framework.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 (or ISO 20022 pain.001 where the counterparty bank supports it) for vendor payments, contractor settlements, cloud-infrastructure charges, and profit repatriation. Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP 600) are legally permissible but commercially irrelevant for a no-code SaaS builder because no physical goods are traded; standby letters of credit may be used solely as performance security in enterprise contracts. Daily merchant-settlement flows from Stripe Billing and Paddle are received via Card/ACH/SEPA/SWIFT according to each MoR agreement, swept into the Bermuda operating account, and then disbursed via MT103 netting cycles to optimize USD/BMD parity conversion costs.Outright FX forward contract (vanilla) with a BMA-licensed bank (Butterfield, HSBC Bermuda) to hedge USD/GBP and USD/EUR net revenue conversion — permitted without BMA approval and subject to standard ISDA or local master agreement documentation.Cross-currency swap (USD to GBP or USD to EUR, fixed/floating legs) for cash-flow matching of cloud-infrastructure and contractor expenses — permissible under Bermuda law with board authorization and appropriate disclosure in financial statements.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) for exotic pairs (e.g., BRL, CNH, INR) through a non-Bermuda licensed dealer — permitted where BMD/USD parity makes onshore forwards unnecessary; the BMD/USD pair itself requires no hedge because of the fixed 1:1 currency-board peg.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda does not impose transfer pricing documentation requirements under local tax law because there is no corporate income tax. However, multinational groups with UK or other foreign operations must maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation if their foreign filing thresholds apply (e.g., UK Local File threshold typically applies where UK group revenue exceeds £200 million and related-party transactions exceed £20 million; intercompany transactions exceeding £5 million may require documentation). As a practical benchmark, prepare documentation where annual intercompany royalties or service fees exceed US$1 million.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to exempted companies carrying on relevant activities, including IP holding, distribution and service centers, financing and leasing, and pure holding business. A no-code website builder must demonstrate adequate economic substance in Bermuda if it is a relevant entity not tax resident elsewhere. If the company is tax resident in another jurisdiction, it must file an annual ESR Declaration confirming its foreign tax residence and provide a certificate of tax residence. Non-compliance carries substantial penalties and information exchange with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of BD$10,000, plus BD$10,000 per additional day of non-compliance; separate penalties up to BD$5,000 plus BD$500 per day for documentation failures. The Registrar may issue compliance notices and ultimately strike the company off.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit non-compliance may be personally liable for civil penalties and, in cases of false declarations, imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to BD$50,000.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BD$50,000; late filing penalties up to BD$500 per day; accounts may be frozen or payment processors terminated. The Minister may revoke tax assurance.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may face personal fines up to BD$500,000 and imprisonment up to 20 years for money laundering. Civil asset recovery and travel bans may be imposed.
Tax Evasion in Foreign Jurisdiction

Statutory sanction: HMRC may assess UK corporation tax at 25% on UK-attributable profits, plus late payment interest and penalties up to 100% of the tax liability for deliberate concealment. Follower notices and accelerated payment notices may be issued.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders may be prosecuted under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion; unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 5 years. Director disqualification orders and veil-piercing by HMRC are possible.
Late Filing / Registered Agent Failure

Statutory sanction: Late filing penalties of BD$100 plus BD$10 per day (maximum BD$1,000); company loses good standing and may be struck off after continued default.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for company debts incurred after striking off and may be disqualified from acting as directors in Bermuda.
