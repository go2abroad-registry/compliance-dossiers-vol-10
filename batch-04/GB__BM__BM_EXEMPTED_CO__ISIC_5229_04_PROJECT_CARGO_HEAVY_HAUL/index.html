Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Registrar of Companies (Minister of Finance) for incorporation and exempted-company tax protection; Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Department of Revenue (portal: https://www.droitax.gov.bm) for customs, payroll, and land tax only. Beneficial ownership is filed through BERMULA (https://www.bermula.bm), administered by the competent authority under the Beneficial Ownership Act 2017.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Engage a Bermuda-licensed Corporate Service Provider (CSP) and file incorporation documents with the Registrar of Companies; receive Certificate of Incorporation and Company Registration Number (CRN). Step 2 - Apply for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; this cements the 0% tax status guarantee until 31 March 2035. Step 3 - File beneficial ownership particulars on the BERMULA portal within 30 days of incorporation and maintain an annual BO return. Step 4 - File an annual Economic Substance Declaration (Form ES) confirming the entity is not a 'relevant activity' holder for cargo logistics (ISIC 5229 is outside the Bermuda ESA relevant-activity list). Step 5 - If importing physical machinery into Bermuda, obtain a Customs Importer Code from Bermuda Customs; otherwise no indirect-tax registration is triggered since the undertaking operates exclusively offshore. Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda Customs Duties only (no VAT, no GST, no sales and use tax; zero-rated indirect tax system for corporate services and offshore logistics)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not Applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Bermuda. Customs duty registration applies only when physically importing goods into Bermuda; import duties range from 0% to 25% of CIF value depending on commodity class, with most capital equipment for international business importable at zero or reduced rates. An exempted company undertaking offshore OOG project cargo freight that does not import goods into Bermuda has zero indirect-tax registration exposure.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not Applicable - Bermuda has no VAT/GST and therefore no export zero-rating mechanism. The 0% effective tax rate arises structurally from the Tax_Exempt_Zone status of Bermuda exempted companies: profits from out-of-gauge heavy-haul project cargo logistics services rendered to non-Bermudian counterparties are exempt from all Bermuda taxation regardless of invoicing, payment source, or export documentation. For OECD Pillar II groups, the 0% rate does not constitute a qualified foreign tax, and the group must evaluate top-up tax under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (QDMTT) if the EUR 750M global revenue threshold is met; otherwise, no compliance action is required.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar. For a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as non-resident for exchange control purposes, both current and capital accounts are effectively convertible: dividend and profit remittance, capital contributions, and trade payments may be routed through permitted currency accounts without prior BMA approval. Resident-designated entities face a more restrictive regime, but exempted companies are ordinarily classified as non-resident and therefore encounter minimal remittance hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender ratio or fixed conversion timeline applies to a Bermuda Exempted Company classified as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972. Export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in a permitted currency such as USD or BMD. Resident exporters, by contrast, are subject to BMA exchange control directions requiring prompt conversion of specified foreign currency receipts into BMD unless a BMA exemption has been granted.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Currency Act 1972 and Exchange Control Act 1972; the Bank of England exercises no direct foreign exchange or monetary mandate in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a SWIFT-enabled payment execution overlay for outbound settlements, but Low as a substitute for a regulated Bermuda bank account. Legal constraints: offshore digital EMIs are not licensed under Bermuda's Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1969, cannot open BMD-denominated accounts, and may be treated as unregulated counterparties by Bermuda banks for AML purposes. Local regulators require all deposit-taking activity to occur through a licensed Bermuda bank or an appropriately licensed branch.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a standard Bermuda Exempted Company outside the OECD GloBE and Pillar Two scope; 15% Corporate Income Tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies only to constituent entities of multinational enterprise groups with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more, effective for Bermuda fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%; Bermuda imposes no dividend withholding tax or remittance tax on distributions declared by a Bermuda Exempted Company, irrespective of shareholder residency and regardless of payment by wire, milestone distribution, or dividend set-off against intercompany balances.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Bermuda transfer pricing documentation threshold exists for out-of-scope exempted companies; mandatory Local File and Master File preparation is triggered for an in-scope MNE group under BEPS Action 13 at group consolidated revenue above EUR 50 million for the Master File and material related-party transactions for the Local File, measured in BMD-equivalent at a 1:1 USD peg; CITA 2023 and the GloBE rules additionally require reliable accounting data for all intercompany heavy haul and project cargo payments.

Economic substance scope: Applicable via the Economic Substance Act 2018 and the Economic Substance Amendment Regulations 2022. The Exempted Company must annually determine whether it performs a relevant activity: out-of-gauge project cargo heavy haul logistics can constitute Shipping where vessel or crew operations are managed, or Headquarters where senior group management is exercised in Bermuda; a vehicle that merely invoices overseas freight services with no Bermuda direction has no relevant activity and lodges a nil ESR declaration. Where a relevant activity exists, the entity must demonstrate physical presence in Bermuda, locally resident directors with active management, adequate employees, and Bermuda expenditure; the Minister of Finance automatically exchanges substance data with the ultimate parent jurisdiction, including the United Kingdom.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via False CIT Filing

Statutory sanction: Automatic late payment interest; civil penalty up to 200% of the evaded top-up tax; daily default penalties of BMD 250; the Bermuda Tax Authority may compound the offence and the Supreme Court of Bermuda may impose an unlimited fine.

Director personal liability: Officers cannot invoke the tax-exempt zone status as a defence once CITA 2023 applies; where documentary falsification is proved, directors face criminal prosecution, disqualification from Bermuda directorships, and civil recovery from personal assets where they knowingly signed false returns.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Enforcement

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to BMD 250,000 per violation, quarterly penalties of BMD 10,000 for missing declarations, and compulsory information exchange with HM Revenue and Customs; unabated contravention leads to strike-off and potential regulatory action against the registered agent.

Director personal liability: The Economic Substance Act permits enforcement directly against directors who caused the company's failure; directors may face Supreme Court enforcement measures including travel bans and passport surrender, personal fines up to BMD 100,000 for contempt, and disqualification for a period not exceeding five years.
Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism Breach

Statutory sanction: Upon conviction a body corporate is liable to an unlimited fine in the Supreme Court of Bermuda; the Bermuda AML regulator may impose civil penalties up to BMD 500,000 per breach of the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008; separate penalties apply for failures by senior management approval functions.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be criminally liable for money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997, carrying penalties up to 14 years imprisonment, confiscation of personal assets, and prohibition from holding directorships in regulated financial institutions.
Trade-Based Money Laundering via Heavy Cargo Over-Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Corporate conviction for money laundering results in an unlimited fine and a confiscation order measured by the benefit accruing from the unlawful conduct; trade sanctions authorities can freeze the Bermudian bank accounts and the Registrar of Companies can strike off the entity.

Director personal liability: Where the exempted company is used as a mere façade, the Bermuda court will pierce the corporate veil; directors face imprisonment up to 14 years, confiscation up to the entire project revenue, and international cooperation requests for surrender from the UK Serious Fraud Office or the National Crime Agency.
Ultimate Beneficial Owner and Annual Return Default

Statutory sanction: The Registrar of Companies strikes off the Bermuda Exempted Company after continued default; all contracts created after dissolution, including milestone wires and letter-of-credit assignments, become legally void, assets vest in the Crown, a penalty of BMD 500 per month of default accrues, and restoration requires a Supreme Court application with costs.

Director personal liability: Directors become personally liable for the company's debts contracted after dissolution; the corporate veil is pierced with respect to obligations entered into in the company's name; the Bermuda Registrar may file criminal complaints against directors giving rise to fines and imprisonment up to 12 months.
