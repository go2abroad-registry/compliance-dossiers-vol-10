Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (portal: tax.gov.bm); entity incorporation and annual compliance filings administered by the Bermuda Registrar of Companies (roc.gov.bm)

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated through the Registrar of Companies and obtains a unique company registry number. Since Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, no CIT TIN exists; a 10-digit Bermuda Tax Identification Number (TIN) is automatically assigned upon payroll-tax registration with the Office of the Tax Commissioner, which is required within 14 days of first employee remuneration. Annual government fees and the Economic Substance Declaration are settled via the Registrar's online portal by 31 January each year. Foreign companies may also apply for a TIN only if they acquire Bermuda payroll or customs obligations; otherwise no further procurement step is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist under Bermuda domestic law. An Exempted Company may apply to the Minister of Finance for a Tax Assurance Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing that no future Bermuda taxes (including income, capital, gains, or withholding taxes) will apply generally until 31 March 2035, or 2050 for qualifying long-term insurance and certain other undertakings. Classification elections for US federal and OECD purposes are determined exclusively in the beneficial owner's residence jurisdiction; a wholly foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is generally treated as a per se foreign corporation non-filer in the US absent a Section 897-type investment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; indirect-type levies are limited to customs import duties on tangible goods and employer/employee payroll taxes on Bermuda-sourced employment income

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold applies because no VAT regime exists. Payroll-tax registration is mandatory from the first BMD 1 of gross remuneration paid to a Bermuda-resident employee; employer tax bands range from 0% to 5.5% on payroll exceeding BMD 750,000 per annum, with employee contributions up to 4%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, services tax, export duty, or digital-services tax on cloud vulnerability scanning and penetration testing SaaS. Intangible cybersecurity services supplied from Bermuda are entirely outside Bermuda indirect-tax scope, so no zero-rating election, VAT refund procedure, or export-tax clearance is required. Customs import duty may apply only to physical test instruments or hardware shipped into Bermuda.

  • No customs export declaration required for intangible SaaS exports; where physical equipment is exported, retain the Bermuda Customs Export Declaration, commercial invoice, and bill of lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate not required - Bermuda imposes no exchange-control or certified-inflow rules

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API reporting obligation, digital stamping, or SAF-T/continuous transaction control framework is in force for Bermuda-exempted entity exporters; commercial invoicing is unregulated except for ordinary anti-money-laundering record-keeping requirements.

There is no VAT clawback or export-zero-rating penalty regime in Bermuda. Fraudulent declarations, tax misrepresentations, or money-laundering-connected invoicing are prosecuted under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Misuse of Tax Information framework; penalties include fines up to BMD 50,000 per offence, imprisonment up to 5 years, and asset forfeiture. Under the Economic Substance Act 2018, an Exempted Company deriving IP-linked SaaS income must file an annual economic-substance declaration; failure to satisfy the applicable substance test can trigger fines up to BMD 250,000, mandatory information exchange with UK/OECD authorities, and potential strike-off. The 180-day repatriation metric is not a Bermuda statutory deadline but reflects UK CFC and OECD transfer-pricing documentation expectations applicable to the parent shareholder; non-compliance there is assessed by the shareholder's home jurisdiction, not Bermuda.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is freely convertible and legally anchored at a fixed 1:1 peg with the United States Dollar (USD), and USD notes are accepted as legal tender in Bermuda. Capital account and current account transactions are fully liberalized; there is no BMA exchange-control approval for dividend remittance, interest and loan repayments, capital disinvestment, or profit repatriation from an Exempted Company. Bermudian banks may freely hold and settle BMD, USD, GBP and EUR; convertibility risk is effectively nil for USD, and the overall investability regime is rated as full convertibility for a cybersecurity SaaS exporter.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender rules and no mandatory conversion ratios. Foreign-currency proceeds from SaaS subscriptions and export service receipts may be retained indefinitely in any currency, freely converted at market rates through licensed banks, or applied directly to offshore disbursements without BMA authorization. Residual restrictions under the Exchange Control Act 1972 are disapplied to exempted companies, so receipt retention and repatriation are unrestricted.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and charged with banking supervision under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; the Bank of England is the ultimate sovereign monetary authority for the British Overseas Territory, but foreign exchange administration and licensing supervision are fully delegated to the BMA in Bermuda.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory local file or master file for standalone exempted companies below the CIT threshold. MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more must prepare OECD-aligned master file and local file covering all Bermuda intercompany transactions; the Bermuda CIT regulations additionally require a country-by-country report notification of the UPE jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 require every relevant activity—including 'intellectual property business' and 'providing services to related parties'—to have adequate people, premises, and expenditure in Bermuda. A cybersecurity SaaS entity developing proprietary scanning technology and earning third-party license fees is scrutinized as an IP business; if the same licenses are provided to a UK or EU group entity, the related-party services limb applies. Full-time employees, physical servers, and board meetings in Bermuda constitute the CIGA; outsourcing software development entirely to a third party abroad inverts CIGA and triggers an automatic exchange of information with the foreign tax authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Misrepresentation (CITA 2023 and Income Tax Acts)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of BD$10,000 plus BD$1,000 per day for continuing failure; accuracy penalty of 5% of the under-declared amount; statutory interest on the top-up tax; and automatic exchange of information with the foreign jurisdiction of the ultimate parent.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false returns are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the unpaid tax; the Supreme Court may issue travel bans, disqualification from acting as a director for up to 15 years, and a custodial sentence of up to 5 years.
Shell Company / Conduit Arrangement (ESR 2018)

Statutory sanction: Up to BD$250,000 penalty, re-characterization of the entity as a non-resident for CIT purposes, mandatory exchange of information to the UK or EU parent jurisdiction, and potential denial of the tax-exemption guarantee for the relevant activity.

Director personal liability: Personal culpability attaches to directors who signed the ESR declaration; they face civil restitution of the BD$250,000 penalty, a UK or Bermuda travel ban until the penalty is satisfied, and imprisonment of up to 2 years for knowingly furnishing false information.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Register Offence (Companies and Limited Liability Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Amendment Act 2017)

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to BD$10,000 per default; the company may be struck off the register; the Financial Sanctions Implementation Unit may add secondary sanctions if any beneficial owner appears on a sanctions list.

Director personal liability: Each director is separately liable on summary conviction to a fine of up to BD$25,000 and imprisonment of up to 2 years; a director of a struck-off company risks civil claims from creditors for trading while dissolved.
Persistent Non-Filing / Striking Off (Companies Act 1981)

Statutory sanction: Registrar strikes the company off the register; all assets vest in the Crown; restoration is possible only by court order with payment of all arrears, penalties, and the strikeback fee.

Director personal liability: Directors continue to be personally liable for the company's debts contracted after the strike-off; the court may order civil restitution, prohibit travel until the assets are traced, and, in cases of concealment, impose a custodial sentence.
