Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance - Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll and miscellaneous taxes) and the Bermuda Registrar of Companies (incorporation registry). Official portal: https://www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities form a Bermuda Exempted Company by registering with the Registrar of Companies; the Certificate of Incorporation number serves as the statutory company identifier. No separate income tax TIN is issued because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax. If the company has Bermuda-sourced employees, it must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain a payroll tax account number. For customs transactions, importers/exporters must engage a licensed customs broker and register with the Bermuda Customs Department. No VAT ID is obtainable or required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election mechanism. A Bermuda Exempted Company automatically qualifies for tax exemption under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (protection from future Bermuda taxes until 2035). It may voluntarily elect to file economic substance returns if the relevant activity is undertaken, and it may make foreign entity classifications (e.g., US Form 8832 check-the-box) for tax purposes in jurisdictions that permit such elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT/GST/Sales Tax; Bermuda applies customs duties on imports and stamp duty on certain instruments

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no indirect tax registration threshold exists. Entities importing goods into Bermuda must submit customs declarations and a customs broker account is required for each import entry; no VAT threshold is prescribed.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda operates no VAT/GST regime, so no export zero-rating claim mechanism exists. Goods exported from Bermuda are exempt from any Bermuda indirect tax by default, and no refundable input tax arises for exporters.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Entry (electronic/paper C1 declaration)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Export Permit or License (only for controlled or prohibited goods)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (only where a payment instrument is used for customs valuation)

No real-time fiscal clearance, electronic invoicing, API stamping, or digital signature mandate exists. Commercial invoices are governed by general commercial law. Import and export declarations are lodged through the Bermuda Customs online automated system (Bermuda Automated Customs System) without fiscal stamping.

No VAT refund or export zero-rating clawback regime applies in Bermuda. However, customs fraud (false declarations, understatement of value, misdescription) is penalised under the Customs Act 2003 with fines up to BMD 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to two years, together with potential forfeiture of goods. A Bermuda Exempted Company that breaches economic substance requirements may be liable for penalties up to BMD 100,000, and the Registrar of Companies may strike the company off the register, resulting in loss of limited liability and tax-exempt status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BMA operates a fully liberalized current account and a substantially deregulated capital account. A Bermuda Exempted Company designated as 'non-resident' under the Exchange Control regime enjoys full currency convertibility, holds and settles in USD, BMD, GBP, and EUR without transaction-level BMA approval, and benefits from a blanket General Permission for ongoing treasury flows. Dividend remittance, interest payments, and capital repatriation to the UK parent are unrestricted in law and are not subject to Bermudian withholding taxes. The residual practical hurdle is documentary: authorized banks must file exchange control returns with the BMA on share subscriptions and any inward remittance exceeding USD 100,000, and will require the company's certificate of non-resident status to be produced at account opening. The BMD peg to USD, combined with US Dollar legal-tender equivalence in the local market, effectively eliminates USD/BMD conversion friction and narrows the treasury FX focus to GBP and EUR exposures generated by consumer payments from Stripe and Shopify.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds. The Exchange Control General Permission regime exempts non-resident Exempted Companies from any duty to convert foreign-currency export receipts into BMD; all proceeds from the cross-border retail sale of smart IoT hardware may be retained indefinitely in USD, GBP, or EUR in offshore or onshore accounts. Authorized banks are required to submit aggregate monthly exchange control statistics to the BMA, but individual transaction reporting is limited to inward capital subscriptions rather than trade receivables, and no timeframe exists within which export receipts must be repatriated or exchanged.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, with supervisory jurisdiction over all domestic authorized banks and currency issuance for the Bermuda Dollar (BMD), which is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-Low. Bermuda maintains no Electronic Money Institution licensing framework; the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 covers only digital-asset custodians and exchanges. Offshore UK/FCA-regulated EMIs and US Money Transmitter platforms (Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) are legally usable in Bermuda solely as supplementary settlement conduits for aggregating Stripe and Shopify Payments receipts, but they are not permitted to establish a Bermudian branch, hold BMD, or provide local debit/clearing infrastructure. A Bermudian Tier-1 bank will treat an EMI flood-in transfer as an unregulated third-party remittance, triggering enhanced due diligence, longer holding periods, and a de-risking review if EMI-sourced volume exceeds 30% of annual treasury throughput. Primary operating liquidity must remain in a licensed Bermudian bank to satisfy solvency look-through tests applied by the auditors and the BMA.
