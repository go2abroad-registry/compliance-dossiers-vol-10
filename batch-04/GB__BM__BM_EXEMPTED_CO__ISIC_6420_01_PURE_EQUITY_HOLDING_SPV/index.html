Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (tax administration); Registrar of Companies, Government of Bermuda (company registry and portal); HM Revenue & Customs of the United Kingdom has no jurisdiction over Bermuda, which is a separate British Overseas Territory outside the UK VAT and direct tax net

E-government filing portal

Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax; accordingly no income-tax TIN is issued to a Bermuda Exempted Company. The entity obtains its Certificate of Incorporation under the Companies Act 1981 and a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which guarantees exemption from all Bermudian taxes on profits, income, dividends, capital gains, and appreciation until 31 March 2035. A Bermuda Business Number (BBN) is procured through the Registrar of Companies for official filings. No minimum capital is required and 100% non-resident ownership is permitted; at least one director (individual or corporate) is required and no local residency condition applies.

Available election forms

For group tax planning, the SPV may be classified through U.S. Form 8832 as either a corporation or a disregarded entity for U.S. federal tax purposes, and OECD/G20 BEPS-compliant hybrid-mismatch analyses may be documented to prevent double-dip outcomes in investor jurisdictions; such elections have no effect on the Bermudian tax status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST regime in Bermuda; the only indirect-style levy is customs duty imposed at importation

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK VAT legislation does not extend to Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory, and the Bermuda Exempted Company holds no UK VAT nexus for its passive equity holding function.

Not applicable: Bermuda has no VAT, therefore no 0% export VAT rate exists to be claimed for goods or offshore services. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar is pegged to the US dollar at par and the BMA imposes no general exchange controls on current or capital account transactions. Profits, dividends, and invested capital may be freely repatriated in any major currency, subject only to normal banking due diligence and the Economic Substance Act documentation requirements. There is no distinction between current and capital account restrictiveness; the regime is fully open for a tax-exempted pure equity holding company.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists. Bermuda has no mandatory conversion ratio and no foreign exchange repatriation timeline for export receipts, because the jurisdiction operates no foreign exchange control regime.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium - Offshore digital EMI accounts are legally permissible for a Bermuda exempted company only where the EMI holds a relevant licence recognized by the BMA or operates on a passported basis from a recognized jurisdiction. Most pure digital-only EMIs without a physical Bermudian presence or a formal BMA licence will be rejected for treasury-tier use. A licensed EMI with compliant onboarding and clear beneficial ownership disclosure may be acceptable for limited transaction volumes, but core liquidity should reside with a Tier 1 commercial bank.

Compliant inbound transfers should be executed over SWIFT MT103 from the parent company's bank account with a clear purpose code corresponding to equity capital or additional paid-in capital.
