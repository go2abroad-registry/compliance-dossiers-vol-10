Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance (government portal: www.gov.bm); corporate registration and Economic Substance Declaration filings via the Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate as an exempted company under the Companies Act 1981 through a licensed Bermuda Corporate Service Provider (CSP) and maintain a registered office in Bermuda. Step 2: Obtain the Registrar of Companies file number and certificate of incorporation. Step 3: Register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain a Tax Registration Number (TRN) if hiring staff; the TRN is the statutory taxpayer identifier (no separate corporate income tax ID exists for tax-exempt companies). Step 4: If part of an MNE group with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million in at least two of the preceding four fiscal years, register as a Corporate Income Tax (CIT) payer under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 within 60 days of entering the CIT net; otherwise no CIT registration is required. Step 5: File the annual Economic Substance Declaration through the CSP with the Registrar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - Bermuda exempted companies are not subject to corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax; no VAT/GST applies. A 15% CIT (a GloBE QDMTT) applies only to Bermuda business units of large MNE groups (EUR 750M+ group revenue); a standalone SME SaaS exporter remains permanently outside CIT scope but must comply with the Economic Substance Act 2018 if engaged in relevant activities.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election is available; exempted companies are juridical persons at entity level. Under the CITA 2023 (only when in scope): election for the USD 1 million Substance-Based Income Exclusion, the Economic Transition Adjustment (Option A: 65% tax-credit reduction; Option B: deduction), net-CIT-loss transfer elections, and GloBE de minimis / immaterial entity election where applicable. For ESR, a pure equity-holding parent may elect to rely on reduced substance requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general indirect tax regime (no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax); customs duties are confined to physical importation of goods

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no indirect tax registration threshold exists in Bermuda for digital services or SaaS subscriptions (Bermudian dollar pegged 1:1 to USD)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - as Bermuda levies no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating mechanism or refund procedure to invoke; cross-border SaaS SEO/analytics subscriptions are simply outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxation, and no Bermudian tax will be charged on invoices to foreign customers.

  • Not required for digital SaaS exports (no Bermudian customs export declaration applies to intangibles)
  • Physical goods exports (if any) require Bermuda Customs export declaration and standard commercial documentation

No VAT/export-refund clawback is possible as no indirect tax is imposed. Risk mitigation centers on: (i) Economic Substance Act 2018 - non-filing or false declaration: fines up to USD 10,000 per offence and potential striking-off; (ii) CITA 2023 - late filing penalty of BMD 250 per month (capped at BMD 2,500), inaccurate return penalties up to BMD 10,000, and retrospective assessment up to 6 years (10 years for fraud); (iii) payroll tax penalties up to 10% of unpaid tax plus interest; (iv) criminal liability for fraudulent misrepresentation to the Tax Commissioner. Maintain substance records, board minutes, and transfer-pricing documentation to defend tax-exempt status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BMD is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 parity with USD. Current-account transactions (SaaS subscription fees, SEO analytics licence revenues, intercompany service payments, and royalty streams) are unrestricted and may be settled in USD, BMD, GBP, or EUR without BMA prior approval. Capital-account items (share capital subscription, shareholder loans, capital reductions, and dividend remittances) are freely permitted for a Bermuda Exempted Company designated non-resident under the Exchange Control Act; the BMA's General Permissions eliminate transaction-by-transaction filing. Profit-remittance hurdles are de minimis: Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or management fees paid by an exempted company, and no capital gains tax applies on disposal of shares. The only substantive hurdles are bank-level AML/economic-substance reviews and, at the UK parent level, the UK CTA 2010 Part 9A CFC rules and OECD Pillar Two Income Inclusion Rule, which are covered in the tax-compliance stages. USD is the de facto functional treasury currency of the operating entity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender of export proceeds, no minimum conversion ratio, no mandatory repatriation window, and no retention-floor requirements for foreign currency earned by a non-resident exempted company. The Company may retain 100% of its USD/GBP/EUR export earnings in any jurisdiction and convert into BMD or USD at its own discretion. The only administrative obligation is the authorised bank's annual exchange-control return to the BMA summarising non-resident customer balances.

Central bank supervision: The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, is the central bank, currency issuing authority, and sole exchange control administrator under the Exchange Control Act 1972 as extended to Bermuda. The BMA issues the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) and maintains an effective currency-board-style peg of BMD 1.00 = USD 1.00. All licensed banks in Bermuda operate as BMA-appointed Authorized Dealers for exchange control administration.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda transfer pricing legislation applies to exempted companies outside a CIT-in-scope MNE group. For groups subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, documentation is mandatory when the group has consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 1.0 billion); local file and master file must follow OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2018 require any exempted company carrying on a 'relevant activity' to maintain substantial economic presence in Bermuda. Measures include physical premises, a minimum number of full-time employees, local board meetings, and adequate operating expenditure. For an SEO data crawler and keyword analytics SaaS, the activity is likely a relevant service or IP business unless the company proves foreign tax residency. A pure shell with no functional implementation of the SEO tool will fail the ESR test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023: fine up to BMD 100,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; civil penalty for tax understated plus interest and penalty equal to 100% of the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who signed or caused the false return can be personally liable for the civil penalty; travel bans, confiscation orders, and disqualification from acting as directors may be imposed.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations penalties: immediate civil penalty of up to BMD 20,000 plus BMD 5,000 per day for continued non-compliance; powers to strike off the company, revoke the exempted licence, and refer the matter for criminal enforcement.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined and disqualified; they are also exposed to common law liability for wrongful trading if the company continues to operate without legal substance and defaults.
Money Laundering (AML)

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) provisions of Bermuda law: criminal fine, confiscation order, and imprisonment up to 20 years; corporate fines and forfeiture of assets.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly control the entity without AML procedures are subject to personal criminal liability, travel restrictions, and freezing of personal assets.
Unauthorised Bermuda Domestic Business

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Companies Act penalties: fine per default plus daily continuation penalties; potential declaration that the exempted status is void and the company is subject to full Bermuda tax filings.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for trading without a licence; the corporate veil can be pierced, making directors jointly and severally liable for the company's debts.
