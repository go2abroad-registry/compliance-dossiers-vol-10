Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Registrar of Companies, Bermuda Department of Corporate and Regulatory Services (Ministry of Finance) via the Bermuda Companies Register portal, supplemented by the Office of the Tax Commissioner (Payroll Tax only) and Bermuda Customs Department (import duty only). Bermuda has no national income tax authority and issues no TIN; the statutory identity is the Bermuda Company Registration Number (BCN).

E-government filing portal

Foreign founders incorporate through a licensed Bermuda Corporate Service Provider; the Registrar issues a Certificate of Incorporation with a unique company registration number within 1-2 business days. No TIN/VAT ID is issued because Bermuda levies no income tax and no VAT/GST. A Payroll Tax employer account with the Office of the Tax Commissioner is triggered only upon hiring Bermuda-resident employees; a Customs importer code is required only for physical imports. For SaaS operations via Stripe and ACH, only the incorporation certificate, registered office certificate, and UBO filings are reciprocity-standard.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — offshore exempted company profits, capital gains, and dividends are exempt from all Bermudian income, corporation, and capital taxes; annual government fee is the only fiscal charge.

Available election forms

Domestic law: no check-the-box regime exists. US check-the-box: Bermuda corporations are on the default per se corporation list, so no US disreg-entity election is permissible. Available elections: (i) election for permit-company status under Companies Act 1981; (ii) election of financial year-end and reporting currency; (iii) election to file consolidated Economic Substance Return (Form E) across a group; (iv) under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018, an entity carrying out a relevant activity (including financing/leasing, but not pure SaaS) must elect and evidence substance. Hybrid mismatch: because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, hybrid mismatch outcomes do not crystallize domestically, but UK CTA 2010 Part 6A and OECD BEPS Action 2 rules may apply to UK-linked financing structures involving the Bermuda entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. The sole indirect fiscal levy is import customs duty under the Bermuda Customs Tariff Act 1970, which has no application to digital SaaS cross-border supplies.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Employers register for Payroll Tax at the first hire of a Bermuda-resident employee (combined employer-employee rate band 4.75%-8.25% of remuneration). Commercial importers require a Bermuda Customs Department registration code for duty assessment on physical goods; digital revenue is outside any indirect-tax registration trigger.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — as a Tax_Exempt_Zone jurisdiction, Bermuda has no VAT/GST mechanism and therefore no export zero-rating or refund scheme. The Bermuda Exempted Company realizes its fiscal benefit through absolute territorial exemption of offshore income rather than exporter refunds. For UK/EU sales, the Bermuda supplier is treated as outside the UK VAT territory; UK VAT (20%) on B2C digital services is collected via UK OSS/VAT MOSS only if the company elects voluntary registration, and no input VAT refund is claimable in Bermuda because none exists.

The Bermuda Customs Management System (BCMS) enables electronic import declarations solely for physical goods; no API tax-clearance gateway exists for software or SaaS invoices.

No VAT clawback exists in Bermuda because no export zero-rating mechanism applies. The enforceable exposures are: (i) late or false Annual Economic Substance Filing (Form E) — up to BD$100,000 plus BD$10,000 per day for continuing default, and director penalties or strike-off; (ii) annual exempted-company government fee arrears attracting incremental fees and potential deregistration; (iii) a UK OSS misuse or fraudulent UK VAT credit claim attracts UK Penalty Regime (up to 100% of tax per FA 2021 Sch 16) and potential personal liability; (iv) US controlling shareholders failing Form 5471 reporting face US penalties up to US$10,000 per form per year; (v) misclassification of the Bermuda entity as a 'tax-exempt shell' in banking AML reviews triggers enhanced due diligence and potential de-risking, which is a commercial penalty equivalent.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar and is freely convertible for non-resident designated accounts and Exempted Companies. Bermuda maintains a two-tier exchange control regime: resident accounts require BMA permission for capital outflows, while non-resident accounts enjoy unrestricted current and capital convertibility. Profit remittance hurdles for a Bermuda Exempted Company are low: dividends require a solvency statement by the directors, compliance with the Companies Act 1981, and, where the payee is a resident account, an exchange-control final permission. No withholding tax is imposed on dividends, interest, royalties, or capital gains, but anti-money-laundering clearance and formal board authorisation are required for all outward distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion requirement applies to export receipts realised by a non-resident designated Bermuda Exempted Company. Export invoices may be settled in BMD, USD, or any freely convertible currency and may be retained offshore indefinitely. Where receipts are credited to a resident operating account in Bermuda, an exchange-control report must be filed with an authorised bank, and the BMA may require a declaration of source and final destination for statistical purposes. No specified conversion ratio or mandatory surrender timeline is imposed for modern SaaS/PaaS export revenue under the Exchange Control Act 1952, provided the monetary transactions exceed the BMA de minimis reporting threshold and are disclosed in the annual exchange-control return.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the central bank and exchange control authority under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1952, the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and the Investment Business Act 2003; the UK Financial Conduct Authority retains prudential oversight relevance only for UK-linked passported activities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Cross-border digital EMI viability is Medium with material legal constraints. Offshore EMI providers that have not established a branch, subsidiary, or formal BMA-licensed money service business presence in Bermuda cannot open local BMD accounts or act as a Bermuda settlement bank. EMIs lawfully passporting in the EEA/UK may service the entity on a cross-border basis for non-Bermudian receipts, but BMA-regulated banks will not rely solely on an unlicensed EMI for AML/CTF compliance. Recommended structure: engage a FCA-authorised EMI (e.g., numbered payment-institution corridor) for Stripe settlement aggregation and use Butterfield or Clarien as the ultimate BMD/USD clearing bank; obtain a BMA exemption letter where the EMI activity is treated as auxiliary to the SaaS treasury function.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Bermuda Exempted Companies outside the scope of the Bermuda corporate income tax regime; 15% only for constituent entities of multinational groups with consolidated global revenue exceeding EUR 750m under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no statutory withholding tax on dividend distributions made by a Bermuda Exempted Company to any shareholder.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within six months after the end of the entity's financial year. A common default is a 31 December year-end with a filing deadline of 30 June; the declaration must be filed before the Registrar's annual closure.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The CIT return is due within 12 months after the fiscal year-end. Estimated tax instalments are payable quarterly during the year, with the final balance due upon filing the return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory transfer-pricing documentation threshold applies to out-of-scope Bermuda Exempted Companies. For in-scope MNE groups subject to the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, contemporaneous documentation is required where the group has worldwide revenue of at least EUR 750m (approximately BMD 750m) and Bermuda connected-party transactions exceed BMD 1m in a fiscal year. The threshold is denominated in BMD, with 1 BMD pegged to 1 USD.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) are fully applicable to every Bermuda Exempted Company. A PropTech SaaS entity deriving revenue from software licences, cloud solutions or real-estate data services will typically be classified as an Intellectual Property Business or a Distribution & Service Business. The entity must demonstrate in each financial year: (i) management and control exercised in Bermuda; (ii) a physical office in Bermuda; (iii) an adequate and qualified employee headcount in Bermuda; and (iv) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda. Pure holding and shareholder activities remain subject to a lighter substance test, but no exemption is available for an active operating digital platform.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Evasion / False ESR Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations, the first default is subject to a civil penalty of BMD 25,000; continued default attracts BMD 50,000 plus daily penalties up to BMD 500 per day. Knowingly filing a false declaration is punishable by a fine up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment of the responsible officers for up to 5 years. The Registrar may strike off the company, arrange its dissolution through the Minister, and exchange information with foreign tax authorities pursuant to Bermuda's Tax Information Exchange Agreements.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors and beneficial owners personally liable for unpaid penalties and third-party losses. Directors face disqualification, travel bans, asset freezes and imprisonment up to 5 years for knowingly certifying false economic substance.
Aggressive Cross-Border Tax Evasion / Tax Abuse

Statutory sanction: The foreign jurisdiction may apply its general anti-avoidance rules, CFC rules, transfer-pricing adjustments and anti-hybrid rules, recharacterising payments, denying deductions and imposing penalties up to 100% of the tax due plus interest. If the group is within the Bermuda CIT threshold, Bermuda will impose 15% CIT on the recharacterised Bermuda income and can penalise false filings. Foreign asset-freeze orders and criminal prosecution may follow under bilateral mutual legal assistance arrangements.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be personally liable for civil restitution, denial of travel, asset seizure and imprisonment under money-laundering statutes if they knowingly sign false transfer-pricing documentation or distribute artificially structured profits.
AML/CFT and Shell Company Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations and Financial Sanctions Enforcement Unit powers, failure to conduct due diligence or to maintain AML/CFT governance attracts penalties up to BMD 100,000 and criminal fines. The Registrar may refuse annual certification and the Supreme Court may order administrative dissolution or a prohibition order against the directors.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and beneficial owners are personally exposed to AML enforcement. Banks may terminate accounts, file suspicious activity reports, freeze assets and trigger travel alerts. Individuals may be prosecuted for money-laundering and, upon conviction, sentenced to imprisonment without the protection of the corporate form.
Corporate Income Tax Filing and Payment Default

Statutory sanction: Late filing attracts 5% of the tax due for each month, up to a maximum of 25%; late payment interest is charged at 3% above the Bermuda central bank discount rate. The Tax Commissioner may issue an estimated assessment, seize assets, and register a statutory charge. Knowingly omitting Bermuda taxable income may expose the company to a penalty of up to 100% of the tax due and administrative fines up to BMD 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid employee withholdings and CIT where they knowingly participate in non-payment. Courts may grant personal civil restitution, travel bans and asset seizures after proving that the director directed or ignored unlawful filing failures. Imprisonment of up to 5 years applies for fraudulent evasion or knowingly false returns.
