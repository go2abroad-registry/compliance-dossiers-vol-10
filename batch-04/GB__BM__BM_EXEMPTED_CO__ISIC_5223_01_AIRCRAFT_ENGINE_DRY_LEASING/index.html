Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance (Bermuda) and Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance. E-government filing portal

For the Bermuda Exempted Company: (1) engage a licensed Bermuda resident registered agent to file incorporation forms under the Companies Act 1981 with the Registrar of Companies; (2) obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and company register extracts; (3) apply to the Minister of Finance for a Section 6 Tax Exemption Certificate (statutory assurance against future Bermuda taxes, up to 50 years, subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope MNE groups); (4) register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax if any Bermuda-sourced payroll exists; (5) where the entity is a member of an in-scope MNE group (consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750m), register for Corporate Income Tax and obtain a CIT registration number; no VAT ID exists because Bermuda does not operate a VAT/GST regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda Exempted Company - non-resident owned, limited liability, zero-tax under Section 6; no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax outside the new 15% Corporate Income Tax Act 2023/QDMTT for in-scope MNE groups; territorial scope is substance-driven and asset-based; payroll tax and customs duty apply as non-income charges).

Available election forms

(a) Section 6 Companies Act 1981 election for a Tax Exemption Certificate (up to 50 years); (b) Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 QDMTT elections, GloBE safe-harbour elections, and loss-allocation elections; (c) no check-the-box regime in Bermuda, but the entity may elect US federal pass-through treatment (disregarded entity or partnership) under US Treasury check-the-box rules and may be treated as fiscally transparent for UK CFC purposes; (d) hybrid mismatch outcomes are neutral because Bermuda imposes no income tax on non-CIT entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax (Bermuda levies customs duty on imports under the Customs Tariff Act 1971 and employer payroll tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995; UK VAT reverse charge may apply to supplies of aircraft engine leasing services received by UK businesses).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold applicable (regime absent). Payroll tax: registration required for any employer with Bermuda-source remuneration (rates approximately 8%-16%). Customs duty: import-entry based. CIT/QDMTT under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023: in-scope MNE group threshold of EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 810 million) consolidated annual revenue in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda has no VAT/GST and therefore no export zero-rating or refund mechanism. For income tax purposes, the aircraft engine dry-leasing income remains outside the Bermuda CIT base only where the entity is not a member of an in-scope MNE group; in-scope members are subject to 15% CIT/QDMTT from 1 January 2025 notwithstanding any Section 6 exemption. A UK recipient of the leasing service is generally liable for UK VAT under the reverse charge (B2B place-of-supply rules), with no Bermuda VAT registration consequence.

No mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping regime in Bermuda. Tax filings (CIT/GloBE Information Return, payroll tax) are submitted electronically through the Office of the Tax Commissioner portal; taxpayers must preserve supporting electronic records for the statutory retention period (generally seven years).

Bermuda imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day benchmark is adopted as prudent treasury policy and UK CFC/substance alignment. The Tax Commissioner may reopen assessments, revoke Section 6 exemption certificates obtained on false or misleading declarations, and assess default penalties and interest under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, including fixed monetary penalties and daily default penalties for late registration/filing, interest on unpaid tax, criminal sanctions for knowing false statements, and clawback of any QDMTT offsets or refunds upon audit adjustment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully liberalised regime. The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar and USD circulates freely alongside BMD. A Bermuda Exempted Company is classified as non-resident for exchange-control purposes; therefore both current-account transactions (lease rentals, operating expenses) and capital-account transactions (equity injection, loan repayment, dividend and profit remittance) are freely convertible with no BMA approval or repatriation permit required. Remittance hurdles are limited to routine bank-level AML verification. Bermuda imposes zero withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or rental payments to non-residents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Bermuda maintains no mandatory export-proceeds surrender requirement, no statutory conversion timeline, and no minimum conversion ratio. Lease-rental receipts and other export proceeds may be retained in USD or any freely convertible currency in a BMA-licensed bank or in foreign accounts. Residual powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972 apply only to Bermuda-resident persons and not to an Exempted Company.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - the integrated financial regulator and central monetary authority of Bermuda, responsible for currency issuance, supervision of banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and residual administration of the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

LOW-MEDIUM as a primary treasury rail; MEDIUM only as an ancillary payment channel. Bermuda does not issue electronic-money-institution (EMI) licences; pure payment and e-money services are not a regulated category under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and holding third-party funds without a deposit-taking licence is unlawful. An offshore EMI (e.g., an FCA-regulated UK EMI) may collect and pay funds for a Bermuda entity without a Bermuda licence, but balances are contractual e-money rather than protected bank deposits, and EMIs are operationally unsuitable for seven-figure USD engine disbursements and lease-rental flows. Recommended use is limited to low-value operational payments (legal fees, subscriptions, registry charges) and as a secondary FX conversion gateway.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant path: the UK parent or shareholder instructs its UK bank to send a USD SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for same-bank interbranch transfers) to the Bermuda entity's designated USD account at HSBC Bank Bermuda or Butterfield. USD clearing is executed over Fedwire or CHIPS via the Bermuda bank's US correspondent (e.g., HSBC Bank USA for HSBC Bermuda; BNY Mellon or Citibank for Butterfield). The wire narrative must state the nature and purpose of the remittance; where the US correspondent requires a payment-purpose code, use IMF Balance of Payments code 3105 (equity capital) or, if permitted, the FFIEC category for direct-investment intercompany capital; the phrase 'equity injection - share subscription' should appear in SWIFT field 70. Verification package: board resolution authorising the capital increase, share allotment or transfer forms, updated certificate of good standing, and source-of-funds statements must be uploaded to the bank's compliance portal before or immediately after the wire; the bank may apply a pre-verification hold of one to two business days on first-time inbound transfers. Bermuda does not use IBAN; the beneficiary is identified by account number and SWIFT BIC.For engine acquisitions, use (i) commercial letters of credit (SWIFT MT700/MT707 series) opened by the Bermuda company in favour of the OEM with documents against payment, or (ii) an aviation escrow account at Butterfield or HSBC where the purchase price is released against the seller's engine transfer documents and bill of sale. All outbound transfers are subject to recipient sanctions screening; no BMA or exchange-control approval is required for any outbound payment of an Exempted Company. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for out-of-scope Bermuda Exempted Companies (no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or corporation tax). A 15% global minimum top-up tax applies only where the entity is a Bermuda constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least 2 of the 4 preceding fiscal years, under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA), effective for fiscal periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no dividend withholding tax and no income tax on distributions by an Exempted Company; the 1966 tax-protection covenant confirms exemption from all present and future Bermuda taxes on dividends until 31 March 2035. A UK parent's receipt of the dividend is taxed or exempted solely under UK CTA 2009 and CTA 2010 rules, not under Bermuda law.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

CIT Return: within 15 months after the end of the reporting period (first period: 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025, due 31 March 2027). CIT Information Return for out-of-scope entities: within 12 months after the fiscal year-end or as notified by the Tax Administration.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing due within 42 days after the return date. No return may be filed for a period ending more than 14 months before the prior return date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and payment due by the 15th day of the month following the reporting month for monthly filers, or within 15 days after the quarter-end for quarterly filers.

Gross Payroll Total Bermuda payroll cash and taxable fringe benefits paid to employees and directors performing services in Bermuda. Non-resident director fees are generally exempt from payroll tax unless attributable to services physically performed in Bermuda; seconded aircraft technical staff in Bermuda must be included. Late remittance: penalty up to 5% of unpaid tax plus statutory interest; default: additional penalty up to 100% of tax; fraudulent evasion: fines up to BMD 2,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years.
Reliefs and Allowances Statutory exemptions, such as the first BMD 10,000 of remuneration per employee per quarter, subtracted from gross payroll. Exemptions are computed on Bermuda-source payroll only; apportionment is required for split-year employment. Late remittance: penalty up to 5% of unpaid tax plus statutory interest; default: additional penalty up to 100% of tax; fraudulent evasion: fines up to BMD 2,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years.
Tax Due Net payroll tax after reliefs at the statutory employer rate, currently 5.25% standard with reduced rates for eligible small employers. The employer is the sole debtor for payroll tax; no deduction from an employee's contractual salary is permitted except as lawfully authorized. Late remittance: penalty up to 5% of unpaid tax plus statutory interest; default: additional penalty up to 100% of tax; fraudulent evasion: fines up to BMD 2,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable for the out-of-scope standalone Exempted Company, as no Bermuda transfer-pricing law applies below the Pillar Two threshold. In-scope entities under CITA 2023 and OECD GloBE rules: the threshold is group consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million, approximately BMD 750 million, in at least 2 of the 4 preceding fiscal years; such groups must maintain a Master File, Local File, and Country-by-Country Report covering the Bermuda leasing entity, evaluate the arm's length return on equity and receivables for the engine-leasing activity, and retain contemporaneous documentation for 7 years.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Requirements, established by the Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 and the Economic Substance Regulations 2018, apply because financing and leasing is a relevant activity. The Exempted Company's aircraft-engine dry-leasing activity must be directed and managed in Bermuda, with board meetings and quorum physically in Bermuda, minutes kept locally, and strategic decisions made in Bermuda, and must maintain adequate premises, full-time employees, and annual operating expenditure proportionate to the leasing profits. A non-resident UK parent may not make key decisions. An Economic Substance Declaration is filed annually and is cross-checked by the Registrar with the EU Code of Conduct Group and UK HMRC under the exchange-of-information framework.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and CITA Gross Misstatement

Statutory sanction: CITA and Tax Administration penalties: fixed penalty up to BMD 10,000 and daily default penalty up to BMD 1,000; penalty up to 100% of the tax understated for intentional negligence; interest running on unpaid top-up tax; forfeiture and civil recovery proceedings in the Supreme Court of Bermuda.

Director personal liability: Veil-piercing: the court may hold directors jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid top-up tax where the company is a mere facade or traded while insolvent; director disqualification up to 15 years; travel bans and seizure of passports; criminal conviction carrying imprisonment up to 5 years under the Bermuda Criminal Code and the Tax Administration Act.
