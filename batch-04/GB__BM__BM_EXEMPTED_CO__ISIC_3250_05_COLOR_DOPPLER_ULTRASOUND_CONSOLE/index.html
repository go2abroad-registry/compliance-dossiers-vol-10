Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Registrar of Companies, Financial Services and Development, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (official portal www.gov.bm; electronic filing via the Bermuda Online Submission System BOSS); auxiliary authorities: Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and social insurance; Bermuda Customs Department, Ministry of Finance, operating the ASYCUDA World electronic single-window for customs and import duty. No national income-tax agency exists because Bermuda levies no income, corporation, capital gains, VAT or sales tax on exempted companies.

E-government filing portal

STEP 1: Incorporate the Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981 by filing the Memorandum of Association with the Registrar of Companies; obtain the statutory Company Number (this serves as the primary corporate identifier; Bermuda does not issue a separate TIN or VAT ID). STEP 2: Engage a licensed Bermuda registered agent and maintain a registered office in Bermuda. STEP 3: Apply to the Registrar for Exempted Undertaking status and obtain a tax-exemption certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, granting statutory immunity from future Bermuda income taxes until 2035. STEP 4: If employees exist, register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax and Social Insurance and obtain a payroll tax account reference. STEP 5: Obtain an Importer/Exporter Code from Bermuda Customs via ASYCUDA World for physical import/export of the color doppler ultrasound consoles or components; a licensed customs broker is required for clearance. STEP 6: File the annual Economic Substance return under the Economic Substance Act 2023; a company whose core income-generating activities occur outside Bermuda is treated as tax-resident elsewhere and has only filing obligations in Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Company is taxed on a nil territorial basis; no Bermuda income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax or indirect sales tax on worldwide profits; statutory income-tax exemption guaranteed until 2035; residual Bermuda obligations comprise payroll tax on employees, social insurance, customs import duty, stamp duty, land tax and the annual economic substance filing. The entity remains outside the UK corporation tax charge unless UK-resident or trading through a UK permanent establishment.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box election and no hybrid mismatch election analogous to US/UK rules. Available mechanisms within Bermuda law: (i) election to be designated an Exempted Undertaking with a Ministerial tax-exemption certificate; (ii) election of non-resident status for Exchange Control purposes (Non-Resident Exempted Company) enabling free foreign-currency dealings and exemption from the Exchange Control Act restrictions; (iii) election of a financial/basis period for the Economic Substance Return; (iv) application for a pure-equity-holding or non-resident exemption in respect of economic substance reporting; (v) for UK-connected groups, submission of UK Form SS (hybrid mismatch disclosure) may be necessary, but only where the Bermuda entity is within scope of UK anti-hybrid rules, not by virtue of Bermuda law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax is imposed in Bermuda. Indirect taxation is limited to: (a) Customs Import Duty under the Customs Tariff Act 1969, charged at the border on the customs value of imported goods with statutory rates generally between 20% and 25%, although medical diagnostic devices such as ultrasound consoles may be duty-free or subject to reduced tariff rates; (b) Stamp Duty under the Stamp Duties Act 1976, principally on land conveyance and share transfers; (c) Payroll Tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995, at up to 10% of Bermuda-sourced employment remuneration; and (d) a 7% land tax and 5% hospital levy. The United Kingdom's VAT regime does not extend to Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None — Bermuda has no VAT registration threshold. All persons importing goods must register with the Bermuda Customs Department and obtain an ASYCUDA World importer/exporter code irrespective of consignment value; payroll tax registration is triggered from the first Bermuda-sourced salary payment to an employee, with no de minimis threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda levies no VAT/GST, so there is no zero-rating mechanism and no output tax to set at 0% for exports. Exports of color doppler ultrasound consoles from Bermuda are entirely outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation whether destined for the UK, EU, US or other markets. Bermuda customs duty is chargeable only on importation into Bermuda; goods manufactured in, processed in, or transshipped through Bermuda and subsequently exported are exempt from customs duty provided the outward movement is documented and no home-use entry has occurred. A Bermuda Exempted Company with no UK establishment owes no UK VAT on offshore export sales; however, if a UK branch or fixed establishment is created, UK VAT registration at the £90,000 threshold and export zero-rating under VATA 1994 s.30 would apply to qualifying exports. Transfer pricing documentation, economic substance filings and the tax-exemption certificate collectively substantiate the non-UK, non-VAT status of the export margin.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry referencing the outbound movement and HS 9018.12 classification)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Bermuda to the consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT MT103/MT202 credit advice or TT Wire confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List disclosing consignee, unit value, Incoterms and equipment description
  • Certificate of Free Sale or Bermuda Regulatory Authority (Ministry of Health) export/import permit for the medical device
  • Non-Preferential Certificate of Origin evidencing place of manufacture where the goods are transshipped UK/EU-origin goods
  • Economic Substance Return and payroll/customs records evidencing the declared trade flow and physical movement
  • Copy of the Exempted Undertaking tax-exemption certificate and company registry number

No electronic-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance/stamping mandate exists because Bermuda imposes no VAT. Import and export declarations must be filed electronically through the ASYCUDA World single-window customs automated system, with digital risk assessment and duty computation. Each entry requires the importer/exporter code, tariff classification (HS 9018.12 for ultrasonic scanning apparatus), commercial invoice, invoice value and, for the color doppler ultrasound console, a Bermuda Regulatory Authority import permit from the Ministry of Health together with a Free Sale Certificate or CE/FDA-equivalent certification where applicable. No real-time tax API or digital stamp duty clearance applies to B2B sales; records must be retained in English for a minimum of seven years.

Bermuda has no VAT zero-rating clawback because it has no VAT. However, fraudulent customs export declarations, misdeclaration of HS code, under-valuation, or evasion of import duty attract penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 1969 including forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties of up to three times the value of the goods or duty evaded, and imprisonment of up to five years on conviction on indictment. False economic substance filings are punishable by penalties up to BMD 250,000 plus daily default penalties under the Economic Substance Act 2023. False payroll tax returns attract penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus imprisonment. Documentary credit and telegraphic-transfer settlement should be completed within 180 days to preserve the arm's-length, non-UK substantive position; if a foreign tax authority (notably HMRC) successfully recharacterizes the Bermuda entity as lacking substance under BEPS Action 6 principles or the UK CFC/anti-hybrid rules, the export margin may be retrospectively reallocated to the UK with interest and penalties of up to 100% of the under-declared tax, together with a potential requirement to recompute Bermuda economic substance compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) and is effectively fully convertible on both current and capital accounts for a non-resident-designated Bermuda Exempted Company holding a BMA Notice-to-Company exchange control permission. Current account operations (trade settlements, commissions, distributor payments) and capital account operations (equity injection, intra-group loans, dividend repatriation) may be executed in USD, EUR, GBP or CNH without per-transaction BMA approval. Dividend remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted once a board-approved solvency certificate and audited financial statements substantiate distributable reserves; no Bermuda withholding, capital gains or profits taxes apply, making profit extraction commercially intact and subject only to the annual Exchange Control Return and fee settlements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio apply to non-resident Bermuda Exempted Companies: 0% surrender, 100% foreign-currency retention is permitted, onshore or in external USD/BMD accounts, subject to the BMA Notice-to-Company arrangements. For any BMD-denominated export receipt, conversion to USD at the fixed 1:1 peg is automatic within the same banking day. The company remains obliged to file the annual Exchange Control Return and to maintain a fully traceable inward-remittance audit trail for 5 years.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the Central Bank and Exchange Control Authority for Bermuda under the Exchange Control Act 1972; the Bank of England (BoE) governs UK sterling clearing rails (CHAPS) and the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) supervises the UK clearing banks that serve as London correspondents for Bermudian-dollar and sterling settlements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire protocol: (1) UK or group treasury initiates CHAPS or SWIFT MT103 to the Bermudian bank's London correspondent using ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUY' (or MT103 field 70 'Capital Contribution'); (2) the bank's New York Chips correspondent credits the Bermuda USD master or escrow account; (3) the Share Allotment Return is filed with the Bermuda Registry within 30 days of the board resolution, and the BMA Notice-to-Company is endorsed for the first drawdown; (4) a notarised board resolution identifying the escrow account and transaction purpose must accompany every remittance; (5) the bank verifies the UBO and sanctions pack and source-of-funds statements before releasing funds for trade use. Loan-versus-equity commingling, director loans and digital-asset transfers are prohibited on this corridor without separate legal re-designation.Clearing rails: (1) Commercial Letters of Credit are issued on the SWIFT MT700 or MT707 rail by Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda under UCP600, with reimbursement via MT742 or MT103 to suppliers; the documentary package comprises the medical-device purchase contract, proforma and commercial invoices, bill of lading, inspection certificate and quality certificates (ISO 13485, MDSAP, CE or UKCA). (2) Open-account trade payments use SWIFT MT103 or MT202 COV TT wires, cleared via Chips (USD) for Asian and US suppliers, TARGET2 or SEPA (EUR) for EU component vendors, and CHAPS (GBP) for UK R&D and logistics providers; FX cut-off is 15:00 Atlantic Time and purpose codes 'TRADE', 'GOODS' or 'SALA' are applied. (3) Chinese component suppliers are settled in USD via the Chips CNY corridor or offshore CNH; onshore CNY settlement is not available to the Bermuda treasury without a licensed China-bank relationship outside this scope.USD/CNH Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) - hedges Chinese component procurement where onshore CNY convertibility is unavailableGBP/USD Deliverable Forward (FWD) - hedges UK R&D payroll, logistics and VAT baseEUR/USD Deliverable Forward (FWD) - hedges EU distributor receivables and German or Italian component purchasesCross-Currency Interest Rate Swap (CCIRS) in GBP/USD and EUR/USD - aligns long-term treasury liabilities to USD revenueZero-Cost Collar and Plain Vanilla FX Option in EUR/USD and USD/CNH - caps hedging cost and preserves upsideBMD/USD Spot Conversion at the fixed 1:1 peg - ensures Bermudian-dollar operational liquidity without external market risk

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for FCA-authorised e-money institutions (EMIs) and LOW for unregulated neobanks. EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex) may onboard the Bermuda Exempted Company only with demonstrated UK/EU substance, a VAT or establishment footprint, and a 12-month audited trading record. Legal constraints: EMIs cannot issue Commercial Letters of Credit, e-money safeguarding affords weaker depositor protection than a Bermuda banking licence, and programme managers frequently de-risk British Overseas Territory clients. Recommended structure: EMI for low-value GBP/EUR collections and supplier disbursements, paired with Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda for USD escrow, LC issuance and capital accounts.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable; no domestic transfer pricing law or threshold exists for the 0% corporate income tax. Multinational groups with consolidated turnover ≥ USD 850,000,000 must file Bermuda CbC reports under the International Business (Country-by-Country Reporting) Regulations.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda ESR regulations 2018 require entities carrying on a relevant activity to have real substance in Bermuda: management and direction, operating premises, qualified employees, and adequate business expenditure. A medical imaging export distributor or IP-holding company must show annual evidence of those elements. Failure to demonstrate substance triggers reporting to foreign tax authorities and monetary fines.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Criminal Code, conviction carries imprisonment up to 10 years and an unlimited fine. The UK may impose penalties up to 100% of the tax understated and issue criminal fines for offshore evasion (FA 2015).

Director personal liability: Directors may be subject to personal civil claims for restitution, worldwide asset freezing orders, travel bans in connection with extradition, and imprisonment where they knowingly participate in the evasion.
Economic Substance Non-Compliance / Shell Company Misuse

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BD$10,000 per default, plus BD$50,000 for each subsequent month of non-compliance, and possible compulsory winding-up; the Registrar may also strike the company off the register.

Director personal liability: Officers may be jointly and severally liable for penalties, subjected to director disqualification, and declared unfit to act as directors in any Commonwealth jurisdiction.
Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing (AML/ATF)

Statutory sanction: The Proceeds of Crime (Bermuda) Amendment Act 2016 prescribes fines of up to BD$1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 15 years; the Anti-Money Laundering Act 2004 also permits civil asset forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and compliance officers may be held criminally liable for willful blindness, face personal asset seizure, travel bans, and extradition to foreign jurisdictions; in severe cases, restrictive commercial insurance exclusions leave estates exposed.
