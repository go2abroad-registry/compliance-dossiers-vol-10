Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Primary: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance, and the Bermuda Registrar of Companies (official portal: www.gov.bm). Customs: Bermuda Customs Department. Secondary (UK): Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC) via www.gov.uk/hmrc, solely if the Bermuda entity establishes a UK permanent establishment, UK payroll, or UK VAT registration obligations. The Bermuda exempted company is not subject to UK corporate tax merely by reason of its status as a British Overseas Territory-corporation; separate UK registration arises only on actual UK taxable nexus.

E-government filing portal

For a non-Bermudian investor forming a Bermuda Exempted Company: (1) Appoint a Bermuda-resident registered office and a licensed corporate secretary/corporate service provider. (2) File the Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981; pay the incorporation fee and the exempted-company annual fee; obtain the Certificate of Incorporation bearing a unique Bermuda company registry number, which serves as the statutory identifier. There is no separate income-tax TIN because Bermuda does not levy income tax. (3) Apply to the Minister of Finance for the tax exemption certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; the certificate is routinely issued for exempted companies and excludes all Bermuda income, profits, capital gains and distributions until 2035. (4) If the company has Bermuda payroll, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner using the prescribed payroll-tax registration form; obtain a payroll-tax employer account number. (5) If the company imports goods into Bermuda, register with Bermuda Customs for an import/export account. (6) File the annual Economic Substance Return through the Registrar's online portal when the company is within the scope of the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018. No VAT ID can be obtained because no VAT/GST exists in Bermuda. Non-resident ownership is permitted; no minimum paid-up capital is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Companies are resident outside the ordinary Bermuda corporate tax net and are statutorily protected from future Bermuda income, corporate, capital-gains and withholding taxes until 2035 under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, subject only to annual government fees, employer payroll tax, land tax, and customs duties where applicable. The entity is not a pass-through or an entity-level taxable person under Bermuda law; its exemption is a jurisdictional tax-exempt status.

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic check-the-box election exists, because Bermuda does not impose income tax at the corporate or shareholder level. The exempted-company tax exemption is obtained by application and is not a periodic elective classification. For foreign jurisdictions (e.g., United States), the entity may be classified annually by the foreign owner under the US check-the-box regime (default: C corporation without a Form 8832 election), but that is a foreign-law election, not a Bermuda election. Hybrid mismatch and OECD anti-avoidance elections are not relevant to Bermuda domestic taxation, and no such election is available under Bermuda law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax; Bermuda does not levy any general consumption tax. The only minor indirect levies are customs import duties on physical goods entering Bermuda and stamp duties on specified instruments, neither of which applies to export sales from Bermuda. UK VAT may apply separately where the Bermuda entity makes taxable supplies in the United Kingdom.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BMD 0 (no VAT/GST registration threshold). A customs account is required only for direct importers of physical goods into Bermuda; a payroll tax employer registration is required only when Bermuda remuneration is paid. There is no turnover threshold giving rise to an indirect-tax registration obligation in Bermuda.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable under Bermuda law: zero-rating is a VAT mechanism and Bermuda has no VAT/GST, thus no export VAT zero-rating claim or refund exists. Sales of goods and offshore services by a Bermuda exempted company are outside the scope of Bermuda consumption taxation. Where the company sells goods into the United Kingdom or European Union, those sales are subject to the importing jurisdiction's VAT and import rules; Bermuda does not provide any zero-rating certificate or VAT refund path.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (bearing the company's registered name and Bermuda registry number)
  • Evidence of physical export (airway bill, courier manifest, port exit clearance or terminal receipt)
  • Bank wire/payment confirmation showing receipt of export funds or foreign exchange service confirmation

Bermuda does not mandate electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government clearing of commercial invoices. Customs export/import declarations must be submitted electronically by a licensed customs broker or importer through the Bermuda Customs electronic filing portal. For tax and corporate records, invoices must be retained for at least six years and made available to the Registrar of Companies, the Office of the Tax Commissioner, or Bermuda Customs upon request.

Since Bermuda has no VAT/GST, there is no statutory VAT zero-rating clawback or refund recovery mechanism. However, false or incomplete documentation submitted to Bermuda Customs, the Registrar of Companies, or a foreign tax authority may attract penalties under Bermuda customs and economic substance legislation, including monetary fines, forfeiture of goods, cancellation or non-renewal of the exempted-company certificate, and in fraudulent cases imprisonment. The Bermuda tax-exemption certificate provides no protection against fraud, misrepresentation, or intentionally artificial arrangements; foreign tax authorities (including HMRC in the UK) may reassess VAT, reverse input tax, and assert penalties independently.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda has no exchange control regime. The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD), and USD is legal tender and circulates freely alongside BMD. Capital account transactions are freely convertible, and profit remittance, dividend distribution, and liquidation proceeds may be transferred without prior central bank approval or surrender obligations. Corporate accounts may be maintained in USD, BMD, or other major currencies at the bank's discretion. The principal compliance gate is AML/ATF and sanctions screening, not currency convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applicable to export proceeds in Bermuda. Export receipts may be retained in the original currency, credited to a domestic or offshore account, and used for discretionary purposes. For companies settled in USD, the 1:1 BMD peg ensures no FX conversion friction. For non-USD receipts, the company may freely instruct the bank to convert at market rates or hold the funds in the original currency.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI-only platforms have limited standalone licensing viability in Bermuda because BMA does not issue a dedicated e-money institution licence. A Bermuda Exempted Company may, however, use a UK FCA-authorized EMI or an EU/EEA-authorised EMI as a payment facilitation wrapper to support Stripe/PayPal settlement and supplier payouts, provided the Bermuda entity remains the contracting party and the EMI conduit is integrated solely for payments. Such arrangements are viable for low-risk e-commerce, but the EMI provider must accept the Bermuda corporate structure and perform its own KYC; residency and substance requirements under Bermuda's Economic Substance Act will not be satisfied by an EMI arrangement alone.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity injections and director loans should be settled via SWIFT MT103 wires from a regulated banking institution to the Bermuda operating account. The receiving bank will require a transfer purpose code such as 'Equity Injection / Share Capital' or 'Intercompany Loan'; the remitter should reference the corporate incorporation number and the economic substance declaration. For direct equity contributions, the board must issue share certificates and file the beneficial ownership return. For intercompany loans, a signed loan agreement noting arm's length interest and repayment terms is mandatory. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% statutory corporate income tax for Bermuda Exempted Companies outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; 15% corporate income tax (Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax and Income Inclusion Rule) applies only to constituent entities of MNE Groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda Exempted Company to non-resident shareholders, irrespective of shareholding percentage or jurisdiction of residence.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory Local File or Master File obligation exists for out-of-scope entities. For in-scope MNE Groups, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 incorporates OECD arm's length standards and requires Transfer Pricing documentation aligned with Master File / Local File and Country-by-Country Reporting standards; the practical triggering threshold is consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million, denominated equivalently in Bermuda dollars.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 applies to all Bermuda exempted companies. The garden hydroponics e-commerce business is a relevant activity if classified as a Distribution and Service Centre or Headquarters. To satisfy ESR, the entity must demonstrate: (i) board meetings held and strategic decisions taken in Bermuda with minutes maintained there; (ii) appropriate physical premises in Bermuda; (iii) qualified full-time employees in Bermuda proportionate to the activity; and (iv) adequate annual operating expenditure incurred in Bermuda. Outsourcing of payment processing (Stripe, PayPal), logistics, customer support or marketing to foreign providers does not constitute Bermuda substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Act 2018: BD$50,000 penalty for first substance failure, BD$100,000 for subsequent failures; ESR non-filing surcharges of BD$3,000 plus BD$150 per day; strike-off and dissolution of the company with assets vesting in the Crown.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly file false declarations face imprisonment up to 5 years and fines; directors may be disqualified from serving as directors in Bermuda for up to 5 years and ordered to personally compensate the company for amounts distributed illegally.
Tax Evasion / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: UK GAAR counteraction; HMRC penalties up to 200% of the tax understated for deliberate and concealed behaviour; UK corporation tax at up to 25% on UK PE profits; diverted profits tax at 25% on artificially diverted profits; potential UK criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue (imprisonment up to 7 years); Bermuda-UK TIEA enables information exchange and evidence sharing.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal liability under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion; HMRC may impose civil travel bans, pursue directors for unlawful dividends, and obtain confiscation orders over UK-located personal assets.
