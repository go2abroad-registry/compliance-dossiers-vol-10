Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Bermuda Exempted Company is outside Bermuda’s income tax base and carries no Bermuda corporate income tax; it is also non-UK resident for corporation tax if its central management and control is outside the UK and it has no UK permanent establishment. Bermuda payroll tax and social insurance apply only to local payroll. Digital publishing revenue from Steam and PlayStation remains outside local corporate tax exposure.

Available election forms

No Bermuda local election is available because the company is already tax-exempt. For U.S. federal tax purposes, a Bermuda Exempted Company is generally a per se corporation and cannot make a check-the-box election under Form 8832 to be treated as a disregarded entity or partnership. No UK election is required if the company remains non-UK resident; hybrid mismatch rules under TIOPA 2010 Part 10 may require a notice or election only if an actual hybrid mismatch arrangement exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: No VAT/GST. United Kingdom: VAT (Value Added Tax) with digital-services place-of-supply rules and online-marketplace deemed-supplier treatment; Steam/Valve and PlayStation Store are electronic interfaces that generally collect and remit VAT for non-established publishers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: Not applicable (no VAT/GST registration threshold). UK: £90,000 annual taxable turnover for UK-established businesses; non-UK-established digital suppliers are required to register from the first taxable supply unless a digital marketplace or electronic interface is the deemed VAT supplier.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda: not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT/GST and no zero-rating or refund system. UK: for digital downloads, export zero-rating is replaced by outside-scope treatment where the place of supply is outside the UK (B2C digital services taxed at customer location; B2B services generally outside scope). Physical exports from the UK may be zero-rated if the goods are exported within the statutory time limit and proper evidence is held. When Steam/PlayStation is the deemed supplier, the publisher does not charge UK VAT and is not entitled to input VAT recovery on platform commissions; its proceeds are outside the scope of UK VAT.

  • Bermuda: no customs export declaration or customs documentation is required for digital software downloads, Steam/PlayStation licences, or stored-value revenue; Bermuda Customs declarations apply only to physical media movements.
  • UK physical-media export (if applicable): Customs Export Declaration, commercial invoice, Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, packing list, inward remittance certificate/bank transfer evidence, and proof of removal from the UK within the applicable statutory time limit.
  • UK digital-services outside-scope substantiation: Steam Partner/Valve payout statements, PlayStation Store payout statements, platform sales analytics, consumer country-of-residence evidence, B2B customer VAT identification numbers, and a record of the platform's deemed-supplier status.

Bermuda: no electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or real-time API requirement. UK: no mandatory B2B e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance; VAT-registered businesses must maintain digital records and file VAT returns through HMRC Making Tax Digital compatible software via HMRC’s MTD API. Platform-generated transaction reports from Steam Partner and PlayStation Partner must be retained as the primary evidence of sales; no separate digital stamping is required.

Bermuda: no VAT or export zero-rating clawback exists; failure to pay the annual company fee may result in strike-off and penalties. UK: if HMRC determines that a zero-rating or outside-scope position is unsupported, it can assess unpaid VAT, impose default interest, and apply deliberate-inaccuracy penalties up to 100% of the tax; fraudulent evasion of VAT or customs duty is a criminal offence under VATA 1994 and CEMA 1979, carrying unlimited fines and imprisonment. Discovery assessments can reach back up to 20 years, and marketplace or electronic-interface operators can be jointly and severally liable where the deemed-supplier rules are abused.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains full current and capital account convertibility. The Bermuda Monetary Authority administers exchange control but does not impose approvals for foreign exchange transactions, capital flows, or inward/outward remittances. The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar (USD), and businesses may freely maintain multi-currency accounts. Profit remittance from a Bermuda Exempted Company is unrestricted, subject only to standard banking due diligence, Economic Substance Act 2018 compliance certification for relevant activities, and CRS/FATCA reporting obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion rules in Bermuda. Exporters and digital publishers may retain 100% of foreign currency receipts in USD, EUR, GBP, or any freely convertible currency without a prescribed timeline. No BMA approval is required for conversion of export proceeds, and settlement balances may be held indefinitely in corporate treasury accounts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for UK/EU EMIs used as supplementary payment corridors; Low for Bermuda-licensed fintech EMIs in the pure fiat space. Bermuda's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 does not license fiat-only electronic money institutions; a full banking licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 is required to hold customer funds domestically. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise or Revolut generally do not support Bermuda-registered corporate entities with BMD/USD local account details. Such EMIs may be used only for cross-currency disbursement optimization, while the primary settlement rail for Steam and PlayStation payouts must remain a fully regulated Bermudian or US clearing bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Bermuda Exempted Company; separate 15% Bermuda corporate income tax only for a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue above EUR 750 million (OECD Pillar Two implementation), not applicable to a typical PC/console indie publisher

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Bermuda levies no withholding tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to any shareholder, including a UK parent, and no Bermuda income or dividend tax applies to the distribution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold exists in BMD because Bermuda has no income tax base. Documentation obligations arise indirectly: (i) Bermuda ESR needs an internal cost and substance file; (ii) if the UK parent or HMRC asserts control/CFC, contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation is required under TIOPA 2010 Part 4 (OECD-aligned), including a Local/Master File when the UK group is in scope; and (iii) a qualified OECD Pillar Two domestic top-up regime may require minute-level computation in BMD.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda ESR apply fully to this Exempted Company. A PC/console game publisher earning Steam/PlayStation proceeds through software licences is regarded as carrying on an Intellectual Property Business (or, if the entity purchases and resells games as principal, a distribution/holding business) and must satisfy core income-generating activities, adequate staff, premises and expenditure in Bermuda. Failure creates a rebuttable presumption of a shell company and enables exchange of information with UK authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Violation / Shell Company Classification

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2019 impose a financial penalty up to BMD 10,000 for failure to file the ESD, rising to BMD 50,000 plus BMD 10,000 per day for continued non-compliance after a notice. The Tax Commissioner may apply to strike the company off and can mandate information exchange to the UK, exposing the UK parent to CFC/DPT assessments.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly make a false ESD are subject to criminal sanctions including fines and imprisonment, travel bans, disqualification as a Bermuda director and civil contribution to the company's penalty. In liquidation, UK directors who approved the sham structure may face misfeasance proceedings.
