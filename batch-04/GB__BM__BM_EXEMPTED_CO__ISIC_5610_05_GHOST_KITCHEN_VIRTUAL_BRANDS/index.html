Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance (Office of the Tax Commissioner) and the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; official government portal is https://www.gov.bm. Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory with a separate tax administration from HMRC; UK taxes do not automatically apply in Bermuda.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company has no Bermudian corporate income-tax registration requirement and no VAT/TIN identification obligation. The statutory identifier is the company number issued by the Registrar of Companies upon incorporation under the Companies Act 1981. A foreign-owned entity forms the company through a licensed registered agent, files its memorandum of association, articles of association and required declarations, receives the Certificate of Incorporation, then obtains the Exempted Company approvals/permit through the Ministry of Finance where required. If the company later employs individuals physically present in Bermuda, it must separately register as an employer for payroll tax with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a payroll tax account number. No general TIN is issued solely for tax purposes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) maintains a statutory 1:1 peg with the USD, administered by the BMA. Bermuda operates a fully liberalised exchange control regime for exempted companies: both current account and capital account transactions are freely convertible, with no restrictions on dividend distributions, profit repatriation, intercompany loans, or liquidation proceeds. Non-resident owned exempted companies holding a BMA Certificate of Exemption receive automatic general permission for foreign currency accounts, capital injections, and outward remittances. Practical remittance hurdles are limited to AML/KYC documentary thresholds rather than balance-of-payments controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio in Bermuda. A Bermuda Exempted Company deriving revenue from ghost kitchen delivery platforms (UberEats, DoorDash) may retain USD, GBP, EUR, or any freely convertible currency in its BMA-licensed bank accounts indefinitely. The BMA, however, requires reporting of any single inbound/outbound payment exceeding BMD 100,000 (or equivalent) under its large-value transaction reporting obligations, and all cross-border flows must be supported by underlying trade or capital documentation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972, supplemented by the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium feasibility, with strict legal constraints. Bermuda does not recognise the UK or EU EMI passporting regime; an offshore digital EMI is not licensed by the BMA to open deposit accounts for a Bermuda exempted company unless it holds a restricted banking licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. In practice, UK FCA-regulated EMIs (Wise, Revolut, Airwallex) may accept a Bermuda exempted company as a client solely as a payment service provider, subject to their own correspondent banking policy. These rails are viable for small GBP/EUR disbursements but are inadequate for primary treasury operations: they frequently reject UberEats/DoorDash aggregated payout wiring, and they lack access to the BMD/USD local clearing. The EMI channel should be limited to a secondary operational buffer, never the capital escrow facility.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections from the UK parent are executed via SWIFT MT103 from a UK bank to the Bermuda entity's USD operational or escrow account through the bank's New York correspondent (CHIPS). Instructions must state purpose code 'Equity Injection - Foreign Direct Investment' (IMF BOP code EQ/5130), reference the Bermuda Exchange Control general permission granted to exempted companies, and attach the executed board resolution authorising share issuance. No BMD conversion occurs given the 1:1 USD peg. For marketplace payouts (UberEats/DoorDash), inbound routing arrives as aggregated ACH or Fedwire settlements from the platform's US operating entity; these must be pre-notified to the Bermuda bank with the platform merchant agreement and a statement of payout period to satisfy large-value reporting.Trade settlements use SWIFT MT103 for discrete supplier payments (kitchen equipment, food-grade packaging, technology licensing) cleared via CHIPS in New York for USD or via target2 for EUR. For high-value capital imports from Asia (e.g., commercial cooking suites), an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (UCP600) is preferred, issued by the Bermuda bank through a London or New York confirming bank; presentation documents must be unicorporated under the BMD/USD peg. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general Bermuda corporate income tax for Exempted Companies; 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT) applies exclusively to in-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750M in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years (Pillar Two aligned, effective for fiscal years commencing on or after 1 January 2025).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% Bermuda withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda Exempted Company; 0% UK withholding tax on dividends paid by UK companies (UK dividends are always paid gross); therefore, profit repatriation from the UK PE to the Bermuda parent and onward to ultimate investors carries zero statutory dividend withholding in both jurisdictions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda (CIT regime): in-scope CIT groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750M must prepare and file a Master File and Local File aligned to the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines; out-of-scope exempted companies have no Bermuda statutory TP return. UK side (UK PE of the Bermuda company): HMRC TP documentation (Local File) is mandatory when the group is 'large' — group turnover exceeds GBP 200M — and cross-border controlled transactions with the Bermuda head office exceed GBP 1M for goods or GBP 500K for services, intangibles, or financing; penalties for failure to keep contemporaneous documentation are up to GBP 50,000 per document category.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply; 'Intellectual Property Business' and certain other relevant activities require core income-generating activities (CIGA), physical presence, employees, and expenditure in Bermuda. Ghost kitchen foodservice (ISIC 5610) is NOT a listed relevant activity and is treated as a Non-Relevant Entity (NRE), requiring only a nil ESD. However, if the virtual-brand trademarks, delivery-app customer databases, or proprietary recipes are held in the Bermuda entity, the activity becomes an IP Business subject to full ESR substance testing in Bermuda; failure attracts the BMD 10,000/month penalties. Simultaneously, the UK will scrutinize whether the Bermuda entity has practical control over the IP (HMRC may reallocate profits under CTA 2009 Pt 4 or apply the Diverted Profits Tax if the Bermuda IP structure has no real commercial substance).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK Tax Residence Mismanagement (Central Management and Control)

Statutory sanction: HMRC treats the company as UK-resident and assesses 25% UK corporation tax on worldwide profits; back taxes plus interest (base rate + 2.5%); penalties for failure to notify (up to 100% of tax) and for erroneous CT600 returns (up to 100%); additional assessment period can extend 20 years for deliberate conduct

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed under CTA 2010 s.217 for tax owed when the company is expected to get into serious difficulties; fraudulent evasion of income tax carries up to 7 years' imprisonment under TMA 1970 s.106A; HMRC can obtain freezing orders, leave-to-enter restrictions, and director disqualification orders (up to 15 years)
Bermuda Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Minister may impose a penalty of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 10,000 for each month the failure continues; false declarations attract a fine of BMD 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years; the company may be struck off the register and its assets revert to the Crown

Director personal liability: Directors and officers of the exempted company can be criminally prosecuted for knowingly permitting a false statement; in addition, the UK may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors/corporate-service-provider associates jointly liable for diverted profits tax and UK VAT where the Bermuda entity is a mere façade
Diverted Profits Tax (DPT) on Artificial Bermuda Structure

Statutory sanction: 25% DPT rate on diverted profits, no deduction for the royalty paid; DPT must be paid within 30 days of the notice; interest accrues; HMRC may also open a corporate tax enquiry and impose penalties of up to double the tax for deliberately concealed arrangements

Director personal liability: HMRC can charge a designated director under s.23 FA 2015 (DPT) for the penalty where the company is insolvent and the director materially contributed to the penalty condition; travel bans (HMRC 'conduct notices') and disqualification as a UK director (CDDA 1986) may apply; in serious cases, cheating the public revenue is an indictable offence with a maximum life sentence
UK VAT and Delivery Platform Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: VAT civil penalty of up to 100% of the VAT evaded (for deliberate concealment); surcharge up to 15% on late-paid VAT; assessment can cover the previous 4 years (deliberate: 20 years); HMRC can wind up the company for unpaid VAT; third-party liability can extend to platform operators in some circumstances

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in VAT evasion commit the criminal offence of fraudulent evasion of VAT under VATA 1994 s.72, punishable by imprisonment up to 7 years and unlimited fines; personal guarantees and joint-and-several liability under Finance Act 2009 Sch 46 (Senior Accounting Officer regime) for large companies; travel restrictions and asset seizure through 'Unexplained Wealth Orders' (UWOs) in high-value cases
