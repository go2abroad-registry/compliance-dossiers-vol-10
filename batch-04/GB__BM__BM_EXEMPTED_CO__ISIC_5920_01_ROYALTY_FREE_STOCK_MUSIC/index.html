Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance – Registrar of Companies (corporate registration); Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax administration); Bermuda Customs Department (customs duties on imports)

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated by submitting a Memorandum of Association to the Registrar of Companies, which issues a Company File Number (CR Number). No separate tax identification number (TIN) or VAT ID is issued because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax or VAT. If the company will have Bermuda-resident employees, the employer must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax purposes within the relevant timeframes; if the company has no employees, no payroll tax registration is required. No indirect tax registration applies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates an offshore special territory regime with the Bermudian dollar (BMD) pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD). There are no exchange controls restricting current account transactions, capital account movements, or profit remittance from Bermuda. BMD and USD circulate interchangeably and settlement can be effected in either currency. The BMA regulates licensed financial institutions, but ordinary commercial companies face no convertibility or repatriation licensing hurdles. For royalty-free stock music licensing, revenue denominated in USD, GBP, or EUR can be freely converted and remitted, subject only to underlying banking KYC and anti-money-laundering checks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda has no statutory export proceeds surrender regime. There is no mandatory conversion timeline and no requirement to repatriate or register export receipts with the BMA. A Bermuda Exempted Company may retain foreign currency earnings in offshore or onshore accounts indefinitely. The only practical obligation is to maintain accurate accounting records and to disclose intercompany balances and dividend/profit distributions in statutory filings and economic substance reporting where applicable.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for banking-grade liquidity rails; Medium for ancillary payment gateways. Offshore digital EMIs authorised in the UK or EEA (e.g., e-money institutions) do not hold a Bermuda banking licence and cannot provide local settlement accounts or clearing-tier banking services to a Bermuda Exempted Company. They may be used only as payment collection front-ends (Stripe, PayPal, etc.) with full funds routed onward to a licensed Bermuda bank. Legal constraint: any entity providing money transmission, e-money, or payment services in or from Bermuda must hold a BMA licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act or the Payment Services Act. The business scope in this dossier does not require such a licence, so an offshore EMI relationship is permissible only as a non-licensed merchant-processing arrangement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for a Bermuda Exempted Company should be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the parent or external investor to a Bermuda-licensed bank. For USD, settlement is effected via CHIPS/Fedwire through the bank's US correspondent. The recommended purpose code is 'Equity Capital Injection' with narrative 'BMD 1:1 USD; share subscription proceeds' to avoid misclassification. Verification documents include board resolutions approving the share issue, a share subscription agreement, allotment letters, the share register, and the Bermuda Registrar's certificate. Since the target company's commercial model is low-risk audio licensing, no BMA regulatory pre-approval is required for the injection itself, but the bank must complete source-of-funds validation before crediting the account.Outbound disbursements for trade and royalty settlements are cleared primarily by SWIFT MT103 for non-documentary payments and by Commercial Letters of Credit for high-value procurement of audio samples or licensed sound recordings. Banks will require evidence of the underlying transaction, such as invoices, licensing statements, and W-8BEN-E tax forms where relevant. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general Bermuda transfer pricing documentation threshold exists in Bermuda Dollars (BMD). For OECD Pillar Two/CbCR purposes, the relevant threshold is EUR 750,000,000 in consolidated group revenue across the preceding four fiscal years. In-scope groups must file a Bermuda CIT return and country-by-country data, and maintain affiliate transaction records consistent with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines even though Bermuda has no separate TP statute.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance (Substance Requirements) Regulations 2018 require non-resident and exempted entities carrying on relevant activities to demonstrate adequate board management, local employees, premises, and core income-generating activities in Bermuda. A stock-music catalogue licensing operation may fall within 'intellectual property holding' or 'distribution and service centre' categories; nil substance filing is not sufficient when income-generating activity actually occurs. From 2025, in-scope MNE groups must also show substance support for GloBE income allocation and QDMTT calculations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Offshore License Structure

Statutory sanction: The residence country may impose unpaid taxes, interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded; UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 may expose the enterprise to unlimited corporate fines for failure to prevent facilitation of UK tax evasion; Bermuda may prosecute directors for fraud, false Registrar filings and money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997, carrying up to 14 years' imprisonment and confiscation orders.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in a sham licensing structure can be held jointly and severally liable for restitution and taxes, subjected to asset freezing orders, civil recovery orders, travel bans, extradition and imprisonment. The corporate veil is regularly pierced where the company operates solely as a hollow instrumentality of the controlling shareholder.
Shell Company Abuse / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018, penalties include BMD 10,000 for non-compliance with information obligations plus BMD 2,000 per day; failure to meet the economic substance test attracts a penalty of up to BMD 250,000 and repeat failures up to BMD 500,000. The Registrar may strike the company off, dissolve it, or exchange adverse information with foreign tax authorities under Bermuda's TIEAs.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may be personally liable to pay the company's ESR penalties, disqualified from acting as directors, and ordered by court to contribute to the company's debts. Trading while knowing the company is a shell with no substance can result in fraudulent trading liability.
