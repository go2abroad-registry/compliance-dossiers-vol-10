Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Tax Administration, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; official portal: www.gov.bm. Customs matters are administered separately by the Bermuda Customs Department via ASYCUDA World.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN or VAT ID. The statutory entity identifier is the Company Number issued by the Registrar of Companies on incorporation of the Exempted Company. If the entity employs staff, it must register with the Tax Commissioner for Bermuda payroll tax and social insurance and obtain an employer/payroll number. If it imports goods into Bermuda, it must obtain a Customs User Code and register in ASYCUDA World. FATCA/CRS reporting uses the Company Number, with 'N/A' recorded where no Bermuda TIN exists. A corporate income tax registration number arises only if the entity becomes a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group with annual consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million under Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax Act 2025.

Not applicable; no VAT or GST registration threshold exists. Bermuda importers must register with the Bermuda Customs Department for import clearance, and customs duty is assessed under the Customs Tariff Act at rates varying by goods classification.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable; because Bermuda has no VAT, GST, or sales tax, there is no zero-rating claim or refund mechanism for exports. Goods exported from Bermuda are generally outside Bermuda customs duty, and the exporter need not substantiate a VAT refund. The export must still be documented through Bermuda Customs for a clean export record.

None. Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, electronic invoicing, API-based tax validation, or digital stamping for indirect tax. Customs declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World for customs clearance only.

No VAT zero-rating clawback regime exists in Bermuda. However, a false export declaration or misdeclaration to Bermuda Customs attracts seizure and forfeiture of the goods, penalties under the Customs Act of up to BMD 10,000 and/or imprisonment for up to five years, and additional duty assessments. If the entity becomes subject to Bermuda corporate income tax under Corporate Income Tax Act 2025, fraudulent underreporting or mischaracterization of cross-border transactions can attract interest, late-payment penalties, and fraud penalties up to 100% of the tax assessed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 parity with the US Dollar under the Currency Act 1972 and is fully backed by USD reserves. Bermuda has no material exchange control restrictions on current account or capital account transactions; profit remittances, dividend distributions, repayment of intercompany loans, and capital repatriation by a Bermuda Exempted Company are freely permitted without BMA prior consent or a foreign investment certificate. Both BMD and USD circulate interchangeably and are legal tender for settlement of all obligations. The only latent impediment is bank corporate governance: the receiving bank may require source-of-funds evidence and board resolutions for large related-party remittances, but there is no statutory approval hurdle. An Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 certificate guarantees no future Bermuda taxes on profit, capital, or distributions for up to 50 years, which reinforces clean dividend and capital outflow treatment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no surrender, conversion, monetization, or repatriation timeline on export proceeds. A Bermuda Exempted Company manufacturing electric cargo e-bikes may retain all receivables in USD, BMD, EUR, or any freely convertible currency in a BMA-licensed bank without any mandatory conversion ratio, waiting period, or central bank notification. Export invoices should be accompanied by standard trade documents (commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading) solely for the bank's AML/ATF record-keeping obligations under the AML/ATF Code of Practice 2020, not for exchange control approval.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; the BMA also administers the Exchange Control Act 1972, but exchange control administration has been conducted on a fully non-restrictive basis since the Exchange Control Regulations were suspended in 1994. The BMA is the licensing and AML/ATF supervisor for banks and money service businesses in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium as a primary treasury rail. A non-Bermudian offshore digital EMI cannot lawfully accept deposits, maintain BMD/USD domestic settlement accounts, or offer local clearing in Bermuda without a BMA banking license under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; EMIs holding a UK FCA e-money license may provide ancillary payment-processing or multi-currency virtual IBAN services, but those services are not regulated as deposit-taking and cannot be used for statutory capital escrow or irrevocable trade settlement. A Bermuda-incorporated fintech would require a BMA Money Service Business license under the Money Services Business Act 2020, which entails substantive local presence, an AML compliance officer, and a physical registered office, making it commercially impractical for this manufacturing special purpose vehicle. Recommended architecture: use a licensed Tier-1 bank as the principal clearing node and a UK/EEA EMI only for marginal disbursement acceleration to small suppliers, keeping the EMI balance below threshold limits and never as the account of record for tax-exempt capital.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing uses a SWIFT MT103 from the UK parent's UK bank to the Bermudian operating account at HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield, with Field 70 remittance information stating 'SHARE SUBSCRIPTION CAPITAL' plus the invoice or subscription agreement reference. Because Bermuda has no exchange control approval, no BMA purpose-code certification is required; however, the bank will process a keying remark in the BMA's returns. The recommended settlement currency is USD, held in a dual-currency BMD/USD account at parity, to avoid any conversion spread. For funding in GBP, the parent wires GBP to HSBC London's Bermuda desk or Butterfield's London office, which converts at the BMD anchor rate; for EUR, conversion is executed through the London or New York treasury desk. All inbound payments should be pre-notified with the bank's relationship manager, and the client should submit the share subscription agreement and board resolution no later than two business days prior to value date to avoid supplementary information holds.Outbound trade settlements use SWIFT MT103 for telegraphic transfers to verified supplier accounts, MT700 MT710 for documentary letters of credit opened by the Bermudian importer for bulk battery, motor, and frame component purchases, and MT202 for cover settlement when a nostro relationship requires intermediary clearing. For suppliers in Asia that require confirmed payment, the Bermudian company should instruct its Bermuda bank to issue an irrevocable LC through a UK or Hong Kong confirming bank; for smaller recurring payments, a standing instructions file and a 24-hour pre-advice via secure email or the bank's API platform is appropriate. The underlying trade terms should be Incoterms 2020 CIF/FOB with bills of lading consigned to the order of the Bermudian company or its finance house, and each disbursement must include the supplier's full SWIFT BIC, account number, and a remittance reference of the purchase order. Bermuda's domestic clearing is executed through the BMA-operated Bermuda Automated Settlement System (BASS), but cross-border wires clear via SWIFT, Fedwire, or CHIPS; local BMD/USD clearing at parity is instant, while cross-border USD wires have a standard one-to-two business day value window.Plain vanilla over-the-counter FX forwards on BMD/USD at a fixed 1:1 peg with either the Bermuda bank or a UK parent relationship bank; premium is minimal since BMD is fully USD-backed.USD/CNH or USD/CNY non-deliverable forwards executed through a UK or Hong Kong licensed bank to hedge component procurement from mainland Chinese suppliers.USD/EUR and USD/GBP deliverable FX forwards and cross-currency swaps under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with an appropriately licensed BMA-recognized or UK FCA-regulated counterparty.Currency options (European style) on third-currency pairs to cap conversion costs on European logistics and distribution revenues, subject to the bank's normal derivative documentation and credit approval.Interest rate swaps and cross-currency basis swaps for matching any trade credit facilities or invoice discounting lines, documented under ISDA with a UK or Australian authorized bank, provided the Bermudian entity is not deemed a retail client.No BMD/USD NDF market is necessary or available given the fixed 1:1 peg; NDFs are permitted only for the third-currency exposure embedded in the e-bike supply chain and must be executed with a bank that has a valid BMA or equivalent home-country license, with mark-to-market collateral if the notional exceeds USD 2 million.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profits tax, no excess profits tax, and no withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties. The Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 guarantees that a Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from all taxes on income and capital, including any future income tax enacted before 31 March 2035, subject to payment of the annual Government fee and compliance with economic substance regulations.

Manufacturing of electric cargo e-bikes is not itself a statutory Relevant Activity; however, if the Bermuda entity procures and distributes finished e-bikes, holds and licences trademarks or patents, or coordinates group purchasing, it must select 'Distribution and Service Centre', 'Intellectual Property', or 'Headquarters' as applicable. Part A — Authorised Share Capital and Fee Computation Stated classes and amounts of authorised ordinary, preference, and redeemable shares, forming the base for the annual Government fee. Overseas investors must fund the exact BMD/USD Government fee before 31 January; foreign-currency remittance delays do not excuse underpayment. The Exempted Company should maintain its share register in Bermuda and promptly file any capital increase with the Registrar. Late filing incurs civil default fines, late-payment surcharges on the Government fee, and potential automatic strike-off. Every director and officer 'knowingly and wilfully' permitting default is personally liable to a statutory default fine, and the court may order disqualification from acting as a director in Bermuda.
Part B — Compliance Confirmation Confirmation that the company has complied with the Companies Act 1981, including maintenance of the registered office, registers of directors and members, and statutory books in Bermuda. Where the board is fully expatriate, the Bermuda registered agent should hold the statutory books physically in Bermuda; offshore cloud storage alone fails the Act's documentation requirements. Late filing incurs civil default fines, late-payment surcharges on the Government fee, and potential automatic strike-off. Every director and officer 'knowingly and wilfully' permitting default is personally liable to a statutory default fine, and the court may order disqualification from acting as a director in Bermuda.
Part C — Financial Statements Filing Upload of audited financial statements for the preceding fiscal year, or, where available, the Ministerial waiver or reliance on a group audit exemption. A wholly-owned subsidiary of a non-Bermudian parent may in narrow circumstances claim non-public filing status; absent a written dispensation from the Minister of Finance, audited Bermuda accounts must be filed with the Certificate of Compliance. Hidden overseas trading accounts must be consolidated into the Bermuda entity's audited accounts where they form part of the Bermuda company's undertaking. Late filing incurs civil default fines, late-payment surcharges on the Government fee, and potential automatic strike-off. Every director and officer 'knowingly and wilfully' permitting default is personally liable to a statutory default fine, and the court may order disqualification from acting as a director in Bermuda.
Part D — Tax-Protected Status Declaration Declaration confirming that the company is an exempted undertaking protected by the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 and is outside the charge of Bermuda income tax. The declaration must be reconciled with the ESD tax-residence election; claiming Bermuda tax protection while simultaneously declaring UK tax residence on the ESD is a fatal inconsistency that the Registrar will challenge through information exchange.
