Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, Government of Bermuda (Bermuda Companies Registry, Hamilton)

Governing statute

Companies Act 1981 (Bermuda), as amended — ss. 4-8 (incorporation), ss. 9-13 (exempted company regime), s. 14 (name restrictions), ss. 15-16 (registered office and agent), ss. 19-21 (directors and secretary); supplemented by the Economic Substance (Registration and Reporting) Rules 2018 and the Companies (Fees) Regulations.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum of Association and Bye-Laws filed electronically via the Bermuda Registrar of Companies e-Registry, accompanied by Form ExCo-1 (Exempted Company Incorporation Questionnaire) and a statutory declaration of compliance.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; authorized share capital is set in the Memorandum of Association in any major currency, with at least one ordinary subscriber share to be allotted and issued. No escrow account or capital deposit requirement applies to an Exempted Company.

Funding & unlock path: Not mandated for a virtual exempted company without premises. If a Bermuda office or warehouse is leased, prior clearance is required from the Bermuda Fire and Rescue Service (fire safety certificate), the Department of Property Management (occupancy permit based on structural inspection), and the Occupational Safety and Health Division (workplace hazard assessment under the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1982).

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; statutory online portal: taxcommissioner.gov.bm (CIT registration and payroll tax filings). Entity incorporation is administered by the Registrar of Companies, Bermuda (roc.gov.bm).

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Company is first incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 through the Registrar of Companies, receiving a Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum of association and by-laws, and a registered office in Bermuda. For Bermuda corporate income tax (CIT) purposes under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the constituent entity must file an entity registration notification with the OTC via the CIT online portal, obtain a Bermuda TIN from the OTC, and receive a certificate of CIT registration. Entities falling below the €750M multinational group revenue threshold are nevertheless encouraged to register for a voluntary TIN to facilitate payroll tax, customs, and economic substance compliance. Bermuda does not issue VAT IDs because there is no VAT system. Effective date for mandatory CIT registration is fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — The Bermuda Exempted Company is first incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 through the Registrar of Companies, receiving a Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum of association and by-laws, and a registered office in Bermuda. For Bermuda corporate income tax (CIT) purposes under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the constituent entity must file an entity registration notification with the OTC via the CIT online portal, obtain a Bermuda TIN from the OTC, and receive a certificate of CIT registration. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda does not maintain a US-style check-the-box classification regime. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, a Bermuda multinational group may elect (1) to be treated as a single tax group for CIT consolidation and filing, (2) to apply the transitional qualified domestic minimum top-up tax safe harbour election, and (3) to elect the application of certifiable qualified domestic minimum top-up tax credits offset against the global minimum top-up tax. Hybrid mismatch elections under OECD Pillar Two rules are available where the Bermuda CIT rules overlap with parent jurisdiction rules. All default entities remain classified as Tax_Exempt_Zone unless the €750M revenue threshold is crossed, at which point entity-level taxation (15% top-up tax) applies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Bermuda; the applicable indirect taxation consists of customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1946, stamp duty on certain instruments, and the payroll tax (Payroll Tax Act 1995), which is a direct employment levy. Cross-border e-commerce deliveries are therefore outside any Bermuda sales tax or VAT charge.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - Bermuda does not operate a VAT/GST regime, so no registration threshold applies. Any person or entity importing goods into Bermuda must register with Bermuda Customs as an importer (no monetary threshold) and obtain a Customs Account Number (CAN) for ASYCUDA World clearance. A licensed Bermuda customs broker must be appointed for physical import clearance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda levies no VAT or GST and has no zero-rating or export refund mechanism. Bermuda exports are wholly outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation, and Bermuda Exempted Companies are exempt from Bermuda income tax on offshore profits (subject only to the new 15% CIT applying to multinational groups exceeding €750M global revenue). For COD e-commerce exports from emerging markets, zero-rating substantiation is required only under the local destination-market VAT rules, not under Bermuda law.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged in ASYCUDA World
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export movement
  • Commercial Invoice clearly stating consignee, value, and export reason
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (Form A / EUR-1 where preferential origin claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/FX settlement evidence from Airwallex or local logistics remittance rails

Bermuda has no real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no continuous transaction control (CTC) regime, no API tax clearance obligation, and no digital stamping requirement. Bermuda Customs operates the ASYCUDA World electronic single window, through which all import/export declarations must be lodged electronically. E-commerce operators with cash-on-delivery settlement must submit cargo manifests, declared values, and supporting documents through ASYCUDA World; no OTC fiscal API integration is required.

Because Bermuda has no VAT zero-rating or refund mechanism, the principal audit exposure arises under the Customs Act 1946 and Customs Tariff Act 1946: false declarations, undervaluation, or fraudulent export documentation attract fines of up to BMD 10,000 per offence, forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment for up to 2 years. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and Income Tax Act 1986, any misstatement, abusive election, or improper claim relating to qualified domestic minimum top-up tax credits or reliefs may result in penalties of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, statutory interest at 10% per annum, retrospective clawback of credited amounts, and a six-year extended assessment window for the OTC.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (Currency Act 1972) and circulates interchangeably with USD. Both capital and current accounts are fully convertible; Bermuda law imposes no exchange controls, no capital-export restriction, no profit-remittance quota, and no withholding tax on dividends or interest paid by an Exempted Company. The only practical remittance hurdle is commercial bank-level source-of-funds and sanctions compliance for outbound flows to emerging-market counterparties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline. Trade receivables from COD e-commerce may be held indefinitely in USD, BMD or any other currency in Bermudian or offshore accounts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), constituted under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; the sole regulator licensing and supervising banks, deposit companies and money service businesses under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High for settlement-layer execution, Medium for full banking substitution. Airwallex is an FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institution, not a Bermuda-licensed bank; it may serve as the multi-currency payout and collection ring-fence for emerging-market COD flows, but BMD liquidity and statutory deposit protection require a BMA-licensed tier-1 bank as the clearing terminus. Legal constraint: any Bermuda-incorporated remittance or money-service activity requires BMA licensing or registration; local logistics partners collecting cash-on-delivery must either hold such licence or operate strictly as disclosed collecting agents of the Exempted Company under a written AML/CTF-compliant agency agreement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder funds are transmitted by SWIFT MT103 from a G7/UK/EU corporate account in USD or BMD; the remittance must state OECD BPM6 purpose 'Direct Investment - Equity Injection' (bank purpose code CAPT/INVP in field 70) and include the funding shareholder's name to match pre-agreed KYC. The receiving BMA bank requires a certified board capital-resolution, the Beneficial Ownership Register, and source-of-funds declarations before crediting the Escrow Account.
