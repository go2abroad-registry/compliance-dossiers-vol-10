Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda (Ministry of Finance) for payroll tax and general tax administration; Registrar of Companies, Bermuda (Ministry of Finance) for entity incorporation and annual government fees; Bermuda Customs Service (Ministry of Finance) for import/export duty administration. All filings are made via the Bermuda Business Portal (portal.one.gov.bm) or direct registry submissions.

E-government filing portal

For a Bermuda Exempted Company, the foundational identifier is the Company Registration Number obtained from the Registrar of Companies upon incorporation under the Companies Act 1981; this number functions as the de facto tax identifier because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax and issues no income-tax TIN. If the entity employs staff, it must register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain an Employer Payroll Tax Account Number under the Payroll Tax Act 1995. If the entity imports or exports physical goods, it must register with the Bermuda Customs Service to obtain a Customs Declarant/Account number for processing declarations under the Customs Act 1960 and the Bermuda Customs Tariff. Non-resident owners may hold 100% of the shares without any local participation requirement, and no minimum capital or tax pre-clearance is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 allows the entity to apply to the Minister of Finance for a Tax Exemption Certificate guaranteeing that no Bermuda taxes (including any future income, capital gains, or profits tax) will apply until March 2035, with the possibility of renewal. There is no domestic check-the-box election because Bermuda has no income tax system; however, the entity may be independently classified by foreign tax authorities (e.g., a US check-the-box election or UK CA 2009 s18A classification). Under the Economic Substance Act 2018, the entity must file an annual Economic Substance Declaration and demonstrate adequate substance in Bermuda if it carries on a relevant activity. Hybrid mismatch rules under UK CTA 2010 Part 6A may affect UK-resident counterparties but have no direct Bermuda filing consequence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST or sales and use tax; Bermuda Customs Tariff (import duties) and Employer Payroll Tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no such tax exists in Bermuda. Customs import duty registration is mandatory for all commercial importers regardless of transaction value or frequency. Payroll tax registration is required only when the entity employs individuals in Bermuda; the payroll tax applies to total remuneration at progressive employer rates (standard employer rate approximately 5.25% with higher rates for high-income non-Bermudian employees), but this is not a turnover-based indirect tax and does not apply to the entity's export sale proceeds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the traditional VAT sense because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST on supplies, whether domestic or exported. Exports of dental ceramic blocks and milling discs are entirely outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax, and the Bermuda Customs Tariff does not levy export duty on most goods. To ensure clean treatment, the entity must file a valid Customs Export Declaration (e-SAD) through Bermuda Customs Service, evidence that the goods physically left Bermuda (bill of lading / air waybill), and maintain the commercial invoice and packing list. No input VAT refund mechanism exists because there is no input VAT to recover.

  • Customs Export Declaration (e-SAD) filed through Bermuda Customs Service and Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice and detailed Packing List identifying ISIC 3250 dental zirconia blocks
  • Proof of export (signed Bill of Lading or Air Waybill) and, where applicable, the destination country's import documentation; Bermuda has no exchange controls, so no government-issued Inward Remittance Certificate is required, but bank SWIFT/TT receipts should be retained for audit.

Not applicable for VAT e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital stamping. Bermuda Customs Service operates an electronic single-window environment using the e-SAD (Electronic Single Administrative Document) and the Customs Declaration System for import and export clearance; all commercial shipments of goods must be declared electronically through this system. For the dental zirconia block manufacturing scope, B2B commercial invoices are governed by ordinary common law and accounting standards, with no statutory digital stamping obligation.

No VAT clawback or zero-rating penalty can arise because no VAT/GST applies in Bermuda. False or fraudulent customs export declarations may trigger penalties under the Customs Act 1960, including fines of up to three times the dutiable value of the goods, forfeiture of goods, and criminal liability for deliberate misdeclaration. Failure to file the annual Economic Substance Declaration or to maintain adequate economic substance where required may result in financial penalties under the Economic Substance Act 2018, including fixed fines and, in serious cases, striking-off by the Registrar of Companies. Failure to pay the annual exempted company government fee results in late-payment penalties and potential dissolution. In the absence of exchange control legislation, there is no statutory deadline for repatriating export proceeds, and no withholding tax or corporate tax clawback applies to offshore sales revenue.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged one-for-one to the US Dollar, and both current-account and capital-account convertibility are effectively unrestricted for a non-resident designated exempted company. The company's BMA-issued Exchange Control Permission authorizes the unrestricted holding and disposition of USD, EUR, GBP and other major foreign currencies without prior central-bank approval for operational remittances, intercompany loans, third-party trade payments, or capital distributions. Dividend, interest, royalty, and liquidation proceeds may be remitted freely to the UK parent with zero Bermuda withholding tax under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, subject only to the company maintaining an authorized bank relationship, filing its annual economic substance notification, and remaining in good standing under the Companies Act 1981. The practical hurdles to full convertibility are bank-level ECDD, the lack of a local physical branch handling cash, and the BMA's expectation that all cross-border flows remain consistent with the approved business profile of dental block manufacturing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio on a non-resident exempted company. Export proceeds received in USD, EUR, GBP, or other foreign currencies may be retained indefinitely in the company's Bermuda-licensed account without being converted into BMD, and there is no 30-day surrender obligation analogous to certain other jurisdictions. Where BMD-denominated receipts arise, conversion into USD occurs at the fixed BMD:USD 1:1 peg through the authorized dealing bank without prior BMA approval. All export receipts must remain traceable to underlying dental-block sales contracts, purchase orders, and invoices, and are reported in the annual BMA exchange-control return and economic substance declaration.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as Bermuda's central bank and exchange-control administrator under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972; the Bank of England exercises no direct exchange-control or prudential oversight over Bermuda-licensed banks

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Medium-Low. A UK (FCA) or EEA-regulated electronic-money institution (EMI) may be deployed as a supplementary collection and disbursement rail for low-value, high-frequency GBP and EUR trade flows from UK and EU dental laboratories, but it cannot replace a Bermuda-licensed Authorized Bank as the principal liquidity and clearing account. Legal constraints: (i) an offshore EMI holds no Bermuda banking licence and cannot perform the exchange-control reporting duties expected of an authorized dealer under BMA policy; (ii) most EMI account terms exclude Bermuda-incorporated exempted companies due to correspondent-banking de-risking and non-resident corporate risk profiles; and (iii) USD clearing through the EMI's US partner bank is normally refused without a parent-bank guarantee from a licensed Bermuda institution. Accordingly, the treasury blueprint uses an EMI only for European receivables collection and keeps core USD liquidity in Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital is injected from the UK parent by SWIFT MT103 wire transfer in USD, or in GBP converted through the BMD:USD 1:1 peg, to the Bermuda Authorized Bank account held in the name of the exempted company. For equity subscriptions, wire instruction field 70 must state 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION — SHARE CAPITAL' and the bank receives a certified board resolution, share-allotment minutes, and the updated Register of Members; for intercompany loans, field 70 must state 'INTERCOMPANY LOAN — FACILITY AGREEMENT' and the supporting signed facility agreement, board resolutions of both parties, and interest-rate schedule must be pre-delivered to the compliance desk. The receiving bank verifies the stated purpose against the company's approved activity profile and the BMA Exchange Control Permission; no prior BMA licence is required for inbound funding, but wires above USD 1,000,000 equivalent trigger enhanced due diligence and may require audited financial projections, a source-of-funds breakdown, and a confirmation that the funds will be deployed for raw-material procurement or capital equipment consistent with the dental-block manufacturing scope.Primary clearing rail: SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for routine supplier payments, refunds, and operating expenses, settled through Fedwire or CHIPS for USD and CHAPS for GBP, with remittance advice always referencing the purchase order, invoice number, and contract code. Secondary rail: irrevocable documentary commercial letters of credit governed by UCP 600 for bulk raw-material purchases such as zirconia powder and milling equipment from Asian suppliers, issued by the Bermuda bank and advised or confirmed through a correspondent bank in the supplier's country; required documents comprise the commercial invoice, clean on-board bill of lading, certificate of origin, packing list, and ISO batch-release certificate, with payment at sight or at 30 to 90 days after shipment. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a standard Bermuda Exempted Company; 15% domestic minimum top-up tax applies only to in-scope large multinational groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company, whether to residents or non-residents.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

CIT return and payment are due no later than 15 months after the last day of the in-scope group's fiscal year. Advance registration is required within 3 months of becoming subject to CIT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing documentation obligation applies to an ordinary exempted company outside the CIT regime. For a multinational group that is in scope for Bermuda CIT (consolidated revenue at or above EUR 750 million), Bermuda applies OECD-aligned transfer-pricing rules; a Local File is expected for material Bermuda intercompany transactions (commonly transactions exceeding BMD 5 million in aggregate for the fiscal year) and a Master File is expected for the group as a whole. Ordinary dental ceramic block manufacturers below the CIT threshold are not required to prepare Bermuda transfer-pricing documentation, but contemporaneous documentation should nevertheless be retained to support cross-border pricing with related-party distributors and raw-material suppliers.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply only to entities carrying on listed relevant activities. The manufacture of biocompatible dental ceramic blocks is not a relevant activity. However, if the exempted company also operates as a distribution and service centre, financing entity, headquarters, or IP holding company in Bermuda, it must satisfy economic substance by having an active management presence in Bermuda, board meetings locally, adequate employees, suitable premises, and commensurate local operating expenditure. The ESR annual declaration is mandatory for any entity carrying on a relevant activity; a purely manufacturing business should document why no relevant activity is present. In-scope CIT multinational groups are separately subject to OECD economic substance and allocation rules under the Bermuda CIT Act, and there is no grandfathering of substance exemptions for entities that become subject to CIT.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false declarations

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to 100% of understated CIT, statutory interest, and daily default surcharges; criminal fines and imprisonment for willful false filing under Bermuda CIT and Companies Act provisions. Foreign tax authorities may use mutual legal assistance and tax information exchange agreements to pursue evasion through the Bermuda authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign false declarations or orchestrate sham structures face personal fines, civil restitution orders, disqualification orders, travel bans, and imprisonment for fraud or money laundering. Courts will pierce the corporate veil when the company is a mere façade.
Shell company / lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations penalties of up to BMD 250,000 per entity, daily default penalties for continuing non-compliance, and administrative strike-off. The Registrar may compel dissolution and restitution of public file misrepresentations.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly allow a shell company to make false substance statements may be personally fined, disqualified, and imprisoned. The court may also issue asset freezing and travel restriction orders to prevent the director from removing corporate funds.
AML / beneficial ownership non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Beneficial ownership fines, daily penalties for non-filing, and AML/CFT supervisory penalties under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Regulations. Criminal liability attaches to intentional money-laundering through the corporate vehicle.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally liable for knowingly permitting a secret ownership structure. Sanctions include personal fines, confiscation orders, disqualification, travel bans, and custodial sentences.
Fraudulent trading and illegal distributions

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies Act 1981, fraudulent trading is a civil and criminal offense. The court may order repayment of distributions, voidance of preferences, and unlimited personal liability for corporate debts incurred in fraudulent trading.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and any person knowingly party to fraudulent trading may be ordered to contribute to the company's assets, are subject to disqualification, and may face travel bans pending civil proceedings and imprisonment for criminal fraud.
Transfer pricing / cross-border profit shifting

Statutory sanction: Bermuda CIT transfer-pricing adjustments plus interest and penalties; local and master filing penalties and daily default penalties; adverse publication of the entity as non-compliant under the CIT administrative framework.

Director personal liability: Directors and responsible officers who sign inaccurate transfer-pricing documentation without reasonable grounds may be personally subject to penalties, disqualification proceedings, and liability for amounts evaded where the arrangement is found to be artificial or sham.
