Applicable AML statute: Governed principally by (1) the Bermuda Companies Act 1981 (incorporation, exempted-company licensing by the Minister of Finance, registered office and filing duties); (2) the Beneficial Ownership Act 2021 and the Beneficial Ownership (Companies) Regulations 2022 (confidential UBO register maintained with the Registrar of Companies); (3) the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Act 2022 (AML/ATF Act 2022, in force 1 September 2023) together with the mandatory AML/ATF Code of Practice, administered by the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as AML/CFT supervisor; (4) the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 (confiscation, restraint and money-laundering offences); (5) the Financial Intelligence Agency Act 2008 (Financial Intelligence Agency as the Bermuda FIU); (6) the Corporate Service Provider Business Act 2000 (licensing of registered-office and corporate-services providers); and (7) the Economic Substance Act 2018 and Economic Substance (Companies) Regulations 2020 (economic substance attestation for relevant activities).
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A physical registered office in Bermuda is statutorily mandatory. Section 15 of the Companies Act 1981 requires every exempted company to maintain a registered office in Bermuda that is open to the public, where process can be served and where the statutory records are kept. A purely virtual office (e.g., a non-physical address, PO Box only, or a remote administrative arrangement without a fixed Bermuda location) is not permissible as a substitute. The accepted market structure is a physical registered-office suite supplied by a BMA-licensed corporate services provider in Hamilton, which may combine mail-forwarding and virtual administrative services, provided the physical address remains the company's statutory seat and the CSP acts as the address custodian and UBO-register agent.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance (Accountant General and Office of the Tax Commissioner) for residual fiscal matters; Registrar of Companies (Bermuda Government) for corporate registration, exempted status, and annual Government Fee assessment; official portal: www.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

No statutory income tax TIN or VAT ID is issued in Bermuda because an exempted company is outside the income tax net. The formal identifier is the Certificate of Incorporation number issued by the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981, supplemented by the annual Exempted Government Fee assessment reference. A foreign owner should request the Certificate of Exempted Status, the memorandum of association, and the register of members. If the company later employs Bermuda-resident individuals, the employer must register for a Payroll Tax Account with the Office of the Tax Commissioner; no payroll tax is payable for employees not working in Bermuda. No economic substance registration is triggered because the activity of operating a bulk transactional email API and newsletter hosting platform is not a relevant activity under the Economic Substance Act 2018, but an annual economic substance declaration may still be required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Bermuda Exempted Company is a legal entity that is exempt from Bermuda income tax, capital gains tax, dividends tax, and withholding tax under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act (protected until 2035). Income from email infrastructure SaaS services supplied to non-Bermudian clients is earned outside Bermuda's tax base. The company is taxed at entity level rather than as a pass-through, but all Bermudian tax liability is zero. Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or comparable entity classification election exists in Bermudian law. An exempted company may elect, with the Registrar of Companies, to be designated as a non-resident undertaking for exchange control purposes. It may apply for a specific Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act to lock in the absence of future taxes. No hybrid mismatch election is available under Bermuda domestic law; however, if the company is treated as transparent by a foreign member state, the UK and EU hybrid mismatch rules may require the Bermudian entity to be regarded as taxable in Bermuda, and no Bermudian tax charge will arise, reducing the efficacy of any hybrid mismatch planning.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. The subject entity, being a 100% foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company designated as 'non-resident' for exchange control purposes, enjoys full current-account and capital-account convertibility without BMA prior approval for foreign-currency transactions. Profit remittance to the UK parent in the form of dividends, interest, royalties, or capital distributions is unrestricted and free of any Bermuda withholding tax, subject only to the entity maintaining its non-resident designation and satisfying the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 filing and substance obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion requirement applies to this non-resident designated Exempted Company; all export proceeds, including SaaS subscription fees and email infrastructure service revenues, may be retained, held, and disbursed in USD, EUR, GBP, or any other convertible currency without mandatory conversion into BMD. Resident Bermuda entities are separately subject to historic BMA surrender timelines for foreign-currency receipts, which do not apply here.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; regulates banks and administers exchange control under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, together with BMA Notices and AML/ATF rules under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as an ancillary collection layer, Low as a primary clearing bank. The BMA licensing framework remains a hard constraint: a Bermuda-domiciled money transmission or e-money service requires a licence under the Financial Institutions Act 2003 (and related payment-services regulation), and unlicensed offshore EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, or Revolut cannot legally clear BMD/USD transactions inside Bermuda. They may be used by the UK parent solely as a European card-collection and conversion pass-through, with final settlement routed by SWIFT MT103 into the Bermuda operational account. The compliance architecture must prohibit the EMI from being advertised as the entity's 'bank' and must maintain all treasury concentration balances at a BMA-licensed bank.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

The ESR return must be filed within six months after the end of the accounting period, or by 31 January of the following calendar year, whichever is later; the corporate registry annual declaration and government fee are due by the anniversary of incorporation.

An email-newsletter infrastructure SaaS company normally qualifies as a 'pure equity holding company' or falls under Software/IT-Services CIGA; if the company is substantially owned by UK tax residents, UK CFC rules must also be analysed. Item D - Core Income Generating Activity Test (ESR) Declaration of core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda, including staffing, premises, servers, and management decisions. If all CIGA is performed outside Bermuda, the company must declare non-compliance or demonstrate economic substance through local management and operational presence. Late filing of the ESR declaration incurs a daily penalty of BMD 200, capped at BMD 30,000 per reporting period; continued failure may result in additional compound penalties, automatic strike-off, and the disqualification of the registered agent.
Item E - Income and Expenditure Declaration Gross revenue attributable to relevant activity and Bermuda operating expenditure, including payroll by category. This is not a tax computation and no Bermuda tax is assessed; the figure is used solely to assess economic substance proportionality. Late filing of the ESR declaration incurs a daily penalty of BMD 200, capped at BMD 30,000 per reporting period; continued failure may result in additional compound penalties, automatic strike-off, and the disqualification of the registered agent.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not triggered locally: Bermuda has no income tax system and therefore no statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold. However, where the Bermuda company is a member of a UK-headquartered multinational group, HMRC transfer-pricing rules apply under Part 4 TA 2010, and group-level Master File documentation becomes mandatory in practice once UK cross-border controlled transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 intercompany revenue or financing, irrespective of Bermuda domestic filing obligations.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 and the Income Tax Amendment Act 2019 apply to exempted companies with relevant activities. Software code hosting, transactional email API services, and newsletter distribution infrastructure fall within the 'providing of services' or 'intellectual property business' categories. To pass the substance test, the Bermuda entity must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Bermuda, including strategic commercial decision-making, employee presence, physical premises, and adequate local operating expenditure. A pure 'mailbox' arrangement is treated as non-compliant, irrespective of its zero-tax structure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Artificial Avoidance

Statutory sanction: UK HMRC may re-characterise the arrangement under the UK CFC rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 9A), impose a UK diverted profits tax of 19% to 25% on alleged artificial avoidance profits, apply accelerated payment notices, and assess UK group relief adjustments. In Bermuda, a false ESR declaration attracts a penalty of up to BMD 250,000 and daily non-compliance penalties of BMD 200 per day.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign incorrect filings or cause the company to make fraudulent representations may face HMRC personal liability notices, disqualification as UK directors for up to 15 years, civil recovery actions for diverted profits, travel bans in cross-border tax fraud investigations, and custodial sentences of up to 7 years under UK tax fraud legislation.
Shell Company ESR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance (Administration and Enforcement) Regulations 2019, the Registrar may impose a maximum financial penalty of BMD 250,000, daily penalties of BMD 200 for continued non-filing, compel production of records under notice, and initiate strike-off proceedings under the Companies Act 1981.

Director personal liability: Directors are subject to personal civil restitution for misleading ESG and ESR declarations, disqualification orders under Bermuda company law, prohibition from acting as a director of any Bermuda entity for a statutory period, and referral to the Financial Sanctions Implementation Unit for related money-laundering offences where deceptive conduct conceals proceeds.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with Bermuda AML/ATF obligations under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Regulations 2018 attracts penalties imposed by the Bermuda Monetary Authority, fines of up to BMD 1,000,000 per breach for corporate entities, and possible strike-off for continued non-filing.

Director personal liability: Directors may face criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to 15 years for money-laundering facilitation, personal fines, civil asset recovery, restriction orders against leaving Bermuda, disqualification from UK company directorships under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, and joint and several liability for illegal payment flows routed through the SaaS platform.
