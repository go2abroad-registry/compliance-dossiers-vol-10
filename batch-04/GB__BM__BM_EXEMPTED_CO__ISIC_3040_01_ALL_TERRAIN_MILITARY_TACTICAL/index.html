Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

A Bermuda Exempted Company may file a statutory election under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing exemption from all future Bermuda taxes (other than payroll tax, land tax, and customs duties) until 2035. It may also elect to be treated as non-resident for Exchange Control Act 1972 purposes, conditioning its permitted business activities. In addition, an in-scope multinational group may file a corporate income tax status election under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 for Pillar Two safe harbour purposes. Bermuda law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda levies selectively applied customs import duties, stamp duties, payroll tax, and land tax only.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Importers of commercial goods must obtain a customs identification number and relevant import licences under the Customs Tariff Act 1968; employers are required to register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax when annual local payroll exceeds BMD 25,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because there is no Bermuda VAT/GST to zero-rate. Exports of tangible goods from Bermuda, including all-terrain military tactical vehicles, are entirely outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation. Where import duty was paid on goods or components subsequently exported, the Exempted Company may claim a duty drawback under the Customs Tariff Act 1968 subject to proof of export within 12 months of importation and registration as an approved exporter or manufacturing warehouse.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, GDPR-style tax clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda. Customs entry documentation for import/export of goods is submitted electronically through the Bermuda Customs e-Declarations portal; the filing is used solely for duty assessment and trade statistics and does not operate as a VAT clearance mechanism.

There is no VAT zero-rating or VAT clawback mechanism in Bermuda. However, false export declarations for duty-drawback claims attract penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 1968, including forfeiture of goods, a penalty of up to three times the full dutiable value, and retrospective recovery of the drawback with interest if the export proof is not substantiated within the 12-month statutory period. Military tactical vehicle exports also require a UK-controlled export licence under applicable Sanctions and Export Control instruments; undertaking or facilitating an unlicensed re-supply triggers UK and Bermuda enforcement action, including unlimited fines, imprisonment, suspension or revocation of the Exempted Company's statutory tax exemption, and remedial clawback of any customs duty relief granted.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is legally pegged to the US dollar at 1:1; current-account convertibility is unrestricted for a non-resident-designated Exempted Company. Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on dividends, interest or royalties, hence profit remittance is effected through an ordinary board-approved dividend or management-fee payment, subject only to Bermuda Companies Act solvency certification and CRS/FATCA reporting. Capital-account transactions are governed by the Exchange Control Act 1972, under which the BMA grants non-resident designation and issues General Consent; once designated, share allotments, capital subscriptions and liquidation proceeds may move freely in any major currency through an authorized dealer, being the operating bank. Material hurdles to convertibility are therefore not Bermudian but extraterritorial: OFAC/OFSI sanctions screening of the ultimate military end-user, EU/UK arms embargoes, and correspondent-bank denial risk for defense-related USD clearing via CHIPS/Fedwire.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender, repatriation timeline, or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds; receivables contracted in USD, GBP, EUR or CNY may remain freely held in foreign-currency accounts without conversion. The applicable regulatory gates are authorization-based: exports of all-terrain military tactical vehicles and controlled components by the group must be covered by a UK export licence under the Export Control Act 2002 and Export Control Order 2008 as extended to Bermuda by Order in Council, or by the export-control authorization of the country of beneficial manufacture; settlement must be supported by invoice, packing list, transport document and end-user certificate, and cleared through the authorized bank's sanctions-screening engine before credit is released.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — Bermuda central bank, currency authority and consolidated financial services regulator; HM Treasury and Bank of England as sovereign parent-state authorities; UK Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) for asset-freeze and arms-embargo administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low-Medium as a supplementary rail only. Bermuda does not maintain a dedicated e-money institution (EMI) licence class; digital payment providers require authorisation under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1991 or the Digital Asset Business Act 2018, neither of which is designed for ordinary cross-border corporate treasury settlement. Offshore EMIs (UK FCA-authorised e-money institutions or EU passported EMI/PSP entities) generally exclude merchant category ISIC 3040 / NAICS 336992, being defense tactical hardware, in their acceptable-use policies; onboarding would trigger immediate reputational review and high account-freeze risk. A pragmatic structure is to restrict EMI usage to governed-office expense disbursements and payroll in low-risk corridors, while directing all defense-export receipts exclusively through tier-1 correspondent-connected commercial banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity capital must be injected by SWIFT MT103/MT202 COV from the UBO's regulated banking account to the Bermuda bank via its USD correspondent, typically JPMorgan, BNY Mellon or Citibank New York, using ISO 20022 purpose code EQUI or CAPT and narrative referencing: Share capital contribution for the named Bermuda Exempted Company, non-resident designated account. Funds must arrive after a Board allotment resolution under the Exempted Companies Act 1981, be receipted into a segregated Capital Escrow Account, and be reconciled against the statutory allotment statement delivered to the Registrar of Companies before share certificates are issued. The operating bank, as authorized dealer, holds BMA General Consent to credit non-resident capital; the BMA must be notified where the issue exceeds delegated-dealer thresholds. Wire verification relies on SWIFT gpi UETR tracking and on the correspondent bank's OFAC pre-screening of the remitter and ultimate funder.Outbound settlements will use four clearing rails: first, single vendor payments under the operable threshold via SWIFT MT103, with USD over CHIPS/Fedwire, EUR via TARGET2, and GBP via CHAPS; second, documentary import and export Commercial Letters of Credit under UCP600 using MT700/MT707 for shipment-collateralized component purchases and final-vehicle deliveries; third, standby letters of credit via MT760 for performance bonds, advance-payment guarantees and warranty obligations to sovereign ministries of defense; and fourth, MT202 COV for cover payments of non-USD legs. Each message carries the UETR, full structured remitter and beneficiary address, invoice and export-licence references, and the final military end-user name; release of payment is subject to the bank's transaction-monitoring engine confirming no embargoed destination, no sanctioned owner, and matching of the LC documents comprising end-user certificate, air waybill or bill of lading, and packing list. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% under the traditional Bermuda Exempted Company regime; 15% for qualifying multinational groups subject to the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (OECD Pillar Two GloBE implementation), with no branch profit tax or capital gains tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda does not impose dividend withholding tax; an exempted company may declare and pay dividends to non-resident shareholders without Bermuda withholding or exchange controls, subject to solvency and sanctioned jurisdiction checks.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the anniversary date of incorporation, unless extended administratively; or within the date fixed by the Registrar for exempted companies with financial statements.

Deadline: · Frequency:

For in-scope entities under the Bermuda CIT Act 2023, the annual CIT return is due within six months after the fiscal year-end; estimated tax payments are due on the 15th day of the fourth, sixth, ninth, and twelfth months of the fiscal year (as prescribed by CIT regulations).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation applies to a purely tax-exempt Bermuda exempted company. For in-scope CIT multinational groups, the Bermuda Tax Commissioner requires contemporaneous Master File and Local File where consolidated group revenue is EUR 750 million or more; Local File is triggered for intercompany transactions exceeding BMD 1,000,000 (or equivalent) in aggregate, determined under OECD-compliant Bermuda CIT guidance. Defense procurement contracts with state buyers should be priced commercially and documented even if below the threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment (2019) and Economic Substance Regulations apply to resident entities carrying on specified 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution, and intellectual property/service business). Manufacture of armored security transport vehicles under ISIC 3040 is not a designated relevant activity; however, a CIT-resident or treaty-claiming entity must demonstrate full management and control from Bermuda, maintain adequate physical presence and payroll, and conduct core income-generating activities in Bermuda to avoid substance regulatory penalties. Defense manufacturing operations may nevertheless be subject to licensing and end-use audits by the financial action task force competent authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Disgorgement of all profits derived from the sham structure; financial penalties up to BMD 1,000,000 under the Revenue (Tax Information Exchange) provisions and the Criminal Justice (Proceeds of Crime) Act; deregistration of the exempted company; automatic referral to the Bermuda Police Financial Crime Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are personally liable for fraudulent involvement: civil restitution of diverted profits, travel ban pending criminal investigation, and imprisonment up to seven years for tax or proceeds-of-crime offences.
Export Control / Defense Trade Sanction Evasion

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed arms brokering is subject to the Bermuda Arms and Ammunition (Prohibited) legislation: custodial sentence up to seven years and an unlimited fine; corporate license revocation; seizure of the vehicles and proceeds.

Director personal liability: Individual board members knowingly engaging can be imprisoned up to seven years, disqualified from corporate management, and held jointly/severally liable to pay contractual compensation to the affected state buyer.
Anti-Money Laundering / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Bermuda proceeds of crime and anti-money laundering penalties reach BMD 100,000 per contravention, plus registration suspension; failure to file beneficial ownership particulars triggers civil penalties and remedial strike-off; banking channels are closed.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined up to BMD 100,000, barred by the Supreme Court from acting as managers, and, in money laundering cases, imprisoned for up to five years; funds and assets are subject to freezing orders and forfeiture.
Economic Substance / Shell Company Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Amendment, the Registrar may impose an initial penalty of BMD 10,000, escalating to BMD 500,000 for continued non-compliance, and may strike the entity off the register or initiate tax residence revocation.

Director personal liability: Directors who attest false economic substance declarations commit an offence punishable by imprisonment up to two years; civil recovery powers allow liquidation proceedings against the entity with director disqualification for up to five years.
