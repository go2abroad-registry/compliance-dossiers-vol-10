Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (portal: https://www.gov.bm/tax-information); Bermuda Customs (Department of Customs) for import duties; Corporate Income Tax (CIT) registration administered by the Tax Commissioner under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN. The Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated by the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981; no tax registration is required for pure holding or exempted activity. If the company employs staff in Bermuda, it must register as an employer for Payroll Tax and Social Insurance with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and the Social Insurance Board; the Payroll Tax employer account number serves as the operative tax identifier. For a locally operating tea salon, a 'Permit to Carry on Business in Bermuda' must be obtained from the Department of Business Services, and the business must register with the Bermuda Revenue Authority for payroll tax. In-scope MNE groups (global revenue EUR 750M+) must separately register for CIT on the Bermuda CIT portal within 30 days of entering scope.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Companies are taxed on no income or capital gains; a Tax Assurance Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 guarantees exemption from future income taxes until 2035 (extendable to 2050 for newly incorporated entities). Local payroll tax of up to 10% applies only to Bermudian employee remuneration, and the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 introduces a 15% CIT for in-scope multinational groups; a standalone specialty tea salon below the MNE threshold remains outside CIT scope.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Bermuda law. Available statutory mechanisms: (1) application for a Tax Assurance Certificate (Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966) from the Registrar of Companies with the Minister of Finance's approval; (2) for in-scope MNE groups, an election to be treated as resident in Bermuda for CIT purposes and to form a Bermuda group for GloBE/Pillar Two filing; (3) employer election for annual Payroll Tax payment by installments or withholding on wages; (4) election to apply Economic Substance Regulations notification requirements (ESN) to the exempted company's relevant activity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or Sales-and-Use-Tax regime in Bermuda; the operative indirect tax is Bermuda Customs Import Duty (ad valorem and specific rates by tariff heading), levied on goods entering the Territory.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Customs Duty is payable at the port of importation on every consignment regardless of value; Payroll Tax registration is triggered at the first day of employee engagement; Social Insurance contribution registration is triggered at the first hire. A local business license must be renewed annually via the Department of Business Services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Bermuda levies no VAT/GST, exports of goods or services (including specialty tea, ceramic salon products, and hospitality services supplied to overseas customers) fall outside the scope of domestic indirect taxation and attract a de facto 0% rate without a formal zero-rating claim. For goods imported into Bermuda and subsequently re-exported, a refund or relief of Customs Duty may be claimed under the Customs Act 1949 (re-export provisions) if the goods remain unused and are exported within six months of importation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Bermuda
  • Commercial invoice and packing list, plus proof of import duty paid (entry number) for re-export relief claims

None — Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoice API submission, digital stamping, or a continuous transaction control (CTC) regime. Customs entries are filed electronically via the Bermuda Customs Automated Commercial Environment but are not subject to continuous fiscal bright-line validation. No VAT e-reporting obligations apply.

The Comptroller of Customs may conduct post-clearance audits for up to six years after export. False declarations, intentional mis-description of goods, or fraudulent re-export claims attract forfeiture of the goods, monetary penalties up to treble the value of the duty evaded, suspension or revocation of the customs bond, and criminal prosecution under the Customs Act 1949 and the Criminal Code Act 1907. Where the tea salon is connected to an in-scope MNE group, the Tax Commissioner may additionally claw back any improperly obtained CIT relief or issue failure-to-file penalties of BMD 10,000 plus BMD 1,000 per month of default, and interest on unpaid amounts at the statutory rate.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar (USD). For a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as 'non-resident' for exchange control purposes, both current and capital account convertibility are broadly available. Profit remittances are permitted without exchange control approval to scheduled territories (including the United Kingdom) and are freely transferable, subject to usual solvency and board resolution documentation. Remittances to non-scheduled territories require BMA notification or prior approval; dividends must be declared in accordance with the Companies Act 1981 and supported by audited financial statements. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax, exchange tax, or capital controls on dividend flows; the practical hurdles relate solely to documentary compliance and evidence of beneficial ownership.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda does NOT impose any statutory export proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for non-resident exempted companies. A non-resident designated entity may retain export receipts in a foreign-currency account and may disburse them for operational or investment purposes without BMA conversion orders. For resident-designated entities, the BMA may require conversion into BMD within six months of receipt, but the subject Exempted Company is classified as non-resident and therefore falls outside the surrender framework. The entity must file annual returns under the Exchange Control Regulations if it maintains accounts in BMD, and every receipt of funds from outside Bermuda must be supported by the underlying trade, investment, or loan documentation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the central monetary and financial services regulatory authority of Bermuda, acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973 (made under the Exchange Control Act 1972).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW. A Bermuda Exempted Company may open a payment account with a UK-regulated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) such as Wise or Ebury for supplemental treasury flows, but primary banking and trade settlement must remain with fully licensed Bermudian banks. UK EMIs are not subject to Bermuda financial supervision and are not accepted as main banking counterpart by Bermuda trade counterparties; in addition, an EMI account may expose the entity to UK 'pass-through' AML requirements and may reject high-risk or regulated activities. The BMA and Bermudian banks require that the entity maintain a meaningful local banking relationship for statutory capital records and exchange control identification. EMIs are therefore viable only as secondary, low-balance clearing facilities, not as the principal liquidity rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from the UK parent or shareholders are routed via SWIFT MT103 to the Exempted Company's capital escrow account at HSBC Bank Bermuda Limited or Butterfield. The wire must be denominated in USD or BMD; payment purpose code must be 'GIF' (General Investment) or bank-specific code for 'Equity Capital', and the reference field must include the share certificate number and the exact investor name. For injections from the Channel Islands, Isle of Man, or UK, no BMA prior approval is required. For injections from outside the UK Overseas Territories and former Scheduled Territories, a formal application on Form BMA/EC/024 must be filed before the payment is accepted. The receiving bank will verify the name of the remitter against the company's register of shareholders, with the following bundle: certified copy of the board resolution approving the allotment, executed share subscription agreement, proof of source of funds (bank statements of the remitter), and a statement of beneficial ownership signed by the corporate secretary.Outbound trade disbursements for tea imports are cleared via SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for open-account (T/T) settlement, or via a commercial letter of credit (LC) at sight governed by UCP600 for higher-value consignments. For payments above 250,000 BMD, the bank requires invoices, packing lists, shipping documents, and proof of customs entry. The standard clearing rail matrix is as follows: (1) Local suppliers in Bermuda — settle via BMD clearing through Butterfield or HSBC local branch, using direct debit or POS; (2) UK suppliers — SWIFT MT103 in GBP with reference code '3000' (Payments), no BMA exchange control approval required because the UK is a Scheduled Territory; (3) Asia-Pacific tea or ceramics suppliers — documentary collection ('D/P' or 'D/A') or LC at sight in USD, using BMD/USD 1:1 peg and Barclays / HSBC correspondent network; (4) EU suppliers — SWIFT MT103 in EUR, subject to SEPA if the bank maintains a Reuter access or SWIFTNet tool. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an out-of-scope Bermuda Exempted Company. Bermuda levies no corporate income tax on the profits of ordinary exempted companies. A 15% corporate income tax (CIT) applies exclusively to constituent entities of in-scope multinational enterprise groups with combined annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million under the Economic Investment Act 2025, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025; the specialty tea salon entity does not meet this threshold and remains subject to a 0% headline rate.
