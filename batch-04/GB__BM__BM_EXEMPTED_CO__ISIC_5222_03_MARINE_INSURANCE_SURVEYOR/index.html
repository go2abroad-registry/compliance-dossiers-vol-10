Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Bermuda exempted company; at least one issued share per subscriber is required. Shares may be denominated in any currency at par, and no escrow, capital duty, or security deposit account is mandated under Bermuda law.

Funding & unlock path: Bermuda Fire & Rescue Service (BFRS) initial inspection and annual certification for fire safety at the registered office premises; Occupational Health and Safety Act 1999 workplace clearance issued by the Department of Workplace Safety; structural load inspection and electrical safety verification for survey equipment storage areas; port facility access security clearance under the Maritime Security (Port Facilities) Regulations; evidence of professional indemnity insurance required for Business Licence issuance.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Bermuda exempted company; at least one issued share per subscriber is required. Shares may be denominated in any currency at par, and no escrow, capital duty, or security deposit account is mandated under Bermuda law.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — United Kingdom; Bermuda Registrar of Companies and the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Bermuda corporate tax registration purposes

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is formed via the Registrar of Companies and does not receive a domestic VAT or payroll tax identification number unless it registers as an employer under the Bermuda Payroll Tax Act or applies for a Certificate of Compliance. For UK tax purposes, a foreign-incorporated entity with no UK permanent establishment or UK source income is not ordinarily required to register for a UK Corporation Tax, PAYE, or VAT number. If the entity subsequently establishes a UK fixed establishment, the overseas company must register with HMRC for Corporation Tax within six months, and for UK VAT before making taxable supplies above the registration threshold. For Bermuda economic substance reporting, the entity must file annual economic substance declarations with the Registrar of Companies and may need a TIN equivalent for tax information exchange purposes in the jurisdiction of ultimate beneficial ownership.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No federal-style check-the-box election applies in Bermuda. A Bermuda Exempted Company may elect non-resident status under the Companies Act 1981 to be treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes, provided it is controlled and managed outside Bermuda. Election for exemption from Bermuda income tax may be obtained for qualifying exempted undertakings, subject to the International Business Companies (Tax Exemption) Act 2022, while entities with UK tax exposure may make a UK corporation tax election only to the extent a deemed UK permanent establishment arises under the OECD Pillar Two rules or the UK's separate legal entity rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No domestic general indirect tax (no VAT/GST); Bermuda levies payroll tax and customs duties; UK VAT may apply only if UK taxable supplies are made

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda has no VAT registration threshold. UK VAT registration threshold is GBP 90,000 of taxable turnover for a relevant UK business; Bermuda payroll tax registration applies once an employer payroll exceeds the exemption threshold, presently calculated based on the annual exempt amount for employees earning up to the minimum wage band.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not applicable in Bermuda because Bermuda imposes no VAT. For UK VAT purposes, the export of goods or the supply of certain offshore services to a recipient outside the UK may be zero-rated under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 Schedule 8, provided the exporter holds evidence of removal from the UK within prescribed time limits and the supply falls within Item 1A of Group 1 (goods) or the reverse charge / belonging rules for services. Marine insurance survey services supplied to a non-UK insurer or an overseas insured in respect of marine cargo may qualify as a zero-rated supply of services if the recipient belongs outside the UK and the service is not treated as supplied where the property is located; services physically performed in Bermuda are not within the UK VAT net unless a UK establishment supplies them.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK Single Administrative Document or electronic declaration via the Customs Declaration Service)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / Proof of Physical Export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence received in the UK or Bermuda
  • Marine Insurance Survey Report and Underwriting Claim Instruction
  • Evidence of Permanent Export (port departure stamps, shipping line confirmation, carrier certificates)
  • Inspection survey invoice and contract with non-UK insurer or foreign insured

Bermuda imposes no electronic invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate. UK VAT invoicing and Making Tax Digital (MTD) rules apply only if the entity registers for UK VAT, requiring VAT-compliant e-invoices and digital record-keeping via MTD-compatible software with HMRC API submissions. Bermuda customs import declarations use the Customs Automated System for duty assessment, but no real-time fiscal stamping or continuous transaction control clearance exists.

For UK VAT purposes, incorrect zero-rating claims for exports attract HMRC assessments, penalty rates under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 for careless or deliberate inaccuracies, and retrospective clawback of output VAT plus interest. Penalty may be up to 100% of the tax lost for deliberate and concealed conduct. Fraudulent evasion of VAT and false export evidence are criminal offences under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 sections 72 and 167, with imprisonment up to seven years and unlimited fines. In Bermuda, false customs declarations are punishable under the Customs Act 1965 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997; any wrongfully claimed exemption from Bermuda economic substance obligations may lead to penalties of BMP 10,000 to BMP 250,000 or designations as a non-compliant entity with potential strike-off.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to USD. Current account transactions (trade in services, claim inspection fees, operational disbursements) are freely convertible and require no BMA approval. Capital account transactions, including the issue or transfer of shares of an exempted company to non-resident investors and the making of any loan, deposit or guarantee to a non-resident, require either specific exchange control permission or confirmation that a general statutory or published permission applies. The BMA treatment of a non-resident-owned exempted company permits primary accounts to be maintained in freely convertible designated currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, CHF and JPY). Profit and dividend remittance is unrestricted, subject only to bank-level verification; there is no withholding tax on Bermuda dividends or remittances. Practical hurdle: receiving banks require the BMA exchange control permission reference or legal confirmation of general permission before accepting a share subscription wire into a capital escrow account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Bermuda. Exchange controls were substantially liberalized in 1993, and all export earnings, survey fees and insurance-related service receipts may be retained in the currency of receipt or converted at the licensed bank's quoted rate without time limit. There is no obligation to surrender foreign currency to the BMA or a designated bank.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972, with the Exchange Control Regulations 1998 governing delegated powers

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium as a secondary corridor, Low as a primary banking relationship. Bermuda does not issue standalone e-money institution (EMI) licences; deposit-taking requires a licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business and Revolut Business are not regulated by the BMA and cannot lawfully open a domestic Bermuda settlement account; however, they remain operationally viable as a supplementary cross-border rail for low-value disbursements to UK and EU subcontractors and for receiving non-regulated payments from Lloyd's underwriters and brokers. Legal constraint: EMIs must never hold the exempted company's statutory share capital or escrowed capital, as they lack regulated custody and cannot issue the BMA-compliant acknowledgements required for exchange control compliance. The treasury structure should therefore maintain a licensed Bermuda bank account for capital and settlement, with an EMI used only as a marginal, non-critical disbursement channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) Obtain BMA exchange control permission (or written confirmation of an applicable general permission) for the issue of ordinary shares to the non-resident parent; (2) execute subscription agreement and board resolution in advance of any transfer; (3) parent wires funds via SWIFT MT103 to the Bermuda operational/escrow account at Butterfield or HSBC; (4) remittance information field must state 'Share subscription - Bermuda Exempted Company - corporate treasury injection' and reference the BMA permission number; (5) the wiring bank must include the company's full legal name, account number, and purpose code matching a non-export goods/service capital transfer; (6) deliver to the receiving bank the subscriber KYC pack, subscription agreement, and source-of-funds evidence; (7) any inbound aggregate above USD 100,000 requires pre-notification to the bank's AML compliance unit and a 2-3 business day hold for enhanced verification. The use of vague purpose codes such as 'payment for services' or 'consulting' will trigger rejection or a 5-10 business day compliance freeze.Primary clearing rail: SWIFT MT103 for all operational disbursements, including subcontractor survey fees, professional indemnity premiums, office costs, and indemnification payments under marine claims survey protocols. For trade-related settlements, a Commercial Letter of Credit (unconfirmed irrevocable sight LC issued by a London or New York nominee bank) is the designated fallback instrument where the beneficiary requires payment undertaking for larger hull repair disbursements. Rail selection: MT103 is preferred for all values; Wise Business (embedded fintech rail) is permissible for UK/EU disbursements below GBP 10,000 equivalent, provided the beneficiary accepts non-bank settlement; LC is used only when contractually mandated. Every outbound payment must carry MT103 field 70 remittance data referencing the survey contract number, vessel IMO number and invoice number. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File required when the MNE group's consolidated revenue exceeds BMD 750 million. Local File required when the Bermuda entity's related-party transactions exceed BMD 1 million in aggregate per fiscal year (or 10% of total revenue if that amount is higher).

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2019 impose substance requirements only for entities carrying on 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution, service centre, and IP). Marine insurance surveying is not a relevant activity; therefore a pure surveyor has no ESR filing obligation. However, if the exempted company also acts as a licensed insurer or holds intangible assets, it must meet the full economic substance test, maintain records, and file an annual ESR declaration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion (CIT Act 2023)

Statutory sanction: Additional tax equal to 100% of the evaded CIT; monetary penalty of BMD 250,000; criminal fine on conviction for fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held jointly and severally liable to pay the unpaid tax (CIT Act s.24); intentional evasion attracts imprisonment up to 5 years, confiscation of assets, and a travel ban pending clearance of the tax debt.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangements

Statutory sanction: Re-characterisation as a taxable domestic entity; disallowance of all intra-group deductions; penalty of BMD 50,000 for failure to maintain commercial substance documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false substance declarations face personal fines up to BMD 250,000, disqualification from acting as directors for 10 years, and potential extradition on fraud charges.
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing Failure

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties up to BMD 1,000,000 per breach; suspension or revocation of the marine survey licence issued under the Insurance Act.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be prosecuted under the Proceeds of Crime Amendment Act 2024; conviction attracts imprisonment up to 10 years, forfeiture of property benefiting from the suspected funds, and personal civil liability for losses.
Bribery and Corruption in Public Procurement

Statutory sanction: Fine equal to twice the benefit obtained; debarment from public contracts for 10 years; forfeiture of survey fees.

Director personal liability: Corporate veil may be pierced to hold directors liable for restitution; imprisonment for up to 7 years under the Bribery Act (Bermuda) 2024.
