Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Company qualifies for an Exempted Undertaking Tax Protection Certificate and is treated as nondomiciled for Bermuda income tax; exempt from income, corporate, capital gains, and withholding taxes within Bermuda; no VAT/GST applies; a separate 15% Bermuda corporate income tax applies only to in-scope multinational groups above the EUR 750 million revenue threshold, which a TikTok Shop SME retail entity does not meet.

Available election forms

Available elections: (i) Exempted Undertaking Tax Protection Certificate election (tax neutrality to 31 March 2035); (ii) CITA group registration and consolidation elections under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, including election to apply the qualified domestic minimum top-up tax with transitional safe harbour, relevant only to Pillar Two in-scope groups; (iii) UK VAT marketplace deemed-supplier election under Finance Act 2020 section 5(2A) and Schedule 9ZA, under which TikTok Shop is deemed to supply and account for VAT on low-value imported consignments not exceeding GBP 135; (iv) no US check-the-box election is applicable to a Bermuda entity; hybrid mismatch ordering rules under OECD BEPS and UK hybrid rules apply in cross-border structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda: no VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax; indirect taxation comprises customs duties on imports and stamp duties on land transactions and share transfers. UK: value added tax (VAT) under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 applies to Bermuda-incorporated sellers making taxable supplies of goods into the UK through online marketplaces, with TikTok Shop treated as the deemed supplier for consignment imports valued at GBP 135 or below under the UK overseas goods rules.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: not applicable - no VAT registration threshold exists; importers must register with Bermuda Customs Department for clearance and duty assessment on a per-consignment basis (Customs Act 1946). UK: general VAT registration threshold is GBP 90,000 per annum, but for non-established overseas sellers there is no threshold and registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply unless the marketplace deemed-supplier rule transfers liability to TikTok Shop for low-value goods (not exceeding GBP 135); goods above GBP 135 imported from Bermuda trigger UK import VAT and seller VAT registration obligations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda levies no VAT, so no 0% export VAT rate or refund mechanism exists in Bermuda; bona fide re-exports may benefit from customs duty relief under the Customs Act 1946. Under UK VAT, a Bermuda company supplying goods from the UK to a third-country destination may zero-rate the supply provided: (i) the Bermuda entity is the taxable supplier; (ii) the goods are physically removed from the UK to a destination outside the UK within three months of supply; (iii) the exporter retains commercial evidence of export such as customs declarations, bills of lading, and airway bills; and (iv) sales proceeds are received into the seller's bank account in the ordinary course of business. For goods imported into the UK and sold through TikTok Shop, import VAT is accounted for at the border with postponed VAT accounting available, and the retail sale may fall under the marketplace deemed-supplier rule instead of zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD Form C88 or electronic declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal from the UK
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Proof of export endorsement (customs cancelled export copy)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence showing sales proceeds received

Bermuda: no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, API, or digital stamping mandate; Bermuda Customs Department accepts electronic customs declarations via the government electronic portal for import/export entry processing. UK: HMRC does not mandate real-time clearance or digital stamping; Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires VAT-registered businesses to maintain digital VAT records and submit VAT returns through HMRC-recognised software APIs; PEPPOL e-invoicing is accepted but optional on a business-to-business basis.

UK HMRC may impose penalties up to 100% of the potential lost VAT for deliberate inaccuracies, together with late payment interest; zero-rating is clawed back where export evidence is absent or deficient and the goods are not removed within the statutory three-month window. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims may lead to criminal prosecution under the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 section 167 and the VAT Act 1994, with civil penalties under the Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24. Bermuda Customs may impose fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment for false declarations or unlicensed export conduct under the Customs Act 1946; the TikTok Shop seller should retain a full digital trade-lane archive including order logs, courier tracking, and settlement records to withstand HMRC and Bermuda audit scrutiny.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 rate to the United States Dollar (USD). For a Bermuda Exempted Company, both current-account and capital-account items are fully convertible, as the Exchange Control Act 1972 is no longer administered for non-resident-owned exempted undertakings. Profit remittance via dividends, interest, or management fees is unrestricted and free of Bermuda withholding tax. The only practical constraint is bank-level source-of-funds and beneficial-ownership verification arising from the AML/ATF Act 2018 and accompanying BMA AML/ATF Guidance Notes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Retail proceeds from TikTok Shop social-commerce sales may be retained in USD, BMD, or any freely convertible currency. The Export of Goods (Control) Order 2018 requires a discretionary licence only for a narrow list of controlled goods; digital retail intermediation under ISIC 4791 does not attach to any such licence requirement.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; prudential oversight coordinated with the UK Financial Conduct Authority for cross-border bank-licensing matters.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD (Bermudian Dollar, pegged 1:1 to USD). Bermuda has no transfer pricing documentation or contemporaneous TP file requirement under domestic CIT because the CIT rate is 0%; the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies a 15% domestic minimum top-up tax only to constituent entities of MNE groups with annual consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, and those groups must maintain OECD BEPS Pillar Two-aligned documentation (GloBE information return, qualified financial statements) to support safe harbour calculations. For the typical TikTok Shop retail entity that is not part of a EUR 750M+ group, no local TP threshold applies. Nevertheless, if any cross-border transaction exists with a UK affiliate or a UK permanent establishment, the UK's transfer pricing statute (Corporation Tax Act 2010, sections 147-203, OECD-based) applies at arm's length from the UK side, and HMRC may require a UK Local File; contemporaneous documentation is strongly recommended whenever intercompany fees, commissions, IP royalties, or profit splits exceed GBP 1 million per accounting period.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018, the Economic Substance Regulations 2018/2019, and associated guidance apply to every exempted company that is a resident entity carrying on a 'relevant activity'. Relevant activities exhaustively include: banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters operations, shipping, distribution and service centres, intellectual property holding, and holding entities. Pure third-party social-media retail (TikTok Shop selling to consumers) is not automatically a relevant activity, but the entity must still file an annual ESR Return declaring 'no relevant activity'. The danger for the TikTok Shop model is morphological: if the Bermuda entity (i) purchases goods from related parties and resells them, (ii) acts as the group's fulfilment or distribution hub for affiliated brands, or (iii) legally owns the brand, trade names, live-stream content, algorithms, or platform supply contracts exploited in the UK, it will be classified as a 'distribution and service centre' or an 'IP holding' relevant activity. Such entities must demonstrate in Bermuda: physical premises; full-time local employees proportionate to CIGA; level of operating expenditure; and significant management decisions taken in Bermuda. A pure booking shell with a London director managing the TikTok business via WhatsApp is non-compliant, exposes the directors to criminal penalties, and invites the UK tax authorities to assert that the entity is UK tax resident and has a UK permanent establishment, thereby deflecting the entire taxable profit into the UK at 25%.
