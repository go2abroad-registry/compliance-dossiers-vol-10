Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Government of Bermuda (Ministry of Finance), acting through the e-Tax Portal at www.gov.bm; incorporation and company registry matters are handled by the Registrar of Companies, Bermuda; customs and import-duty administration is carried out by HM Customs Bermuda (Department of Customs) via the ASYCUDA World clearance platform.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity intending to operate as a Bermuda Exempted Company must appoint a licensed Bermuda resident agent or registered agent, file the Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association with the Registrar of Companies, and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation (company register number). Bermuda does not issue a general income-tax TIN because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no VAT, and no capital gains tax. A payroll tax employer account with the Office of the Tax Commissioner must be opened through the e-Tax Portal only if the company employs local staff. Importers/exporters must additionally obtain a customs broker code or direct-trader registration with HM Customs Bermuda for ASYCUDA World declarations. There is no statutory VAT ID number to procure.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — a Bermuda Exempted Company is ordinarily exempt from Bermudian corporate income tax, VAT, capital gains tax, and withholding tax; it may obtain an Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 guaranteeing freedom from post-incorporation taxes until 2035, subject to the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 which imposes a 15% corporate income tax on constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025. The company is treated as tax-resident only in respect of any payroll tax obligations arising from local employment.

Available election forms

(1) Application for an Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which locks in tax-exempt status against future Bermudian taxes on profits, income, dividends, and capital gains until 2035; (2) election to be treated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes under the Exchange Control Act 1972, subject to Bermuda Monetary Authority permission, which removes local restrictions on dealings in foreign currency and shares; (3) for in-scope multinational groups, transitional registration and group-consolidation elections under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (15% CIT regime); (4) no check-the-box or US-style pass-through election exists under Bermuda law — the entity is inherently a taxable-entity classification for US/Bermuda purposes, though for US Federal income tax purposes a Bermuda Exempted Company may file Form 8832 to elect classification if it has a US tax nexus.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Bermuda — the indirect tax system consists of customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1973 (administered by HM Customs Bermuda) and payroll tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 (administered by the Office of the Tax Commissioner); goods in transit and exports are outside the customs duty base.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT registration threshold. HM Customs Bermuda requires every importer / exporter or licensed customs broker to be registered on ASYCUDA World for lodgement of import and export declarations; consignments are generally subject to duty on import value, with de minimis / low-value relief not forming part of the tax registration framework. Payroll tax registration is triggered only when an employer has local Bermudian employees, and the employer payroll tax threshold is subject to the rates in force under the Payroll Tax Act.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable by design — because Bermuda levies no VAT or GST, there is no 0% export VAT rate and no input-VAT refund mechanism to substantiate. The functional equivalent for a Bermuda Exempted Company engaged in GDP pharma freight is: (i) genuine exports and transshipment cargo are outside the customs duty base, and goods in transit or temporarily warehoused in a licensed customs-bonded facility (including clinical trial medicines held under GDP conditions) are not subject to import duty; (ii) re-exported goods may qualify for duty relief or inward-processing / temporary-importation treatment on application to HM Customs Bermuda; (iii) the company must maintain its GDP (Good Distribution Practice) authorisation for pharmaceutical freight, retain customs export declarations in ASYCUDA World, and demonstrate the physical movement of goods out of Bermuda to avoid any inference of local consumption or diversion into the Bermudian market. Bermuda law imposes no foreign-exchange repatriation deadline; the 180-day figure below is a pipeline convention derived from source-market cash repatriation discipline rather than a Bermudian statutory requirement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (lodged electronically in ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (proof of physical export movement)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (full commodity description, HTS / HS code, value, and GDP temperature conditions)
  • GDP Certificate / Wholesale Distribution Authorisation issued by the competent pharmaceutical authority
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence (accepted as foreign-exchange control evidence only where the source-country or counterparty bank requires it; not a Bermuda statutory document)
  • Certificate of Origin (only where a preferential trade claim is made)

Bermuda has no real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API stamping, or continuous-transaction-control (CTC) mandate. HM Customs Bermuda mandates electronic filing of all import and export declarations through the ASYCUDA World system, with customs duty payable before physical release of goods; digital stamping or fiscal QR codes are not used on commercial invoices. Payroll tax returns and payments are filed electronically through the e-Tax Portal of the Office of the Tax Commissioner.

Bermuda's Customs Act 1973 and Customs Tariff Act 1973 subject false declarations, misdeclaration, diversion of transshipment cargo into local consumption, and fraudulently claimed duty relief to clawback of unpaid or underpaid duty plus monetary penalties, forfeiture of the goods and related conveyances, and criminal prosecutions leading to fines and imprisonment. There is no VAT clawback liability because no VAT exists. Separately, for a Bermuda Exempted Company that is a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, fraudulent or negligent filings or evasion of the 15% CIT carry significant statutory penalties, interest on unpaid corporate income tax, and potential custodial sentences for responsible officers. Tax and customs audits in the GDP pharma freight sector will scrutinise the completeness of the export document chain, the authenticity of outbound movement records, temperature-monitoring logs, the absence of sale or consumption in Bermuda, and timely repatriation of export proceeds where applicable under source-market rules.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar (USD), and both currencies circulate interchangeably. Current account convertibility is unrestricted for trade in goods and services, including freight forwarding and logistics fees. A Bermuda Exempted Company is classified as a non-resident authorized user under the Exchange Control regime, granting general permission to maintain foreign currency accounts, receive inward remittances, and remit capital abroad without per-transaction exchange control approval, provided funds originate outside Bermuda. Capital account transactions, including share capital injections, intercompany loans, loan repayments, and dividends to non-resident shareholders, are freely permitted for Exempted Companies under BMA General Permission, subject to bank-level AML/ATF due diligence and BMA reporting of large foreign currency flows. Profit remittance is not blocked by statute and is not subject to Bermuda withholding tax; however, any conversion of non-resident USD funds into BMD for on-island operating expenses requires exchange control purchase permission obtained by the authorized dealer bank. The 2023 Economic Substance (Amendment) Act and the Corporate Service Provider Business Registration Act 2023 impose substance and reporting obligations but do not restrict currency movement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no specific surrender timeline for export or service receipts. A Bermuda Exempted Company engaged in ISIC 5229 GDP pharma freight may retain 100% of foreign currency export proceeds in USD or any freely convertible currency, and may net receipts against trade payables. The only compliance requirements are: (i) all hard-currency receipts must be routed through a licensed authorised dealer in Bermuda or a recognized correspondent bank; (ii) the company must maintain underlying trade documentation (invoices, contracts, bills of lading, GDP logistics records, delivery notes) for a minimum of five years to substantiate the commercial nature of the flows; and (iii) transactions exceeding USD 100,000 must be reported to the BMA through the bank's currency flow declaration framework.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the delegated administrator of the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the licensing and supervisory authority under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as the primary treasury bank; MEDIUM only as a supplementary disbursement corridor. Offshore digital EMIs, including Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, and similar non-Bermuda-licensed payment firms, are not authorised under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and may not take Bermuda deposits, issue BMD-denominated accounts, or provide regulated escrow or custodial services. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections from the UK parent to the Bermuda Exempted Company must be routed by SWIFT MT103 (with MT202 COV for high-value cover payments) to the USD or multi-currency account held at Butterfield Bank or HSBC Bermuda. The beneficiary account must be stated in the BMD/USD format established at account opening, and the payment must reference a compliant purpose code such as 'Share Capital Subscription' or 'Additional Paid-In Capital'. For amounts exceeding USD 100,000, the receiving bank will require the inward remittance declaration (BMA exchange control form or the bank's internal currency flow certificate), together with the board resolution authorising the share issuance or loan drawdown and an updated share register issued by the Bermuda registered agent. The funds must originate from the UK parent's verified corporate account; third-party or personal remittances will trigger enhanced due diligence and may be rejected. The company must preserve the SWIFT confirmation, the bank's inward remittance advice, and the updated register of members for BMA inspection for at least five years.Trade disbursements for GDP pharma freight settlements are cleared through: (i) SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers in USD, GBP, or EUR from the Bermuda multi-currency operating account to vendors, freight forwarders, and customs brokers; (ii) MT202 COV for correspondent-bank cover payments on high-value invoices; (iii) commercial letters of credit issued under UCP600 rules (SWIFT MT700) with confirmation by Butterfield Bank or HSBC Bermuda for pharmaceutical cargo purchases requiring bank payment undertaking; and (iv) USD ACH direct debit and credit lines via the bank's US correspondent routing for recurring low-value vendor payments. All outbound payments must match the underlying trade document set (invoice, packing list, bill of lading, GDP temperature log where applicable) and must be released only after bank-side sanctions screening and customer due diligence verification of the beneficiary. No outbound exchange control approval is required for non-resident Exempted Company funds held in foreign currency accounts.Onshore FX forward contracts between USD and BMD are unnecessary and generally not offered because the BMD/USD exchange rate is fixed at 1:1 under the BMA's currency board arrangement; no hedge is required for USD-denominated exposure.Cross-currency FX forwards (GBP/USD and EUR/USD) executed with Butterfield Bank, HSBC Bermuda, or an approved international counterparty under an ISDA or ICOM master agreement, used for converting trade receivables and vendor payables with a maturity not exceeding 12 months.Structural cross-currency swaps (GBP/USD and EUR/USD) for matching long-term lease obligations, capital equipment financing, or multi-year client contracts, subject to bank credit approval and BMA reporting if the notional exceeds USD 5 million.Plain vanilla currency options (GBP/USD and EUR/USD) purchased on a zero-premium collar or costless collar basis for protection against adverse exchange rate movements on confirmed pharma logistics contracts.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW as the primary treasury bank; MEDIUM only as a supplementary disbursement corridor. Offshore digital EMIs, including Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, and similar non-Bermuda-licensed payment firms, are not authorised under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and may not take Bermuda deposits, issue BMD-denominated accounts, or provide regulated escrow or custodial services. The BMA does not recognise an EMI as a qualifying bank reference for corporate KYC, and Bermuda tier-1 banks will decline any onboarding package that relies on an EMI for source-of-funds verification. Permissible use is limited to a non-Bermuda GBP/EUR collection account for UK and EU pharma clients, from which funds are swept by SWIFT MT103 into the Bermuda operating account; the EMI must hold a valid FCA UK EMI licence or an equivalent EU passport, and the account must never touch BMD or hold client money on behalf of the Bermuda entity.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or accumulated earnings tax on a Bermuda Exempted Company. The Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CIT Act) introduces a 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax and Income Inclusion Rule, but only for constituent entities of large multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more; a standalone GDP pharma freight SME is outside that scope. Material risk: if the Exempted Co is managed and controlled in the United Kingdom, UK Corporation Tax at 19%–25% (2024/25: main rate 25%, small profits rate 19%) applies on the full corporate profits under CTA 2009 s.14, nullifying the Bermuda tax exemption.
