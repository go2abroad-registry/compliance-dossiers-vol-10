Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance - portal: https://www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated by the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981. The incorporation process issues a Certificate of Incorporation; no separate TIN/VAT ID is allocated because Bermuda imposes no income tax or VAT. If the company engages in domestic payroll or imports goods, it must obtain a Payroll Tax Number and Customs Registration from the Office of the Tax Commissioner. Foreign entities may readily obtain a Certificate of Exemption under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing no future Bermuda tax on profits, income, or capital until 2035.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - No VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Importers of goods are required to register as customs importers and pay duties at rates under the Customs Tariff Act 1966.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Bermuda levies no VAT/GST and therefore has no export zero-rating regime. Exports of goods and services are outside the scope of Bermuda customs duty; no claim for 0% VAT refund arises.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BMD is pegged 1:1 to USD and circulates interchangeably. ECA restricts only 'resident' undertakings; a Bermuda Exempted Company with BMA non-resident designation is deemed external, so capital-account transfers (equity injections, shareholder loans, dividends, IP royalties) and current-account payments (tournament fees, sponsorship) are freely convertible and remittable without BMA approval. Practical gates: maintain the non-resident designation certificate, file Economic Substance (Company and Private Investment Funds) Act 2018 returns, and deliver FATCA/CRS self-certifications. No repatriation permit or profit-remittance license applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Bermuda. Export receipts (software licensing, esports platform and tournament-management fees) are retained freely in USD, GBP, EUR, or any permitted foreign currency, with a 0% conversion requirement for non-resident-designated exempted companies. The receiving bank files statistical exchange-control returns with the BMA; no direct company filing is required.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - sole monetary and exchange-control authority, operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Exchange Control Act 1972 (ECA), and the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory; the BMA exercises fully delegated monetary powers, and no UK PRA/FCA authorization is required for the Bermuda treasury operations of this entity.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM for direct onboarding of the Bermuda entity; HIGH in a dual-entity structure. Bermuda is outside the EEA, so UK FCA-licensed EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business) cannot passport e-money services into Bermuda, but may onboard a Bermuda-incorporated client on a case-by-case cross-border basis.
