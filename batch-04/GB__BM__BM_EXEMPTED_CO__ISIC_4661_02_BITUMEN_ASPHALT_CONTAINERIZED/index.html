Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A registered office located at the premises of a licensed Bermuda corporate services provider is permissible and standard practice, satisfying the statutory requirement that every exempted company maintain a physical registered office in Bermuda for service of process and official notices. However, because the company will carry on wholesale distribution (a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity under the Economic Substance Act 2018), a purely virtual mailbox without local employees, board direction, and core income-generating activities (contract negotiation, procurement, logistics direction, monitoring of carriage and insurance) conducted in Bermuda will not satisfy the economic substance test and exposes the company to penalties. Core income-generating activities must therefore be managed and directed from Bermuda notwithstanding that warehousing and containerized cargo handling occur overseas.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is imposed under the Companies Act 1981 for an exempted company; share capital may be denominated in any currency and no escrow account is required; the standard government fee is calculated on authorized share capital (USD 1,780 for authorised capital up to USD 12,000).

Funding & unlock path: No fire safety, structural load inspection, or workplace clearance is required for a paper-holding exempted company with no physical premises in Bermuda; if the company later operates a local warehouse or office, it must obtain a fire safety certificate from the Bermuda Fire and Rescue Service, a building occupancy certificate confirming structural load capacity, and comply with workplace safety inspections administered by the Department of Workforce Development under Bermuda occupational safety and health regulations.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is imposed under the Companies Act 1981 for an exempted company; share capital may be denominated in any currency and no escrow account is required; the standard government fee is calculated on authorized share capital (USD 1,780 for authorised capital up to USD 12,000).

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial licensing authority applies to a Bermuda exempted company conducting solely wholesale trading of drummed and bagged bitumen under ISIC 4661; general business licensing, if local Bermuda trading occurs, is administered by the Registrar of Companies through the Companies (Business Licenses) Act 1998.

Category: Not applicable — Bermuda does not issue an operating or manufacturing permit for containerized wholesale supply of penetration grade bitumen; no statutory industrial license is triggered by the arm's-length trading and logistics model.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fully convertible, open-account currency regime for both current and capital account transactions. A Bermuda Exempted Company may be designated 'non-resident' for exchange-control purposes under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (provided no resident Bermuda shareholders, beneficiaries or creditors exist), which confers essentially unrestricted freedom to hold, borrow and transfer USD, EUR and other currencies. Profit remittance and capital repatriation are not subject to exchange-control approval, withholding tax or currency surrender, but the practical hurdles are: (i) all foreign-currency transactions must be effected through a BMA-authorised dealer, being a licensed Bermuda bank; (ii) correspondent banks will apply OFAC, UK OFSI and EU sanctions screening to every cross-border instruction, which is material for the bitumen wholesale trade; (iii) the exempted company must maintain valid economic substance filings under the Economic Substance Amendment Act 2021 (headquarters or pure equity holding regime); (iv) annual government fees and statutory returns must be current before any bank will process high-value remittances; and (v) beneficial-ownership and register of directors filings under the Companies Act 1981 must be up to date. Dividend and interest remittances to the UK parent are exempt from Bermuda withholding tax by virtue of the exempted company's tax exemption certificate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda does not impose mandatory surrender, statutory conversion ratios or repatriation deadlines on export proceeds for a non-resident-designated exempted company. The historical requirement under the Exchange Control Act 1972 that residents sell foreign currency to an authorised dealer within a prescribed period does not apply to an entity designated non-resident for exchange-control purposes. The company may therefore retain bitumen trade receivables in USD, EUR or other foreign currencies, net them against procurement payables, or apply them to hedging instruments without conversion to BMD. Nevertheless, the BMA expects that export proceeds are credited to an account held with a Bermuda authorised dealer (or, with BMA consent, an approved external bank), and the company must retain a complete audit trail comprising commercial invoices, bills of lading, quantity and quality inspection certificates, and SWIFT payment confirmations to evidence the trade cycle and counter trade-based money laundering inquiries.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as constituted under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, functioning as the central bank, integrated financial regulator and exchange-control administrator for Bermuda under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and Exchange Control Regulations 1973. The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is issued by the Bermuda Government, is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar, and the USD is also legal tender in Bermuda. The Bank of England and HM Treasury exercise indirect oversight of Bermuda as a UK Overseas Territory, particularly for sanctions alignment via Orders in Council, but do not directly administer Bermuda's exchange-control or banking licensing regimes.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, and only as a supplementary rail, never as the primary treasury solution. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex or Currencycloud-fronted platforms may lawfully be used to collect GBP and EUR trade receivables from European buyers of containerized bitumen and to settle low-value logistics costs, offering faster UK/EU clearing and lower FX margins. The legal constraints are material: (i) no such EMI holds a BMA licence as a bank, authorised dealer or deposit-taker, so it cannot hold BMD, cannot open the capital escrow account for equity injections, and cannot act as a settlement account for exchange-control purposes; (ii) the BMA's AML/ATF guidance and the Corporate Service Provider Business Act 2012 framework require the exempted company's main operating account to be with a Bermuda-licensed bank; (iii) UK FCA-regulated EMIs are subject to safeguarding rules and are not covered by Bermuda deposit insurance; and (iv) EMIs may de-risk commodity-trading clients without notice. The recommended architecture is a dual-rail structure: the Bermuda bank remains the treasury and trade-finance anchor, while the EMI is used for low-value working-capital receipts with nightly sweeps to the Bermuda bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and capital-subsidy wiring must be executed as SWIFT MT103 (or CHAPS/Fedwire via correspondent) from the UK parent's bank to the Bermuda bank's USD account. Required wire fields: beneficiary name must match the exempted company's constitutional name exactly; beneficiary account number; field 70 must carry the reference 'BPM6 E510 CAPITAL INJECTION' together with the board resolution number and share-allotment reference; purpose code must align to IMF BPM6 E510/E511 (direct investment equity capital) which the Bermuda banking system applies consistently with UK statistical reporting. Supporting documents demanded at onboarding or at first receipt: original board resolution authorising the share issue; allotment letters and updated share register; copy of the BMA non-resident exchange-control designation letter; UBO declaration and source-of-funds statement evidencing the legitimate origin of the capital; and confirmation that Bermuda capital duty does not apply. For multi-investor structures, a Butterfield or HSBC capital escrow sub-account may be used, with funds held in USD pending allotment. Capital may not be injected through an EMI or fintech; it must clear through a Bermuda authorised dealer, and funds are typically made available within 1 to 3 business days after full compliance sign-off.Outbound clearing rails comprise: (i) direct supplier settlements by SWIFT MT103 with charges agreed as SHA or OUR, with field 70 containing the invoice number, date, bitumen grade and quantity in metric tonnes; (ii) irrevocable at-sight Commercial Letters of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700 by Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda in favour of the refinery, with field 45A goods description matching the inspection certificate and documents comprising commercial invoice, 3/3 clean-on-board bills of lading, SGS or Bureau Veritas inspection certificate, packing list and certificate of origin; (iii) Standby Letters of Credit via SWIFT MT760 as performance guarantees to offtakers; (iv) documentary collections via SWIFT MT400/MT710 for established counterparties; (v) freight and demurrage settlements by MT103 to vessel owners or brokers referencing the booking note; and (vi) USD clearing through the Bermuda bank's New York correspondent (typically Citibank or BNY Mellon for Butterfield, and HSBC New York for HSBC Bermuda).
