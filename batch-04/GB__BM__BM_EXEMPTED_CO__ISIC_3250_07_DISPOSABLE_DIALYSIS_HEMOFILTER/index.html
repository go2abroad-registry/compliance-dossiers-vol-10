E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated by the Registrar of Companies without income tax registration at formation. Trigger-based registration steps: (i) employer payroll tax registration with the OTC within 30 days of hiring any employee; (ii) Social Insurance registration with the Social Insurance Board; (iii) importer/exporter code registration in ASYCUDA World for all customs transactions; (iv) for MNE groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750 million, mandatory CIT registration with the OTC under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 (15% CIT) with appointment of a Bermuda-resident tax representative; (v) associated Economic Substance (ES) filing obligation with the Registrar.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or pass-through election. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda applies customs duties (import tariff), stamp duty, land tax, and payroll tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – no VAT registration threshold exists in Bermuda. Any importer (regardless of turnover) must hold an ASYCUDA World Importer Code; employer payroll tax registration applies to all entities with Bermuda payroll (no revenue threshold).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Bermuda operates a no-VAT / no-GST territory; exported hollow-fiber polyethersulfone dialysis hemofilters are outside the customs duty base and no input VAT refund mechanism exists. Duty relief is available for goods exported within a prescribed period under the Customs Tariff Act 1973 (re-export, transshipment, and temporary importation regimes) and for exports of local products where applicable.

No real-time e-invoicing or fiscal digital stamping mandate for domestic B2B/B2C supply. No continuous transmission (CTC) or clearance of invoices applies.

No VAT zero-rating clawback regime exists because no VAT is levied. Penalties attach to customs duty relief abuse and statutory non-compliance: (i) Customs Tariff Act 1973 – penalty up to three times duty evaded plus forfeiture of goods for false export declarations; (ii) CITA 2024 – 100% penalty of underpaid CIT plus interest and late-filing penalties; (iii) Economic Substance Act – administrative penalty of BMD 10,000 per failure plus BMD 1,000/day ongoing, BMD 50,000 for knowingly false ES filings, and possible imprisonment of directors for fraudulent filings; (iv) general fraud penalties for money laundering and false documentation under the Bribery Act 2016 and Proceeds of Crime Act 1997.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar, and USD is accepted de facto as circulating currency. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted: trade payments, royalties, management fees, and interest remittances by a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company may be executed by an authorised dealer without prior BMA approval, provided the company holds a Non-Resident (Bermuda) Certificate under the Exchange Control Act 1972. Capital-account items, including share-issuance subscription payments, loan capital, and reorganisation contributions, are convertible upon completion of BMA notification or the authorised dealer's internal exchange-control review; the principal remittance hurdles are documentary rather than quantitative, namely proof of non-resident designation, source-of-funds evidence, and a board resolution authorising the transfer. Dividend and profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are permitted at any frequency, are subject to zero withholding tax for Exempted Companies, and encounter no BMA conversion queue or remittance cap.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Act 1972 obliges exporters to offer foreign-currency proceeds to an authorised dealer within 30 days of trade settlement. Because BMD is pegged at par to USD and USD circulates freely, USD-denominated payments for hollow-fibre dialyzer exports may be retained in an approved foreign-currency account at a Bermuda authorised bank without mandatory conversion. Non-USD proceeds (EUR, GBP, CAD, CNY) must be surrendered to the authorised dealer for conversion into USD or BMD within the 30-day statutory window unless the BMA grants a form-based retention approval. The mandatory conversion ratio is thus 100% for non-USD receipts absent prior BMA approval, while USD receipts attract no statutory minimum conversion ratio and are treated as automatically retained within the peg regime.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting as the Exchange Control Authority for Bermuda under the Exchange Control Act 1972; the Bank of England retains only residual monetary and financial-stability oversight over Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory, while all day-to-day foreign-exchange authorisation, licensing of authorised dealers, and compliance surveillance are vested exclusively in the BMA.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Bermuda does not operate an e-money-institution (EMI) licensing regime analogous to the UK FCA or EU EMD2; offshore digital EMI platforms cannot access BMA settlement or chip-and-STP clearing, and holding treasury liquidity with an unlicensed offshore EMI would breach Exchange Control Act 1972 custodial expectations. The Digital Asset Business Act 2018 covers only virtual-asset services, not fiat e-money issuance, so fiat treasury balances cannot be placed with a Bermuda fintech absent a full banking licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. An EMI may be used downstream in the UK or EU solely for retail distributor collections, while the primary liquidity-clearing relationship must sit with a licensed Bermuda bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Injection of share capital into the Bermuda Exempted Company is routed by SWIFT MT103 from the UBO's home jurisdiction to the designated USD/BMD Capital Escrow Account at HSBC Bank Bermuda Limited, with the remittance field quoting 'Share Capital Subscription — [entity name] — Capital Call 1' and referencing the board resolution number. The receiving authorised dealer requires a pre-advice package comprising the executed subscription agreement, capital call notice, Non-Resident (Bermuda) Certificate, beneficial-ownership form, and the Bermuda Exchange Control form where conversion into BMD is requested; no BMA prior approval is required for a non-resident-certified company if funds remain on the USD ledger, but conversion into BMD triggers a form-based exchange-control authorisation. Verification follows a two-step protocol: bank-side SWIFT KYC matching against the pre-advice and post-receipt reconciliation to the issued share register, with all inbound value dated as received and held non-interest-bearing until board application to operating accounts.Trade settlements for imported raw materials (polyethersulfone resin, polyurethane potting compound, spin-pack hardware) and sterilisation services route via SWIFT MT103/TT wires for amounts under the LC threshold, and via irrevocable documentary Letters of Credit (UCP 600) for repeat consignments, issued through Butterfield's trade-finance desk in BMD/USD. The preferred clearing rail is: (i) sight or usance commercial LC with a 2% tolerance against proforma invoice and full shipping-article set; (ii) MT103 payment on negotiation with purpose field 'Manufacture of medical devices'; and (iii) SWIFT MT202 for interbank cover legs. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda local-currency threshold exists because ordinary exempted companies are outside Bermuda's income tax system. For in-scope multinational groups, the operative threshold is EUR 750 million consolidated group revenue; such groups must maintain master-file and local-file-equivalent records supporting the GloBE/Pillar Two computation and be prepared to produce them to the Bermuda Tax Commissioner. ESR documentation is separately required where a relevant activity is present.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Substance Requirements) Act 2018 applies to Bermuda exempted companies conducting any 'relevant activity' enumerated in the Act. Medical-device manufacturing under ISIC 3250 is not itself a listed relevant activity; however, if the entity also operates as a regional distribution hub for dialysis products, holds intangible property in Bermuda, or earns financing or headquarters income, it will be treated as carrying on a relevant activity. In such cases, the entity must demonstrate that core income-generating activities are performed locally by adequate staff, premises and expenditure, and must file an annual ESR return.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Facilitation of Foreign Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: HMRC may invoke the UK-Bermuda exchange of information arrangement, recharacterise the transactions and impose full UK corporation tax, interest and penalties up to 100% of understated tax. False ESR or Annual Declaration filings are criminal offences under Bermuda law, punishable by fines up to BMD 25,000 and imprisonment for up to five years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false declarations or cause the company to enable tax evasion can be criminally prosecuted in Bermuda or the UK, subjected to confiscation orders, disqualified, banned from travel, and imprisoned for up to five years.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Registrar of Companies may strike off and dissolve the exempted company, revoke its exemption, and impose ESR penalties up to BMD 250,000, plus daily default penalties up to BMD 10,000 for continued non-compliance. Failure to file Annual Declarations for two consecutive years triggers automatic strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for company debts incurred after a statutory demand, barred from serving as directors, ordered to make civil restitution to creditors, and subject to foreign asset freezes and travel bans.
AML / CDD and Funds Movement Failure

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018 attracts civil penalties up to BMD 250,000, criminal fines and forfeiture. Money laundering offences are punishable by imprisonment up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly facilitate moving illicit funds are treated as principal offenders, face criminal imprisonment, asset seizure, civil recovery orders, and cancellation of their right to act as officers.
Medical Device License / Product Regulatory Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda health authorities may seize non-compliant products, suspend the business licence and impose fines up to BMD 50,000; traders may be prohibited from selling devices for up to two years. In the United Kingdom, MHRA enforcement can lead to product recall, prohibition notices and additional penalties.

Director personal liability: Officers who knowingly permit unlicensed manufacturing or certify false conformity data may face personal fines, imprisonment, disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, and civil restitution claims from patients or purchasers through veil-piercing litigation.
