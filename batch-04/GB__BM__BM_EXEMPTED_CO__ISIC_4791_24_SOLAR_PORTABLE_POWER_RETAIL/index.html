Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance: (i) Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and social insurance administration (portal: www.taxcommissioner.gov.bm); (ii) Registrar of Companies for incorporation, exempted undertaking status, and economic substance filings (portal: www.roc.gov.bm); (iii) Bermuda Customs Department for import/export duty administration.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda has no corporate income tax or VAT regime, so no general corporate TIN or VAT ID is issued. A foreign entity obtains statutory identity through: (1) registration of a Bermuda Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981, receiving a Certificate of Incorporation and Bermuda Registered Number from the Registrar of Companies; (2) application for a Tax Protection Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (3) if it employs Bermuda-resident staff, registration with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for a payroll tax account number and with the Department of Social Insurance for a contributory ID; (4) submission of an annual Economic Substance Declaration via the Bermuda e-Registry. No further TIN/VAT procurement step exists because no taxable corporate or consumption tax is in force.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company is a tax-exempt undertaking under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, exempt from all Bermuda income, corporate profits, capital, and asset taxes until at least 31 March 2035; territorial scope limited to Bermudian-source employment liabilities (payroll tax and social insurance) only where the company locally employs individuals, which this offshore e-commerce structure does not.

Available election forms

Bermuda does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. Available statutory elections and designations: (i) application for tax protection certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (ii) election of status as an exempted undertaking with the Registrar of Companies, permitting 100% non-resident ownership and tax-holiday protection; (iii) designation as a pure equity holding entity or as a relevant-activity undertaking for economic substance compliance purposes; (iv) the shareholder's U.S. federal Form 8832 check-the-box election (disregarded entity versus association) where group US tax classification is required; (v) election of accounting period and currency for payroll tax withholding if Bermuda-resident employees are ever engaged.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales/use tax in Bermuda; indirect taxation comprises Customs Import Duty under the Customs Tariff Act 1986 and Customs Act 1949, plus employer/employee payroll tax as a separate direct levy.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. Bermuda does not levy VAT or GST, therefore no turnover-based registration threshold exists. Commercial importers must obtain customs registration and file import entries through Bermuda Customs; annual economic substance declaration is mandatory for all exempted companies regardless of revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Statutorily inapplicable: Bermuda levies no value-added tax, so there is no 0% export rating, no export VAT refund mechanism, and no input VAT credit to substantiate. Offshore sales of portable solar generators and solar panels by the Bermuda Exempted Company fall entirely outside Bermuda indirect taxation. Where goods are imported into Bermuda under a temporary importation or bonded warehouse regime and subsequently re-exported, customs duty drawback or remission may be claimed under the Customs Act 1949, subject to re-export within the prescribed period and preservation of goods in original condition.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Entry (export declaration)
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading or air waybill
  • Certificate of origin where required by the destination jurisdiction
  • Duty drawback or temporary importation application with supporting customs approvals

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs requires electronic import/export declarations via the national automated customs clearance system at the port of entry. B2C online retail invoices for portable solar power equipment may be issued in free commercial format without government pre-clearance.

No VAT clawback exists in Bermuda because no VAT is imposed. Fraudulent customs declarations, duty evasion, and false drawback claims are prosecuted under the Customs Act 1949 and Customs Tariff Act 1986, attracting monetary penalties up to BMD 10,000 per offence (or higher upon indictment), forfeiture of goods, retrospective duty assessments, and imprisonment. Materially false economic substance filings additionally expose the company to financial penalties from the Bermuda Economic Substance Authority, while cross-border e-commerce pricing remains subject to the UK parent's HMRC transfer-pricing and CFC attribution review, which may impose adjustments and penalties if arm's-length conditions are not demonstrably satisfied.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BMD is fully convertible for current-account and capital-account purposes for entities designated as non-resident under the ECA. The Bermuda Exempted Company (BM_EXEMPTED_CO) will apply for non-resident designation through its licensed registered agent, which frees all inward and outward remittances from case-by-case BMA approval. Current-account flows (trade payments, platform commissions, logistics fees) are unrestricted; capital-account flows (share subscriptions, shareholder loans, dividends, returns of capital) require only corporate documentation and no exchange-control license. Profit remittance hurdles: (i) maintain the non-resident designation and Certificate of Exemption (Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, statutory protection to 2035); (ii) file the annual economic substance declaration under the Economic Substance Act 2018; (iii) present to the remitting bank a board resolution and latest management accounts for dividend or loan-repayment remittances. No additional BMA consent is triggered for BMD-denominated dividend payments because BMD and USD are parity-pegged and fungible.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory timeline, mandated conversion ratio, or surrender requirement on export or e-commerce sales proceeds. A non-resident designated exempted company may retain 100% of receipts in USD, GBP, EUR, or any freely convertible currency without conversion into BMD and without a repatriation deadline. The residual obligations are statistical: the BMA's annual external-assets and liabilities return and the annual economic substance filing, neither of which imposes a treasury lock-up or mandatory conversion.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), constituted under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and exercising statutory exchange-control powers delegated under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (ECA). The BMA operates a currency-board-style system issuing the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) at an irrevocable legal parity of BMD 1.00 = USD 1.00, with the US Dollar accepted as equivalent legal tender across the jurisdiction.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (standard Bermuda Exempted Company); 15% for in-scope multinational enterprise groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 where annual consolidated group revenue is at least €750 million.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory dividend withholding tax. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, branch profits, or distributions to non-resident shareholders, and no exchange control restrictions apply to the repatriation of profit from an exempted company.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 60 days after each anniversary of the date of incorporation; the initial return is due after the first anniversary and every subsequent calendar year thereafter.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The CIT-1 return must be filed within 15 months after the end of the fiscal year; for the first transitional fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025, the filing deadline is extended to 18 months after fiscal year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 - under the standard zero-rate regime, Bermuda prescribes no monetary transfer pricing documentation threshold; any material cross-border connected-party transaction must be documented and remunerated at arm's length under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018. For in-scope CIT groups, Bermuda adopts the OECD transfer pricing guidelines and may compel Master File and Local File documentation where related-party turnover or intercompany balances exceed the thresholds prescribed by CIT regulations (presently aligned with the OECD/CIT de minimis safe harbour).

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (ESR) apply to exempted companies carrying on a 'relevant activity'. The direct online retail of portable battery generators and solar panels falls within 'Distribution and Service Centre' activity. The company must be directed and managed exclusively from Bermuda, maintain adequate physical premises and full-time employees proportionate to its activity, incur sufficient local expenditure, and perform its core income-generating activities in Bermuda. These core activities include purchasing, inventory management, distribution logistics, price setting, and contract negotiation. The entity must file an annual Economic Substance Declaration, and a shell arrangement that merely books revenue in Bermuda without local substance is unlawful.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Returns

Statutory sanction: Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and the Criminal Code Act 1907, penalties include a monetary assessment of up to 200% of the understated tax (minimum BMD 250,000), daily default surcharges of BMD 500, civil fraud exposure, strike-off of the company, and upon criminal conviction imprisonment not exceeding five years with unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or knowingly direct fraudulent booking practices are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of the evaded tax; the Bermuda court may issue worldwide asset freezing orders against them, impose travel bans where flight risk is established, disqualify them from serving as directors for up to 10 years, and sentence them to imprisonment for up to five years.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Registrar of Companies and the Minister of Finance may issue a rectification order, impose a financial penalty of up to BMD 250,000 plus monthly continuing penalties of BMD 10,000, and, upon repeated failure, strike the company off the register and void its exempted company status, thereby eliminating the 0% tax protection.

Director personal liability: Each director must certify the ESR declaration; knowing misrepresentation exposes directors to personal civil fines of up to BMD 100,000, disqualification from corporate office, asset-freeze and travel restriction orders, and liability for the company's unpaid statutory penalties if the company is stripped of limited liability protection.
AML, Sanctions, and Payment Rail Misuse

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Regulations 2020, conviction on indictment carries imprisonment of up to 14 years and an unlimited fine; civil or regulatory penalties of up to BMD 1,000,000 may be imposed, payment intermediary agreements may be terminated, and the Bermuda registry may suspend the company's certificate of exemption.

Director personal liability: Directors, the compliance officer, and the designated MLRO are individually subject to criminal prosecution; courts may issue confiscation and deprivation orders against personal assets, prohibit the individual from serving in any regulated financial role, impose travel bans pending investigation, and, upon conviction, imprison the individual for up to 14 years.
