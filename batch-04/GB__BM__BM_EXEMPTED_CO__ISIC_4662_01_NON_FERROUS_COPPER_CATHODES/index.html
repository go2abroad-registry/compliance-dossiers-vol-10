Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Bermudian law; an exempted company is treated as exempt from local tax via the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. The company may also elect to file as a non-resident undertaking under the Economic Substance Regulations for economic substance reporting. Hybrid mismatch elections are not applicable because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax on exempted companies; classification in foreign jurisdictions (e.g., UK or US) depends on their respective tax residence and controlled-foreign-company rules.

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Bermuda. No economic turnover threshold applies for indirect tax purposes. Customs duty registration is required for importers without a turnover threshold; payroll tax registration is triggered upon any Bermudian employment engagement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda does not levy VAT, therefore no export zero-rating regime exists. International wholesale trading of copper cathodes by a Bermuda Exempted Company is outside the scope of Bermudian indirect tax. No input VAT recovery or export refund mechanism is available locally. Zero-rating or VAT recovery may only arise in a foreign VAT jurisdiction such as the United Kingdom if a taxable presence or relevant supply nexus exists.

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate exists for Bermuda VAT because no VAT regime applies. Bermuda does not administer export VAT, so no clawback for fraudulent zero-rating exists locally. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed rate of 1:1 to the United States Dollar. Exchange control is administered by the BMA under a regime of 'general permission' and 'specific permission'. For a Bermuda Exempted Company designated as 'non-resident' for exchange control purposes, current account transactions, capital account movements, and profit/dividend remittances are not subject to routine individual approval, provided the funds originate from outside the BMA's exchange control area and are settled through a licensed Bermuda bank. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal for pure offshore wholesale trading companies that are not carrying on a 'relevant activity' under the Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Amendment Act 2018; the key practical conditions are an approved non-resident status, up-to-date BMA annual returns, and a clean AML/KYC record at the paying bank. Capital account transactions by residents remain restricted, but the non-resident Exempted Company is outside that restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender or forced conversion of export proceeds. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, a general permission permits a non-resident Exempted Company to retain export proceeds in any currency and to deposit them into an external account or a Bermuda licensed bank account without conversion. There is no statutory timeline for repatriation and no mandatory conversion ratio because the BMD/USD parity and general permission operate together to render conversion a purely commercial choice. The company must simply maintain proper accounting records and, upon request, provide the BMA or its appointed bank with evidence of the underlying physical copper cathode trade.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting as the monetary authority, supervisor, and exchange control administrator under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Exchange Control Act 1972, and subsidiary Exchange Control Regulations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating is Low. Bermuda does not have a distinct e-money institution licensing regime under local law; deposit-taking activity is reserved to licensed banks under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. A non-resident overseas EMI, including a UK FCA-regulated EMI, cannot validly provide fiat e-money settlement accounts to a Bermuda Exempted Company on a standalone basis because no EU/EEA passport extends to Bermuda and the BMA does not license pure electronic money institutions. A Bermuda Digital Asset Business licence under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 may cover virtual asset wallet custody but not fiat treasury clearing. Therefore, cross-border fintech EMI rails are viable only as a secondary top-up channel after primary banking at a licensed Bermuda commercial bank, and even then only with strict BMA and correspondent-bank legal opinions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and shareholder loan capital from a UK or global parent or sister company must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 through the recipient bank's correspondent network into the account of the Bermuda Exempted Company at a BMA-licensed bank. The wire instruction must include a concise purpose field referencing 'CAPITAL / EQUITY INJECTION', the name of the Bermuda Exempted Company, and the approved non-resident designation. Because the company holds a BMA general permission as a non-resident, no prior BMA approval is required, but the bank will require a certified copy of the share allotment resolution and, if the injection is a shareholder loan, a signed loan agreement. Verification is satisfied by providing the source of funds and a provenance letter from the remitting bank. BMD credits are booked at parity at 1:1 USD, and the capital must be used solely for the stated copper cathode trading treasury purposes.Outbound trade disbursements for the purchase of Grade A refined copper cathodes use a hybrid of documentary commercial letters of credit (UCP 600), LME warrant-backed settlement, and open-account bank payment obligations. The primary clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for USD/EUR/GBP payments, commercial documentary credits advised by the Bermuda bank and confirmed by a top-tier international bank, and LME warrant transfer initiated through registered LME clerks/warehouses with payment made against presented warehouse receipts. USD payments clear via Fedwire or CHIPS through the New York correspondent of HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield; EUR clears via TARGET2; GBP clears via CHAPS. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None for a standard exempted company outside the CIT Act 2024. For CIT-covered MNE groups, the trigger is consolidated annual revenue exceeding EUR 750m; the Bermuda Tax Administration expects contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation (Master File/Local File) where controlled transactions exceed BMD 5 million in the year, and may require a benchmarking study for the commodity resale function.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda’s Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) enumerate 'distribution and service centres' as a relevant activity. Wholesale arbitrage of Grade A refined copper cathodes falls within that category. The exempted company must prove core income-generating activities are performed in Bermuda, maintain physical premises and staff, incur sufficient Bermuda operating expenditure, and carry on board management in Bermuda. The ESR return must be filed annually, irrespective of nil tax liability; failure to satisfy the substance test can result in penalties, strike-off, and automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and UK de Facto Residence

Statutory sanction: UK Corporation Tax at 25%, CFC attribution, transfer pricing adjustments, and penalties up to 200% of the omitted corporation tax under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24. HMRC can also issue charging assessments and pursue the tax through the Tunisian? Actually, through the exchange of information under the UK-Bermuda DTA.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil is available where the company is a sham and its directors knowingly orchestrated the UK management structure. Directors face personal civil restitution for the avoided tax, travel bans prohibiting departure from the UK or Bermuda pending investigation, and imprisonment for up to 7 years under the Fraud Act / Failure to Prevent Tax Evasion offence.
Shell Company / Breach of Economic Substance Regulations

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Economic Substance Regulations culminating in BMD 250,000, compulsory information exchange with HMRC, and possible striking-off or liquidation of the exempted company.

Director personal liability: Veil piercing for a pure letterbox company is directly available: directors may be required to restore the company’s capital and foreign profits, and may face Bermuda court asset freezes, travel bans, and a 5-year directorship disqualification.
AML/Bank Fraud and Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda’s Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) Act and the AML/ATF Code, imprisonment up to 14 years and unlimited fines for money laundering; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may levy fines up to BMD 200,000 and revoke any regulated-service provider approvals.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize or remain knowingly blind to disguised source-of-funds structures face personal criminal liability, asset confiscation, and travel bans; courts will pierce the veil to impose restitution on directors who caused losses to innocent finance providers.
Transfer Pricing and DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: UK CFC/transfer pricing adjustments, recharacterisation of the intermediary as a mere depot or agent, income tax on the full margin, and penalties up to 100–200% of the underpaid tax; local guidelines require arm’s-length compensation equal to a routine distribution return.

Director personal liability: The veil is pierced where the interposition of the Bermudian entity serves no commercial purpose. Directors may be made jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall; they may also be disqualified from acting as UK directors for up to 15 years and subjected to removal of travel privileges during the tax probe.
