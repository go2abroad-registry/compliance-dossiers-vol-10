Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Government of Bermuda – Office of the Tax Commissioner (Corporate Income Tax and payroll tax; portal: www.gov.bm/tax-information); Registrar of Companies (Companies Act 1981 registration and company records); Bermuda Customs Department (customs duties and border clearance). Bermuda has no VAT/GST authority.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned exempted company is first incorporated with the Registrar of Companies and receives a Bermuda Company Registration Number. No VAT ID exists because Bermuda levies no VAT/GST. If the entity is a constituent entity of a multinational group with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more, it must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and obtain a CIT reference/TIN for filing and payment. Non-resident licensors without a Bermuda corporate presence are not required to obtain a Bermuda tax ID for royalty income.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – A Bermuda Exempted Company is ordinarily tax-neutral under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. However, an in-scope constituent entity of a large multinational group is subject to entity-level Bermuda CIT at 15% of Bermuda CIT taxable income. The entity is not a pass-through vehicle for Bermuda tax purposes.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box mechanism exists. The Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 permits designation of a local filing entity to file a consolidated CIT return for a group, and transitional/administrative elections may apply for group registration and tax base calculations. Economic Substance (Companies) Act filing obligations are mandatory rather than elective. Hybrid mismatch and entity classification elections recognised in other jurisdictions are not generally available in Bermuda.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Bermuda imposes no national VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; the only significant indirect fiscal levies are customs duties, stamp duty, and payroll tax. Cross-border royalty and intellectual property licence income is not subject to Bermuda VAT/GST.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable because Bermuda has no VAT/GST registration threshold. For physical imports or exports, an entity may require customs registration or an importer authorisation with the Bermuda Customs Department, but no economic turnover threshold applies for VAT purposes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT/GST export zero-rating regime exists in Bermuda because cross-border supplies are not subject to indirect tax. Royalty or patent-licence payments received by a Bermuda Exempted Company from non-resident licensees are not zero-rated; they are simply outside Bermuda indirect taxation. If the entity is in-scope for Bermuda CIT, such royalty income may be taxable at 15% and is not eligible for zero-rating relief.

  • Customs Export Declaration – only if physical goods are involved; no export VAT zero-rating form is required
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / courier receipt evidencing export movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT credit confirmation showing receipt of royalty consideration
  • Underlying patent/trademark licence agreement, royalty calculation model, and intercompany transfer pricing documentation
  • Bermuda Economic Substance Statement and supporting corporate records showing substance in Bermuda

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda. Tax record-keeping follows the Companies Act 1981 and, where applicable, the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; no prescribed VAT invoice format is required.

Because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating clawback mechanism and no statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline; the 0-day field reflects the absence of any Bermuda exchange-control obligation. Tax audit risk focuses on the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023: inaccurate filings can attract interest, monetary penalties, retrospective reassessments, and, in serious cases, criminal sanctions. Customs duty evasion on goods can lead to seizure, penalties, and prosecution. Failure to meet Bermuda economic substance requirements can also result in substantial penalties and possible loss of the exempted company's tax-neutral assurances.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged one-to-one with the US Dollar (USD), and USD and BMD are freely convertible. For Bermuda exempted companies classified as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act, no exchange-control approval is required for current-account or capital-account transactions in permitted currencies. Profit remittances, dividends, royalties, and licensing fees can be repatriated without substantive restriction, provided the paying account is held with an authorised dealer in Bermuda and the underlying transaction is supported by board resolutions and the licensed IP agreement. Non-permitted or non-designated currencies may require an exchange-control permit from the BMA, but this does not affect USD/BMD or major hard-currency flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda does not impose a statutory surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds earned by exempted companies. BMA exchange-control supervision focuses on reporting and the classification of the entity as resident or non-resident; non-resident exempted companies are not directed to convert export receipts into BMD, nor are they subject to a prescribed remittance timeline. Any inward foreign-currency receipts should be accompanied by the relevant underlying contract and bank reference to satisfy anti-money-laundering (AML) source-of-funds checks.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Exchange Control Act 1972, the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and the Digital Asset Business Act 2018.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Cross-border digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) solutions such as Wise, Revolut, or Payoneer are not viable as the primary or sole treasury clearing rail for the Bermuda IP HoldCo. Such offshore EMIs are not licensed or regulated as banks by the BMA, cannot hold designated BMD accounts, and generally do not support commercial letters of credit or high-value SWIFT royalty clearing. Their permissible role is ancillary disbursement of small licensing fees via a UK or EU parent treasury account, subject to transfer-pricing consistency and AML transparency. Any digital asset or payment service operated in or from Bermuda may separately require registration under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018; engaging an unlicensed EMI would create a regulatory breach.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections should be routed by SWIFT MT103 for high-value transfers or MT202 for interbank cover payments, from the parent entity's corporate bank in the UK or another approved jurisdiction to the Bermuda IP HoldCo's BMD/USD operational account at Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda. The ISO 20022 purpose code should be entered as 'INTC' (intercompany payment) or 'CFIN' (capital financing); field 70 must state 'Equity contribution to Bermuda IP licensing subsidiary' or 'Subordinated loan for IP asset acquisition and licensing activities'. The receiving Bermuda bank will verify: certified board resolution approving the capital movement; signed capital injection agreement; UBO identification; and confirmation that no BMA exchange-control permit is required for USD/BMD-denominated flows. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% CIT under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated global revenue ≥ €750 million; otherwise 0% — a Bermuda exempted company with no in-scope MNE parent bears no corporate income tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda does not impose withholding tax on dividends declared by exempted companies, regardless of the recipient's residence, and there is no local tax on distributions.
