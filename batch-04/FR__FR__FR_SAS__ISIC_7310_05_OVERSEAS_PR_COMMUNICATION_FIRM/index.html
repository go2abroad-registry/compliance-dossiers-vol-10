Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible for a French SAS in the PR and communications sector, provided the registered office is addressed by a valid domiciliation agreement with an approved provider authorized to receive corporate mail and RCS notices. A purely virtual office is sufficient for RCS registration, but French tax and social authorities may assess the effective seat (siège réel) for commercial-substance purposes. The provider must be approved under Code de commerce article L.123-11-2.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital account convertibility under EU and French law; no restrictions on profit remittances, dividends, or capital repatriation for resident French entities.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds required under EU free movement of capital rules; services-based transactions (e.g., PR retainers) are fully convertible.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability; French regulations prohibit standalone offshore digital EMIs without local banking partnerships or correspondent relationships, due to strict AML and payment services directives.
