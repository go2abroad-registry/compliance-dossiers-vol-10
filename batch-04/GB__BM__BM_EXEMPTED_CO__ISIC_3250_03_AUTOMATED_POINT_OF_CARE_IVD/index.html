Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda does not have a domestic check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. A Bermuda Exempted Company is taxed on a zero-rate basis under Bermuda law (Income Tax Act 1986). UK or other foreign tax classifications and elections (e.g., US Form 8832 check-the-box, UK CFC or NOM elections) are determined by the relevant foreign jurisdiction, not by Bermuda. Bermuda permits corporate elections relating to share restrictions and constitution matters but these are non-tax elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; indirect taxation limited to Bermuda Customs Duties (import duties) and Excise Duties on certain goods; no general sales tax or value added tax on supplies of goods or services

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, so there is no registration threshold. For customs purposes, any importer/exporter must register and clear goods through Bermuda Customs, regardless of turnover. For payroll tax, any employer with Bermuda resident employees must register irrespective of payroll amount.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable domestically; Bermuda does not impose VAT/GST, so there is no export zero-rating mechanism and no VAT refund system. A Bermuda Exempted Company exporting IVD equipment or microfluidic cartridges is not subject to export tax or VAT in Bermuda. To substantiate tax benefits in foreign jurisdictions (e.g., UK VAT zero-rating for goods exported to a non-UK destination), the company must satisfy the foreign jurisdiction's conditions, including physical export within statutory deadlines and obtaining contemporaneous evidence of departure.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs T-1/BL electronic form; or UK export declaration if goods move via UK/EU hub)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note signifying physical international transport
  • Commercial Invoice (prepared by Bermuda exporter, describing IVD analyzers / microfluidic cartridges by HC/HS code)
  • Packing List and cargo manifest
  • Certificate of Origin (Bermuda/UK) if preferential origin claimed
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer advice evidencing FX proceeds repatriation to the Bermuda operating account
  • Destination-country import entry or broker documentation showing arrival and clearance
  • Any applicable export permit/license for IVD or controlled medical equipment

Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance e-invoicing (no CTC/digital stamping). Invoices may be issued in paper or electronic format without government pre-clearance. The only electronic clearance mandate is for customs declarations: importers/exporters must submit electronic declarations to the Bermuda Customs & Border Control using the prescribed digital system (e.g., T-1, BL, or E-Form). For UK VAT, if the Bermuda entity is separately UK VAT registered, UK Making Tax Digital (MTD) obligations apply, but this is not a Bermuda mandate.

Bermuda does not impose VAT clawback penalties for export transactions. However, the following liabilities apply: (1) under Bermuda Customs law, false declarations or smuggling trigger forfeiture, penalties up to the value of the goods plus damages, and criminal sanctions; (2) under UK VAT, if the Bermuda entity is UK VAT-registered and claims export zero-rating without complying with proof-of-export rules, HMRC may assess 100% of the tax underdeclared plus interest, and fraudulent conduct may lead to penalties of up to 100% or criminal prosecution; (3) the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 imposes penalties (BMD 10,000–250,000) for failure to demonstrate adequate economic substance in Bermuda, which may be trigged by failure to repatriate and deploy export proceeds; (4) false or backdated shipping documents may cause the transaction to be recharacterised as a domestic sale, leading to import duty liability and loss of export evidence; and (5) the Bermuda AML/ATF regime may impose regulatory sanctions for suspicious financial flows such as non-return of export proceeds.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a currency-board regime with the Bermudian dollar (BMD) pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar. Both current-account and capital-account flows are fully convertible for exempted companies; no exchange-control permission is required where the entity has been granted Unrestricted Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972. Profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are free of prior BMA approval, subject only to standard bank-level source-of-wealth verification and maintenance of Economic Substance Compliance. There are no profit remittance hurdles, withholding taxes, or conversion quotas for non-resident owned exempted companies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratios for export receipts in Bermuda. Export proceeds may be retained in USD, BMD, or any other currency of the transaction. If conversion is requested, the receiving bank conducts conversion at the fixed 1:1 BMD/USD peg through the BMA clearing infrastructure. No surrender timeline applies to the IVD manufacturing entity's export collections.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for unlicensed offshore digital EMIs. A foreign EMI cannot maintain a settlement account with a Bermuda clearing bank unless it is separately licensed or registered by the Bermuda Monetary Authority under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 or the Digital Asset Business Act 2018. For this IVD manufacturing entity, a regulated Bermuda commercial bank account is the only viable gateway for SWIFT-cleared global payments.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Bermuda Exempted Company holding the standard Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 certificate (tax exemption guaranteed until 31 March 2035); 15% headline rate applies only to in-scope multinational enterprise groups with consolidated global revenue of at least EUR 750 million under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA 2023), effective for tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividend distributions by an Exempted Company, irrespective of shareholder residence; the Tax Protection Certificate further immunizes distributions from all Bermuda income taxation until 31 March 2035, and distributions to a UK or other non-resident parent are made gross.

Mandatory even where the exempted company has no Bermuda-source income; omission is an immediate default. Line 1 - Taxable Remuneration All salaries, wages, commissions, bonuses, and taxable benefits paid for services rendered in Bermuda, including compensation of Bermuda-resident IVD engineering and administrative staff. Remuneration for services performed entirely outside Bermuda is excluded from Bermuda payroll tax.
