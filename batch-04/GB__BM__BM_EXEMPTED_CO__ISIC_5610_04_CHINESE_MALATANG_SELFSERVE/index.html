Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Department of Revenue – Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; official portal: www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities forming a Bermuda Exempted Company obtain a Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Companies via a licensed corporate service provider. Bermuda does not levy income tax, VAT, or sales tax, and therefore no general corporate tax identification number is issued. Statutory registrations apply only in specific trigger situations: (1) if the entity employs individuals in Bermuda, it must register as an employer for Payroll Tax with the Office of the Tax Commissioner; (2) if it imports goods into Bermuda, it must register with the Bermuda Customs Administration for a Customs Duty Account; (3) a Bermuda Business Number (BBN) may be required for electronic government transactions. The standard procedure is: (a) incorporate and file constitutional documents; (b) apply for a Tax Solvent Certificate / Tax Protection Certificate where available; (c) register for payroll tax only where local employees exist; (d) register for customs duty only where physical imports occur; (e) obtain sectoral licensing where required (e.g., food franchise and trademark licensing activities are generally unregulated but may require trade licensing).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BMD is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 USD peg, and both capital and current accounts are effectively open for a Bermuda Exempted Company designated 'non-resident' under the Exchange Control Act 1972. That designation confers general exchange-control permission: the entity may hold, borrow, lend, invest and remit in any foreign currency (USD, GBP, CNY) without case-by-case BMA approval. Profit remittance is unrestricted: Bermuda levies no income tax, corporate tax, withholding tax on dividends, interest or royalties, or capital gains tax applicable to the Exempted Company, so no tax clearance is required before remitting royalty receipts or capital distributions to the UK parent. The only practical hurdle is settlement infrastructure: all non-BMD flows must clear through US-dollar correspondent banking in New York (CHIPS/Fedwire), and no domestic CNY or GBP clearing exists in Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio applies to a Bermuda Exempted Company designated 'non-resident' under the Exchange Control Act 1972: it may retain 100% of export and royalty proceeds denominated in USD or any other foreign currency, with no prescribed surrender timeline and a 0% mandatory conversion obligation. The BMA retains a reserve power to impose exchange-control directions on Bermuda-resident entities, but the non-resident designation removes this Exempted Company from the general requirement to offer foreign currency to an authorized dealer; a notice of non-resident designation issued by the registered agent must be maintained on file for each banking relationship.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — Bermuda's central bank and integrated financial services regulator; administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations; the Bermuda dollar (BMD) operates under a hard peg of BMD 1 = USD 1.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium feasibility with material legal caveats. BMA-regulated fintech or EMI presence in Bermuda is minimal; practical support comes from UK/EU FCA-authorized digital EMIs (for example Airwallex, Currenxie, Revolut Business) that can lawfully maintain USD/GBP multi-currency operational accounts for a Bermuda Exempted Company and host SWIFT MT103 royalty wires. Legal constraints: (1) funds are held by the EMI's program bank, not the EMI itself, creating tiered counterparty and insolvency risk; (2) most EMI compliance policies restrict offshore entities unless the customer demonstrates substantive economic activity and a clean UBO chain; (3) an EMI cannot provide BMD domestic clearing, Local POS acquiring, or Commercial LC issuance, all of which require a licensed Bermuda bank; (4) Bermuda economic-substance filings must still evidence core income-generating activity in Bermuda, so the primary treasury account should remain with a fully licensed Bermuda bank, with the EMI used only as a secondary sweep rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: UK parent (GB) initiates an SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV for USD-cleared cover) from its UK bank into the Bermuda Exempted Company's USD account at HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield, with the US-dollar leg cleared through the correspondent network (CHIPS/Fedwire). UK outward-reporting purpose code 'INVC' (Investment Capital) must be quoted, with a field-70 narrative reading 'Bermuda Exempted Company share subscription — capital injection.' Because the Exempted Company holds non-resident exchange-control designation, no BMA approval is required for the inward remittance. Verification bundle for the receiving bank: board resolution of the UK parent authorizing the contribution; allotment resolution and share certificate of the Bermuda entity; updated register of members; registered-agent compliance sign-off; and a source-of-funds pack comprising the UK shareholder's bank statements and audited financials. Capital may be subscribed in USD or BMD at the 1:1 peg; no statutory minimum capital applies, but a subscription amount commensurate with the master-franchise expansion plan strengthens the entity's economic-substance file.Two distinct clearing rails apply. (1) Master-royalty and licence disbursements to the Chinese Malatang brand owner are executed by SWIFT MT103 in USD (with MT202 COV) from the Bermuda bank through a New York correspondent (for example BNY Mellon or Citibank) to the Chinese beneficiary bank's US correspondent, with onshore CNY conversion occurring at the receiving bank; alternatively, settlement in offshore CNH is available where the Chinese counterparty holds a CNH account. (2) Equipment imports (self-serve broth vending machines and POS hardware) from China are supported by an Irrevocable Documentary Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Bermuda bank, advised or confirmed through a Chinese advising bank, with inspection-certificate and transport-document conditions; low-value items may be handled by documentary collection (D/P). Local POS revenue from UK/European franchisees is captured by each franchisee's acquiring bank and remitted to the Bermuda treasury account as consolidated monthly royalty wires (SWIFT MT103), with sub-franchise agreement references quoted in field 70 for automated reconciliation against the royalty schedule. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% — Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA); applies solely to Bermuda businesses forming part of an MNE group with consolidated annual revenue ≥ EUR 750 million; otherwise the exempted company bears a 0% income tax rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda does not impose any dividend withholding tax, income tax, or capital gains tax on distributions made by an Exempted Co to non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File/Master File for sub-threshold exempted companies under Bermuda law. Mandatory documentation (CbCR, Master File, Local File) applies only to MNE groups with consolidated revenue ≥ BMD 750,000,000 (≈ EUR 750M) under the CIT/GloBE framework. For a small franchise master licence vehicle, maintain a contemporaneous arm's-length royalty benchmark (TNMM/CUP) to defend the royalty rate before HMRC.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda ESR 2018 applies. The Malatang master licensing activity is an Intellectual Property Business requiring CIGA in Bermuda: strategic IP acquisition/exploitation decisions, risk assumption, and contract negotiations. The entity must maintain local premises, full-time employees, and proportionate expenditure. A dormant or pure-equity exemption is unavailable where active royalty income exists. UK CFC rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 9A) and HMRC's global anti-avoidance review will independently test substance against UK activities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / False Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment of responsible officers up to 5 years; Registrar strike-off and dissolution; UK CFC charge on apportioned profits (up to 25% UK CT).

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors jointly and severally liable for penalties and unpaid UK CFC tax; HMRC may apply CGT/IT charges on personal benefits; travel bans and passport surrender on asset dissipation.
Tax Evasion — False Filings

Statutory sanction: Bermuda: fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years per offence; UK: tax-geared penalty up to 200% of avoided tax and criminal investigation for fraud.

Director personal liability: Personal criminal liability, civil restitution of avoided tax plus interest, director disqualification (Companies Act 1981 and UK CDDA 1986), and international extradition arrest in serious cases.
AML / Economic Crime

Statutory sanction: Bermuda AML/ATF Regulations 2008: unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 14 years under anti-money laundering legislation; UK POCA 2002 parallel offences; bank account freezes and client funds forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors liable for failing to prevent facilitation of tax evasion (UK Criminal Finances Act 2017); personal confiscation orders, travel bans, and disqualification from holding financial services functions.
UK CFC / Transfer Pricing Attack

Statutory sanction: UK CFC apportionment at up to 25% of diverted IP-condition profits; transfer pricing adjustment of intercompany royalty; interest and penalties up to 200% of the avoided UK tax.

Director personal liability: Directors of the UK parent face personal liability for unremitted PAYE-like CFC charges arranged by them; HMRC may issue personal liability notices and impose travel bans for deliberate wrongdoing.
Non-Filing and Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: Automatic default fines, daily surcharges, then dissolution by the Registrar; assets vested in the Crown; loss of the tax protection certificate and UK bank accounts frozen.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for continuing penalties; restoration proceedings at personal cost; creditors may sue directors for trading after strike-off notice; immigration bans for fraudulent continuation.
