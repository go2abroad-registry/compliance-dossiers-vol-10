Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; official portal: https://www.gov.bm/tax (secure employer payroll-tax filing is via the Office of the Tax Commissioner's electronic filing platform).

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is not issued an income-tax TIN or VAT ID because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax and no VAT/GST. The statutory procedure is: (1) incorporate under the Companies Act 1981 through the Registrar of Companies and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and Bermuda company registration number; (2) if the company employs persons in Bermuda, register as an employer for Bermuda Payroll Tax with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain an Employer Payroll Tax Account Number, with registration due within 14 days of becoming an employer; a zero-employee holding company has no payroll-tax registration; (3) if importing goods, register as an importer with the Bermuda Customs Department or appoint a licensed customs broker; (4) optionally apply for a Tax Assurance Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. No general income-tax registration or tax clearance is required solely to hold an exempted company.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box or entity-classification election because exempted companies are per se outside the Bermuda income-tax net. The principal statutory election is to apply for a Tax Assurance Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which protects the company against any future Bermuda tax on profits, income, distributions, capital, assets, gains or appreciation enacted before 28 March 2035. There are no hybrid-mismatch elections in Bermuda; hybrid treatment is a function of the investor's home-country rules. If the exempted company carries on a relevant activity under the Economic Substance Act 2018, it must make annual economic-substance filings; pure FPV drone manufacturing and retailing is not in itself a relevant activity, but related IP holding or financing activities could trigger substance reporting.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax regime. Bermuda's indirect/fiscal levies are import customs duties and employer payroll tax; neither is a VAT or general consumption tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no Bermuda VAT registration threshold exists. For payroll tax, an employer becomes registrable at the first payment of Bermuda remuneration, i.e. a BMD 0 threshold; for customs duty, an importer is registrable upon first importation to Bermuda, also with no turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, exports from Bermuda are outside the scope of Bermuda consumption tax; no VAT zero-rating claim, export refund, or input-tax recovery mechanism exists in Bermuda. A Bermuda Exempted Company selling FPV racing drones abroad exports without charging Bermuda output tax. Bermuda is outside the UK VAT and customs territory; if goods are shipped from Bermuda to the United Kingdom, UK import VAT and customs duty arise at importation unless a relief such as temporary admission or customs warehousing applies. If the same entity has a UK taxable establishment making UK supplies, UK VAT registration and the normal UK zero-rating of exports to non-UK persons are evaluated under UK law, with the place-of-supply and agent/principal rules governing any UK VAT liability.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed with the Bermuda Customs Department before export
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exportation from Bermuda to the foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit confirmation evidencing receipt of export consideration
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for the FPV drones
  • HS classification and product documentation for the unmanned aerial-vehicle consignment

Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal electronic invoicing, tax-authority clearance of invoices, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. Import/export declarations are filed electronically with the Bermuda Customs Department, and payroll returns are filed electronically, but commercial invoices are not cleared by the tax authority and no API clearance regime applies. Records and invoices must be retained to substantiate customs classifications and payroll filings.

There is no Bermuda VAT zero-rating to claw back. However, false export or customs documentation can lead to seizure/forfeiture and fines under the Bermuda Customs Act, and false statements to the Tax Commissioner can lead to payroll-tax penalties and regulatory consequences. If foreign VAT zero-rating, for example UK VAT on exports, is claimed on the strength of Bermuda documents, the relevant tax authority may disallow the zero-rating and assess the VAT, interest, and penalties of up to 100% of the tax, with criminal liability for fraudulent evasion. Investors should also ensure compliance with Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2018 and anti-money-laundering regulations; deliberate use of a Bermuda exempted company for artificial tax avoidance can be challenged under foreign CFC, GAAR, or anti-hybrid rules, resulting in recharacterization and retrospective clawback of intended tax benefits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar (USD), and the BMA maintains full convertibility between BMD and USD for all licensed financial institutions. Bermuda operates under a liberal exchange-control regime: current-account transactions are freely convertible, and capital-account transfers relating to a Bermuda Exempted Company — including inbound equity subscriptions, intercompany loans, and distribution of dividends or profits to non-resident shareholders — are generally approved without material restriction. Profit remittance to non-resident beneficial owners is permitted subject only to standard anti-money-laundering (AML), tax residence, and beneficial-ownership filings. No exchange-control quota or prior approval is required for payments of exported goods or services where the Bermuda Exempted Company is treated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes. The BMA may impose reporting obligations on banks for statistical monitoring, but there is no exchange-control surcharge, stamp duty on repatriation, or minimum holding period for foreign currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender, conversion, or holding-period requirement on export proceeds earned by a Bermuda Exempted Company. Export receipts in USD, BMD, or any freely convertible foreign currency may be retained in the company's offshore banking accounts; the BMA does not require conversion of foreign currency into BMD. The applicable statutory/regulatory framework requires licensed banks to file currency transaction reports and suspicious activity reports under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2020 and the BMA AML/ATF Code of Conduct, but no export-invoice conversion ratio or repatriation timeline applies. If the exempted company maintains a BMD account, a bank may credit export proceeds in equivalent USD under the fixed pegged rate without special BMA authorization.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and delegated powers under the Exchange Control Act 1962 and Exchange Control Regulations 1962; Her Majesty's Treasury as ultimate sovereign authority via Bermuda's overseas territory relationship with the United Kingdom.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for ancillary payment-disbursement accounts, Low as a primary treasury relationship. Bermuda does not maintain a separate EU-style Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licence; deposit-taking and payments in Bermuda require a class of BMA banking or payment-services licensing under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and the BMA's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 regime. A non-resident offshore EMI can lawfully provide USD payment rails for trade disbursements if the EMI holds a BMA-recognised money-service-business licence or operates through an exempted-company agent; however, the BMA treats EMIs as higher-risk for money laundering, and domestic Bermudian banks are generally unwilling to allow a pure offshore EMI to act as the company's principal settlement bank for inbound equity or export collections. Recommended legal architecture: use a Bermudian licensed or branch-recognised bank as treasury anchor, and integrate fintech EMIs only as low-value auxiliary rails for customer refunds or e-commerce payment aggregation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol is a SWIFT MT103 sent from the non-resident parent/holdco bank to the Bermuda Exempted Company's Bermudian operational bank account, with full legal-name matching on remitter and beneficiary fields. For equity capital, remittance details (Field 72) must state 'Bermuda Exempted Company capital contribution under Companies Act 1981; banking purpose: BMD/USD deposit'; the inbound USD amount is converted at 1:1 where credited to BMD, or held in a USD currency account. Prior to any sizeable inbound capital injection, the Bermudian commercial bank will require a copy of the resolution of directors, an updated register of members, a signed support letter from the beneficial owner, and a certificate of solvency if the transfer exceeds a bank's enhanced due-diligence threshold. There is no BMA exchange-control licence for non-resident exempted-company equity, but the bank may require a written 'Exchange Control - General Licence / Specific Licence' application when the company has resident Bermudian shareholders; for a wholly non-resident Exempted Company, remittance purpose code 'NTRZ / FXA / EQUITY CONTRIBUTION' is applied in accordance with SWIFT and correspondent-bank conventions.Primary clearing rails are: (i) SWIFT MT103 for straight-through interbank wire transfers to Hong Kong, Chinese, Taiwan, and European suppliers of brushless motors and carbon-fiber pre-preg materials; (ii) commercial Letters of Credit (LC) governed by UCP600, issued by a Bermudian commercial bank and advised/confirmed through the supplier's bank, for large fabricated-component contracts above US$250,000 per shipment; (iii) documentary collections (D/P, D/A) for freight-on-board drone-component shipments to mitigate risk without full LC cost; and (iv) PayPal-Stripe cross-border payout rails for retail and marketplace receipts from the target payment rails. For e-commerce, the company will hold USD settlement accounts and convert to GBP/EUR only as needed for UK and European operating expenses. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory local transfer pricing documentation threshold in BMD applies to an out-of-scope Bermuda Exempted Company (consolidated group revenue below EUR 750 million). In-scope multinational groups subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (aggregate revenue at or above BMD 1,000,000,000, roughly EUR 750 million at parity) must prepare and maintain OECD-aligned Local File, Master File, and GloBE information returns; the functional analysis for FPV drone procurement, process engineering, and online distribution must attribute arm's-length profit to any Bermuda principal functions.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2018 apply. If the exempted company conducts a 'Relevant Activity' (most plausibly Distribution and Service Centre, or Headquarters) in Bermuda, it must demonstrate: (i) economic substance proportionate to activity, (ii) management and control exercised in Bermuda (board quorum, strategic minutes), (iii) adequate physical premises and full-time employees, and (iv) c. BMD operating expenditure commensurate with income. A pure equity-holding entity satisfying passive holding requirements enjoys a reduced substance test. Non-compliance triggers penalty up to BMD 250,000, automatic information exchange to HMRC, and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Act 2018 Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to BMD 250,000 per breach plus BMD 25,000 daily default fines; mandatory exchange of information with UK HMRC and onward sharing with overseas tax authorities; court-ordered strike-off and asset restraint orders.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers face personal civil restitution for penalties paid by the company, director disqualification orders, court-imposed travel bans preventing departure from Bermuda, and imprisonment of up to 5 years for knowingly submitting a false or misleading Economic Substance Declaration.
Beneficial Ownership Register Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BMD 25,000 for the company and each defaulting officer; restriction notices issued against shares; freezing of dividend distributions and suspension of voting rights pending remediation; potential compulsory winding-up.

Director personal liability: Personal fines and imprisonment of up to 1 year for each director who knowingly permits non-compliance; disqualification as a director across all Bermuda entities; personal assets exposed to civil recovery orders.
UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 - Failure to Prevent Facilitation of Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Unlimited corporate fine in the UK, worldwide asset-freezing orders, confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, debarment from Stripe/PayPal payment rails, and mandatory disclosure to the Bermuda Tax Commissioner with reciprocal enforcement of UK orders.

Director personal liability: Executive directors may be prosecuted for conspiracy to defraud HMRC, carrying custodial sentences of up to 7 years in the UK with extradition from Bermuda under the UK-US-Bermuda Mutual Legal Assistance framework; personal liability for civil restitution and permanent travel restrictions.
Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Control Deficiency

Statutory sanction: For regulated service providers: penalties up to BMD 1,000,000 under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008. For non-regulated retailers: loss of payment-processor access, forced account closure, customs hold on shipments, and registration cancellation by the Bermuda Registrar.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly involved in laundering proceeds face imprisonment of up to 14 years in Bermuda, personal forfeiture of tainted assets, asset restraint orders, and travel bans preventing exit from the jurisdiction.
Deemed UK Permanent Establishment / Management and Control Dispute

Statutory sanction: HMRC assessment of the entire trading profit to UK corporation tax (19-25%), interest on unpaid tax, penalties of up to 100% of the tax understated, and re-characterisation of the Bermuda entity as a sham for UK tax purposes; Bermuda Registrar may simultaneously strike off the company for failing to maintain local substance.

Director personal liability: UK-resident directors are personally liable for penalties arising from incorrect tax returns, face disqualification as UK company directors, and may be subject to HMRC civil fraud proceedings with restitution and freezing injunctions; egregious cases carry imprisonment of up to 7 years.
