Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Check-the-box election is not available because Bermuda levies no direct taxation. The relevant statutory elections are: (a) Section 7 Certificate of Exemption election guaranteeing tax exemption until 2035; (b) exchange-control non-resident election filed with the Bermuda Monetary Authority; (c) designations and filings under the Economic Substance (Company and Limited Partnership) Act 2018, including pure equity holding company status; hybrid-mismatch elections are inapplicable absent an income tax base.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST — Bermuda imposes only Customs Duties, import stamp duty, and payroll tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT/GST regime and therefore no economic turnover threshold for VAT registration; importers must nonetheless be registered with the Bermuda Customs Department and file customs declarations through a licensed customs broker for dutiable imports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT export zero-rating regime. Relief for international towage and salvage operations is instead available through customs duty exemption or conditional remission on temporarily imported vessels, salvage gear, and equipment subsequently re-exported, subject to Bermuda Customs Department approval and the terms of the Bermuda Customs Tariff; offshore services supplied outside Bermuda are not subject to Bermuda indirect taxation.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration / Temporary Import Re-Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill demonstrating export from Bermuda
  • Lloyd's Standard Form of Salvage Agreement or towage/salvage service contract setting out the offshore engagement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence showing foreign-currency consideration from the salvage/towage principal
  • Vessel registry and statutory safety certificates for the tug or salvage craft
  • P&I Club Letter of Undertaking or security instrument, where applicable

No real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API clearance mandate exists. Customs import/export declarations are filed electronically via the Government of Bermuda's customs electronic declaration system, with supporting documentation retained physically or electronically for audit.

Bermuda has no VAT zero-rating that could be clawed back; however, fraudulent customs declarations, false export evidence, or failure to re-export goods within the approved temporary-import period attract penalties under the Customs Act 1968, including seizure and forfeiture of goods, fines up to treble the duty evaded or BMD 10,000 per offence, imprisonment, and retrospective clawback of any duty remission or exemption with interest. Non-compliance with economic substance filing obligations may additionally trigger fines and strike-off action by the Registrar.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is legally pegged at parity to the U.S. Dollar (USD 1.00 = BMD 1.00) and circulates interchangeably with USD within Bermuda. Bermuda maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account for exempted companies that have been granted non-resident status or a standing Exchange Control permission. Profit repatriation, dividend distributions, interest, liquidation proceeds and intra-group transfers are freely remittable in USD, BMD, GBP or EUR without prior BMA approval; the only practical hurdle is that resident-status exempted companies require case-by-case BMA permission, and banks withhold remittance until the company's non-resident exchange control classification is evidenced in the onboarding documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (s. 27 and the Exchange Control Notices), exports of goods and services from Bermuda must be paid for within six months and, unless exempted, the export proceeds must be offered for sale to an authorised dealer in Bermuda in convertible currency. Bermuda exempted companies granted non-resident status operate under a standing BMA exemption: there is no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio into BMD. Towage and salvage invoices may be settled directly into USD, EUR or GBP bank accounts maintained with the Bermuda bank or an approved offshore account, without conversion.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the integrated financial regulator, central monetary authority and Exchange Control Administrator established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium feasibility with strong legal constraints. Bermuda has no EMI-licensing regime analogous to the UK or EU; the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 restricts deposit-taking to licensed banks, and the BMA expects Bermuda-supervised entities not to rely on unlicensed payment intermediaries. UK/EU fintech EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business, Currencycloud) may be used by the offshore holding entity for low-value ancillary operational payments abroad, but cannot host the capital escrow account, collect towage/salvage invoices, or process statutory dividend remittances for a Bermuda exempted company without engaging an authorised bank in the settlement chain.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity is routed as a SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer from the parent or remitting bank to the Bermuda Exempted Company's operational or capital-escrow account. The application must carry a capital-purpose code (ISO 20022 purpose 'EQUI' — equity with participation; or bank-specific 'CAPITAL' instruction) and must include the remitter's full legal name and account number to support beneficial-owner traceability. The receiving bank will require: (i) the company's Exchange Control non-resident permission; (ii) the capital-escrow release instruction (if funds are held pending satisfaction of the escrow condition); and (iii) a copy of the board resolution authorising issuance and receipt of shares. Funds are generally credited on the same business day and released from escrow only after BMA/AML verification is completed.Clearing rails for settlement: SWIFT MT103 (customer transfer) for routine towage invoice payments, subcontractor disbursements and operational costs; Commercial Letters of Credit, issued under the SWIFT MT700 form, for high-value salvage and arbitration-award settlements; MT910 statements for verified confirmation; MT199 for pre-advice or status tracking. USD settlements clear same-day via the New York CHIPS/Fedwire corridors; GBP payments clear via CHAPS; EUR via TARGET2. For disbursements exceeding USD 100,000, the bank will expect a settlement memo referencing the underlying contract and BMA-enhanced due diligence on the payee.Plain-vanilla forward contracts (USD/BMD parity hedge and GBP/EUR settlement hedges) executed via a Bermuda-authorised dealer or offshore counterparty.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for BMD where the counterparty cannot physically deliver BMD, given the 1:1 USD parity arrangement.Cross-currency swaps for long-duration towage or salvage charter receivables in EUR/GBP versus USD funding exposure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold for non-CIT Exempted Companies. For CIT-scope MNEs, Local File and Master File preparation is triggered when the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 1.0 billion) and the Bermuda entity engages in controlled transactions, or when the Bermuda Revenue Agency issues an information notice for related-party flows above BMD 10 million.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Companies) Act 2018 and related regulations apply. Offshore towage and marine salvage can constitute a shipping business where the company earns income from tugboat operations, chartering, or salvage rewards. The Exempted Company must maintain directed and managed activities in Bermuda, perform core income-generating activities in Bermuda, and have adequate employees, premises, and operating expenditure. Pure equity holding treatment is not available for active towage/salvage income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

CIT Evasion and False Statements

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to BD$100,000 per return plus monthly default surcharges; criminal conviction for tax evasion may result in fines up to BD$250,000 and imprisonment up to five years.

Director personal liability: Officers and directors who authorized or signed a false CIT return may be jointly and severally liable for penalties, subject to criminal prosecution, and barred from leaving Bermuda while charges are pending; courts may pierce the corporate veil where the company is a mere conduit for fraud.
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Initial ESR penalty of BD$10,000 plus BD$10,000 per month of continuing non-compliance; repeated failure permits the Registrar of Companies to strike off the entity and exchange information with the foreign tax authority of the ultimate parent.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified for up to five years and personally fined for failing to remediate substance violations; travel bans and asset seizure may follow where the shell is used to shelter foreign taxable profit.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Revenue Agency may recharacterize the transaction, assess additional CIT at 15%, add statutory interest, and impose documentation penalties of up to BD$50,000 for failure to provide an OECD-compliant transfer-pricing analysis.

Director personal liability: CFOs and directors who knowingly approve fabricated related-party pricing are personally exposed for fraudulent civil wrongs, penalty surcharges, and criminal prosecution where the arrangement is designed to defeat the CITA 2023.
AML, Sanctions, and Maritime Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime Act and applicable financial sanctions legislation, penalties include fines up to BD$1,000,000, forfeiture of the funds or vessel, and imprisonment up to ten years for money laundering offences.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize or execute sanctionable wire transfers or escrow releases face personal criminal liability, asset freezes, and travel bans; the corporate veil will be pierced if the exempted company is used as an instrument of maritime fraud or sanctions evasion.
