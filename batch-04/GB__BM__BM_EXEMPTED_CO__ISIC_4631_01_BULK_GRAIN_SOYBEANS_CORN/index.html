Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (electronic registry portal: www.gov.bm). Income-tax administration: Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, administering the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA) with administrative services (registration, filing, assessment, collection, enforcement) provided by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) under the 2024 Bermuda-CRA service agreement. Customs (indirect imports): Bermuda Customs Department (Comptroller of Customs), Ministry of Finance, under the Customs Management and Duty Act 2010 and the Customs Tariff Act.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — Incorporate under the Companies Act 1981 through a licensed local law firm/corporate service provider; file the electronic incorporation application with the Registrar of Companies; obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and Company Registration Number (CRN). Step 2 — Obtain Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) 'non-resident' designation so that 100% non-Bermudian ownership and unrestricted foreign-currency operations are permitted. Step 3 — File for a Tax Exemption Certificate (TEC) under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing exemption from future Bermudian taxes until 31 March 2035. Step 4 — Annually file with the ROC portal the Company Annual Return (within 60 days after the anniversary of incorporation) and the Economic Substance Declaration (ESD). Step 5 — If the entity is a member of an MNE group with consolidated group revenue of at least €750 million, register for Bermuda CIT with the Office of the Tax Commissioner (CRA-administered process) and obtain a CIT tax identification number; an out-of-scope brokerage is not issued a CIT TIN and no VAT/GST registration exists in Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — The Bermuda Exempted Company is not a flow-through but a tax-exempt corporation: Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, dividend tax, or net-worth/asset tax on exempted companies, and the TEC shields the entity from any newly introduced Bermudian income taxes to 2035. The only modern exception is CITA, which from fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025 taxes in-scope MNE groups (consolidated revenue ≥ €750 million) at 15% via the IIR, UTPR and Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax; a small agri-commodity brokerage with no group at that threshold remains wholly exempt. Territorial scope: services rendered and contracts performed outside Bermuda (offshore bulk grain brokerage) are not sourced in Bermuda and incur no Bermudian tax; no minimum capital, no statutory licence, and no local ownership is required for this ISIC 4631 brokerage business.

Available election forms

No US-style 'check-the-box' or elective flow-through classification exists under Bermudian law, and no hybrid-mismatch check-the-box election is available. Available statutory elections/designations: (1) Tax Exemption Certificate (TEC) under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (2) BMA 'non-resident' exchange-control designation; (3) for in-scope CITA groups, the GloBE computation rules (IIR/UTPR/DMTT) apply compulsorily with no elective opt-out; the CITA is not a check-the-box regime and contains no hybrid-mismatch election; (4) UK parents of the Bermudian entity must separately apply the UK Multinational Top-up Tax rules to the entity's low-taxed profits, but that is a UK group-level obligation, not a Bermudian election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — No VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax is levied in Bermuda. The indirect fiscal regime comprises: (a) customs import duty under the Customs Tariff Act and Customs Management and Duty Act 2010 (applied only to goods physically imported into Bermuda for local consumption); (b) payroll tax on Bermudian employer remuneration; and (c) stamp duty on certain instruments. Offshore brokerage commission for bulk soybeans/corn arrangements is outside all three instruments.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists (BMD 0). An importer of dutiable goods into Bermuda must hold a customs registration and declare each import electronically; no turnover-based threshold triggers indirect-tax registration. Payroll tax registration is required only when the entity has Bermudian employees.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim, no export-refund mechanism, and no export duty on agricultural commodities. The Exempted Company's offshore brokerage of bulk soybeans and yellow corn (ISIC 4631) involves goods purchased, shipped and sold entirely outside Bermuda; commission income is thus outside any Bermudian transaction tax and is protected by the TEC. For UK group purposes, the supplier is an offshore person with no UK establishment, so the UK imposes no UK VAT supply in scope. Should the goods ever be imported into Bermuda for local consumption, Bermuda customs duty would arise on the Bermudian importer of record at the applicable Tariff Act rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration issued by the customs authority at the port of loading (e.g., Brazil/Argentina/US export filing; no Bermuda filing applies because the goods never enter Bermuda)
  • Full set of original shipped Bills of Lading (order-type), together with the charterparty or booking note evidencing freight terms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate — SWIFT/telex credit advice or bank statement evidencing negotiation of the irrevocable transferable L/C and inward settlement of the brokerage commission
  • Commercial invoice, weight/quality certificate (SGS/Intertek), and phytosanitary/agricultural certificates where required by the L/C terms

No national e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, government API validation, or digital fiscal stamping mandate exists in Bermuda. Commercial invoices are issued under general contract practice with no fiscal clearance. Bermuda Customs operates an electronic declaration/cargo-processing portal for licensed customs brokers to clear imports; this clearance channel does not require per-invoice fiscal validation or e-signature and is irrelevant to goods that never enter Bermuda.

No VAT clawback or export-incentive penalty can arise in Bermuda because no export zero-rating system exists; the TEC prevents retrospective Bermudian income taxation of the brokerage to 2035. Residual compliance risks: (i) Customs — false statements in import declarations expose the importer of record to duty recovery, forfeiture, civil penalties and, for fraud, criminal sanctions under the Customs Management and Duty Act 2010; goods shipped wholly outside Bermuda create no customs exposure. (ii) Economic substance — failure to file the annual ESD or to disclose the non-resident status attracts statutory penalties up to BMD 250,000 for initial default, up to a further BMD 500,000 for continuing default, and officer liability. (iii) CITA — an in-scope MNE group that fails to register, files late, or understates top-up tax faces interest, late-filing and understatement penalties administered by the Office of the Tax Commissioner (with CRA execution), plus the 15% top-up tax itself; fraudulent CIT evasion is a criminal offence. (iv) AML/CFT — as a commission broker settling via SWIFT and documentary L/C, the entity must preserve the full trade file (LC, bills of lading, invoices, SWIFT records) for at least five years; BMA financial-penalty exposure arises for deficient record-keeping. The 0-day FX figure reflects the absence of any statutory Bermudian FX repatriation deadline; an entity designated 'non-resident' by the BMA retains and settles proceeds in any currency free of exchange control.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged to the United States Dollar (USD) at a fixed 1:1 rate and is freely convertible at par. Bermuda maintains no exchange-control regime restricting current-account or capital-account transactions for exempted companies: trade brokerage commissions, disbursements, equity injections, shareholder loans, dividends, and redemption proceeds may flow inward and outward without BMA prior approval. Profit remittance is fully unimpeded because the Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt and may obtain a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing no future Bermudian taxation for up to 50 years; FATCA and CRS reporting obligations are informational only and do not constrain convertibility or repatriation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender ratio, mandatory conversion timeline, or repatriation obligation for export or brokerage receipts. The exempted company may retain 100% of USD or foreign-currency proceeds in any Bermuda-licensed or foreign bank account. The sole statutory overlay is the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008, which mandates suspicious-transaction reporting, customer due diligence for occasional transactions above the statutory threshold, and retention of transaction records for at least five years; no central-bank surrender or conversion of trade proceeds is required.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the central bank and integrated financial services regulator for Bermuda, a British Overseas Territory; the Bank of England retains no direct supervisory competence over Bermuda-licensed banks, although UK Prudential Regulation Authority rules apply only to the UK parent treasury function.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium, subject to structural legal constraints. Bermuda does not maintain a standalone 'Electronic Money Institution' licence; a non-bank payment provider must be licensed under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 or the Corporate and Trust Service Provider Act 2012. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Airwallex cannot operate as Bermuda-resident clearing institutions, cannot issue BMD-clearing accounts in Bermuda, and are not covered by Bermuda deposit insurance. Their permitted role is ancillary: an FCA-authorized UK EMI may serve as a GBP/EUR collection corridor for European counterparties, while primary working capital, USD settlement, and documentary trade finance must remain with BMA-licensed banks. The Bermuda onboarding bank will require full transparency of any EMI used as an incoming-funds source, including the EMI's regulatory licence, AML controls, and benefit-owner details.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers or MT202 COV bank-to-bank transfers from the UK parent or ultimate shareholder, with Field 70 remittance information stating 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - INCREASE IN ISSUED CAPITAL'. USD wires clear through Fedwire or CHIPS into the receiving Bermuda bank's US correspondent and are credited at the 1:1 BMD/USD parity; GBP injections clear through CHAPS and are converted at the bank's published FX rate. The receiving bank requires a verification pack comprising a board resolution approving the share issuance, a share subscription agreement, a capital-introduction application form, and CRS/FATCA self-certification before crediting the Capital Escrow Account; funds are released only against a corresponding entry in the statutory share register under the Companies Act 1981.Trade disbursements utilize two primary clearing rails. First, the documentary trade rail: irrevocable and transferable Letters of Credit governed by UCP 600 and eUCP, issued by the buyer's bank and advised through Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda for bulk soybean and yellow corn purchases, with settlement effected through SWIFT MT700/MT740 message series and correspondent-bank clearing. Second, the open-account or settlement rail: SWIFT MT103 payments with explicit trade narratives such as 'TRADE PAYMENT - COMMODITY PURCHASE' and Incoterms-consistent documentation packages including commercial invoices, full-set bills of lading, phytosanitary certificates, and weight or quality certificates. For the pure brokerage model, third-party cargo proceeds are collected through an escrowed collection-clearing account at Clarien Bank and released only against broker commission notes and buyer confirmations, minimizing idle working-capital float. The UK-GB nexus enables use of the UK Faster Payments scheme solely for sub-threshold GBP credits; all commercial trade settlement remains on SWIFT, CHIPS, Fedwire, or CHAPS.Plain-vanilla FX forwards: BMD/USD at fixed 1:1 parity and USD/GBP, USD/EUR forwards with tenors from one to twelve months, executed through BMA-licensed banks including Butterfield and HSBC Bermuda without exchange-control approval.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs): permissible for hedging USD/BRL, USD/ARS, and USD/UAH exposures arising from South American and Black Sea grain procurement, settled in USD through the Bermuda bank's London or New York trading desk.FX and commodity cross-currency swaps: USD/BMD currency swaps combined with an overlay of CBOT and ICE corn and soybean futures to price-hedge the underlying physical cargoes, carried through an ICE Futures-affiliated clearing broker under BMA-compliant margin arrangements.Currency option collars and zero-cost structures: BMA-licensed banks permit purchased and collared options on USD/GBP and USD/EUR only for bona fide transactional trade cover, with strict prohibition of speculative uncovered positions under Bermuda AML/CFT governance.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: Medium, subject to structural legal constraints. Bermuda does not maintain a standalone 'Electronic Money Institution' licence; a non-bank payment provider must be licensed under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 or the Corporate and Trust Service Provider Act 2012. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Airwallex cannot operate as Bermuda-resident clearing institutions, cannot issue BMD-clearing accounts in Bermuda, and are not covered by Bermuda deposit insurance. Their permitted role is ancillary: an FCA-authorized UK EMI may serve as a GBP/EUR collection corridor for European counterparties, while primary working capital, USD settlement, and documentary trade finance must remain with BMA-licensed banks. The Bermuda onboarding bank will require full transparency of any EMI used as an incoming-funds source, including the EMI's regulatory licence, AML controls, and benefit-owner details.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (zero percent) for all Bermuda Exempted Companies under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, with statutory protection from any future Bermuda tax on income, profits, dividends, or capital gains until 31 March 2035.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to non-resident shareholders, even on distributions of international trading profits.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda statutory local currency: Bermudian Dollar (BMD). No existing CIT, therefore no general transfer pricing documentation threshold in BMD of gross revenue or intercompany transactions is imposed. However, the Economic Substance Act 2019 mandates that a Bermuda entity with relevant activity (headquarters, distribution and service centre, or IP holding) must demonstrate adequate substance proportionate to its core income-generating activities; for an ISIC 4631 grain brokerage with a Bermuda office, the operational threshold is: (i) at least 1 full-time Bermuda-based employee, (ii) a physical Bermuda office, and (iii) annual board meetings held in Bermuda. No Local File/Master File is due, but contemporaneous TP evidence (contracts, approved arm's-length margins for a 1-3% brokerage commission on vessel demurrage and forwarding) must be maintained for 6 years to satisfy the 'documentation' pillar of a Registrar audit.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance (ES) Doctrine applies under the International Tax Authority (Economic Substance) Act 2019 and the Economic Substance Regulations 2020. The exempted company must file an annual ES Declaration and, if in scope, maintain real substance commitments. Bulk agricultural commodity brokerage is not a directly listed 'relevant activity' except where the entity is treated as a 'distribution and service centre' (warehousing/wholesaling of grain) or 'headquarters' (strategic management of overseas procurement). For a pure brokerage vehicle with no Bermuda workforce, the ES filing shows 'No relevant activity', and no core income-generating activities are assessed. However, to avoid OECD-induced substance red flags in a UK parent audit, the Exempted Co must: (a) maintain an actual brick-and-mortar presence if it distributes grain into Bermuda or from a Bermuda logistics hub; (b) ensure board minutes evidence commercial purpose of the Bermuda vehicle; (c) avoid holding any IP without full ES compliance; and (d) prepare an annual internal TP memo even absent a statutory threshold.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Evasion (Shell Company / Sham Arrangement)

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act 2019 and the Registrar of Companies Act 1981: default fine of BMD 10,000 per month; punitive fines of up to BMD 250,000 (US$250,000) for serious non-compliance; Court-ordered direction to file corrected returns, revocation of the Exempted Company's certificate, and mandatory restoration costs. The company may be struck off and its assets vested in the Official Receiver in the event of persistent default.

Director personal liability: Each director and the Corporate Service Provider can be personally jointly and severally liable for unpaid fines and restitution of USD 250,000; for a dishonest declaration, individuals face up to 2 years' imprisonment and a BMD 100,000 personal fine. The Bermuda Court may issue a travel ban and asset freeze against directors residing in or visiting Bermuda; foreign resident directors are not immune because the offending declaration is made in Bermuda, and BITA will refer to the UK Economic Crime Unit for extradition of UK-domiciled directors.
False Annual Return and Misrepresentation to the Registrar

Statutory sanction: Penalty for false declaration under the Bermuda Criminal Code 1907 (Section 92): fine of up to BMD 50,000; imposition of a default penalty of BMD 100 per day until the corrected return is filed; cancellation of the exempted status with retroactive effect, exposing the company to a 5% payroll tax assessment on the entire remuneration of the foreign trading desk if any Bermuda payroll existed.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil liability for all debts of the company incurred while the company was in default; forced reinstatement fees up to BMD 2,500; disqualification from acting as a Bermuda director for up to 5 years; a deliberate misrepresentation triggers personal criminal prosecution with up to 5 years' imprisonment for perjury to the Registrar, plus travel bans applicable to executives flying through Bermuda or the UK.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Beneficial Ownership Infringement

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Amendment) Act and the Beneficial Ownership (Amendment) Act 2022: administrative penalty of BMD 50,000 for non-registration of a register; criminal penalty on the company of BMD 250,000 and confiscation of the cargo proceeds; non-licensed banking/fund activity brings fines up to BMD 1,000,000 under the Investment Business Act 2003.

Director personal liability: Bermuda broadened personal criminal liability for AML breaches: directors, compliance officers, and MLROs can be fined up to BMD 250,000 and imprisoned up to 5 years for knowingly permitting a prohibited transaction. If the grain deals are linked to sanctioned Russian agricultural or Turkish transshipment, US OFAC secondary sanctions and UK criminal confiscation orders may extend to UK-resident directors, with a Director Disqualification Office order preventing the individual from managing any UK company for 15 years.
Trading While Insolvent and Piercing the Corporate Veil

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda Court may impose 'wrongful trading' liability, ordering contribution to the company's assets equal to the deficiency; penalties can exceed USD 1 million for a deliberately engineered insolvency; the company may be wound up with assets clawed back into the estate.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed personal guarantees for letters of credit are directly executable; absent guarantees, a court can pierce the corporate veil if the Exempted Co was a mere alias of its UK parent, leaving the UK parent's directors personally liable for restitution to creditors; fraudulent trading carries a BMD 500,000 fine and 7 years' imprisonment; the court can freeze personal bank accounts in Bermuda and the UK and issue an international travel ban under the Crime (Internation Co-operation) Act.
