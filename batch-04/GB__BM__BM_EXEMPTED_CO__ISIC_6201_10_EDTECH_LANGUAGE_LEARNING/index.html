E-government filing portal

Step 1: Appoint a licensed Bermuda registered agent (law firm or corporate service provider) to file the Memorandum of Association, Articles of Association, registered office confirmation, and incorporation application with the Registrar of Companies. Step 2: Obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, which assigns the statutory Bermuda Company/Registration Number (this number serves as the entity's sole tax-relevant identifier; no TIN or VAT ID is issued because no VAT/income-tax regime exists). Step 4: File the annual economic substance declaration (International Tax Compliance - Economic Substance Act) and annual company return with the Registrar. Step 5: If the company later hires Bermuda-resident staff or imports physical goods, register separately with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax and Customs Duty accounts.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists because Bermuda has no corporate income tax. Material statutory elections and protections: (a) EUTPA Tax Assurance Certificate election, extending exemption from future Bermuda income, profit, capital gains, and distribution taxes to 2050; (b) election to be designated as a non-resident entity for purposes of the Exchange Control Act 1972, granting capital-free status and removing exchange-control restrictions on foreign-currency receipts and dividend repatriation; (c) for UK tax purposes, the exempted company has no UK tax residence and is not subject to CTA 2009 hybrid-mismatch anti-abuse rules unless it is a party to a hybrid instrument or entity arrangement; (d) mandatory, non-elective compliance with the Bermuda Economic Substance Act (ESR filing), as it conducts mobile-business activity; (e) no VAT grouping or fiscal unity elections available due to the absence of any indirect tax regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general Sales and Use Tax regime in Bermuda. Bermuda levies only targeted indirect duties: Customs Duties on physical imports, Payroll Tax chargeable on employers in respect of Bermuda-resident employees, and Stamp Duty on specified Bermuda instruments (e.g., share transfers and real estate). Digital cross-border subscription services are outside all of these levies.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda maintains no VAT/GST/sales-tax system and therefore no economic turnover threshold triggering indirect-tax registration. EdTech subscription revenue from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing has no Bermuda indirect-tax nexus; the customer-facing VAT/GST obligations are discharged by the app store operators or by the customer's local reverse-charge regime in the destination jurisdiction.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because Bermuda has no VAT/GST, there is no 0% export-rating mechanism, no output tax to offset, and no VAT-refund reconciliation claim. All supplies of interactive language-education software and automated tutoring services made by the exempted company to overseas customers are simply outside the scope of any Bermuda indirect tax. Where the customer is a UK or EU business, the place-of-supply rules assign liability to the customer under reverse charge; where the customer is a consumer, Apple or Google collects the applicable local VAT/GST digitally at checkout and the Bermuda entity receives net-of-tax proceeds. The entity's compliance substance therefore rests on economic-substance filings, annual company returns, and transfer-pricing documentation, not on export tax documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar under the Currency Act 1972. Full convertibility is available for both current and capital account transactions; no exchange control approval or central bank clearance is required for dividend remittances, profit repatriation, or intra-group capital flows. Bermuda imposes no withholding taxes on outbound dividends, interest, royalties, or management fees. The sole practical hurdles are compliance with BMA anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CFT) regulations, international sanctions screening, and proof of economic substance where the company presents as a non-resident owned exempted entity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory obligation exists to surrender, convert, or repatriate export proceeds in Bermuda. An exempted company may retain receipts in any freely convertible currency, including BMD, USD, EUR, or GBP, without mandatory conversion timelines or ratios. The BMA does, however, expect accurate record-keeping of all cross-border receipts for anti-money-laundering audit trails.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Bermuda does not have a dedicated e-money institution (EMI) licence category; issuing e-money or providing payment services within or into Bermuda constitutes a regulated activity under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and the relevant money service businesses legislation. An offshore EMI that has obtained a licence in another jurisdiction (e.g., an FCA-authorised UK EMI) cannot operate in Bermuda solely under the foreign licence. It would trigger a local licensing requirement if it maintains a physical presence or actively solicits Bermudian clients. For a Bermuda exempted company that only uses an offshore EMI for global SaaS collections without serving Bermudian domestic customers, such a structure is permissible on a cross-border passporting-equivalent basis only if the EMI does not create a local branch or bank relationship. Principal legal constraints: lack of statutory equivalence for foreign EMI licences, BMA expectation that regulated treasury operations flow through licensed local banks, and the absence of a domestic deposit insurance or payment system membership for unwrapped EMIs.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions are routed via SWIFT MT103 wire transfers through HSBC, Butterfield, or another Bermudian bank's correspondent network. The remitting shareholder must clearly state the purpose code in the SWIFT instruction. Recommended ISO 20022 purpose codes for equity funding into a Bermuda exempted company: 'EQUI' (equity capital contribution) or 'FDI' (foreign direct investment) depending on the remitter's classification. For intra-company loans, 'LOAN' or 'DCRD' should be used. The beneficiary field must include the company legal name, the operational account reference, and the full address of the Bermuda registered office. The BMA and the receiving bank will run the wire through OFAC, EU, UK, and UN sanctions screening. The sender should upload a board resolution, a copy of the share subscription agreement, and a certificate of incorporation when the wire exceeds the bank's internal reporting threshold (typically USD 10,000). No Bermudian exchange control verification is required, but the bank may request a 'source of funds' declaration in line with its AML/CFT policies.Outbound trade settlements from the Bermuda exempted company will primarily use the USD and BMD clearing infrastructure. Direct disbursements are executed through SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer) with field 57 identifying the beneficiary bank and field 70 providing trade or invoice references. For larger or documentary trade transactions, Bermudian banks issue or at the request of the company transfer commercial letters of credit (LC) via SWIFT MT700; corresponding banks confirm the LC and pay against presentation of shipping and inspection documents. The Bermuda bank's USD clearing is performed through its correspondent relationship with major New York clearing banks (e.g., JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, HSBC Bank USA); the transactions are ultimately settled via CHIPS and Fedwire. Payment timing for MT103 transfers is normally value plus one business day; LC transactions follow industry-standard UCP 600 practices. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on the 20th day of the month following the end of each calendar quarter (April 20, July 20, October 20, January 20). A nil return is required for a non-resident employer with no Bermuda payroll.

Deadline: · Frequency:

For a fiscal year ending 31 December, the first CIT-1 is due 15 months after year-end (31 March 2027 for FY 2025); subsequent returns are due within 12 months after year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 (no general de minimis threshold). For CIT-covered MNEs, contemporaneous Local File and Master File align with OECD Pillar Two documentation standards when group revenue ≥ EUR 750 million; BRA may nevertheless request transfer-pricing documentation in any audit where related-party transactions are material.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2018 imposes a substance requirement on exempted companies undertaking 'relevant activities', which include distribution and service, financing, headquarters, and IP holding. A Bermuda Exempted Company operating an interactive language-learning subscription platform must demonstrate, in the absence of a pure IP-asset holding model, that its core business functions are supported in Bermuda: substantive board meetings, senior executives, physical office, full-time employees, and adequate operating expenditure. Where the transaction is structured to derive income from the platform’s intangible software, the IP-holding substance test applies. Failure to satisfy the economic substance test attracts financial penalties and, in the worst case, strike-off or winding-up.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Certificate

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda’s Tax Administration and the CIT Act 2023, penalties up to 150% of the evaded tax, criminal fines up to BMD 100,000, and imprisonment up to 10 years; the company may be struck off for failure to satisfy economic substance.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permitted the company to trade on behalf of a foreign business can be personally assessed for unpaid foreign taxes under piercing-the-veil principles; travel bans to the UK and EU may be imposed during extradition or asset-recovery proceedings.
AML/CFT Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to BMD 1,000,000 per contravention under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations 2023; the Registrar may refuse renewal of the exempted company’s permit.

Director personal liability: The MLRO and each director may be held personally criminally liable and imprisoned up to 10 years; travel bans and international asset freezes are commonly issued in ongoing investigations.
Artificial Profit Shifting / Transfer Pricing Mismatch

Statutory sanction: Under the corporate income tax rules for covered MNEs, the BRA will adjust taxable profits and impose a 5% penalty on the understatement (25% in fraudulent cases); the receiving jurisdiction may assessthrough CFC rules and levy withholding taxes on deemed royalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who executed non-arm’s length agreements may be personally liable for fraudulent trading under the Companies Act 1981; they can be disqualified from acting as directors and sued for restitution by creditors or tax authorities.
Registry Compliance Default

Statutory sanction: Default fine of BMD 50 per day, up to BMD 5,000; the Registrar may strike the company off the register and forfeit its assets to Bermuda’s Government.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid annual fee; persistent non-filing can lead to a court order restricting the directors from managing any company in Bermuda for five years.
Foreign VAT/GST Registration Neglect

Statutory sanction: The foreign tax administration may assess unpaid VAT/GST (e.g., 20% UK VAT, 19% German VAT), plus penalties of up to 100% of the tax and interest; failure to correct exposes the subscription revenue to economic double taxation.

Director personal liability: Directors can be pursued in foreign courts for personal liability if the company is treated as having fixed establishment in the EU; travel bans to the Schengen Area may be imposed following EU judgment enforcement.
