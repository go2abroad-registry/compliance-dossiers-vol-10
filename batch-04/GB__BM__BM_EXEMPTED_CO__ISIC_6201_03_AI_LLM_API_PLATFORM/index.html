Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Registrar of Companies (ROC), Bermuda Corporate Registry, Ministry of Legal Affairs (entity formation and corporate register); Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance (payroll tax and employer registration); Bermuda Customs Department (import duties and indirect tax administration). No income tax authority exists because Bermuda does not impose corporate or individual income tax.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishes a Bermuda Exempted Company through an authorised Bermuda service provider. The ROC issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a unique company number via the Bermuda Corporate Registry e-Registry. There is no separate TIN or VAT ID. A Payroll Tax Account Number must be obtained from the OTC only if the exempted company has Bermuda-based employees or pays Bermuda-source payroll/remuneration. For a pure AI-LLM API platform with no Bermuda staff and no physical goods import, no further tax registration is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone; exempted companies are not subject to Bermuda corporate income tax on worldwide income. The entity may obtain a statutory tax exemption certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 protecting it from future Bermuda taxes introduced before 31 March 2035. Economic substance obligations under the Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to relevant business activities, including certain IP activities, but do not create income tax liability.

Available election forms

No Bermuda check-the-box election exists. An exempted company may apply for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Certificate. No hybrid mismatch or jurisdiction-specific tax classification elections are available under Bermuda domestic law. US federal income tax check-the-box classification is determined separately under United States tax law when relevant and is not managed through Bermuda filing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales and use tax. Bermuda levies customs duty on the importation of physical goods and stamp duty on a limited set of legal instruments. Digital services, API access, royalties, and software-as-a-service are outside the customs duty net.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold. No indirect tax registration is required for AI/LLM API platform revenues. Customs duty registration may be required only if the entity directly imports physical goods into Bermuda, which is outside the scope of this digital export model.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda does not impose VAT, GST, or any export tax on services. Income from cross-border AI/LLM API services is not subject to Bermuda taxation, so no zero-rating claim exists. No export VAT refund regime is available or required.

  • No export documentation bundle is required for intangible AI/LLM API services; such services are outside Bermuda customs control. If the platform later exports physical equipment, standard customs export declaration and shipping records would apply.

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no e-invoicing API, no digital stamping system, and no government pre-clearance of invoices for VAT purposes. Bermuda does not operate an electronic tax clearance obligation for B2B or B2C digital invoices.

There is no VAT or export zero-rating to claw back. The main relevant fiscal risk is misuse of the tax exemption certificate or failure to maintain economic substance; breaches may result in administrative penalties and fines under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018 and the Companies Act 1981. If the exempted company makes customs declarations for physical goods, inaccurate declarations can attract seizure and penalties under the Customs Act. No income tax clawback exists.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD). Bermuda has no foreign exchange control legislation, and both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible. There are no statutory restrictions on the repatriation of profits, dividends, or liquidation proceeds, provided that the underlying transactions comply with Bermuda company law, anti-money laundering (AML) regulations, and any applicable sanctions screening obligations. BMD and USD are treated as interchangeable in the domestic banking system, and USD currency is legal tender in Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda maintains no export proceeds surrender regime. Exporters are not required to convert foreign currency receipts to BMD, nor are they subject to any prescribed conversion timeline or mandatory conversion ratio. Export proceeds may be retained in original foreign currency, held in USD-denominated accounts, or converted freely at market rates through licensed Bermuda banks.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Bermuda does not operate a distinct e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime. Offshore digital EMIs generally cannot obtain full local clearing membership or direct settlement access in Bermuda. Feasibility rating is Medium for ancillary payment processing via UK- or EU-regulated EMIs, provided such EMIs accept Bermuda Exempted Companies as customers. Legal constraints include the absence of a Bermuda EMI license, reliance on SWIFT and correspondent banking, and heightened AML due diligence for non-resident offshore entities. Primary treasury operations through licensed Bermuda banks are strongly preferred.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be routed via SWIFT MT103 wire transfers denominated in USD or BMD to a licensed Bermuda bank account held by the Bermuda Exempted Company. No BMA foreign exchange approval is required. Payment purpose codes should clearly indicate 'Equity Capital Contribution' or 'Share Subscription Funds.' The receiving bank will apply standard AML checks and request an accompanying board resolution, share certificate, and a completed tax self-declaration under the CRS/FATCA framework. For AI API platform operations, the foreign parent or investors may wire to a Butterfield or HSBC operational account using the company name and Bermuda entity registration number as the beneficiary identifier.Outbound trade disbursements and operating payments are executed through SWIFT MT103 messages, USD-cleared based on CHIPS/Fedwire correspondent accounts of Bermuda banks, or through commercial letters of credit (L/C) issued by licensed Bermuda or UK correspondent banks. Given the 1:1 BMD/USD peg, USD is the natural clearing currency for global payments. The platform should maintain dedicated disbursement sub-ledgers for API vendor costs, cloud infrastructure payments, licensing fees, and intercompany settlements, all routed through SWIFT with full beneficiary compliance and sanctions screening.USD/BMD is a fixed 1:1 peg, eliminating the need for BMD/USD forward hedging; BMD is effectively a USD proxy and can be settled at par.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/EUR or USD/GBP may be executed through international banks to hedge AI API platform revenue or vendor costs denominated in European and UK currencies.Cross-currency swaps with USD as the base currency may be used to swap EUR/GBP floating rate funding into fixed-rate USD obligations, providing effective economic conversion at the BMD/USD peg.Plain vanilla foreign exchange forwards on major convertible currencies are permissible through Bermuda-licensed banks or their offshore counterparts for trade payables and receivables.Interest rate swaps and basis swaps in USD are permissible for managing treasury liquidity under a Bermuda Exempted Company without engaging in speculative trading.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for non-resident owned Bermuda Exempted Companies under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) applies only to Bermuda constituent entities of multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more (Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Bermuda does not impose dividend withholding tax on distributions from an Exempted Company to non-resident shareholders).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

CIT return due on the 15th day of the month immediately following the end of the 15-month period after the accounting period end (i.e., 15 months and 15 days after fiscal year-end). Form EC due within 60 days after the anniversary of incorporation.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1: Company Identification Bermuda company registration number, corporate legal name, registered office address, and tax identification number (TIN) assigned by the Tax Commissioner. Non-resident shareholders must provide their country of tax residence and, if applicable, a permanent establishment declaration in Bermuda. Late filing of CIT-1 attracts a fixed penalty of BMD 500 per month per return, with an additional 5% of the outstanding tax liability per month of default, capped at 50% of the total tax due. For Form EC late filing, a penalty of BMD 250 plus BMD 25 per day applies. Misdeclaration penalties for CIT can reach 100% of the additional tax assessed. Directors are severally liable for unpaid tax penalties if the company is wound up without full settlement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For standalone Bermuda Exempted Companies, no local statutory TP documentation threshold applies. For CIT-scoped groups, Local File and Master File are mandatory when the aggregate value of covered controlled transactions exceeds BMD 1,000,000 in a filing year. Country-by-Country reporting is required for groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750M or more, in line with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2018 apply to all exempted companies with operations in scope of international business activity. As the primary activity of an AI/LLM API platform (ISIC 6201) constitutes 'Intellectual Property Business', the exempted company must demonstrate (i) active management and control exercised at a board level in Bermuda; (ii) adequate physical premises, qualified full-time employees, and operating expenditures in Bermuda; (iii) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda; and (iv) annual registration and economic substance filings with the Registrar. Failure to satisfy the substance test results in fines, an order to unwind, or a requirement to change the beneficial ownership structure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (False Tax Declaration)

Statutory sanction: On conviction in the Supreme Court of Bermuda, a fine of up to BMD 250,000 (or three times the amount of tax evaded, whichever is greater) and an additional civil penalty of 200% of the unpaid tax. Offending corporate documents may be struck off from the Register of Companies.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly participating in tax evasion face personal fines of BMD 50,000 and imprisonment for up to 10 years; disqualification from acting as a director for 10 years; and civil liability for the company's unpaid tax, interest, and penalties up to the amount of the evasion.
Shell Company (Failure of Economic Substance)

Statutory sanction: The Registrar of Companies must impose a penalty of BMD 25,000 for the first offence, BMD 50,000 for subsequent offences, and may order compulsory striking-off (dissolution) of the company. The company loses its protected tax-exempt status for future years.

Director personal liability: Each director is jointly and severally liable for the BMD penalties. Additionally, the Supreme Court may issue a travel ban prohibiting the director from leaving Bermuda until all penalties and unpaid taxes are settled. Criminal prosecution for false statutory declarations carries a maximum sentence of 5 years' imprisonment.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT Violation)

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018, a corporate fine of up to BMD 100,000 per breach, as well as a regulatory penalty of BMD 1,000 per day for failure to sustain statutory records. The Bermuda Monetary Authority can revoke the company's digital asset business licence if applicable.

Director personal liability: Directors found grossly negligent in AML compliance may be personally fined BMD 25,000 per infraction and sentenced to up to 5 years' imprisonment. They are also personally liable to victims for losses traced to the missing controls, and are disqualified from serving as directors of any Bermuda financial institution.
Late Filing or Non-Filing of Final Income Tax Return

Statutory sanction: An automatic fixed fine of BMD 500 plus BMD 25 per day for each return (Form EC). For CIT-scoped companies, a non-filing penalty of 5% of the CIT due per month, subject to a maximum of 50% of the total tax liability; in addition, the Tax Commissioner may estimate and assess a default CIT amount up to 150% of the apparent tax base.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for penalties if the company was used to facilitate non-compliance, and they may be ordered to pay an amount representing the entire unpaid CIT and penalties. A director who knowingly signs a false declaration commits an offence punishable by BMD 100,000 fine and 7 years' imprisonment.
