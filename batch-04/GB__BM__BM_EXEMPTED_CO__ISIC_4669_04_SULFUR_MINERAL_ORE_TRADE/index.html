Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; official government tax portal: https://www.gov.bm/taxes. Company registration is administered by the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a domestic corporate income tax identification number or VAT/GST registration number to exempted companies. A Bermuda Exempted Company obtains a certificate of incorporation bearing a registered company number from the Registrar of Companies. If the company has Bermudian payroll, it must register for Bermuda Payroll Tax and obtain an employer number from the Office of the Tax Commissioner. Foreign entities carrying on business through a Bermuda Exempted Company do not obtain a VAT ID because no VAT/GST regime exists. The statutory tax protection is secured through an exemption certificate issued pursuant to the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist under Bermudian law. The tax-exempt status of a Bermuda Exempted Company is automatic by statute, not elective. A company may elect permit-company status under the Companies Act 1981, but that election affects local ownership and licensed activity, not tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None. Bermuda does not levy a national value-added tax or goods and services tax; the main indirect taxes are customs import duties under the Customs Tariff Act and duty on imported goods administered by Bermuda Customs.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. Because Bermuda has no VAT/GST regime, there is no economic turnover threshold requiring indirect tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. There is no '0% export VAT' claim to make and no VAT refund mechanism to trigger.

Bermuda has no country-by-country electronic invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance system, API integration requirement, or digital stamping regime for domestic or export transactions. Customs declarations are handled through Bermuda Customs and may be filed electronically, but no continuous transaction control (CTC) clearance is imposed.

There is no VAT clawback risk in Bermuda because no export zero-rating or refund regime exists. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is issued by the BMA and trades under a fixed parity with the U.S. Dollar at BMD 1 = USD 1. The exchange control regime under the Exchange Control Act 1972 applies distinctly to residents and non-residents. A Bermuda Exempted Company is non-resident for exchange control purposes and therefore enjoys unrestricted convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. Dividends, profit distributions, and capital redemptions may be remitted without BMA approval provided the company remains non-resident and no transaction involves a resident person or a specifically controlled import/export permit. There are no procedural profit remittance hurdles under Bermuda law; the only practical barrier is the bank's own AML/KYC verification of source of funds and ultimate beneficial ownership.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a non-resident operated Bermudian Exempted Company, statutory export surrender rules are not applicable. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and BMA Exchange Control Directions, a company that is resident for exchange control purposes must surrender foreign currency export proceeds to an authorized dealer within 180 days of receipt unless an exemption or retention allowance is granted. Because the subject Exempted Company is non-resident, sulfur ore trade proceeds may be retained, held, and disbursed freely in the international clearing system, subject only to the bank's internal documentation requirements for the underlying trade.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Bermuda does not have a distinct payment e-money institution (EMI) licensing class under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018, which covers digital asset services rather than fiat payment services. UK and EU EMIs cannot contractually onboard a Bermudian Exempted Company as a regulated client because the company is not resident in the EEA or the UK. A practical alternative is a UK-licensed EMI with a branch-relationship model, but most such EMIs will reject non-resident offshore corporate clients. The compliance-favourable route remains a Bermudian licensed bank or a UK bank with a full branch in Bermuda to manage treasury and clearing operations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be wired through SWIFT using the appropriate equity contribution purpose code. Because Bermuda maintains a fixed BMD/USD peg, the bank will clear USD in a Nostro account without any FX conversion. The remitter should use a purpose code reflecting 'Equity Capital Contribution' and include a payment narrative referencing the board resolution and capital subscription number. The company should maintain a detailed capital allocation memorandum, a BMA exchange-control notification where borderline, and a copy of the exempted company certificate to evidence its non-resident status. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No stand-alone Bermuda transfer pricing documentation obligation applies to out-of-scope exempted companies. For in-scope MNE groups, OECD-aligned Master File and Local File obligations attach where the group consolidated revenue is at least EUR 750 million (BMD equivalent); the Bermuda Local File must be prepared when the Bermuda entity's related-party turnover exceeds BMD 8,000,000 or the OTC otherwise directs, with content aligned to the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and Chapter V documentation standards.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Requirements (ESR, effective 2019) apply mandatorily because wholesale trading and brokerage of mineral ores constitutes 'Distribution and Service Business', a designated relevant activity. The exempted company must prove in its annual ESR return: (i) management and control exercised in Bermuda; (ii) adequate full-time employees with suitable qualifications in Bermuda; (iii) adequate physical premises in Bermuda; (iv) adequate annual operating expenditure incurred in Bermuda; and (v) core income-generating activities physically performed in Bermuda. A pure letterbox or shell structure with all operations conducted by a UK parent will fail ESR, exposing the company to penalty assessments, automatic information exchange with HMRC, and potential striking-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Initial financial penalty of BMD 5,000 and continuing penalty of BMD 5,000 per month of default; mandatory information exchange with UK HMRC under the TIEA; deregistration and winding-up of the company under the Companies Act 1981; loss of the statutory 2050 tax protection

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly made a false ESR declaration face fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years; disqualification from acting as a Bermuda director for up to 5 years; civil liability to creditors for trading without lawful substance
UK CFC and Tax Evasion via Profit Diversion

Statutory sanction: UK CFC charge under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A: apportionment of the Bermuda company's non-excluded profits to the UK parent; HMRC assessments with interest and tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the tax evaded; issuance of follower notices and accelerated payment notices

Director personal liability: Directors exposed to the corporate criminal offence of failure to prevent the facilitation of tax evasion (Criminal Finances Act 2017): unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 10 years; personal asset freezing orders and UK travel bans under HMRC enforcement powers
Sanctions and Commodity Trade Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 as extended to Bermuda by Order in Council: unlimited criminal fines, asset freezing of the proceeds in the correspondent banking chain, mandatory rejection of the LC by the confirming bank, and referral to the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation for civil monetary penalties

Director personal liability: Directors with knowledge or reasonable suspicion face imprisonment up to 10 years for UK sanctions breaches; personal civil liability to the bank for fraudulent misrepresentation; inclusion on the consolidated travel ban list preventing entry to the UK, EU, and Bermuda
AML and Proceeds of Crime Violations

Statutory sanction: Offences under the Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Anti-Money Laundering Regulations 2008: money laundering carries up to 14 years' imprisonment and an unlimited fine; the Bermuda Financial Intelligence Agency may impose civil penalties and require account closure; correspondent banks must file suspicious activity reports and terminate the banking relationship

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly ignore internal AML controls are personally liable to fines up to BMD 1,000,000 in aggravated cases, imprisonment up to 14 years, and permanent debarment from holding directorship positions in any Bermuda-regulated entity
Beneficial Ownership Register Default

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Beneficial Ownership (Companies) Regulations 2018: BMD 10,000 for the company and BMD 4,000 per officer per day of continuing default; the register becomes 'sanctioned', blocking the company from all corporate acts including dividend distributions and the filing of valid annual returns

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly files a false beneficial ownership return is liable to imprisonment up to 2 years and disqualification; adverse marking of the company triggers enhanced due diligence for all correspondent banks and may expose the director to personal money-laundering charges
Dormant Shell Misrepresentation to Banking and LC Counterparties

Statutory sanction: Liability for fraudulent misrepresentation under Bermuda and English law: immediate termination of all banking and LC facilities, freezing of account funds pending enhanced review, mandatory filing of suspicious activity reports, and regulatory enforcement by the Bermuda Monetary Authority against the company and any licensed service provider involved

Director personal liability: Directors face personal civil restitution to the bank for losses caused by misrepresentation, a 10-year ban on acting as directors of any Bermuda company, and criminal liability for obtaining financial services by deception with imprisonment up to 5 years
