Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is not registered for corporate income tax or VAT because Bermuda levies no income tax and has no VAT/GST. The company must obtain a Bermuda Registered Number (BRN) from the Registrar of Companies upon incorporation and, if engaged in local payroll, register as an employer for Payroll Tax. If importing goods into Bermuda, the company or its licensed customs broker registers with Bermuda Customs for import duty purposes. No general business TIN is required for zero-rate or exempt income tax status.

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections are available due to the absence of direct taxation. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda imposes import customs duty, payroll tax, and stamp duty on certain local instruments. No indirect tax applies to offshore wholesale supply or export of goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Bermuda has no VAT/GST and no corresponding registration threshold. Customs duty registration applies only to importers of goods into Bermuda.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda does not operate a VAT system and therefore does not grant 0% VAT export zero-rating or VAT refunds. Offshore sales of petrochemical polymers from Bermuda are outside any Bermuda indirect tax net. For goods transiting or re-exported through Bermuda, customs duty may be suspended or exempted under Temporary Importation provisions, but no input VAT credit mechanism exists.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or statutory e-invoicing mandate applies. Bermuda Customs uses the ASYCUDA World electronic customs clearance platform for import and export declarations.

No VAT zero-rating fraud or clawback regime exists because there is no VAT/GST. However, false export declarations to avoid Bermuda customs duty on re-exported goods may attract customs penalties, forfeiture of goods, or prosecution under the Customs (Export/Import) Acts. Economic substance filings are subject to Bermuda Economic Substance Act penalties for false or incomplete declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to the United States Dollar. Exchange control distinguishes between resident and non-resident persons. A Bermuda Exempted Company designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act enjoys full current-account and capital-account convertibility. Capital injections, share subscriptions, repayment of shareholder loans, dividends, and profit remittances are permitted without prior BMA exchange-control approval. The principal practical hurdle is bank-level source-of-funds and beneficial-ownership verification, not statutory currency convertibility. No Bermudian withholding tax is imposed on outbound dividends, interest, royalties, or management fees, reducing remittance friction. For payments involving Bermuda residents, the underlying transaction may require exchange-control permission, but the non-resident exempted company is not subject to those restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no mandatory export-proceeds surrender rules for a Bermuda Exempted Company. The statutory timeline is inapplicable, and the mandatory conversion ratio is zero percent. Export sales of virgin polyethylene and polypropylene granules may be fully retained in the original invoiced currency, normally USD, EUR, GBP, or offshore CNY. The company is not required to convert export proceeds into BMD. Licensed banks must still report material foreign-currency flows under general BMA reporting, anti-money-laundering obligations, and sanctions screening, but no domestic FX surrender or export-proceeds mobilization obligation applies.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the autonomous monetary authority and financial services regulator of Bermuda, acting as the designated authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973. The Bank of England has no direct exchange-control or reserve-management jurisdiction over Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low as a primary treasury rail; Medium only for ancillary GBP/EUR collections. Bermuda does not operate a standalone electronic-money-institution or payment-service-provider licensing regime comparable to the UK FCA e-money framework. An offshore digital EMI from the UK, EU, or APAC is generally not an appropriate clearing or custody layer for the core USD trade flows, letter-of-credit issuance, or supplier disbursements of a bulk petrochemicals trading company. A digital asset business in Bermuda would require a licence under the Digital Asset Business Act. Consequently, primary corporate banking must be held at a BMA-licensed bank; an EMI may be used only as a secondary operational collection account for small-basket payments and not for LC-backed petrochemical settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inward capital injections from the UK parent or shareholder must be routed by SWIFT MT103 in USD, or in BMD at par, to the Bermuda Exempted Company's account at a BMA-licensed bank. The beneficiary bank will require the exact legal name of the exempted company, the account number, and the bank's SWIFT BIC. Use a clear remittance reference in Field 70: 'Capital subscription - [shareholder name] - [Bermuda company name] - [share call number]'. Use ISO 20022 purpose code 'FIVD' or 'INVP' where accepted by the corresponding bank, or alternatively 'Capital Account Funding' in free-format text. For amounts above USD 500,000, provide same-day SWIFT MT199 pre-advice to the receiving bank and submit the executed board resolution, capital call notice, and source-of-funds statement. Since the exempted company is non-resident and USD is the deposit currency, no BMA FX permit is required and no mandatory conversion is triggered.Trade settlement for bulk polyethylene and polypropylene purchases is executed via the following clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for open-account and after-shipment payments, referencing the invoice number and purchase contract and using commodity purpose codes such as 'CMDT' or 'TRAD'; (2) SWIFT MT202 COV for correspondent-bank reimbursements connected to trade settlements, where the instructed beneficiary is a supplier bank rather than the supplier itself; (3) irrevocable documentary Letters of Credit under UCP600 issued in USD for high-value or first-time suppliers, supported by the full commercial invoice, clean-on-board bills of lading, packing list, certificate of quality, and insurance documents; (4) bank-to-bank reimbursement and LC negotiation via SWIFT MT400 and MT750 messages where the LC is confirmed by a top-tier international bank. Bermuda is outside SEPA and TARGET2, so EUR and GBP intercompany or supplier payments require a corresponding clearing bank. The primary USD leg should be settled through the New York correspondent bank over Fedwire or CHIPS.Vanilla over-the-counter deliverable FX forward contracts in USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD executed exclusively against documented commodity trade exposures.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for non-fully-convertible or restricted settlement currencies, including offshore CNY (CNH), INR, or other corridor currencies, with standard dual-day fixing fallback and no speculative intent.Cross-currency swaps with principal exchange and periodic interest settlements under an ISDA 2002 master agreement and CSA, limited to committed trade receivable and payable flows.European-style long FX options, purchased only, providing downside protection on forecast polymer sales receipts or procurement costs with a maximum tenor of 12 months.FX overlay swaps executed through the BMA-licensed bank or a highly rated international counterparty, provided the counterparty, the underlying trade exposure, and the hedging instruments are outside OFAC/HMT sanctions restrictions.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% on worldwide income under the Bermuda exempted company regime; 15% top-up corporate income tax applies to in-scope constituent entities of MNE groups under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 (CITA) for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda exempted company to any non-resident shareholder; no Bermuda dividend tax is levied under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

ESR Form ES-1 must be filed within six months after the relevant fiscal year-end (e.g., by 30 June for a 31 December year-end); the Annual Compliance Declaration and government fee are due on or before 31 January of each year; CIT Return Form CIT-1 is due within 15 months after the fiscal year-end for fiscal years commencing on or after 1 January 2025.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 (equivalent to the EUR 750 million consolidated revenue threshold of an MNE group triggering the GloBE/CIT regime); Bermuda imposes no domestic transfer pricing documentation threshold, but in-scope groups must maintain OECD Pillar Two records and file the GloBE Information Return through the BTA, and the UK parent must retain group transfer pricing documentation under UK Finance Act 2010 schedules for UK filing purposes.

Economic substance scope: Yes: the Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply. Wholesale distribution of bulk petrochemical polymers qualifies as a 'Distribution and Service Centre' relevant activity and the exempted company must demonstrate in Bermuda: (i) direction and management by local senior executives, (ii) adequate qualified employees, (iii) physical premises, and (iv) minimum annual expenditure proportionate to the gross revenue from polymer trading; failure to satisfy the full-substance test or reliance on pure equity-holding status will trigger a qualified or negative economic substance assessment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Initial penalty of BMD 10,000; continuing default penalty of BMD 5,000 per day; aggregate penalties up to BMD 250,000; the Registrar may issue enforcement notices, impose a public statement, and strike the exempted company off the Bermuda register with consequential forfeiture of assets.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false economic substance declaration are liable for civil restitution of diverted profits, disqualification from acting as directors in Bermuda for up to 15 years, travel restrictions, and imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly providing false information.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and UK CFC Attribution

Statutory sanction: Under UK controlled foreign company rules and transfer pricing legislation, HMRC may reattribute the Bermuda profit to the UK parent, impose unlimited penalties for deliberate failure to take reasonable care, and apply interest on the understated UK corporation tax; the Bermuda entity simultaneously faces ESR penalties for non-substantive distribution activity.

Director personal liability: UK senior accounting officers face personal fines of up to GBP 5,000 for failing to establish and maintain adequate tax governance arrangements; directors may be disqualified for up to 15 years, subjected to civil restitution orders, and criminally prosecuted for tax evasion with imprisonment up to 7 years.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008, penalties include fines up to BMD 500,000, forfeiture of the proceeds of the underlying trades, and imprisonment up to 15 years for money laundering offences; the Registrar may also impose administrative fines and strike-off for beneficial ownership non-disclosure.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or assist in undisclosed LC-based settlements face criminal money laundering charges, confiscation proceedings, travel bans, and imprisonment; the UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 can be applied extraterritorially where the underlying petrochemical trades touch the UK financial system.
CIT Act 2024 Non-Compliance and Late Filing

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to BMD 250,000 for failure to file the CIT Return; interest accrues at a statutory rate of 10% per annum on unpaid top-up tax; the BTA may issue default assessments and the Registrar may strike the company off the register; information is exchangeable with HMRC under Bermuda's international tax transparency arrangements.

Director personal liability: Directors become jointly and severally liable for the unpaid 15% top-up tax where the non-filing is deliberate; the BTA may impose personal recovery orders, travel bans, and director disqualification; knowing participation in tax evasion carries imprisonment up to 7 years and unlimited fines.
