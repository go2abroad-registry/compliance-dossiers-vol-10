Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance; corporate registration is administered by the Bermuda Registrar of Companies. The official tax portal is the Bermuda Government eServices portal at https://www.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated by the Registrar of Companies and receives a Bermuda Business Number (registry identifier). There is no general statutory TIN issued at incorporation. For payroll tax purposes an employer account (payroll tax number) must be registered with the Office of the Tax Commissioner. For multinational groups meeting the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 criteria, a separate Corporate Income Tax registration and filing profile must be obtained through the Office of the Tax Commissioner eServices. Foreign entities neither need to pre-register as tax residents for ordinary exempted company status, nor obtain an in-country tax clearance prior to incorporation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Bermuda law. The Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 contains limited elections aligned with OECD Pillar Two, including the qualified domestic minimum top-up tax (QDMTT) election and the GloBE transition-period asset valuation election. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available to Bermuda exempted companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Duty on imports only; no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax is imposed in Bermuda.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda levies no VAT/GST and imposes no indirect-tax registration threshold. Instead, every importation of goods into Bermuda is subject to Bermuda Customs duty irrespective of the importer's turnover; a customs declaration is required for non-de-minimis shipments and certain excise-controlled goods require additional licensing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - no VAT system exists in Bermuda, so exports cannot be zero-rated for VAT purposes and no input VAT refund mechanism applies. Goods exported from Bermuda are outside the Bermuda customs duty chargeable on imports; the ultimate destination country is responsible for the applicable sales or use tax, VAT, or import duty on cross-border online retail supplies.

Not applicable - Bermuda has no real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime for commercial invoices. Standard electronic invoices issued by the e-commerce platform are sufficient for corporate record-keeping. Customs import clearance is effected electronically through the Bermuda Customs Department declaration system, while export consignments from Bermuda are generally duty-free and require electronic travel documentation rather than fiscal clearance.

No VAT clawback exposure exists because Bermuda does not operate an export refund system. Customs fraud exposure arises only on imports into Bermuda: deliberate misdescription, undervaluation, or omission of goods is penalised under the Bermuda Customs Act, including goods forfeiture, monetary penalties up to BMD 10,000, and potential criminal liability. For multinational groups within the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, artificial routing of e-commerce revenue through a Bermuda entity to avoid OECD Pillar Two top-up tax may trigger retrospective recharacterisation, interest, and penalties up to 175% of the undercharged tax.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged to the U.S. dollar at 1:1. Under the Exchange Control Act 1972, a Bermuda Exempted Company that is designated 'non-resident' may hold, convert, and repatriate foreign currency freely for current account and capital account purposes, including profit remittances, dividends, and repayment of shareholder loans. The principal practical hurdle for a resident-designated entity is prior BMA authority for transactions involving Bermudian resident holdings or resident currency. For the described non-resident Exempted Company, no exchange control approval is required for ordinary e-commerce revenue collection, supplier settlements, or dividend/distribution payments to non-resident shareholders. No Bermuda withholding taxes, capital gains taxes, or remittance levies apply to the non-resident exempted structure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a Bermuda Exempted Company classified as 'non-resident', there is no statutory surrender obligation and no mandated conversion ratio. All export receipts, including e-commerce sales proceeds from the sale of auto aftermarket parts and tuning accessories, may be retained in a Permitted Currency Account in USD, GBP, EUR, or any currency approved by the BMA. A resident-designated Bermuda entity would generally be required to offer all foreign currency export proceeds to a Bermuda authorized dealer for conversion into Bermudian dollars within a prescribed period, but such resident status should be avoided and is not consistent with the non-resident exempted structure. Because the online retail transaction involves no physical export from Bermuda, the practical surrender analysis is limited to routing platform settlement funds through the Bermuda operational account without BMD conversion.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), with the Bank of England retaining no direct exchange control jurisdiction over Bermuda; the BMA administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 and supervises all deposit-taking institutions in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-High, with important legal constraints. UK-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) and cross-border payment platforms, such as Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Stripe, and PayPal, may accept a Bermuda Exempted Company as a client provided the EMI is FCA-authorised under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and the final bank account is maintained at an EU/UK commercial bank. EMIs are not banks and cannot provide lending, letters of credit, or deposit-taking services. Many EMIs internal policy refuses legal entities incorporated in non-EEA 'offshore' jurisdictions, but the low-risk classification of online auto parts retail, combined with a documented source of funds and confirmed non-resident status, normally permits onboarding. The EMI must apply equivalent AML/KYC/CDD measures to BMA requirements and must be able to perform sanctions screening through SWIFT/correspondent banking restrictions. The use of such EMIs is viable as a secondary liquidity rail but should not replace a BMA-regulated domestic Bermudian bank account for capital escrow or core treasury settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The initial share capital injection should be routed via SWIFT MT103 from a UK bank account to the Bermuda Exempted Company's operational account at HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield. The paying bank must use a proper wholesale payment purpose classification corresponding to 'Direct Investment Equity Capital' or 'Capital Contribution to an Overseas Subsidiary', with narrative text 'Capital Injection for Ordinary Share Capital – No Goods or Services'. Beneficiary details must include the full legal name of the Exempted Company, the Bermuda account number, SWIFT/BIC of the receiving Bermudian bank, and no IBAN (Bermuda is not part of SEPA and does not use IBAN). Field 23E should be set to CRED where immediate credit to beneficiary is required, and field 71A should be OUR or SHA to manage correspondent charges. Since the company is designated non-resident, no BMA exchange control approval is required for the inward remittance; nevertheless, the receiving bank may request a copy of the board resolution authorising share issuance, a share register extract, and a source of funds declaration if the amount exceeds the bank's internal threshold. For EUR or GBP inward payments, a two-share settlement via SWIFT is used because Bermuda is outside SEPA and CHAPS. The Bermuda bank will typically convert the incoming currency through its internal FX desk at the interbank rate plus 10–20 pips.Outbound Treasury Clearing: Standard supplier payments for automotive parts restocking should be executed via SWIFT MT103, using the beneficiary supplier's bank and account details; payment narratives must state the commercial invoice number and purchase order reference. For suppliers requiring documented trade assurance, a Commercial Letter of Credit may be issued by a Bermudian bank using SWIFT MT700 with shipping documents such as commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and inspection certificate; settlement is achieved through MT103/MT202 upon presentation of conforming documents. Retail sales disbursements from Stripe and PayPal should be batched daily or weekly to the Bermudian operational account using the PSP's payout mechanism, and then swept to interest-bearing accounts or FX forwards as needed. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Medium-High, with important legal constraints. UK-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) and cross-border payment platforms, such as Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Stripe, and PayPal, may accept a Bermuda Exempted Company as a client provided the EMI is FCA-authorised under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and the final bank account is maintained at an EU/UK commercial bank. EMIs are not banks and cannot provide lending, letters of credit, or deposit-taking services. Many EMIs internal policy refuses legal entities incorporated in non-EEA 'offshore' jurisdictions, but the low-risk classification of online auto parts retail, combined with a documented source of funds and confirmed non-resident status, normally permits onboarding. The EMI must apply equivalent AML/KYC/CDD measures to BMA requirements and must be able to perform sanctions screening through SWIFT/correspondent banking restrictions. The use of such EMIs is viable as a secondary liquidity rail but should not replace a BMA-regulated domestic Bermudian bank account for capital escrow or core treasury settlement.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Bermuda does not impose corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or tax on dividends for exempted companies under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (EUTPA), with tax protection contractually guaranteed to 31 March 2035 and likely extension thereafter.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda law imposes no withholding tax, no dividend tax, and no equalisation levy on dividends paid by an exempted company to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 60 days after the anniversary of incorporation (example: 15 March incorporation requires filing and fee payment by 14 May). A company in liquidation must file a cessation declaration within 30 days of winding up.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 6 months after the end of the financial period. A company with a 31 December year-end must file by 30 June; the financial period must equal the accounting reference period used in the annual validation filing.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The tax year runs 1 January to 31 December; the annual PR-1 reconciliation is due on or before 31 January following the year end, and monthly instalments are due by the 15th day of the following month.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Bermuda transfer-pricing local-file or master-file threshold exists. Nevertheless, under the Economic Substance (Companies) Regulations 2018, an exempted company carrying on Distribution and Service Centre Business with related-party transactions must maintain contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation regardless of turnover. Benchmark for sound practice: prepare a Bermuda TP Local File where annual related-party transactions exceed BMD 500,000, and a group Master File where the consolidated group revenue exceeds EUR 750 million; documentation must be supplied within 30 days of written demand by the Registrar or HMRC under the exchange of information arrangement.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 and the Economic Substance (Companies) Regulations 2018 apply in full. The relevant activity is Distribution and Service Centre Business (ISIC 4791 online retail with Bermudian purchasing, inventory, pricing, and logistics functions). Conditions: (a) the company must be directed and managed in Bermuda, meaning board meetings are physically held in Bermuda at adequate frequency and the minutes evidence real decision-making; (b) core income-generating activities — purchasing, inventory management, pricing, logistics decisions, and assumption of risk — must be performed in Bermuda by employees or by a Bermuda-based outsourced service provider; (c) the company must hire an adequate number of full-time equivalent employees in Bermuda; (d) it must incur adequate operating expenditure in Bermuda; (e) it must file the annual ESR declaration. The Pure Equity Holding exemption applies only if the entity merely holds shares of subsidiaries and derives income exclusively from dividends and capital gains.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Offshore Tax Evasion and UK Diverted Profits

Statutory sanction: In the UK: Diverted Profits Tax at 25% on avoided profits, Schedule 36 penalties up to 100% of the tax, and under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 the corporate offence of failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion carries an unlimited fine; in Bermuda: ESR penalty up to BMD 250,000, strike-off, asset forfeiture, and mandatory exchange of bank and beneficial ownership data with HMRC under the 2008 UK-Bermuda Exchange of Information Agreement.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal criminal liability under the UK offshore evasion offence (imprisonment up to 7 years); in Bermuda, officers who sign false ESR or beneficial ownership filings risk up to 5 years imprisonment and BMD 50,000 fines; UK travel bans and worldwide freezing orders may be issued; directors who sign false solvency certificates are personally liable in restitution.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Registrar-imposed ESR financial penalty up to BMD 250,000 plus a daily default penalty of BMD 5,000; automatic strike-off and cancellation of the Certificate of Compliance; information exchange with HMRC leading to a 25% Diverted Profits Tax charge and interest; merchant account termination by Stripe or PayPal under the risk policy for shell e-commerce vehicles.

Director personal liability: Directors and senior managers are jointly and severally liable for the corporate penalties; the Bermuda court may disqualify directors from managing any Bermuda company for up to 5 years; illegal distribution recovery under Companies Act 1981 section 54 pierces the corporate veil and renders directors personally liable to restore the misapplied assets.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 (as amended), money laundering carries imprisonment up to 15 years and unlimited fines; the AML/ATF Rules 2015 impose regulator financial penalties and public reprimand; the Beneficial Ownership (Companies) Regulations 2017 impose fines up to BMD 50,000 and 2 years imprisonment for non-compliance; the company may be struck off and the payment-rail providers may permanently close the merchant account.

Director personal liability: Directors who consent, connive, or act negligently become criminally liable; confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 may seize personal assets; the Bermuda court may issue travel bans pending mutual legal assistance requests from UK authorities; the resident representative also bears custodian liability and may terminate the client relationship, triggering an immediate winding-up default.
Illegal Distributions and Insolvent Voidance

Statutory sanction: Companies Act 1981 section 54 renders the distribution void; officers are liable to repay the full amount; insolvent winding-up rules permit claw-back of unlawful dividends and fraudulent preference; annual government fee penalties remain personally collectable; the company may be struck off with assets vested in the Crown.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to restore unpaid capital and misapplied funds; fraudulent trading liability attaches to every person knowingly a party to the misapplication, with unlimited personal liability for company debts; courts may issue director disqualification orders and criminal sanctions on evidence of intent to defraud, plus extradition exposure under the UK-Bermuda mutual assistance framework.
