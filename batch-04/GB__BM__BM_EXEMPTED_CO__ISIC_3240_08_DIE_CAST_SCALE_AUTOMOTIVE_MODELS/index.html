Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) at a fixed 1:1 parity with the United States Dollar (USD). The Exchange Control Act 1972 and associated regulations restrict certain currency transactions, but Bermuda Exempted Companies designated as non-resident for exchange control purposes are permitted full current-account and capital-account convertibility, subject to BMA or authorised bank approvals where applicable. Profit remittance and dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are freely allowed for the BMA-designated non-resident Exempted Company, provided that corporate records, solvency tests, and Beneficial Ownership Act disclosures are in order. There are no exchange control hurdles for a compliant Exempted Company transferring USD or BMD proceeds offshore.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement applies to a Bermuda Exempted Company holding non-resident exchange control status. The company may retain export receipts in BMD or USD and use them for working capital, imports, or offshore remittances without mandatory conversion. Resident-status companies are subject to a general obligation to offer foreign currency proceeds to an authorised dealer within a prescribed period, but this does not apply to the non-resident-designated Exempted Company in the target structure. Board minutes and the exchange control designation certificate should be retained to evidence the exemption.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for Bermuda Exempted Company treasury core. The BMA does not issue an EU/UK-style electronic money institution (EMI) authorisation, and no EEA or UK EMI passporting exists into Bermuda. Licensed banks must conduct all local deposit-taking and payment services. Offshore digital EMIs may be used only as ancillary collection vehicles for GBP/EUR e-commerce receipts, subject to KYC acceptance and the digital-asset business licensing requirements under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 if tokenised value transfer is involved. EMIs cannot provide BMD/USD local clearing, trade finance, or escrow-grade banking services for this entity.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% domestic corporate income tax for a Bermuda Exempted Company that is not a constituent entity of a large multinational enterprise group; 15% for in-scope constituent entities of an MNE group with consolidated global revenue of EUR 750 million or more under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory dividend withholding tax in Bermuda. Dividends paid by a Bermuda Exempted Company to non-resident shareholders are exempt from Bermuda income tax and are not subject to withholding tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 9 months after the end of the fiscal year; the first CIT return generally covers a fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Within 6 months after the exempted company's fiscal year end for a standard-risk entity; within 3 months for a high-risk entity. Filing dates follow the fiscal year end stated on the annual company return.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Non-Filing

Statutory sanction: In Bermuda: default assessments, late filing penalties, false declaration penalties, and possible striking off. In foreign source jurisdictions: recharacterisation of the Bermuda entity as a tax shell, CFC income inclusion, GloBE top-up tax, retrogressive tax assessments, interest, and significant penalty percentages.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly permit false declarations or conceal the absence of substance can be held personally liable for restitution, disgorgement, and fines. Courts may impose travel bans pending investigation; criminal tax evasion convictions may lead to imprisonment.
Shell Company Abuse / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Substantial non-compliance penalties of BMD 75,000 for a first occurrence and BMD 150,000 for a second or subsequent occurrence, plus daily default penalties; the Registrar may strike the exempted company off the register and publish a notice of non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from acting as directors of Bermuda companies; personal liability may attach for penalties where the director signed a false economic substance return or failed to correct materially misleading information.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Bermuda does not levy a general transfer pricing penalty on out-of-scope companies, but foreign tax authorities may adjust profits under their transfer pricing rules, impose withholding tax on imputed royalties, assess interest, and apply penalties up to 50% of the tax deficiency. OECD multilateral instrument reporting may expose the structure to automatic exchange of information.

Director personal liability: Directors of the foreign affiliate and Bermuda company may be jointly pursued for participating in a sham pricing arrangement; serious cases attract civil restitution orders, director disqualification, and extradition triggers for cross-border tax fraud.
AML / Anti-Money Laundering Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Regulations 2008, failure to maintain effective AML procedures, report suspicious transactions, or conduct customer due diligence can incur criminal fines, civil penalties, asset forfeiture, and imprisonment. Regulatory sanctions may include license revocation or a direction to cease business.

Director personal liability: Directors, Money Laundering Reporting Officers, and beneficial owners can be criminally liable and personally fined; courts may impose travel bans, seize personal assets, and sentence offenders to imprisonment for serious or repeated AML failures.
Unlicensed Money Service Business Activity

Statutory sanction: Carrying on regulated financial activity without a licence is an offence under the Bermuda Financial Services statutes, resulting in disciplinary fines, enforcement action by the Bermuda Monetary Authority, injunctions, and criminal penalties for continuing business after a stop order.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally named in enforcement proceedings, ordered to pay restitution to affected customers, banned from holding financial services positions, and, in egregious cases, imprisoned for unlicensed conduct.
False Declarations and Defective Beneficial Ownership Filing

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies Act 1981 and CIT Act 2023, making a false declaration attracts criminal fines and imprisonment; the Registrar may strike off the company and foreign authorities may treat all prior tax filings as void or fraudulent.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved false declarations face personal criminal liability, civil fraud claims, disqualification, travel restrictions, and confiscation of proceeds attributable to the misrepresentation.
