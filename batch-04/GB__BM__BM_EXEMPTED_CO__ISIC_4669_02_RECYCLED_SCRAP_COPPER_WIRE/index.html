Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: A Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt on its Bermuda-source and foreign-source trading profits, capital gains, and distributions, with no withholding taxes, unless it is a constituent entity of an in-scope MNE group (then subject to 15% CIT under the GloBE/Pillar Two implementation). Cross-border trading in shredded non-ferrous scrap (ISIC 4669 / NAICS 423930) is not subject to Bermuda income tax in the ordinary case.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election and no UK hybrid mismatch election is available in Bermuda. Statutory elections under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 include: (1) Qualified Refundable Tax Credit (QRTC) election calculated on eligible Bermuda payroll (capped at 65% of local payroll) to reduce the effective CIT rate toward 5% for qualifying substance activities; (2) group loss election and allocated CIT filing arrangements among Bermuda constituent entities; and (3) transitional elections regarding the deemed start date of CIT registration. Hybrid mismatch consequences affecting a UK ultimate parent are managed under UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A, not at the Bermuda entity level.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST system. Bermuda imposes import customs duties under the Customs Act 1967 and Customs Tariff Act; no sales and use tax, no export duty, and no services tax applies. Scrap copper wire exports are outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT registration threshold because Bermuda does not levy VAT/GST. Customs importer/exporter registration is mandatory for all persons trading goods without a monetary threshold; the annual exempted company government fee (BMD/USD approximately 1,745 minimum, scaled by issued share capital) is a licensing fee, not an indirect tax threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Bermuda - because no VAT exists, exported shredded copper scrap wire is outside the scope of Bermuda indirect tax; there is no 0% VAT claim, input VAT refund, or export VAT rebate mechanism. The ASYCUDA World export declaration serves as the only fiscal export evidence. If physical goods are routed through the United Kingdom mainland, UK VAT zero-rating for direct export (VAT Act 1994, Schedule 4, Item 1) would apply only to a UK-registered principal and requires standard HMRC export evidence; the Bermuda entity itself has no UK VAT registration nexus.

  • ASYCUDA World Electronic Export Declaration (HS code 7404.00 - copper waste and scrap)
  • Bill of Lading or ocean waybill evidencing the overseas destination port
  • Commercial invoice (importer/exporter identity, commodity description, metric tonnage, unit price)
  • Weight certificate and container packing list with verified tare and net weight of shredded copper wire
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing USD settlement under documentary letter of credit or telegraphic transfer
  • Sales contract and certificate of analysis stating copper content percentage for customs valuation consistency

ASYCUDA World is the exclusive electronic customs clearance and single-window declaration platform. Import/export entries, cargo manifests, duty computations, and payments are filed digitally in real time with risk-based selectivity. No continuous transaction control or fiscal stamping of invoices applies because there is no VAT. Export consignments of recycled copper wire require a complete electronic export declaration, commercial invoice, and packing list before loading; customs may impose a digital hold for weight, valuation, or copper-content verification.

Bermuda Customs may audit export entries for up to six years; false declarations attract penalties up to BMD 10,000 plus up to treble the duty evaded, forfeiture of goods, and liability of the carrier. Although scrap exports carry nil duty, misdescription, undervaluation, or false copper-content certification is prosecuted under the Customs Act. The Tax Commissioner may retrospectively disallow QRTC claims and claw back CIT refunds with interest where economic substance documentation is not substantiated. Breach of the Bermuda Economic Substance Act (distribution and service center relevant activity) attracts financial penalties of BMD 25,000 to 250,000 and possible compulsory dissolution of the exempted company. For UK-registered exporters, fraudulent VAT zero-rating claims attract a 100% penalty and retrospective clawback of input VAT under HMRC procedures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fully convertible regime for exempted companies with no exchange-control approvals required for non-resident capital, profits, dividends, loan servicing, or liquidation proceeds. Current-account trade flows (invoice settlement, freight, agency commissions) and capital-account flows (equity injection, intercompany debt, share redemption) are free and unrestricted. The BMD/USD parity peg ensures near-zero convertibility risk for USD-denominated scrap metal receipts; residual friction is limited to settlement currencies not readily convertible at the peg (EUR, GBP, CNH, INR), which are addressed through the treasury hedging rails below. Profit remittance hurdles are purely corporate-governance based: a board resolution, a solvency certificate under section 54 of the Companies Act 1981, and updated beneficial ownership filings with the BMA.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists in Bermuda for surrender, conversion, or timed repatriation of export proceeds. Receipts from shredded non-ferrous scrap and copper wire consignments (HS 7404) may be retained in any freely convertible currency without mandatory conversion ratios. Nevertheless, the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018 oblige the exempted company to retain transaction records, bills of lading, customs declarations, weight certificates, bank statements, and underlying invoices for at least six years so that each receivable can be matched to physical cross-border movement of goods.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), exercising statutory exchange control and monetary issuance powers under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, maintaining the Bermuda Dollar (BMD) at a fixed 1:1 peg to the US Dollar, with HM Treasury retaining ultimate constitutional oversight for Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as a primary settlement rail, and MEDIUM as a treasury overlay. UK FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (Revolut, Wise Business, Payoneer, or similar) can deliver virtual IBANs, low-cost FX conversion, and rapid account opening for a newly incorporated Bermuda exempted company, but they are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in Bermuda, cannot issue Bermuda-dollar accounts, cannot issue or confirm commercial letters of credit, and do not participate in the Bermuda clearing system. Legal constraint: the BMA does not license EMIs as banks, and correspondent banks will de-risk an ISIC 4669 scrap-trading entity that attempts to settle trade flows exclusively through an EMI. Therefore, EMIs should be confined to ancillary flows such as collections from small buyers, travel and expense disbursements, or FX conversion before funds are swept to the Bermudian settlement account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound equity injections are executed via SWIFT MT103 from the UK parent or non-resident shareholder's own bank, carrying the IMF Balance of Payments purpose code F5243 (Direct Investment Equity) or the bank's equivalent capital-transfer narrative such as 'Paid-up share capital -- Bermuda Exempted Company'. The remitting bank must complete UK Purpose of Payment and sanctions screening; the receiving Bermudian bank then verifies source of funds for each shareholder, after which the funds are credited to the operating or escrow account. The company secretary must pass a board allotment resolution, issue fully paid ordinary shares, and file the share-issue return and any beneficial-ownership update with the BMA. No exchange-control authorisation is required, and the 1:1 BMD/USD peg ensures parity conversion if funds arrive in USD.Trade settlements are executed exclusively through licensed correspondent banking rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 direct transfers for documentary collection and open-account TTs up to the bank's EDD threshold, accompanied by commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, weight certificate, and HS 7404 customs declaration; (2) irrevocable at-sight commercial Letters of Credit governed by UCP 600, issued by the Bermudian relationship bank and advised or confirmed by a first-class London, European, or Asian correspondent bank for consignments above USD 500,000; and (3) standby LCs for supplier performance guarantees. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (standard Bermuda Exempted Company); 15% for in-scope MNE groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, effective 1 January 2025, implementing OECD Pillar Two QDMTT

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax, advance corporation tax, or dividend distribution tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to any shareholder, including non-residents.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 for standard Exempted Companies (no mandatory TP documentation); in-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenue of at least €750 million (approx. BMD 900 million) must prepare Master File and Local File in accordance with Chapter V of the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, alongside Country-by-Country Report obligations where applicable.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2017 (Bermuda) requires an Exempted Company engaged in any 'relevant activity' to demonstrate substantial economic substance in Bermuda. The cross-border physical consignment of shredded non-ferrous scrap (ISIC 4669 / NAICS 423930) falls within the 'distribution and service centre' category. To comply, the entity must maintain a physical office, a bank account, qualified local directors, staff with decision-making authority, and must perform core income-generating activities (contract negotiation, contract management, procurement, risk and inventory control) in Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Non-Compliance / Shell Company Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2017, a first-offence penalty of BMD 10,000, a continuing penalty of BMD 10,000 per month, a court-ordered compliance order requiring immediate physical operations, and potential winding-up by the Supreme Court. The in-scope entity may also lose its ability to obtain a Tax Assurance Certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve a false substance return are personally liable for civil fines up to BMD 10,000, removal from directorship, travel restrictions, and a five-year director’s disqualification. Persisting non-compliance may expose directors to criminal liability and personal restitution for unpaid government fees.
Tax Evasion / UK Corporate Tax Residence Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under UK CTA 2010 s.35, the company is UK tax resident and subject to corporation tax at up to 25% on worldwide profits. Submitting false tax returns may constitute fraud under FA 2000; criminal penalties include unlimited fines and imprisonment of up to seven years, plus late-payment interest at the HMRC statutory rate.

Director personal liability: Under TMA 1970 s.114 and CTA 2010 s.108, directors who knowingly enabled the deceptive residence position are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid UK corporate tax. Personal civil orders may include asset freezes, director disqualification, and travel bans preventing directors from leaving the UK.
Anti-Money Laundering Deficiency (AML / CDD)

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Regulations 2019, contraventions attract civil penalties up to BMD 250,000 for individuals and BMD 500,000 for legal persons; persistent failures may lead to revocation of the corporate registered office, freezing of bank accounts, and forced strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Compliance Officer may be criminally liable if they exhibit wilful blindness to money-laundering channels. The Proceeds of Crime Act 2010 (Bermuda) provides for fines up to BMD 250,000, imprisonment up to 14 years, confiscation of personal assets, and exclusion from acting as a company director in Bermuda.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure (In-Scope MNE)

Statutory sanction: The Bermuda CIT Act 2023 empowers the Commissioner, upon adjustment, to levy an underpayment penalty of 10% of the additional QDMTT due; a failure to file the CbCR attracts a fixed daily penalty of BMD 1,000 per day; the total penalty is not capped for deliberate concealment.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve sham intercompany pricing may be pursued for wrongful trading under the Insolvency Act 1998 and may be personally liable to restore the amount of avoided tax and interest if corporate assets are routed to a related party to defeat the claim.
