Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

A Bermuda Exempted Company may apply to the Minister of Finance for a Tax Protection Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which guarantees that, until the certificate's expiry (typically through 2035 or a later extended period), no Bermuda tax will be imposed on profit, income, gains, dividends, or distributions. There is no check-the-box or hybrid election comparable to US/European regimes; Bermuda does not impose income tax, and OECD hybrid mismatch rules are not directly enacted. Payroll tax registration is elective based on employment threshold; no fiscal transparency election applies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax (Bermuda imposes import customs duty and payroll tax only)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no VAT/GST registration regime; customs duty liability arises per import entry regardless of threshold. A Bermuda Customs declaration (Form C75) is required for any import exceeding BMD 100 (or absolute duty-free allowances).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because Bermuda levies no VAT/GST on goods or services, there is no export zero-rating regime and no VAT refund mechanism. Exports from Bermuda are outside any consumption tax; no export VAT recovery is available.

No real-time e-invoicing or digital stamping mandate for tax purposes. Bermuda Customs operates an electronic single-window clearance system; importers/exporters must file electronic declarations (C75/export entries) through an accredited customs broker or the Customs Trader Portal, attaching commercial invoices, bills of lading, and packing lists. No continuous transaction controls or fiscal stamping apply.

No VAT exists to claw back. However, H.M. Customs & Excise may audit export declarations; false statements, undervaluation, omission of restricted surgical goods, or unauthorized re-import of goods previously exported can subject the company to forfeiture, administrative penalties up to three times the customs duty involved, and criminal liability (fines and imprisonment) under the Customs Act 1958 and Customs Tariff Act 1962. The Department of Revenue can likewise re-assess payroll tax within 6 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD). Bermuda maintains a liberalized exchange-control regime: current-account payments (trade, services, dividends, interest, royalties) are freely convertible for exempted companies under the BMA General Permission without prior approval, provided transactions are routed through an authorized dealer (a Bermuda-licensed bank). Capital-account flows for exempted companies are likewise unrestricted for non-resident shareholders; BMA notification is required only for statistical reporting and for transactions requiring an individual licence under specific sectoral rules. Profit remittance and dividend distribution are fully permissible with no withholding tax, no exchange surrender, and no capital-control barriers. The only material hurdle is bank-level AML/source-of-funds due diligence, not statutory convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds in Bermuda. The Exchange Control (Amendment) Act 2005 abolished compulsory surrender and all foreign-currency retention limits. An ISIC 3250 surgical-suture manufacturing entity may retain 100% of export receipts in an authorized dealer's foreign-currency account without time limit. Only standard reporting obligations apply: the bank files a Foreign Exchange Transaction statistical return with the BMA for transactions above prescribed thresholds, and the company must maintain underlying trade documents (invoices, airway bills, certificates of origin) for AML/ATF record-keeping under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), exercising powers under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Exchange Control Act 1972 (as amended by the Exchange Control (Amendment) Act 2005), the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, and subsidiary Exchange Control Regulations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM for non-BMD cross-border disbursement rails; LOW for domestic BMD clearing and deposit services. Bermuda does not operate an EU-style EMI licensing or passporting regime; deposit-taking requires a full banking licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., UK FCA-authorised or EU-authorised EMIs such as Wise Business or Airwallex) may lawfully offer a Bermuda exempted company multi-currency IBANs and outbound MT103/SEPA routing because the company is a non-resident offshore vehicle, but such EMIs cannot accept BMD deposits, cannot participate in Bermuda's domestic clearing system, and are not recognised as authorised dealers by the BMA. The Digital Asset Business Act 2023 governs digital-asset and stablecoin businesses, not conventional fiat EMIs. Recommended architecture: Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda as the primary BMD/USD clearing and LC-issuing bank, supplemented by a UK/EU EMI only for Euro-area and UK supplier payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Recommended routing is a SWIFT MT103 wire in USD (or BMD at the 1:1 peg) from the shareholder's bank to the Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda capital-escrow account, payment reference 'Share Capital Subscription – [Exempted Company Name] – BMA Registration Number'. Under the BMA General Permission, no prior exchange-control approval is required for a non-resident capital injection into an exempted company. The onboarding bank requires: (i) a certified board resolution authorising the share issue and account credit; (ii) a signed share subscription application from each subscriber; (iii) a completed bank Foreign Currency Declaration – Capital form for statistical reporting; and (iv) a source-of-funds letter for amounts above USD 1,000,000. After clearing (typically T+1 through CHIPS/Fedwire), the bank issues a capital receipt; the allotment is thereafter notified to the Bermuda Registrar of Companies via the annual return or a return of allotment filing. The funds may be immediately swept into the operational account for working capital and trade settlements.Two compliant clearing rails are deployed for the ISIC 3250 manufacturing supply chain: (1) SWIFT MT103 bank-to-bank telegraphic transfer for recurring vendor payments, consumables, and any disbursement up to approximately USD 250,000, referencing the invoice number and CA/UKCA certificate; and (2) an irrevocable, confirmed Commercial Letter of Credit (at-sight or usance) issued by HSBC Butterfield's trade-finance desk for bulk polyglycolic-acid resin purchases, stainless-steel needle wire, and capital equipment, advised through a correspondent bank in the supplier's jurisdiction. All disbursements are cleared in USD via CHIPS/Fedwire, in GBP via CHAPS, and in EUR via TARGET2, with MT202 COV used exclusively for interbank reimbursement, never for direct beneficiary payments. The mandatory trade-settlement pack comprises the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, certificate of origin, and applicable medical-grade material quarantine or CE certificate; the bank will reconcile each wire against the supporting pack for AML/ATF compliance.Plain vanilla deliverable FX forwards (USD/GBP, USD/EUR, USD/JPY) up to 24 months executed with the relationship bank under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement and the 1998 FX Definitions.Single-date FX swaps and cross-currency swaps (XCS) for long-term intercompany loan and balance-sheet hedging between BMD/USD and GBP or EUR.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (CCIRS) to hedge term intercompany debt aligned with the underlying loan maturity and cash-flow profile.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permissible only with BMA-authorised or G10 international bank counterparties where the underlying trade exposure is in a restricted-deliverability currency (e.g., CNY, INR) and only for genuine trade hedging, not speculative positions.Zero-premium collars and at-the-money options are contractually available under Bermuda law, subject to bank credit approval, BMA derivative-product record-keeping, and board authorisation under the Companies Act 1981.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Bermuda Exempted Companies; 15% under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 10,000,000 annual aggregate related-party revenue or cross-border payments triggers mandatory Local File and Master File documentation for entities within CITA scope. Entities outside CITA scope must retain benchmarking/transfer pricing records to support economic substance declarations.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Amendment Regulations 2018 apply. A Bermuda exempted company carrying on a relevant activity, including manufacturing and distribution of surgical consumables, must maintain managed-and-directed activity in Bermuda, locally incur operating expenditures, and employ qualified local staff. Surgical suture/needle assembly is an active manufacturing trade; the 'pure equity holding' exception is inapplicable.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exempted Status Declaration

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and the Bermuda Criminal Code: fines up to BMD 1,000,000, imprisonment up to 10 years, and an additional 50% penalty on tax under-assessed where fraud or negligence is established.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may be personally assessed for unpaid CIT and penalties if they knowingly authorized the false declaration. Courts may issue disqualification orders of up to 15 years, asset-freezing orders, and travel bans.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Fines of BMD 25,000 to BMD 500,000 per failure under the Economic Substance Amendment Regulations 2018; strike-off from the Bermuda Companies Register; suspension of exemption certificates.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for outstanding penalties. Continuation of business after a strike-off is a criminal offense carrying imprisonment and a further daily fine. Directors may face bans on serving as officers of any Bermuda company.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalties up to BMD 250,000 per contravention, plus a daily default penalty not exceeding BMD 5,000 per day. For conviction on indictment, a fine and imprisonment for up to 5 years may be imposed.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are criminally liable for deliberate CDD and reporting breaches. The court may also make a prohibition order preventing them from acting as company directors for a period not exceeding 15 years.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: Where CITA applies, the Bermuda tax authority may adjust profits, impose interest at 10% per annum, and levy penalties of up to 100% of the adjusted tax. Filing false transfer pricing documentation is separately actionable with fines up to BMD 500,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve artificial pricing may be personally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties in cases of fraud, with exposure to imprisonment up to 10 years, compulsory restitution of stripped profits, and confiscation of personal assets.
