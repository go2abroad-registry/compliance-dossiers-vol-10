Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. A Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, corporation tax, and withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, and management fees under the Tax Exemption Certificate valid through 2035. Residual Bermuda fiscal liabilities are limited to employer payroll tax on Bermuda-sourced payroll, customs duty on physical imports, and, for multinational groups above the EUR 750M consolidated revenue threshold, the 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax under the CIT Act 2023 effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, including the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax. No UK-style pass-through status exists in Bermuda; for US federal tax purposes the entity may be classified under the default per se corporation rule or elect partnership or disregarded entity status via Form 8832. The entity is not a UK tax resident if central management and control is exercised in Bermuda.

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists. Where the Exempted Company is owned by a US parent, entity classification is governed by US Treasury Regulation Section 301.7701-3 (Form 8832, generally due within 75 days of the proposed effective date); the default classification for a Bermuda incorporated company is per se corporation under Reg. Section 301.7701-2(b)(8), with an election available to be treated as a partnership or disregarded entity for US federal tax purposes. Bermuda has no participation exemption election and no foreign tax credit pooling mechanism. The CIT Act 2023 adopts the OECD GloBE model rules for in-scope groups, including elections for deferred tax recognition and qualified refundable tax credits. UK-side exposure: if central management and control is exercised in the UK, UK corporation tax applies under CTA 2009 Section 14 despite Bermuda incorporation, and UK CFC rules under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A may attribute Bermuda passive royalty or service income to UK shareholders, requiring robust board governance and substance planning.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD), which circulates interchangeably in the economy. Exempted companies are classified as non-resident for exchange-control purposes, thereby enjoying full current- and capital-account convertibility without prior BMA consent. Capital inflows, retained foreign-currency earnings, and dividend or profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted and free of Bermudian withholding tax, subject only to standard anti-money-laundering (AML) verification and BMA statistical balance-of-payments reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds, no minimum conversion ratio, and no statutory custody timeline for foreign-currency receipts. The exempted company may retain export and service income indefinitely in USD or any freely convertible currency; the BMA requires only statistical reporting and imposes no conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the integrated financial-services regulator and exchange-control authority exercising delegated powers under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972 within the United Kingdom overseas-territory framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for UK-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) as supplemental settlement rails for GBP and EUR receipts and vendor disbursements; LOW as a substitute for a Bermudian bank account. Legal constraints: Bermuda has no dedicated EMI licence category; the BMA regulates deposit-taking activity under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999. Offshore digital EMIs may not take Bermudian deposits or clear payments into the BMD/USD peg system. The exempted company may validly use an FCA-authorised UK EMI as merchant of record or payment facilitator, provided all treasury balances ultimately settle through, and are reconciled with, a licensed Tier-1 Bermuda bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route share-capital injections via SWIFT MT103 (or CHAPS or Fedwire where applicable) from the UBO's account to the Bermuda Exempted Company's USD account at a Tier-1 Bermudian bank. Remittance instructions must carry: (i) the full legal entity name and Bermuda company or BRN registration number; (ii) a SWIFT payment-purpose code for capital contribution (e.g., 'CAPT' — payment of capital) or, for invoiced software services, an appropriate services code; and (iii) a board resolution and share-register update evidencing the share issuance. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for ordinary Bermuda Exempted Companies outside the OECD GloBE threshold; 15% under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope MNE Groups, adjusted to 16.2% for the first two fiscal years (FY2025-FY2026)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no dividend withholding tax on ordinary dividends, constructive distributions, or deemed dividends to non-resident shareholders; distributions may be declared and paid without Bermuda authority clearance

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 statutory threshold for ordinary out-of-scope exempted companies (no Bermuda transfer pricing statute applies); mandatory Local File and Master File obligations arise exclusively for CIT Groups whose ultimate parent's consolidated revenue exceeds approximately BMD 815,000,000 (EUR 750,000,000) in at least two of the preceding four periods; prudent contemporaneous documentation is recommended for annual intercompany transactions exceeding BMD 500,000

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply to all registered exempted companies regardless of CIT status. Relevant activities (headquarters, distribution and service centre, IP holding, pure equity holding, finance and leasing) require full local management and control in Bermuda, proportionate local employment, recurring board meetings in Bermuda, and annual ESR-01 disclosure. An API scraping and proxy gateway business concluding contracts and governance from the UK while using a Bermuda mailbox as a shell is a classic economic-substance false declaration and will be struck off and reported to HMRC

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False CIT Declaration

Statutory sanction: Fixed and daily penalties up to BMD 250,000; under-assessment penalty up to 100% of the tax shortfall; interest at 8.33% per annum; estimated assessments converted into a statutory charge over the Bermuda bank accounts; criminal prosecution with imprisonment on indictment

Director personal liability: Directors and the Bermuda registered agent who sign false CIT computations are personally liable for the penalty and costs; courts may issue a travel ban against officers of the foreign parent and an asset restraint order pending resolution
Shell Company / Abuse of the Exempted Companies Regime

Statutory sanction: Compulsory strike-off under the Companies Act 1981; loss of the EUTPA tax protection certificate; fine up to BMD 250,000; information exchange to HMRC under the limited UK-Bermuda DTA, followed by UK Tax Avoidance and Exploitative Arrangements penalties

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent trading; personal civil liability to the Revenue; director disqualification up to 15 years; travel bans through the Bermuda court and UK Fraud and Terrorist Finance mechanisms
AML / Unlicensed Digital Asset Business

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed digital asset business: fine up to BMD 250,000 plus BMD 10,000 per day of continuing breach; forfeiture of digital assets; compulsory AML audit; prohibition order; adverse naming and publication by the Bermuda Monetary Authority

Director personal liability: Officers are criminally liable on indictment; the BMA may revoke the registered agent's endorsement and impose monetary penalties equal to the value of illicit flows; the ultimate beneficial owner faces a UK Serious Organised Crime money laundering investigation with asset freezing and confiscation orders
Transfer Pricing Circularity / UK Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: HMRC transfer pricing adjustment; penalties up to 100% of the UK tax; conduct-based daily penalties up to GBP 10,000 per day under Schedule 24 FA 2007; Bermuda CIT group adjustment if the EUR 750 million threshold is met; denial of double taxation relief unless the substance analysis is corrected

Director personal liability: UK directors are liable for penal restitution for unreasonable tax advantages; possible disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act; Bermuda officers face statutory compliance duties under the CIT Act with personal fines of BMD 50,000 for continuing non-compliance
