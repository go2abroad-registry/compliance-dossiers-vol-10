Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Registrar of Companies (RoC), Ministry of Finance; no general income tax authority for exempted companies. Official company registry portal: https://www.roc.gov.bm/

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must incorporate through a licensed Bermuda registered agent; the RoC issues a Bermuda Company Number (BCN) which serves as the sole statutory identifier. No VAT/TIN is issued because Bermuda levies no VAT, GST, or sales tax. If the exempted company becomes an employer, it must separately register for payroll tax with the Bermuda Tax Commissioner and the Department of Social Insurance to obtain a PAYE registration number.

Default tax classification

No Bermuda income tax election (e.g., check-the-box) exists. An exempted company may elect to be treated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST/sales tax and therefore has no registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Inapplicable in Bermuda because there is no consumption tax to zero-rate. Goods exported from Bermuda are not subject to any Bermuda VAT/GST. For goods sold into the UK/EU, the UK/EU export zero-rating rules may apply only in the destination jurisdiction when certain conditions are met, but that is outside Bermuda's tax system. Bermuda customs export clearance remains mandatory for physical exports.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (Form CUS-EXP)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (where FX control or payment proof is required)

Not applicable – Bermuda does not mandate electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping for invoices.

No VAT clawback regime exists in Bermuda. However, false customs export declarations may attract penalties under Bermuda's Customs (Import/Export Control) Act 1952, including fines up to BD$10,000, forfeiture of goods, and/or imprisonment. For UK/EU e-commerce sales, HMRC or relevant EU tax authorities may assert UK/EU VAT compliance obligations and apply retrospective assessments, interest, and penalties for unremitted VAT or improper zero-rating claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar (USD). Exempted companies are classified as non-resident for exchange control purposes, granting full current and capital account convertibility without prior BMA approval for foreign currency transactions. Profit remittance and dividend distribution to non-resident shareholders are free of exchange control restrictions. Exempted companies may hold and transact in any currency; no surrender or conversion obligations apply on inbound or outbound payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion timeline applies to Bermuda Exempted Companies. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency indefinitely, but comprehensive underlying records, invoices, and bank statements must be maintained for AML and audit compliance. There is no minimum percentage of export proceeds required to be converted into BMD or USD.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., specialized cross-border payment processors) are viable for non-resident exempted companies, subject to their own licensing in the UK or EU and acceptance of Bermuda entities. However, a Bermuda-regulated e-money issuer must obtain a BMA license under the Electronic Money Act. For vintage luxury resale, EMIs often impose enhanced due diligence and may reject high-risk payment categories; thus, a hybrid structure (domestic bank + EMI) is recommended.

Root cause: Large inbound payments from escrow platforms or payment processors to a newly formed exempted company

Defense evidence: Supply the underlying commercial contracts, platform settlement statements, a business plan with volume forecasts, beneficial ownership registry extracts, and an external AML/CFT policy. Additional bank references from the source jurisdiction may be required.
Cross-border payouts to suppliers or consignors in high-risk jurisdictions

Root cause: Cross-border payouts to suppliers or consignors in high-risk jurisdictions

Defense evidence: Submit vendor due diligence files, trade invoices, shipping and logistics tracking records, and a clear rationale for each cross-border payment. Incorporate a sanctions screening process and maintain an audit trail of all payouts.

First ESR failure: civil penalty of BD$10,000; subsequent failure: BD$100,000. Knowingly providing false information: fine of BD$10,000 and/or up to one year imprisonment. Validation could also trigger exchange of information with the UK and adverse EU/OECD listing consequences.
Part D - Physical Presence Number of full-time employees in Bermuda, physical premises address, and operating expenditure in BD$. A pure holding entity may elect reduced substance requirements; an active e-commerce operator cannot report zero employees without exposing the entity to ESR failure. First ESR failure: civil penalty of BD$10,000; subsequent failure: BD$100,000. Knowingly providing false information: fine of BD$10,000 and/or up to one year imprisonment. Validation could also trigger exchange of information with the UK and adverse EU/OECD listing consequences.
Part E - Income Disclosure Gross income from the relevant activity and total operating expenditure. If no relevant activity is declared, income boxes are completed with a nil statement but the declaration itself remains mandatory. First ESR failure: civil penalty of BD$10,000; subsequent failure: BD$100,000. Knowingly providing false information: fine of BD$10,000 and/or up to one year imprisonment. Validation could also trigger exchange of information with the UK and adverse EU/OECD listing consequences.
Part F - Declaration Signed declaration by a director or authorised officer confirming accuracy. False declarations attract criminal penalties for the signing officer personally. First ESR failure: civil penalty of BD$10,000; subsequent failure: BD$100,000. Knowingly providing false information: fine of BD$10,000 and/or up to one year imprisonment. Validation could also trigger exchange of information with the UK and adverse EU/OECD listing consequences.
Field 1 - Reporting Entity Company registration number and registered agent confirmation. Non-resident ultimate individual owners are reportable; corporate nominees are not acceptable. Non-compliance may result in Restriction Notices preventing exercise of ownership rights, monetary penalties by the Registrar, and, for deliberate concealment, criminal prosecution of the responsible officer.
Field 2 - Beneficial Owner Particulars Full legal name, date of birth, residential address, nationality, tax identification number, and the nature and percentage of control. Where the shareholder is a UK corporate parent, look through to the controlling individuals; the UK parent's registered address cannot serve as the beneficial owner's residential address. Non-compliance may result in Restriction Notices preventing exercise of ownership rights, monetary penalties by the Registrar, and, for deliberate concealment, criminal prosecution of the responsible officer.
Field 3 - Management Declaration Where no individual is identified, a senior manager declaration may be required. This exception is construed narrowly and does not apply to a single-shareholder box holding in a resale e-commerce operation. Non-compliance may result in Restriction Notices preventing exercise of ownership rights, monetary penalties by the Registrar, and, for deliberate concealment, criminal prosecution of the responsible officer.
Field 4 - Material Change Flag indicating share transfers, change of control, or new beneficial owner. Double-check with the marketplaces and escrow agreements: redemption of preferred equity or conversion notes may itself trigger a reportable change. Non-compliance may result in Restriction Notices preventing exercise of ownership rights, monetary penalties by the Registrar, and, for deliberate concealment, criminal prosecution of the responsible officer.
