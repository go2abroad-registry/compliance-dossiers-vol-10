Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda is a separate non-UK tax jurisdiction. The competent tax administration is the Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Hamilton, Bermuda (tax functions administered through the Bermuda government portal at https://www.gov.bm). Company-law registration is with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies (Ministry of Finance); customs and export controls are with the Bermuda Customs Service. HM Revenue and Customs (United Kingdom) is not the registration authority for this Bermudian entity but is a counterparty for international information exchange and CFC legislation.

E-government filing portal

A foreign parent uses a Bermuda-licensed corporate service provider (CSP) to incorporate an Exempted Company with the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981. The Registrar issues the Certificate of Incorporation and a unique company number; the company simultaneously obtains a Certificate of Exemption under the Exempted Companies Act 1981 and is recorded as an exempted undertaking. No Bermudian TIN or VAT ID is issued on incorporation; the company number is the primary corporate identifier. If required for UK double-tax-relief or CFC analyses, the company may apply to the Office of the Tax Commissioner for a certificate of tax residence. Payroll-tax employer registration arises only if Bermuda-resident staff are employed; customs importer registration arises only on first physical import. If the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies (MNE group with global revenue of EUR 750m or more), the company must register as a constituent entity and receive a CIT reference. Otherwise, no further tax-ID registration obtains.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged to the US dollar (USD) at 1:1 and is freely convertible for non-resident exempted companies. Capital account transactions are generally permitted without prior exchange control approval where the exempted company is classified as non-resident, provided it does not carry on business in Bermuda and is beneficially owned by non-residents. Profit remittance and dividend distribution are unrestricted for such non-resident exempted companies; any transfer in foreign currency must be executed through an authorised dealer and may require a BMA notification or negative consent filing. Resident-engaged transactions and dealings in BMD may require exchange control permission under the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds and no statutory conversion ratio for non-resident exempted companies. Export receipts denominated in foreign currency may be retained in foreign currency accounts and deployed for offshore operational expenditures. For entities treated as resident for exchange control purposes, the BMA may require foreign currency receipts to be offered to an authorised dealer for conversion into BMD within a specified period unless a general permission or specific exemption applies. The target Bermuda Exempted Company, operating as a non-resident publishing entity, is outside the scope of any surrender or conversion mandate.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Cross-border digital EMI onboarding for a Bermuda Exempted Company is rated Medium. Reputable UK and European electronic money institutions (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business) generally require a physical or registered presence in their licensing jurisdiction and may not accept Bermuda risk profiles. Bermuda itself has a regulated 'digital assets' fintech license but no EMI passporting regime equivalent to the EU/UK. Offshore EMIs in other low-tax jurisdictions may provide USD and EUR accounts, but legal constraints include limited ability to obtain bank identification codes and restrictions on serving gaming payment flows. The most viable arrangement is a Bermuda bank account at Butterfield or HSBC supplemented by an authorised-currency payout aggregator for AdMob/AppLovin proceeds.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general corporate income tax for Bermuda Exempted Companies outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA); 15% CIT applies solely to constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more in at least two of the preceding four fiscal years, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends. Distributions by a Bermuda Exempted Company to UK or any foreign shareholders are made gross without any Bermudian deduction.

Knowingly false declarations: criminal fines and imprisonment up to 2 years.
Box 6 - Bermuda Employees Full-time equivalent employees physically present in Bermuda performing core income-generating activities. A zero-employee return converts the filing into an automatic substance-test failure for IP businesses; fully remote, UK-based engineering and ad-ops teams count as zero Bermuda employees. Failure to file the ES Return: default fine of BMD 10,000 plus continued penalty of BMD 1,000 per day of default, risk of strike-off, and automatic exchange of information with UK HMRC and US IRS. Knowingly false declarations: criminal fines and imprisonment up to 2 years.
Box 7 - Bermuda Operating Expenditure Annual operating expenditure (BMD) incurred in Bermuda for the relevant activity. Subsistence-level service fees paid to a Bermuda corporate service provider are generally disregarded; genuine local payroll and office rent are the decisive evidence. Failure to file the ES Return: default fine of BMD 10,000 plus continued penalty of BMD 1,000 per day of default, risk of strike-off, and automatic exchange of information with UK HMRC and US IRS. Knowingly false declarations: criminal fines and imprisonment up to 2 years.
Box 8 - Place of Core Income-Generating Activities Jurisdiction where the core income-generating activities are actually conducted. If strategic decisions, game IP licensing and ad-rate negotiations occur in the UK, the accurate entry is the United Kingdom, triggering CIGA failure and UK-residence exposure. Failure to file the ES Return: default fine of BMD 10,000 plus continued penalty of BMD 1,000 per day of default, risk of strike-off, and automatic exchange of information with UK HMRC and US IRS. Knowingly false declarations: criminal fines and imprisonment up to 2 years.
Box 9 - Physical Presence Declaration Confirmation of physical office/premises in Bermuda and whether board meetings are held in Bermuda. Nominee-registered premises without actual management meetings do not satisfy the physical-presence test. Failure to file the ES Return: default fine of BMD 10,000 plus continued penalty of BMD 1,000 per day of default, risk of strike-off, and automatic exchange of information with UK HMRC and US IRS. Knowingly false declarations: criminal fines and imprisonment up to 2 years.
Section A - Registered Office Physical registered office address in Bermuda maintained by a licensed corporate services provider. The office must be staffed and accessible in Bermuda; a PO box is insufficient. Default penalty of BMD 200 per month of non-filing, suspension of certificate of compliance, automatic strike-off after persistent default, and disqualification of directors from acting as directors of any Bermuda company.
Section B - Directors Full names, dates of birth and residential addresses of all directors. UK-resident directors controlling the Bermuda board must be disclosed; HMRC receives this data under the UK-Bermuda TIEA. Default penalty of BMD 200 per month of non-filing, suspension of certificate of compliance, automatic strike-off after persistent default, and disqualification of directors from acting as directors of any Bermuda company.
Section C - Shareholders Registered shareholders and percentage ownership. Disclose ultimate parents and intermediate holding companies on the chain. Default penalty of BMD 200 per month of non-filing, suspension of certificate of compliance, automatic strike-off after persistent default, and disqualification of directors from acting as directors of any Bermuda company.
Section D - Beneficial Ownership Ultimate beneficial owner(s) (natural persons holding 25% or more) filed on the Bermuda Beneficial Ownership Register. UK founders holding via nominee structures must be disclosed; concealment triggers AML penalties and cross-border information sharing. Default penalty of BMD 200 per month of non-filing, suspension of certificate of compliance, automatic strike-off after persistent default, and disqualification of directors from acting as directors of any Bermuda company.
Section E - Company Secretary Name of the Bermuda-resident secretary (individual or licensed firm). The secretary must hold a Bermuda trust licence or be a practising lawyer/accountant in Bermuda. Default penalty of BMD 200 per month of non-filing, suspension of certificate of compliance, automatic strike-off after persistent default, and disqualification of directors from acting as directors of any Bermuda company.
Line 1 - Bermuda Net Taxable Income Bermuda-source and apportioned income as computed under CITA 2023. Out-of-scope exempted companies are not required to register or file; in-scope groups must consolidate each constituent entity. Failure to file: BMD 100,000 maximum penalty for deliberate non-compliance plus daily default surcharges, interest on unpaid CIT at the statutory TAA rate, and containment/strike-off measures; directors of the Bermuda entity may be named on penalty certificates.
Line 11 - Transfer Pricing Adjustments Adjustments to income to reflect arm's length results under the Corporate Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Rules 2024. Same documentation standard as OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines; no TP file applies below the EUR 750 million group threshold. Failure to file: BMD 100,000 maximum penalty for deliberate non-compliance plus daily default surcharges, interest on unpaid CIT at the statutory TAA rate, and containment/strike-off measures; directors of the Bermuda entity may be named on penalty certificates.
Line 19 - GloBE Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT) Computation of Bermuda QDMTT to bring effective rate to 15% for in-scope entities. Applies only to constituents of groups with EUR 750 million or more consolidated revenue; a standalone hyper-casual games company is outside scope. Failure to file: BMD 100,000 maximum penalty for deliberate non-compliance plus daily default surcharges, interest on unpaid CIT at the statutory TAA rate, and containment/strike-off measures; directors of the Bermuda entity may be named on penalty certificates.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 (pegged 1:1 to USD) consolidated annual group revenue - the CITA in-scope threshold triggering mandatory Bermuda TP documentation, Master File/Local File and CbCR-aligned filings under the Corporate Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Rules 2024. Below this threshold, no statutory Bermuda TP file is required for out-of-scope exempted companies; however, upon UK tax-residence reclassification, UK transfer pricing rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 4) apply immediately without any de minimis threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 apply to every registered Bermuda entity. 'IP business' (exploitation of game IP, including ad-monetized hyper-casual titles) and 'distribution and service centre' (delivery of digital game services to related parties) are designated relevant activities. The company must: (i) file an annual ES Return, (ii) conduct core income-generating activities in Bermuda, (iii) maintain adequate physical presence, employees and expenditure in Bermuda proportionate to income, and (iv) for IP businesses apply the OECD nexus approach requiring local R&D, branding or product development activity. A zero-substance Bermuda entity deriving AdMob/AppLovin ad revenue from game IP is a designated high-risk case subject to enhanced scrutiny and automatic information exchange with HMRC and the IRS.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company (IP Business)

Statutory sanction: ESR 2018: penalty up to BMD 250,000 for IP businesses failing the substance test; BMD 10,000 default penalty for non-filing plus BMD 1,000 per day; automatic comprehensive information exchange with UK HMRC/IRS; cancellation of the tax exemption certificate; strike-off from the Bermuda register.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for fines and costs where the company fails to remedy; disqualification from acting as director of any Bermuda company; departure prevention orders (travel bans) may be issued against directors for unpaid penalties; criminal liability for knowingly false ES declarations with imprisonment up to 2 years.
UK Corporation Tax Evasion / Deemed UK Tax Residence

Statutory sanction: UK corporation tax at up to 25% on worldwide profits (19% small-profits rate where applicable) with backdating to the commencement of UK control; statutory interest on unpaid tax; penalties up to 200% of the tax for deliberate concealment; potential CbCR and public country-by-country reporting obligations.

Director personal liability: Senior accounting officer and director penalties of GBP 5,000 per uncorrected failure (FA 2009); personal liability for fraudulent tax evasion with imprisonment up to 7 years (TMA 1970); HMRC may issue charging orders over personally-held game IP or commercial contracts where value has been diverted.
Transfer Pricing / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: CITA 2023 and TP Rules 2024: primary TP adjustments bringing taxable profits to 15% CIT; interest and penalties up to BMD 100,000 for non-compliance; in parallel, HMRC may apply TIOPA 2010 Part 4 adjustments with penalties up to 100% of underpaid UK tax and, for offshore asset arrangements, penalties up to 200%.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly sign false TP documentation or facilitate profit shifting face personal fines in Bermuda and, in the UK, liability under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion - unlimited fines and imprisonment of directing minds up to 7 years on conviction.
