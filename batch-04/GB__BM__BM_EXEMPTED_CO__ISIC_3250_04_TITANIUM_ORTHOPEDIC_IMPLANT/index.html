Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; eTax portal at https://etax.gov.bm; Bermuda Customs Department under the Ministry of Finance for import/export administration. HMRC of the United Kingdom is relevant only if the Exempted Company establishes a separate UK taxable presence; no UK tax registration is otherwise required for the Bermuda entity.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 by the Registrar of Companies, which issues the Bermuda Company Registration Number serving as the primary statutory identifier. The Office of the Tax Commissioner issues a Bermuda Tax Identification Number upon registration via the eTax portal: (i) mandatory for every Bermuda constituent entity of an MNE group within the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 (global consolidated revenue of at least €750 million in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years); and (ii) available on application for all other entities for FATCA/CRS compliance, AML-compliant banking, tax residency certificates, and Economic Substance (Companies) Act 2021 annual declaration filing. No VAT identification number is obtainable or required because Bermuda neither imposes nor administers a VAT/GST regime. Non-resident ownership is permitted with no minimum capital requirement, and a licensed registered office is required to maintain statutory filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda does not operate a US-style check-the-box classification regime. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, a qualifying Bermuda constituent entity may make statutorily available elections, including the transitional Country-by-Country Reporting safe harbour election, the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax safe harbour election, the substance-based income exclusion election, and ancillary deferred-tax computation elections. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available domestically; Bermuda applies the OECD Global Anti-Base Erosion Model Rules to the extent incorporated under the CIT Act, and any hybrid-entity treatment is constrained by the economic substance requirements applicable to exempted companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Bermuda imposes no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; indirect taxation is limited to import customs duty under the Customs Tariff Act 2023 and customs administration under the Customs Act 2010

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover-based registration threshold exists because Bermuda has no VAT/GST system. All importers and exporters must nevertheless obtain a Bermuda Customs Importer/Exporter Code through the ASYCUDA World registration procedure irrespective of turnover value. For Corporate Income Tax, registration is triggered exclusively for constituent entities of MNE groups with global annual consolidated revenue of at least €750 million (approximately BMD 750 million under the Bermuda dollar peg to the US dollar) in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Bermuda: Bermudian exports are entirely outside the scope of any Bermudian indirect tax, so no statutory zero-rating claim is required or available. Exports of titanium orthopedic implants (HS codes 9021.31 / 9018.49; NAICS 339113) from Bermuda attract no export duty under the Customs Tariff Act 2023. Where the goods are sold into the UK or EU, import VAT and customs duty may apply at the port of entry; the buyer's entitlement to 0% acquisition treatment rests upon documentary proof of export from Bermuda and physical movement of the implants, which the seller must substantiate through complete shipping and customs records.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export entry through the e-Trade Single Window)
  • Commercial Invoice and Detailed Packing List reflecting HS 9021.31/9018.49 classification of porous titanium hip joint prosthetics
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of the titanium orthopedic implants from Bermuda
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT credit advice matching invoice value for foreign-exchange repatriation conclusiveness
  • Certificate of Origin where Commonwealth preference or destination-country preferential tariff treatment is claimed

Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-linked e-invoicing, or digital stamping. All import and export declarations must be lodged electronically through the Bermuda Customs Department ASYCUDA World Single Window (e-Trade system). B2B electronic invoicing is permitted on commercial platforms (EDI or digitally signed PDF) without government invoice clearance; where exports are destined for the United Kingdom or EU, the exporter must satisfy the buyer's requirements under EU/UK e-invoicing and VAT record-keeping standards but no Bermudian fiscal clearance attaches to those invoices.

Because Bermuda levies no VAT or GST, classic export VAT zero-rating fraud and retrospective clawback mechanisms are inapplicable. However, false export declarations are criminalised under the Customs Act 2010 and attract forfeiture or seizure of goods, penalties up to BMD 25,000, and fines of up to three times the duty evaded. The Office of the Tax Commissioner may reassess Corporate Income Tax and GloBE/QDMTT liabilities within a six-year limitation period, extended to twelve years where fraud is established, with late-filing fines, negligence penalties of 50% of the understated CIT, and fraud penalties of 100% in addition to economic-substance declaration sanctions. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day window will be treated by the Bermuda Monetary Authority and the Tax Commissioner as an indicator of artificial export structuring and will heighten audit scrutiny.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) under a currency board arrangement, ensuring full convertibility for current account transactions, including trade in goods and services. For capital account transactions, Bermuda operates an exchange control regime under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and Regulations 1973, but exempted companies are treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes, giving them unrestricted freedom to receive, hold, and remit foreign currency. Dividend and profit remittances are freely permitted with no withholding taxes, subject only to standard bank transactional due diligence and BMA reporting thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds applies to Bermuda Exempted Companies. As non-resident entities, they are excluded from the resident exchange control surrender obligations, and there is no statutory timeline or conversion ratio. Export receipts may be retained indefinitely in any foreign currency or in BMD, provided the company maintains proper accounting records and the bank has standing instructions for AML/CTF monitoring.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, with significant legal and operational constraints. Bermuda Exempted Companies may technically open accounts with UK or EU Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Business, Revolut, or CurrencyCloud, but de-risking policies often cause EMIs to decline offshore structures or impose Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD). EMIs are not a substitute for a licensed Bermuda bank, and the BMA does not regulate foreign EMIs operating in Bermuda without a physical branch. Any use of an EMI must be ancillary to the primary Bermuda banking relationship and must not alter the company's tax-residency or economic-substance profile.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For equity injections and shareholder loans, routing must occur via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's bank to the company's US Dollar or Bermudian Dollar account at a licensed Bermuda bank. The wire narrative must include the BMA-approved purpose code for direct investment or equity participation (e.g., 'CBS' for capital subscription, or 'SPI' for share premium). The receiving bank requires a board resolution authorizing the share issuance, a signed share subscription agreement, current UBO registration, and a source-of-funds statement. Inward transfers exceeding USD 100,000 may trigger a manual BMA exchange control notification, but approval is not withheld for non-resident exempted companies.Trade settlements may be executed via SWIFT MT103 (standard commercial payments for raw material purchases and component imports) or via Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) issued by the Bermuda bank in favor of suppliers. For LC-backed trade, the payment is made upon presentation of compliant documents: commercial invoice, full set of bills of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, and quality certificate evidencing titanium grade and implant manufacturing conformity. Outbound disbursements are unrestricted for non-resident exempted companies, provided the bank's transaction monitoring finds no ML/CTF flags and the payment corresponds to a valid commercial contract.
