E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election for a small exempted company. The company may apply under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 for a certificate guaranteeing exemption from future Bermuda income taxes, commonly issued until 2035. A large multinational constituent entity subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 may elect resident status or join a tax consolidation group for CIT filing. Foreign owners may make classification elections (e.g., US Form 8832) only in their home jurisdiction; such elections do not alter Bermuda exempt status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: BMD 0 - no VAT/GST threshold exists. UK: a non-established Bermuda seller must register for VAT before making any taxable supply from a UK FBA warehouse; there is no monetary threshold for non-UK-established businesses. For a UK-established business the annual taxable turnover threshold is GBP 90,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK VAT zero-rating applies to goods exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK, including sales made by the Bermuda exempted company through Amazon FBA stock, provided: (i) goods are supplied to an overseas customer, (ii) the goods physically leave the UK within 3 months of supply, and (iii) the exporter holds acceptable HMRC export evidence under Notice 703. Sales delivered to UK consumers from UK stock are standard-rated at 20%. Bermuda has no export tax or VAT refund regime. The retained export evidence file also supports any VAT repayment claim from HMRC where input VAT on export costs exceeds output VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic via UK CDS or combined transport document)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill / courier shipping proof
  • Commercial Invoice (HS code, value, seller and buyer details)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank remittance evidence of overseas payment
  • Amazon FBA warehouse removal and export scan records

UK does not currently mandate real-time fiscal e-invoicing or digital stamping; Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT requires digital record-keeping and API-based quarterly VAT returns for all VAT-registered businesses. Export declarations must be submitted electronically through HMRC's Customs Declaration Service (CDS). Bermuda Customs uses ASYCUDA World for electronic declarations but does not impose an e-invoicing clearance mandate.

HMRC may retrospectively claw back zero-rated VAT where export evidence is not obtained within the statutory period, assessing VAT at 20% plus default interest. Inaccurate returns attract penalties up to 100% of the VAT amount under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007, and fraudulent evasion carries imprisonment up to 7 years and an unlimited fine under VATA 1994. Bermuda has no VAT clawback, but false declarations to the Tax Commissioner or Bermuda Customs can result in deregistration, administrative penalties, and criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) and is freely convertible for both current account (trade in goods and services, royalties, e-commerce sales proceeds) and capital account (equity contributions, loans, dividends) transactions in an offshore exempted company context. Bermuda does not impose exchange controls on non-resident-owned exempted entities. Profit remittance and dividend distribution are permitted without prior BMA approval, provided the company meets liquidity and solvency tests under the Companies Act 1981 and files required statutory documents. There are no regulatory barriers to converting e-commerce sales proceeds from USD, GBP, EUR or other major currencies into BMD or USD; the practical hurdle is banking partner compliance, not exchange control.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no time limit for repatriating trade receipts into Bermuda. An Amazon FBA exporter may retain foreign currency balances in offshore or local bank accounts. The company must nonetheless maintain full books and records to evidence the source of funds for anti-money laundering (AML) and economic substance reporting to the BMA, especially if the entity claims tax residence or seeks a tax residency certificate.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as the principal financial services regulator; with UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) for certain cross-border payment and e-money matters and HM Treasury sanctions oversight for Bermuda as a UK Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for foreign fintech electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Airwallex, Stripe Treasury, or MultiPass, but only as supplemental payment gateways, not as the primary licensed custody bank. Legal constraints: (i) an overseas EMI does not hold a Bermuda deposit-taking licence and therefore cannot offer regulated deposit accounts; (ii) BMA-regulated banks must route all client funds through a licensed bank; (iii) EMI accounts are treated as e-money or payment instrument accounts, not as banking accounts, which may disqualify the entity from obtaining a BMA tax certificate or meeting the economic substance bank account requirement; (iv) the EMI must perform its own KYC/AML and may impose additional transfer restrictions. A Bermuda Exempted Company should maintain a Tier 1 domestic bank account as the master account and use EMIs for collection and disbursement liquidity routing only.

Shareholder capital injections into the Bermuda Exempted Company must be routed through the company's banking account using a purpose of payment that clearly identifies the remitter and the transaction as 'equity contribution' or 'share subscription' under Companies Act 1981. A Certificate of Good Standing from the Bermuda Registrar, a board resolution approving the capital increase, and the shareholders' register must be available for the receiving bank. Amazon FBA-related disbursements to logistics providers, fulfillment centers, and customs brokers should be made via the EMI payment rails to reduce FX cost, with final settlement through the master bank account. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% standard Bermuda Exempted Company income tax rate; 15% CIT under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA) for in-scope multinational enterprise (MNE) groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, effective for fiscal years commencing on or after 1 January 2025

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or liquidation proceeds paid by an exempted company to any shareholder, resident or non-resident

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 750,000,000 (group consolidated revenue threshold aligned with OECD Pillar Two and CITA); Bermuda's transfer pricing framework and mandatory Master File / Local File documentation apply only to in-scope MNE groups; out-of-scope exempted companies have no statutory TP filing requirement, but contemporaneous pricing evidence for intra-group Amazon marketplace and fulfilment arrangements should be retained for ESR and UK CFC defence purposes

Economic substance scope: Fully applicable under the Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2018 and ESR Rules 2019. A Bermuda exempted company operating an Amazon FBA general retail business is characterized as a 'Distribution and Service Centre' where it purchases from related parties or provides services to related parties. The company must demonstrate: (i) management and control exercised in Bermuda through physical board meetings; (ii) adequate qualified full-time employees in Bermuda; (iii) adequate operating expenditure in Bermuda; (iv) available physical premises; and (v) local retention of core income-generating contracts and risk-bearing functions. A purely passive holding company is subject to a reduced substance standard. Annual ESR notification and return filing are mandatory.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Statements under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023

Statutory sanction: On conviction, a fine of up to BMD 250,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 5 years; civil penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid CIT; statutory interest on unpaid amounts; public tax compliance orders and asset freezing orders may be imposed

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil: directors, officers, and the Bermuda tax compliance officer are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties where they knowingly participated; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed during criminal investigation; directors face disqualification from holding Bermuda corporate offices
Shell Company / Economic Substance Regime Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: BMD 50,000 penalty for failure to satisfy the economic substance test; BMD 10,000 for late or defective filing; daily continuing penalties; automatic exchange of information with UK HMRC under the UK-Bermuda TIEA; strike-off and dissolution with company assets (including Amazon payout balances) forfeited to the Crown

Director personal liability: Directors and the corporate service provider may be personally fined and disqualified; the relevant authorities may require civil restitution of benefits obtained through the substance failure; directors trading after strike-off are personally liable for all debts arising
Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Control Failures (AML/CFT)

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years for carrying on an unregistered regulated financial activity; additional monetary penalties by the Bermuda AML supervisory authority; freezing of bank and EMI accounts; confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997

Director personal liability: Directors and the appointed MLRO are personally liable for AML failures; director disqualification, travel bans, and asset confiscation may apply; the corporate veil is pierced where the director directed or consented to the unregistered activity
UK CFC Attribution and Fake Offshore Control

Statutory sanction: HMRC CFC assessment on the attributable profits of the UK parent at the UK corporation tax rate; penalties for deliberate error of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, enhanced to 200% in aggravated cases; statutory interest from the original filing date

Director personal liability: Senior Accounting Officer (SAO) personal penalties of up to £5,000 per accounting period for failure to maintain reasonable tax accounting arrangements; criminal penalties for tax evasion include imprisonment up to 7 years; courts may issue freezing orders against directors' personal assets if the corporate veil is pierced
Registrar Strike-Off and Unauthorized Trading

Statutory sanction: Automatic strike-off and dissolution after the statutory default period, with all company assets (including Amazon settlement proceeds and bank balances) vesting in the Crown; restoration requires a court order with substantial costs, penalties, and HMRC tax clearance

Director personal liability: Directors who act or trade after the company is struck off are personally liable for all contracts and debts incurred; directors' authority over the Amazon merchant account is terminated; restitution orders may require directors to reimburse the Crown for vested assets
