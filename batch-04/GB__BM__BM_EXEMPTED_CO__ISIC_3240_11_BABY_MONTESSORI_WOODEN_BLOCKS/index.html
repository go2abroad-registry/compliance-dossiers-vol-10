Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BMD is fully convertible and pegged 1:1 to USD; both currencies circulate interchangeably by law. Bermuda maintains an open capital account with no restrictions on foreign-currency accounts, no prior-approval requirements for inward or outward remittances, and no limits on dividend, interest, royalty, or trade-payment outflows from an Exempted Company. The profit-repatriation route is frictionless: no withholding tax, no capital-gains tax, and no exit tax apply under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (protection until 31 March 2035). The only practical hurdles are (a) the need to pass the BMA-licensed bank's source-of-funds review, and (b) the obligation to maintain economic substance in Bermuda under the Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2018 for relevant activities. This business—trading of wooden toys with overseas procurement and e-commerce fulfilment—can be structured as a non-relevant activity, preserving full tax-exempt status while retaining a UK parent for operational substance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender, no mandatory conversion, and no repatriation timeline for export proceeds. Export receipts in USD, GBP, or EUR may be retained indefinitely in any BMD/USD/GBP/EUR account, applied directly to trade payables, or swept to the UK parent without BMA consent. For UK-linked clearing efficiency, each inward remittance must state a recognised purpose code (UK BoE code '1208' for goods exports; Bermuda bank standard code '3010' for transfer-to-own-account), and the beneficiary bank will apply standard OFSI/OFAC sanctions screening. Supporting documentation—commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, and proof of Amazon/Stripe settlement—must be retained for five years.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, serving as the central bank, currency board, and foreign-exchange administrator for the Bermudian dollar (BMD); HM Treasury (United Kingdom) retains ultimate monetary sovereignty, while the BMA implements all exchange-control, anti-money-laundering, and sanctions-screening directives. The BMA operates the currency peg at BMD 1:1 to USD and licenses all deposit-taking institutions, fintech payment providers, and money service businesses in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High feasibility as supplemental payment-disposal rails, but not a substitute for a BMA-licensed bank. UK FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, ClearBank) provide rapid GBP/EUR/USD virtual IBANs, API-native Faster Payments and SEPA connectivity, and conversion spreads of 0.10%-0.60% for Amazon Pay and Stripe payout sweeps. These EMIs are legally permissible for receiving marketplace settlements and making operational disbursements, provided the Bermuda Exempted Company maintains its primary settlement account at a BMA-licensed deposit-taking bank; using an EMI as the sole treasury account would contravene the substantive-presence expectations of the corporate-registry and could attract enhanced due diligence from correspondent banks. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All initial and subsequent capital injections must originate from the UK parent's regulated bank account via SWIFT MT103, denominated in GBP or USD, using UK purpose code '1208' (goods-related transfer) or Bermuda standard code '3010' (transfer to own account). The MT103 must populate the full name, address, and account number of both remitter and beneficiary to allow seamless OFSI/OFAC screening; funds must never be routed through crypto venues, unlicensed money-service businesses, or third-party payment aggregators. For injections above USD 100,000, the Bermuda receiving bank will require an attested shareholder-loan agreement or share-subscription letter, a board resolution, and a no-tax-liability confirmation letter from the BMA; after count, funds are immediately available for the purchase of raw beechwood, packaging, and logistics services.Clearing rails for trade settlements: (a) SWIFT MT103 with structured remittance information (ISO 20022) for overseas suppliers of beechwood, non-toxic paint, and packaging materials, referencing the purchase order code BM-BW-2025-XXX; (b) commercial Letters of Credit (UCP 600) for timber purchases above USD 50,000 per transaction, issued through Butterfield's trade-finance desk to mitigate supplier-performance risk; (c) UK Faster Payments (FPS) for GBP-denominated domestic disbursements, Amazon Pay settlement sweeps to the UK operating account, and Stripe payout reconciliation; (d) CHAPS or SEPA Immediate for invoices above GBP 100,000 requiring same-day settlement; and (e) an internal notional-pooling structure across USD/GBP/EUR to offset Amazon EU (EUR) settlements against EU supplier costs and minimise conversion leakage. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a standard Bermuda Exempted Company (no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or tax on distributions). A 15% rate applies only to a Constituent Entity of a multinational group with consolidated global annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more in at least two of the prior four fiscal years, under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to UK residents or any other non-resident shareholder; distributions are paid gross with no Bermuda deduction or tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 30 days following each anniversary of the date of incorporation, which constitutes the statutory filing year-end for Registrar purposes.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within six months after the end of the company's financial period (for a 31 December year-end, due by 30 June of the following year).

Deadline: · Frequency:

The CIT Return must be filed within 15 months after the end of the fiscal year; estimated quarterly instalments are due on the 15th day of the 4th, 6th, 9th, and 12th months of the fiscal year, with a final balancing payment due with the return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda transfer pricing documentation obligation for a standard Exempted Company. Mandatory Master File, Local File, and CbC Report documentation applies only to Constituent Entities of an MNE group with consolidated global annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more (approximately BMD 750 million under the Bermuda Dollar peg of 1:1 to USD).

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) require every registered entity to file an annual ESD. Substantive economic substance applies only to relevant activities: banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centres, IP holding, and pure equity holding. Manufacturing of Montessori wooden blocks (ISIC 3240) is not a relevant activity; the entity must nevertheless maintain its registered office, resident representative, and adequate books and records in Bermuda. If the entity expands into Bermuda-based distribution, IP ownership, or intra-group financing, it becomes subject to the adequacy and core income generating activity (CIGA) test, requiring physical premises, employees, and board decisions in Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent CIT / ESD Filing

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2018: criminal fines up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years; under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 s.45: unlimited corporate fine on the UK parent for failure to prevent facilitation of UK tax evasion; confiscation orders may be made under POCA 2002.

Director personal liability: Directors may be prosecuted personally, disqualified under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, subjected to UK HMRC board accountability measures, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 7 years; civil restitution orders may require directors to pay the full tax evaded with interest.
Shell Company and Insufficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ITA financial penalty up to BMD 250,000 plus daily penalties of BMD 100 per day; the entity may be struck off the Register, its Exempted status revoked, and information passed to HMRC under the UK-Bermuda exchange programme; EU and UK anti-shell measures may deny treaty benefits and impose deemed UK tax of 25% on the UK parent.

Director personal liability: Signatory directors of false declarations face personal fines up to BMD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; directors may be personally assessed for the company's penalties where non-compliance occurred with their consent or was attributable to wilful neglect.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Register Failures

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to BMD 50,000 per offence under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Amendment Act 2017; criminal fines and imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering; the Bermuda Financial Intelligence Agency may freeze assets and revoke the entity's licence.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally liable for each day of default with daily penalties up to BMD 500; ancillary orders include travel bans, disgorgement of profits, and imprisonment up to 10 years for wilful blindness to laundering of sale proceeds.
Unlicensed Local Business in Bermuda

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies Act 1981: penalties up to BMD 100,000, confiscation of local profits, revocation of the Exempted Company certificate, and compulsory conversion to a local company with majority Bermudian ownership.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines up to BMD 50,000 plus a continuing daily fine of BMD 1,000; the court may issue a disqualification order barring each director from managing any Bermuda entity for up to 10 years.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and UK Diverted Profits Tax

Statutory sanction: HMRC transfer pricing adjustments; UK Diverted Profits Tax of 25% on diverted profits; penalties up to 100% of the tax understated under FA 2007 Sch. 24; UK CFC charge under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A attributing the Bermuda profits to the UK parent; interest accrues from the due date.

Director personal liability: HMRC may pursue directors for offshore tax evasion, issue personal liability notices and 'naming and shaming' sanctions, impose departure prohibition orders (travel bans), seek director disqualification for up to 15 years, and attach joint and several liability to directors who signed false accounts or returns.
