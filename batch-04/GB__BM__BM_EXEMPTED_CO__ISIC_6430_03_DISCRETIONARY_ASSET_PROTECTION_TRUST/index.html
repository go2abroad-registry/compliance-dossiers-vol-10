Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Revenue Authority (BRA) and Office of the Tax Commissioner within the Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda — official portal: www.gov.bm; entity registry: Registrar of Companies, Bermuda Companies Registry — eServices: www.gov.bm/companies-registry

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned structure first causes the incorporation of a Bermuda exempted company under the Companies Act 1981, obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation and company registration number from the Registrar of Companies. Immediately after incorporation, the company applies to the Minister of Finance for a Tax Assurance Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, securing statutory exemption from any future Bermuda income tax, capital gains tax, profits tax, dividend tax, and withholding tax until 31 March 2035. Concurrently, where the company administers third-party trusts for remuneration, it must obtain a class B or class E trust business licence from the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Trusts (Regulation of Trust Business) Act 2001. No general corporate TIN or VAT ID is issued because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax and no value-added tax; payroll-tax account registration under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 applies only if the entity employs Bermuda-resident staff, and customs ASYCUDA World importer registration applies only for physical imports of dutiable goods into Bermuda.

Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST. Indirect taxation in Bermuda comprises only customs duty on imported tangible goods under the Customs Tariff Act 1968 and the employer-paid payroll tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995; neither levy reaches fiduciary trust administration fees or offshore financial services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT/GST regime or registration threshold. Bermuda Customs registration through the ASYCUDA World declaration system becomes mandatory only upon the first importation of dutiable physical goods; discretionary trust administration and cross-border fiduciary services remain entirely outside the scope of Bermudian indirect taxation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no VAT or GST on exports or on offshore services; a Bermuda exempted company's fiduciary and asset-protection-trust fees are outside the scope of any indirect tax. Consequently no substantive zero-rating claim or VAT-refund procedure is available or required. The operative exemption derives from the Minister's Tax Assurance Certificate, which guarantees that no Bermudian taxation will apply to the company's worldwide income, gains, or distributions until 31 March 2035.

  • Customs Export Declaration via Bermuda Customs ASYCUDA World (required only for physical re-exports of tangible assets from Bermuda customs territory)
  • Certificate of Incorporation and the Minister of Finance's Tax Assurance Certificate (evidencing exempted tax status for banks, counterparties, and regulators)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the Bermuda banker, or equivalent outgoing wire instructions, evidencing the source and application of settled trust funds
  • BMA trust business licence certificate under the Trusts (Regulation of Trust Business) Act 2001 together with the current anti-money-laundering and countering-the-financing-of-terrorism beneficial-ownership file maintained under the AML/ATF Code 2023

No e-invoicing, digital stamping, or real-time fiscal clearance mandate applies to fiduciary services or trust administration in Bermuda. The only digital clearance mechanism is the UNCTAD ASYCUDA World platform used by Bermuda Customs for import/export declaration and cargo clearance. Corporate services filings and BMA regulatory returns are submitted electronically through the Registrar and BMA portals, but no tax-invoice archiving or national fiscal-clearance API is prescribed.

Because the entity carries no VAT liability, clawback exposure arises under statutory and regulatory regimes rather than indirect-tax rules: the Exchange Control Act 1972 penalises unauthorised foreign-exchange transactions with fines and imprisonment; the AML/ATF Code 2023 imposes civil monetary penalties of up to BMD 1,000,000 per contravention; and the BMA may suspend or revoke the trust licence under the Trusts (Regulation of Trust Business) Act 2001. A knowingly false or misleading representation in the Tax Assurance Certificate application invalidates the exemption with retrospective effect, exposing the entity to any newly enacted Bermudian tax, restitutionary claims from beneficiaries or settlors, disqualification of directors, and cancellation of the exempted company status by the Registrar.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a de jure full convertibility regime. Current-account and capital-account transactions are unrestricted for exempted companies designated as 'non-resident' under the Schedule to the Exchange Control Act 1972. The BMD is pegged at strict parity to USD (1 BMD = 1 USD) via a currency board footprint; US dollar notes and funds are universally accepted. Profit remittance: no statutory withholding taxes, capital gains taxes, or income taxes apply to a Bermuda Exempted Company, and dividends or capital distributions to UK or foreign beneficiaries require no BMA exchange-control approval. Exempted companies may hold and settle freely in USD, GBP, EUR, or any convertible currency without BMA conversion licensing. The only residual hurdle is Economic Substance Act 2018 filing compliance; ISIC 6430 fiduciary trust administration is not a relevant activity, thus no enhanced substance test applies beyond the statutory trust business licence gate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender or mandatory conversion obligation exists for Bermuda Exempted Companies. The Exchange Control Act 1972 imposes a 0% surrender ratio and no statutory conversion calendar for foreign-currency export receipts or fee income earned by non-resident-designated exempted companies. All USD, GBP, EUR, and other foreign currency receivables may be retained indefinitely in offshore or on-island accounts. The only administrative duty is accurate statistical reporting of cross-border flows on the bank's annual BMA return; BMD accounts are operationally posted at parity with USD but this reflects the fixed peg, not a surrender rule.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, acting as the integrated financial services regulator, currency board, and foreign exchange control authority for the Bermudian Dollar (BMD).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

MEDIUM-LOW viability. Bermuda does not maintain a standalone EMI or payment-institution licensing category under the Banking Act 1969; any person or entity accepting fiduciary client funds must route them through a BMA-licensed bank or through a foreign bank expressly classified as 'approved' by the BMA under the Trusts (Regulation of Trust Business) Act 2001. Bermuda does not maintain a standalone EMI or payment-institution licensing category under the Banking Act 1969; any person or entity accepting fiduciary client funds must route them through a BMA-licensed bank or through a foreign bank expressly classified as 'approved' by the BMA under the Trusts (Regulation of Trust Business) Act 2001. Leading UK FCA-authorized EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Multitude) generally decline Bermuda-registered high-risk trust vehicles due to correspondent-bank de-risking, particularly where discretionary asset-protection structures are involved. Feasibility exists only if: (i) the EMI has a live Bermuda correspondent banking relationship or a segregated omnibus account at a BMA-licensed bank; (ii) the trust business licence is fully issued; and (iii) the EMI accepts the BMA Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008 compliance framework as the governing standard. Legal constraint: client money for the trust must not be commingled with operating funds; an external EMI wallet cannot serve as the primary fiduciary clearing rail.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File or Master File threshold is prescribed under Bermudian law because there is no CIT base. Nevertheless, if the exempted company engages in cross-border intra-group transactions and annual turnover exceeds BMD 10,000,000, best-practice requires contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation consistent with OECD TP Guidelines to resist a UK HM Revenue & Customs challenge and to satisfy BERMUDA ESR record-keeping obligations.

Economic substance scope: The Income and Economic Substance Act 2017 (ESR) is fully applicable. A Bermuda Exempted Company administering trusts and providing fiduciary services is conducting a relevant activity and must maintain, on a rolling 12-month basis: (i) board meetings held in Bermuda; (ii) adequate physical premises in Bermuda; (iii) full-time employees in Bermuda in proportion to the fiduciary activity; and (iv) real economic expenditure in Bermuda. The Commissioner of the BMA verifies substance annually; a purely passive asset-protection trust with no active management is presumed to fail ESR unless it is tax resident in the UK and evidence of UK substance is provided.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure (Income and Economic Substance Act 2017)

Statutory sanction: First-instance penalty of BMD 250,000 and daily default fine of BMD 25,000; the BMA may publish the company's name, strike the company off the Register, and revoke any trust license held under the Trust (Regulation of Business) Act 2001.

Director personal liability: Each director may be personally fined up to BMD 100,000 and imprisoned for up to 1 year for knowingly causing the company to default on ESR filings or substance requirements; in practice the Registrar also obtains a disqualification order barring the director from acting in any company in Bermuda for 5 years.
Unlicensed Fiduciary / Trust Business (AML and BMA Licensing)

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction: fine not exceeding BMD 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; for unlicensed trust business alone, a fine of BMD 50,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years, plus forfeiture of the trust assets under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2007.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for any restitution order equal to the value of trust assets dissipated; the court may issue a travel ban and frozen assets order; for knowingly facilitating a shell charity or opaque asset-protection scheme, the director faces up to 5 years' imprisonment and joint and several civil liability with the company and the trust.
Sham Trust and Tax Evasion (UK and Bermuda Exchange of Information)

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda law, the Supreme Court may declare the trust void and impose confiscation orders up to BMD 2,000,000; under UK law, HM Revenue & Customs may assess the full undisclosed income and gains at up to 200% of potential lost revenue, plus penalties under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 for facilitation of tax evasion; a separate BMA penalty for supplying false ESR information is up to BMD 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who are UK residents are exposed to UK criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion of income tax (imprisonment up to 7 years), failure to notify liability (up to 2 years), and fraudulent evasion of VAT (up to 7 years); civil liability includes restitution for the full market value of the trust portfolio and lifetime credit-action restrictions; the Bermudian court will pierce the corporate veil, imposing personal liability on the trustee-directors, their professional agent, and any legal counsel who structured the sham arrangement.
