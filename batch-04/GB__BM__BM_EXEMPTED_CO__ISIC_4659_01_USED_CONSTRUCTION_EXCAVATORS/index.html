Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Government of Bermuda, online portal: https://www.gov.bm/ (taxation section); entity incorporation is administered by the Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance.

E-government filing portal

Foreign owners incorporate a Bermuda Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981 by filing a Memorandum and Articles of Association with the Registrar of Companies. There is no corporate income tax or VAT registration requirement for an exempted company. The company obtains a Certificate of Incorporation, which serves as its primary statutory identifier. For payroll tax purposes, an employer must register with the Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number; for customs importation, the company must register with Bermuda Customs as an importer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs duties on imported goods, a Tourism Tax on accommodation and certain services, and payroll tax (levied on remuneration).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Bermuda does not operate a VAT/GST registration threshold. Customs registration is required for importers, and payroll tax registration is mandatory from the date an employer first pays qualifying remuneration, with no minimum threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable; Bermuda does not impose VAT or GST, and therefore has no export zero-rating regime. The export of used construction excavators from Bermuda is generally outside the scope of indirect taxation. Bermuda imposes no export duty on movable capital equipment, and the transaction will not give rise to input VAT credits or refunds.

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate is in force for tax purposes in Bermuda. Bermuda Customs operates an electronic single administrative document system for import/export declarations, but this is a border-control mechanism, not a fiscal clearance of invoices.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains a freely convertible currency regime with no exchange controls or rationing. The Bermuda dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar and is issued by the BMA; both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible. An Exempted Company may freely receive capital injections, retain export proceeds in any currency, and remit profits as dividends, interest, or management fees without prior BMA approval. The only practical hurdles to profit remittance are compliance with the Economic Substance Act 2018, the International Business Companies (Companies and Corporate Bodies) Act, and Bermuda's anti-money laundering (AML) requirements, which mandate substance, good standing, and adequate recordkeeping but do not impose convertibility or repatriation thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or fixed repatriation timeline applies under Bermuda law. Export receipts for used construction machinery may be held in BMD or any foreign currency in local or international bank accounts, and disbursed according to the company's commercial needs. The sole administrative touchpoint is the annual filing with the BMA by the registered agent; this return does not require conversion or surrender of any foreign currency receipts.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, acting as the central bank and financial services regulator for the British Overseas Territory of Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Bermuda does not operate a domestic e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime; digital payment services are generally regulated as digital asset businesses under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018 or, for fund storage and transfer, require a bank licence. Consequently, an Exempted Company may utilise offshore EMIs licensed in the UK or EU/EEA (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business) for low-value operational payments, provided the EMI does not maintain a physical or regulated Bermuda nexus and the payment is not denominated in BMD. Feasibility is rated Medium for ancillary treasury operations, but the core settlement of used hydraulic earthmover transactions must remain on the books of a Bermuda or international bank because documentary credits, large FX hedges, and commercial letters of credit are not reliably supported by EMIs. The BMA does not prohibit the opening of offshore EMI accounts by a Bermuda Exempted Company, but the company must demonstrate that no regulated digital-asset activity is carried on in or from Bermuda.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections into the Bermuda Exempted Company must be routed via SWIFT MT103 or equivalent TT from the parent or lender's bank to the company's Bermuda account. The wire must include the beneficiary account title exactly matching the bank mandate and reference the commercial purpose (e.g., 'Capital Contribution - Exempted Company - BMD/USD'). For receipts over USD 100,000, the bank may request a board resolution, source-of-funds letter, and UBO attestation before crediting. There is no BMA approval requirement; however, the payment should be classified under the balance-of-payments code 'Direct Investment' where the bank asks for a purpose category, and all supporting documentation (loan agreement, subscription agreement, or share issuance resolution) must be retained for the BMA's AML compliance review.For settling supplier invoices, auction costs, and freight in the used earthmover wholesale business, the company should use the following primary rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 wires for payments at or under USD 50,000, supported by commercial invoice and shipping documentation; (ii) commercial letters of credit (sight or usance) for high-value purchases, issued from the Bermuda bank to the seller's bank, with the credit standing against a full set of bills of lading, commercial invoice, insurance certificate, and independent inspection certificate; (iii) documentary collections (D/P or D/A) for lower-risk, pre-established suppliers; and (iv) Bank Payment Obligation (BPO) or supply-chain finance where the bank offers such digitized clearing. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0.00% for a Bermuda Exempted Company trading exclusively outside Bermuda under an Exemption Certificate granted pursuant to the Companies Act 1981, s.129B; the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (OECD Pillar Two) imposes a 15% domestic top-up tax on in-scope MNE groups, effective for financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, only where consolidated group revenue reaches the EUR 750 million threshold.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by an Exempted Company, and no capital gains or distribution taxes apply to outbound dividends paid to non-resident shareholders; the Exemption Certificate protects the entire exempted period.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda has no statutory Local File or Master File regime for ordinary trading entities. Mandatory Country-by-Country Report (CbCR) notification and filing arise under the Bermuda Tax Information Exchange (CBC Reporting) Regulations 2016 when the group's Ultimate Parent Entity has consolidated annual revenue of at least US$750,000,000 (BMD 750,000,000 at the 1:1 peg). Below that threshold no Bermuda Local File or Master File is locally compulsory, but contemporaneous OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation should be maintained to defend UK CTA 2010 Part 4 enquiries and Bermuda ESR substance reviews.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 (as amended) apply to relevant activities. Wholesale remarketing of secondhand hydraulic earthmovers and rollers (ISIC 4659) constitutes a 'distribution and service centre' and, where in-house financing is extended to buyers, may engage 'financing or leasing'. To satisfy the doctrine, the Exempted Company must prove: (1) management and direction from Bermuda through board meetings held there; (2) adequate physical premises and qualified employees in Bermuda; (3) adequate annual operating expenditure in Bermuda; and (4) performance of core income-generating activities in Bermuda (sourcing, logistics control, negotiation, and invoicing). Failure triggers civil penalties, automatic exchange of information with the UK and EU authorities, and possible revocation of the 0% exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Statements to the Tax Commissioner

Statutory sanction: Civil restitution of understated tax plus statutory interest; penalty surcharge of up to the full amount of tax evaded; criminal fines up to BMD 25,000 per specification; terms of imprisonment up to 5 years under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 and the Criminal Code; suspension or cancellation of the Exemption Certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers with knowledge or consent are jointly and severally liable for the tax and civil restitution; courts may order asset freezes and travel bans pending prosecution; imprisonment applies to directors who orchestrated false filings; the corporate veil is pierced where the exempted company is used as a sham vehicle.
Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018, a civil financial penalty of up to BMD 250,000 for failing the economic substance test, an additional penalty of up to BMD 10,000 for late or failed ESR filings or information requests, remedial directions from the Regulatory Authority, potential strike-off by the Registrar, and automatic exchange of information with UK/EU authorities; where group revenue exceeds EUR 750 million, the entity becomes chargeable to the 15% CIT Act 2023 top-up tax for the relevant period.

Director personal liability: Directors who fail to procure ESR compliance may be disqualified and held personally accountable for civil restitution and penalties; a beneficial owner who strips the shell of all economic attributes forfeits limited liability protection; UK-resident directors face HMRC information notices, cross-border travel bans, and in flagrant cases extradition proceedings and imprisonment.
Money Laundering / Trade-Based Financial Crime (AML)

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Act 1997: up to 14 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines for money laundering; regulatory penalties under the AML/ATF Code of up to BMD 500,000 for the entity and its compliance officer; forfeiture of the excavator inventory, bank balances, and trade documents; debarment from Wire Transfer and SWIFT LC payment rails.

Director personal liability: Senior officers are criminally liable on a consent-and-connivance basis; directors face civil restitution, worldwide freezing orders, asset tracing through UK and EU courts, travel bans, and arrest or extradition protocols; the corporate veil is pierced when the exempted company is a façade for the directors' laundering arrangements.
