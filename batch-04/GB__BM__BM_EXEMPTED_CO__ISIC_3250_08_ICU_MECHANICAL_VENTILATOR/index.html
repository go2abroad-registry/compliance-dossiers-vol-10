Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner (OTC), Ministry of Finance, Bermuda – administrative portal www.gov.bm (payroll tax, land tax, customs duty); Bermuda Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance – incorporation, annual exempted company fees, and Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection (EUTP) permits, filing via licensed Bermuda corporate service providers. No Bermuda corporate income tax authority exists; HMRC (UK) is relevant only where the company undertakes voluntary UK VAT registration or maintains central management and control in the UK.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Exempted Company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 by a licensed Bermuda corporate service provider which files the certificate of incorporation, non-resident status declaration, and registered office details with the Registrar of Companies. There is no Bermuda corporate TIN because no income tax exists; the company obtains a Bermuda company file number, pays the annual exempted company fee, and may apply for an EUTP tax-assurance certificate and consent to be treated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act 1972. If Bermuda-resident employees are hired, the employer must register with the OTC within 14 days under the Payroll Tax Act 1995; to recover UK input VAT on UK-sourced ventilator components, the company also submits a voluntary UK VAT registration (VATA 1994 Sch 1 para 2).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – a Bermuda Exempted Company is non-resident for Bermuda exchange-control purposes and is exempt from any Bermuda income, capital, or withholding tax; an EUTP certificate guarantees that any future Bermuda tax legislation will not apply to the company until 31 March 2035, with later protection for legacy certificates. The company is not subject to UK corporation tax unless centrally managed and controlled in the UK; pure manufacturing of ICU ventilators is not a relevant activity under the Economic Substance (International Business Companies) Amendment Act 2019, so no Bermuda economic substance filing is required for this manufacturing scope.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box election. Available elections: (i) apply for an EUTP tax-assurance certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (ii) apply for non-resident status consent under the Exchange Control Act 1972; (iii) apply for voluntary registration for UK VAT under VATA 1994 Sch 1 para 2 despite the absence of a UK establishment; (iv) elect an optional payroll-tax withholding basis where Bermuda employees exist. Hybrid-mismatch elections are inapplicable because Bermuda levies no income tax; if the company becomes UK tax-resident, UK CTA 2009 Part 6A hybrid-mismatch rules would characterise it as opaque where Bermuda law does not recognise a corresponding tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None in Bermuda – no VAT, GST, or general sales tax; Bermuda instead imposes customs import duty on goods entering Bermuda, payroll tax on Bermuda-source remuneration, and land tax on Bermuda property. UK VAT applies only if the entity voluntarily registers for UK VAT to recover input VAT on UK-sourced components and manufacturing costs; otherwise, UK VAT embedded in purchases is an irrecoverable cost of the export.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – Bermuda has no VAT/GST and thus no turnover threshold for indirect-tax registration; every importer of goods into Bermuda must account for customs import duty from the first consignment, and every Bermuda employer must register for payroll tax within 14 days regardless of size. For optional UK VAT registration, the £90,000 statutory threshold is irrelevant because a non-established person is registrable if it makes or intends to make taxable supplies in the UK (VATA 1994 Sch 1 para 2).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT, so no local zero-rating or refund route exists. Where the company voluntarily holds a UK VAT registration and exports ICU mechanical ventilators permanently from the UK to an overseas customer for use outside the UK, the export is zero-rated under VATA 1994 Sch 8 Group 1 (goods), provided the supplier holds official and commercial evidence of export no later than three months after the end of the prescribed accounting period of supply (HMRC Notice 720). The ventilators must be permanently exported to a non-UK recipient, comply with MHRA/CE conformity for the destination market, and no embargoed or dual-use end-user indicators may appear in the due-diligence record.

  • UK Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document C88 / CDS output) with Master Reference Number (MRN)
  • Commercial Invoice showing ventilator make, model, serial numbers, HS 9019.20, Incoterms, and export value
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from the UK
  • Packing List and Certificate of Origin confirming UK/GB originating goods for the TCA or third-country destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing collection of the Government LC proceeds within the 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation window

None in Bermuda – no statutory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital tax stamping regime applies to Bermudian exempted companies or to GB-origin ventilator exports. If the company holds a UK VAT registration, HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires quarterly digital VAT returns via compatible software, but there is no mandatory clearance of e-invoices before issue; Government LC and Swift Wire settlements use standard SWIFT MT103 / ISO 20022 messages without fiscal stamping.

A UK-registered exporter that fails to obtain adequate export evidence before the three-month rule faces HMRC VAT assessment with statutory interest and an inaccuracy penalty under Finance Act 2007 Sch 24 of up to 100% of the VAT. Deliberate fraudulent zero-rating claims additionally trigger criminal liability under VATA 1994 s. 72, fines up to £4,000 plus twice the VAT, and retrospective assessment for up to 20 years, or 4 years for careless errors. Bermuda customs fraud under the Customs Act 1948 renders exported goods liable to forfeiture with fines up to three times their value and revocation of any duty reliefs; the Bermudian EUTP tax guarantee cannot be clawed back because the manufacture and cross-border sale occur outside Bermuda's tax net.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions in respect of exempted companies, subject to the Exchange Control Act 1972 and subsidiary regulations. The BMD is freely convertible into USD at the fixed peg; however, for bona fide Bermudian-dollar convertibility, payment systems settle in BMD, with USD correspondence widely accepted. Profit remittance to foreign shareholders is unrestricted for exempted companies and does not require BMA prior approval provided the company is not a resident of Bermuda for exchange control purposes; however, proceeds from domestic (Bermuda-source) activities may have specified reporting duties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda does not impose statutory export proceeds surrender requirements or mandatory conversion ratios. Exempted companies may retain export receipts in any foreign currency in external accounts. Limited reporting obligations arise only under anti-money laundering regulations requiring records of cross-border wire transfers, and if the company transacts in BMD with non-residents, BMA regulations may require bank notifications. No mandatory repatriation or conversion to BMD is applicable for medical device export revenues.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) — the integrated financial services regulator and monetary authority of Bermuda; there is no separate central bank, and BMA operates the currency board arrangements with the Bermudian dollar pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium. Offshore digital EMI providers registered under the BMA's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 or Electronic Money Institutions (EMI) passported from UK/EU can provide ancillary payment channels, but direct processing of Government LC instruments is not viable. Legal constraints include: (1) EMIs cannot hold Government LC proceeds on behalf of the exempted company without a full deposit-taking licence; (2) BMA AML/CFT regulations require the EMI to be regulated in Bermuda or have a nexus; (3) KYC requirements for government procurement contracts require bank-graded audit trails; hence EMIs are limited to secondary trade settlement and not primary bank rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocols: (1) inbound equity injection or shareholder loan must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's bank to the Bermuda exempted company's USD account at Butterfield or HSBC, using purpose code 'S1103 – Disbursement of Investment Equity' (IMF BPM6 standard) or BIC-coded instruction 'INVEST/4100'; (2) funds representing export advance payments should be tagged with purpose code 'S1201 – Trade Credit' and referenced to the sale contract; (3) all wires must include the entity LEI and reference to the commercial invoice or share subscription agreement; (4) verification by BMA is not required for fund inflows under the capital account, but the bank will apply falls below 10,000 BMD threshold; for amounts above 100,000 BMD, a declaration of source of funds and an updated board resolution must be on file. Government LC drawdowns are settled directly onto the designated operational account, with presentation documents (commercial invoice, bill of lading, inspection certificate) validated through the correspondent network.Clearing rails for trade settlements: (1) SWIFT MT103 for non-LC reimbursements and advance vendor payments in USD, EUR, or GBP; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) issued by the end-buyer (e.g., Ministry of Health) are confirmed by a top-tier correspondent (e.g., Citi, HSBC) and negotiated through the Bermuda bank; (3) Government LC disbursements are cleared via the receiving bank's US or UK correspondent, using TARGET2 for EUR and CHIPS/Fedwire for USD; (4) outbound disbursements for raw material purchases and supplier payments use the same MT103 protocol with purpose codes referencing import of commodities; (5) Bermuda's Automated Clearing House (ACH) is not used for cross-border trade; all disbursements are routed through the banking system's SWIFT network. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, applicable only to Bermuda resident/incorporated entities that are members of an in-scope multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million; all other exempted companies remain subject to Bermuda's traditional zero-corporate-income-tax regime.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda does not impose any withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company, irrespective of shareholder residency.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The CIT return must be filed within 15 months after the end of the company's fiscal year; for a 31 December year-end, the statutory due date is 31 March of the second following year. Provisional CIT instalments are due quarterly during the tax year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 15,000,000 (Bermuda Dollar pegged 1:1 to USD) annual related-party turnover for mandatory Local File and Master File under the CIT Act Transfer Pricing Rules; below this, a simplified contemporaneous TP record is required if intercompany management fees or financing transactions exceed BMD 2,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to exempted companies conducting relevant activities: for an ICU ventilator manufacturer, the core manufacturing activity is not a relevant activity, but a 'Headquarters' relevant activity arises if the Bermuda entity directs or controls a group business outside Bermuda. If relevant activity exists, the entity must file an annual economic substance declaration and maintain substantive activities, with board meetings and CIGA management in Bermuda. Pure holding exemptions apply only to a non-resident parentholding companies and not to an operating manufacturing entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent CIT Return Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution for tax evasion under Bermuda's Criminal Code Act 1907 and the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; monetary penalties of up to 250% of the tax evaded, confiscation of assets, and a fine of BMD 250,000; directors and officers face imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years.

Director personal liability: Bermuda courts will pierce the corporate veil for fraud, fraudulent trading, and shadow-director deceptions. Directors may be personally liable for the full amount of unpaid tax plus restitution to creditors, and may be the subject of travel bans, worldwide freezing orders, and surrender of passports. A director who knowingly signs a false CIT return is personally indictable for perjury and can be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Amendment Regulations 2019, the Registrar may impose a penalty of up to BMD 250,000 plus BMD 10,000 per day for continuing non-compliance; the company may be struck off and its assets forfeited; joint and several fines apply to each director.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who allow the company to operate as a shell face personal disqualification from acting as a director in Bermuda for up to 15 years, personal daily fines, and criminal liability for making false statements to the Registry; the court may order personal contribution toward unpaid duties and penalties.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime Act and AML Regulations, the penalty is a fine of up to BMD 500,000 per contravention and up to 10% of the transaction value for negligent reporting; criminal confiscation can reach 100% of the traced proceeds. The Bermuda Revenue Authority can also impose a civil monetary penalty of BMD 250,000 per year of unreported beneficial ownership.

Director personal liability: Directors with culpable knowledge of undisclosed beneficial ownership or suspicious government-LC payments are liable to personal prosecution, imprisonment for up to 14 years, and a court-ordered travel ban pending extradition; the corporate veil is pierced where the company is used as a conduit for money laundering.
Transfer Pricing Overstatement of Costs / International Arbitrage

Statutory sanction: Under CIT Act transfer pricing rules, the Bermuda Revenue Authority may make an upward adjustment of taxable income and impose a 50% penalty on the resulting under-declared tax, in addition to interest of 12% per annum; the UK HMRC can also apply UK transfer pricing penalties and counteraction under UK CTA 2010.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve a transfer pricing scheme without a functional analysis are deemed to have authorised reckless tax avoidance; they may be jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties, and face UK directors' disqualification proceedings and Bermuda personal liability for breach of fiduciary duty.
