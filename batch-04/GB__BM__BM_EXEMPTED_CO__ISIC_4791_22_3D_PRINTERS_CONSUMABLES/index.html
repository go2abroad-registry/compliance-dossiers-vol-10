Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), UK Government; online portal: HMRC Online Services / Government Gateway (gov.uk)

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is not resident for UK tax purposes absent central management and control or a UK permanent establishment. For UK VAT purposes, a non-established taxable person making taxable supplies in the UK must register for UK VAT using Form VAT1 or the online VAT registration service, providing incorporation certificate, director and beneficial owner details, expected UK taxable turnover, and, where applicable, a UK VAT representative or agent. For direct tax, a permanent establishment triggers UK corporation tax registration via the Corporation Tax online service (Form CT41G) if a chargeable presence arises. As a Bermudian exempted company, no UK Companies House incorporation is required; only the requisite registrations with HMRC for any UK tax obligations apply.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Bermuda Exempted Company is not resident for UK tax purposes absent central management and control or a UK permanent establishment. For UK VAT purposes, a non-established taxable person making taxable supplies in the UK must register for UK VAT using Form VAT1 or the online VAT registration service, providing incorporation certificate, director and beneficial owner details, expected UK taxable turnover, and, where applicable, a UK VAT representative or agent. For direct tax, a permanent establishment triggers UK corporation tax registration via the Corporation Tax online service (Form CT41G) if a chargeable presence arises. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No UK check-the-box classification applies to a foreign incorporated entity because the UK uses a territorial and residence-based corporate tax system rather than an entity-level election regime. If a UK permanent establishment arises, the Bermudian exempted company may apply the OECD Model PE attribution rules, but no statutory flow-through election exists. UK group relief elections may be available only if the company becomes UK tax resident or is within a UK group. No hybrid mismatch elections are generally available; UK hybrid mismatch rules under Part 6A TIOPA impose disallowance rather than a statutory election, except for optional adjustments under the hybrid mismatch legislation if a timing mismatch can be eliminated.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 taxable turnover threshold for UK-established businesses. Non-established businesses with no UK establishment have no threshold and are compulsorily registrable when they make any UK taxable supply; suppliers of goods sold online to UK consumers may, subject to post-Brexit distance selling rules, be required to register when they make supplies of goods in the UK exceeding the GB-specific distance sales threshold, where applicable under UK VAT law. Import VAT also applies at the point of customs clearance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A zero-rated export supply applies where goods are exported to a destination outside the UK, including to an overseas customer, and the supplier obtains and retains valid commercial evidence of export within the required time limits (generally 3 months for goods removed from the UK). Supplies of goods dispatched or transported by the supplier, the customer, or an agent to a non-UK destination qualify under Item 1(a), Group 7, Schedule 8 VAT Act 1994. Services sold from the UK to an overseas business customer are generally outside the scope of UK VAT under the general B2B reverse charge rules, rather than zero-rated. A Bermudian exempted company without UK establishment can register for VAT to reclaim input tax incurred in the UK solely in support of taxable outward supplies, but such repayment is conditional upon the supplies being taxable or outside the scope with full credit. Goods subject to VAT must leave the UK in the course of a transaction and be exported within statutory time limits. B2C remote services, including electronically supplied services to UK consumers, are subject to the place-of-supply rule requiring the supplier to charge UK VAT if the consumer is in the UK.

The UK generally does not impose a mandatory e-invoicing clearance mandate. HMRC operates an HMRC VAT online account system, but invoicing must follow the mandatory invoice content requirements of the VAT Regulations 1995. Businesses must submit VAT returns via MTD (Making Tax Digital) compatible software; all VAT-registered businesses must file electronically in XML using HMRC’s Application Programming Interface (API) via MTD, with digital links maintained between functional software. There is no pre-clearance digital stamping by HMRC. Import VAT is paid or deferred at customs release. Where goods are sold through online marketplaces, the marketplace may be liable for VAT under UK section 95/94 VATA 1994 and the Online Marketplace Rules, with HMRC data-sharing from payment providers. Post-Brexit rules require customs declarations and e-commerce import VAT collection at point of sale for consignments up to GBP 135.

HMRC may conduct a VAT verification inspection and require repayment of VAT zero-rated or input tax reclaimed if the exporter cannot produce sufficient commercial export documentation, including where the export evidence does not identify the goods, the supplier, the importer, or the route. Where the intended export does not occur, the VAT becomes accountable with default interest. Standard penalties for inaccuracies in VAT returns or MTD returns are up to 30% of the potential lost revenue for prompted disclosure, 70% for unprompted disclosure, and 100% for deliberate and concealed errors. Civil evasion penalties may rise to 100% of the VAT due plus penalties up to 100% of the lost revenue. Failure to register when required carries a penalty based on the unpaid VAT, up to 100% for deliberate concealment. Criminal prosecution may apply under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 and the Fraud Act 2006 for dishonest evasion, including fraudulent attempts to claim export zero-rating. Where a Bermudian company intentionally misdeclares, HMRC may issue a cross-border discovery assessment under FA 2009 s.77 or s.78, subject to extended time limits where offshore assets or non-disclosure exist. HMRC also may apply special penalties for MTD failures of GBP 5 per day plus additional daily penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD), and USD circulates interchangeably. Bermuda maintains a fully liberalised current and capital account for non-resident controlled exempted companies. There are no exchange controls, no requirement for BMA approval on dividend distribution, capital repatriation, or intercompany loan repayment to non-residents, and no impediment to consolidating USD, GBP, or Euro treasury positions. Resident-entity restrictions under the Exchange Control Act 1972 do not apply to Bermuda Exempted Companies classified as 'non-resident' for exchange control purposes, provided ownership is held by non-residents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion requirements apply. Bermuda imposes no obligation to repatriate export receivables, no timeline for converting foreign currency receipts into BMD/USD, and no restriction on retaining hard currency proceeds in offshore operational or escrow accounts. Trade settlement and treasury currency selection (predominantly USD) are entirely at the discretion of the company.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), in coordination with the Ministry of Finance under the Exchange Control Act 1972 (as amended) and the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2018.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

UK/EU-regulated EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) are rated Medium for auxiliary settlement acceleration with the UK parent entity, but Low as a substitute for the Bermuda treasury anchor. The BMA does not license or supervise foreign digital EMIs; maintaining the Bermuda Exempted Company's primary operational or escrow account at an unregulated-in-Bermuda EMI would raise AML/regulatory scrutiny and obstacle to bank attestation. Recommended architecture: Butterfield/HSBC Bermuda as the authorised treasury anchor, with a UK FCA-regulated EMI solely for the UK parent's payout acceleration (Stripe/Shopify settlement bridging) and downstream intercompany funding to the Bermuda account.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general rate for out-of-scope Bermuda Exempted Companies; 15% flat rate under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (CITA) for constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million

Section A - Entity Status Name, registration number, accounting period start and end dates, and whether the entity is resident in Bermuda for CIT purposes. For an out-of-scope exempted company, confirm non-CITA-resident status and exclude the entity from CITA-related fields. Section C - Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) Narrative description of decision-taking, warehouse and logistics management, supplier negotiations, and custody of IT and payment infrastructure carried on in Bermuda. If no relevant activity is declared, state 'None'; if the Registrar challenges the classification, evidence of Bermudian management is required. Failure to file by the due date: BMD 10,000 plus BMD 2,000 per day of continued default. Substantive failure to satisfy the economic substance test: compliance penalty of BMD 250,000 per breach and a continuing penalty of BMD 50,000 per day, plus publication of the default and potential exchange of information with HM Revenue and Customs.
Section E - Place of Effective Management (POEM) Jurisdiction where board meetings are held, minutes are adopted, and strategic commercial decisions are taken. Schedule 1 - Group Status Ultimate parent entity, consolidated group revenue, and confirmation of whether the EUR 750 million threshold is met. If the UK parent's group revenue is below the threshold, file a nil declaration or an out-of-scope letter, retaining group audited accounts as evidence. In-scope non-compliance is subject to the CITA 2023 and Tax Administration Act 2023 enforcement provisions: fixed penalties for late registration and filing, continuing daily default assessments, statutory interest on unpaid tax, and court-ordered recovery; deliberate concealment exposes directors to criminal sanctions.
Schedule 3 - Loss Utilisation Deferred tax asset recognition and carryforward of tested losses. Bermuda recognises loss carryforwards up to five years for CITA purposes; include the reconciliation only for in-scope entities. In-scope non-compliance is subject to the CITA 2023 and Tax Administration Act 2023 enforcement provisions: fixed penalties for late registration and filing, continuing daily default assessments, statutory interest on unpaid tax, and court-ordered recovery; deliberate concealment exposes directors to criminal sanctions.
