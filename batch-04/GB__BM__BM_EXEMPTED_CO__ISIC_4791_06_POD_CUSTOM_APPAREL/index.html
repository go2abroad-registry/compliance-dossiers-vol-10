Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Bermuda Exempted Company, the statutory identifier is the Bermuda Company Registration Number issued by the Registrar of Companies upon incorporation. Bermuda does not impose corporation income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT, so no general TIN or VAT ID is issued in the ordinary course. If the exempted company employs individuals resident or working in Bermuda, it must separately register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and social insurance contribution purposes and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number. No other national tax identification is required for a non-resident owned exempted company that conducts no Bermudian business.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Companies are not subject to Bermuda income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, or indirect value-added tax. Their statutory impost is limited to annual government fees, and, where applicable, Bermuda payroll tax and social insurance contributions in respect of Bermuda-based employees.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election mechanism. An exempted company may elect to operate as 'non-resident' for exchange-control purposes. Additionally, a Bermudian entity engaged in relevant activities under the Economic Substance Act 2018 must file an annual economic substance declaration. For United Kingdom tax exposure, the entity is prima facie outside the UK corporation tax net, but UK resident shareholders or directors may trigger UK CFC attribution or deemed domicile rules; no elected tax consolidation mechanism is available in Bermuda.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Bermuda VAT or GST; import customs duty on tangible goods levied under the Bermuda Customs Tariff Act 2012.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. No economic turnover threshold triggers VAT/GST registration in Bermuda because no such tax exists. A commercial importer must obtain customs registration and may be required to file import entries regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda levies no VAT or GST on exports or on any other supply, so there is no export zero-rating claim available. POD custom apparel exported from Bermuda is outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation; only customs export control or statistical filing requirements apply.

  • Customs Export Declaration or Export Manifest, where required by Bermuda Customs for controlled, statistical, or consignment-tracking purposes
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Transport Document (Air Waybill or Bill of Lading)

No electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based real-time VAT reporting mandate is in force. Bermuda Customs operates an electronic clearance platform (Customs Integrated Revenue Network, CIRN) requiring electronic import declarations for commercial shipments. VAT-style fiscal invoicing is not applicable.

Export zero-rating clawback penalties are not applicable because there are no VAT refunds to claw back. Bermuda Customs retains audit power over export declarations; inaccurate or fraudulent declarations can result in goods seizure, forfeiture, and monetary penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 2012. Separately, for United Kingdom shareholders or controlling management, failure to repatriate or attribute sales profits within 180 days may carry UK CFC or transfer-pricing consequences outside the Bermuda regime.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda maintains free currency convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD), and no exchange control regulations restrict profit remittance, dividend payment, or repatriation of capital. The exempted company may hold, convert, and transfer funds in any major currency without central bank approval. Capital account transactions, including foreign direct investment injections and intercompany loans, are freely permissible, subject only to standard AML/KYC vetting by the licensed banking institution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio. Exporters are free to maintain export earnings in any currency without a prescribed timeline for repatriation or conversion to BMD/USD. The BMA does not require foreign exchange declaration for individual trade settlements.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium for payment aggregation; Low as a standalone corporate banking substitute. Offshore digital EMIs licensed in the UK/EEA may lawfully provide payment processing for the POD business but cannot hold customer funds or provide deposit-like accounts in Bermuda without a BMA license. Stripe and PayPal are acceptable customer-facing rails; however, settlement should be to a licensed Bermudian bank account to ensure regulatory congruence with BMA anti-money laundering requirements. Legal constraints: (i) EMIs unlicensed in Bermuda are prohibited from conducting deposit-taking business locally; (ii) cross-border EMI onboarding is viable only where the EMI serves as a payment intermediary and does not assert functional bank status; (iii) all treasury concentration must remain in a BMA-licensed bank.

A company that fails to file the Annual Return within the statutory deadline is subject to late filing penalties, daily continuing fines under the Companies Act 1981, and may be struck off the register by the Registrar of Companies. Officers who knowingly permit the default are personally liable to the daily penalties. A struck-off company cannot lawfully trade, use Stripe or PayPal settlement balances, or enter into new contracts.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold in Bermuda dollars triggers a mandatory Local File or Master File for this entity. Bermuda does not currently require transfer pricing filings from a non-CIT-qualifying exempted company. If the entity were to become subject to the Bermuda CIT Act 2023 as part of a qualifying multinational group, OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation may be required, but no local currency filing threshold applies to this low-turnover POD retail business.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda’s Economic Substance Regulations apply only to 'relevant activities', such as banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centres, and intellectual property holding. E-commerce retail of custom apparel through a print-on-demand model is not a relevant activity for Bermuda ESR purposes. Nevertheless, the entity must maintain a registered office, adequate records, and a visible board presence to support its tax-exempt status and avoid characterization as an empty shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution for fraudulent statements under Bermuda law, with fines and imprisonment; potential recharacterization of the entity as tax resident elsewhere; application of UK corporation tax or foreign income tax, interest, and penalty assessments of up to 100% of the tax understated.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause the filing of false statements may be prosecuted personally, disqualified from serving as directors, subjected to civil restitution for taxes lost, and in serious cases denied exit from Bermuda pending investigation or sentenced to imprisonment.
Shell Company / Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike the company off the register; the entity may be disregarded or re-residentized by foreign revenue authorities; HMRC may impose corporation tax, diverted profits tax, and penalties; payment processors may terminate the merchant account if the corporate veil is determined to be a facade.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil and attach the underlying income and tax liabilities to the individuals directing the shell. Directors may receive disqualification orders, travel bans, confiscation orders, and personal civil liability for debts incurred through the sham structure.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: If the entity is within the scope of the Bermuda CIT Act 2023, the Bermuda tax authority may recharacterize transactions and impose additional tax, interest, and penalties. Even outside Bermuda CIT scope, UK transfer pricing legislation permits HMRC to adjust the UK participant’s profits and to impose penalties for deliberate transfer pricing errors. Foreign tax authorities may deny deductions and apply withholding or branch profit taxes.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute artificial transfer pricing contracts as a sham to evade tax are exposed to personal penalty notices, civil actions by minority shareholders, disqualification, and, where assets are moved abroad, cross-border asset tracing and travel restrictions.
Illegal Profit Repatriation / Insolvent Distribution

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies Act 1981, directors who authorize an unlawful distribution are jointly and severally liable to repay the amount distributed plus interest. The company may also be subject to insolvency proceedings, creditor clawback actions, and civil penalties; the distribution may be voidable as a preference in a subsequent liquidation.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to creditors for the excess distribution. Courts will lift the corporate veil where distributions were made to evade liabilities; offending directors may be disqualified, ordered to repatriate funds, restrained from leaving the jurisdiction, and, in insolvency fraud cases, imprisoned.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda’s beneficial ownership legislation and anti-money laundering framework impose monetary penalties for each day a false or missing filing continues; willful concealment or false declaration is a criminal offence. Payment processors may freeze accounts, and the Bermuda Registrar may strike off the company for continued non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Every officer in default is personally liable for the continuing penalties. In serious AML cases, directors may face imprisonment, asset confiscation, disqualification, and travel bans. The corporate veil is pierced where the exempted company was used as the instrument of financial crime.
Non-Payment of Government Fees

Statutory sanction: The company is removed from the register and ceases to have legal capacity to trade; its assets, including receivables held through Stripe and PayPal settlement accounts, may be frozen or declared forfeit under Bermuda law; striking off also prevents the entity from bringing legal proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to trade after strike-off incur personal liability for debts and contracts entered into during the unregistered period. A court may order civil restitution, disqualification, and, if directors abscond with company assets, the imposition of travel bans and criminal proceedings.
