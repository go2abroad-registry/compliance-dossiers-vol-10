Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; Bermuda Customs Service (border/indirect tax); Registrar of Companies (incorporation registry); portal: https://www.gov.bm/tax-commissioner (SmartGov e-Tax and Economic Substance portal)

E-government filing portal

No general corporate TIN exists in Bermuda. Step 1: incorporate the Exempted Company via the Registrar of Companies to obtain the Bermuda Company Number (primary statutory identifier). Step 2: if employing Bermuda-resident staff, register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner within 15 days and obtain an Employer Tax Account Number under the Payroll Tax Act 1995. Step 3: register with Bermuda Customs Service for a Customs Importer Code and appoint a licensed customs broker for import/export declarations. Step 4: if engaged in an ESR-relevant activity, file annual Economic Substance declarations via the ESR portal; no VAT ID is issued in Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Bermuda Exempted Company holds statutory tax-exempt status; no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, or indirect VAT/GST; exemption from future taxes guaranteed to 2035 via the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; managed-and-controlled entities may still attract Economic Substance reporting if within scope.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election available (no CIT regime). Statutory elections: (i) tax protection certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (ii) non-resident designation under the Companies Act 1981 for exchange-control purposes; (iii) employer election between the standard payroll tax and combined social insurance contribution schedules under the Payroll Tax Act 1995; (iv) ESR 'resident entity' status election where managed and controlled in Bermuda under the Economic Substance (Bermuda) Act 2018.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Duty (import tariff regime under the Customs Tariff Act 1972); no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax is levied in Bermuda; duty assessed ad valorem on CIF value at importation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Any person importing commercial goods must obtain a Customs Importer Code and file a per-shipment electronic customs declaration through a licensed broker; no de minimis exemption for commercial medical-device consignments in BMD.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not applicable as Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST and no export duties. Goods manufactured in or re-exported from Bermuda are outside the indirect tax base; the exporter records 'no duty applicable' on the electronic export declaration. Duty-paid inputs subsequently exported, or incorporated into manufactured goods that are exported, may qualify for a 100% duty drawback under the Customs Tariff Act 1972 where exportation is evidenced within 12 months of importation.

  • Electronic Customs Export Declaration submitted via ASYCUDA World (Single Export Document)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export from Bermuda
  • Duty Drawback Application Form with proof of original duty payment (Customs Tariff Act 1972)
  • Manufacturer's Declaration or Certificate of Origin where a preferential tariff treatment is claimed

Real-time electronic customs declaration and clearance are mandatory through the Bermuda Customs Automated System (ASYCUDA World platform). Importers/agents transmit the customs declaration, commercial invoice, and transport data in real time; there is no e-invoicing clearance engine, fiscal digital stamping, or continuous transaction control regime for tax purposes.

Fraudulent export declarations or drawback claims attract forfeiture of goods, fines up to BMD 10,000 or treble the duty evaded, plus interest and retrospective duty clawbacks under the Customs Tariff Act 1972 ss. 28-30; false Economic Substance declarations are penalized up to BMD 250,000 per annum under the Economic Substance (Bermuda) Act 2018; trade-based money-laundering misrepresentations additionally trigger BMA AML enforcement and director disqualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at par to the US dollar and USD is legal tender circulating interchangeably. Both current account (trade in goods/services, royalties, dividends) and capital account (equity injections, intercompany loans, portfolio flows) transactions are freely convertible; no prior exchange-control approval is required for a non-resident-owned Exempted Company, which operates under the BMA no-objection framework. Profit remittance and dividend upstreaming to the UK parent are unrestricted, subject only to AML/ATF source-of-funds verification and BMA balance-of-payments statistical reporting. There are no blocked-funds, forced-repatriation, or discriminatory conversion mechanisms affecting medical-device export receipts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no designated repatriation window for export proceeds. Under the Exchange Control General Exemption Notice applicable to Exempted Companies, proceeds from intraocular acrylic lens exports may be retained in offshore external accounts, held in USD, or freely converted without BMA prior consent. The only obligation is an information-reporting duty: the licensee bank must file a BMA External Account Return and an annual balance-of-payments transaction report, with no surrender or conversion enforcement.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972, with delegated authority from the UK Treasury and HM Treasury sanctions oversight; the BMA also administers the Bermuda Automated Settlement System (BASS) and the Bank Accounts Classification regime.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low for Bermuda domestic clearing rails. Offshore digital EMIs cannot access BMD settlement, BASS clearing, or BMA-regulated banking infrastructure; the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 reserves deposit-taking to licensed banks. A UK- or EU-authorised EMI (e.g., Wise or Airwallex) is viable only as an ancillary non-clearing transit account for GBP/EUR trade receipts and small-vendor disbursements, provided it does not hold the primary Escrow or Operational account and it operates as a pass-through payment corridor, not as the nexus of corporate liquidity. No Bermuda fintech licence (Digital Asset Business, FHC, Innovation Hub) authorises deposit-taking, so EMI usage is strictly supplementary.
