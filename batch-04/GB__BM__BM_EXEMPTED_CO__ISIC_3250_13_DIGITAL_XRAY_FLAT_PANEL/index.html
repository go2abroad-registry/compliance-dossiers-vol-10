Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Government of Bermuda – Ministry of Finance; Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax); Bermuda Customs and Border Control Agency (customs duties); Registrar of Companies (incorporation and Economic Substance returns); official portal: www.gov.bm

E-government filing portal

No corporate income tax exists in Bermuda, hence no corporate TIN/VAT ID is issued. A Bermuda Exempted Company obtains legal existence via a Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Companies (company registration number). If the exempted company becomes an employer of Bermuda-resident staff, it must register for Payroll Tax with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number. For import/export of physical goods, the company must register as a declarant with Bermuda Customs and Border Control Agency and may appoint a licensed customs broker. Non-resident exempted companies may apply for 'non-resident' designation under the Exchange Control Act 1974 to be free of exchange-control restrictions. An annual Economic Substance Declaration is required if the entity conducts a 'relevant activity'; the manufacture of X-ray flat-panel detectors is not classified as a relevant activity under the Economic Substance Regulations 2018.

Standard VAT / GST rate

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda does not operate a VAT/GST system, so there is no zero-rating mechanism, no input VAT credit, and no export VAT refund to claim. Bermuda generally imposes no customs duties on exported goods; goods exported from Bermuda are outside the scope of the Customs Tariff Act. Physical exports of X-ray flat-panel detectors will require compliance with any destination-country import documentation and with Bermuda export control restrictions applicable to dual-use medical/electronics equipment, if any.

  • Commercial invoice containing full product description, HS Code (e.g., 9022.14), and declared value
  • Packing list with unit quantities and serial numbers
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing outbound shipment from Bermuda
  • Certificate of Origin (re-export of non-Bermuda-origin components; ECS/REX or supplier origin documentation)
  • Bermuda Customs Export/Entry Declaration (where such filing is required for controlled or statistical goods)
  • Manufacturer's declaration or technical conformity documentation for the DR detector panels

No VAT e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists. Import clearance is effected through the Bermuda Customs Border Management System (CADP/eCustoms); exporters and importers file electronic declarations and pay duty through the customs portal, but there is no tax-authority e-invoicing clearance layer.

No VAT clawback exposure exists because Bermuda has no VAT/GST. Scrutiny applies under customs law: false export/import declarations, customs duty evasion, or concealment of dutiable goods attract civil penalties up to three times the duty value, forfeiture of goods, and criminal sanctions including fines and imprisonment under the Customs Act 1961 and Customs Tariff Act 1970. The Tax Commissioner may audit payroll tax and social insurance records and impose interest, penalties, and back-collection for employer under-reporting. The Economic Substance Authority may levy penalties of up to BMD 26,000 per failure plus BMD 57,700 for non-filing of economic substance returns and, for continuing default, escalation to the Registrar for strike-off. Bermuda also participates in OECD automatic exchange of information, so export invoice values and revenue repatriation data may be scrutinised by foreign tax authorities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is statutorily pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar, and USD circulates as parallel legal tender throughout Bermuda. The Exchange Control Act 1972 distinguishes exchange-control residents from non-residents; a Bermuda Exempted Company owned entirely by non-Bermudian shareholders and transacting no domestic Bermuda business is designated non-resident, thereby possessing unrestricted current- and capital-account convertibility with no BMA approval, no capital-flow quotas, and no limits on foreign-currency account balances or invoicing. Profit remittance to the UK parent by dividend, capital reduction, or liquidation distribution is unrestricted and free of Bermuda withholding tax; the sole practical condition is a director-signed solvency certificate where the remitted amount exceeds reported retained earnings. The BMD's hard USD peg eliminates currency conversion risk for USD-denominated digital X-ray flat-panel OEM trade flows, and there is no distinction between BMD and USD in the domestic clearing system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory rule: an exchange-control resident in Bermuda must offer export receipts in foreign currency to an authorised dealer within one month of receipt and faces a mandatory conversion ratio of 100% for the Bermudian Dollar. A Bermuda Exempted Company holding non-resident status is expressly exempt from this surrender duty; export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in USD, EUR, GBP, or any other currency, and no mandatory conversion ratio applies. Because BMD and USD are interchangeable at par, the Bermuda entity holds its USD export receivables from EMEA X-ray panel distributors directly in a USD operating account at a BMA-licensed bank with no conversion event and no BMA reporting obligation beyond routine suspicious-transaction monitoring.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), established under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and vested with exchange-control administration under the Exchange Control Act 1972; it issues the Bermudian Dollar, licenses all banks and trust companies, and supervises the AML/ATF framework. The Bank of England exercises ultimate monetary sovereignty as the Crown Dependency's parent authority, yet all day-to-day currency, banking, and exchange-control supervision resides exclusively with the BMA.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH as a secondary collection-and-payment rail; LOW as the sole banking layer. UK/EEA EMI licences do not passport to Bermuda, so regulated cross-border EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie, and Wise Business must onboard the exempted company under their own programmes rather than via an EU passport. Bermuda is not on the FATF or EU non-cooperative lists, and the Low_Risk_Standard banking classification for an ISIC 3250 medical-device hardware OEM materially improves approval probability; nevertheless, EMIs apply enhanced due diligence to zero-tax jurisdictions and will demand the Bermuda Economic Substance Act certificate, the Tax Exemption Certificate, and supply-chain documentation. Legal constraints include the inability to use EMI infrastructure for SWIFT MT700 letters of credit, trade finance, or same-day BMD settlements. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for the UK parent to capitalise the Bermuda exempted company: the UK entity instructs a SWIFT MT103 single customer credit transfer from its London clearing account to the Bermuda operating account at HSBC Bank Bermuda Limited or Butterfield, transmitted via the New York correspondent clearing network (CHIPS/Fedwire) in USD. The remitter records purpose code OECD BPM6 Direct Investment D.5 'equity injection' in the SWIFT remittance-information field, referencing the board-approved share subscription and the Bermuda Companies Act 1981 allotment resolution. No BMA exchange-control approval or notification is required because the exempted company is designated non-resident; the receiving bank credits the USD capital escrow sub-account and issues a credit advice carrying the internal BMA regulatory code 'Non-Resident Capital Receipt'. Where injection occurs later than 120 days after incorporation, the constitutional filing must be updated with the share-allotment particulars. For GBP/EUR capital injections, the Bermuda bank converts at its dealing rate; amounts above USD 5,000,000 equivalent should be executed in tranches with pre-agreed forward rates to eliminate mid-market spread leakage. The UK parent must retain the MT103 sender reference, subscription agreement, and bank compliance receipt for the substance and CFC-file audit trail.Supplier and distributor liquidity is cleared through two primary rails matching the target payment rails of SWIFT LC and TT Wire. First, TT wire settlements: SWIFT MT103 USD messages issued by HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield through their New York correspondents to Asian and EU supplier accounts, using ISO 20022 purpose codes such as TRADE plus the purchase-order and invoice reference in the remittance field; same-day USD value is available because the BMD/USD parity eliminates conversion latency. Euro-denominated disbursements are cleared over TARGET2 through the bank's EUR correspondent using MT103 COVE instructions, while GBP disbursements for UK intercompany recharges settle over CHAPS. Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Default rule: every Bermuda exempted company must appoint an independent auditor each financial year. The audit obligation may be waived for a given year only by unanimous written shareholder resolution, and only if no external requirement (bank covenant, group consolidation, regulatory licence, or listing rules) mandates audited financial statements. A company falling within the Economic Substance regime or holding a Bermuda regulatory licence will not qualify for the waiver.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% headline rate for Bermuda exempted companies under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; 15% Corporate Income Tax effective 1 January 2025 for Bermuda constituent entities of in-scope multinational enterprise groups whose consolidated global revenue is EUR 750 million or above (OECD Pillar Two / GloBE alignment). UK corporation tax does not apply directly to a Bermuda-resident exempted company provided it is managed and controlled in Bermuda; UK CFC rules may, however, attribute its profits to a UK corporate shareholder.
