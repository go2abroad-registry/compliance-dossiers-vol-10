Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed by the Companies Act 1981 for a Bermuda Exempted Company; the authorised capital stated in the Memorandum is typically US$12,000 divided into 12,000 ordinary shares of US$1.00 each. No statutory escrow account is required for incorporation. Share subscription proceeds are receivable by the company and may be held in any licensed bank. If the company later seeks Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) licensing for a regulated financial activity, the BMA may impose minimum capital and escrow conditions at that subsequent stage.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: Certificate of Inspection from the Bermuda Fire & Rescue Service for any occupied premises, covering combustibility, exit routes, and fire extinguisher servicing. Structural: a licensed Bermuda engineer's load evaluation for studio soundproofing and equipment installations. Occupational: compliance inspection under the Occupational Safety and Health Act 2014, including electrical safety certification, first-aid provision, and a written safety policy if more than five employees are engaged.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

None applicable — a Bermuda Exempted Company carrying on a digitally-delivered audiovisual services business (subtitle transcription, AI voice cloning, and dubbing post-production) does not require an industrial or manufacturing licence. The general licensing authority for any physical trade premises is the Ministry of Economic Development under the Trade and Business Licensing Act 1990; however, off-premises ICT-enabled services are not a licensable trade absent a physical Bermuda office.

Category: No industrial operating permit is required. If and only if the company later opens a physical studio branch in Bermuda, a Trade and Business License will be required under the Trade and Business Licensing Act 1990 (Category: Professional and Technical Services); that licence is administrative and does not constitute an industrial manufacturing permit.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Bermuda (26 Church Street, Hamilton HM 12); corporate registration and licensing via the Registry of Companies; customs matters via the Bermuda Customs Department. The official portal for corporate registration is https://www.roc.gov.bm; tax guidance is published by the Ministry of Finance at https://www.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a general statutory TIN or VAT ID for all entities. A foreign-owned Exempted Company obtains its primary identifier (the company registration number) at incorporation from the Registry of Companies. If the entity becomes an employer in Bermuda, it must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for a payroll tax account number and with the Bermuda Social Insurance scheme for a social insurance employer number. If the entity is a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group subject to the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, it must separately register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner to obtain a corporate income tax (CIT) taxpayer identification number and be issued a post-box/TIN by the Ministry of Finance for CIT filing purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. A Bermuda Exempted Company is granted a statutory exemption from Bermuda income and corporation taxes under section 13A of the Exempted Companies Act 1981, effective until 28 March 2035, subject to the payment of the annual government fee. This exemption does not apply to large multinational groups within the scope of Bermuda's 15% Corporate Income Tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, which takes effect for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Available election forms

Bermuda domestic law does not provide a US-style 'check-the-box' election or separate hybrid mismatch elections. The Tax Commissioner administers CIT as a single entity-level tax with no transparency election. For in-scope multinational groups, certain elections are available under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, including the transitionally safe harbour election and the qualified domestic minimum top-up tax election, where applicable; otherwise no elective classification mechanisms exist.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda has no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax. Indirect taxation is limited to import customs duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1972, stamp duty on certain instruments and property transactions, and employer payroll contributions (payroll tax under the Payroll Tax Act 1995).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists because Bermuda imposes no value-added tax. Customs registration is required for any importer of dutiable or restricted goods regardless of turnover. Payroll tax registration is triggered when a Bermuda company first hires a Bermuda-resident employee, irrespective of turnover. For CIT, registration is triggered when the entity is a constituent entity of an MNE group meeting the EUR 750 million consolidated revenue threshold; there is no local currency (BMD) turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda does not levy VAT or GST on supplies, so there is no export zero-rating regime to claim. Exported goods are outside the scope of Bermuda customs duty; however, a claim for customs duty relief on re-exported goods requires evidence that the goods were imported only temporarily or are re-exported in the same or substantially transformed state. For services exports (including AI subtitle and dubbing services), no tax charge exists and no substantiation requirement is imposed.

  • N/A for VAT zero-rating because no VAT regime exists; the following documentation supports a customs duty drawback or re-export exemption claim, where applicable:
  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs Form / Single Entry System filing)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Bermuda
  • Commercial Invoice describing the exported goods
  • Packing List
  • Evidence of foreign payment or inward remittance (bank SWIFT advice) if the goods are sold to a non-Bermuda buyer

No real-time electronic fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control mandate exists for VAT or payroll in Bermuda. Import declarations are processed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Single Entry System, operated by Customs brokers authorised by the Bermuda Customs Department. Employers file payroll tax returns via the Tax Commissioner's online portal; no third-party API clearance or government stamping is imposed.

No VAT clawback or zero-rating penalties apply in Bermuda. However, false claims for customs duty drawback, re-export exemption, or temporary importation relief are audited by the Bermuda Customs Department and are subject to penalties under the Customs Tariff Act 1972, including fines of up to BMD 1,000 and/or three times the duty amount, forfeiture of goods, and criminal prosecution. Retrospective customs duty assessments may be raised where goods are removed from Bermuda without the required export evidence. For CIT, in-scope constituent entities face penalties for failure to register, file, or correct tax returns, including arrears interest and fixed penalties set out in the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar (USD) and circulates interchangeably with USD. Bermuda maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility; non-resident-owned exempted companies operate under the BMA General Permission and require no prior exchange-control approval to receive, hold, convert, or repatriate funds in any currency. Dividends, interest, service fees, and liquidation proceeds are freely remittable with no withholding tax and no Bermudian income or capital gains tax. Practical hurdles are limited to banking KYC documentation, Economic Substance Act 2018 filing compliance, and proper statutory record-keeping (register of members, charges, and directors).

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export or service proceeds. The company may retain 100% of USD, GBP, and EUR receivables indefinitely. Proceeds from AI subtitle and voice-cloning services should be reconciled monthly and recorded in the audited financial statements supporting the tax-exempt certificate renewal.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as exchange control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and prudential regulator under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; the BMA also supervises AML/ATF compliance under the AML/ATF Act 2018.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda exempted companies are outside the corporate income tax net; the 2023 Corporate Income Tax Act imposes a 15% domestic top-up tax only on multinational groups with consolidated revenue above €750 million, effective 1 January 2025)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — no Bermuda withholding tax is levied on dividends paid by an exempted company to non-resident shareholders, whether in cash or in specie

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda transfer pricing statute applies to a tax-exempted company. For groups brought within the 15% CIT regime, the local file is required where the Bermuda entity engages in controlled transactions exceeding BD$2.0 million per counterparty per year; master file is required where the multinational group has total consolidated revenue of at least BD$1.95 billion (Pillar Two threshold)

Economic substance scope: Applicable under Economic Substance Regulations 2018. The AI/creative post-production activity may constitute an intellectual property business if it earns income from proprietary subtitling algorithms, voice-models or related IP; the company must demonstrate that core income-generating activities occur in Bermuda, employ adequate staff, incur sufficient operating expenditure, and be directed and managed from Bermuda; annual substance declarations are mandatory

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / UK Permanent Establishment Exposure

Statutory sanction: In Bermuda: fine up to BD$10,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years for false statements under the Companies Act; in the UK: HMRC can impose a failure-to-notify penalty up to 200% of the tax that would have been chargeable, plus interest, and apply General Anti-Abuse Rule (GAAR) counteractions up to 60% of the tax advantage

Director personal liability: Directors may be issued personal liability notices under HMRC COP9; in serious cases civil restitution may attach to their personal assets; international travel bans and up to 7 years' imprisonment for deliberate tax evasion under UK Criminal Finances Act 2017
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda ESR: initial penalty BD$10,000; continuing contravention attracts BD$20,000 per further year, daily default fines of BD$500, and the Registrar may strike the company off the register with loss of legal personality

Director personal liability: Every director or officer who knowingly permits default is jointly and severally liable for the company's penalties; directors may be disqualified for a term up to 5 years and subject to a personal fine of BD$25,000
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Amendment Act 2018: failure to file a BO return attracts fines up to BD$25,000; false or misleading filings can lead to criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to 2 years; the company's shares may be subject to restriction orders

Director personal liability: Directors, as 'senior managing officials', face separate criminal liability for non-compliance; UK authorities can additionally seek Unexplained Wealth Orders over property connected to the director where the company is used to hide proceeds of crime; civil recovery proceedings can capture director remuneration paid by the shell
