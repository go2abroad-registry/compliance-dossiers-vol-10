Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Registrar of Companies, Bermuda (Ministry of Finance) for incorporation, annual returns and economic substance filings; Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda for employer payroll tax registration and administration; Bermuda Customs Department (Ministry of Finance) for importer/exporter registration and customs duty administration. Official portal: www.gov.bm with dedicated e-filing modules for company annual returns, payroll tax and customs entries.

E-government filing portal

No corporate income-tax TIN exists in Bermuda. The statutory identifier is the Company Number issued on incorporation under the Companies Act 1981. SOP: (1) file the Memorandum and Articles of Association with the Registrar of Companies and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (2) file the Declaration of Exemption under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 to evidence statutory tax exemption; (3) if employing Bermuda-resident staff, register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax (employer account number) and with the National Pension Scheme administrator; (4) if moving physical goods through Bermuda, register with the Bermuda Customs Department as importer/exporter or appoint a licensed customs broker; (5) register with the Bermuda Competent Authority (Department of International Tax Cooperation) for FATCA/CRS and obtain a GIIN if the entity is a financial institution; (6) file the annual economic substance declaration with the Registrar where a relevant activity is carried on. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — a Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, dividend/interest/royalty withholding taxes and any tax computed on profits, income, assets or turnover, statutorily guaranteed until 31 March 2035. The exemption does not cover employer payroll tax, customs duties, stamp duty on local land/instruments, social insurance, or UK/EU VAT and UK corporation tax arising from a UK/EU permanent establishment or UK central management and control.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Bermuda. Available elections: (1) exempted-company status under the Companies Act 1981, with prohibition on carrying on local business without a permit; (2) Declaration of Exemption filed under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 securing the direct-tax exemption guarantee; (3) economic substance treatment: an entity receiving income from foreign connected persons may be classified as carrying on a relevant activity (distribution and service centre) and must elect to maintain core income-generating activities in Bermuda; a 3PL serving only unrelated customers is outside the relevant-activity scope; (4) FATCA/CRS classification election (financial institution vs non-financial entity) reported to the Bermuda Competent Authority. UK residence risk must be separately assessed if strategic management is in the UK.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_VAT_No_GST_No_Sales_Tax — Bermuda imposes customs import duties, employer payroll tax, accommodation tax and stamp duty; there is no value-added, goods-and-services or sales tax at any level.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST/sales-tax registration threshold exists because no such tax is levied (local currency BMD, pegged 1:1 to USD). Mandatory Bermuda Customs importer registration applies to all importers regardless of value; payroll tax registration becomes mandatory upon engagement of the first Bermuda-resident employee.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

N/A_as_VAT_Zero_Rating — Bermuda has no VAT, so no 0% export VAT rate or VAT refund mechanism exists. Duty-equivalent treatment: (1) goods physically exported from Bermuda are customs-duty-exempt; (2) goods imported with genuine intent to re-export may obtain duty relief (duty drawback/temporary admission) where outward movement is evidenced; (3) cross-border services supplied by the Bermuda exempted company (overseas warehouse coordination, 3PL contract management, e-commerce fulfilment administration) are outside any Bermuda indirect tax. Important cross-border note: for UK/EU operations the Bermuda exemption does not apply — a UK fulfilment warehouse creates a UK VAT registration duty from the first taxable supply (the 90,000 GBP registration threshold does not shield a non-UK-established business), with import VAT and online-marketplace rules applying.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic export entry filed via a licensed customs broker through the Bermuda Customs Department single-window)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing outward physical movement of goods from Bermuda
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List matching the export entry
  • Re-export evidence for previously imported goods (original Customs Import Entry reference, duty receipt or temporary-admission approval, and outward manifest confirmation of exit)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate — not applicable in Bermuda (no exchange-control or inward-remittance certification requirement); a bank credit advice or remittance record satisfies counterparty or audit needs

No_Real_Time_Fiscal_Clearance — Bermuda does not mandate real-time e-invoicing, fiscal data transmission, API clearance or digital stamping. The Bermuda Customs Department operates an electronic single-window for import entries and export declarations, filed by licensed customs brokers through the customs portal; no QR-code fiscal control or continuous transaction controls apply.

No VAT clawback is possible because no VAT is levied. Penalty exposure: (1) customs false-declaration offences — forfeiture of goods plus fines of up to treble the duty evaded and imprisonment for wilful false export/re-export declarations; (2) Economic Substance Act 2018 — failure to pass the core-income-generating-activity test for a relevant activity or to file the annual declaration triggers fines of up to BMD 250,000 (first failure) and BMD 500,000 (continuing failure), and possible strike-off; (3) the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 guarantee is absolute for direct taxes until 31 March 2035 but does not cover payroll tax, customs duty, social insurance or CRS/FATCA penalties; and (4) HMRC retains full powers to assess UK VAT and corporation tax on any UK permanent establishment, plus relevant interest and penalties. The 180-day repatriation figure herein is a compliance-pipeline default; Bermuda itself imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline on exempted companies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged at a fixed 1:1 rate to the US dollar and is freely convertible through authorised dealers. Current-account convertibility for trade and logistics payments is unrestricted in practice for non-resident-designated exempted companies. Capital-account convertibility is also available for the target entity because a Bermuda Exempted Company may be classified as non-resident, which removes prior approval requirements for share capital, shareholder loans, distributions and redemption proceeds. Profit remittance is not subject to Bermuda withholding tax or exchange-control permit; practical impediments are limited to bank KYC/AML file completeness, beneficial-ownership verification, and the need to evidence non-resident status. A top-up corporate income tax under Bermuda's Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 may apply only to large in-scope multinational groups with consolidated group revenue above EUR 750m; it does not create a remittance barrier for a typical cross-dock 3PL exempted company.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1972 framework, a resident exporter must ensure that export proceeds are received in Bermuda in the prescribed manner and offered for sale to an authorised dealer without unreasonable delay; BMA delegated authorisations effectively require resident exporters to convert non-permitted foreign-currency receipts through the authorised dealer, creating a surrender ratio of 100% for such currencies. A Bermuda Exempted Company that is designated non-resident and maintains external/USD accounts is treated as outside the surrender regime, so USD/BMD export receipts of the cross-dock 3PL business may be retained and used directly, giving an effective mandatory conversion ratio of 0%. The statutory timeline is fixed by the dealer's exchange-control authorisation, but bank practice requires that export proceeds be credited to the company's operational account within six months of shipment and any variance be justified to the BMA-authorised dealer.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) in its dual capacity as the central bank and as the authority administering the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973; day-to-day approvals are delegated to authorised banks and licensed corporate service providers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Viability rating: Medium with major constraints. Bermuda does not license electronic money institutions per se; payment-service functions require a BMA-regulated bank or a licensed money-service business arrangement. An offshore EMI such as Airwallex or a UK FCA-licensed EMI can be used as a treasury overlay for FX conversion and low-value multi-currency payments, but it cannot replace a domestic licensed bank account for the Bermudian entity. Legal constraints: (i) the BMA AML/CFT regime requires the customer relationship to be established by a fit-and-proper locally regulated institution; (ii) EMIs do not provide Bermuda ACH clearing or BMD-denominated local settlement; (iii) the Bermuda exempted company's statutory bank confirmation and audit trail must include a licensed BMA bank; and (iv) the target account documentation can use an EMI only as a pre-funded payment router, not as the principal depository of capital or export proceeds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant route: the UK parent treasury sends a SWIFT MT103/credit transfer through its clearing bank to the Bermuda bank's US-dollar correspondent account, crediting the Bermuda Exempted Company's USD operational account. The remittance is coded as a capital transaction, with narrative such as EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / SHARE SUBSCRIPTION, never as trade payment. If the sending bank requires an ISO 20022 purpose code, use the bank-mapped foreign-direct-investment or equity-injection code (e.g., CFDI-style code) rather than a goods/trade code. The receiving Bermudian authorised dealer records the inward investment for BMA exchange-control statistics and confirms external-account treatment; no prior BMA approval is required for a non-resident exempted company. Verification pack: board capitalisation resolution, share subscription agreement, source-of-funds documentation, debit advice from the UK parent bank, authorised signatory instruction, and the final MT103 confirmation with the unique end-to-end transaction reference.Use USD-denominated clearing for all external trade disbursements to avoid BMD conversion slippage. Standard supplier, warehouse and carrier payments are settled by SWIFT MT103 through the Bermudian bank's US correspondent using Fedwire/CHIPS, with the remittance reference tied to the invoice or contract. For high-value purchase-order commitments over the approved corporate threshold, issue an irrevocable documentary commercial letter of credit via SWIFT MT700/MT707 through the HSBC Bermuda or Butterfield trade desk; the LC can support deferred documentary settlement and protects both buyer and supplier. Local Bermuda-dollar obligations such as payroll and domestic rent settle over Bermuda ACH. Airwallex may be used as a treasury overlay only after pre-funding from the licensed Tier 1 bank account; it routes low-value EUR/GBP and multicurrency disbursements to foreign suppliers without displacing the primary bank relationship. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Viability rating: Medium with major constraints. Bermuda does not license electronic money institutions per se; payment-service functions require a BMA-regulated bank or a licensed money-service business arrangement. An offshore EMI such as Airwallex or a UK FCA-licensed EMI can be used as a treasury overlay for FX conversion and low-value multi-currency payments, but it cannot replace a domestic licensed bank account for the Bermudian entity. Legal constraints: (i) the BMA AML/CFT regime requires the customer relationship to be established by a fit-and-proper locally regulated institution; (ii) EMIs do not provide Bermuda ACH clearing or BMD-denominated local settlement; (iii) the Bermuda exempted company's statutory bank confirmation and audit trail must include a licensed BMA bank; and (iv) the target account documentation can use an EMI only as a pre-funded payment router, not as the principal depository of capital or export proceeds.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Bermuda Exempted Companies on all worldwide income; 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) applies exclusively to in-scope members of multinational enterprise (MNE) groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an Exempted Company to any shareholder, whether resident or non-resident, and no advance corporation tax or dividend distribution tax exists.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda ESR regime imposes no fixed BMD documentation threshold; however, an entity carrying on a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity must maintain contemporaneous records evidencing CIGAs, expenditure, premises, and employees in Bermuda. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, in-scope MNE groups (revenue at or above EUR 750 million) must maintain a full suite of OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation, including a Group Master File and Local File, prepared in English; the threshold is the EUR 750 million consolidated revenue test, not a BMD-denominated amount, and the Tax Commissioner may require country-by-country reporting data.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to any exempted company carrying on a 'relevant activity' under the Economic Substance (Notice) Regulations 2019, as amended in 2023. A cross-dock 3PL entity providing warehousing, inventory management, and order-fulfilment services to related persons constitutes a 'distribution and service centre business'; it must satisfy three cumulative tests: (1) management and control exercised in Bermuda; (2) adequate physical premises, full-time employees, and operating expenditure in Bermuda proportionate to its activity; and (3) core income-generating activities physically performed in Bermuda. If the 3PL serves only unrelated third-party customers, the entity is outside the relevant-activity definition but must still file an annual ESR declaration; deliberate misclassification is treated as non-compliance. Failure results in financial penalties up to BMD 250,000, monthly default penalties of BMD 25,000, forced strike-off, and mandatory information exchange with HMRC.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — UK Corporation Tax (Sovereign Layer)

Statutory sanction: UK corporation tax charged at 25% (main rate) on all worldwide profits; HMRC penalties up to 100% of the tax liability for deliberate and concealed errors under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007; interest accrues from the due date; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion of income tax under Finance Act 2000 s.144 carries an unlimited fine; HMRC may issue a Personal Liability Notice and a payment direction under the Senior Accounting Officer regime.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and any person actively managing the company are jointly liable for penalties; fraudulent evasion convictions carry imprisonment up to 7 years; HMRC may apply for director disqualification orders under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 for up to 15 years; civil restitution of unpaid tax plus interest is enforceable against personal assets where the corporate veil is pierced for sham or façade.
Shell Company — Bermuda ESR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2023: financial penalty up to BMD 250,000; continuing default attracts an additional BMD 25,000 per month up to a cap of BMD 250,000; the Registrar may strike the company off the register and the Government may exchange information with HMRC under the UK–Bermuda TIEA, triggering UK enquiries.

Director personal liability: Officers and directors face imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly furnishing false declarations or permitting non-compliance; directors' personal fines apply where the offence is committed with consent, connivance, or neglect; the Bermuda court may impose travel bans, asset freezes, and orders for restitution of benefits derived from the non-compliant entity.
AML and Beneficial Ownership — UK Register of Overseas Entities (ROE)

Statutory sanction: Continuing offence fine of up to GBP 2,500 per day; imprisonment up to 5 years on conviction on indictment; the UK land title is subject to a restriction preventing any sale, transfer, or charge; any transaction completed while unregistered is void against the registered proprietor; daily default fines escalate until compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors, members, and officers may be prosecuted personally where the offence is committed with consent or knowledge; directors face disqualification for up to 15 years; travel bans and seizure of UK assets may be ordered; non-compliant entities cannot enforce property rights in UK courts, causing severe commercial loss.
Tax Fraud — Bermuda CIT Act 2024 Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Bermuda CIT Act 2024: fixed penalty of BMD 1,000 for late registration plus BMD 1,000 per month of default; late CIT Return penalties and monthly surcharges as prescribed; statutory interest on unpaid top-up tax; knowingly false or misleading statements attract a fine up to BMD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years; the Tax Commissioner may issue a default assessment based on estimated income and enforce recovery through the Bermuda courts.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are criminally liable for fraudulent misdeclarations made with consent or neglect; the Tax Commissioner may apply for an order prohibiting departure from Bermuda until outstanding penalties are settled; personal civil restitution of the tax gap applies; litigation costs and aggravated damages may be awarded; shadow directors of the ultimate overseas parent are equally exposed.
Transfer Pricing Distortion — Arm's Length Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda CIT Act 2024, the Tax Commissioner may recharacterise related-party transactions and assess additional top-up tax at 15% plus interest; HMRC, as sovereign-layer authority, may impose transfer pricing adjustments under TIOPA 2010 Part 4, increasing UK corporation tax and applying penalties up to 100% of the understated tax; double tax is not automatically relieved due to Bermuda's absence of a comprehensive DTA; cross-border documentation penalties up to GBP 3,000 per failure apply in the UK.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign transfer pricing documentation known to be false face criminal penalties in the UK up to 7 years' imprisonment under the Fraud Act 2006; Bermuda directors face fines up to BMD 250,000 and 2 years' imprisonment; civil recovery actions may pierce the corporate veil where transfer pricing is used as an instrument of UK tax evasion; professional advisors may also incur liability for facilitating avoidance.
