Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance - Office of the Tax Commissioner (Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 and payroll tax); Her Majesty's Bermuda Customs Service (import/export duties); Registrar of Companies, Registry General (corporate registry and Certificate of Exemption issuance). Official portals: https://www.gov.bm/registry-general, https://www.gov.bm/customs, and the Tax Commissioner's CIT e-filing portal.

E-government filing portal

Bermuda does not issue a VAT or general tax identification number; the exempted company's statutory identifier is its company registration number (CRN) obtained on incorporation. Procedure: (1) Incorporate with the Registrar of Companies via the Registry General to obtain the CRN and Certificate of Incorporation; (2) apply to the Minister of Finance for a Certificate of Exemption under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which must be procured within one year of incorporation to secure the statutory tax-exemption guarantee; (3) if importing goods, register with Her Majesty's Bermuda Customs Service to obtain a customs account/import registration number; (4) if employing staff in Bermuda, register as an employer with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a payroll tax account number; (5) if the entity falls within the scope of the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 (in-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more), register for CIT and obtain a CIT filing reference. No VAT ID is required because no VAT regime exists in Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

1) Certificate of Exemption (tax protection) - statutory election available to every Bermuda exempted company guaranteeing that no tax imposed or to be imposed by Bermuda on profits, income, gains, appreciation, or assets shall apply to the company until 31 March 2050 for new certificates; the certificate is issued by the Minister of Finance and is non-assignable. 2) QDMTT Safe Harbour election under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 - an in-scope ultimate parent entity may elect to apply the Bermudian Qualifying Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (15%) as a safe harbour against top-up taxes in other jurisdictions; this election is made on the CIT registration and annual return. 3) US check-the-box election - although not a Bermudian statutory election, a Bermuda exempted company treated as a foreign disregarded entity may file US Form 8832 to elect classification as a corporation or partnership for US federal tax purposes; this is solely a US tax law election with no Bermuda law effect. 4) ESR (Economic Substance) registration and election - entities carrying on relevant activities must elect their relevant activity category and file the annual economic substance declaration with the Registrar of Companies. 5) No hybrid mismatch election exists under Bermudian domestic law; Bermuda does not maintain a hybrid mismatch regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or Sales and Use Tax - Bermuda imposes customs import duties (Customs Tariff Act 1968) on imported goods and stamp duty on certain land transfers and instruments; the UK's VAT Act 1994 (20% UK VAT) is not in force in Bermuda as a British Overseas Territory. The effective indirect tax burden on imports ranges from 5% to 22% for general consumer goods and up to 25% for luxury or restricted goods; there is no indirect tax on exported goods or services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST/SUT registration threshold exists because Bermuda has no such tax. Separate thresholds apply: (a) customs importer registration is mandatory for all persons importing goods, with no monetary minimum; (b) payroll tax registration applies to every employer on Bermudian payroll, regardless of amount; (c) the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 imposes a 15% CIT on constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more (approximately BMD 750 million); entities below this threshold remain fully exempt.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

N/A for VAT purposes because no VAT exists. For customs purposes, exports of goods from Bermuda are not subject to export duty; goods imported and subsequently re-exported in an unaltered state may claim a duty drawback or refund of import duty under the Customs Act 1948, provided the re-export occurs within the approved period (generally 12 months from import) and the goods have not been consumed or altered. Services rendered by a Bermuda exempted company - including e-commerce retailing, order routing, and distribution facilitation for outdoor camping equipment - fall within 'offshore' business and, under the Certificate of Exemption, are excluded from all Bermudian taxation. The outward movement of goods from Bermuda does not trigger any zero-rating administrative approval because no indirect tax attaches to exports.

  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export
  • Commercial invoice (with full description, HS classification, and value)
  • Packing list
  • Proof of import duty payment and import entry (required only when claiming duty drawback on re-export)
  • Certificate of Exemption (tax protection certificate) issued by the Minister of Finance
  • Company registration certificate / CRN (supporting exporter identity and fiscal residence evidence)

None - Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, government API connectivity, digital stamping, or continuous controls for invoices. Private payment provider e-invoicing (Stripe, PayPal) is fully accepted without government data transmission. Import and export declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World (UNCTAD customs management system) operated by Her Majesty's Bermuda Customs Service, which requires Customs Declaration Numbers (CDN) for physical goods crossing the border; digital services and electronically delivered goods are outside the customs declaration requirement.

Bermuda imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline or exchange control requirement; the 180-day convention reflects prudent banking and substance-good-standing expectations for exempted companies holding offshore receivables via payment providers. Penalties: (a) Under the Customs Act 1948, false export declarations, forged drawback claims, or fraudulent re-export documentation render the goods liable to forfeiture and the offender to a fine of up to three times the value of the goods or the duty evaded, plus possible imprisonment; customs drawbacks are clawed back in full with interest upon discovery of material misstatement. (b) Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024, failure to register, file, or pay CIT or QDMTT triggers interest from the due date, administrative penalties of up to 10% of unpaid tax, and additional monthly filing penalties of BMD 250 to BMD 25,000; the Tax Commissioner may reopen returns within 6 years (unlimited in fraud or wilful default cases) and retrospectively assess with clawback of any CIT reduction claimed. (c) Under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, any misrepresentation in the Certificate of Exemption application, or any unauthorised local business activity by the exempted company, causes the tax protection certificate to be revoked with retroactive effect, exposing the company to all current and future Bermudian taxes that would otherwise have been exempt, together with civil penalties and potential director disqualification.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged at parity to the US Dollar (USD) at the official rate of 1:1, eliminating FX conversion spread between the payment-processor settlement currency and the local statutory currency. Bermuda applies a dual-regime exchange control framework distinguishing resident from non-resident entities. A Bermuda Exempted Company conducting all business outside Bermuda can obtain Non-Resident Designation from the BMA via Form RES 11, which places the company outside the capital-account approval process. For a Non-Resident designated company: current account payments (imports of camping equipment, logistics, platform commissions, marketing fees) are freely remittable through BMA-licensed authorized dealers; capital account transactions (share capital injections, intercompany loans, dividend distributions, treasury sweeps) are likewise permitted without BMA prior authorization, provided the flow is evidenced by board resolutions and underlying commercial documentation. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal: Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, no withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties, and no accumulated earnings tax; the sole hurdles are corporate law formalities - declaration of a dividend out of distributable reserves, solvency statement, and filing of the annual return with the Registrar of Companies. USD proceeds held through Stripe and PayPal may be retained indefinitely or converted at parity without restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender or mandatory conversion rules apply to this entity. The Exchange Control Regulations 1973 require only persons resident in Bermuda for exchange-control purposes to offer export receipts in foreign currency to an authorized dealer within the statutory period. A Bermuda Exempted Company designated as Non-Resident is expressly exempt; therefore, USD e-commerce receipts from ISIC 4791 retail activities may be held in full in USD accounts, swept to US correspondent clearers, or converted to BMD for local expenses without a minimum conversion ratio, prescribed timeline, or BMA filing.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) - the integrated central bank, monetary authority, and financial services regulator of Bermuda, administering the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, with significant legal constraints. Bermuda is not on the FATF grey list and is a UK Overseas Territory with strong AML standards, so an offshore EMI is not legally prohibited from servicing a Bermuda Exempted Company on a cross-border basis. However, Bermuda has no EMI licensing regime; an offshore EMI that establishes a local branch, maintains a Bermuda-registered address, or holds itself out to Bermuda residents would require licensing under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 or a BMA payments authorization. In commercial practice, leading UK and EU EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud) frequently decline standalone Bermuda-domiciled corporate applications because the absence of a domestic EMI passport creates correspondent and compliance complexity; approvals are normally conditioned upon a UK or EU parent entity, UK directors, and projected flows above USD 1,000,000. The viable fallback is a split treasury: (i) Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda for the primary corporate account, capital escrow, and SWIFT trade payments; (ii) a US or UK payment-account or EMI solution integrated with Stripe and PayPal for card payouts and fee disbursements. Legal due diligence must cover source-of-funds declarations, restriction of the EMI account to outward operating payments (not customer deposits), and absence of UK/EU deposit protection for Bermuda entities.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial share capital is injected by SWIFT MT103 wire transfer from the UK subscribing shareholder to the USD account of the Bermuda Exempted Company at the designated authorized dealer (Butterfield or HSBC). The remittance information field must state SHARE SUBSCRIPTION [ENTITY NAME] / ALLOTMENT RESOLUTION [DATE] and the originating UK bank will classify the purpose under its capital-transfer category, typically 'Investment in shares' or 'Direct investment abroad'; no specific BMD control document is required for a Non-Resident designated company. Before deployment, the receiving bank requires: (i) a board resolution allotting the shares; (ii) an updated register of members and share certificates; (iii) a registered agent confirmation letter; (iv) source of funds evidence comprising the originator bank statement and, for amounts exceeding USD 250,000, a notarized declaration of wealth; and (v) a purpose-of-account letter confirming deployment toward inventory procurement, marketing, and working capital. The receiving bank credits the USD or BMD account at 1:1 parity with no FX spread; funds are cleared for operational use typically within 2-4 business days after the financial crime committee completes verification. For compliance with the UK parent's treasury policy, the injection is recorded in the Bermuda company's books as share premium and reported in the economic substance annual return.Trade disbursements use a four-rail structure: (1) SWIFT MT103 for supplier invoices and logistics costs below USD 200,000, with payment narrative PAYMENT FOR IMPORTED OUTDOOR CAMPING EQUIPMENT - INVOICE [NUMBER] and goods-trade purpose; (2) Commercial Documentary Letter of Credit issued by the Bermuda bank via SWIFT MT700 for batch procurement from Asian manufacturers, requiring commercial invoice, full set of clean on-board bills of lading, packing list, insurance certificate, and certificate of origin; (3) Fedwire/CHIPS USD clearing through the Bermuda bank's US correspondent for urgent disbursements to US advertising and logistics partners; and (4) corporate Mastercard/Visa virtual cards for recurring SaaS subscriptions and platform fees. Stripe and PayPal payouts arrive as aggregated SWIFT MT103 or US ACH credits from their banking partners (predominantly JP Morgan and Barclays); for ACH-originated flows, instruct the payment processor to switch to SWIFT USD settlement so the Bermuda receiving bank can post same-day value. BMD-denominated local payments (registered agent fees, BMA annual fees) are debited from the BMD sub-account; excess USD liquidity is swept daily to an interest-bearing money market account at the same bank via SWIFT MT101 batch transfer to preserve the 1:1 peg integrity and optimize yield.Outright FX forwards in USD/EUR and USD/GBP contracted with the BMA-licensed authorized dealer, tenor up to 12 months, supported by a commercial underlying exposure letter from the treasury function.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on CNY/USD to hedge Chinese supplier procurement exposure without requiring settleable offshore RMB, permissible for Non-Resident designated companies without BMA prior approval.Cross-currency swaps on USD/EUR and USD/GBP for converting Euro-denominated retail proceeds into USD liquidity, executed under the board-approved treasury derivative policy and hedge-accounting documentation.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium, with significant legal constraints. Bermuda is not on the FATF grey list and is a UK Overseas Territory with strong AML standards, so an offshore EMI is not legally prohibited from servicing a Bermuda Exempted Company on a cross-border basis. However, Bermuda has no EMI licensing regime; an offshore EMI that establishes a local branch, maintains a Bermuda-registered address, or holds itself out to Bermuda residents would require licensing under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 or a BMA payments authorization. In commercial practice, leading UK and EU EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud) frequently decline standalone Bermuda-domiciled corporate applications because the absence of a domestic EMI passport creates correspondent and compliance complexity; approvals are normally conditioned upon a UK or EU parent entity, UK directors, and projected flows above USD 1,000,000. The viable fallback is a split treasury: (i) Butterfield or HSBC Bermuda for the primary corporate account, capital escrow, and SWIFT trade payments; (ii) a US or UK payment-account or EMI solution integrated with Stripe and PayPal for card payouts and fee disbursements. Legal due diligence must cover source-of-funds declarations, restriction of the EMI account to outward operating payments (not customer deposits), and absence of UK/EU deposit protection for Bermuda entities.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File/Master File apply when the group worldwide revenue exceeds BMD 1.35 billion (approximately EUR 750 million). For e-commerce retailer entities below this threshold, no full TP documentation is required, but contemporaneous evidence is expected for cross-border connected-party purchases above BMD 500,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to exempted companies conducting relevant activities, which generally excludes pure online retailing of physical camping goods. However, if the company also holds intangible property, licenses brands, or engages in intra-group distribution services, it must meet the economic substance test by demonstrating physical presence, employees, management, and adequate spending in Bermuda. Annual ESR filings are mandatory even when the entity is not within a relevant activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Filing

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the tax evaded; administrative fine up to BMD 25,000; criminal conviction may attract a fine up to BMD 250,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: A director who signs a false return may be jointly and severally liable for restitution of unpaid tax, disqualified as a director for up to 10 years, subjected to a travel ban, and prosecuted criminally where fraud is proved.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: ESR non-compliance penalties: BMD 100,000–BMD 250,000 per breach; possible strike-off from the Bermuda Registry; cancellation of exempted status; retroactive assessment to 15% CIT on deemed profits.

Director personal liability: Officers face personal fines up to BMD 50,000, mandatory disqualification orders, and potential liability for group back-taxes if the company fails to restore economic substance within the grace period.
Anti-Money Laundering / AML Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Regulations 2023, corporate fines reach BMD 500,000; AML officers face custodial sentences up to 14 years for money laundering and up to 7 years for tipping-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and designated compliance officers are personally criminally liable for participation or wilful blindness; sanctions include prison terms of 3–7 years, lifetime AML officer disqualification, and travel restrictions pending criminal asset tracing.
