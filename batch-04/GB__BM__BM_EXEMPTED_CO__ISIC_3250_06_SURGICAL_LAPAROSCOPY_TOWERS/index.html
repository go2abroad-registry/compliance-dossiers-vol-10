Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The only relevant statutory election is the application for a Tax Exemption Certificate under Bermuda’s exempted company legislation, normally effective until 2035 and renewable annually. Bermuda law provides no check-the-box entity-classification election or hybrid-mismatch election; group-level UK/CBCR and controlled-foreign-company rules may still be assessed at the UK or international level.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Bermuda does not levy a VAT, GST or general sales-and-use tax. It levies narrow indirect taxes: customs duties on imports, stamp duty, hotel occupancy tax, and a payroll tax on employer payroll.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST/SUT registration threshold exists in Bermuda dollars (BMD). A Payroll Tax registration threshold is not turnover-based; registration arises as soon as the exempted company has any local employee.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Bermuda has no VAT/GST and therefore no export zero-rating claim or VAT refund mechanism. Goods exported from Bermuda fall outside Bermuda’s indirect tax base; qualifying imports that are later re-exported may be relieved from customs duty under re-export/duty-suspension rules.

  • Re-Export Undertaking or Duty-Suspension Statement (where customs duty relief is claimed)

None – Bermuda imposes no real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API stamping or digital clearance mandate. Not applicable to VAT zero-rating because no VAT or refund system exists. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Bermuda operates a fully convertible currency regime tied to the Bermudian Dollar (BMD) at a 1:1 fixed peg to the US Dollar (USD). There are no exchange control restrictions on current account transactions, trade-related payments, or dividend distributions for Exempted Companies. Capital account transactions are effectively unrestricted, but the BMA requires statistical disclosure for direct cross-border capital flows exceeding BMD 1,000,000. Profit repatriation is fully permissible without prior BMA approval provided annual economic substance reporting (ESR) obligations are up to date and audited financial statements support the distribution. Non-resident shareholders face zero withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties under Bermuda's territorial tax exemption regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists in Bermuda. Exempted Companies are free to maintain export receipts in any major currency, including USD, EUR, and GBP, without a mandatory conversion timeline. Trade settlements for surgical laparoscopy hardware invoiced in USD can be retained offshore in BMD/USD bank accounts without BMA remittance mandates. The absence of surrender ratios means treasury teams may execute voluntary conversions at optimal FX market levels.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) in coordination with the UK Prudential Regulation Authority for systemic liquidity, and the Bermuda Ministry of Finance for exchange control policy

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium (legally permissible but constrained). Bermuda does not issue standalone e-money institution (EMI) licenses to non-resident offshore entities. An Exempted Company may hold a multi-currency settlement account at a UK-regulated EMI (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business) for ancillary payables, but primary treasury operations must run through licensed Bermuda commercial banks. The BMA's regulatory perimeter excludes passporting rights from UK EMIs into Bermuda. Use of unlicensed digital EMI rails for trade settlements above USD 50,000 per transaction is high-risk and would fail robust KYC underwriting at Tier-1 banks.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% Bermuda corporate income tax on a standard Exempted Company; 15% domestic minimum top-up tax applies only to Bermuda constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated annual revenue of at least EUR 750 million under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (OECD Pillar Two).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividend distributions paid by an Exempted Company, including distributions to non-resident shareholders and UK-resident parent companies.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda statutory Local File or Master File threshold is triggered for a standard Exempted Company outside the scope of the CIT Act 2023. For an MNE group subject to Bermuda's 15% top-up tax, transfer pricing documentation will follow OECD Pillar Two and any administrative guidance issued under the CIT Act; in BMD terms, no filing threshold has yet been gazetted for bodies of evidence or country-by-country reports.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2018 applies to relevant activities consisting of banking, insurance, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, distribution, service centers, shipping, and intellectual property holding. Manufacture of endoscopic surgical hardware under ISIC 3250 is not a named relevant activity on its own, but a significant distribution or service-center function incidental to the endoscopy business will trigger ESR filing obligations. Any entity in scope must demonstrate core income-generating activities, employees, premises, and board management in Bermuda, and must file an annual ES Declaration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exempted Status Claim

Statutory sanction: Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, tax evasion penalties of up to double the understated tax plus daily default fines; loss of good standing; strike-off; release of information to foreign tax authorities. Criminal liability for fraudulent misrepresentation carries fines of up to BMD 50,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly cause or permit the evasion are jointly and severally liable for the evaded top-up tax; civil recovery may be enforced against personal assets. Directors may also face travel bans, disqualification orders, and custodial sentences of up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance (ESR) Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The Economic Substance Act 2018 imposes a fixed penalty of BMD 10,000, a daily penalty of BMD 500 for continued non-compliance (maximum BMD 50,000), and court-referred penalties for serious or repeated breaches, including dissolution and automatic exchange of information with the UK or other foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly participate in the incorrect or false declaration are personally liable for financial penalties, may be subject to disqualification, and may face criminal fines and imprisonment for up to 2 years in cases of perjury or fraud.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Transparency Failure

Statutory sanction: Failure to maintain beneficial ownership records may result in a fine of up to BMD 25,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years, plus daily default fines. Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997, systemic AML deficiencies attract regulatory sanctions, monetary penalties, license revocation, and freeze/confiscation orders on funds or medical equipment.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed to BMA enforcement action, civil penalties, and disqualification. In serious cases involving predicate offenses, directors may be criminally convicted and imprisoned for up to 14 years, and personal assets traceable to the unlawful enterprise may be confiscated.
