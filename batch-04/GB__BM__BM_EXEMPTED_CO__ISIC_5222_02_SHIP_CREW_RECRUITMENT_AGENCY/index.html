E-government filing portal

Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, and an Exempted Company receives a Business Register Number (BRN) automatically at incorporation under the Companies Act 1981 through a Bermuda-resident registered agent/attorney. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Bermuda Exempted Company is protected by the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing exemption from all Bermuda corporate income, dividend, capital gains and profits taxes until March 2035 (extendable to 2055); it remains subject to Payroll Tax on Bermuda-source employee remuneration, social insurance contributions, land tax and customs duties.

Available election forms

None domestically — Bermuda law provides no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the Exempted Company may file IRS Form 8832 to elect disregarded-entity, partnership or corporation classification. For UK/OECD purposes, the entity must file a Bermuda Economic Substance Annual Declaration; if management and control are exercised from the UK, UK CFC, residence and anti-hybrid rules may apply, but no domestic anti-hybrid election is available under Bermudian law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax — Bermuda imposes only customs and import duties (Customs Act 1957, Customs Tariff Act 1951) and stamp duties on certain instruments; the crew recruitment agency service itself is not subject to any Bermuda indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Bermuda has no VAT/GST registration threshold. Importers of commercial goods must register as customs importers or bonded users with the Bermuda Customs Department and, where required, hold a customs broker licence; crew-management services supplied to non-resident shipowners are outside the scope of any Bermudian indirect tax charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Bermuda operates no VAT/GST regime, so no export zero-rating claim or input-VAT refund mechanism exists. Crew recruitment and vessel-manning services rendered to non-resident shipping companies are offshore services outside Bermuda's tax net and are covered by the Tax Protection Certificate shield. If the recipient shipowner is UK-established, the UK B2B place-of-supply rule may deem the service supplied in the UK, triggering UK VAT at 20% under the reverse-charge mechanism; no zero-rating applies to crew-supply services.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar (USD), and USD is accepted as legal tender for all domestic transactions. The exchange control regime distinguishes between resident and non-resident entities. An Exempted Company is deemed non-resident for exchange control purposes and therefore enjoys full current and capital account convertibility without obtaining prior BMA approval for ordinary treasury operations. BMD and foreign currency accounts may be maintained freely at an authorized bank. Profit remittance by way of dividend, interest, or fee repatriation is not blocked or subject to any withholding or conversion stipulations, provided the company is solvent and the payment is authorized by the board of directors. For resident entities, current account payments are automatically permitted, while capital account movements require BMA notification or consent; for non-resident Exempted Companies, this hurdle is effectively disapplied.

Export proceeds surrender rules

In Bermuda, resident exporters are subject to the Exchange Control Act 1972 surrender obligations requiring that eligible foreign currency export proceeds be offered to an authorized dealer in exchange for BMD within a statutory surrender window, generally 180 days from receipt, at a mandatory conversion ratio of 100%. However, a Bermuda Exempted Company classified as non-resident is expressly exempt from mandatory conversion or surrender rules for receipts derived from overseas shipowning clients. Export proceeds and service fees earned by the crew recruitment agency may be retained in the original currency (USD, GBP, EUR) or any freely convertible currency without timeline restrictions or conversion ratios. The only statutory requirement is that the beneficiary account be correctly labelled as a 'non-resident operational account' and that the authorized bank maintain standard exchange control records for periodic BMA examination.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972, together with the Exchange Control Regulations 1973.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Moderate with legal constraints. Bermuda does not operate a dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime; an offshore digital EMI seeking a local BMA presence must obtain either a banking licence, a deposit-taking licence, or a restricted financial investment business licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and the Financial Investment Business Act 2003. Major cross-border fintech EMIs (e.g., Wise, Payoneer) generally cannot hold Bermuda settlement accounts without a BMA licence and an authorized dealer designation under the Exchange Control Act. They may, however, be used as non-settlement payout rails solely for paying seafarers outside Bermuda, provided no Bermudian resident account is credited and no local currency clearing is involved. The treasury should therefore concentrate liquidity in BMA-licensed banks and use EMIs only as a low-value disbursement channel for individual crew payroll in jurisdictions where Swift MT103 is uneconomical.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

High-frequency outward payments to individual seafarers domiciled in FATF grey-listed or sanctioned jurisdictions (e.g., Myanmar, Yemen, Syria, Belarus).

Root cause: High-frequency outward payments to individual seafarers domiciled in FATF grey-listed or sanctioned jurisdictions (e.g., Myanmar, Yemen, Syria, Belarus).

Defense evidence: Completed crew onboarding pack for every seafarer, including certified passport copy, seaman's book, valid STCW or equivalent maritime certificates, signed individual employment contract with wage ledger, and an incorporated vessel-specific payroll schedule showing gross-to-net calculations. The agency must produce an OFAC, EU, UN and UK sanctions screening report matched to each crew member, a vessel IMO number, and a written authorization from the vessel owner or ship manager identifying the payroll run and period.
Disproportionate aggregate inward amounts from a single shipowner relative to payroll funds out, suggesting possible money laundering or unlicensed remittance.

Root cause: Disproportionate aggregate inward amounts from a single shipowner relative to payroll funds out, suggesting possible money laundering or unlicensed remittance.

Defense evidence: A fully executed ship manning and crew management agreement between the Bermuda Exempted Company and the shipowner, current invoices cross-referenced to the vessel name, IMO number and payroll period, and a monthly reconciliation summary demonstrating agency fee (typically 3–8%) plus actual crew wages. Additional support includes the shipowner's certified incorporation documents, beneficial ownership declaration, and an explanation of the business relationship with reference to the underlying vessel commercial operations.
Cash or digital prepaid card advances for crew mobilisation/demobilisation expenses, classified as potential structuring or undisclosed cash flow.

Root cause: Cash or digital prepaid card advances for crew mobilisation/demobilisation expenses, classified as potential structuring or undisclosed cash flow.

Defense evidence: Detailed per-seafarer travel itinerary, port agent invoices, ticket receipts, signed cash advance forms (with crew member signature and passport number), and the internal travel allowance policy, together with board approval minutes authorizing the cash advancement mechanism and an AML policy clause covering cash-like transactions.
Opacity in ownership structure due to nominee shareholders, discretionary trusts, or layered holding companies that obscure ultimate beneficial ownership.

Root cause: Opacity in ownership structure due to nominee shareholders, discretionary trusts, or layered holding companies that obscure ultimate beneficial ownership.

Defense evidence: A formal beneficial ownership register filed with the Bermuda Registrar under the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Amendment Act 2017, a chain-of-control organisational chart, certified copies of trust deeds or foundation constitutional documents, an UBO identification form with passport and address verification, and either a BMA no-objection letter or reliance on the bank's direct BMA register inspection.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections into the Bermuda Exempted Company should be routed as a SWIFT MT103 from the lending or subscribing bank to the company's BMD or USD operational account at a BMA-licensed bank. The beneficiary account must be designated 'non-resident operational account'. The message field 70 (Remittance Information) must state the precise purpose, e.g., 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION - ISSUANCE OF SHARES' or 'INTERCOMPANY LOAN', and the corresponding supporting documentation (directors' resolution, subscription agreement or loan agreement) must be pre-delivered to the receiving bank. Prior BMA approval is not required for non-resident capital movements, but the bank will verify the source of funds and may require a certification of beneficial ownership before crediting. To maintain a clean audit trail, the remitting shareholder should ensure that the ordering customer name exactly matches the registered shareholder name, and all intercompany financing exceeding USD 100,000 should also be registered in the company's accounting records and reported to the Bermuda Registrar where applicable.Outbound crew payroll and supplier settlements are cleared primarily via SWIFT MT103 individual credit transfers or MT202 COV for multi-bank intercompany settlement. For recurring monthly crew payroll, the treasury should establish a multi-currency host-to-host payroll module with the Bermuda bank, allowing automated batch generation of MT103 messages to seafarer beneficiary banks worldwide. For voyage-level disbursements, such as advance payment to a port agent, bunker supplier, or sub-agent, the agency may instruct its Bermuda bank to issue a commercial letter of credit (L/C) in favor of the beneficiary, or if open account terms exist, a plain SWIFT MT103 with invoice/contract narrative. All outgoing payments from a non-resident account are immediately remittable without exchange control gatekeeping, provided the bank's real-time sanctions screening is satisfied and the transaction is authorised by dual signatures or token-based 2FA under the bank's payment control policy. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Bermuda levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or any form of direct taxation on profits for standard exempted companies; however, a 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) applies under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 exclusively to Bermuda-resident constituent entities of in-scope multinational enterprise groups whose consolidated annual revenue is at least EUR 750M in at least two of the four fiscal years immediately preceding the tested fiscal year.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Part 1 - Company Status Details Legal name, Bermuda registration number, date of incorporation, exempted company designation, and any licence or authorization status. Non-resident owners must ensure the corporate name and registration number exactly match the Certificate of Incorporation; any corporate name change requires prior Registrar approval. Late filing triggers a statutory default fine plus a continuing daily penalty imposed by the Registrar; repeated default may lead to strike-off of the company from the Register of Exempted Companies, loss of the tax protection undertaking, and restoration only by court order with payment of arrears, penalties, and legal costs.
Part 2 - Registered Office Bermuda registered office street address at which process may be served; a post office box alone is insufficient. Use the physical street address of the licensed registered agent; maintaining a Bermuda registered office is a non-waivable statutory requirement. Late filing triggers a statutory default fine plus a continuing daily penalty imposed by the Registrar; repeated default may lead to strike-off of the company from the Register of Exempted Companies, loss of the tax protection undertaking, and restoration only by court order with payment of arrears, penalties, and legal costs.
Part 3 - Directors and Officers Full names, residential or usual addresses, nationality, and dates of appointment of all directors and officers. Non-Bermudian directors must provide identification documentation and a valid address for service of process; a natural-person Bermuda-resident director is strongly advisable for ESR compliance. Late filing triggers a statutory default fine plus a continuing daily penalty imposed by the Registrar; repeated default may lead to strike-off of the company from the Register of Exempted Companies, loss of the tax protection undertaking, and restoration only by court order with payment of arrears, penalties, and legal costs.
Part 4 - Share Capital Authorized share capital, classes and par value of shares, and total issued share capital of the exempted company. Beneficial ownership particulars are maintained in the confidential register at the registered office and are not publicly disclosed in the annual return for private exempted companies. Late filing triggers a statutory default fine plus a continuing daily penalty imposed by the Registrar; repeated default may lead to strike-off of the company from the Register of Exempted Companies, loss of the tax protection undertaking, and restoration only by court order with payment of arrears, penalties, and legal costs.
Part 5 - Compliance Declaration Certificate of continuing compliance with the Companies Act 1981, including the economic substance declaration where a relevant activity is conducted. Where the crew recruitment agency is treated as a service centre, the director must certify compliance with the Economic Substance Regulations before signing the return.
