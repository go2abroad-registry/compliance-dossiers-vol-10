Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no domestic check-the-box election. For US federal tax purposes the Exempted Company can file Form 8832 to elect classification as a disregarded entity or partnership. Under CITA 2023, an in-scope entity may elect group treatment/consolidated filing with Bermudian affiliates, loss-transfer elections, and the application of the OECD Pillar Two qualified income inclusion rules; hybrid mismatch allocations follow the GloBE rules, and no Bermuda law creates a separate hybrid-mismatch check-the-box.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax — Bermuda relies exclusively on import customs duties, stamp duties and payroll tax; supplies of goods and services (including offshore commodity trading) are outside the scope of any consumption tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT/GST registration threshold in Bermuda because no such tax exists. An importer of dutiable goods must obtain a Bermuda Customs Importer Code; goods merely transshipped or exported by ocean vessel are declared in ASYCUDA World as transit cargo and attract zero customs duty. A commercial sugar cargo policy of 60-90 days deferred payment terms does not trigger any indirect tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No zero-rating claim is available or required because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST on exports. Ocean-going shipments of VHP raw cane sugar (HS 1701.14) chartered from non-Bermuda ports to non-Bermuda ports are outside Bermuda customs territory; the Exempted Company only files a transit/export declaration when vessels touch Bermuda. Under CITA 2023, the trading profit of the Bermuda Exempted Company is subject to 15% CIT only if the company belongs to an MNE group with global revenue ≥ EUR 750M and the activity is managed and controlled in Bermuda; a fully offshore principal-to-principal arbitrage structure with no Bermuda nexus produces no Bermuda taxable income.

  • ASYCUDA World Transit/Export Declaration (Bermuda Customs, if vessel enters Bermuda waters)
  • Original Ocean Bill of Lading evidencing port-to-port carriage of VHP raw cane sugar
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (ISIC 4631 / HS 1701.14)
  • Certificate of Origin and Certificate of Polarization (ICUMSA pol reading, sugar polarization ± 96°Z)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/LC payment evidence
  • Charterparty, vessel nomination or mate's receipt

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance stamping, or government API clearance mandate exists in Bermuda. Commercial invoices are privately issued and do not require digital stamping. All customs clearance, transit, and export messages must be transmitted electronically through the ASYCUDA World single-window system, and CIT/payroll filings are made through the Bermuda Revenue Authority Taxpayer Access Point (TAP). Electronic records must be retained for at least six years.

Since there is no VAT, no VAT clawback or zero-rating penalty can arise. Enforcement risks are: (i) formalities penalties (administrative fines) for failure to file or fraudulent relief claims under CITA 2023 — up to BMD 100,000 plus interest and 10-20% underpaid-tax penalties for negligent or fraudulent returns; (ii) economic substance penalties under the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 for a distribution-and-service-centre activity: initial fine BMD 250,000, continuing default BMD 500,000, and potential imprisonment of a director; (iii) customs penalties under the Customs Act 1946 for a false transit/export declaration — forfeiture of cargo and/or a fine of up to three times the goods' value; (iv) retrospective reassessment and interest if artificial sugar-arbitrage transactions are recharacterised under CITA anti-avoidance rules and the GloBE IIR framework.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 parity to the US Dollar, and USD legal tender circulates interchangeably in Bermuda under a quasi-currency-board arrangement, resulting in full de facto current account convertibility and unrestricted capital account movements for non-resident designated entities. An Exempted Company granted 'non-resident' status under the Exchange Control Act 1972 may hold, borrow, lend, and remit funds in any currency without BMA approval; profit remittance, dividend distribution, capital reduction, and liquidation proceeds may be transferred abroad freely. Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no withholding tax, no capital gains tax, and no remittance tax; the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 allows the Minister of Finance to issue a certificate guaranteeing the company will not be subject to any Bermuda tax enacted after 31 March 2035. The only practical hurdles to profit remittance are corporate formalities under the Companies Act 1981, specifically the directors' solvency statement for distributions and share-premium reductions, and bank-level AML/KYC verification of the underlying trade transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no export proceeds surrender obligation, no statutory conversion ratio, and no FX approval requirement for a non-resident designated Exempted Company; export receivables may be retained in any currency in any BMA-licensed bank or offshore account and redeployed without permission. The only mandatory compliance elements are: (i) five-year retention of trade and payment records, including export contracts, invoices, bills of lading, and SWIFT confirmations, under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008; and (ii) annual statutory corporate filings with the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981. Wholesale commodity trading in raw cane sugar is not a 'relevant activity' under the Bermuda economic substance legislation, so no economic substance test or filing arises from the trading activity itself.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), constituted under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, serving as Bermuda's central bank, currency board authority, exchange control administrator, and prudential regulator of all licensed banks and deposit-taking institutions. Exchange control powers are exercised under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973, with BMA-licensed commercial banks acting as 'authorised dealers'. The BMA also enforces the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999, the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008, and the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Amendment Act 2019 for corporate transparency.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as a primary banking rail, MEDIUM-LOW as an ancillary payment channel. UK/EEA Electronic Money Institutions (Revolut Business, Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer) cannot passport their home-state licences into Bermuda and are not recognised deposit-takers under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; they cannot issue, advise, or confirm letters of credit and cannot provide secured trade facilities. A Bermuda Exempted Company may lawfully use such EMIs for operational payments (freight, port costs, brokerage fees) only where the EMI's own compliance framework permits servicing a Bermuda corporate client; however, the resulting transaction trail must be fully reconcilable in the primary bank's AML/KYC monitoring and in the company's statutory accounting records. Bermuda's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 and the Business Development (Sandbox) Act 2020 create a BMA-licensed fintech category, but no 'EMI equivalence' licence exists for offshore non-Bermuda entities. For physical vessel-borne sugar trade, commercial settlement (LC, MT103, MT760 guarantees) must remain executed through a BMA-licensed bank with established correspondent banking relationships.

Root cause: Trade documentation authenticity — missing or inconsistent weight, polarization, and moisture analysis on VHP raw cane sugar, raising the risk of value manipulation, LC fraud, or invoice/surveyor mismatch.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (i) the subscriber's bank sends a SWIFT MT103 (for amounts below USD 10 million) or MT202 with full cover through the BMA bank's correspondent, normally a US clearer via CHIPS/ABA for USD, or a London/EU clearer for GBP/EUR; (ii) the remittance information must state 'SHARE CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION - CONTRIBUTION TO CAPITAL OF BERMUDA EXEMPTED COMPANY [NAME] - REF [BOARD RESOLUTION DATE]', and the sending bank must apply the IMF BPM6 classification code E411 ('Foreign Direct Investment - Equity Capital') or the UK HMRC ITRS code A10 where the remitter is UK-resident; (iii) the receiving bank requires a pre-notification package comprising the certified certificate of incorporation, board resolutions authorising the account opening and share allotment, the register of members, the beneficial-ownership register, and the non-resident designation confirmation from the CSP; (iv) upon receipt, the bank credits a USD Escrow sub-account and releases funds to the operational account only after the share certificate is issued and the register of members is updated. No BMA exchange-control approval is required for non-resident designated companies; the only BMA interface is routine cash-transaction reporting under AML/ATF rules. For injections above USD 1 million, the beneficial owner must supply a source-of-funds declaration and the remitting bank's confirmation of the underlying instrument.Clearing rails for physical sugar settlements, in order of preference: (1) Revocable? No - irrevocable documentary Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700 and governed by UCP600, with a 30 to 90-day usance tenor under a master purchase contract; the seller's bank may act as advising or confirming bank, and the same LC facility may be structured as a revolving LC replenished upon each vessel's successful discharge. (2) Back-to-back or transferable LC (MT710/MT720 series) where the company on-sells sugar to a final refinery/commodity house and mirrors the documents received from the original producer. (3) Direct SWIFT MT103 wire payments for deposits, freight, demurrage, insurance premiums, and ancillary logistics costs, with the beneficiary reference field stating 'TRADE PAYMENT - RAW CANE SUGAR VHP [QUANTITY] - CONTRACT NO. [X]'. (4) SWIFT MT760 standby LC or guarantee to secure the company's obligations as charterer or as buyer under a vessel-owner contract. Settlement is honoured only against a complying presentation: full set 3/3 original clean on-board bills of lading, commercial invoice, independent surveyor weight certificate, ICUMSA-polarization analysis certificate, radiation-free certificate, certificate of origin, fumigation certificate, and a letter of indemnity where a bill of lading is tardy. USD disbursements clear via the bank's US correspondent; non-USD disbursements clear via London or Frankfurt; the trade facility draws on a borrowing base of 80-90% of cargo value against the presented documentary collateral.Outright FX forwards (USD/GBP, USD/EUR, BMD/GBP, BMD/EUR) executed with the relationship bank under the ISDA 2002 Master Agreement, or under a bilateral FX-line confirmation; no BMA permit is required for non-resident designated Exempted Companies.Cross-currency swaps (USD/GBP, USD/THB, USD/SGD) for aligning trade-finance borrowings with non-USD settlement currencies where sugar is purchased from Thailand, Brazil, or Europe, subject to the bank's standard treasury credit approval and standard ISDA CSA collateral terms.European-style FX options (USD/GBP, USD/EUR) to protect the EBITDA margin from freight, port, and insurance cost volatility when such costs are denominated in Sterling or Euro; permitted only with a BMA-licensed bank or an authorised derivatives intermediary.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on restricted or illiquid currencies (e.g., USD/ARS for Argentinian sugar origin, USD/EGP for Egyptian origin) executed through the bank's offshore derivatives desk, with settlement in USD on a notional delta basis without requiring onshore currency access.BMD/USD hedging instruments are not applicable because the Bermudian Dollar is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar; a spot conversion at par is available at any authorised dealer and entails no market risk requiring a hedge.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW as a primary banking rail, MEDIUM-LOW as an ancillary payment channel. UK/EEA Electronic Money Institutions (Revolut Business, Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer) cannot passport their home-state licences into Bermuda and are not recognised deposit-takers under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999; they cannot issue, advise, or confirm letters of credit and cannot provide secured trade facilities. A Bermuda Exempted Company may lawfully use such EMIs for operational payments (freight, port costs, brokerage fees) only where the EMI's own compliance framework permits servicing a Bermuda corporate client; however, the resulting transaction trail must be fully reconcilable in the primary bank's AML/KYC monitoring and in the company's statutory accounting records. Bermuda's Digital Asset Business Act 2018 and the Business Development (Sandbox) Act 2020 create a BMA-licensed fintech category, but no 'EMI equivalence' licence exists for offshore non-Bermuda entities. For physical vessel-borne sugar trade, commercial settlement (LC, MT103, MT760 guarantees) must remain executed through a BMA-licensed bank with established correspondent banking relationships.
