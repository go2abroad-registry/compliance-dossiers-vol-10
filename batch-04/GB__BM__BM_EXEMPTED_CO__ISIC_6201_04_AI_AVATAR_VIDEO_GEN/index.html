Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Tax Authority (Office of the Tax Commissioner) — BermudaTaxes portal; Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Finance (corporate registry, exempted-company annual government fee); Bermuda Customs Service (customs duties and import/export clearances).

E-government filing portal

A foreign founder establishes the Bermuda Exempted Company through a licensed Bermuda registered agent under the Companies Act 1981: (1) file the Memorandum of Association, subscribe shares, and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Companies; (2) the unique Company Number issued by the Registrar functions as the primary statutory taxpayer identifier; (3) if the company employs Bermuda-resident staff, register as an employer for payroll tax and social insurance with the Office of the Tax Commissioner and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number; (4) if the entity becomes a constituent entity of a multinational group with consolidated global revenue of EUR 750 million or more, register for the 15% Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (CIT Act 2023, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025) through the BermudaTaxes portal within the statutory notification window (generally 30 days of becoming in-scope) and obtain a CIT registration account; (5) for ordinary AI SaaS exporters below that threshold, no income-tax registration or annual filing is required beyond the Registrar of Companies annual return and government fee.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Bermuda Exempted Company outside the scope of the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 is not subject to Bermuda income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, dividend withholding tax, or remittance tax. It is an entity-level legal person taxed at a 0% effective rate on its income, and it is not a pass-through vehicle for Bermuda tax purposes. Residual Bermuda charges are limited to payroll tax and social insurance on Bermuda-source salaries (if employees are engaged in Bermuda), customs import duty on physical goods, stamp duty on certain instruments, the 5% Foreign Currency Purchase Tax on specific non-trade conversions, and the annual exempted-company government fee.

Available election forms

No Bermuda income-tax classification election (check-the-box) exists for an ordinary Bermuda Exempted Company; all such companies are treated as separate taxable persons under Bermuda law. If the entity later falls within the CIT Act 2023 as part of a large multinational group, the Tax Administration Act 2024 and CIT Act provide for a Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) safe-harbour election, transitional relief elections aligned with the OECD GloBE Pillar Two rules, and group-level loss and credit allocation elections. For US federal income tax purposes only, the Bermuda Exempted Company is an eligible foreign entity and may be able to file IRS Form 8832 (check-the-box) to elect partnership or disregarded-entity classification, subject to CFC, anti-hybrid, and anti-abuse rules; hybrid mismatch analysis is performed solely under the investor's home jurisdiction rules (e.g., UK CTA 2010 Part 6A or US IRC Section 267A), not under Bermuda law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax in Bermuda. The only Bermuda indirect taxes are customs import duties (Customs Tariff Act 1971), stamp duty on specified instruments and land transactions (Stamp Act 1976), and the 5% Foreign Currency Purchase Tax on certain Bermuda-dollar-to-foreign-currency conversions for non-trade purposes. Cross-border AI video-generation SaaS services are outside all Bermuda indirect taxes.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no turnover-based indirect-tax registration threshold exists in Bermuda because there is no VAT/GST. For customs purposes, any importer of physical goods into Bermuda must file an electronic import declaration through ASYCUDA World without any minimum monetary threshold; an exempted company importing AI server hardware would also engage a licensed customs broker. No FCPT or stamp-duty registration regime applies to a pure digital exporter.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Bermuda has no VAT or sales tax, there is no 0% export rate, no input-VAT credit, and no refund or zero-rating application. The AI avatar video-generation SaaS subscriptions exported from Bermuda are outside the scope of Bermuda taxation entirely: no Bermuda CIT (for the exempted company below the EUR 750M MNE threshold), no Bermuda GST on the supply, and no Bermuda withholding tax on export consideration. For customers in UK/EU VAT jurisdictions, the place of supply of digital services is the customer's jurisdiction, so the customer accounts for reverse-charge VAT (where applicable) and no Bermuda fiscal charge arises. Tangible goods exported from Bermuda likewise attract no Bermuda export duty, so no substantive refund claim is required.

  • Customs Export Declaration — electronic filing in ASYCUDA World under the Customs Tariff Act 1971; required only for tangible goods physically exported from Bermuda and not applicable to cross-border AI SaaS or digital delivery
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill — transport document for tangible exports only; for digital services, the SaaS master agreement, usage and delivery logs, and bandwidth consumption records serve as delivery evidence
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment instruction — evidence of offshore consideration received from non-Bermuda customers, retained for economic-substance, transfer-pricing, and foreign tax-credit substantiation; no Bermuda exchange-control approval is required for such receipts

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government API validation mandate applies to Bermuda domestic or exported digital supplies. Physical import/export clearances are effected electronically through the ASYCUDA World single-window system. Invoices, contracts, delivery logs, and substance records must be retained under the Tax Administration Act 2024 record-keeping rules (generally six years) to substantiate the tax-exempt position and support reverse-charge compliance in the customer's VAT jurisdiction.

Bermuda has no VAT, so no export zero-rating clawback exposure exists. Material compliance exposures are: (1) tax administration penalties under the CIT Act 2023 and Tax Administration Act 2024 for failure to register, late returns, false statements, or fraudulent evasion — statutory monetary penalties plus daily continuing penalties and criminal liability; (2) Economic Substance Act 2015 — fines up to BMD 250,000 and up to three years' imprisonment for non-compliance with enforcement notices where a relevant activity (e.g., financing, IP, or headquarters business) is conducted; (3) payroll-tax and social-insurance underreporting — interest, penalties, and criminal sanctions; (4) customs misdeclaration — seizure and condemnation of goods plus penalties of up to three times the duty evaded; and (5) no Bermuda statutory FX repatriation deadline exists — the 180-day parameter is a common international documentary benchmark and is not imposed by the Bermuda Monetary Authority. Falsifying export records to obtain benefits in another jurisdiction would be prosecuted locally as fraud or through mutual legal assistance channels.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at parity to the United States Dollar (USD) (1 BMD = 1 USD), giving de facto full convertibility for USD-denominated treasury flows. Current account convertibility is unrestricted for exempted companies: payments for software licensing, cloud infrastructure, marketing, and contractor services may be settled in USD via any BMA-authorised dealer without case-by-case exchange control approval. Capital account convertibility is likewise liberal for non-resident owned exempted companies; capital contributions, loans, and profit remittances in USD or other major currencies are permitted through the 'External Account' mechanism, provided the company has been designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Act. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal but procedural: a company must present a board resolution, audited financial statements or management accounts, and a current certificate of solvency or solvency declaration to its banker to execute dividend and capital withdrawals. If a local BMD balance is ever required, conversion at the 1:1 peg is automatic, and the risk of inconvertibility is effectively nil because US dollar clearing is the standard settlement rail throughout Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. An exempted company receiving foreign currency in respect of its SaaS exports may retain such proceeds indefinitely in an External Account or a Non-Resident USD Account with an authorised dealer. There is no obligation to convert into BMD, and no central bank surrender certificate is required. In practice, because the BMD is pegged 1:1 to the USD, any invoice expressed in BMD is settled at par with USD, eliminating the need for a separate surrender calculation. For an AI avatar video generation business collecting subscription fees via card processors and international wires, all export intangible revenues can be held and settled in USD without exchange control restriction.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), operating as the central bank and foreign exchange administration under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Banks and Deposits Companies Act 1999, and the Exchange Control Act 1972. The BMA regulates both monetary stability and the licensing of all authorised dealers and deposit-taking institutions in Bermuda.

Inbound equity or loan capital from the UK parent or non-resident shareholders is routed via SWIFT MT103/TT wire in USD over Fedwire/CHIPS to the Bermuda bank's correspondent account (typically Citibank New York or JPMorgan Chase for Butterfield/HSBC). The remitter must include a detailed payment narration: 'Capital injection for ordinary shares of [Bermuda Company]' or 'Shareholder loan under facility agreement dated [date]'; for MIRO code accuracy, the UK remitter should use purpose code 'P0020 — Equity Investment' or the applicable IFM/customs purpose code for equity participation in a non-resident enterprise. The receiving Bermuda bank will require verification documents before crediting the capital account: a board resolution approving the issue or loan, an executed subscription agreement, the regulatory capital notification form if applicable, and a source of funds statement from the remitting shareholder. Where funds arrive from the UK through Stripe/Paddle settlement (e.g., pre-seed financing via payment processing on behalf of customers), the bank will treat them as operating inflows unless accompanied by a reconciliation statement; to maintain the Clean Capital Escrow designation, separate sizeable injections must be sent via direct wire with a clear purpose code, and not through card processors.For recurring operating expenditures (cloud GPU capacity, content moderation, legal fees, and subcontractor payments), the primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 in USD/BMD via the Bermuda bank's correspondent network. Vendor payments under USD 5,000 may be batched through the bank's online platform using transfer instructions keyed to the vendor's IBAN and BIC; amounts above that threshold require standard anti-money-laundering checks. For one-time high-value technology procurement or licensing arrangements (e.g., entering into an enterprise AI infrastructure agreement above USD 250,000), a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) with SWIFT MT700 is permissible to cover delivery milestone or acceptance-condition payments; however the underlying service is intangible, so a standby LC for performance guarantee is more common than a documentary collection. Dividend distributions to the UK parent are cleared via MT103 with narration 'Dividend distribution from Bermuda exempted company', accompanied by a board resolution and solvency certificate; no Exchange Control approval is required for non-resident shareholders. For subscription-based SaaS receipts from worldwide customers, the settlement rail is not a corporate wire but card-network settlement through Stripe/Paddle, which aggregates payouts via MT103 in USD to the Bermuda bank; the bank's monitoring will require a mapping of these payouts against the merchant contracts.Forward currency contracts in USD/BMD at par (no economic necessity given the peg, but available through Butterfield and HSBC derivatives desks)Cross-currency forwards between USD and GBP/EUR for redeeming UK shareholder distributions or paying European contractors; execution via the bank's London branch or a BMA-authorised dealerNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in BMD or USD for hedging future GBP/EUR cash flows when no underlying physical BMD settlement is requiredCurrency swaps between USD and GBP/EUR for longer-dated balance-sheet hedging, subject to board approval and ISDA documentation with the licensed counterpartyForeign exchange spot contracts through the Bermuda bank's online dealing platform when converting BMD to USD at par or USD to GBP for operational expenses

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM for Bermuda-exempted treasury. Legal constraint: no independent EMI licence exists in Bermuda; any fintech electronic-money institution would need a full bank licence under the Banks and Deposits Companies Act 1999 or, if handling digital assets, registration under the Digital Asset Business Act 2018. Offshore EMI providers such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Airwallex can be used only as auxiliary payment-region rails for receiving card subscription proceeds from EU/UK and for making small vendor disbursements. They are not regulated by the BMA and cannot be used to evidence a Bermuda banking connection, satisfy exchange-control residency designation, or hold capital escrow balances. For a jurisdiction substance file, the EMI must be linked to the primary Butterfield or HSBC account via automatic sweeps, and all material custody balances must reside in the BMA-supervised bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD equivalent of EUR 750 million group consolidated revenue (BMD pegged 1:1 to USD). In-scope MNE groups must prepare and maintain OECD-aligned Local File and Master File for each Bermuda constituent entity. Smaller exempted companies have no mandatory local Bermuda TP documentation threshold, but the UK parent must maintain contemporaneous worldwide documentation covering all cross-border transactions with the Bermuda entity if UK taxable profits are affected.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) 2018 apply to resident entities carrying on relevant activities, including pure holding and IP-related businesses. An exempted company deriving income from AI video synthesis IP must file an economic substance declaration or annual economic substance return. If it claims Bermuda tax residence, it must demonstrate directed and managed in Bermuda, physical premises, qualified employees, and adequate expenditure. Non-resident exempted companies may be outside ESR, but the UK parent must still justify no CFC charge.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / deliberate CIT under-declaration (Bermuda CIT Act 2023)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 200% of the underpaid tax plus daily default surcharges; interest at 1% per month; on criminal conviction fines up to BMD 200,000.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers face civil restitution, travel bans, disqualification for up to five years, and imprisonment not exceeding four years.
Shell company / economic substance failure / beneficial ownership non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the Bermuda register and loss of separate legal personality; ESR penalties up to BMD 50,000 plus BMD 5,000 per day; BO register penalties up to BMD 10,000 and two years imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil, impose personal liability for UK and Bermuda taxes, issue travel bans, and order imprisonment up to four years for false BO declarations.
Transfer pricing mispricing / UK CFC attribution

Statutory sanction: UK HMRC transfer pricing adjustment increasing UK taxable income; penalties up to 100% of the additional tax for careless or deliberate documentation failures; Bermuda CIT Act also disallows non-arm's-length arrangements in the Pillar Two calculation.

Director personal liability: Senior accounting officers may be personally penalised GBP 5,000 each under UK Finance Act 2022; directors who knowingly facilitate CFC profit diversion can face criminal liability and imprisonment up to seven years.
AML and deepfake fraud / proceeds of image-generation software misuse

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Act penalties: unlimited fines on indictment, asset forfeiture, loss of Stripe/Paddle merchant access, and BMA regulatory fines up to BMD 500,000 for AML failures.

Director personal liability: Directors involved in or wilfully blind to money laundering may face prosecution, asset restraint and freezing orders, travel bans, civil recovery, and imprisonment up to fourteen years.
