Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Bermuda Ministry of Finance; tax portal and business filing facilities via gov.bm and the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; customs processes via Bermuda Customs Department under the Revenue Division

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company files for incorporation with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies. For tax purposes it does not receive an income tax TIN because Bermuda levies no income tax. It must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for Payroll Tax Account Number if it has employees, and obtain a Customs Business/Importer Code from the Bermuda Customs Department for any import/export or courier-related customs transactions. Non-resident ownership is permitted and no minimum capital is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No Bermuda check-the-box election exists because Bermuda exempted companies are automatically treated as non-resident and exempt from Bermuda income tax. Hybrid mismatch elections under UK or US CFC/check-the-box rules may be made in the investor's home jurisdiction if the entity is treated as a tax-transparent or opaque entity there, subject to those jurisdictions' rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Bermuda applies import duty on goods imported into Bermuda and payroll tax on Bermuda-source payroll. Export of goods is outside the scope of Bermuda indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because none exists; import duty registration is triggered by the first importation into Bermuda without any turnover threshold, stated in BMD

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda has no VAT or GST and therefore no export zero-rating mechanism. Goods shipped from Bermuda are generally outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation; no refund claim or zero-rating approval is required.

Not applicable. Bermuda does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based e-invoicing. Customs declarations are filed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs management system for clearance of imported and exported goods.

False export declarations, undervaluation of goods, failure to produce supporting shipping documentation, or deliberate misdescription of exported cargo are offences under Bermuda customs legislation. Conviction may result in monetary penalties per consignment, forfeiture of the goods, imprisonment up to five years, and revocation or suspension of the Bermuda customs broker licence or operator licence. Because no VAT refund exists, there is no VAT clawback regime; however, customs duty drawback claims, if made, are subject to substantiation audits and can be retroactively denied with penalties for fraudulent claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. Bermuda operates an open exchange control system for non-resident owned exempted companies: capital account transactions, equity injections, and profit remittances (dividends, management fees, liquidation proceeds) are freely permitted without prior BMA approval, subject to reporting thresholds for statistical and AML/CFT purposes. There are no practical hurdles to converting BMD to USD, GBP, or EUR, as the fixed peg provides full convertibility through the licensed banking system; however, any transaction involving sanctioned jurisdictions triggers enhanced due diligence and potential denial.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio apply to export receipts or service-derived foreign currency earnings. Bermuda does not require a Bermudian exporter or cross-border courier service provider to repatriate export proceeds within a statutory timeline or convert a specified percentage into BMD. Foreign currency receipts may be retained in a licensed bank in BMD or any convertible foreign currency account. Any filing obligation is limited to statistical trade reporting under the Bermuda Customs regime, not exchange control surrender.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969 and the Exchange Control Act 1972; the BMA is the integrated financial services regulator and central monetary authority for Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Medium-Low. Bermuda has no domestic EMI licence law, so a UK FCA-authorised e-money institution or a Gibraltar-based EMI may be engaged on a white-label basis for card acquiring and cross-border payments. Such an EMI must maintain a correspondent banking relationship with a Bermuda licensed bank to clear BMD/USD settlements. Significant legal constraints arise from the BMA's customer due diligence expectations, sanctions screening, and the fact that foreign EMIs are treated as third-party processors requiring contractual due diligence. Direct settlement in BMD is usually impossible; USD/GBP settlement via segregated client accounts is acceptable for a courier business collecting from clients in multiple jurisdictions.

The wire instruction must clearly state 'equity contribution – capital injection' and reference the Bermuda exempted company's BMA identification number. The ISO 20022 purpose code must be 'EQUI' (Equity) or 'GUPI' (General Investment Payment), with the recipient legal name and address matching the account mandate. Part C — Tax remittance Computed employer-only payroll tax due to the Government of Bermuda. Where the express courier payroll is booked in another jurisdiction but employees operate in Bermuda, the Bermuda entity remains secondary liable; under-declaration triggers substantial penalties. Late payment incurs a 10% surcharge plus interest at the prescribed statutory rate; willful non-filing attracts criminal fines up to BMD 10,000 and director disqualification.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing Local File/Master File monetary threshold (in BMD or otherwise) applies because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax and has no codified transfer-pricing regime. However, economic-substance and UK-parent transparency rules may compel contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation when the Bermuda entity is part of a multinational group, performs intragroup courier logistics or financing services, or is a UK-Controlled Foreign Company tested under UK CFC rules.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations 2019 impose a substance test on exempted companies engaged in 'relevant activities'. Express courier operations involving logistics, distribution centre services, or headquarters/management functions will attract enhanced substance requirements: physical presence (owned or leased premises), qualified full-time local employees, adequate operating expenditure, and core income-generating activities actually performed in Bermuda. A pure 'mailbox' company with no local staff, no local board meetings, and no logistics decision-taking will fail the test; details are reported to the UK HM Revenue & Customs and the UK registrar under the Base Erosion and Profit Shifting initiative.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Exemption Misuse

Statutory sanction: Bermuda imposes fines for making false statements to the Registrar or Tax Commissioner; the company may be struck off, its registration cancelled, and all economic benefit retroactively assessed. Under UK CFC anti-hijack rules, profits may be apportioned and taxed in the UK at up to 25% corporation tax plus penalties of 10-50% of the understated liability.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners who knowingly use a sham exempted company to evade a foreign tax obligation are exposed to civil restitution, worldwide asset freezes, travel bans, and imprisonment (up to 12 years in the UK for tax evasion under the Criminal Finances Act 2017). The corporate veil is pierced where the company is a mere façade.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Failure to satisfy Bermuda's Economic Substance Requirements results in a written compliance notice, financial penalties up to BMD 250,000 for continuing non-compliance, automatic reporting to UK taxation authorities, and possible forced dissolution. If the company is an intermediate holding for courier vehicles, a second-level penalty and default assessment can arise.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for knowingly permitting substance failures; they risk disqualification orders, personal fines up to BMD 100,000, and civil claims from shareholders and counterparties for resultant tax leakage.
AML/CFT and Remittance Fraud

Statutory sanction: Regulatory sanctions include fines up to BMD 250,000 per contravention, revocation of the corporate service provider licence, asset confiscation, and prohibition from operating a money-services business. The Bermuda Courts can impose custodial sentences of up to 14 years for money-laundering offences.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who consent or are negligently party to unmonitored payment flows face immediate arrest, pre-charge asset restraint, and imprisonment. The Privy Council standard allows the court to lift the veil to recover wages, contracts, and stolen funds from controlling directors.
False Annual Return and Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Bermuda's Registry will impose a late/incorrect filing penalty of BMD 1,000 plus BMD 50 per day, and may strike the company off after 90 days. The Economic Substance Authority may separately levy a deliberate-failure penalty of BMD 10,000 for the first day and BMD 500 per day thereafter, plus an information exchange report to HM Revenue & Customs.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are criminally liable; sanctions include disqualification from acting as a director in Bermuda for up to 10 years, personal fines, and imprisonment up to 5 years. Courts will pierce the corporate structure and impose personal restitution for all unpaid tax and penalties if the company is a sham.
Transfer Pricing Documentation and UK CFC Attribution

Statutory sanction: HM Revenue & Customs may recharacterise the Bermuda entity as a CFC and apportion its profits to the UK parent under the CFC rules; a tax assessment with interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax understated can follow. If the Bermuda entity is merely a seller of services to a UK connected party, UK diverted profits tax at 31% applies on the total mismatched payment.

Director personal liability: UK directors and shadow directors of the Bermuda subsidiary are jointly liable for diverted profits tax under Finance Act 2015, Part 3; this liability extends to civil recovery orders and, in cases of fraud, criminal prosecution for evasion with custodial sentences.
