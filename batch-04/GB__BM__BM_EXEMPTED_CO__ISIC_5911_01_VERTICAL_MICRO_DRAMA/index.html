Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Registrar of Companies (https://www.roc.gov.bm); Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner and Government portal (https://www.gov.bm); Bermuda Customs Department; for any UK tax/VAT nexus, HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) (https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs). Bermuda has no general income tax, but the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 may require registration for in-scope multinational groups under the domestic minimum top-up tax.

E-government filing portal

Foreign owners incorporate a Bermuda Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981 through a licensed Bermuda registered agent. The Registrar of Companies issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a company/government file number used as the primary corporate identifier. No Bermudian income-tax TIN or VAT ID is issued because Bermuda imposes no income tax and no VAT/GST. If the company employs Bermuda staff, the employer must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and social security and obtain a payroll tax account number. If the company becomes UK-resident, has a UK permanent establishment, or makes direct UK-taxable supplies, it must separately register with HMRC for UK Corporation Tax and/or UK VAT. If the entity is a constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group, Bermuda Corporate Income Tax registration and filing obligations may apply from the relevant accounting period.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda law has no check-the-box election and no hybrid-mismatch election. An Exempted Company may apply for a Tax Protection Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which protects profits, income, capital gains, and distributions from Bermuda taxation until at least 2035. UK tax residence, entity classification, and hybrid mismatch treatment are determined by UK tax law and the facts, not by a Bermuda election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Bermuda VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax exists. UK Value Added Tax (VAT) may apply extraterritorially if the Bermuda entity makes direct taxable supplies of digital services to UK consumers or otherwise establishes a UK VAT registration nexus.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda: nil; there is no VAT/GST regime and no indirect-tax registration threshold. UK: a non-established supplier making direct B2C digital supplies to UK consumers is generally required to register for UK VAT from the first supply when the supply is not collected by a deemed online marketplace; the normal £85,000 UK VAT threshold does not apply to non-established businesses. Sales through Apple App Store and Google Play are typically subject to marketplace deemed-supplier VAT collection, while Stripe card payments are not treated as a marketplace supply and may trigger direct HMRC registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Bermuda: because Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating claim and no indirect-tax refund mechanism. Digital micro-drama streaming income from non-Bermudian users is outside the scope of Bermuda indirect taxation and does not require a zero-rating certificate. If the entity separately becomes UK VAT-registered, supplies of digital content to non-UK consumers are generally outside the scope of UK VAT based on place-of-supply rules; physical export of goods may be zero-rated only where statutory export evidence is retained.

  • No Bermuda VAT export declaration or tax clearance certificate is required for intangible vertical-micro-drama streaming rights, app-store distribution, or digital content licensing.
  • If physical media or ancillary merchandise is exported, retain the customs export declaration, Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, commercial invoice, packing list, and proof of export.
  • For cross-border digital revenue substantiation, retain Apple/Google financial reports, Stripe payout summaries, intercompany IP licence agreements, inward remittance certificates or equivalent bank credit advice, and a copy of the Bermuda economic substance declaration; a 180-day documentation-retention benchmark applies for export/royalty income substantiation even though Bermuda imposes no legal FX repatriation deadline.

Bermuda imposes no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API reporting, or digital stamping obligation. If the Bermuda entity registers for UK VAT with HMRC, books and records must be maintained digitally and VAT returns filed through Making Tax Digital-compatible software; e-invoicing is not generally mandatory in the UK. App-store and payment-processor transaction reports should be retained as primary VAT substantiation.

Bermuda has no VAT export zero-rating and therefore no VAT clawback risk. Scrutiny focuses on Bermuda economic substance compliance, beneficial-ownership filings, payroll tax/social security obligations, and customs duty declarations on physical imports. Failure to file an economic substance declaration can attract late-filing and daily penalties, exchange-of-information consequences, and potential striking-off. If the entity triggers UK VAT registration and claims zero-rating or outside-scope treatment without sufficient evidence, HMRC can disallow the relief, assess and claw back the VAT, charge interest, impose penalties up to 100% of the VAT at risk for deliberate or concealed behaviour, and pursue criminal sanctions for fraudulent export documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BMD is pegged at 1:1 to the United States Dollar. For a Bermuda Exempted Company, the Exchange Control Act 1972 exemption certificate removes all restrictions on current-account and capital-account transactions, subject to AML/CFT reporting to the BMA. Current-account remittances such as dividends, royalties, licence fees, and app-store revenue distributions are fully convertible into any foreign currency at market rates. Capital-account inflows including equity subscriptions and shareholder loans and outflows including capital redemption or liquidation proceeds are also freely transferable. No prior BMA approval is required for such transactions by exempted companies, provided that the bank's normal source-of-funds and purpose-of-payment verification is satisfied. There are no discriminatory conversion taxes or withholding-type exchange control levies imposed on profits remitted abroad.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement exists in Bermuda. Export receipts, including proceeds from digital streaming, app-store sales, and international licensing arrangements, are not required to be repatriated to Bermuda for conversion into BMD, nor is there any prescribed conversion ratio or holding period. The company may maintain foreign-currency settlement accounts in USD, EUR, GBP, or any other freely convertible currency and may decide independently whether to convert such receipts into BMD or another currency. The BMA does not impose any filing obligation specifically on export proceeds, but retains the power to require banks to report unusual patterns under the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing legislation. In practice, banks may request supporting invoices and contractual documentation to evidence that digital content sales constitute genuine export trade for purposes of the company's business profile.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), the integrated financial services regulator, central bank, and issuer of the Bermuda Dollar (BMD), operating under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Moderate. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer, or Stripe Treasury may accept a Bermuda Exempted Company if the company can demonstrate genuine transactional activity and a clear nexus to an operating bank account in Bermuda. However, legal constraints include: (i) no EMI is domestically licensed by the BMA, so the EMI is subject to the regulatory and sanctions screening framework of its home jurisdiction (e.g., UK FCA, EU or US states); (ii) fintech platforms frequently reject offshore exempted structures due to perceived non-resident beneficiary opacity or absence of local substance; (iii) Apple IAP and Google Play Billing will typically settle into a bank account where the developer-of-record is tax-resident or domiciled, which favours a Bermudian corporate account rather than an offshore EMI; (iv) if onboarding with such an EMI succeeds, the account may be treated as a 'pass-through' in KYC reviews and require immediate onward settlement to the principal operating account at a tier-1 Bermudian bank. Accordingly, EMI accounts are best used as ancillary collection/settlement conduits, not as the primary corporate treasury or liquidity buffer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital transfers must be effected as SWIFT MT103 messages from the shareholder's bank account in an FATF-compliant jurisdiction to the Bermuda-based account of the Exempted Company in the requested currency. The wire instruction narrative should identify 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION (CAPITAL INJECTION); COMPANY REF. ###-###' and must not be fragmented across sub-USD 10,000 tranches to avoid currency transaction reporting in Bermuda. The receiving bank will request a board resolution allotting shares, a share subscription agreement, certified register of shareholders, and a source-of-funds letter signed by the beneficial owner. Where the capital injection exceeds such internal reporting thresholds as the bank may apply under BMA rules, a currency declaration or forex notification may be filed by the bank on behalf of the client with the BMA. For shareholder loans, a formal loan agreement with an Executive Committee resolution and an arm's-length interest rate is required to satisfy substance and safe-haven compliance.Outbound disbursements for trade-related obligations may be routed through multiple clearing rails depending on amount and destination: (i) SWIFT MT103 payment orders are used for low-to-medium value production fees, licensing payments, affiliate profit-share, and recurring SaaS subscriptions; (ii) Commercial Letters of Credit are issued via SWIFT MT700 for high-value production financing tranches payable against cargo or delivery deliverables, with MT707 amendments and MT798 as supporting; (iii) App-store and digital-streaming settlements are received by Stripe or PayPal into the Bermudian account, and where required onward disbursement to non-resident licensors occurs through same-day USD correspondent clearing via CHIPS or Fedwire; (iv) European revenue payments are transmitted through SEPA Credit Transfers after EUR conversion managed under a spot or forward contract; (v) all instructions should cross-reference to the underlying invoice, the licensing agreement, or the delivery confirmation to maintain a clean audit trail for the depository bank's sanctions and AML screening systems.Outright forward exchange contracts with BMA-licensed Bermudian banks or OECD-domiciled banking counterparties for EUR, GBP, USD, and CNY exposures.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing restricted currencies such as CNY, where historical and contractual exposure to streaming revenue originating in China exists.Cross-currency interest rate and principal swaps for converting floating-rate debt or extending the tenor of receivables exposure.European-style FX options purchased as protective puts or covered calls from regulated banks, provided the company does not engage in naked option writing.FX margin-intermediated currency hedging transacted through a licensed EMI treasury desk with a designated segregating custodian and periodic counterparty credit monitoring.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Moderate. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer, or Stripe Treasury may accept a Bermuda Exempted Company if the company can demonstrate genuine transactional activity and a clear nexus to an operating bank account in Bermuda. However, legal constraints include: (i) no EMI is domestically licensed by the BMA, so the EMI is subject to the regulatory and sanctions screening framework of its home jurisdiction (e.g., UK FCA, EU or US states); (ii) fintech platforms frequently reject offshore exempted structures due to perceived non-resident beneficiary opacity or absence of local substance; (iii) Apple IAP and Google Play Billing will typically settle into a bank account where the developer-of-record is tax-resident or domiciled, which favours a Bermudian corporate account rather than an offshore EMI; (iv) if onboarding with such an EMI succeeds, the account may be treated as a 'pass-through' in KYC reviews and require immediate onward settlement to the principal operating account at a tier-1 Bermudian bank. Accordingly, EMI accounts are best used as ancillary collection/settlement conduits, not as the primary corporate treasury or liquidity buffer.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 - Bermuda imposes no Local File or Master File requirement because there is no corporate income tax base. Contingent rules: (i) where the Bermuda entity is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue equal or exceeding BMD 750 million (EUR 750 million), the Ultimate Parent Entity must file the OECD Country-by-Country Report and the Bermuda entity must evidence cooperation; (ii) foreign tax authorities (notably HMRC and the IRS) will require contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation for related-party royalties, distribution fees and app-store revenue routing involving the Bermuda company; (iii) the Bermuda QDMTT computation applies OECD allocation rules only to in-scope MNE groups.

Economic substance scope: Applicable - Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019 and Economic Substance Regulations 2018. A Bermuda Exempted Company carrying on a relevant activity (Distribution and Service Business; Intellectual Property Business for the micro-drama catalogue; or Holding Business if it owns operating subsidiaries) must satisfy all three limbs in Bermuda: (1) managed and directed in Bermuda with quorate board meetings physically held in Bermuda; (2) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda; (3) adequate qualified employees, physical premises and annual expenditure proportionate to the income. The entity must file the annual ESD and retain supporting board minutes, employment records and expense records. Failure results in statutory fines, strike-off, and for IP businesses, automatic recharacterization of the arrangement as a shell structure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK Tax Residence Displacement

Statutory sanction: UK corporation tax at 25% on worldwide profits (CTA 2009 s.14); interest on unpaid tax; HMRC penalties up to 100% of the tax (with 200% for deliberate concealment); UK GAAR counteraction at 60% of counteracted tax; Bermuda authorities may revoke the 1966 Act tax protection certificate and refuse the ESD non-resident declaration.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed inaccurate non-residence declarations may face personal penalties; HMRC can pursue Senior Accounting Officer penalties of GBP 5,000 per control failure; in fraud cases, directors face criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue with custodial sentence of up to 7 years, confiscation orders, travel bans, and director disqualification in both the UK and Bermuda.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Amendment Act 2019: fixed penalty of BMD 10,000 for non-filing or inaccurate declaration; BMD 10,000 monthly continuing penalty; significant failure can trigger escalation to the statutory maximum penalty, strike-off from the register, and cancellation of the exempted company status; the Tax Commissioner may also impose information-gathering orders and asset freezes.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who signed a false ESD are personally liable for the penalty amounts; director disqualification for up to 5 years; persistent breach allows the Registrar to strike off the company, after which directors can be held personally liable for debts and face enforcement proceedings by creditors and the Crown.
Beneficial Ownership and AML/ATF Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalties of up to BMD 10,000 per entity and per officer for late or incomplete BO filings; fraudulent concealment of the UBO is a criminal offence punishable by fine and imprisonment of up to 2 years; the Bermuda Monetary Authority may direct the closure of bank accounts and the company may be de-risked by correspondent banks, effectively freezing commercial operations.

Director personal liability: Directors face criminal prosecution for money laundering and terrorist financing predicate offences where merchant receipts are deliberately routed or disguised; personal fines, confiscation of proceeds, imprisonment up to 2 years under the BORA and up to 14 years under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 for serious money laundering, plus travel bans and freezing orders.
Transfer Pricing Recharacterization and Statutory Royalty Stripping

Statutory sanction: Foreign adjustments under OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines: disallowance of the royalty deduction in the UK at 25% corporation tax, with tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the understated tax plus interest; countries may impose diverted profits tax (UK DPT at 25%) or deny treaty benefits; the Bermuda QDMTT, where applicable, neutralizes the tax advantage for in-scope groups.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Bermuda company who approve non-arm's length royalty rates without supporting valuations can be pursued by HMRC as 'shadow directors' of the arrangement; penalties for deliberate transfer-pricing errors can be recovered from officers; egregious cases lead to criminal prosecution for tax evasion, imprisonment for up to 7 years under UK tax evasion offences, extradition risk, and personal civil restitution to the tax authorities.
