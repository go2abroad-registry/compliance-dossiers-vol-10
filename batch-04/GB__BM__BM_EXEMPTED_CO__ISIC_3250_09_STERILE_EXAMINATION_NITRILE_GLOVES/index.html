E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Bermuda has no US-style check-the-box regime; a Companies Act 1981 exempted company is per se opaque. Available statutory elections and designations include: (i) the Exempted Undertaking Tax Protection election under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, which statutorily freezes zero rating for Bermuda income, profit, capital gains and distribution taxes until at least 2035; (ii) the Economic Substance classification election, under which the entity must declare whether it performs a relevant activity (pure equity holding company, financing/leasing, insurance, headquarters, IP, distribution and service centre) and file the supporting substantial-activity report; (iii) for multinational groups within OECD Pillar Two, the election to be within the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2024 QDMTT framework, applying a 15% qualified domestic minimum top-up tax to in-scope constituent entities with consolidated revenue above EUR 750 million, thereby preserving Bermuda's primary right to tax residual profits; and (iv) formation-level transparency is available only if the vehicle is established as a Bermuda exempted partnership or exempted limited liability company, not as a Companies Act 1981 exempted company. Hybrid mismatch analysis under UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6FA will apply to cross-border payments if a UK payer claims a deduction that is not included in Bermuda's current zero-tax base.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Bermuda; indirect taxation is enforced through Bermuda Import Duty under the Customs Tariff Act. Where finished sterile examination gloves or inputs enter the UK sovereign leg, UK import VAT at 20% applies at the point of import and is normally accounted for by the UK importer or postponed under PVA.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Bermuda (BMD): no economic-turnover threshold exists because no VAT or GST is levied; an importer/exporter customs code is required from the first transaction. UK sovereign leg: the GBP 90,000 VAT registration threshold applies only to UK-established taxable persons; a non-established Bermuda entity that owns goods in the UK or makes taxable UK supplies is registrable without a turnover de minimis, and online-marketplace supplies may oblige HMRC registration irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Bermuda has no VAT, so no Bermudian zero-rating claim is necessary and no Bermudian export duty is levied on gloves exported from Bermuda. For the UK sovereign leg, VAT zero-rating applies to the supply of goods physically exported from the UK provided: (a) the goods leave the UK within three months of the supply, or within twelve months where a UK export declaration is made and contractual export evidence exists; (b) the exporter obtains and retains at least two independent, non-interchangeable documentary proofs of export, such as the customs export declaration, commercial invoice, transport document, or packing list; and (c) the exporter preserves the evidence in the form required by HMRC Notice 703 for at least six years. Contract-manufacturing chains, toll conversion, and warehouse/free-zone export sales are assessed under the same underlying physical-export rule.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bermuda BSAD/CUS-10; UK CDS export declaration for UK-origin goods)
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill (master/house) evidencing physical export
  • Inward Foreign-Currency Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 evidence of export proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice with HS classification (4015.12 / 6116.10) for sterile examination nitrile gloves
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin / CE Declaration of Conformity for regulated medical glove batches

Bermuda: no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or API clearance mandate is in force; export and import declarations are processed electronically through the Bermuda Customs Automated Manifest System / Single Administrative Document portal. UK: HMRC mandates Making Tax Digital for VAT, requiring functional compatible software for digital record-keeping and quarterly VAT returns; export and import declarations must be lodged through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) or an approved EDI channel, with optional inventory-linked clearance. There is no invoice-stamping requirement, but CDS risk rules may select consignments for physical examination before zero-rating is accepted.

Bermuda: no VAT clawback exists, but inaccurate customs declarations or false export statements expose the exporter to penalties under the Customs Act 2019, including fines up to BMD 10,000, seizure of goods, and administrative sanctions; false economic substance filings can attract fines up to BMD 100,000. UK: a mis-claimed zero-rating triggers an HMRC assessment restoring the 20% VAT on the supply, plus interest and a tax-geared penalty of up to 30% for careless inaccuracy and up to 100% for deliberate and concealed conduct; dishonest evasion may lead to criminal prosecution with unlimited fines and imprisonment under VATA 1994 section 72. The normal assessment window is four years, extended to six years for deliberate or concealed behaviour; HMRC may deny input VAT recovery and impose joint liability in missing-trader or acquisition-fraud arrangements involving PPE, and may require border re-inspection of consignments under CDS risk management.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar and circulates alongside US currency. Bermuda Exempted Companies are treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes, allowing unrestricted capital and current account convertibility in foreign currency. Profit remittances and dividends may be paid freely in USD or other acceptable currencies without prior BMA licensing, provided the company has no Bermuda-resident ownership or domestic-business designation. Bermuda-dollar domestic transactions, however, are subject to licensed-dealer requirements, and any company wishing to hold or transact in Bermuda dollars must use a BMA-authorised exchange control dealer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement, compulsory conversion timeline, or minimum conversion ratio applies to a Bermuda Exempted Company engaged in offshore manufacturing and sales of medical gloves. Export proceeds may be retained in USD-denominated operating accounts, used directly for trade disbursements, or converted through licensed banks at market rates. For banking narrative integrity, each export payment should be supported by a commercial invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, health product certificate (e.g., CE, FDA, MHRA), and the corresponding SWIFT reference.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, the Exchange Control Act 1972, and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium with material legal constraints. UK or EU e-money institutions (EMIs) are not recognised as Bermuda deposit-taking institutions and cannot issue a BMA banking licence. They may provide ancillary payment initiation, multi-currency e-money wallets, and disbursement execution through segregated client accounts at sponsor banks, but they cannot provide the settlement finality, trade-finance instruments, or FX execution expected from a Bermuda commercial bank. For this sterilized medical gloves operation, the primary treasury route should be a BMA-licensed bank; a regulated EMI may be used only as a secondary low-value disbursement rail for supplier prepayments and petty trade expenses, with contractual pass-through and client-money safeguards.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 3,000,000 (approximate local currency threshold for inbound/outbound controlled transactions if the entity becomes subject to the 2025 CIT; no mandatory transfer pricing documentation for pure exempted companies below the multi-national top-up tax threshold)

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2018 apply to exempted companies carrying on 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centres, intellectual property, and holding entity). ISIC 3250 manufacturing of nitrile gloves does not constitute a relevant activity; therefore automated ESR reporting is not triggered solely by that business scope. However, if the entity also derives income from distribution or service centre activity involving the gloves, a politically exposed concern arises. The company must file an economic substance compliance return if it engages in any relevant activity, and must meet the core income generation activity (CIGA) test in Bermuda with adequate physical presence, employees, and expenditure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / CIT Misreporting

Statutory sanction: Under Bermuda CIT Act 2023: civil penalty of up to 200% of the omitted tax, criminal fine of BMD 100,000–1,000,000; underamendments may trigger the UK 'offshore tax evasion' cross-border penalty regime

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise misreporting are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; they may face a UK serious fraud investigation, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 7 years
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda ESR: financial penalty of BMD 10,000–250,000 and mandatory strike-off from the register; directors individually fined BMD 5,000–25,000 per violation; a finding of 'domicile shopping' may trigger UK GAAR adjustments and HMRC penalties of 30% of all tax lost

Director personal liability: Directors can be disqualified from serving as directors of any Bermuda company; personal liability for unpaid corporate tax under section 92 of the Companies Act (fraudulent trading); administrative court may freeze personal assets and impose a UK travel ban
AML & Sanctions Violation (Payment Rails: Swift_LC / TT_Wire)

Statutory sanction: Under the Bermuda Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and AML/ATF Code 2008: penal monetary fines up to BMD 1,000,000 per breach, plus anti-money laundering compliance orders; Swift LC fraud may invoke UK FCA and US OFAC penalties

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for failing to file Suspicious Activity Reports; may face a UK Unexplained Wealth Order, asset freeze, and criminal imprisonment for up to 14 years
Transfer Pricing / TP Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: CIT Act 2023 and UK parent regulations: penalty of 10% of the income adjustment plus surcharge interest; failure to file documentation results in a fixed penalty of BMD 50,000 plus daily default of BMD 5,000

Director personal liability: If the transfer pricing misstatement creates a false tax benefit, directors can be liable to a civil restitution order and disgorgement of profits; persistent conduct supports a 'wrongful trading' finding under Bermuda law
