A Bermuda Exempted Company must at all times maintain a registered office in Bermuda at a physical street address under section 153 of the Companies Act 1981. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner of Bermuda (OTC), Hamilton, Bermuda (www.gov.bm, taxcomm.gov.bm), administering payroll tax and administering the Corporate Income Tax Agency (CITA) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope multinational groups; Bermuda Customs Service, Ministry of Finance, operating the Bermatix (ASYCUDA) electronic clearance portal at customs.gov.bm; Registrar of Companies, Bermuda, under the Companies Act 1981. For any UK nexus, Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC) is competent for UK corporation tax and UK VAT registration, with registered filing via the Government Gateway portal.

E-government filing portal

Appoint a licensed Bermuda registered agent and deliver incorporation documents to the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 1981, obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation, the Certificate of Exempted Status, and the Bermuda Company Number (BR No.), which operates as the entity's primary tax/customs identifier. If employing Bermuda-based staff, register as an employer with the OTC and obtain a Payroll Tax Account Number within 30 days of becoming an employer. If importing or exporting goods, register as an importer/exporter with Bermuda Customs, appoint a licensed customs broker, and obtain an electronic account on the Bermatix (ASYCUDA World) system. If the entity belongs to a multinational group with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750M or more, register with CITA for Bermuda Corporate Income Tax (top-up tax and QDMTT) prior to the first filing period beginning on or after 1 January 2025. No VAT registration is possible or required in Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - Bermuda Exempted Company is not subject to Bermuda income tax (Bermuda levies no general corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax), is exempt from foreign-exchange control, and benefits from the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (as amended), which statutorily guarantees no future Bermuda taxes on income, gains, or capital until 31 March 2035, subject to the reserved Pillar Two Corporate Income Tax regime (including QDMTT and IIR) for in-scope multinationals effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025. The entity's trading activity inside a customs bonded depot is likewise outside the scope of Bermuda taxation; the entity is characterized at entity level for Bermuda purposes and may be treated as a tax transparent or opaque entity for foreign owners only under foreign jurisdictions' rules.

Available election forms

Bermuda does not maintain a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime. Available formal elections include: (1) election to incorporate as an exempted company waiving the 60% Bermudian beneficial ownership requirement; (2) election to obtain and rely on the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection certificate (tax guarantee until 2035); (3) election to redomicile into or out of Bermuda by continuance under the Companies Act 1981, preserving the exempted status; (4) for in-scope MNE groups, election to apply the QDMTT Safe Harbour and designation of a single filing entity for CITA purposes; (5) for UK VAT purposes, if the entity makes UK supplies, election/application for UK VAT registration under VAT Notice 700/1, including voluntary registration where the taxable supply threshold has not been met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Bermuda has no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use-tax. The applicable indirect system is Bermuda Customs Duty (tariff-based) under the Customs Management and Duties Act 2010, with duty-suspended status for goods in a bonded warehouse or free-trade-zone depot. If a UK taxable presence or UK supply is established, the UK VAT system (Value Added Tax Act 1994) would apply under UK place-of-supply rules.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for Bermuda VAT (no VAT/GST registration threshold exists). Customs duty registration of the bonded depot operator is mandatory irrespective of turnover. For UK VAT registration, the current statutory turnover threshold is GBP 90,000 of UK taxable supplies (from 1 April 2024); for the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax top-up regime, the registration trigger is consolidated group revenue of EUR 750M.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a Bermuda VAT concept because Bermuda imposes no VAT or GST. Under Bermuda customs law, goods exported from Bermuda, including goods withdrawn from a bonded depot and shipped to a foreign destination, are exempt from Bermuda customs import duty, with no domestic refund mechanism engaged. If the Bermuda Exempted Company is or becomes a UK taxable person and holds UK customs warehousing, UK VAT zero-rating for exported goods applies (VAT Notice 703) where the goods physically leave the UK within statutory time limits and the supplier retains valid export evidence; offshore services supplied to a non-UK recipient may be zero-rated under VAT Notice 741A, subject to place-of-supply rules and the recipient's business status.

  • Bermuda Customs Export Declaration (Bermatix e-export entry / ASYCUDA export declaration)
  • Bonded Depot Withdrawal/Removal Note (customs release order from the bonded warehouse authority)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the bonded depot
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List with HS tariff classification
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a Bermuda-licensed bank evidencing receipt of foreign sales proceeds
  • Bond Register / Duty Suspension Schedule showing duty status of the removed goods
  • UK Customs Export Declaration (via the UK Customs Declaration Service) and C88 evidence if goods transit through the UK
  • Proof of legitimate discharge of customs duty on any goods diverted to domestic consumption

Bermuda imposes no electronic-invoicing clearance mandate and no real-time fiscal stamping requirement. Customs clearance is exclusively electronic through the Bermatix ASYCUDA World system, requiring certified digital entries, tariff classification, and electronic duty/bond account settlement for bonded warehouse inbound/outbound movements; all export entries are lodged as e-manifests. For a UK VAT-registered entity, quarterly Making Tax Digital (MTD) VAT returns must be transmitted to HMRC through approved MTD-compatible software, with mandatory digital record-keeping, although individual invoices are not cleared in real time.

Misuse of the Bermuda bonded-depot or free-zone regime - including false export declarations to obtain duty suspension or drawback, or diversion of goods to home use without duty payment - is subject to penalty of up to 100% of the duty evaded, forfeiture of goods, and deregistration of the bonded facility under the Customs Management and Duties Act 2010, plus interest and criminal liability. Fraudulent export zero-rating or drawback claims attract retrospective clawback, customs audit assessment for up to seven years, and penalties of up to three times the duty/refund claimed. For any UK VAT exposure, HMRC may issue assessments clawing back wrongly zero-rated VAT for up to four years (extended to 20 years for dishonest conduct); civil penalties of up to 100% of the potential VAT lost (Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24) or 30% under VATA 1994 s.67 for careless/reasonable-care failures, and 100% under VATA 1994 s.60 for deliberate withholdings; criminal liability for fraudulent evasion may arise under VATA 1994 s.72, with imprisonment and director disqualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermuda Dollar (BMD) is pegged to the U.S. Dollar at a fixed 1:1 parity and both currencies circulate interchangeably. For a Bermuda Exempted Company carrying on business exclusively as a non-resident bonded depot operator, both current-account and capital-account convertibility are effectively unrestricted. The company may receive foreign currency, maintain offshore or onshore foreign-currency accounts, and remit dividends, loan repayments, intra-group funds and liquidation proceeds to non-resident shareholders without obtaining prior BMA approvals. The only material hurdles are administrative: proving non-resident status, filing beneficial-ownership and economic substance confirms, and satisfying the bank's source-of-funds and AML/ATF inquiries.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender requirement, mandatory conversion ratio, or fixed surrender deadline for export proceeds received by a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company. Export settlements may be retained in BMD, USD, GBP, EUR or other bank-approved currencies, and may be transferred offshore without BMA conversion permission. In bank practice, export receivables should be collected within normal commercial terms (commonly 180 days from invoice or bill-of-lading date) and the bank may require a Bermuda Customs entry, warehouse receipt, invoice and proof of export before crediting a treasury settlement account; a resident-status Bermuda account would face authorized-dealer checks, but the exempted FTZ depot falls outside that class.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972, the Banking Act 1969 and the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; the U.K. Treasury and the Bank of England retain only residual sovereignty and do not administer day-to-day local FX or clearing in Bermuda.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. A U.K.-authorised electronic-money institution (EMI) or challenger fintech can open a GBP/EUR e-money facility for a Bermuda Exempted Company and may serve as an auxiliary payment rail for receivables and supplier payables, but it is not a permitted primary treasury bank. It is neither a BMA-licensed deposit-taker under the Banking Act 1969 nor a licensed digital-asset business, and the business of deposit-taking, trade-collateral custody, or commercial letters of credit must rest with a Bermuda tier-1 bank. If the EMI uses a BMA-licensed sponsor and keeps funds in segregated client accounts, it may be used for fast FX conversion and small-value payments, but every large cross-border settlement should remain on SWIFT rails through Butterfield/HSBC.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For capital contribution or shareholder loan from the U.K. parent/UBO into the Bermuda Exempted Company, use SWIFT MT103; beneficiary account is numeric (Bermuda does not use IBAN) plus the bank's BIC. For GBP, route via CHAPS to Butterfield/HSBC in London and then on the same-day GBP/USD conversion to the Hamilton account; for USD, route via Fedwire/CHIPS through the bank's New York correspondent, or use SWIFT MT202 COV for interbank cover. Remittance field 70 should state 'CAPITAL INJECTION / SHAREHOLDER LOAN — COMMERCIAL PURPOSE', with an ISO 20022 Category Purpose Code of EQUI or LOAN where the correspondent requires it. Verification packet includes board resolution; updated share register; share certificate; remitter's six-month bank statements; certificate of good standing; source-of-funds declaration; and a signed indemnity. No BMA prior licence is needed for non-resident capital inflows, but the bank will map the injection to the economic substance filing and ensure it is not a disguised financing arrangement.For settlements to overseas suppliers and warehouse service providers, the primary rail is SWIFT MT103 with compliance narrative 'BONDED WAREHOUSE TRADE DISBURSEMENT'; for bulk payments, convert ISO 20022 pain.001/pain.002 files into MT101/MT102 through the bank's host-to-host channel. High-value goods purchases should be paid by irrevocable commercial letters of credit under UCP600 issued via SWIFT MT700/MT707, with transport documents, cargo insurance, packing lists, certificates of origin and commercial invoices as presentation; customs-bond and free-zone performance obligations may be secured by standby LCs via MT760 in favor of the Bermuda Customs Department or free-zone authority. USD clearing is through CHIPS/Fedwire correspondents, GBP through CHAPS/Faster Payments, EUR through TARGET2/SEPA, while BMD/USD on-island sweeps execute at 1:1 through the bank's internal ledger. There is no Bermuda domestic RTGS; therefore all foreign-currency disbursements require prior liquidity prefunding and cut-off management in the correspondent network.Spot FX and intra-day sweeps between USD and BMD at the fixed 1:1 peg with the same licensed bank.Vanilla deliverable FX forwards (USD/GBP, USD/EUR, BMD/USD) with a BMA-licensed authorised dealer, documented under an ISDA Master Agreement, to hedge committed trade payables.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on offshore currencies of trade counterparties (e.g., CNY, INR, BRL) where local deliverable markets are inaccessible; settled in USD.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (CIRS) to match a USD/GBP/EUR funding base with trade receivables, provided the board approves the hedge and the bank is an authorised dealer.European-style FX options on USD/GBP and USD/EUR for contingent or tender-linked bonded warehouse contracts, with premiums paid from the operational account and no naked speculative positions.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. A U.K.-authorised electronic-money institution (EMI) or challenger fintech can open a GBP/EUR e-money facility for a Bermuda Exempted Company and may serve as an auxiliary payment rail for receivables and supplier payables, but it is not a permitted primary treasury bank. It is neither a BMA-licensed deposit-taker under the Banking Act 1969 nor a licensed digital-asset business, and the business of deposit-taking, trade-collateral custody, or commercial letters of credit must rest with a Bermuda tier-1 bank. If the EMI uses a BMA-licensed sponsor and keeps funds in segregated client accounts, it may be used for fast FX conversion and small-value payments, but every large cross-border settlement should remain on SWIFT rails through Butterfield/HSBC.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 for in-scope multinational enterprise groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million; 0% corporate income tax for all other Bermuda exempted companies, including FTZ bonded depot operators.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Bermuda-specific transfer pricing documentation threshold exists for exempted companies outside the CIT regime. Under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the threshold is the GloBE in-scope test of EUR 750 million consolidated group revenue, equivalent to approximately BD$810 million, as reflected in the group Country-by-Country Report; in-scope CIT entities must prepare and retain contemporaneous disclosure of intra-group arrangements. All Bermuda exempted companies, regardless of revenue, must file the annual Economic Substance Declaration and maintain records demonstrating that core income-generating activities occur in Bermuda.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Entities and Derivative Transactions) Act 2018, as amended by the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2023, applies to Bermuda exempted companies. A company classified as a Distribution and Service Centre, which includes bonded warehouse depot and logistics operations in a free trade zone, must prove that its core income-generating activities are carried out in Bermuda: receipt and custody of goods, management of inventory, distribution from the bonded depot, local procurement decisions, and oversight by an adequate number of Bermudian-resident employees and directors. The exempted company must maintain physical premises and operating expenditure in Bermuda proportionate to the activity. A wholly passive, foreign-managed brass-plate entity will fail the test even if it is nominally tax-exempt.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

CIT Evasion and False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Penalty not exceeding 100% of the top-up tax understated, plus interest at 4.5% per annum compounded monthly; an additional fine not exceeding BD$100,000 per false return for wilful concealment; tax clearance and FTZ licence suspension.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Bermuda resident agent who knowingly authorise a false CIT return can be subject to personal enforcement for the unpaid tax, restitution orders in favour of the Crown, travel bans, and imprisonment for up to five years on conviction of fraud.
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Fixed penalty of BD$50,000 for incomplete or incorrect substance filing; escalated penalty of BD$250,000 for failure to meet the economic substance test; corrective directions from the Registrar; and ultimately strike-off of the exempted company from the Bermuda Register.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorise a false substance declaration are personally liable for the BD$250,000 penalty and may be disqualified as directors for up to ten years; imprisonment of up to five years applies to wilful false statements; a travel ban may be imposed pending investigation.
Customs and AML Evasion in the Bonded Depot

Statutory sanction: Confiscation and forfeiture of the bonded goods; revocation of the FTZ licence; monetary penalty under the Customs Duty Act 2010 of up to three times the duty evaded; additional AML penalties under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing) Regulations 2008 of up to BD$1 million or ten times the illicit benefit, whichever is higher.

Director personal liability: Directors of the exempted company are treated as nominated officers under the AML regime; failure to report suspicious activity exposes the director personally to unlimited fines and imprisonment for up to ten years; personal seizure orders and travel restrictions may be issued.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of Bermuda taxable income to the arm's-length result; additional top-up tax liability with interest; penalty of up to 100% of the transfer pricing adjustment under the CIT Act; denial of the QDMTT safe harbour for the affected year.

Director personal liability: UK parent directors who knowingly sign non-arm's-length intercompany agreements may be assessed personally for the resulting top-up tax under the UK corporate criminal attribution rules; personal liability orders, travel bans, and disqualification as directors of any UK company may follow.
Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Fixed penalty of BD$25,000 for non-compliance; continuing default penalty of BD$500 for each day the register remains inaccurate; restriction notices against the transfer of shares of the exempted company.

Director personal liability: Officers who knowingly submit false beneficial ownership information face imprisonment for up to two years and a personal fine of BD$50,000; liquidation proceedings can be commenced against the company and its assets frozen.
