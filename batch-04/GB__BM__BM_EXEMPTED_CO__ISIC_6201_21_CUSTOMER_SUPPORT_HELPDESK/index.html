Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda; official revenue portal and e-services: https://www.gov.bm/tax-revenue (Government of Bermuda Tax Revenue Office).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned exempted company is incorporated via the Registrar of Companies. For any Bermudian tax account (payroll tax, social insurance, customs duty, tourism tax, or land tax), the company must apply for a Tax Registration Number (TRN) with the Office of the Tax Commissioner. The exempted company's registered agent submits the registration form together with the certificate of incorporation, registered office details, intended business activity, and directors/shareholders list. A company with no Bermuda payroll, no Bermuda imports, and no local permit (non-resident operation) will not be required to hold a Bermudian tax ID; the TRN is first required when the company engages a Bermuda employee, imports dutiable goods, or carries on a taxable local activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: The Bermuda exempted company is outside the scope of Bermuda income tax; it is not subject to Bermudian corporation tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. Any true tax exposure is limited to payroll tax (on Bermuda-sourced employee remuneration), social insurance contributions, stamp duties, customs duties, and land tax. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The Bermuda exempted company is outside the scope of Bermuda income tax; it is not subject to Bermudian corporation tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. Any true tax exposure is limited to payroll tax (on Bermuda-sourced employee remuneration), social insurance contributions, stamp duties, customs duties, and land tax. The exempted company may also obtain a government assurance under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (as amended), protecting it from future Bermuda taxes on profits, income, gains, or distributions until the 2035/2045 guarantee period.

Available election forms

No Bermuda domestic check-the-box regime exists because Bermuda does not levy an income tax on exempted companies. Where the exempted company is subject to U.S. federal taxation, an eligible Bermuda entity may make a U.S. check-the-box election on IRS Form 8832 (either as a partnership or as a disregarded entity) to alter its U.S. federal default classification. Bermuda law does not provide a hybrid mismatch election; hybrid arrangements must be assessed under OECD BEPS principles, and any preferential tax protection must be supported by economic substance under the Economic Substance (Requirements) Act 2018.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Bermudian VAT or GST. Bermuda applies selective indirect taxes: customs duties under the Customs Tariff Act 1978, payroll tax, stamp duty, and tourism-related taxes. For an export-oriented exempted company supplying customer-support and helldesk services, the applicable indirect tax is nil.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. For payroll tax, registration and filing arise from the first Bermuda-remitted salary (BMD 0 per employee); for customs, an importer must be registered with Bermuda Customs before clearing dutiable imports; tourism tax registration is required only for hotels or vacation rental businesses.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST on B2B or B2C supplies of services, including customer-support and helpdesk services, so there is no zero-rating procedure or refund claim to administer. Physical export of goods from Bermuda is likewise outside any VAT zero-rating system; the only relevant indirect-tax relief is the customs-duty treatment of goods temporarily imported or re-exported, which is governed by Bermuda Customs tariff rules and inward/outward processing approvals.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list with UCR/consignee reference
  • Bill of lading or air waybill evidencing physical export from Bermuda
  • Bermuda Customs export/outward manifest declaration (for goods departing Bermuda; electronic lodgement through the Bermuda Customs electronic single-window system)
  • Foreign inward remittance certificate or FX receipt evidence evidencing the cross-border payment (for AML and FX record-keeping)
  • Re-export certificate or End-Use authorisation (if the goods entered Bermuda under temporary-importation or duty-relief procedures)

None. Bermuda has no real-time e-invoicing clearance, fiscal stamping, or continuous transaction control API for general commerce. Bermuda Customs uses an electronic single-window system for the lodgement of import/export entries and payment of duties; commercial invoices and supporting documents need not be fiscally stamped for tax clearance. Invoices must satisfy common-law commercial standards and support payroll tax, stamp duty, and customs records.

There is no VAT zero-rating refund to claw back. However, fraudulent misstatement or false documentation in support of a customs relief, tax protection certificate, or economic substance filing may trigger Bermuda Customs penalties, including seizure and forfeiture of goods, monetary fines directed by the Tax Commissioner, and criminal prosecution for false declaration. Misuse of an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act assurance can result in withdrawal of the tax exemption certificate and retrospective assessment of Bermudian taxes on profits or income from the original transaction date, plus interest and penalties. Under the Economic Substance (Requirements) Act 2018, non-compliance may result in financial penalties, a directed remedl be required within 30 days, and, in serious cases, striking-off of the exempted company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged at 1:1 to the US Dollar. Bermuda operates a system of exchange control administered by the BMA, but an Exempted Company may apply for designation as 'non-resident' for exchange-control purposes, thereby eliminating all restrictions on both capital and current account convertibility. For non-resident designated entities, current account payments are freely convertible, capital account flows require no prior BMA approval, and profit remittance to shareholders of any jurisdiction is entirely unrestricted and free of withholding tax. For entities without non-resident designation, current account transactions are freely permitted through authorised dealers, while capital account transfers and profit remittances may require exchange-control authorisation. In practice, the Exempted Company structure is granted non-resident designated status, creating a fully open convertibility regime with zero remittance hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory conversion ratio or statutory surrender timeline for Bermuda Exempted Companies designated as non-resident under the Exchange Control Regulations. Such entities may retain all export proceeds in BMD, USD, or any other approved foreign currency. For entities that do not obtain non-resident designation, residents must offer any foreign currency export receipts to an authorised dealer within 7-30 days, but this rule is universally avoided for the low-risk SaaS helpdesk model by securing a non-resident designation at incorporation.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: MEDIUM. Bermuda has no dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing framework; all deposit-taking and electronic payment activities fall under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and the Digital Asset Business Act 2018. A pure fintech EMI chartered outside Bermuda (e.g., in the UK, EU, or Singapore) can be used as an additional collection and disbursement rail for the SaaS helpdesk operation, provided it does not maintain a physical presence or solicit customers within Bermuda. However, the BMA may assert regulatory nexus if the EMI transacts in BMD or provides services to Bermuda-resident clients. Offshore digital EMIs face one practical blocker: Bermudian-registered exempted companies are sometimes refused by foreign EMI counterparties due to the offshore jurisdiction perception, despite the low-risk helpdesk profile. Domestic Bermuda banks remain the primary banking rail; an offshore EMI should be treated as a secondary settlement layer only.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Funds must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's home bank to the Bermuda bank account. The intended purpose code on the MT103 should be 'G' (Capital Transfer / Capital contribution) or 'S300' (Investment in Equity / Venture Capital) depending on the bank's SWIFT message interpretation; principal reason: 'Capital Injection into exempted company for working capital and software development costs'. The beneficiary must include the legal entity name exactly as registered, and the beneficiary account should be labelled 'Capital Escrow Account / Operating Account'. To avoid clearing delays, the remitter's bank must be a reputable authorised bank in a low-risk jurisdiction. A board resolution, share subscription agreement, and an AML/KYC source-of-funds letter from the remitting bank must be lodged with the receiving bank before the inbound transfer is booked. Where the injection exceeds USD 1,000,000, enhanced verification documentation (audited accounts, contract evidence) is required by the Bermuda bank's internal policy.Outbound settlements for the customer support helpdesk business are processed primarily using SWIFT MT103 corporate-to-corporate payment messages for vendor payments, subcontractor remuneration, intra-group cost recharges, and software licensing fees. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% ordinary Bermuda corporate income tax rate for a standard Bermuda Exempted Company; a 15% corporate income tax under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 applies only to a constituent entity of a large multinational group with consolidated annual revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750 million, effective for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% Bermuda withholding tax on dividends paid by a Bermuda Exempted Company. Dividends may be repatriated to non-resident shareholders without any Bermuda statutory withholding, income tax, or remittance tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Bermuda imposes no general transfer-pricing documentation obligation on a standard exempted company outside the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023. The relevant threshold is the CIT Act multinational group test: consolidated annual revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750 million, approximately BMD 810 million under current EUR/BMD rates. Above that threshold, OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation, including Master File and Local File, and any required contemporaneous evidence will apply to the in-scope group. For the standard helpdesk SaaS entity, the prudent threshold is therefore zero: maintain contemporaneous pricing evidence for intercompany SaaS fees, support service charges, and cost allocations because foreign tax authorities may recharacterize underpriced cross-border arrangements.

Economic substance scope: The Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 and Economic Substance Regulations 2021 require every exempted company carrying on a relevant activity to demonstrate real economic presence in Bermuda. A customer support helpdesk and conversational bot SaaS business should evaluate whether it is a distribution and service centre, headquarters, or intellectual property business. If the category is relevant, the entity must show direction and management in Bermuda, adequate qualified employees, physical premises, operating expenditure, and performance of core income-generating activities in Bermuda. A registered-office-only arrangement is insufficient and exposes the entity to penalties, information exchange, strike-off, and foreign anti-avoidance recharacterization.
