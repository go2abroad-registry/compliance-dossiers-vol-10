Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Office of the Tax Commissioner, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bermuda (tax administration) and Bermuda Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Economy and Labour (entity registration). Official portals: www.gov.bm and the Registrar of Companies e-registry (roc.gov.bm).

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate as a Bermuda Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981 with the Registrar of Companies, obtaining a Certificate of Incorporation and a Bermuda Entity Number (B-number) serving as the statutory corporate identifier; (2) obtain the Certificate of Exemption confirming the 50-year tax-exemption guarantee under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (3) register for Bermuda Payroll Tax (employer) with the Office of the Tax Commissioner on Form PT/1, even with zero Bermuda payroll, using the B-number as the reference TIN; (4) if the multinational group reports consolidated revenues of at least EUR 750 million, register for the 15% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (effective 1 January 2025) and obtain a Bermuda CIT registration number; (5) file the annual Economic Substance declaration with the Ministry of Finance evidencing exempted undertaking status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - The Bermuda Exempted Company is a non-resident, limited liability entity operating in a zero-income-tax jurisdiction; it is exempt from Bermuda income, capital gains, capital transfer, and withholding taxes on all VPN SaaS profits except for the globally-scoped 15% CIT payable solely by multinational group members satisfying the EUR 750 million consolidated revenue threshold.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Bermuda. Statutory elections available: (a) election for Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) application under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023; (b) election for aggregation of computation rules for a fiscal period; (c) election to utilise loss carry-back/carry-forward under CIT rules; (d) election for the 50-year (extendable to 2066) tax protection guarantee under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (e) hybrid mismatch elections are immaterial at entity level given the absence of an income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax - Bermuda's indirect fiscal instruments are limited to customs import duties, a Payroll Tax on Bermuda-sourced remuneration, and stamp duties on specific instruments; digital VPN subscriptions and privacy software sold to non-residents by an exempted company fall entirely outside Bermuda's indirect tax net.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Bermuda imposes no VAT/GST and therefore has no economic turnover threshold triggering indirect tax registration. A Payroll Tax registration (Form PT/1) is triggered only when the exempted company employs Bermuda-based staff; the standard employer payroll tax rate is 2.95% on monthly remuneration above BMD 26,000 per employee (2024 rates). Offshore VPN/SaaS turnover generates zero indirect tax registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is structurally inapplicable because Bermuda levies no VAT/GST. All VPN subscription and digital privacy services supplied to non-Bermudian customers constitute 'foreign direct business' under the exempted undertaking regime and are wholly exempt from Bermuda taxation; no export declaration, VAT certificate, or fiscal clearance is required to export digital services. The 15% CIT (from 1 January 2025) applies only to in-scope Pillar Two multinational groups and is not an indirect/export tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration - required solely for physical goods movements; VPN software delivered via internet requires no customs documentation.
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill - required only where network hardware (e.g., VPN appliances) is physically exported from Bermuda; not applicable to pure SaaS.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT receipt - recommended evidence of offshore revenue repatriation to Bermuda bank accounts; Bermuda imposes no exchange-control mandate.

None - Bermuda has no mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based tax reporting, or digital stamping regime. The Bermuda Customs Department operates ASYCUDA World exclusively for physical import/export declarations and cargo manifests; the VPN service provider may issue invoices freely without any government fiscal clearance.

Fraudulent or erroneous export zero-rating claims are inapplicable absent VAT; nevertheless, the Tax Commissioner may re-assess in-scope CIT profit for up to six years after filing, with an unlimited period where fraud or wilful default is shown. Penalties include a 100% uplift of the tax under-assessed, interest at 6.5% per annum, administrative fines of up to BMD 50,000 for non-compliance with Economic Substance record-keeping, and revocation of the Certificate of Exemption with retrospective loss of 1966 Act protections. For Payroll Tax mis-reporting, penalties reach 100% of the unpaid tax plus criminal fines up to BMD 10,000.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar (BMD) is fixed at par to the US dollar (USD). Exchange control applies only to residents; an exempted company designated non-resident under the Exchange Control Act may freely hold and transact in any foreign currency. Capital and current account convertibility is unrestricted for non-resident designated companies, and profit remittances in foreign currency require no BMA permit. Remittance in BMD requires exchange control permission, but companies operating predominantly in USD, EUR, or GBP circumvent this friction. No statistical or administrative hurdles are imposed on outbound dividends, interest, royalties, or software licensing fees, provided that audited financial statements and a board resolution approving the distribution are retained for inspection.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Bermuda imposes no statutory surrender obligation for export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio. Non-resident designated exempted companies may retain all export receipts in any freely convertible currency without a surrender timeline. Resident entities, if any, are required to obtain general or specific exchange control permission to hold foreign currency accounts, but such permission is routinely granted. For all outbound software and VPN subscription revenues, documentary evidence (invoices, payment gateway settlement statements, and bank credit notes) must be maintained for at least five years to support the FX clearing record and tax residency position.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA), acting as the monetary authority and exchange control administrator for Bermuda under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1973 (Bermuda)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility: Low to Medium for offshore digital EMIs. A UK or EU EMCI/EMI (e.g., authorised under the UK EMRs) cannot establish a payment branch or provide domestic payment services in Bermuda without a BMA licence under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 or a money service business registration. Cross-border servicing of a Bermuda exempted company is viable only on a non-domestic, outbound collection and disbursement basis, with the EMI acting as a pure processor of card or bank transfers initiated from overseas. The VPN application's enhanced AML risk means the primary bank must be a Bermuda tier-1 institution; EMI rails are acceptable solely as a front-end collector with settlement into the Butterfield or HSBC account. For high-volume recurring subscription revenue, integration of an EMI API-like Stripe/Paymentwall is feasible, but the EMI itself must not be relied on as a liquidity custodian.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% statutory baseline for an ordinary Bermuda Exempted Company; flat 15% domestic corporate income tax applies only to a Bermuda constituent entity of an in-scope multinational group under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million, effective for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Bermuda imposes no statutory withholding tax on dividends paid or distributed by an Exempted Company to UK or any other non-resident shareholders; no dividend withholding tax is codified in the Companies Act 1981 or the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BMD 0 for standard exempted companies, which are outside any local transfer-pricing documentation, Local File, or Master File regime because Bermuda levies no corporate income tax. For in-scope multinational groups under the Corporate Income Tax Act 2023, the effective mandatory documentation threshold is consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million (approximately BMD 750 million), enforced through the GloBE Information Return and the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines as adopted for Pillar Two purposes.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Substance Requirements) Act 2018 applies to every Bermuda exempted company carrying on a relevant activity. Pure VPN SaaS retail sales of network privacy applications are generally outside the relevant activity list, but if the company holds, licenses, or exploits proprietary VPN network or encryption intellectual property, it is treated as an Intellectual Property Business and must demonstrate: (i) management and control exercised in Bermuda; (ii) full-time Bermuda-resident employees with suitable qualifications; (iii) sufficient operating expenditure in Bermuda; (iv) physical premises; and (v) core income-generating activities performed in Bermuda without outsourcing. The Registrar will automatically exchange the substance determination with UK HMRC and other EU/OECD competent authorities, and the CIT Act 2023 imposes a parallel substance floor for in-scope groups.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Revenue Fraud

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fines on conviction under Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 and the UK Finance Act 2016 corporate criminal offence; confiscation orders for the full undeclared revenue and the VPN application assets; the Registrar may strike off the company and cancel the tax-protection certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors and the beneficial owner are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of the evaded tax plus HMRC penalties of 100% to 200% of the lost revenue; travel bans and worldwide asset freezes may be imposed; imprisonment for up to 14 years applies on both Bermuda and UK prosecutions.
Shell Company / Zero-Substance Abusive Structure

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BMD 250,000 under the Economic Substance (Substance Requirements) Act 2018; the adverse substance determination is automatically exchanged with UK HMRC and EU/Caribbean peers; the company may be recharacterised as UK tax-resident, exposing 19% to 25% UK corporation tax, late-payment interest, and penalties; CIT Act 2023 non-compliance penalties apply to in-scope groups.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified for up to 15 years under section 157 of the Companies Act 1981 and ordered to make civil restitution to the Crown; travel bans are available until fines and substance penalties are satisfied; imprisonment applies for knowingly false substance statements.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalties of GBP 2,500 per day for non-registration under the UK Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022; Bermuda AML/ATF regime penalties of up to BMD 100,000 per violation, even where the software vendor is not itself a licensed financial institution; payment processor termination and freezing of settlement balances for AML breaches.

Director personal liability: Directors and persons with significant control face up to 5 years' imprisonment for Companies House UBO non-registration; personal civil fines up to GBP 500,000; travel bans and seizure orders may be issued against the VPN application intellectual property and the Stripe/Paymentwall account balances.
Corporate Fraud / False Certification

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Companies Act 1981 summary conviction fines of BMD 10,000 per false item; restraint orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 up to the value of the fraud; cancellation of the certificate of incorporation, removal from the register, and disqualification of the corporate veil.

Director personal liability: Veil-piercing restitution orders attach to the director's personal assets; the Supreme Court may grant travel-ban injunctions and order removal or deportation of non-Bermudian directors; imprisonment up to 10 years for false accounting applies; professional indemnity insurance is void for fraud.
