Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bermuda Ministry of Finance – Office of the Tax Commissioner (payroll tax) and Bermuda Customs and Revenue Department (customs duty/border control); the Registrar of Companies administers company registration. The central Government portal is www.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is incorporated under the Companies Act 1981 by the Registrar of Companies, obtaining a unique company number; no general income-tax TIN exists because Bermuda levies no income tax. The entity must register with the Bermuda Customs and Revenue Department to obtain an Importer/Carrier Account Number for customs operations, with the Registrar issuing a Certificate of Compliance / Economic Substance Declaration. If the entity employs local staff, it must register with the Office of the Tax Commissioner for payroll tax and receive a Payroll Tax Account Number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election is available under Bermuda law. An Exempted Company may elect to be designated as non-resident for Exchange Control Act purposes (subject to Bermuda Monetary Authority permission), to operate under the Segregated Accounts Companies Act 2000, or to be treated as a supervised entity under sectoral legislation. For foreign tax purposes (e.g., UK/IRS), Bermuda entities are generally deemed per se corporations, and the relevant test may be central management and control, not a Bermuda filing election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Duty (no VAT/GST or general sales tax; a payroll tax applies to local remuneration but is not an indirect consumption tax)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Customs registration is mandatory for any importer or bonded carrier; payroll tax registration is required when the entity first pays Bermuda-source remuneration, with no turnover threshold. Amounts are in Bermudian dollars (BMD), pegged 1:1 to USD.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No export zero-rating exists because Bermuda has no VAT/GST. Customs duty exemption applies to goods in transit or transshipment through Bermuda that remain under customs bond and are subsequently re-exported. A bonded truck fleet operating as a feed to an international airport is eligible for duty-suspended movement if the goods are loaded in bond, transported under a Customs Transit/Transshipment Permit, physically exported or moved to a duty-free airport zone, and the relevant Customs documentation certifies that no diversion into local consumption occurred.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA/SAD)
  • Bill of Lading / Master Air Waybill evidencing export movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or equivalent evidence of receipt in Bermuda/overseas proceeds)
  • Bonded Transport / Transshipment Permit issued by Bermuda Customs
  • GPS Seal Report / Cargo Security Manifest for bonded truck movements
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List

Bermuda does not impose an electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate. Customs declarations are processed electronically via the ASYCUDA World system; bonded movements are tracked using the Bermuda Customs Single Administrative Document and, where applicable, GPS-sealed container/truck data. No API-based real-time VAT clearance applies because no VAT regime exists.

Bermuda Customs and Revenue Department may audit bonded-carrier records, GPS telemetry, and customs declarations. Erroneous or fraudulent claims for duty-free/bonded movement are not 'zero-rating clawbacks' in the VAT sense, but trigger seizure/forfeiture of goods, fines of up to three times the value of the duty evaded under the Customs Act 1967, suspension or cancellation of bonded-carrier licences, and criminal prosecution for knowingly false declarations. Bermuda's economic substance regime also requires adequate physical presence and core income-generating activity records; failure to substantiate export movements may lead to information penalties and, where relevant, enhanced documentary scrutiny by the Bermuda Tax Commissioner.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian Dollar (BMD) is pegged 1:1 to USD and is freely convertible. Bermuda operates a two-tier regime: resident Bermudian-dollar accounts remain subject to BMA capital-account control, whereas non-resident External Accounts held by a 100% non-resident-owned exempted company are exempt from transaction-by-transaction approval and may be maintained in USD, GBP, EUR or any convertible currency. Profit remittance, upstream dividends and liquidation proceeds from External Accounts are unrestricted. The active trade business is conducted in the UK/EU so no Bermuda-resident account classification is triggered; the UK applies no capital controls, leaving the only practical hurdle as statistical disclosure on wires above BMD 5,000,000 equivalent.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Bermuda or the UK. Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1973 and the BMA's general permission, export and trade receipts may be retained indefinitely in their original currency within an External Account. The bonded airport-feeder business may therefore accumulate USD/GBP/EUR air-freight revenue without any conversion obligation, enabling natural FX hedging of airport disbursements and eliminating forced daily conversions.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) acting as exchange-control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and Exchange Control Regulations 1973; the UK Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) supervise UK clearing banks holding company funds. BMA-licensed Authorised Dealer banks (Butterfield, Clarien) administer the liberalized permission regime on a delegated basis.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-to-High for ancillary payment routing, Low as a primary liquidity host. Legal constraints: EMIs cannot accept deposits without a Bermuda banking licence under the Banks and Depository Companies Act 1999, so float is unprotected e-money rather than insured deposits; EMIs cannot issue MT760 standby letters of credit for customs transit bonds; the BMA Digital Asset Business Act 2018 is not triggered for fiat e-money, but sub-custody concentration and throughput caps limit use.
