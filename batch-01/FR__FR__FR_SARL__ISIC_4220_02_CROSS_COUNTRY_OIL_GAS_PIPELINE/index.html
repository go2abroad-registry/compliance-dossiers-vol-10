Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

The SARL has no statutory minimum share capital (minimum freely fixed, normally €1 or higher); paid-in capital must be effective at formation. Cash contributions must be deposited in a French bank account opened in the name of the company 'en formation' and blocked until registration; the banker issues an 'attestation de dépôt de fonds'. If the articles provide for staged release of capital, the unpaid balance must be paid within 5 years; for cash contributions, at least one-fifth (20%) is payable at subscription. Given the large-scale gas pipeline EPC activity, a paid-up capital of €50,000–€100,000 is recommended to satisfy bank tender warranties and surety lines.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: hot-work permits ('permis de feu') are mandatory for every orbital welding station, with fire-extinguishing standby and gas-cutting safety measures under the Labour Code (Articles R. 4224-14 et seq.); site accommodation and storage cabins require a fire-safety certificate issued by the local commission de sécurité when the site is open to the public; structural-load inspections for deep trench shoring (paroi blindée) must be certified for trenches deeper than 1.20 m in accordance with Article L. 4535-2 of the Labour Code; occupational clearance is evidenced by welder qualification cards, ATEX-zone work permits for high-pressure gas mains, machine CE-marking and periodic technical inspections, and the SPS coordination plan approved by DREETS.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains full euro convertibility with no exchange controls: Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union are applied without restriction, so current-account and capital-account transfers (dividends, repayment of shareholder advances, interest, fees, liquidation proceeds) are free for residents and non-residents without any central-bank licence or prior forex approval. The only residual frictions are (i) the DG Trésor foreign-investment screening regime for non-EEA investors in sensitive sectors, where cross-country oil and gas pipeline EPC activities are covered by Decree No. 2019-1590 if the investment confers control over French energy-infrastructure capabilities; and (ii) the corporate-law profit-remittance tests: the SARL must satisfy the statutory legal reserve requirement (5% of annual net profit until 10% of share capital is reached under Article L.232-10 of the Code de commerce), pass the balance-sheet net-asset test for distributions, and provide full KYC/source-of-funds evidence to the paying French bank. Dividend remittance wires are executed with purpose code DIVD and may require audited financial statements and a board/shareholders' resolution confirming the economic ownership of the beneficiary.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), acting as the French central bank and Eurosystem member, jointly with the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury of the Ministry of the Economy) as the foreign-exchange and capital-control administration, and with the ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution) as the prudential supervisor of credit institutions and electronic-money institutions.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (standard rate for financial years ending on or after 1 January 2022); 15% reduced rate on the first EUR 42,000 of taxable profit for qualifying SMEs (Art. 219 CGI).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

25% domestic withholding on gross dividends paid to non-resident corporate shareholders (Art. 119 bis 2 CGI); 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for a qualifying parent holding at least 10% for 2 years; DTA-reduced rates generally 5% to 15% (e.g., France-US treaty: 5% for 10%+ corporate shareholders, 15% portfolio; France-UK and France-Germany: 0% under Directive alignment).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File and Local File: EUR 400,000,000 in annual turnover or gross assets (entity or French group aggregate) under Art. L13 AA LPF; simplified annual TP return (Form 2258-SD): EUR 50,000,000; Country-by-Country Reporting: group consolidated turnover of EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: France does not operate a designated ESR statute of the type used in offshore financial centres; however, economic substance is mandatory through the abuse-of-law doctrine (Art. L64 LPF), the ATAD-inspired GAAR and anti-hybrid rules (Art. 205 A CGI), and the substance requirements embedded in DTA entitlement. A French SARL engaged in cross-country oil and gas pipeline EPC must have genuine management, personnel, premises and risk-taking capacity in France; a 'letterbox' entity channelling project profits to a zero-tax affiliate will be disregarded and re-assessed.
