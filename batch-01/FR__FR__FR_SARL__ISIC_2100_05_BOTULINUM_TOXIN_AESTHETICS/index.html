Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) – portal: impots.gouv.fr; professional VAT portal: gérer la TVA within impots.gouv.fr

E-government filing portal

A foreign SARL must obtain French SIREN/SIRET identifiers from INSEE via the INPI/CFE one-stop shop. For VAT, the entity must apply to DGFiP for a French intra-community VAT number (n° TVA intracommunautaire). If no French permanent establishment, a tax representative accredited by DGFiP may be required. Application file: exterior proof of legal existence, articles of association, identity documents, expected French turnover, and optionally a compliance mandate for non-EU residents.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign SARL must obtain French SIREN/SIRET identifiers from INSEE via the INPI/CFE one-stop shop. For VAT, the entity must apply to DGFiP for a French intra-community VAT number (n° TVA intracommunautaire). If no French permanent establishment, a tax representative accredited by DGFiP may be required. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Default: separate corporate income tax (IS) registration for the SARL. Small SARLs may elect pass-through taxation where receipts are taxed in the hands of the associates under CGI Art. 239 bis AA. No general US-style check-the-box election; France applies limited elections, including option for territorial extension, group integration (intégration fiscale) and branch permanent-establishment protection.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For EU-established taxable persons: 91,900 EUR annual turnover excluding VAT for sales of goods or 37,500 EUR for taxable services (2025 franchise de base). For non-EU or foreign SARL without French nexus: no threshold, VAT registration is mandatory as soon as the first taxable supply occurs in France or the reverse charge applies to imported services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of botulinum toxin formulations qualify for French 0% VAT when (1) the goods are physically shipped to a destination outside the EU; (2) transport begins and is completed within 12 months of the sale; (3) the seller holds objective evidence of exportation; (4) the goods are not consumed or diverted within the EU. For services strictly ancillary to exported goods (loading, quality inspection, cold-chain transport to the frontier), zero-rating is also available. B2B services rendered to non-EU contract research organizations are not zero-rated export services but follow the place-of-supply rules (outside France, therefore not subject to VAT).

  • Customs Export Declaration (DAU/EXS electronic export summary declaration and movement reference number)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note (with exporter's identity and destination)
  • Commercial invoice (signed, itemized, stating HS code 3002 or ISIC 2100 product category)
  • Packing list (with lot numbers, cold-chain labels, time-temperature data for biologics)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/telex bank credit confirming receipt from the foreign buyer
  • Certificate of non-EU customs entry or territoriality attestation when the first destination is an EU hub and final dispatch is to a third country

E-invoicing and e-reporting reform under Loi de Finances implementing the EU ViDA framework. Mandatory acceptance of structured electronic invoices via a registered PDP (Platforme de Dématérialisation Partenaire) or the public Chorus Pro portal for all B2B transactions established in France: mandatory issuance from 1 September 2026 for large and mid-size enterprises, from 1 September 2027 for small/medium enterprises. E-reporting of cross-border and domestic transaction data via API connection to DGFiP or certified platforms; no per-transaction real-time fiscal stamping is required for goods exports, but export substantiation documents must be retained and submitted upon audit demand.

DGFiP will require continuous reconciliation between export declarations, transport documents, and foreign-currency receipts. Inadequate documentation leads to immediate re-invoice of VAT at 20%, plus late-payment interest (intérêts de retard) at 0.2% per month and monetary penalties. A deliberate denial or misstatement of export evidence triggers the 40% bad-faith penalty (manquement délibéré) and an 80% penalty for dissimulation of taxable facts or abusive behavior (répression de l'abus de droit under CGI Art. 1729). Fraudulent zero-rating claims may also be prosecuted under CGI Art. 1741 with criminal fines up to €500,000 and imprisonment. Retrospective reassessment extends to 6 years for normal audit periods, and to 10 years when the taxpayer carried on a concealed activity.
