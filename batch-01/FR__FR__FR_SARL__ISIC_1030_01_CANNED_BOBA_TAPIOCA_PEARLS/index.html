Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum share capital; the articles freely fix the capital, but all shares must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions must be paid up at least to one-fifth of their amount at incorporation, with the balance callable within five years. Funds representing cash contributions must be deposited in a blocked French bank or notarial account before registration; a bank attestation is filed with the RCS. No general escrow account is otherwise imposed.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance requires prior review by the Service Départemental d'Incendie et de Secours or an approved inspector, including evacuation routes, RIA or sprinkler coverage and ICPE fire compartments. Structural clearance requires an engineering certificate that floors, mezzanines and aseptic tanks comply with Eurocodes and the building permit conditions. Occupational clearance consists of a DREETS labour inspection, a document unique d'évaluation des risques, electrical conformity certification, emergency stop systems, personal protective equipment and medical surveillance for workers exposed to heat, steam or noise.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum share capital; the articles freely fix the capital, but all shares must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions must be paid up at least to one-fifth of their amount at incorporation, with the balance callable within five years. Funds representing cash contributions must be deposited in a blocked French bank or notarial account before registration; a bank attestation is filed with the RCS. No general escrow account is otherwise imposed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France is in the euro area; the euro is fully convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions. EU Treaty Articles 63-65 prohibit restrictions on payments and capital movements between Member States and, subject to safeguard measures, with third countries. There is no general FX-licence, convertibility approval, or capital-control mechanism for a food-manufacturing SARL; the only applicable frictions are sanctions screening under EU regulations, targeted FDI screening if an investment were made in a sensitive French sector, and bank AML/KYC due diligence. Profit remittance is not blocked by exchange control, but dividend payments must satisfy French corporate law, be recorded in the SARL's accounts, and may carry non-resident withholding tax unless relieved by treaty, the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, or a domestic exemption. Cross-border payments are subject to mandatory EUR anti-money-laundering data transmission under EU Regulation 2023/1113 and to Banque de France balance-of-payments statistical reporting where the reporting threshold is met.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France imposes no surrender obligation, no statutory conversion deadline, and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. An exporting SARL may retain EUR, USD, CNY, THB, VND, or other currencies in its bank accounts without central-bank permission; the mandatory conversion ratio is 0 percent. Export proceeds are subject only to general commercial record-keeping, customs/Intrastat and VAT declarations for goods, and the banking intermediary's reporting of cross-border transactions for Banque de France statistics above applicable thresholds. There is no timeline within which foreign currency must be repatriated or exchanged into EUR.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France, with the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury) administering balance-of-payments and external financial reporting; banking supervision is delegated to the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Medium-High only as an ancillary collection/payout rail and Low as the principal treasury rail. An EU/EEA payment institution or electronic-money institution may provide services in France through its home-state licence and PSD2 passporting, subject to ACPR notification. An offshore digital EMI domiciled outside the EEA cannot lawfully maintain a resident payment account or hold client funds for a French SARL without obtaining a French or EEA authorisation; relying on such an institution would create licensing, sanctions, tax, documentary-letter-of-credit, and business-continuity risks. EU-licensed EMIs may be used for low-value SEPA supplier payouts and expense accounts, but they are not a substitute for a tier-1 French bank for capital injection, commercial letters of credit, or SWIFT MT103 trade payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For a capital injection from non-resident shareholders, use a SEPA Credit Transfer if the remitter is in the EEA or a SWIFT MT103/TT transfer if the remitter is outside the EEA. The funds should be remitted in EUR to the SARL's blocked capital account or operational account in France; no central-bank FX approval is needed because France applies no capital-control surcharge or convertibility restriction. In the payment reference field, state the exact purpose, for example 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / SHARE SUBSCRIPTION / SHARE PREMIUM — FR_SARL'. France does not prescribe a mandatory ISO 20022 purpose code for inbound capital, but the beneficiary bank will classify the transaction for Banque de France balance-of-payments reporting as a direct-investment capital flow and may request a declaration where the statutory threshold applies. The bank will verify that the remitter is the registered shareholder, the UBO and source of funds are documented, the shareholder resolution and corporate authorisations are valid, and any required legal formality such as deposit of funds, notarial deed for in-kind contributions, or capital-increase publicity has been completed. Shareholder loans should be documented by a formal loan agreement with commercial interest terms and should be referenced as 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN' to avoid misclassification as distributive income.Use SWIFT MT103/TT for direct payments to non-EEA suppliers of tapioca starch, packaging materials, and logistics services; use commercial documentary Letters of Credit issued under UCP600 by a French tier-1 bank, normally transmitted by SWIFT MT700/MT710, when suppliers require a bank payment undertaking for large one-off container purchases. Use SEPA Credit Transfers to EUR IBAN accounts for EU suppliers and service providers. Documentary collections under URC 522 can support lower-risk open-account transactions. The same bank infrastructure channels export bills, pre-shipment finance, and trade advances. All USD-denominated outgoing payments will be routed through the bank's correspondent network and must be screened for EU and OFAC sanctions, and all Hedging schedules should be confirmed against real commercial receivables and payables.Outright EUR/USD and EUR/CNH forward contracts with a licensed French credit institution, supported by an ISDA or French master agreement and documented commercial exposure.FX swaps for temporary conversion between EUR and USD, CNY, THB, or VND where the bank provides a deliverable quote.Cross-currency swaps for medium-term USD financing, intra-group loans, or recurring export receivables.Non-deliverable forwards for soft or restricted currencies such as VND or IDR where no deliverable onshore forward market is available to the French SARL.FX options and option collars used exclusively to hedge real trade flows, with no speculative leverage, and compliant with EMIR transaction reporting and MiFID II client categorisation.
