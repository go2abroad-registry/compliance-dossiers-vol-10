Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) kept by the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce of the jurisdiction of the registered office; filing is made electronically via the official national Guichet Unique operated by the Institut National de la Propriété Industrielle (INPI) at formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce), Book II, Articles L.223-1 to L.223-43 (SARL), L.123-1 et seq. (RCS registration), and R.123-104 et seq.; complemented by Décret n° 84-406 on the commercial and companies register and the unified Guichet Unique procedure under Articles R.123-1 et seq.

Formation / constitutional filings

Dossier unique de création d'entreprise (Single Incorporation File) comprising the signed articles of association (statuts), identification documents of the founding members and managers, proof of registered office occupancy, bank blockade certificate for cash contributions, and the beneficial ownership declaration (RBE). The file triggers registration in the RCS and issuance of the Kbis extract.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital is imposed after Loi PACTE n° 2019-486; capital is fixed freely in the by-laws and each share must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions are deposited in a blocked professional account opened in the name of the company in formation and released after registration. In-kind contributions exceeding €30,000 require valuation by an independent appraiser (commissaire aux apports) appointed by the shareholders; the valuation report is attached to the incorporation file.

Funding & unlock path: Commission Consultative Départementale de Sécurité et d'Accessibilité (CCDSA) approval / assessment is required for the hospital building as a Type U establishment (public health facility); the company submits the fire-safety documentation, the medical gas shut-off interlocks, the pressure-relief venting and the structural load calculations to the departmental fire and rescue service (SDIS) before commissioning. Occupational clearance requires a worksite coordination plan (plan général de coordination), a company-specific prevention plan with the hospital operator, inspection at completion by the DREETS labor inspectorate, verification of atmospheric exposure and the provision of individual and collective gas detection equipment compliant with Code du travail Articles R.4222-6 and R.4412-97.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital is imposed after Loi PACTE n° 2019-486; capital is fixed freely in the by-laws and each share must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions are deposited in a blocked professional account opened in the name of the company in formation and released after registration. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) — Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE). Official portals: impots.gouv.fr (national tax portal) and formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr (single registry filing portal).

E-government filing portal

A French SARL is first registered with the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce via the Guichet Unique / formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr; the completed registration produces the extrait Kbis and a SIREN/SIRET, which constitute the French business tax identification. DGFiP then allocates the French VAT number (FR + 11 digits) and records it in the SIRENE database. For a non-resident medical gas pipeline contractor without a French permanent establishment, direct VAT registration is required with the territorial SIE prior to making taxable supplies, imports, or intra-Community acquisitions in France; the applicant must supply evidence of foreign incorporation, board authorization, fiscal residency, and, for non-EU entities, may be required to appoint an accredited tax representative. The SIREN serves as the principal identification for corporate income tax and local territorial economic contributions.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope. A French SARL is a separate limited liability taxpayer subject by default to impôt sur les sociétés (IS). France applies a territoriality principle: profits are taxable in France when derived from operations carried on in France or attributable to French establishments. The SARL is not a pass-through entity absent the exceptional small-family income tax election.

Available election forms

Under CGI Art. 239 bis AA, a qualifying closely held family SARL may elect income tax treatment (IR), but the default is entity-level corporation tax. IS taxpayers may elect tax consolidation under Art. 223 A et seq. for a 95% or more French subsidiary group. France has no check-the-box election; entity classification follows the French legal and tax form. Anti-hybrid mismatch rules under the ATAD transposition in CGI Art. 212 and 238 quaterdecies restrict deduction regimes and require coherent treatment of foreign entity classifications.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For non-established suppliers there is no general economic VAT registration threshold: registration is triggered by the first taxable supply, import, or intra-EU acquisition in France. For domestic small traders, the franchise en base de TVA thresholds are €91,900 for supplies of goods and €38,300 for supplies of services (2024), but no franchise is available to a non-resident EPC contractor. For intra-EU B2C distance sales the €10,000 EU-wide annual threshold applies; above this, French VAT may be declared through the OSS portal. A France-established entity is obliged to register from its first taxable transaction, regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under CGI Art. 262-I-1° to supplies of goods exported outside the EU, provided title has passed and the goods physically leave EU territory within the legally allowed period. Services directly connected to exported goods, and services performed and consumed outside France, generally fall outside French VAT territorial scope, allowing 0% treatment. For hospital medical gas pipeline EPC, zero-rating is available for goods shipped from France to a non-EU hospital; on-site installation and construction services performed abroad are outside French VAT territoriality, while local French works remain taxable at 20% unless another exception applies. The exporter or its agent must hold contemporaneous documentary proof of export before the issue of the zero-rated invoice.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / D.A.U. with DELTA electronic acceptance report)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, CMR or EU exit summary declaration showing actual departure from EU
  • Commercial invoice and packing list reflecting export customer, Incoterms, and customs value
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence demonstrating receipt of proceeds from the foreign buyer
  • Signed delivery / installation handover certificate (PV de réception) evidencing physical completion and cross-border supply

France has a phased mandatory e-invoicing and e-reporting regime: large and medium enterprises from 1 September 2026, small and medium-size enterprises from 1 September 2027. B2B invoices, including export invoices, must be transmitted in a structured electronic format (Factur-X, Universal Business Language, or Cross-Industry Invoice) through an accredited Plateforme de Dématérialisation Partenaire (PDP) connected to the public Plateforme Publique de Facturation / Chorus Pro. Real-time transmission of transaction data, payment status, and cross-border supply data to DGFiP is required through APIs; there is no physical digital stamping, but the system creates a continuous fiscal audit trail. Import/export customs declarations remain in the DELTA and ICS2 electronic systems.

DGFiP and French Customs may jointly verify export substantiation. If documentary proof is missing or inconsistent, the zero rate is reversed and French VAT is assessed at 20%, with 0.20% monthly late interest. Bad-faith inaccuracies attract the 40% penalty under CGI Art. 1729; fraudulent manoeuvres, counterfeit export documents, or abusive schemes attract the 80% penalty. Failure to comply with e-invoicing/e-reporting is separately sanctionable. The standard audit and clawback period runs to the end of the third calendar year after the relevant filing year; the period is extended to six years where the business activity has not been properly declared, and to ten years for concealed or fraudulent export operations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility. As a euro-area member, France applies the EU capital-movement liberalization of Articles 63-66 TFEU: there is no exchange-approval regime, no surrender or repatriation requirement, and no foreign-exchange budget allocation. Capital and current account flows — dividends, interest, royalties, loan drawdowns and repayments, liquidation proceeds, and cross-border supplier settlements — are unrestricted. The only frictions are fiscal-treaty and reporting-based. Dividend remittances to a non-resident parent attract French withholding tax (default 25% at source under Article 119 bis CGI, reduced to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for a qualifying 10% EU parent, and typically to 5% under most bilateral treaties for a 10% non-EU participation); interest on intra-group loans is deductible within the Article 212 CGI erosion limits and may face treaty-dependent withholding. Credit institutions must report all cross-border payments to the Banque de France for Balance-of-Payments purposes, and above the operative BOP reporting threshold (in practice EUR 12,500 per transaction) the client must state the statutory transaction purpose code; cash movements of EUR 10,000 or more require a customs declaration.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion obligations apply to French resident exporters. Export receipts may be retained indefinitely in euros or in foreign currency accounts (comptes en devises) at banks in France or abroad, and may be freely converted or on-paid. France imposes no mandated timeline, no minimum conversion ratio, and no central-bank purchase requirement. The only obligations are statistical: the Banque de France Balance-of-Payments regime captures cross-border transactions through the banking channel, and customs single-window declarations (DELTA-X / DEB) record goods exports for trade statistics.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France) is the central bank and administrator of the French Balance-of-Payments statistics; prudential supervision and authorization of banks and e-money institutions is exercised by the ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution); investment-screening and exchange-control competence rests with the Direction Générale du Trésor under Articles L. 151-1 et seq. and R. 151-1 et seq. of the Code Monétaire et Financier (CMF).
