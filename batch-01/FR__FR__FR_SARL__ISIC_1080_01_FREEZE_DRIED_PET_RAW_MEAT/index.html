Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual offices are generally not acceptable for a manufacturing SARL. French law allows 'domiciliation' by a commercial distributing company (société de domiciliation) under Commercial Code Articles L.123-21-1 and D.123-200, but the arrangement must be anchored to a physical address in France. Since the business scope covers vacuum freeze-drying of pet food, which is a food-processing activity subject to approval under EU Regulation 852/2004 and the French Public Health Code, a genuine industrial site with a designated building, machinery, cold storage, and food-safety laboratory is statutorily required. Pure virtual-office registration is therefore not permitted; a domiciliation agreement is only usable for limited administrative purposes and does not satisfy the substance test for the manufacturing permit.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

For an SARL no statutory minimum share capital is required. The capital must be fully subscribed; cash contributions must be at least 20% paid up at incorporation and the remainder within five years. In-kind contributions must be fully paid up on formation. Cash contributions are deposited in a bank or notary account and blocked until the company receives its Kbis extract (Code de commerce, articles L.223-7 and R.223-5).

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification by an accredited bureau (APAVE or Bureau Veritas) in accordance with the Code de la construction et de l'habitation for industrial premises; structural load inspection of freezer/dryer floor zones; employee workplace clearance under the Code du travail, completion of the Document Unique d'Évaluation des Risques, verification of pressure vessels, electrical installations, ventilation and refrigeration systems, and registration with the local fire and occupational safety authorities.

Paid-in capital rule: For an SARL no statutory minimum share capital is required. The capital must be fully subscribed; cash contributions must be at least 20% paid up at incorporation and the remainder within five years. In-kind contributions must be fully paid up on formation. Cash contributions are deposited in a bank or notary account and blocked until the company receives its Kbis extract (Code de commerce, articles L.223-7 and R.223-5).

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France operates a full-convertibility regime under Article 63 TFEU and Articles L151-1 et seq. of the Code monétaire et financier; there is no administrative segmentation of current and capital account transactions, and both categories are freely effected without central-bank approval. The EUR is a fully convertible reserve currency. For profit remittance, a SARL may distribute dividends only after formal annual approval of the financial statements; the withheld flat tax on distributions is 30% in the ordinary domestic case (12.8% income tax plus 17.2% social levies), which may be reduced under the applicable double tax treaty for the foreign shareholder, and the payment is free of trade-control licenses or FX permits. The only operational hurdles are: (i) correct classification of the remittance in the Banque de France balance-of-payments (BR) reporting; (ii) proof that the distribution complies with the SARL's distributable profits; and (iii) for non-EU shareholders, the standard bank compliance and source-of-funds documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion requirement exists in France; export receipts in foreign currency may be credited to a designated foreign-currency account and retained indefinitely (0% mandatory conversion ratio, no statutory conversion timeline). The only binding obligations are statistical: French residents must, through their appointed credit institution, document all cross-border settlements for the Banque de France balance-of-payments, with transaction-by-transaction reporting generally applied to movements of EUR 50,000 or more; corporations generating annual aggregate cross-border settlements of EUR 50 million or more are subject to direct monthly declarations. For a freeze-dried pet-food SARL, retaining the export invoice package (French customs export declarations, Bill of Lading/CMR, EU veterinary certificates, commercial invoices) is essential to substantiate each receipt and to support a clean BR reporting line.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France) as monetary, balance-of-payments, and banking supervisor; Direction Générale du Trésor (DG Trésor / French Treasury) within the Ministry of Economy for foreign investment screening, international capital movement policy and financial sanctions; European Central Bank (ECB) in the Eurosystem for monetary policy and TARGET2 settlement.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: HIGH for operational collections, e-commerce/seller-platform settlements, payroll, and small inbound/outbound payments via EU-passported e-money institutions (Wise, Revolut Business, Qonto, SumUp); the French market recognizes passports under PSD2, but passported EMIs should nevertheless be verified in the ORIAS registry or by the Banque de France notification status. Feasibility: LOW as a substitute for statutory capital escrow: under French law, the incorporation deposit certificate (certificat du dépositaire des fonds / attestation de dépôt des fonds) used to open the SARL and obtain the Kbis must be issued by a licensed credit institution, the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, or a notary; a pure EMI license is not accepted. Additional constraints: EMIs cannot issue the bank reference letters required by landlords, insurers, or certain B2B customers, and their counterparty transaction-screening is often narrower, which may create friction for large raw-meat trade settlements. Recommended architecture: Tier-1 French bank as the treasury master account and capital escrow depositary, with one EU EMI as a collection/currency-conversion side pocket subject to daily cash sweeps into the French master account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant mechanism: (a) the foreign shareholder remits via SWIFT MT103 (TARGET2 or SEPA Credit Transfer acceptable for EU shareholders) to the SARL's dedicated capital escrow or operational account; (b) purpose code in field 70/77B uses ISO 20022 'CAPC' (Capital Payments) for equity contributions or 'FINS' for shareholder loans, and the remittance advice must cross-reference the SARL corporate name, the subscription agreement, and the intended share registry entry; (c) the receiving bank requires a gérant certification that the funds are free of third-party rights, together with the French BR 'nature d'opération' mapping (direct foreign investment equity category for share capital; associate-loan category for a 'compte courant d'associé'); (d) for non-EU inbound investments, verify that the activity does not fall within France's strategic-sector screening regime (régime des investissements étrangers en France) — freeze-dried pet raw-meat manufacturing is non-sensitive, so no prior authorization is expected, but post-closing declarations and documentary preservation are prudent; (e) verification: upon receipt, the bank issues the capital-deposit attestation or an account statement needed by the notary/CPE to complete the definitive Kbis.
