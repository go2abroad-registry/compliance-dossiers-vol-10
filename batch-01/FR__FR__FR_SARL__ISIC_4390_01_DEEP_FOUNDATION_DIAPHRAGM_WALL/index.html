Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce du lieu du siège social, via the Guichet unique des formalités d'entreprises operated by the Institut National de la Propriété Industrielle (INPI) at formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr.

Governing statute

Code de commerce, Livre II, Titre II – Articles L.223-1 to L.223-43 (régime de la Société à Responsabilité Limitée); Articles R.223-1 et seq.; Code civil general contract provisions.

Formation / constitutional filings

Dossier unique de création d'entreprise, comprising certified statuts, declaration of subscription of shares, evidence of capital deposit, legal-publication certificate, registered-office consent, and declaration of non-conviction of the proposed gérant.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital obligation under Article L.223-2. The share capital is freely fixed by the articles, must be fully subscribed, and cash contributions must be deposited in a blocked bank account or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, released solely upon registration of the company.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety clearance from the Service Départemental d'Incendie et de Secours for site accommodation, electrical cabling and fuel stores; structural-load certification by a qualified engineer for trench-boxing, slurry tanks, crane foundations and earth-retention systems; occupational-clearance certificate issued to the company's health-and-safety coordinator covering compliance with the Code du travail, the Plan Particulier de Sécurité et de Protection de la Santé (PPSPS), and the collective-prevention tasks specific to deep excavations.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital obligation under Article L.223-2. The share capital is freely fixed by the articles, must be fully subscribed, and cash contributions must be deposited in a blocked bank account or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, released solely upon registration of the company.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
The subscriber shareholders, acting at the constitutive meeting of the Société à Responsabilité Limitée, resolve: (1) adopt the Articles of Association; (2) confirm the subscription of all shares of the company’s initial capital, fully paid on issue; (3) approve the deposit of cash contributions with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations or a bank escrow account, release only upon registration; (4) set the registered office at the address indicated in Article 4 of the statutes, with the lessor’s consent; (5) appoint [Name] as Gérant to represent the company, complete filings before the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés and the Guichet Unique; and (6) authorize the Chairperson to arrange legal publication and issue copies.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

Direction Régionale de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement (DREAL) for environmental authorization; OPQIBI / QUALIBAT for professional qualification of deep-foundation and diaphragm-wall contracting.

Category: Professional-qualification certificate OPQIBI 1202 – 'Parois moulées et ouvrages souterrains de génie civil'; where applicable, a prefectoral environmental operating certificate under the Installations Classées pour la Protection de l'Environnement (ICPE) regime.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP), Ministry of Economy and Finance — Portals: impots.gouv.fr (professional corporate tax and VAT filing) and formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr (Guichet Unique for company/VAT registration); intra-EU VAT validation via VIES at ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies.

E-government filing portal

A French SARL obtains its INSEE identifiers (9-digit SIREN and 14-digit SIRET) automatically at incorporation through the Guichet Unique; the competent Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) then assigns the intra-community VAT number in the format FR + 2 check digits + SIREN upon filing the activity-commencement declaration (état 110) and issues the tax management reference (numéro de gestion) used for all DGFiP returns. A foreign EU contractor carrying out deep-foundation works in France registers directly for French VAT with the SIE covering its place of operation or activity; a non-EU contractor must appoint an accredited fiscal representative under article 289 A of the CGI and is issued a dedicated French VAT number even in the absence of a permanent establishment.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A French SARL obtains its INSEE identifiers (9-digit SIREN and 14-digit SIRET) automatically at incorporation through the Guichet Unique; the competent Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) then assigns the intra-community VAT number in the format FR + 2 check digits + SIREN upon filing the activity-commencement declaration (état 110) and issues the tax management reference (numéro de gestion) used for all DGFiP returns. A foreign EU contractor carrying out deep-foundation works in France registers directly for French VAT with the SIE covering its place of operation or activity; a non-EU contractor must appoint an accredited fiscal representative under article 289 A of the CGI and is issued a dedicated French VAT number even in the absence of a permanent establishment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Default: SARL profits are taxed at entity level under Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) at 25%, with the first €42,423 of annual profit eligible for the 15% reduced rate where turnover does not exceed €10,000,000. Available elections: (i) small active SARLs may elect pass-through income taxation under article 239 bis AA CGI for a maximum 5-year period; (ii) family SARLs ("SARL de famille") may elect quasi-partnership transparency under the same provision; (iii) groups may elect fiscal integration under article 223 A CGI, consolidating IS at the level of the 95% parent; (iv) hybrid-mismatch outcomes on intra-group financing are neutralised automatically under article 212 bis CGI and are not elective; (v) for VAT, the simplified real regime (réel simplifié) with annual filing is the default for eligible contractors, with an option to return to the monthly normal regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Franchise en base de TVA (article 293 B CGI) exemption thresholds for 2025: €91,900 for supply of goods and works and €36,800 for supply of services; exceeding either threshold triggers mandatory French VAT registration and full VAT invoicing from the first euro. B2B deep-foundation and diaphragm-wall contracts performed at French sites will in practice force opt-in registration because principals require deductible 20% VAT invoices; works on immovable property located outside France remain outside French VAT scope irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to goods physically exported from the EU under article 262-I-1° of the CGI where the DGFiP-identified exporter dispatches the goods outside the EU within 90 days of shipment and retains full proof of exit. For cross-border geotechnical works, services relating to immovable property located outside France (whether in the EU or a third country) fall outside French VAT scope under article 259-1° of the CGI; works executed on French soil for a foreign principal remain taxable at 20%, while intra-EU subcontracting between EU-established persons is reverse-charged to the recipient (autoliquidation) pursuant to Directive 2006/112/EC.

  • DAU/Single Administrative Document lodged via the DELTA customs system, including valid export declaration number and, where applicable, authorised exporter status
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR consignment note evidencing physical exit of goods from French/EU territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing collection of export proceeds in convertible currency

Mandatory e-invoicing under the Facturation Électronique reform (Loi de Finances pour 2024 timeline): from 1 September 2026 all VAT-established businesses must receive structured electronic invoices (Factur-X/EN 16931) through certified Partner Dematerialisation Platforms (PDP) or the public portal (PPF); from 1 September 2027 all must issue e-invoices, with e-reporting of transaction and payment data to DGFiP on the same two-phase schedule. France imposes no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping for domestic B2B VAT; instead it uses deferred transmission via certified PDP APIs. B2G invoicing to French public entities through Chorus Pro has been mandatory since 2020, and intra-EU sales listings are required monthly.

On audit, DGFiP re-verifies the entire export evidence chain (physical exit, exporter identity, payment trace); any deficiency recharacterises the transaction as a domestic taxable supply, triggering recovery of 20% VAT with late-payment interest at 0.2% per month (article 1727 CGI) and penalties escalating from flat fines to 40% for bad faith (mauvaise foi) and 80% for fraudulent manoeuvres (article 1729 CGI). Artificial relocation of construction services abroad may additionally be challenged as abuse of law under article L.64 of the LPF with the 80% penalty; criminal tax-evasion prosecution under article 1741 CGI carries up to 5 years' imprisonment, a €500,000 fine and confiscation of the commercial advantage obtained.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France is a euro-area State and applies unrestricted current and capital account convertibility under Articles 63 to 65 TFEU. The euro is unconditionally convertible. Profit remittances and dividends of the FR SARL are not subject to French exchange-control or Treasury prior licence; the only practical preconditions are (i) distributable reserves certified by the statutory auditor when required, (ii) settled corporate income tax and withholding-tax declarations, and (iii) compatible EU anti-tax-avoidance substance rules. Foreign-currency accounts (e.g., USD, GBP, DZD) may be opened and retained in France; balances are freely transferable, subject solely to Banque de France statistical reporting, EU/UN and French Treasury sanctions screening, and the SARL's documented source-of-funds file.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender of export proceeds into euro applies in France; no percentage of foreign-currency export receipts is required to be converted or repatriated. The SARL may invoice exports of geotechnical services and construction works in EUR or in any freely convertible currency. Export receipts may be credited to a designated foreign-currency account and retained offshore if commercially justified. For settlements with non-residents above the Banque de France balance-of-payments statistical threshold (for non-financial corporates, practically aggregate cross-border flows above EUR 15 million per calendar year for sampled entities), the SARL must file BOP declarations directly with the Banque de France using the official motive/purpose codes; below that threshold, the clearing bank automatically includes the flows in its own regulatory reporting.

Central bank supervision: Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury, Ministry of Economy, Finance and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty), acting jointly with Banque de France (BdF) and the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR) for prudential supervision, payments oversight, and balance-of-payments statistics.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for ancillary digital operations; Low-Medium as the only rail for cross-border treasury. ACPR-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Qonto, Nium, or Ebury can carry the SARL's euro treasury, execute instant SEPA transfers and direct debits, and provide simple EUR/USD or EUR/GBP conversion. Legal constraints: an EMI is not a credit institution under CRR/CRD IV; it cannot grant trade credit or working-capital facilities, cannot issue UCP600 letters of credit or performance bonds, and cannot legally hold construction-retention money under French public procurement rules. The blueprint therefore uses the Tier-1 bank as the settlement bank and the EMI only for ancillary vendor payments, with all treasury positions netted through the bank account.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Non-EEA UBOs holding more than 25% of the SARL through an intermediary entity located in a listed tax-non-cooperative jurisdiction or offshore financial centre.

Root cause: Non-EEA UBOs holding more than 25% of the SARL through an intermediary entity located in a listed tax-non-cooperative jurisdiction or offshore financial centre.

Defense evidence: Kbis extract issued within the previous three months; notarized and apostilled identity documents of all UBOs; French UBO declaration receipt under article L.561-46 CMF; share register and organizational chart; audited financial statements and tax residency certificate of the intermediate entity to evidence economic substance; independent bank reference letters and transactional history explaining the source of funds.
Incoming capital injection from a shareholder or holding company whose payment profile resembles round-tripping or unregistered financial lending.

Root cause: Incoming capital injection from a shareholder or holding company whose payment profile resembles round-tripping or unregistered financial lending.

Defense evidence: Authenticated shareholders' resolution approving the capital increase or blocked-account arrangement; certified articles of association; legal opinion confirming the absence of disguised loan or thin-capitalization issue; SWIFT UETR and Banque de France purpose code; confirmation of source of funds (divestiture, salary, audited retained earnings); board treasury minutes justifying the funding need for ISIC 4390 contract mobilization.
Milestone invoices payable by documentary credit from a foreign State-owned contractor located in a jurisdiction with sanctions or high-corruption indices.

Root cause: Milestone invoices payable by documentary credit from a foreign State-owned contractor located in a jurisdiction with sanctions or high-corruption indices.

Defense evidence: Signed construction contract with ICC Incoterms, payment schedule and dispute jurisdiction; tender award evidence avoiding direct-interest conflicts; insurance and parent-company guarantees; certificate of quantities and work progress (métré/attachement) countersigned by the engineer; negative media and sanctions screening (World-Check/LexisNexis) on the payer and all intermediaries; Sapin II anti-corruption assessment of local agents.

Root cause: Treasury anomalies incompatible with the ISIC 4390 / NAICS 238910 cash cycle: excessive advance mobilization payments, split deposits, or rapid transfers to third-country subcontractors before equipment and labor are visibly deployed.

Defense evidence: Detailed mobilization calendar; invoices for diaphragm wall equipment (trench cutters, slurry treatment plants, tremie pipes, polymer/bentonite); site permits and environmental filings (loi sur l'eau / ICPE); project cash-flow budget; subcontracts and supplier contracts; visual/video progression records; external engineering certificates verifying physical quantities.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant route is a SWIFT MT103 (or ISO 20022 pacs.008) credit to the FR SARL's euro IBAN, carrying the Banque de France balance-of-payments purpose code for equity investment / increase of capital (direct investment category) and retaining the UETR for traceability. The bank files the flows in the balance-of-payments; the SARL has a direct statutory declaration duty only if annual cross-border flows exceed the Banque de France threshold for non-financial corporates (practically EUR 15 million in combined receipts/payments) or if selected in a BdF survey. For non-EEA investors acquiring 25% or more of the SARL's voting rights, the parties must screen the transaction against article L.151-3 of the Code monétaire et financier (strategic sectors); geotechnical and diaphragm wall services are not per se a protected sector, but confirmation is warranted when the end-client operates in defense, energy, transport, or critical infrastructure. The documentation envelope for the bank: Kbis, articles of association, UBO register, shareholders' resolution, source-of-funds evidence, certified accounts, and a short legal memorandum showing absence of prohibited financial assistance.Clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 / ISO 20022 pacs.008 for ordinary milestone payments to EU and non-EU subcontractors, using the UETR and the correct BOP service code (construction and engineering services under BPM6); (2) UCP600 commercial and standby credits issued by the relationship bank via SWIFT MT700 when the SARL receives its client's LC or back-to-back imports slurry equipment; (3) performance bonds and retention guarantees (caution personnelle et solidaire / caution de retenue) lodged through the issuing bank's SWIFT network. All outflows must be reconciled with underlying contract liabilities, EU customs declarations, French VAT reverse-charge where applicable, and internal treasury sign-off limits. USD payables for bentonite and heavy machinery should be settled through the same bank with a covering FX forward to lock the treasury margin.Plain vanilla FX forwards (change à terme) with tenors aligned to the contract milestone schedule (six to thirty-six months), documented against confirmed export receivables or import payables.European-style FX options and zero-premium collars with ACPR-regulated French/EEA credit institutions, used to manage USD/EUR, GBP/EUR, and DZD volatility on the construction portfolio.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) under a 2002 ISDA Master Agreement with EMIR trade reporting and uncleared margin rules (variation margin and initial margin) where the SARL's aggregate derivative notional exceeds the applicable EMIR risk-mitigation threshold.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs), permissible only for emerging-market currencies with restricted convertibility (e.g., Algerian dinar, Egyptian pound), strictly backed by an underlying export contract receivable; NDFs cannot be used for speculation or uncovered positions under the SARL's board-approved treasury policy.
