Statutory Name Clearance & Restricted Words

Official search database

Guichet unique des formalités des entreprises operated by the Institut national de la propriété industrielle (INPI): https://formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr; company-name and trademark searches should also be conducted through the Registre national des entreprises and INPI trademark databases.

Name reservation / lock period

France has no general statutory 30-day company-name reservation certificate comparable to systems using a formal negative certificate. Conduct and document the name search before filing, including RNE, INPI trademark, EUIPO, domain, and trade-name checks. If the Guichet unique or registry rejects the proposed name, obtain the electronic rejection notice, identify whether the ground is similarity, protected terminology, public-order concern, or filing inconsistency, and correct the name or supporting evidence. Resubmit through the Guichet unique within the stated correction period. For an adverse formal registry decision, seek rectification before the competent registry or commercial court and, where legally available, challenge the decision under the applicable administrative or judicial procedure. Use a different compliant name immediately if incorporation timing is critical; do not rely on an informal reservation.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS), maintained by the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce; filings centralized through the Guichet Unique, the official online portal (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) operated by the Institut National de la Propriété Industrielle (INPI).

Governing statute

French Code de commerce (Commercial Code): Articles L.223-1 to L.223-43 (SARL formation, capital, and management), L.223-2 (no minimum share capital), R.223-1 to R.223-26 and R.123-1 et seq. (RCS registration formalities); Décret n°2021-1730 (Guichet Unique).

Formation / constitutional filings

Demande d'immatriculation (Formulaire Unique de transmission de données) together with the executed Statuts (Articles of Association) and the attestation of publication in a Journal d'Annonces Légales.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SARL (Article L.223-2 of the Commercial Code); the Statuts may set the capital at any positive amount, commonly €1, and the entire cash contribution must be fully paid at subscription. Cash contributions are blocked in a dedicated account opened with a bank or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations; the funds are released to the company only upon production of the RCS registration certificate (K-bis).

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance: favorable inspection notice from the departmental Commission de la Sécurité Contre l'Incendie et la Panique (CSICEP) for the industrial premises; structural integrity certificate issued by an approved Bureau de Contrôle covering column process loads and GMP clean-room floors; workplace clearance under the Code du travail (ventilation R.4222-1 et seq., electrical safety, and chemical agent risk prevention R.4412-1 et seq.) evidenced by a registre de sécurité.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SARL (Article L.223-2 of the Commercial Code); the Statuts may set the capital at any positive amount, commonly €1, and the entire cash contribution must be fully paid at subscription. Cash contributions are blocked in a dedicated account opened with a bank or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations; the funds are released to the company only upon production of the RCS registration certificate (K-bis).

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
The shareholders (associés), upon incorporation of the SARL under the French Code de commerce, hereby: (1) acknowledge receipt of the Statuts and approve the corporate name; (2) ratify the subscription and full release of capital contributions by each founding associate; (3) adopt the registered office address and authorize its relocation by the Manager; (4) appoint the first Manager (Gérant) and determine powers and remuneration; (5) approve the corporate object expressly covering pharmaceutical fractionation of heparin; (6) authorize the opening of a bank account, filing before the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce, and publication of the legal notice; (7) order the Manager to execute all formalities necessary for registration.

Agence Nationale de Sécurité du Médicament et des Produits de Santé (ANSM) — Direction de l'Inspection des Etablissements pharmaceutiques; coordination with DREAL for classified installations.

Category: Autorisation d'ouverture d'établissement pharmaceutique for the manufacture, import and quality control of low-molecular-weight heparin crude and active substances of animal origin under Articles L.5136-1 and R.5136-14 of the Code de la santé publique (EU GMP equivalent licensing).

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France operates as a full member of the Eurozone with unrestricted capital and current account convertibility under EU free movement rules and the French Monetary and Financial Code. Profit remittances to shareholders are permitted without restriction provided tax filings and AML reporting are satisfied. No capital controls apply to EU-resident entities; non-resident transactions require prior declaration for amounts exceeding €100,000.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or forced conversion of export proceeds required in France. Export receipts must be declared to customs and tax authorities within 30 days of shipment. Conversion occurs at prevailing market rates; banks process via SWIFT with purpose codes. Statutory reporting thresholds apply for AML compliance.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Permitted under EU Electronic Money Institution regime supervised by Banque de France and EBA. Viability rating: Medium. Legal constraints include mandatory authorization, minimum capital of €350,000, strict AML/KYC obligations, and enhanced due diligence for pharmaceutical sectors.
