Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital; however, cash contributions must be fully paid up at subscription or at least 20% paid up at incorporation (balance within 5 years). Capital must be deposited in a blocked escrow account with a French bank or the Caisse des Dépôts until registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the local fire department (Service Départemental d'Incendie et de Secours – SDIS) for premises > 300 m²; structural load inspection by a certified engineer (Bureau de Contrôle) to ensure floor load capacity for heavy equipment; workplace clearance (permis de travail) from the DREETS for compliance with the Labor Code (Code du travail) – including electrical safety, emergency exits, and ergonomic standards.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital; however, cash contributions must be fully paid up at subscription or at least 20% paid up at incorporation (balance within 5 years). Capital must be deposited in a blocked escrow account with a French bank or the Caisse des Dépôts until registration.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP); day-to-day corporate and VAT compliance handled by the territorial Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE); large international groups file with the Direction des Grandes Entreprises (DGE); non-resident businesses use the DGFiP non-resident department. Primary portals: impots.gouv.fr (filing, TIN status, VIES, OSS/IOSS access), formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr (single registration window), and douane.gouv.fr (DELTA export declarations).

E-government filing portal

STEP 1 — Incorporate the SARL through the mandatory Guichet Unique at formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr, submitting certified articles of association, proof of registered office lease, appointment of the gérant (manager), directors' identification and the beneficial-owner declaration (RBE). INSEE then issues the 9-digit SIREN and 14-digit SIRET, and the competent Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce records the company in the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés. STEP 2 — DGFiP automatically opens a fiscal dossier and transmits the intra-Community VAT number (format: FR + two-digit key + 9-digit SIREN), together with the corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés) account reference and a payment account for the SIE. VIES verification is available after issuance. STEP 3 — For a non-resident parent establishing a French fixed establishment rather than a French SARL, the same Guichet Unique or, where no French entity is incorporated, the DGFiP non-resident registration route (including the declaration d'existence form 1442-SD) is used to obtain a SIREN and French VAT number; the EU One-Stop-Shop is only available for distance sales of goods/imports and does not replace full VAT registration for French warehousing, manufacturing or export operations. STEP 4 — File the first or periodic VAT return (CA3) with the SIE and retain the French VAT ID before claiming any 0% export treatment. Total typical issuance time: 2-4 weeks from incorporation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a standard commercial SARL is taxable as a separate legal person on Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) and is not pass-through. France additionally applies the territoriality principle under Article 209-I CGI: profits of the French business establishment are taxed in France, while profits attributable to a genuine foreign permanent establishment may be exempt; no tax-exempt zone status applies to an ordinary metropolitan France SARL.

Available election forms

France has no check-the-box election. Available statutory mechanisms include: (1) fiscal integration election under Article 223-A CGI for a 95%+ French group, consolidating IS results; (2) VAT group election under Article 256-C CGI allowing jointly-controlled group members to form a single taxable person; (3) for qualifying small companies, the reduced 15% IS rate on the first EUR 42,500 of annual profit (requiring revenue below EUR 10 million, applied via the tax return election); (4) election for the simplified real tax accounting regime (régime simplifié d'imposition) below the statutory turnover ceiling; (5) optional 3-year IS exoneration for new qualifying businesses in assisted areas under Article 44-sexies CGI; (6) the authorization-based 'bénéfice mondial ou consolidé' election under Article 209-quinquies CGI to switch from territoriality to worldwide taxation; and (7) mandatory — not elective — application of the EU Anti-Tax-Avoidance Directive hybrid-mismatch rules and the OECD Pillar Two GloBE top-up tax (QDMTT/IIR) for groups above EUR 750 million. Ordinary SARLs do not qualify for the 'SARL de famille' personal income tax option unless the shareholders are family members, which is not available to a foreign-controlled SARL acting as an exporter/manufacturer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 10,000 for intra-EU distance sales of goods where the OSS threshold applies; for B2B manufacturers and exporters there is no threshold — VAT registration is compulsory from the first taxable transaction under Article 286 CGI. The domestic 'franchise en base' exemption (Article 293-B CGI: EUR 85,000 for goods, EUR 25,000 for services) does not benefit an exporting SARL that must recover input VAT on components, machinery and logistics, and export sales must be declared with zero-rating entitlements through a full VAT registration.

France mandates structured electronic invoicing under Ordonnance n°2022-1473 and Article 289-bis CGI. Phased application: from 1 September 2026 all businesses must be able to receive e-invoices and large/medium enterprises (grandes entreprises and ETI) must issue them; from 1 September 2027 all small and micro enterprises must issue. Invoices must transit through an accredited Partner Dematerialization Platform (PDP) or the Public Invoicing Portal (PPF) using structured formats compliant with EN 16931 (Factur-X, UBL, CII or EDI); cross-border exports and B2C transactions are covered by e-reporting of line-level transaction and payment data to DGFiP. The French model is continuous transmission to the tax administration with platform acknowledgement, not real-time tax-authority pre-clearance or digital stamping of each invoice; penalties for non-compliant e-invoicing apply from the enforcement dates under Article 1737 CGI (per-invoice fines and refusal of deduction).
