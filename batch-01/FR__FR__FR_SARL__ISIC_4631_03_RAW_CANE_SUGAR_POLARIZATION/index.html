Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The competent local Commercial and Companies Registry (Registre du commerce et des sociétés, RCS), maintained by the clerk of the territorially competent Commercial Court (greffe du tribunal de commerce); company-creation formalities are submitted through the INPI-operated Guichet unique des formalités d’entreprises.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce), Articles L.223-1 to L.223-43 and R.123-1 et seq.; SARL formation, management, and capital rules are principally set out in Articles L.223-1, L.223-2, L.223-7, L.223-18, and L.223-30.

Formation / constitutional filings

Electronic company-creation formalité submitted through the Guichet unique, including executed articles of association (statuts) and the required declarations and supporting documents; successful filing results in RCS registration and issuance of a SIREN number and Kbis extract.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 22 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum share capital applies to a SARL. At least 20% of cash contributions must be paid on incorporation; the balance must be paid within five years of RCS registration. Cash contributions are deposited before filing with an eligible depositary, such as a bank, notary, or Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, and released upon proof of registration. Contributions in kind are generally fully contributed at formation; an auditor for contributions may be required, subject to statutory exemptions.

Funding & unlock path: No general civil-defense clearance is required solely for wholesale trading. A warehouse must satisfy applicable building, fire-safety, and workplace rules; obtain planning and building approvals where required; maintain fire equipment and emergency procedures; and arrange workplace risk assessment and prevention measures. Public-access premises may require ERP fire-safety and accessibility compliance. Structural inspections and fire-authority review depend on the building classification, works, and use.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum share capital applies to a SARL. At least 20% of cash contributions must be paid on incorporation; the balance must be paid within five years of RCS registration. Cash contributions are deposited before filing with an eligible depositary, such as a bank, notary, or Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, and released upon proof of registration. Contributions in kind are generally fully contributed at formation; an auditor for contributions may be required, subject to statutory exemptions.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
A SARL has no board of directors. The following initial decisions should be adopted by the founding shareholder(s) and recorded in the incorporation file: “The shareholder(s) resolve to incorporate [Company Name] as a société à responsabilité limitée under the attached articles of association; approve the subscription and allocation of the shares set out therein and acknowledge payment of the required portion of cash contributions; adopt [address] as the registered office; and appoint [name, address] as gérant, who accepts the appointment and is authorized to complete the registration formalities, open corporate accounts, and take actions necessary to commence business. The company shall assume, after registration, the commitments entered into on its behalf in accordance with applicable law.”
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial licensing board or manufacturing authorization is ordinarily required for wholesale trading and vessel shipment of raw cane sugar. For a facility potentially subject to classified-installation controls, the competent authority is the Departmental Prefect, with applications handled by the relevant regional environmental inspectorate (DREAL, or the competent regional authority).

Category: No general operating or manufacturing permit is required solely for wholesale sugar trading. A separate premises-specific declaration, registration, or authorization may apply if storage or other site activities fall within the French classified installations regime (Installations classées pour la protection de l’environnement, ICPE).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) — e-filing and VAT portal: impots.gouv.fr; business formalities single window (Guichet unique des formalités d'entreprises) operated by the Institut National de la Propriété Industrielle (INPI) at formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr; customs, EORI and export-licence administration: Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (DGDDI) at douane.gouv.fr; commercial register: Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce (RCS).

E-government filing portal

Non-resident shareholders establish the SARL through the electronic single window of the INPI. INSEE then issues the 9-digit SIREN, the 14-digit SIRET establishment number and the NAF/APE activity code. Following confirmation of the statutory registration, the DGFiP allocates the French intra-EU VAT identification number (format FR + two-digit control key + nine-digit SIREN) on the basis of the VAT registration application and the company's tax option; the VAT number must be stated on all invoices and on the EU recapitulative statement (ESA/DES). The company must simultaneously obtain an EORI number from the DGDDI for customs declarations and, if it trades within the EU, register for the Intrastat/DEB reporting framework. The Kbis extract issued by the RCS serves as the official proof of existence and of the legal representative's powers. Standard processing time is 2 to 6 weeks after the registration file is complete; the VAT number is usually delivered together with the income-tax filing reference and the professional e-procedures access (espace abonné).

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — a French SARL is subject by default to corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés, IS) at 25% for fiscal year 2025, with a reduced 15% rate on the first EUR 42,500 of taxable profit for an eligible SME (turnover up to EUR 10 million and at least 75% of capital held by individuals). France applies the territoriality principle of Article 209-I CGI: only profits arising from operations carried out in France are taxable, while profits of a foreign permanent establishment are generally exempt, which is relevant to an arbitrage trader with foreign vessels and lifting points. — Non-resident shareholders establish the SARL through the electronic single window of the INPI. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a French SARL is subject by default to corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés, IS) at 25% for fiscal year 2025, with a reduced 15% rate on the first EUR 42,500 of taxable profit for an eligible SME (turnover up to EUR 10 million and at least 75% of capital held by individuals). France applies the territoriality principle of Article 209-I CGI: only profits arising from operations carried out in France are taxable, while profits of a foreign permanent establishment are generally exempt, which is relevant to an arbitrage trader with foreign vessels and lifting points.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or comparable entity-classification election exists under French law. Available elections: (i) option for pass-through individual income tax (Article 239 bis AA CGI) for a closely-held SARL (natural persons holding at least 50% of the share capital, maximum 50 shareholders, corporate shareholders limited to 34% of capital), valid for 5 fiscal years — generally not recommended for export arbitrage due to the loss of the 15% reduced IS rate; (ii) election to apply the VAT franchise exemption (Article 293 B CGI) — available until the annual turnover threshold is exceeded but incompatible with export activity because it bars input-VAT recovery; (iii) option for the simplified (régime simplifié) or normal (régime réel normal) corporate tax filing regime; (iv) mandatory self-assessment of import VAT (reverse charge under Article 1695 CGI), in force since 1 January 2022 — eliminates the cash-flow burden on the import of raw cane sugar; (v) tax integration (intégration fiscale) under Articles 223 A et seq. CGI for a French parent holding at least 95% of its French subsidiaries — an optional group regime; (vi) hybrid mismatch protection: France transposed ATAD (Articles 209-IX, 212 bis and 212 quater CGI) — no election exists, but an entity may file protective documentation and, in a dual-inclusion income case, apply the minority-interest or trading-limitation rule of Article 212 bis-III CGI.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 91,900 (2025) annual turnover for supplies of goods, and EUR 37,500 for services (franchise en base de TVA under Article 293 B CGI); reduced thresholds: EUR 101,000 and EUR 41,000. Mandatory registration regardless of turnover applies to exporters of goods, intra-EU traders, and any company that must deduct input VAT; a physical-vessel sugar wholesale trader is therefore obliged to register for VAT from the first shipment and cannot rely on the franchise.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Articles 262-I-1° and 262 ter-I CGI, supplies of goods dispatched or transported outside the EU (physical export) and intra-EU dispatches to a VAT-registered customer are exempt with the right to deduct input tax (effective 0%). Conditions for the SARL: (i) the sugar must be delivered to a purchaser established outside France or the EU; (ii) the goods must physically leave EU customs territory within 90 days of dispatch and the exporter must hold proof of export within the deadlines set by BOI-TVA-EXPORT-20-10-20; (iii) export invoices carry the Incoterms and vessel details; and (iv) for sugar imported into France for re-export, the import must be declared under a suspensive customs procedure (customs warehousing, transit) or the mandatory import-VAT reverse charge under Article 1695 CGI must be self-assessed to neutralise the cash flow. Offshore chain transactions where the raw sugar never enters French customs territory are outside the scope of French VAT (Article 258-2 CGI). For EU refiners as customers, the intra-EU zero-rating requires the customer's valid VAT ID on the invoice, the goods dispatched to another EU state, and the EU recapitulative statement (ESA/DES) to be filed. Where a tariff-rate quota or preferential origin is claimed for raw cane sugar, the commodity must comply with the relevant EU import-licence or TRQ allocation certificate and the qualitative polarization specification of the Common Market Organisation (Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013).

  • Customs export declaration (Déclaration d'Exportation, DAE/DEX) accepted and discharged by the DGDDI in the DELTA-X / Export single window, with clean exit status and date of release
  • Bill of Lading (connaissement maritime) — original or recognised electronic (e-B/L) evidencing EU port of loading, non-EU port of discharge, vessel name, consignee and freight terms
  • Commercial invoice and packing list stating the buyer, Incoterm, quantity, polarization and quality of the VHP raw cane sugar
  • Export contract and purchase order (or the Revolving LC / Swift Wire instruction) evidencing the foreign counterparty and the terms of sale
  • Inward remittance or settlement evidence — SWIFT MT103, conformity certificate or LC utilisation statement showing receipt of the foreign-currency proceeds
  • Sugar-specific trade documentation where relevant: EU import/export licence, TRQ allocation certificate, non-preferential or preferential certificate of origin and the refining-end-use declaration for raw cane sugar

France mandates structured e-invoicing (facturation électronique) for all domestic B2B supplies from 1 September 2026 (all undertakings must be able to receive e-invoices; large and mid-size enterprises must issue them) and from 1 September 2027 for every enterprise, including SMEs. Invoices must be transmitted via a certified Dematerialisation Platform Partner (PDP) or the government's Portail Public de Facturation (PPF), using a structured format compliant with EN 16931 (Factur-X / UBL). Cross-border transaction and payment data (e-reporting) is filed electronically with the DGFiP through the PPF. France does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping system (unlike Italy or Latin America); compliance is ensured by periodic CA3 VAT returns and DES/ESA recapitulative statements filed via EDI or impots.gouv.fr. The PPF API specifications (PPF-API v1.0) apply to platform-to-platform transmissions.

The DGFiP may within the standard three-year assessment period (Articles L. 169 and L. 176 of the Livre des Procédures Fiscales — LPF, extended to 6 years for non-filed declarations and to 10 years where the export activity is carried on abroad or through an offshore vehicle) re-examine the export file and, if the documentary bundle is incomplete, falsified or untimely, reverse the 0% rate. Consequences: (i) reassessment to the standard 20% VAT, plus monthly late interest of 0.2% (Article 1727 CGI); (ii) penalties of 40% for bad faith (mauvaise foi) or 80% for fraudulent manoeuvres (manœuvres frauduleuses) under Article 1729 CGI; (iii) application of the abuse-of-law procedure (Articles L. 64 and L. 64 A LPF) for artificial schemes, adding an 80% penalty and a fine of EUR 10,000 to EUR 100,000; (iv) criminal prosecution under Articles 1741-1742 CGI for fraudulent VAT evasion, punishable by five years' imprisonment and a cumulative fine of EUR 500,000 (aggravated to seven years and EUR 2 million in cases of organised fraud); (v) customs sanctions under the Code des Douanes for materially inaccurate export declarations — confiscation of goods, a fine of up to twice the value of the goods, and withdrawal of the exporter's customs authorisation; (vi) for sugar, the loss of the licence security or guarantee and exclusion from future TRQ allocations. Company directors and de facto managers may be held jointly liable for the tax debt (Article L. 267 LPF) in the event of serious or repeated mismanagement.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Euro is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts in France. No exchange-control authorization is required for trade settlement, profit repatriation, dividend distribution, or loan servicing, pursuant to Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and the OECD Code of Liberalisation of Capital Movements. Residual constraints are exclusively declarative: (i) EU cash-transport declarations above EUR 10,000 under Regulation (EU) 2018/1672, (ii) Banque de France balance-of-payments cotation reporting by the paying bank, (iii) EU/OFAC sanctions screening, and (iv) disclosure of foreign-located accounts on French Form 3916. A French SARL may remit dividends to non-resident shareholders without prior approval, subject to the domestic 25% withholding tax which may be reduced or eliminated under tax treaties and the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. France has abolished any mandatory surrender of export proceeds; the SARL may retain 100% of receipts in EUR or in foreign currency within authorized comptes en devises, with no statutory conversion timeline, no minimum conversion ratio, and no authorized-dealer retention limit. The only binding obligations are statistical and fiscal: the paying bank must codify every export collection under the Banque de France balance-of-payments cotation catalogue using the merchandise-export classification, and the company must declare any fully foreign-located account on Form 3916 attached to its annual corporate tax return.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France) as the national monetary authority operating within the Eurosystem of the European Central Bank (ECB); the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury) retains sovereign competence for financial relations with foreign states under the Code monétaire et financier; the ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution) licenses and supervises credit institutions, payment institutions, and electronic-money institutions in France.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH for operational collections, FX conversion, and administrative disbursements; LOW for vessel-scale LC and trade-finance mandates. ACPR-licensed and PSD2-passported EMIs (Qonto, Shine, Wise Business, Ebury, Airwallex) permit fully remote multi-currency onboarding, SEPA Instant, sponsored SWIFT wires, and competitive USD/EUR conversion, but they cannot issue or confirm UCP600 documentary credits, lack direct SWIFT membership, commonly impose per-transfer ceilings incompatible with 12,000-15,000 mt sugar parcel settlements, and offer no commodity trade-finance intermediation. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity contributions and shareholder subordinated-loan injections may enter France by SEPA transfer for EUR or by SWIFT MT103 for USD and third currencies without prior authorization. The receiving bank applies the Banque de France cotation classification: equity subscription is coded as a direct-investment operation, intercompany loans under the financial-credit class, and commercial prepayments against sugar cargo under merchandise-trade codes. Field 70 of the MT103 must carry the SARL's SIREN/SIRET, the contract or LC reference, and the UBO's RBE identifier. Any cash component equal to or exceeding EUR 10,000 triggers the mandatory declaration duty under Article L561-15 of the Code monétaire et financier. Formation capital must be held in a blocked account opened in the SARL's name, with the attestation de dépôt des fonds issued by the depositary institution before RCS registration.Primary rails: (i) irrevocable revolving documentary letters of credit governed by UCP600, issued by the French Tier-1 bank in favor of the origin seller for each 12,000-15,000 mt VHP raw-cane-sugar parcel, typically 90-180 days usance; (ii) SWIFT MT103 wires for premium, demurrage, and open-account margin balances, annotated with the merchandise-import purpose text and contract references; (iii) standby letters of credit under ISP98 for performance and price-adjustment undertakings; and (iv) cash-against-documents settlement through the load-port agent for spot purchases. The SARL may additionally access forfaiting of the LC-accepted USD bills of exchange to release liquidity without disrupting the delivery schedule.EUR/USD outright FX forward (30-180 day tenors) settling the USD payable on each revolving-LC maturity, booked under ISDA/CSA documentation with the French Tier-1 bank and subject to EMIR margin rules.EUR/USD FX swap (spot-versus-forward, tom-next, or rolling forward) for intra-maturity liquidity mismatches between USD LC disbursements and EUR operating cash.EUR/USD OTC vanilla currency options (purchased puts on USD payables) with premium payment, classified as hedging-only under MiFID II professional-client eligibility.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in USD referencing BRL (Brazilian real), THB (Thai baht), or COP (Colombian peso) where origin-side cost exposure in less-convertible markets requires offshore hedging, with settlement in EUR.Cross-currency interest-rate swap (EUR/USD CCIRS) to refinance the LC usance and forfaiting facility into matched-currency funding for extended tenors.Price-plus-FX combined commodity swap on ICE Futures No. 11 world raw-sugar differentials with an embedded EUR/USD conversion clause, executed on a regulated EU trading venue or as an EMIR-cleared swap.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH for operational collections, FX conversion, and administrative disbursements; LOW for vessel-scale LC and trade-finance mandates. ACPR-licensed and PSD2-passported EMIs (Qonto, Shine, Wise Business, Ebury, Airwallex) permit fully remote multi-currency onboarding, SEPA Instant, sponsored SWIFT wires, and competitive USD/EUR conversion, but they cannot issue or confirm UCP600 documentary credits, lack direct SWIFT membership, commonly impose per-transfer ceilings incompatible with 12,000-15,000 mt sugar parcel settlements, and offer no commodity trade-finance intermediation. The compliant structure is a tandem model: EMI for sub-EUR 50,000 payment legs and receivables, with the French Tier-1 bank hosting the escrow account, revolving LC, USD clearing account, and FX hedging line.
