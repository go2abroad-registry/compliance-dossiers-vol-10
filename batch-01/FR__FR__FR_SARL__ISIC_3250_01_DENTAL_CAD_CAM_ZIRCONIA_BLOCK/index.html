Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for SARL; the articles of association freely determine capital. Paid-up capital must be fully subscribed and, if contributed in cash, deposited with a French bank or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations pending registration, then released upon receipt of the K-bis extract.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance (Commission de sécurité) is required for the building occupancy category ('ERP' if any public access; 'IGH' if tall building); otherwise, a fire safety attestation from the local fire brigade (Service Départemental d'Incendie et de Secours, SDIS) for industrial establishments must be obtained prior to commissioning. Structural load inspections are carried out by a qualified bureau de contrôle (e.g., SOCOTEC, Bureau Veritas) to confirm floor loads for heavy milling machines. Occupational health clearance documents include the Company's risk assessment (Document Unique d'Évaluation des Risques - DUER), the workplace medical inspection validated by the occupational physician (médecin du travail), and, if employees are exposed to ceramic dust, an atmospheric monitoring protocol meeting the workplace limit values (VLEP, e.g., 8-hr TWA for silica respirable fractions). The civil defense clearance is evidenced by the SDIS visit report and the certificate of structural compliance issued by the building controller after installation of equipment.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for SARL; the articles of association freely determine capital. Paid-up capital must be fully subscribed and, if contributed in cash, deposited with a French bank or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations pending registration, then released upon receipt of the K-bis extract.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP); official portal: impots.gouv.fr; one-stop business registration: INPI Guichet Unique; customs export administration: Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (DGDDI).

E-government filing portal

For a French SARL: (1) file incorporation through the INPI Guichet Unique; (2) DGFiP allocates the SIREN/SIRET and the French VAT intracommunity number (numéro de TVA intracommunautaire) directly after registration; (3) open a professional tax e-filing account on impots.gouv.fr and submit the initial corporate income tax and VAT declarations. For a non-established foreign supplier without a French branch, apply to the DGFiP Service des Impôts des Entreprises Étrangères using the official VAT identification application, providing foreign tax residence proof, constitutional documents, and evidence of French taxable activity; non-EU suppliers normally must appoint a French tax representative authorized by DGFiP.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For a French SARL: (1) file incorporation through the INPI Guichet Unique; (2) DGFiP allocates the SIREN/SIRET and the French VAT intracommunity number (numéro de TVA intracommunautaire) directly after registration; (3) open a professional tax e-filing account on impots.gouv.fr and submit the initial corporate income tax and VAT declarations. For a non-established foreign supplier without a French branch, apply to the DGFiP Service des Impôts des Entreprises Étrangères using the official VAT identification application, providing foreign tax residence proof, constitutional documents, and evidence of French taxable activity; non-EU suppliers normally must appoint a French tax representative authorized by DGFiP.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

France does not operate a check-the-box regime. A French SARL is default taxed as a resident company at entity level (impôt sur les sociétés) on worldwide profits. A narrow election exists under CGI article 239 bis AA permitting certain qualifying SARLs to be taxed like partnerships for a limited period, without creating full transparency for foreign tax purposes. ATAD hybrid mismatch rules are transposed in CGI articles 212 bis and 212 ter and disallow deduction / non-inclusion outcomes from hybrid instruments, branches, or disregarded entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Non-resident or non-established foreign suppliers: no turnover threshold; any French taxable supply triggers VAT registration unless the reverse-charge mechanism applies. Domestic small-business franchise thresholds: EUR 85,000 for sales of goods and EUR 37,500 for services, with EUR 93,500 and EUR 41,250 tolerance ceilings respectively. Zero-rated export turnover does not count toward these thresholds, and the operating French SARL should normally register to recover input VAT on production costs and claim export VAT refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A French SARL may apply the export zero rate under CGI article 262-I-1° and EU VAT article 146 to dental zirconia blocks physically dispatched or transported to a destination outside the EU. The goods must actually leave the EU customs territory and be supported by compliant export evidence retained by the supplier. The zero rate applies to the sale of goods, whether transport is arranged by the French seller, the foreign buyer, or a third party, provided the export is completed within the statutory period and is evidenced by the export customs declarations. Services directly connected to exported goods or performed for foreign recipients may also fall outside French VAT scope under B2B place-of-supply rules in CGI articles 259, 259 A, and 259 C.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration en Douane / DAU or ENS) electronically lodged and released through DGDDI
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, CMR consignment note, or multimodal transport document evidencing movement to a non-EU destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment/proceeds statement evidencing receipt and repatriation of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and description of the dental ceramic goods with HS/CN classification
  • Proof of physical exit from the EU (customs exit verification, GAC/ICS export tracking, vessel/aircraft/crossing manifests)

France has introduced mandatory structural e-invoicing and e-reporting. B2B e-invoicing is phased from 1 September 2026 for large and medium enterprises and from 1 September 2027 for small and micro enterprises. Invoices must be exchanged through a DGFiP-registered Dematerialisation Platform (PDP) or Partner Platform (PPF) using Factur-X, UBL, or CII formats; B2G invoices are transmitted through Chorus Pro. Invoice data, payment statuses, and cross-border B2C transaction data must be reported to the DGFiP central tax hub in near real time through platform APIs. There is no per-invoice fiscal stamping in the Italian model, but the absence of compliant transmission or platform acceptance blocks the recipient's VAT deduction.

The export zero-rating remains valid only if the SARL retains a complete, chronological export dossier and reconciles it with VAT returns and customs manifests. The DGFiP may verify export records and retrospectively claw back VAT recovered or deemed exempt if the evidence is missing, internally inconsistent, or fails to prove exit from the EU within the required timeline. Reassessment carries default interest at 0.20% per month, with penalties of 40% for deliberate underdeclaration and 80% for abusive manoeuvres or fraudulent intent. Fictitious exports, forged documents, or fraudulent export VAT credit laundering can trigger criminal sanctions, seizure of goods and bank accounts, substantial fines, and disqualification penalties. Each shipment must therefore be fully documented and export proceeds repatriated within the 180-day compliance window.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France applies full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions under EU free movement of capital rules. No general FX licence is required and profit remittance in the form of dividends is permitted without exchange control approval. Non-EU direct investment and capital movements are subject to balance-of-payments reporting to the Banque de France; dividend and trade payments are settled freely through resident banks. No material profit repatriation hurdle exists beyond standard corporate law distribution tests, anti-money laundering documentary diligence, and tax withholding declarations where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio in France. Exporters may retain proceeds in foreign currency accounts and are not required to repatriate or convert receipts into EUR. Balances must nevertheless be declared for balance-of-payments statistical purposes under Banque de France reporting instructions, and cross-border movements exceeding regulatory thresholds must be reported by the resident bank or declared by the exporter within the applicable deadline.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (BdF) and Direction Générale du Trésor, with banking supervision delegated to the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low rate of viability for offshore digital EMI providers. A non-EU/EEA electronic money institution may not operate in France without an ACPR authorisation or a valid passport from its EU home state. Use of unlicensed offshore EMIs to hold SARL funds or issue French IBANs creates regulatory, tax and payment-settlement risk. A licensed French or EEA EMI, such as a separately regulated fintech with a Banque de France clearing account, is more viable but remains subordinate to a full commercial bank for trade-finance and documentary credit services.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions must be initiated as a SWIFT MT103 (or equivalent SEPA wire for EUR) to the SARL's Euro-denominated account using the French IBAN. The payment message should carry the corporate legal name, likely-to-match account details, and a purpose code designating a share capital contribution or a shareholder loan, such as 'Capital contribution' or 'Financial investment' for the Banque de France balance-of-payments declaration. The remitting bank is required to include the underlying beneficiary shareholder's identity and the transaction rationale. For direct investments from outside the EU, the resident bank or the SARL must comply with Banque de France reporting thresholds and maintain supporting legal documentation including the convertible-bond or capital-increase instrument, funds-source proof, and tax declaration references.Settlement of imports of raw materials and export-related payments shall be made through the resident operation account using SWIFT MT103 messages, recommending EUR settlement via SEPA Credit Transfer where the counterpart carefully validates the French IBAN and BIC. For high-value or documentary transactions, a commercial letter of credit (L/C) issued subject to UCP600 from a French confirming bank is the preferred clearing rail, supported by bills of exchange, collection orders, and export invoices.
