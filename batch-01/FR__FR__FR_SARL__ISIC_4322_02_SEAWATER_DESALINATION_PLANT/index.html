Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

French SARL has no statutory minimum capital; the articles may fix a nominal capital of EUR 1 or higher, but all cash contributions must be fully subscribed and irrevocably deposited into a blocked escrow account held by a French bank, notary, or Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations before registration; funds may be released only upon issuance of the Kbis extract.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification issued by the departmental fire and rescue service (SDIS) based on emergency evacuation and fire-fighting systems; structural load inspection report validated by a licensed engineer; electrical installation conformity certificate (CONSUEL); occupational health and workplace safety clearance from DREETS/DIRECCTE; and, where applicable, construction site authorization from the municipal mayor.

Paid-in capital rule: French SARL has no statutory minimum capital; the articles may fix a nominal capital of EUR 1 or higher, but all cash contributions must be fully subscribed and irrevocably deposited into a blocked escrow account held by a French bank, notary, or Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations before registration; funds may be released only upon issuance of the Kbis extract.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) of the French Ministry of Economy, Finance and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty; e-portal: impots.gouv.fr (professional Espace Professionnel). The local SIE (Service des Impôts des Entreprises) handles VAT registration, filing, and refunds.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned SARL is created through the National Single Window (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) at the Greffe du Tribunal de commerce, obtaining an INSEE SIREN, SIRET establishment number and KBIS. The DGFiP then attributes the French intra-Community VAT number under the FR + key + SIREN formula. A non-resident company without a French entity upstream of a desalination EPC contract must apply to the competent SIE for an Article 289 A CGI VAT identification, submitting the ownership certificate, board/PoA, appointment of an accredited tax representative if no EU establishment, and evidence of any fixed establishment or local contracting activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned SARL is created through the National Single Window (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) at the Greffe du Tribunal de commerce, obtaining an INSEE SIREN, SIRET establishment number and KBIS. The DGFiP then attributes the French intra-Community VAT number under the FR + key + SIREN formula. A non-resident company without a French entity upstream of a desalination EPC contract must apply to the competent SIE for an Article 289 A CGI VAT identification, submitting the ownership certificate, board/PoA, appointment of an accredited tax representative if no EU establishment, and evidence of any fixed establishment or local contracting activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

France has no check-the-box regime. A single-member EURL may, if not exclusively carrying commercial activity with corporate shareholders, exercise an option under Article 239 bis AA CGI to be taxed as a partnership (pass-through) at the shareholder level. Multi-member SARL is compulsorily subject to French corporate income tax (IS). A group meeting the 95% shareholding and pre-agreement conditions may elect OT fiscal integration under Articles 223 A ff. CGI. Hybrid mismatch and entity misclassification adjustments are mandatory ATAD/CGI Articles 212 ter/quater rules, not optional elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For domestic established taxpayers, the franchise en base de TVA limits are EUR 91,000 annual turnover for goods (tolerance EUR 101,000) and EUR 36,600 for services (tolerance EUR 40,900); exceeding the tolerance triggers mandatory VAT registration and collection. A construction/EPC SARL carrying out seawater desalination works must register from the first euro when it invoices taxable recipients, because the place of supply of immovable-property works is France; the small-business threshold is therefore of limited application to this ISIC 4322 EPC scope.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported physically from France to a destination outside the European Union are exempt from VAT with a right to deduct input VAT under Article 262-I CGI; the exporter must prove the movement out of the EU by retained customs and transport evidence. Services connected with immovable property located outside France (e.g. desalination works performed on a plant site abroad) are outside the scope of French VAT under Article 259-A CGI, and services supplied B2B to a customer established in a third country are generally taxable in the customer's jurisdiction, allowing French input VAT recovery where the purchases are directly connected to the zero-rated or out-of-scope export output.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration en douane d'exportation - DAU/B65) with the export ledger acceptance stamp
  • Bill of Lading (or CMR/Air Waybill) evidencing physical departure from the EU customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing settlement of the export invoice in the exporter's French account

Mandatory B2B e-invoicing and e-reporting in France phases in under the Facturation électronique reform: all taxpayers must be able to receive structured e-invoices from 1 September 2026; large enterprises must issue them from 1 September 2026, and SMEs/VSEs from 1 September 2027. Invoices must be transmitted through an approved Plateforme de Dématérialisation Partenaire (PDP) or the Public Portal (PPF) in EN 16931-compliant formats (Factur-X, UBL, CII). No real-time digital stamp or DGFiP pre-clearance exists; however, transaction data and invoice metadata are transmitted via e-reporting to DGFiP, enabling pre-filled VAT returns and audit analytics. API interoperability and electronic-seal authentication mandates follow the PPF technical specification; non-compliant issuance is a tax-law violation and exposes the issuer to sanctions.

DGFiP will re-classify any unsupported or sham export invoice to a domestic taxable supply, subject to the statutory limitation period, with VAT reassessed at 20%, disgorgement of input VAT recovered, plus 0.20% per month statutory interest. Bad faith provides a 40% penalty increase, while organised tax fraud (manœuvres frauduleuses) triggers the 80% penalty and criminal prosecution under Article 1741 CGI, including personal imprisonment and fines for directors, together with the joint recovery of VAT and penalties from the corporate assets and any unjustified refunds.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France operates a liberalized capital account regime under EU law with full convertibility for the Euro. Non-resident capital movements are unrestricted; resident outflows require prior declaration to the Banque de France for transactions exceeding €100,000 thresholds. Profit remittance from French SARL subsidiaries to foreign parents is permitted without restriction provided transfer pricing compliance and beneficial ownership disclosure are satisfied.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds applies under French law. Foreign exchange receipts must be declared to the Banque de France within 30 days of receipt; conversion to Euro occurs at prevailing market rates unless contractually stipulated otherwise. Project-related export invoices require supporting documentation for tax and AML verification.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard corporate income tax rate (Article 219 CGI) for fiscal years opened from 2022; reduced 15% rate on first €42,000 of taxable profit for eligible small and medium enterprises.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

25% withholding tax on gross dividends paid to non-resident companies (Article 119-2 CGI), subject to reduction under tax treaties or Parent-Subsidiary Directive (0% for qualifying EU/EEA parent entities); a 3% additional distribution levy (Article 235 ter ZCA CGI) also applies unless treaty-exempt.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three months of the close of the fiscal year; for a fiscal year ending 31 December, the final due date is 30 April, extended to 15 May for electronic filing.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: €400 million group consolidated turnover (or €50 million standalone threshold for response obligations) requires preparing and filing the Master File/Local File and TP documentation with the annual return (Article L13AA LPF).

Economic substance scope: France applies the OECD arm's-length principle and an abuse-of-law doctrine (Article L64 LPF) requiring substance over form; a French entity claiming treaty or Directive benefits must maintain genuine economic substance (staff, premises, real decision-making); shell companies are denied benefits under BEPS Action 6 and the EU anti-tax-avoidance directives.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion (fraude fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 5 years and fine up to €500,000 (Article 1741 CGI); tax reassessment plus penalty of 40% for deliberate misconduct or 80% for organised fraud (Article 1729 CGI).

Director personal liability: Directors who ordinate the concealment may be jointly liable for the company's tax debt; a judicial management ban, asset seizure, travel ban, and imprisonment may be imposed (Article L653-3 Commercial Code).
Transfer pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: Re-assessment of taxable profits under Article 57 CGI; adjustment of interest deductibility under Article 212 CGI; additional tax of up to 80% in bad-faith cases and 0.20% monthly interest.

Director personal liability: SI will be recharacterised as hidden distributions; directors may incur joint liability if they authorised payments with knowledge of sham transactions; criminal exposure under Article 1741 if intended to evade IS.
Shell company / lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of French residency and treaty/Directive relief; application of the abuse-of-law procedure (Article L64 LPF) with penalty 80% of the tax avoided; reassessment of profits as French-source.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced for misuse of corporate assets (abus de biens sociaux) under Article L242-30, leading to personal liability for unpaid taxes and up to 5 years' imprisonment for directors.
