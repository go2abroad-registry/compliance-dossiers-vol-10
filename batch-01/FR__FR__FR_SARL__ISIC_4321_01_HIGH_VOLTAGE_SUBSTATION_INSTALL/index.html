Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

For the French SARL: (1) incorporate via the INPI single electronic window (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr), which simultaneously files with INSEE, DGFiP and the Commercial and Companies Register; (2) obtain the 9-digit SIREN and 14-digit SIRET from the INSEE SIRENE register; (3) DGFiP automatically attributes the French EU VAT number in the mandatory 13-character format FR + 2-digit checksum key + 9-digit SIREN; (4) verify the VAT ID on the VIES database. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France is a Eurozone member with fully convertible EUR for both current-account and capital-account transactions, grounded in Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. There is no exchange-control authorisation regime, no convertibility restriction, and no ceiling on inward or outward remittances. Capital-account operations (equity subscription, shareholder loans, dividend distribution) require no prior approval, subject to statistical reporting to Banque de France for individual transactions of EUR 12,500 or more, submitted by the bank or the entity on a monthly basis by the 15th business day of the following month, using the balance-of-payments nomenclature for direct investment. Profit remittance by the SARL is lawful once distributable reserves are certified in the annual accounts (Code de commerce Art. L232-11); the principal constraint is tax formalities, namely French withholding tax at 25% under CGI Art. 119-2 where applicable, mitigated by the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive (Directive (EU) 2011/96, as amended) or an applicable double-tax treaty. For an electricity-infrastructure contractor, the only substantive obstacle is the foreign-investment control framework (Décret n°2019-1590; CMF Art. L151-3 if the activity is deemed critical); a pure installation-services mandate without ownership or operation of the substation normally remains outside the regulated list, but a written Treasury position should be obtained before onboarding.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender, forced conversion ratio, or statutory conversion deadline applies to export proceeds under French or EU law. The SARL may freely retain receivables in the original invoice currency or convert them in the spot market at its own discretion, since France abolished exchange-surrender obligations in 1989-1990. The residual statutory burden is purely informational: every cross-border payment in excess of EUR 12,500 must be reported by the bank to Banque de France for balance-of-payments statistics; direct-investment flows and intercompany balances must also be included in the annual survey of foreign direct investment; and payments to sanctioned jurisdictions are blocked under EU restrictive measures irrespective of the surrender framework.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (the French Central Bank) in coordination with the European Central Bank within the Eurosystem; banking licensing and AML-CFT supervision is exercised by the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR); foreign-investment screening is administered by the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW for capital escrow, project-milestone cycling, and large supplier disbursements; MEDIUM for low-value operational expense accounts. Under the French Monetary and Financial Code (CMF Arts. L521-1 et seq.) and PSD2, only an electronic-money institution (EMI) licensed in France or validly passported from another EEA member state may lawfully hold funds and issue an IBAN for the SARL. An offshore or non-EEA EMI without a French or EU passport cannot provide payment services at all and creates both a regulatory and criminal-law irregularity. Even a fully authorised EEA EMI cannot substitute for a tier-1 credit institution because EMIs are prohibited from granting credit, providing negative-balance features, maintaining project escrow with unidentified third-party beneficial ownership, or supporting commercial documentary letters of credit. Any blueprint must therefore treat EMIs as ancillary channels for travel cards and small merchant payments, while all project capital and trade settlements run exclusively through regulated banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity or shareholder-loan funds should be wired exclusively to a dedicated EUR capital-account IBAN. EU-origin funds clear via SEPA Credit Transfer; non-EU funds use SWIFT MT103 with the beneficiary field reading 'FR_SARL, SIREN/RCS number, apport en numéraire / capital subscription' and the remittance information (SWIFT field 70) limited to 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' so that the bank can classify the flow as direct-investment equity under the Banque de France balance-of-payments nomenclature. For injections denominated in USD, CNY, GBP, or CHF, instruct the receiving bank in writing to execute an intraday or T+2 spot conversion into EUR on the capital account and, under no circumstances, to leave the funds in a suspense or convertible-only account without conversion. The bank is statutorily responsible for capturing any movement equal to or above EUR 12,500 for Banque de France reporting and will verify the RBE, screen against sanctions, and request supporting corporate documentation; foreign-investment clearance from the French Treasury is required only where the installation scope is assessed as critical under Decree No. 2019-1590. No prior central bank visa or exchange-control approval is otherwise needed.Outbound treasury rails are selected by payment type: (i) EU and Eurozone subcontractors receive milestone payments via SEPA Credit Transfer, or SEPA Instant for values under EUR 100,000, cleared through TARGET2 with immediate finality; (ii) non-EU subcontractors are paid by SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers, with the beneficiary bank instructed through the correspondent chain and the remittance fields quoting the invoice number, milestone certificate, and underlying construction contract; (iii) major plant purchases, particularly 500kV gas-insulated switchgear from Asian manufacturers, are settled exclusively by an irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued by the tier-1 bank via SWIFT MT700, available at sight against presentation of a full set of clean-on-board bills of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, factory acceptance test certificate, and insurance documents; a confirmed or standby LC via MT760 may be added when the supplier's credit standing is marginal; (iv) contract advances are released against advance-payment bonds or demand guarantees. No French restriction limits settlement currency, but the blueprint standardises EUR billing for intra-EU and USD billing for non-EU equipment contracts, with CNY settlement permitted through the LC corridor in Hong Kong or mainland China where physically available. Under the French Monetary and Financial Code (CMF Arts. L521-1 et seq.) and PSD2, only an electronic-money institution (EMI) licensed in France or validly passported from another EEA member state may lawfully hold funds and issue an IBAN for the SARL. An offshore or non-EEA EMI without a French or EU passport cannot provide payment services at all and creates both a regulatory and criminal-law irregularity. Even a fully authorised EEA EMI cannot substitute for a tier-1 credit institution because EMIs are prohibited from granting credit, providing negative-balance features, maintaining project escrow with unidentified third-party beneficial ownership, or supporting commercial documentary letters of credit. Any blueprint must therefore treat EMIs as ancillary channels for travel cards and small merchant payments, while all project capital and trade settlements run exclusively through regulated banks.
