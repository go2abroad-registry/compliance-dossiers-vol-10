Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Euro is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. France applies EU capital movement liberalization; no prior exchange-control authorization is required for passive foreign investment or dividend remittance. However, a French SARL must observe EU and French anti-money laundering law, the 30% withholding tax on dividend distributions to non-resident individuals, and any applicable tax treaty reduction. Bilateral or cross-border payments above EUR 10,000 are captured by the French bank's balance-of-payments report to Banque de France; the company is not required to obtain central bank approval. In exceptional sectors, France's inward FDI screening regulation may impose prior authorization for foreign investment in a company manufacturing items critical for public health.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. France does not impose a forced sale or conversion timeline for export proceeds. Exporters may keep receivables in euro or foreign currency, can re-invoice intra-group, and have no mandatory surrender ratio. Export proceeds must be recorded in the accounting books in accordance with French GAAP, and the customs export declaration must match the revenue amount. For export settlement via a French bank, the bank provides the SARL with an SWIFT statement that serves as balance-of-payments and customs evidence. Foreign-currency receivables are subject to year-end revaluation for statutory financial statements and income tax purposes.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France; Direction Générale du Trésor (Treasury Directorate) as the French Foreign Exchange Administration

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

Operational feasibility: Medium; capital escrow feasibility: Low. Under PSD2, an electronic money institution (EMI) is a payment institution, not a full credit institution, and is generally not empowered to issue the statutory attestation de blocage required to register the SARL's share capital deposit. EMI accounts such as Qonto, Shine, or Revolut Business may be used for day-to-day TT wires, FX conversion, and SEPA transfers, but as secondary liquidity rails they lack the documentary credit, escrow certification, and métier of a full French bank. For capital injection and large cross-border MT103 flows involving medical-device exports, an EMI cannot replace a tier-1 commercial bank.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Multi-layered beneficial ownership or opaque trustee/foundation shareholders

Root cause: Multi-layered beneficial ownership or opaque trustee/foundation shareholders

Defense evidence: Schematic group ownership diagram identifying all ultimate individuals; audited consolidated financial statements; constitutional documents of each holding layer; certified public register excerpt or certificate of incorporation; legal counsel opinion confirming the corporate ownership chain and absence of bearer-share or quarantine elements.
Capital injection sourced from a FATF Grey List jurisdiction or a non-cooperative tax jurisdiction

Root cause: Capital injection sourced from a FATF Grey List jurisdiction or a non-cooperative tax jurisdiction

Defense evidence: Formal source-of-funds statement from the remitting bank; notarized share subscription agreement; transaction trail evidencing funds from the remitter's business revenues or existing bank balance; tax residence certificate of the investor; audited balance sheet and profit-and-loss figures supporting the underlying accumulation of wealth.
Trade-payment mismatches, including invoice amounts exceeding customs values or letter-of-credit discrepancies

Root cause: Trade-payment mismatches, including invoice amounts exceeding customs values or letter-of-credit discrepancies

Defense evidence: Certified commercial invoices and purchase orders; customs export/import declaration data (DEB/DES); Incoterms 2020 clause in the contract; transport documents (CMR, AWB, bill of lading) and insurance certificate; ISO 13485 certificate and EU CE marking of the medical device; internal payment-to-invoice reconciliation schedule.
PEP or politically exposed person presenting as manager or beneficial owner

Root cause: PEP or politically exposed person presenting as manager or beneficial owner

Defense evidence: Independent sanctions/PEP screening report; public professional profile and contact details; sworn declaration of net worth and funding origin; certified police or judicial clearance from the person's country of residence; compliance diligence memorandum prepared by the SARL's external legal counsel, concluding that the PEP is a disclosed and law-abiding actor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign shareholders initiate a SWIFT MT103 wire from their home-country bank to the SARL's dedicated capital-blocked account (Compte de Blocage) opened in France. The wire beneficiary must be exactly the SARL's name followed by the mandate reference, and the SWIFT narrative must state the purpose code for equity subscription, within the Banque de France balance-of-payments classification for share capital in a resident non-financial company. If the foreign shareholder is non-EU, the transaction is screened under EU and French inward FDI rules; if the dialysis manufacturing activity is considered critical health infrastructure, ministerial authorization may be required before subscription. The bank issues the attestation de blocage to the corporate registrar; upon issuance of the Kbis extract, the funds are released to the SARL's operating account. No separate foreign-exchange approval is required for euro-denominated injections.The SARL's principal outbound treasury requirement is import of polyethersulfone resin, hollow fiber membranes, potting adhesives, and sterilization services. These disbursements will be carried out through four rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 wire for open-account and pre-payment terms; (2) an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) issued by the SARL's French bank for high-value raw-material imports, with the supplier presenting transport, insurance, and quality certificates; (3) SEPA credit transfer for same-day euro payments within the EEA; and (4) documentary collection for medium-risk suppliers. The bank will require the commercial invoice, transport document, customs declaration, and underlying purchase order; under French customs law, the SARL must retain export/import records for at least ten years. Settlement is executed only after sanctions/AML adverse-media checks on both counterparties.Outright EUR/USD forward contract under the FBF master agreement for hedging trade receivables and raw-material payables.Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) for illiquid or restricted-convertibility emerging-market currencies only when an underlying physical exposure is proven to a French bank treasury desk.Cross-currency swap (EUR/USD or EUR/GBP) to hedge medium-term foreign-currency capital expenditure or intra-group loans.FX swap (spot versus forward combination) for temporary liquidity rebalancing on foreign-currency accounts.
