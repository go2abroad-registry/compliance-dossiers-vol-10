Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-up capital for a SARL; the partners freely fix the share capital in the Articles of Association. Cash contributions must be deposited and blocked with a bank, a notary, or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations before filing and cannot be withdrawn until the company is registered. In-kind contributions must be fully paid up at signature of the Articles; if the value exceeds EUR 30,000 or comprises real property, a commissaire aux apports report is mandatory unless an exemption under Article L.223-9 applies. For the proposed cryogenic pump manufacturing activity, the founders should demonstrate adequate equity capitalization commensurate with industrial operating risk.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification issued after inspection by the SDIS (Service Départemental d'Incendie et de Secours), covering cryogenic-specific hazards such as cold burns, oxygen-enriched atmospheres, and gas dispersion; structural load and blast-zone verification for storage vessels and pressure-transfer areas; emergency evacuation and internal operation plan verified; and an occupational workplace clearance by DREETS confirming safe working conditions, electrical conformity, and prevention of physical and chemical hazards.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP); taxpayer portal: impots.gouv.fr; business registration via the INPI guichet unique at formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr; direct VAT and corporate income tax (IS) compliance handled by the Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) of the registered office; customs and EORI procedures administered by the Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (DGDDI).

E-government filing portal

Within one month of incorporation, file the acte constitutif via the INPI guichet unique to obtain INSEE-assigned SIREN (9 digits) and SIRET (14 digits); the SARL's APE/NAF code is set to 28.13Z 'Fabrication d'autres pompes et compresseurs', corresponding to ISIC 2813. The DGFiP simultaneously issues the intra-community VAT number (numéro de TVA intracommunautaire) in the format FR + 2-digit control key + 9-digit SIREN. A non-resident manufacturer creating a French permanent establishment must file form 1447-C-E (initial registration) with the local SIE within 60 days of commencing business; amendments and cessation are filed on forms 1447-SD and 1447-M respectively. The exporter must separately obtain an EORI number (FR + SIREN) from DGDDI before lodging export declarations.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation. The SARL is by default subject to Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) at 25%; a reduced 15% rate applies to the first €42,500 of taxable profit for qualifying small businesses (Art. 44 sexies-0 A CGI). France applies the territorialité principle: only profits derived from activities carried on in France are taxable (Art. 209 CGI); tax residence is determined by registered office or effective place of management in France. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The SARL is by default subject to Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) at 25%; a reduced 15% rate applies to the first €42,500 of taxable profit for qualifying small businesses (Art. 44 sexies-0 A CGI). France applies the territorialité principle: only profits derived from activities carried on in France are taxable (Art. 209 CGI); tax residence is determined by registered office or effective place of management in France. No general tax-exempt zone applies in Metropolitan France; EU outermost overseas regions are outside this engine's scope.

Available election forms

Pass-through election to Impôt sur le Revenu (IR) is available without prior consent to SARL de famille (Art. 239 bis AA CGI) and, by petition to the tax administration, to small SARLs meeting cumulative conditions (fewer than 50 employees, turnover ≤ €10 million, all or part of the partners being natural persons) under Art. 239 bis A CGI; the IR option runs for 5 years and is renewable. Conversely, a family SARL can elect IS. A French-resident parent holding ≥95% may include the SARL in the fiscal integration regime (Art. 223 A CGI). France transposes ATAD anti-hybrid rules (Art. 209 VII CGI); no elective hybrid-mismatch or US-style check-the-box exists, and any switch between IS and IR is treated as a deemed disposal of assets and is audit-sensitive.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold for an established manufacturer: registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply in France. The franchise en base de TVA (Art. 293 B CGI) applies only to micro-enterprises below €91,900 annual turnover for goods and is in any case unsuitable for a manufacturer claiming zero-rated exports, as it forfeits input VAT recovery. Intra-EU distance sales of goods to French consumers trigger the €10,000 single EU-wide OSS threshold; B2B supplies to French customers by a non-established supplier fall under the reverse charge and do not require French registration; import VAT is payable at customs clearance on imported components and finished goods.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of LNG cryogenic pumps, spare parts and accessories to a non-EU buyer qualifies for exemption with the right to deduct input VAT (effective 0% export rate under Art. 262-I-1° CGI), provided the physical export occurs within 90 days of the delivery date and the exporter proves, by documentary evidence, that the goods left French and EU customs territory under a single supply contract (BOI-TVA-CHAMP-30-20-20). Direct exports, triangular and chain exports, and exports on consignment to non-EU destinations are eligible. Services directly related to the exported goods (e.g., commissioning supervision) are zero-rated; standalone offshore engineering or consultancy services fall outside the scope of French VAT under Arts. 259 to 259 B CGI, subject to B2B place-of-supply rules.

  • DAU (Document Administratif Unique) — the electronic export declaration lodged through the DELTA-X customs system, showing the exporter's EORI number, the goods nomenclature code (NC 8413.70 for pumps) and the definitive export code in Box 37
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR (official transport document evidencing departure from EU customs territory to the non-EU destination)
  • Commercial invoice (facture d'exportation) stating Incoterms, marks and numbers, HS code and the 0% VAT justification
  • Packing list / weight and volume note for the consignment
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of the foreign-currency proceeds; for letter-of-credit transactions, each drawing under the LC must be traceable
  • Export contract / irrevocable purchase order from the non-EU importer (amount, currency, Incoterms, payment terms)
  • Transport insurance certificate (optional but strongly recommended for audit substantiation)

France is implementing a compulsory dematerialized e-invoicing and e-reporting reform coordinated by DGFiP. VAT-taxable persons established in France must receive e-invoices in a structured format and transmit transaction and payment data (e-reporting) in (near) real time through an accredited Plateforme de Dématérialisation Partenaire (PDP) interconnected to the Portail Public de Facturation (PPF). Following the 2025 simplification of the original schedule: e-invoice issuance becomes mandatory for large enterprises and mid-caps on 1 September 2026 and for SMEs/VSEs on 1 September 2027; e-reporting of B2B and B2C transaction data applies from the same dates. Accepted data formats are UBL, UN/CEFACT Cross-Industry Invoice and Factur-X (EN 16931 compliance). No fiscal digital stamping or physical invoice seal is used as in MENA jurisdictions; compliance is enforced through validated PDP cycles, refusal and acceptance flows, and transmission receipts routed via the PPF API gateway (no human-readable hard-copy mandate).

Export zero-rating is subject to strict documentary audit: if the physical export is not completed within 90 days of delivery or the evidentiary bundle is incomplete, the sale is reclassified as a taxable domestic supply and the SARL is reassessed on unreported French VAT (retrospective clawback), together with denial of input VAT recovery on related purchases. Penalties escalate under Art. 1729 CGI: 40% for intentional misdeclaration (mauvaise foi), 80% for fraudulent maneuvers (manœuvres frauduleuses) and for abuse of law, plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.2% (Art. 1727 CGI). A fraudulent zero-rating claim extends the standard assessment limitation period from 3 to 10 years (Art. L. 169 LPF) and can trigger personal liability of the SARL's directors under Art. L. 267 LPF where they knowingly organized the company's insolvency or fraudulent non-collection of VAT.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible for ordinary current-account and capital-account transactions. France generally imposes no exchange-control approval for capital contributions, shareholder loans, dividends, royalties, interest, trade payments or liquidation proceeds. Transfers remain subject to EU and French sanctions, anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing, tax, customs, beneficial-ownership and financial-reporting rules. A French SARL may receive and remit foreign currency through regulated payment institutions or credit institutions, subject to bank due diligence and transaction-purpose verification. Dividend and interest remittances may trigger French withholding tax, subject to domestic exemptions, applicable tax treaties, the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, interest and royalty rules, beneficial-ownership requirements and anti-abuse provisions. Foreign direct investment screening may apply to acquisitions or investments involving activities affecting public order, public security or national defence; ordinary cryogenic-pump manufacturing requires case-specific screening rather than automatic approval. Certain LNG, cryogenic, industrial or dual-use products may also require export-control classification and authorization before shipment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France has no general statutory requirement for exporters to surrender export proceeds to a French bank, no general mandatory conversion ratio and no universal statutory deadline for converting foreign-currency export receipts into euros. Export receipts may generally be retained in foreign-currency accounts or converted at the company's discretion, subject to contractual bank terms, accounting in euros, tax and customs records, sanctions controls, anti-money-laundering monitoring and any applicable balance-of-payments or statistical reporting. Export documentation, commercial invoices, transport evidence, customs declarations, purchase orders and proof of receipt should be retained under French accounting and tax record-retention rules. Cash movements of EUR 10,000 or more across the EU external border are subject to customs declaration requirements, and banks may impose internal documentation thresholds or reporting requirements.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France, operating within the Eurosystem framework of the European Central Bank; foreign-exchange, payment-services, sanctions and capital-movement compliance also involves the French Treasury, the Direction Générale du Trésor, the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution, the Autorité des Marchés Financiers and, for customs matters, the Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for operational payment accounts and treasury sub-accounts; Low as a substitute for a French bank capital-deposit account, regulated trade-finance provider or complex industrial treasury bank. An offshore digital EMI cannot lawfully provide French banking services unless it is authorized under the applicable EU passporting framework or otherwise licensed in France. EMI safeguarding accounts are not bank deposit accounts and generally do not provide lending, bank guarantees, documentary letters of credit or statutory capital-certification functions. Use of an EMI should be limited to permitted payment services after verification of its EEA authorization, passporting status, safeguarding model, IBAN jurisdiction, sanctions coverage, transaction limits, account-closure policy and ability to support corporate beneficial-owner and industrial trade documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route euro funding through SEPA Credit Transfer where the sending and receiving institutions participate; use SWIFT customer credit transfer for non-SEPA currencies or non-EEA banks. Name the French SARL as beneficiary, use its exact registered name and IBAN, and identify the transfer as paid-in capital, capital contribution, share-premium contribution, shareholder loan or intercompany funding. France does not impose a general central-bank pre-approval requirement for ordinary inbound capital. The receiving bank should receive the subscription agreement, shareholder or board resolution, source-of-funds and source-of-wealth evidence, funding-entity financial statements, ownership chart, loan agreement if applicable, tax-residence information and the expected funds-flow schedule before settlement. French banks may request transaction-purpose information and may apply their own payment-purpose coding; France does not have a single universal statutory purpose-code system equivalent to regimes that require a central-bank code for every corporate wire. Funds intended for statutory share capital should be sent to a capital-deposit account and should not be treated as operational cash until the bank issues the certificate of deposit and the incorporation or capital-increase formalities are completed. Maintain a payment audit trail, reconcile the bank statement to the general ledger and document any conversion from foreign currency into euros under the bank's execution rate.Use SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer for eligible euro suppliers; use SWIFT customer credit transfer, commonly represented by an MT103 or its ISO 20022 successor, for non-SEPA payments. For documentary trade, use an irrevocable commercial letter of credit subject to UCP 600, preferably confirmed where country or bank risk warrants confirmation. Documentary collections may be structured under URC 522. Confirm the beneficiary, bank coordinates, sanctions status, end user, product classification, Incoterms, shipping documents, customs evidence, insurance and invoice before release. For imports, align payment release with the purchase order, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, certificate of origin, inspection evidence and customs declaration. For exports, retain proof of shipment, export declaration, end-user statement and payment reconciliation. Specify OUR, SHA or BEN charge allocation, establish dual approval for high-value payments and prohibit third-party settlement unless documented and pre-approved. Banks may reject or hold payments involving sanctioned countries, controlled goods, unclear end users, unusual routing or incomplete trade documentation.Deliverable EUR/foreign-currency forwards with an EU-authorized bank or investment firm, documented under an ISDA or equivalent master agreement and supported by a demonstrable underlying commercial exposure.Non-deliverable forwards for currencies that are not freely deliverable or where local-market restrictions make physical settlement impractical; NDFs are generally settled by net cash difference and remain subject to counterparty eligibility, EMIR, MiFID II, sanctions and bank suitability requirements.Currency swaps and cross-currency swaps for recurring funding, intercompany loans or balance-sheet currency mismatches, subject to ISDA documentation, collateral terms, EMIR reporting and appropriate accounting treatment.FX options, collars and option-based hedges where the bank has completed appropriateness and documentation requirements; avoid speculative or leveraged structures unrelated to identifiable commercial exposures.Hedges should be approved under a board-level treasury policy specifying permitted currencies, counterparties, maturities, hedge ratios, collateral thresholds, valuation, accounting treatment, confirmation, independent reconciliation and termination controls.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium for operational payment accounts and treasury sub-accounts; Low as a substitute for a French bank capital-deposit account, regulated trade-finance provider or complex industrial treasury bank. An offshore digital EMI cannot lawfully provide French banking services unless it is authorized under the applicable EU passporting framework or otherwise licensed in France. EMI safeguarding accounts are not bank deposit accounts and generally do not provide lending, bank guarantees, documentary letters of credit or statutory capital-certification functions. Use of an EMI should be limited to permitted payment services after verification of its EEA authorization, passporting status, safeguarding model, IBAN jurisdiction, sanctions coverage, transaction limits, account-closure policy and ability to support corporate beneficial-owner and industrial trade documentation.
