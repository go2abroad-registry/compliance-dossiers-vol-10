Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Conditionally permitted. A SARL may register its siège social with a licensed domiciliation company (société de domiciliation agréée) under Articles L.123-11-2 to L.123-11-9 of the Code de commerce, or at the gérant's principal residence for non-industrial activity. However, an industrial manufacturer under ISIC 2640 (ANC headphones and smart speakers) must maintain a genuine operational establishment in France — production, logistics, quality-testing or R&D facilities — as INPI, DREETS and the tax administration apply substance-over-form scrutiny. A purely virtual office without any physical operational base creates high risk of refusal of VAT registration, denial of CFE business-tax treatment, and rejection of the Crédit d'Impôt Recherche.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Articles L.223-1 and L.223-7: no statutory minimum capital for a SARL; the share capital is fixed freely in the statutes and must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions must be paid up to at least one-fifth (20%) upon incorporation, with the balance within five years; in-kind contributions are fully released at subscription. Paid-in cash must be placed in a blocked escrow account with a notary, a French credit institution, or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations until the K-Bis is obtained.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: conformity inspection by the Commission Consultative Départementale de la Sécurité et de l'Accessibilité (CCDSA) and favorable opinion for workplaces open to more than 50 staff; structural load clearance by a certified technical controller before occupation; workplace clearance by the DREETS labor inspector covering electrical installations, machine guarding, emergency exits and evacuation signaling, recorded in the registre de sécurité du travail.

Paid-in capital rule: Articles L.223-1 and L.223-7: no statutory minimum capital for a SARL; the share capital is fixed freely in the statutes and must be fully subscribed. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains full capital account convertibility under EU law. Current account transactions are unrestricted. Profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are permitted following tax filing and beneficial ownership verification; no capital controls apply to legitimate corporate distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender required for Euro-denominated exports. For non-Euro currency export receipts exceeding EUR 50,000 equivalent, declaration to Banque de France is mandatory within 10 working days with conversion at official market rates unless prior approval obtained.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France
