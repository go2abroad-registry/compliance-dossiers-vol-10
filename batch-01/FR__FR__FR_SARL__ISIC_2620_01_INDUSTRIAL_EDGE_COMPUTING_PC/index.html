Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) — portal: impots.gouv.fr; customs and export controls: Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (DGDDI) — douane.gouv.fr; business registry filing via Guichet Unique — formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr

E-government filing portal

For a French SARL: (1) file incorporation with the Guichet Unique online portal; (2) obtain SIREN (9-digit) and SIRET numbers, automatically assigned by INSEE upon registration; (3) obtain the French intra-Community VAT number ('FR' + 2-digit key + SIREN) from the competent Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) — mandatory before the first taxable transaction; for non-established foreign entities, registration is made with the DGFiP non-resident unit with Form 14743-SD and, if non-EU, appointment of a tax representative approved by the DGFiP tax representative service (Service de l'administration fiscale — état des représentants fiscaux); (4) open a French or EU bank account and publish the Kbis extract from the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce; all filings use electronic signatures and certified accountants are mandatory for financial statements.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — SARL defaults to Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) at 25% standard rate on domestic and foreign-source profit subject to territoriality (Art. 209 CGI); foreign PE profits can be exempt if the company provides proof of foreign tax paid and the PE is not in a tax haven; scope of industrial manufacturing is fully taxable under IS with full input VAT deduction. — For a French SARL: (1) file incorporation with the Guichet Unique online portal; (2) obtain SIREN (9-digit) and SIRET numbers, automatically assigned by INSEE upon registration; (3) obtain the French intra-Community VAT number ('FR' + 2-digit key + SIREN) from the competent Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) — mandatory before the first taxable transaction; for non-established foreign entities, registration is made with the DGFiP non-resident unit with Form 14743-SD and, if non-EU, appointment of a tax representative approved by the DGFiP tax representative service (Service de l'administration fiscale — état des représentants fiscaux); (4) open a French or EU bank account and publish the Kbis extract from the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce; all filings use electronic signatures and certified accountants are mandatory for financial statements.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — SARL defaults to Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) at 25% standard rate on domestic and foreign-source profit subject to territoriality (Art. 209 CGI); foreign PE profits can be exempt if the company provides proof of foreign tax paid and the PE is not in a tax haven; scope of industrial manufacturing is fully taxable under IS with full input VAT deduction.

Available election forms

1) Pass-through election (option IR): SARLs meeting 2-of-3 size tests (≤50 employees, ≤EUR 10M assets, ≤EUR 10M turnover) and managed from France may elect income tax treatment under Art. 239 bis AA CGI for a maximum of five fiscal years; 2) VAT group election (assujetti unique) under Art. 256 C CGI for integrated entities; 3) consolidated corporate tax group (intégration fiscale) under Art. 223 A CGI if parent holds ≥95%; 4) no US-style check-the-box regime exists; hybrid mismatch rules under ATAD (Art. 212 bis CGI) and DAC6/MDR cross-border arrangement disclosure apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic franchise en base de TVA: EUR 85,000 main turnover threshold for sales of goods in France (2025; reduced ceiling EUR 93,500; EUR 37,500 threshold for services at reduced ceiling EUR 41,750); non-established foreign suppliers: zero threshold, registration from the first taxable transaction; intra-EU distance sales: EUR 10,000 EU-wide annual ceiling; B2B industrial export dispatches: compulsory registration from the first transaction.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to: (1) definitive exports of goods outside the EU under Art. 262-I-1° CGI, provided physical departure from EU territory is evidenced; (2) intra-EU dispatches to a buyer holding a valid intra-EC VAT number under Art. 262 ter-1° CGI, provided a VIES-validated VAT certificate and transport documentation to another member state are held; (3) auxiliary services (transport, handling, insurance) directly linked to the exported goods under Art. 262-I-2° CGI. The exempt character is maintained if all proof is in hand before the 5th day of the month following the dispatch; goods must physically leave the EU within 90 days of delivery. Input VAT on manufacturing costs remains fully deductible.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration d'Exportation — submitted via DELTA-X electronic customs system, attested by customs clearance number)
  • Transport Evidence: Bill of Lading (B/L for ocean freight), CMR consignment note (road), or Air Waybill (AWB for air freight) evidencing exit from EU territory
  • Commercial Invoice (facture commerciale) stating Incoterms, export price, destinations, and harmonized HS codes (ISIC 2620 / HTS 8471 / HS 8471.50)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate — bank transfer proof (SWIFT MT103) or receiving bank certificate confirming final settlement of export proceeds
  • Intra-EU dispatch proof: buyer's VIES certificate (extract from the European VAT database) and, for industrial computers exceeding civilian thresholds, dual-use export license issued by DGDDI/SBD when applicable

Mandatory e-invoicing (facturation électronique) through DGFiP-certified Partner Dematerialization Platforms (PDPs) or the Public Billing Portal (PPF). Receiving obligation for all VAT-registered entities from 1 September 2026; issuance obligation from 1 September 2026 for large enterprises and from 1 September 2027 for mid-caps and SMEs. Mandatory structured formats: Factur-X (hybrid), UBL 2.1, or UN/CEFACT CII — transmitted via the four-corner PEPPOL-style network; e-reporting data (supply chain and platform transaction records) must be transmitted before the 10th day of the month following the transaction; no real-time fiscal digital stamping is applied (unlike Italian SDI clearance); non-compliant invoices are null for input VAT recovery and subject to EUR 15 per invoice penalty (capped at EUR 15,000).

The DGFiP maintains dedicated export verification units performing documentary coincidence audits (invoice vs. customs declaration vs. transport document vs. bank remittance). Failure to substantiate export within the prescribed deadlines triggers re-invoicing of the 20% VAT and late-payment interest at 0.2% per month (Art. 1727 CGI). Penalty escalation: 40% for deliberate misstatement (manquement délibéré, Art. 1729 CGI); 80% for wilful fraud, abuse of law, or fictitious export operations (Art. 1729-b CGI); criminal sanctions under Art. 1741 CGI and Art. 414-2 Code des douanes reach up to EUR 750,000 fine and 7 years imprisonment; customs declaration falsification triggers penalties of up to three times the evaded duties plus confiscation of transported goods; retrospective clawback covers the entire VAT period, interest, and penalties without statute-of-limitation relief for organized fraud (extended to 10 years for hidden activities).

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains a freely convertible Euro currency regime within the EU single market. Capital account transactions are liberalized with prior notification thresholds for amounts exceeding EUR 100,000; profit remittances to shareholders are permitted post-tax settlement and beneficial ownership verification, subject to French tax residency rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

French exporters must declare export proceeds to customs authorities within 15 days of shipment via the Douane system. No mandatory surrender or central bank conversion is required; funds may be retained in foreign currency accounts. Full reporting to tax authorities is mandatory for VAT and corporate income tax compliance.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France
