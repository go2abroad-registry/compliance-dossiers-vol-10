Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No mandatory minimum share capital is imposed by Code de commerce art. L.223-2 for a standard SARL; founders determine the capital in the Statuts. The declared capital must be fully subscribed and, as to monetary contributions, fully paid and placed on a blocking account (attestation de dépôt des fonds) until RCS registration; contributions in kind require a commissaire aux apports report (L.223-9). For ISO 13485 medical-device OEM manufacturing, ANSM will additionally require evidence of adequate financial capacity, quality management system, and product-liability insurance (responsabilité civile professionnelle).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility under EU free-movement-of-capital provisions. No FX surrender requirements or prior licensing for trade settlements, profit repatriation, or dividend upstreaming. Euro is the functional currency; foreign-currency accounts are freely permitted. Compliance with French balance-of-payments reporting thresholds and anti-money laundering (AML) verification requirements applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. No statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained in any currency or EUR. Statistical declaration to Banque de France is required for cross-border flows exceeding EUR 12,500 per transaction or monthly aggregate, with direct investment flows subject to ex-post reporting.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Central Bank) in conjunction with the Prudential Supervision and Resolution Authority (ACPR) for banking conduct, and the French Treasury (Direction Générale du Trésor) for capital transaction monitoring and foreign investment review.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for offshore (non-EU) EMI riverboard solutions. Legal constraints under PSD2 require ACPR licensing or EU passporting; services from non-EEA EMIs are not compliant for establishing a main operating account or capital injection channel. EU-licensed EMIs (e.g., fintech platforms) are accepted but generally limited to payment routing, not the primary treasury repository. For this cross-border medical device trade, a tier-1 domestic bank is mandatory for trade finance and LC operations.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (plus 3.3% contribution sociale on IS exceeding €763,000, yielding 25.825% top marginal rate)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

25% for dividends paid to non-resident corporate shareholders (domestic rate under article 119 bis CGI); 30% aggregate flat tax (PFU) for non-resident individuals. Exemption applies to qualifying EU/EEA parent companies holding at least 10% for two years under the Parent-Subsidiary Directive.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For fiscal years ending 31 December, the return must be submitted by the second working day following 1 May (normally 3 May); for other fiscal years, within 3 months of the fiscal year-end; electronic filing may extend the deadline to 5 May for calendar-year companies.

Late filing: 10% of the assessed tax for failure to file by the statutory deadline (40% after a formal notice; 80% for abuse or fraud), plus monthly late-payment interest of 0.20%. Incomplete or inaccurate balance-sheet schedules may give rise to a separate fixed penalty of €150 per omitted item. Criminal tax evasion is punishable by up to €75,000 fine and 5 years' imprisonment.

Must reconcile exactly with the branch accounts of a French PE and with the treaty-attribute rules for a non-resident investor. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Full Local File and Master File mandatory where the French entity's annual net turnover or gross assets exceed €400 million, or where the parent/controlling group exceeds €400 million; Country-by-Country reporting applies to groups with consolidated revenue ≥ €750 million. Below these thresholds, a simplified transfer-pricing file must still be kept available on request.

Economic substance scope: France is not an Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) jurisdiction, but applies a strong substance-over-form doctrine, the abus de droit anti-abuse rule, the OECD Principal Purpose Test under BEPS MLI, and EU ATAD CFC controls. A French SARL manufacturing digital X-ray flat panel detectors must demonstrate genuine commercial substance in France—office, plant, qualified personnel (engineers and production staff), decision-making, and risk-bearing—to avoid recharacterization of transactions or denial of treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (fraude fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 80% of the additional corporate income tax for deliberate incompleteness or false statements (article 1729 CGI), plus monthly late-payment interest; criminal fine up to €75,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; the judgment may be published on the DGFiP website.

Director personal liability: The gérant or director who personally participates in the fraud may be made jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties, subjected to a travel ban, prohibited from managing a French company, and sentenced to 5 years' imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Default / Incorrect TP

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine of 5% of the transfer-pricing reassessment (article 1735 B), plus 40% bad-faith penalty and late-payment interest; the tax administration may recharacterize the overpriced remuneration as a constructive dividend subject to 25% withholding tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly certify false transfer-pricing documentation face personal civil liability for the resulting tax shortfall; in cases of abus de droit, the corporate veil may be pierced and the director may incur personal fines and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Beneficial Ownership Concealment (AML/CTF)

Statutory sanction: Failure to file the beneficial ownership register (RBE) is punishable by 6 months' imprisonment and a fine of €7,500 per director; payments to ETNC entities face a 75% withholding tax; artificial arrangements are voided with an 80% anti-abuse penalty.

Director personal liability: Company directors and the de facto beneficial owner are jointly liable for restitution of unpaid taxes and penalties; asset freezes and travel bans may be imposed; serious AML violations carry up to 5 years' imprisonment.
Treaty Abuse and Excessive Non-Resident Distributions

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty exemption and re-application of the 25% dividend withholding tax, together with the 3% distribution surcharge; 80% penalty for misuse of law (abus de droit); monthly late-payment interest continues accruing; the case may be referred to the criminal authorities.

Director personal liability: The legal representative who fails to collect the required withholding tax becomes personally liable for the withheld amounts; directors may face civil damages, criminal convictions for fictitious dividend payments, and travel/asset restrictions.
