Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A French SARL must have a registered office in France. Domiciliation with a compliant commercial domiciliation provider is generally permissible if the provider is authorized and the arrangement satisfies the French Commercial Code, domiciliation-contract, identification, records-access, and anti-money-laundering requirements. A manager may in certain circumstances establish the registered office at a personal residence subject to statutory conditions and lease, condominium, planning, and landlord restrictions. A virtual office is not sufficient as the sole operational substance for manufacturing armored subsea telecommunications fiber cables. The company should maintain appropriate manufacturing, testing, warehousing, engineering, quality, environmental, export-control, procurement, and personnel arrangements. Depending on equipment and processes, environmental authorization or declaration under the ICPE regime, workplace-safety compliance, customs registration, export-control authorization, dual-use authorization, defense-related authorization, telecom-sector compliance, and product or technical certification may be required. A general manufacturing license is not automatically required solely because the company is incorporated as a SARL.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No legal minimum share capital for an SARL. If a share capital is stated, at least 20% of each cash subscription must be deposited in a blocked bank account or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations prior to incorporation; release occurs upon registration. In-kind contributions must be fully paid in.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP), Ministry of Economy and Finance — official portal: https://www.impots.gouv.fr; company formalities (Guichet Unique des Formalités des Entreprises) operated by the INPI: https://formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr; competent desk for non-resident businesses: Service des Impôts des Entreprises Étrangères (SIEE).

E-government filing portal

A French SARL automatically obtains its statutory tax identification numbers upon incorporation filing through the mandatory Guichet Unique (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr). INSEE and the DGFiP issue the SIREN (9-digit TIN), the SIRET (establishment ID) and, after validation, the French intra-Community VAT number (format FR + 2-digit key + SIREN), verifiable on the European Commission VIES system; issuance is free of charge and generally completed within one week. A foreign entity without a French establishment seeking a French VAT ID must file Form 3519-SD with the SIEE through the non-resident registration module on impots.gouv.fr; where the applicant is established in a State considered non-cooperative by France, or in the cases set out in Article 289 A of the Code Général des Impôts (CGI), the appointment of a fiscally co-debtor representative (représentant fiscal) established in France and jointly and severally liable for VAT is mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

France does not provide a US-style 'check-the-box' election; entity classification is determined by private-company law. A commercial SARL is by default subject to entity-level corporation tax (impôt sur les sociétés, IS) under Article 206-1 CGI. A qualifying SARL whose shareholders are mainly individuals may elect, with unanimous shareholder consent, to be treated as transparent for income tax purposes on a five-year term under Article 239 bis AA CGI (régime des sociétés de personnes). Conversely, entities that would otherwise be transparent may elect optional entity-level IS under Article 206-3 CGI. Anti-hybrid rules implementing ATAD 1 and 2, codified in Article 212 bis CGI, neutralize hybrid financial instruments and hybrid entity mismatches; a foreign election creating a double deduction or a deduction-without-inclusion in respect of a French entity is disregarded by the DGFiP.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France operates a liberalized FX regime aligned with EU and IMF Article VIII standards. Current account transactions are freely convertible. Capital account transactions, including profit remittances and equity injections, require mandatory prior declaration to the Banque de France for values exceeding €100,000 via specific reporting mechanisms. Profit repatriation is permitted without prior approval subject to full tax compliance, AML verification, and economic substance requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

French exporters must repatriate foreign exchange proceeds within six months of shipment. Proceeds shall be surrendered to a French bank account in euros at the prevailing market rate. Non-compliance triggers Banque de France reporting obligations and potential administrative penalties.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France
