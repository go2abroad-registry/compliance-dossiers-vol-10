Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital for a French SARL. The articles freely fix the capital amount. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) — portal: impots.gouv.fr; VAT registration via Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) in the relevant territorial department; European VIES data exchange for intra-community VAT numbers.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity conducting taxable operations in France must apply for a French SIREN/SIRET number and a TVA intracommunautaire identification number by filing the national registration form (form No. 14755 for non-resident taxable persons) with the DGFiP competent for non-resident taxpayers. If the foreign entity has no fixed establishment in France and is a non-EU resident, a French tax representative (fiscal representative) may be required to file VAT returns and pay VAT. The application must specify the precise nature of the supplies in France, expected turnover, business identification details from the home jurisdiction, and the appointed legal representative. Proof of the entity's existence, statutory registration, and director/manager identity must accompany the application. Upon completion, the DGFiP issues a French VAT identification number, which becomes the primary reference for customs, invoicing, electronic reporting, and refund claims.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a French SARL is by default subject to corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés, IS) on its worldwide profits, with the territorial-scope exception for foreign permanent establishments. It is not treated as a pass-through entity unless an election is made under the specific statutory rules described below.

Available election forms

Under Article 206 of the Code Général des Impôts (CGI), a SARL is subject to IS by default. However, under Article 239 bis AA CGI, a SARL whose shares are held exclusively by individuals and whose manager is one of those individuals may elect to be taxed as an income-tax pass-through entity (régime des sociétés de personnes), losing IS status for an indefinite period. This election is irrevocable. Additionally, hybrid mismatch conversion elections under French anti-hybrid legislation (implementing ATAD) may apply to reclassify disregarded payments for French tax purposes; however, no US-style 'check-the-box' election exists in French domestic law.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France is a Eurozone jurisdiction with full current-account and capital-account convertibility under the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and the French Monetary and Financial Code. No exchange control licence is required for ordinary commercial payments, capital injections, trade settlements or dividend remittances. Repatriation of profits is permitted, but dividends paid by a French SARL to non-French shareholders are subject to French withholding tax at 30% under domestic law unless the Parent Subsidiary Directive (2011/96/EU) or an applicable double-tax treaty reduces or eliminates the levy. The SARL must maintain a shareholder residence location certificate, board minutes approving the distribution and proof of effective beneficial ownership to secure treaty relief.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France does not impose mandatory surrender of export proceeds and sets no statutory conversion ratio. Export receipts may be credited to authorised foreign-currency accounts held with French credit institutions and converted at commercial market rates. For balance-of-payments statistical purposes, the collecting bank must file a declaration with Banque de France for every cross-border transaction with a value exceeding EUR 50,000. There is no repurchase obligation by the central bank, and no local currency delivery requirement exists for euro-denominated export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), acting concurrently with the Direction Generale du Tresor (French Treasury) and the Autorite de Controle Prudentiel et de Resolution (ACPR) for banking supervision and foreign-exchange statistical control.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. France recognises Electronique Money Institutions (EMIs) authorised by the ACPR under PSD2, and a licensed offshore EMI passporting into France may hold safeguarding accounts and provide euro-denominated IBANs for operational payments, FX conversion and payment instruction processing. Legal constraints are that an EMI cannot grant credit, cannot accept deposits as a bank does, must keep e-money funds safeguarded in segregated accounts, and must apply the full French AML/CTF and KYC framework. For a non-European founder, an EMI can be used as a secondary payment rail, but it cannot replace a tier-1 French bank for capital escrow, documentary credits or treasury hedging.
