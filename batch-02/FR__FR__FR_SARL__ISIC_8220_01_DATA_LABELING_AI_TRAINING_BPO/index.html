Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP), Ministère de l'Économie, des Finances et de la Souveraineté Industrielle et Numérique; portals: impots.gouv.fr (tax accounts and e-filing), formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr (Guichet Unique operated by INPI for corporate registration), and Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (DGDDI) via Delta X for customs export declarations.

E-government filing portal

1) Register the SARL through the Guichet Unique des Formalités des Entreprises (INPI), which automatically assigns the 9-digit SIREN and 14-digit SIRET identification numbers; 2) file the VAT registration application with the DGFiP's Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) competent for the registered office, which issues the intra-Community VAT identification number (numéro de TVA intracommunautaire, format FR + 2-digit key + 9-digit SIREN); 3) a non-resident entity without a French seat must file Form 1547-ST (demande d'immatriculation à la TVA), supply evidence of its foreign tax registration and, if established in the EU, notify its own tax authority of the French establishment; non-residents may be required to appoint a VAT fiscal representative under Article 289 A CGI; all registrations must be completed before the first taxable transaction.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: SARL is a non-transparent corporation subject to Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) at the standard 25% rate, with a reduced 15% rate on the first EUR 42,500 of annual taxable profit if SME criteria are met; territorial scope under Article 209 I CGI: profits from activities carried on through a French establishment are taxable in France, while profits attributable to foreign permanent establishments are excluded from the French tax base; tax residence for treaty purposes is determined by the place of effective management (siège de direction effective), generally France for a SARL whose registered office and central control are in France.

Available election forms

(i) Article 239 bis AA CGI election: a SARL less than 5 years old, with fewer than 50 employees and annual turnover below EUR 10 million, may elect full pass-through taxation in the hands of partners under Impôt sur le Revenu (election effective for 5 years); (ii) VAT regime elections: franchise en base de TVA small-business exemption, and after registration the choice between régime réel simplifié and régime réel normal; (iii) OSS/IOSS registration for EU B2C distance sales and electronic services; (iv) French tax consolidation (intégration fiscale) for a 95% owned group; (v) no US-style check-the-box exists; entity classification is fixed by commercial law and the CGI, with hybrid mismatch outcomes governed solely by EU ATAD 1/2 transposition.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Franchise en base de TVA exemption applies when prior-year annual turnover is below EUR 36,800 for services (prestations de services) and EUR 91,900 for supplies of goods; tolerance thresholds of EUR 39,100 (services) and EUR 101,000 (goods) permit one additional year of exemption after an overrun; compulsory VAT registration arises upon exceeding the applicable limit, and voluntary registration is available under Article 293 B CGI.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical exports of goods outside the EU are exempt with credit (0% VAT) under Article 262-I CGI, provided the goods leave the EU customs territory within the statutory deadline and are evidenced by an accepted export declaration; services directly linked to exported goods (transport, handling, storage in customs zones) qualify for the same 0% regime under Article 262-II CGI; for B2B supplies of AI data labeling and training services to a non-resident taxable person, the place of supply is the customer's establishment (Article 259-1 CGI), so the supply is outside French VAT territorial scope (hors champ) with no output VAT, while the French supplier retains input VAT recovery; for B2C electronic services to non-EU consumers the supply is deemed situated in the consumer's country, and for EU consumers the supplier charges the customer-country VAT via OSS.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Document Administratif Unique) accepted and authenticated by the DGDDI through Delta X
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR consignment note evidencing physical exit from the EU customs territory
  • Commercial Invoice and signed Export Sales Contract stating incoterms and place of supply
  • Proof of the foreign customer's non-resident status (VIES validation certificate or foreign tax residency certificate)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment records evidencing FX repatriation and settlement in the contractual currency

Mandatory e-invoicing reform under the 2023 Finance Law: from 1 September 2026 all VAT-taxable persons established in France must receive structured e-invoices and all large and mid-sized enterprises must issue them; from 1 September 2027 SMEs and micro-enterprises must issue them; B2B domestic transactions must flow through certified Plateformes de Dématérialisation Partenaires (PDP) or the public Portail Public de Facturation (PPF); e-reporting of B2C and cross-border transaction data to the DGFiP applies on the same timeline; permitted formats are Factur-X, UBL and CII (EN 16931); transmission uses APIs between the PPF and taxpayer software; there is no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping requirement in France.

The ordinary tax audit window is 3 years (Article L.169 LPF), extended to 10 years for international tax abuse, transfer pricing and VAT evasion; deliberate misdeclaration attracts a 40% bad-faith surcharge (manquement délibéré) under Article 1729 CGI plus 0.20% monthly late-payment interest under Article 1727 CGI; fraudulent maneuvers or abus de droit trigger an 80% surcharge; unfounded export zero-rating or hors-champ claims cause full VAT clawback, loss of deduction rights, and fines from EUR 15,000 to EUR 50,000; tax fraud (Article 1741 CGI) is punishable by up to EUR 500,000 fine and 5 years' imprisonment (organised fraud: EUR 750,000 and 7 years); export substantiation records must be retained for 6 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France is an EU/EMU member and grants full current-account and capital-account convertibility for resident and non-resident counterparties under the TFEU freedom of payment and capital movement. No administrative FX authorization is required to receive equity injections, invoice clients, or repay trade debts. Profit remittance is lawful but subject to corporate tax clearance, statutory annual general meeting approval, and, for distributions to foreign shareholders, French dividend withholding tax (generally 25% domestic rate, mitigated by EU Directives or bilateral tax treaties). A French SARL can freely convert EUR receipts into foreign currency and hold balances in foreign-currency accounts at an authorized credit institution. Restrictions apply only to EU/EEA sanction compliance and to inward foreign direct investment in sensitive sectors (FDI review by the French Treasury), neither of which applies to an ISIC 8220 AI data-labeling BPO.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender regime in France. Export receipts in convertible foreign currency may be retained, held in foreign-currency deposit accounts, or converted at any time at market rates; the mandatory conversion ratio is 0%, and no statutory repatriation timeline is imposed. The only statutory obligation is objective balance-of-payments reporting: resident companies must provide the Banque de France with declaration data enabling monetary statistics; banks are responsible for identifying individual cross-border flows and for filing reports when required. For invoicing in EUR, no declaration is needed; for non-EU foreign currency receipts, the books must show the FX position and the bank may request proof of commercial substance.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), with policy coordination by the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury) under the French Monetary and Financial Code (Code monétaire et financier); banking and payment institution supervision is exercised by the ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM for carefully selected EU/EEA-authorized digital banks and e-money institutions (e.g., Wise, Qonto, Shine, PayPal), and LOW to PROHIBITED for unlicensed or offshore EMIs based outside the EEA that do not hold an ACPR licence or EU passport. Faithful compliance with PSD2 requires the EMI to be authorised in a member state and passported to France via the ACPR. EMIs cannot constitute a full treasury substitute: they are acceptable for low-value mass payouts (PayPal/Wise rails) and merchant collections, but cannot provide French statutory share-capital blocking, French IBAN settlement with the Banque de France, trade-financing (letter of credit), or corporate tax remittance. As an operational reserve, an EEA-passported EMI may be used as a second-line disbursement rail, provided all receipts are reconciled with the primary French bank and the AML/KYC file for the final beneficiaries is maintained.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant injection requires a SWIFT MT103 (or equivalent SEPA credit for EUR) from the subscribing shareholder to the French bank acting as capital depository, addressed to the 'SARL [name] en formation' with the account type 'Compte de dépôt de capital'. Field 70 must carry unambiguous purpose wording such as 'Souscription au capital social / apport en numéraire' (Share subscription). Receiving bank verification: the bank will freeze the funds until the KBIS and statutory legal publication are complete, then issue an 'attestation de dépôt de fonds' and release to the operational account. For cross-border flows from non-EU jurisdictions, the remitter must state the source of funds in the remittance instruction, supply an FX conversion certificate if funds are converted from USD to EUR, and comply with EU sanctions declaration. The French treasury reporting code should be set to direct investment (capital participation); no separate Banque de France authorisation is required, but the bank will append a balance-of-payments purpose code to the MT103.For the BPO treasury, outbound disbursements should be classified into (a) payroll/subcontractor payouts — via SEPA Credit Transfer (SEPA CT) and SEPA Instant for EU recipients, using the beneficiary's IBAN; (b) intra-group royalties/platform fees — via SWIFT MT103 with EUR/USD settlement through a correspondent bank and the purpose code 'Services bancaires / frais d'exploitation' and transfer-pricing documentation; (c) foreign supplier settlement for cloud/GPU/annotation tools — via SWIFT MT103 or Commercial Letter of Credit (only if mandated, with UCP 600 rules) depending on the vendor's jurisdiction; and (d) shareholder distributions — via SWIFT MT103 using the purpose code for dividends and attaching an attestation of withholding tax compliance. Faithful compliance with PSD2 requires the EMI to be authorised in a member state and passported to France via the ACPR. EMIs cannot constitute a full treasury substitute: they are acceptable for low-value mass payouts (PayPal/Wise rails) and merchant collections, but cannot provide French statutory share-capital blocking, French IBAN settlement with the Banque de France, trade-financing (letter of credit), or corporate tax remittance. As an operational reserve, an EEA-passported EMI may be used as a second-line disbursement rail, provided all receipts are reconciled with the primary French bank and the AML/KYC file for the final beneficiaries is maintained.
