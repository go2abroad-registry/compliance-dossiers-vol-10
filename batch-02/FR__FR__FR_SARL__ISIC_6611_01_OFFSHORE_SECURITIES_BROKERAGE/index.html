Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Not permitted for a regulated securities brokerage. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital required for an SARL; the capital is set freely in the Articles. Cash contributions must be paid up to at least 50% upon subscription, the balance within five years; in-kind contributions must be fully paid up at formation. Funds must be deposited in a blocked account held with a bank, notary, or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations until RCS registration is completed.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance from the Service Départemental d'Incendie et de Secours (SDIS) and a fire safety certificate for the premises classified as an ERP (établissement recevant du personnel), including smoke detectors, extinguishers, emergency exits, and evacuation plans. Structural load inspection and approval of the leased premises from the building owner's technical file, especially for server rooms or heavy trading floors. Occupational health compliance must be validated by the regional occupational health service (SSTI) and CARSAT, including electrical safety verification, first-aid equipment, and the mandatory Document Unique d'Évaluation des Risques Professionnels (DUERP).

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital required for an SARL; the capital is set freely in the Articles. Cash contributions must be paid up to at least 50% upon subscription, the balance within five years; in-kind contributions must be fully paid up at formation. Funds must be deposited in a blocked account held with a bank, notary, or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations until RCS registration is completed.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France applies full capital and current account convertibility under EU Treaty free-movement provisions; the euro is freely convertible and no exchange licence is required for lawful current commercial settlements or capital movements. Dividend and profit remittances from a French SARL to non-resident shareholders are permitted without prior foreign-exchange approval, subject to corporate formalities, tax withholding certification or treaty relief, and anti-money-laundering source-of-funds verification. For a regulated securities broker, inward foreign capital is additionally screened by the bank through KYC/AML controls and by the reporting obligations applicable to financial transactions exceeding statutory thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France does not impose any surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Export receipts may be freely retained in foreign currency or converted to EUR. However, French resident companies must report cross-border transactions to the Banque de France for balance-of-payments statistical purposes when thresholds or defined categories are triggered; cash movements of €10,000 or more require customs declaration; and direct investment and foreign account balances above the statutory reporting limits must be declared annually.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France, with prudential supervision for banks and e-money institutions exercised by the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR) and balance-of-payments/foreign-exchange statistical control administered under the Direction Générale du Trésor.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. A French-regulated electronic money institution (approved by ACPR) may provide e-money wallets and payment accounts for operating expenses, but it is not a viable full-service treasury counterparty for a high-risk offshore securities brokerage requiring segregated client funds, FX hedging, and regulated settlement services. Unlicensed offshore digital EMIs are not acceptable as primary banking rails because their source-of-funds verification, audit trail, and sub-account structures are generally insufficient for French AML/KYC and the ACPR interpretation of safeguarding obligations. Any EMI solution must hold a French or EEA licence, provide segregated safeguarding accounts with a credit institution, and be acceptable to the French statutory auditor.

Defense evidence: Certified copy of the ACPR or EU securities licence; the AMF registration certificate; constitutional documents of the SARL; audited financial statements; structural and business-activity narrative; transaction-monitoring and sanctions-screening policy; outsourcing and treasury policy; list of correspondent banks and projected monthly clearing volumes; source-of-funds evidence for regulatory capital; tax compliance certificate from the French tax authorities.

Defense evidence: Valid passports and proof of residence for all natural persons at 25% or more; registry of beneficial owners (Registre des Bénéficiaires Effectifs); bank references from the sending institution; independent professional asset-verification reports; source-of-wealth letters; tax returns or tax residence certificates; explanation of the economic purpose of the broker cross-holding chain; sanctions and PEP screening results.

Root cause: Treasury operations require swift movement between segregated client accounts, escrow accounts, and broker operational accounts that may be interpreted as misuse of client money.

Defense evidence: Client money management and reconciliation policy compliant with Articles L.533-10 et seq. and R.533-11 et seq. of the French Monetary and Financial Code; daily segregation and reconciliation methodology; agreement with the bank on the operating structure; legal opinion on client asset safeguarding and custody; internal audit sign-off; ACPR reporting acknowledgements; bank-issued attestation of client-money account designations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder capital, subordinated loans, or liquidity injections from the offshore parent must be routed via SWIFT MT103/MT202 for USD or other foreign currency, or SEPA Credit Transfer / TARGET2 in EUR using the SARL's IBAN at a French Tier 1 bank. The bank mandate shall record the payment reference using a recognised EU/French balance-of-payments code for 'direct investment – equity capital' or 'intercompany treasury operations' plus the underlying transaction identifiers. For compliance, the bank will require a board resolution approving the injection, evidence of source of funds, confirmation that no administrative authorisation is required under the French Monetary and Financial Code, and an instrument or share-issuance certificate. Funds intended for securities settlement or client-money cover are not interchangeable and must be separately designated before crediting; escrow and operational rails must not be commingled.Outbound trade and treasury disbursements are cleared through (i) SWIFT MT103/MT202 for cross-currency settlement through the clearing account at HSBC Continental Europe or BNP Paribas, (ii) SEPA Instant Credit Transfer for EUR payments up to the network limit for broker operating expenses and counterparty margin settlements, and (iii) documentary or standby commercial letters of credit issued under UCP 600 only for physical trade-related deals, not for client equities, CFDs or derivatives. For settlement of client securities transactions, delivery-versus-payment (DVP) through a French CSD or an international central securities depository is mandatory; the bank must confirm that outgoing settlement instructions carry proper field 72 bank-to-bank and ISO 20022 transaction-type codes to distinguish own funds, client money, and the broker's proprietary capital.
