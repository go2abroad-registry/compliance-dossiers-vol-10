Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The competent local clerk of the Commercial Court (greffe du tribunal de commerce) maintains the Trade and Companies Register (Registre du commerce et des sociétés, RCS). Formation filings are submitted through the INPI-operated Guichet unique des formalités des entreprises.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce), Articles L.223-1 et seq. (SARL); formation and registration provisions include Articles L.210-1 et seq. and R.123-1 et seq.

Formation / constitutional filings

Electronic declaration of business creation and supporting documents filed through the Guichet unique; the SARL's signed articles of association (statuts) are a mandatory supporting instrument. The former paper M0 form is not the standard filing route.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum share capital applies. At least 20% of cash contributions must be paid on incorporation; the balance must be paid within five years of registration. Cash contributions are deposited with an authorized bank, notary, or other legally authorized depositary, and released after RCS registration. Contributions in kind are fully contributed at formation.

Funding & unlock path: No universal pre-opening fire or structural certificate applies to an office-only logistics company. Premises open to the public may require ERP fire and accessibility compliance and approvals under applicable rules. Warehouses and workshops must meet applicable building, fire-safety, workplace, and ICPE requirements; structural capacity must be suitable for intended loads. Employers must complete occupational risk assessment and prevention measures, including the required DUERP.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum share capital applies. At least 20% of cash contributions must be paid on incorporation; the balance must be paid within five years of registration. Cash contributions are deposited with an authorized bank, notary, or other legally authorized depositary, and released after RCS registration. Contributions in kind are fully contributed at formation.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No general industrial licensing board is required for a SARL providing project-cargo logistics coordination. The competent authorities depend on the activities performed: DREAL (Direction régionale de l’environnement, de l’aménagement et du logement) for relevant road-transport and regulated-facility matters; the prefecture and local authorities for route-specific exceptional-transport permissions.

Category: No general manufacturing or industrial operating permit. If the company itself conducts regulated road haulage for hire, it may require registration in the transport register and a Community licence or other applicable road-transport authorization; a freight-forwarding commissionnaire de transport activity may require registration in the register of transport and freight-forwarding operators. Exceptional-load movements require applicable route and vehicle authorizations.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

As a euro-area member, France maintains full currency convertibility for both current-account and capital-account transactions under EU law, particularly Articles 63 and 64 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. Non-resident shareholders may freely repatriate dividends, branch profits, liquidation proceeds and sale-of-ownership proceeds without previous exchange-control authorization. Profit remittance from a French SARL is not blocked by currency rules, but continues to be subject to ordinary French withholding taxes (default 25% on dividend distributions, subject to reduction under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive or applicable double-tax treaties) and to standard anti-money-laundering verification of the underlying economic purpose. The only convertibility-related requirements are statistical balance-of-payment declarations to the Banque de France for direct investment positions crossing defined reporting thresholds and the bank-driven transmission of cross-border cash-flow details for monetary statistics.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France does not impose any statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export receipts. A French exporting SARL may invoice and collect in any freely convertible currency and retain proceeds in euro or foreign-currency accounts. There is no prescribed settlement timeline for converting repatriated export proceeds, and any mandatory conversion ratio is nil. The residual regulatory duties are limited to the periodic balance-of-payments reporting obligation under the French external financial relations regulation, triggered when the aggregate cross-border flows exceed published thresholds, together with the need to present the bank with the governing transport and project-cargo contracts for AML/KYC purposes when funds are received.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), acting within the Eurosystem; prudential banking supervision is delegated to the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR). Foreign-exchange regulatory policy and external financial relations reporting are administratively coordinated by the Direction Générale du Trésor of the Ministry of Economy, Finance and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore non-EU digital EMIs have low banking viability in France unless they are registered or passported under the Payment Services Directive (PSD2) and are supervised by the ACPR. The SARL may only lawfully use an EMI account for principal treasury flows when the provider holds a French payment institution licence or an EU passport with an ACPR relationship. Such an EMI can be used efficiently for cross-border customer payments and multi-currency settlement, but its long-term availability for milestone and LC transaction flows is narrower than that of a full tier-1 bank. Overall feasibility: High for an EU-licensed EMI with a French IBAN and ACPR notification; Low for any offshore provider without a valid EU passport because it creates sanctions, recovery and audit risks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections into the French SARL should be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the parent's corporate bank, referencing the ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' or 'INVE' (equity capital/investment) together with an explicit remittance note citing the share-capital increase or shareholder current account. The receiving French bank performs sanctions screening, AML verification and source-of-funds checks; the injection must correspond to the subscribed capital in the notarial or corporate registration documentation. If the capital injection amounts to a qualifying direct investment, the French SARL or investor must file the applicable balance-of-payment declaration with the Banque de France, including post-closing reporting where the statutory thresholds are exceeded. The verification gate requires board resolution, subscribed-share confirmation, a signed bank order, and proof of the investor's identity and economic substance.Treasury disbursements for project-cargo heavy-haul operations will be cleared through three principal rails: (1) SEPA Credit Transfer using ISO 20022 pacs.008 messages for euro payments within the EEA; (2) SWIFT MT103 for non-euro or non-EEA counterparty payments, with the transaction quoted in the original currency and converted at a transparent market-based rate; and (3) Commercial Letter of Credit under UCP 600 opened/appointed via SWIFT MT700/MT707 messages for equipment procurement or for major client milestone settlements. All outbound disbursements must be supported by the underlying project-cargo contract, invoices from subcontractors or port and haulage vendors, and adequate proof of transport service execution. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File triggered when the French entity's net turnover (CA HT) exceeds EUR 50,000,000; Master File triggered when the consolidated group turnover exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Below these thresholds, a taxpayer with cross-border related-party transactions must still be able to demonstrate arm's length pricing upon request.

Economic substance scope: France has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations of the UAE type. However, the general anti-abuse rule (abus de droit, Article L64 LPF) and EU ATAD provisions require real economic substance. A French SARL used as an invoice pass-through without employees, management, equipment, or commercial risk will be disregarded for tax purposes and treaty benefits will be denied for lack of beneficial ownership.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 80% of the evaded tax for abuse of law; criminal fine up to EUR 750,000; imprisonment up to 5 years (7 years in aggravated circumstances); asset confiscation, seizure of bank accounts, and publication of the conviction.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties under Article L267 LPF; criminal courts can impose travel bans, prohibition from managing commercial companies for up to 15 years, and imprisonment.
Late Filing / Non-Payment of Corporate Income Tax

Statutory sanction: Late-filing surcharge of 10% of tax due, increasing to 40% after formal notice and 80% for fraud; late-payment interest of 0.20% per month; fixed fine of EUR 150 per missing statement; tax lien against the company's real property.

Director personal liability: The gérant may be personally assessed for unpaid corporate tax when insolvency results from mismanagement or intentional default; travel bans can be issued under the tax enforcement procedure to prevent directors leaving France with assets.
Transfer Pricing Misreporting

Statutory sanction: Reassessment under Article 57 CGI at arm's length pricing plus 0.20% monthly interest; penalty of 40% for deliberately inaccurate pricing; absence of Local File or Master File triggers an additional fine of up to EUR 10,000 per missing document; publication of the adjustment may be ordered.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign artificial transfer pricing agreements can be held civilly liable for the resulting tax deficiency; criminal prosecution for fraud or money laundering can lead to imprisonment and a travel ban.
Shell Company / Abuse of Law

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the SARL under Article L64 LPF; re-attribution of taxable profits to the actual beneficiary; 80% penalty on the evaded tax; denial of DTA benefits; additional EUR 10,000 fine for non-declaration of beneficial owners.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced when the SARL is a sham; directors become personally liable for the entire tax debt, may be forbidden from leaving French territory, and risk imprisonment of up to 5 years for fraudulent declaration.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fine up to EUR 10,000 for non-declaration of beneficial owner (up to EUR 50,000 for legal entities); if money laundering is established, criminal sanctions of 5 years' imprisonment and EUR 375,000 fine for natural persons; asset forfeiture orders.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly facilitate suspicious cross-border payments can be prosecuted as accomplices to money laundering; civil liability for damages, travel bans, and restrictions on managing commercial entities may be imposed.
