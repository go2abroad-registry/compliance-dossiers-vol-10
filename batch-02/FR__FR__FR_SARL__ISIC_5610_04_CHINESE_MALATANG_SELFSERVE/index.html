Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

There is no statutory minimum capital for a SARL; the capital is freely fixed in the statutes. Cash contributions must be paid up to at least 20% of the subscribed amount before the incorporation application, the balance payable within five years, and in-kind contributions must be fully delivered at formation. The cash contribution is held in a blocked escrow bank account with a licensed deposit institution or the notary and may be released only upon issuance of the Kbis extract.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate issued by the departmental commission of safety for public-access establishments classified Type N (restaurants), comprising at least one visit or dossier review after opening; electrical installation compliance certificate (Consuel) for all cooking appliances, extraction systems, and lighting; structural load inspection confirming that the floor slab can withstand self-serve broth stations, refrigerated counters, ventilation ducting, and seating layout; workplace clearance evidenced by the Document Unique d'Évaluation des Risques, crew medical fitness checks, and HACCP hygiene training certificates kept on the premises.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFIP) – portal: impots.gouv.fr; business registration portal: formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr / guichet unique

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a French SARL completes company formation via the Guichet Unique (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) with the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés, obtaining SIREN and SIRET numbers. The DGFIP then automatically issues the French Intra-Community VAT number (numéro de TVA intracommunautaire) after the SARL is allocated to the national VAT regime. For a foreign entity not established in France but making taxable supplies in France, the DGFIP requires a direct application for a French VAT number using cerfa forms or the non-established taxable person procedure; a tax representative must be appointed if the entity is established outside the EU.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity establishing a French SARL completes company formation via the Guichet Unique (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) with the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés, obtaining SIREN and SIRET numbers. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

France has no general check-the-box election. A SARL is subject to corporate income tax (impôt sur les sociétés) by default. A family SARL or a small SARL whose partners are all natural persons may irrevocably opt, limited to five fiscal years, for transparent taxation under income tax (Article 239 bis AA CGI). Hybrid mismatch rules are implemented under Articles 234 et seq. CGI pursuant to ATAD, with no elective check-the-box intermediary treatment; VAT grouping (assujettis uniques) is available on application under Article 256 C CGI.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 91,900 (2025 franchise base de TVA threshold for sales of goods) and EUR 36,800 (for services) for French-established small undertakings; for non-established foreign suppliers, no threshold is available – any first supply of taxable goods or services in France, or sale through a fully taxable agent/warehouse, triggers compulsory VAT registration and reporting obligations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to physical exports of goods outside the European Union under Article 262-1-I CGI where the seller ships the goods out of EU territory, or exports for the buyer who is established outside the EU. Export-related services (e.g., freight, storage, processing) may also be zero-rated under Article 262-1-II CGI when directly linked to the export. The taxpayer must hold documentary proof of the physical departure from the EU and issue an invoice expressly stating 'exonération export' with reference to the applicable provision; which must be produced at the time of the transaction and retained for the statutory six-year archiving period.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DAU / IE-501 or SAD) with validated exit from the EU customs territory
  • Bill of Lading (ocean/air/road) or other satisfactory transport evidence (CMR, AWB)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bordereau de change / attestation de rapatriement des fonds) evidencing payment receipt from the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice with Incoterm 2020 export terms (EXW, FCA, FOB, etc.)

French B2B e-invoicing mandate applies under Ordonnance n° 2021-1190 and new CGI Article 289. From 1 September 2026 all French-established taxable persons must receive and send structured electronic invoices (Factur-X or EDI format) through a certified Partner Dematerialization Platform (PDP) or the Public Invoicing Portal (PPF), with e-reporting of B2C sales and cross-border transactions via the same infrastructure. Real-time clearance is achieved through the mandatory transmission of invoice data to the DGFIP for fiscal verification before any input VAT deduction is recognized.

DGFIP customs auditors verify the physical reality of exports through documentary evidence and cross-border data reconciliation. Unjustified zero-rating leads to the reversal of output VAT with delinquent interest, plus penalties under Article 1729 CGI: 40% penalty for deliberate misstatement, 80% penalty for hidden compulsory disclosures or active document falsification, and 80% recourse against abuse of law. In addition, Article 1741 CGI criminal sanctions apply for tax fraud, carrying up to 5 years' imprisonment and a substantial statutory fine; retrospective clawback is possible for up to three years (six years for undisclosed business activity under the right-to-audit period).

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains full de jure and de facto convertibility of the euro (EUR) for both current and capital account transactions according to OECD Code of Liberalisation and IMF Article VIII status. No exchange control approval is required for inbound equity injection, shareholder loans, royalty receipts, or profit repatriation to an EU/EEA resident parent company. For non-EU (e.g., Chinese) shareholders, no separate French FX authorisation applies, but the beneficiaire must file a DGT foreign direct investment (FDI) declaration only for sensitive activities under the EU FDI Screening Regulation; fast-casual restaurant franchising is NOT a sensitive sector, so no prior FDI authorisation is required. Profit remittance hurdles: an EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive exemption (0% French WHT) applies to dividends paid to an EU/EEA corporate parent holding ≥5% for 24 months; for a Chinese corporate parent, domestic French withholding is 25% (Art. 119 bis CGI), reduced to 10% under the France-China DTT if the parent holds ≥25% of share capital on the dividend payment date; standard French CIT (25%) is levied on profits from royalty and franchising income.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory timeline: N/A. France abolished all export-proceeds surrender and mandatory conversion obligations; exporters may retain 100% of proceeds in EUR or freely hold foreign currency for an unlimited period. However, cross-border monetary links must be declared to Banque de France on a monthly/quarterly basis (reporting of external transactions 'REX' / 'liaisons financières avec l'étranger') where cumulative flows exceed EUR 750,000 per calendar year or EUR 15 million for related-party trade finance. The reporting obligation applies to the resident FR_SARL and is filed through the Banque de France OneGate portal using the CMF purpose codes; the restaurant franchisor's royalty receipts from non-resident franchisees constitute 'services' flows (code 678) and must be reported as exports of services, but no conversion ratio applies.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (BdF) under the monetary policy framework of the European Central Bank (ECB); exchange control statistics and balance-of-payments reporting are administered by BdF as agent of the Direction Générale du Trésor (DGT) of the French Ministry of Economy, Finance and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty. Legal basis: Code monétaire et financier (CMF), Articles L151-1 et seq. and R152-1 et seq.; EU capital movement liberalisation under TFEU Article 63.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for operational satellite accounts and low-value franchise royalty collection; LOW for capital escrow and primary treasury account. Legal constraints: an Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensed in France (e.g., Qonto, Lydia Solutions) or passporting into France under a European EMI (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Bunq) may operate as money transmitters but cannot void the requirement of a credit institution account for SARL capital constitution; under CMF Art. L521-3, only ACPR-licensed credit institutions can receive paid-in share capital and issue a 'certificat du dépositaire des fonds' executed by a notaire/registrar. EMIs may be used for POS settlement of the Malatang outlets and cross-border supplier disbursements, subject to IBAN segregation and no customer-fund lending.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: Chinese shareholder instructs SWIFT MT103 (or CIPS/CNY cross-border payment) to the FR_SARL Euro account at BNP Paribas/SocGen with beneficiary bank BIC, IBAN, and full legal name; critical fields require the correct purpose code — for equity injection use 'Capital increase, non-resident direct investment' (Banque de France CMF-BOP 3: 'B9-CRE' or 'K3' code family); the French receiving bank collects the 'motif du règlement' and the 'État de la contrepartie'. Verification steps: (i) Banque de France requires the entity to register the inward FDI in the statistical survey when cumulative external debt/equity exceeds €750,000; (ii) the Chinese remitting bank must obtain SAFE's FDI foreign exchange registration and the Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) certificate for the Chinese parent; (iii) a Creditor/Debtor reference must show 'Capital apport' to allow the French notaire to issue the capital deposit certificate; use IBAN in 'Account With Institution' and include mode code 'U' for settlement of capital. For CNY-RMB inbound wires, the receiving French bank converts on receipt at market rate, but a pre-arranged EUR/CNH forward is recommended to lock conversion.Clearing rails for the franchise supply chain: (i) SWIFT MT103 for EUR/CNY settlement of inventory imports and equipment purchases from Chinese suppliers; (ii) Documentary collections (CAD/DP) and Commercial Letters of Credit (LC at sight or usance) opened by the French bank in favour of Chinese beneficiary banks under UCP600, particularly for the first tranche of Malatang woks, POS terminals, and self-serve condiment stations; (iii) SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) for royalty rebates to French-franchisee operational accounts; (iv) CIPS/CNY direct clearing where the Chinese supplier provides a CNH account, with the French bank executing via Paris offshore CNY market. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: HIGH for operational satellite accounts and low-value franchise royalty collection; LOW for capital escrow and primary treasury account. Legal constraints: an Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensed in France (e.g., Qonto, Lydia Solutions) or passporting into France under a European EMI (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Bunq) may operate as money transmitters but cannot void the requirement of a credit institution account for SARL capital constitution; under CMF Art. L521-3, only ACPR-licensed credit institutions can receive paid-in share capital and issue a 'certificat du dépositaire des fonds' executed by a notaire/registrar. EMIs may be used for POS settlement of the Malatang outlets and cross-border supplier disbursements, subject to IBAN segregation and no customer-fund lending.
