Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) kept by the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce; incorporation formalities are filed electronically through the Guichet Unique operated by the Institut National de la Propriété Industrielle (INPI).

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce), Articles L.210-1 to L.210-10 (general commercial companies), L.223-1 to L.223-43 (SARL) and R.223-1 et seq.; Monetary and Financial Code (Code monétaire et financier), Articles L.532-9 et seq. (investment services and CIF regime); AMF General Regulation (Règlement général), Articles 312-1 et seq.

Formation / constitutional filings

Unified incorporation dossier 'M0' (Dossier unique d'immatriculation) filed via the INPI Guichet Unique portal, accompanied by the signed statuts (Articles of Association) and the mandatory declaration of beneficial owners (déclaration des bénéficiaires effectifs).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 25 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital for the SARL (freely set above €1 under Article L.223-2 of the Commercial Code); however, the Multi-Family Office holding an AMF/ORIAS licence requires prudential minimum own funds of €125,000 (external AIFM) or €50,000 (MiFID discretionary portfolio management). The founders therefore subscribe and escrow €225,000 in a blocked French bank account (compte bloqué), released to the gérant only upon issuance of the RCS registration certificate (K-bis).

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP), Ministère de l'Économie, des Finances et de la Souveraineté industrielle et numérique; local corporate-tax and VAT administration is handled by the Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) of the registered office. Principal digital portals: https://www.impots.gouv.fr (professional EDI/EFI filing area), https://formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr (Guichet Unique for entity creation and registration), and https://portailfacturation.gouv.fr (Portail Public de Facturation, PPF). For the discretionary multi-family office (ISIC 6430), prior authorization from the Autorité des Marchés Financiers (AMF) and ACPR as a portfolio-management company (MiFID II, art. L. 532-9 CMF) or registration as a Conseiller en Investissements Financiers is a legal precondition to engaging in regulated activity, and the licence reference must be communicated to DGFiP.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: file the French SARL incorporation dossier with the Guichet Unique (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr), including the statuts, non-resident director KYC (passport identity sheet, proof of address, certificate of non-bankruptcy), registered-office lease, and bank capital-blocking certificate. Step 2: INSEE allocates the 9-digit SIREN, SIRET and code APE 6630Z (gestion de fonds / multi-family office); the Guichet automatically transmits the file to DGFiP. Step 3: DGFiP issues the French VAT number (FR + 2-character control key + SIREN), verifiable on the EU VIES database, and opens the corporate income-tax file at the competent SIE; the first fiscal year-end and the statutory filing cycle (liasse fiscale n° 2065 plus annexes 2050-2067) are registered. Step 4: the AMF/ACPR authorisation, the AML/KYC compliance pack (banking_aml_risk_tier: High_Risk_Special_License), and the designation of a licensed depositary bank must be filed with the tax administration. No separate French domestic tax-registration certificate exists: the SIREN/SIRET and VAT number bundle constitutes the TIN for all DGFiP dealings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

France has no full check-the-box regime but provides targeted elections: (i) CGI art. 239 bis AA allows an SME SARL (capital continuously held ≥ 75% by individuals or qualifying partnerships; fewer than 50 employees; turnover under €10m; unlisted) to elect income-tax passthrough (impôt sur le revenu) for five fiscal years; (ii) a single-member EURL owned by an individual is IR-transparent by default and may elect entity-level IS under CGI art. 206(1); (iii) a French parent holding ≥ 95% may elect tax consolidation (intégration fiscale, CGI art. 223-A); (iv) exempt financial services may be opted into VAT (CGI art. 260-B) to restore input-VAT recovery; and (v) EU ATAD hybrid-mismatch designation and documentation obligations apply to cross-border hybrid entities and instruments (BOI-IS-GPE-20-60-40, arts. 205 A CGI).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no general registration threshold for established taxable persons: VAT registration is compulsory from the first taxable transaction unless the franchise en base de TVA applies. As from 1 March 2025 (Loi de finances 2025, CGI art. 293 B), the franchise ceilings are €101,000 for supplies of goods (tolerance €111,000) and €39,100 for service supplies (tolerance €43,100); the EU OSS distance-sales threshold is €10,000. A multi-family office whose discretionary portfolio-management fees qualify as exempt fund-management services (CGI art. 261-C-1-a) has no VAT registration duty; ancillary taxable advisory, corporate-administration and reporting fees are aggregated toward the €39,100 services threshold, and the option to tax under CGI art. 260-B may be exercised on qualifying exempt financial services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported outside the EU are zero-rated under CGI art. 262-I only where the exporter proves physical dispatch to the non-EU destination within 90 days of the supply (EU Reg. 282/2011 art. 43(2)); intra-community supplies are zero-rated under art. 262-II upon proof of the acquirer's valid EU VAT ID, the EC Sales List (DEB), and transport evidence. B2B services supplied to recipients established outside France are outside the scope of French VAT (CGI art. 259-1), invoiced without French VAT and subject to reverse charge at the customer's seat. For the multi-family office, discretionary portfolio management and investment advisory services rendered to non-French taxable private-client mandataries are either outside scope or exempt (art. 261-C-1-a) where they qualify as fund-management activities; service agreements, AML/KYC client files, fee invoices and mandate KPI reports must evidence the foreign establishment and economic substance of each delegation.

  • Customs Export Declaration: French DELTA / Déclaration en Douane (DAE) with MRN evidencing export from the EU customs territory, plus the DEB/Intrastat declaration where intra-union flows are involved
  • Transport proof: original Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR consignment note, endorsed by the carrier, naming the French supplier as consignor and the non-EU buyer as consignee
  • Inward payment evidence: bank credit advice / SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation, or a bank attestation corroborating export proceeds, retained to substantiate the 180-day FX repatriation benchmark

France does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, government digital stamping or an Italy-type SDI. The e-invoicing mandate (Ordonnance 2022-1470, as modified by Ordonnance 2024-893) requires domestic B2B e-invoicing and B2B/B2C e-reporting via the Portail Public de Facturation (PPF) or a certified Partenaire de Dématérialisation (PDP) using API connectivity: from 1 September 2026 for large and mid-size taxpayers, and from 1 September 2027 for all taxpayers including SARLs. Mandatory structured formats are Factur-X (hybrid PDF/A-3 plus XML), UBL 2.1, and CII (UN/CEFACT); the PPF API manages invoice routing, format acceptance, rejet and interoperability validation, but periodic VAT returns (CA3/3519) remain the fiscal-clearance event rather than transaction-level stamping.

DGFiP may reassess unsubstantiated zero-ratings within the general 3-year VAT limitation period (LPF art. L. 176), extended to 6 years for concealed or dissimulated operations and to 10 years for real-estate and certain listed-asset transactions. Failure to produce the statutory export bundle triggers output-VAT clawback with intérêt de retard (0.20% per month, CGI art. 1727), 5%/10% late-filing penalties (art. 1728 CGI), 40% for bad-faith underdeclaration (art. 1729 CGI) and 80% for manœuvres frauduleuses, in addition to criminal tax-fraud exposure (Code pénal art. 1741, up to €150,000 fine and 7 years' imprisonment), customs sanctions (Code des douanes art. 413 bis), and retrospective denial of refund credits; the general anti-abuse rule (LPF art. L. 64, CGI art. 205 A) may re-characterize artificial export or offshore-service structures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France meets the IMF Article VIII obligation, granting full current-account convertibility and unrestricted capital-account movement intra-EU under TFEU Articles 63 to 66. Profit remittance by an SARL to non-resident shareholders is freely permitted once the corporate income tax return (form 2065) is filed and the applicable withholding or distribution formalities under form 2561 are met; no specific foreign-exchange approval is required. The sole material restrictivity is the prior-authorization mechanism for foreign direct investment in sensitive sectors created by Decree No. 2019-1590 of 31 December 2019, which does not apply to the ISIC 6430 multi-family-office scope. Currency conversion from EUR to any OECD/EU currency is commercially unrestricted for the MFO's treasury function.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline applies to French-domiciled receipts. Under Article L. 564-1 of the Code monétaire et financier, all cross-border settlements must be routed through an authorised credit institution or payment institution for monetary statistics and AML/CFT traceability. The MFO retains full discretion to maintain export or service proceeds in EUR or foreign currency. Reporting obligations are limited to the Banque de France's monthly balance-of-payments survey (form EME) only where cumulative cross-border payments exceed EUR 750,000 per calendar month, and to the FADET direct-investment declaration module for capital transactions, due within 20 business days of the transaction.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France) acts as the national central bank and is the responsible statistical and credit-institution supervisory authority, while the European Central Bank (ECB) sets Eurozone monetary policy. Foreign-exchange administration and capital-movement screening fall under the Direction Générale du Trésor (DG Trésor) of the Ministry of Economy, Finance and Recovery. Banking prudential control and AML/CFT enforcement for credit institutions are carried out by the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR), with the Autorité des Marchés Financiers (AMF) supervising investment services and discretionary portfolio-advisory activity.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. ACPR-licensed electronic-money institutions (for example Qonto or Lydia Solutions) and EU-passported EMIs may technically open a euro-denominated payment account and execute SEPA/SWIFT transfers, but an EMI cannot serve as a custody bank, settlement bank, or client-asset segregator for a discretionary portfolio-advisory MFO. Under Article L. 533-10 of the Code monétaire et financier, a regulated credit institution or an AMF-approved custodian must hold client assets. The EMI rail is therefore viable only as a secondary operating-expense conduit, never as the primary clearing or capital-escrow platform.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections are credited through TARGET2 for EUR transfers or via SWIFT MT103/MT202 COV for multi-currency wires, with the remittance information referencing the shareholder board resolution and the statutory purpose of capital contribution. The French bank requires the operation to be flagged as a direct-investment inflow for Banque de France balance-of-payments statistics via the FADET electronic declaration (category 300/301 for company creation or capital increase), filed within 20 business days of receipt. Verification documents include the updated extrait Kbis, the UBO register, evidence of paid-up capital and a tax-clearance certificate (attestation de régularisation fiscale) for the SARL. Amounts above EUR 1,000,000 may trigger ACPR large-exposure due diligence and require 48-hours advance notice. No prior DG Trésor approval is required outside the sensitive-sector regime.Outbound trade settlements are executed over SWIFT MT103 for direct payment obligations and MT202 COV for correspondent reimbursement. Documentary trade is supported by irrevocable commercial letters of credit issued under UCP 600 using the MT700 message sequence, with downstream confirmation through an EU correspondent. Sub-EUR-100,000 euro payments in the EEA clear via SEPA (SCT and SCT Inst); high-value euro payments use TARGET2 same-day RTGS. For custody-wire operations, disbursements follow the MT304 (account entry) or MT540/MT542 free-of-payment standards under standing instructions with the custodian.
