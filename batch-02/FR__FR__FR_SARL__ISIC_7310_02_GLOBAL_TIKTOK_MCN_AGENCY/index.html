Competent corporate registry

Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce within the territorial jurisdiction of the registered office (Paris Commercial Court Registry for Paris), operating via the INPI Guichet Unique (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) and the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS); INSEE assigns SIREN/SIRET.

Governing statute

Code de commerce (France): Articles L.223-1 to L.223-43 (Société à Responsabilité Limitée formation and governance); Articles L.123-1 to L.123-38 and R.123-101 to R.123-118 (Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés and central formalities); Law n°2019-486 (PACTE) Art. 13; EU Services Directive 2006/123/EC transposition.

Formation / constitutional filings

Déclaration de création d'entreprise (M0) filed electronically on the INPI guichet unique, together with the signed Statuts (Articles of Association) and the déclaration de conformité; registration evidenced by the extrait Kbis.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum legal capital €1 (Article L.223-2 Code de commerce) — no statutory minimum for the unregulated creator/MCN agency; capital must be fully subscribed. Cash contributions must be released at least 20% upon subscription (Article L.223-7) and the outstanding balance paid within 5 years; funds must be deposited in a blocked bank account (compte bloqué) with a French credit institution or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations until the Kbis extract is obtained.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate and periodic fire-safety visit by the commission de sécurité (Code de la construction et de l'habitation — 5th-category ERP when offices are open to third parties); workplace health and safety inspection under the Code du travail (Article L.4121-1), including the DUERP (single risk assessment document), evacuation rituals, first-aid devices, and ACMO designations; structural load and occupancy clearance confirmed by the registered office lease-owner's building maintenance file.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum legal capital €1 (Article L.223-2 Code de commerce) — no statutory minimum for the unregulated creator/MCN agency; capital must be fully subscribed. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France is a eurozone member and applies full convertibility of the euro on both current and capital account. Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union Articles 63-65 guarantee free movement of capital and payments with respect to third countries, subject only to E.U. sanctions, French FDI screening in defined sensitive sectors, and balance-of-payments statistical reporting. Profit remittance by an SARL is not blocked by exchange control: dividends, management fees, loan interest and liquidation proceeds may be transferred abroad without Banque de France prior authorization. A 30% withholding tax applies to outbound dividends to non-resident shareholders unless reduced under a tax treaty; that is an income-tax consequence rather than an FX prohibition. The MCN agency business (ISIC 7310 advertising / creator agency) is not a regulated sensitive sector for FDI purposes, although a screening confirmation should be filed if the French operation collects certain connection or data-identifying traffic that could fall under Article L151-3 CFM.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France has no export proceeds surrender requirement, no minimum mandatory conversion ratio, and no prescribed statutory repatriation timeline for receipts denominated in convertible foreign currencies. The euro being the national settlement currency, exporters may lawfully retain foreign-currency balances in E.U.-licensed bank accounts and may convert them at commercial conditions. The only operational discipline is statistical and declarative: cross-border financial relations with non-residents involving individually or cumulatively amounts equal to or greater than EUR 50,000 per calendar month must be captured by the bank and reported to the Banque de France through the MEDOC-BEBA balance-of-payments filings, and every cross-border payment order must carry the correct Banque de France transaction purpose code. Foreign-currency cash physically moved across French borders exceeds EUR 10,000 must be declared to customs, and payments routed through non-bank offshore channels could violate Article L561-15 CMF regarding the obligation to use an established intermediary for qualifying sums.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), acting jointly with the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR) for banking prudential supervision and AML/CTF enforcement; legal foundation is the French Code Monétaire et Financier Articles L151-1 to L165-5, supplemented by E.U. Regulation (EC) No 924/2009 on cross-border SEPA payments and E.U. Regulation (EU) 2015/847 on information accompanying funds transfers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High for E.U.-authorized electronic money institutions passported into France under PSD2 single-licence rules; Low-to-Medium for offshore or non-E.U. digital EMIs. Wise B2B is viable because Wise's Belgian EMI licence is passported into France; nevertheless it must be treated as a secondary disbursement utility and cannot replace the mandatory French bank relationship for statutory capital deposits, tax domiciliation, or legal clearing accounts. An entirely offshore EMI without E.U./EEA authorization may not lawfully provide payment services to a French-resident SARL, and reliance on such a rail without ACPR notification would create a KYC and licensing exposure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

At SARL formation the subscribed capital must be fully paid and deposited into a blocking account opened at a licensed French credit institution, before incorporation, or alternatively with a notaire or the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations. A non-resident shareholder should transfer the funds via SWIFT MT103 for non-EUR or via SEPA Credit Transfer for EUR, using the Banque de France capmov purpose code for 'capital subscription / constitution of société', the SARL's SIREN/SIRET number, the block-account IBAN, and the shareholder's full corporate/individual legal name and address. The bank will not release the blocked funds until the RCS Kbis certificate is produced; the account is then converted into the operational account. Post-incorporation capital injections by foreign parents should be documented as a share capital increase authenticated before a notaire, while shareholder current-account funding must be covered by a formal compte courant d'associé agreement with arm's-length interest, subordination wording and repayment schedule to prevent recharacterization as hidden distribution. Individual cross-border flows of EUR 50,000 or above are captured automatically by the receiving French bank in the monthly MEDOC-BEBA declaration to the Banque de France; no prior FX authorization is required.For E.E.A. creator payments the primary route is SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer using ISO 20022 pain.001 messages from the French operational account. For non-EEA creator payouts, disbursements must be executed by SWIFT MT103 through the bank's clearing network with full beneficiary name, account number, BIC/IBAN, address and precise payment narrative. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard corporate income tax rate (Impôt sur les Sociétés, effective for fiscal years opened on or after 1 January 2022); 15% reduced rate applies to the first EUR 42,000 of taxable profit for qualifying SMEs (turnover below EUR 10 million).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

12% under domestic law (CGI Article 119 bis 2); increased to 75% when the beneficiary is domiciled in a non-cooperative state (ETNC); 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for an EU parent holding at least 10% of voting rights for an uninterrupted two-year period; treaty rates generally 0% to 15% depending on the DTA.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Full French transfer pricing documentation (Master File and Local File) is mandatory when the gross annual turnover of the French entity, or of the French group head, exceeds EUR 400 million (CGI Article L.13 AA); below this threshold, a simplified documentation obligation applies, upon request of the tax administration, where annual turnover exceeds EUR 50 million and intra-group transactions with a single related party exceed EUR 100,000; a lower standard arm's-length documentation file should nevertheless be prepared for all material intercompany flows.

Economic substance scope: France does not apply offshore-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) as in the Caribbean or Gulf jurisdictions; instead, substance is enforced through the general anti-abuse rule (abus de droit, Article L.64 of the Livre des Procédures Fiscales), the French tax-residency rule based on effective management (siège de direction effective, Article 209 CGI), DAC6 mandatory disclosure, and the principal purpose test (PPT) in tax treaties. A French SARL acting as a TikTok MCN agency must demonstrate real business substance - local employees, physical presence, French bank accounts, and management decisions taken in France - to prevent recharacterization as a conduit, denial of treaty benefits, and reassessment at the level of the beneficial owners.
