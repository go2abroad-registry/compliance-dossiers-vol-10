Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No legally fixed minimum share capital for a SARL. The articles must state a capital amount; cash contributions must be subscribed and at least 20% of any cash share capital must be paid up before registration. The paid-up portion must be deposited into a blocked escrow account held with a French bank, Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, financial institution, or notary; the balance is payable within five years of incorporation. In-kind contributions must be fully paid up upon transfer.

Funding & unlock path:

Paid-in capital rule: No legally fixed minimum share capital for a SARL. The articles must state a capital amount; cash contributions must be subscribed and at least 20% of any cash share capital must be paid up before registration. The paid-up portion must be deposited into a blocked escrow account held with a French bank, Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations, financial institution, or notary; the balance is payable within five years of incorporation. In-kind contributions must be fully paid up upon transfer.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

Although the target ISIC activity is a service-sector study-abroad and immigration visa consultancy, no industrial manufacturing license is required. The relevant administrative authority is the Direction Départementale de l'Emploi, du Travail et des Solidarités (DDETS) for labour compliance and the local municipality for business-premises and fire-safety clearance. If the company operates a physical office open to the public, an 'autorisation d'exploitation' or 'permis de construire' may be required by the planning authority. Therefore, the competent industrial authority is defined as Direction Départementale des Territoires (DDT) for structural safety and Directorate of Labour for occupational compliance.

Category: No official industrial licence title applies to an international student placement and immigration visa advisory office. The company holds a Kbis extract as its general operating licence. Where a physical client-reception office is opened, the operating authorization is the 'Certificat d'Occupation' or 'Permis de Construire' issued by the local town hall, together with the registration of the premises for fire-safety and accessibility.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) under the Ministère de l'Économie, des Finances et de la Souveraineté industrielle et numérique; main portals: impots.gouv.fr (declaration and payment of TVA/IS), formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr (Guichet Unique des Formalités des Entreprises for TIN issuance), and the DGDDI (Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects) for customs/export clearance. Intra-community VAT validation via the EU VIES system.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the SARL by filing the M0 creation file through the Guichet Unique (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr); INSEE automatically allocates the SIREN (9-digit national tax/statistical identifier) and SIRET (14-digit establishment number), and the DGFiP issues the n° TVA intracommunautaire (format FR + 2-digit key + SIREN) within 7–15 working days. Step 2: Open a corporate bank account in France and file the statement of existence (liasse fiscale 2065-SD) at the competent SIE (Service des Impôts des Entreprises); electronic filing via EDI–TDFC/EFI is compulsory. Step 3 (foreign non-established supplier variant): a supplier not established in France must file a direct VAT registration application with the DGFiP (Cerfa n° 16161*01) at the non-resident taxpayer office (SIE non-résidents, Noisy-le-Grand) before the first taxable transaction; EU-established suppliers register directly, whereas non-EU suppliers must appoint an accredited fiscal representative with a bank guarantee (Art. 164 quinquies A CGI). Step 4: obtain an AEO/status certificates if customs export flows are contemplated; no separate national tax ID is otherwise required for an SARL.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — an SARL is by default a separate taxable person subject to French corporate income tax (impôt sur les sociétés, IS; standard rate 25% for 2025) on its worldwide results (Art. 206-1 and 209-I CGI); there is no unlimited territorial scope for an SARL resident in France. Pass_Through_Eligible — a qualifying commercial SARL whose members are all natural persons may irrevocably opt for income-tax transparency (imposition à l'impôt sur le revenu des associés) for a 5-fiscal-year period under Art. 239 bis AA CGI; no 'check-the-box' mechanism exists in French law. Tax_Exempt_Zone — not applicable for metropolitan France; no free-zone status affects a study-abroad/visa SARL.

Available election forms

(i) Art. 239 bis AA CGI option to be taxed as a société de personnes (IR) instead of IS — 5-year irrevocable commitment, available to SARLs with exclusively natural-person shareholders exercising a commercial, industrial, artisanal or certain liberal activities; (ii) VAT small-business option: renunciation of the franchise en base de TVA exemption (Art. 293 B CGI) to become a fully taxable person and recover input VAT — generally recommended for B2B services providers; (iii) VAT accounting options: election of the cash-basis régime (TVA sur les encaissements) or the simplified annual régime (régime simplifié d'imposition) instead of the normal monthly régime; (iv) IF group taxation (intégration fiscale, Art. 223 A CGI) once a French parent acquires ≥95% of qualifying subsidiaries; (v) no hybrid-mismatch or check-the-box election exists in France — such configurations must use treaty MFN/EOI routes instead.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains full euro-area convertibility under Article 63 TFEU and the French Monetary and Financial Code. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted: cross-border service fees, tuition disbursements and management charges may be paid without any exchange-control authorisation, subject only to balance-of-payments (BOP) statistical disclosure to the Banque de France when the transaction exceeds the EUR 12,500 per-operation reporting threshold (bank-captured ISO 20022 purpose codes). Capital-account convertibility allows 100% non-resident ownership and free injection of share capital or shareholder advances; no prior FX approval or conversion requirement applies. Profit-remittance hurdle is purely tax-driven: distributions to a non-resident SARL shareholder incur French withholding taxation at the statutory 25% (treaty-reducible), requiring the distributing company to hold signed board minutes, financial statements, and a French tax residence certificate to evidence treaty eligibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender, conversion ratio or timeline exists under French or euro-area law for export proceeds. Foreign-currency service and education-related export receipts may be retained indefinitely in multicurrency accounts, with only the Banque de France BOP statistical return applying above the EUR 12,500 threshold. For this study-abroad and immigration advisory (ISIC 7810) business, export proceeds comprise cross-border advisory and placement fees; all are free from surrender obligations and may be invoiced in EUR or foreign currency at arm's length.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), Eurosystem member; foreign-exchange and treasury oversight auxiliary to the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury) and prudentially enforced by the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR) for credit institutions and payment institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM with material legal constraints. A non-French digital EMI may operate in France only under an EU PSD2/EMD2 passport registered with the ACPR; without this registration it cannot hold client money, open an FR IBAN, or access SEPA/TARGET2 clearing. Escrow and 'séquestre' functions are legally reserved to credit institutions holding a banking licence, so offshore EMIs cannot deliver the capital escrow account required for this SARL. Because ISIC 7810 immigration/visa advisory is internally classified Medium_Risk_Enhanced, French fintechs (notably Qonto, Mango Pay via ACPR passport, and N26 France) will generally accept only the operational account after full RCBE extraction, invoicing evidence, and a physical-interview waiver; they remain an auxiliary treasury rail rather than a substitute for tier-1 bank clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) the foreign shareholder remits contribution via SWIFT MT103 (non-EEA) or SEPA Credit Transfer carrying the beneficiary SARL's IBAN and BIC, with ISO 20022 purpose code 'CAP.INCREASE' (capital increase) or French instruction 'APPORT CAPITAL'; (2) the receiving French bank credits the funds to a blocked funds account (compte bloqué) and issues the statutory 'certificat de dépôt des fonds' attesting availability; (3) the notary proceeds with the capital-increase modification of the SARL articles; (4) funds are released into the operational account upon completion of the legal formality; (5) the Banque de France BOP statistical return for the direct investment is filed through the bank's reporting gateway above the EUR 12,500 threshold; (6) verification is completed by matching the remitter account name to the RBE beneficial-owner register, obtaining the shareholder subscription minutes, and confirming the origin-of-funds statement; the SARL also keeps the deposit certificate as evidence for French tax and anti-money-laundering inspections.Settlement hierarchy: (a) SEPA Instant / SEPA Credit Transfer for EUR payments to university and partner beneficiaries in the EEA; E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM with material legal constraints. A non-French digital EMI may operate in France only under an EU PSD2/EMD2 passport registered with the ACPR; without this registration it cannot hold client money, open an FR IBAN, or access SEPA/TARGET2 clearing. Escrow and 'séquestre' functions are legally reserved to credit institutions holding a banking licence, so offshore EMIs cannot deliver the capital escrow account required for this SARL. Because ISIC 7810 immigration/visa advisory is internally classified Medium_Risk_Enhanced, French fintechs (notably Qonto, Mango Pay via ACPR passport, and N26 France) will generally accept only the operational account after full RCBE extraction, invoicing evidence, and a physical-interview waiver; they remain an auxiliary treasury rail rather than a substitute for tier-1 bank clearing.
