Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France provides full current-account and capital-account convertibility under Article 63 TFEU and Directive 88/361/EEC; the euro is freely convertible and there are no exchange-control approvals, surrender obligations or conversion quotas. Profit remittances (dividends repaid by the SAS to foreign shareholders, repayment of shareholder current accounts, royalties) are unrestricted as to amount and direction, subject to anti-money-laundering identification and reporting obligations (EU Regulation 2015/847 travel rule; FATF/ACPR standards) and, where applicable, French withholding taxes or double-tax-treaty reduced rates. Practical statutory constraints: any cross-border payment received by the SAS in excess of €12,500 triggers Banque de France balance-of-payments reporting (MESURE system) and therefore requires clear purpose codes; cash business-to-business settlement in France is capped at €1,000 per invoice under Article L.112-6 of the Code Monétaire et Financier.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are none. Since the full liberalisation of French foreign-exchange controls (effective 1990), exporters such as the freeze-dried pet-food manufacturing SAS may keep export proceeds in EUR or any convertible currency without a repatriation deadline and without any mandatory conversion ratio. Compliance is limited to: (i) the automatic balance-of-payments declaration to the Banque de France by the bank for each transfer equal to or exceeding €12,500; (ii) the lodgement of export declarations with the French customs administration (DGDDI) via the Dematélisation single-window for animal-origin products; and (iii) documentary compliance with veterinary and health certification (TRACES) for raw-meat derived goods destined to non-EU buyers.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France, acting as the national central bank of the Eurosystem under the European Central Bank) exercises monetary policy, payments-market oversight and balance-of-payments data collection; foreign-exchange administration and international financial relations are handled by the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury) under the Code Monétaire et Financier (Articles L.411-1 et seq. and R.152-7). The ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution) is the prudential regulator licensing and supervising all French credit institutions and payment institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium — EU/EEA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions with an ACPR or passported e-money licence (EMD2/PSD2) are workable as supplementary rails for low-value marketplace receipts (e.g., Platform_Pay collection sub-account), with safeguarding of client funds in a segregated account; they cannot provide overdraft, trade finance, factoring, FX hedging or documentary-credit services, and French Tier-1 correspondent banks often refuse to open nostro accounts for EMIs, making the EMI non-operable for high-value injections and supplier LC disbursements. Offshore (non-EU) digital EMIs and neo-banks are not permitted to open payment accounts for a French-domiciled SAS unless they hold an ACPR branch licence (Articles L.526-3 and L.526-35 CMF); in practice, their non-standard KYC files and absence of a French establishment are underlying red flags under ACPR doctrine. Recommended structure: main treasury in a Tier-1 French bank, a passported EU EMI (Luxembourg/France/Lithuania licensed) for platform collections, with a daily auto-sweep of the EMI balance into the Tier-1 account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Standard compliant routing: (i) the contributing shareholder issues a SWIFT MT103 transfer in EUR (TARGET2; if the sending bank is a third-country institution outside the EEA, SEPA is not available, so an MT202 COV correspondent leg is added to the parent bank's EUR nostro); (ii) remittance instructions must contain (a) the French balance-of-payments purpose code 'S211' for direct investment in an operating affiliate or 'F120' for intra-group loans, (b) the beneficiary identifier being the SAS name and IBAN of the operational treasury account, and (c) a reference to the capital-contribution or shareholder-loan contract. The receiving French bank will automatically file the MESURE balance-of-payments report to Banque de France when the transferred amount equals or exceeds €12,500; no prior authorisation is required. For contributions above €500,000, the bank expects the SAS to provide a certified decision of the associé unique (sole shareholder), the executed deed, and a notarised certificate of funds to evidence the legal origin; non-EUR injections are converted on the spot or pre-hedged, and any later FX sale generates a dealing ticket that serves as transfer-pricing evidence for tax purposes.For procurement of raw freeze-dried meat, packaging and logistics: (1) intra-EU/SEPA payments — SEPA Credit Transfer and SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (now capped at €100,000 per payment under the revised EU Instant Payment Regulation) for low-value invoices, with TARGET2 same-day settlement for high-value EUR; (2) third-country disbursements — SWIFT MT103 (cover method MT202 COV) over the bank's correspondent network, with US Dollar legs via Fedwire/CHIPS, GBP via CHAPS, and CNY via CIPS where the receiving bank maintains a renminbi account; (3) documentary trade — irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit issued by the French house bank under UCP 600 (with optional confirmation or silent confirmation for high-risk supplier geographies), standby L/Cs for supplier advance-payment risk, and documentary collections under URC 522 for frequent moderate-value parcels; (4) digital market rails — Platform_Pay and consumer-payment wallets may be used for sample sales and small-batch order collection, but cannot replace direct treasury payments above de minimis thresholds.Deliverable forward purchase and sale contracts (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, EUR/CNY) for hedged commercial flows, with 1-24 month tenors under an ISDA 1998 FX or 2021 FX Definitions Master, provided the SAS demonstrates a real underlying exposure (Article L.211-40 CMF) and maintains a board-approved hedging policyNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in restricted-delivery currencies (INR, KRW, TWD, ARS) permitted under ACPR market-conduct and EMIR rules where physical delivery is legally impossible, with daily mark-to-market and CSA-style collateralisationCross-currency swaps (fixed/fixed and fixed/floating EUR-USD, EUR-GBP) for structural hedging of shareholder loans, capex financing or multi-year export contracts, documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with Credit Support Annex and subject to EMIR clearing-threshold assessment by the French house bankFX swaps (rollovers) and Tom/Spot swaps as short-term liquidity-bridging instruments between inbound foreign-currency export receipts and outbound EUR purchase orders, executed with the Tier-1 house bank at market-quoted swap points
