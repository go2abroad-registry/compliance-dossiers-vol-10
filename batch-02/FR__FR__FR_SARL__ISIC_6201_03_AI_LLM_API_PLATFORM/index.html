UBO Ultimate Beneficial Owner Screening Rules Official Registry Only

>=25% ownership / control

Applicable AML statute: Code monétaire et financier, Articles L. 561-2 et seq. and R. 561-1 et seq. govern AML/CFT obligations and beneficial-owner identification for persons within scope. Code de commerce, Articles L. 561-46 and R. 561-55 et seq. govern beneficial-owner declarations to the national register. A beneficial owner generally includes a natural person who ultimately owns more than 25% of capital or voting rights, or otherwise exercises control. Registration obligations and access to beneficial-owner information are subject to current French law and applicable EU law; access is not unrestricted public access.
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A SARL must have a registered office in France. A domiciliation provider may be used if it is authorized and the company enters into a compliant domiciliation agreement. A virtual office may satisfy the registered-office requirement, but it does not by itself establish operational substance, satisfy banking or payment-provider onboarding, or demonstrate compliance with tax, employment, consumer, data-protection, and sector-specific obligations. An AI API business should maintain records and operational arrangements proportionate to its activities and customer base.

Statutory Name Clearance & Restricted Words

Official search database

INPI Guichet unique des formalités des entreprises (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr); the National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI) also provides trademark and business-name search services

Name reservation / lock period

France does not generally provide a statutory company-name reservation period comparable to a 30-day reservation. Search the INPI and relevant business registers before filing; conduct a separate trademark clearance search. If a filing is rejected or a formal deficiency is notified through the Guichet unique, follow the stated correction period and submit the requested evidence or amended filing. Request the written grounds and use the administrative or judicial remedy identified in the decision, observing its stated deadline and competent forum. A name-availability search is not a guarantee against later opposition or infringement claims.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum capital; capital may be set at €1. If capital is paid in cash, it must be deposited in a blocked bank account or with a notary (Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations) until registration; in-kind contributions must be fully paid up at incorporation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification for office premises (ERP – Établissement Recevant du Public) if open to public; otherwise, compliance with workplace safety codes (Code du travail) including electrical safety, emergency exits, and occupational health checks. No special structural load inspections required for standard office use.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) via the impots.gouv.fr portal; VAT registration is managed by the Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE) and the Direction Régionale des Douanes et Droits Indirects for customs-adjacent matters.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident entity must appoint a French tax representative for VAT purposes unless established in the EU or covered by the One Stop Shop regime. Registration for a French VAT number (numéro de TVA intracommunautaire) is filed with the SIE using Form 1515-sd/1560-sd and is validated through the national SIREN/SIRET registry. Domestic SARLs obtain automatically their SIREN/SIRET upon incorporation, with corporate income tax registration effected via the Déclaration d'identité (Form 14795-SD) and completion of the annual corporate tax return (Form 2065-SD). The tax ID for corporation tax is the SIREN; the VAT ID is issued after objective taxable activity is confirmed.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

A SARL is by default subject to French corporate income tax (impôt sur les sociétés). It may elect, subject to conditions and shareholder consent, to be taxed as a pass-through under impôt sur le revenu for a maximum five-year period if it qualifies as a small or medium-sized enterprise whose members are natural persons participating in management or in a family SARL. France has no general check-the-box regime; group consolidation is available through the 'intégration fiscale' mechanism for French-incorporated subsidiaries. Hybrid mismatch rules under ATAD and Article 212 bis CGI apply only to qualified domestic or cross-border arrangements, not as a general entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 85,000 for supplies of goods and EUR 37,500 for supplies of services for established small businesses under the franchise en base exemption. Non-established foreign suppliers of digital services have no registration threshold and must register for French VAT or use the EU One Stop Shop form the first euro of taxable supplies where the place of supply is France.

Mandatory e-invoicing for domestic B2B transactions applies under the 2026-2028 phased reform, transmitted through approved dematerialized platforms connected to the Portail Public de Facturation (PPF). B2B invoices must be issued in structured electronic formats (EN 16931 or PEPPOL) with guaranteed electronic transmission and audit trail. E-reporting of cross-border B2B and B2C transactions is mandatory; blockchain stamping or real-time 'scontrino' clearance is not used in France. For AI API platform sales, customer-issued invoices and transaction logs must be retained in the PPF-compatible archive for six years and enable indirect tax verification.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France maintains a fully convertible Euro currency regime under EU law. Capital account transactions are unrestricted for residents, with profit remittances permitted without prior approval. Standard statistical reporting to the Banque de France applies to transactions exceeding €100,000.

Export proceeds surrender rules

French exporters must repatriate foreign currency receipts within 90 days; no mandatory central bank surrender or conversion ratio exists. Foreign currency accounts may be maintained for operational liquidity.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France

Stripe Fit: High
