Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce in the territorial jurisdiction of the registered office, accessed through the formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr or Infogreffe electronic guichet.

Governing statute

French Commercial Code (Code de commerce), Articles L.210-1 to L.210-9 (general company provisions), L.223-1 to L.223-43 (SARL formation and management), and R.223-1 to R.223-22 (registration formalities).

Formation / constitutional filings

Déclaration de constitution d'une société (M0) filed together with the statuts and annexes.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital for a SARL (Article L.223-2 Code de commerce). The Articles of Association may set the capital freely, but the entire subscribed cash capital must be fully paid up and deposited in an escrow-account with a French bank or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations before registration. In-kind contributions must be delivered before signature of the statuts; if the contributions have an indeterminate value, a commissaire aux apports must report to the partners.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety inspection and certification by the relevant municipal or departmental fire and rescue service for all premises providing workstations; structural load inspection certificate for any container stacking area or racking; and an occupational-employment clearance issued by the DDETS confirming compliance with the Code du travail concerning access to work, evacuation routes, emergency equipment and workplace medical checks.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital for a SARL (Article L.223-2 Code de commerce). The Articles of Association may set the capital freely, but the entire subscribed cash capital must be fully paid up and deposited in an escrow-account with a French bank or with the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations before registration. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) – portal: impots.gouv.fr; corporate income tax filings and VAT administration via the local Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must file a French VAT registration application form (Form 1550-EU-SD) with the competent SIE before commencing taxable supplies. Non-EU entities ordinarily apply through the required French Establishment (déclaration d'établissement) or appoint a tax agent if no permanent establishment exists; the SIE then issues the French TIN and individual VAT number. The SARL itself is obtained by registration at the Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (CFE) with the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce, followed by automatic assignment of the SIRET and VAT identification.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity must file a French VAT registration application form (Form 1550-EU-SD) with the competent SIE before commencing taxable supplies. Non-EU entities ordinarily apply through the required French Establishment (déclaration d'établissement) or appoint a tax agent if no permanent establishment exists; the SIE then issues the French TIN and individual VAT number. The SARL itself is obtained by registration at the Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (CFE) with the Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce, followed by automatic assignment of the SIRET and VAT identification.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No general check-the-box election exists in France. Tax consolidation (intégration fiscale) is available under Articles 223 A et seq. of the Code Général des Impôts for groups of at least 95% controlled resident companies. Hybrid mismatch elections are not elective; the anti-hybrid rules under ATAD (Articles 210 ter et seq. CGI) apply mandatorily. Certain joint ventures may elect for transparent treatment only in narrow, statute-defined circumstances.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic small-business thresholds for established French businesses: €85,000 annual turnover for goods and €37,500 for services (franchise en base). Non-established foreign suppliers have no de minimis registration threshold and must register for French VAT before supplying taxable goods or services inside France, subject to OSS simplification for intra-Community distance sales beyond the annual EU-wide threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods dispatched or transported outside the European Union under Article 262(I) of the CGI when the supplier holds valid proof of export. For services, zero-rating applies mainly to international transport and directly related services; intermodal container leasing supplied to a non-resident operator for use in international transport may qualify where the service is directly linked to the export or import of goods, provided the recipient is established outside France and the service is not effectively used in the French domestic economy.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration d'Exportation – DAU)
  • Bill of Lading (Connaissement) or AWB / CMR transport document
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Avis d'Accréditation / Preuve de paiement en devises)

Mandatory B2B e-invoicing applies through the Chorus Pro portal or certified dematerialization platform (Plateforme de Dématérialisation Partenaire) under the progressive schedule applicable from 1 September 2026 for large and medium-sized enterprises and 1 September 2027 for small and mid-sized enterprises, with real-time e-reporting of B2C transaction data and fiscal clearance through reactive data transmission (F format). All invoicing must include compliant QR codes and a regulatory digital signature where required.

The tax administration may disregard zero-rating and reassess VAT at 20% if the export documentation is incomplete, inaccurate, or non-contemporaneous. Deliberate concealment or false documentation constitutes tax fraud (fraude fiscale) and triggers a 100% VAT collection penalty (majoration de 100%), interest at 0.20% per month, and a fine of €750,000 plus imprisonment for executive officers under Article 1741 CGI. Export refund claims falsely substantiated are automatically clawed back with penalties and may trigger criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

France operates under full capital account convertibility as an EU member state with no restrictions on profit remittances subject to corporate tax compliance and beneficial ownership declarations. Current account transactions benefit from automatic convertibility via authorized intermediaries with no capital controls applicable to resident French entities.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts must be surrendered to an authorized bank within one month of receipt, converted to EUR at the official ECB reference rate, with supporting documentation required for repatriation including commercial invoices, transport documents, and proof of delivery.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Central Bank of France) and European Central Bank (ECB)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Limited viability due to stringent French EMI regulations under AMF and ECB oversight; offshore digital EMIs face licensing barriers and AML compliance mandates rendering cross-border operations non-compliant for French entities.
