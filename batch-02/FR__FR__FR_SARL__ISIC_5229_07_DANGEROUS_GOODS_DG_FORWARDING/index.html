Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum for an SARL; the capital is freely fixed in the Statuts. The capital must be fully subscribed by the associates, and cash contributions must be paid up to at least one-fifth (20%) at incorporation, with the balance due within five years. For dangerous goods forwarding, the transport licensing authority additionally requires a financial guarantee (garantie financière) covering the forwarder's civil liability, maintained for the duration of registration on the Registre des commissionnaires de transport.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the local Commission de Sécurité (avis de la commission de sécurité) including verification of means of fire-fighting, 'permis de feu' for hot works, and emergency response plans under the 'Plan de Prévention des Risques' (Risks); structural load inspection by an approved technical control bureau (bureau de contrôle — SOCOTEC/Apave) confirming floor loading capacity for racked DG pallet storage; occupational health clearance by the service de prévention et de santé au travail (occupational medical service) including pre-employment medical fitness, ATEX zoning classification and certification of electrical equipment for explosive atmospheres; installation of gas and flammable liquid detection, lightning-protection conformity assessment, and submission of the internal emergency and security plan (Plan d'Opération Interne) to DREAL where required by the authorization.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direction Générale des Finances Publiques (DGFiP) — portal impots.gouv.fr. VAT registration for non-established operators is handled by the competent Service des Impôts des Entreprises (SIE). Customs and EORI matters are under Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (DGDDI), portal douane.gouv.fr.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity with a French VAT obligation but no French establishment must file an application for French VAT identification with DGFiP, attaching: certificate of incorporation, foreign VAT number, proof of address and bank details, description of supplied services, and, if non-EU established, a formal commitment to appoint a French tax representative. DGFiP issues a VAT identification number with the 'FR' prefix. In parallel, the entity obtains an EORI number from DGDDI. If the foreign entity creates a French fixed establishment or operates through the SARL, the SARL must complete French company and establishment registration with the SIE and the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity with a French VAT obligation but no French establishment must file an application for French VAT identification with DGFiP, attaching: certificate of incorporation, foreign VAT number, proof of address and bank details, description of supplied services, and, if non-EU established, a formal commitment to appoint a French tax representative. DGFiP issues a VAT identification number with the 'FR' prefix. In parallel, the entity obtains an EORI number from DGDDI. If the foreign entity creates a French fixed establishment or operates through the SARL, the SARL must complete French company and establishment registration with the SIE and the Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

France does not apply a check-the-box entity classification regime. A SARL is by default subject to corporate income tax (impôt sur les sociétés). Limited income-tax passthrough treatment is possible only for certain qualifying small SARLs under Article 239 bis AA CGI where all shareholders are individuals and functional criteria are met. A French resident parent may elect tax consolidation under Article 223 A CGI for qualifying wholly-owned subsidiaries, and ATAD-based hybrid mismatch limitations apply to deny deductions in defined cross-border cases.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 91,900 of annual turnover excluding tax for sales of goods and EUR 36,800 for services under the VAT franchise regime, with stepped lower thresholds; for DG freight-forwarding services, the relevant general threshold is EUR 36,800, but B2B supplies may trigger separate registration obligations where France is the place of supply absent an exempt threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods to destinations outside the European Union qualify for 0% VAT exemption provided the goods physically leave French/EU customs territory and the supplier receives and retains documentary proof of export within the statutory period. For DG freight-forwarding services, transports directly tied to exported dangerous goods can qualify under the ancillary export service criteria if the service is supplied to the exporter and the existence of the export is duly evidenced.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Déclaration en Douane / SAD lodged via DGDDI Delta), showing tax status 'AT' for VAT exemption
  • Bill of Lading for sea freight or Air Waybill for air freight, evidencing actual physical departure from the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT wire / Letter of Credit record proving foreign buyer settlement and repatriation of export proceeds
  • Signed commercial invoice, packing list, and UN-certified Dangerous Goods Declaration (IMDG / IATA DGD) for regulated hazardous cargo

Mandatory domestic B2B e-invoicing is being rolled out by DGFiP from 1 September 2026 for large enterprises and from 1 September 2027 for SMEs, using structured formats (Factur-X / UBL) transmitted through the Portail Public de Facturation (PPF) or certified dematerialization platforms (PDPs). Cross-border e-reporting to DGFiP is mandatory for B2B and B2C data. France does not use transactional digital stamping or per-invoice real-time fiscal clearance, but all relevant payments and invoice data must remain accessible for automated inspections and the statutory accounting file (Fichier des Écritures Comptables).

DGFiP may recharacterize an export zero-rating claim as taxable if the documentary bundle is incomplete or the goods are not shown to have left France/EU within the required time. A reassessment triggers VAT at 20% plus late interest (intérêt de retard), and, where intent or bad faith is established, penalty surcharges of 40% to 80% under Article 1729 CGI. Fraudulent abuse of export exemption or false customs declarations may lead to criminal penalties, including fines up to €50,000 and imprisonment, alongside retrospective clawback of refunded input VAT.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Euro is fully convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions; France applies the EU Treaty's free movement of capital (TFEU Article 63) and has abolished administrative exchange-control authorization for transfers within and into the Eurozone. There is no prior-approval regime for foreign-currency purchases, foreign-currency account opening (USD/GBP/CHF/JPY), or dividend and profit repatriation, but France retains statistical balance-of-payments (BOP) verification: each resident undertaking settlement with a non-resident at or above EUR 12,500 must file a 'déclaration de règlement' with the Banque de France through its licensed bank. Profit remittance to a non-resident shareholder is legally unrestricted and subject only to a reduced withholding tax under the applicable tax treaty (default 25% domestic rate), plus the ordinary obligation to document the distribution with board minutes and audited financial statements. The practical barrier for a hazardous-goods freight forwarder is not convertibility but the bank's heightened AML/KYC and sanctions-compliance documentation required to open and maintain the operating accounts that clear the flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

France imposes no export-proceeds surrender ratio, no mandatory conversion of foreign-currency export receipts, and no statutory deadline for the repatriation of export revenue; a French SARL may lawfully hold USD and other currency collections in a domestic foreign-currency account or convert them into EUR at times of its choosing. The only binding obligation is the Banque de France balance-of-payments statistical declaration for any transaction with a non-resident equal to or greater than EUR 12,500; this declaration is informational only and carries no conversion or surrender consequence, and in practice the bank executes the declaration on the client's behalf via its BOP reporting channel.

Central bank supervision: Banque de France (Bank of France), operating in coordination with the Direction Générale du Trésor (French Treasury) for foreign-exchange policy, with the ACPR (Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution) for prudential licensing and anti-money-laundering supervision, and with AMF (Autorité des Marchés Financiers) for market and ISDA-framework oversight; all powers are codified in the French Monetary and Financial Code (Code monétaire et financier, CMF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for core high-risk DG operations and letter-of-credit rails; MEDIUM only for auxiliary multi-currency expense accounts. ACPR-licensed and EU-passported electronic money institutions operating in France (notably Qonto, Shine, and Revolut Business) may open EUR, GBP, and USD IBANs with fast onboarding, but their transaction-risk policies commonly exclude freight forwarders dealing in regulated hazardous chemicals and their platforms cannot issue or advise documentary letters of credit (MT700) or provide Banque de France balance-of-payments reporting with the level of evidential detail expected for a High_Risk_Special_License entity. Use an EMI strictly for low-value operating disbursements such as software, insurance, and local subcontractor expenses; the principal clearing, escrow, and trade-finance flows must remain on Tier-1 commercial bank rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder capital injection must be routed through SWIFT MT103 (or RTGS/EURO1 equivalent for EU-origin funds) from the parent's bank to the SARL's EUR-denominated French operational account using the full beneficiary IBAN (FR76 + BIC) and the remittance narrative stating 'CAPITAL INJECTION - direct investment - [entity name and SIREN number].' The French receiving bank records the transaction under the Banque de France balance-of-payments classification for direct investment (investissements directs) and files the mandatory statistical declaration if the amount is at least EUR 12,500; no ex-ante authorization is required for an inbound investment, but the bank will request a notarized shareholder resolution and the updated Kbis to verify the sender's authority. If the injection is denominated in a non-euro currency such as USD, the French bank converts the funds at its spot rate on value date and credits the EUR account, or alternatively the parent may wire through a EUR correspondent account to avoid conversion friction and reduce cost. Post-closing, the SARL must ensure that the share capital increase is filed at the registry within one month and reflected in the Kbis, and in 2025 the intra-group treasury documentation should also include a term-sheet or intercompany funding agreement to substantiate the remittance purpose for both the bank and tax authorities.Primary clearing rail for DG trade settlements is the SWIFT MT103 message sent via SWIFT gpi for end-to-end tracking and same-day credit, with the field 70 remittance information containing the invoice reference, UN-number or HS tariff code, and transport document number; for larger or riskier purchases the SARL must use commercial letters of credit issued as MT700 by its French bank and advised through a confirming bank at the overseas seller, with MT707 amendments and MT710/MT720 transfers as required under UCP600. The bank will require ISO 20022 XML CBPR+ message formats for all payments and will insist that each instruction carries a Unique End-to-End Transaction Reference (UETR) and a valid purpose code (e.g., TRDP for trade payables); for payments to shipping companies, freight agents, and overseas warehouses, the SARL should maintain a standing approval matrix with dual signatories. Settlement of LC drawings is confirmed against presentation of the full document set (commercial invoice, bill of lading, DG packing certificate, insurance certificate, and certificate of origin), and any payment to a sanctioned state or blocked party is absolutely prohibited under the compliance policy.Outright FX Forwards (change à terme) against EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, and EUR/CHF, accessible for tenors up to 12 months under the French bank's ISDA 2002 Master Agreement, restricted to hedging documented DG receivables and payables under the 'besoin réel' (real need) doctrine.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) for currencies without a French-cleared deliverable market (e.g., KRW, IDR, PHP, TWD, and certain African franc-zone synthetic trades), permitted for commercial purposes only and settled in EUR or USD on the fixing rate.European plain vanilla FX Options (EUR/USD and EUR/GBP calls and puts) for asymmetric protection against adverse freight-rate or currency movements; barrier, exotic, and speculative option structures are prohibited for unregulated non-financial companies.Cross-Currency Interest Rate Swaps (CCIRS) and short-term FX swaps (change manuel) matching EUR-USD assets and liabilities when the SARL carries foreign-currency debt or long-dated DG contracts; each instrument requires an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement, a Credit Support Annex (CSA), and a delegation of authority to the corporate treasurer.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard (France: 25% corporate income tax rate from 2022; reduced 15% rate for small businesses on first €42,500 of profit under certain conditions)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Statutory withholding of 25% (Art. 119bis-2 CGI) at time of dividend payment to non-resident parent, subject to exemption/relief under EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive; final withholding rate 12.8% for EU-domiciled corporate shareholders where conditions met

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Multinational groups with headcount > 50 employees (or >400 for SME thresholds) and French turnover/ balance-sheet above €50 million (consolidated > €400m and >50 employees triggers Master/ Local File); local TP documentation (documentation de prix de transfert) mandatory under Art. L.13 AA of the LPF when the French entity exceeds €50m of transactions with related parties or €400m consolidated turnover or headcount above 50 (Form 2257 filing threshold: related-party transactions > or accounts > €50m); specifically Form 2257 (TP declaration) must be filed by entities with annual related-party turnover/gross assets above €50 million or group headcount above 50

Economic substance scope: France has no standalone ESR (Economic Substance Regulations) regime akin to UAE/DIFC; however, the doctrine of substance-over-form (substance prévaut sur la forme) plus Art. 1655 bis (abuse of law, abus de droit) require real economic activity; DG forwarding SARL must demonstrate actual dispatch centres, qualified ADR/DGSA (Transport of Dangerous Goods Safety Adviser) employment, dedicated hazmat warehousing and transport licences, and corporate substance; artificial arrangements lacking local decision-making trigger the GAAR (Art. L.64 LPF), the anti-artificial-arrangements doctrine, and can lead to recharacterization and 80% penalty plus 5-year criminal exposure

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Fraude fiscale)

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalties under Art. 1729 CGI: 40% majoration if bad faith, 80% if abus de droit or dissimulation of activity; criminal conviction under Art. 1741 CGI: up to €1,000,000 (increased to €2,000,000 if using bank accounts abroad) and prison up to 7 years (formerly 5), extended to 10 years for organised group

Director personal liability: Director (gérant) personally liable to tax administration for civil restitution of evaded tax, penalties and interest; criminal exposure includes travel bans and detention; supervisory board members can face joint liability; mises en demeure and mise en jeu de la responsabilité civile of Court of Cassation (Cass. crim. 2019) pierces the veil for fraudulent concealment
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement (Abus de droit)

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of profits under Art. 1655 bis CGI (abus de droit) and Art. L.64 LPF: 80% penalty on restated income plus late interest; denial of treaty benefits under MLI principal-purpose test; potential nullity of a licence under the Code du transport (regulatory suspension of the DG forwarding licence)

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of reassessed tax plus penalties; director travel bans through judicial control (interdiction de quitter le territoire) in money-laundering-linked proceedings; imprisonment of the gérant up to 7 years when abuse of law is characterized as tax evasion
AML / Money Laundering (Blanchiment)

Statutory sanction: Articles 324-1 et seq. French Criminal Code: up to 10 years imprisonment and €750,000 fine (or half the value of laundered funds if higher); Tracfin declarations for cash transactions ≥ €10,000; DG forwarding licence suspension and DNA-Type (Direction de la surveillance du territoire) scrutiny; asset freezing and confiscation under Art. 131-21 Penal Code

Director personal liability: The gérant and compliance officer face personal criminal liability; corporate veil lifting under Cass. crim. 2018; travel bans, freezing of personal bank accounts, and disqualification from professional forwarding activity for up to 10 years; statutory emergency custodial measure for hiding assets: additional 3 years imprisonment
